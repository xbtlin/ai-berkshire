# Valuation judgement

Cheap. PE 6.3 times, PB about 1.0 times, is in a historically low division. Compared to ROE 13% plus profitability, the current valuation gives a full margin of security. 5% of the dividends provide the minimum protection for holding.

7. Eastern wealth (300059.SZ)

# Valuation judgement

The real valuation may be higher than the surface, given the cyclical nature of performance (currently in active market life). If the market cools, profits may fall by 30-40%, and the PE will expand rapidly.
