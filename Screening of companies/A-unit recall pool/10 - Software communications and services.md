## 1. Kingsoft Office (688111.SH)

**Market value**: about 115 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 50 times

### Core investment logic

Domestic office software is the absolute leader, with 672 million monthly active users forming a data flywheel. AI transformation is accelerating (WPS AI monthly active users are 80 million, +307% year-on-year), and the innovation and innovation policy drives the replacement of government and enterprises. The business model upgrade from "selling licenses" to "selling AI services" is being realized.

### Moat analysis

The superposition of multiple layers of moats - user scale and habit barriers (672 million monthly active users), policy barriers for government and enterprise innovation (the government market is almost irreplaceable), switching costs accumulated in 30 years of document technology, and data barriers in the AI era.

### Valuation judgment

On the expensive side. Even taking into account the high growth expectations of AI, 50 times PE will still take a long time to digest unless the AI ​​payment rate increases rapidly.

## 2. China Mobile (600941.SH)

**Market capitalization**: about 2 trillion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 15 times | **Dividend rate**: about 4-5%

### Core investment logic

China's largest telecommunications operator, with a near-monopoly of user base (1 billion+ users) and network infrastructure. From "pipeline merchants" to "communications + technology", government and enterprise business and AI computing power have become the second growth curve. High dividends + low valuation constitute a solid return.

### Moat analysis

Extremely wide moat - license monopoly + infrastructure scale effect + 1 billion user base constitute triple barriers. The three oligarchs in the telecommunications industry have a stable structure and the possibility of price war is low. The huge investment in 5G/computing power network further raises the entry barrier.

### Valuation judgment

Cheap. 15 times PE corresponds to a reasonably low level among global telecommunications leaders, and the dividend rate of 4-5% is extremely attractive.

## 3. SF Holdings (002352.SZ)

**Market value**: about 190-200 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 16 times

China's mid-to-high-end express delivery market is the absolute leader, with a market share of over 60% for time-sensitive parcels. The international layout (the integration of Kerry Logistics and the commissioning of Ezhou Huahu Airport) opens up the space for global imagination. The expected profit in 2026 is 12 billion+.

Reasonable. 16 times PE corresponds to a profit of 12 billion, and the current pricing is basically reasonable.

## 4. Glodon (002410.SZ)

**Market value**: about 23 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 55-60 times

It is the absolute leader in the field of construction informatization, with a market share of over 70% in cost software. SaaS cloud transformation has entered the harvest period. AI+BIM is expected to open up a new growth dimension, but the prosperity of the construction industry is the key variable.

## 5. Focus Media (002027.SZ)

**Market value**: approximately 88-107 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 13-17 times

### Core investment logic

The absolute leader in the elevator media industry (market share 64%), the mandatory exposure scenario of "high reach + high frequency + high completion" is irreplaceable. Asset-light operations bring ultra-high ROE (30%+) and excellent cash flow. The advertising market is remarkably resilient during the recovery cycle.

### Moat analysis

First-mover advantage + scale effect form a moat - high-quality point resources in core cities are limited and most are locked in by focus (long-term leases), making it difficult for new entrants to obtain point networks of the same quality. However, the business structure is single and its performance is highly dependent on the macroeconomic cycle.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonably cheap. 13-17 times PE corresponds to the light asset and high ROE model, which is obviously low.

## 6. Hengsheng Electronics (600570.SH)

**Market value**: approximately 49 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 35 times

### Core investment logic

China's financial IT industry is the absolute leader, with core product lines accounting for 50-90% of the market. The digital transformation of financial institutions is a long-term trend, and AI-enabled investment research and trading will open up new growth space. Selected on the global FinTech 100 list for 15 consecutive years.

Strong customer stickiness is the core moat - the market share of securities trading systems is 50%, fund investment transactions are 83%, bank financial management is 80%, and trust asset management is 90%. Financial institutions' extreme requirements for system "stability + security" make switching costs extremely high.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonably expensive. The premium of 35 times PE corresponding to the financial IT leader is understandable, but it needs to be supported by profit growth.
