## 1. CATL (300750)

**Market capitalization**: 2.1 trillion | **PE(TTM)**: 25 times

### Core investment logic

The world's leader in power batteries, with a market share of 37%+, its scale effect and technology iteration speed far exceed its competitors. Net profit in 2025 is 72.2 billion (+42%), and the energy storage business has become the second growth curve, opening up the ceiling.

### Moat analysis

The technological moat is extremely deep: Kirin batteries, Shenxing superchargers, and condensed matter batteries continue to lead the industry. Scale moat: The world's largest production capacity and obvious cost advantage. Customer lock-in: Binding to Tesla, BMW, Mercedes-Benz and other global mainstream car companies, switching costs are extremely high. The brand effect enables it to enjoy a premium in energy storage tenders.

### Valuation judgment

25 times PE corresponds to a profit growth rate of 42%, and PEG<1 seems reasonable. However, the market value of 2.1 trillion means that future growth will inevitably slow down, and it is necessary to determine the long-term stable profit center.

## 2. BYD (002594)

**Market capitalization**: 1 trillion | **PE(TTM)**: 23 times

### Core investment logic

The global sales champion of new energy vehicles (4.27 million units), the vertical integration model (battery + chip + vehicle) builds strong cost control. Globalization is accelerating, and overseas revenue accounts for 38.7%, replicating China's successful path.

### Moat analysis

Vertical integration is the core barrier: from minerals to batteries to complete vehicles, the entire industry chain is self-developed and produced, and cost control is unmatched. The scale effect combined with the speed of technology iteration makes it difficult for competitors to catch up. The brand has made a breakthrough from low-end to high-end (looking up and gaining momentum), and its product matrix covers the price range of RMB 50,000 to RMB 1 million. However, the automotive industry is naturally highly competitive, and its moat is not as wide as that of consumer goods.

### Valuation judgment

23 times PE corresponds to a reasonable high growth rate, but the price war in the automobile industry continues, and whether profit margins can be maintained is a core variable. If the net profit margin is stable at 5%+, the current valuation will be attractive.

## 3. Sungrow (300274)

### Core investment logic

Global leader in inverters + largest energy storage business (37.3 billion/+49%), two-wheel drive. Overseas revenue exceeds 60%, enjoying high overseas gross profits. Integrated optical and storage solution capabilities build differentiated competitiveness.

### Moat analysis

Inverter technical barriers + brand recognition + global channel network build competitive advantages. It leads the world in energy storage system integration capabilities and has rich experience in large-scale ground power station projects. However, hardware products ultimately face price competition, and the width of the moat is limited. 2026Q1 profit -40% exposes the risk of performance volatility.

## 4. Inovance Technology (300124)

**Market capitalization**: 180 billion | **Static PE**: 42 times | **2026E PE**: 26 times

### Core investment logic

"China Siemens" is the core beneficiary of domestic substitution in the field of industrial control. The triple growth logic of general automation + new energy vehicle electric drive + humanoid robots is superimposed. ROE is 20%+ all year round, and the management has strong execution capabilities.

### Moat analysis

Technical barriers: Core industrial control products such as servo systems, frequency converters, and PLCs are self-developed, and their performance is close to or even surpasses that of foreign brands. Strong customer stickiness: Once industrial control products are embedded in the production line, switching costs are extremely high. R&D driven: R&D rate is 10%+, and we continue to expand categories. Humanoid robots are forward options. However, in the face of giants such as Siemens and ABB, globalization is still a shortcoming.

### Valuation judgment

The static PE of 42 times is too expensive, but if profits are realized in 2026, 26 times is reasonable. The core risk is that the valuation has been priced in to higher expectations, leaving little room for error.

## 5. Sanhua Intelligent Control (002050)

**Market capitalization**: 180 billion | **PE(TTM)**: 37 times

### Core investment logic

The hidden champion of thermal management, with a global market share of over 20%. The three major tracks of refrigeration and air conditioning + new energy vehicle thermal management + data center liquid cooling benefit from it at the same time. It is a rare cross-industry platform parts company.

### Moat analysis

The absolute leader in the segmented field: Electronic expansion valves account for over 50% of the global market, and technical barriers + scale effects create a deep moat. Customers include mainstream car companies and air-conditioning companies around the world, and switching costs are high. Data center liquid cooling is a new growth pole, benefiting from the explosion of AI computing power. The product may seem simple but requires extremely high precision manufacturing processes, making it difficult for latecomers to copy it.

### Valuation judgment

The PE of 37 times is obviously too expensive, and the market has fully priced in its growth potential. You need to wait for valuations to be digested or performance to exceed expectations to have a margin of safety.
