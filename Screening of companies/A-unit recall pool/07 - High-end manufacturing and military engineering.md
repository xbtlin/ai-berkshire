## 1. Sany Heavy Industry (600031.SH)

**Market capitalization**: approximately 187 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 18-22 times | **PB**: approximately 2.4 times

### Core investment logic

It is a global leader in construction machinery and ranks first in the global market share of excavators. Overseas revenue accounts for more than 50%, truly realizing the leap from "Chinese leader" to "global leader". Digital and intelligent transformation (Sroot Internet Industrial Internet Platform) + global layout are two-wheel drive. Construction machinery is at the bottom of the cycle, domestic operating rates have bottomed out and rebounded, and overseas markets continue to increase volume.

### Moat analysis

Brand + channel + service network constitute barriers to globalization. SANY has a complete agent and service network around the world. Once customers use SANY equipment, after-sales service and accessory supply form a lock-in effect. Digital intelligence capabilities (remote diagnosis, predictive maintenance) further deepen customer stickiness. The scale effect gives it an advantage on the cost side. But on the other hand, the construction machinery industry is obviously cyclical, and global giants such as Caterpillar and Komatsu still have technological advantages in the high-end market. Sany still lags behind international leaders in terms of profit margins.

### Valuation judgment

Valuations at the bottom of the cycle are reasonably low. A PE of 18-22 times does not seem low, but it is at the bottom of the industry cycle (profits are at a low level), and the PE corresponding to normalized earnings may be 12-15 times. PB 2.4x versus normalized ROE of 15%+ is attractive. The bottom of the construction machinery cycle is often the best time to buy.

## 2. AVIC Optoelectronics (002179.SZ)

**Market value**: approximately 78.5 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 28-35 times

### Core investment logic

It is the absolute leader in military connectors, with a market share of over 40% in the defense market. Connectors are the "nervous system" of weapons and equipment, and each generation of equipment upgrades requires higher density and higher reliability connectors. Technical barriers + military product certification barriers are extremely high, and it will take 5-8 years for new entrants to pass all qualification certifications. The civilian field (communications, new energy vehicles) is opening up the second growth curve.

### Moat analysis

Double barriers form a very strong moat. Technical barriers: High-reliability connectors involve in-depth accumulation of materials, processes, and designs, and the cost of failure is extremely high (equipment failure may lead to loss of life). Certification barriers: Military product suppliers need to pass strict confidentiality qualifications, quality system certification and installation verification, which take several years. Once imported, it is difficult to replace them. But on the other hand, the military industry is constrained by the defense budget and has a limited growth rate limit; and it is in an order adjustment period from 2023 to 2025, and its performance fluctuates greatly.

### Valuation judgment

Expensive but can wait. PE 28-35 times is at a mid-to-high historical level, corresponding to current profits (low due to the pace of orders). If military industry orders pick up and profits return to normal levels in 2026-2027, the forward-looking PE may drop to the 20-25 times range. A good track and a good company, but we need to wait until profits return to a reasonable level before evaluating the price/performance ratio.

### Rating

**Conclusion**: As a leader in military connectors, the dual barriers of technology and certification are extremely high. The current valuation is on the expensive side, and it is recommended to wait for profits to recover before conducting in-depth research.

## 3. Ziguang Guowei (002049.SZ)

**Market value**: about 59 billion yuan | **PE(2026E)**: about 38 times

### Core investment logic

The leader in special chips (military FPGA, security chips), with extremely high certification barriers. The entry threshold for the special chip market is extremely high (three barriers of confidentiality qualification + military product certification + technology accumulation). Once the product passes the installation verification, the probability of replacement is extremely low. With a gross profit margin of 60%+ and a net profit margin of 40%+, its profitability is among the top among A-share chip companies.

### Moat analysis

Certification barriers + technical barriers form a very strong moat. There are only 2-3 domestic suppliers of special FPGAs, and Ziguang Guowei leads the market share. The market share of security chips (SIM cards, bank cards, and ID cards) ranks among the top three in the world. But on the other hand, performance fluctuates greatly - military product orders have the characteristics of "big and small years", and 2024-2025 is at the trough of orders, with profits declining sharply; and the total scale of the special chip market is limited (tens of billions), and there is a growth ceiling.

### Valuation judgment

On the expensive side. PE(2026E) 38 times, corresponding to the expected recovery after the profit trough. If profit recovery falls short of expectations, valuations will come under pressure. Considering the performance volatility and growth ceiling, the current valuation lacks a sufficient margin of safety.

## 4. Green harmonics (688017.SH)

**Market value**: approximately 39 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 429 times

### Core investment logic

The leading domestically produced harmonic reducer breaks the long-term monopoly of Japan's Harmonica. The harmonic reducer is a core component of industrial robots and collaborative robots (accounting for 10-15% of the robot cost). It has high technical barriers and large room for domestic substitution. Humanoid robot narratives provide imaginative space for company valuations.

### Moat analysis

Technical barriers exist but are not insurmountable. Green's harmonics are close to the level of Harmonica in terms of accuracy, life and other indicators, and have advantages on the cost side. But on the other hand, domestic competitors such as Clarion and Double Ring Transmission are catching up quickly, and harmonic reducers are not a "winner-takes-all" market; and the growth rate of traditional industrial robots has slowed down, and it will take at least 3-5 years for large-scale mass production of humanoid robots, and there is a lack of short-term profit support.

### Valuation judgment

Seriously overrated. A PE of 429 times means that the current stock price is entirely driven by the forward expectations of humanoid robots, rather than supported by fundamentals. Even based on the most optimistic expected profit in 2027, the PE is still 80-100 times. This is a target of "dream pricing" rather than "value pricing". For value investors, current valuations do not offer any margin of safety.

**Conclusion**: Technical barriers to domestically produced harmonic reducers are real. But the 429 times PE is completely concept-driven, with zero margin of safety. Research is not recommended for the time being.
