# 2. Warlu Hisung Lt. (60426.SH)

# Core investment logic

The core competitiveness lies in flexible production platforms – the same set of devices can be flexible in switching products such as urea, DMF, adipic acid, acetic acid, etc., to produce the highest-profit product. Net profits are expected to be $6.8 billion in 2026 (compared to 17%). The start-up of the base in Xingzhou will open up new growth space.

Cheap. PE 9-10 times the corresponding ROE 15% plus a steady bonus. In the chemical industry, the low-cost advantage of Walluhein has enabled it to remain profitable even at the bottom of the cycle (and never lost in history), a certainty worth premium but not given by the market.

# A moat of water

The global city of Anselmi has a 50% + scale effect.
