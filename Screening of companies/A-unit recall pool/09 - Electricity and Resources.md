## 1. Yangtze Power (600900.SH)

**Market capitalization**: approximately 640 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: approximately 19.5 times | **Dividend rate**: approximately 3.6%

### Core investment logic

It exclusively owns six world-class cascade power stations on the main stream of the Yangtze River (Three Gorges, Gezhouba, Xiluodu, Xiangjiaba, Wudongde, Baihetan), and the six-tank joint debugging of "one drop of water generates six times of electricity" creates deterministic cash flow. In a low interest rate environment, high dividends (committed to not be less than 70%) + depreciation release (depreciation of the Three Gorges unit will gradually expire) constitute the certainty of long-term profit growth.

### Moat analysis

Supply-side resource monopoly is the ultimate moat - hydropower resources in the main stream of the Yangtze River are non-renewable, and development rights are granted by the state and have been fully injected into the company; the synergistic effect of the six reservoirs joint debugging contributes an additional 6-7 billion kilowatt-hours per year; policy barriers are extremely high, and there is no possibility of new entrants.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonable. The PE of 19.5 times is at a 3-year historical low, significantly lower than the power industry average of 29.7 times; the dividend rate of 3.6% is extremely attractive in a low interest rate environment.

## 2. China Shenhua (601088.SH)

**Market capitalization**: about 1 trillion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 15-16 times | **Dividend rate**: about 6%

### Core investment logic

The integrated coal, electricity and transportation industry chain has formed internal synergies, and its ability to withstand cycles far exceeds that of pure coal companies. After completing the 100 billion-level asset restructuring in 2025, resource reserves and profitability will further increase. High dividend yield (about 6%) + low valuation constitute a strong margin of safety.

### Moat analysis

The integrated industrial chain (coal mines + power plants + railways + ports + shipping) constitutes a systemic barrier that non-single-link competitors can copy; its coal resource reserves rank first in the country; its own transportation network reduces costs and ensures supply stability.

### Valuation judgment

Cheap and reasonable. The PE corresponding to the 2026 profit forecast is only 7-8 times, with a dividend of 6%, and a sufficient margin of safety.

## 3. Zijin Mining (601899.SH)

**Market value**: about 1 trillion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 14-15 times

### Core investment logic

The global mining expansion strategy has strong execution capabilities, and the dual main lines of gold and copper benefit from global inflation expectations and energy transformation trends. Output continues to grow at a high rate (the compound growth rate of copper and gold output from 2025 to 2027 is 15%+), and it has transformed from a "cyclical stock" to a two-wheel drive target of "resources + growth".

### Moat analysis

The moat is relatively limited - the essence of mining is a competition of resource acquisition capabilities + operational efficiency. Zijin's advantage lies in its management's strong project acquisition and mine operation capabilities (leading low-grade mine development technology), but it does not have monopoly resource barriers, and copper and gold prices are still the core variable.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonable. 14-15 times PE is reasonably low corresponding to the high growth stage, but the cyclical nature of mining stocks and the risk of gold and copper price fluctuations need to be considered.

## 4. Shaanxi Coal Industry (601225.SH)

**Market value**: about 220 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 13 times | **Dividend rate**: about 5%

### Core investment logic

Northern Shaanxi has outstanding endowments of high-quality coal mines (the lowest echelon of coal cost per ton in the industry), a stable high dividend policy (dividend rate is about 5%), and although the coal price center has moved downward, its profitability is strong. Profit growth is expected to resume in 2026 (estimated to be 19.6 billion, +17% year-on-year).

### Moat analysis

Resource endowment is the core advantage - the coal seams in the northern Shaanxi mining area are thick, shallow, and mining costs are low; however, the moat width is not as wide as China Shenhua's integrated model, and it is still essentially a single coal mining enterprise, which is greatly affected by the coal price cycle.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonable. The PE of 13 times is within the reasonable valuation range of the coal industry, and the dividend rate of 5% provides a safety cushion.

## 5. Huaneng Hydropower (600025.SH)

**Market value**: about 170 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 19-20 times

### Core investment logic

It owns the hydropower development rights in the Lancang River Basin and is the second largest hydropower operator after Yangtze River Power. New energy power stations have been put into operation one after another, opening up the second growth curve, and the integrated layout of water, wind, solar and energy storage has increased forward profits.

### Moat analysis

The resource monopoly logic is similar to that of Yangtze River Electric Power—the hydropower resources in the Lancang River Basin are monopolized and policy barriers are high. However, the width of the moat is slightly less than that of Yangtze River Electric Power: the basin is smaller and lacks the synergistic effect of the six reservoirs joint regulation.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonable. 19-20 times PE is equivalent to Yangtze Power, but its growth potential is slightly stronger (new energy is put into production).

## 7. SDIC Power (600886.SH)

**Market value**: about 120 billion yuan | **PE(TTM)**: about 16-17 times

### Core investment logic

The core asset is the controlled Yalong River Hydropower (the third largest hydropower domain in China), which has huge potential for integrated development of water, wind, solar and storage. The planned installed capacity in the Yalong River Basin exceeds 60 million kilowatts, less than half of which is currently developed, and there is clear room for long-term growth.

### Moat analysis

The exclusivity of development rights in the Yalong River Basin forms the core moat, a resource monopoly logic similar to that of Yangtze River Electric Power and Huaneng Hydropower. However, the company also has thermal power assets that drag down the overall asset quality.

### Valuation judgment

Reasonably low. The PE of 16-17 times is at a historical low, and the long-term development value of the Yalong River has not been fully priced.
