# The quality of business #

Business models are relatively clear: high-speed light modules are available for mega-data centres worldwide, essentially "sell shovels" for AI infrastructure. Revenues increase as AI capital spending increases, with customers like Google, Microsoft, Meta, and others. Net profits in 2025 are estimated at about $15 billion (estimated), and institutions expect $23-26 billion in 2026, with a very rapid increase.

But it is important to note that the nature of the light module is still manufacturing, with fast technology (400G 1,800G -11.6T) and a life cycle of about 2-3 years per generation, requiring sustained R & D investment to remain ahead of the curve. It is not a real "one-in-a-breeding" simple business. The Māori rate of 30% + is excellent in hardware manufacturing, but far from software or platform-based companies.

- ** Technology lead**: 800G market share over 50%, 1.6T leads 6-12 months of industry, "Mage generation, R & D generation, pre-research generation" rhythm
- **Customer binding**: Deep embedded in Google OCS ecology as core supplier of light modules, high customer conversion costs
- **Scaling effect**: the largest light module manufacturer in the world, with cost advantages resulting from capacity size and good rate control
- ** But on the other hand**: New Euphoria and other competitors are catching up fast, the light module industry has experienced a long succession in history (e.g. Finisar was acquired by II-VI), and technical barriers are not insurmountable

# Management quality #

The executive has done well in strategic judgement – the acquisition of the Sioux Xinjiang was a decisive step, with the recent technological deployment of the CDO, Silicon Light and other cutting edges.

# Valuing appeal #

Given the profit rate of 120-150%, PEG is about 0.3-0.5, which does not seem to be "unvalued." But the question is: the cyclical risk of AI’s capital spending, the cyclical fluctuations in the history of the light module industry (which fell dramatically in 2018-2019), and whether the current market value of trillion dollars adequately reflects optimistic expectations.

# A comprehensive score #

# One sentence to sum up

The absolute king of the AI light module track, the technological leader plus customer binding, constitute a strong moat, but the trillion market value has implied very high growth expectations, which are suitable for attention in the event of a panic or acceleration in the industry.

Semiconductor equipment is a typical "sell shovel to gold-digger" model. Once the client has certified the equipment, the replacement costs are extremely high (re-validation of the process is required for several months to one year). Erosion equipment is one of the three core links in chip manufacturing, with large market spaces and high technical barriers. In 2025, the net profit was about $2.1 billion (year + 37 per cent), a steady increase.

But note that the semiconductor equipment industry is clearly cyclical, driven by downstream crystal-crystal plant capital spending. And MSMEs still face global competition from big players like Lam Research, and Tokyo Electronics, with domestic markets contributing the bulk of their revenues.

# The moat's strong #

- **Technology barrier**: 5-7 years of research and development of etching equipment, with MSMEs in the 5nm and below, the only domestic etching equipment provider entering international frontline customers
- **Customer sticky**: long time cycle for authentication of equipment, high replacement cost once imported
- ** Patents moat**: Companies accumulate a large amount of core patents and design deliberately distinguish them from international competitions, avoiding abuse while building their own barriers
- ** But on the other hand**: in global markets, Panforest and Tokyo together account for 70 per cent + share of etching markets; MSMEs are growing highly dependent on alternative national production policy drivers and the geopolitical environment

# Management quality #

The founder, Dr. Yoon Zhiyu (who was a 60-year-old entrepreneur), was a top technical expert in the industry for 13 years in the application of materials. The management team, Duh-shun and Ng Twee, are senior international semiconductor equipment. The company has a full-share incentive and a strong core team. In 2024, Yoon proposed an ambitious goal of "covering 50-60% of the equipment in the key area of integrated circuits over the next 5-10 years" that reflects a very long strategic vision and implementation.

# Valuing appeal #

The PE (TTM) is about 93 times higher than the projected net profit of $3.075 billion in 2026, even if it is 64 times higher. Semiconductor equipment is recognized as a good track, but the market has already given a very high valuation. Compared to the global Lam Research PE, the moderate valuation premium is about 25 times higher than the "country-substitution" logic and higher acceleration expectations.
