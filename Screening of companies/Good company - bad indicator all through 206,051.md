## Common characteristics of these companies

1. **Gross profit margin is all >30%** — the lowest is Meituan 35%, the highest is Meta 81%
2. **ROE all>12%** — indicating that capital efficiency far exceeds opportunity cost
3. **Free cash flow is all positive** — you make real money
4. **None diluted shareholders** — Most were buying back shares and reducing shares

This is the financial portrait of a "first-class good company": high gross profit (pricing power) + high ROE (capital efficiency) + strong cash flow (real profits) + no dilution (respect for shareholders).
