# VIII. Use of advice

** Prices to be validated**: The data in this report are from morning star articles at multiple points, and stock prices may have changed.
2. ** Uncertainty rating is important**: high uncertainty is also often found in high-volume stocks (e.g. HUBS, ADNT). The uncertainty rating for morning stars (Low/Medium/High/Very High) reflects confidence in fair value estimates
3. ** Unit A and Port Unit with limited coverage**: Morning Stars cover about 64 shares of Unit A and Ports Unit with limited coverage, so Chinese stocks are far less numerous than US shares in this report
4. **Ascorland downgrading risk**: Adobe and Salesforce recently reduced from the Broader Protector to the Narrow Protector River (AI competition), similar downgradings may occur in other companies
** Periodical factors**: underestimation by companies at the bottom of the industry cycle, such as ADNT, CNH, may reflect cyclical factors rather than permanent underestimations
