## The first tier: extremely high-quality business (★★★★★)

These companies have "oligopoly/monopoly-level" pricing power + extremely high earnings quality + clear growth paths.

### 1. Baichu Electronics (688188)｜47.8 billion
**One sentence**: The "brain" of laser cutting equipment, with a gross profit of 78% and a net profit of 57%, is the king of profit quality on the Science and Technology Innovation Board.
- The market share of medium and low power is >60%, the equipment cost only accounts for 5%, and customers are not price sensitive
- Extremely high operating leverage: revenue 2.196 billion → net profit 1.113 billion
-Risk: The growth rate of laser equipment is slowing down, and the high-power market still needs to break through

### 2. Montage Technology (688008)｜303.1 billion
**One sentence**: One of the three oligarchs in the world's memory interface chips (36.8% share), both volume and price have increased in the DDR5 era.
- Competing with Renesas and Rambus, the technical barriers are extremely high (only 3 companies can do it)
- Gross profit 62%, net profit 39%, Fabless model with light assets and high returns
-Risk: DDR5 penetration rate slows down after peaking, market value already implies higher expectations

### 3. Anji Technology (688019)｜48.4 billion
**One sentence**: CMP polishing fluid is the benchmark for domestic substitution, with ROIC 20% and a compound growth rate of 40%+ for 7 consecutive years.
- Domestic share of 30%, breaking foreign monopoly, 14nm node has been supplied on a large scale
- Gross profit is 57%, net profit is 31%, and the "consumables" model of semiconductor materials has extremely strong customer stickiness
-Risk: The rhythm of downstream fab expansion fluctuates, and advanced process products still need to make breakthroughs

### 4. Huafeng Measurement and Control (688200)｜55.8 billion
**One sentence**: The largest semiconductor test system supplier in China, with a net profit margin of 40%, and profitability second only to Bai Chu.
- Breaking foreign monopoly in the field of analog/digital-analog mixed testing
- SoC test system breakthrough opens up a larger market
-Risk: Semiconductor cycle fluctuations, accounts receivable collections require attention

### 5. Qiangyi Shares (688809)｜58.6 billion
**One sentence**: MEMS probe card faucet, ROE 30%, gross profit 65%, core consumables for AI computing chip testing.
- Leading the market share of domestic probe cards, benefiting from the explosive demand for AI chip testing
- Consumables model: The greater the customer's testing volume → the more purchases, and the deeper the binding with downstream
-Risk: Top five customers account for >82%, heavy reliance on a single customer

## Investment Inspiration

**Common portrait of "real gold" on the Science and Technology Innovation Board:**
1. Industry status: Top 1-3 in the segment (no company is fourth or fifth)
2. Pricing power: median gross profit margin 45% (continuously making money means customers can’t live without you)
3. Preference for light assets: Fabless/consumables/software models account for the majority (heavy asset models have difficulty passing the FCF indicator)
4. Domestic substitution logic: Most benefit from the trend of localization of semiconductors/medical/military industries

**"Border Companies" worth continuing to track (possibly upgraded to pass):**
- Haiguang Information: If the 5-year FCF is verified to be positive, it may be the most underestimated AI computing power company
- Tuojing Technology: The leader in semiconductor thin film equipment, growing very fast, waiting for confirmation of FCF data
- Zhongkong Technology: Industrial DCS champion in 14 years, 2025 is a cycle low rather than a deterioration in fundamentals
