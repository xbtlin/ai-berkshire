## 1. Business model and competition barriers

### 1.1 Breakdown of main business (income structure in 2024)

| Business Segment | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Proportion | Year-on-year growth rate | Gross profit margin |
|---------|------------|------|---------|--------|
| Railway sector | 188.90 | 58% | -1.86% | 29.95% |
| Urban rail field | 82.20 | 25% | -3.58% | 33.47% |
| Overseas business | 24.41 | 7% | +64.20% | 24.61% |
| General Contracting | 26.89 | 8% | -64.96% | 11.98% |

Source: [2024 Annual Report Comments-Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-03-31/doc-inerqmnh3624987.shtml)

**Essence of business model**: Covering the entire industrial chain of rail transit control system "design consulting → equipment manufacturing → system integration → operation and maintenance services". The railway + urban rail core business accounts for 83%, making it a typical "national infrastructure supporting" enterprise.

### 1.2 Industry status and market share

- **High-speed rail control system**: Domestic market share is about 60%, absolute leader
- **Urban Rail Signaling System**: Domestic market share is approximately 48.5% (including subsidiaries CASCO and CRSC Urban Rail), ranking first
- **Global Ranking**: One of the world's largest rail transit control system suppliers
- Possess 5,500+ registered patents and 100% independent train control core technology

Source: [Tencent News-Transportation Power Report](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20230428A01MRE00)

### 1.3 Moat analysis

**Strong moat factors**:
- **Extremely high technical barriers**: Rail transit signaling systems are related to driving safety, and the certification cycle is long (3-5 years), making it almost impossible for new players to enter
- **Strong customer stickiness**: China Railway Group/Metro Company is the core customer, and the replacement cost is extremely high
- **Standard Setter**: Participate in the formulation of domestic rail transit signaling technology standards
- **Scale effect**: R&D investment exceeds 2 billion per year, and small and medium-sized enterprises are unable to follow up.

**Moat weakening factors**:
- State-owned enterprise system, insufficient market incentives
- Competitors in the urban rail field are gradually increasing (Traffic Control Technology, Hollysys, etc. are eating into their share)
- Rely on government railway/urban rail investment plan, limited bargaining power

**Sharp comment**: ROE averages about 9% per year (historically), and has continued to decline to about 7% in the past two years. As an asset-heavy state-owned enterprise, the return on capital is mediocre. The gross profit margin of 29% seems good, but after deducting the general contracting of projects, the gross profit margin of the core business is 30-33%, and the net profit margin is only 12% - the administrative expense rate is 7.3% + the research and development expense rate is 5.9%, which eats up a lot of profits.

### 2.2 Cash flow quality

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Net operating cash flow (100 million) | 52.36 | About -13 (-0.12 yuan per share) |
| OCF/Net Profit | 150% | Negative |
| Accounts receivable/net profit | 771.83% | 678.21% |
| Cash-to-cash ratio (5-year average) | 0.94 | — |

Source: [2024 Annual Report-Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-03-31/doc-inerqmnh3624987.shtml), [2025 Annual Report Brief Analysis](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260326A025GZ00)

**Sharp Comment**: Cash flow is the biggest flaw of this company. Operating cash flow will turn negative in 2025, and accounts receivable will remain high for a long time (nearly 7 times net profit). The five-year average cash-to-cash ratio is 0.94, which means that for every 100 yuan of book profit earned, only 94 yuan in cash is recovered. As a company that "makes paper profits", its free cash flow has been unsatisfactory for a long time and its ability to pay dividends is limited. The core reason is that customers (National Railway Group, various subway companies) have long payment collection cycles, which is a typical "big Party A and small Party B" pattern.

## 3. Growth

### 3.1 Revenue growth drivers

1. **Railway Stock Renovation Market**: Over 10,000 kilometers of high-speed railways across the country have entered a period of major renovation, and signaling system upgrades are urgently needed.
2. **Overseas market outbreak**: Overseas revenue in 2024 +64%, overseas revenue in 2025H1 +55.9%, has become a core growth pole
3. **New low-altitude economic track**: Build a "low-altitude airspace control + drone manufacturing + service operation" layout, with a contract of 30 million yuan (very small size) signed in 2025H1
4. **Urban rail aftermarket**: demand for maintenance and upgrade of existing lines

### 3.2 Orders on hand

- **As of the end of June 2025**: Orders on hand 117.563 billion yuan
- Equivalent to **3.6 times** of revenue in 2024, ensuring normal operations for at least 3 years
- New contracts signed in the first half of 2025 amount to 17.568 billion yuan

Source: [Sina Finance-Orders in Hand Report](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-09-29/doc-infscaav8404282.shtml)

### 3.3 Overseas expansion progress

- Overseas revenue in 2024 will be 2.441 billion yuan (+64.2%), accounting for 7%
- 2025H1 overseas revenue is 944 million yuan (+55.9%)
- Mainly covers the exports of national railway signaling systems along the "Belt and Road"
- Overseas gross profit margin is 24.61%, lower than domestically, but with fast growth and large space

Source: [Galaxy Securities Research Report](https://bigquant.com/square/paper/9e7306d6-6b11-4fe3-8ab2-0a607b2b2c6d)

**Sharp comment**: The growth story is logical but lacks explosive power. Railway reconstruction is a "maintenance" rather than a "growth" demand; the low-altitude economic contract of 30 million is insignificant compared to the 34.6 billion revenue, and it will not contribute substantial revenue for at least 3-5 years; the overseas business growth rate is impressive but the base is small, and the gross profit margin is lower than domestic. Overall judgment: A mature company with low single-digit growth, not a high-growth stock.

Source: [Tencent Securities](https://gu.qq.com/sh688009), [Oriental Fortune](https://data.eastmoney.com/stockdata/688009.html)

**Valuation Judgment**: 15 times PE corresponds to 7% ROE and single-digit growth, which is far from cheap. PB is only 1.1 times, reflecting the market's concerns about its asset quality (large amounts of accounts receivable). Compared with similar state-owned enterprises (CRRC PE 20x+), the valuation is slightly lower, but the earnings quality is also worse.

### 4.2 Main risk points

1. **Accounts receivable risk**: Accounts receivable account for 7 times of net profit. If bad debts are concentrated and exposed, profits will be seriously eroded
2. **Railway Investment Cycle Risk**: If national railway investment declines sharply, the company’s revenue will be under pressure
3. **Cash flow deterioration risk**: Operating cash flow has turned negative in 2025. If it continues to deteriorate, it will affect dividends and reinvestment capabilities.
4. **Intensified urban rail competition**: Competitors such as traffic control technology continue to encroach on urban rail share
5. **Governance efficiency of state-owned enterprises**: ROE continues to fall, reflecting the low efficiency of capital allocation.
6. **2026Q1 performance decline**: revenue -3.5%, net profit -7.2%, growth momentum weakened

### 4.3 Investment Conclusion

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Business model | ★★★★ | Rail transit signaling leader, high barriers and stable status |
| Profitability | ★★☆ | ROE is only 7%, return on capital is mediocre |
| Cash flow quality | ★★ | Long-term "paper profits", huge accounts receivable |
| Growth | ★★☆ | There are bright spots overseas but overall low growth |
| Valuation attractiveness | ★★★ | 15x PE is not expensive but not cheap either |
| **Overall Rating** | **★★☆** | **2.5 stars/5 stars** |

### Final judgment

CRSC is a typical case of "good business, bad company". The rail transit signaling system itself has extremely high barriers and a solid market share. However, under the state-owned enterprise system, capital allocation efficiency is low, cash flow quality is poor, and growth is weak. 15 times PE corresponds to 7% ROE and single-digit growth, with no obvious margin of safety.

**Not suitable as a core position**. It is worth reassessing unless:
- PE dropped below 10 times (stock price 3.4 yuan)
- Overseas business accounted for more than 20% and gross profit margin improved
- Substantial improvement in cash flow quality (cash-to-cash ratio continues to be >1)
