## Core Discovery

1. **Semiconductor equipment is the best quality track** - 7 companies passed, and the "shovel salesman" model is the most solid after being tested against inferior products.
2. **High gross profit is the common denominator of good companies** - The median gross profit margin of the company is ~45%, compared with the median of all 100 companies ~25%
3. **The overall profit quality of the Science and Technology Innovation Board is worrying** - 56% of companies were eliminated due to losses, "technological growth" ≠ "good business"
4. **Border companies worthy of long-term tracking** - 18 companies such as Haiguang Information, Tuojing Technology, and China National Communications Commission need to wait for more financial data verification
