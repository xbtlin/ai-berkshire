# Series Guide for "Understanding Moutai"

> 5 main articles that break down Moutai layer by layer and reveal its true structure.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Moutai is the most special asset in China's capital market - without question.

- The bulls say: **91% gross margin**, zero interest-bearing debt, 24 consecutive years of positive growth, and the world's strongest consumer-moat business
- The bears say: the liquor industry is seeing declines in volume, price, and profit; market prices have fallen below guidance; seven chairmen in 5 years, 3 of whom went to prison; young people no longer drink liquor

**Both sides are looking at the same company: one sees an "eternal money printer," the other sees a former king that is slowing down.**

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and then explain what has changed**, so that you can decide whether it is worth buying.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Source every data point** - estimates are marked as "estimated"
4. **Do not invent financial data** - all numbers come from annual reports, quarterly reports, or reliable third-party sources; no fake precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
