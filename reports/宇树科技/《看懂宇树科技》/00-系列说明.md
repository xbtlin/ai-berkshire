## Series Positioning

Unitree is not a company that "nobody pays attention to." After its robot dance went viral at the 2025 Spring Festival Gala, it became the **most visible but least understood** company in China's robotics sector.

- The bulls say it is the world's No. 1 humanoid robot shipper, with 60% gross margin and 335% annual growth, essentially "the DJI of robots"
- The bears say **RMB 1.7 billion** of revenue cannot support a **RMB 42 billion** valuation, 73% of revenue depends on research and education, the AI brain is second-tier, and Tesla will crush it as soon as it arrives

**Both sides are only seeing half the truth.** This series aims to first make clear what the company *is*, and only then leave it to you to decide whether it is worth investing in.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - many key Unitree metrics, such as humanoid robot customer GMV or overseas revenue by country, are not fully disclosed, so this series only uses the prospectus and the most reliable sources as anchors
4. **Do not compute probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
