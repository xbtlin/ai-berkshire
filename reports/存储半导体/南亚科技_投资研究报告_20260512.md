# Nanya Technology Investment Research Report

**Research date:** 2026-05-12 | **Ticker:** 2408.TW | **Low-valuation screen rank:** No. 3

---

## One-Sentence Conclusion

> Nanya Technology is a "mediocre business that happened to catch a good cycle" - the old P/E screen of 6.3x is stale, and the current forward P/E is about 7.1x. The Q1 2026 net margin of 53% comes entirely from the DDR4 supply vacuum created when the big three shifted to HBM, not from Nanya's own competitive advantage. A USD 2.5 billion strategic investment (Kioxia / Sandisk / Solidigm / Cisco) provides a 2-3 year demand cushion, but long term this is a small 3% share DRAM maker with no moat.

## Key Analysis

### The truth behind the "low P/E"

The 6.3x P/E figure is based on trailing 2025 EPS of NTD 2.13. Today:
- TTM P/E is about 24.5x (including the low-profit period)
- Forward P/E is about 7.1x (based on 2026E EPS of NTD 42.55)
- Nanya is in the early stage of the DRAM supercycle, not the end stage

### Structural opportunity: the "suction effect" from the big three

Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron are shifting wafer capacity massively toward HBM (each GB consumes about 3x the wafer area), which has sharply reduced DDR4 / LPDDR4 supply. As one of the few still producing a lot of traditional DRAM, Nanya unexpectedly became "scarce capacity that everyone wants."

### The USD 2.5 billion strategic investment is the key variable

The private placement completed in March 2026 had an unusual investor lineup - NAND makers (Kioxia / Sandisk / Solidigm) and a networking giant (Cisco) bought DRAM equity. The core purpose is **to lock in supply security**, with long-term supply agreements attached. This gives Nanya 2-3 years of demand certainty.
