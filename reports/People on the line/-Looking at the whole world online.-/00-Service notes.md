## Series Positioning

ZhongAn Online is a company that is hard to "understand" - not because its business is complicated, but because it **wears two completely different outfits at the same time**.

- The left outfit says "the first internet insurance stock" - founded in 2013 by Ant, Tencent, and Ping An, listed in Hong Kong in 2017, and carrying the aura of "insurtech"
- The right outfit says "No. 8 in the property-and-casualty insurance industry" - 2025 total premiums of **RMB 35.735 billion**, combined cost ratio of **95.8%**, and five consecutive years of underwriting profit

**The same company wears both outfits, but the stories they tell are entirely different.**

The bulls say internet insurance penetration is only 15-18%, ZhongAn is the leader, pet insurance grew 88%, new-energy-vehicle insurance grew 206%, and the stock trades at only 0.77x P/B - severely undervalued.

The bears say Ant reduced its stake from 19.9% to 6.43%, Tencent from 15% to 4.84%, even the founding shareholders are exiting, technology output revenue is less than 3% of the total, and the "technology company" label does not fit.

This series aims to **first strip away both outfits and see what is really inside** - and then let you decide whether it is worth buying.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - some ZhongAn business data is not fully disclosed, so this series only uses the most reliable sources and does not force in filler
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
