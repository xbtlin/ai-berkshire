# The right way to value insurance companies

Insurance companies generally use P/B valuations instead of PE. The reason is that the profits of insurance companies are highly influenced by investment returns, that PEs are too volatile and unreliable. P/B is more stable and reflects the "gold content" of net assets.

**P/B Core formula for valuation: Reasonable P/B = ROE/ Capital Cost (COE)**

The ROE in 2025 is about **7-8%**. If the capital cost assumption is 10%, then the reasonable P/B = 7-8% / 10% = **0.7-0.8 times **.

** This means that the current 0.77 times P/B is within reasonable range -- not expensive, but not cheap.** The price price that the market has given to the people already reflects its ROE level.

To get P/B back to more than one times, it takes ROE to rise to 10%+. This means that net profits need to rise from 1.8 billion to about 2.5 billion -- not certain given the historical record of profit fluctuations.

# Summing up: What kind of company is the Communion?

After four, we go back to the original question: ** Which dress should the C.A. wear?**

The answer may not be satisfactory:** neither dress is suitable.**

It's not a pure technology company that does little to make a technology export, and that's a billion dollars in revenue that doesn't make it worth the value of technology.

It is not a good insurance company, ROE 7-8 per cent, with a high profit volatility and a low moat.

** It's a company that "intensity insurance, good work, but not very good."** The landscape insurance model was created but it has a low profit rate and a strong reliance on channels. The transition is in the right direction (from withdrawal to health and car insurance), but the transition is still on its way.

The current P/B 0.77-fold valuation is broadly reasonable - not a clear overestimation, nor a clear underestimation.** It reflects a combination of market judgement on the current profitability and risk of CPS.**

If you believe that the transition of the people will succeed — health risks, car risks, technology breakthroughs — there is room for upswing in the current price.

If you fear Ali's ecological dependence, profit fluctuations, and shareholder retention — then it is not too late to intervene until more positive signals emerge.

** This is a company that needs to be followed, not a company that has to be decided now.**
