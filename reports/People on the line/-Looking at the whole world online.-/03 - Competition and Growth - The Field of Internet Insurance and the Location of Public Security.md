# A key question: how will AI change the industry?

The People's Security has indeed been in the forefront of the industry in AI applications:

- AI Automation of claims processing rate is over **45%**
- High coverage of smart nuclear safety
- "The Man" AI half-yearly call from the center **450 million **

AI The impact on the insurance industry may be at three levels:

** Efficiency layer**: Automation of claims settlement, smart nuclear security - People's Security is already working, which is the most definite benefit in the short term
** Product layer**: Individualized pricing based on big data - traditional companies are doing it, and the advantage will be reduced
** Channel **: AI assistant may change the way insurance is sold - this is variable for all insurance companies

**AI is a double-edged sword to the public.** In the short term, AI helps to bring about efficiency gains; in the long run, when all insurance companies use AI, the technology differences will be wiped out. AI has lowered the technical threshold of the insurance industry, which may eventually de-light the moat.

** The counter-view**: The pre-emptive advantage became more evident in the AI era - the more data accumulate, the better models, the stronger the flying wheel effect. The 10 years of data build-up is difficult to catch up with.

** Rebuttal**: The data wheels are less powerful than the Internet platforms in the insurance industry. Insurance data are common to the industry (e.g. accident probability, disease incidence) and are not as exclusive as social network data.
