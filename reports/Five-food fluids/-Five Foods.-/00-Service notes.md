## Series Positioning

Wuliangye is not an under-followed company -- it is the **most controversial and information-confused** company in the A-share baijiu sector.

- The bulls say: PE of **11**x, dividend yield of **5.6%**, cash on the books of **127.4bn** -- a baijiu cash machine has been mistakenly sold off
- The bears say: two consecutive chairmen have fallen from office, **30.3bn** of revenue was inflated, the moat is narrowing, and cheapness has a reason

**Both camps are looking at the same company and arriving at completely opposite conclusions.** Even more troublesome, both sides can support their judgments with hard data.

This series aims to **clarify "what it is" and explain "why it is cheap"** -- so you can judge for yourself whether it is "worth buying."

## Writing Principles

1. **Data first, logic second, conclusion last** -- no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Attach the opposing view to every core judgment** -- let readers weigh the evidence themselves
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than force it in** -- Wuliangye's 2025 annual report has a data break caused by accounting adjustments, so this series clearly marks which data are trustworthy and which are questionable
4. **Not investment advice** -- disclaimer at the end of every article
