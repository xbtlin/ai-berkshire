## What Is Happening in the Baijiu Industry?

In 2025, Shanxi Fenjiu reported net profit of RMB 12.25bn, historically surpassing Wuliangye's RMB 8.95bn. Outside Moutai, the baijiu market is being rewritten. The old "Moutai-Wuliangye-Yanghe" hierarchy became "Moutai-Fenjiu-Wuliangye" in less than three years.

To understand Wuliangye's industry position, we first need to see the industry itself clearly: **the baijiu industry is going through a historic volume contraction.**

### Output: Down Nearly 70% in 8 Years

| Year | Output (10,000 kiloliters) | YoY |
|------|------------|------|
| 2016 | **1,358** (peak) | — |
| 2018 | 871 | -27.3% |
| 2020 | 741 | -5.8% |
| 2022 | 671 | -6.2% |
| 2023 | 449 | -33.1% |
| 2024 | **414** | -1.8% |
| 2025H1 | 192 | -5.8% |

*Source: National Bureau of Statistics*

From 13.58mn kiloliters to 4.14mn kiloliters: **8 consecutive years of decline, leaving industry output at less than one-third of its peak.**

But what about revenue? In 2024, baijiu industry sales revenue was **RMB 796.4bn**, up **36%** from RMB 583.6bn in 2020. Output fell 44%, while revenue rose 36%.

**This shows the entire industry has been "trading price for volume": selling less, but at higher prices. Profit is accelerating toward premium products and leading companies.**

However, by 2025 this game had finally stopped working. Total revenue at 19 listed baijiu companies fell **18.13%** year over year (Sina Finance). The era of simultaneous volume and price contraction has arrived.

### Concentration: Oligopolization Is Accelerating

| Metric | 2015 | 2020 | 2024 | Trend |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| CR5 (top five revenue share) | <20% | ~30% | **45%** | Rapid concentration |
| CR10 | ~30% | ~40% | **~60%** | Oligopolization |
| Listed-company CR3 | — | — | **65.4%** | Three giants dominate |

*Sources: Forward Industry Research Institute, listed-company financial reports*

The baijiu industry is moving from "a hundred flowers blooming" into an oligopoly phase. **The story is now basically the story of the top five companies.**

## 2025 Annual Report Ranking: A Dramatic Reshuffling

| Rank | Company | 2025 revenue (RMB 100mn) | YoY | Key change |
|------|------|--------------|------|---------|
| 1 | Kweichow Moutai | **1,721** | -1.2% | Firmly No. 1, but first slight decline since listing |
| 2 | Wuliangye | **405** | -54.6% | Accounting adjustment; revenue "halved" |
| 3 | Shanxi Fenjiu | **387** | **+7.5%** | **Only positive growth** |
| 4 | Luzhou Laojiao | **257** | -17.5% | Fell out of the top three |
| 5 | Yanghe | **192** | -33.5% | Decline accelerated |

*Sources: company 2025 annual reports*

**Three major signals:**

**First, Shanxi Fenjiu broke through historically.** Full-year 2025 revenue grew 7.5%, expanding against the cycle amid broad weakness. In 2025Q3, quarterly revenue of RMB 8.96bn temporarily surpassed Wuliangye's quarterly RMB 8.17bn. Net profit of **RMB 12.25bn** surpassed Wuliangye's **RMB 8.95bn** for the first time.

To be clear, Wuliangye's 2025 figures are seriously distorted by accounting adjustments. On the normal 2024 basis, with RMB 31.9bn of profit, Wuliangye still leads Fenjiu by a wide margin. But the trend is clear: **Fenjiu is catching up, while Wuliangye is losing speed.**

**Second, the former "Moutai-Wuliangye-Yanghe" structure is becoming "Moutai-Fenjiu-Wuliangye."** Yanghe has fallen behind the fastest, while Fenjiu has risen the fastest. The "absolute dominance" of strong-aroma baijiu has been broken by light-aroma baijiu.

**Third, the industry split between "Moutai far ahead, everyone else struggling" is intensifying.** Moutai fell only 1.2%, while the industry average fell 18%. Moutai is not merely "crossing the cycle"; it is taking share from others within the cycle.

## Price-Band War: Wuliangye's "Sandwich" Predicament

### Ultra-Premium (RMB 2,000+): Moutai Has a Monopoly, and No One Can Challenge It

| Metric | Feitian Moutai |
|------|---------|
| Ex-factory price | RMB 1,269 (after the March 2026 adjustment) |
| Original-carton wholesale price | **RMB 1,700-1,730** |
| Loose-bottle wholesale price | **RMB 1,565-1,580** |
| Channel profit per bottle | **~RMB 400** |

*Sources: Sichuan Daily New Media, The Paper*

Feitian Moutai is the only baijiu whose wholesale price is higher than its ex-factory price. It is a financial product, not just a consumer product. Channels compete for allocation.

### Premium (RMB 800-1,500): Puwu and Guojiao Are Both Fully Inverted

| Product | Ex-factory price | Wholesale price (early 2026) | Inversion |
|------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Eighth-Generation Puwu | RMB 1,019→900 | **RMB 880-920** | **-10% to -14%** |
| Guojiao 1573 | ~RMB 980 | **RMB 750-800** | **-18% to -23%** |
| Qinghualang | RMB 1,099 | RMB 630 | -43% |
| Junpin Xijiu | RMB 1,498 | RMB 588 | **-61%** |

*Sources: Sina Finance, 21jingji.com, channel research*

**Key findings**:

1. Not one product in the thousand-yuan price band can stand above its ex-factory price; all are inverted
2. The wholesale price gap between Puwu and Guojiao has narrowed from RMB 200 historically to almost zero; consumers and distributors no longer see it as self-evident that Puwu should be more expensive than Guojiao
3. Products in lower price bands, such as Qinghualang and Xijiu, show even worse inversion; premium baijiu is "collapsing downward"

### Sub-Premium (RMB 300-800): The Bloodiest Battlefield

The sub-premium segment is often called the "sandwich": squeezed from above by price cuts in premium liquor, and from below by upgrades in mass-market liquor.

- Sub-premium baijiu sales led by group-buying have **generally fallen 30-50%**
- Government group-buying has **nearly halved**
- The RMB 500-800 price segment faces the greatest survival pressure. This is exactly the range into which Puwu and Guojiao have "fallen" after wholesale prices slid

### Wuliangye's Specific Predicament

```
       ↑ Cannot reach Moutai above: brand momentum gap is too large, no financial attributes
       |
  Wuliangye ← Caught from the side by Guojiao: wholesale price gap has narrowed to almost zero
       |
       ↓ Overtaken from below by Fenjiu: light-aroma differentiation + banquet channel
```

**Wuliangye is facing a three-sided squeeze: Moutai is unreachable above, Guojiao is pressing closely from the side, and Fenjiu is overtaking from below.**

## Wuliangye vs Moutai: Forever the "Perennial No. 2"?

### Historical Perspective: Wuliangye Was Once the "King of Baijiu"

Many people do not know this history: Wuliangye was not always No. 2.

| Period | Competitive landscape |
|------|---------|
| 1994-2003 | **Wuliangye dominated**: ex-factory price was RMB 70 above Moutai's, and sales volume ranked first |
| Around 2005 | Inflection point: Wuliangye went all-in on private-label products, with more than 100 sub-brands, severely diluting the brand |
| 2008 | Wuliangye lost the revenue crown for the first time |
| After 2012 | Moutai fully overtook it, and the gap has kept widening |

*Sources: Sina Finance, industry historical materials*

**Wuliangye declined not because the product got worse, but because management was short-sighted: it traded short-term revenue for brand purity through private-label expansion.** More than 100 sub-brands diluted the value of the word "Wuliangye," while Moutai maintained extreme focus by effectively insisting on one core product.

### Possibility of Catching Moutai

**Arguments for catching up**:
1. Wuliangye's capacity (**160,000 tons**) far exceeds Moutai's (~50,000 tons), theoretically giving it more room to grow
2. If the Classic Wuliangye series opens up the ultra-premium market (RMB 1,500+), Wuliangye could break upward
3. If governance improves after management renewal, suppressed brand value could potentially be released

**Counter-test, which is more persuasive**:
1. **Consumer mindshare has solidified**: the belief that "Moutai = the best baijiu" is deeply rooted and cannot be changed simply by spending on advertising
2. **Natural process barrier**: sauce-aroma production has a long cycle (5 years+), high process complexity, and naturally constrained capacity. Strong-aroma production does not have the same scarcity; the larger Wuliangye's capacity, the less scarce it becomes
3. **Financial-attribute gap**: Moutai has investment and collectible value because older bottles become more valuable. Wuliangye has almost none of this. Moutai has an additional "financial moat"
4. **Price anchoring effect**: consumers have already formed the view that "Wuliangye = thousand-yuan liquor," making an upward breakout very difficult
5. **Compound growth gap**: over the past decade, Moutai grew at about **12%**, while Wuliangye grew at only about **2%**. The gap is widening exponentially

**Conclusion: the probability that Wuliangye overtakes Moutai is extremely low.**

The more realistic question is not "can it catch Moutai," but:

## The More Realistic Threat: Is the No. 2 Position Still Secure?

### Fenjiu: The Real Challenger

Shanxi Fenjiu's 2025 performance is striking:

| Metric | Wuliangye (2025 adjusted) | Shanxi Fenjiu (2025) |
|------|---------------------|----------------|
| Revenue | RMB 40.5bn | **RMB 38.7bn** |
| Net profit | RMB 8.95bn | **RMB 12.25bn** |
| YoY growth | -54.6% | **+7.5%** |

*Sources: company 2025 annual reports*

Wuliangye's 2025 data is heavily distorted by accounting adjustments, so a direct comparison is unfair. But even on the normal 2024 basis (RMB 89.2bn vs ~RMB 36.0bn), Fenjiu's catch-up speed cannot be ignored: Fenjiu revenue has compounded at about **20%** over 5 years, versus about **7%** for Wuliangye over the same period.

**Fenjiu's differentiation strategy**:
- Light-aroma vs strong-aroma: category differentiation that avoids direct competition with Wuliangye
- Deep cultivation of banquet channels: rapidly increasing penetration in weddings, birthday banquets, and similar occasions
- Flexible price bands: the Qinghua series spans RMB 300-1,000, precisely targeting the sub-premium segment

**On the other hand**, Fenjiu's brand ceiling is still currently below Wuliangye's. In the pure premium market above RMB 1,000, Fenjiu's recognition and share are far below Wuliangye's. Fenjiu's surpassing is more about a "mid-range rise" than a "premium replacement."

### Luzhou Laojiao: Close-Quarters Combat

Luzhou Laojiao's Guojiao 1573 is Wuliangye's direct competitor in the thousand-yuan price band.

| Comparison dimension | Wuliangye | Luzhou Laojiao |
|---------|--------|---------|
| 2025 net profit | RMB 8.95bn (adjusted) | **RMB 10.8bn** |
| PE | ~11x | ~15x |
| Accounting cleanliness | **C+** (incident-prone) | **A-** (clean) |
| Wholesale price recovery | Still inverted | **Recovered to ~RMB 990** |
| Accounts receivable | Surged **163%** | **Almost zero** |

*Sources: company annual reports, channel research*

**One notable fact**: in 2025, Luzhou Laojiao proactively suspended shipments and controlled volume, pushing Guojiao 1573's wholesale price back up from a low of RMB 875 to RMB 990. Wuliangye's Puwu, by contrast, remains inverted.

**Positive view**: Wuliangye's brand momentum, channel scale, and capacity advantage still far exceed Luzhou Laojiao's. Over a 30-year horizon, Wuliangye remains the strongest contender for the title of "No. 1 strong-aroma brand."

**Negative view**: over a 1-2 year horizon, Luzhou Laojiao leads Wuliangye across channel health, financial-report cleanliness, and valuation safety. The market is voting with its feet. Luzhou Laojiao's PE is higher than Wuliangye's, which means the market trusts Laojiao more.

## Industry Endgame: Will Baijiu Follow the Path of Japanese Sake?

### The Warning From Japanese Sake

| Metric | Japanese sake | Chinese baijiu |
|------|---------|---------|
| Consumption trend | 2004-2022 CAGR **-3%** | 2016-2024 output CAGR **-13.7%** |
| Premiumization | Ginjo/junmai CAGR +2% | Premium baijiu share continues to rise |
| Demographics | Aging + low birth rate | **2022 population inflection point has passed** |
| Young consumer preference | Shifting to beer/foreign spirits/RTD | Shifting to low-alcohol drinks/craft beer/fruit wine |

*Sources: Sina Finance, HSBC Qianhai*

**Similarities, which are bearish signals**:
- Total volume continues to shrink, and Chinese baijiu's decline rate (**-13.7%/year**) is even faster than Japanese sake's (-3%/year)
- Generational replacement among young people is irreversible; consumers under 30 account for only **8%** of baijiu consumption
- The core consumer group aged 30-59 is shrinking at **-1%/year**

**Differences, which are relatively constructive**:
- Baijiu is much more deeply embedded in Chinese culture, including weddings, funerals, business, and social occasions, than sake is in Japan
- Brand concentration is higher; Moutai's "psychological monopoly" is far stronger than that of any Japanese sake brand
- The market scale of RMB 800.0bn+ remains huge even if volume shrinks by 20-30%

### Endgame Judgment

**Baijiu is unlikely to follow Japanese sake's path of "comprehensive decline." It is more likely to follow a "Japanese whisky" path: total volume keeps shrinking, leading brands grow against the trend, and the long tail dies off in large numbers.**

Forecast:
- **Volume**: output may shrink another **30-40%** over the next 10 years
- **Revenue**: total industry revenue may remain stable through price-for-volume substitution
- **Structure**: the final landscape may become "**1 super player (Moutai) + 3 strong players (Wuliangye/Fenjiu/Luzhou Laojiao) + N regional champions**"

### Implications for Wuliangye

1. Wuliangye is highly likely to survive to the endgame; its brand, channels, and scale are sufficient
2. But the "perennial No. 2" position is no longer safe; Fenjiu's threat is real
3. The key variable is whether Wuliangye can complete channel destocking and governance repair during this cycle

## Industry Cycle: Where Is the Bottom?

### Current Cycle Positioning

The current baijiu downturn began in 2024Q2 and had lasted about 2 years by May 2026.

Compared with the previous cycle:

| Dimension | 2012-2015 cycle | 2024-2026/27 current cycle |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Trigger | Eight-point Regulation + plasticizer incident | alcohol bans + consumption downgrading + population inflection point |
| Government consumption shock | Share fell from 40% to 5% | Marginal impact limited, already below 5% |
| Demand substitution | Business consumption took over | **Business + consumer demand both contracting, no substitute** |
| Recovery time | ~3 years | Expected to be longer |

*Sources: industry research reports, China Lianhe Credit Rating*

**This cycle will be harder to exit than 2012-2015 because it lacks "replacement incremental demand."** In 2012, lost government consumption was replaced by business consumption. This time, business and consumer demand are shrinking together. Where is the next buyer of last resort?

### Destocking Progress

| Category | Inventory status (2026Q1) | Expected destocking time |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Feitian Moutai | Close to safe inventory | **Basically complete** |
| Puwu/Guojiao | 1-2 months, down 30%+ after Spring Festival | 2026Q2 |
| Sub-premium | 3-4 months, pressure still high | 2026Q2-Q3 |
| Regional brands | More than 70% have elevated inventory | 2026 second half+ |

*Source: Eastmoney channel research*

**Inventories are improving, but they are far from cleared. The industry fundamentals bottom is expected around 2026Q4-2027Q1.**
