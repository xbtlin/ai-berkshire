## One-Sentence Core Judgment

**Wuliangye is a "good business," but not a "great business." It has a brand, a moat, and cash flow, but it lacks Moutai's "consumer mindshare monopoly + irreplaceability." In Duan Yongping's language, Wuliangye's differentiation is not strong enough, its pricing power has been disproved in a weak cycle, and its moat is slowly narrowing rather than widening.**

## 1. Business Model Essence: Defining the Core Business

### 1.1 What business is Wuliangye in?

**Essential definition**: A branded franchise in premium strong-aroma baijiu. The core asset is the brand recognition embedded in the three words "Wuliangye": the largest share in the RMB 800-1200 price band, supported by quality barriers created by 600-year-old cellar pits and strong-aroma brewing techniques.

Duan Yongping's first question when judging a business is: "If you are the consumer, why would you buy it?"

Answer: In business banquets, wedding banquets, and gift-giving occasions, Wuliangye is the "best choice below Moutai": it carries enough brand face while costing roughly half as much as Moutai. Consumers are not buying the liquid; they are buying the **face value of a social currency**.

**But there is a fatal qualifier here: "below Moutai." That positioning is both Wuliangye's safe zone and its ceiling.**

That is the fundamental difference between Wuliangye and Moutai.

## 2. Price System: Ex-Factory Price, Wholesale Price, and Retail Price

### 2.1 8th-Generation Puwu Price System (2025-2026)

```
Ex-factory price (nominal) -> Invoice price (actual) -> Wholesale price (market) -> Retail price (terminal)
  1019 yuan                   900 yuan                  790-850 yuan              820-950 yuan
                              (from 2026)               (volatile)                (wide channel / occasion differences)
```

| Price node | Amount (yuan / bottle) | Notes |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Ex-factory price (nominal) | 1019 | Unchanged since 2024 |
| Distributor invoice price | 900 | From 2026, including 119 yuan / bottle subsidy |
| Distributor actual cost | ~800-850 | After rebates and expense support |
| Market wholesale price (2026Q1) | ~790-850 | Volatile range, with a slight rebound around Spring Festival |
| E-commerce platform price | 820-860 | JD / Tmall including platform subsidies |
| Suggested retail price | 1399 | Nominal only |

### 2.2 Price Inversion: The Core Problem

**Degree of inversion**: Distributors buy at an invoice price of 900 yuan (actual cost after expenses: ~800-850 yuan), while the market wholesale price is 790-850 yuan.

- **Severe inversion period (mid-2025)**: Wholesale price once fell to around 810 yuan, while distributor procurement cost was around 920 yuan -> **loss of roughly 70-110 yuan per bottle**
- **After invoice-price reduction (from 2026)**: Wholesale price of 850 yuan vs invoice price of 900 yuan -> inversion narrowed to ~50 yuan
- **If rebates are included**: Actual cost of ~800-850 yuan vs wholesale price of 850 yuan -> roughly breakeven or slightly profitable

**Duan Yongping's pricing-power test**: "Something that can raise prices shows it has a moat."

In 2025-2026, Wuliangye not only failed to raise prices; it was **forced to lower its invoice price**. This was the first time in nearly a decade. The prior price cut traces back to the previous deep baijiu adjustment cycle in 2014.

**Positive case**: Wuliangye's price reduction is a proactive strategic choice: narrowing the gap between ex-factory price and wholesale price, protecting channel profits, and pursuing a channel strategy of "short-term give-up, long-term stability."

**Reverse test**: If consumers truly believe Wuliangye is worth 1019 yuan, why has the wholesale price stayed in the 800s for so long? Pricing power is not about the price you set; it is about whether **the market is willing to pay your price**. The market is voting with its feet: Wuliangye currently lacks pricing power above 1000 yuan.

### 2.3 Comparison with Moutai Wholesale Prices

| Product | Ex-factory price | Wholesale price | Premium / inversion |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| Feitian Moutai | 1169 yuan | ~1500 yuan (end-2025) | **Premium of about 28%** |
| 8th-generation Puwu | 1019 -> 900 yuan | ~850 yuan | **Inversion of about 6%** |

The gap is obvious: Moutai carries a positive premium, while Wuliangye suffers negative inversion. This is the dividing line between "real pricing power" and "pseudo pricing power."

## 3. Moat: Brand Strength, Pricing Power, and Consumer Mindshare

### 3.1 Wuliangye's Moat Inventory

| Moat type | Strength | Assessment |
|-----------|------|------|
| **Brand awareness** | ★★★★ | The leading brand in the RMB 1000 price band, the "king of strong-aroma baijiu" |
| **Ancient cellar pits** | ★★★★ | Ming-dynasty cellar pit group (600+ years of continuous use); older pits produce better liquor quality |
| **Locked-in consumption occasions** | ★★★ | The "safe choice" for business banquets, wedding banquets, and official-use occasions |
| **Scale effect** | ★★★★ | Annual sales of 162,000 tons, far above Moutai's ~50,000 tons |
| **Production-region advantage** | ★★★ | Yibin, "China's baijiu capital," with favorable climate / water source / microbial environment |
| **Channel network** | ★★★ | National distribution network, but it is being consolidated and reduced |
| **Pricing power** | ★★ | Exists in name; in practice, the market has forced price cuts |
| **Consumer mindshare monopoly** | ★★ | The "first choice below Moutai," but highly substitutable |

### 3.2 Quantified Brand-Value Comparison

| Metric | Wuliangye | Moutai | Gap multiple |
|------|--------|------|---------|
| Brand value (2025 Tsinghua ranking) | 3,060 RMB 100m | 6,626 RMB 100m | **Moutai is 2.2x Wuliangye** |
| Market cap (2026.4.30) | 3,768 RMB 100m | ~18,000 RMB 100m | **Moutai is 4.8x Wuliangye** |
| Gross margin | 77.05% | 91.6% | 14.6 percentage-point gap |

**Key finding**: Wuliangye spends 2x Moutai's selling expenses, yet achieves less than half of Moutai's revenue and profit. This shows Wuliangye's brand does not have Moutai's ability to "sell while lying down"; it must keep spending heavily on marketing to maintain sales.

Duan Yongping once said: "I know many friends who drink baijiu and drink nothing except Moutai." That is the ultimate expression of a consumer mindshare monopoly.

### 3.3 Consumer Mindshare Analysis

**Moutai's mental position**:
- "China's best baijiu"
- "Hard social currency" - giving Moutai needs no explanation
- "The only option" - consumers do not need to choose between Moutai and other brands
- "Investment / collectible product" - supported by financial attributes

**Wuliangye's mental position**:
- "The best choice other than Moutai"
- "Premium value-for-money" - a substitute when Moutai cannot be found or afforded
- "High priority but not unique" - consumers compare Wuliangye with Guojiao 1573
- "A safe choice for wedding / business banquets" - not wrong, but not stunning

**Duan Yongping differentiation test**: "Differentiation is something users need that other competitors cannot satisfy."

What is Wuliangye's differentiation?

- vs Moutai: Wuliangye has no differentiated advantage, only a price advantage
- vs Guojiao 1573: differentiation lies in broader brand recognition and a more complete channel network
- vs Jiannanchun: differentiation lies in brand tier (RMB 1000 vs RMB 500 band)

**Core problem**: Wuliangye's differentiation mainly sits in brand recognition within the "RMB 1000 strong-aroma" price band, but Guojiao 1573 is eroding that differentiation. If the price band is raised above 1500 yuan, Wuliangye has almost no competitiveness.

## 4. Distributor System: Channel Reality

### 4.1 Channel Inventory

| Metric | Data | Assessment |
|------|------|------|
| Inventory balance (2025H1) | 177.46 RMB 100m | +5.06% vs end-2024 |
| Core distributor inventory ceiling | 1.5 months of sales | Company control standard |
| Actual channel inventory | Estimated 2-3 months | Above the company's disclosed standard (inferred) |
| Net reduction in distributors (2025) | Cumulative reduction of 90 | Active cleanup + natural attrition |

### 4.2 Distributor Profit (2025-2026)

| Period | Distributor procurement cost | Market wholesale price | Gross profit per bottle |
|------|-------------|---------|---------|
| 2025H1 | ~920 yuan | ~850 yuan | **-70 yuan (loss)** |
| 2025H2 | ~920 yuan | ~810-830 yuan | **-90~-110 yuan (deep loss)** |
| From 2026 (after invoice-price reduction) | ~800-850 yuan | ~850 yuan | **0~+50 yuan (slight profit / breakeven)** |

**Vicious cycle in channel ecology**:
```
Wholesale-price inversion -> distributor losses -> rushed inventory dumping to recover cash -> cross-region selling intensifies -> wholesale price falls further -> more inversion
```

### 4.3 Cross-Region Selling Problem

Although Wuliangye launched a "digital liquor certificate" system to trace product flows, cross-region selling remains severe:
- E-commerce platform subsidized prices as low as 820-860 yuan
- Some offline channels are even lower, with reports mentioning extreme low prices of 640-750 yuan (including various discounts / tail inventory)
- Large supermarkets and e-commerce platforms have become "price destroyers"

### 4.4 Channel Reform Measures

| Measure | Content | Effect assessment |
|------|------|---------|
| Invoice-price reduction | 1019 -> 900 yuan | Narrows inversion, but acknowledges the fact of a "price cut" |
| Distributor cleanup | Reduced by 90 in 2025 | Optimizes the channel, but also means sell-through capacity has declined |
| Direct-operated store expansion | Self-operated stores increased to 200+ | Stronger price-control capacity, but limited scale |
| Higher e-commerce share | E-commerce revenue share ~15% | Double-edged sword: low online prices cannibalize the offline price system |
| Digital liquor certificate | Tracks product flow | Effective as technology, but hard to enforce |

**Reverse test**: Wuliangye's channel reform is essentially "paying off old debts." Over the past few years, in pursuit of revenue growth, the company pushed too much inventory onto distributors; now it must pay a higher price to digest that inventory. Moutai never needs to do this because demand exceeds supply.

## 6. Core Question: Is the Moat Widening or Narrowing?

### 6.1 Evidence That the Moat Is Narrowing

| Evidence | Details |
|------|------|
| **Loss of pricing power** | First invoice-price reduction in nearly a decade; wholesale price has long been below ex-factory price; suggested retail price of 1399 yuan is nominal only |
| **Brand-value gap widening** | Brand-value gap with Moutai widened from 1.5x to 2.2x (2025) |
| **Market-cap gap widening** | Market-cap gap with Moutai widened from 3x to about 4.8x |
| **Selling expenses surging** | 2024 selling expenses of 106.92 RMB 100m, +37.15% YoY. Spending ever more money to maintain sales means natural brand pull is declining |
| **Intensifying competition** | Guojiao 1573 continues to erode the RMB 1000 price band; Jiannanchun is rising in the RMB 500-800 band |
| **Channel health deteriorating** | Distributor losses, cross-region selling, inventory backlog. A healthy moat does not leave channel partners losing money for long periods |
| **Management-governance collapse** | Two chairmen investigated in succession; financial-reporting credibility severely damaged |
| **Lagging youth strategy** | Moutai's youth strategy is a natural extension of brand momentum, while Wuliangye is passively playing catch-up |

### 6.2 Evidence That the Moat Still Exists

| Evidence | Details |
|------|------|
| **Leader in the RMB 1000 price band** | 8th-generation Puwu remains No. 1 in the RMB 800-1200 price band and is hard to replace in the short term |
| **Ancient cellar pits cannot be replicated** | Ming-dynasty cellar pits used continuously for 600 years; cellar age is a core barrier for strong-aroma liquor quality |
| **Consumption occasions are resilient** | Wuliangye remains the "safe choice" for business and wedding banquets |
| **Scale advantage** | Annual production of 162,000+ tons, far above peers; scale effects create cost advantages |
| **Cash reserve** | Cash and cash equivalents of 1385 RMB 100m (2025Q1), creating a very high financial margin of safety |
| **Series liquor growth** | Series liquor revenue grew 11.79%, and a multi-product matrix is forming |

### 6.3 Overall Judgment

**The data point to a moat that is slowly narrowing.**

Key logic chain:
```
Baijiu industry downturn -> demand shrinks -> consumers become more rational -> brand loyalty declines (especially for non-Moutai brands)
-> Wuliangye faces more substitute competition -> forced to cut prices to protect volume -> pricing power weakens -> moat narrows
```

But this is not a "moat collapse." It is a shift from a "wide moat" to a "medium moat." Wuliangye's brand position in RMB 1000 strong-aroma baijiu remains solid; it is just no longer as "worry-free" as it used to be.

**On the other hand**: An industry downturn is the best time to test the true strength of a moat. If Wuliangye can complete channel clearing, repair its price system, and replace management during this cycle, the moat could widen again after the cycle turns. Historically, Wuliangye faced similar difficulties during the 2014 deep baijiu adjustment and ultimately took 3 years to recover.

## 7. Ultimate Comparison with Moutai: Will Wuliangye Always Be the "Perennial No. 2"?

### 7.1 Historical Perspective: Wuliangye Was Once the "Baijiu King"

| Period | Competitive landscape |
|------|---------|
| 1994-2003 | **Wuliangye dominated**: pricing exceeded Moutai (2005 ex-factory price was 70 yuan higher than Moutai), with the highest sales volume |
| Around 2005 | Turning point: Wuliangye engaged in aggressive brand licensing (100+ sub-brands), severely diluting the brand |
| 2008 | Wuliangye lost the revenue crown for the first time |
| After 2012 | Moutai comprehensively overtook it, and the gap has continued to widen |

**Historical lesson**: Wuliangye's decline was not because the product got worse. It was because management was short-sighted: it traded brand licensing for short-term revenue and sacrificed brand "purity." Moutai, by contrast, always insisted on extreme focus around "one product."

### 7.2 Possibility of Catching Up

**Arguments for catching up**:
1. Wuliangye's capacity (162,000 tons) is far above Moutai's (~50,000 tons), theoretically giving it more room to grow
2. If the Classic Wuliangye series opens the ultra-premium market (1500 yuan+), it could break upward
3. If governance improves after management replacement, suppressed brand value could be released

**Arguments that catching up is impossible (reverse test, and more persuasive)**:

1. **Consumer mindshare is already fixed**: Moutai = "the best baijiu" is deeply embedded and cannot be changed simply by spending money on advertising. Duan Yongping's comment that "many friends drink nothing except Moutai" captures this. Once such mindshare forms, it is almost irreversible
2. **Natural barrier from process differences**: Sauce-aroma brewing has a long cycle (5+ years), higher process complexity, and naturally constrained capacity. That "scarcity" cannot be replicated by strong-aroma baijiu. The larger Wuliangye's capacity, the less scarce it appears
3. **Financial-attribute gap**: Moutai has investment and collection attributes (older bottles become more valuable), while Wuliangye does not. This gives Moutai an additional "financial moat"
4. **Pricing-anchor effect**: Moutai sits above 2000 yuan, while Wuliangye sits around 800-1000 yuan. Once consumers form the price perception that "Wuliangye = RMB 1000 liquor," breaking upward becomes extremely difficult
5. **10-year compound growth-rate gap**: Moutai is about 12%, Wuliangye only about 2%; the gap is widening exponentially

**Conclusion: The probability that Wuliangye "surpasses Moutai" is extremely low, almost impossible.**

The more realistic question is not "can it catch Moutai," but "can it keep the No. 2 position?" Guojiao 1573 is attacking the RMB 1000 price band with increasing force, and Luzhou Laojiao's management execution is widely regarded as stronger than Wuliangye's. If Wuliangye continues to suffer governance problems, the No. 2 position is not ironclad.

### 7.3 Strategic Meaning of "Perennial No. 2"

From an investment perspective, being the "perennial No. 2" is not necessarily bad. When Duan Yongping invested in Apple, he never obsessed over whether Apple had the largest mobile-phone market share. The key question is:

**Can Wuliangye, as the "No. 2," continue to make money?**

The current answer is **yes, but its earning power is declining**:
- Normalized net profit may fall from 320 RMB 100m (peak) to 180-230 RMB 100m
- Even at 180 RMB 100m of net profit and 15x PE, it is worth 2700 RMB 100m (vs current 3768 RMB 100m, still leaving downside)
- Even at 230 RMB 100m of net profit and 20x PE, it is worth 4600 RMB 100m (about 20% upside from current levels)

## 8. 2025 Financial-Statement Restatement: Special Risk Warning

### 8.1 Event Summary

On 2026-04-30, Wuliangye released an "Announcement on Correction of Prior-Period Accounting Errors," retrospectively adjusting the first three quarters of 2025:

| Metric | Before adjustment | After adjustment | Reduction |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| 2025Q1 revenue | 369.40 RMB 100m | 170.86 RMB 100m | **-198.54 RMB 100m** |
| 2025 first-three-quarter revenue | 609.45 RMB 100m | 306.38 RMB 100m | **-303.07 RMB 100m** |
| 2025 full-year revenue | ~891 RMB 100m (estimated) | 405.29 RMB 100m | **-54.55%** |
| 2025Q1 net profit | 148.60 RMB 100m | 44.16 RMB 100m | **-104.44 RMB 100m** |
| 2025 full-year net profit | ~318 RMB 100m (estimated) | 89.54 RMB 100m | **-71.89%** |

### 8.2 Substance of the Restatement

- **Company explanation**: "Accounting error correction," adjusting the timing of revenue recognition based on the principle of prudence
- **Market interpretation**: In substance, it changed "recognizing revenue after channel stuffing to distributors" into "recognizing revenue after terminal sell-through"
- **Deeper implication**: A large portion of revenue in the previous 2-3 years may have been "recognized early"; products were counted as sold once shipped to distributor warehouses, even though they may still have been sitting in the channel

### 8.3 Duan Yongping Lens

> "Dishonest management can turn a good business into a bad one."

The financial-statement restatement exposed problems far beyond financial technique:
1. **How much of the past few years' high growth was real?** If "shipping to distributors = revenue recognition" was a long-running practice, revenue data from 2022-2024 may all contain "water"
2. **How trustworthy is the new management team?** The combination of a big bath and high growth in 2026Q1 itself raises suspicion of "first digging a hole, then claiming credit"
3. **Systemic SOE governance risk**: Wuliangye Group is 55% state-owned, and two chairmen have fallen in succession. This is not an individual problem; it is an institutional problem

## 9. Summary: Would Duan Yongping Buy Wuliangye?

Apply Duan Yongping's three standards for the final judgment:

| Standard | Assessment | Meets standard? |
|------|------|---------|
| **Good business model** | Baijiu is a good business, but Wuliangye's differentiation and pricing power are weaker than Moutai's; differentiation is not strong enough, and the moat is narrowing | ⚠️ Partially meets |
| **Good corporate culture** | Two chairmen investigated in succession, financial-reporting credibility collapsed, and SOE governance has systemic defects | ❌ Does not meet |
| **Reasonable price** | Based on normalized profit of 180-230 RMB 100m, current PE is 16-21x; not expensive, but not cheap either | ⚠️ Ambiguous |

**Final judgment: Duan Yongping would most likely not buy Wuliangye.**

The logic is simple: if you want to invest in baijiu, why not buy Moutai directly?

Duan Yongping has owned Moutai since 2012. He bought at 180 yuan and added at 120 yuan. His reason was never "baijiu is good," but "Moutai is good." In Duan Yongping's framework, within the same industry, invest only in the best company: when the industry declines, only the best company lives well; when the industry rises, the best company rises the most.

**Wuliangye is a good business, but under Duan Yongping's standards, it is not good enough: differentiation is not strong enough, pricing power is not firm enough, and corporate culture has problems. If the industry contains an obviously better choice (Moutai), Wuliangye is not worth investing in.**

> Duan Yongping: "Investing does not require courage. If you need courage to make an investment decision, it means you do not understand the business well enough."

For Wuliangye, too much "courage" is required to assume management will improve, the moat will widen again, and pricing power will recover. Each of these assumptions carries uncertainty. Investing in Moutai requires almost none of these assumptions.
