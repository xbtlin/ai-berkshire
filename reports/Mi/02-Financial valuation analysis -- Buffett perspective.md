##2.3 Elements of analysis

** The decline in the Māori rate of mobile phone operations is the most worrying: the Māori rate for mobile phones fell from 12.6 per cent to 10.9 per cent in 2025 and the same rate of revenue fell by 2.8 per cent, mainly because of the cost of the global price hike for memory chips (which was nearly four times higher than the previous year). ** Internet service is "profit cow"**: only 8% of income, but the Māori rate is 76.5%, contributing disproportionately to profits. This is the most favourable part of the Māori business model - light assets, high repurchases, and approximation to the "toll-shop" economy.

** The rate of gross domestic product (Māori) in automobiles has increased rapidly**: from 18.5 per cent in 2024 to 24.3 per cent in 2025, with operating profits now being re-established for the first time (90 million yuan). On the other hand, the annual operating profits are only $900 million, corresponding to $106 million, with operating profits of less than 1 per cent, and there is still a distance from real "profits".

** ROE improvement**: from 11.3% in 2023 to about 19% in 2025. It is noted, however, that this increase benefited in part from the non-operational investment gains included in the net GAAP profit of 2025. ROE is still a good level if calculated as adjusted net profits of about 17-18%.

#7.3 SETP Key assumptions and uncertainties

** Issues that require honest identification**:

1. **The independent valuation of Internet services is controversial**: Internet services (advertisement + game + financial technology) are highly dependent on the user base of mobile phones and IOTs and should not be valued on the basis of independent Internet companies. If mobile phone users lose their use, Internet services are also shrinking.

2. ** Estimates of the operating profit margin of mobile phones may be optimistic**: 5% are estimates. Given the projected increase in the price of deposits and the decline in the volume of goods produced in 2026, the real profit margin may be lower.

3. ** The comparability of multiple PSs is problematic: the PS is about 0.8 x, and the Biadi PS is about 1.2 x. The 1.2x PS for millet cars is actually already implying higher growth expectations.

4. ** Group discounts**: A diversified business group usually has a group discount of 15-25%. At 20% discount, the SOTP valuation will be reduced from HK$42.72 to approximately HK$34.

** If the PE for the Internet is reduced to 20% discount for 15x+groups**:

- Every share. 30.3 HK$.
