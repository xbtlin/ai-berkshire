## Seven and three-year outlook

### Optimistic scenario

- ARR increased from 300 million to 1.5-2 billion US dollars, LibTV became the industry standard tool for AI video production
- Overseas Lovart opens up the design market, and the proportion of international revenue increases to 30%+
- Valuation of US$5-10 billion, possible IPO
- Analog path: Canva early days → Canva now ($40 billion valuation)

### Neutral scenario

- ARR increases to $600-1 billion, but growth slows
- AIGC products from major manufacturers (Byte, Tencent) divert some users
- The homogeneity of AI video tools is increasing, and LibTV needs to continue investing to stay ahead.
- Valuation range of US$5-8 billion

### Pessimistic scenario

- Large model capabilities are rapidly democratized, and tool layer barriers are weakened (upper-layer tools lose differentiation after the underlying model is open sourced)
- ByteDream/KeLing uses traffic advantage + free strategy to squeeze paying users
- AI content copyright/compliance risks explode and supervision tightens
-Hindered overseas expansion (data compliance, geopolitics)
- The price ceiling per customer is low, and 300 million ARR is the peak rather than the starting point

### Core judgment

Yanyu Technology is currently one of the fastest growing companies in China’s AI application layer. There is a high probability that it will continue to grow rapidly to reach the ARR level of US$1 billion within 3 years. The biggest risk is that the barriers at the tool layer are not deep enough - when the underlying model iterates too fast, the upper-layer application may become a "shell". The founder’s product sense (from Byte) and commercialization capabilities from the C-side to the B-side are the core competitiveness.
