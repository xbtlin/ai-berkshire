# I, AZZ Inc. (NYSE: AZZ) - King of Hot Zinc, forgotten AI Foundation

# 1. Bottleneck positioning: Why is the thermal zinc unbreakable link in the AI infrastructure

One fact that is easily overlooked:** Each transformer, every transmission tower, every data centre steel structure must be treated with thermal zinc corrosion**

The AI data centre is not just a GPU, a server. A 100 MW-class super-large data centre requires a dedicated transformer, high voltage transmission line, steel-structured plant, cable bridge racks, cooling towers -- all steel units, all of which require thermal zinc. Without thermal zinc, steel will be rusted and scrapped in 5-10 years, while the data centre design life is usually 25-30 years.

AZZ is the largest independent thermal zinc service provider in North America, with a market share of about 25-30% (source: Mordor Intelligence/MarkWide Research industry report). Forty-two thermal zinc plants in the United States form a dense network of geographical coverage. The special feature of thermal zinc is that steel structures are large, transport costs are high, and customers tend to choose the nearest zinc-plating plant.

** Why the market didn't price AI logically?** The reason is simple: the hot-plating zinc is too "traditional." The director of the AI thematic fund on Wall Street never even studies a zinc-plating company. The AZZ investor group is mainly an industry/material fund, and they're concerned with building cycles, not with the expansion of AI's computing power. That's the information.

#3. Precoat Metals Business Analysis

** Impact on the overall investment argument:** Precoat Metals is a stabilizer rather than an engine of growth. It provides a steady cash flow to help pay off the debt. The real AI story is in metal coatings.
