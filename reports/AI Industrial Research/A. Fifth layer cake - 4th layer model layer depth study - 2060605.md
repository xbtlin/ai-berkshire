# The fourth core logic: who's making real money, who's burning money for life?

# One, the fastest income growth in history, but also the most expensive game ever

Anthropic annualized income rose from $1 billion in January 2025 to $47 billion in May 2026, a 14-month increase of 47 times. This rate went beyond all records in human business history — Google spent five years from zero to $10 billion, Meta spent six years, ChatGPT spent three years. Andthropic did 47 billion in 14 months.

But note the denominator. Anthropic just completed a $65 billion H-round financing worth $965 billion. OpenAI acknowledged in S-1 that it would cost $122 per dollar, projected losses of $14 billion in 2026 and additional capital of $207 billion by 2030. This is not a "normal loss during a high-growth period"; it is a race to burn money and finance it.

Core paradox: Income growth at the model level is alarming, but the profit margin is frightening.

# Two, Anthropic goes beyond OpenAI, and the business market is turned around

The biggest surprise in 2026 was not how strong AI was, but how well Anthropic became a leader from later. According to Ramp’s spending data, Anthropic’s share of corporate AI spending jumped from 10% in early 2025 to over 65% in February 2026.

In the most critical area of code generation, Claude has 42-54 per cent of the global market share, and OpenAI has only 21 per cent. Claude Code’s annualized income, which in February 2026 exceeded $2.5 billion, is the core engine of Anthropic growth.

ChatGPT has 50 million pay-for-subsidy subscribers and 900 million monthly users, and branding recognition crushes all competitions. The business market, Anthropic, is leading, and the consumer market, OpenAI, is the king of a two-line war.

# Three, models are being rapidly commercialized -- that's the most important structural judgment.

The gap between open-source and closed-source models has been reduced from 17.5 percentage points to 0.3 percentage points in one year in the MMLU baseline test.

More importantly, at the cost end: the cost of training a front-line model has fallen from about $100 million in 2023 to $20-40 million in 2026. MoE architecture, DPO training methods, synthetic data technology are all accelerating.

What does that mean? Models are becoming an infrastructure, like electricity or bandwidth -- important, but difficult to maintain pricing rights over the long term. Today’s frontier model is the model of open-source communities six months later.

# Four, "sell shovels" is more stable than "models"

If the model is commercialized, who has the right to price? The answer is two types of companies:

** Category I: Data owners.** The quality of the AI model depends on the quality of the training data. Reddit sits on 20 years, billions of real human discussions, the largest structured human view database on the Internet. Google and OpenAI pay for it. Data are consumables - the more many models and the more training, the greater the demand for data. And the availability of quality data is limited.

** Category II: Data labelers.** Scale AI provides training data labels for all major model companies, and Scale sells shovels regardless of who wins or loses. The AI training data set market was about $7.5 billion in 2026 and is projected to reach $52.4 billion in 2035, with a 24 per cent compound growth rate.

By contrast, model companies ask what your moat is. If DeepSeek can achieve 90% of the cost, the API pricing for the closed-source model is bound to be compressed.

# The investment value of the IPO tide in the second half of the year # V, 2026

On 1 June 2026, Anthropic submitted a confidential S-1 with an estimated value of $965 billion. OpenAI submitted a secret S-1 on 22 May 2026, with an estimated target of $852 billion to $1 trillion, which was scheduled to be marketed in September. Cerebras showed a first surge of 108 per cent on 14 May, and SpaceX (including x AI) planned to market Nasdaq in June with an estimated value of $1.75 trillion.

This is the biggest wave of IPOs since the 2000 Internet bubble. Three things for investors:

** Supply shock** — A massive influx of highly valued AI stocks into the open market dilutes the entire portfolio.
** Transparency of information** - The S-1 document will disclose for the first time the true financial position of these companies, and OpenAI's S-1 has revealed an alarming level of loss (US$ 1.22 per dollar earned).
** Price opportunities** — When Anthropic and OpenAI are listed, investors will be able to make a real rational comparison between listed companies AI (Google, Meta, Reddit) and newly listed AI.

Core view: Do not rush to buy on the first day of IPO. When S-1 data comes out, it may be found that the "old company" is the better deal.

##1. Reddit (RDDT) - AI train data miners, marketed for social media

** Corporate profile and bottlenecks location**

Reddit is the largest anonymous community discussion platform in the world, with billions of posts and reviews accumulated over 20 years, covering almost all areas of human knowledge, from quantum physics to cooking skills. Its unique value lies not in social relationship maps (which are Meta's territory), but in structured human opinion data — organized according to topic, with voting ranking, and multiple rounds of discussion. This is precisely the best ingredient to train AI to understand how humans think.

The bottleneck for Reddit is "AI Training Data Miner". Google and OpenAI have both signed data authorization agreements. But the market still priced it mainly on social media/advertising companies, and the strategic value of data authorization is seriously underestimated.

** Core financial/valued data**

- Q1,2026 battalion: $663 million (year-on-year + 69 per cent) with an increase of 60 per cent over seven consecutive quarters
- Q1,2026 net profit: $204 million and net interest rate 31 per cent
- Māori: 91.5% - top level of Internet companies
- Q1 data authorization income (Other Review): $39 million (year-on-year + 15 per cent)
- Total year data authorized contract value: over $200 million (Google+OpenAI+Other), with analysts projecting $400 million in 2027
- DAU: 126.8 million (year-on-year + 17 per cent)
- Global ARPU: $5.23, USA ARPU: $9.63
- Current stock price: approximately $170 and market value of approximately $33 billion
- Foreground P/E: about 29-34 times (source: GuruFocus, June 2026)

Source: Reddit Q1 2026 (SEC 10-Q/8-K), CNBC, Yahoo Finance

** Non-alternative analysis**

Reddit data are highly irreplaceable. For three reasons: (1) 20 years of historical accumulation cannot be replicated — you cannot recreate a community that has been discussing sedimentation for 20 years; (2) data are structured — sorted by subreddit, sorted by vote, and with context dialogue chains, naturally suitable for AI training; (3) content is continuously generated — millions of new posts and reviews per day, live data streams, not static data sets.

Reddit’s core income remains advertising (Q1-94%), and it will take time to move from "acne" to "core driver." And, if model companies move to synthetic data training in the future, demand for real human data may decline – although there is no evidence that this will happen soon.

** Valuation judgement**

By comparison with social media peers (Snap forwards about 50 times P/E and Pinterest about 25 times), the 29-34 times forward P/E of Reddit is within reasonable range. But this pricing does not reflect the future value of the data-authorization business. If the data-authorization income is expected to reach $400 million in 2027, and 80% incremental profit, this business alone is worth $3-50 billion (valued at 15-20 times). In other words, the market gives you a Reddit, which is priced by advertising companies, a data-authorization business that is almost for nothing.

** Risk hint**

- Slow growth in data authorization revenue (Q1 + 15 per cent, well below advertising + 74 per cent), which may indicate that the current contract price is locked at a lower level
- Regulatory risk: copyright and privacy issues for AI training data are under review in the EU and the USA
- User growth depends on internationalization, but the ability of international users to realize is much lower than that of American users
- If synthetic data are mature, the scarcity premium for real human data may decline

** One sentence concludes:** The market priced Reddit as an advertising company, but it sits on the most scarce resources of the AI era – massively structured human data. Of all the relevant model layers of listed companies, Reddit may have the best risk returns.

##2. Scale AI (unlisted) - AI training data labeled arms dealers, valued at 29 billion

** Corporate profile and bottlenecks location**

Scale AI was founded in 2016 by Alexander Wang, 23, and initially marked as an automated driver, and now is the largest ASI training data tag platform in the world. Its business model is similar to that of an AI arms dealer – whoever wins OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Meta needs Scale to mark training data. After Meta acquired 49% of the equity in 2025, Scale was valued at $29 billion.

Scale's bottleneck is positioned as "A.T.T.T.T.T.T.T.T.D., which is a tool for the development of the data system.

** Core financial/valued data**

- Revenue in 2025: approximately $2 billion (Sacra estimates), an increase of about 130 per cent over the same year
- 2026: over $1 billion (the CEO publicly projected) - Note that this seems to contradict the 2 billion in 2025, possibly with conservative guidance or a difference in calibre
- Latest valuation: $29 billion (after the Meta investment in June 2025)
- Total financing: approximately $15.9 billion, spanning nine rounds
- Annualized income from business operations: approximately $200 million, expected to exceed the data mark for 18 months
- P/S (estimate): approximately 14.5 times (collected at $2 billion)

Sources: Sacra, Tracxn, KuCoin, TechCrunch

** Non-alternative analysis**

The advantages of Scale are that: (1) the client covers almost all major model companies, creating a standard position in the industry; (2) the extension of the label to government and business AI tools has created multiple lines of business; and (3) the strategic investment of Meta has increased resources and client stickivity.

First, there is a risk that the data mark will fall in the technical threshold – with the AI model itself increasing, the Auto-Labeling is encroaching on the artificial market. Second, the 49% shareholding of Meta means that Scale is deeply tied to Meta, and other model companies (OpenAI, Anthropic) may be less dependent on Scale because of competitive relationships. Third, the trend of large customers to build their own tagging capacity is accelerating.

** Valuation judgement**

If data labeling operations are eroded by AI automation, Scale needs to be successfully transformed into an enterprise AI platform to support the valuation. Currently, business operations are only $200 million in annualized income, and are far from supporting the $29 billion valuation.

** Risk hint**

- 49% of Meta shares could lead to loss of other major clients.
- When AI automatically labels the technology, core operations are at risk of being subverted
- Financial data on unlisted companies is not transparent and most of the data collected are estimated by third parties
- IPO time is not clear.

** One sentence concludes:** Scale is an arms dealer in the AI arms race, but Meta’s deep binding and AI’s automatic threat of markings makes it less wide than it looks. Wait for IPO to see the real financial data before judging.

- Dependence on distribution of AWS/Google Cloud - Anthropic has no own consumer access
- The 96.5 billion valuation implied the assumption that any deceleration would lead to a violent re-entry.

** One sentence concludes:** Anthropic is the most alarming growth story in the AI area in 2026, but the $965 billion valuation has been set to be carried out perfectly. ##4. Alphabet/Google (GOOGL) - Model + Distribution + Calculus 3 Flyers

What Google is unique about the model layer is not that it is the strongest model (Claude and GPT lead on multiple benchmarks), but rather that it has the most complete vertical integration in the AI industry: the Gemini self-researching model (Gemini) has its own distribution channel (Google searches for 2 billion users, Android) its own cloud platform (GCP) and self-research chip (TPU). No second company has these four layers at the same time.

Google's bottleneck is "One-Stop Platform for AI." It doesn't need to win a model war -- it just needs a model "good enough" and then crushes its opponent through search, cloud, Andre's distribution.

** Core financial/valued data**

- Q1,2026 net profit: $62.6 billion (year-on-year +81 per cent)
- Q1,2026 Google Claude: $20 billion (+63 per cent of year)

- Gemini Monthly User: over 750 million
- Gemini Corporate Paying Seats: Over 8 million
- Annual growth of AI product income: +200%
- Capital expenditure guidelines 2026: $18190 billion
- Current market value: approximately $4.38 trillion
- Current P/E: approximately 17-18 times

Source: Alphabet Q1 2026 Financial Reporting (SEC 8-K), CNBC, Investment.com

** Non-alternative analysis**

The irreplaceable role of Google in the model layer is reflected in the flying wheel effect: (1) search + Andre provides a distribution channel for 2 billion + users, with any AI key reaching; (2) GCP provides business customer sticky, with Claude ' s backlog order of $460 billion implying a high level of income determination over the next two years; (3) TPU chips reduce their own reasoning costs and create cost advantages.

But Gemini of Google is still behind ChatGPT on the consumer end. The global AIT chat robot market, with about 60% of ChatGPT and 13.5% of Gemini. Google’s advantage is not to win the AI chat robot battle, but to embed AI into a billion-grade product of search, mail, documents, maps, etc., allowing users to use AI unwittingly.

** Valuation judgement**

The market value of $4.38 trillion corresponds to about 17-18 times P/E, taking into account the 22 per cent increase in revenue, 81 per cent increase in profits and the volatility of the Claude operation (+63 per cent), which is a reasonable or even low valuation, compared to Nvidia P/E, which is about 40 times the value of Microsoft, which is one of the lowest premium rates paid by large technology companies for AI growth.

Key logic: Claude’s backlog of 46 billion orders means that even if growth slows, revenues over the next two years will be extremely visible. The realization of AI’s search ads is just beginning.

** Risk hint**

- AI search (e.g., Percy, ChatGPT search) may erode the share of the search ad
- 1800-1900 billion in capital spending is a big bet. If AI needs less than expected, ROI will be ugly.
Gemini's awareness of the consumer brand is still far below ChatGPT.

** One sentence concludes:** Of all relevant listed companies at the model level, Google may be the most undervalued - 17-18 times P/E bought the model plus distribution plus arithmetic + 46 billion backlog orders.

** One sentence concludes:** Cohere is the most reasonable player in the model layer, but it faces a free competition from open-source models that need to prove that "private deployment of a business" can support a sufficiently large market.

##6. Mistral AI (not listed) - the only option for European sovereign AI

** Corporate profile and bottlenecks location**

Mistral was founded in Paris in 2023 by Arthur Mensch, former Meta and Google Deepmind researchers, as the most competitive model company in Europe. Its unique position is not only technological, but geopolitical: in the context of the growing emphasis on "digital sovereignty" and "AI sovereignty", Mistral is the only company in Europe that has the capacity to provide a front-line AI model.

Mistral’s bottleneck is "European ASI sovereignty option." Mistral is the only option when European governments and businesses do not want to hand over data to American companies.

** Core financial/valued data**

- ARR 2025: Over $400 million
- Target 2026 ARR: over $1 billion
- Updated valuation: approximately $14 billion (including $830 million in debt financing in March 2026)
- Total financing: approximately $3.05 billion
- Top shareholders: ASML (EUR 1.3 billion in C rounds in 2025)
- March 2026: $830 million in debt financing for the purchase of 13,800 Nvidia chips
- Important clients/contracts: Airbus, BMW (May 2026)
- Acquisition: Emmi AI (May 2026, Industrial Physics AI)

Sources: Tracxn, Sacra, Pulse24, Klover.ai, TechFundingNews

** Non-alternative analysis**

Mistral’s irreplaceable high reliance on geopolitical narratives. From a purely technical point of view, Mistral’s model is not the best – it lags behind Claude and GPT on the main benchmark. But in the European market, it has two irreplaceable advantages: (1) data sovereignty – European enterprises and governments can ensure that data do not leave Europe; and (2) policy protection – EUAI regulation requires a natural bias towards European home-grown companies.

ASML, as the largest shareholder, provides strategic connectivity to the chip supply chain, and Airbus and BMW contracts validate industrial applications. But the problem is that if geopolitical barriers alone are relied on, the ceiling is limited to European markets.

** Valuation judgement**

The key question is whether Mistral can turn the narrative of "European AI sovereignty" into real silver. If the value of the 14 billion dollar is indeed 1 billion ARR in 2026, it is reasonable to estimate it in the same category.

** Risk hint**

- The technical gap with the United States and China could widen
- The European AI market is limited in size and the ceiling is much lower than the global market
- Dependence on debt financing purchasing power, financial leverage is increasing
- Free substitution in open-source communities (Llama, DeepSeek) is eroding the pay market
- Buying Emmi AI into industry is a new bet.

** One sentence concludes:** Mistral is the beneficiary of the geopolitical dividend, but investing in it is essentially a political narrative that is held in the name of "European AI sovereignty" and can translate into a continuing business demand.

** Non-alternative analysis**

** Risk hint**

- Closed-source attempts like Muse Spark may create a conflict of trust with open-source communities

** One sentence concludes:** Meta is not a model company and is one of the biggest beneficiaries of AI - open source models that help it reduce costs, improve advertising efficiency and weaken the price-fixing power of competitors. 125-145 billion capital spending is the only big bet.

** Non-alternative analysis**

The Crebras technology is truly unique – crystal-crystal chips have a significant delay and power advantage over GPU in a given reasoning setting. OpenAI’s large contract and AWS collaboration have proven the commercial viability of the technology.

Nvidia’s reasoning chips are also fast-tracked (Blackwell series), AMD, Google TPU, and an AI chip starter are competing in the race track. Cerebras’ crystal-crys, while unique, do not mean that they cannot be bypassed – if efficiency gains can be achieved through software optimization or standard GPS, the need for specialized hardware will be greatly reduced.

** Valuation judgement**

The market value of 80-95 billion is about 160-190 times the price of 510 million, and P/S is an IPO valuation, not a rational pricing. Even with the most optimistic 2026 forecast of revenues (between $1 billion and $1 billion), P/S is 60-90 times the price. The concentration of 86% (Abu Dhabi) is an extreme risk – the loss of a client can destroy most of the collection.

OpenAI's $20 billion contract sounds attractive, but it is multi-year, and the annualized contribution to the collection takes time to be validated.

** Risk hint**

- The client concentration is 86% the most extreme of all IPOs in recent years.
- P/S 160-190 with little room for error
- The Nvidia, AMD, the big ones are arguing that the chip track is getting more competitive.
- The end of the 180-day IPO sales period could cause a lot of pressure.
- The glycline chip is now in physical limits.

** One conclusion:** Cerebras is unique in technology but is evaluated crazy — P/S 160-190 times, customer concentration 86%, which is not an investment, it is a gamble. If you believe in the long-term need for the reasoning chip, let's say it falls to a reasonable valuation.

##10. xAI (unlisted, incorporated in SpaceX) - X platform data monopoly

** Corporate profile and bottlenecks location**

XAI was created by Elon Musk in 2023 to develop the Grok model and to integrate it deeply into X platform (former Twitter). SpaceX acquired the largest merger record in February 2026 with $1.25 trillion in MVA XAI (xAI $250 billion). In May 2026, xAI officially ceased to exist as an independent entity, and Grok and X were placed in the SpaceXAI department under the SpaceX flag. SpaceX was listed in June 2026 by Nasdaq, with an estimated valuation of $1.75 trillion.

The bottlenecks for xAI are "X Platform Data Monopoly". X (former Twitter) has the largest real-time open discussion data in the world, and Grok is the only model that can directly use these data.

** Core financial/valued data**

- Revenue in 2025: approximately $3.2 billion (SpaceX IPO document disclosure)
- Operating loss in 2025: $6.4 billion
- X and Grok subscription income: approximately $365 million
- Data authorization income: approximately $88 million
- AI solutions and infrastructure income: $465 million
- Valuation at consolidation: $250 billion (as SpaceX subsidiary)
- SpaceX target IPO valuation: $1.75 trillion
- P/S (independent estimate): approximately 78 times (estimated at 3.2 billion and 250 billion)

Sources: CNBC, TechCrunch, StartupHub.ai, Wikipedia (SpaceXAI), Motley Fool

** Non-alternative analysis**

xAI's irreplaceable data monopoly is almost entirely from platform X. X has big real-time public discussion, press reviews, and hot topic data. These data are unique to the AI model that trains to understand what's happening in the world.

But there are obvious weaknesses in the non-alternativeity. First, the user activity and data quality of X are constantly questioned after Musk has taken over -- a large number of advertisers and users have lost. Second, the value of real-time data depends on the user base and activity, both of which are in decline. Third, Grok is not leading the baseline test -- it is a model that can be "but not the best".

** Valuation judgement**

The $250 billion valuation is for 3.2 billion and 6.4 billion, which is not a company that can be assessed for traditional financial indicators. It is now part of SpaceX, where investors cannot buy XAI alone. The value of XAI is included in the SpaceX target valuation of 1.75 trillion, but XAI is clearly a value impairment (an annual deficit of 6.4 billion).

For investors, the only way to participate is by indirectly holding xAI through SpaceX IPO. But you are essentially buying SpaceX's rocket business and Starlink, and xAI is just an accessory.

** Risk hint**

- xAI is no longer an independent entity, and cannot invest alone.
- $6.4 billion a year, $3.2 billion -- it's burning money, not commercial.
- The continued loss of users and advertisers of X platforms
- Grok is not a technical leader, and branding is far less known than ChatGPT and Claude.
- Musk's attention is distracted by a number of companies, including Tesla, SpaceX, X, xAI, Neuralink.

** One sentence concludes: ** xAI's data monopoly on platform X is a theoretical barrier, but the $6.4 billion loss and shrinking X ecology make the value of the barrier questionable. Unless you look at Musk's integration capacity with great seriousness, it is not recommended that XAI be paid.
