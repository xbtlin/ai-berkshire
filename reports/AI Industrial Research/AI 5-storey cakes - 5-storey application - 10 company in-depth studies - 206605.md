# I. Core logic: the truth of investment at the application level

# Most AI concept units still have low AI income

This is the cruelest reality of the 2026 AI application.

Hundreds of companies are labeled "AI concept" in the market, but they can actually produce AI income data. Agentforce ARR in Salesforce, while at $1.2 billion, or 20.5%, is in its annual $4.5 billion plate, less than 3%. Adobe's Firefly ARR just passed $250 million, or less than 1% of the total. Large model API and MaaS platform revenue is $385 million, or 1.4% of the 27.1 billion total.

Real "Ali income" is more than 50% of listed companies, and there may be no more than three globally: Palantir (almost 100% of revenues revolve around AI decision-making systems), Tempus AI (the core business is AI precision medicine), and small market-market companies like C3.ai. Most so-called AI companies are essentially "traditional business plus AI enhancement".

This is not bad. On the contrary, it reveals the core investment logic of the application: **AI is the means, not the end. The real money is not "a company that does it," but "a company that makes more money with AI."**

# The valuation splits to extreme levels

As of early June 2026, the valuation differences between companies in the same level of AI application were striking:

Palantir's P/E is seven times more than Salesforce and nearly ten times Adobe. But Palantir's revenue (approximately $6.5 billion annually) is only one-seventh of Salesforce, one-fourth of Adobe. The market's pricing logic for Palantir is "the AI system of the AI era," and the pricing logic for Salesforce and Adobe is "the AI renovation of traditional software companies".

This narrative difference creates a huge valuation arbitrage.

# Real opportunity: companies whose potential for realization is overlooked by traditional valuation frameworks

The market made a classic mistake: ** "AI purity" for pricing, not "AI liquidity".**

Palantir is indeed one of the purest AI companies, but its clients are concentrated in government and large enterprises, with long procurement cycles and speed of expansion limited to the size of the sales team. In contrast, Salesforce is host to the largest corporate CRM customer pool worldwide, with every existing customer as a potential buyer of Agentforce, with minimal cross-salers. Adobe has 850 million live-time users, Firefly directly embedded in existing products such as Photoshop, Illustrator, Acrobat, and the cost of switching is saved for users.

The companies that are "punishable" by the traditional valuation framework are those companies: the market sees 22 times the rate of increase in the Salesforce + 13 per cent for PE, 16 times the rate of increase in Adobe + 12 per cent for PE — but it completely ignores the growth rate of more than 200 per cent in AI operations by these companies and the potential for improved profitability over the next 2-3 years for the AI penetration increase.

Conversely, Palantir +85% is an alarming increase, but 153 times the PE means that the market has already pushed all of the optimistic expectations for the next five years into prices. Once the rate of increase has dropped from 85% to 50%, stock prices may not have been slower, but direct declines.

# Databricks 2026 IPO's puncture meaning

Databricks may be the most important technology IPO in the second half of 2026.

The latest round of financing was valued at $134 billion, with more than $5.4 billion in revenue from Run Rate, 65 per cent more than the same year, and $1.4 billion in annualized income from AI products. If IPOs were priced at $150-$200 billion, they would be about 28-37 times P/S, which would be a new anchor for AI’s valuation of the Applied Layer.

The point of Databricks is not only in itself, but also in its redefinition of the valuation benchmark for the "AI data platform" category. Snowflake (currently worth about $90 billion, forward P/E 85 times) will be re-examined as a result – if Databricks were to get a higher valuation of AI products at higher speeds and better speeds, would the valuation logic of Snowflake be further compressed or he would be in high seas?

** Summarizing the investment methodology at this level: "No more pure company AI," looking for "a company with the strongest ability to realize but undervalued by the traditional valuation framework". Between 153-fold PE Palantir and 16-fold PE Adobe, there are huge gaps in understanding and pricing**

- Robotaxi ' s scalation schedule has been postponed repeatedly.
- The political controversy (DOGE, etc.) in Elon Musk continues to undermine brand image and willingness to buy cars
- China's market competition is hot and local brands like Biade are double-plugged in technology and prices
- 355 times PE means any negative news could trigger a crash.
- Optimus robots have been commercialized for at least three to five years. They're now pure concepts.

** One conclusion: Tesla is the best carrier if you believe that autopilot and human robots can reshape human society; but 355 times PE means you're paying for a utopian, and it's not worth the wager.**
