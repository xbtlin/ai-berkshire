#6.3 Is the valuation gap reasonable?

** PDD PE 10x vs Amazon PE 33x gap reflects:**

1. ** Business quality differentials (reasonable)**: AArazon has two high-profit, high-precision engines for advertising AWS+; PDF profits are highly dependent on a domestic market in China
2. ** Growth certainty difference (reasonable)**: Amazon ' s 2.3 times greater certainty than the PDD in 10 years
** Geopolitical risk discount (reasonable but potentially excessive): PDF as a medium sum, subject to a triple discount of VIE structural risk plus US-M decoupling risk plus tariff risk
** Cash feedback discrepancy (reasonable)**: Amazon has started buying back, PDD holds $60.4 billion in cash but does not buy back

** My judgment:**
- PDD 10x PE is cheap, but "Little is cheap" -- this valuation implies market pricing for Temu's geopolitical risks and domestic growth decline.
- Amazon 33x PE is not expensive - AWS+ advertising increases in profits support premiums and is much more certain than PDF
- If PDD starts buying back/sending back on a large scale, PE may recover from 10 x to 15 x, which in itself is an additional 50% return
- ** Under the current valuation, the expected return on PDF was slightly higher than that on Amazon (4.3 per cent vs 3.6 per cent), but much more than that.**
