# National Production Substitute: True or False?

"National substitution" is the investment logic most often mentioned in the long light-eared core. But it needs to be unbundled.

# Positive argument: Nationally produced alternative space is really big

** Fact I** China is one of the largest machines and visual markets in the world, with industrial automation still below Japan-Hand, with penetration increasing spatial certainty.

** Fact II** In the industrial CIS area, Chinese equipment dealers used almost 100% of Sony and Ansommy's products. Long-light e-cube cores are the only national producers with the capacity to provide alternatives.

China’s industrial equipment suppliers, for supply chain security reasons, have taken the initiative to find a national CIS supplier. In the context of persistent uncertainty about the sanctions on chips, "bought" is more important than "better bought."

** Fact IV**: AI visual testing (defects detection, 3D measurements) requires higher performance CIS, which drives ASP ups and increases demand.

# Back-check: Do not overestimate replacement speed

** Issue I: Industrial CIS replacement cycle is extremely long**

A machine visual device imports new sensors with a validation cycle of **6-18 months**. The equipment operator does not immediately replace the Sony chip, which has been in use for years, because of "national production replacement" -- the cost of switching is too high (the whole line needs to be re-engineered). The replacement is gradual, not explosive.

** Question two: "National production replacement" does not mean "only buy long light-eared cores".**

Howell is also a Chinese firm, and much larger. Howell is an option if industrial equipment dealers seek to replace national production.

** Issue III: Alternative ceilings depend on the terminal market**

China's industrial automation investments are cyclical. The almost zero growth in the income of the long light-ear core in 2023 (604 million – 60.5 million) suggests that the "home-based replacement" logic cannot be pushed down against industry when industrial investment slows down.

# And conclusions

** The substitution of national production is a real proposition in the area of industrial CIS, but the pace is slower than expected in the market and the ceiling is lower than the narrative suggests.**
