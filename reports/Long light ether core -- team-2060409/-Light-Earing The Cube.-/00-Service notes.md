## Series Positioning

Changguang Chenxin is not a "hot" company - most investors have never heard of it. But it is the **only player that can compete with the global top three** in China's high-end industrial/scientific CMOS image-sensor market, and on its April 2026 Hong Kong listing day it jumped 75%, pushing its market cap to HKD 30 billion.

- The bulls say: global No. 3, only Chinese player, 67% gross margin, and domestic substitution has only just begun
- The bears say: RMB 300 million of annual profit for a RMB 30 billion market cap, a TAM of only USD 3.3 billion, and the foundry lifeline is controlled by others

**Both sides have a point.** That is exactly why the company deserves serious research.

This series aims to **first explain what this company is, and only then let you decide whether it is worth buying** - not to hand you the conclusion.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - as an IPO newcomer, Changguang Chenxin has limited information, so this series does not force in fake precision
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
