# Eight, final judgment

** If only one vote:**

- ** Seeking certainty - > Illustratively investing in Temu through PDF Holdings.** PDD is about 10 times more than PE today, and even if Temu only achieves a neutral scenario, the total PDD (domestic +Temu) profit growth can support 15-20% annualized returns. The key is that multiple domestic operations provide profit security cushions.

- ** High-elasticity - > et al. Shein IPO.** If IPO pricing is $30 billion, net profit in 2031 is $4 billion, equivalent to 7.5 times PE, assuming the market gives 15-20 times PE, the market value can be $60-80 billion, five years more.

** Sort of core variables (applicable for both):**
1. The movement of Chinese and United States tariffs (determinate factor)
2. The EU legislation on cross-border electricity suppliers is in place
3. Monetization of advertising/take rate up
4. Level of growth realization in emerging markets (Latin America, South-East Asia, Middle East)
5. Progress in localizing supply chain development
