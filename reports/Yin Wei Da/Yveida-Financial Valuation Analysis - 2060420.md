# Eight, final conclusion

# # Valuation appeal rating: <unk> (3/5)

** Reason**:

Yveita is a company of great quality** - the Deep CUDA Eco-plus Hardware Monopoly, the profitability to crush rivals, the balance sheet, and the cash flow. 22x PE is not expensive for 65% of the companies.

** But the margin of security is not sufficient at $110. **:
- DCF conservative valuation is only $90, and current prices contain more optimistic expectations
- The trend of slowing up is clear (65% - > may be lower in the future)
- There's hardly any security margin in pessimism.
- 30% of the security margin Buffett wants does not exist at the current price.

*Buffett says, "Bought good companies at reasonable prices, far better than bought mediocrity companies at excellent prices." "Eng Weida is certainly a good company, but $110 is only "reasonable," and it's not "better."*

** Security margin percentage**:
- Based on EPS probabilistic weighting: **+58% (safe margin)**
- Based on DCF probabilistic weighting: **-18% (no security margin)**
- ** Combined judgement: approximately + 20% security margin** (with approximately 38% space for two methods ~ $177 vs $110, but about + 20% after considering implementation risk discounts)

** One sentence summing up**: Good company, reasonable price, but not the fat ball Buffett will be determined to do -- give it a second thought when $85-90.
