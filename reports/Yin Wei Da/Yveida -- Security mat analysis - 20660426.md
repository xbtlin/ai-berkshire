# Barfitt: "This price cannot bear any mistakes."

> The security cushion is not a single indicator, but a '** how much I'm likely to make + wrong **' multiplier. The NVDA now has the probability of 50% small, 30% small, 40% low, 20% double. This risk returns more than I would ever do.

> ** Control warehouse within 3% if necessary ** - so that even zeroing does not affect life."
