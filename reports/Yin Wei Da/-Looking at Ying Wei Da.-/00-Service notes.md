## Series Positioning

Nvidia does not lack attention - it is the world's most valuable company, and hundreds of analysis articles are written about it every day.

But most analysis has two problems:

1. **The bulls only look at growth**: AI compute demand is exploding, the CUDA moat is bottomless, Jensen Huang is a genius - therefore the stock "only goes up"
2. **The bears only look at the bubble**: it is a replay of Cisco in 2000, customers will design their own chips, and gross margins are unsustainable - therefore it will "inevitably crash"

**Both sides are right, but neither side is complete.** Nvidia is a company in which "growth is certain, monopoly is loosening, and valuation is expensive" are all true at the same time.

This series aims to **look at those three things together** - first explain what it is, and then let you decide whether it is worth buying.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - many key Nvidia data points (such as inference market share and chip-replacement ratios) are not officially confirmed, so this series only uses the most reliable sources
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer

## Comparison With "Understanding PDD" and "Understanding Tencent"

| Dimension | Tencent | PDD | Nvidia |
|-----------|---------|-----|--------|
| Asset quality | WeChat is irreplaceable | Algorithmic ad efficiency is extreme | Near-monopoly in AI compute |
| Valuation | Reasonably cheap | Extremely cheap | **Expensive** |
| Management | Highly transparent | Low transparency | Very high capability + no succession plan |
| Core risk | Regulatory tail risk | Temu political risk | **Cycle + customer self-design + valuation** |
| Certainty | High | Medium | **High in the short term, lower in the long term** |

Nvidia's special feature is: **among the three, it has the best quality, but also the highest price.** A great company and a great investment are not the same thing.
