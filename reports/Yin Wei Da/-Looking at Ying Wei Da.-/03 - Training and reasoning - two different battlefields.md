# Train the battlefield: Yin Weidah remains the only option — but not forever

# Why is training hard to shake

The training front-line model (GPT-5, Claude 4, Gemini 3) requires ** tens of thousands of GPUs running for months**. In this scenario, there are two things that others cannot do:

**1. NVLink / NVSwitch Full Connect**

British Wedge 200 NVL72 can connect 72 pieces of GPU to one another, with bandwidth **1.8 TB/s** -- equivalent to a supercomputer. The competition has only 1/2 to 1/6 of the English version.

The more connected, the more efficient the training. In the 100,000-carat training cluster, NCCL + NVLink + InfoniBand is the hardtest mound** in the British State.

**2. System-level reliability**

Training a front-line model can cost tens of millions of dollars. If training takes place in the 80th day because of hardware or software failure, it's going to start from the beginning -- or at least back to the nearest checkpoint.

Yvetteda has accumulated many years of experience with failed detection and automatic recovery of training in large clusters. Meta's academic paper shows that failure detection for 100,000 calories + recovery needs **3 minutes ** level response.** There is no open front model training failure that is attributed to "non-CUDA pitfalls".**

# Except for Google

**Google is the only company in the world that has proven to be completely free of the Grand Forward Model of British Weida GPU training**

Gemini 3 is fully trained on TPU - no British-Vuida GPU is used. TPU Ironwood (v7) has a cluster size of 9,216 chips/Pod, full interconnection with 3D torus, total cluster strength of 42.5 ExafLOPS.

But outside Google — OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, xAI — still depend deeply on British Weida at the end of the training.

** Counterpart **: AWS's Trainium2 has deployed nearly half a million in Project Rainier, and Anthropic is using its training model. Although there is no public evidence that Trainium2 can train the most cutting edge model at the GPT-5 level, the gap is narrowing. The 2027 Trainium3 may be the second alternative to the training end.

# A neglected variable: test-time compute

The reasoning market has a variable that benefits British Weida **test-time compute scaling**.

Take Claude's "thinking mode" and OpenAI's o1/o3: each reasoning is no longer simple "input output" but a model produces a lot of internal calculations before answering. This increases the need for a single reasoning **10-100 times**.

** If AI is increasingly using the "in-depth reflection" model, the demand for the reasoning level of the calculation will be greater than the market had expected** - this is the biggest growth driver of British Weddge's reasoning business.

** Inverse view**: The efficiency of the algorithm is also increasing rapidly. DeepSeek V3 trained a model close to the GPT-4 level with $5.6 million, at a training cost of 1/20. If a similar efficiency revolution occurs at the end of the reasoning, the assumption of "infinite growth in the demand for arithmetic" will be broken.
