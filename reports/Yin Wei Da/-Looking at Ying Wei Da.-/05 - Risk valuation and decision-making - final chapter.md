# Risk two: the DeepSeek event — the pre-dance of the fall of narrative

On January 27, 2025, China AI, DeepSeek, released the V3 model - training costs approximately $5.6 million (1/20 for GPT-4), with performance close to GPT-4 levels.

** Ying Wei Da fell by 17% that day, and the market value evaporated at $589 billion — the largest single-day market value loss in US history**

A month later, the market recovered. The reason was that the Jeffersonian paradox came into effect — efficiency gains stimulated more demand (Anthropic ARR 5 months up three times, four big cloud producers CapEx + 67%).

** But the real lesson of the DeepSeek event is not "calculation efficiency," but "statement risk" **:

- The market is extremely sensitive to any single event where the GPU demand may be overestimated**
- $5 trillion worth of money, 17% = $589 billion in a day for a paper.
- **The next trigger could be: **Anthropic Ironwood performance data, Wa-Cann ecological breakthrough, super-scale client CapEx first negative

** Meaning of investors**: Even if your long-term judgment is right (AI demand continues to grow),** short-term narrative collapse can evaporate 30-40% of the market value within days**. Having a British Weddge requires a great deal of psychological resilience.
