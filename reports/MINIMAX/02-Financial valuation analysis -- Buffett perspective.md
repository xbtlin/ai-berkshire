## 1. Company Profile

MiniMax (Xiyu Technology) was founded in December 2021 by Yan Junjie, the former vice president of SenseTime Technology. It is one of China's leading general large model companies. Core products include:
- **Talkie**: AI social/companion application, rapid growth in overseas users
- **Conch AI/Hoshino**: Domestic AI assistant and role-playing
- **Hailuo AI**: AI video generation
- **MiniMax API**: Model as a Service (MaaS) for B-side

The company will be listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in early 2025 with the stock code 9922.HK.
