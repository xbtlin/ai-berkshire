# Nine, core conclusion

# The industry judgement from the Manger perspective

**1. Qualifying industry: good track, bad business**

The AI model is the most important technological revolution of the past decade, and market space is unquestionable. But from a Manger perspective, typical "bad business" features: capital intensity, rapid technological turnover, low conversion costs, price war, and regulatory uncertainty.

> "Some industries are naturally built to consume capital without a bottom hole. Aviation from the Leiters to today creates a total negative value for shareholders. The AI industry has similar risks."

**2. MINIMAX ' s survival strategy is basically correct**

Select two differentiated paths for **socializing AI+ video generation overseas **, avoiding the pro-opposition confrontation between a pure text model and giant and DeepSeek, which is strategically sound.

**3. Final roll-out**

China's AI model industry is about the end of the game:
- ** Model layer**: 3-5 survivors (bytes, Ali, DeepSeek + 1-2 Unicorns)
- **Application **: 1-2 winners per vertical track, by scene
- **MinimaX location**: If Talkie and Hailuo can do Top 2 on their respective tracks, they have an independent chance of survival; otherwise they may be subject to merger

The final comment of Manger: "The key to investment is not to find good industries, but good firms with a lasting competitive advantage in good industries. The AI industry is certainly good, but companies with a lasting competitive advantage may have only two or three. Investors' job is to find out if MINIMAX is one of them -- frankly, it's too early to draw this conclusion."
