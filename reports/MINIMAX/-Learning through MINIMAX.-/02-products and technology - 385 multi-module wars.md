# Disney copyright action -- Can't get around Can

In September 2025, Disney, Sphere Film, Warner Brothers jointly sued MINIMAX in the United States Federal Court of California, alleging that ConchAI violated copyrights over **50 ** IPs for film and video productions during training and production.

Points of order:
- Complainant claims up to US$ **150,000 for each torted work** and up to US$ **7.5 million for 50 items combined**
- And a court order to prevent conch AI from continuing to violate.
- The plaintiffs noted that conch AI already has content filtering mechanisms (which can intercept violence and pornography), but ** chose not to intercept copyright content**

MINIMAX responded in a letter of offer: considering broader scenarios such as potential follow-up proceedings, compensation in the worst case scenario may amount to US$ **75 million**, with a "limited proportion" of corporate financial resources. (Note: This figure is the worst scenario estimate in the letter of offer, which is higher than the maximum of US$ 7.5 million claimed by the plaintiff in the present case.)

**Cool analysis**: The amount of compensation per se is not fatal, but **the risk of prohibition** is the key - if the court prohibits conch AI from operating in the United States, it will directly attack overseas operations. Moreover, this action could trigger more follow-up prosecution by copyright holders.

** Counterpart **: AI is almost a business disease in copyright litigation - OpenAI, Stability AI, and Midjoourney face similar litigation. The presumably settlement is settled by settlement or licence fees.
