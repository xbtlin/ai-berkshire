# Billion races, hell competition

The global-generation AI market sizes are about US$ **90-100 billion** (estimated at 2025, IDC/Gartner consolidated), increasing at an annual rate of about **50%** and is expected to reach US$ **300 billion** in 2028. The Chinese market is about **50 billion yuan** and annual growth rates **70%**. These figures are the top scores in any industry.

The problem is that good lanes are not good business. History has repeatedly shown that the more people enter, the faster the more fast, often the first places where capital inflows, price wars are the first to blow and profits disappear.

Reviewing history:
- ** Aviation** is a trillion-class track, but from the Leiters to today, the net value of global airlines for shareholders is ** negative**
- ** During the Internet bubble**, hundreds of companies.com financed and eventually survived less than **5%**
- **The photovoltaic industry** and the global market is trillion-grade, but China’s photovoltage industry is losing its head because of the heavy price war.

The same script is being repeated in the AI model industry:** The track is trillion-grade, but the competition pattern is hell-class.**
