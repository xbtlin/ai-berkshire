# Five, six layers risk list

# Risk one: Keep burning money (severe: <unk> )

After adjustment, the deficit of**251 million** was reduced by a monthly burn of**21 million**. Although IPO financing has been able to refinance the blood, it may be necessary to refinance it after three to four years if income growth is not as expected. Refinancing means dilution, which means stock price pressure.

# Risk two: Big-head crush (Several: <unk> )

Bytes, Ali, and Tung-jung have **10 times more than ** capital and flow advantages. If giants are serious about the track (AI socialization, video generation) that MINIMAX is doing, the pre-emptive advantage of MINIMAX may quickly disappear.

# Risk three: copyright litigation (serious: <unk> )

Copyright litigation against Hollywood giants like Disneys is not as simple as paying for money – conch AI could be banned from operating in the United States if the court issues a ** injunction. This could be a fatal blow to a company that earns 70% of its income overseas.

# Risk four: Geopolitics (Several: <unk> )

MINIMAX’s overseas revenues exceeded **70%**, and Talkie’s users exceeded 50% in the US. If Chinese-American relations deteriorate further, China-AI’s operations in the US may face a TikTok-style review.

# Risk five: AA foam burst (Several: <unk> )

Historical experience: **90% of companies in the technological revolution ended up dead. If the bubble in the AI industry breaks, the valuation of the non-profit AI company will be the first to be stressed. MINIMAX is currently 200-400 times PS, with huge downside space.

# Risk six: technology is being levelled (Several: <unk> )

AI's technology is extremely fast -- it can be pursued by competitions in 6-12 months. MINIMAX's lead on voice and video is not an insurmountable moat.
