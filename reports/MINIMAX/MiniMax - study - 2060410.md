- Revenue grew 159% while sales expense fell 40%, signaling a shift from burn-for-growth to efficiency-driven growth
- **Overseas revenue accounts for more than 70%**, making MiniMax one of the most globalized Chinese AI companies
- Consumer-facing products contribute 67% of revenue (AI social), while B2B API contributes 33% (up 297%)
- Full-year 2026 revenue could exceed USD 150 million

## 3-5 Year Outlook

**3 years out (2029):**
- Likely still among the top surviving Chinese independent AI startups (with Moonshot and Zhipu as the "survivor trio")
- Talkie could rank in the global AI social top 3, with revenue of USD 500-800 million
- The platform API may surpass consumer products as the main revenue source

**5 years out (2031):**
- Bull case: a "Chinese version of Character.AI + ElevenLabs + Runway" rolled into one
- Base case: a global AI platform company with a moat built from multimodal capability
- Bear case: crushed by big tech or acquired
