## 2. Financial quality analysis

### Income quality: stable but not amazing

- 3-year revenue CAGR is approximately 6%, which is above average in the global IT services industry
- The income structure is relatively balanced: about 48% in North America, about 34% in Europe, and about 18% in growth markets.
- The top 10 customers account for about 15% of revenue, and customer concentration is not high
- The book-to-bill ratio (Book-to-Bill) continues to be >1.0, indicating that orders on hand are growing

### Profit rate: stable but with ceiling

- The net profit margin of about 11% may not seem high, but it is excellent for a service company with 700,000 employees.
- Limited room for profit margin improvement - labor costs account for about 55-60% of revenue, which is a rigid cost
- AI may improve efficiency in the short term, but customers will also demand price cuts, and the extent of profit margin improvement is questionable.
- The good news is that operating profit margin has expanded by 30bps year-on-year in the past two quarters, indicating that management is controlling costs.

### Cash flow: the real highlight

- FY2025 free cash flow $10.9 billion, FCF/net profit >130%, excellent cash conversion
- Capital expenditures of only ~$600 million (less than 1% of revenue), an advantage of an asset-light model
- This means that almost all of the profits can be returned to shareholders or used for acquisitions

### Return on Capital

- ROIC is about 27%, far exceeding the cost of capital (estimated at 8-10%), indicating that every dollar reinvested is creating value
- Sources of high ROIC: light assets + high utilization + scale effect

## 3. Capital allocation

Total FY2025 shareholder returns were approximately $8.3 billion, accounting for 72% of operating cash flow:

| Project | Amount |
|------|------|
| Share buybacks | $4.62 billion |
| Dividends | ~$3.7 billion |
| Total rewards | ~$8.3 billion |

In addition, the company invests more than $3 billion annually in acquisitions, with FY2025 completing multiple strategic acquisitions to complement AI, cloud and industry capabilities.

- Quarterly dividend $1.63/share (increased by 15% in FY2025 and another 10% in FY2026)
- We have increased dividends for more than 20 consecutive years. The current dividend payout rate is approximately 54%, which is safe and sustainable.
- The current dividend yield is about 3.4% (based on ~$190 stock price estimate), the highest range in history

Buffett’s preferred capital allocation model: the money earned is either repurchased, distributed as dividends, or reinvested with high ROIC. Accenture does all three, which is a plus.

## 4. Valuation analysis: Is PE 14 times a panic price?

### Historical valuation comparison

| Time | PE (TTM) |
|------|-----------|
| 2021 High | ~38x |
| 2023 average | ~28x |
| 2024 average | ~25x |
| Current (2026.5) | ~14x |
| 10-year historical average | ~26x |

The current PE is 14 times, which is about 47% discount to the 10-year average. This is one of the lowest valuation ranges since Accenture went public.

### Why did the stock price fall to this position?

1. **DOGE cuts federal contracts**: Accenture labeled as a prime example of "government waste" as DoD terminates $5.1 billion in IT contracts
2. **AI replacement fear**: The market is worried that AI tools will reduce companies’ need for external consulting
3. **Macro uncertainty**: Tariff policy causes companies to delay IT investment decisions
4. **52-week decline of approximately 46.5%**: Significantly underperformed the S&P 500

### Valuation rationality calculation

Assuming a conservative scenario:
- FY2026 adjusted EPS approximately $13.70 (midpoint of company guidance)
- Given 18x PE (well below the historical average of 26x, reflecting AI risk discount)
- Fair value = $13.70 x 18 = **$247**
- Current share price ~$190, potential upside ~30%

Assume a pessimistic scenario:
- AI reduces long-term growth to 2-3% and margins by 1 percentage point
-Gives 15x PE
- Bottom value = $12.50 x 15 = **$188**
- Current stock price is near a pessimistic bottom

### Free cash flow yield

- Current market capitalization is approximately US$119.5 billion
- FY2025 FCF USD 10.9 billion
- FCF Yield = 109 / 1195 = **9.1%**
- This level is extremely rare among large technology services companies, indicating that the market is pricing in a lot of pessimism

## 5. Core Conclusions

From Buffett's perspective:

**Favorite place**:
- Strong cash flow, FCF conversion rate >130%
- ROIC 27% indicates a real competitive advantage
- Good capital allocation discipline, continuous repurchases + dividends
- PE 14x provides a considerable margin of safety

**Worrying Areas**:
- Net profit margin of 11% is not typical for a "wide moat" company
- Long-term AI substitution risks make the timeline for valuation repair uncertain
- Federal operations are subject to policy risks and may continue to do so

**Overall Rating**: 14x PE prices in a fair amount of panic. If the fear of AI replacement proves to be excessive (which is likely to be the case in the short term), there is considerable room for correction in valuations. But this is not a "buy with eyes closed" price. You need to make your own judgment on the long-term impact of AI on the consulting industry.
