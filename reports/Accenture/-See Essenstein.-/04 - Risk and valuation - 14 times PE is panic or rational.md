# Risk I: AI Structural Alternative (Several: Highest)

This is the central cause of the cut-off and the most controversial risk.

**Exact logic**:
- AI programming tools double the efficiency of development – * Half the "head" clients need * * Essenzel's income falls *
- Agenic AI matures to replace standardized transport and peacekeeping testing <unk> Host service eroded
- Long-term, there's a fundamental contradiction between the business model of "sell head" and "AI" that makes people devalue.

** See how logical **:
- Business IT transition is not "writing code" -- it's about organizational change, process re-engineering, benefit coordination, and this is not going to work.
- Every round of technological revolution in history was predicted to "kill the consulting industry" and it always made the consulting industry bigger.
- Even if the number of people needed was reduced, the odd number would increase -- because AI lowered the threshold for companies to try new technologies.
- Essenzel himself could use AI to improve efficiency and reduce costs, but profit rates could rise.

** Key judgement indicators**:
- ** Per capita income trends**: If per capita income continues to rise, it means AI is helping Essenzel "upgrade" instead of "substitute"
- **Renewed hosting service**: if the renewal rate drops, it means that the client is "go on his own."
- ** Proportion of AI projects in new signed orders**: if rising, indicates that AI is increment rather than replacement

** Current data**: Q2 SY2026 Newly signed bills** record $22.1 billion**, growth in hosting services ** 10%**, AI penetration** 80%**, large transactions** ** short-term data are more supportive.** However, long-term trends need to be followed up on a continuous basis.

# Risk three: macroeconomics and tariffs (moderate severity: medium)

- Customs policies have led to delays in IT spending decisions by business clients
- Q3 SY2026 Revenue guidelines implied only **1-5% ** (n/n/n) **, management language prudently
- The risk of recession is rising, and consultancy budgets are usually one of the first projects to be cut by the enterprise

** But historical data provide some comfort**: Essencio's income fell only **3% during the 2020 new crown epidemic, and there was no catastrophic decline during the 2009 financial crisis. IT spending will be delayed but not lost — because businesses cannot have IT systems.

# Risk four: Indian competition catch-up (Several: Medium)

The market value of TCS is beyond Essengen, and Infosys is fast catching up in digital transformation. The cost advantage of Indian companies is ** structural** - not disappearing from exchange rate fluctuations.

** Key question**: Would AI close the gap between "high end" and "low end" in terms of quality of delivery? If the answer is "yes" -- Indian companies will sweep the market with cost advantages. If the answer is "no" -- Essenzel's pricing rights can be maintained.

** Current judgement**: Not in the short and medium term. The complex business AI transformation requires deep industry knowledge, global coordination capacity, and customer trust, which are not low-cost solutions. But in the world five years later, nobody can say it.

There are no major cases of failures, but the probability – the more we do, the more likely we are to turn.
