# TCS market value goes beyond Essenge -- what does that mean?

Two years ago, this was inconceivable: an Indian outsourcing company outperformed the world’s IT consulting industry’s head. This is not just a change in market value ranking. It's saying a deeper question ** which is more valuable than "the big one" and "the cost lead."** The market is revocing.

To understand this, a global overview of IT advisory and service markets is larger than **1.2 trillion dollars**, and the industry is growing at about **6-7%** (CAGR). There are several salient features of this industry:

1. ** High degree of fragmentation**: Even the largest Essenzel represents only about 12% of global corporate consulting contracts**
2. ** Medium entry threshold**: Technical barriers are low, but scale barriers, brand barriers and customer relations barriers are high
3. ** Price war never stops**: especially in the area of offshoring, Indian firms use structural low-cost shocks to high-end markets
4. ** Periodic medium**: corporate IT expenditures are delayed but not lost when the economy falls

(Source: Market Reports World, Annual Report of Accenture)

** Word: This is not a winner-takes business, but the long run is much better than small companies.**

** The counter-view: The technology giant and Essenzel are more cooperative than competitive.** Microsoft needs Azure to help deploy its clients. Google needs Asentge to help its clients migrate to GCP. The value of the translation layer is still there.

# How wide is the moat?

Undo it in four dimensions:

# Economy of scale

**78.6 million global delivery networks are the most visible barriers.** No new entrants can replicate them in the short term. The benefits of scale include global procurement bargaining, flexible deployment of manpower across regions, and multi-industry expertise.

** But size is also a double-edged sword**: managing the complexity of 78.60 million people is a risk in itself. If AI brings small firms’ delivery capacity closer to large ones, size advantage may become a burden of scale.

# Client conversion costs

The system integration project is a natural lock-in, with a high rate of renewal of hosting services and embedded knowledge locking.** The conversion costs are medium and not insurmountable.**

# Brand and trust

Brand value **41.5 billion**, Global IT Service Number One. Big CIO's psychological security in selecting Essencio is not to be underestimated -- "It's not my fault to pick Essenge, it's my pot to pick a company that has never heard of."

# Methodology and tools

The industry-specific solutions have accumulated in terms of efficiency in delivery through their own platforms, myWizad, Synops, etc.** These are not protected by the laws as patents, and competitors can learn to imitate.**

** The moat's general comment: medium wide.** It's broader than most competitors, but not the "almost impossible to subvert" level of Coca-Cola.

# And the power to protect the city

** Short term (1-3 years), AI transition needs are significant incremental**

Each big enterprise wants to use AI, but hardly any of them knows how to use it. They need help with the answer: Which model? How to integrate with the existing system? How to guarantee data security? How to train employees?

** This is the golden window of the consulting firm.** The $5.9 billion GenAI order of Essenzel SY2025 is evidence -- companies are in line to hire people to help them with AI.

And Essencio has a pre-eminent advantage in this area:

- **550,000** completed AI training, largest industry
- Deep collaboration with Anthropic, Microsoft, Gemini.
GenAI has infiltrated **80% of the big deal - not the stand-alone line, but all the projects.

# Helping the opponent to climb the wall

** Medium- and long-term (3-10 years), AI tools may depreciate size advantage.**

The logic is clear:

1. **AI programming tool** (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, etc.) doubles or even more the output of a good developer. ** Clients used to need 100 people.
2. **Agentic AI** (AI agent with autonomy) may replace some of the low-end counselling and maintenance services * The most standardized portion of hosting services will bear the brunt of this
** Small companies borrowed AI in the war** AI lowered delivery threshold, 5 people's fine consulting firms + AI tools could have done what 50 people could have done before * Essencio's size advantage was diluted

** Timeline is the key variable. ** If this process takes 10 years -- Essentzer has enough time to transform. If it takes 3 years -- 14 times PE, it may not be cheap enough.
