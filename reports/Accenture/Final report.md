## Summary of investment thesis

### Why is it worth paying attention to?

1. **Valuation is at an extremely low level in history**: PE 14 times, a 47% discount to the 10-year average of 26 times, and a 52-week stock price drop of 46.5%
2. **AI is a short-term net positive**: GenAI orders reached US$5.9 billion in FY2025 (doubled), and Q1 FY2026 AI revenue increased by 120% year-on-year
3. **Cash flow machine**: FY2025 free cash flow $10.9 billion, FCF yield 9.1%, dividend yield ~3.4%
4. **The best management**: Julie Sweet overturned 50 years of organizational structure, 550,000 people completed AI training, and first-class execution
5. **Sufficient margin of safety**: Even in the extremely pessimistic scenario (12x PE), the downside is limited

### Why be cautious?

1. **AI replacement is a real long-term risk**: It’s not “crying wolf” – AI programming and Agentic AI will indeed reduce the need for consulting
2. **"Sweet first, then bitter" structure**: Short-term AI transformation brings orders, while long-term AI maturity may erode the industry foundation
3. **Federal Business Political Risk**: DOGE cuts impact 1-1.5% growth, and policy direction is uncertain
4. **Indian competitors are catching up**: TCS’s market value has surpassed Accenture, and its cost advantage exists structurally

## Comments on four analysis dimensions

### Business model perspective

IT consulting is an "okay" business - not the kind of "no brainer money making" business, but with high scale barriers, strong customer stickiness, and good cash flow. Accenture occupies the best position in this business. The core contradiction is: the revenue model of this business is "selling heads", and AI is devaluing "heads". This conflict will not break out in the short term, but what about in 5 years?

> Conclusion: The business model itself is rated 3.5 points, and Accenture’s position in it is added 0.5 points, for a total of 4 points.

### Financial valuation perspective

The financials are solid: 27% ROIC, $10.9 billion in FCF, and 20+ consecutive years of dividend increases. PE 14x is the lowest valuation range in the past decade, and FCF yield 9.1% is extremely rare. A conservative valuation (18x PE) corresponds to $247, with 30% upside from current levels. But the premise for valuation repair is that AI replaces fear alleviation.

> Conclusion: The current price provides a good margin of safety, but it is not a "cigarette butt" level bargain. 4 points.

### Industry competition perspective

Accenture is an industry leader with the world's largest brand value ($41.5 billion) and a leader in AI transformation. But the moat is not "insurmountable" - customers can choose other suppliers for each new project. AI may simultaneously widen and erode the moat: widening because the need for transformation is soaring, and eroding because small companies can also use AI to improve their delivery capabilities.

> Conclusion: The moat is medium to wide, and the competitive landscape in the AI era is being reshaped. 4 points.

### Risk management perspective

Julie Sweet is the biggest plus in this investment case. In the age of AI, management’s judgment and execution are more important than ever. She has proven her ability. In terms of risks, AI replacement and federal business reduction are the two core risks, but the 14 times PE has been partially compensated.

> Conclusion: 5 out of 10 for management, moderately high risk but compensated by valuation. Overall 4 points.

## Investment advice

### What kind of investors is it suitable for?

**Fits**:
- Value investors who can withstand stock price fluctuations in 1-2 years and focus on 3-5 years
- Investors who have independent judgment on the impact of AI and believe that AI will be a net positive for the consulting industry in the short term
- Investors who value cash flow and dividends and hope to gain from declines

**Not suitable**:
- Investors who believe that AI will replace the need for consulting on a large scale within 2-3 years
- Investors who pursue high growth and are unwilling to bear the possibility of long-term depressed valuations
- Investors with zero tolerance for US policy risks (DOGE, etc.)

### Operation suggestions

| Strategy | Specific Recommendations |
|------|----------|
| Position opening range | $170-200 (near the current price) |
| Target holding period | 2-3 years |
| Position recommendations | Medium position (3-5%), heavy positions are not recommended |
| Conditions for adding position | The stock price falls below $150 (PE ~11x), extreme panic price |
| Reduce position/sell conditions | PE rises back to above 22x (~$300), or AI replacement speed accelerates significantly |
| Key tracking indicators | GenAI order growth, operating profit margin changes, federal business progress |

### Core judgment

Buying Accenture at 14 times PE is essentially betting on two things:

1. **AI’s impact on the consulting industry is gradual rather than sudden** - Short-term AI transformation needs > AI substitution effect
2. **Julie Sweet’s management team can successfully lead the company through the AI transformation period** – from “selling people” to “selling AI-empowered solutions”

If these two calls are correct, current prices offer a very good risk-reward ratio. If judgment is wrong - especially if the AI ​​replacement speed is much faster than expected - the safety cushion of 14x PE is not thick enough.

**My tendency: It is worth establishing an observation position (2-3%), and wait for the Q3 FY2026 financial report (June 2026) to confirm the GenAI order growth rate and profit margin trend before deciding whether to increase the position. **
