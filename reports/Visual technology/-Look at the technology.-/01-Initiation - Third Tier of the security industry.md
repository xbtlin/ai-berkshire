## Why should we study a "Security No.3" company?

In Binjiang District, Hangzhou, three top players in the global security industry live within 3 square kilometers: Hikvision, Dahua Technology, and Uniview Technology. There is a saying in the industry called "Hai Dayu", which sounds like one-third of the world.

Looking at the financial report, this statement is untenable:

| Indicators | Hikvision | Dahua Technology | Uniview Technology |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Revenue (2025) | **92.5 billion** | **32.7 billion** | **~5.8 billion (estimate)** |
| Net profit | **14.2 billion** | **3.9 billion** | **~330 million (estimate)** |
| Gross profit margin | **45.9%** | **~40%** | **~30%** |
| R&D investment | **11.9 billion** | **~4 billion** | **~500 million** |
| Market capitalization (May 2026) | **~320 billion** | **~58 billion** | Not independently listed |

*Data source: Hikvision 2025 annual report, Dahua Technology 2025 annual report, Qianfang Technology 2024/2025 annual report split estimate*

Hikvision is **16 times** the revenue of Uniview, **45 times** the profit, and **24 times** the R&D investment. Putting the three together is not a "three kingdoms struggle for hegemony", but more like "one super, one strong, one seeking survival."

So why spend time studying Uniview?

Three reasons:

**First**, Uniview is a mirror of the pattern of China’s security industry. To study it is not to study "can we buy Uniview?" but to study "what is the competitive structure of the security industry like?" The living conditions of the third oldest person in the industry can often reveal the true logic of the industry better than the oldest person.

**Second**, Uniview is a typical example of "good assets trapped in a bad structure". As a wholly-owned subsidiary of Qianfang Technology (002373.SZ), it contributes **76%** of the parent company's revenue and almost all profits. However, Qianfang Technology will suffer a huge loss of **1.193 billion** in 2024, mainly due to the impairment of smart transportation business and goodwill. A profitable subsidiary is held hostage by a loss-making parent company—a structure worthy of study in itself.

**Third**, in December 2024, Uniview was included in the Entity List by the U.S. Department of Commerce. The impact of this incident continues to ferment, posing substantial constraints on its overseas business and long-term development.

## What is Uniview?

### Origin: Split from H3C

The history of Uniview Technology can be traced back to the video surveillance department of H3C Communications.

In 2011, Zhang Pengguo led a group of technical backbones from H3C to become independent and founded Uniview Technology. Zhang Pengguo graduated in 1994 and worked successively at the National Defense Research Institute, Huawei, and H3C Communications. At the age of 25, he won the first prize for the Ministerial Science and Technology Progress Award. He is a manager with a technical background.

This background determines Uniview’s genes: **technology-focused, IT-structure-focused, and high-end projects**—different from Hikvision’s channel genes and Dahua’s cost-effective genes.

### Product: What it does

Uniview's core business is video surveillance products and solutions, including:

- Front end: camera (IPC), dome camera, thermal imaging, etc.
- Backend: Network Video Recorder (NVR), storage device
- Platform: video management platform, intelligent analysis platform
- Solutions: urban governance, transportation, energy, corporate security, etc.

To put it simply, it is "selling cameras and supporting systems". However, Uniview has been transforming into AIoT (Intelligent Internet of Things) in recent years and launched the Wutong large model in an attempt to shift from "selling hardware" to "selling intelligence".

### Equity Structure: Trapped in Qianfang Technology

In 2017, Qianfang Technology completed the acquisition of 100% equity of Uniview Technology for approximately **3.7 billion**. Since then, Uniview has become a wholly-owned subsidiary of Qianfang Technology and is no longer an independent legal entity.

This means:

- Investors cannot buy equity in Uniview Technology alone
- Uniview's profits and cash flow were absorbed into Qianfang Technology's consolidated statements
- Uniview’s valuation diluted by Qianfang Technology’s loss-making business

Qianfang Technology has two main businesses: intelligent Internet of Things (mainly Uniview, accounting for **70%** of revenue) and smart transportation (accounting for **30%**). In 2024, the intelligent Internet of Things sector will contribute revenue of 5.059 billion, a year-on-year increase of 5%; the smart transportation sector will contribute revenue of 2.182 billion, a year-on-year decrease of **26.4%**.

**Uniview is making money, Qianfang is losing money** - This misalignment is the key to understanding the value of Uniview's investment.

## A set of facts that will make you re-examine "Hai Dayu"

The title "Hai Dayu" implies that the three companies are players of the same level. However, the following sets of data show that the gap is already structural:

### Profit rate gap: a reflection of pricing power

| Indicators | Hikvision | Dahua Technology | Uniview Technology |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Gross profit margin | **45.9%** | **~40%** | **~30%** |
| Net profit margin | **15.3%** | **11.8%** | **5.7%** |
| ROE | **17.3%** | **10.4%** | **~6% (Uniview’s own estimate)** |

*Data source: 2024/2025 annual reports of each company*

What does a gross margin margin of **15 percentage points** mean? For the same camera sold for 1,000 yuan, Hikvision made a gross profit of 459 yuan, while Uniview only made 300 yuan. The gap in net profit margin is even wider - Hikvision makes a net profit of 15.3 yuan for every 100 yuan of revenue, while Uniview only makes 5.7 yuan.

This is not an accidental fluctuation. **Uniview's net profit margin has dropped from 11.5% in 2019 to 5.7% in 2024**, almost halving. The continued decline in profit margins is the most direct evidence that pricing power has been eroded.

### The R&D Investment Gap: Your Ticket to the AI Arms Race

The security industry is transforming from "visible" to "understandable". The AI ​​penetration rate is currently only **8%-10%** (data from China Business Industry Research Institute), and the structural growth space lies in AI intelligence.

But AI upgrades require huge R&D investment. Hikvision’s R&D investment in 2025 will be **11.9 billion**, while Uniview’s investment will be approximately **500 million** – a difference of **24 times**. Uniview's Wutong large model has certain technical reserves and has been deployed in more than 100 projects, but it is not in the same dimension as Hikvision in terms of investment level.

**Opposite view**: R&D is not simply a comparison of absolute values. Uniview's team is leaner and more efficient, and can use open source large models such as DeepSeek to distill lightweight specialized models to reduce R&D costs. Wutong Large Model has supported partners to train more than 10,000 small models. However, whether "high efficiency" can make up for "low investment" is currently undecided.

### Cash flow gap: the difference in survival margin

| Indicators | Hikvision | Dahua Technology | Qianfang Technology (including Uniview) |
|------|---------|---------|---------------|
| Operating cash flow (100 million yuan) | **253** | **39** | **1.4** |
| Net cash ratio | **1.78 times** | **1.01 times** | Not applicable (loss year) |

*Data source: 2024/2025 annual reports of each company*

Hikvision's operating cash flow is **181 times** that of Qianfang Technology. Hikvision has sufficient funds to invest in AI research and development, global expansion, and buyback dividends; Qianfang Technology cannot even generate positive free cash flow stably.
