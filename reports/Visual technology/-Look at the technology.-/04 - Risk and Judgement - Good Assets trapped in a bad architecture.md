# First layer: List of U.S. entities - overseas throats are stuck

** Incident**: 10 December 2024, U.S. Department of Commerce listed Utsaw technology on the list of entities on the grounds of "helping to monitor human rights violations by ethnic minorities such as the Uighurs".

** Direct impact**:

- A supplier using United States technology needs a licence to export and applies the principle of "presumption of refusal"
- The European and American high-end security market is largely lost — customers such as government, finance, critical infrastructure, etc. do not take the risk of compliance to purchase products of sanctioned enterprises
- Supply chain links that use parts of the United States may be hampered

** Official response**: "There is no evidence, and there is no significant adverse impact on the company's operations.

** How do you assess the term "impact control"?**

See precedent. The shares of Hackonway and Da Hwa were added to the list of entities in October 2019.

- Heikang, whose overseas income remained growing in 2020-2025, and in 2025, which was about **20+billion yuan**

- Heikang's response is to increase the input of the "one-way" countries and emerging markets.

*Source: The Hague Vision 2020-2025, United States Federal Gazette*

**Omight is more vulnerable than Haikang.**

- Heikang was the world's first under sanctions, with enough brands and channels to buffer. The market share in North America is only **4.3%.
- Heikang has sufficient cash flows (all operating cash flows of $25.3 billion) to adjust the strategy.
- Heacon can self-research chips (Hais instead) to reduce reliance on the U.S. supply chain.

The real impact on the list of entities may be less than on Heicon. And the demand for security in the "one-way" countries is still growing rapidly, and Chinese security brands have a clear value-for-money advantage in these markets.

** Sensitization**: The impact of the list of entities is long-term and irreversible. The view will not be closed, but the door to the high-end markets abroad is closed. This is a strategic blow to a company that tries to "globalize the siege".
