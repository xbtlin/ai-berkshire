## A Set of Numbers That Is Hard to Read

On May 13, 2026, Alibaba released a set of financial results that left almost everyone struggling to interpret the company.

Revenue crossed RMB 1 trillion for the first time, a rare milestone in the history of China's internet sector. But in the same report: Non-GAAP net income plunged 62%, free cash flow swung from positive to negative RMB 466 x 100mn, and the core e-commerce business lost share for the fourth consecutive year.

Two questions split the market in half: is this a sick elephant under siege, or a giant deliberately destroying parts of itself in order to regenerate? Before reading any interpretation, start with the raw numbers for the two fiscal years:

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2026 | Change |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Total revenue | RMB 9,964 x 100mn | **RMB 10,237 x 100mn** | +3% (**first time above RMB 1tn**)|
| GAAP net income | RMB 1,260 x 100mn | **RMB 1,059 x 100mn** | **-16%** |
| Non-GAAP net income | RMB 1,581 x 100mn | **RMB 607 x 100mn** | **-62%** |
| Free cash flow | RMB 739 x 100mn | **RMB -466 x 100mn** | **swung negative** |
| Adjusted EBITA | RMB 1,731 x 100mn | **RMB 764 x 100mn** | **-56%** |
| Full-year capital expenditure | ~RMB 896 x 100mn | **RMB 1,261 x 100mn** | +41% |
| Net cash | ~RMB 3,890 x 100mn | - | Still ample |
| Cash and liquid investments | RMB 5,971 x 100mn | **RMB 5,208 x 100mn** | -RMB 763 x 100mn |
| Shareholder returns (FY2025) | **USD 165 x 100mn** (USD 119 x 100mn buybacks + USD 46 x 100mn dividends) | - | Most aggressive among China ADRs |

*Sources: Alibaba FY2025/FY2026 financial reports, SEC filings*

Put these numbers together and the picture is deeply contradictory:

- **Revenue exceeded RMB 1 trillion** -- but growth was only 3%
- **Non-GAAP net income collapsed 62%** -- but not because the business deteriorated; management **chose** to pour profit into AI infrastructure and instant retail
- **Free cash flow swung negative** -- but the balance sheet still held **RMB 5,208 x 100mn** in cash
- **Alibaba repurchased USD 119 x 100mn of stock in FY2025** -- but FY2026 Q4 posted a quarterly operating loss of RMB 8.48 x 100mn, the first since 2021

**If you think Alibaba is in decline, the 62% profit collapse is genuinely alarming.**
**If you think Alibaba is being reborn, RMB 1 trillion of revenue, RMB 5,000 x 100mn of cash, and 34% cloud growth leave room for imagination.**

This series will not tell you which judgment is right. It will show you **what you need to see clearly before making that judgment.**

## Alibaba's Real Profile

Alibaba in 2026 is no longer the "king of Chinese e-commerce" it was in 2020. It has changed.

If we summarize what it is today in one table:

| Business segment | FY2026 revenue | Share | Growth | Profitability | One-line description |
|---------|-----------|------|------|---------|-----------|
| China commerce (Taotian + instant retail) | RMB 5,542 x 100mn | 54% | +9% | Contributes the vast majority of profit, but heavily dragged down by losses in instant retail | A cash cow under siege |
| Alibaba Cloud Intelligence | RMB 1,581 x 100mn | 15% | **+34%** | EBITA margin around 9%, improving | The piece that most resembles a good business |
| International Digital Commerce (AIDC) | RMB 1,442 x 100mn | 14% | +9% | Near breakeven | From cash burn to stopping the bleeding |
| Other (Cainiao / Digital Media and Entertainment / DingTalk, etc.) | RMB 1,672 x 100mn | 17% | -25% | Large losses, including the impact of divested businesses | Focus, or baggage? |

*Source: Alibaba FY2026 Q4 results, May 13, 2026*

**Alibaba's true identity today is a large holding group transitioning from an "e-commerce empire" into a dual-engine company built around "AI cloud + consumption."**

The direction is clear: focus on "e-commerce + cloud + AI" while cutting non-core assets, including Intime, sold at a RMB 93 x 100mn loss, and Sun Art Retail, sold at a RMB 130 x 100mn loss.

But the cost of the transition is also real: FY2026 profits collapsed, free cash flow turned negative, and the company is fighting three wars at once.

## Three Common Misreadings of Alibaba

### Misreading 1: "Alibaba Is an E-commerce Company"

That was fine five years ago. It is no longer accurate today.

Open the FY2026 report and Alibaba's revenue mix has fundamentally changed:

- **China commerce** (including instant retail): 54% -- still the largest segment, but its share is falling
- **Alibaba Cloud**: 15% -- 34% growth, with AI revenue exceeding 30% of external cloud revenue
- **International e-commerce**: 14% -- AliExpress, Lazada, Trendyol
- **Other**: 17% -- Cainiao, Digital Media and Entertainment, DingTalk, and more

Alibaba's CEO Eddie Wu also serves as group CEO, Taotian CEO, and Alibaba Cloud CEO -- the top seat across the three most important businesses. His strategic narrative is to turn Alibaba into "China's Amazon": e-commerce as the cash cow, cloud as the growth engine.

**On the other hand**: Alibaba Cloud's FY2026 revenue of RMB 1,581 x 100mn is less than 1/5 of AWS, which is about USD 1,200 x 100mn, or roughly RMB 8,700 x 100mn. Its 9% margin is far below AWS's 36%. Whether the "China's Amazon" narrative holds depends on whether Alibaba Cloud can raise its margin to 18-20% over the next 5 years. That has not happened yet.

### Misreading 2: "Alibaba's Moat Is Deep"

That was true before 2020.

| Year | Taotian e-commerce share | Pinduoduo share | Douyin + Kuaishou share |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| 2021 | **51.3%** | 15.9% | 9.2% |
| 2024 | **37.3%** | 21.0% | 21.4% |

*Sources: QuestMobile, China Merchants Securities*

**It lost 14 percentage points in four years.** Who took them?

- **Douyin e-commerce** (+12 percentage points): 800mn DAUs with free traffic versus Taobao's 300-400mn DAUs that require paid acquisition. That is a business-model-level squeeze.
- **Pinduoduo** (+5 percentage points): the ultra-low-price positioning has taken root, and "check Pinduoduo first for price comparison" has become a consumer habit.

Alibaba Cloud's moat is actually **widening**: its AI cloud market share is 35.8%, larger than the combined share of ranks 2 through 4 (source: IDC 2025H1). But a narrowing e-commerce moat and a widening cloud moat **cannot simply be added together**, because e-commerce contributes more than 90% of profit while cloud is still only modestly profitable.

Duan Yongping said in 2023: "I cannot see through e-commerce. I do not know where the moat really is, and I do not know who will still be doing relatively well 10 years from now." He increased his Alibaba position in 2024 and then cut it by more than 25% in 2025. His actions cast the vote.

### Misreading 3: "The Profit Collapse Means Alibaba Is Finished"

FY2026 Non-GAAP net income fell 62%, which looks frightening. But it needs to be unpacked:

| Cause of profit decline | Estimated impact | Nature |
|-------------|---------|------|
| Instant retail (Taobao Flash) spending | ~RMB 860 x 100mn loss (Morgan Stanley estimate) | **Active investment** |
| AI infrastructure capital expenditure | Full-year capex of RMB 1,261 x 100mn | **Active investment** |
| Divested businesses (Intime, Sun Art) | Revenue actually grew 11% on a comparable basis | One-off factor |

CFO Toby Xu stated clearly: **excluding losses from instant retail, China commerce group's EBITA would have delivered mid-single-digit growth.**

So the profit collapse is not "the business got worse." It is management **actively choosing** to invest profits into two wars: AI infrastructure and instant retail.

**But that does not mean there is no risk.** Active investment is not the same as correct investment. A RMB 3,800 x 100mn three-year AI investment plan, plus an estimated RMB 860 x 100mn annual loss in instant retail -- what if this money is spent and produces no return?

Joe Tsai himself has warned that "AI data centers are in a bubble." When the chairman is worried about a bubble and the company is still increasing investment, is that vision or risk-taking?

## To Understand Alibaba, You Need to Answer Several Core Questions

If Alibaba today had to be defined in one sentence:

> **Alibaba = an e-commerce cash cow (Taotian) that is under siege but still highly profitable + an AI cloud engine (Alibaba Cloud) that is accelerating but has not yet released its profit potential + several frontier businesses that are either stopping the bleeding or burning cash (AIDC, Flash, Cainiao) + RMB 5,200 x 100mn in cash.**

To understand this company, at least the following questions need to be answered:

| Question | Where this series covers it |
|------|-------------------|
| Will Taotian continue losing e-commerce share? How long can the cash cow hold up? | Part 02 |
| Can Alibaba Cloud become "China's AWS"? Is the RMB 3,800 x 100mn investment worth it? | Part 03 |
| International e-commerce, instant retail, Cainiao: which are assets and which are baggage? | Part 04 |
| Can management be trusted? Is the valuation expensive or cheap? | Part 05 · Finale |

Each question has been answered by someone in isolation. But few people put them together and measure them with the same ruler. That is exactly why the market disagrees so sharply on Alibaba: **the Alibaba you see depends on which puzzle piece you choose.**

---

Before entering each segment, remember several orders of magnitude:

- **RMB 1tn of revenue, but only 3% growth** -- large, not fast
- **Non-GAAP net income of RMB 607 x 100mn, down 62%** -- profit is disappearing, but by management choice
- **FY2025 shareholder returns of USD 165 x 100mn** -- USD 119 x 100mn buybacks + USD 46 x 100mn dividends, the most aggressive among China ADRs
- **Alibaba Cloud revenue of RMB 1,581 x 100mn, up 34%** -- AI-driven acceleration, with Q4 AI product revenue of RMB 89.71 x 100mn (RMB 358 x 100mn annualized)
- **Instant retail revenue of RMB 785 x 100mn, up 47%** -- high growth, but an estimated RMB 860 x 100mn annual loss
- **Taotian e-commerce share of ~37%** -- down 14 percentage points in four years and still falling
- **PE around 17x** -- 47% below the historical average of 32.5x
- **Net cash of ~RMB 3,890 x 100mn** -- ample ammunition on the balance sheet
- **Three-year AI investment plan of RMB 3,800 x 100mn** -- the CEO says this is "conservative"

*Sources: Alibaba FY2025/FY2026 financial reports, Yahoo Finance, Investing.com*
