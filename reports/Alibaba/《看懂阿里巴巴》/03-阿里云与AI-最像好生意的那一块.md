## Alibaba Cloud Determines Whether Alibaba Is a "Value Stock" or a "Growth Stock"

The answer to that question sits inside Alibaba Cloud.

The conclusion from the previous piece: Taotian lost 14 percentage points of share in four years, and its flywheel shifted from "self-accelerating" to "externally maintained." An Alibaba that only had Taotian would be a slowly declining value stock: highly profitable, but with growth already capped, and eventually deserving a discount.

But Alibaba has another asset whose numbers look so sharp they almost belong to a different company: FY2026 revenue growth of 34%, AI product revenue growing at triple-digit rates for 11 consecutive quarters, and AI cloud market share of 35.8%, higher than the combined share of ranks 2 through 4. That asset is **Alibaba Cloud**. It is the **only segment in the group delivering both high-speed growth and profit expansion**, and it is where management has placed its RMB 3,800 x 100mn bet.

Whether Alibaba Cloud can take over from Taotian will determine whether Alibaba's next decade looks like a Microsoft-style rebirth or an IBM-style stagnation.

## 2. How Is AI Driving Alibaba Cloud's Growth?

### 2.1 AI Revenue Has Become the Core Growth Driver

Alibaba Cloud's AI-related product revenue has maintained **triple-digit growth for 11 consecutive quarters**.

FY2026 Q4 AI product revenue was RMB 89.71 x 100mn, or RMB 358 x 100mn annualized. A year earlier, this number was almost zero. AI products exceeded 30% of Alibaba Cloud's external revenue for the first time, meaning **1 out of every 3 yuan of cloud revenue now comes from AI**.

The main growth drivers are:
- **Explosion in AI inference demand**: after large-model applications land, inference compute demand is dozens of times larger than training demand
- **Qwen open-source ecosystem driving cloud consumption**: Qwen has more than 6 x 100mn global downloads and 170,000+ derivative models. Developers debug and deploy these models on Alibaba Cloud, creating compute consumption
- **Acceleration of enterprise AI deployment**: the Bailian platform serves more than 980,000 enterprises and developers

### 2.2 Qwen's Competitiveness

**Strengths:**
- Globally leading open-source ecosystem: No. 1 by HuggingFace downloads in 2026Q1, ahead of Meta Llama
- The Qwen3 series has 8 open-source models covering the full range of sizes and modalities
- Strong enterprise / B-end deployment advantages, supported by Alibaba Cloud's customer base

**Weaknesses:**
- Clear gap in C-end MAU: Qwen App has about 1.66 x 100mn MAU (QuestMobile definition) versus Doubao's **3.45 x 100mn** MAU, a gap of more than 2x
- Average monthly usage frequency per user: Qwen **19.8 times** versus Doubao **54.8 times**, a 2.7x stickiness gap
- Retention after the Spring Festival "RMB 30 x 100mn free-order" marketing campaign was below 50%; users bought through subsidies are not real users

*Sources: QuestMobile 2025-12, ai-berkshire Qwen thematic report*

**Alibaba's AI strategy is "open source for traffic, cloud for monetization."** The model is free to use, but inference compute must be purchased from Alibaba Cloud. This logic is consistent with Amazon's AWS playbook: use my tools to build applications, and the more successful your applications become, the more compute you buy.

**On the other hand**: ByteDance's C-end advantage with Doubao may transmit into the cloud layer. In the 2025 large-model API call market, ByteDance Volcano Engine held 46.4% share, with Alibaba Cloud ranking second. If "more people use Doubao -> more developers use Volcano Engine -> Volcano Engine's compute share rises," that is the cloud-computing version of the "Douyin disrupts Taobao" path.

## 3. RMB 3,800 x 100mn of AI Investment: Vision or Bubble?

### 3.1 Investment Scale

Eddie Wu promised: "Over the next three years (FY2026-FY2028), investment in cloud and AI infrastructure will exceed the total of the past ten years." The market estimates this figure at roughly **RMB 3,800 x 100mn**.

Eddie Wu also implied that "RMB 3,800 x 100mn is conservative."

FY2026 full-year capital expenditure already reached **RMB 1,261 x 100mn**. In one year alone, Alibaba spent RMB 1,261 x 100mn.

### 3.2 Is This Money Worth It?

**Positive argument (bull case):**

This is "offensive capital expenditure," similar to Amazon's AWS path in 2005-2015. AWS also spent aggressively for 10 straight years, suppressing profit to very low levels, in exchange for the later 30%+ margin and trillion-dollar market-cap contribution.

Supporting data:
- Alibaba Cloud AI revenue is already accelerating into actual monetization (RMB 358 x 100mn annualized, triple-digit growth for 11 consecutive quarters)
- China's AI inference demand is genuinely exploding, with annual token-call growth by dozens of times
- Eddie Wu's 5-year target: cloud + AI commercialization revenue above **USD 1,000 x 100mn** (about RMB 7,100 x 100mn)

**Negative argument (bear case):**

- Joe Tsai himself has said "AI data centers are in a bubble." When the company's chairman publicly issues that warning, investors should not ignore it.
- Alibaba Cloud's margin is only 9%, versus AWS at 36%, a 4x gap. If Alibaba Cloud never reaches AWS-like margins, the RMB 3,800 x 100mn investment is simply piling scale onto low returns.
- China's AI industry is in a price war. DeepSeek built a Top 3 model with 1/10 of the compute, proving that "stacking compute" may not be a moat.
- ByteDance Volcano Engine, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud are all increasing investment. Price competition may compress everyone's margins.

### 3.3 Key Verification Milestones

| Timing | What needs to be seen |
|------|-----------|
| FY2027 (before March 2027) | Alibaba Cloud external revenue growth stays above 30%, AI revenue share exceeds 40% |
| FY2028 | Alibaba Cloud annual revenue exceeds RMB 2,500 x 100mn, EBITA margin rises to 12-15% |
| FY2030 | Evidence that Eddie Wu's USD 1,000 x 100mn target can at least reach half, or USD 500 x 100mn |

If these milestones are delivered one by one, Alibaba Cloud will become Alibaba Group's largest profit engine, and the RMB 3,800 x 100mn investment will be worth it. If AI revenue growth falls below 15% and margin gets stuck at 12%, this money becomes sunk cost.

## 4. Alibaba Cloud's Moat: Three Layers

### 4.1 First Layer: Scale and Customer Lock-In

- 26.8% share of China's IaaS market, No. 1 for many consecutive years
- Government and enterprise customers face very high data migration costs; once deployed on Alibaba Cloud, moving to Huawei Cloud or Tencent Cloud can take months or even years
- Compliance advantage for sensitive state-owned data: Alibaba Cloud has deep experience in financial cloud and government cloud

### 4.2 Second Layer: AI Model Ecosystem

- Qwen open-source models have 6 x 100mn global downloads and 170,000 derivative models
- Bailian platform has 980,000 enterprises / developers, forming a closed loop of "model-driven traffic -> cloud consumption"
- Alibaba Cloud is China's only cloud vendor with "foundation large models + IaaS + downstream application entry points (Taobao / DingTalk / Amap / Alipay)" at the same time

### 4.3 Third Layer: Irreplaceability in the Chinese Market

- US-China decoupling prevents foreign clouds from serving Chinese government and enterprise customers effectively
- AWS China + Azure China have only 5-7% combined share, and are constrained by geopolitics
- Alibaba Cloud + Huawei Cloud + China Telecom Cloud split most of the government and enterprise cloud market

**These three layers make Alibaba Cloud the Alibaba business with the deepest moat, and the only moat that is still widening.**

### 4.4 Threats to the Moat

But the moat is not crack-free:

- **ByteDance Volcano Engine**: using Doubao's advantage in the AI application layer, it is cutting from C-end into B-end. Its large-model API call share of 46.4% has already surpassed Alibaba Cloud. Volcano Engine's share was only 5-6% in 2024 and surged to 8-12% in 2025, an astonishing growth rate.
- **Huawei Cloud**: with information-innovation policy support and government-enterprise relationships, it has already surpassed Alibaba Cloud in hybrid cloud / government cloud.
- **Carrier clouds** (China Telecom / China Mobile / China Unicom): combined share exceeds 30%, and they are rising quickly on infrastructure advantages.

**Ten-year base-case forecast:**
- Alibaba Cloud China share: 33-36% -> **28-32%** (slight decline, but still No. 1)
- ByteDance Volcano Engine: 8-12% -> **18-22%**
- Huawei Cloud: 18-21% -> **22-26%**

The structure will shift from "Alibaba Cloud dominates alone" to "three-way competition + state-owned clouds."

## 5. Is Eddie Wu's "China's AWS" Narrative Credible?

Eddie Wu's strategic vision is to turn Alibaba into "China's Amazon": e-commerce as the cash cow, cloud as the growth engine and valuation anchor.

### 5.1 Microsoft-Style Success Path

The closest historical analogy is **Microsoft's 2014-2024 transformation**:

| Dimension | Microsoft | Alibaba |
|------|------|------|
| Leadership change | Satya Nadella took over in 2014 | Eddie Wu took over in 2023 |
| Strategy | Mobile First -> Cloud First | E-commerce empire -> AI cloud + consumption |
| Business pruning | Exited phones, scaled back consumer businesses | Sold Intime and Sun Art Retail |
| Old-business cash flow | Office 365 annual revenue of USD 100bn | Taotian annual profit of ~RMB 2,000 x 100mn |
| Transformation result | Market cap rose from USD 3,000 x 100mn to USD 3tn | ? |

### 5.2 IBM-Style Failure Path

But there is also a failure case: **IBM 2010-2024**.

| Dimension | IBM | Alibaba's risk |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Strategic direction | Bet on Watson AI + cloud | Bet on Qwen + Alibaba Cloud |
| Problem | Old business (mainframes) declined too quickly, new business (Watson) had weak execution | Taotian keeps losing share; Qwen's C-end product power is weaker than Doubao's |
| Result | Revenue stagnated for 10 years, market cap underperformed for a long time | ? |

**The dividing line between the two paths is whether old-business cash flow can last until the new business becomes profitable.**

Microsoft succeeded because Office 365 cash flow was extremely stable, buying Azure 10 years of transformation time. IBM failed because mainframe revenue declined too quickly and could not support Watson's R&D investment.

For Alibaba: Taotian has annual profit of RMB 2,000 x 100mn, and Alibaba Cloud's EBITA margin is slowly improving (9% -> target of 18-20%). If Taotian can defend 30%+ share and maintain RMB 100bn-level profit, it can give Alibaba Cloud a 5-8 year transformation window. If Taotian's share decline accelerates below 25%, the cash cow falls first while Alibaba Cloud has not yet matured. That is the IBM script.

### 5.3 Base-Case Judgment

**Probability that Alibaba Cloud becomes "China's AWS": about 40-50%.**

Optimistic path: within 5 years, EBITA margin rises to 18-20%, annual revenue exceeds RMB 4,000 x 100mn, and Alibaba Cloud becomes the group's largest profit engine.
Pessimistic path: price competition compresses margins, Volcano Engine eats into share, margin gets stuck at 12%, and the business becomes "decent but not spectacular."

## 6. Core Conclusion: Alibaba Cloud's Ten-Year Profile

| Dimension | Current | Ten years later (base-case forecast) |
|------|------|----------------|
| Annual revenue | RMB 1,581 x 100mn | **RMB 4,000-7,300 x 100mn** |
| EBITA margin | ~9% | **18-20%** |
| Operating profit | ~RMB 140 x 100mn | **RMB 800-1,400 x 100mn** |
| Position in group | Second-largest revenue source, modestly profitable | **Profit engine alongside Taotian** |
| Moat | Widening | China's AI cloud leader, global Top 4 |

*Forecast sources: Goldman Sachs and ai-berkshire 10-year profit-scenario report*

Using Duan Yongping's standard: **Alibaba Cloud is the Alibaba business that most resembles a "good business"**. It has user stickiness, potential pricing power, scale economies, and compounding effects.

But the premise of a "good business" is that the ROIC inflection point arrives before 2030. If RMB 3,800 x 100mn of investment does not produce a real margin uplift, the "seed of a good business" becomes a "capital black hole."

Goldman Sachs values Alibaba Cloud separately at about **USD 54/share** in its SOTP valuation. That means Alibaba Cloud alone is worth about 40% of Alibaba's current market cap. Whether the market accepts that valuation depends on whether AI revenue growth can continue over the next several quarters.
