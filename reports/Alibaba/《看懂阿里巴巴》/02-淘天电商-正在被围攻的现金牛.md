# Taotian E-commerce: The Cash Cow Under Siege

> Understanding Alibaba series · Part 02
> Approx. reading time: 15 minutes

---

## It Contributes 90% of Profit, with a Moutai-Level Margin, but Is Being Attacked from All Sides

An e-commerce platform with a 44% EBITA margin -- in the same order of magnitude as Moutai.

That is Taotian today: the real profit engine behind Alibaba's RMB 1tn revenue base, generating more than 90% of the group's earnings. Without Taotian, Alibaba Cloud's RMB 3,800 x 100mn AI bet, instant retail's RMB 860 x 100mn annual loss, and FY2025's USD 165 x 100mn in buybacks would all be water without a source.

But this machine is having its walls dismantled. Losing 14 percentage points of e-commerce share in four years is not a one-quarter fluctuation; it is structural weakening in the foundation. To understand Taotian is to understand whether the foundation of Alibaba's entire investment story is still there.

---

## 1. How Does Taotian Actually Make Money?

Many people think Taobao/Tmall is a company that "sells things." It is not.

**Taotian's core revenue comes from two areas:**

| Revenue type | FY2026 amount | Essence |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Customer management revenue (CMR, i.e., advertising) | About RMB 3,000 x 100mn+ (estimated) | Merchants buy traffic through ads on Taobao |
| Commissions + technology service fees | - | Take rate on GMV |

Taotian is essentially an **advertising company**. It does not hold inventory and does not bear logistics. It does one thing: allocate consumer attention to merchants, who pay for that attention.

The advantages of this model are **very high gross economics** (44% EBITA margin), **no need for heavy assets**, and **excellent cash flow**.

The drawback is that **it depends entirely on traffic**. When consumer attention moves from Taobao to Douyin and Pinduoduo, ad spending follows.

## 2. How Much Share Has It Actually Lost?

This is the single most important question in the Alibaba investment story.

### 2.1 Four Years of Share Changes

| Year | Taotian | Pinduoduo | Douyin + Kuaishou | JD.com |
|------|------|--------|---------|------|
| 2021 | **51.3%** | 15.9% | 9.2% | ~20% |
| 2022 | ~46% | ~18% | ~14% | ~18% |
| 2023 | 39.2% | ~20% | ~19.3% | ~17% |
| 2024 | **37.3%** | ~21% | **21.4%** | ~17.4% |
| 2026E | ~36.2% | ~22% | ~22.7% | ~16% |

*Sources: QuestMobile 2025 annual report, China Merchants Securities 2026-01, Zheshang Securities forecasts*

**It lost 14 percentage points in four years, or an average of 3.5 percentage points per year.**

The more important point is the trend: Douyin e-commerce's 2025 GMV was about RMB 4.0-4.3tn and was still growing 25-30%. If the gap narrows by about RMB 9,000 x 100mn per year, **Douyin e-commerce GMV is expected to catch up with Taotian in 2028-2029**.

### 2.2 Why Did It Lose Share?

Not because Taobao got worse. Because the "paradigm" of e-commerce changed.

**Paradigm 1: Search e-commerce (Taobao's home field)**
Consumers have a clear need -> open Taobao and search -> compare prices -> place an order. Taobao dominated this paradigm for 15 years.

**Paradigm 2: Interest e-commerce (Douyin's home field)**
Consumers watch short videos / livestreams -> content creates desire -> impulsive purchase. Douyin's 800mn DAUs mean almost free traffic; Taobao's 300-400mn DAUs mean traffic has to be bought.

**Paradigm 3: Value-for-money e-commerce (Pinduoduo's home field)**
Consumers know what they want -> go directly to Pinduoduo for the lowest price. Pinduoduo's C2M model (factory directly to consumer) enables structurally low prices through supply-chain efficiency, not just subsidies.

Taobao's problem is that **it is no longer the first choice in any of the three paradigms.**

- Want cheap? Check Pinduoduo first
- Want content-led discovery? Open Douyin
- Want speed? JD.com or Meituan Flash
- Want quality? Tmall has an advantage, but JD.com is catching up

This is what Duan Yongping meant by "not knowing where the moat is."

### 2.3 Has the Share Decline Bottomed?

**Evidence that it has bottomed:**
- FY2026 Q4 CMR grew +8% on a comparable basis; merchants are still spending
- 88VIP membership surpassed **6,200 x 10k** with double-digit growth, showing stronger stickiness among high-value users
- Taobao Flash opened a new instant retail battlefield, with daily order peak of **1.2 x 100mn orders**
- Douyin e-commerce is still growing quickly, but the base effect should naturally slow it down

**Evidence that it has not bottomed:**
- Zheshang Securities forecasts share will keep falling to 36.2% in 2026
- Douyin e-commerce shelf-based GMV has risen to 40%, invading Taobao's core "search shopping" territory
- FY2026 Q4 CMR grew only 1% year over year on an unadjusted basis, suggesting the benefit from last year's technology service fee increase has already faded

**Judgment: the rate of decline is slowing, but it has not truly bottomed.** Going from losing 3-4 percentage points per year to losing 1-2 percentage points per year is "slower bleeding," not "successful hemostasis."

## 3. The Truth About "Monetization-Rate Improvement"

The bulls' favorite story is this: even if GMV growth slows, revenue can still grow as long as Taotian raises its monetization rate, meaning it charges merchants a higher percentage of transaction value.

**Supporting data:**
- A new 0.6% basic software service fee was introduced in September 2024
- Penetration of full-site promotion tools continues to increase
- FY2025 Q4 CMR growth was +12%, far above GMV growth

**On the other hand:**

| Platform | 2024 take rate (monetization rate) |
|------|--------------------------|
| Pinduoduo | ~7.6% |
| Taotian | ~3.9% |

*Source: estimates from platform financial reports*

Taotian's take rate is indeed below Pinduoduo's, so in theory there is room to raise it. But price increases have a ceiling:

1. **Competitive constraints**: merchants are multi-homing. If Taobao raises prices, merchants shift traffic to Pinduoduo / Douyin. The 0.6% technology service fee in 2024 already triggered merchant dissatisfaction.
2. **Diminishing marginal effect**: FY2026 Q4 CMR grew only 1% year over year, or +8% on a comparable basis, indicating the pricing benefit faded after just one year.
3. **The fundamental issue**: revenue growth driven by price hikes is "extractive growth," not "flywheel growth." Over the long run it accelerates merchant outflow.

Duan Yongping's test is simple: **a good business "spends less and collects more."** Taotian is indeed "collecting more," but "spending less" is no longer true at all. Instant retail is losing RMB 860 x 100mn per year, and the AI transformation of Taobao requires open-ended investment.

## 4. Taobao Flash: Lifeline or New Cash-Burning Black Hole?

In December 2025, Ele.me was officially renamed "Taobao Flash," positioned as "deliver everything in 30 minutes." This is one of Alibaba's biggest strategic bets in 2025-2026.

### 4.1 Results

| Metric | Data | Source |
|------|------|------|
| FY2026 full-year revenue | **RMB 785 x 100mn** (+47%) | Alibaba results |
| Daily order peak | **1.2 x 100mn orders** (August 2025) | Alibaba disclosure |
| Order-volume share | ~42-45% (near Meituan) | JPMorgan / Hongcan Research Institute |
| Q4 order growth YoY | **2.7x** | Jiang Fan Q4 earnings call |
| 88VIP members | **6,200 x 10k** | FY2026 Q4 |

### 4.2 Cost

| Metric | Data | Source |
|------|------|------|
| FY2026 estimated loss | **~RMB 860 x 100mn** | Morgan Stanley |
| Loss per order at peak | ~RMB 4.7/order | CICC |
| Latest loss per order | ~RMB 1.5/order | Multiple sources |
| Combined cash burn across three platforms | **~RMB 1,730 x 100mn** | Sina Finance |

### 4.3 Management Commitments

Jiang Fan laid out a clear three-step path on the FY2026 Q4 earnings call:

| Timing | Target |
|------|------|
| End of FY2027 | Positive unit economics (UE) |
| FY2028 | Instant retail GMV above **RMB 1tn** |
| FY2029 | Instant retail business **overall profitable** |

### 4.4 Judgment: Opportunity or Trap?

**The positive view:**
- Instant retail is a real consumption-upgrade need, "deliver everything in 30 minutes," with the market expected to reach USD 1,267 x 100mn in 2029 (source: GlobeNewswire)
- Taobao's main app has 4 x 100mn+ DAUs to funnel into Flash, so customer acquisition cost is far lower than building it independently
- Taobao Flash lifted Taobao DAUs by +20%, sending traffic back into e-commerce

**The negative view:**
- RMB 860 x 100mn in annual losses. Even if profitable by FY2029, cumulative losses may exceed RMB 2,000 x 100mn
- Meituan has 10 years of delivery network and merchant relationships, and still has more than 60% share by GTV
- Regulators have already intervened: in March 2026, Economic Daily published "The Food Delivery War Should End"
- User retention after subsidies fade is the biggest unknown
- Instant retail's per-order economics are naturally thin: delivery cost is RMB 6-7/order, commission rate is 5-7%, and long-term net margin may be only 3-5%

**The most likely outcome: in 10 years, Taobao Flash takes 25-30% of instant retail and becomes the industry's number two. It will be profitable, but only marginally so.** Its strategic value is greater than its financial value: preventing Meituan from using local-services traffic to move backward into distant-field e-commerce.

## 5. Is Taotian's "Flywheel" Still Spinning?

Taotian's classic flywheel: more merchants -> more products -> lower prices -> more consumers -> more merchants.

**Evidence that the flywheel has been disrupted:**
- Lower-end merchants are leaving: Pinduoduo offers RMB 0 entry versus Taobao's 0.6% technology service fee
- Cross-platform price comparison has become normal among consumers, and overlapping users across the three platforms rose 19.9% YoY
- Douyin built a new wheel outside Taotian's flywheel: content seeding -> impulse consumption -> repeat purchase

**Evidence that the flywheel is still spinning:**
- Tmall brand flagship stores remain China's most mature merchant ecosystem, and brand merchants face high switching costs
- 6,200 x 10k 88VIP members lock in mid-to-high-end consumers
- Taobao still has the industry's largest SKU count
- Full-site promotion tools show that merchants are still willing to spend money on Taotian

**Judgment: the flywheel is not dead, but it has shifted from "self-accelerating" to "externally maintained."** CMR growth comes from price increases rather than organic GMV growth. That is "extractive" growth, not flywheel growth.

---

## 6. Core Conclusion: Taotian's Five-Year Profile

| Dimension | Current | Five years later (base-case forecast) |
|------|------|----------------|
| GMV share | ~37% | **28-32%** (still falling, but more slowly) |
| Annual revenue | ~RMB 5,500 x 100mn | **RMB 6,500-7,300 x 100mn** (CAGR 4-5%) |
| EBITA margin | ~44% (excluding Flash) | **35-38%** (recovers after Flash drag peaks) |
| Moat | Narrowing | Downgraded from a "franchise" to an "ordinary good business" |
| Positioning | Alibaba's profit engine | Still the largest profit source, but no longer the growth engine |

*Forecast sources: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and ai-berkshire internal research notes*

Taotian remains an extraordinary money-making machine: a cash cow with annual profit at the RMB 2,000 x 100mn level, which is top-tier even among global internet companies.

But it is no longer the monopoly platform where "everyone comes to me." It has become a composite of "brand e-commerce + mid-to-high-end consumption + instant retail": differentiated, but with shrinking differentiation.

For investors, the core question is not "will Taotian die?" It will not. The question is: **can Taotian hold up long enough for Alibaba Cloud to take over as the new growth engine?**
