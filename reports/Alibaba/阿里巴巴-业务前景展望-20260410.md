## Alibaba in 5 Years

**From an "e-commerce empire" to an "AI-cloud-driven technology group."**

- Taobao and Tmall's share falls from 45% to ~35%; it remains the largest revenue source but is no longer the growth engine
- Alibaba Cloud's share rises from 12% to ~20%, becoming the core profit and valuation engine (similar to AWS for Amazon)
- CEO target: external cloud + AI revenue above USD 1,000 (100mn) within 5 years
- Biggest risk: when Douyin E-commerce catches Taobao and Tmall in GMV (expected in 2028-2029), and the threat from ByteDance Volcano Engine at the AI application layer
