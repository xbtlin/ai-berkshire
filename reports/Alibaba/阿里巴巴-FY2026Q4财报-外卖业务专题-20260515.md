Jiang Fan's original comment (Q4 earnings call):

### 3.2 Market Share

**Based on average daily order volume:**

| Source | Time | Meituan | Alibaba ecosystem | JD |
|------|------|------|--------|------|
| Hongcan Industry Research Institute | 2025 Q3 (peak) | 46.9% | **42.8%** | ~10% |
| JPMorgan | November 2025 | 50% | **42%** | 8% |

**Based on GTV (gross transaction value):**

Meituan still maintained a GTV share of roughly **60%+**, because its average order value was materially higher than competitors' (about 1.5x). Orders above RMB 15 accounted for more than 2/3 of GTV, while orders above RMB 30 accounted for roughly 70%.

**Key distinction:** By order volume, Alibaba was already close to Meituan; by GTV, Meituan's advantage remained clear. The reason is that Taobao Flash Sale's early mix was dominated by low-AOV tea and beverage orders.

**Share comparison before the food-delivery war (end-2024):** Meituan was about 65-70%, while Ele.me was about 30-33%. Through a year-long battle, Alibaba lifted its order-volume share from roughly 30% to about 42%.

### 3.6 Subsidy Intensity and Retreat

**Subsidy scale:**
- Taobao Flash Sale announced a RMB 500 x 100mn subsidy program (mid-2025)
- The three platforms provided cumulative subsidies of RMB 800-1,000 x 100mn in 2025 Q2-Q3
- Subsidy formats included large-value red packets, limited-time free orders, official fixed-price offers, and merchant commission reductions or waivers

**Regulatory intervention:**
- August 1, 2025: The three platforms issued a joint statement on "standardizing promotions"
- March 25, 2026: Economic Daily published "The Food-Delivery War Should End," which was reposted on the State Administration for Market Regulation website
- April 2026: Regulators summoned platforms for meetings; large-value subsidies had already contracted meaningfully
- April 17, 2026: The State Administration for Market Regulation issued the largest-ever food-safety fine in the sector (RMB 35.97 x 100mn)

**Adjustments after subsidies retreated:**
- Commission rate increased from 6.4% to 7.4%
- Merchants assumed a larger share of red packets (for an RMB 9 red packet, merchants paid RMB 8 while the platform paid only RMB 1)
- Non-tea-and-beverage orders already exceeded 75% of the mix, and average order value (AOV) continued to rise sequentially

## 4. Management's Profitability Roadmap

Jiang Fan laid out a clear three-step path on the Q4 earnings call:

| Timing | Target |
|---------|------|
| **End-FY2027** (March 2027) | Unit economics (UE) turn positive |
| **FY2028** | Instant-retail GMV exceeds **RMB 1 trillion** |
| **FY2029** | Instant-retail business reaches **overall profitability** |

Jiang Fan's original comments (Q4 earnings call):
> "We are confident in achieving positive unit economics by the end of fiscal year 2027."
> "We remain committed to the fiscal year 2028 target of exceeding RMB 1 trillion in overall instant-retail transaction scale."
> "On the basis of that scale, we expect the instant-retail business segment to achieve overall profitability in fiscal year 2029."

CFO Toby Xu (Q2 earnings call, 2025.11):
> "This quarter is the peak of investment in Taobao Flash Sale."

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### Meituan Food Delivery

- 2025 full-year revenue of RMB 3,649 x 100mn (+8%); full-year net loss of RMB 234 x 100mn (swing from profit to loss)
- Core local commerce operating loss of RMB 69 x 100mn
- Maintained 60%+ GTV share, with a clear leadership position in the mid-to-high-AOV dine-in meal market
- Goldman Sachs lowered its long-term Meituan profit-per-order expectation from RMB 1.5 to RMB 0.8

### JD Food Delivery

- Entered the market in February 2025, serving more than 240mn users
- Share of about 7-8% (JPMorgan basis); JD claimed a peak of 31% (during 618, with questionable data definitions)
- New businesses segment full-year loss of RMB 466 x 100mn
- Differentiation: full-time riders with five social insurances and one housing-fund contribution (150,000 full-time riders), and self-operated Qixian Xiaochu restaurant operations

### Douyin Food Delivery

- Has effectively exited the food-delivery logistics competition
- Officially stated in July 2025 that it had "no plan to build its own food-delivery business"
- Focused on in-store local services (about 40% share vs. Meituan's 60%)
- "Douyin Express Delivery" is limited to selected branded chains and does not participate in the delivery-speed arms race

## 6. Integration of AI and Food Delivery

On May 11, 2026, the Qwen app and Taobao were fully connected, allowing users to order food delivery, shop, and pay directly through conversations inside Qwen without jumping to another app. The integration also covers multi-terminal scenarios such as in-car voice ordering and Qwen AI glasses ordering.

Eddie Wu (Q4 earnings call):
> "We are at a critical inflection point in the evolution from conversational chatbots to autonomous AI agents."

## 7. Strict Separation of Facts and Opinions

### Confirmed Facts (sources: financial reports / earnings calls / official disclosures)

1. FY2026 instant-retail revenue was RMB 785.2 x 100mn, +47% YoY
2. Q4 instant-retail revenue was RMB 199.88 x 100mn, +57% YoY
3. Q4 order scale was 2.7x the prior-year period, and the non-restaurant portion was 3x
4. Ele.me has been renamed Taobao Flash Sale
5. Management committed to positive UE by end-FY2027, RMB 1 trillion GMV in FY2028, and overall profitability in FY2029
6. The CFO stated explicitly that Q2 was the peak investment quarter
7. 88VIP membership reached 62.00mn
8. Full-year capital expenditures were RMB 1,260.63 x 100mn

### Investment-Bank Estimates (not officially confirmed; subject to estimation error)

1. Taobao Flash Sale's FY2026 full-year loss was about RMB 860 x 100mn (Morgan Stanley)
2. Per-order loss declined from RMB 4.7 to about RMB 1.5 (cross-checked across CICC, Goldman Sachs, LatePost, and other sources)
3. Order-volume share was about 42% (JPMorgan, November 2025)
4. Meituan's GTV share remained above 60% (Goldman Sachs)

### Key Uncertainties

1. **Can share be retained after subsidies fade?** After regulators stopped aggressive subsidies, the retention rate of price-sensitive users remains unknown
2. **Can the UE breakeven timetable be delivered?** Management committed to end-FY2027, but delivery depends on how far commission rates can rise and how much fulfillment efficiency can improve
3. **Is the RMB 1 trillion GMV target feasible?** FY2026 instant-retail revenue was RMB 785 x 100mn; GMV is larger, but the RMB 1 trillion target still requires sustained high growth
4. **How durable is JD Food Delivery?** After RMB 466 x 100mn of losses, whether Richard Liu will continue investing is the biggest variable
5. **How long can Alibaba absorb the losses?** It holds RMB 5,208 x 100mn in cash, but free cash flow has turned negative by RMB 466 x 100mn; if AI and instant retail both keep burning cash, the pace of cash consumption deserves close attention
