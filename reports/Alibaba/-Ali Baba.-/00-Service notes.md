> Five main essays unpack Alibaba layer by layer, revealing its real structure.
>
> Approximate reading time for the series: 75 minutes; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series Positioning

Alibaba may be the **most discussed, yet most deeply misunderstood** company in the history of China's internet sector.

- Bulls say: revenue above RMB 10,000 (100mn), 3,890 (RMB 100mn) net cash, annual buybacks of USD 119 (100mn), Alibaba Cloud AI growth of 40% — this is a transforming giant seriously undervalued by the market
- Bears say: e-commerce share lost 14 percentage points in four years, Non-GAAP net income is almost flat, free cash flow collapsed 53%, and it is fighting three wars at once — this is a declining empire

**Both sides are looking at the same company and citing the same financial report**. The difference is whether you view Alibaba as one whole, or break it into separate pieces.

What this series aims to do is **first clarify each piece, then let you judge for yourself "what the whole is worth"** — rather than reaching the conclusion for you.

## Writing Principles for the Series

This series follows the hard rules below:

1. **Present the data first, then build the logic, and only then reach conclusions** — no preset bull or bear stance
2. **Attach a counterargument to every core judgment** — let readers weigh the evidence themselves
3. **Delete unreliable data when in doubt** — Alibaba's business is extremely complex, and different measurement bases diverge sharply; this series uses only the most credible sources
4. **Do not calculate "probability-weighted expected return"** — assigning scenario weights is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **Not investment advice** — disclaimer at the end of every essay

## Comparison with "Understanding Pinduoduo" and "Understanding Tencent"

| Dimension | Tencent | Pinduoduo | Alibaba |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| Asset quality | WeChat is irreplaceable + evergreen games | Extreme financial efficiency + algorithmic advertising machine | E-commerce cash cow + cloud/AI option |
| Valuation | ~18x PE, reasonable | ~10.5x PE, extremely cheap | ~17x PE, 47% below historical average |
| Management transparency | High | Low | Medium (Joe Tsai publicly admitted mistakes, but business complexity is high) |
| Risk structure | Regulatory tail risk | Temu political gamble + trust discount | Three-front war + AI capex gamble |
| **Core tension** | High growth certainty but valuation is not extremely cheap | Extremely cheap but management is opaque | **The most profitable business is weakening, while the most promising business has not yet matured** |

If you finished the Tencent series thinking "high certainty," and finished the Pinduoduo series thinking "cheap but hard to see clearly," then Alibaba's keyword is — **transition**. What you need to judge is whether this transition is worth waiting for.
