## Current Core Conclusions (Based on 3 Sub-Reports; to Be Revised After Adding the Li Lu Perspective)

### FY2036 Profit Forecast: Two Estimation Lenses

| Lens | Base-case forecast | Probability-weighted expected value | Confidence |
|------|---------|--------------|--------|
| Financial modeling (Buffett perspective, Non-GAAP net income) | 4,591 (RMB 100mn) | 4,880 (RMB 100mn) | Medium |
| Industry structure (Munger perspective, operating profit) | 3,500-3,900 (RMB 100mn) | 3,685 (RMB 100mn) | Medium (no WebSearch) |

**Difference between the two forecasts**: mainly the Alibaba Cloud revenue assumption — the financial Agent uses a base case of 7,306 (RMB 100mn) (20% CAGR), while the industry Agent uses 4,000-4,800 (RMB 100mn) (more conservative). The gap is roughly 2,500 (RMB 100mn) of revenue × 19% margin ≈ 475 (RMB 100mn) of profit.

**Reasonable blended range**: FY2036 base-case annual profit of roughly **3,800-4,600 (RMB 100mn)**, with an expected value of roughly 4,200-4,800 (RMB 100mn).

### Three-Scenario Overview (Financial Agent Basis)

| Scenario | Probability | Non-GAAP net income | Key assumptions |
|------|------|----------------|----------|
| Bull | 30% | 7,162 (RMB 100mn) | Cloud 25% margin, AI 60%+ of cloud revenue, AIDC turns profitable |
| **Base** | **50%** | **4,591 (RMB 100mn)** | Cloud 19% margin, Taobao and Tmall 35% margin, AIDC breaks even in FY28 |
| Bear | 20% | 2,131 (RMB 100mn) | Cloud price war, Taobao and Tmall share erosion, AIDC remains loss-making |

### Valuation Conclusion (Based on the Base-Case Forecast)

- DCF-implied intrinsic value: **25,477 (RMB 100mn)** (base case, PE 14x, 10% discount rate)
- Current market cap: 23,120 (RMB 100mn) (US-listed shares USD 318B / Hong Kong shares HKD 2.49tn)
- **Margin of safety only 22%** — below Buffett's required 30%; "close to fair value, but not obviously cheap"
- Trigger buy levels: build positions in tranches below HKD 100 for Alibaba's Hong Kong shares / USD 110 for the US-listed shares (margin of safety would be 47% at that point)

## Snapshot of Core Judgments from Three Perspectives

### Business Model (Duan Yongping Perspective) — "In 10 Years It Is a Good Company, but Only 2.5 Good Businesses Remain"

| Segment | Current business quality | Business quality in 10 years |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| **Alibaba Cloud** | 4 | **5** |
| **Taobao and Tmall** | 4 | **3** |
| **AIDC** | 2 | 3-4 |
| Local Services | 1 | 2 |
| Cainiao | 2 | 2-3 |
| Digital Media and Entertainment | 1 | 1-2 |

**Core tension**: Over the next 3-5 years Alibaba is a "company that spends money" (3,800 (RMB 100mn) capex + Instant Commerce subsidies + AIDC still losing money + AI large-model burn) — violating Duan Yongping's principle of "spend less money, collect more money."

**Major turning point**: In 2024-2025, Alibaba proactively divested Intime and Sun Art, which together had generated 225 (RMB 100mn) of losses — the first large-scale admission in its history that some businesses were "non-core," followed by an active exit. 88VIP users are approaching 6,000 (10,000 users) (vs 3,500 (10,000 users) in FY25Q4). **Duan Yongping himself increased his Alibaba position in 2024 Q4** (H&H International).

### Financials/Valuation (Buffett Perspective) — "FY2036 Expected Annual Profit of 4,880 (RMB 100mn), but the Margin of Safety Is Not Enough"

- **Group FY36 base-case revenue of 24,086 (RMB 100mn)**, a 10-year CAGR of 9.2%
- **Alibaba Cloud steady-state EBITA margin base case of 19%** (vs AWS 38%, Azure 40-45%, GCP 14%) — "catching up" rather than "matching"
- **Key variable**: whether Alibaba Cloud's EBITA margin can rise steadily (by +0.5-1pp per quarter)

### Industry Structure (Munger Perspective) — "A Mildly Upgraded Microsoft Path"

- **Historical analogy ranking**: Tencent (closest, also China regulation + Hong Kong listing environment) > Microsoft (cloud-transition comparison) > IBM (negative lesson)
- **Top 3 key uncertainties**: persistent macro deflation in China (±1,500 (RMB 100mn)), Taobao and Tmall take rate (±800 (RMB 100mn)), Alibaba Cloud OM (±700 (RMB 100mn))
- **Current valuation implication**: Alibaba's Hong Kong market cap of HKD 2.5-2.8tn / US market cap of USD 3,000-3,300 (100mn) — broadly pricing in the base case, implying a 10-year annualized return of 8-12%

## Bull vs Bear Snapshot (Full Version Pending the Li Lu Perspective)

### 🟢 Bull Case
1. Alibaba Cloud already has a three-layer closed loop: "China's only foundation-model + IaaS + downstream-entry ecosystem (Taobao/DingTalk/Amap/Alipay)"
2. 6,000 (10,000) 88VIP users show strengthening stickiness among middle-class consumers
3. Duan Yongping increased his Alibaba position in 2024 Q4
4. Proactive divestiture of non-core assets (Intime + Sun Art) — management is evolving toward "focus"
5. AIDC losses narrowed 59% YoY in FY26Q3, approaching breakeven
6. Cash + short-term investments of 5,602 (RMB 100mn); the company can afford the capex burn

### 🔴 Bear Case
1. FY26Q3 operating profit -74%, Non-GAAP EPS -71% — profits collapsed during the heavy-investment period
2. Taobao and Tmall CMR only +1%; take-rate expansion is boxed in by Pinduoduo/Douyin
3. Alibaba Cloud's China share faces a two-front squeeze from ByteDance Volcano Engine and Huawei Cloud
4. 3,800 (RMB 100mn) of AI capex; the 10-year ROIC inflection point is unproven
5. Long-term macro deflation in China — e-commerce, local services, and cloud all under pressure at once
6. Current margin of safety is only 22%, below the >= 30% required by Buffett/Duan Yongping
