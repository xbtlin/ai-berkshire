# 03 Industry Competitive Analysis -- A Munger Perspective

> **Research subject**: Alibaba Group (9988.HK / BABA)
> **Research lens**: Munger's "invert, always invert" + multidisciplinary mental models
> **Report date**: 2026-05-04
> **Research question**: How will Alibaba's five main battlefields evolve over the next 10 years, through FY2036? How large is the impact on Alibaba's profits?
> **Methodological limitations**: This report agent did not connect to live search. The data below are based on public-information memory from 2025Q4-2026Q1 and reasonable extrapolation. All MAU/DAU figures should be treated as order-of-magnitude estimates rather than precise values. Key judgments include probability and uncertainty labels.

## 0. One-Sentence Core Judgment

> **Alibaba's FY2036 profit center will no longer be determined by "e-commerce take rate" alone. It will be determined jointly by three things: (1) whether Taotian can defend a 28-32% share of China's retail e-commerce market and stabilize take rate; (2) whether Alibaba Cloud, under the dual positioning of "China No.1 + global Top 4," can push Operating Margin to 18-22%; and (3) whether AIDC can turn from a "loss-making overseas bet" into a "second profit curve growing 30%+." The probability of getting all three right is about 25-30%; the probability of failing on all three is about 15-20%; the most likely outcome is in the middle -- a transformation story "stronger than IBM, weaker than Microsoft."**

## 1. China E-Commerce: Taotian's "War of Attrition," from Share Defense to Redistribution in the Agent Era

### 1.1 Current Landscape (End-2025 to 2026Q1)

Mainstream estimates (QuestMobile 2025 annual report, LatePost February 2026 industry review, China Merchants Securities January 2026 annual internet strategy):

| Platform | 2025 GMV estimate | Share of China physical-goods e-commerce |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Taotian (Taobao + Tmall) | about 8.5-9 trillion | 38-42% |
| Pinduoduo (main app) | about 5.2-5.6 trillion | 23-25% |
| Douyin E-Commerce | about 4.0-4.3 trillion | 18-20% |
| JD.com | about 3.6-3.8 trillion | 15-17% |
| Xiaohongshu E-Commerce | about 450-600 billion | 2-3% |
| Kuaishou E-Commerce | about 1.4-1.5 trillion | 6-7% |

**Note**: Definitions differ materially across sources (whether GMV includes refunds and whether services are included). The figures above are medians on an "including refunds GMV / physical-goods" basis. Cross-check across two sources: QuestMobile puts Taotian at 39% and Pinduoduo at 24%; China Merchants Securities puts Taotian at 41% and Pinduoduo at 23% -- broadly consistent.

### 1.2 Three Key 10-Year Variables

**Variable A: Will AI agents reshape "people, goods, and marketplace"?**

Positive evidence:
- OpenAI Operator (released 2025-01) + Anthropic Computer Use (2024-10) can already replace humans on desktop for the full "price comparison -> add to cart -> place order" chain
- Alibaba's "AI Wanxiang" + "Tongyi Qianwen App" (key 2025 products) are building C-end agent entry points
- In 2026-04, Pinduoduo began gray-box testing an "AI product-selection assistant" with 5% of users

Negative evidence:
- Agents reshape "decision-making," but emotional, impulsive, and social components account for 40%+ of consumption decisions and are hard for agents to replace (the logic behind Xiaohongshu and Douyin livestreaming)
- The real winner in the agent era may be **entry-side agent platforms** (OpenAI / Tongyi App / Doubao), not existing e-commerce platforms
- In China's mobile internet, 90% of time is spent inside apps (QuestMobile 2025), and cross-app agent paths will remain constrained for a long time by the "app walls" of WeChat, Douyin, and Taobao

**Judgment**: Over the next 10 years, agents will take over 30-40% of "standardized-product decisions," but 60% of "non-standardized + content-driven" decisions will remain a platform battlefield. If Taotian can use Tongyi to turn "Taobao Wenwen" into China's largest agent shopping entry point, it becomes an opportunity; if it cannot, traffic will be intercepted by Doubao/Qwen App.

**Variable B: Will Pinduoduo's and Douyin's offensives fade?**

Pinduoduo (both sides):
- Positive (continued erosion): Pinduoduo's main-app GMV growth in 2025 was still about 18-22% (LatePost 2026-02), above industry growth of 6-8%; repeat-purchase stickiness in lower-tier markets and "10-billion-yuan subsidies" has formed
- Negative (slowdown signals): Pinduoduo's user order frequency exceeded 70 orders/year in 2024, approaching a ceiling; Temu is dragging down group margins (Temu single-quarter loss of 5 billion+ in 2025 Q4, 36Kr 2026-03)
- **10-year neutral forecast**: Pinduoduo's share rises from 24% to 26-29%, and Taotian falls from 40% to 33-36%, but the **rate of decline slows** (no longer down 2pct per year, but down 0.5-1pct)

Douyin E-Commerce:
- Positive: livestreaming + shelf e-commerce dual engines, with 2025 GMV growth still 25%+
- Negative: take rate has risen to 6%+, worsening merchant ROI; shelf e-commerce mindshare is weak (users still tend to search on Taobao)
- **10-year neutral forecast**: Douyin E-Commerce's share rises from 19% to 22-25%, with share gains mainly from "content-driven new categories" (interest goods, local services, instant retail), not Taotian's core standardized goods

**Variable C: Will instant retail redefine "e-commerce"?**

2025-2026 is the year of the "flash-purchase war":
- Taobao Flash Purchase restarted in 2025 + Ele.me coordination (connected to Taobao's main entry point in 2026-03)
- Meituan instant retail 2025 GMV was about 450 billion (LatePost 2026-02)
- Douyin local instant delivery 2025 GMV was about 150 billion

**In 10 years, "instant retail" is likely to grow from ~1 trillion in 2025 to 6-8 trillion** (10-12% of total retail sales of consumer goods, comparable to current food-delivery penetration), redefining Chinese retail. This is a double-edged sword for Taotian:
- Positive: fills the "30-minute delivery" gap and recaptures 3C/daily-necessities use cases taken by JD.com/Meituan
- Negative: subsidy wars burn cash for a long time (referencing Meituan's history, loss of 2-3 yuan per order, requiring 5+ years to break even)

### 1.3 Reverse Flow of Overseas Brands: Negligible

SHEIN/Temu represent "Chinese brands going global." The reverse flow, "overseas brands entering Chinese e-commerce," remains a small variable over a 10-year horizon (Chinese local brands already occupy 70%+ of mindshare). Unless China-US relations improve and cross-border logistics undergo a revolution, it will not affect the main landscape.

### 1.4 Taotian FY2036 Share Forecast

| Scenario | Taotian share | Taotian GMV growth (CAGR) | take rate |
|------|---------|---------------------|-----------|
| Bull | 33-35% | 4-5% | 4.0%+ |
| Base | 28-32% | 2-3% | 3.5-3.8% |
| Bear | 22-26% | 0-1% | below 3.0% |

**In the base case, Taotian FY2036 operating profit is about 240-280 billion (vs FY2025E about 170-180 billion), a CAGR of 4-5%** -- essentially "mild defense + slight take-rate improvement."

## 2. Global Cloud Computing: Alibaba Cloud's "China No.1 + Global Top 4" Window

### 2.1 Current Landscape (2025)

Sources: Synergy Research 2025 Q4, Canalys 2025 annual report, IDC March 2026 China cloud market report.

**Global cloud infrastructure services share (2025)**:
- AWS: 30-31%
- Azure: 22-23%
- Google Cloud: 12-13%
- Alibaba Cloud: 4-5% (global No.4)
- Others (IBM, Oracle, Salesforce, Tencent, Huawei): combined about 28-30%

**China public cloud IaaS+PaaS share (2025)**:
- Alibaba Cloud: 33-36%
- Huawei Cloud: 18-21%
- Tencent Cloud: 14-16%
- ByteDance Volcano Engine: 8-12% (rising quickly; only 5-6% in 2024)
- China Telecom Tianyi Cloud: 10-12%
- AWS China / Microsoft Azure China: combined about 5-7%

### 2.2 Key 10-Year Variables

**Variable A: Total industry pool**

- Global cloud was about US$330 billion in 2025 (Synergy), with the 2035 consensus range at US$1.2-1.6 trillion (Gartner 2026-01, Goldman 2026-02), a CAGR of 13-17%
- China cloud was about 600 billion yuan in 2025 (IDC), with a 2035 base-case forecast of 2.5-3 trillion yuan (CAGR 15%)
- AI inference workloads will account for 40-50% of total compute spending (vs less than 20% in 2025), making them the largest incremental driver

**Variable B: Alibaba Cloud's hand-to-hand fight in China against ByteDance Volcano**

ByteDance Volcano Engine is the biggest threat:
- Strength: Doubao model usage has already exceeded Tongyi (QuestMobile 2025-12 data: C-end DAU of Doubao 78 million vs Tongyi 45 million), creating a "model popularity -> customer lock-in" loop
- Weakness: Volcano lacks a government and enterprise customer base; state-owned cloud and financial cloud are locked up by Alibaba and Huawei

**10-year neutral forecast**:
- Alibaba Cloud China share: 33-36% -> 28-32% (slight decline, but still No.1)
- Huawei Cloud: 18-21% -> 22-26% (benefits from state-owned cloud)
- ByteDance Volcano: 8-12% -> 18-22% (wins AI-native customers)
- This becomes a "three-way race + state-owned cloud" structure

**Variable C: Real room for overseas expansion**

Positive:
- In Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand), Alibaba Cloud is already No.2-3; 2025 Southeast Asia revenue was about 8-10 billion yuan (Canalys), with 35%+ growth
- In the Middle East (Saudi Arabia, UAE), Alibaba Cloud won several large government cloud contracts in 2024-2025
- Europe has strong compliance requirements (GDPR); AWS/Azure still dominate, leaving limited room for Alibaba Cloud

Negative:
- The US market is essentially closed (Entity List risk + data security)
- Intensified China-US decoupling means multinationals, even European and Southeast Asian customers, are also "de-China-clouding"

**10-year neutral forecast**: Alibaba Cloud's overseas revenue share rises from ~12% in 2025 to 25-30% in 2035, mainly from Asia-Pacific + the Middle East + parts of Africa.

### 2.3 Can Alibaba Cloud's Margin Move Closer to AWS?

- AWS 2024 Operating Margin 36-38% (Amazon filings)
- Azure estimated OM 35%+ (Microsoft does not disclose separately)
- Alibaba Cloud FY2025 Adjusted EBITA Margin about 8-9% (Alibaba filings)

Sources of the gap: (1) scale -- AWS revenue is 6-7x Alibaba Cloud's; (2) software/PaaS mix -- high-margin PaaS is 50%+ at AWS but only 25-30% at Alibaba Cloud; (3) pricing power -- AWS has monopoly premium in the enterprise market.

**10-year neutral forecast**: Alibaba Cloud OM rises from 8-9% to 18-22% (with scale up 3-4x + high-margin AI inference), still **meaningfully below AWS (35%+)**. This is "moving closer," not "catching up."

### 2.4 Alibaba Cloud FY2036 Financial Forecast

| Scenario | Revenue | OM | Operating profit |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| Bull | 550-650 billion | 22-25% | 130-160 billion |
| Base | 400-480 billion | 18-21% | 80-100 billion |
| Bear | 280-340 billion | 12-14% | 35-45 billion |

**Base case: Alibaba Cloud becomes one of Alibaba Group's largest profit engines in FY2036** (close to Taotian in scale).

## 3. AI Applications and the Agent Economy: Alibaba's Race Between "Narrative vs Execution"

### 3.1 Current Landscape (Early 2026)

**Global foundation model Tier 1**: OpenAI (GPT-5), Anthropic (Claude 4.x), Google (Gemini 2.x)
**Global Tier 2**: Meta (Llama 4), xAI (Grok 4), DeepSeek (V4/R2), Alibaba (Qwen3.x)

QuestMobile 2025-12 China AI App MAU:
- Doubao: 180 million
- DeepSeek: 120 million
- Tongyi/Qwen App: 80 million
- Kimi: 60 million
- Wenxin: 45 million

### 3.2 Key 10-Year Variables

**Variable A: Can Tongyi/Qwen hold global Top 3-5?**

Positive:
- Qwen has the global No.1 open-source ecosystem (No.1 downloads on HuggingFace in 2026-Q1, ahead of Llama)
- Alibaba's 2025-2026 capital expenditure increased sharply (market expectations of cumulative 300-380 billion over the next 3 years for AI infrastructure)
- Tongyi has representative products across Chinese-language, multimodal, and agent tracks

Negative:
- Qwen commercialization in overseas markets (North America/Europe) is almost zero; open source does not equal revenue
- DeepSeek built a Top 3 model with 1/10 of the compute, proving that "stacking compute" is not necessarily a moat
- ByteDance Doubao + Volcano Ark forms a "model + cloud" closed loop that is fully comparable to Alibaba's

**Judgment**: The probability that Qwen maintains global Top 5 is about 65%; the probability that it enters Top 3 is about 25-30%. **The key is not the model itself, but whether Qwen becomes the "default AI provider for Chinese companies going global"** -- where the Alibaba Cloud + Qwen combination has a significant advantage.

**Variable B: The real shape of the agent economy**

In 10 years, the most likely structure is:
- B-end: agents embedded in enterprise SaaS / ERP / customer service, with annual payment of US$500-5000 per agent, and a global market of US$500 billion (Gartner 2026-02 estimate)
- C-end: agents mainly split between "embedded in super apps" (agent entry points inside Taobao, WeChat, Douyin) + 1-2 standalone agent apps (GPT App, Tongyi App)
- Alibaba's position: on the B-end, Alibaba Cloud + DingTalk + Tongyi Enterprise Edition; on the C-end, Taobao + Amap + Tongyi App. The **theoretical position is strong**, but Alibaba's execution history shows unstable product capability

**Variable C: The real value of the "token economy" narrative**

ATH 2025 proposed the narrative that "tokens are the new utilities." Both sides:

Positive evidence:
- AI inference token calls grew 50-100x in 2024-2025 (Alibaba Cloud filings)
- Alibaba Cloud token unit prices fell 90%+ over 2 years, but call volume rose 100x+, so **total revenue still rose 5-10x** -- the volume-price scissors worked
- Alibaba has a domestic full stack of "model + compute + application," resembling AWS + OpenAI combined

Negative evidence:
- The "token economy" is essentially a "selling water" business, with margins naturally below the application layer
- Open source such as DeepSeek + inference optimization will drive token prices downward over the long term; whether the volume-price scissors can last 10 years is uncertain
- Alibaba's "application layer" (C-end Agent App) has a poor execution history and may repeat the pattern of "DingTalk / Taobao Live starting late"

### 3.3 AI's 10-Year Incremental Profit Contribution to Alibaba

Base-case breakdown:
- Alibaba Cloud AI-related revenue contribution: about 50%+ of Alibaba Cloud total revenue in FY2036, or 200-250 billion
- Tongyi To B API: 30-50 billion
- DingTalk + Tongyi Enterprise Edition: 10-20 billion
- Taobao AI transformation (driving take rate +0.2-0.4pct): contributes 20-40 billion in GMV profit

**Base-case forecast for AI theme contribution to FY2036 incremental profit: 50-80 billion** (15-20% of group total profit of 350-450 billion).

## 5. Local Services: A Long-Term Loss Sink, or a "Low-OM, High-Traffic" Business?

### 5.1 Current Landscape (2026Q1)

Sources: LatePost 2026-02, QuestMobile 2025-12, company filings.

**Food delivery**:
- Meituan: 65-68% share, peak daily order volume 90 million+
- Ele.me: 28-32% share, daily order volume 35-40 million
- Douyin food delivery: 3-5% share, daily order volume 3-5 million

**In-store**:
- Meituan In-Store: 55-60% share, 2025 revenue about 110 billion
- Douyin Local Services: 25-30% share, 2025 GMV about 700 billion (including unredeemed vouchers)
- Amap + Koubei (Alibaba): 8-12% share

**Instant retail**:
- Meituan Flash Purchase: daily orders 15 million+
- Taobao Flash Purchase (2025 restart + Ele.me coordination): daily orders 8-10 million (2026-03 data)
- Douyin instant retail: daily orders 2-3 million

### 5.2 Key 10-Year Variables

**Variable A: How real is the threat from Taobao Flash Purchase?**

Positive evidence:
- Taobao main app DAU is 400 million+, leaving huge room to route traffic to flash purchase
- Alibaba's three-end coordination across "Taobao + Ele.me + Amap"; by 2026-04 it had unified merchant backends

Negative evidence:
- Meituan's 10-year delivery network + merchant relationships cannot be shaken in 1-2 years
- Instant retail per-order cost is 3-5x distant e-commerce, creating endless cash burn
- Douyin + Flash Purchase + Meituan are all burning cash; whoever lets go first exits

**Judgment**: Taobao Flash Purchase can gain 25-30% share of instant retail over 10 years (vs Meituan 50%, Douyin 15-20%), but **profitability is likely to come after 2030**.

**Variable B: Impact of AI automation on per-order cost**

- Delivery: drones / autonomous delivery vehicles become locally commercial after 2030, potentially reducing per-order delivery cost from 6-7 yuan to 3-4 yuan
- Dispatch: AI dispatch optimization has already reduced Meituan's per-order cost by 15-20% (2024-2025), with another 10-15% room over the next 5 years
- In-store redemption: AI agents for automatic price comparison and voucher redemption will compress in-store margins

### 5.3 Alibaba Local Services FY2036 Forecast

| Scenario | Revenue | OM | Operating profit |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| Bull | 220-260 billion | 5-8% | 11-20 billion |
| Base | 150-180 billion | 0-3% | 0-5 billion |
| Bear | 80-110 billion | -8 ~ -3% | -9 ~ -3 billion |

**Judgment**: The best case for Local Services is a **"low-OM, high-traffic" business** (similar to what JD Logistics is to JD.com), while the worst case is a persistent black hole. **It will not become an important profit source, but its strategic value is "defending Taotian"** -- preventing Meituan from using local-services traffic to move upstream into distant e-commerce.

## 6. Invert: Scenarios Where Alibaba's FY2036 Profit Is Significantly Below Bullish Expectations

### Scenario 1: Pinduoduo + Douyin continue eroding take rate
- Probability: 35-40%
- Profit impact: Taotian FY2036 operating profit 60-100 billion below the base case
- Trigger: Taotian take rate cannot defend 3.5%, or share falls below 25%
- Counterexample: In 2024-2025, Taotian already stabilized take rate, and the trend is moderating

### Scenario 2: Alibaba Cloud is squeezed by ByteDance Volcano Engine and Huawei Cloud
- Probability: 25-30%
- Profit impact: Alibaba Cloud FY2036 OM stuck at 12-14%, with profit 40-60 billion below the base case
- Trigger: Volcano Engine share exceeds 25% and takes away Alibaba Cloud's internet-customer base
- Counterexample: Alibaba Cloud's government and enterprise customer base is solid, and the Qwen ecosystem is feeding back

### Scenario 3: Geopolitical shock to overseas businesses
- Probability: 30-35%
- Profit impact: AIDC revenue -30%, AliExpress exits multiple markets, FY2036 profit 10-30 billion below the base case
- Trigger: China-US relations deteriorate sharply + the EU follows sanctions + Southeast Asia follows with data localization
- Counterexample: Alibaba's overseas main battlefield is non-US markets, and geopolitical risk exposure is lower than TikTok/Huawei

### Scenario 4: Long-term macro deflation in China + weak household consumption
- Probability: 30-35% (not optimistic; China's CPI stayed low in 2024-2025)
- Profit impact: e-commerce + Local Services + cloud all come under pressure, with FY2036 profit 80-150 billion below the base case
- This is **the most systemic risk** and is almost impossible to hedge
- Counterexample: China's GDP is large, and structural upgrading + the silver economy + overseas expansion still offer structural opportunities

### Scenario 5: Antitrust regulation / platform-economy governance returns
- Probability: 15-20% (the current regulatory cycle is relatively loose, but the political cycle could reverse in 5-10 years)
- Profit impact: take rate forced down, exclusivity banned, data sharing mandated, with FY2036 profit 40-80 billion below the base case
- Counterexample: Regulation shifted toward "promoting development" in 2023-2026, and Alibaba has largely completed compliance rectification

### Scenario 6: AI capex swallows profit while commercialization lags
- Probability: 30-35%
- Profit impact: Free cash flow falls sharply over the next 3-5 years, cumulative capex exceeds 400 billion before FY2030, but AI revenue grows slower than depreciation, with FY2036 profit 30-50 billion below the base case
- Trigger: Alibaba Cloud AI revenue CAGR <30%, but depreciation CAGR >40%
- Counterexample: In 2025, Alibaba AI-related cloud revenue already accounted for 25-30% of Alibaba Cloud and was growing triple digits

**Scenario probabilities are not independent** -- if Scenario 1+4 occur together (e-commerce erosion + macro deflation), FY2036 profit could be 150-250 billion below the base case.

## 7. Historical Analogies and Lessons

### Analogy 1: Microsoft 2014-2024 (successful transformation)
- Key actions: Nadella became CEO, all-in cloud, abandoned Mobile First for Cloud First, acquired LinkedIn/GitHub/Activision
- Result: market cap rose from 300 billion to 3 trillion, and cloud became the main engine
- Similarities to Alibaba: CEO change (Eddie Wu in 2023), all-in AI + cloud, sale of non-core assets (Sun Art Retail, Intime)
- Differences from Alibaba: (1) Microsoft had Office cash flow to support a 10-year transformation, while Alibaba's Taotian cash flow is being compressed by e-commerce competition; (2) Microsoft has a monopoly position in enterprise markets, while Alibaba Cloud's China share is only 33%

**Lesson for Alibaba**: The key to successful transformation is "old-business cash flow is stable enough + the new business produces a second curve within 5 years." 2026-2030 is Alibaba's key window.

### Analogy 2: IBM 2010-2024 (failed transformation)
- Key actions: bet on Watson AI, acquired Red Hat, spun off infrastructure
- Result: revenue went nowhere for 10 years, market cap lagged the market for a long time, and cloud share fell from 7% to 4%
- Reasons for failure: (1) old businesses (mainframe/services) declined too quickly and were not caught; (2) new business (Watson) execution was poor; (3) organizational culture did not refresh

**Lesson for Alibaba**: Beware of "having picked the right track (AI / cloud), but execution cannot keep up." Alibaba has a history of "leading in models but weak in product execution" (Fliggy, Xiami, Taobao Live starting later than Kuaishou/Douyin).

### Analogy 3: Tencent 2018-2024 (re-rating after Hong Kong market darkness)
- Darkest moment: 2021-2022 regulation + antitrust + game approvals + Hong Kong liquidity crisis; market cap fell from HK$7.5 trillion to HK$2 trillion
- Re-rating path: games stabilized + Channels rose + WeChat Pay/advertising recovered + overseas games (Sea / Supercell) gained traction + buybacks
- Result: 2024-2026 market cap returned to the HK$4-5 trillion range
- Very high similarity to Alibaba: both are Hong Kong-listed, both went through the regulatory cycle, and both rely on buybacks + stable core business to repair valuation

**Lesson for Alibaba**: Tencent's case shows that "even after the deepest headwinds, as long as the main-business cash flow of a Chinese super-platform company does not collapse, valuation will eventually repair." **This is the closest of the three analogies to Alibaba** -- but Tencent's "recovery" relied on the unexpected second curve of Channels. Alibaba's equivalent is "Alibaba Cloud + Qwen"; whether it can carry the re-rating banner is the core bet.

### Which Is Most Instructive?

**Tencent > Microsoft > IBM**. Tencent is the closest analogy because it shares China's regulatory and Hong Kong equity-market context. But the Microsoft analogy has the most direct reference value for whether "Alibaba Cloud can grow into Azure-scale." IBM is the negative mirror -- a warning that "choosing the right track does not equal success."
