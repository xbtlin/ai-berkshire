# 01 Business Model Analysis - Duan Yongping Perspective

> **Research Subject**: Alibaba Group (9988.HK / BABA)
> **As of**: FY26Q3 (2025-12-31)
> **Report Date**: 2026-05-04
> **Research Question**: Will Alibaba's business segments still be good businesses 10 years from now (FY2036)? Which segments will remain the core profit engines?

---

## 1. Core Judgment in One Sentence

**The data indicates** that Alibaba is likely still to be a good company 10 years from now, but the truly good businesses may be down to only **2.5 segments**: Alibaba Cloud (the core good business), Taobao and Tmall (half a good business, with pricing power eroded but moats still intact), and AIDC/AliExpress (a potential good business, but not yet proven). Local Services, Cainiao, Digital Media and Entertainment, and other physical retail assets do **not qualify as good businesses** under Duan Yongping's standard of "spending little and collecting a lot" - they are "necessary costs" Alibaba must bear for ecosystem defense, not profit machines. The group as a whole has been reset from an "all-purpose platform company" into a capital-intensive AI infrastructure company driven by the twin engines of "AI + cloud + e-commerce." **The nature of free cash flow is shifting from the "money-printing machine" of the past 10 years to the "capex black hole" of the next 5 years** (a 3-year RMB 380 billion capex plan has already been announced, and the CEO has hinted at an even larger plan).

## 3. Duan Yongping's "Will It Remain Unchanged for Ten Years?" Test (Segment by Segment)

### 3.1 Taobao and Tmall Group

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will users still need this business 10 years from now? | China's retail e-commerce penetration is ~30%, with another 5-10pct of upside; but consumers are now dispersed across Pinduoduo/Douyin/JD | **Yes**, but user demand is not "exclusive" to Taobao and Tmall |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | 88VIP nearly 60 million members (FY26Q3), and mindshare among higher-ticket consumers remains; but Pinduoduo owns the mid-to-low end, Douyin owns content-driven discovery, and JD owns self-operated 3C | **Partly**, with differentiation mainly in mid-to-high-end physical goods |
| Direction of pricing power (monetization rate)? | Taobao and Tmall take rate ~3.9% vs Pinduoduo ~7.6% (2024 basis); FY26Q3 CMR only +1%, limiting take-rate upside | **Weakening**, with price wars becoming normalized |
| What unique "unchanging things" does it have? | 1. Alipay-Taobao-Cainiao ecosystem loop; 2. merchant ERP data accumulated across hundreds of thousands of brands; 3. 88VIP mindshare | Merchant-side moat is the strongest |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: It has been downgraded from an "old Buffett-style franchise" to an "ordinary good business." It will still exist 10 years from now, but "spending little and collecting a lot" no longer holds - to defend share, **Taobao Flash Purchase spending and AI-driven reconstruction of the Taobao App will require endless investment**.

### 3.2 Cloud Intelligence Group (Alibaba Cloud)

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it 10 years from now? | China's cloud infrastructure market grew +26% YoY in 2025 Q4 (Omdia), driven by AI agents; global hyperscaler capex in 2026 exceeds USD 600+ billion | **Absolutely yes**, demand growth is unlikely to peak within 5-10 years |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Tongyi Qianwen/Qwen MAU reached 300 million (February 2026), No. 1 share in China's AI cloud market, now ahead of ByteDance/Huawei/Tencent; 2025 IaaS share 32.8% (vs 30.1% in 2024) | **Yes**, differentiation is strengthening |
| Pricing power? | AI inference demand is highly elastic and switching costs are high (data localization + API lock-in); but ByteDance's Volcano Engine is pressuring prices | Strong long-term pricing power, short-term price wars |
| Unique "unchanging things" | 1. China's only cloud provider with "foundation model + IaaS + downstream entry points (Taobao/DingTalk/Amap/Alipay)" at the same time; 2. compliance advantage for sensitive state-owned data; 3. physical decoupling from OpenAI/AWS makes it "irreplaceable" in China | Three-layer unique moat |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: **This is Alibaba's segment that most resembles a "good business"** - it has all four elements: user stickiness, pricing power, economies of scale, and compounding. But Duan Yongping would ask: will capex intensity (RMB 380 billion over 3 years) permanently eat into shareholder free cash flow? By analogy with AWS's path - cash burn in the first 10 years, cash printing in the next 10 - **the key variable is the ROIC inflection point around 2030**.

### 3.3 AIDC (International Digital Commerce)

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it 10 years from now? | Matching cross-border e-commerce / global small merchants with global consumers is a permanent need | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | AliExpress Choice unit economics are improving in Europe + Latin America; Trendyol leads in Turkey; but Lazada is fully pressured in Southeast Asia by Shopee/TikTok Shop; Temu (Pinduoduo) / TikTok Shop are forming strong resistance in Europe and the U.S. | **Uncertain**, regional markets are fragmented and lack a global network effect |
| Pricing power? | Fully managed models compress merchant margins and limit platform take rate; logistics is the key differentiator (after Cainiao integration) | Neutral |
| Unique "unchanging things" | The combination of Chinese manufacturing capacity + global fulfillment (Cainiao logistics) is rare outside Temu | Single moat |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: FY26Q3 loss narrowing 59% YoY is a positive signal, but **"narrowing losses" does not equal "good business"**. The key 10-year question is not whether Alibaba can become profitable (it probably can), but whether it can defend a clearly defined territory in the four-way battle among Lazada / Temu / Shopee / TikTok Shop. **This is a "watch for another 5 years" segment, not a proven good business**.

### 3.4 Local Services Group (Taobao Flash Purchase + Amap + Ele.me)

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it 10 years from now? | China's instant retail market is expected to reach USD 126.7 billion by 2029 (GlobeNewswire 2026.4); food delivery + instant retail are high-frequency necessities | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Meituan order share 51% vs Taobao Flash Purchase 42% (UBS 2026.2); short-term share gains depend on subsidies, while long-term success requires rider networks + merchant density | **Difficult** |
| Pricing power? | Duopoly price-war structure; Alibaba's 2026 strategy is explicitly to "go for No. 1 regardless of cost," implying sustained capex + OPEX | **Extremely weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things" | Integration of Amap LBS data + Taobao traffic + Alipay payment; this is a "low-cost traffic entrance" Meituan lacks | Traffic synergy advantage |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: A textbook "must-do defensive business." **Ten years from now, Alibaba will likely still rank second, while still losing money or barely profitable** (refer to Meituan's long-term take rate of ~6-7% and single-digit net margin). "Spending little and collecting a lot" absolutely does not hold, so **it should be treated as a defensive cost center rather than a profit source**.

### 3.5 Cainiao

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it 10 years from now? | Cross-border logistics is an absolute necessity | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Cross-border is its strength, while domestic is pressured by Tongda operators/JD Logistics; after the 2025 IPO withdrawal, it has been folded into AIDC | **Strong cross-border, weak domestic** |
| Pricing power? | Logistics is fundamentally a scale business, with 5-10% gross margin and no pricing power | **Weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things" | Alibaba e-commerce's internally generated orders + global overseas warehouse network | Moderate |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: Logistics is not a good business (low gross margins, capital-intensive, no pricing power); Duan Yongping rarely invests in logistics companies. But as AIDC's "fulfillment back office," it is necessary, and **should be viewed as AIDC's "cost department," not as an independent profit center**.

### 3.6 Digital Media and Entertainment (renamed Hujing Digital Media and Entertainment)

| Test Question | Data/Evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it 10 years from now? | Entertainment content is a permanent demand | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Youku ranks No. 3/4 in long-form video, while iQIYI/Tencent Video/Bilibili have stronger content ecosystems; Alibaba Pictures is shifting toward Damai Entertainment (performance ticketing) | **Difficult** |
| Pricing power? | Long-form video membership fees are severely commoditized | **Weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things" | Alibaba Pictures' film investment and distribution capability (some projects profitable); Damai's performance ticketing monopoly | Damai has a moat |

**Duan Yongping perspective**: Content is a classic "not good business" - hits depend on luck and scale is not economical. **FY25 still recorded a full-year loss of RMB 550 million**, and the FY25Q4 profit turnaround has uncertain durability. Ten years from now, it is likely to be further divested or reorganized.

### 3.7 Other (Freshippo, DingTalk, and Exited Physical Retail)

- **Intime and Sun Art were cleared out in 2024-2025** (RMB 22.5 billion loss on exit) -> from Duan Yongping's perspective, this is **the right admission of error**.
- **Freshippo**: Several rounds of spin-off/sale rumors have surfaced; retail is inherently a low-margin, asset-heavy business.
- **DingTalk**: ARR > USD 200 million (FY25 first half), China's SaaS leader; 700 million+ users, but monetization remains low. **DingTalk is a potential good business**, but it is too small relative to Alibaba Group (less than 0.5% of revenue).

## 6. Key Judgments

### 6.1 Which 1-2 segments will still be Alibaba's "profit core" 10 years from now?

**The evidence indicates Alibaba's 10-year profit core will be Alibaba Cloud (70% probability) + Taobao and Tmall (still the base business)**:

- **Alibaba Cloud**: FY26Q3 revenue RMB 43.284 billion, +36%, EBITA +25% - the **only segment within the group simultaneously delivering "high growth + profit expansion."** China's cloud market is expected to reach USD 600+ billion by 2030 (linear extrapolation at current growth rates). If Alibaba maintains a 30% share, that implies USD 180+ billion in revenue; at an AWS-like 25-30% EBITA margin, it could contribute USD 45-54 billion in EBITA - **more than the entire group's FY26 profit**.
- **Taobao and Tmall**: FY26Q3 CMR was only +1%, indicating growth has plateaued, but as a "cash cow" it can still contribute RMB 120-150 billion EBITA/year (based on FY25 scale), serving as Alibaba's cash-flow stabilizer.

### 6.2 Which segments will become "nice-to-have" or "loss black holes"?

- **Digital Media and Entertainment (Hujing Digital Media and Entertainment)**: Nice-to-have; likely spun off or separately listed within 10 years and removed from consolidated statements.
- **Freshippo**: Sale rumors have lingered for years, but strategic importance has declined; likely to be divested over the long term.
- **Local Services' "Taobao Flash Purchase"**: **A short-term loss black hole** (FY26Q3 spending dragged group EBITA), but strategically impossible to abandon; it is a "necessary loss."
- **Cainiao (standalone view)**: Downgraded from "independent IPO candidate" to "AIDC fulfillment department," with questionable independent profitability.

### 6.3 Under Duan Yongping's "spend little, collect a lot" standard, will Alibaba be a good company 10 years from now?

**Positive evidence**:
- Alibaba Cloud has AWS-like good-business characteristics (a four-layer moat of data, scale, technology, and ecosystem distribution)
- 88VIP approaching 60 million indicates stickiness among mid-to-high-end users is still strengthening
- Free cash flow remains positive (although eaten into by capex), and the balance sheet has ample cash
- Duan Yongping increased his Alibaba position in 2024 Q4 (H&H International), indicating his judgment that "the price already reflects most of the pessimism"
- Proactive divestiture of non-core assets (Intime, Sun Art) - management is moving toward "focus"

**Negative evidence**:
- "Spending little" absolutely does not hold: 3-year RMB 380 billion capex + Flash Purchase spending + AIDC still loss-making + AI foundation-model spending - **Alibaba over the next 3-5 years is a "spending company," not a "collecting company"**
- Taobao and Tmall take-rate upside has been blocked by Pinduoduo/Douyin, **weakening the core good business's pricing power**
- Duan Yongping himself has also said he is "less enthusiastic about Alibaba than Tencent" (based on business-model judgment)
- Multi-front operations (e-commerce, cloud, AI, local services, cross-border, logistics) violate Duan Yongping's preference for "focus"

**Overall score (Duan Yongping perspective)**:
- Current overall Alibaba business quality: **3-3.5 / 5**
- Overall Alibaba business quality 10 years from now: **3.5-4 / 5** (depending on whether Alibaba Cloud can deliver AWS-style profit release)

**Core judgment**: **Alibaba will be a good company 10 years from now, but only 2.5 of its businesses will still be good businesses**. Looking only at Alibaba Cloud, Duan Yongping would be willing to invest; but the investment case must be discounted for "capital dissipated in other segments to support Alibaba Cloud's capex." That is why Goldman Sachs uses a sum-of-the-parts valuation (cloud segment alone at $54/share, group target price $205) - the market has accepted that "the group is an SOTP story, not a holistic good business."

## 7. Key Uncertainties

1. **When will Alibaba Cloud's capex reach an ROIC inflection point?** Whether the computing capacity enabled by RMB 380 billion of investment can translate into commensurate profit scale in 2028-2030 is the largest single-point risk in the 10-year judgment. **Confidence: low** - the entire U.S. and China AI industry has not yet validated this.

2. **Will AI disrupt e-commerce search/recommendation?** Integrating Taobao into the Qwen App is Alibaba's offensive move, but if ChatGPT-like products (or ByteDance's Doubao) become next-generation shopping gateways, Taobao and Tmall's "owned traffic" moat could be bypassed. **Confidence: medium** - Qwen App retention after reaching 300 million MAU needs to be observed.

3. **When will Taobao Flash Purchase stop bleeding cash?** Alibaba's current strategy of "going for No. 1 regardless of cost" could cause Local Services to lose RMB 50-100 billion over 2-3 years (referencing Meituan/Pinduoduo's historical spending). **Confidence: medium-high** that spending will continue at least until 2027.

4. **AIDC overseas geopolitical risk**: Tightening European tariff/compliance rules for Temu/Shein/AliExpress (2024-2026 De Minimis rule changes) could materially alter Choice model economics. **Confidence: medium**.

5. **GMV growth curves for Pinduoduo/Douyin E-commerce**: Pinduoduo 2024 GMV RMB 5.1 trillion, Douyin RMB 4.3 trillion +30% - if the two together continue taking RMB 0.5-1 trillion of annual incremental GMV, Taobao and Tmall's take-rate upside will be further capped. **Confidence: high** (the trend is established).

6. **Management stability**: Eddie Wu's CEO tenure and strategic continuity (appointed 2023.9, now ~2.5 years); whether Joseph Tsai's SOTP-led restructuring path could be disrupted. **Confidence: medium** - management currently shows a firm "AI + e-commerce" dual-engine strategy.

7. **Shareholder return policy**: Alibaba continues buybacks + dividends while increasing capex (FY26 buyback scale vs capex balance); if continued capex increases lead to reduced buybacks, **the "value returned to shareholders" chain could be impaired from Duan Yongping's perspective**. **Confidence: medium**.
