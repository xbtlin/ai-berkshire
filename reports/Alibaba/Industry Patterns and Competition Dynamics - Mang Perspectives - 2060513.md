## I. China's E-Commerce Industry Overview: Scale and Structure

### 1.1 Market Size

China's online retail sales reached RMB 15.5 trillion in 2024, up 7.2% year over year, keeping China as the world's largest online retail market for the 12th consecutive year. Online retail sales of physical goods were RMB 13.08 trillion, up 6.5%.

**Key judgment:** Growth in China's overall e-commerce market has shifted from high-speed expansion, with double-digit growth in 2015-2020, to mid- to low-single-digit growth of 6-8%. This means the industry has entered an era of stock competition, where share gains increasingly become a zero-sum game.

### 1.2 GMV and Market Share by Platform (2024)

| Platform | 2024 GMV (trillions) | Market share | YoY growth |
|------|-------------------|----------|----------|
| Taotian Group | ~8.0 | ~37.3% | ~6% |
| Pinduoduo | ~5.2 | ~21.0% | ~20%+ |
| JD.com | ~4.5 | ~17.4% | ~13% |
| Douyin E-commerce | ~3.5 | ~13%+ | ~30% |
| Kuaishou E-commerce | ~1.39 | ~5%+ | ~17% |

**Note:** Market share figures vary by methodology. Tmall's share is 32.7% on a B2C basis, while Taotian overall, including C2C, is approximately 37.3% on a full-scope basis. The figures above use the full-scope basis.

## II. Evolution of the Competitive Landscape: Share Migration Map, 2021-2025

### 2.1 How Much Has Alibaba Lost?

| Year | Alibaba share | Pinduoduo share | Douyin + Kuaishou share | JD.com share |
|------|----------|------------|---------------|----------|
| 2021 | 51.3% | 15.9% | 9.2% | ~20% |
| 2022 | ~46% | ~18% | ~14% | ~18% |
| 2023 | 39.2% | ~20% | ~19.3% | ~17% |
| 2024 | 37.3% | ~21% | ~21.4% | ~17.4% |
| 2026E | ~36.2% | ~22% | ~22.7% | ~16% |

**Over four years, Alibaba's share fell from 51.3% to 37.3%, a loss of roughly 14 percentage points.**

### 2.2 Who Took the Share?

The share migration is very clear:

- **Douyin + Kuaishou:** Their share rose from 9.2% to 21.4%, an increase of roughly 12 percentage points, making them the largest "raiders." Live-streaming and short-video e-commerce absorbed Alibaba's lost share almost one-for-one.
- **Pinduoduo:** Its share rose from 15.9% to 21%, an increase of roughly 5 percentage points, mainly eroding Alibaba's lower-tier-city and price-sensitive user base.
- **JD.com:** Its share was broadly flat to slightly down. JD.com was not the winner of the share war, but it also did not bleed as heavily as Alibaba.

### 2.3 Key Turning Points

- **2024:** Douyin E-commerce entered the top three for the first time, while JD.com fell out of the top three by GMV for the first time under some methodologies.
- **Based on current growth rates:** Douyin E-commerce GMV may catch up with Taotian in 2028-2029, an extremely disruptive timetable.

## III. Analysis of Core Competitors

### 3.1 Pinduoduo: The Low-Price King + Temu's Overseas Growth Engine

**Domestic e-commerce:**
- 2024 GMV of approximately RMB 5.2 trillion, firmly ranking second
- Its core weapon is "extreme low price." This is not a simple price war, but structurally low pricing achieved through the C2M model, which connects consumers directly with factories
- The RMB 10 billion subsidy program has evolved from a marketing tool into a normalized operating mechanism
- More than 900 million users, with extremely high penetration

**Temu overseas:**
- As of 2025, Temu had 530 million global monthly active users and cumulative downloads exceeding 1.2 billion
- Operations covered 86+ countries/regions, with a 6.7% share of the U.S. e-commerce market
- In 2025, revenue contribution is expected to exceed 50% of Pinduoduo Group
- It has innovated with a dual "fully managed + semi-managed" model, balancing scale with merchant autonomy
- In Poland, it has already surpassed local giant Allegro to rank first

**Threat level to Alibaba: Very high**
- Domestic: Pinduoduo continues to suppress Taotian's position in the low-price mindset. In users' purchase decision journey, "check prices on Pinduoduo first" has become a habit
- Overseas: Temu directly competes with AliExpress and is growing much faster
- The question Munger would ask: "Is Pinduoduo's low-price advantage structural or cyclical?" The answer is structural, driven by the C2M model plus lower operating costs. That makes it difficult for Alibaba to fight back through simple cash burn

### 3.2 Douyin E-commerce: The Biggest Disruptor

**Scale and growth:**
- 2024 GMV of approximately RMB 3.5 trillion, up 30% year over year
- 2025 target of RMB 4.2-4.3 trillion, with growth still at 25-30%
- If the gap narrows by roughly RMB 900 billion per year, it could catch up with Taotian within 3-4 years

**Why it is disruptive:**
1. **Structural advantage in traffic cost:** Douyin has 800 million DAUs, with nearly "free customer acquisition." Taobao has 300-400 million DAUs and must keep spending on traffic acquisition. This cost gap of more than 10x is not merely a competitive advantage; it is a crushing advantage at the business-model level.
2. **Dual-wheel drive from content e-commerce to shelf e-commerce:** In 2024, shelf-scenario GMV rose to 40% of total GMV, up from 30% in 2023. This shows Douyin is no longer just an "impulse purchase" platform. It is invading Taobao's core territory: active search-based shopping.
3. **Brand launch ground is shifting:** More brands are using Douyin as the core venue for new product launches and official brand announcements, while traditional e-commerce platforms are increasingly becoming "distribution shelves."
4. **Rise of merchant live-streaming:** In 2024, merchant live-streaming accounted for more than 30% of GMV, and the number of merchants rose 113% in one year. This means Douyin E-commerce is shifting away from dependence on top influencers toward a healthier merchant-operated ecosystem.

**Threat level to Alibaba: Very high (highest)**
- This is a fundamental challenge to Taotian's business model. When users' attention and time are on short-video platforms, the starting point for shopping decisions changes from "open Taobao and search" to "scroll Douyin and get inspired"
- Munger would say: "When the consumer's shopping entry point migrates irreversibly, the value of the old entry point is bound to be diluted"

### 3.3 JD.com: Differentiated Barriers from First-Party Retail + Logistics

**Core data:**
- Full-year 2024 revenue of RMB 1,158.8 billion, with 2024 GMV of approximately RMB 4.5 trillion
- 2025 Q1 revenue of RMB 301.1 billion, up 15.8% year over year, the highest growth rate in nearly three years
- JD Logistics 2024 revenue of RMB 182.8 billion, with a 4.3% profit margin, the best level since listing

**Differentiated competitiveness:**
- **First-party retail + self-built logistics model:** 1,600+ warehouses and 2,000+ cloud warehouses, covering all counties and districts nationwide, with fulfillment speed in core cities improved to 4 hours
- **Instant retail (JD Seconds Delivery):** Covers 2,300+ counties, districts, and cities nationwide, partners with more than 500,000 stores, and can deliver in as fast as 9 minutes
- **Food delivery business:** Daily average orders are expected to exceed 20 million, with more than 1 million merchant storefronts onboarded, competing through differentiated "quality food delivery + rider rights protection"
- **3C category barrier:** User mindshare remains solid in the 3C digital category

**Threat level to Alibaba: Medium**
- Competition between JD.com and Alibaba is more differentiated than homogeneous. JD.com's user base, which values quality and service, differs from the users being fought over by Pinduoduo and Douyin
- However, JD.com's expansion in instant retail directly pressures Alibaba's Ele.me and flash-shopping businesses

### 3.4 Kuaishou: Gatekeeper of the Lower-Tier Market

**Core data:**
- Full-year 2024 e-commerce GMV of RMB 1.39 trillion, up 17.3% year over year
- Lower-tier market penetration of 67%, far above Douyin's 52% and Taobao's 48%
- Full-year 2024 revenue of RMB 126.9 billion

**Competitive characteristics:**
- It has a unique trust-based e-commerce model in third-, fourth-, and fifth-tier cities, the "laotie economy"
- Growth has clearly slowed, with 2025 Q2 GMV growth falling to 15%, creating a growth bottleneck
- Its scale is only 1/6 of Taotian's, so the direct threat to Alibaba is limited

**Threat level to Alibaba: Low to medium**
- Kuaishou mainly competes with Pinduoduo in lower-tier markets, and its direct impact on Alibaba is smaller than that of Pinduoduo and Douyin
- But Kuaishou further fragments e-commerce traffic, making it harder for Alibaba to rebuild market dominance

## IV. Cloud Computing Industry: Alibaba Cloud's Moat

### 4.1 Market Share (2025)

| Vendor | IaaS market share (2025Q2) | AI cloud market share (2025H1) |
|------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Alibaba Cloud | 26.8% (first) | 35.8% (first) |
| Huawei Cloud | 12.9% (second) | 13.1% (third) |
| Tianyi Cloud | 12.3% (third) | - |
| Volcano Engine (ByteDance) | - | 14.8% (second) |
| China Mobile Cloud | 9.4% (fourth) | - |
| Tencent Cloud | 7.9% (fifth) | 7.0% (fourth) |
| Baidu AI Cloud | - | 6.1% (fifth) |

### 4.2 Competitive Landscape Analysis

**Alibaba Cloud's advantages:**
- IaaS market share of 26.8%, growing for five consecutive quarters, with a solid lead
- AI cloud market share of 35.8%, far ahead of the second-place Volcano Engine at 14.8%, a huge lead
- Alibaba Cloud held 33% of China's cloud infrastructure market in 2025 Q1

**Major competitors:**
- **Huawei Cloud:** Ranks first in hybrid cloud, ahead of Alibaba, and has unique advantages in the government and enterprise market, but trails Alibaba in public-cloud IaaS
- **Telecom operator clouds (Tianyi/China Mobile/China Unicom):** Their combined share has already exceeded 30%. With government-enterprise relationships and infrastructure advantages, they are rising quickly and cannot be ignored
- **Volcano Engine (ByteDance):** Has emerged rapidly in the AI cloud market with a 14.8% share. It ranks first in the large-model API market with 46.4% share, driven by Doubao, making it Alibaba Cloud's biggest challenger in the AI era
- **Tencent Cloud:** Its share has declined somewhat, and its AI cloud share is only 7%, indicating weaker competitiveness

**Munger perspective:** Cloud computing is one of Alibaba's few businesses where the "moat is deepening." The AI large-model wave has driven explosive demand for compute. As China's largest public-cloud platform, Alibaba Cloud is benefiting from this tailwind. But Volcano Engine, backed by ByteDance's ecosystem, including the Doubao large model and Douyin application scenarios, is catching up rapidly. Alibaba Cloud's lead is not unassailable.

## V. AI Large-Model Competition: Tongyi Qianwen's Position

### 5.1 Competitive Landscape

**Consumer users (monthly active users in February 2025):**
- DeepSeek: 180 million
- Doubao (ByteDance): 101 million
- Tencent Yuanbao: 26.36 million
- Kimi: 24.51 million
- Tongyi Qianwen App: Not in the top five

**Enterprise API call market (June 2025):**
- Doubao: 46.4%
- Alibaba Cloud (Tongyi series): 27% (ranked first by average daily call volume, but second by market share)
- Baidu: 17%

**Open-source ecosystem:**
- Tongyi Qianwen has exceeded 600 million global downloads, open-sourced 300+ models, and produced more than 170,000 derivative models, ranking first among global open-source models
- ModelScope community: 16 million+ users and 81,800 models
- Bailian platform: Serves more than 980,000 enterprises and developers

### 5.2 Key Judgments

Tongyi Qianwen shows a pattern of "strong on the enterprise side, weak on the consumer side" in AI large models:
- **Enterprise/open source:** It has the world's leading open-source ecosystem and strong enterprise API call volume. This creates excellent synergy with Alibaba Cloud: "models bring traffic, cloud monetizes it"
- **Consumer side:** In consumer-facing AI applications, it is far behind DeepSeek and Doubao, with insufficient user mindshare
- **Technical level:** Qwen2.5-Max ranked 7th on the LMArena leaderboard, ahead of DeepSeek-V3, placing its technical capabilities in the top tier

**Munger perspective:** Alibaba's strategic choice in AI large models is correct: pursue open source and use models to drive cloud-computing revenue. This resembles Munger's "fishing" strategy: the free model is the bait, and paid compute is the hook. But the absence of a strong consumer AI application is a hidden concern. If users are using DeepSeek and Doubao, the inference compute behind those applications may flow to ByteDance's Volcano Engine rather than Alibaba Cloud.

## VI. International E-Commerce: AliExpress/Lazada/Trendyol vs Temu/Shein/TikTok Shop

### 6.1 Competitive Landscape

**Comparison of the "four little dragons" of Chinese cross-border e-commerce (2024-2025):**

| Platform | Monthly active users | Covered markets | Core model | Growth |
|------|-----------|----------|----------|------|
| Temu | 530 million | 86+ countries | Fully managed + semi-managed | Very high |
| AliExpress | 422 million | 200+ countries | Fully managed + first-party retail | Medium |
| Shein | 170 million | 150+ countries | Owned brand + marketplace | Slowing |
| TikTok Shop | Rapidly growing | Expanding | Content e-commerce | High |

### 6.2 Analysis of Alibaba's International E-Commerce

- International retail revenue grew 19% year over year in fiscal 2025, approaching breakeven
- AliExpress was overtaken by Temu at the end of 2023, with 422 million independent visitors versus 467 million
- In Southeast Asia, Lazada faces a dual squeeze from Shopee and TikTok Shop
- Chinese e-commerce platforms have collectively taken roughly 50% of online retail share in Southeast Asia's core markets

### 6.3 Key Judgments

The situation for Alibaba's international e-commerce business is mixed:
- **Concern:** With more extreme low prices and faster expansion, Temu has surpassed AliExpress in multiple markets. Although Shein's growth has slowed, with 2024 H1 revenue growth falling from 40% to 23%, it still has brand power. TikTok Shop is rising quickly in Southeast Asia and directly pressuring Lazada.
- **Positive:** Alibaba's overall international business revenue grew 19% and is approaching breakeven, suggesting the largest loss-making phase may have passed. AliExpress still has a meaningful user base in mature markets such as Europe.

## VII. Industry Trends: Structural Variables

### 7.1 AI's Impact on E-Commerce

- AI large models have deeply penetrated the e-commerce value chain: 78% of live-streaming e-commerce applications use generative AI to optimize operations
- AI is reshaping shopping entry points, from "search-based shopping" to "AI recommendation-based shopping." This is a long-term threat to Taobao's core advantage in "search"
- In 2024, China's brand e-commerce services industry reached RMB 446.85 billion in market size, and AI technology has moved from an auxiliary tool to a core driver
- Alibaba has a first-mover advantage in AI e-commerce applications, through Tongyi Qianwen plus e-commerce scenario data, but Douyin's recommendation algorithm is also powerful

### 7.2 Regulatory Environment Changes

- The antitrust "stick" has largely landed, and policy pressure on Alibaba is easing
- The State Council's Regulations on Network Data Security Management have taken effect, strengthening data security management
- ESG indicators have been incorporated into platform assessment systems. Large platforms such as Alibaba and JD.com have compliance advantages
- Cross-border e-commerce regulation is tightening, especially U.S. scrutiny of Temu and Shein, which may indirectly benefit Alibaba's more compliance-oriented layout

### 7.3 Consumption Trends: K-Shaped Divergence

- The consumer market shows clear K-shaped divergence: basic consumption pursues extreme value for money, while emotional consumption is willing to pay high premiums
- Nearly 60% of young people are willing to pay for "emotional value"
- "Spend little money to get big things done" has become a broad consensus. This benefits Pinduoduo and Douyin, but hurts Tmall, where brand premiums are being compressed
- Live-streaming e-commerce has nearly 600 million users, with penetration jumping from 4.9% in 2019 to 37.8% in 2024

## VIII. Ultimate Judgment from a Munger Perspective: Is the Moat Repairing or Still Eroding?

### 8.1 First, Answer a More Fundamental Question: What Exactly Is Alibaba's Moat?

When Munger analyzes moats, he usually looks at four dimensions:

1. **Brand mindshare:** Taobao/Tmall's brand mindshare is being diluted. The default consumer behavior of "go to Taobao first when shopping" has been broken. Now it is: "go to Pinduoduo for cheap products, browse Douyin for video-led shopping, go to JD.com for 3C, and maybe go to Tmall for brands."
2. **Network effects:** Alibaba's strongest moat, more buyers attract sellers and more sellers attract buyers, is weakening. Merchants are operating across multiple platforms, and consumers are comparing prices across multiple platforms. But the 88VIP membership system, with more than 5.6 million members, is rebuilding user stickiness.
3. **Economies of scale:** Alibaba still has scale advantages, with RMB 8 trillion in GMV, but marginal returns are declining. By contrast, Pinduoduo has achieved higher margins with lower operating costs.
4. **Switching costs:** Switching costs for e-commerce platforms are inherently low. A user can download a new app in 30 seconds. But Alibaba is raising switching costs through 88VIP, Taobao Flash Shopping, the Alipay ecosystem, and other mechanisms.

### 8.2 Judgment by Business

| Business | Moat trend | Rationale |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Domestic e-commerce (Taotian) | Erosion slowing, partial repair | The pace of share loss has slowed from -4% per year to -1% per year, 88VIP users are growing at a double-digit rate, and take rate is improving, but the absolute share level is still falling |
| Cloud computing (Alibaba Cloud) | Clear repair/deepening | AI cloud market share of 35.8% is far ahead; IaaS has grown for five consecutive quarters; AI large models are driving compute demand and creating a positive feedback loop |
| AI large models (Tongyi Qianwen) | Under construction, high potential | The open-source ecosystem ranks first globally, but consumer applications lag DeepSeek/Doubao; enterprise-side synergy with cloud computing is the right path |
| International e-commerce | Bleeding is being stanched | Revenue grew 19% and the business is approaching breakeven, but it faces fierce competition from Temu/TikTok Shop |
| Instant retail (Flash Shopping) | New moat being built | Daily order peak reached 120 million, market share has caught up with Meituan (45.2% vs 45.0%), but the investment is enormous, including RMB 50 billion in subsidies |

### 8.3 Ultimate Conclusion

**Alibaba's competitive position is undergoing "divergent repair." It is not a comprehensive recovery, nor is it continuing to deteriorate across the board. Instead:**

**Areas improving:**
- Cloud computing/AI: This is where Alibaba's strongest moat sits, and it is deepening further in the AI era
- Instant retail: Taobao Flash Shopping has nearly caught up with Meituan, opening a new growth space
- Monetization rate: Take rate continues to improve, allowing the same GMV to generate more revenue
- 88VIP membership system: Lock-in of high-value users is strengthening

**Areas still deteriorating:**
- The absolute value of e-commerce market share is still falling, although the pace of decline is slowing
- Alibaba is at a disadvantage in the battle over the "shopping entry point" in content e-commerce/live-streaming e-commerce, as Douyin is reshaping consumers' shopping journey
- The low-price mindset has been firmly captured by Pinduoduo and is hard to reclaim
- Consumer AI application, namely the Tongyi Qianwen App, lacks user mindshare

**Munger-style summary:**

> "Alibaba is no longer the unshakable giant that monopolized Chinese e-commerce. The quasi-monopoly position represented by its former 51% market share is gone for good. But it is also not a company that is collapsing. It is evolving from an 'e-commerce platform company' into a complex of 'AI + cloud computing + e-commerce + instant retail.' The question is whether the e-commerce share lost during this evolution and the hundreds of billions in subsidies invested can be offset by the value created by new businesses, including cloud computing, AI, and flash shopping."

> "I would pay particular attention to three signals: (1) whether Taotian's market share decline has truly stopped, not merely slowed; (2) whether Alibaba Cloud's AI revenue growth can remain ahead, as Volcano Engine is closing in quickly; and (3) whether Taobao Flash Shopping's RMB 50 billion investment has built a new moat or merely filled a bottomless pit."

### 8.4 Market Consensus vs My Challenge

| Market consensus | My challenge |
|----------|----------|
| "The decline in Alibaba's e-commerce share has bottomed" | A slower rate of share loss is not the same as a bottom. Douyin E-commerce is still expanding at 30% growth and may catch up with Taotian GMV in 2028. Alibaba's e-commerce share may continue to drift lower |
| "Alibaba Cloud is a deterministic growth engine" | I broadly agree, but the rise of Volcano Engine must be watched. Doubao's large-model API market share has already reached 46.4%. If inference compute demand migrates with it, Alibaba Cloud's lead may be eroded |
| "Taobao Flash Shopping is the second growth curve" | Flash Shopping has indeed caught up with Meituan in share, but the sustainability of RMB 50 billion in subsidies is questionable. Meituan lost money on food delivery for ten years before building a barrier. Does Alibaba have that patience and those resources? |
| "AI will reshape Alibaba's competitiveness" | AI helps Alibaba more on the enterprise side, through cloud computing and enterprise services, while its improvement to consumer-side experience is limited. The force truly reshaping the shopping experience may be Douyin's recommendation algorithm, not Alibaba's AI |
