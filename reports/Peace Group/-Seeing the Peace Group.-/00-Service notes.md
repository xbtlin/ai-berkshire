# Series Guide for "Understanding Ping An Group"

> 4 main articles that break down China's largest integrated financial group layer by layer and reveal what it really is.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 60 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Ping An Group is one of the most contradictory names in China's capital market.

- The bulls say: PEV **0.65x**, NBV growing 30% for two consecutive years, dividend yield **4.8%**, and 14 consecutive years of dividend growth - absurdly cheap
- The bears say: long-term interest-rate declines inevitably hurt spreads, the RMB 54 billion Hanjiahuifeng exposure is still not fully digested, and Ma Mingzhe's successor at age 68 is unresolved

**Both sides are reading the same annual report, but they reach completely opposite conclusions.**

The complexity is that Ping An is an insurer (exposed to rate cycles), a bank (exposed to asset-quality cycles), a technology company (exposed to investment-period losses), and a healthcare / eldercare platform - all at once. No single simple label can describe it.

This series aims to **first break the structure apart and then let you decide whether it is worth buying** - not to hand you the conclusion.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - insurance-company financials are extremely complex, so estimates are marked as "estimated" and actuarial assumptions are sourced
4. **Do not provide an exact target price** - three-scenario valuation is shown as a range only, not false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
