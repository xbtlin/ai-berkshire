# Risk two: profit spread -- "Gree rhinoceros" in insurance industry

This is the greatest systemic risk in the insurance industry today and deserves clarification.

What's the profit margin?

Simply put: Insurance companies commit to a rate of return (predicted interest rate) when they sell the policy, and if the return on investment is consistently below the promised rate of return, the insurance company pays. This is "breathing loss".

The Japanese insurance industry experienced a tragic spread crisis in the 1990s - interest rates fell sharply from 6 per cent to 0 per cent, and many insurance companies went bankrupt.
