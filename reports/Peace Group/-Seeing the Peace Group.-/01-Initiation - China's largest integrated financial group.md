# Three common illusions about peace

# I'm not sure: Peace is insurance

This is the most common cognitive bias.

Peace does begin with insurance, but today's peace is a ** full-licensed financial group** — with insurance, banks, securities, trusts, and fund plates. This is almost unique in China's financial sector.

More importantly, the safe business model is not "sell insurance for premiums", but:

** First step:** Obtaining clients through insurance agents or silver insurance channels (251 million individual customers)
** Step 2:** Making bank accounts open, buying money, using safe doctors through cross-selling
** Step 3:** The more products the customers hold, the higher the retention rate (client retention rate for products of category 3 or more **99%**)
** Step 4:** Internal passenger cost is lower than external **35-45%**

This "customer migration" route is the safest moat — not insurance per se, but integrated financial ecology.

** Reverse view:** Cross-sales are good, but in reality the rate of conversion of bank customers to insurance is not high. How many retail customers of Peacebanks overlap with customers of Life Insurance? The annual report has never been detailed. And excessive cross-sales can raise regulatory concerns — data access between subsidiaries may be limited by tightening data privacy regulations.

# Imagination II: Inline value 1.5 trillion = Peace value 1.5 trillion

The value in the package (EV) is the insurance company's "net asset enhancement version" -- it is equivalent to adjusted net assets plus valid business values.

The current market value is about $1.05 trillion**, corresponding to PEV about **0.65 times**. It looks like "six-fifty for a dollar of assets."

However, the EV has a fatal premise:** it relies on actuarial assumptions, particularly the return on long-term investments.**

Safe 2025 To downgrade the EV long-term return assumption from 5% to **4.0%-4.5%**. If the real return on investment is lower in the long run than that assumption, such as if interest rates continue to fall, and the return on solid assets drops to below 3%, the EV will "shrunk."

In other words: **EV is not a bank deposit, it is an assumed estimate. ** 0.65 times PEV looks cheap, but if there is a false assumption, the real EV may be 20-30% lower than the book.

** Anti-negative:** Peace has reduced the assumption from 5% to 4.0% to 4.5%, the most conservative level in recent years. 2025 real combined investment return **6.3%**, far above the assumed assumption. If 4%, 6.3%, the EV is ** undervalued.

2021 Happiness Thunder, which is a safe step of about **54 billion. This is the worst investment failure in the history of peace, leading directly to a 29% drop in net profit from the return to the mother in 2021.

Many investors have therefore labeled peace as "poor investment capacity".

But the truth is:

** Happiness was a serious miscalculation, but one failure could not define an investment team that managed $6.49 trillion in funds.**

** Inverse:** Happiness in summer reveals not just a bad investment, but a risk of over-concentration in real estate. The 54 billion loss suggests that internal wind control is failing at a critical time. Although the exposure is dropping, 3.1% multiplied by the 649 trillion dollar still has a real estate investment balance of about **200 billion** — which is not entirely safe if the real estate market hits a second round.
