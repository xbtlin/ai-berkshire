** From 1.2 million to 350,000, the number of agents decreased by 70 per cent. But the NBV (369 billion) in 2025 almost doubled the bottom of the valley (21.1 billion) in 2023.**

What does that mean?

Per capita output can rise at a very rapid rate. The annual growth in the annual NBV per agent in 2025 was **17.2%**, which is a typical route for "mass-downs", eliminating unqualified agents, leaving behind clients who sell higher-value products and serve higher net value.

** Counterpart:** The number of agents is still declining (year-on-year 2025 - 3.3%), which means that the "hectare" has not stopped completely. Can the per capita productivity increase be sustained? If there is another loss of 350,000 people left behind, NBV growth may be stagnant. And the increase in agents has not yet been restored, which means that the life risk "blood-building" remains questionable.

# Silver: Biggest surprise in 2025

** Why did the silverware break out?**

1. ** Downline interest rate + maturity of high interest asset:** Bank finance returns fell by 3%, clients needed new "stable returns" products after large deposits expired and the risk of red risk became an alternative
2. **The channel advantage of the Safe Bank:** Peace is the only financial group with large banks and large insurance companies, and the natural advantage of the silver bond is the natural advantage
3. ** Product transformational alignment:** Safely using the Red Treblink as a core flagship from 2025-2026, with premium size exceeding **40% **

Source: Open Source Securities Research, New Wave Finance, Watchers Network

** Inverse:** The high growth of the silver insurance channel is partly dependent on the "depositary move-in effect of the downside interest rate". This effect is one-off -- when deposit rates fall low enough, the move-in effect will diminish. And the NBV profit rate of the silver insurance channel is usually lower than the agent channel, and the high rate of increase may be at the expense of the "value content".

** Core issues:** 81.8 billion large model calls, 650 scenes - these figures are good, but how much is the contribution to group profits? When can the investment in technology translate into measurable profit improvements?

This logic makes sense, but it's not quantifiable -- you never know how much technology is contributing to 96.8 percent of CO.

# The medical owner piece

The safe "Medical Old Age" strategy is a long-term story:

- ** Safe Doctor:** Build "Go to the line, go to the shop, go home, go to the end" to the service network
- ** Big North Medical Group:** Self-employed medical resources
- ** Resting community:** high-quality old-age community, tied to high-end life risk products
- ** External cooperation:** More than 10,000 medical institutions

The logic is that China's ageing accelerated (over 65 years of age and over **15%**), and that it was safely bound through "insurance + medical old-age services" to increase the stickyness and retention rates of the insurance products.

** Inverse:** Long losses since the safe doctor was listed, billions of dollars are needed for community investment, and the profit model is not clear. If profit-truding points are not seen in the next 5 years, the market will not price the story.
