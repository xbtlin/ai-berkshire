# What is the market worried about? # # The decomposition of the value discounted.

Peaceful PEV discount from three floors:

# First tier: discounted at interest rate

This is the most central discount factor.

China 10 years of return on public debt from **3.6%** in 2018 to approximately **1.7%** in 2026. For insurance companies, this means:

1. **The return on additional solidized assets has been declining steadily:** Peace is provided for a large portion of the $6.49 trillion in insurance funds, which are allocated to bonds and deposits. After the expiry of old assets, the return on new assets has become increasingly low.
2. ** Net investment return pressure: ** net investment return in 2025 ** 3.8 per cent**, a decrease of 0.4 percentage points from the same year.
3. **Little spread risk:** If the return on investment is consistently below the policy 's stated rate of return, the insurance company is "foresee insurance".

** Peace Management:** "There is no loss on the spread in the new list or stock business." They are based on the shift in product structure to a bonus (the lower premium rate is 1.25%-1.75%**), and the increase in the ratio of death and expense benefits reduces reliance on the spread.

** Market doubts:** Management says there is no spread, but the market is not convinced. The current PEV 0.65 implied return on investment assumption is only about **1.3 - 1.7% ** - that is, the market assumes only 1.7% return on investments at the time of pricing. 2025 real combined investment return was **6.3%**.

** If you believe that peace will make it more than 4% a long time, then 0.65 times PEV is clearly understated. If you think interest rates will be close to zero in the long term, as Japan does, the current valuation may still be cheap.**

# The essence of market differences

The market's disagreement over peace is essentially a problem, when all the above analyses are aggregated:

> ** Will the interest rate environment in China be "Janjaized"?**

** If not (interest rate 2-3% steady):**
- The safe return on investment is maintained at 4% or more.
- EV assumption (4.0-4.5%) is conservative, real EV is underestimated.
- 0.65 times PEV seriously underestimated, reasonable PEV should be 0.8-1.0 times
- plus 4.8% dividends and NBV growth, with annualized returns of **15-20%**

** If so (interest rate closes to 0-1 per cent):**
- The safe return on investment could be 2-3%.
- The return assumption in EV (4.0%) may be too high, and the real EV will need a discount 20-30%
- The stock policy spread problem may change from "non-existent" to "exactly present".
- 0.65 times PEV may not be cheap, the Japanese Insurance Unit is standing at 0.3-0.5 times PEV transactions

** This is a macro issue, not a corporate one.** If you have confidence in China’s macroeconomics (GDP growth is 4-5%, inflation is back around 2%), the peace margin is underestimated. If you think China is on the same road as Japan, then all Chinese insurance shares should not be bought, not just safe.
