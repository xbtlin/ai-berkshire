The CVM formally implemented the Pilot Scheme for the Administration of Securities and Listings Abroad of Enterprises in China and accompanying guidelines to replace the original approval system with a filing system, effective 31 March 2023.

- ** Time frame for review**: full and complete clearance and disclosure by the CVM within 20 working days; incomplete and 5 days for notification of correction, which the enterprise is required to submit within 30 days
