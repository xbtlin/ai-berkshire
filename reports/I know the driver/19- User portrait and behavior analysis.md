#3.1 "Look-Select- Buy" 3 stage model

The driver decomposed the decision on the purchase of a vehicle into three stages:

# # Phase 1: "Look" (I'm interested)
- ** Behavioural characteristics**: browse information streams, watch short videos and full vehicle measurements
- ** Content preferences**: full vehicle assessment in video form, new vehicle release, test driving experience
- ** Knows the role of the driver**: To reach potential users of interest by algorithms that recommend and shake the flow

# # Phase 2: "Select" (intentional direction)
- ** Behavioural characteristics**: entering garages to see detailed parameters, comparing car types, reading slogans
- ** Decision-making factor**: class and price of vehicle are the primary considerations (over 70 per cent of the average)
- ** Most commonly used functions**: garage details, atlases, list of money
- ** Know the role of the driver**: provision of structured vehicle data and horizontal comparison tools
