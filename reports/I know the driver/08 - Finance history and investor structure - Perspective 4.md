# VIII. Core conclusions and investment recommendations

# 8.1 Key findings

1. ** Finance history is extremely simple**: Knows that the driver has only been in the round of external financing for seven years, and that the A-round investor is all byte-dancing old shareholders. This means that the byte-dancing growth of the driver is entirely dependent on the byte-dancing internal resources, and that external capital is more geared towards preparing for IPOs and providing exit routes for the old shareholders.

** bytes beat actual controls**: Although the business level is independent, bytes beats remain in physical control through the VIE structure and equity pledge.

3. **The valuation is rapidly expanding**: from the A round of $3 billion to the IPO target of $5-7 billion (supprising), the valuation may double in less than two years. It requires attention to whether specific financial data at the time of the IPO support this valuation.

4. ** Low transparency**: As an unlisted company, key information such as financial data (collecting, profit, cash flow), employee options, specific equity ratios, etc., that understand the driver ' s financial data, is not made public.

# 8.2 Rational assessment of Pre-IPO investments

** Accomplishments:**
- Bytes is the first IPO.
- Strong user growth and large market space for automobiles
- endorsement by top-level underwriters and investment agencies

**Risk:**
- Valuation may be fully priced, and post-IPO uplift space depends on market sentiment
- Core competitiveness (flow) is highly dependent on byte ecology and post-independence sustainability is questionable
- Limited vertical media cavity ceilings (reference car house with long-term market values of $3-4 billion)
- The Pre-IPO phase is highly asymmetrical and the information disadvantage of the bulk investors

** Recommendation:** The most pragmatic way to engage with individual investors is to wait for open subscription from the Ports Unit IPO, rather than to pursue a high threshold channel for Pre-IPO stages. The information asymmetric and liquidity risks from Pre-IPO channels are much greater than the potential additional benefits.
