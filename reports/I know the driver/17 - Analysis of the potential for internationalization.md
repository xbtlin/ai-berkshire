** Final judgement**: Internationalization is an understanding of the driver's growth dimension of "potential but far from being realized". Current investment decisions should not be based primarily on internationalization, but should be kept focused on the signal in this direction. If we understand that the driver has no movement abroad in the year after IPO, and that the autohouse's overseas edition continues to expand, it would be a negative sign – that the driver either has no intention of internationalizing or has no ability to go out of the sea independently after he has left the byte system.

"Never pay a premium for a thing that you might do." And when it starts, it does the grades." -- a time-honoured conservative judgment.
