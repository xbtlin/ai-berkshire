## Summary of analysis of each dimension

### Business model (Duan Yongping’s perspective)

- CPS delivery fee based on performance is the right direction, but the collective boycott of the price increase in 2023 exposed the lack of pricing power
- The starting point of the flywheel (traffic) is highly dependent on the byte ecosystem and is a "semi-closed loop" rather than a completely closed loop.
- 7.5 million creators and 30,000 distributors do not constitute exclusive lock-in - they all operate on multiple platforms
- Among the five scenarios after 10 years, only "becoming a car trading infrastructure" (probability 20%) is the ideal outcome
- Duan Yongping concluded: This is not a business where "you can win without doing stupid things"

### Finance and Valuation (Buffett’s Perspective)

- Revenue is estimated to be 3.5-5.5 billion yuan, taking the median of 4.5 billion, which corresponds to about 4.8 times of PS, which is higher than 2.3 times of Autohome.
- Based on Autohome's mature profit level, the margin of safety is estimated to be approximately US$2 billion. The current valuation of US$3 billion has no margin of safety.
- The IPO target of US$4 billion to US$5 billion requires strong growth data support, and Autohome’s market value of US$2.5 billion is a warning of the ceiling of the industry.
- The asset-light model is a plus, but its profitability is completely unproven
- The ceiling of the CPS model’s liquidity rate is low (~0.3%), and the automotive information platform does not touch the transaction capital flow

### Industry and Competition (Munger’s Perspective)

- The penetration rate of new energy has exceeded 50% and will exceed 60%, and more than half of new cars bypass dealers - the "available market" of the platform is physically shrinking
- The lead conversion rate plummeted from 5% to 1.5%, and the industry is producing more and more invalid information
- Autohome's revenue and profit have dropped for two consecutive years (revenue in 2025 is 6.45 billion, -8.3%), which may be a signal of industry recession.
- The bargaining power of the platform in the industry chain is weak, and it is in a two-way squeeze between the upstream (traffic platform) and the downstream (direct sales of car companies)
- Munger's summary: I don't like businesses that have to keep running to stay where they are.

### Risk and Management (Li Lu’s Perspective)

- "Illusion of Independence" - The equity is pledged to Byte's related parties. On the surface, He Jian holds 60% of the shares, but the actual control is questionable.
- CEO He Jian discloses very little information, and the management team is almost completely opaque, which is abnormal for a company about to IPO.
- Deep dependence on Douyin traffic is the biggest single risk - if Byte adjusts its strategy or builds its own closed loop for car trading
- The winter beta controversy (Huawei stopped cooperation) exposed the fragility of the credibility of content platforms
- More than half of dealers are losing money, and their paying customer base is shrinking
- Li Lu’s summary: This is not a business that “can be held with peace of mind for 10 years”

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. ByteDance’s first IPO target, with its own traffic and attention
2. The CPS model is the first to be implemented on a large scale in the industry, with 28,500+ dealers participating, and the direction is right.
3. DAU growth rate far exceeds that of Autohome, with a high proportion of young users and large room for growth.
4. Citigroup + Goldman Sachs have been selected as underwriters, and the signal for IPO promotion is clear
5. The asset-light model can theoretically achieve high ROE and strong free cash flow
6. Some new energy vehicle companies have begun to "put aside their obsession with direct sales" and return to dealers, which is good for the short term.
7. The exercise price is US$0.001. If the listing is successful, the threshold for realizing the option value is extremely low.

### Bearish logic
1. The moat does not belong to you - the core competitiveness is byte traffic and algorithms
2. No pricing power - price increase was resisted and forced to turn to CPS "burden reduction" strategy
3. The new energy direct operation model is draining fuel from the bottom, and the "available market" of the platform is shrinking.
4. The finances are completely opaque and profitability has not been verified in any way.
5. The ownership structure is complex - "nominally independent, substantively controlled", and the protection of the interests of public shareholders is questionable
6. The continuous decline of rival Autohome implies that the industry ceiling is limited
7. The valuation of 3 billion already contains a lot of growth expectations, and the IPO pricing of 4 to 5 billion US dollars is even more aggressive.

## Summary

Dingchedi is a fast-growing company hatched by ByteDance. Its CPS model is in the right direction, its user base is young and growing rapidly. But judging from the standards of value investment, it has three fundamental problems: the capital moat is borrowed, the pricing power is not in its own hands, and the industry ceiling is being lowered. **

The overall rating given by four analysts is 2.4/5 - this is not a "win if you don't do stupid things" business, but a business that needs to continue to do the right things to survive. With the financial data completely opaque, there is no margin of safety for the current valuation of US$3 billion, let alone the possible pricing of US$4-5 billion after the IPO.

**For option holders:** If the exercise price is extremely low (such as $0.001), the option will have considerable value as long as the company successfully IPOs. The core risks are "whether it can be listed" and "whether the post-listing valuation can be sustained." It is recommended to think of options as high-odds call options - it is possible to make big profits, but you also need to be mentally prepared for the "unlisted" scenario.
