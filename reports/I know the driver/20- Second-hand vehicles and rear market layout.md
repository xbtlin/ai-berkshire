1. ** The second-hand vehicle business model is clear but limited**: the light-model route of the "Platforms + Nation-wide Buy" is understood to be based on the advantage of the tremor flow, but there is still a long way to go to become a leader in the industry.

4. ** The depth of realization is insufficient compared to that of the car house**: the understanding of the size and flow of the driver has been achieved and even beyond the car house, but there are still significant gaps in the capacity to make up the vehicle cycle.
