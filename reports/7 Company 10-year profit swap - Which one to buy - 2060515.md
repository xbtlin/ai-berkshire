## Classification discussion

### First echelon: high certainty + reasonable returns

**Tencent**——The best comprehensive choice
- Annualized return ~11.6%, high certainty
- The widest moat (the WeChat social graph is irreplaceable)
- The current stock price is HK$460, only 3% away from the 52-week low, and the analyst target price is HK$725 (+58%)
- Risk: AI investment return + supervision
- **Suitable for: Long-term investors who want some sleep**

### Second tier: high returns but high uncertainty

**Alibaba**——Highest book return
- Annualized return ~14.3%, the best number
- However, FCF has turned negative, ROE is only 8%, and integration risks are high
- Cloud business is the only bright spot, Taotian is losing share
- **Suitable for: People who believe that Tsai Chongxin + Wu Yongming can recreate Ali**

**Meituan**——Bottom Flip Option
- Current losses, but if profits recover after the competitive landscape stabilizes in 2027, the returns will be considerable
- Instant retail is the real growth track
- Risk: Taobao flash sales + Douyin double attack, profit margins will permanently decline
- **Suitable for: Contrarian investors who dare to buy when the company is losing money and believe that competition will ease**

**Bubble Mart** - the fastest growing but the least certain
- Profit growth of 309% in 2025, 10-year weighted return ~11%
- But the IP life cycle is unpredictable and LABUBU has fallen back
- "The next Disney" or "the next Crocs" - no one can answer 10 years in advance
- **Suitable for: Investors who are willing to tolerate high volatility for high growth and can judge consumer trends**

### The third tier: cheap but for a reason

**Pinduoduo** - the cheapest
- PE after cash is only 3.1x, FCF yield is 15.8% - the most attractive numerically
- But the growth rate has reached a cliff (+59% → +10%), and the annual growth rate of the main website is only 2.3%.
- Temu is a political option: double it if you win, return to the starting point if you lose
- Whether $60.4 billion in cash is divided or repurchased is a core governance issue
- **Suitable for: People who believe that Temu’s geopolitics can work and can accept the lack of feedback from management**

**Moutai**——the most stable but the most expensive
- The highest certainty, but excluding cash PE 18.7x + growth rate is only 2.7% = annualized return of about 6%
- 6% is not bad in a low interest rate environment, but it has little advantage over bank financial management
- **Suitable for: asset allocators who pursue absolute safety and do not seek high returns**

**Kuaishou** - no growth in sight
- The growth of the main site has stagnated (Q1 only +2.3%), and profits have barely grown in 10 years
- Keling AI is the only variable, but its valuation is discounted by the market
- **Not recommended: Unless you have independent judgment on Keling AI, it is better to buy Tencent for the same money**

## Key Reminders

1. **10-year forecasts are essentially a gamble. ** The value of this report lies not in accurately predicting profit figures, but in understanding each company's growth logic and risk structure.
2. **Certainty is more important than rate of return. ** Alibaba’s annualized book value of 14% seems to be the highest, but the certainty is only "medium" - if the pessimistic scenario comes true, your money may not rise for 10 years.
3. **All 7 companies are near their 52-week lows. ** This either means that the market is giving you an opportunity in a panic, or it means that the market knows some bad news that you don't. Stay in awe.
4. **AI is the largest common variable. ** The 10-year profits of 5 of the 7 companies (Tencent/Alibaba/Pinduoduo/Kuaishou/Meituan) are affected by AI - AI may either enhance their efficiency or subvert their business models.
