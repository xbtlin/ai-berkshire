## Core contradiction: first-rate hardware, second-rate AI

- Strong motion control (reinforcement learning BeamDojo framework), but weak AI autonomous decision-making
- R&D investment only accounts for 7.73% of revenue (90.2 million yuan), which is far lower than the 20-30% of AI companies
- A large amount of the 4.2 billion yuan raised by the IPO was used to make up for AI shortcomings
- There is a generational gap compared to Tesla’s end-to-end large models
- The VLA/WMA model has been open sourced, but it is not mature enough

## 3-5 year outlook

**3 years later (2029)**:
- Highly likely to be among the top three in global humanoid robot shipments, with China leading the way
- Listing on the Science and Technology Innovation Board is completed, with a market value ranging from 50 billion to 100 billion
- Facing head-on competition from Tesla Optimus mass production
- Annual revenue of 5-10 billion yuan

**5 years later (2031)**:
- If AI can fill in the shortcomings → "DJI in the field of robotics"
- If AI continues to lag behind → the role of "hardware OEM" will be hit by dimensionality reduction by AI giants
- **Most likely**: The world's leading robot hardware platform company, occupying the mid- to low-end market, and the high-end intelligent application layer is dominated by AI giants

## One sentence summary

> Yushu Technology ranks first in the world in humanoid robot shipments and has a profit margin of 60%. However, its core contradiction lies in its "first-rate hardware and second-rate AI". In the critical period when humanoid robots evolve from "action actuators" to "autonomous agents", large AI models and embodied intelligence capabilities are the ultimate barriers. Whether Yushu can become the "big frontier in the robot world" depends on whether the 4.2 billion IPO funds raised can make up for the shortcomings of the AI ​​​​brain.
