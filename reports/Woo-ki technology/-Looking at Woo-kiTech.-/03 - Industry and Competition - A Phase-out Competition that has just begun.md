# A industry characteristic that is easily overlooked: no web effects

Internet companies (micro-mail, treasure-sweeping) have a strong web effect — the more users they use, the more valuable they are, the more the moats will automatically deepen.

** The human robot has no network effect. ** You bought an grove R1 that won't be better for your neighbor.

What does that mean?

- Low brand loyalty: the next generation who is cheap and who uses it better.
- Technical barriers need to be sustained. They can't be "sleep for money."
- Later, you can cut in directly with better products without "catching up on the user."

** The human robotics industry is more like the auto industry than the Internet industry ** - no winner eats all but the competition.
