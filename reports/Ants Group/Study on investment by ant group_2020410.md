# Appendix A: Gold Controls Deeply Parsed

# A.1 What's a gold license?

The licence plate of the financial holding company is a licence plate approved by the People ' s Bank of China, pursuant to the Financial Holdings Supervisory Management Pilot Scheme (implemented in November 2020).** The core requirement is that a financial holding company must be established if the two or more financial institutions are not controlled by a financial enterprise or are actually controlled by the financial institution and if the threshold is met.**

The company does not operate financial operations itself, but only "shells" to hold various financial license plates, with the following objectives:
- **Segregate risk**: preventing risk transmission between different financial operations
- **Unified regulation**: Integrating financial operations dispersed under different regulatory systems into a unified vision
- **Preventing regulatory arbitrage**: avoiding financial evasion as a technology company

Key threshold: The amount of registered capital paid is no less than $5 billion, and the total assets of the holding financial institutions are over $100 billion.

# AI Research Limitations Statement

1. **Information abundance level B**: ants are unlisted companies, core financial indicators such as revenue collection, cash flow, ROE are extrapolated and actual data may be significantly biased
2. ** Limited market share data sources**: different data on payment market share from different sources (number of transactions vs transaction amounts), 36.2% and 59.7% of data need cross-checking
3. **AI knowledge cut-off**: some Agent based on a knowledge base analysis prior to May 2025, possibly missing significant changes between the second half of 2025 and the beginning of 2026
4. ** Low confidence in valuation calculations**: unclear caliber of ant harvest (between 120-200 billion reported from different sources), leading to significant errors in valuation multiplier calculations
5. ** Regulatory risks are difficult to quantify**: unpredictability of regulatory policy changes in China is a core risk that the model cannot accurately capture
