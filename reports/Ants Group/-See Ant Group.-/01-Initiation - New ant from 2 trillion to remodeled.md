## A set of numbers that will allow you to re-understand ants

In 2020, Ant Group was only 11 days away from the largest IPO in history. The issue price is 68.8 yuan per share, corresponding to a valuation of **2.1 trillion yuan (approximately US$315 billion)**; the hearing for the dual listing of the Science and Technology Innovation Board + Hong Kong Stock Exchange has passed, and the fundraising scale exceeds Saudi Aramco - the global financial market is waiting with bated breath.

Then everything came to an abrupt end. On October 24, Jack Ma delivered a 21-minute speech at the Bund Financial Summit in Shanghai, criticizing the Basel Accord as a "senior citizen's club" and banks as a "pawn shop mentality." Two weeks later, on November 3, 2020, the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange simultaneously announced the suspension of listing. This money was not raised.

After the IPO was suspended, Ant entered a three-year rectification period.

Today, 5 years later, put the key numbers together:

| Metrics | 2020 (pre-IPO) | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|-------------------|---------|---------|------|
| Valuation | **2.1 trillion yuan** | 635 billion (Hurun) | 700-750 billion (estimate) | **-65%** |
| Net profit | 31.2 billion | **38.3 billion** (+61%) | **~15.3 billion** (-60%) | Ups and downs |
| R&D investment | ~10 billion (estimated) | 23.5 billion | **28.98 billion (record high)** | +190% |
| Alipay MAU | ~700 million | 890 million | ~900 million+ | +30% |
| Alipay market share | ~55% | 45.7% (by amount) | **36.2% (by number of transactions, Q1) ** | Continued decline |
| Ant International revenue | Almost zero | **27 billion ($3 billion)** | ~33 billion ($3.7 billion, estimated) | From scratch |
| Jack Ma’s voting rights | **53.46%** | **6.2%** | 6.2% | Change of actual controller |

This set of figures reveals a counter-intuitive fact: **Ant’s valuation has shrunk by 65%, but its users are growing, internationalization has grown from zero to 27 billion in revenue, and R&D investment has nearly tripled. **

The sudden 60% drop in profits in 2025 has confused the market even more - Ant is spending **28.98 billion** on AI and new businesses. Is this a "strong man cutting off his wrist" or a "bottomless pit"?

(Data source: Ant Group 2020 prospectus; Alibaba 2025-2026 fiscal year financial report equity method calculation; Hurun Global Unicorn List 2025; [Beijing Business Daily 2026-05-13](https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2026/0513/593232.shtml))

## What kind of company is Ant? Three-story disassembly

If you were to define Ant Group in one sentence - most people would say "Alipay parent company". It’s not wrong, but it’s woefully incomplete.

A more accurate definition is:

> **Ant Group = a super traffic portal with 900 million monthly active users (Alipay) + a data-driven financial technology monetization machine (credit + financial management + insurance) + a rapidly growing international financial infrastructure (Ant International) + an AI bet that has spent 29 billion and still can’t see the end. **

Looking at it hierarchically:

### The first layer: Traffic entrance-Alipay

Alipay is the "gate" for Ant. **890 million monthly active users** open it every day to scan codes, transfer money, pay bills, and buy things. This traffic is not Ant’s profit center - the payment business rate has dropped to the **0.2-0.4%** range, and the cumulative profit from fee reductions in the past three years has exceeded **21 billion**.

The real value of Alipay is to guide 900 million users to Ant’s financial and service ecosystem. For example: Alipay is the "shopping mall gate", letting you in for free; Ant makes money by "stores in the mall".

**But this door is being diverted away from customers by WeChat Pay. ** In Q1 of 2025, WeChat Pay’s share in terms of number of transactions reached **59.7%**, and Alipay dropped to **36.2%**. WeChat Pay’s monthly active users exceeded **1.47 billion**, which is 1.6 times that of Alipay. In offline small-amount high-frequency scenarios (catering, retail), the penetration rate of WeChat payment is **63.1%**, and the average daily usage frequency is **1.5 times that of Alipay**.

Counter-argument: The decline in share is exaggerated. In terms of amount (rather than number of transactions), Alipay still maintains its advantage in large-value payment scenarios, with the average monthly payment amount of users being **2.3 times** that of WeChat Pay. The number of "touch" NFC payment users has exceeded **200 million**, and the number of daily payments will exceed **100 million** in January 2026. Alipay still cannot be replaced in terms of "low-frequency large amounts" and "financial services".

### Second layer: Monetization machine - digital finance

This is where Ant really makes money.

| Segment | Revenue 2024 | What we do | Profit contribution |
|------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Ant Consumer Finance (Huabei/Jiebei) | **15.2 billion** | Consumer credit (loans ~2.1 trillion, including United Loans) | Net profit 3.05 billion |
| MYBank | **21.3 billion** | Small and micro enterprise loans | Net profit 3.17 billion |
| Ant Fund | **16.3 billion** | Fund sales agency (non-goods holdings 1.57 trillion, No. 1 in the industry) | Net profit ~1 billion |
| Ant Insurance | ~7 billion (estimated) | Internet insurance intermediary (first in the industry) | Net profit ~240 million |

**Credit Technology is Ant's largest profit engine** - Ant Consumer Finance + MYBank will have a combined net profit of over **6.2 billion** in 2024, accounting for 16%+ of the group's profits. But this number is significantly underestimated: Ant’s self-operated loans only account for ~15% of the outstanding loan balance, 98% of the funds come from cooperative banks, and Ant charges about **1% technical service fee**. In other words, Ant does not bear the credit risk, but takes away a stable technology share.

Opposition view: This "asset-light" model is being squeezed by regulation. In April 2026, new regulations from eight departments will adjust the payment and credit bundling model, and the frequency of Huabei usage may drop by **30-40%**. Discussions of the interest rate cap being lowered from 24% to 20% are the biggest variable in the industry. The bad rate of Ant Financial has increased slightly for three consecutive years (1.69% → 1.74% → 1.78%).

(Data source: Ant Consumer Finance/MyBank 2024 Annual Report; Ant Fund 2025H1 Report; [Securities Times](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/3765475.html))

### The third layer: Growth engine - internationalization + AI

Ant International's revenue in 2024 will be **27 billion yuan ($3 billion)**, accounting for approximately 20% of the group's revenue, and will grow to approximately **$3.7 billion** in 2025. Alipay+ has covered more than **100 countries and regions, 90 million+ merchants**, connected to 40 international payment partners and 1.8 billion user accounts.

In terms of AI, Ant’s R&D investment in 2025 reached a new high of **28.98 billion**. The number of users of the AI ​​health manager "Ant Afu" exceeded **100 million** during the Spring Festival in 2026. Alipay's "AI Pay" also exceeded **100 million users** and the number of payments in a single week exceeded **120 million**, becoming the world's first AI-native payment product with both the number of users and the number of payments exceeding 100 million.

Opposition view: Ant International plans to IPO in Hong Kong, but as of May 2026, it has not yet submitted to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. There are huge differences in valuations - Bloomberg Intelligence gives a range of **$8 billion-24 billion**. Investment in AI is the main reason for a 60% drop in profits in 2025, and it is still unknown when the "burning money" will be realized.

(Data source: [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3310945/ant-groups-global-unit-pulls-us3-billion-revenue-ahead-spin); [Science and Technology Daily 2026-02-24](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-02/24/content_476005.html))

## What is the difference between Ant, Tencent and Pinduoduo?

If you have watched "Understanding Tencent" and "Understanding Pinduoduo", ants and they are **three completely different species**:

| Dimension | Tencent | Pinduoduo | Ant Group |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| Core Assets | WeChat + Games | Algorithmic Advertising Machine | Alipay + Financial Technology |
| Business Model | Social Monopoly → Traffic Monetization | Extreme Efficiency → Advertising + Commission | Payment Portal → Financial Service Fee |
| Moat strength | Extremely strong (WeChat is irreplaceable) | Moderate (efficiency advantage) | Strong (data network effect) but being eroded |
| Profit quality | High certainty | Extremely high efficiency | Strong rebound but large fluctuations |
| Listing Status | Hong Kong Stock Listing | US Stock Listing | **Unlisted** |
| The biggest risk | Regulatory tail | Temu politics + management opacity | **Financial holding license + AI entrance subversion** |
| What kind of investors are suitable for | Stable type | Arbitrage type | **Requires extremely high patience and information tolerance** |

The biggest difference between ants and them is two words: **opaque**.

Tencent releases detailed financial reports every quarter, and Pinduoduo is at least a listed company. As for Ant, a giant with a valuation of 700 billion, all investors can see are: a line of "equity method investment income" in Alibaba's financial report, scattered annual reports of subsidiaries, and various media projections.

**If you want to invest in a company that you can't clearly see inside, the first thing you need to figure out is not "how much it's worth" but "what it looks like." ** This is exactly what the next 4 posts will do.

## A little teaser: What does a 60% plunge in profits mean in 2025?

Leave a hook. Ant's net profit in 2025 dropped from 38.3 billion to approximately **15.3 billion** (according to equity method calculation in Alibaba's 2026 fiscal year annual report), a decrease of **60%**. During the same period, R&D investment soared from 23.5 billion to **28.98 billion**.

What does this set of numbers mean? Two completely different interpretations:

**Bulls**: Ant is taking the initiative to "sacrifice short-term profits for long-term space" - investing money in AI and internationalization, just like Amazon's 20 consecutive years of losses to cultivate AWS. Ant Afu's users exceeded 100 million and AI payments exceeded 100 million, which are signals that the investment is beginning to bear fruit.

**Bear Bears**: The halving of profits is not a "strategic investment", but a "deterioration of the fundamentals" - the payment share continues to lose, credit is suppressed by regulation, and the return on AI investment is far away. Of the 28.98 billion R&D investment, how much is "effective investment" and how much is "defensive cash burn"?

Which one is closer to the truth? In the next article, we will break down Ant’s six major businesses one by one.
