# Five major fixes: each changes the ants' DNA

After the interview with the 4th of December, the ant received five explicit requests for correction.

# Demand one: return to the source of payment and break the improper connection between payment and financial products

** Before re-engineering**: Open payment treasures, recommend flowers, borrow loans, balance treasures - payments and financial seamless connections, and users "finance" without knowing.

** After correction**: Flowers/lending to ants to remove (the card-free company) are isolated from paying treasurers. The recommendation for the financial products to pay the front page has been significantly reduced.

** Impact**: The frequency of use of flowers may decline **30-40%** but credit operations are more compliant and healthier.

# Request two: set up a financial holding company

** Before restructuring**: Ants operate as a "technology company" in financial operations such as payments, consumer loans, funds, insurance, banking, etc., avoiding bank-level capital and regulatory requirements.

** After correction**: Ant submitted a gold license application in July 2023. But as of May 2026,** gold licence ** has not been approved. Only three gold license plates have been issued: China-based, Beijing-based, commercial-based, all-state/state-owned. Ants will be the largest private-sector case**.

** This is the biggest obstacle to the overall listing of ants.** Without a gold license, IPO offers cannot provide a clear statement of regulatory risk. Ants 'business is running, but the legal identification card at the group level has not been obtained' -- this has been in place for nearly three years.

# Demand three: press credit leverage

** Before re-engineering**: Flowers/lendings have been **2 trillion** in loan balances, with nearly 100 times the leverage. The core method is ABS revolving lending.

** After re-engineering**: The registered capital of ants to grow to 18.5 billion, the maximum leverage is 10 times higher, and self-financed loans are about 300 billion. ABS leverage is completely compressed, and credit lines are changed from "loans to pay for their own loan" to "loans to help banks to collect their technical fees".

** Impact**: Profit margins fall, but risk is significantly reduced. It's like a person moving from "lending stock" to "taking out a consulting fee for another person's share" -- less, but not too much.

# Call four: personal information protection and correspondence compliance

** Before re-engineering**: Payment treasures accumulated full-scale data on payments, consumption, financial management, socialization, etc. of 1 billion users, and sesame credit ratings were widely used without a card plate.

** Restructured**: Establish a money pond letter (with personal ID plates) to include ant data assets in the regulatory framework.

# Call for five: management and governance restructuring

** Before re-engineering**: Maun actually controls ants through stockholding and voting agreements, voting rights amounting to **53.46%**.

** After re-engineering**: The Maun vote fell to **6.2%**, 10 management members were given decentralized votes. Ants entered the "no real control" state. Maun appeared in December 20, 20th anniversary, returned to Ari Park in 2025 - "Nominal exit," "Nominal influence" still exists.

# The value evaporated 65%: Where's the money?

The ant valuation fell from **2.1 trillion** in 2020 to the current level of approximately **70-750 billion**, evaporating about **1.4 trillion**. This is not a single cause, but rather a four-layered cause.

## First tier: Business structure is subject to forced change (40% contribution ~ evaporated)

The pre-reform ant, in essence, is a "low capital-high-leveraging quasi-bank" -- a capital of 3 trillion dollars to leverage 2 trillion dollars in credit, with huge profit margins. The re-reform is then turned into a "technology company that helps banks with technology services" -- a more stable income but a lower ceiling.

** Pre-reorganization profit scenario**: if 2 trillion dollars are in loan balance at 3% spread, theoretical profit cap is 60 billion.

** Restructured profit reality**: ant removal + net net profit of Netbanks + 6.2 billion - less than a magnitude.

#2nd layer: Regulatory uncertainty premium (~25% contribution evaporated)

The outstanding gold license plates, the opaque listing schedule, and the political sensitivity of Maun - factors that make investors demanding higher "uncertain discounts" on ants.

#3rd floor: Changes in the market environment ( contribution ~20%)

2020 is the global science-equity bubble, with an ant 2.1 trillion worth of **67 times PE** – the net profit of 31.2 billion years – in itself a foam price. Even without modification, this valuation is not sustainable.

#4th floor: deteriorating patterns of competition (~15% contribution evaporated)

The share of the micro-mail payments rose from 39% to 59.7%, and the payment treasures decreased from 55% to 36.2%, and the registered capital of the shivering payments surged by 20 times to 31.5 billion. The figure RMB 2.0 was launched on 1 January 2026.

** Inverse view **: The valuation evaporation 65% also means "bumble is drained." The current valuation of 7,000-75 billion corresponds to 38.3 billion net profits in 2024 **18-20 times PE** -- if the total net gain of 15.3 billion in 2025 is used, the PE is up to **46-49 times**. If the 2025 is the "strategic input year" and profits are restored to 40 billion plus, the current valuation is not expensive.

# Gold license: last door on the market

Gold is the key to understanding the ants' current situation.

** What is a gold license?** Financial institutions of two or more types of non-financial enterprises, of a certain size, must have financial holding companies under the central bank’s unified supervision. Ant holding has been responsible for payments, withdrawals, banks, funds, insurance, letters-indulgence – entirely triggering the application conditions.

** Why is it important?** There is no gold license = the missing "legal ID" at the group level = IPO cannot move forward. Each subsidiary has a single license plate that can operate properly, but the parent company needs this "housebook" on the market.

** By May 2026, the Central Bank had only issued three gold-based licences (Sino-Kin, Beijing-Hin, and the market) that were all state-owned. Ants are the largest private applicants, and approval is necessarily more prudent. Political sensitivity (the Maun factor) is also a variable.
