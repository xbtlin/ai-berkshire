>

---

## Series Positioning

Ant Group is **the most unusual company in China's internet world** - it was two days away from what would have been the largest IPO in history, and then it was abruptly paused overnight.

- October 2020: valuation of **RMB 2.1 trillion**, with the world's largest IPO about to be born
- November 2020: the IPO was suspended and a three-year regulatory remediation process began
- July 2023: the company paid a **RMB 7.123 billion fine** and repurchased employee shares at a valuation of about **RMB 567.1 billion** - a 73% evaporation in valuation
- 2025: net profit fell from RMB 38.3 billion to about **RMB 15.3 billion**, while R&D spending hit a record **RMB 28.98 billion**
- Since May 2025: media reports said Ant International planned to pursue an independent Hong Kong listing; as of May 2026 it still had not filed with HKEX, and the group itself remains unlisted

**Five years later, what has changed at Ant? What has not changed? And what is post-remediation Ant really worth?**

This series aims to **compare Ant before and after remediation so that you can see the company's real structure**, rather than to hand you a conclusion about whether it is worth investing in.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Ant is unlisted, so some data is estimated** - every estimate is marked as "estimated" or "non-official" and is never disguised as precise
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
