## Series Positioning

Lingjing Wanwei is not a company that is widely discussed - it is one of the startups in the emerging AI short-drama sector that is **pursued by capital but not yet carefully scrutinized**.

- The bulls say the AI short-drama market will reach **RMB 22-31.4 billion** in 2026, Lingjing has raised three rounds in a year, monthly revenue is **RMB 20 million**, and the hit *Apocalypse Supermarket* generated **RMB 12 million** in revenue in 5 days, showing top-tier execution
- The bears say **90% of companies in the industry lose money**, the hit rate is only **0.12%**, big tech players are entering aggressively, and the founder's background is opaque - this is basically "content gambling"

**Both sides are looking at the same company, yet reaching completely different conclusions.**

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and only then let you decide how to think about it** - not to decide for you.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - Lingjing Wanwei is unlisted, financial data is extremely scarce, and this series only uses data verified by multiple sources
4. **Label all estimates as "estimated"** - startup information asymmetry is severe, so inference and fact must be clearly separated
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
