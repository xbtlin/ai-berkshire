#What exactly is ear time?

> "Understanding Ear Time" Series · Part 01 · Beginning
> Reading time approx. 8 minutes

---

## A confusing name

"Ear Time" has been circulating on the Chinese Internet for nearly two years. Some people say it is the next podcast unicorn, and some think it is about to go public. Four independent analysts cross-checked and came to the same conclusion - **it's not a company at all**.

QiChacha, Tianyancha, and IT Juzi all found no such "person". There is no industrial and commercial registration entity, no financing records, no founder information, no independent App, and no revenue data.

All search paths ultimately point to the same thing:

> **"2024 Podcast Industry Report: The time for your ears is now"**

This is a podcast industry report jointly released by **Ritan Park x Himalaya x Ipsos** in July 2024. "Ear Time" is its title, a slogan - not a company, not a platform, not an investable target.

**This is the most important conclusion of this series, which was unanimously confirmed by four independent analysts after cross-validation: ear time = industry report title, valuation = 0. **

But this conclusion is not an end in itself. The four words "ear time" attract attention because behind them stands a rapidly changing track - **Chinese Podcast**. Whether this track is worthy of attention is the real question that needs to be answered.

## Chinese podcast track: a set of key figures

Let’s look at the basics first.

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Sources |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Number of Chinese podcast listeners | **About 220 million** (ever heard) | **About 150 million** (Monthly active, not declining) | CPA / PodFest China |
| Monthly active listeners | ~80 million | **~100 million** | PodFest China 2025 |
| Number of podcast programs | 50,000+ | 70,000+ | Apple Chinese Podcast Index |
| Podcast advertising market | About 1.0-1.5 billion yuan | **About 1.8-2.2 billion yuan** | JustPod Annual Report (estimate) |
| Overall online audio market | — | **About 45-50 billion yuan** | Industry comprehensive (estimate) |
| Podcast listener penetration | ~22% | — | CPA (vs ~50% US) |

**Note**: Confusion in Chinese podcast listener data is a common problem in the industry. CPA reported 220 million, but some statistics put the monthly active base at only around 100 million. Figures from different sources vary greatly, which in itself shows that the industry has not matured enough to have unified statistical standards.

A key comparison: China’s podcast listener penetration rate is about **22%**, and the United States is about **50%**. The bulls say "there is still double the space"; the bears say "Chinese users have different consumption habits, and 30% may be the ceiling." There is truth to both arguments, and this is where the disagreement lies.

## What the "Ear Time" report itself says

Since it is a report, what are the noteworthy findings in the report?

### User portrait

- Over **80%** of users surveyed listen to podcasts **3 days or more per week** (Source: Ritan Park x Himalaya x Ipsos Report)
- **76.2%** Respondents listen for more than **half an hour** a day
- Nearly **40%** of respondents listen to more than **1 hour** a day
- Female users make up the majority, but male users listen longer
- High income earners and business managers are most dependent on podcasts

### Commercial signals

- Nearly half of listeners have purchased a paid podcast in the past year
- **52.7%** of listeners are tolerant of advertising in podcasts
- **51%** Respondents have purchased products based on podcast recommendations

**Opposite View**: This report was jointly released by Ritan Park and Himalaya – both interested parties in the podcast industry. Report data naturally has a tendency to "self-fulfilling prophecies." For example, "nearly half of the listeners have purchased paid content," but the source of the sample and the sampling method are not fully disclosed, raising doubts about the representativeness of all netizens.

## The three subjects behind the report

Although the four words "Ear Time" are not a company, the three entities that released it each represent a key role in the Chinese podcast track.

### Ritan Park: The "Content Leader" of Chinese Podcasts

- **Company name**: Beijing Ritan Park Culture Media Co., Ltd.
- **Founders**: Li Zhiming (Uncle Li, holding **51.41%**), Feng Guangjian
- **Main IP**: "Japanese Park" podcast (launched in 2016, with a total playback volume of over **1 billion**, and has been Apple Podcast of the Year for five consecutive years)
- **Core Business**: Sunlight Party MCN (the largest podcast MCN in China, signed **60-70** podcasts) + brand podcast customization + paid content
- **Financing**: The first one in 2018 was an angel round (millions of RMB), and there has been no public follow-up financing since then
- **Estimated annual revenue**: **30 million - 100 million yuan** (estimate, low confidence level)

Ritan Park is an iconic presence in the Chinese podcast industry. But its essence is an asset-light company of "content agency + IP operation", and its moat comes from taste and connections, not technology or capital.

### Himalaya: Track giant, just acquired by Tencent

This is a major information update for this series -

**On May 18, 2026, Tencent Music announced the completion of the acquisition of Himalaya, with a total consideration of approximately 18.6 billion yuan. ** Himalaya has become a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tencent Music. (Source: Tencent Music Announcement, Interface News)

This means: Himalaya did not independently list on the Hong Kong stock market (it had four previous IPO sprints), but ended its independent development story by being acquired.

| Himalayan Key Data | Values | Sources |
|----------------|------|------|
| 2023 revenue | **6.16 billion yuan** | Prospectus |
| Adjusted net profit in 2023 | **224 million yuan** (turnaround) | Prospectus |
| Monthly active users | **About 303 million** | Prospectus (including IoT) |
| Online audio market share | **40%-50%** | State Administration for Market Regulation |
| Acquisition consideration | **About 18.6 billion yuan** | Tencent Music Announcement |

**The acquisition price of 18.6 billion yuan is a 40% discount compared to the previous valuation of approximately 30 billion yuan when the IPO was filed. ** The exit price of the industry leader itself illustrates the fact that the valuation ceiling of the Chinese audio track is lower than many people think.

### Ipsos: global research organization

A French listed company, it has no equity connection with "Ear Time" and purely provides research capabilities. No separate analysis is required.

## Panorama of the track: five-layer ecology

Chinese podcasts are not a single market, it is a layered structure:

| Hierarchy | Representing Players | Roles |
|------|---------|------|
| **Platform/Distribution** | Himalaya (Tencent), Xiaocosmos, Apple Podcasts, QQ Music, NetEase Cloud | Traffic portal, master users and data |
| **MCN/Production** | Ritan Park, JustPod, Shengdong Lively | "Three Content Giants", brand podcast agents |
| **Creator/IP** | Ouchi Secret Talk, Limited Culture, Zhixing Bistro, Business Is This | Content Producer |
| **Tools/Technology** | Riverside, Descript, ElevenLabs, NotebookLM | Recording, editing, AI generation |
| **Data/Service** | PodFest, CPA, Podcast Journal | Industry Research, Community |

**Core pattern in one sentence**: Himalaya (now Tencent) is large and comprehensive, Microcosm is small and beautiful, Lychee and Dragonfly are lagging behind, Bilibili and Douyin are outflanking from the direction of video podcasts.

## Why this topic deserves a series

You may ask: Since "Ear Time" is not a company, why do you still write it?

Because the track behind it is experiencing three major events:

1. **Ximalaya was acquired by Tencent** (2026.05) - Industry leaders are no longer independent, and the track pattern is restructured
2. **Little Universe’s commercial revenue increased by 760% year-on-year** (2025 data, source: Little Universe Annual Report) - Podcast commercialization reaches an inflection point
3. **The explosion of AI podcast generation tools** (NotebookLM Chinese version, Doubao, etc.) - the threshold for "making a podcast" has been reduced to close to zero

Add these three things together, and the Chinese podcasting landscape is standing at a crossroads. **Understanding the true appearance of this track is much more important than worrying about whether Ear Time is a company. **
