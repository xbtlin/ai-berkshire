# The podcast market is only 2 billion

In 2025, the Chinese Pod Advertisement Market was about $1.8-2.2 billion a year ** (JustPod annual report, estimated). In the same year, the short video ad market exceeded **30 billion**. The podcast ad was less than **1%** of the latter.

This figure is particularly striking in the light ring of "high quality, good advertising acceptance, high brand premium."** "high value per unit" and "small amount" are two sides of the same coin -- podcasts are more like a bottle of good wine than a big river.**

The best way to understand how a podcast makes money is to tear down a real company. Ear time is not a company, but its core publisher, the Japan Talk Park, is one of the first and deepest players to commercialize Chinese podcasts.

# A sharp question

So far, the commercialization of the Chinese Podcast industry has shown a contradictory picture:

- ** High unit value**: CPM 100-150, well above average audio and video
- ** High user quality**: First- and second-line cities, high academic qualifications, high incomes
- ** High acceptance of advertising**: Over 50% tolerance of advertising
- ** But the total plate is very small**: the advertising market is only 2 billion and the entire online audio is only 50 billion.

** If the podcasts are so good, why is the track so small?**

The answer may be simple:** The size of the podcast user is strictly capped.** In an era when short videos occupy the average Chinese daily for two hours + attention, those who are willing to listen to 30-60 minutes of audio are destined to be a minority.

This is not bad — the high net market of a few is worth it. It means:** The podcast industry is more likely to have a number of "small and beautiful" companies, and less likely to have a next twitch.**
