** Reverse view**: Integration of the tether system may have positive effects - it is more commercialized and more user base, and podcasts, as one of the content categories of the Himalayas, may gain greater distribution channels through the tether ecology. Integration is not necessarily bad.

# The small universe is different

The universe is born in a group of instantaneous people, and from the first day it's taken the community route -- review areas, time tags, podcast discovery algorithms. It's more like a podcast bean petal than a podcast Himalayan.

** The benefits of this positioning**: very sticky users, unique community culture and clear branding.
** The problem of this positioning**: the community model naturally excludes the "breakdown" -- when the user is in too many, community culture dilutes. The DAU of the small universe is more than **1 million**, but one to two orders of magnitude are one to two.

# Valuation puzzle

Small universes are not publicly financed to date. In 2025, there were rumours that Pre-IPO rounds were valued **5-1 billion dollars ** in the middle (unofficially, low confidence). If true, it was not cheap with its tens of millions of users and start-up commercialization.

** Anti-view**: The small universe may be the only independent platform in the Chinese podcast track that has the chance to run out. It has the user, the data, the distribution -- it's the MCN that can never do it. If the podcast track's plate goes up from 2 billion to 5 billion, the small universe is the biggest beneficiary.
