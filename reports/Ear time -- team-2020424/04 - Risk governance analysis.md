## 7. Worst case scenario analysis

**Most likely failure path**

1. **Financing interruption + commercialization not as expected**: Cash flow will be exhausted within 18-24 months, and the team will be forced to downsize
2. **"Modularly copied" by major manufacturers**: Byte/Tencent uses lower CAC to make similar products, and the growth of independent App users has stagnated
3. **Core anchor/host leaves**: Content collapse leads to DAU cliff
4. **Acquired at a low price or disbanded**: The team was merged into the Himalaya/NetEase Cloud/Byte Audio department, and the brand disappeared

**Historical Comparable Failure/Exit Cases**

- **Lizhi FM** (renamed Litchi Group): After being listed on Nasdaq, the stock price has been below US$1 for a long time. It has faced multiple delisting warnings and was forced to transition to overseas markets and AI.
- **Koala FM/Listening Companion**: From C-side audio to car B-side, the brand has faded out of the public eye
- **Duoting FM, Penguin FM**: Tencent audio products have been shut down one after another (Penguin FM will be shut down in September 2023)
- **PingWest Pinwan, Guoke.com**: Content/media companies are gradually marginalized after being acquired by strategic parties
- **JustPod, lively voice**: Choose the "small but beautiful" route, rely on brand-customized podcasts to survive, and do not follow the high growth curve of VC

**Why the smart money doesn’t invest**

1. **Low ceiling**: Chinese podcast MAU has been less than 100 million for a long time, and the payment rate is single digits
2. **No network effect**: The strong ones in the UGC community will always be strong (Microcosmos has already occupied its place), and there will be no barriers for new entrants.
3. **The monetization model is not sexy**: small advertising pool, weak payment, difficult subscription, and low e-commerce delivery efficiency
4. **Besieged by big manufacturers**: There is Himalayan Ecology on the top, and Byte/Tencent Cash Flow on the bottom.
5. **AI subverts expectations**: Generative audio may rewrite the content production cost curve, and existing content assets will rapidly depreciate.

## 8. "Industry disease" in the Chinese podcast industry

**Exit/Closing Cases of Star Companies in the Past 5 Years**

| Company | Ending | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Penguin FM (Tencent) | Shut down | September 2023 |
| Duoting FM | Business shrinks, brands fade out | Around 2020 |
| Koala FM | Transformation into car B-side (listening companion) | 2018-2020 |
| Dragonfly FM | Several attempts at IPO failed, and the pace of financing slowed down | Continued |
| Lizhi Group | Risks of U.S. stock delisting, switching to AI overseas | 2023-2025 |
| Himalaya | Hong Kong stocks submitted their financial statements for the second time, and their valuations were significantly reduced | 2024-2025 |

**Common ways to die for "listening to book APP/audio startup companies"**

1. **Shut down by major manufacturers**: Audio was one of the first businesses to be cut when the parent company adjusted its strategy (Penguin FM is a typical example)
2. **Copyright costs crush gross profit**: The cost of purchasing long-form audio/audiobook copyrights is higher than advertising revenue
3. **Commercialization has four levels of "advertising→membership→live broadcasting→AI", and each level cannot be jumped**
4. **The founding team cashed out and left**: Under the pressure of early VC expiration and exit, the team was forced to accept low-valuation mergers and acquisitions
5. **Vertical communities "fail to break out of the circle"**: Once a niche community breaks out of the circle, its culture will collapse; if it does not break out of the circle, its scale will peak.
6. **Content Compliance Incident**: A single content review incident can cause the app to be removed from the shelves for several months, missing the growth window.
