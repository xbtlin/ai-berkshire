The sustainable path of the JustPod (B2B) and the sound (Pipple IP+ community) is dead if ear time bets on "Himalaya"; if the focus is "China's Wondery" or "China's Acast" (SaaaS).** The estimate is reasonable between valuations: RMB 150 million** (estimated by the average PS 2-4 times the average of the second line audio companies).
