#9. AI impact

** Enabling side**:
- AI-assisted editing, transcription, subtitles (upgraded production efficiency, cost reduction 30-50%)

- **NotebookLM class tool** (in Chinese in May 2025) allows anyone to turn a single key into a double-talking podcast, with over 5 million users -- this directly impacts the scarcity of "shower content production"

- The content supply explosion will further spread its attention and the scarce premium on head podcasts may fall

** Specific implications for the day talks**:
- Short-term benefits (production of drop-downs + video expansion)
- Medium-term pressure (AI generates a list of podcasts who have been signed by the waist)
