## A set of facts that will make you re-examine AI pharmaceuticals

HK$47 billion. This is the "potential maximum amount" of a cooperation contract announced by Jingtai Technology in August 2025 - equivalent to the entire market value of a small Hong Kong stock company, compressed into one contract terms. The day the news came out, the stock price doubled in a single month.

But there is a fact behind this number that you must know: Of the HK$47 billion, only US$51 million (approximately HK$400 million) has actually been received. The remaining HK$46.5 billion depends on how far the cooperative drugs can go - and the clinical success rate in pharmaceutical history has never exceeded 10%.

This is Jingtai Technology: a company that deeply divides the market. It is the first company to successfully "de-P" (convert from a specialized technology company to a normal listed company) under the 18C rules of the Hong Kong stock market. The IPO price is HK$5.28. Less than two years after its listing, the stock price followed a dramatic curve:

- Listed in June 2024: **5.28 HKD**
- October 2024 low: **4.20 HKD** – break, market cold
- High point in August 2025: **15.12 Hong Kong dollars** - DoveTree’s US$6 billion deal announced, doubling in a single month
- May 2026: **~8.2 HKD** - gave up the gains and returned to near the starting point

**In less than two years, the same company has been repriced by the market at least three times**.

But Jingtai is different from those AI companies that purely speculate on concepts - it has real customers (**17** among the top 20 pharmaceutical companies in the world), real revenue (**803 million yuan** in 2025, +201% year-on-year), and **first profit** in 2025 (adjusted net profit**258 million yuan**).

The question is: Is this "first profit" an inflection point, or is it a mirage propped up by a one-time big order?

## What is Jingtai Technology?

Define it in one sentence:

> **Jingtai Technology = uses AI algorithm + quantum physics + automated robots to replace the traditional laboratory manual drug research and development platform. **

Take a look at the three keywords:

### 1. AI algorithm

The company has more than 200 AI models in vertical fields, covering core aspects of drug research and development such as crystal form prediction, molecular design, and protein structure prediction. Among them, the crystal form prediction accuracy reached **98%** - this number was verified in Pfizer's blind test in 2016, and it became famous in one battle.

### 2. Quantum Physics

The three founding team members are all postdoctoral fellows in quantum physics at MIT. The company's underlying methodology is not pure machine learning, but "computing + AI based on the first principles of physics." This is the core difference between it and most AI pharmaceutical companies - **Others are driven by data, while Jingtai is driven by both physics and data**.

Opposition: The physics-driven method is computationally intensive and costly. If pure AI methods (such as Google's Isomorphic Labs) match accuracy, the advantage of physical methods may be erased.

### 3. Automated robots

**More than 200 robot workstations**, capable of conducting experiments 24/7. This is the biggest difference between Jingtai and pure software-based AI pharmaceutical companies - it not only "calculates", but also "does". After the algorithm predicts, the robot immediately verifies it, and the data returns to the model and iterates again.

**This closed loop of "calculate-do-verify-iterate" is the core narrative of Jingtai**.

## DoveTree’s big deal: What does HK$47 billion mean?

On August 5, 2025, Jingtai Technology announced the signing of a cooperation agreement with DoveTree. This is the largest single cooperation in the history of AI pharmaceuticals.

| Terms | Amount |
|------|------|
| Down payment (already received) | **US$51 million** (approximately HK$400 million) |
| Second payment | **US$49 million** (approximately HK$385 million) |
| Potential milestone payment + sales share | **Up to US$5.89 billion** (approximately HK$46.2 billion) |
| **Total contract amount** | **About US$6 billion (approximately HK$47 billion)** |

On the day the news was announced, Jingtai's stock price soared, rising from about HK$7.5 to a record high of HK$15.12 within the next month.

**But there is a key cognitive trap**:

HK$47 billion is the "potential maximum amount" and is not a guaranteed income. How much can actually be realized depends on how far the drugs in the cooperation pipeline can go - and the historical success rate of drug development is less than **10%**.

To put it another way: If HK$47 billion is regarded as "determined revenue", it is assuming that all partnered drugs are successfully launched. This has never happened in the history of pharmaceuticals.

**Confirmed portion**: a down payment of US$51 million (approximately RMB 360 million), which has been included in FY2025 revenue and contributed **approximately 67%** of the revenue of the drug discovery business.

**Part to be verified**: The arrival time and conditions of the second $49 million payment. If this money is delayed or defaulted, it will be a significant negative signal.

## AI pharmaceutical industry: a track where no one has reached the finish line yet

To understand Jingtai, you must first understand an industry-level fact:

> **As of May 2026, no drug designed by AI has been successfully launched in the world. **

This is not to say that AI is useless - more than **80** AI-assisted designed drugs have entered the clinical trial stage around the world. The most advanced ones include:

| Drug/Company | Progress | Notes |
|----------|------|------|
| GB-0895 / Generate Biomedicines | **Global Phase III clinical trial** (launched in December 2025) | The world's first AI-designed antibody entering Phase III |
| SIGX1094 / SIG Biotech (Jingtai Incubation) | **Phase I clinical**, the lesions were reduced by 20% in the 200mg dose group | Obtained FDA Orphan Drug + Fast Track designation, and was shortlisted for the Galen Award |
| ISM001-055 / Insilicon | **Phase II clinical** | Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis |

**The most optimistic prediction**: AI-designed drugs may enter marketing approval around 2027. But the value of the word "possible" is understood by anyone who has done drug research and development.

Opposition view: If no AI drugs are successfully launched in the next 3-5 years, the entire industry may face a crisis of confidence similar to the 2001 Internet bubble. The valuations of AI pharmaceutical companies are based on the belief that they will eventually succeed - once the belief is shaken, the valuations may plummet.

## A preview: The essential difference between Jingtai and traditional CRO companies

The business model of traditional CRO companies (such as WuXi AppTec) is "selling heads" - customers pay, and I send chemists to do experiments for you. The gross profit margin is 35-40%, and growth depends on recruiting people.

Jingtai’s narrative is: I don’t sell people, I sell platforms—AI calculations, robot production, data precipitation, and model iteration. The gross profit margin is 70%, and growth depends on reuse.

If this narrative is true, Jingtai's endgame will be more like AWS** in the field of drug research and development - an infrastructure-level platform that everyone who makes drugs must use.

If this narrative does not hold true, Jingtai is an expensive CRO company with a revenue of 800 million, a loss of 5 billion, and relying on large orders to survive.
