**Isomorphic Labs is the source of the risk of a "reduced blow" to Crystal.** The reason is simple:

1. **Infinite**: The market value of Alphabet, Google parent company, is about $2 trillion, and Isomorpic Labs is not short of money
2. ** Top talent**: Demis Hassabis was the creator of AlphaFold and the Nobel Prize winner in chemistry 2024
3. **Arithmetic crush**: Google has the top global TPU cluster and cloud computing infrastructure
4. **IsoDDE Accuracy**: In protein-format structural predictions, IsoDDDE's accuracy** is more than twice that of AlphaFold 3**

Anti-Attitude (Defence for Crystal):

1. **Isomorphic Labs has not been clinical**, whereas the crystal-thai hatching Higlology has SIGX1094 in I. "Access" and "Drug" are two things.
** Crystal has robotic laboratories, not Isomorphic.** AI pharmaceuticals are not just calculations - they need "wet experiments" to be tested. Pure algorithms have to rely on external CROs for experiments, which slow down speed.
3. **The history of Google’s drug production is not a good one.** Calico under Alphabet (established in 2013, focusing on ageing research) has been invested for over a decade and has not produced significant results to date.
4. ** Clinical delay is a negative sign.** The forecast of "Annual Clinication at the Year" by Hassanis 2025 has been postponed to the end of 2026, indicating that it is more difficult than could be imagined to cross from algorithms to medicines.

** Core judgement**: Isomorphic Labs is the biggest long-term threat to Crystal, but it is unlikely that it will be taken directly from its clients in the short term (2-3 years). The reason is that the pharmaceutical industry has long cycles, high switching costs and Isomorphic has not even entered the clinical field.** But in five years ' dimension, if Isomorphic Labs is fully commercialized, the technical barriers to Crystal may be significantly eroded**.

# Core judgement: three conclusions on the pattern of competition

Conclusion I: Jingtai is in the first line of the AI pharmaceutical industry

There are no more than five A.I. pharmaceutical companies that can get a large amount of cooperation from leading drug companies like Pfizer. Jinghai is one of them, and the only ones that make a profit.

Conclusion II: Isomorphic Labs is the most serious threat in the long term, but its short-term impact is limited

Google has money, people, and arithmetic, but it is much harder to do drugs than to do them. As Isomorphic Labs’ clinical delay has shown.** There is no direct competition within 2-3 years, and the situation is uncertain after five years.**

Conclusion III: The A.I. pharmaceutical industry is still in "testing" rather than "harvesting"

There are no ALI drugs on the market, most companies are still losing, and valuations are generally high -- a track that requires "belief" support. If you don't believe that AI will eventually change drug research and development, all companies in this track will not be worth investing, including Jinghai.
