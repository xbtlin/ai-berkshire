# What are the two lines of business?

# Line 1: Drug discovery solutions (67% income)

This is Crystal's core business. Simply put, ** The drug firm outsourced the "finding drugs" to Crystal.

The pain of traditional drug development is that of "the needles of the sea" -- from the billions of potential molecules, the candidates are found to treat disease, drug and energy. Traditional methods are tested by chemists, on a trial basis, for ** 3-5 years, ** and cost hundreds of millions of dollars.

Jingtai's approach:

```
Step 1: The AI model selects thousands of the most likely to succeed from billions of molecules
Step 2: Automated robots test thousands of molecules.
Step 3: Experimental data backstream AI model, modeling more accurate
Step 4: Lock a few candidates out of thousands and deliver them to clients
```

The core platform of this closed ring is called **ID4** - a collection of AI model clusters, robotic laboratories and data systems.

** There's a fundamental judgement here**: If the fee pattern is upgraded from 1.0 to 3.0 (sales share), the income ceiling of Crystal Tart will go from "service fee" to "percentage of drug sales" -- a completely different level of magnitude.

The counter-opposition: The price model is raised only if the drug is marketed successfully. The historical success rate of drug research and development is less than 10%. The so-called "sales share" is expensive on paper until any co-operative drug is marketed successfully.

# Core judgement: Is business model good or not?

Back to the eternal chase: ** Where is the business? A word of description.**

> It is good that the AI+ data wheels in theory can generate positive feedback -- the more projects are done, the more data are available, the more models are accurate, the more willing the client is to cooperate. If the wheels actually turn, the marginal costs are zero, the Māori rate is increasing, and the customer's stickyness is increasing.

** But there is one precondition**: whether the wheels are really spinning.

Current evidence:
- ** Positive** DoveTree $6 billion in large bills, two re-news (totalling about $600 million), 70% Māori, data of 26 million and growing
- ** Negative**: First-time earnings are highly dependent on a single large, suspect profit after graduation, and the income base is still small (800 million vs. 40 billion dollars for a drug named Minkandr).

** Conclusion**: The business model is oriented towards the replacement of artificial CROs by AI+ robots, but not sufficiently validated - an additional 2-3 years are needed to see whether recurrent income after graduation can independently support profitability.
