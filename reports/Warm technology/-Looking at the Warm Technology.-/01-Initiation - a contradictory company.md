## A set of data that confuses you

On April 13, 2026, Nuanwa Insight Technology Co., Ltd. submitted a listing application to the main board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange for the second time. JPMorgan Chase and HSBC serve as joint sponsors. The first submission was in September 2025, and it automatically expired because it failed to pass the hearing within 6 months.

This company has a big title: **The largest independent AI technology company in China’s insurance industry** (Source: Frost & Sullivan).

But when you open its prospectus, you'll see a confusing set of data:

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Trends |
|------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| Revenue (100 million yuan) | 6.55 | 9.44 | **10.24** | Continuous growth |
| Revenue growth rate | +89.9% | +44.1% | **+8.5%** | Growth rate plummeted |
| Net loss (100 million yuan) | -2.40 | -1.55 | **-2.69** | Expansion of losses |
| Adjusted net profit (100 million yuan) | 0.19 | 0.58 | **0.61** | Positive for three consecutive years |
| Gross profit margin | 58.3% | 49.8% | **47.2%** | Declining year by year |
| Number of customers (houses) | 112 | 160 | **245** | Continuous expansion |
| ZhongAn’s revenue share | 61.8% | 45.2% | **43.3%** | Slowly declining |
| Customer Retention Rate | 74.2% | 77.7% | **69.6%** | Decline in 2025 |
| Revenue Retention Rate | 160.2% | 134.0% | **96.5%** | Below 100% |

Source: Nuanwa Technology April 2026 updated prospectus, 36Kr, Sina Finance

Putting these numbers together - a company with annual revenue exceeding RMB 1 billion, the number of customers doubling to 245, and coverage of nine of the top ten insurance companies in China has a cumulative net loss of over RMB 660 million in three years, gross profit margin has declined for three consecutive years, and more than 40% of its revenue comes from its own major shareholder**.

What’s even more contradictory is: based on GAAP, the company lost a total of **660 million** over the three years; but based on adjusted net profit, the company made a total of **138 million** over the three years.

If you think this is a "growth-stock pain period" - you may be underestimating the structural issues.
If you think this is "suspected of financial fraud" - you may have misunderstood the accounting treatment.

What this series wants to do is to break apart each layer of contradictions and let you judge for yourself.

## Three sets of contradictions

### Contradiction 1: Net loss vs adjusted net profit - is it making money or losing money?

This is where things get most confusing.

For the whole year of 2025, Nuanwa Technology had a net loss of **269 million yuan**, a year-on-year expansion of **74.2%** - which looks scary. But adjusted net profit for the same period was **60.6 million** - again looking pretty good.

The difference between the two figures is **330 million**. What is this 330 million difference?

Primarily **Losses from changes in fair value of convertible redeemable preferred stock and convertible instruments**. To put it simply: Nuanwa issued preferred shares in previous rounds of financing, and these preferred shares are classified as financial liabilities in accounting terms. As the company's valuation changes, the book value of this "liability" will fluctuate, resulting in profits and losses on paper.

**Key Judgment**: This is not an operating loss, but a "paper loss" caused by accounting processing. Once the company goes public and the preferred shares are converted into common shares, these losses will automatically disappear.

**Contrary view**: Even excluding the impact of preferred shares, the adjusted net profit margin is only **5.9%** (60.6 million / 1.024 billion), which is extremely meager. A company with a gross profit margin of 47% has a net profit margin of only 6%, indicating that the expense structure is still too heavy. Moreover, "adjusted net profit" is a non-standard indicator defined by the company itself, and management has an incentive to embellish it.

### Contradiction 2: There are more and more customers, but old customers cannot be retained

The number of Nuanwa Technology's customers will grow from 112 in 2023 to **245** in 2025, more than doubling in three years. The number of insurance companies covered increased from 62 to **115**, including nine of the top ten insurance companies in China.

Looks good. But two indicators hidden in the prospectus exposed the problem:

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Customer Retention Rate | 74.2% | 77.7% | **69.6%** |
| Revenue Retention Rate | 160.2% | 134.0% | **96.5%** |

Source: Nuanwa Technology prospectus, Sina Finance

**Customer retention rate dropped from 77.7% to 69.6%**—meaning that nearly **30% of old customers will be lost** in 2025.

What's more serious is that the revenue retention rate fell below 100% - 96.5%, which means that the renewal amount of old customers has shrunk. The income from new customers can no longer completely make up for the loss of income from old customers.

**Key judgment**: For a good SaaS/technology service business, the revenue retention rate should continue to be above **120%** (old customers not only renew, but also purchase more services). A very healthy 160% in 2023, but plummeting to 96.5% in two years is a **red flag**.

**Opposite view**: The insurance technology industry is in its early stages, and it is normal for customers to churn after trying it out. The total number of Nuanwa customers is still growing rapidly, and its coverage continues to expand. As long as the final remaining customers are large customers (nine of the top ten insurance companies have covered them), quality is more important than quantity.

### Contradiction 3: Claimed to be "independent" but inseparable from Zhongan

Nuanwa Technology was incubated by Zhongan Insurance in 2018, with Lu Min and Zhongan Online each holding **31.65%**. Zhongan is Nuanwa’s largest shareholder, largest customer, and key business partner.

Over the past three years, ZhongAn Online’s revenue contribution to Nuanwa is as follows:

| Year | Revenue from Zhongan (100 million yuan) | Proportion of total revenue |
|------|---------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 4.00 | **61.8%** |
| 2024 | 4.27 | **45.2%** |
| 2025 | ~4.43 | **43.3%** |

Source: Nuanwa Technology Prospectus

On the surface, ZhongAn’s share has dropped from 62% to 43% – dedependency seems to be advancing. But note: **The absolute amount of revenue from ZhongAn is actually growing** (from 400 million to about 440 million). The decrease in share is not because Zhongan gave less money, but because the income of non-Zhongan customers grew faster.

**Key Judgment**: A so-called "independent" technology company, **more than 40% of its revenue comes from its own major shareholder**. Is this market-oriented business cooperation or "book prosperity" supported by related-party transactions? If ZhongAn decides to build its own team tomorrow to do these things, what will happen to Nuanwa?

**Contrary view**: ZhongAn and Nuanwa co-founded the relationship. ZhongAn holds 31.65% of the shares and is deeply involved in incubation. The interests of both parties are highly bound. ZhongAn has no incentive to "abandon" Nuanwa - building its own team may not be cheaper than outsourcing it to Nuanwa. Moreover, the absolute amount of revenue contributed by ZhongAn is growing, indicating that cooperation is deepening rather than shrinking.

## What exactly is Nuanwa Technology?

If you have to define Nuanwa Technology in one sentence:

> **Nuanwa Technology = an AI technology company that covers the entire process of insurance underwriting + claims settlement. Its annual revenue has just exceeded 1 billion, and its adjusted net profit has just turned positive. However, it is deeply tied to Zhongan Online, and its customer retention rate is declining. **

To understand this company, you need to answer at least these questions:

| Question | Which part of this series should I start |

| What drives the growth of 1 billion revenue? Can it be sustained? | Chapter 03 |
| ZhongAn relies on + losses + decline in retention rate. How big is the risk? | Chapter 04 |
| As the "first insurance AI stock", is it worth paying attention to? | Chapter 04 |

Each question has a clear answer when viewed individually, but when viewed together, the conclusion becomes blurry. This is what makes Nuanwa Technology the most worthy of dismantling as an investment target.

- Customer retention rate dropped to **69.6%**
- Revenue retention rate fell below **100%**

Which of the two faces is more real? Or are both true? This is the question that the next three articles will answer.
