## Series Positioning

Nuanwa Technology is not a company that is widely discussed - it is a company that **most people have never heard of, but that has sparked intense debate inside the insurtech world**.

- The bulls say the insurance AI sector has strong long-term certainty, revenue has almost tripled in three years, and the company now covers 9 of China's top 10 insurers
- The bears say nearly half of revenue depends on support from controlling shareholder ZhongAn Online, losses have exceeded **RMB 660 million** over three years, gross margin has been falling year by year, and its insurance brokerage business has faced repeated complaints

**Both sides are looking at the same company and drawing completely opposite conclusions.** One side sees the aura of "the largest independent AI tech company in insurance," while the other sees dependency on ZhongAn and ongoing losses.

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and only then let you decide whether it is worth watching** - not to decide for you.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - Nuanwa Technology is not yet listed, and many key metrics (such as product-level gross margin splits or customer-level revenue detail) are not fully disclosed, so this series only uses the prospectus and reliable sources as anchors
4. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
