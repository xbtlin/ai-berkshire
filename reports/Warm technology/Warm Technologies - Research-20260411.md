# I. Business model analysis (song-song perspective)

"Do the right thing, do the right thing."

# 1.1 What do you do?

WarmwaTech is an insurance AI technology firm** that provides insurance companies with AI-driven solutions covering the life cycle of insurance transactions.

- **AI insurance solutions** (about 75 per cent of receipts): product development, precision marketing, smart nuclear insurance
- **AI settlement** (about 25 per cent of revenue): Smart claim settlement, anti-fraud, health management

In addition, the company has developed seven major technical products: the medical knowledge base, the Medical Data Platform (MDP), the Business Conservation and Fraud Control, the Business Insurance and Anti-Fraud Platform, the Medical Services Platform, the Customized Insurance Services Platform and the AI Medical Services Platform.

##1.2 Income mode

The income pattern of warm technologies is mainly **the cost of technical services + the trade share**:

- SaaS-based insurance/claims system services for insurance companies
- Technical services charged on the basis of insurance case processing
- Part of the income is from product customization services and wind control advice

** The key question for Yongping: Is this a good business?**

In business models, warm technologies are at the middle of the insurance value chain** - neither insurer (without insurance risk) nor final consumer. This position has both advantages and disadvantages:
- **Li**: Light assets, technology-driven, scalable
- ** Negative**: Limited ability to negotiate upstream (insurance companies), especially overdependence on the security of large shareholders
