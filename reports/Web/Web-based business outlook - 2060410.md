## Key Highlights

**Gaming is the absolute lifeline (82% of revenue):**
- *Fantasy Westward Journey* has run for 22 years and set a record with 3.58 million simultaneous players - a super cash cow
- *Marvel Rivals* generated USD 136 million in global revenue in its first month, accumulated 40 million players, and 2025 was still only a "warm-up year" - the biggest overseas breakthrough
- *Naraka: Bladepoint* topped Steam global best-seller charts on its fourth anniversary
- Overseas revenue is only about 10%, making it the biggest growth lever

**Gap versus Tencent Gaming:**

| Metric | Tencent | NetEase | Ratio |
|--------|---------|---------|------|
| 2025 gaming revenue | RMB 241.6 billion | RMB 92.1 billion | **2.6x** |
| Overseas gaming revenue | RMB 77.4 billion | about RMB 9.0 billion | **~8x** |
| Gap trend | - | - | **Widening** |

The structural reason for the widening gap is that Tencent built its global footprint through acquisitions (Supercell / Riot / Epic), while NetEase relies mainly on self-developed games. Tencent also dominates the mini-program game market, which is worth RMB 53.5 billion.

**Unless Marvel Rivals becomes a global evergreen game generating more than RMB 10 billion a year, the gap will likely keep widening.**

## NetEase in Five Years

**"A high-margin gaming aristocrat" - excellent profitability, a solid moat, but slower growth than Tencent.**

- Gaming will still be more than 80% of revenue, so the company is fundamentally a gaming company
- Overseas expansion is the biggest variable: if Marvel Rivals becomes a major hit, overseas revenue mix could rise from 10% to 20%
- Net margin stays above 30%, and free cash flow remains abundant
- Youdao is a seed for a second growth engine, but its scale is still too small
- The gap with Tencent may widen from 2.6x to about 3x, but absolute profit growth can still remain attractive (annualized 8-10%)
