Why is Neverland so long? Three reasons:

** First, social tie is extremely deep.** The core of Dreams is played around social systems like gangs, marriages, teachers, etc. A player who has been playing for over a decade, with friends in gangs, "wife" in games, tens of thousands of dollars of virtual assets in accounts -- migration costs are extremely high.

** Second, the economic system forms a self-cycle.** Dreams and Travel have a highly complex virtual economic system – a prop trade between players, gem synthesis, and equipment production that creates a self-functioning economic cycle. ** Third, continuous content update.** The unbroken version of the update, the new documentaries, the new play method - keeping old players fresh and attracting new players.

** Antithetical view**: A stronger game of long-term youth also has a life cycle. The core user group of dream-west travels is ageing – the 30-40-year-olds are more and less able to consume. Young users are losing interest in this "classical" net.

Eight times the gap in overseas income is not a game or two — a structural gap caused by two completely different globalization paths.

** Path to the Quest: Buying time with money.** Acquisition of Supercell (tribal conflict), holding shares of Riot (hero coalition), investing in Epic (Bold Night) - Direct access to top products and teams worldwide through capital operations.

** Web-friendly path: self-researching.** The cost of closing seven overseas workshops is a concrete reflection of the cost of the route, depending on the product that they have developed to hit the global market - lower costs, but lower success rates.

# # Seven workshops closed lessons

The closure of seven overseas workshops is not a small number — it illustrates the systemic difficulties encountered in the attempt to establish local research and development teams overseas.

The problem is that:** The core competitiveness of game research and development depends largely on cultural understanding.** The Internet is easily culturally sensitive in China’s markets (as evidenced by 23 years of Chinese cultural practice in Dreams), but it is difficult to replicate it in the markets of Europe and America and Japan and Korea.

After closing the studio, the Internet-friendly overseas strategy shifted more to "research and development in China for global distribution" – a model that is the case with the vagaries. This reduces the cost risks, but also limits the possibility of "localizing innovation" in overseas markets.

** Reverse view**: Seven studios closed can also be understood as "timely stop damage" - Ding is aware that the path of overseas localization research and development is not working, and it is shut down decisively, concentrating resources on "China R & D+ Global Distribution". This is not necessarily bad from the capital allocation point of view.
