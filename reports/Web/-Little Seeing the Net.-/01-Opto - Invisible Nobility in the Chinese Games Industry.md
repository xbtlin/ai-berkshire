## A set of data that makes you re-examine NetEase

In 2025, NetEase handed over an excellent report card:

| Indicators | 2025 Data | What It Means |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Total revenue | 112.6 billion yuan | Hundreds of billion-level Internet company |
| Game revenue | 92.1 billion yuan (+11%) | China’s second largest game company |
| Non-GAAP net profit | 37.3 billion yuan (net profit rate 33%) | For every 3 yuan earned, 1 yuan is net profit |
| Gross profit margin | 64.3% | Close to the level of luxury goods companies |
| Free cash flow | ~49.6 billion yuan (capital expenditure is only 1.1 billion) | The money earned hardly needs to be reinvested |
| Net cash | 163.5 billion yuan | 31% of market capitalization |
| R&D investment | 17.7 billion yuan (accounting for 15.7% of revenue) | Higher than most Chinese Internet companies |

Net interest rate of 33%, free cash flow of 49.6 billion, and capital expenditure of only 1.1 billion - these numbers translated into the vernacular are: **NetEase is a machine that can continue to "print money" with little need for "refueling". **

But at what level is the market pricing it?

| Valuation Metrics | Value | What It Means |
|----------|------|----------|
| Stock Price | $113.61 | — |
| Market capitalization | $72.9 billion (approximately 530 billion yuan) | — |
| PE (GAAP) | ~15 times | China Internet average is lower |
| PE (Non-GAAP) | ~13x | Closer to actual profitability |
| Forward PE | ~12.3x | Based on analysts’ consensus expectations |
| EV/EBITDA | 9.35x | Cheaper after cash |
| P/FCF | 9.9x | ~10 years of free cash flow to buy the entire company |
| Net cash to market value | 31% | 30% of market value is cash on hand |

Translate this into vernacular: The market price for NetEase is equivalent to "the 163.5 billion in cash on the account will be given to you at a 30% discount, and the business is only worth less than 10 years of profits." **

A company with a net profit margin of 33%, free cash flow of 49.6 billion, and still growing is priced at 13 times PE-either the market has seen some serious problems, or the market has made a mistake.

Figuring out which situation is the case is the question this series aims to answer.

## Three common misconceptions about NetEase

Before we dismantle them, let’s break down three of the most widespread misconceptions.

### Illusion 1: NetEase is just a game company

Open up most people’s impressions of NetEase—Fantasy Westward Journey, Onmyoji, and Eternal Calamity. "Aren't you just making games?"

This judgment is not entirely wrong, but it is not entirely right either.

Games indeed account for 82% of NetEase’s total revenue and are the absolute core. But if you only see games, you will ignore two other businesses that are undergoing qualitative changes:

| Business | Revenue 2025 | Key Changes |
|------|-----------|---------|
| NetEase Cloud Music | 7.76 billion | Operating profit 1.62 billion, +38.5% year-on-year - has passed the profit turning point |
| Youdao | 5.9 billion (+5%) | Among them, AI subscription revenue is 400 million (growth rate of more than 50%) - switching tracks |

Cloud Music has gone from "losing money every year" to "earning 1.6 billion annually." Youdao has transformed from "online education" to "AI education". The two businesses have a combined revenue of 13.7 billion - not a small amount for any independent company.

**NetEase is indeed a company with games as its core, but it is not just "a game company". ** A more accurate description is: a content group with games as its engine and music and education as its two wings.

**Contrary view**: Cloud Music and Youdao together only account for 18% of total revenue, and their contribution to overall profits is even smaller. In the final analysis, NetEase's fate still depends on the game - if there is a problem with the game, Liangyi cannot save it.

### Illusion 2: NetEase is Tencent’s “little brother”

"Tencent is the boss, NetEase is the second" - this sentence is correct in terms of industry rankings. But it would be a big mistake to conclude that "NetEase is not worth studying alone".

Take a look at a set of comparisons:

| Indicators | Tencent (Games) | NetEase (Games) | Gap |
|------|-----------|-----------|------|
| Game revenue in 2025 | 241.6 billion | 92.1 billion | 2.6 times |
| Overseas game revenue | ~77.4 billion | ~9 billion (estimated) | ~8 times |
| Mini program games | 53.5 billion market exclusive | Basically absent | Infinite |

In terms of scale, Tencent is 2.6 times larger than NetEase. But size isn’t everything – look at another set of numbers:

| Indicators | Tencent (overall) | NetEase |
|------|-----------|------|
| Non-GAAP net profit margin | ~28% (estimate) | **33%** |
| Gross profit margin | ~53% (estimate) | **64.3%** |
| Capital expenditure/revenue ratio | High | Only 1% (1.1 billion/112.6 billion) |

**NetEase’s money-making efficiency is higher than Tencent’s. ** Gross profit margin is 11 percentage points higher, net profit margin is 5 percentage points higher, and capital expenditures are almost negligible.

To use an analogy: Tencent is a global Internet empire with businesses covering social networking, games, finance, and cloud computing. NetEase is more like a "boutique workshop" focusing on content - not that large in scale, but with extremely high profit margins and abundant cash flow.

**This is not a relationship between "eldest brother and younger brother", but a relationship between "empire and nobles". ** The empire does everything, the nobles only do things that make money.

**Contrary view**: High profit margins may be due to insufficient aggressive investment. Tencent has acquired game companies (Supercell, Riot, Epic equity) around the world and established a global moat. Part of NetEase's high profit margin comes from "spending less" - but spending less also means less layout, and it may fall further behind in the long run.

### Illusion 3: Profits are growing = everything is fine

Non-GAAP net profit in 2025 is 37.3 billion, a considerable growth rate. But profit growth does not mean there are no hidden dangers.

There are at least three problems hidden behind the beautiful profit figures:

**First, game revenue is highly concentrated. ** 82% of revenue comes from games, and game revenue is highly dependent on several evergreen products - Fantasy Westward Journey, which has been in operation for 23 years, Everlasting Eternal Journey, and Eggman Party. Once the life cycle of a core product comes to an end, where will the replacement be?

**Second, overseas is almost blank. ** Overseas revenue only accounts for about 10%, while Tencent’s overseas revenue has reached 30%. The closure of 7 overseas studios shows that the road to overseas is far more difficult than imagined. Whether Marvel Contest can reverse this situation is the biggest variable.

**Third, the 53.5 billion mini program game market is completely absent. ** China's mini program game market will be worth 53.5 billion in 2025, and will be monopolized by Tencent - NetEase has almost no presence on this fast-growing track.

**It is a fact that profits are growing, but the sustainability of growth depends on the answers to the three questions above. **
