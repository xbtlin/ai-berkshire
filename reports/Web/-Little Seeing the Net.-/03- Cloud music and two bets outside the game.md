# The meaning of profit-making

The history of Internet-based music is a story of "Young Arts and Culture Growing Up and Making Money."

Early on, cloud music was known for its "love" – users wrote their own words in comment areas, independent musicians found their first listeners on the platform. But they were not making money: copyright costs, fee rates, and operating efficiency – long-term losses.

The turnaround occurred in 2024-2025: more refined copyright procurement, optimized membership, control of operating costs - profit margin reversed from negative, with operating profits of $1.62 billion in 2025, an increase of 38.5 per cent.

**16.2 billion of net profit is only 4% of the total profit of $3.73 billion – not a small number. But the point is not today's absolute value, but rather the trend.** A platform that has just proved profitable, with a profit rate rising rapidly — the curve's slope is more informative than the absolute one.

# 1 million independent musicians: the most underestimated moat

On the Internet, IYC music brings together over 1 million independent musicians — a figure that is far ahead of China's online music platform.

Why does independent musicians matter?

** First, copyright costs are extremely low.** The cost of copyrights for independent musicians is much lower than for three major record companies (the Global, Sony, Warner). More users are left on the platform because of independent music, which means less reliance on "high-cost copyright".

** Second, to form a different content.** The song on QQ music, and the soda music, too. But one million independent musicians started the work on the Net Cloud -- other platforms may not find or find cultural culture without comment.

** Third, community viscous.** Cloud music comment areas, song-list culture, private FM - these functions are also available on other platforms, but users of cloud music have significantly more intensive use and emotional connections to these functions. The interactive networks that are formed between one million independent musicians and users are difficult to replicate from other platforms.

** Anti-conversation**: Independent musicians have limited business value. Less than 1% of the 1 million musicians are likely to generate real income. Most users still have a core demand for listening to mainstream music – and the mainstream music platforms do. "Censorship of the Zone" is good, but it is hard to make it a profit.

# AI subscription: The real problem behind 400 million dollars

400 million AI subscriptions, more than 50% increase — the numbers look good, but a few things need to be taken into account.

** The first $400 million is in the total of $5.9 billion, only 7%.** AI subscriptions to the "Small" that still exist in the business, far from becoming a "big tree".

** Second, how long can the 50% increase last?** AI educational subscriptions are a brand-new category -- how much users are willing to pay for "AI tutoring English" "AI helping to write" "AI translating" for how long, and there is no mature reference system. The speed of the increase may be due to a low base.

** Third, the pattern of competition is completely uncertain.** On the AI education track, there are not only a few paths, but also science and science, a good future, and a variety of large model companies (enthus, Ali's generalities, byte bean buns). The advantage of a road is the combination of "education gene plus AI's capabilities" — if the educational capacity of the universal model is good enough, specialized education AI tools may be marginalized.

# Sub-large model: the possibility of differentiation

There's a big model of the way to focus on the educational scene -- it's a "vertical route."

The advantages are clear: the educational landscape requires great accuracy (the math question cannot be miscalculated), adaptation to the level of different students (due to the teaching of material), and understanding of China’s educational system and examination system. These are not the most common models.

But the disadvantage is also clear: the cost of vertical large models is not low, but commercial ceilings may not be high. The ultimate willingness to pay for education depends on parents and students – while Chinese parents are willing to pay for education, but the willingness to pay for the "AI tool" is untested.

** Reasonable expectation**: There is a pre-existing advantage in AI education, where AI subscription income may increase to $1 billion-15 billion over the next three years. But it may take more than five years to achieve a stable profit and make a significant profit overall.

** The counter-view**: AI education may be a "winner-takes-all" track -- who has the best model, who has the most data, and who eats most of the market. A pro-coupled model may not technically lead the big generic model giants in the long run. And a $5.9 billion collection, in the context of AI's increasingly "burning money" investment, may not support sustained high-intensity research and development inputs.
