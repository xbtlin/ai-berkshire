# II. Business essence: "The right business"

One sentence definition:** The Yangtze Power is an asset-based business that converts natural precipitation into low-cost electricity and long-term dividends on the scarcest hydropower nodes of the Ganges.**

The good news of this business is straightforward: there is no fuel cost, there is long-term demand, assets are long-lived, marginal costs are low, and cash flows are stable. Fire power is subject to fluctuations in coal prices, wind is subject to fluctuations in consumption and output, and large hydropower, once built, has few variables at the end of the cost.

The business model is not subscription-based, but has features similar to “recurring income”: water supply, electricity generation, access to the Internet, and grid settlements. The customer is not a consumer, but a grid and a power system; demand is not created by marketing, but by social demand and power system movement.

It's not as clear as that: it's not a high-growth business. The development of high-quality large hydropower resources is complete, and the future of Yangjia power is more like "stock asset efficiency + debt decline + dividends cashing out" than "loading double + income up."
