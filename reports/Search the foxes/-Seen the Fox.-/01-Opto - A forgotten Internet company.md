## A confusing set of numbers

There is a crack between Sohu, which was listed on Nasdaq in 2000, and the actual state of the company 26 years later, which is almost impossible to explain with normal logic. There is $1.2 billion in cash on the books, and the market value is only $360 million. You spend 1 yuan to buy, which corresponds to 3.2 yuan of net cash on the books - the market valuation of the business part is negative.

This is not a clerical error. Here are the real numbers as of May 2026:

| Indicators | Latest data | Notes |
|------|----------|------|
| Current Stock Price (May 2026) | **About $14** | Source: Yahoo Finance |
| Current market capitalization | **About $360 million** | About 26M ADS in circulation |
| Cash on account + short-term investments + long-term time deposits | **About $1.2 billion** | Source: 2025 Annual Report |
| Net cash (net of all debt) | **Approximately $1.16 billion** | Interest-bearing debt only $036 million |
| Net cash/market cap | **About 3.2x** | Cash on hand is more than 3 times market cap |
| Enterprise value (EV) | **About -$800 million** | Negative number - the market "discounts" you to take the business |
| 2025 full-year revenue | **$584 million** (-2.4%) | 5-year cumulative shrinkage of approximately 30% |
| 2025 Non-GAAP Net Loss | **-$51 million** | True loss excluding one-time tax reversals |
| P/B | **0.32 times** | 3 net breakout |

**The market not only values the business at zero, but also offers you $800 million to "take" it. This is an extreme case in global capital markets. **

But if you think this is "picking up money" - you probably don't understand.
If you think this is a "value trap" - you may not understand it.

## What exactly is Sohu?

Most people's impression of Sohu is still in the era of "three major portals". But the Sohu in 2026 is already two completely different companies from the Sohu in that memory.

### Revenue composition (2025 annual report, source: PR Newswire)

| Business segments | Revenue in 2025 | Year-on-year | Proportion | True nature |
|---------|-----------|------|------|---------|
| Online games (Changyou) | **$506 million** | +1% | **86.6%** | Cash cow, relying on Tian Long Ba Bu IP |
| Marketing Services (Advertising) | $060 million | **-18%** | 10.3% | Continued shrinkage |
| Others | $018 million | — | 3.1% | Negligible |

**The truth is: Nearly 90% of Sohu’s revenue comes from the game subsidiary Changyou, and the core is the IP of “Dragon Babu” launched in 2007. **

Portal? Already marginalized. Sohu Video? Monthly income is less than 20 million, a fraction of iQiyi. Sogou? Sold to Tencent in 2021 for $1.18 billion.

So Sohu’s real picture today is:

> **Sohu = a medium-sized game company wearing a portal coat + a US$1.2 billion cash management fund + Zhang Chaoyang’s personal IP live broadcast studio. **

Don't be confused by the four words "Internet portal". Sohu and the company you imagined are no longer the same thing.

## Three common misunderstandings about Sohu

### Misunderstanding 1: Sohu is still one of the "three major portals"

Around 2000, Sina, Sohu, and NetEase were collectively known as the three major Internet portals in China. But 25 years later, the fate of the three companies is very different:

| Company | Positioning in 2000 | Current Situation in 2026 | Current Market Capitalization |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **NetEase** | One of the portals | Transforming the game empire (Fantasy Westward Journey, Eggman Party) | **About $70 billion** |
| **Sina** | King of portals | Privatized and delisted in 2020, parasitic on Weibo | Delisted |
| **Sohu** | One of the portals | Portals are shrinking, games are eating their laurels, and videos are marginalized | **About $360 million** |

**NetEase and Sohu had almost the same starting point—they were founded in the same year, came from the same portal, and were listed overseas. The difference in market cap after 25 years is about 194x. **(Source: Public financial reports of each company, Yahoo Finance)

The core difference is that NetEase has found the trillion-level second curve of games, while Sohu has tried three second curves - Sogou, Video, and Changyou - all of which failed or were divested.

**Contrary view**: Although Sohu has a small market capitalization, its cash on hand is far greater than its market capitalization and its net assets are solid. It is not a "loser" from an asset perspective. This view makes sense, but it points to liquidation value, not operating value—these two things are fundamentally different.

### Misunderstanding 2: Zhang Chaoyang’s physics class can save Sohu

Zhang Chaoyang has been giving live lectures on physics (quantum mechanics, theory of relativity) every week on Sohu Video since 2021, and has unexpectedly become popular. As of the end of 2025, there have been more than **240 lectures and 24,000 minutes of content**, with approximately **15 million fans on the entire network, and a book of the same name has also been published. (Source: CCTV, China Popular Science Network)

The theme of the 2025-2026 New Year's Eve speech is "Solar System", and the hard-core science popularization effect is remarkable.

**But the problem is: the more popular the physics class becomes, the more Sohu’s advertising revenue shrinks. **

Marketing services revenue in 2025 will be **-18%** year-on-year. Zhang Chaoyang’s personal IP traffic has not been effectively converted into Sohu’s commercial monetization.

What’s even more fatal is: Zhang Chaoyang’s IP is a personal asset, not a company asset. Fans follow the person "Zhang Chaoyang", not the platform "Sohu". Once Zhang Chaoyang goes off the air (he turns 62 this year), Sohu's last vestiges of public recognition will disappear.

**Contrary view**: Zhang Chaoyang’s IP has brought differentiation to the Sohu brand. Although the advertising CPM of knowledge marketing is low, the brand recall is 23% high (source: Sohu public information). In the long term, there is room for brand premium. This makes some sense, but the scale is too small - in the **850 billion yuan** Chinese Internet advertising market, Sohu's share is less than 60,000 (source: industry research estimates), and personal IP cannot drive an advertising business with a statistical error level.

### Misunderstanding 3: 1.2 billion in cash = safety cushion

This is the most common argument used by bulls: With $1.2 billion on the books, the market value is only $360 million. Isn’t it just “4 cents to buy 1 yuan”?

The math is correct. But this argument has an implicit premise: **cash can reach shareholders. **

Sohu’s situation is:

- Cash is mostly deposited in RMB in VIE structures in China
- Dividend remittance is subject to foreign exchange controls + 10% withholding tax
- Sohu has **not paid dividends for a long time** in its history
- Non-GAAP loss of $0.51 billion in 2025, business is burning cash

**But there are also positive changes**: Sohu completed a **$150 million stock repurchase plan** in 2025 (approximately 8.1 million ADS shares have been repurchased as of February 2026, costing approximately $106 million). This is an important signal for management to move from "silence" to "returning to shareholders". (Source: Sohu 2025 Q4 financial report)

**But the opposite side also has to say**: Although the ratio of buybacks to market capitalization is high (about 25-30%), it shows that management believes that the stock price is undervalued. But the question is - is the buyback a one-time action or an ongoing policy? If a long-term mechanism is not formed, the discount will not disappear.

## What is the essential problem of Sohu?

After reading the above data, Sohu’s core contradiction is already very clear:

**On one side: extremely cheap valuation + cash reserves far above market value + management starting buybacks. **
**The other side is: all main businesses are in structural decline + the core IP has longevity + the founder is 62 years old and has no successor. **

This is not a company "waiting for a revival." This is a company that needs to answer "what's the endgame?"

There are three possible endings:

| Final outcome | Probability judgment | Impact on shareholders |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Privatization and delisting** | High (refer to the precedents of Changyou and Sina) | Depending on the premium, retail investors may be liquidated at a low price |
| **Continuous repurchases + dividends release value** | Medium | Slowly reduce discounts, but patience is required |
| **Maintain the status quo ("living dead")** | Higher | Annual business losses erode cash, and discounts will not be used for a long time |

**Different final assumptions will lead to completely different investment judgments. ** This is the question that the next three articles will answer.
