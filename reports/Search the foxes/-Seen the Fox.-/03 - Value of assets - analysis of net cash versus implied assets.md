The bulk of the cash in search of the fox is stored in Chinese territory in renminbi.


# Eight, this core conclusion

1. ** Net cash on search accounts is about $1.15 billion, 3.2 times the market value.** Even after deduction of exit taxes, loss preparation, transaction costs, the adjusted net cash balance is about $840 million, or 2.3 times the market value.

2. **The independent valuation of the free-flowing game is about $800-1.2 billion**, but the market gives a negative implied valuation. This means that the market considers business to be a "burden" rather than an "asset".

3. **The total reasonable valuation of the Division plus was $1.4-2.2 billion, with market value only $360 million, or 75-83 per cent discount.** This discount is extremely rare among the global listing companies.

4. ** The underlying reason for the discount is not that the market does not know, but that the market does not believe that cash reaches shareholders.** The VIE structure, management history, no business growth, the liquidity dilemma of the small and medium-sized shares - the four-fold factor superimposed.

5. ** The $150 million buyback in 2025 is a key positive sign**, but a continuing need is needed to make a real difference in discounts.
