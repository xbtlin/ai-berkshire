## Series Positioning

XtalPi is one of the **most controversial AI concept stocks** in the Hong Kong market.

- The bulls say that AI changing drug discovery is a civilizational-level trend, XtalPi is a world-leading AI + robotics drug-discovery platform, and the DoveTree **USD 6 billion** deal plus Lilly's **USD 345 million** renewal prove platform value
- The bears say the stock trades at a **~40x P/S**, has raised **HKD 6 billion** through three placements in less than two years after listing, depends on a single customer for **45%** of revenue, and there is still no AI-designed drug approved anywhere in the world

**Both sides are looking at the same company and reaching completely opposite conclusions.** The disagreement is not about the data - it is about how to judge what stage AI pharma is actually in.

This series aims to **lay out the facts and make the risks explicit**, so that you can decide for yourself whether this is a company worth following.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Do not invent data** - AI pharma still has many undisclosed metrics, so this series only uses reliable sources and marks estimates as "estimated"
4. **Do not predict the stock price** - provide the framework, not the answer
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
