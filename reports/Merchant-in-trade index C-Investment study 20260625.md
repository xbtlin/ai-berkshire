# Run the leverage analysis

The White Wine industry has a strong leverage feature:
- ** Positive leverage**: When production increases, fixed costs (shelter pool depreciation, stock of gin) are spread thin and marginal profit margins are extremely high
- ** Negative leverage**: High-end alcohol can maintain profit margins by controlling premium prices when demand falls, but medium- and low-end alcohol faces a flat drop in prices
- Current stage: industry is in a negative-leveraging release period, high-end robust, sub-end and middle-end pressure severe

# Ask: Where is the business?

** Description**: White wine is a business of "the older the better the better" - the more the wine the better, the higher the brand, the thicker the stock of the gin, and the less the investment in research and development is needed, and not the technology will be subverted.

But the core problem is:** demand is undergoing structural changes** – business dinner scenes are shrinking, young people are not inclined to drink white wine, and consumption is continuing pressure. This is not cyclical, but rather a long-term trend from consumption intergenerational shifts.
