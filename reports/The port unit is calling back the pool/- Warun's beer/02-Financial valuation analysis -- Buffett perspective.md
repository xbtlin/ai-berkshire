- After the liquor impairment was cleared, the pressure on goodwill was significantly reduced

## 2. In-depth analysis of profitability

### 2.1 Beer Business: Profit Engine

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Beer revenue (100 million yuan) | 364.9 | 364.9 | Flat |
| Beer sales (thousands of liters) | 1087 | 1103 | +1.4% |
| Ton price (yuan/thousand liters) | 3355 | 3308 | -1.4% |
| Beer gross profit margin | 41.1% | Expected to be 42%+ | Continuous improvement |
| Beer EBITDA | ~8.5 billion | Expected to be 9 billion+ | Continued growth |

**The truth about the drop in ton prices**: The ton price will drop slightly by 1.4% in 2025, but this is not a failure of high-endization. The reason is that the base includes the restorative growth of low-end products, and sales of sub-high-end and above still achieved mid-to-high single-digit growth. From a structural point of view, the proportion of high-end products continues to increase, but the rebound in low-end volume has lowered the average.

**Gross profit margin continues to expand**: from approximately 37% in 2020 to more than 42% in 2025, indicating that product structure upgrades are truly transformed into profitability.

### 2.2 Liquor Business: Cleared Baggage

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Liquor revenue (100 million yuan) | 21.5 | 15.0 | -30.4% |
| Liquor EBITDA (100 million yuan) | 8.1 | 2.6 | -67.3% |
| Impairment of goodwill (100 million yuan) | 0 | 28.8 | One-time liquidation |

The liquor business currently accounts for only about 4% of revenue, and even if it continues to deteriorate, the overall impact will be limited. In turn, if the industry stabilizes in 2026, any improvement will be purely incremental.

## 3. Capital return and capital allocation

### 3.1 ROE analysis

China Resources Beer's ROE composition (DuPont analysis):
- Net interest rate: ~15% (excluding non-profit)
- Asset turnover: ~1.2x
- Equity multiplier: ~1.7x

ROE is about 18-20%, which is an excellent level among consumer goods companies. More importantly, as high-endization advances and capital expenditures decline, ROE is expected to continue to increase. SPDB International predicts that ROE will increase from 19.6% to 22.6% in 2026-2028.

### 3.2 Capital allocation capability

What Buffett values most is how management distributes retained profits:

**Dividend**: The full-year dividend in 2025 is 1.021 yuan per share, a year-on-year increase of 34.3%, and the dividend payout ratio is as high as 98.2%. This shows that management believes that there are no better investment opportunities at present and is willing to return profits to shareholders - which is the right choice.

**Repurchase**: The company will continue to repurchase shares in 2025 to further increase the value per share.

**Capital expenditure**: The peak of capacity construction has passed, maintenance capital expenditure accounts for about 80-90% of depreciation, and the free cash flow conversion rate is high.

**Acquisition Discipline**: The liquor acquisition was a failure, but management has admitted its mistake through impairment. Future capital allocation discipline remains to be seen.

### 3.3 Market value created by every additional 1 yuan of retained profit

From 2020 to 2025, China Resources Beer's accumulated retained profits were approximately 10 billion yuan (after deducting dividends), and the changes in market value during the same period were relatively stable. Taking into account the one-time impact of liquor impairment, the actual value creation efficiency is not bad - the main beer business creates approximately 1.2-1.5 yuan in market value for every 1 yuan of profit retained.

## 4. Intrinsic value estimation

### 4.1 DCF valuation (conservative assumptions)

**Core Assumptions**:
-Beer revenue will increase by 3-5% annually from 2026 to 2030 (volume parity and price increase)
- Net profit margin gradually increased from 15% to 17%
- The liquor business will maintain its current level without increasing or decreasing.
- WACC：8%
- Sustainable growth rate: 2%
-Capital expenditure as a percentage of revenue remains at 5-6%

**Valuation results**:
- Free cash flow forecast in 2026 is about 5.5-6 billion yuan
- DCF valuation corresponds to approximately HK$35-40 per share
- The current stock price is about HK$30, with 15-30% upside potential

### 4.2 Relative valuation

| Valuation methodology | Core parameters | Valuation (HKD per share) |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| Excluding non-PE | 15x 2026E excluding non-net profit | ~33 HKD |
| EV/EBITDA | 10x 2026E EBITDA | ~HKD35 |
| Dividend discount | 3% dividend rate corresponding | ~34 HKD |
| DCF | WACC 8%, g=2% | ~37HKD |

**Reasonable valuation range: HK$33-38**

### 4.3 Comparison with peers

| Company | PE (2026E) | ROE | Dividend rate | Revenue growth rate |
|------|-------------|-----|--------|----------|
| China Resources Beer | ~15x | ~19% | ~3.5% | 2-3% |
| Tsingtao Beer | ~18x | ~15% | ~3% | 1-2% |

*The extremely high ROE of Chongqing Beer is due to the asset-light model + high leverage

Among comparable companies, China Resources Beer's ROE is at an excellent level, but its valuation is the cheapest. This is partly due to the impact of liquor impairment on market confidence.

## 5. Safety Margin Assessment

### 5.1 Current Valuation Level

- Current stock price: approximately HK$30 (May 2026)
- Corresponding to non-PE deduction in 2026: about 13-14 times
- Corresponding free cash flow yield: about 7-8%
- Corresponding dividend rate: approximately 3.5%

### 5.2 Downside risk calculation

**Pessimistic Scenario**:
- High-endization has stagnated, and ton prices have fallen instead of rising.
- Liquor continues to deteriorate and requires a second impairment
- Macroeconomic drag on beer consumption accelerates shrinkage
- Corresponding valuation: PE fell back to 12 times, stock price is about HK$25 (down about 17%)

**Extreme Scenario**:
- Industry price war breaks out
- Problems with Heineken cooperation
- Corresponding valuation: PE fell back to 10 times, stock price is about HK$20 (down about 33%)

### 5.3 Safety margin judgment

**★★★★☆(Plenty)**

The current valuation has reflected the pessimistic expectations of liquor impairment, and the profit growth and high dividends of the main beer business provide good downside protection. Buying a consumer leader with an ROE of 19% and a low double-digit growth rate at 13-14 times PE has a sufficient margin of safety but is not extremely cheap.

## 6. Core judgment from Buffett’s perspective

### 6.1 Do you have "economic franchise"?

**Partially available**. China Resources Beer has pricing power (high-endization proves this), has consumer recognition (Snowflake brand), and has an unreplicable distribution network. However, compared with Moutai and Coca-Cola, the brand franchise in the beer industry is relatively weak - consumer loyalty depends more on channel access rather than "must have it".

### 6.2 Is "buying excellent companies at reasonable prices" true?

**Established**. 15 times PE, 19% ROE, 3.5% dividend yield, 98% dividend payout ratio - this is a high-quality cash flow company that is willing to return profits to shareholders, and its valuation is in a reasonably low range.

### 6.3 Can you understand ten years from now?

**High probability in ten years**:
- China's beer market is still an oligopoly, with China Resources still the top two
- High-endization further deepens, and ton prices and profit margins continue to increase
- Total volume may drop from 35 million kilolitres to 28-30 million kilolitres
- Liquor business may have been sold/independent/contributed slightly

There is a high probability that EPS ten years from now will be higher than today, which provides confidence for long-term holding.
