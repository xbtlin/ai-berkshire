## Research background

China Resources Beer is the leading company in China's beer industry with the largest sales volume. It owns the "Snowflake" brand and the operating rights of Heineken in China. The company is in the harvest period of high-end transformation, with sales of mid-range and above products accounting for more than 50%.

The 2025 annual report is the first annual report after the new chairman of the board of directors, Zhao Chunwu, took over from Hou Xiaohai. The company made a provision for goodwill impairment of 2.877 billion yuan for the liquor business (Jinsha Liquor Industry), which was cleared in one go, resulting in a 28.87% year-on-year decrease in reported net profit. However, after deducting impairment losses, the net profit attributable to the parent company was 5.724 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 19.6%. The main beer business continued to improve.

## Core investment logic

1. **The high-endization of beer enters the harvest period**: The sales volume of sub-high-end and above products continues to grow in mid-to-high single digits, and Heineken + Laoxue + Hongjue have strong growth rates
2. **Liquor impairment will be cleared and then proceed with ease**: 2.877 billion goodwill will be impaired in one time, and the liquor business is expected to stabilize in 2026
3. **Strong cash flow + high dividends**: Operating cash flow in 2024 is 6.928 billion yuan (+67%), and the dividend payout rate in 2025 is 98.2%
4. **Reasonable valuation**: Non-PE deduction is about 15 times, corresponding to 19-20% ROE, which is cost-effective among consumer leaders
