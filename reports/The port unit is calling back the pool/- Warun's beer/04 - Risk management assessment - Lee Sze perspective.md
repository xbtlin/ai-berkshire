##1.2 Core risk depth analysis

Risk one: uncertainty about management change

** Background**:
- He resigned as Chairman of the Board in June 2025 and joined the ruling group in January 2026
- Zhao Chunwu took over as Chairman of the Board in September 2025
- The members of the management team like Kim Han-kwan have been adjusted accordingly.

** Analysis**:
Huo Xiaohai, the core designer of the Hua Yun beer high-end strategy, completed a fundamental shift from "sale to king" to "quality first." His departure raised market concerns about strategic continuity.

Zhao Chunwu, former president of Hua Yun's beer, was the core executive of Hsiao Hai's era and was highly familiar with his business. But the leap from "executor" to "leader" was a smooth one, and it was a matter of observation:
- The annual report 2025 was the first in Zhao Chun Wu, and the 19.6 per cent increase in non-performance indicates that implementation is not out of line
- Fifteen-five, "Stable Growth, New Deal."
- Significant impairment of clean liquor goodwill, which means that the new management is not shy.

** Conclusion**: Short-term (1 year) risk is manageable and strategic inertia is sufficiently maintained.

Risk two: high-end rhythm influenced by macro

** Logical chain**:
The economy is down, the social scene is down, the consumption of high-end beer is down, the price of tons is slowing down, and profit is growing less than expected.

** Reverse argument**:
- In 2025, the number of medium and high units increased at the lower end and above, even in the case of macro weakness
- "The Ratchet Effect": Consumers are not easily downgraded after upgrading.
- Huo Yu's price belt is comprehensive, and even some consumers are downgraded to take over their own products.

** Conclusion**: The general direction of high-endization is not reversed, but the pace may slow. Annual increases in tonnage between 3 and 5 per cent may fall to 1-3 per cent.
