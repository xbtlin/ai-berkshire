# II. Investment arguments

#2.1 Core is multi-logistic

Argument one: beer high-end into harvest

** Evidence**:
- Increase in sales of sub-high end and above in high units over the years
- The growth of the giraffe sales is nearly 20%, snow + 60%, Red Lord + 100%
- 50 per cent sales in middle and above, close to 25 per cent at the lower end
- Māori rate increased from 37 per cent in 2020 to 42 per cent in 2025
- 19.6 per cent increase in net profit deductions in 2025

** Logical**: The upward trend in consumption + "Little drink, good drink" drives the product structure upwards, and Hua Yu, with the strongest channels of implementation and the most complete brand matrix, is the biggest beneficiary of the high end trend.

Argument two: White wine is down and light

** Evidence**:
- One-time discount of 2,877 million goodwill in 2025
- The white-sales camp received only 4 per cent, even though the effects of the continued deterioration were limited
- Management's saying 2026 is a good time.
- The balance sheet is cleaner after the impairment is cleared.

** Logic**: Market pessimism about the impairment of white wine has been fully factored into stock prices, the impairment of Xu Yu beers has become a "pure beer company", and valuation should revert to the intrinsic value of the beer-based industry.

Argument three: strong cash flow plus very high shareholder returns

** Evidence**:
- Operating cash flow of $6,928 million in 2024 (+67 per cent)
- 98.2 per cent distribution rate in 2025, $1.021 per share rate (+34.3 per cent)
- Repurchase of ongoing shares
- Capital spending is on the decline.

** Logical**: The build-up of capacity has passed and free cash flows have been released. Management has passed confidence to the market through high points + buyback. 3.5% dividends + low double-digit profit growth, providing a potential total return of 15% + annualized.

#2: #2.2 Core view logic

# Inverse I: Totals are shrinking for a long time

China’s total beer production has fallen from a peak of 5062 million litres to about 35 million litres, and demographic changes (age + low birth rate) will cause further contraction. Will the high-end tonnage increase continue to cover a decline in total coverage?

** Rebuttal**: Even if the total is reduced by another 20 to 28 million litres, the revenue can be maintained at a positive rate as long as the price of the tonnage increases by 3-5% per annum. More important, the profit increase is much faster than the profit increase (structural optimization + lower cost + impact of scale).

# # Inverse two: Management change risks

Hsiaohai is the soul of a high-end strategy whose departure may lead to a decline in its implementation.

** Rebuttal**: High-end has changed from "strategic choice" to "organizational capability" and Zhao Chunwu is a long-term implementer with a manageable risk of continuity. The first annual deduction of 19.6% is a positive sign. But it takes one to two years to observe.

# # Inverse three: Valuation is not cheap anymore

Deduction from the non-PE is about 15 times less expensive than historical low (12-13 times).

** Rebuttal**: 15 times the PE corresponds to 19% ROE and low double digits growth, and PEG is about 1.2-1.5 times more than reasonable in consumer goods. And 2025 is the low base year for impairment of white wine, and the profit rate may accelerate in 2026.

Hua Yun's beer is a leader in a business that ** is in a certain upward trajectory. The high-end transition in the beer industry is now in harvest, the white wine packs were cleared in 2025, and management demonstrated shareholder-friendliness through high dividends and buybacks.

It is not the hot ten-fold "ten-year" sign, but it is a ** functioning cash flow machine** — a certainty that is itself valuable in the current uncertain market environment.
