### First half of fiscal year 2026 (April-September 2025)

| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year |
|------|------|------|
| Revenue | HK$38.986 billion | -1.1% |
| Operating profit | HK$6.823 billion | +0.7% |
| Profit attributable to shareholders | HK$2.534 billion | +0.1% |

### Interpretation of financial characteristics

1. **Significant revenue decline but strong profit resilience**: Revenue fell by 17.5% but net profit fell by only 9%. The reason is that the gross profit margin jumped from 24% to 29.5% - the product structure has successfully transformed into "priced gold" with high gross profit.

2. **The secret of gross profit margin jump**:
   - The proportion of gold products priced at "one price" increased (about 1 billion yuan more sold in half a year)
   - The rise in gold prices has increased the proportion of labor costs relative to raw material costs.
   - Reduce discounts and promotions

3. **A large number of store closures**: 850+ retail points have been closed in the mainland (fiscal year 2025 + first half of fiscal year 2026), indicating that the past excessive channel expansion is being corrected.

4. **Structural reasons for revenue decline**:
   - Decline in the number of pieces (high gold prices inhibit purchasing desire)
   - Reduced revenue from store closures
   - The macro consumption environment is weak

## 2. In-depth analysis of profitability

### ROE analysis

Chow Tai Fook ROE is about 20%, breakdown:
- Net profit margin: ~6%
- Asset turnover: ~1.5x
- Leverage multiple: ~2.2x
- **ROE ≈ 6% × 1.5 × 2.2 ≈ 20%**

**The 20% ROE breakdown reveals the nature of this business: low profit margins are compensated for by high leverage and fast turnover. **

| Characteristics | Value | Meaning |
|------|------|------|
| Low net profit margin (6%) | Low | Making money is "hard" and the profit margins are thin |
| High turnover rate (1.5x) | Medium | Fair gold inventory turnover |
| High leverage (2.2x) | High | Gold stocks take up a lot of money |

### Compare with peers

| Company | ROE | Net Margin | Leverage | Characteristics |
|------|-----|--------|------|------|
| Chow Tai Fook | 20% | 6% | High | Driven by high leverage |
| Lao Fengxiang | 25%+ | 4% | Higher | Very thin profits and extremely high turnover |
| Lukfook Jewelry | 15% | 8% | Low | Franchise-based and lighter |
| Long-established gold | 30%+ | 15%+ | Medium | High-end positioning and high premium |

**Core findings**: Gold jewelry retail generally follows a "low profit margin + high leverage" model. Chow Tai Fook's ROE of 20% looks good, but the quality is not as good as Haidilao's 35-40% (Haidilao is mainly driven by high profit margins).

### The impact of gold price on profitability

This is the most critical variable in understanding Chow Tai Fook:

**When gold prices rise:**
- ✓ Inventory appreciation (gold held is more valuable)
- ✓ Increase in gross profit margin (increase in the relative proportion of labor/design fees)
- ✓ Consumers' "buy-up" mentality drives demand
- ✗ The price per customer increases but the number of pieces decreases (fewer people can afford it)

**When gold prices fall:**
- ✗ Inventory depreciation
- ✗ Consumers wait and see (wait for cheaper prices before buying)
- ✗ Gross profit margin may be compressed
- ✓ The number of pieces may increase (more people can afford it)

**Conclusion**: Chow Tai Fook's profits are highly correlated with the price of gold. Investing in Chow Tai Fook is essentially betting on the trend of gold prices.

- Chow Tai Fook's leverage mainly comes from **gold inventory financing** - which requires a large amount of funds to hold physical gold inventory
- When interest rates rise, inventory financing costs increase, eroding profits
- If gold prices fall sharply, inventory impairment may result in one-time losses

## 4. Cash flow analysis

### Cash flow characteristics

The cash flow of jewelry retail has its own particularities:
- Operating cash flow fluctuates greatly (depending on inventory increases and decreases)
- During the period of rising gold prices: inventory may increase → operating cash flow will be under pressure
- Stable gold price period: normal turnover → better cash flow

### Free cash flow estimate

| Indicators | Estimates |
|------|--------|
| Operating cash flow | ~HKD 6-8 billion (highly volatile) |
|Capital expenditure | ~1.5-2 billion Hong Kong dollars (store maintenance + new store opening) |
| Free cash flow | ~HK$4-6 billion |
| FCF/market capitalization | ~5-7% (assuming market capitalization of HK$80 billion) |

**Buffett's focus**: Free cash flow is greatly affected by gold prices and inventory management. When gold prices rise rapidly, companies may take the initiative to increase inventory, causing FCF to be temporarily negative. This makes FCF less predictable than Li Ning and Haidilao.

### Valuation rationality assessment

**The meaning of PE 16 times**:
- For a company with declining revenue, is a PE of 16 times reasonable?
- If the net profit in fiscal year 2026 (full year) returns to HK$5.5-6 billion, the PE will be about 13-15 times
- Considering the dividend yield of 5-6%, even if the stock price does not rise, the dividend return is good

**Comparison with peers:**

| Company | PE | ROE | Growth rate | Dividend rate |
|------|-----|-----|------|--------|
| Chow Tai Fook | 16x | 20% | -10%~0% | 5-6% |
| Luk Fook | 10x | 15% | -15% | 7% |
| Lao Fengxiang | 12x | 25% | -5% | 3% |
| Old Shop Gold | 40x+ | 30%+ | 50%+ | 1% |

Chow Tai Fook PE is in the middle position in the industry. More expensive than Luk Fook (brand premium), cheaper than the old store (no growth story).

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Method 1: Dividend discount method**
- The current annual dividend is approximately HK$4-5 billion
- Assuming dividend growth rate of 0-2%/year
- Discount rate 8%
- Valuation: HK$50-70 billion

**Method 2: Profit Valuation Method**
- Net profit in a normal year is HK$5.5-6.5 billion
- Give 13-15 times PE → HK$71.5-97.5 billion

**Method Three: Liquidation Value (Conservative Bottom Line)**
- Gold inventory market value + brand value + channel value
- Gold stocks alone may be worth HK$30-40 billion
- Adding brands and channels → Bottom line is about HK$60 billion

**Valuation range: HK$60-90 billion**
**Current market value: approximately HK$80 billion**

**Conclusion: The valuation is in the middle of the reasonable range. The 5-6% dividend yield provides a good underlying return. **

## 7. Core judgments under Buffett’s framework

**Positive factors:**
- A 5-6% dividend yield is attractive in the current interest rate environment
- Gross profit margin increased from 24% to 30%, indicating that the product upgrade is effective
- The 90-year-old brand has real value in the field of gold jewelry
- Store closing optimization shows that management is doing "the right thing"
- PE valuation of 16 times is reasonable and there is no bubble

**Negatives:**
- Revenue fell 17.5%, the trend is uncertain
- High leverage and low profit business model is not ideal
- Heavy reliance on the price of gold - a variable you have no control over
- The entry barrier to the industry is low and there are many competitors
- The rise of online channels may further weaken the value of offline stores
- A large number of store closures indicate mistakes in past expansion strategies

### Buffett’s core doubts

"I don't like the idea that a business's fate depends on the price of a commodity. Chow Tai Fook's profits are highly correlated with the price of gold - it makes money when the price of gold goes up, and it loses money when the price of gold goes down. That's not the certainty I want."
