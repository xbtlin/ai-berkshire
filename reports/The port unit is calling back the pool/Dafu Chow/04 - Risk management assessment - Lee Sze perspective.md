## 1. Management evaluation

### Equity structure and actual controller

The actual controller of Chow Tai Fook is the **Zheng Family** (New World Development Group), which holds most of the company's shares through family trusts. This is a typical "Hong Kong wealthy family business" structure.

**Key figures:**
- **Cheng Zhigang**: Former executive director, third-generation successor of New World Group, Harvard education background. Focusing on art, youth and innovation has promoted Chow Tai Fook's brand rejuvenation strategy. But he resigned in 2024 due to personal reasons.
- **Huang Shaoji**: Managing Director, responsible for daily operations. Practical managers.
- **Management Team**: Mainly managed by professional managers, with the Zheng family as the major shareholder providing strategic direction.

### Advantages and Disadvantages of Family Business

**Advantages:**
- The interests of major shareholders and small shareholders are highly consistent (shareholding concentration)
- Long-term orientation and will not sacrifice brand for short-term performance
- 90-year brand heritage and sense of mission
- High dividend tradition

**Disadvantages:**
- Decisions may be influenced by internal family politics
- After Adrian Cheng resigns, there may be a management transition period within the family
- Governance transparency in family businesses may be less than standards for listed companies
- Related transactions with New World Development require attention

## 2. Institutional risk analysis

### Gold price risk ★★★★★ (maximum risk)

This is a risk that management cannot control, but it has the greatest impact on the company:
- Gold prices have risen from approximately US$1,700/oz in 2020 to approximately US$2,300-2,500/oz in 2026
- The probability of a sharp correction after a sustained rise is increasing
- If gold prices fall back 20-30%, Chow Tai Fook may face:
  - Inventory impairment of HK$1-2 billion (one-time)
  - Revenue down 15-25% (consumers wait and see)
  - Net profit cut in half or more

**Key understanding**: Investing in Chow Tai Fook = indirectly long gold. Do you accept this risk?

### Consumption environment risk ★★★☆ (medium)

- Gold jewelry is optional consumer goods (except for weddings)
- Consumers will postpone purchases during economic downturns
- But "safe haven demand" may be partially hedged (people buy gold for safety when the economy is bad)

### Risk of channel change ★★★☆ (medium)

- A large number of store closures means that offline channels are shrinking
- Online channels are on the rise but difficult to completely replace
- Franchisee management is a continuous challenge

### Risk of intensified competition ★★★☆ (moderate)

- Laopu gold suddenly emerges in the high-end market
- Brands such as Zhou Taisheng are attacking in the sinking market
- But Chow Tai Fook’s brand and channel advantages will not disappear in the short term

### Related party transaction risk ★★☆☆ (lower but requires attention)

- There are related transactions between Chow Tai Fook and New World Development Group
- Need to pay attention to whether there is any benefit transfer behavior
- No major issues found so far

## 3. Li Lu type risk framework

### "What's the worst case scenario?"

**Scenario 1: Gold price drops sharply (-30%)**
- Inventory impairment of HK$2-3 billion
- Revenue down 20-25%
- Net profit may drop from 5.5 billion to 2-3 billion
- Impact: Share price may drop 40-50%
- **Probability: 10-15%**

**Scenario 2: Continued downturn (high gold price + weak consumption)**
- The number of pieces continues to decline, and revenue continues to shrink.
- Gross profit margin improvement cannot fully offset the decline in volume
- Net profit fell year by year to 4-4.5 billion
- Impact: PE may be compressed to 12-13 times, and the stock price will fall by 20-30%
- **Probability: 25%**

**Scenario 3: Industry clearing + successful product transformation**
- Small brands withdraw from the market, Chow Tai Fook’s share increases
- The proportion of fixed-price products increased to 50%, and the gross profit margin increased to 35%
- Net profit rebounded to 6.5-7.5 billion
- Impact: Share price may rise 30-50%
- **Probability: 25%**

**Scenario 4: Maintain the status quo**
- Revenue changes in the low single digits
- Net profit maintained at RMB 5-6 billion
- 5-6% dividend + unchanged PE = annualized return of 5-8%
- **Probability: 35%**

### "What are the chances of surviving?"

**5-year survival probability: 99%+**
**Probability of maintaining profitability for 5 years: 90%+**
**Probability of remaining the industry leader in 5 years: 95%+**

Chow Tai Fook is a 90-year-old brand with a strong brand accumulation and channel network. Even if the price of gold pulls back sharply, the company won't go out of business - just profits will fall. This is a "won't die but may not make money" risk profile.

### Downlink protection analysis

| Protective factors | Strength |
|---------|------|
| Brand value (90 years) | ★★★★★ |
| Physical gold inventory (with reserve price) | ★★★★☆ |
| High Dividend Tradition | ★★★★☆ |
| Industry leading position | ★★★★☆ |
| Low PE | ★★★★☆ |
| Management’s willingness to buy back | ★★★★☆ |

**Maximum downside protection**: The physical gold inventory held by Chow Tai Fook is itself "collateral" - even in the worst-case scenario, liquidation of these golds can cover most of the market value.

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Conservative valuation (scenario of 20% gold price correction):**
- Normalized net profit of HK$4 billion
- Give 12 times PE → HK$48 billion
- **Margin of safety: Current market value of 80 billion →-40%**

**Neutral valuation (status quo scenario):**

**Optimistic valuation (product transformation successful):**

## 5. Comparison with "Buy Gold ETF"

This is a question that must be faced when investing in Chow Tai Fook: **If you are optimistic about gold, why not buy gold ETF directly? **

| Dimension | Chow Tai Fook | Gold ETF |

| Dividend | 5-6% | 0% |
| Manage risk | Yes | No |
| Leverage | Yes (amplifies fluctuations) | No |

**Conclusion**: If you are purely long gold, ETF is a simpler and clearer choice. The additional value of investing in Chow Tai Fook lies in: brand management + dividend income. But it also takes on additional risks: management + operation + leverage.

## 6. Perspective of Civilization Evolution

### The role of gold in human civilization

Gold is the most durable "store of value" in human history - it has never lost its place in people's minds for thousands of years. In the legal currency system, gold's role as the "ultimate safe-haven asset" is likely to continue in the long term.

The Chinese people's love for gold is particularly deep-rooted - "gold" represents wealth, luck and inheritance in Chinese culture. This cultural gene determines that gold jewelry consumption has a deep social foundation in China.

### But civilization is changing

- The younger generation may not attach as much importance to "saving gold" as their parents did
- Digital assets (Bitcoin, etc.) have replaced the "investment" role of gold among some groups
- The decline in marriage rates means a structural decrease in demand for wedding gold jewelry
- "Pleasant consumption" replaces "value-preserving consumption", and fashion accessories may divert some demand
