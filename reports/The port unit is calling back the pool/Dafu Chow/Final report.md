# Chow Tai Fook (1929.HK) Investment Research Final Report

---

## One sentence summary

Chow Tai Fook is the leader in the gold and jewelry industry. Its management transformation is in the right direction and its dividends are generous. However, it is essentially a "high leverage and low profit" channel business that relies heavily on gold price trends and is not suitable for value investors who pursue certainty.

## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **High Dividend Yield (5-6%)**: Very attractive in the current interest rate environment
2. **Product transformation is effective**: Gross profit margin increased from 24% to 30%, and the proportion of priced products increased
3. **Steady leading position in the industry**: 90-year-old brand + 5,900 stores
4. **Management is friendly to shareholders**: high dividends + buybacks
5. **PE valuation of 16 times is reasonable**: no bubble
6. **Store closing optimization**: Cut down inefficient stores to improve overall profitability quality
7. **If gold prices continue to rise**: Inventory appreciation + consumption may exceed expectations

### Bear Case

1. **Gold Price Dependence**: The company’s fate is dominated by an uncontrollable variable
2. **Revenue continues to decline**: -17.5% shows pressure on the demand side
3. **High Leverage, Low Profit**: Net profit rate is only 6%, operating is "hard work"
4. **Low switching costs for consumers**: Limited brand differentiation
5. **A large number of store closures**: This shows that there were major mistakes in the past expansion strategy.
6. **Structural decline in wedding demand**: The decline in marriage rate is a long-term trend
7. **If gold prices peak and fall**: profits may be cut in half

## Buying Conditions and Catalysts

### Buying conditions (need to be met at the same time)
1. PE falls below 12-13 times (providing sufficient safety margin)
2. Have some confidence in the mid- to long-term trend of gold prices (at least it will not fall sharply)
3. Accept 5-6% dividend as the main source of return (not pursue high growth)

### Judgment of current price
- PE 16 times + 5-6% dividend rate = can be considered as a "dividend-paying" position
- But need to accept the risk of valuation compression caused by falling gold prices
- If you are purely pursuing safety, it is better to wait for PE 12-13 times

### Potential Catalyst
- Gold prices continue to rise
- Increase in the proportion of priced products drives further improvement in gross profit margin
- Increase buyback efforts
- The effect of store closings in the second half of the year is reflected in profit improvement

### Sell/avoid signal
- Gold price falls below $2,000/oz
- Gross profit margin dropped below 25%
- Cut dividends
- Major shareholders reduce holdings
- Large-scale store closures lead to accelerated revenue decline

## Final conclusion

**Current judgment: It can be configured as a small position of dividend-paying position, non-core target**

Chow Tai Fook’s investment logic can be summarized as:
- **What are you buying? ** A "golden agent" with a 5-6% dividend yield
- **What risks do you take? **Double whammy when gold prices fall (inventory impairment + consumption decline)
- **What are your return expectations? ** Annualized 8-10% (5-6% dividend + 2-4% growth/valuation repair)
- **The biggest variable? ** Gold price trend

**Suitable configuration method**:
- If you are optimistic about the long-term trend of gold: it can be used as a "gold + dividend" position of 5-10% in the portfolio
- If you have no judgment on the gold price: wait for PE to be 12-13 times or the gold price to fall back before considering it
- If you are looking for certainty: Li Ning and Haidilao are better choices
