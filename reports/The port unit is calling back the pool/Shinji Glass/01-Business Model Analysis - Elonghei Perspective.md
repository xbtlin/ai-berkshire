# Xinyi Glass (0868.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business nature, user value, moat, pricing power, business model sustainability
> Data cutoff: May 2026 | Based on 2025 annual results

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

Xinyi Glass is essentially a "asset-heavy manufacturing + regional monopoly" business. The company mainly operates three major sectors: float glass (55.3% of revenue), automotive glass (32.9% of revenue), and architectural glass (11.8% of revenue).

**Float glass** is a basic business, the products are relatively homogeneous, and the price is driven by the supply and demand cycle. The investment for a float glass production line is about 300-500 million yuan, the construction period is about 2 years, and it needs to run continuously for 8-10 years after ignition (cold repair cycle). This "easy to enter, hard to retreat" characteristic determines that the industry's supply elasticity is extremely low. The customers of float glass are mainly real estate and infrastructure, and demand is highly related to the macro economy.

**Automotive glass** is a high value-added business, with a gross profit margin of 54.1% in 2025, contributing 57.3% of the gross profit. Automotive glass needs to be certified by car companies, and it usually takes 2-3 years from sample delivery to mass production. Once it enters the supply chain system, it will form strong customer stickiness. Xinyi's automotive glass mainly focuses on the after-sales market (AM), taking into account original equipment (OEM). The product mix is ​​biased toward high value-added coatings, thermal insulation, and smart glass.

**Architectural glass** is a deep processing business, which processes the original float glass into functional glass such as hollow, laminated, Low-E and so on. The gross profit margin is about 28.5%, which plays a role in vertical integration.

To put it simply: float glass provides scale and cost advantages, automotive glass provides profits and growth, and architectural glass provides industrial chain extension.

## 2. User value and demand certainty

### Float glass: rigid demand, cyclical fluctuations

Glass is an irreplaceable basic material for modern architecture and transportation. As long as human society continues to build houses and cars, it will need glass. This demand is extremely certain, but the growth rate depends on the economic cycle.

About 65% of China's float glass demand comes from real estate (doors, windows, curtain walls), 20% from automobiles, and 15% from others (furniture, electronics, etc.). During the real estate downturn cycle from 2023 to 2025, the demand for float glass is under significant pressure. The national real estate completion area is still declining in 2025, but the decline narrowed to 8.3%.

### Auto Glass: Structural Upgrades

The long-term logic for automotive glass is better:
- **Increase in bicycle glass area**: Canopy glass, HUD windshield, and panoramic sunroof trends, the glass area for bicycles has increased from the traditional 3-4 square meters to 5-6 square meters
- **Improved value of bicycle glass**: From ordinary glass to functional upgrades such as heat insulation, dimming, antenna integration, HUD projection, etc., the value of bicycles is doubled
- **Increased penetration rate of new energy vehicles**: New energy vehicles are more inclined to use large-area canopy glass, and Xinyi has technical reserves in this field

## 3. Moat assessment

### 1. Cost advantage ★★★★☆

Xinyi Glass is one of the world's largest float glass manufacturers, with a daily melting capacity of nearly 30,000 tons. The scale effect is reflected in:
- **Energy consumption cost**: The unit energy consumption of large kilns is about 15-20% lower than that of small kilns
- **Raw material procurement**: Centralized procurement of raw materials such as soda ash, quartz sand, etc., with strong bargaining power
- **Logistics Radius**: Glass is a typical "heavy quality and low value" product, with a transportation radius of about 300-500 kilometers. Xinyi has multiple locations across the country (including overseas), forming regional cost barriers.

When the entire float glass industry suffers losses in 2025, Xinyi still maintains a gross profit margin of 18%, indicating a significant cost advantage.

### 2. Customer authentication barrier ★★★★☆

Automotive glass has a long certification cycle and high switching costs. Xinyi has entered the supply chain system of many global mainstream car companies, including the after-sales market and some OEM supporting products. Once this customer relationship is established, it is difficult for competitors to replace it in the short term.

### 3. Capacity scale barrier ★★★☆☆

The float glass industry requires a large amount of capital investment. The investment for a 1,000 tons/day production line is about 400-500 million yuan. Xinyi's total production capacity has created a significant first-mover advantage, making it difficult for new entrants to reach the same scale in a short period of time. But this barrier does not constitute an absolute threshold for existing competitors such as Kibing.

### 4. Brand and channel ★★☆☆☆

Float glass is a commodity, and brand premiums are limited. Automotive glass has certain brand recognition in the after-sales market, but overall, the glass industry is not a brand-driven business.

**Moat comprehensive rating: ★★★☆☆ (above average)**

Xinyi's moat mainly comes from cost advantages and customer certification barriers, and it belongs to the "low-cost producer" type. This kind of moat has an obvious protective effect during the industry downturn (others lose money and you make a small profit), but during the industry upturn it cannot prevent competitors from expanding production and sharing the profits.

## 4. Pricing power analysis

### Float glass: no pricing power

Float glass is a typical commodity, and its price is determined by supply and demand. As an industry leader, Xinyi can influence supply by controlling the pace of production, but it cannot set prices independently. Float glass prices will continue to fall from 2024 to 2025, and the company can only passively bear it.

### Auto Glass: Limited Pricing Power

Automotive glass has stronger pricing power than float glass due to certification barriers and functional differentiation. Xinyi Auto Glass's gross profit margin has remained above 50% for many years, reaching a record high of 54.1% in 2025, indicating that the company has certain pricing power on high value-added products.

However, it should be noted that Xinyi's share of the global automotive glass market is far less than that of Fuyao Glass (global share exceeds 40%), and its pricing power is also not as good as Fuyao's.

**Pricing Power Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (Float) / ★★★☆☆ (Auto Glass) **

## 5. "Good" and "bad" business models

### Good things

1. **Two-wheel drive model**: Float glass provides scale chassis, and automotive glass provides profit flexibility. During the float down cycle in 2025, automotive glass contributed 57.3% of gross profit, effectively smoothing the cycle fluctuations.
2. **Vertical integration**: From raw film to deep processing to automotive glass, internal collaboration reduces costs
3. **Stable cash flow**: Even in the down cycle, operating cash flow remains healthy (approximately 5.6 billion yuan in 2024, a year-on-year increase of 25%), and dividends continue to be high (dividend payout rate is approximately 49-50%)
4. **Overseas layout**: Production capacity in Malaysia and Indonesia has been implemented, and the Saudi base will be launched in 2026, opening up space for future growth.

### Bad aspects

1. **Cyclicity of float glass**: The business accounting for more than half of the revenue is highly related to real estate. Under the long-term downward trend of China's real estate, this part of the business is facing structural pressure.
2. **Capital Intensive**: Maintaining production capacity requires continuous capital expenditure, and ROE is depressed to 10-12% at the bottom of the cycle
3. **The gap between auto glass and Fuyao**: Fuyao’s global share exceeds 40%. Xinyi’s auto glass is mainly for the after-sales market, and there is a clear gap with Fuyao in the OEM supporting field.
4. **Commodity attributes**: Float glass lacks differentiation and the company has limited control over the price cycle.

## 6. Key issues

**Question 1: If you could only describe Xinyi Glass’s business in one sentence, what would you say? **

Xinyi Glass is a comprehensive glass manufacturer with float glass as its basic base and automotive glass as its profit engine. Its core competitiveness is low cost and scale effect.

**Question 2: Will this business still exist in ten years? **

The certainty of demand for glass as a material is unquestionable. However, the growth space of float glass is limited, and the real growth lies in the functional upgrade of automotive glass and overseas markets. If Xinyi can increase its automotive glass revenue share from 33% to more than 40%, the quality of its business will be significantly improved.

**Question 3: Are you willing to bet all your wealth on this business? **

Not willing. The certainty of this business is not high enough. Float glass is too cyclical. Although automotive glass is good, its share is limited. The moat is a "relative advantage" rather than an "absolute advantage". It is more suitable as a cycle bottom allocation variety in a portfolio rather than as a core position.
