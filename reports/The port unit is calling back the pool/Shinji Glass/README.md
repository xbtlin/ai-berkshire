## Research Framework

This study adopts a four-dimensional analysis framework:

1. **Business model analysis** — business essence, moat, pricing power
2. **Financial Valuation Analysis** — Earnings quality, capital allocation, safety margin
3. **Industry competition analysis** — industrial structure, competition barriers, and changing trends
4. **Risk and Management Assessment** — Core risks, governance structure, capital allocation capabilities

## Research background

Xinyi Glass is one of the largest integrated glass manufacturers in China, with a daily melting capacity of nearly 30,000 tons of float glass, ranking among the top in the world. Currently at the end of the down cycle of the float glass industry (starting in 2023Q4), the automotive glass business has grown against the trend and has become the core pillar of profits. The company is achieving international breakthroughs through overseas production capacity deployment in Malaysia, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia and other countries.
