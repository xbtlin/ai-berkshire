## One sentence summary

Xinyi Glass is a float glass leader that is "physically healthy and at a cycle low". Its automotive glass business provides structural highlights, but overall it is a "reasonably priced medium-quality asset" and is not an exciting investment opportunity.

---

## Core Discovery

### Positive factors

**1. Auto glass has become a real profit engine**

Automotive glass contributed 57.3% of gross profit in 2025, and gross profit margin reached a record high of 54.1%. This is not a pure float glass cyclical stock and the market may be undervaluing the auto glass business. Automotive glass benefits from structural trends such as increased bicycle glass area, functional upgrades, and increased penetration of new energy vehicles.

**2. Balance sheet extremely healthy**

The net debt ratio is only 14.3% and there is ample cash. Even in the worst-case scenario, the company will not face financial crisis. This is the top level among Hong Kong stock manufacturing industries.

**3. Reliable shareholder returns**

A dividend payout ratio of approximately 50%, a dividend rate of approximately 5%, and active repurchases all indicate that management has a clear commitment and execution ability to return returns to shareholders. In a falling interest rate environment, such dividend yields are attractive.

**4. The float industry is at the bottom of the cycle**

The entire industry is losing money (except Xinyi), cold repairs are expected to accelerate, and the probability of price rebound in 2026 is high. If a price recovery of 15-20% is achieved, the profit elasticity will be huge.

**5. Management is reliable**

The Li Xianyi family holds 31.28% of the shares, and their interests are tied to shareholders. The historical capital allocation record is good and there are no major strategic mistakes. During the downturn, we proactively cut capital expenditures (halved to 2-2.5 billion) to demonstrate financial discipline.

### Negative factors

**1. The real estate downturn is a structural rather than a cyclical problem**

The long-term downward trend in Chinese real estate may permanently reduce the demand center for float glass. The optimistic narrative of a "cycle bottom" needs to be treated with caution - this bottom may be lower and longer than the historical cycle.

**2. The gap between automotive glass and Fuyao is huge**

Fuyao’s global share exceeds 40%, while Xinyi’s is only 3-5%. Xinyi's automotive glass mainly focuses on the after-sales market, and it faces extremely high barriers to breakthrough in the OEM market. The gross profit margin of 54% is very good, but the scale ceiling may be limited.

**3. ROE level is not excellent enough**

ROE currently about 10%, normalizes to about 12-15%. It's acceptable for the heavy-asset manufacturing industry, but far less than the level of more than 20% of outstanding companies. This means that the rate of return on reinvestment of retained earnings is limited.

**4. The current valuation lacks an obvious margin of safety**

The current PE of 15-16 times may not seem high, but it is the PE at the bottom of the cycle. Normalized PE is about 11-12 times, reasonable but not cheap. If a more adequate margin of safety is needed, the stock price needs to fall back below HK$9.

**5. Opacity of Xinyi’s related-party transactions**

Related-party transactions between Xinyi Glass, Xinyi Solar, Xinyi Energy and Xinyi Power Storage increase the complexity of analysis. Xinyi Solar's deteriorating earnings are dragging down Xinyi Glass's associates' profits and losses.

## Investment thesis test

### Argument 1: Float glass cycle bottom reversal

**Supporting evidence**: The entire industry is losing money, cold repairs are expected to accelerate, and infrastructure demand will support the start of the "15th Five-Year Plan" in 2026. The industry predicts that prices will rebound by 15-20%.

**Contrary evidence**: Real estate completions may continue to decline, production capacity may exit less quickly than expected, and the "bottom" may be a long platform rather than a V-shaped reversal.

**Judgment**: The general direction of the cycle reversal may be correct, but the timing and magnitude are highly uncertain. This should not be used as a core investment thesis.

### Argument 2: Auto glass is the second growth curve

**Supporting evidence**: Revenue increased by 8.8%, gross profit margin reached a new high of 54.1%, the proportion of high value-added products continued to increase, and overseas production capacity supported growth.

**Evidence against**: The global share is only 3-5%, which is significantly different from Fuyao. Breakthrough in the OEM market is difficult, and there may be a ceiling for growth space.

**Judgment**: Automotive glass is Xinyi’s most high-quality business, but it will take a long time to increase its revenue share from 33% to 40-50%.

### Argument 3: PE 11-13 times has a margin of safety

**Supporting evidence**: Normalized PE is about 11-12 times, 5% dividend yield provides downside protection, and the balance sheet is safe.

**Evidence against**: The current PE is actually 15-16 times, ROE is only 10%, and the valuations of similar companies in Hong Kong stocks are quite different.

**Judgment**: Valuation is reasonable but not cheap. The margin of safety is moderate and insufficient to compensate for the uncertainty of the business model.

## Suitable investor profile

**Fits**:
- Income-oriented investors seeking high dividends and low valuation cyclical stocks in Hong Kong stocks
- Cyclical investors who believe the float glass cycle will reverse in 2026-2027
- Value investors who are willing to buy mid-quality assets at reasonable prices and hold them for the long term

## Key variables that need to be tracked later

1. **Float glass price trend**: Will there be a substantial recovery in the second half of 2026?
2. **Automotive glass revenue share**: Can it continue to increase from 33% to 40%?
3. **Overseas production capacity ramping progress**: Capacity utilization and profit contribution of Malaysia, Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia
4. **Changes in Xinyi Solar’s Profitability**: The impact of profits and losses of associated companies on the income statement
5. **Real estate completion data**: National year-on-year change trend in completed area
6. **Management’s Capital Allocation Decision**: Whether to maintain discipline at the bottom of the cycle and avoid overexpansion

## Research conclusion

Xinyi Glass is an industry leader with solid management and healthy finances, but the core contradiction it faces is: **The largest business (float glass) is in decline, and the best business (automotive glass) is not large enough**.

The current share price (approximately HK$10.95) reflects the market's pricing of the bottom of the float cycle, with reasonable valuation but lack of a clear margin of safety. If the float cycle reverses as expected, investors can obtain cyclically elastic returns plus a 5% dividend return; if the reversal is delayed or falls short of expectations, investors will face opportunity costs.

It's not a target that people "can't wait to buy", but it's also not a target that has obvious problems that need to be avoided. Stay in the observation pool and wait for better prices (below HK$9) or clearer catalysts (substantial rebound in float prices, breakthrough in large automotive glass OEM orders).

**Operation recommendation level: Observation (not the current buying opportunity)**
