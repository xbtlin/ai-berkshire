# Xinyi Glass (0868.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital allocation efficiency, balance sheet health, free cash flow, valuation safety margin
> Data cutoff: May 2026 | Based on 2025 annual results

---

## 1. Panoramic view of profitability

### 1. Performance trends in the past five years

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Net profit rate | ROE |
|------|-------------|---------------|--------|-----|
| 2021 | 260+ | 78+ | ~30% | ~28% |
| 2022 | 222 | 38.5 | 17.3% | ~15% |
| 2023 | 243 | 48.8 | 20.1% | ~17% |
| 2024 | 223.24 | 33.69 | 15.1% | ~12% |
| 2025 | 208.29 | 27.29 | 13.1% | ~10% |

A few key observations:

**Revenue has declined for two consecutive years**: -8.1% year-on-year in 2024 and -6.7% year-on-year in 2025. The decline was mainly due to the decline in both volume and price of float glass, reflecting shrinking real estate demand and overcapacity in the industry.

**Net profit fell sharply**: From the cycle high in 2021 to 2025, net profit fell by approximately 65%. This shows the typical characteristics of cyclical industries - high profits are not sustainable, and low profits do not represent the norm.

**ROE falls from highs**: The unusually high ROE of nearly 28% in 2021 has fallen back to about 10%. An ROE of 10-12% is a reasonable level for asset-heavy manufacturing, but not excellent.

### 2. Segment earnings quality

The 2025 segment data reveals an important structural change:

| Segment | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Gross profit margin | Gross profit ratio |
|------|-------------|--------|---------|
| Float glass | 115.14 | 18.0% | 31.9% |
| Auto Glass | 68.61 | 54.1% | 57.3% |
| Architectural glass | 24.54 | 28.5% | 10.8% |

**Automotive glass has become the actual profit main body**. While float glass contributed 55% of revenue, automotive glass contributed 57% of gross profit with 33% of revenue. This means that the profit quality of Xinyi Glass is actually much better than that of pure float glass companies such as Kibin Group.

The 54.1% gross profit margin of automotive glass deserves further consideration: this level is not only much higher than that of float glass, but is even close to the level of some consumer goods companies. This reflects the ability to generate premium prices through product differentiation (functional glass) and customer stickiness (certification barriers).

## 2. Balance sheet health

### 1. Liability structure

By mid-2025:
- **Net debt ratio**: 14.3%, a decrease of 2 percentage points from the end of 2024
- **Cash and bank deposits**: 2.033 billion yuan, an increase of 18.9% from the end of 2024

The net debt ratio of 14.3% is a very conservative level in the manufacturing industry. Xinyi Glass intentionally controls leverage, which provides an important financial safety cushion during industry downturns.

### 2. Capital Expenditure Discipline

Capital expenditure in 2025 will be approximately 2.0-2.5 billion yuan, which will be reduced by approximately half compared with 2024. Management actively reduced capital expenditure during the industry downturn, reflecting its prudent capital allocation philosophy. However, it should be noted that Xinyi’s overseas expansion (Malaysia, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia) is still progressing, and capital expenditures may rise again in the future.

### 3. Asset quality concerns

Xinyi Glass holds an interest in Xinyi Solar (0968.HK). Xinyi Solar has suffered a sharp decline in profits in recent years due to overcapacity in the photovoltaic industry. As an associated company, changes in Xinyi Solar's profits and losses will affect Xinyi Glass's income statement. In the first half of 2025, Xinyi Solar's reduced profit is one of the reasons for the decline in Xinyi Glass's net profit.

## 3. Cash flow analysis

### Operating cash flow

Operating cash flow in 2024 will be approximately 5.6 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 25%. Even if net profit drops significantly, operating cash flow remains healthy, indicating good profit quality - depreciation and amortization provide a "non-cash cost buffer."

For a continuous production industry such as float glass, operating cash flow is usually better than net profit because:
- Depreciation costs are large (depreciation of kilns and factories), but it does not affect cash
- Working capital requirements are relatively stable
- Customers are mainly construction companies and car companies, and the payment cycle is controllable

### Free Cash Flow

Free cash flow = operating cash flow - capital expenditures

Assuming that operating cash flow in 2025 is approximately 5 billion yuan and capital expenditure is approximately 2.2 billion yuan, free cash flow will be approximately 2.8 billion yuan. Compared with the net profit of 2.729 billion yuan, free cash flow basically matches the net profit, which is a healthy signal.

## 4. Shareholder Return Analysis

### 1. Dividend policy

Xinyi Glass has maintained a high dividend payout ratio for a long time:
- Dividend payout rate in 2024 is approximately 48.5%
- 2025 final dividend 21.5 HK cents/share
- Current dividend yield is about 5%

A 5% dividend yield is attractive in the Hong Kong stock market, especially in a downward interest rate environment. More importantly, Xinyi has the ability and willingness to maintain high dividends - management has repeatedly stated that it will maintain a stable dividend payout ratio of 49-50%.

### 2. Stock buyback

In the first half of 2025, the company conducted a repurchase of nearly HK$50 million, which was a positive signal but a small amount. The grant of 38 million share options in March 2026 shows management's confidence in the mid- to long-term prospects.

### 3. Comprehensive evaluation of shareholder returns

Being able to maintain a dividend rate of 5% and a payout ratio of about 50% at the bottom of the cycle shows that the company has strong cash flow generation capabilities and management's commitment to shareholder returns is reliable. But an ROE of 10-12% means that the rate of return on reinvestment of retained earnings after deducting dividends is not high.

## 5. Valuation analysis

### 1. Current valuation level

As of May 2026:
- **Share price**: approximately HK$10.95
- **2025 PE**: approximately 15-16 times (based on EPS 62.31 cents RMB)
- **Forecast PE** in 2026: approximately 14-15 times (based on brokerage forecasts)
- **PB**: about 1.3-1.5 times

### 2. Historical valuation comparison

| Cycle stage | PE range | PB range |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Cycle high (2021) | 8-10 times | 2.5-3 times |
| Mid-cycle | 10-14 times | 1.5-2 times |
| Cycle low (current) | 14-16 times | 1.3-1.5 times |

Here is a classic trap in the valuation of cyclical stocks: **The high point of PE is exactly the time to buy, and the low point of PE is the signal to sell**. The current PE of 15-16 times seems "not cheap", but if float glass enters a recovery period in 2026, the profit rebound will cause the PE to fall quickly.

### 3. Normalized earnings valuation

The correct valuation method for cyclical stocks is not to look at the current PE, but to look at the PE under "normalized earnings".

Xinyi Glass's mid-cycle normalized net profit is about 3.5-4 billion yuan (taking the average level of 2023 and 2024). Based on the current market capitalization of approximately HK$48 billion (approximately RMB 44 billion), the normalized PE is approximately 11-12.5 times.

For a leading manufacturing company with an ROE of about 12-15% (normalized), a dividend rate of 50%, and a net debt ratio of less than 15%, a PE of 11-12 times is basically reasonable. It is not an obvious undervaluation, but it is not overvalued either.

### 4. Thoughts on valuation at the bottom of the cycle

If the price of float glass rebounds by 15-20% in 2026 (industry forecast), Xinyi's float business gross profit margin may rebound from 18% to more than 25%. Coupled with the continued growth of automotive glass, net profit is expected to rise to 3.5-4 billion yuan in 2026. By then, the PE will fall back to 12-13 times, and the valuation will appear more reasonable.

But the risks of this logic are: the strength of real estate recovery is uncertain, the speed of float glass production capacity reduction is uncertain, and the extent of price rebound is uncertain.

### 5. Safety margin assessment

**Positives:**
- 5% dividend yield provides downside protection
- Net debt ratio is only 14.3%, and the balance sheet is solid
- Automotive glass business provides profit resilience
- The float industry is at the bottom of the cycle, with limited downside space

**Negatives:**
- The current PE is not low (15-16 times)
- ROE is at a historical low (about 10%)
- The timing of float recovery is uncertain
- The long-term downward trend in China's real estate may change the industry's demand structure

**Margin of Safety Rating: ★★★☆☆ (Moderate)**

The current valuation is not expensive but not cheap either. If the stock price falls below HK$9 (corresponding to a normalized PE of approximately 10 times), the margin of safety will be more adequate.
