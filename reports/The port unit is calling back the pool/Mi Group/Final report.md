## Two and four-dimensional cross-validation: consensus and disagreement

### The unanimous conclusion of the four dimensions

1. **The "whole ecology of people, cars and homes" has real value, but the depth of the moat is questionable. ** 1.079 billion IoT devices, 754 million monthly active users, and 410,000 vehicles delivered - these numbers prove the scale of the ecosystem. But in every single track of mobile phones, cars, and IoT, Xiaomi faces at least one stronger opponent (Huawei, BYD, Apple). Ecological competitiveness comes from the "aggregation effect", but if any link is broken down, the aggregate effect will collapse.

2. **Lei Jun is the biggest asset and the biggest risk. ** The four dimensions all point to the same conclusion: Xiaomi’s strategic correctness, brand appeal, and success in its automotive business are almost all based on Lei Jun alone. The batch departure of co-founders and the continuous adjustment of the organizational structure indicate that Xiaomi has not found an effective path to "de-Lei Jun".

3. **The automobile business is the decisive variable in valuation. ** If the automobile business can achieve 10 billion+ profits in 2027 and the gross profit margin stabilizes above 20%, Xiaomi's overall valuation can support the current level or even higher. If the auto business falls into a price war and profit margins fall, the overall valuation will face downward pressure. This is a "0 or 1" judgment, not a linear deduction.

### Where there are differences in the four dimensions

**Business model analysis believes that Xiaomi’s user value is clear (4/5), but financial analysis believes that this value has not yet been fully converted into shareholder returns (3.2/5). ** This disagreement points to a core issue: Xiaomi has chosen the strategy of "users first, profits later" (mobile phone gross profit margin is 8.3%), which is a good thing for users, but means a longer waiting period for shareholders.

**Industry competition analysis gives a higher score to the automotive business (4/5), but risk analysis points out that price wars and production capacity bottlenecks may erode this advantage (3/5). ** This difference reflects the tension between "current performance" and "future risk" - the success of SU7 and YU7 is a fact that has already happened, but the competitive environment in 2026 is completely different from that in 2024.

## 3. Investment Conclusion

### Conclusion 1: Xiaomi is a "good but not excellent" company

The overall rating of 3.5/5 means: Xiaomi has surpassed most Hong Kong stock companies, but there is still a clear gap between what Buffett calls a "great company" (ROE 20%+, strong pricing power, and deep moat). It is more like a company with "high capabilities and high ambitions, but low profit margin ceiling" - similar to Samsung rather than Apple.

### Conclusion 2: The current valuation is in the "reasonably expensive" range

18-22 times PE corresponds to a growth rate of 25-30%. The valuation is reasonable but not cheap. SOTP valuation implies that the current price has basically reflected neutral expectations. If the stock price falls back to 15-16 times forward PE (corresponding to a stock price of approximately HK$40-45), it will provide a more attractive buying window.

### Conclusion 3: Suitable for "satellite positions" rather than "core positions"

| Dimensions | Judgment |
|------|------|
| Suitable for core positions? | No. ROE is not high enough, free cash flow is negative, and key person risks are high |
| Suitable for satellite positions? | Yes. Growth certainty is high, and the diversified ecosystem provides unique investment exposure |
| Recommended position | 3-5% of total position |
| Time to buy | Wait for a correction to the range of HK$40-45, or wait for the 2026 Q1 financial report to confirm that the gross profit margin of mobile phones has stabilized |

### Conclusion 4: Key observation indicators

Continue to track the following indicators as triggers for adding or reducing positions:

**Add position signal:**
- Mobile phone gross profit margin rebounded to above 10% for two consecutive quarters
- Automobile quarterly operating profit stabilized at 2 billion+
- Car deliveries exceed 600,000 vehicles in 2026
- The emergence of a second manager who can act independently

**Reduce position/exit signal:**
- Mobile phone gross profit margin fell below 5%
- Automobile gross profit margin fell below 15%
- Lei Jun significantly reduces investment in business for any reason
- Listed on the U.S. Sanctioned Entity List
- Free cash flow has been negative for three consecutive years with no improvement trend

---

## 4. Final judgment

**Summary in one sentence: Xiaomi is a "conditionally optimistic" investment target - the condition is that the profit margin of the automobile business continues to improve, Lei Jun remains at his peak, and the gross profit margin of mobile phones stops falling and stabilizes. If all three conditions are met at the same time, Xiaomi is worth holding; if any one condition breaks, it needs to be re-evaluated. **

This is not an opportunity to "buy with eyes closed", nor is it a risk that needs to be avoided. It is an investment target that requires continuous tracking and disciplined participation.
