## Investment thesis preview

**Bull logic:** It only took 2 years for the automobile business to grow from 0 to 100 billion. The ecological synergy of the "People's Automobile Family" has begun to materialize. The large volume of YU7 in 2026 is expected to promote a jump in profits.

**Bearish logic:** The gross profit margin of mobile phones is under pressure (8.3%), automobile competition is intensifying, it relies heavily on Lei Jun personally, the Indian market is shrinking, and the valuation has partially reflected optimistic expectations.
