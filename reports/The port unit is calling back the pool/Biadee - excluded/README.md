## Investment background

BYD is the world's leader in new energy vehicles. In 2025, its pure electric vehicle sales will surpass Tesla and become the world's number one. The company adopts a vertical integration model, covering core components such as batteries, motors, electronic controls, and semiconductors. Currently in a period of global expansion, the overseas sales target is 1.3 million vehicles in 2026. Key risks include price wars suppressing profit margins, EU tariff barriers, and geopolitical uncertainty.
