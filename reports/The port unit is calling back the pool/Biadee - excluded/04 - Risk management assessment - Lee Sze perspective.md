# BYD (1211.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: Management quality and capabilities, corporate governance, risk list, safety margin
> Data cutoff: May 2026

## 1. Management evaluation

### Wang Chuanfu: founder entrepreneur

**Basic information**:
- Born in 1966 in Wuwei, Anhui Province, Master's degree in Metallurgical Physics and Chemistry from Central South University
- Founded BYD in 1995 (originally a battery manufacturer)
- As chairman and president, he has absolute control over the company
- Approximately 17% stake in 2025 (through direct and indirect means)

### Circle of Competence Assessment

**Technical Judgment (★★★★★)**:

Wang Chuanfu is a rare case in the Chinese business world of a "technical founder" taking the helm of a very large enterprise. His several key technical judgments were almost all correct:

1. **1995**: It was judged that lithium batteries would replace nickel-cadmium batteries and BYD Battery was founded.
2. **2003**: Determined that vehicle electrification was a major trend and entered the automotive industry (widely ridiculed at the time)
3. **2008**: Adhere to the lithium iron phosphate route (safety + low cost) and maintain stability when the industry is pursuing ternary lithium batteries.
4. **2020**: Launch of blade battery to solve energy density problem with structural innovation
5. **2021**: DM-i plug-in hybrid strategy accurately cuts into the market pain points
6. **2024-2025**: Comprehensive transformation of intelligence, with an army of 120,000 R&D personnel fighting

**Strategic execution (★★★★★)**:

- From batteries to cars, from cars to semiconductors, from domestic to global - every leap is successful
- Global sales will be 4.6 million vehicles in 2025, an increase of more than 10 times from 430,000 vehicles in 2020
- It only took 3-4 years to go from scratch to 1 million+ vehicles overseas.

**Capital allocation capability (★★★☆☆)**:

- Advantages: R&D investment of 63.4 billion per year, firmly invested in the future
- Advantages: Global factory layout (Hungary, Turkey, Brazil, Thailand)
- Disadvantages: Almost no dividends (only 3.26 billion in dividends in 2025, dividend rate 0.4%), weak awareness of shareholder returns
- Disadvantages: Some investment directions (such as cloud rail, mobile phone OEM) have not seen significant returns in the long term.

**Risk Appetite (Aggressive)**:

Wang Chuanfu is an extremely confident entrepreneur. The positive side of this confidence is bold decision-making and quick execution, but the negative side is the possibility of over-expansion. The radical investment of RMB 63.4 billion in R&D + global factory construction is "far-sighted" during the industry's upward period, but may become "burden" during the industry's downward period.

If Wang Chuanfu is unable to continue leading the company for any reason, it will be difficult to find a replacement in the short term.

## 2. Corporate governance assessment

### Governance Structure

- In 2025, 23 internal governance systems were revised in accordance with the new "Company Law"
- Board composition: including independent directors, but the founder has strong control
- Board of Supervisors: established and operated in accordance with regulations

### Employee motivation

- 2025 Employee Stock Ownership Plan: no more than 25,000 employees will participate, the total capital will not exceed 4.1 billion yuan, and will be unlocked in three phases
- The stability of the 120,000-person R&D team is crucial to continued technological leadership.

### Governance concerns

1. **Chairman and President in one person**: Lack of power checks and balances, high decision-making efficiency but weak error correction ability
2. **Transparency of external communication**: Compared with international car companies, there is room for improvement in the timeliness and detail of information disclosure.
3. **Weak awareness of shareholder returns**: 0.4% dividend rate, almost all profits are reinvested, but the opinions of small and medium-sized shareholders are not fully solicited

## 4. Li Lu’s perspective: What stage is this company?

### Judgment of company development stage

Li Lu divides enterprise development into four stages: entrepreneurship, growth, maturity, and decline.

BYD is currently in the stage of "transition from the late growth stage to the mature stage":
- Domestic market share is already at a high level, and growth mainly depends on replacement of existing stocks.
- The overseas market is still in the growth stage (the proportion is increasing from 10% to 40%+)
- The success of the migration of technological advantages from electrification to intelligence has yet to be verified.

### Evaluation of "Buying stocks means buying people"

**Is Wang Chuanfu worth betting on? **

Front:
- 30 years of entrepreneurial journey, from scratch to become the world's number one, proving strong strategic judgment and execution capabilities
- Made correct choices at key nodes many times (entering cars, insisting on lithium iron phosphate, DM-i hybrid, globalization)
- With a background in science and engineering, he has first-hand knowledge of the technical direction rather than relying on consulting companies.

Negative:
- Excessive concentration of power and lack of effective checks and balances
- "Engineer culture" may lead to insufficient attention to "soft power" such as brand building and user experience
- Aggressive expansion style may bring risks during industry downturns

**Li Lu's conclusion**: Wang Chuanfu is one of the best entrepreneurs in China's manufacturing industry. His circle of competence is "technical direction judgment + large-scale manufacturing execution", which happens to be the ability BYD needs most at this stage. However, as the company enters a mature stage, it needs more "brand management + global operations + capital allocation" capabilities. It remains to be seen whether these are within Wang Chuanfu's circle of competence.

## 5. Comprehensive assessment of safety margin

### What price has a sufficient margin of safety?

**Conservative estimate of intrinsic value**:
- Assuming the average net profit from 2026 to 2028 is 40 billion yuan
- Give a reasonable PE 18-20x (reasonable valuation of the manufacturing leader)
- Corresponding reasonable market value: HK$720-800 billion
- Consider 30% safety margin: HK$500-560 billion

**The current market value is approximately HK$900 billion, which is significantly higher than the conservative valuation and has insufficient safety margin. **

### Under what conditions does the margin of safety appear?

1. **Share price fell 30-40%**: A correction from the current price to a corresponding PE of 15-18x
2. **Profit recovery exceeds expectations**: Profit will reach 50 billion+ in 2026, and the current PE will naturally decrease
3. **Overseas growth exceeds expectations**: Overseas sales reached 1.5 million+, opening up long-term growth space

## 7. Key conclusions

**The management is extremely good, risks can be identified but cannot be ignored, and the current price safety margin is insufficient. **

Li Lu's investment philosophy emphasizes that "good company + good management + good price" are indispensable. BYD meets the first two conditions (excellent company + excellent management), but the third condition (good price) has not yet been met at current valuation levels.

**Suggested Strategies**:
- Incorporate into the core observation pool for continuous tracking
- Wait for any of the following trigger conditions before considering opening a position:
  - Stock price pulls back below 18x 2026 expected PE
  - Or overseas sales/profit margins may show an unexpected turning point signal
  - Or there will be a breakthrough in intelligent driving capabilities, verifying the "second growth curve"
