## 2. Core investment logic

### Look at the bull case

1. **Globalization is the real "second curve"**: Overseas sales will exceed 1 million vehicles in 2025, with a target of 1.3 million vehicles in 2026. Overseas gross profit margin (19.46%) is higher than domestic (16.66%). If the overseas share increases from 25% to 40-50%, the overall profit margin is expected to rebound.

2. **The price war will eventually end, and the leaders will benefit the most**: China's new energy vehicle industry is going through a consolidation period similar to that of the home appliance industry in the 2000s. As the lowest-cost player, BYD will be the "last man standing" and profit margins will rebound as industry concentration increases.

3. **The speed of intelligent replenishment is faster than expected**: 120,000 R&D team + 63.4 billion R&D investment + urban NOA sinks to 100,000-class models. If the intelligent catch-up is successful, it will eliminate the market’s worries about "leading in electrification but lagging behind in intelligence".

4. **Generationally leading technology**: second-generation blade battery + megawatt flash charging + "400km in 5 minutes", still maintaining a 1-2 year lead in core electrification capabilities.

### Bear Case

1. **Structural downward shift in profit margin**: Price war is not a short-term phenomenon but the new normal of the industry. With high exit barriers + local protection + constant new entrants, the industry may be in a state of low profit margins for a long time.

2. **Intelligent competition pattern reverses**: If Tesla FSD/Huawei ADS takes the lead in reaching L4 level, intelligence may become the first factor in car purchase, and BYD’s electrification advantage will be weakened.

3. **Comprehensive escalation of geopolitics**: Tariff barriers in Europe and the United States continue to increase, and overseas growth logic is damaged. The overseas target of 1.3 million vehicles may not be achieved.

4. **Free cash flow trap**: Accounting profit is 32.6 billion but free cash flow may only be 10-20 billion. A lot of profits are locked up in expansion, and the actual return to shareholders is extremely low (dividend yield 0.4%).

## 6. Investment advice

### Current action recommendations

**Rating: Watch and wait (not a buying opportunity)**

Reason:
1. 26x PE does not have a margin of safety for a company whose profits fell by 19% year-on-year and profits in 2026Q1 were cut in half.
2. Free cash flow is significantly lower than accounting profit, and actual returns to shareholders are limited.
3. The competitive landscape of the industry will not improve in the short term, and profit margins will continue to be under pressure.

### Conditions that trigger buying (re-evaluation is required if any one is met)

1. **Valuation falls**: The stock price fell by 30%+, corresponding to the expected PE in 2026 below 18x
2. **Profit turning point confirmed**: Gross profit margin improved quarter-on-quarter for two consecutive quarters + net profit turned positive year-on-year
3. **Overseas exceeded expectations**: Overseas sales exceeded 400,000 vehicles in a single quarter, and overseas profits accounted for more than 50%
4. **Intelligent breakthrough**: Urban NOA user penetration rate/praise rate has reached the industry-leading level

### Position recommendation (if already held)

- Current position: It is recommended to control it within 3-5% of the portfolio
- It is not recommended to add positions: wait for a better price
- Liquidation is not recommended: the long-term logic has not been destroyed and the leading position is stable
