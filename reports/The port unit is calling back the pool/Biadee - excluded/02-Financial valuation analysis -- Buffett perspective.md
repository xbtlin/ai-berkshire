# BYD (1211.HK) Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital allocation efficiency, free cash flow, valuation safety margin
> Data cutoff: May 2026 (including 2026Q1 financial report)

---

## 1. Profit quality assessment

### Sustainability of revenue growth

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit attributable to parent companies (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year |
|------|-------------|------|-------------------|------|
| 2022 | 4241 | +96% | 166 | +446% |
| 2023 | 6023 | +42% | 300 | +81% |
| 2024 | 7771 | +29% | 402 | +34% |
| 2025 | 8040 | +3.5% | 326 | -19% |
| 2026Q1 | 1502 | -11.8% | 40.85 | -55.4% |

**Key Findings**:

1. **Growth slows down sharply**: Revenue dropped rapidly from +96% in 2022 to +3.5% in 2025, and even fell 11.8% year-on-year in 2026Q1. The "golden period" of growth (electric vehicle penetration rate from 10% to 40%) has passed, and future growth will rely more on overseas expansion and share improvement.

2. **Profit growth significantly lags behind revenue**: Revenue +3.5% but profit -19% in 2025, revenue -11.8% but profit -55.4% in 2026Q1. This shows that BYD is in the stage of "exchanging price for volume", and the profit elasticity is much lower than the revenue elasticity.

3. **High profit volatility**: From 40.2 billion in 2024 to 32.6 billion in 2025 to only 16.3 billion in 2026Q1, the predictability of profits is poor and does not meet Buffett's preference for "stable profit growth".

### Gross profit margin and expense ratio analysis

**Gross profit margin trend**:
- 2024: Gross profit margin of automobile business is approximately 22.3%
- 2025: Automobile business gross profit margin 20.49% (-1.82pct)
- 2026Q1: Comprehensive gross profit margin 18.81% (-1.26pct year-on-year, +1.37pct month-on-month)

**Interpretation**: The core reason for the decline in gross profit margin is the price war. In 2025, BYD will significantly reduce the prices of many models (Qin PLUS will be reduced to 79,800 yuan), sacrificing profits in exchange for market share. The overseas gross profit margin (19.46%) is higher than the domestic one (16.66%), indicating that the overseas pricing strategy is healthier.

**The "double-sidedness" of R&D expenses**:
- R&D investment in 2025 will be 63.4 billion yuan, accounting for 7.9% of revenue, nearly twice as much as net profit
- R&D expense treatment (non-capitalization) significantly reduces current profits
- Positive understanding: The company is using current profits to invest in future competitiveness
- Negative understanding: If R&D efficiency is not high, these investments may not be converted into future profits

## 2. Capital allocation efficiency

### ROE analysis

| Year | ROE | Trend |
|------|-----|------|
| 2023 | ~24% | Up |
| 2024 | ~26% | Peak |
| 2025 | ~15% | Significant decrease |
| 2026Q1 (annualized) | ~17.5% | Stabilizing at a low level |

**ROE drops from 26% to 15% teardown**:

- Net profit margin decline: from 5.2% (2024) to 4.1% (2025), core drag factors
- Asset turnover rate: dropped slightly from about 1.3x, revenue growth slowed
- Financial leverage: maintained at a high level (equity multiplier is about 4x), indicating that the company relies on debt to operate

**Key Question**: An ROE of 15% is still excellent for an asset-heavy manufacturing company, but the trend is downward. If the price war continues, ROE may further drop to 12-13%.

### Capital expenditures and free cash flow

As a heavy-asset manufacturing company, BYD has huge capital expenditures:
- Operating cash flow in 2025: approximately 80 billion yuan
-Capital expenditure (fixed assets + incremental construction in progress): approximately 60-70 billion yuan
- Free cash flow: about 10-20 billion yuan (far lower than accounting net profit of 32.6 billion yuan)

**2026Q1 is even more severe**: Operating cash flow dropped sharply by 67.48% year-on-year, reflecting the pressure of price wars on cash withdrawals.

**The core contradiction from Buffett's perspective**: Accounting profit is 32.6 billion, but free cash flow may only be 10-20 billion. A large amount of profits are "locked" in the continuously expanding fixed assets, and the money that shareholders can actually get is far less than the reported profits.

## 3. Balance sheet quality

### Advantages

1. **Abundant cash reserves**: 167.8 billion yuan in cash, enough to cope with short-term operating fluctuations
2. **Interest-bearing liabilities are controllable**: Although the asset-liability ratio is high (about 77%), a large amount is operating liabilities (accounts payable, advance receipts), and the proportion of interest-bearing liabilities is relatively reasonable
3. **No goodwill impairment risk**: The company is mainly self-researched and self-built, and there is almost no goodwill brought by large-scale acquisitions.

### Hidden worries

1. **Large scale of accounts receivable**: government subsidy receivables and large customers have longer account periods
2. **The scale of fixed assets continues to expand**: Factories are being built around the world (Hungary, Türkiye, Brazil, Thailand, etc.), and depreciation pressure will continue to increase in the future.
3. **Highly leveraged operations**: During industry downturns, high leverage will amplify profit fluctuations

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

| Indicator | Value | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Market capitalization | ~HK$900 billion | May 2026 |
| 2025 PE (TTM) | ~26x | Based on 32.6 billion net profit |
| Forecast PE in 2026 | ~20x | Based on agency forecast of 44.7 billion net profit |
| PB | ~4x | Corresponding to 15% ROE |
| Dividend yield | ~0.4% | Very low payout ratio |

### Multi-scenario valuation

**Optimistic scenario** (30% probability):
- Profit will reach 44.7 billion in 2026 (agreement consensus), and 57.8 billion in 2027
- Grant 25x PE, corresponding to a target market value of 1.12 trillion-1.45 trillion Hong Kong dollars
- Upside: 25-60%

**Neutral scenario** (probability 40%):
- The price war continues, with profits of 35-40 billion in 2026
- Grant 20x PE, corresponding to a target market value of HK$700-800 billion
- The current valuation is basically reasonable and the space is limited

**Pessimistic scenario** (30% probability):
- Global trade friction escalates + domestic price war intensifies, profit will be 25-30 billion in 2026
- Valuation reaches 15x PE, corresponding to target market value of HK$375-450 billion
- Downside risk: 40-50%

### Comparison with historical valuation

BYD’s historical PE range:
- 2020-2021 bull market high: 200x+ (bubble stage)
- 2022-2023: 30-50x
- 2024: 25-35x
- Current: 26x (TTM)

From an absolute valuation perspective, 26x PE is not cheap for a manufacturing company with declining profits and shrinking gross profit margins. Buffett began to reduce his holdings in BYD in 2022 and completely cleared BYD in 2024, which may be based on the judgment that "the valuation no longer has a sufficient margin of safety."

## 6. Key financial conclusions

### Positive factors

1. **Cash reserves of 167.8 billion yuan**, with financial resilience through cycles
2. **High growth of overseas business**: Overseas sales volume +55% in 2026Q1, and overseas gross profit margin is higher
3. **R&D investment is converted into technical barriers**: If R&D expenditure of 63.4 billion continues to produce results, it will constitute a long-term competitive advantage.
4. **Scale effect has not reached its limit**: There is still room for global expansion to release production capacity utilization

### Negative factors

1. **Profit quality is worrying**: Accounting profit is 32.6 billion, free cash flow may only be 10-20 billion
2. **ROE trend downward**: from 26% to 15%, not yet bottomed out
3. **Valuation is not cheap**: 26x PE corresponds to an asset-heavy company with declining profits
4. **Extremely low returns to shareholders**: The dividend rate is only 0.4%, almost no dividends are paid, and all profits are reinvested

### Safety margin judgment

**The margin of safety at current prices is insufficient. ** Based on 26x PE, downward profit trend, and free cash flow far lower than accounting profit, BYD's current valuation is more suitable for "watching and waiting" rather than "decisive buying". If the stock price falls back to 18-20x 2026 expected PE (corresponding to about 30% downside), the safety margin will be more sufficient.
