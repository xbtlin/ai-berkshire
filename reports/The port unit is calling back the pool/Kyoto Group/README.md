## Research Framework

This study adopts a four-dimensional parallel analysis framework:

1. **Business model analysis** — business essence, moat, user value
2. **Financial Valuation Analysis** — Earnings quality, capital allocation, safety margin
3. **Industry competition analysis** — competitive landscape, industry trends, ecological niche
4. **Risk Management Assessment** — core risks, management capabilities, governance structure

## Core investment variables

- Takeaway business: nearly 30 billion will be invested in 2025, with a target market share of 30% in 2026, which is the largest variable in profit
- Logistics barriers: JD Logistics’ revenue in 2025 will be 217.1 billion (+18.8%), with more than 90,000 integrated supply chain customers
- Shareholder return: US$3 billion in buybacks + US$1.4 billion in dividends in 2025, total return ~10%
- Competitive landscape: E-commerce share was eroded by Douyin, slipping from second to third (GMV caliber)
- Growth slows down: 2026Q1 revenue growth dropped to 4.9%, and the impact of the decline in state subsidies for home appliances is apparent
