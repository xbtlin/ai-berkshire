## 1. Investment thesis (Bull Case)

**Core logic: Logistics barriers + Valuation bottom + Shareholder returns = Protected value investment**

1. **Valuation has a safety margin**: 10 times PE is at a historical low; retail core profit of 51.4 billion corresponds to only 7 times PE; segment valuation shows a 40-60% discount
2. **Logistics barriers cannot be copied**: JD Logistics’ revenue in 2025 will be 217.1 billion, with more than 90,000 integrated supply chain customers. This is a moat formed by ten years of investment.
3. **Generous shareholder returns**: Repurchase of US$3 billion + dividends of US$1.4 billion in 2025, with a total return of 10%, leading among Chinese concept stocks
4. **Profit margin improvement trend**: Retail operating profit +25% year-on-year, platform and service-oriented transformation is releasing profits
5. **If the takeaway is successful, the valuation will be revalued**: If the market share reaches 30% in 2026 and losses begin to be reduced, the market will revalue JD.com’s platform

---

## 2. Objection to thesis (Bear Case)

**Core logic: slowing growth + continued cash burn + erosion of share = value trap**

1. **Revenue growth plummeted**: only +4.9% in 2026Q1, state subsidies for home appliances declined + high base, weak organic growth
2. **The money-burning black hole of takeaways**: 30 billion will be invested in 2025, and "depends on the competitive situation" in 2026 - the scale of losses is uncontrollable
3. **Structural threat to Douyin**: GMV has surpassed JD.com, falling from second to third, and share erosion continues
4. **Profit recovery is far away**: Non-GAAP net profit dropped from 47.8 billion to 27 billion, and it is uncertain when it will recover
5. **Founder’s Gambling Sex**: The lesson of retreating after huge losses in community group buying is right in front of us, and food delivery may make the same mistake again

## 4. Valuation anchoring

### Scenario analysis

| Scenario | Probability | 2027 profit estimate | Reasonable PE | Target market value | Corresponding increase |
|------|------|----------------|--------|----------|----------|
| Optimistic | 25% | Non-GAAP 45 billion | 12x | 540 billion | +45% |
| Neutral | 50% | Non-GAAP 35 billion | 10x | 350 billion | -5%~flat |
| Pessimistic | 25% | Non-GAAP 25 billion | 8x | 200 billion | -45% |
| **Weighted** | — | — | — | **360 billion** | **Flat** |

The weighted valuation is close to the current market value, indicating that market pricing is basically reasonable. But if the probability of the optimistic scenario coming true is higher than 25%, then there is an opportunity at the current price.

### Bottom-line valuation (extremely pessimistic)

- JD Logistics market value: ~200 billion Hong Kong dollars (listed and can be directly observed)
- JD Health market value: ~100 billion HKD
- Market value of Dada Group: ~HK$10 billion
- Net cash: ~36.7 billion yuan
- **Total realizable assets: ~HK$300 billion**

This means that even if the core e-commerce business is given a zero valuation, JD.com's "liquidation value" is close to the current market value. This provides a hard base.

## 6. Action suggestions

### Conditions suitable for buying

1. The food delivery business shows clear signs of narrowing losses in the next 1-2 quarters
2. Retail profit margin remains above 4.5%
3. The stock price fell back to PE 8-9 times due to short-term negative news (quarterly results were lower than expected)
4. Investors have the patience to hold for 2-3 years

### Not suitable for buying conditions

1. Highly skeptical of the food delivery business (thinking there is a high probability of failure)
2. Unable to withstand short-term fluctuations (losses from takeaways may cause quarterly results to continue to be lower than expected)
3. Pursue high growth (JD.com is already one trillion in size, and it is difficult to achieve a growth rate of 20%+)
4. Zero tolerance for geo-risks in Chinese concept stocks

## 7. Final judgment

**JD.com is an investment target with a "clear bottom line and vague upper limit". **

The bottom line is clear: logistics network value + net cash + continued repurchase dividends provide hard downside protection. Even if all new businesses fail, JD.com's liquidation value would be close to its current market cap.

The upper limit is vague: whether food delivery can be successful, whether share can be stabilized, and when profits will be restored—the answers to these key questions will only become clear in the next 2-3 years.

**For investors who can tolerate uncertainty and have the patience to wait, JD.com with a PE of 10 times is a choice with a reasonable risk-reward ratio. But it's not a "must-heavy position" opportunity - because the timing and strength of the catalyst are uncertain. **

Suggested positioning: **Observe positions/small positions**, wait for clear signals from the takeaway business (whether it is successful loss reduction or decisive stop loss), and then decide whether to increase the position.
