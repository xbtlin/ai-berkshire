# JD Group — Industry Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: competitive landscape, industry trends, ecological niche, reverse thinking
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. Panorama of China’s e-commerce industry

### Market size

China's e-commerce market has entered a mature stage. In 2025, national online retail sales will exceed 15 trillion yuan, and the online penetration rate will be close to 35%. The incremental space has shifted from "moving offline to online" to "stock game + category deepening + service upgrade".

### GMV Market Share (2025)

| Platform | GMV Estimation | Share | Trends |
|------|---------|------|------|
| Taotian Group | ~8 trillion | ~31% | Slowly declining |
| Pinduoduo | ~5.2 trillion | ~19% | Steady rise |
| Douyin e-commerce | ~3.5 trillion | ~24% (Goldman Sachs caliber including live broadcast) | Rapid rise |
| JD.com | ~4 trillion | ~16% | Slight decrease |
| Kuaishou E-commerce | ~1.8 trillion | ~7% | Stable |

The most eye-catching change: **Douyin e-commerce GMV has surpassed JD.com**, from almost zero in 2020 to nearly 3.5 trillion in 2025, completing the ten-year journey of traditional e-commerce in five years. JD.com fell from second place to third place in the e-commerce "iron triangle".

### Differences in income caliber

But the GMV caliber is misleading. JD.com mainly relies on self-operated revenue, and most of its 1.3 trillion revenue is actual product sales. The revenue of Douyin and Pinduoduo is much lower than GMV (mainly charging commissions and advertising fees). From the perspective of "real money passing through", JD.com is still one of the largest retail companies in China.

## 2. Analysis of core competitors

### Pinduoduo: The ruler of low-price mentality

Pinduoduo's core weapon is "extreme low price + social fission". It has established an unshakable reputation in the fields of daily necessities, agricultural products, and white-label products.

Threats to JD.com:
- Stealing away price-sensitive users, JD.com’s “tens of billions of subsidies” are essentially a passive response
- Established stronger user stickiness in lower-tier cities
- Temu's overseas expansion provides Pinduoduo with a second growth curve

JD.com’s response space:
- Quality positioning is differentiated from Pinduoduo and does not compete on the same dimension
- Pinduoduo has yet to make an effective breakthrough in the high-priced category of 3C home appliances.
- JD Logistics’ fulfillment experience cannot be replicated by Pinduoduo

### Douyin E-commerce: A game-breaker for content e-commerce

Douyin e-commerce has been the biggest variable in the past five years. Its "interest recommendation + short video cultivation + live broadcast transaction" model has achieved explosive growth in non-standard categories such as clothing, beauty, and food.

Threats to JD.com:
- Content e-commerce is naturally suitable for impulse consumption, eroding JD.com’s share in clothing, beauty and other categories
- Douyin is improving its "shelf e-commerce" capabilities, and its mall GMV has exceeded 40%
- Contract fulfillment capabilities are also catching up quickly (connected to Tongda Logistics)

JD.com’s differentiation:
- For standard product categories such as 3C home appliances, users are more likely to "search and compare prices" rather than "grow content"
- JD.com’s after-sales service system (door-to-door installation, trade-in) is irreplaceable in the major appliance category
- Enterprise procurement (JD enterprise business) is an area that Douyin is difficult to enter.

### Meituan: The champion of instant retail

JD.com enters food delivery and directly competes with Meituan. Meituan’s dominance in the local lifestyle sector is similar to JD.com’s position in 3C home appliances.

Competitive situation:
- Meituan’s food delivery market share is about 65% (2025), and JD.com’s food delivery is about 15%
- JD.com’s 2026 target of 30% market share means it will steal about 10 percentage points from Meituan
- Meituan’s rider network, business relationships, and user habits are all deep barriers
- But JD.com’s logistics and distribution capabilities and subsidies are posing a real threat

### Alibaba: Transformation of an old rival

Alibaba is going through a painful transformation period. Taotian's share is declining, its cloud business is being caught up by competitors, and its overseas business continues to suffer losses. But Alibaba is still JD.com’s most direct target:

- Both are transforming from platform/self-operated to "omni-channel"
- All are facing share erosion from Douyin
- All are investing more in overseas business
- Valuation levels are converging (both about 10 times PE)

## 3. JD.com’s ecological niche

### Core advantage areas

JD.com has an irreplaceable position in the following categories/scenarios:

1. **3C Digital**: The preferred platform for users, guarantee of authenticity + professional services to form a strong mind
2. **Major appliances**: Integrated door-to-door delivery and installation service, difficult for competitors to copy
3. **Enterprise Procurement**: JD.com’s enterprise business serves millions of enterprise customers, and To B barriers are deep
4. **Instant delivery**: Instant retail scenarios covered by JD Daojia + Dada
5. **Medicine and Health**: JD Health has become one of the largest online pharmaceutical retail platforms in China

### Vulnerable areas

1. **Clothing, Shoes and Hats**: Dominated by Taotian and Douyin e-commerce companies
2. **Daily Necessities**: Suppressed by Pinduoduo’s low-price strategy
3. **Live streaming e-commerce**: JD.com’s live streaming ecosystem is far weaker than Douyin and Taobao
4. **Takeaway Catering**: Latecomers face the deep barriers of Meituan

### Niche stability

JD.com's ecological niche is essentially "quality e-commerce + logistics service provider". This positioning is solid in the short term - no competitor is willing to spend ten years building another logistics network. But in the long term, if the consumption downgrade trend continues and users' willingness to pay for "quality premium" decreases, JD.com's ecological niche may be compressed.

## 4. Industry trend analysis

### Trend 1: From "traffic is king" to "supply chain is king"

The focus of e-commerce competition is shifting from front-end traffic acquisition to back-end supply chain efficiency. This is good for JD.com – JD Logistics’ integrated supply chain capabilities are industry-leading.

### Trend 2: The rise of instant retail

Consumers are increasingly unwilling to wait. "Half-hour delivery" and "one-hour delivery" are becoming standard expectations. JD Daojia, Meituan flash sales, and Ele.me near-field e-commerce companies are all competing for this market. JD.com’s warehouse and distribution network has structural advantages in this trend.

### Trend Three: Going overseas

It has become a consensus for Chinese e-commerce companies to go overseas. JD.com's internationalization strategy does not follow cross-border e-commerce (Temu, SHEIN models), but adheres to the "local e-commerce" model - local employees, local procurement, and local delivery. This mode is heavier and slower, but also has more barriers.

### Trend 4: Normalization of supervision

Antitrust supervision, platform economy anti-unfair competition, and data security—these have become the norm in the industry. The competition investigation into the food delivery industry launched in January 2026 may limit the subsidies provided by each platform, which will instead benefit JD.com, which has supply chain advantages (reduce pure money-burning competition).

## 5. Reverse thinking: Why might JD.com not perform as well as expected?

Several uncomfortable questions must be faced honestly:

1. **Takeaway may be another community group buying**: JD.com invested heavily in community group buying and eventually withdrew completely. The competition barriers for food delivery are higher, the opponents are stronger, and the risk of failure cannot be ignored.

2. **Is the self-operated model obsolete in the era of consumption downgrade? ** If China's economic growth continues to slow down and consumers increasingly pursue cost-effectiveness, JD.com's "quality premium" strategy may be ineffective.

3. **Growth ceiling of 740 million users**: The total number of Chinese Internet users is about 1.1 billion. 740 million active users means that the penetration rate has exceeded 67%, and the room for growth is limited.

4. **The threat of Douyin may be underestimated**: Douyin is not only taking away GMV share, but also changing users' shopping habits - from "search shopping" to "recommendation shopping". This is a threat to the foundation of JD.com’s model.

5. **Management Challenges of 900,000 Employees**: JD.com is one of the largest private employers in China. Rising labor costs, management efficiency, and social responsibility pressure are continuing challenges.

## 6. Durability of competitive advantage

JD.com’s competitive advantages can be divided into two levels:

**Persistence layer** (valid for 5-10 years):
- Logistics infrastructure
- 3C home appliance category thinking
- Corporate procurement channels
- Integrated supply chain capabilities

**Vulnerable layer** (may erode in 2-3 years):
- User growth momentum
- Price competitiveness brought about by tens of billions of subsidies
- The pursuit of live streaming and content e-commerce
- Takeaway market share (subsidy driven, non-organic growth)

## 8. Key conclusions

JD.com’s competitive position in China’s e-commerce industry is undergoing subtle but important changes. Logistics barriers and quality mentality are still powerful weapons of differentiation, but the marginal trend of market share is unfavorable - GMV caliber has been surpassed by Douyin, and the space for user growth has narrowed.

From an investment perspective, the core judgment lies not in "whether JD.com will die" (no), but in "whether JD.com can find a second growth curve in new battlefields such as AI, instant retail, and internationalization." If it can only hold on to the basics of 3C home appliances and logistics, then JD.com may become a "high dividend, low growth" value asset - this is not necessarily a bad thing, but it will not bring excess returns.

The most alarming thing is Douyin e-commerce. It is not just a new competitor, but is changing the rules of the game in the entire e-commerce industry - from "people looking for goods" to "goods looking for people". JD.com needs to prove that under this new paradigm, the value of logistics and supply chain has not declined, but has become more important.
