# I. Management portraits

# Liu Keungdong: Founder Control Entrepreneur

Liu is a soul man and a real control agent in Kyouta. Although the CEO position has been given to Xu Jin, he still controls the direction of the company through a super-voting structure.

** Management style features:**
- ** Strong implementation orientation**: strong return towards the end of 2024, leading multiple rounds of organizational restructuring and reducing reporting levels
- **Pilling management**: provision for five-storey reporting lines from him to the lowest level of staff
- ** Time distribution**: about 60% in talent and organization, 20% in business experience and strategic planning, and 20% in new business
- ** Strategic decision-making**: He's the one who's got the key decisions on self-building logistics, military sales, etc.

** Management team:**
- CEO Xiaoyu (former CFO): Financial background, sound and long-term, good at cost control and capital allocation
- The Kyoto Logistics, Health and Production companies have independent management teams.
- Multiple senior staff rotations or separations since 2024, with frequent organizational changes

# The virtue of management

1. ** Strategic long-term vision**: self-established logistics in 2007, sustained for 10 years with everyone ' s opposition, ultimately proven right
2. ** Strong learning ability**: fast-track adjustment strategy 2024-2025, from "Rally for scale" to "Ride" priority
3. ** Revealing self-revolution**: overturning the existing organizational structure on several occasions and avoiding short-term pains
4. ** Shareholder ' s awareness of return**: three years in a row of large buybacks + dividends, cumulative $4.2 billion

# The fear of management

** Critical human risk**: The strategic direction of Kyouta is highly dependent on Liu Jiangdong's personal judgement that if he leaves day-to-day management, the company may be swinging in direction
2. ** Decision-making style radical**: The fact that the takeout business is invested in $30 billion a year, and the community buys the same big investment and withdraws, indicates that the "strategic loss" border sense is not clear enough
3. ** Inadequate senior management stability**: frequent change of CEO from Xu Lei to Xu Jian; multiple core executive separations and management continuity doubts
4. ** Founder personal dispute**: Although the 2018 events have been over many years, they still have residual effects on the international image of the company

# II. Governance structures

- External shareholders cannot influence major strategic decisions
- The company's direction is entirely determined by Liu Keungdong.

# III. List of core risks

# Risk I: Strategic failure in takeout operations (impact: )

This is the single largest risk factor at present.

- **Injection of about 30 billion ** in 2025, which directly results in net profit cutbacks
- Management committed to 2026 as "below 2025" but qualified it: "depending on the competitive posture."
- The target of 30 per cent of the city in 2026 (currently 15 per cent) requires a 65 per cent share of the United States regiment to take it.
- If self-sufficiency cannot be achieved within 2-3 years, the community buy-out cycle may be repeated
- The January 2026 AMA increased policy uncertainty about subsidy strategies

** Worst-case scenario**: Sales continued to lose more than three years, accumulated more than 80 billion dollars, eventually exited or marginalized, similar to community group purchasing lessons.

** Best case scenario**: in 2026 the city reached 25-30% and in 2027 the UE (unit economic model) was converted to become a high frequency flow portal for the Kyoto ecology.

# Risk two: Slow growth in core categories (impact: )

- Household electricity is the largest product category in Kyoto, with the national electricity market retailing volume of 893.1 billion (-4.3 per cent) in mid-2025 and is expected to decline further to 833.2 billion (-6.7 per cent) in 2026
- The fall of the domestic remedial policy, with a one-time stimuli in 2025
- 3C digital growth is dependent on product replacement cycles, which have increased in recent years

If the core category continues to contract, the rate of growth in the Camp will likely be sustained at a low number of digits over the long term, and the valuation multipliers may be further compressed.

# Risk three: Structural threats from the vibrating electrician (impact: )

The shattering electric power provider is moving from "interested power" to "global power":
- The shelf scene, which is over 40% GMV, is no longer just live impulse consumption.
- Logistics compliance shortboards (access to the system) were completed
- APPs are used daily by users in the long term
- Building of ownership branding and supply chain capacity

This is not a short-term threat, but a paradigm shift in the electricity industry. Kyoto needs to think about how to remain competitive in the "integrator" era.

# Risk four: geopolitical and regulatory risks (impact: )

- Central American tension may affect the Central General Valuation
- NASDAQ raised the listing threshold, and while Kyouta met the requirements, the systematic discounts may be sustained
- The tightening of domestic antimonopoly and platform economic regulation, which may limit subsidy strategies and market expansion
- Data security and cross-border data transmission regulations may increase compliance costs

# Risk 5: Labour risk for 900,000 employees (impact: )

The total number of employees in the country is over 900,000 (including couriers) and is one of the largest private employers in China.

- Increase in the minimum wage rate and increase in human costs
- Increased social security compliance pressure
- The labour rights of riders/sellers are receiving increasing attention from society
- AI and automation may change the pattern of labour in the logistics industry, but a large amount of manpower is still required in the short term

# IV. Long-term certainty analysis

# High-level certainty factor

1. **The value of the logistics network**: The basic fact that commodities need to be distributed will not change, regardless of the changing patterns of power suppliers. The network effects and size advantages of the logistics in Kyoto will be difficult to reverse in 5-10 years.

2. **3C appliances ' s physiology**: Consumers ' demand for valuable electronics is rigid for the good and after-sale. This advantage of the class will not disappear in the short term.

3. **ToB barrier for enterprise procurement**: Once the enterprise ' s customer ' s procurement system is connected, the migration costs are extremely high.

# # Low-determinate elements

1. ** Success of takeout**: total uncertainty, 50/50 probability
2. **The stability of the GMV share**: Depending on the speed of development of the diagonal power provider and the response strategy of Kyouta
3. **When profits will return to 2024**: Depending on the rate of loss on the takeout, it may take 2 to 3 years
4. ** Success of internationalization**: Many attempts have been unsuccessful in history and localization models need to be validated over a long period of time

# VII. Key findings

The risk picture of Kyoto can be summarized as:** bottom line is secure, but the ceiling is uncertain**.

Bottom-line security comes from: an irreplaceable, net cash balance sheet for logistics networks, a continuous shareholder return, and a class of barriers to 3C electricity. Even in the most pessimistic scenario, Kyoto will not "dea" and will become a low-growth value asset.

The upper limit is uncertain: whether the takeout will succeed, whether the vibration will be contained, whether internationalization will break, and when profits will recover. The answers to these questions will become clear in the next 2-3 years.

Management is a double-edged sword. The energy and enforcement of Liu Jiandong guarantee that Kyoutung will not fall into bureaucracy and internalism (as compared to some large companies), but his radical style also means that companies may make high-risk and high-return bets. Investors need to be conscious of the characteristics of this "founder-type enterprise" – you buy more than just a business, you have a human judgement.
