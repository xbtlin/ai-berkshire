# JD.com — Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital allocation, cash flow, safety margin
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. Income and growth quality

### Revenue scale and trends

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026Q1 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Total revenue (100 million yuan) | 10,847 | 11,588 | 13,091 | 3,157 |
| Year-on-year growth rate | +3.7% | +6.8% | +13.0% | +4.9% |
| Merchandise revenue (100 million yuan) | — | — | 10,238 | — |
| Service revenue (100 million yuan) | — | — | 2,853 | — |

Key observations:
1. **Revenue growth in 2025 will reach 13%, a new high in recent years**, mainly driven by the takeaway business and the recovery of the basic e-commerce market
2. **The growth rate in 2026Q1 dropped sharply to 4.9%**. The core reason is the decline of state subsidies for home appliances (retail sales in the home appliance market are expected to decline by 6.7% in 2025) and the high base effect
3. **The proportion of service revenue has increased from approximately 18% to 22%**, and the growth rate (23.6%) is much higher than that of commodity revenue (10.3%). There is a clear trend of structural optimization.

A hidden worry about the quality of growth is that after excluding the consolidated effects of new businesses such as takeout, the organic growth rate of core e-commerce may only be single digits. This means that JD.com's "natural growth engine" is slowing down.

### User growth

Annual active users exceeded 740 million, with double-digit growth for 10 consecutive quarters. User growth mainly comes from:
- Penetration into lower-tier cities (channels such as Jingxi)
- New users brought by the takeout business (accumulated service exceeds 240 million ordering users)
- Ten billion subsidy strategy to attract new customers

However, the quality of user growth needs to be examined - the retention rate of users attracted by food delivery subsidies after the subsidy is withdrawn is a key variable.

## 2. Profit quality analysis

### Profit Panorama

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| GAAP net profit (100 million yuan) | 414 | 196 | -52.7% |
| Non-GAAP net profit (100 million yuan) | 478 | 270 | -43.5% |
| JD.com’s retail operating profit (100 million yuan) | 411 | 514 | +25.0% |
| 2026Q1 GAAP net profit | — | 5.1 billion | — |
| 2026Q1 Non-GAAP net profit | — | 7.4 billion | — |

This set of data reveals a key fact: **JD Retail’s profitability is actually improving significantly (+25%), and the decline in profits is entirely due to losses in new business**.

In 2025, the group's net profit will be cut in half, but the operating profit of the retail segment will reach a record high of 51.4 billion yuan. This shows that there is no problem with the profitability of the core e-commerce business, but the problem lies in the "strategic losses" of new businesses such as food delivery.

### Profitability Trends

JD Retail's operating profit margin will increase from approximately 3.7% in 2024 to approximately 4.6% in 2025, mainly due to:
- Increase in commissions and advertising revenue brought about by the increase in the proportion of POP merchants
- Continuous optimization of supply chain efficiency
- Scale effects dilute fixed costs

However, at the group level, due to heavy losses in new businesses (takeout and internationalization), the overall net profit margin was compressed to about 1.5%.

## 3. Cash flow and balance sheet

### Cash flow status

JD.com has maintained positive operating cash flow in the long term. Despite a sharp decline in profits in 2025, operating cash flow remains healthy, mainly because:
- Have strong bargaining power with suppliers under the self-operated model (account period advantage)
- Depreciation of logistics assets is a non-cash expense, and actual cash flow is better than profit performance
- Increase in the proportion of light asset income brought about by platform transformation

### Balance Sheet Health

JD.com is in a **net cash position** with net cash of approximately RMB 36.7 billion. This means:
- No debt pressure
- Have sufficient ammunition to support new business investments such as takeout
- Even if food delivery continues to burn money for 2-3 years, it will not threaten the company's financial security

###Capital Expenditure

JD Logistics is the largest capital expenditure direction. In 2025, JD Logistics’ self-operated overseas warehouse area will double, and domestic warehousing capacity will continue to expand. However, as the logistics network matures, the intensity of capital expenditure is expected to decrease in the future, releasing more free cash flow.

## 4. Return to shareholders

### Repurchase

- Launch a buyback program of up to US$5 billion in August 2024 (valid until August 2027)
- Cumulative repurchases in 2025 will be approximately US$3 billion, accounting for 6.3% of outstanding shares
- Remaining quota as of May 2025 is approximately US$3.5 billion

### Dividends

- Accumulated dividends of US$4.2 billion for three consecutive years
- Dividend of US$1 per ADS in 2025, total dividend of approximately US$1.4 billion
- Dividend payout ratio is approximately 50% (based on GAAP net profit)

### Total shareholder return

In 2025, repurchase of US$3 billion + dividend of US$1.4 billion = US$4.4 billion, with a total return of approximately 10%. This level belongs to the first echelon among Chinese concept stocks.

But it should be noted: **The high rate of return is partly because the denominator becomes smaller due to falling profits**, and such a high rate of return may be unsustainable in the context of narrowing profits.

JD.com’s valuation is at the bottom of the e-commerce industry, close to Alibaba and significantly lower than Pinduoduo and Meituan.

### Reasonableness of valuation

There are legitimate reasons for low valuations:
1. **Growth Slows**: The growth rate in 2026Q1 is only 4.9%, and the market is worried that the growth engine will stall.
2. **Profit Uncertainty**: The scale and duration of money burning in takeaways are unpredictable
3. **Intensified competition**: Douyin e-commerce erodes market share, and GMV has surpassed JD.com
4. **Discount of Chinese Concept Stocks**: Systematic Discount of Geopolitical and Delisting Risks

Low valuations also have unreasonable elements:
1. **Retail core profits are concealed**: The PE corresponding to 51.4 billion retail operating profits is only about 7 times
2. **Logistics assets are undervalued**: JD Logistics has an independent market value of approximately HK$200 billion, but it has been ignored after the consolidated statements.
3. **10% shareholder return** provides ample safety cushion

### Safety Margin Assessment

If JD.com is split and valued:
- JD Retail: 51.4 billion operating profit, 12 times PE = 616.8 billion yuan
- JD Logistics: 7.7 billion net profit, 20 times PE = 154 billion yuan
- Other businesses (including food delivery): -30 billion loss, zero valuation
- Total: approximately 770 billion yuan, minus 20% discount on holding price = 616 billion yuan
- Current market value: approximately 360-380 billion yuan

According to segment valuation standards, the current market value is at a discount of approximately 40-60%. Even discounting the various component valuations, the margin of safety remains substantial.

## 6. Key financial risks

1. **Expanding losses in food delivery**: Management said that investment in 2026 will be lower than in 2025, but "depends on the competitive situation" - which means that if Meituan fights back fiercely, losses may exceed expectations.
2. **Home appliance cycle is down**: Home appliances are JD.com’s largest category. The market is expected to decline by 6.7% in 2026, directly affecting core revenue.
3. **The pace of profit margin recovery is uncertain**: Non-GAAP net profit will drop from 47.8 billion to 27 billion in 2025. Whether it can recover in 2026 depends on the speed of food delivery loss reduction
4. **Exchange rate risk**: Hong Kong stocks and ADRs are dual-listed, and fluctuations in the RMB exchange rate affect the actual returns of overseas investors.

## 8. Key conclusions

JD.com's financial situation presents a situation of "ice and fire": the profitability of its core retail business has reached a record high (operating profit of 51.4 billion), but large-scale losses in new businesses such as food delivery have cut off the group's profits.

From a valuation perspective, the current PE of 10 times has fully reflected the pessimistic expectations. If investors believe that: (1) takeaway losses will begin to narrow in 2026; (2) retail profit margins will continue to improve; (3) shareholder return policies will be maintained - then the current price has a good risk-reward ratio.

But we need to clearly realize: **10 times PE does not mean "cheap"**. If food delivery continues to burn money for three years, the home appliance market continues to shrink, and Douyin continues to erode its share, then the profit center may move further downward, and the 10 times PE may become 12 times or 15 times. The difference between a value trap and a value depression depends on execution in the next two years.
