# CK Hutchison (0001.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

## 1. Core risks

### Risk Matrix

| Risk type | Severity | Probability of occurrence | Comprehensive rating |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| ROE continues to be depressed (value trap) | High | High | ★★★★★ |
| The comprehensive discount will not be eliminated for a long time | High | High | ★★★★☆ |
| Global Trade Slowdown (Ports) | Medium | Medium | ★★★☆☆ |
| Impact of retail e-commerce (Watson’s) | Medium | Medium | ★★★☆☆ |
| UK Telecom Consolidation Risks | Medium | Medium | ★★★☆☆ |

### The biggest risk: "Value Trap"
ROE 2-3% means that even if you buy at 0.5 times PB, the annualized return you get is only 4-6% (=ROE/PB). It's better to just buy 5% bonds. Unless ROE improves or discounts are eliminated, "cheap" may always remain "cheap."

## 2. Management evaluation

### Li Zeju’s capital allocation
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Front:
1. 3UK merged with Vodafone - although there was a loss, it ended the "cash burning" state
2. UKPN for sale – lock in profit at high price
3. Net debt ratio dropped to 7% - extremely conservative financial situation
4. The continued sales of non-core assets are in the right direction.

Negative:
1. Too slow – the market has been waiting for years
2. Lack of "amazing moves" - such as Watsons' IPO
3. ROE improvement is not obvious

### Attitude towards shareholders
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

- Dividends are stable but not generous (4-5%)
- Limited buyback activity
- No special dividends or large-scale value release actions
- 7% net debt ratio means there is huge leverage/repurchase space but it is not used

## 3. Scenario analysis

### Optimistic scenario (probability 20%)
- Announcing the spin-off and listing of Watsons
- Large scale buybacks (utilizing low leverage)
- Comprehensive discount narrowed from 50% to 30%
- Share price rises 40-60%

### Baseline scenario (probability 55%)
- Simplify slowly, sell 1-2 small assets per year
- ROE improved slightly to 3-4%
- Dividend maintained at 4-5%
- The stock price fluctuates slightly, and the annual return is 5-7%

### Pessimistic scenario (probability 25%)
- The release of value has not been realized for a long time
- Global recession affects ports and retail
- ROE declines further
- Discount widened, share price fell 15-20%

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Risk Management Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

CK Hutchison's risk-benefit ratio is not attractive enough: it has PB protection on the downside but relies on uncertain catalysts on the upside. ROE 2-3% is the worst figure among the 13, indicating that the current group structure is "consuming" rather than "creating" value.

From Li Lu's perspective: "For a company with an ROE of 2-3%, even if the PB is only 0.4 times, your cumulative return after holding it for 10 years is only 20-30%. This is not an exciting long-term investment. Unless you see clear signals of splitting/simplification, your capital can get better returns elsewhere."
