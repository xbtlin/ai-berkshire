## One sentence conclusion

CK Hutchison is a comprehensive group with "good assets but poor ROE" - PB of 0.4 times seems cheap, but an ROE of 2-3% means that this "cheapness" may be eternal. Unless we see clear catalysts such as a Watsons spin-off or a massive buyback, we wouldn't recommend taking a heavy position.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic (event-driven)
1. SOTP is discounted by 50%. If the value is released, there is huge room for upside.
2. The net debt ratio is only 7%, and there is huge room for repurchase/special dividends
3. Assets are being sold to simplify the structure (UKPN, 3UK, etc.)
4. The possibility of Watsons’ spin-off and listing exists
5. PB 0.4x provides extreme downside protection

### Bearish logic (basic type)
1. ROE 2-3% - the worst among the 13, with extremely low capital efficiency
2. Synthetic discounts may be "structural" - won't go away without a catalyst
3. 3UK’s consolidated loss of 10.5 billion shows that not all past decisions were successful.
4. Lack of clear growth engine
5. Lack of courage for major reforms after taking over

## Buying conditions

1. **Watson’s announces spin-off and listing** – the strongest catalyst
2. **Management announces large-scale buyback (10 billion+)** - taking advantage of low leverage
3. **PE drops below 12 times** (based on basic profit), corresponding to a higher dividend rate
4. Otherwise, capital should be allocated to targets with higher ROE.

## Position suggestions

- **Current price**: Active allocation is not recommended (ROE is too low)
- **If a catalyst appears**: 3-5% position can be opened (event-driven logic)
- **Alternative option**: If you are optimistic about the Yangtze River system, buy Yangtze River Infrastructure directly (purer + 28 consecutive years of growth)

## Keep track of key points

1. Any signals of Watsons spin-off/IPO
2. Buyback and special dividend announcement
3. Progress on further asset sales
4. ROE trend
5. Shareholdings and strategic trends of the controlling family
