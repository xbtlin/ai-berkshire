# CKH Holdings (0001.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: Cheung Hutchison Industrial Holdings Limited
- **Stock Code**: 0001.HK
- **Industry**: Conglomerate
- **PE(TTM)**: ~19x
- **ROE**：2-3%

| Reported net profit | HK$11.84 billion | (including 3UK’s consolidated loss of HK$10.5 billion) |

| Retail revenue | HK$209.3 billion | +10% |

## Investment Highlights
1. Global diversified group - ports, retail, infrastructure, energy, telecommunications
2. Asset sales release value (3UK merger, UKPN sale, etc.)
3. Very low net debt ratio (down to 7%)
4. Li Ka-shing family’s long-term capital allocation capabilities

## Core Risks
1. ROE is only 2-3% - the lowest among the 13 companies
2. Severe comprehensive discount (the market hates complex groups)
3. The 3UK merger resulted in a one-time loss of HK$10.5 billion
4. Business is too scattered and it is difficult to form synergy
