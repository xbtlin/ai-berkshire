# CKH Holdings (0001.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|------|
| Reported net profit | HK$11.8 billion | Including 3UK consolidated loss of 10.5 billion |
| Underlying net profit | HK$22.3 billion | +7% (excluding one-off) |
| ROE | 2-3% | Very low |
| Port EBIT growth | +8% | Stable |
| Retail EBIT growth | +12% | Not bad |

**Profitability Rating: ★☆☆☆☆ (1.5/5)**

ROE 2-3% is "unacceptable" under Buffett's framework. Even excluding one-time losses, the basic profit of 22.3 billion corresponds to total assets of over one trillion, and ROE is still less than 3%. This shows that asset utilization efficiency is extremely poor.

### Impact of 3UK merger
The merger of 3UK and Vodafone UK resulted in a one-off non-cash loss of HK$10.5 billion, reflecting the long-term value destruction of the British telecommunications business. Although this loss is non-cash, it illustrates a problem: not all past investment decisions were successful.

### Balance Sheet
Front:
- Net gearing ratio is only 7% (including the sale of UKPN)
- A large amount of hidden asset value (property, infrastructure rights, etc.)
- Ample liquidity

This is CK Hutchison's "hidden advantage" - extremely low leverage means minimal financial risk.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): ~19x (based on reported profit); ~15x based on underlying profit
- PB: about 0.4-0.5 times (a significant break)
- Dividend yield: about 4-5%

### How big is the "comprehensive discount"?

Estimated by segment valuation (SOTP):
| Business | Valuation Methodology | Valuation |
|------|----------|------|
| Cheung Kong Infrastructure + Power Industry Holdings | Market value method | ~150 billion HKD |
| Ports | 10x EBITDA | ~HK$170 billion |
| Watsons Retail | 10x EBITDA | ~HK$180 billion |
| Telecommunications (3UK 49%) | Benchmarking | ~HK$50 billion |
| Others (Energy/Investment) | | ~HK$50 billion |
| **Total** | | **~HK$600 billion** |
| **Current market capitalization** | | **~300 billion HKD** |
| **Comprehensive discount** | | **About 50%** |

**Market value is only 50% of segment valuation** - This is a typical "group discount" of Hong Kong stock conglomerates.

### What does PB 0.4-0.5 times mean?
The market believed that CK Hutchison's management failed to effectively release asset value, so it priced the company at a 50% discount on net assets. This is both a risk signal (value trap?) and an opportunity signal (what if value is unlocked?).

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**It's hard. ** The business is too fragmented - you need to judge multiple variables such as global port trade, retail consumption, telecommunications competition, and infrastructure supervision at the same time.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Available in each business, but not at the group level. ** Watsons has brand barriers and Port has location barriers, but "putting them together" does not have the effect of 1+1>2.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
**Capital allocation is incompetent but operational improvement is insufficient. ** The Li family is good at "buying and selling assets" but not good at "doing every business to the extreme".

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**There is a huge margin of safety at the bottom angle of the asset. ** PB 0.4-0.5 times, SOTP discount 50%. But ROE of 2-3% means that this "cheapness" may be a value trap - because low ROE continues to consume value.

## 4. "Value Trap" or "Value Release"?

### This may be a value trap because:
1. ROE < cost of capital - value continues to be destroyed
2. The conglomerate discount is "structural" - difficult to eliminate unless broken up
3. Lack of willingness for major changes after taking over

### This may be worth releasing because:
1. Assets being sold (UKPN, British Rail) - entering the "simplification" process
2. The net debt ratio drops to 7% - repurchase or special dividends can be issued
3. If streamlined to 2-3 core businesses + dividends, ROE can be significantly improved
4. PB 0.4x means there is huge room for releasing only part of the value

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★☆☆☆(2/5)**

CK Hutchison is a typical "cheap for a reason" case - the valuation is cheap (PB 0.4 times, SOTP discount 50%), but it is cheap for a reason (ROE is extremely low, the group structure is complex, and value is difficult to release).

Buffett would not buy this kind of company - he prefers simple, high ROE, easy-to-understand businesses. But if you are a "Special Situations Investor" and believe that CK Hutchison is heading towards the path of "simplification + value release", then the 50% discount does provide huge potential upside.

Key catalysts: Watsons spin-off, large-scale buybacks, special dividends, further asset sales. Without a catalyst, the discount is likely to remain in place for a long time.
