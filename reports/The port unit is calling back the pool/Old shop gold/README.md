#Laopu Gold (6181.HK) Investment Research

## Company Profile

Laopu Gold is a high-end ancient gold jewelry brand in China. It will be listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in June 2024. The company was founded by father and son Xu Gaoming and Xu Dongbo. It focuses on "ancient gold" craft products and is positioned in the high-end consumer market. It only operates in top commercial centers such as SKP and Vientiane City.

## Research background

Laopu Gold is one of the most controversial consumer stocks in Hong Kong stocks in the past two years. Bulls believe this is the rise of the "Chinese version of Hermès" - 45 stores have generated revenue of 27.3 billion, annual sales of a single store are nearly 1 billion, ROE exceeds 60%, and the net profit in the single quarter of 2026Q1 has reached 70% of the full year of 2025. Bears are questioning whether growth is just a by-product of the gold price boom cycle. Is the 16 billion inventory under the "zero hedging" strategy a time bomb? Will the ancient bulwark of gold stand the test of time?

This study attempts to answer a core question: How much of the super growth of Laopu Gold is attributed to brand power, and how much is attributed to the gold price cycle? **
