## 1. Answers to core questions

**How much of the super growth of Laopu Gold is attributed to its brand power, and how much is attributed to the gold price cycle? **

Based on in-depth analysis of four dimensions, our judgment is: **About 60% is attributed to the gold price cycle (including the price increase effect and consumer psychology of chasing prices), and 40% is attributed to true brand power and business model innovation. **

The basis is as follows:
- Breakdown of 2026Q1 revenue increase: 35% comes from price increases (directly related to gold prices), 25% comes from store expansion, and 40% comes from same-store sales growth
- However, same-store sales growth is still partly driven by the "rush buying effect" driven by expectations of rising gold prices.
- The second-hand recycling price is only 50-30% off the original price, indicating that consumers have not yet fully recognized the brand value that is independent of gold prices.
- When the price of gold plummeted by more than 8% in a single day in March 2026, the market's concerns about the old stores sharply amplified

**Brand power is real, but it has not yet fully passed the stress test of the "gold price down cycle". **

## 3. Five core advantages of Laopu Gold

### 1. Redefined a category
Laopu upgraded gold from a "commodity priced by the gram" to a "cultural consumer product priced by the piece", achieving a 55% brand premium. This kind of category innovation is rare in the history of Chinese consumer goods.

### 2. Ultimate single store efficiency
With 45 stores, the annual sales of a single store are nearly 1 billion yuan, which is rare in the global retail industry. This efficiency is not accidental, it is a comprehensive reflection of brand potential, location strategy and operational capabilities.

### 3. Management’s strategic restraint
Despite a growth rate of 200%+, it still refrains from opening stores, insists on high-end positioning, and does not engage in franchises. This kind of determination to "make money but not make money" is extremely rare among Chinese consumer goods companies.

### 4. Overseas incremental space
Singapore and Hong Kong have verified demand, and research institutions estimate that 39 stores can be opened overseas, with an increase of 6-15.6 billion yuan. This is a growth potential that has not yet been fully priced in by the market.

### 5. Stickiness of high-net-worth customers
Black gold card members contribute 70% of sales, the number of members increased by 74.3% to 610,000, and the overlap with luxury goods consumption is 82.4% - the stickiness and spending power of the core customer group are supported by evidence.

## 4. Five core risks of Laopu Gold

### 1. Gold price dependence (biggest risk)
The "zero hedging" strategy + 16 billion exposed inventory makes the company's profits and asset values highly tied to gold prices. A 20% drop in gold prices may result in a double blow of inventory impairment of 3.2 billion + shrinking demand.

### 2. Structural contradictions in cash flow
Operating cash flow is negative (net outflow of 2.2 billion in 2025H1), and bank loans of 6.2 billion are due in 2026. The "high profitability" of the income statement did not translate into actual cash inflows.

### 3. Barriers to competition are being eroded
Ancient gold has changed from "exclusive to old shops" to "four sisters coexisting". The homogenization of craftsmanship has accelerated, and a large number of competing products have been introduced into shopping malls. The proportion of self-production has dropped to 59%, and the R&D team has only 20 people - the "process barrier" narrative is facing challenges.

### 4. The brand is not time-tested enough
15 years of brand history vs. Hermès 180 years. Laopu's brand has not yet experienced a complete gold price cycle (up + down), and it is impossible to confirm whether the brand value exists independently of the gold price.

### 5. Corporate Governance
The family controls 78% of the shares, the founder wears three hats, and there is no external check and balance on key decisions. Radical financial strategies (zero hedging + high leverage stockpiling) are essentially the founder’s personal judgment and involve concentrated risks.

## 5. Strategic suggestions for different types of investors

### A. Value investors who pursue certainty

**Suggestion: Wait and wait for a better buying opportunity**

The current price of HK$592 (corresponding to approximately 18.6 times PE for 2026E) lacks a sufficient margin of safety under the neutral assumption. The most ideal buying conditions are:
- Gold prices experienced a correction of more than -15%
- The stock price of Laopu subsequently fell back to the range of HK$300-400.
- But the company's same-store sales figures remain positive

This combination of conditions will simultaneously validate brand power and provide a margin of safety.

### B. Growth investors willing to bear volatility

**Suggestion: You can establish a small position for observation**

The growth rate and single-store efficiency of long-established stores are rare in global consumer goods. The forward PE of 18.6 times corresponds to a PEG of 200%+ growth rate, which is extremely low. But it requires:
- Positions are controlled within 3-5% of the portfolio
- Set a clear stop loss line (such as gold prices falling below a certain key level, or same-store growth turning negative)
- Closely track quarterly financial data, especially operating cash flow and inventory changes

### C. Core questions that all investors need to answer

Before investing in an established store, you need to make your own judgment on the following issues:

1. **Where will the gold price go in the next three years? ** If you think gold prices will stay high or continue to rise, Laopu is an excellent leverage tool; if you think gold prices may pull back sharply, the current exposure is troubling.

2. **Is the brand premium of ancient gold sustainable? ** If you believe that the old shop is becoming the "Chinese version of Cartier", the current valuation is far from expensive; if you think that ancient gold is just a fad, then you shouldn't buy it no matter how cheap it is.

3. **How much do you trust the founder’s personal judgment? ** Laopu's "zero hedging" strategy, high-leverage stockpiling, and aggressive pricing—all key decisions stem from Xu Gaoming's personal judgment. Investing in a long-established store is essentially investing in the person’s judgment.

## 7. Final conclusion

Laopu Gold is a company** with real innovation, extreme efficiency and excellent management. It has successfully opened up a new high-end track in China's gold and jewelry industry. Revenue of 27.3 billion, net profit of 4.8 billion, and ROE of 65% in 2025 - these numbers are not luck, but a reflection of ability.

But it is also a company that is highly bound to the gold price cycle, has a radical financial strategy, and is facing challenges to its brand barriers**. 16 billion in exposed inventory, 6.2 billion in loans about to expire, and negative operating cash flow - these figures cannot be ignored.

The core odds of investing in a well-established store are clear: if the gold price remains high + the brand continues to be strong, the stock price may double; if the gold price drops sharply + competition intensifies, the stock price may halve. The current price reflects more neutral to optimistic expectations, leaving an insufficient margin of safety for value investors.

**Summary in one sentence: A good company, but not the best time to buy. Waiting for the gold price to give you an opportunity to "stress test" can not only verify the brand power, but also provide a margin of safety. **
