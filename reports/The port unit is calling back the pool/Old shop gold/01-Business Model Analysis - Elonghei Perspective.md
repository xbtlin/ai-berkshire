# Analysis of business model of Laopu Gold (6181.HK)

> Analysis dimensions: business nature, user value, differentiated positioning, business model sustainability
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. What is the essence of business?

Laopu Gold is essentially engaged in the brand premium business of gold consumer goods.

The logic of the traditional gold and jewelry industry (Chow Tai Fook, Lao Feng Xiang, China Gold) is "gold price + labor costs", with gross profit margins generally between 10% and 30%. It is essentially a low value-added model for bulk commodity retail. The breakthrough of Laopu Gold is that through ancient craftsmanship, high-end channels and cultural narratives, gold has been redefined from a "commodity priced by the gram" to a "cultural consumer product priced by the piece", achieving a brand premium rate of approximately 55% and a stable gross profit margin of around 41%.

Judging from the data, the price per gram of old stores has exceeded 2,000 yuan in 2025, while the international gold price during the same period was approximately 600-700 yuan per gram. The extra premium consumers pay for "ancient craftsmanship + brand experience" is the core profit source of this business.

**Key judgment: The essence of this business is not to sell gold, but to sell "cultural identity with gold as the carrier." **

---

## 2. What value is created for users?

### 1. Functional value: value-preserving properties + craft aesthetics

Gold naturally has the property of preserving value, which is the underlying logic behind consumer purchases. Ancient craftsmanship (more than 30 processes such as filigree, engraving, inlay, etc.) gives the product aesthetic differentiation, which is different from standardized machine-made gold jewelry.

### 2. Emotional value: identity expression of Chinese luxury goods

The overlap between Laopu Gold and luxury consumer groups such as LV and Hermès increased from 77.3% to 82.4%, indicating that its consumers are essentially a segmented group among high-net-worth individuals who pursue "Chinese aesthetics"**. Black gold card members (annual consumption of more than 500,000) contribute 70% of sales. What these consumers buy is not only gold jewelry, but also an expression of cultural identity and social currency.

### 3. Channel value: scarcity creation

The long-established stores only exist in top shopping malls such as SKP series (6 stores) and Vientiane City series (12 stores), with only 45 stores nationwide. This extremely restrained channel strategy creates a natural sense of scarcity—queuing, waiting for goods, and making appointments become part of the consumer experience, similar to Hermès’ distribution logic.

### User Value Assessment ★★★★☆

Long-established stores have indeed created differentiated value for high-end consumers, but it should be noted that the foundation of this value is the dual support of "gold preservation + brand premium". Once gold prices fall sharply, consumers may return to the "settlement by the gram" mentality, and the foundation of brand premiums will be shaken. The fact that the recycling price in the second-hand market is 50-30% off the original price shows that the market has not fully recognized the value of its brand independent of the gold price.

---

## 3. Dismantling the core links of the business model

### 1. Product strategy: "One price + regular price increase"

The old store adopts the "fixed price" model (not priced by the gram), and product pricing is decoupled from the price of gold. This is a key mechanism to achieve brand premium. The three cumulative price adjustments in 2025 will be about 45%. Consumer acceptance is good, and there is even a phenomenon of "rush buying before price increases".

What deserves more attention is the adjustment of product structure: the proportion of pure gold products dropped from 52.1% to 39%, and the proportion of inlaid products increased to 61%. The gross profit margin of inlaid products (45%-47%) is higher than that of pure gold products, and they are less dependent on gold prices, which shows that management is deliberately reducing its sensitivity to gold prices.

### 2. Channel strategy: ultimate quality

With 45 stores in 16 cities, the annual sales of a single store are nearly 1 billion - this efficiency is rare in the global retail industry. Compared with Chow Tai Fook's nearly 8,000 stores, the old store's choice is "it would rather open fewer stores, but also ensure the square footage and brand potential of each store."

In 2026, the management made it clear that it would not open new stores in the mainland, but would instead conduct "site selection upgrades" and "channel upgrades" - moving existing stores to better locations and larger areas. This strategy shows that management maintains restraint on "store opening growth" and pays more attention to the quality of single stores.

### 3. Procurement and Inventory: "Zero Hedging" Strategy

This is the most controversial aspect of the entire business model. Laopu does not use hedging tools such as gold futures or leasing, and directly purchases gold raw materials with its own funds. At the end of 2025, inventory reached 16 billion yuan (+290% year-on-year), and bank borrowings were 6.264 billion yuan (+350% year-on-year).

This strategy is a profit amplifier for "hoarding and adding value" when the gold price is rising, but it becomes a risk amplifier for "inventory impairment" when the gold price is falling. When the price of gold plummeted by more than 8% in a single day in March 2026, the market's concerns about the old store sharply amplified.

### 4. Breakdown of growth drivers (2026Q1)

According to analyst dismantling, the revenue increase in 2026Q1 is:
- **About 35% comes from product price increases** - a reflection of brand pricing power
- **About 25% comes from store expansion** - including new overseas stores
- **About 40% comes from same-store sales growth** - growth in real demand

The 40% same-store growth shows that demand is still expanding and is not driven purely by price increases and store openings.

## 4. Comparison with the real luxury model

| Dimension | Hermès | Long-established gold |

| Second-hand value retention rate | Premium | 50-30% off |
| Supply chain control | Extremely strong | Proportion of self-production dropped to 59% |
| R&D investment | High | Low (team only 20 people) |

**Key Gap**: The gross profit margin ceiling of established stores is obvious. The management admitted frankly that it "will not raise the price above 50%" because the cost of gold raw materials is rigid, and the brand premium space is not as good as that of leather goods, ready-made clothing and other categories. This means that the profit growth of old stores relies more on "volume growth" rather than "profit margin improvement."

## 5. Business model sustainability assessment

### Favorable factors

1. **Consumption Upgrading Trend**: China’s high-net-worth individuals are increasingly identifying with “Chinese aesthetics”, and ancient gold is in line with the national trend.
2. **Pricing Power Verification**: Three price increases in 2025 were well received by consumers, and Q1 net profit margin further improved after another price increase in February 2026.
3. **Overseas incremental space**: Singapore and Hong Kong stores have verified overseas demand. Research institutions estimate that about 39 stores can be opened overseas, with an increase of 6-15.6 billion yuan.
4. **Product structure optimization**: The increase in the proportion of inlaid products reduces direct dependence on gold prices

### Disadvantages

1. **The dependence on gold prices has not been fundamentally eliminated**: The 41% gross profit margin ceiling determines that when gold prices fall sharply, profit margins will be significantly under pressure.
2. **Technology barriers are eroded**: Junpei, Linchao, Baolan and other brands are following suit, and the golden track of ancient methods is changing from "Blue Ocean" to "Red Ocean"
3. **Scale ceiling**: The management actively limits the speed of store openings, and it is doubtful whether the 45-store model can support sustained high growth.
4. **Structural Contradictions in Cash Flow**: High stocking model leads to net operating cash outflow, reliance on bank borrowings, 6 billion loans due in 2026

## Core conclusion

The business model of Laopu Gold has real innovations - it has successfully upgraded gold from a commodity to a cultural consumer product. The efficiency of a single store is amazing, and the user stickiness (black gold card members contribute 70% of sales) has verified the existence of brand power.

But this model has a fundamental tension: **It attempts to use the narrative of "luxury goods" to sell a "commodity"**. Gold is not leather or cloth. It has globally unified and transparent pricing, and consumers will always have a "price per gram" ruler in their minds. This determines that the brand premium space of long-established stores has an upper limit, and it will face the return of consumer psychology during the downward gold price cycle.

The most important thing in doing business is to figure out "what money you make." Among the money earned by Laopu Gold, how much money comes from "brand premium" and how much money comes from "increased gold prices". This ratio directly determines the durability of the business. At present, the two are intertwined and difficult to completely separate - this is both the opportunity and the risk of investing in established companies.
