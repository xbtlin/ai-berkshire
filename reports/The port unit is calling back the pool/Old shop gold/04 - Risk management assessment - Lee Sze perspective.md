# # Management profiling

# # 1. The founder's inter-industry transformational capacity #

Xu Goming has moved from a division of the Bureau of Aquaculture to a gold jeweler, a scale that is itself a sign of great learning ability and commercial smell. From the creation of the gold treasure in 2004, the opening of the first Gufa gold store in 2009, and the opening of the port in 2024, with 27.3 billion profits in 2025 – it is not an accident that the industry has been deep-deeped in 20 years.

But it is important to note that Xu's three positions as Chairman, Managing Director, and Director of Commodity Research and Development are highly centralized. This is an efficiency advantage in the start-up phase, but it may become a governance bottleneck when the size of the company expands rapidly.

# 2. Strategic restraint

Management has shown rare restraint in the following areas:

- ** door-to-door expansion restraint**: With 200%+, only nine stores were added in 2025. In 2026, it was clearly stated that the mainland did not open new shops, but only "site upgrades." This "no profit or no gain" was a rare feature in Chinese consumer goods.
- ** Classes focus restraint**: only ancient gold jewellery, not silver, not platinum, not cross-cutting.
- ** Channel purity**: access to top malls, no association, no sinking market.

The mindset behind these decisions is: ** Instead of making money, it also protects brand power**. This contrasts sharply with the "opportunity" mentality of many Chinese consumer goods firms.

##3. Pricing capacity and commercial sensitivity

The three price increases in 2025, 45% cumulatively, and the second price increases in February 2026 - not only did they not contain demand, but they were "pre-price-grabbing." Management has a very good grasp of the pace and magnitude of the price increases:

- Make expectations before each price rise.
- The increase is in the range of "conspicuous to consumers."
- Observe the market flow and sales data immediately after price increases

This demonstrates a deep management understanding of consumer psychology.

## 4. Questions requiring vigilance

- **Fieship corporate governance**: Xu's father and son control 78.27% of the shares, and corporate governance is not sufficiently balanced. Key decisions (e.g. the Zero Slash strategy) are essentially the personal judgement of the founders and lack external checks and balances.
- ** Low R & D inputs**: R & D teams are only 20 people, and the share of inputs in the revenue base is declining. Is this level of input sufficient for a firm with the "process" as a core barrier?
- ** The proportion of self-incarcerated births has declined**: the proportion of self-incarcerated births has fallen to 59%, which is in contradiction with the brand narrative of "non-relict process transmission". The increase in outsourcing means that control over the supply chain is declining.
- ** Radical leveraging strategy**: Bank borrowing increased from 1.37 billion to 6.26 billion, essentially using leverage to make gold — this is the personal judgement of the founder on the value of gold, not conservative financial management.

# II. Key risk panorama

Risk one: the risk of a fall in gold prices

** Risk description**: The gold price fell by over 8% per day in March 2026, with a cumulative decline of 15.27%. The old "zero-squillion" strategy means that 16 billion stocks are completely exposed to gold price fluctuations.

**Quantified impact**:
- 10 per cent decline in gold prices: inventory faces a impairment of approximately 1.6 billion, equivalent to 33 per cent of net profit in 2025
- 20 per cent decline in gold prices: inventory faces a impairment of approximately 3.2 billion, equivalent to 66 per cent of net profit in 2025
- 30 per cent decline in gold prices: inventory impairments may be close to total net profits and consumption demand may contract at a time

** Management response**:
- Direct transmission of gold to sale price reduction through "one-thing plus periodic price."
- Increasing the mosaic ratio (61%) reducing the dependency on the full sum
- Monthly procurement plan developments

**Assessment** Management responses are not cured. Whatever product structure is adjusted, the cost of gold raw materials is always the subject of cost. The risk of gold prices is completely exposed without a hedge. More importantly, when gold prices fall, consumers may reduce purchases because of psychological expectations of "buy-and-loss" and thus double-flash "stock impairment plus demand contraction".

Risk two: Competition increases risk

** Risk description**: The ancient gold track has changed from "the old shop's one big" to "the four sisters" or even more. The department store is significantly increasing the presence of the ancient gold.

** Potential impact**:
- Old shop brand premium has narrowed from 55% to 30-40%
- The old shop's "sole scarcity" has declined since the top mall was branded as ancient papkin.
- Consumers have more choices.

** Management response**:
- Keep your high-end position intact.
- Keep pushing the price gap between the competition and the price gap.
- Targeting core clients through membership systems (610,000 members, black card systems)

** Evaluation**: Management's strategy is to "go up" -- to distance its followers through continuous price increases and brand upgrades. This works in the short term, but long-term success depends on whether brand power can be sustained. History has been successful in the case of Amasee, and in the case of COACH.

# Risk three: cash flow and liquidity risk #

** Risk description**:
- 2.215 billion dollars in net cash flow from operating in the first half of 2025
- Bank loans of $6,264 million, of which over $6,026 expired
- 16 billion yuan in inventory, a very high percentage of current assets

** Worst-case scenario**: If gold prices fall significantly + banks tighten credit terms, the old shop may face triple pressure of "depreciation of stock + maturity of loans + tight cash flow". While gold stocks have liquidity (which can sell gold raw materials), emergency realization usually means discounting.

**Assessment** The expiration of the six billion-dollar loan in 2026 is a hard time constraint. If the 2026 value of the loan is maintained at a high and sustained rate of performance, renewal is not an issue; but if adverse circumstances arise, the loan may become a trigger for liquidity pressure.

# Risk four: shift consumption trends to risk #

** Risk description**:
- The "Cool Old Money Wind" and the "Colva Gold Hot" have a popular cyclical nature.
- Young consumers' aesthetic preferences change fast.
- If consumers turn to other types of products (e.g. emeralds, platinum, etc.) in the coming years, the old track will become less attractive.

**Assessment** This is a low risk in the short term - ancient gold is highly aligned with the trend of the country and the value-added properties of gold are increasing in economic uncertainty. But the dimensions of five years or more require continuous observation.

Risk five: Release and value pressure of shareholders

** Risk description**:
- The old shop was listed in June 2024 and the locks of the major shareholders will expire regularly in 2025-2026
- Xu's father and son control 78% of the stock, any signal reduction could trigger market panic.
- The stock price fell from a high of HK$ 1108 to approximately HK$ 592, down to almost 47%

** Assessment**: Whether major shareholders are losing their hold is unpredictable. But the high concentration of 78 per cent means that even a smaller share of the stock is likely to have a significant impact on the circulation market.

# IV. Management ' s awareness of and responsiveness to risks

# On the good side

1. **Proactive restructuring of products**: Increased proportion of mosaics from 39% to 61%, reducing direct dependence on the full/gold price
2. ** Restraint in door-to-door expansion**: open shop without blindness and maintain brand scarcity
** Forward-looking overseas layout**: Singapore, Hong Kong have proven that Tokyo, Japan, is about to open, spreading regional risks
4. **Exact pricing strategy**: Continued price increases without discouraging demand, expanding profit margin

# The worrying side

1. ** The "zero slam" strategy is too confident**: 16 billion stocks are not hedged, which is an implicit assumption that the price of the bet will not fall. As a listed company, this risk management approach is too radical.
2. **Research and development inputs are declining**: the proportion of R & D inputs is falling in companies with "process" as the core barrier - a contradictory signal
3. ** Financial leverage fast-track**: bank borrowings four and a half times a year, but management rarely discusses the debt strategy in public
4. **Cyprimo-control risk**: Lack of external checks and balances in key decisions and high reliance on individual judgement of the founder of the company for the fate of the company

# Core conclusion

Seo is a capable, visionary, and determined entrepreneur. He has succeeded in defining the ancient gold class, creating an amazing single shop efficiency, and maintaining rare strategic restraint. These qualities are very valuable among Chinese entrepreneurs.

But his risk management philosophy is disturbing. The "zero-squillity" strategy, 16 billion nudity inventory, and 6.2 billion bank loans that are about to expire -- together, these decisions constitute a highly sensitive leverage structure for gold prices. If gold prices continue to rise, they magnify profits; if gold prices fall significantly, the same structure will magnify losses.

Investments in gold are inherently subject to two simultaneous judgements: judgement about managerial competencies (strong enough) and judgement about the price of gold (the real difficulty). If you do not have a strong view of the price trends over the next three years, the security margin is not sufficient at the present price.

The ideal time for buying may be a significant return in gold prices (-over 15 per cent) and a consequent drop in old shop stock prices below HK$300, but company sales data remain resilient — a time when it can truly be proved that brand power exists independently of gold prices, while providing sufficient security margins.
