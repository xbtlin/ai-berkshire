# Two, Old Pao's Rivers: One by one

# Adequacy I: Brand premium capacity #

** Positive evidence**:
- 55% brand premium (pg sale more than twice the value of gold)
- Three price increases in 2025 and another price increase in February 2026, with good consumer acceptance
- Reconciling 82.4% with luxury consumer
- 70% of sales by members of the Black Gold Card (up to 500,000 per year).
- Membership increased by 74.3 per cent to 610,000

** Reverse evidence**:
- Second-hand market recovery price is only 5-7 discounts, which is far less than the premium recovery for Ames.
- The price of gold falls when consumer willingness to buy falls sharply - which means that "principal expectations" remain a key factor in purchasing decisions.
- Brand history is only about 15 years old, and cultural deposits are not enough to support true intergenerational loyalty.

**Defence**: brand power is real, but it has not reached the luxury level of "free pricing independently of gold." It is now more like "high end consumer" than "luxury" -- consumers are still price sensitive, but less sensitive than traditional gold.

# Adequase II: Channel barriers #

** Positive evidence**:
- 45 shops, all at top malls like SKP, Vientiane.
- Single shop sells nearly $1 billion a year - Newcomers are hard to get the same position at top malls.
- The malls have a high degree of recognition for the old shop's ability to flow.

** Reverse evidence**:
- The number of top malls is limited, but competitors (Guppe, Lin Dynasty, etc.) are also in the same kind of mall
- The access barrier is essentially a "pre-optimal + pedal proof" and is not insurmountable.
- If the old shop's heat cools, the mall may introduce more ancient gold-branded customers.

** Judgement**: Channel barriers are real but not permanent. The key is whether the old shop can sustain the "queuing effect" -- once the door is no longer in line, the core logic of the channel barrier will be relaxed.

# Three moats: process barriers, #

** Positive evidence**:
- More than 30 processes (filacs, engraved, insulated, etc.) are required for the ancient arts process
- It takes 5-8 years to develop a skilled silk craftsman
- Unstandardized manual features are difficult to replicate

** Reverse evidence**:
- The rate of self-birth has dropped from a high to 59%, and the narrative of the "non-relict processor" has been challenged
- Research and development teams are only 20, and the input-to-collect ratio is declining.
- The Shenzhen Water Beast copying is low-end consumption awareness in the shock
- The big brands Zhou Dafu, tidalunji and others are increasing their ancient gold input, and the process gap is closing.
- Junpei, Lin Dynasty, Paulan have been named the four sisters of ancient law with the old shop.

** Sensitization**: Process barriers are the most worrying link. The ancient gold process is changing from "the mastery of the few" to "the standard capabilities generally available in the industry." The 5-8 culture cycle means that the craftsmen that have been in the industry since 2019-2020 are mature and process homogenization may accelerate in the next 2-3 years.

# Accompanied by the river: consumer mind #

** Positive evidence**:
- Consumer awareness of "Colfa Gold = Old Shop" has been established.
- The old gold search on social media is far too hot.
- The consumer behavior of "stewarding before price increases" means the brand has fan properties.

** Reverse evidence**:
- Some consumers often mention emerging brands (Gonpe, Lin Dynasty) and consumer loyalty is still being tested
- "Golden of ancient law" is not "Old shop" - the spirit of the class is easily shared by the competition.
- The trend is cyclical. There's uncertainty as to how long the "Cool Old Money Wind" lasts.

# IV. A multi-thinking model to look at competition patterns

#1 Supply and demand model: demand is real but supply is expanding

Demand side: High-end gold consumption in China is indeed increasing, as evidenced by 40 per cent of the growth in the old shop 2026Q1.

Supply end: The speed of the ancient gold track from the Blue Sea to the Red Sea is accelerating. The department store is entering the ancient gold age — a report in May 2026 shows that most high-end malls are significantly increasing the number of places in the ancient gold brand.

** Conclusion**: In the next 2-3 years, the old shop may face an asymmetric "demand growth of 30% vs supply growth of 100%" and competitive pressure will increase significantly.

##2. Psychological model: a game between scarce and crowd effects

One of the keys to the old shop's success is the "queuing effect" -- it's worth it when people line up to buy it. It's a double-enhanced combination of **the crowd effect and the scarcity effect.

But the psychological effect is fragile. Once the heat cools (no longer lined up), or competition produces a similar queue effect, the old shop's mental advantage is diluted.

#3 Evolutionary model: survival of the fittest or pre-optimal advantage?

The old shop is the pioneer and the definer of the ancient gold track. The value of the pre-emptive advantage is that it defines the criteria for the class, occupies the best channel position, and creates the initial brand mind.

But evolution tells us that the first is not necessarily the final winner. Later, the first is able to learn the advantages of the first and avoid the shortcomings of the first.

##4. Historical analogies: lessons from COACH

The brand premium was significant when the COACH (college) was defined as a "lightly luxury" leather in the United States. But when the Michael Kors, Kate Spade, and others came into the same price slot, the brand scarcity of COACH was severely diluted and eventually had to maintain market shares through price reduction and channel sinking, and brand values were drastically reduced.

Is it possible that old shop gold is in a similar situation? When the four golden sisters of ancient law become the ten golden sisters of ancient law, can the old shop's 55% premium be maintained?

V. PROVIDING INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT

# Short term (1-2 years): Old-fashioned competitive advantage remains strong

- The most powerful brand, the best channel, the most consumer.
- Competition is still early and there are wide disparities in size
- If the gold price stays high, the industry climate supports growth.

# Midway (3-5 years): Increased competition, beginning of fragmentation

- Brandings like Junpei may take over the market in the secondary mall.
- Commopolymization of ancient gold processes is accelerating, and the price margin may be shrinking
- Old shop needs proof of "the ability to grow after the cycle of rising gold."

# Long (5-10 years): The pattern depends on whether brands exceed gold prices

- Long-term competitiveness is possible if the old shop succeeds in building brand values independent of gold prices (like Cartier's jewellery).
- If brands remain dependent on the gold cycle, they will face double squeezes of "down-price plus increased competition."

# Core conclusion

To summarize the old shop's competitive position, it's like ** the biggest fish in a reservoir that's opening its doors **

The reservoirs are rising (the ancient gold industry is very well-populated), and the old shop is the largest fish (brand, channel, and overall headline), so growth is alarming in the short term. But two trends are alarming: water levels cannot rise forever (the gold price is running), and more fish can come in when the gates open (competers pouring in).

The key validation node is: if gold prices fall by 20 per cent one day and the ancient gold brands increase to more than 10, will the old shop be able to maintain a brand premium of more than 45 per cent and a billion-dollar single-store efficiency? If it does, it is a real Chinese luxury brand; if it is not, it is only a beneficiary of the gold price cycle.

The evidence is not sufficient to make a final judgement. That is the biggest uncertainty about investing in old gold.
