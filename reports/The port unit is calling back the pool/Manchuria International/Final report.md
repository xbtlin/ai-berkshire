## 3. Core investment logic

### 3.1 Why is it worth paying attention to?

**First, the scarcity of the world’s largest pork company**. WH Group is the only pork company in the world that has completed the entire industry chain layout in the three major markets of China, North America and Europe. In 2025, total revenue will be US$28.026 billion and operating profit will be US$2.612 billion (+8.7%), both reaching record highs.

**Second, the spin-off of Smithfield releases implicit value**. After Smithfield's IPO on Nasdaq in January 2025, its current market value is approximately US$9.95 billion. Together with Shuanghui Development's A-share market value of approximately 85 billion yuan, the combined NAV of the two is approximately US$17.6 billion, while Wanzhou Hong Kong's stock market value is approximately US$15.5 billion, and there is still a holding discount of approximately 12%. Before the spin-off, the discount was more than 40%.

**Third, extreme financial safety margin**. Net leverage is only 0.3 times, available cash is US$2.3 billion, unused credit facilities are US$5.4 billion, and Fitch Ratings BBB+ rating. In 2026, when the pig cycle is on the decline, this balance sheet means that Wanzhou has almost no risk of financial crisis, while highly leveraged small and medium-sized breeding enterprises are being eliminated.

**Fourth, high dividend returns**. The dividend is HK$0.61 per share. Based on the current stock price of approximately HK$9.4, the dividend yield is approximately 6.5%. The dividend payout ratio is approximately 35% (excluding special dividends), and cash flow coverage is sufficient.

### 3.2 Why do we need to be cautious?

**First, the pig cycle is going down**. In the first half of 2026, the price of pigs fell to 10-11.4 yuan/kg, and once fell below 9 yuan/kg in April. Although the entire industry chain model provides hedging, sluggish consumption may drag down overall performance.

**Second, China’s meat product consumption is structurally slowing down**. Shuanghui's meat product sales in 2025 will be -3.8% year-on-year, and the traditional high-temperature ham category continues to shrink. The effectiveness of the "four modernizations" transformation needs to be verified.

**Third, geopolitics is uncontrollable**. WH Group is one of the Hong Kong stocks with the greatest exposure to Sino-U.S. relations - the U.S. contributes 67% of revenue and China contributes 33%. Any escalation of Sino-US trade friction may directly impact stock prices.

**Fourth, management handover period**. Wan Long is 86 years old. His second son Wan Hongwei will take over Shuanghui in 2024. The external evaluation is "moderate" and he still needs to prove his ability with performance.

## 5. Scenario analysis

### 5.1 Optimistic scenario (probability about 25%)

- Pig prices will bottom out in the second half of 2026, and pork business profits will improve
- Shuanghui's "four modernizations" transformation has achieved results, and meat product sales have stabilized
- Smithfield continues record profits
- Catalyst: Further reduction of Smithfield holdings to unlock value and increase dividends

**Target price estimate**: PE recovers to 12 times → stock price is about HK$11-12 (upside of about 20-28%)

### 5.2 Baseline scenario (probability about 50%)

- Pig cycle remains depressed until end of 2026
- China business flat, North America and Europe solid
- Group profits fluctuate slightly, dividends maintained
- The stock price fluctuates around HK$9-10, relying on dividends to obtain returns

**Annualized Return**: Dividend 6.5% + small capital appreciation = approximately 8-10%

### 5.3 Pessimistic scenario (probability about 25%)

- The pig cycle worsens beyond expectations and continues until 2027
- Sino-U.S. relations are tense and pork tariffs rise
- Management transition is not smooth
- Catalysts: Shuanghui's performance declined sharply, and Smithfield's reduction in holdings was interpreted negatively by the market

**Target price estimate**: PE compressed to 7-8 times → stock price is about HK$7-8 (downside of about 15-25%)

## 6. Evaluation of the analogy with "Meat version of See's Candy"

Some analysts compared WH Group to Buffett's See's Candy - "powerful brand + pricing power + cash flow machine". This analogy has its merits and limitations:

**Similarities**:
- Brand premium - Shuanghui's brand power in China's meat products market is indeed like See's in the US chocolate market
- Light capital return - the capital efficiency of the meat processing end is relatively high
- Strong cash flow - continues to generate free cash flow above reinvestment needs

**Key differences**:
- See's raw materials are stable raw materials such as cocoa beans, while Wanzhou's raw materials are pork - subject to violent fluctuations in the pig cycle
- See's is a pure brand business, and Wanzhou's breeding and slaughtering processes have lowered the overall return rate.
- See's faces far less competitive threats than Shuanghui - changes in consumer trends are eroding Shuanghui's traditional categories

**Conclusion**: Wanzhou's meat products business (especially Shuanghui ham sausage) has "See's-like" characteristics, but after adding the entire industry chain of breeding and slaughtering, it is more like "half See's + half bulk commodity business".

## 8. Final judgment

WH Group is a company with "strong profitability, a solid moat but not an ironclad wall, and a cheap valuation but with reasons for cyclical discounts"**.

**It is not an exciting investment target** - there is no high-growth story, no technological disruption narrative, and no room for imagination to recreate glory. But it is a **"reassuring" investment target** - the world's largest, most financially secure, and most generous pork company with generous dividends. Buy it at a very low price when the pig cycle is at its lowest, rely on dividends to recover costs, and wait for capital gains when the cycle picks up.

**For investors looking for high-interest defensive stocks in Hong Kong stocks, WH Group is worthy of being included in the candidate list. But you need to be mentally prepared: the pig cycle may cause you to see floating losses within 1-2 years, and dividends are your "holding reward". **

>Finally, I quote a sentence to summarize the investment characteristics of this company: **People all over the world eat pork every day, and WH Group is the largest pork company in the world - this fact will not change because of the pig cycle. The question is just this: How much are you willing to pay for this certainty? **
