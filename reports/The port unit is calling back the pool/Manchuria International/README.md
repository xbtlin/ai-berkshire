## Core conclusion

**Comprehensive rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5) - Global pork leader with attractive valuation, but attention needs to be paid to the downside risk of the pig cycle**

WH Group is the world's largest pork food company and owns two core assets: Shuanghui of China and Smithfield of the United States. Full-year revenue in 2025 is US$28.026 billion (+8.0% year-on-year), and operating profit is US$2.612 billion (+8.7% year-on-year). Smithfield was independently listed on Nasdaq (SFD) in January 2025. After the IPO, the stock price has risen by about 55%, partially releasing the valuation discount.

But challenges cannot be ignored: pig prices will enter a downward cycle in 2026, and the spot price once fell below 9 yuan/kg in April, setting a new historical low; Sino-US trade relations are still uncertain; founder Wan Long is 86 years old, and the intergenerational handover of management is still in progress.

**Core Judgment**: WH Group is a rare company in the global meat industry that has a presence in the three major markets of "China + North America + Europe". After the spin-off and listing of Smithfield, its business transparency has been significantly improved. The current Hong Kong stock price is about HK$10.8, and the total market value is about HK$133.4 billion, which corresponds to a PE of about 10 times in 2025 (pre-split caliber). If we only look at WH Group’s remaining China + European businesses, the implied valuation is even lower. High dividends (a total dividend of HK$0.5 per share in 2024, including special dividends) provides a certain safety cushion. Key observation points: (1) When will the bottom of the pig cycle be confirmed; (2) Shuanghui's "four modernizations" transformation results; (3) Subsequent changes in Smithfield's shareholding ratio and valuation linkage effects.

## Company Profile

WH Group Limited is the world's largest pork food company and was listed on the main board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2014. The company completed its global layout by acquiring Smithfield Foods for US$4.7 billion in 2013, forming a full pork industry chain system covering the three major markets of China, North America and Europe.

**Core Assets**:
- **Shuanghui Development** (000895.SZ): holds 70.33% of the shares, Asia's largest meat processing company, with revenue of 59.46 billion yuan in 2025
- **Smithfield Foods** (SFD, Nasdaq): holds about 80% of the shares, the largest pork food company in the United States, and will be independently listed in January 2025
- **European Business**: Including Argal Group (50.1% shareholding) and meat processing assets in Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Hungary and other countries
