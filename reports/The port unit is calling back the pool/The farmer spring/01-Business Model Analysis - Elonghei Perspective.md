#Nongfu Spring (9633.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, differentiation and moat
> Data cutoff: May 2026

---

## 1. What is the essence of this business?

### 1.1 Water business: turning natural resources into brand premium

The core business of Nongfu Spring can be summarized in one sentence: **Get water from high-quality water sources, sell the water to consumers across the country through brands and channels, and earn brand premiums**.

The water itself has almost no cost - Nongfu Spring's water resource fee only accounts for a very small proportion of revenue. A bottle of mineral water worth 2 yuan, the cost of the water itself is less than a few cents. The real cost lies in PET bottles, transportation logistics and channel distribution. However, consumers are willing to pay 2 yuan for the recognition of "natural water" and "water source" rather than the price of tap water. This premium space is the source of Nongfu Spring's profits.

This business has several key features:

- **High Frequency Rigid Needs**: Drinking water is the most basic need of human beings. It is consumed frequently and is not affected by the economic cycle.
- **Low unit price and high turnover**: low price per bottle, extremely low consumer decision-making costs, and extremely high repurchase rate
- **The product itself has limited differentiation**: Water is water, the real difference lies in brand mentality and channel access

### 1.2 Beverage business: Use R&D and branding to create increments

Nongfu Spring is not only a water company, but also a **beverage platform**. In 2025, tea beverages will surpass packaged water for the first time and become the largest source of revenue (21.596 billion, accounting for 41.1%). Together with functional drinks, juices, etc., the beverage business will contribute a total of approximately 64% of revenue.

Oriental Leaf is the most successful case - this product was rated as the "worst drink" when it was launched in 2011. However, Nongfu Spring persisted for more than ten years and never gave up, and finally reaped huge rewards when the sugar-free tea trend came. In the first half of 2025, Oriental Leaf's sales exceeded 8 billion, and its market share in the sugar-free tea field exceeded 50%.

This reflects an important trait: **Having the patience to do the right thing, even if it is not recognized by the market in the short term**.

## 2. Moat analysis

### 2.1 Water source barrier ★★★★★

Nongfu Spring currently has 16 high-quality water sources across the country (three will be added in 2025 and one more in 2026), covering most regions in Northeast, North, Northwest, South, Central, East and Southwest China. This is an **non-replicable resource barrier**:

- High-quality natural water sources are scarce resources, and the acquisition period for water abstraction licenses is long and the approval is strict.
- Build factories around water sources to achieve "direct sales from the production area", significantly reducing logistics costs (water transportation costs account for a very high proportion)
- The nationwide layout of 16 water sources means that water can be supplied nearby, forming a structural advantage in logistics costs.
- It will take at least 10-15 years for competitors to copy this layout

This is the deepest moat of Nongfu Spring. Access to water sources has first-mover advantages and exclusivity, making it difficult for latecomers to find water sources of the same quality and in suitable locations.

### 2.2 Brand Mind ★★★★☆

"Nongfu Spring is a bit sweet" and "We don't produce water, we are just porters of nature" - these two advertising slogans are well-known in China and have successfully tied the brand to "natural" and "healthy".

Nongfu Spring’s brand building has several noteworthy features:

- **Differentiated positioning**: When everyone was making purified water, Zhong Suisui chose to make natural water, successfully occupying the minds of consumers with the debate of "natural water vs purified water"
- **Continuous Investment**: Maintain high-intensity investment in brand advertising every year
- **Strong brand extension ability**: From water to tea, juice, and functional drinks, brand trust can be transmitted across categories

But the brand moat is also fragile - in the Wahaha incident in 2024, the public opinion storm caused Nongfu Spring's online sales to plummet by 90%, and the packaged water business lost nearly 4 billion in revenue throughout the year. This shows that brands can be damaged quickly in the social media era. Although they can recover, the process is painful.

### 2.3 Channel Network ★★★★★

Nongfu Spring has more than 4,000 dealers, covering 2.37 million+ terminal retail outlets nationwide, of which 1.87 million+ are located in third-tier and below cities. The value of this channel network lies in:

- **Deep Penetration**: From convenience stores in first-tier cities to canteens in sixth-tier towns, they are almost everywhere
- **Mature dealer system**: The dealer system reform was completed in 2016, with clear hierarchies, sufficient profit margins, and high dealer loyalty
- **Unique household channel**: Directly reaching household users through water stations and community vending machines. This channel is difficult for competitors to copy in the short term.
- **Strong new product distribution capabilities**: Any new product launched can be quickly spread across the national market with the help of existing channels

The channel network is one of Nongfu Spring’s most difficult capabilities for competitors to copy. Building a nationwide beverage distribution network requires decades of investment and operational accumulation.

### 2.4 Product Innovation Capability ★★★★☆

Nongfu Spring is not a "one-trick" company, but a platform enterprise that continuously launches new products and has successful cases:

- **Oriental Leaf**: The absolute leader in the field of sugar-free tea, contributing 8 billion+ revenue in 2025
- **Tea π**: Rejuvenating fruity tea
- **Scream**: Sports drinks
- **Vitamin Water**: functional drink
- **NFC Juice**: high-end freshly squeezed juice
- **New products in 2026**: National promotion of Oriental Leaf Chrysanthemum Pu'er, and extension to multiple specifications

This "large single product + continuous innovation" model makes the company not dependent on a single product and reduces the concentration risk of growth.

## 3. The "badness" of the business model

### 3.1 Heavy asset attributes

Plant construction at water sources, logistics and transportation, and channel maintenance—the drinking water industry is essentially an asset-heavy business. Although the asset-light part (brand premium) contributes most of the profits, each entry into a new water source requires a large amount of capital investment.

### 3.2 Product substitution risk

Water is water, and consumers have limited brand loyalty. During the public opinion turmoil in 2024, a large number of consumers turned to competing products, indicating that in categories with "zero functional differences", brand loyalty is not as strong as imagined.

### 3.3 Channel dependence

Nongfu Spring’s sales are highly dependent on its offline dealer network. Under the impact of online channels and instant retail (Meituan flash sales, Ele.me, etc.), the value of traditional channels may face revaluation. However, water and beverages are naturally suitable for offline instant consumption scenarios, and this risk can be controlled in the short term.

## 4. User value analysis

### 4.1 What are consumers willing to pay for?

- **Convenience**: Available everywhere
- **Security**: Big brand = quality assurance
- **Health Perception**: The psychological expectation that natural water is "healthier" than pure water
- **Taste**: Habitual consumption, Oriental leaves and other products have real taste differences

### 4.2 Consumer switching cost

Frankly, the switching costs for consumers are low – there is no cost to buying a bottle of competing water. However, Nongfu Spring’s ubiquitous channel coverage and strong brand awareness make consumers more likely to choose Nongfu Spring when they “grab a bottle of water.” This is a **habitual choice** rather than a lock-in effect.

## 6. Core Conclusions

Nongfu Spring's business model is essentially the trinity of "water source resources + brand premium + channel network". These three reinforce each other: the water source provides the basis for product differentiation, the brand creates a premium in consumers' minds, and the channels ensure that products reach consumers.

From the perspective of "doing the right thing", Nongfu Spring has several admirable qualities:

1. **Long-termism**: Oriental Leaf will reap huge rewards after ten years of persistence
2. **Differentiated thinking**: The strategic courage to choose natural water instead of pure water.
3. **Channel development**: 2.37 million terminal outlets cannot be built overnight
4. **Platform Development**: From a single water company to a multi-category beverage platform

But we also need to be vigilant: this business has low consumer switching costs, limited product differentiation, and the brand's vulnerability in the public opinion environment. Nongfu Spring’s moat is more like a wide but not particularly deep river—enough to block most competitors, but still capable of being breached in extreme circumstances (such as the public opinion turmoil in 2024).

**To sum it up in one sentence: Nongfu Spring is a good business, but it is not a perfect business. Its advantage lies in "doing the right thing long enough" rather than some insurmountable technical barrier. **
