# One, clock: Founder painting

Jong-hoon, born in 1954, the founder and chairman of the farmers’ springs, held about 84.4 per cent of the shares, was a former agent of the Wakhah, and founded the farmers’ spring in 1996. ** Cost controls are stringent**:

** Long-termist orientation**:
- The Eastern leaves have been coming out for 10 years.

# 1.3 Integrity and integrity assessment

This evaluation needs to be more cautious:

** Positive signal**:
- The stock market has never been reduced since Jong-hoon was listed, but has grown at a low level (an increase of HK$2 billion in 2024)

Jong-hoon is a typical first generation of private entrepreneurs in China — very capable, very controlling, deeply sensitive to business, but with a gap between corporate governance and transparency and international standards.

# II. Management team assessment

##2.1 Management Composition

In his annual message in 2025, Jong-gi, "Congratulating the management team on the test, growing mature", was an important signal that the company was making the transition from "founder-driven one-man-driven" to "team-driven".

Characteristics of the management team:

- ** Practical**: Most executives have grown up from grass-roots positions and become familiar with business operations
- **Cultural identity**: Growing up in the farmer spring system for a long time, with deep recognition of the corporate culture
- ** Strong implementation**: Rapid operational recovery after the 2024 wave of public opinion demonstrated the team ' s ability to implement
- ** Limited internationalization perspective**: management teams are predominantly indigenous and lack internationalization experience

#2.2 The replacement problem #

This is the biggest governance risk for the farmer springs**.

The problem is immediate. The situation is:

- Jong-woo's son** is a potential successor, but is under pressure in public opinion on the issue of American citizenship
- Company** does not have a clear succession plan** or open succession arrangements
- The management team, though "increasing maturity", lacks a recognized "two."

The case of other family businesses is compared to the case of the inheritance:
- **vaha**: after the death of Zongqing, Joong Jilli took over, but management was in turmoil, brand strategy swinging
- **Li Jinx**: smooth transmission through family charters and professional management
- **Cola**: The transition from family business to career manager has been completed long ago

The risk of farmers’ inheritance in the springs cannot be underestimated. Jong-hoon once said that "the springs of farmers will always belong to China," but that a sense of feeling cannot substitute for an institutionalized inheritance arrangement.

# III. Corporate governance assessment

#3.1 Equity structure

The bells hold 84.4% of the shares - a double-edged sword:

** Advantage**:
- Management is highly aligned with shareholders ' interests (the majority of shares held by Jong-hoon)
- There will be no conflict between management and shareholders' agents.
- Founders can make long-term decisions without short-term market pressure

♪ The fear ♪
- Little shareholders have little voice.
- All major decisions are essentially made by one person.
- If the founder misjudges, there is no effective mechanism for correcting the error.

#3.2 Disclosure of information

As a listed company with a Hong Kong stock, the information disclosure of the farmers ' springs meets the requirements of the Hong Kong Customs Exchange, but there is still a gap between best international practices:
- The breakdown of the business modules could be more detailed
- Limited disclosure of dealer data and channel information
- Relatively brief management discussion

##3.3 Associated transactions

The risk of a transfer of benefits between the two companies is of concern. No major linkage transaction is currently found, but the risk of major shareholders controlling multiple listed companies at the same time remains.

# Five, security marginal thinking

# 5.1 What price provides enough security margins?

Taking into account the core risks of the farmer springs (lower speed + replacement + higher valuation), a discount is required on a reasonable valuation basis:

- ** Reasonable valuation**: 30-35 times forward PE (equivalent to approximately HK$40-47)
- ** margin security price after discount**: 25-30 times forward PE (equivalent to approximately HK$33-40)
- **Extratra attractive price**: 20-25 times forward PE (equivalent to approximately HK$ 27-33)

In 2024, the public opinion boom fell to a high margin of security near HK$ 34.85.

# 5.2 What's the situation where the security margin expands?

- Re-emergence of public opinion (medium probability)
- A significant slowdown in the quarterly growth of Eastern leaves has triggered market panic (high probability)
- Systematic decline in global or Chinese markets (unpredictable)
- Market concerns are raised by exposure to the issue of replacements (low probability but high impact)

# 5.3 What's the situation where the security margin shrinks?

- The Eastern leaves continue to grow beyond expectations.
- New items and "exploitation."
- The successors' plan is clear.
- Substantive progress in internationalization

# 6.1 Political and commercial relations

The farmers' springs, as private enterprises, face several special considerations in the current policy environment:

- ** Positive**: Drinking water and beverages are the beneficiary of the "pro-consumption" policy and do not cover sensitive areas

- **neutral**: Bell-tone is relatively low-key and not involved in political issues

# 6.2 Social emotional risks

The central issue of the 2024 public opinion boom is not the product, but the national sentiment. In the current social climate, such risks may reappear.

#6.3 Consumer culture change

Chinese consumers are experiencing a shift from "brand-led" to "value-for-money-led" (especially in the bottom-up economy). The sustainability of the farmers' market premium in the springs, in the face of a downward trend in consumption, is a matter that needs to be kept under constant observation.

# Eight, core conclusion

The assessment of farmer spring management and risk can be summarized as "one outstanding advantage, two core concerns":

** Apparent advantage**: Jong-hoon is one of China’s top entrepreneurs in consumer goods, with strategic vision, marketing skills and long-termism. Under his leadership, the farmer has grown from a local water company to a 100 billion-grade drinking platform.

** Core worry one - successor issues**: 72-year-old founder, 84.4% absolute shareholding, and no clear succession arrangements - this is a risk that hangs on top. By the lessons of Vakha, the time of succession of founders may be the most vulnerable moment for the enterprise.

** Core distress two - public opinion vulnerability**: The 2024 wave of public opinion proves that brand reputation can be quickly harmed in the social media era. And such risks are exogenous and uncontrollable, and management can only prepare for crisis response programmes ahead of schedule.

** How should investors deal with these risks?** Not avoiding the company, but leaving enough security margins on valuation. Good companies + bad prices = poor investments. Good companies + good prices = good investments. The key is price.
