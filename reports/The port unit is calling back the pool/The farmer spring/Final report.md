## 3. Core investment logic

### 3.1 Reasons to be bullish

**First, this is a top-notch business. ** Drinking water and beverages are the most basic human consumption needs. They are high-frequency and rigid needs and are not affected by economic cycles and technological disruption. Globally, water and beverage companies (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Danone, Nestlé) are among the investment targets with the best long-term returns.

**Second, Nongfu Spring is the best player in this business. ** The nationwide layout of 16 water sources cannot be replicated, the channel depth of 2.37 million+ terminal outlets will be difficult to surpass within ten years, and the brand mentality of "natural water" firmly dominates consumer awareness. ROE 44%, net profit margin 30%, no interest-bearing debt - the financial indicators are almost impeccable.

**Third, Oriental Leaf opened a second growth curve. ** The transformation from a single water company to a beverage platform is successfully underway. For the first time, tea beverages surpassed packaged water to become the largest source of revenue, proving the company's category expansion capabilities. The penetration rate of sugar-free tea in China is still in its early stages. Based on the Japanese market experience, this track still has a growth period of 5-10 years.

**Fourth, the track record of management’s long-termism. ** It took 10 years for Oriental Leaf to explode, 20 years of continuous investment in water source layout, and in-depth reform of the dealer system - these all prove that management can make correct but difficult long-term decisions.

### 3.2 Reasons to be bearish

**First, the valuation is not cheap. ** 40 times + PE means that the market has paid a high price for the company's excellence. Compared with global peers (Coca-Cola 25 times, Nestlé 20 times, C’estbon 20 times), the premium is obvious. If growth slows down, valuations may face a "Davis Double Kill" - a decline in profit growth + a contraction in valuation multiples occurring at the same time.

**Second, high growth is unsustainable. ** Oriental Leaf's 29% growth rate is based on the dividends during the explosive period of the sugar-free tea track. As the base increases and competition intensifies, growth will inevitably slow down. In 2-3 years, the growth rate of tea beverages may drop to 10-15%, and packaged water will grow in single digits. By then, the company's overall growth rate may drop to 10-12%, and the current valuation level will appear to be on the high side.

**Third, the issue of successor remains unresolved. ** Zhong Suisui is 72 years old, holds 84.4% of the shares, and has no clear succession plan - this is a mid-term risk that cannot be ignored. Wahaha’s lesson is at hand.

**Fourth, the vulnerability of brands in the social media era. ** In 2024, public opinion turmoil will cause the packaged water business to lose nearly 4 billion in revenue and evaporate 30 billion + Hong Kong dollars in market value. This exogenous risk cannot be eliminated through operational improvements.

## 7. Final conclusion

### 7.1 Qualitative judgment

Nongfu Spring is a high-quality company that deserves long-term attention and holding**. It has the following rare qualities:

1. Being in a good industry with sustainable growth (water and beverages)
2. Occupy the leading position and have a deep moat (water source + brand + channel)
3. Extremely high financial quality (ROE 44%, net interest rate 30%, no debt)
4. Management has a long-term track record
5. Successfully transforming into a platform beverage company

### 7.2 Quantitative Judgment

The current price of HK$49/40 times PE is **not a good time to buy**. The reason is:

- Valuation has fully reflected the company’s excellence
- The high growth stage may have passed halfway, and a slowdown in future growth is a high probability event
- Succession risk has not yet been fully priced by the market
- Lack of sufficient margin of safety to deal with possible negative shocks
