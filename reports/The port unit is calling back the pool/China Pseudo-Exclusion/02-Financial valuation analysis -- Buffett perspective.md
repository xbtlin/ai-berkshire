# Sinopec (0386.HK) financial valuation analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 278.4 billion yuan | 307.5 billion yuan | Down 9.4% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 31.8 billion yuan | 50.3 billion yuan | Down 36.8% |
| Gross profit margin | ~19.7% | ~18.5% | Slight improvement |
| Net profit margin | ~11.4% | ~16.4% | Significant decline |
| ROE | 6-7% | 9-10% | Significant decline |

**Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**

The ROE level of 6-7% is far lower than Buffett's preferred standard of more than 15%. More importantly, profits will plummet by 36.8% in 2025, indicating that profitability is unstable and highly affected by cycles.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- Net cash flow from operating activities: 16.25 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 1.31 billion yuan
- The quality of cash flow is acceptable, but the ratio to net profit is low, indicating that there are accounts receivable or inventory occupation

### Balance Sheet
- The asset-liability ratio is relatively stable, but as an asset-heavy enterprise, depreciation pressure on fixed assets continues.
- Working capital management faces inventory risks (fluctuations in crude oil and refined oil prices lead to uncertainty in the value of inventories)

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): 15-17 times
- PB: about 1.0-1.1 times
- Dividend rate: approximately 4.5-5% (based on full-year dividend of 0.2 yuan per share)

### Historical valuation range
Sinopec's historical PE has fluctuated between 8-20 times, and the current 15-17 times is at an upper-middle level. Taking into account the sharp decline in profits in 2025, the current PE faces the risk of "low base elevation" - that is, if profits continue to decline, the actual PE may be higher.

### DCF Valuation Thoughts
If we take the current net profit of 31.8 billion yuan as the basis:
- Assuming long-term growth rate of 2% (inflation level)
- Discount rate 8%
- Intrinsic value of approximately NT$530 billion
- Corresponding to approximately 4.4 yuan (RMB) per share

But the question is: Is 31.8 billion yuan a normalized profit level? There is a huge gap between 50.3 billion yuan in 2024 and 31.8 billion yuan in 2025. In the past five years, profits have fluctuated significantly between 30-60 billion yuan, making it difficult to determine the "normalization" level.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**Difficult to determine. ** The demand for refined oil products is facing a structural decline (replacement by new energy vehicles), and chemical product price fluctuations are difficult to predict. Sinopec's profit model in 10 years may be significantly different from today.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Partially available. ** The gas station network and scale advantages are lasting, but the product itself has no differentiation advantage.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
**Neutral. ** The management of central enterprises has good execution capabilities, but lacks an incentive mechanism to maximize the interests of shareholders. A high payout ratio is a positive sign.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**Safety margin is limited. ** PE 15-17 times is not cheap for a cyclical stock with an ROE of only 6-7% and huge profit fluctuations. The historical bottom PE can be as low as 8-10 times.

## 4. Capital allocation efficiency

The payout ratio of 81% sends a clear signal: Management believes that the rate of return on reinvestment within the company is not high, and it is better to return money to shareholders.

This is great for dividend investors, but it also means:
1. The company’s own return on capital has reached a bottleneck
2. Limited room for growth
3. Future value creation will mainly depend on dividends rather than capital appreciation.

In terms of repurchases, the company also has a small amount of repurchases, with dividends + repurchases totaling 81% of profits, and shareholders have a positive attitude towards returns.

## 5. Comparison of peer valuations

| Company | PE | ROE | Dividend Yield |
|------|------|------|--------|
| Sinopec (0386) | 15-17x | 6-7% | ~5% |
| PetroChina (0857) | 10-11x | 5% | ~5% |

In comparison, CNOOC's price/performance ratio is significantly better than that of Sinopec - higher ROE, lower PE, and higher dividend rate. Sinopec's valuation premium may come from the stability expectations of its "downstream + terminal" layout.

## 6. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

Sinopec currently does not meet Buffett's typical investment target characteristics: low ROE, unstable profits, and no obvious margin of safety in valuation. It is more suitable as a "high dividend defensive" allocation rather than a "long-term compound interest" core position. If you want to allocate in the oil industry, CNOOC may be a choice that is more in line with value investment standards.

The reasonable logic of investing in Sinopec is: buy when the PE falls back to 10-12 times, lock in a dividend rate of 6-7%, and obtain "quasi-fixed income" returns. The current buying time of 15-17 times PE is not ideal.
