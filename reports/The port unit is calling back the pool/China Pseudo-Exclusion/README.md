## Investment Highlights
1. The largest refining and chemical company in China, with advantages of industrial chain integration
2. High dividend payout ratio (81%) and considerable dividend yield
3. The gas station network forms a natural moat
4. Actively deploy the transition to new energy sources (charging piles, hydrogen energy)

## Core Risks
1. International oil price fluctuations directly affect profitability
2. Demand for refined oil products peaks (replaced by new energy vehicles)
3. Chemical product prices are highly cyclical
4. The governance efficiency of state-owned enterprises needs to be improved
