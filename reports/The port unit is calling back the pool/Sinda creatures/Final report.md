## 2. Investment thesis

### 2.1 Core arguments

**Innovent Biologics is one of the best carriers of China's innovative drugs from the "following" to "leading" era, but the current valuation needs to be supported by the success of the core pipeline. **

Specific expansion:

**Reasons to be bullish (in order of importance):**

(1) **IBI363 is a potential game changer in the global IO circuit**
As the world's first PD-1/IL-2 bispecific fusion protein, its data in "cold tumors" (IO-treated lung squamous cell carcinoma ORR 50%, melanoma, MSS colorectal cancer) are currently the best of its kind in the world. If the third phase is successful, peak sales may reach US$5-10 billion. Cinda retains 40% US profit sharing + all rights in Greater China.

(2) **The transformation from Biotech to Biopharma has passed the turning point**
Revenue in 2025 is RMB 13 billion, IFRS profit is RMB 800 million, positive operating cash flow, and cash reserves of RMB 24.3 billion - these data show that Cinda has got rid of its dependence on financing and has the ability to self-generate.

(3) **The evolution of BD licensing model has opened up the value ceiling**
From traditional licensing to Co-Co model (Takeda US$11.4 billion) to platform-level early stage cooperation (Eli Lilly US$8.85 billion), Innovent's innovative platform is being repeatedly verified and paid for by the world's top pharmaceutical companies.

(4) **The second growth curve is clear**
Masdutide (the world's first dual-target GLP-1 approved for weight loss + diabetes) + non-tumor pipeline is growing rapidly (estimated to be 3.27 billion yuan in 2026), reducing sole reliance on PD-1.

(5) **Industry Beta is still**
China's innovative pharmaceutical industry is in a period of accelerated globalization, with BD's overseas sales reaching a record high of US$135.6 billion in 2025. This industry-level tailwind is a continued positive for Cinda.

**Reasons to be bearish (ordered by risk):**

(1) **The probability of failure in the third phase of IBI363 is about 40-50%**
Phase III oncology has a high historical failure rate and global launch has been delayed by more than 6 months. If it fails, the valuation may be discounted by 30%.

(2) **Core products face centralized purchasing and homogenization**
Bevacizumab will almost certainly be centralized and the growth rate of sintilimab will slow down. The gradual decline of these "cash cows" requires new products to fill their positions in a timely manner.

(3) **The competition on the GLP-1 track is extremely fierce**
13 NDA types are being accepted + 35 types in the third phase are in progress. Although Masdutide has good data, it was approved late and its channels are limited. There is uncertainty about whether it can survive in the red ocean.

(4) **41 times PE implies limited fault tolerance**
Current prices have fully reflected "optimistic but not extremely optimistic" expectations. If growth slows down or there is negative news on the pipeline, there is considerable room for a correction in valuations.

(5) **The management team is deeply suspicious**
The continuity of R&D leadership after Liu Yongjun’s departure and the achievability of the 20 billion goal—these soft factors will affect the confidence of long-term investors.

## 6. Final conclusion

### Investment Rating: Watch / Candidate

**Summary in one sentence**: Innovent Biologics is one of the companies with the greatest global potential in China's innovative drug industry. Its fundamentals are in a critical period from quantitative change to qualitative change. However, the current valuation requires the success of the core pipeline to be realized, and the safety margin is average.

**Operation Suggestions**:

- There is no rush to open a large position at the moment. The price of HKD 87.70 is in the middle of the reasonable range, and there is no obvious opportunity for undervaluation.
- You can start paying attention with a small position (1-2%) to establish tracking and knowledge accumulation
- Key buying timings: (1) The stock price falls back to the range of HKD 65-70; (2) The third phase of IBI363 is launched and there are positive progress signals; (3) When both appear at the same time, it is the best window to open a position.
- Key sell/avoidance signals: (1) IBI363 third period data is negative; (2) Management reduces holdings on a large scale; (3) Product sales growth is less than 15% for two consecutive quarters

### Issues that require continuous tracking

1. The launch time and enrollment progress of the third global phase of IBI363 - these are the core variables
2. Quarterly sales data of Masdutide - verifying the climbing speed of the second curve
3. The timing and price reduction of bevacizumab centralized procurement - quantifying downside risks
4. Stability of the management team - especially talent retention in core R&D positions
5. Quality of new BD transactions – verifying the sustainability of innovation platform capabilities
