# III. Deep review of financial quality

#3.1 Quality of Income

** Positive signal:**
- Income from product sales as a percentage of 91.2 per cent, core income from recurrent drug sales rather than one-time BD income
- Health care is being provided for multiple products, with a growing degree of diversification of sources of income
- Rapid increase in the share of non-cancer products, reducing reliance on single lanes

** Alert signal:**
- The Sindeli bill still accounts for about 40 per cent of product revenue, with a high concentration of individual products
- BD down payment is a one-time income, and its continuity depends on the quality of the pipeline and the market environment
- Price negotiations pressure on products every 1-2 years under the health-care pricing mechanism

#3.2 Balance sheet quality

The combined three indicators of $24.3 billion cash reserve, 87.2 per cent Māori, and cash flow towards business are indicative of the shift from "Biotech fragile" to "Biopharma healthy" balance sheet.

Companies have no significant interest-bearing liabilities and no risk of debt servicing. This is a rare advantage in biotech, meaning that companies are not forced to finance dilution of shareholder interests in times of capital market depression.

#3.3 Capital allocation efficiency

The capital allocation strategy of Sindha over the past few years is noteworthy:
- R & D pipelines have shifted from Wide Web to Focus High Values: in research registration/critical clinical downs from 37 to 16
- R & D cost rate has been reduced from 30% to about 20%, but the absolute amount remains at reasonable levels
- BD model is upgraded from "seller" to "share profit" and the efficiency of the unit pipe is greatly enhanced.

This strategy of "scaling the front, concentrating" is correct at the management level. It also means that if several of the main lines of emphasis are frustrated at the same time, the options are limited.

** Rating: <unk> (4/5)**
Financial quality improved rapidly, balance sheet health, cash flow positive. But the concentration of income was high, and the "trail value" of the core pipe was over-represented in the total valuation, increasing the volatility of the valuation.

# Core conclusion

The financial situation of the Sindas is at a turning point from "mass to quality." Revenues have gone out of 10 billion, full profit, cash flow -- all of these are key milestones in the growth of biotech enterprises.

But, from a valuation perspective, the current 41-fold price of the PE already reflects these benefits to a large extent. The real decision about future evaluation space for Sindha is not the growth of existing products (the market is fully priced), but the global three-stage clinical outcome of core tubes like IBI363.

For value investors who pursue a security margin, the current price is not sufficiently secure. But for growing investors who are willing to take the risk of pipelines and watch China’s trend towards globalization of innovation, Sindha is one of the most likely “from China to the world” in the track.
