## Company Profile

Founded in 2011, Innovent Biologics is a leading innovative biopharmaceutical company in China, dedicated to the development, production and commercialization of innovative drugs for the treatment of major diseases such as tumors, metabolism, and autoimmunity. The company has commercialized 16 products, and its core product sintilimab (tyvyzumab, a PD-1 inhibitor) is the first PD-1 monoclonal antibody in China to enter medical insurance. In 2025, the company's revenue will exceed 10 billion for the first time and achieve full profitability, marking a key leap from Biotech to Biopharma.

## Key Investment Thesis

**Bull logic:**
- Revenue is growing rapidly, and the 20 billion target in 2027 is clearly visible
- IBI363 is the first of its kind in the world, and Takeda’s US$11.4 billion cooperation proves its value
- Successfully transformed from Biotech to Biopharma to achieve self-hematopoiesis
- Masdutide opens the second growth curve of metabolic diseases
- Eli Lilly’s US$8.85 billion cooperation reflects the value of early-stage innovation platform

**Short-selling logic:**
- The PD-1 track is crowded, and the growth rate of sintilimab may have peaked
- Bevacizumab faces risks of centralized procurement
- IBI363 global phase III clinical trials are delayed and uncertainty remains high
- Competition on the GLP-1 track is extremely fierce, and 13 NDAs have been accepted
- PE is about 41 times, fully reflecting optimistic expectations
