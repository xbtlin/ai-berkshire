### Interpretation of financial characteristics

1. **Revenue growth peaked**: Only grew by 1.1%. The core hotpot business may have experienced negative growth. The high growth of takeaway and multi-brands barely maintained overall positive growth.
2. **Profit is under pressure**: Net profit is 4.042 billion yuan, compared with core operating profit of 5.403 billion yuan, there is a gap of about 1.4 billion yuan (may come from non-operating projects or one-time expenses).
3. **New growth engines**: Takeaway (+112%) and multi-brand (+215%) growth rates are astonishing, but the base is still small, accounting for only about 10% of total revenue.
4. **Store expansion slows down**: The net increase is about 43 stores (including franchises), which is far lower than the expansion rate during the peak period of 2019-2020.

## 4. Balance sheet and cash flow

### Balance Sheet Characteristics

- **Right of use assets (lease)**: As an offline chain, lease is the largest "hidden liability"
- **Interest-bearing liabilities**: Moderate, not excessive
- **Operating liabilities**: Larger accounts payable (have a stronger say in suppliers)
- **Cash reserves**: sufficient to cover operational and expansion needs

### Cash Flow Analysis

| Indicators | Estimates |
|------|--------|
| Operating cash flow | ~8-9 billion yuan |
| Capital expenditure (maintenance + new stores) | ~3-4 billion yuan |
| Free cash flow | ~4-5 billion yuan |
| FCF/market capitalization | ~5-6% (assuming market capitalization of HK$80 billion) |

**Key Observations**:
- Operating cash flow far exceeds net profit (Characteristics of the catering industry: large advance receipts + large depreciation)
- Capital expenditures are mainly used for new store openings and renovation of existing stores
- Abundant free cash flow supports higher dividends and expansion

**Buffett will ask**: If we stop opening new stores, FCF will increase significantly. But the question is – without opening new stores, where will growth come from?

## 5. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

| PB | ~7-8 times |
| EV/EBITDA | ~10x |

### Valuation rationality assessment

**The meaning of PE 20 times**:
- For a company with a growth rate of only 1%, a PE of 20 times means that the market is still giving a "premium to high-quality companies"
- If the growth rate drops to 0 or even negative growth, PE may be compressed to 15 times
- If new business (takeaway/multi-brand/franchise) drives growth back to 5-8%, PE may maintain or expand slightly

**Valuation comparison with peers/similar companies:**

| Company | PE | ROE | Growth rate | Description |
|------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Haidilao | 20x | 35-40% | 1% | High quality, low growth |
| Yum China | 22x | 15% | 5% | Moderate Quality Moderate Growth |
| McDonald's | 25x | 150%+ | 3% | Global Franchise Model |
| Starbucks | 20x | 40%+ | 5% | Global Brands |

Haidilao's PE of 20 times is within a reasonable range among global restaurant chains, but considering that the growth rate is only 1%, the valuation premium mainly comes from high ROE and brand quality.

### Intrinsic value estimation

**DCF Valuation:**
- FCF 4.5 billion/year, with a growth rate of 3%/year in the next five years
- Terminal growth rate 2%, discount rate 8%
- Estimated intrinsic value: approximately HK$75-85 billion

**Profit Valuation Method:**
- Net profit in 2026: 4.5 billion × 18 times PE → HK$81 billion
- Net profit in 2026: 4.5 billion × 15 times PE → HK$67.5 billion

**Valuation range: HK$67.5-85 billion**
**Current market value: approximately HK$80 billion**

**Conclusion: The current valuation is basically reasonable, neither cheap nor expensive. **

## 6. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Management’s capital allocation capability ★★★★☆

- **Dividend**: Maintain a reasonable dividend ratio (30-40%) and be friendly to shareholders
- **Expansion Rhythm**: From crazy store opening to rational expansion, learn "moderate"
- **Franchise System**: Introducing franchising is the right choice for asset-light development
- **Multi-brand exploration**: Pomegranate plans to use existing resources to explore new categories, with controllable investment
- **Cost Control**: The turnover rate returning to 4.0 indicates the success of operational efficiency optimization

### The most critical capital decision

From 2021 to 2022, Haidilao will close a large number of stores due to excessive expansion (about 300 loss-making stores were closed). This was a major mistake by management - expanding against the trend during the epidemic and misjudged the pace of recovery.

However, subsequent corrections also show that management has the ability to "admit mistakes": decisively close stores to stop losses and return to the core operating logic of turnover rate.

## 7. Core judgments under Buffett’s framework

**Positive factors:**
- ROE 35-40% is a sign of a truly high-quality company
- Organizational capabilities constitute a real moat
- Brand power is unparalleled in China's catering industry
- Abundant cash flow and sustainable dividends
- Management learns from expansion mistakes and becomes more rational

**Negatives:**
- Growth peaked (1% growth rate)
- The catering industry is highly competitive, and tastes and trends change
- High labor costs are a structural problem (long-term rise in labor costs)
- PE 20x is not cheap for a low growth company
- Franchise system may dilute brand value in the long term
