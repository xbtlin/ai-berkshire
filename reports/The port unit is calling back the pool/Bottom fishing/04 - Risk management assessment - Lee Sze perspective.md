## 1. Management evaluation

### Equity Structure and Core Personnel

| People | Positions | Shareholdings |
|------|------|------|

### Zhang Yong: The founder’s "retreat" and "advance"

Zhang Yong is the soul of Haidilao - he created the corporate culture of "abnormal service" and the master-apprentice management system. But his role has undergone important changes in recent years:

1. **2021**: Yang Lijuan takes over as CEO, Zhang Yong retires behind the scenes
2. **2024**: Yang Lijuan becomes CEO of Tehai International, and Gou Yiqun takes over as CEO of Haidilao
3. **Zhang Yong still maintains final control through 60%+ shareholding**

**Evaluation**: Zhang Yong's initiative to step aside is a positive sign - it shows that he is building an "organizational system that does not rely on individuals." But it also means that Haidilao is shifting from "founder-driven" to "professional manager management." Whether this transition period is smooth will directly determine the company's future execution capabilities.

### Gou Yiqun: The third CEO

- Former CFO, with natural sensitivity to numbers and operational efficiency
- From the "development period" to the "refined management period", it is a reasonable choice for the CFO to take over as CEO
- But the most important thing for a catering industry CEO may be "perception of people" and "sense of consumer trends" - are these Gou Yiqun's strengths? To be verified

### The core contradiction of management

**Zhang Yong’s "depersonalization" challenge**:
Haidilao's management culture is highly dependent on Zhang Yong's personal philosophy (such as the flywheel of "employee happiness → good service → customer satisfaction → profit"). When Zhang Yong gradually withdraws from daily management, can this culture be sustained in an institutionalized manner?

This is similar to Buffett's question for Berkshire - can the founder's "culture" survive after he leaves?

## 2. In-depth analysis of incentive mechanism

### Master-apprenticeship: Haidilao’s organizational password

| Tier | Source of income | Description |
|------|---------|------|
| Employees | Piece rate + dividends | More work, more reward, incentive services |
| Store Manager | Our store’s profit sharing (~2.8%) | Directly linked to operations |
| Master | Apprentice store profit sharing (~0.4%/home) | Encourage the training of new store managers |
| Coaching staff | Regional profit sharing | Managing multiple stores |

**Core Essence**: Through the profit sharing mechanism, every store manager becomes an "entrepreneur" and every master becomes an "investor". This solves the most difficult problem for chain restaurants - how to keep stores operating far away from the headquarters efficient.

**The moat of this mechanism**: Competitors can theoretically copy this system, but without decades of cultural accumulation and talent reserves, what they copy is only the form rather than the essence.

### Equity incentives

- Core management team shareholding binding
- Zhang Yong’s 60% shareholding ensures long-term alignment of interests
- But it also means that small shareholders have no say - if Zhang Yong makes a wrong decision, no one can check and balance it

## 3. Institutional Risk Analysis

### Food Safety Risk ★★★☆ (Moderate but fatal)

- 1,383 stores, any problem in any one may become a hot spot on the entire network
- Haidilao's kitchen hygiene problems have been exposed many times, but each time it was dealt with quickly
- The larger the scale, the higher the statistical probability of problems
- **Core Judgment**: Haidilao's food safety management level is among the highest in the industry, but it cannot be 100% trouble-free. Once a major incident occurs (such as large-scale food poisoning), the brand may be severely hit.

### Labor policy risk ★★★☆ (medium)

- Haidilao has tens of thousands of employees and is labor-intensive
- Rising minimum wage standards and social security compliance requirements continue to push up labor costs
- If stricter working hour limits are introduced in the future, Haidilao's service density model may be affected

### Consumption environment risk ★★★☆ (medium)

- During an economic downturn, consumers may eat out less often
- Haidilao, with a unit price of 100+, is more susceptible to consumption downgrades than mass catering
- But the "social attribute" of hot pot provides a certain degree of anti-cyclicality

### Risk of intensified competition ★★☆☆ (lower)

- The threshold for the hot pot industry is low, and new brands continue to pour in.
- But Haidilao’s size and brand advantage make real threats few
- More threats come from other categories (such as barbecue, barbecue) competing for consumer time/budget

## 4. Li Lu type risk framework

### "What's the worst case scenario?"

**Scenario 1: Growth stagnates completely and profits decline slightly**
- Revenue remains unchanged at 43 billion, profit remains at 3.5-4 billion
- Result: It becomes a pure dividend stock, and the PE is compressed to 13-15 times.
- Impact: Market value dropped from 80 billion to 50-60 billion (-25%-38%)
- **Probability: 30%**

**Scenario 2: Major food safety incident**
- Large-scale food poisoning or major health scandal occurs
- Short-term revenue decline of 10-20%, brand repair will take 1-2 years
- Impact: The stock price may plummet by 30-50% in the short term, but there is a high probability of recovery
- **Probability: 5-10%**

**Scenario 3: The consumption environment deteriorates severely**
- Economic recession has led to a sharp decline in the frequency of dining out
- Revenue fell by 15-20%, profit fell by 30-40%
- Impact: short-term pain but the company can weather it
- **Probability: 10%**

**Scenario 4: The franchise system is out of control**
- Frequent quality problems occur in franchised stores, damaging the brand
- Forced to withdraw franchise rights or close franchise stores
- Impact: Growth story shattered, valuations revised downwards
- **Probability: 10%**

### "What are the chances of surviving?"

**5-year survival probability: 99%+**
**Probability of maintaining profitability for 5 years: 95%+**
**Probability of remaining the industry leader in 5 years: 90%+**

There is almost no survival risk in Haidilao. There are only a handful of companies in China's catering industry that can achieve revenue of 40 billion+, and their organizational capabilities and brand accumulation provide a strong moat. Even in the face of adversity, the company has sufficient resources and capabilities to weather the storm.

## 5. Safety Margin Assessment

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Conservative valuation (zero growth):**
- Sustainable FCF 4 billion/year, discount rate 8% → HK$50 billion
- Franchise/multi-brand option value is approximately HKD 5-10 billion → HKD 55-60 billion
- **Margin of safety: The current market value of 80 billion is too expensive**

**Neutral valuation (3% growth):**
- DCF method: approximately HK$75-85 billion
- **Margin of safety: basically reasonable**

**Optimistic valuation (5% growth driven by new business):**
- DCF method: approximately HK$100-110 billion
- **Margin of safety: 20-30% room for upside**

### Safety margin judgment

| Scenario | Valuation | Relative to current 80 billion |
|------|------|-------------|
| Conservative | 55-60 billion | -25%~-31% |
| Neutral | 75-85 billion | -6%~+6% |
| Optimistic | 100-110 billion | +25%~+38% |

**Conclusion: Current valuation is in the neutral range, neither cheap nor expensive. There are upsides if growth resumes and downside risks if growth stalls. **

## 6. Perspective of Civilization Evolution

### The position of restaurant chains in consumer society

With urbanization, the accelerated pace of life and the improvement of consumption power, the proportion of dining out in Chinese people's dietary structure will continue to increase. Chaining is a major trend - consumers need "certain experience", and brand chains provide this certainty.

Haidilao represents the highest level of catering chain in China. From the perspective of civilization evolution, its existence proves that Chinese enterprises can establish world-class management capabilities in the service industry.

### Key Issues in the Long-Term Perspective

- Will the hot pot category become an eternal dining mainstream like burgers in the United States? **Most likely**
- Can Haidilao maintain its leading position in the industry for decades like McDonald's? **Possible but with more challenges**
- How big of a chain enterprise can the Chinese catering market ultimately support? **The answer may be bigger than expected**
