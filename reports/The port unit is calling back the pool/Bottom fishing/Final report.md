## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **Super high ROE (35-40%)**: It is a real "high-quality company" indicator, indicating extremely high capital efficiency
2. **Organizational Capability Moat**: The master-apprentice management system is unique in China’s catering industry and difficult to copy.
3. **Brand Power**: China’s No. 1 catering brand, with strong consumer trust and awareness
4. **New growth engines**: Takeaway (+112%), multi-brand (+215%), and franchise systems provide incremental imagination
5. **Management has consistent interests**: The founder holds 60%+ shares and is highly bound to shareholders
6. **Cash flow quality**: Operating cash flow far exceeds net profit, and profit has high gold content

### Bear Case

1. **Growth peaks**: Core business revenue only increased by 1.1%, and the hot pot market is becoming saturated.
2. **PE is too expensive at 20 times**: For low-growth companies, the valuation premium may be unsustainable
3. **Franchise Risk**: Franchise system may dilute brand value and service quality
4. **Structural increase in labor costs**: Labor-intensive models face long-term cost pressures
5. **Insufficient verification of multiple brands**: Most of the 20 brands are extremely small, and the probability of success is doubtful.
6. **Uncertain consumption environment**: Economic downturn may affect the frequency of dining out

## Buying Conditions and Catalysts

### Ideal buying price
- PE 15-16 times: Provides a reasonable safety margin
- PE 13-14 times: excellent buying opportunity
- Current PE 20 times: wait and see

### Buy Catalyst
1. Revenue growth returns to 5%+ (driven by multi-brand/franchise/takeaway)
2. The market correction resulted in valuations being compressed to 15 times PE
3. Announcement of increased dividends/buybacks
4. A new brand reaches scale (such as reaching 100+ stores)

### Sell/avoid signal
- The turnover rate continues to be below 3.5 (demand worsens)
- Major food safety incidents
- Frequent quality problems occur in franchise stores
- Massive management turnover
- PE expands to more than 25 times (bubble)

## Final conclusion

**Current judgment: High-quality targets, waiting for better buying prices**

Haidilao is the target with the "highest company quality" among the five companies:
- ROE 35-40% proves strong capital efficiency
- Organizational capabilities and brand constitute a real moat
- Management's interests are highly aligned
- Very high cash flow quality

But the current PE of 20 times is not cheap enough for a company with a growth rate of only 1%. The ideal buying strategy is:

1. **Waiting for a correction**: Actively build positions when PE drops to 15-16 times
2. **Waiting for growth to resume**: If new business drives growth back to 5%+, the current PE of 20 times is acceptable
3. **Long-term tracking**: Even if you don’t buy, it is worth continuing to pay attention to as a core member of the "high-quality target pool"
