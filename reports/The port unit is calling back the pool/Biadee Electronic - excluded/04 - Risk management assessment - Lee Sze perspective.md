# I. Management assessment

#1.1 Corporate governance structure

The Biadee electronics governance structure is the key to understanding this company:

- **Practial control person**: Wang Tzufu (indirect control through Biadi shares)
- ** Executive Director/Management**: Wang Xinqiang et al., with low shareholding (King Xin Xing Xing ́s share about 0.38%)
- **Men control**: Biadi shares hold about 65% of Biadi electronics

**LiJerdial assessment**: Biadi Electronics is essentially a Biadi Group “manufacturing services subsidiary” rather than an independent operating entity. Management’s decision-making authority is limited to parent company strategies. This is fundamentally different from LeeJu’s “entrepreneurshipal independent management team” advocated.

##1.2 Management capacity

** Positive evidence**:
- Successful integration of Jeppe operations in China (acquired of $14.29 billion, with a conversion of capacity within 12 months)
- Forward layout AI server (from 2023, before most of its peers)
- Automobile electronics from zero to 27 billion.
- Accredited to the core suppliers on the British Weidelberg platform

** Negative evidence**:
- No gains in 2025 and Māori ratio management capacity is questionable
- The overall profit rate has not risen or decreased since the takeover of Jeppe, and integration has not created overvalued
- Overdependence on revenues from associated transactions (car electronic plates)
- A very low share of red (RMB 0.156 shares only) and not sufficiently friendly to small and medium-sized shareholders

##1.3 Management incentives

The very low management share (less than 1 per cent of the total) means that:
- Financial interests of management are not highly consistent with small and medium-sized shareholders
- Decision-making more serves the corporate strategy of the parent company Biadi, rather than maximizing the benefits of Biadi's independent electronic shareholders
- Lack of "owner mentality" -- this is a major reduction in Lee Siu's investment framework.

# 1.4 Integrity and transparency

- Annual reports are more regular and meet the requirements of Hong Kong Customs
- But the fairness of associated transaction pricing is difficult to independently verify
- Is the AI server business guidelines too radical? Is management a "tale" or a practical expression?

# II. List of key risks

##2.1 Associated transaction risk # (highest risk)

** This is the most central risk to Biadi electronics.**

- Automobile electronics business ($27 billion, or 15 per cent) is almost entirely from the parent company Biadey.
- Is the associated transaction pricing fair? Is the parent company using transfer pricing to reconcile the profits of two listed companies?
- Biadi electronics has no ability to refuse when the parent company decides to internalize certain parts and components production or introduce secondary supplier diversion orders

**Li's test**: When a company's most definitive source of growth (car electronics) is entirely dependent on the related party, the security of interests of small and medium-sized shareholders is fragile. The parent company can change the profitability of its subsidiaries by adjusting the transfer price and the scope of its business at any time.

##2.2 Client concentration risk

- Consumer electronics are highly dependent on apples (buying Jeppe and becoming Apple Core Business).
- Apple supply chain management is known for "multiple plus price."
- If Apple reduces the share of substitute workers in Biadey Electronics, the impact could be over $10 billion.

** Historical lessons**: Soyuz, Latitude etc. have experienced apple order transfers leading to significant fluctuations in performance.

#2.3 Exchange rate risk

- Large amounts of income in US dollars/port dollars at a cost in renminbi
- The appreciation of the renminbi in 2025 caused significant exchange losses
- With a gross profit rate of only 6 per cent, exchange rate fluctuations of 1-2 per cent may consume most of the profits

While companies have foreign exchange hedging instruments, the cost of hedges itself erodes profits.

##2.4 New business implementation risks

- AI servers earned only 940 million yuan in 2025 and guided 10 billion yuan in 2026 - a need for more than 10 times the increase
- Growth logic will be vexed if the bytes beat and the core clients shrink orders or the next generation platform chooses other generations of business
- Robot business is in its early stages, with near zero revenue.

# 2.5 Industry cycle risk

- Business first hit by the era of high consumer electronic cyclicality and poor innovation in the products of large clients
- AI Arithmetic investment risks bubbles - if it were to be "AI Winter" in 2027, the demand for AI servers could drop sharply

#2.6 Technological route risk

- The liquid cold technology route is currently mainstream, but if there is a subversive radiation program, the input of Biadei electronics may sink.
- In the electronics area of automobiles, the impact is difficult to predict if the Biadi car's intellectuals programme is to switch suppliers or change the technology route

# 3.2 Historical lessons from the group subsidiary's investment

Risk models common to parent subsidiary structures in the Hong Kong stock market:
- Transfer of profits through related transactions by parent companies
- Subsidiaries provide services to their parent companies at low cost, "anti-feeding" parent companies

# Four, long-term value judgements

# 4.1 Two possibilities in ten years

** Scenario A: Successful transition (probability 30%)**
- AI server + robotics over 20%
- Automobile electron ratio is over 25%.
- Increase in the combined Māori rate to over 10%
- Net annual profits exceed $10 billion.
- Valuation gets a "Technology Platform" premium, PE 20+
- Counterpart market value HK$ 200 billion +

** Scenario B: status quo (50 per cent probability)**
- Consumer electronics is still the main industry.
- New business has grown but not yet subversive.
- Māori rate fluctuates between 6 and 8 per cent
- $4.6 billion per annum net profit
- PE's 12-16 times.
- Counterpart market value of HK$50-$90 billion

** Scenario C: Deterioration (20% probability)**
- Apple order loss, parent company restructuring
- The competition on the AI server has resulted in a flat-out of the Maori rate.
- The continuous contraction of consumer electronics
- The net annual profit fell to $2-3 billion
- PE drops to 8-10 times.
- Counterpart market value HK$ 20-30 billion

##4.2 Calculating expectations

Weighted market value = 2000x 30% + 700x 50% + 250x 20% = 600 + 350 + 50 = 100 billion HK$

The current market value is about HK$64 billion. The expectation is about HK$100 billion, implying about 56% of the space to go.

4.3 Li's "Know-One" test

Core issue of Lee Sze investment:** Can you still hold it in extreme circumstances?**

- If the stock price falls from HK$28 to HK$15 (the profit continues to deteriorate in 2025), do you have any confidence in siloing?
- If AI server operations were to be only 3 billion yuan in 2026 instead of 10 billion dollars, do you still believe in long-term values?
- If the parent company has a linkage trading programme that is not favourable to minority shareholders, do you have a strategy to respond?

For most investors, the uncertainty of Biadi electronics is too high — the lack of the “high degree of certainty” required by Liszú.

** Core conclusion**: From a Lee-soo perspective, Biadee electronics is not about business models or industry locations, but about corporate governance and independence**.
