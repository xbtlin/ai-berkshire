# BYD Electronics (0285.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, moat, user value, management philosophy
> Rating: ★★★☆☆

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 Essential definition

BYD Electronics' core business is **Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS)**-helping brand customers OEM electronic products. This is a typical "coolie" business: the customer gives you a design plan, and you are responsible for production and manufacturing, earning a meager processing fee.

The revenue structure in 2025 clearly demonstrates this essence:
- Intelligent terminal business (assembly + parts): 151.5 billion yuan, accounting for 84.4%
- New energy automotive electronics: 27 billion yuan, accounting for 15.1%
- AI computing power infrastructure: 940 million yuan, accounting for 0.5%

More than 80% of his income comes from helping others assemble mobile phones and laptops. The gross profit rate of 5.99% means that for every 100 yuan of income earned, less than 6 yuan is gross profit, and the net profit rate after deducting various expenses is less than 2%.

### 1.2 How does Duan Yongping view this business?

Duan Yongping repeatedly emphasizes doing "the right thing". The business he likes has several characteristics:
- Differentiated products or services
- No need for frequent large-scale capital expenditures
- Able to generate sustained and stable free cash flow
- A unique positioning in the minds of consumers

In comparison, EMS OEM is weak in these four dimensions:
- **Limited differentiation**: The essence of OEM is standardized services, and customers can switch suppliers
- **Large Capital Expenditure**: Continuous investment in production line equipment is required
- **Thin Profit**: A business with a gross profit margin of 6% has extremely low error tolerance.
- **No brand premium**: The end consumer does not know who is doing the OEM work

Duan Yongping might say: "This is not a good business, but it may be in a good ecosystem."

## 2. Moat analysis

### 2.1 Potential moat

**(1) Economies of scale**
BYD Electronics' annual revenue is close to 180 billion yuan, ranking seventh in global EMS. The benefits of scale are procurement bargaining power and capacity utilization optimization. But the advantage of scale is not absolute in the EMS industry - Foxconn is larger and cannot stop latecomers.

**(2)Vertical integration capability**
This is the biggest differentiation of BYD Electronics compared to pure foundries. Backed by BYD Group, the company has vertical integration advantages in the following aspects:
- New material research and development (molds, metals, glass, ceramics)
- Battery technology (energy storage, liquid cooling)
- Auto parts manufacturing experience
- Robotics and Automation

This cross-field technology accumulation allows BYD Electronics to provide "one-stop solutions" rather than simple assembly.

**(3) Associated ecological binding**
Parent company BYD is the world's leader in new energy vehicles. The revenue of the automotive electronics business in 2025 will be 27 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 27.7%. The certainty of this part of the revenue is extremely high - as long as BYD Auto continues to grow, the automotive business of the electronics sector will be guaranteed.

**(4)Customer stickiness**
After acquiring Jabil, BYD Electronics entered Apple's core supply chain. Once introduced by large customers, the switching cost will be high (certification cycle, yield ramp, production line customization), and they will not be easily replaced in the short term.

### 2.2 The fragility of the moat

**(1) OEM has no pricing power**
EMS companies have extremely weak pricing power. When major customers such as Apple and Samsung lower prices, foundries can only passively accept it. The gross profit margin will decrease by 0.95 percentage points in 2025, partly due to the deterioration of the product mix - the proportion of low-margin products has increased.

**(2) Related party transaction dependence**
Automotive electronics revenue mainly comes from parent company BYD. This is both an advantage and a hidden danger - once the parent company adjusts its strategy (such as internalization or the introduction of competition), BYD Electronics lacks bargaining chips.

**(3) Technical barriers are not high**
In the field of consumer electronics assembly, Luxshare Precision, Foxconn, etc. can provide similar services. Technical differences are more about efficiency and cost than irreplaceability.

## 3. User value and business logic

### 3.1 Who are the real "users"?

The users of BYD Electronics are not end consumers, but brand customers (Apple, Samsung, Huawei, BYD Auto, etc.). For these customers, the value provided by BYD Electronics is:
- Large-scale manufacturing capabilities (hundreds of thousands of workers, global production capacity layout)
- Cost competitiveness (labor + vertical integration reduces costs by 15-20%)
- One-stop service (from design to assembly to logistics)
- Quick response (flexible allocation of production capacity)

### 3.2 Positive argument: platform transformation

BYD Electronics is not willing to be a pure OEM and is transforming into a "platform high-end manufacturing enterprise":
- **Automotive Electronics**: domain controllers, smart driving products, thermal management, deeply bound to the new energy vehicle wave
- **AI Server**: Nvidia GB200/GB300 platform supplies liquid cooling and connectors, with revenue guidance of 10 billion yuan in 2026
- **Robot**: independently developed AMR robot, equipped with NVIDIA platform

If these new businesses can be scaled up, the company will gradually shift from "low-margin OEM" to "high value-added manufacturing platform."

### 3.3 Counter-argument: The essence of OEM is difficult to change

- AI server revenue will only be 940 million yuan in 2025, accounting for 0.5% of total revenue. Even if it reaches 10 billion yuan in 2026, it will only account for about 5%
- Although the gross profit margin of automotive electronics is higher than that of consumer electronics, it is still subject to the pricing of related transactions
- Consumer electronics accounts for 84% of revenue, which means that the overall gross profit margin is difficult to significantly improve in the short term.
- Revenue is nearly 180 billion but net profit is only 3.5 billion. What you earn is "hard-earned money"

## 4. Long-term evolution path of business model

### 4.1 Optimistic scenario

If the AI server and robot business reaches a scale of 20-30 billion yuan (gross profit margin 20-30%) in 2027-2028, and the automotive electronics business continues to grow to more than 50 billion yuan, the company's comprehensive gross profit margin is expected to increase to 8-10%, and the net profit will double to 7-8 billion yuan. Valuation has shifted from "foundry discount" to "tech platform premium".

### 4.2 Pessimistic scenario

Competition for AI servers is fierce (Foxconn, Wistron, etc. are all making plans), and BYD Electronics lacks a first-mover advantage; its share of large consumer electronics customers has been eroded; and the parent company's related-party transaction pricing suppresses profits. The company may stay in the OEM dilemma of 5-6% gross profit margin for a long time.

### 4.3 Baseline Scenario

New business is advancing steadily but not reaching the expected scale. Automotive electronics maintains a growth rate of 25-30%, and consumer electronics remains stable. The comprehensive gross profit margin slowly increased to 7-8%, and the net profit returned to the level of 4-5 billion yuan. PE remains at 14-16 times, and the stock price rises moderately.

## 5. Key judgments

### 5.1 Duan Yongping’s question

**Q: What will this company look like in ten years? **

Two possibilities:
1. Become a "platform manufacturing giant" similar to TSMC in the field of chip foundry - covering consumer electronics, automobiles, AI, and robotics, but the gross profit margin is much lower than TSMC
2. It is still a large but not strong EMS company, with huge scale but meager profits.

**Q: What is the "constant" of this business? **

The constant in the EMS industry is that brand owners are always pursuing lower-cost, higher-efficiency manufacturing partners. BYD Electronics' competitiveness lies in cost - but the cost advantage can be pursued.

**Q: Doing the right thing vs doing things right? **

BYD Electronics is strong in "doing things right" (manufacturing efficiency, vertical integration), but "doing things right" (choosing high value-added tracks) is still in the verification stage. The AI ​​server and robot are in the right direction, but are too small.
