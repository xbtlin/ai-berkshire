# BYD Electronics (0285.HK) financial valuation analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital return, free cash flow, safety margin
> Rating: ★★★☆☆

---

## 1. Review of earnings quality

### 1.1 Core data of income statement

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Trends |
|------|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 130.1 billion | 177.3 billion | 179.5 billion | Slowing growth |
| Gross profit | — | 12.3 billion | 10.76 billion | Down 12.6% |
| Gross profit margin | 7.4% | 6.94% | 5.99% | Continued decline |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 4.04 billion | 4.27 billion | 3.52 billion | Down 17.6% |
| Net profit margin | 3.1% | 2.4% | 1.96% | Continued narrowing |

### 1.2 The dilemma of increasing revenue without increasing profits

2025 will be the most serious year for BYD Electronics to "increase revenue without increasing profits":
- Revenue increased slightly by 1.2% to 179.5 billion yuan
- Net profit fell sharply by 17.6% to 3.52 billion yuan
- Gross profit margin dropped from 6.94% to 5.99%, a decrease of nearly 1 percentage point

**Cause Analysis**:
1. **Deterioration of product mix**: Demand for some models from major customers (Apple) is sluggish, revenue from high-margin components has decreased, and the proportion of low-margin assembly business has increased.
2. **Storage Price Increase**: Rising raw material costs erode profits
3. **Exchange loss**: The appreciation of the RMB leads to a reduction in the conversion of overseas income settled in US dollars.
4. **Q4 gross profit margin is only 4.02%**: The single-quarter gross profit margin in the fourth quarter fell to 4%, a year-on-year decrease of 1.85 percentage points, indicating that the deterioration of profitability is accelerating.

### 1.3 Buffett’s test standards

Earning quality characteristics that Buffett values:
- **Stability**: BYD Electronics’ net profit fluctuates greatly (increased by 118% in 2023 and dropped by 17.6% in 2025), and does not have stability ❌
- **Predictability**: It is greatly affected by the order rhythm of large customers and exchange rate fluctuations, making prediction difficult ❌
- **Authenticity**: EMS companies have average cash flow and profit matching, and need to pay attention to the accounts receivable cycle ⚠️
- **Sustainability**: Consumer electronics OEM profits have been under long-term pressure, but automotive electronics has growth certainty ⚠️

## 2. Analysis of return on capital

### 2.1 ROE level

Based on the net profit of 3.52 billion yuan in 2025, assuming net assets of about 35-40 billion yuan, ROE is about 9-10%. This is low for a company with revenue approaching 180 billion yuan.

For comparison:
- Luxshare Precision ROE is about 15-18%
- Foxconn ROE is about 12-15%
- TSMC ROE is about 25-30%

### 2.2 Capital Allocation

BYD Electronics' largest capital allocation move in recent years is the acquisition of Jabil's business in China for 15.8 billion yuan (actual 14.29 billion yuan) in 2023. From the results:
- **Positive**: Revenue will jump from 130.1 billion to 177.3 billion in 2024, with an increase of approximately 47 billion mainly from Jabil’s merger
- **Negative**: After the merger, the overall gross profit margin dropped (from 7.4% to 6.94% and then to 5.99%), indicating that the gross profit margin of Jabil's business is lower than the company's original business

The incremental profit obtained from the 14.29 billion yuan acquisition is limited, and the return on invested capital is not outstanding.

### 2.3 Capital expenditure requirements

EMS companies need to continue to invest in production line equipment to maintain competitiveness. BYD Electronics' annual capital expenditure is about 5 billion to 8 billion yuan, which is a heavy burden on its net profit of 3.5 billion yuan. Free cash flow = operating cash flow - capital expenditures, the actual free cash flow that can be distributed to shareholders is limited.

## 3. Review of balance sheet

### 3.1 Key Accounts

- **Goodwill and Intangible Assets**: A certain amount of goodwill has been accumulated after the acquisition of Jabil, so we need to pay attention to the risk of impairment.
- **Accounts receivable**: There is a certain period for settlement of large customers. Compared with the scale of revenue, the proportion of accounts receivable deserves attention.
- **Cash reserves**: The company pays a dividend of RMB 0.156 per share (end of 2025), and the dividend rate is low

### 3.2 Leverage level

The company's asset-liability ratio is moderate and it is not a highly leveraged operation. However, it should be noted that the EMS industry itself is a model of small profits but quick turnover. Even if the leverage ratio is not high, small fluctuations in profits will have a significant impact on ROE.

### 4.2 Historical valuation comparison

BYD Electronic's historical PE center is about 12-16 times. The current PE of 16 times is above the historical center level. Considering that profits will fall by 17.6% in 2025, if profits resume growth in 2026 (brokerage firms predict net profits of 4.2-5.2 billion yuan), the dynamic PE will fall back to 12-15 times.

### 4.3 Buffett-style valuation thinking

**Intrinsic Value Estimate (Conservative)**:
- Assuming net profit returns to 4.5 billion yuan in 2026
- Give the EMS industry a reasonable PE of 12-14 times
- Reasonable market capitalization = HK$54-63 billion
- Corresponding to approximately HK$24-28 per share

**Intrinsic Value Estimate (Optimistic)**:
- Assuming that new business (AI server + robot) will contribute incremental profits of 1.0-1.5 billion yuan in 2027
- Superimposed on the continued growth of automotive electronics, net profit reached 5.5-6.5 billion yuan
- Give 15-18 times PE (new business premium)
- Reasonable market capitalization = HK$82.5-117 billion
- Corresponding to approximately HK$37-52 per share

### 4.4 Safety margin judgment

| Scenario | Fair Value | Current Stock Price | Margin of Safety |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Conservative (pure EMS valuation) | HKD 24-28 | HKD 28.30 | No margin of safety |
| Neutral (profit recovery) | HKD 30-35 | HKD 28.30 | About 6-19% |
| Optimistic (new business realization) | 37-52 HKD | 28.30 HKD | About 31-84% |

**Key Judgment**: The current stock price has no margin of safety under conservative valuation, but if you believe that profits will recover and new business will increase in 2026-2027, there will be some room for upside.

## 5. Cash flow and shareholder returns

### 5.1 Free Cash Flow

Free cash flow characteristics of EMS companies:
- Operating cash flow is greatly affected by fluctuations in working capital (seasonal changes in accounts receivable/payable)
- Strong sustainability of capital expenditure (production line renewal, new production capacity construction)
- True "free" cash flow is much lower than reported net profit

### 5.2 Dividends and buybacks

The final dividend for 2025 is RMB 0.156 per share, equivalent to approximately HK$0.17 per share. The dividend rate based on the stock price of HK$28.3 is only about 0.6%. This has little appeal to income investors.

Buffett's attitude: If a company cannot generate sufficient free cash flow and return to shareholders, then investor returns entirely depend on the market's expectations for the company's future growth. This increases investment uncertainty.

But judging from the current stock price (near the 52-week low) and the expected profit recovery in 2026, it is not obviously overvalued. If investors are confident that the new business can significantly increase profit margins within 2-3 years, the current window may be a reasonable entry; however, if the valuation is based solely on EMS OEM, the safety margin is insufficient.

## 8. Key financial observation points

1. **Q1-Q2 gross profit margin in 2026**: Can it rise to above 7%? If it continues to be below 6%, there is no hope of profit recovery
2. **AI server revenue**: Management guides 10 billion yuan in 2026, and the actual redemption rate is the core variable of valuation
3. **Automotive electronics independent third-party customers**: In addition to the parent company, can we expand external car company customers?
4. **Exchange Impact**: The impact of RMB exchange rate trends on U.S. dollar-denominated revenue
5. **Capital Expenditure Pace**: Will new business investment further suppress short-term free cash flow?
