## Research background

BYD Electronics is the seventh largest EMS company in the world. After acquiring Jabil's business in China for 15.8 billion yuan in 2023, it became the core OEM of Apple's supply chain. The company's business covers three major sectors: smart terminals (mobile phone/laptop assembly and parts), new energy automotive electronics, and AI computing infrastructure.

**Core Conflict**: The company has certain growth from its parent company BYD Auto (automotive electronics increased by 40%+ annually), and has entered new high-margin tracks such as AI servers/robots. However, the gross profit margin of its main business, consumer electronics OEM, is only about 6%, and there will be an increase in revenue but no increase in profits in 2025 (revenue of 179.5 billion yuan increased slightly by 1.2%, and net profit of 3.5 billion yuan fell by 17.6%). Investors need to judge: Can the new business change the nature of foundry manufacturing?
