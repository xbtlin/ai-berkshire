# III. Analysis of industrial trends

#3.1 Trends I: AI computing infrastructure outbreak

This is the biggest structural opportunity for Biadi electronics.

- The British Weilda GB200/GB300 series requires a large amount of liquid cold radiation, power management, precision connectors
- Biadet electronics has been certified by British Weida, with $940 million in the year 2025 on the AI server.

**Munge's "psychological model" application**: this is typical of the "steak business" -- A.I.'s gold-selling people tend to make a more steady profit. But beware: when there are too many shovels, shovels become merchandise. Fuscon, Latsaka, Celestica are stealing this cake.

#3.2 Trends II: Automotive Wire Security

The electronic content of new energy cars is much higher than that of fuel trucks.
- Domain controllers, smart driving hardware.
- Thermomanagement system
- Car screens interact with humans.

Biadi electronics is a core supplier within the group, and this part of the income growth is highly certain (27.7% in 2025). Manger would think it is the "most certain part."

#3.3 Trends III: Consumption of eEMS to the top

Global smartphones have dropped from a peak of 1.5 billion in 2016 to 1.1 to 1.2 billion, with insufficient growth power. ASI mobile phones may have introduced a switch, but have limited marginal increases in replacements.

This means that the Biadi electronic consumer business (84 per cent of income) is difficult to achieve a significant increase, relying on a higher share and an optimal product structure.

#3.4 Trends IV: Geopolitical Reshaping Supply Chains

In the context of Chinese technology decoupling:
- **Risk**: Apples may reduce China ' s surrogate ratio if China and the United States relations deteriorate
- **Oportunities**: Chinese brands (Hao, Mi, etc.) prefer domestic business
- Biadi electronics ' globalized production profile (India, Viet Nam, etc.) helps to hedge geo-risks

# IV. Application of multi-thinking models

##4.1 Biology model: Eco-level competition

In the EMS sector ecosystems, the unique ecological position of Biadi electronics is:** the transboundary manufacturing platform of Automobile Electronics+Consumable Electronics+AI**.

- Eco-position of Fuscomb: super-large-scale consumer electronic surrogate
- Precision ecological position: high-precision consumer electronics plus fast-expanding categories
- Biade Electronic Eco-position: Inner to the Automobile Industry Chain + External EMS

The advantage of this transboundary ecological position is that "cross-subsidization" - the technological accumulation of auto electron can be migrated to AI servers (liquid cooling, power management, heat management). But the risk is that resources are dispersed.

# 4.2 Physics model: critical point thinking

The new business of Biadey Electronics is approaching a critical point:
- AI servers have grown from 940 million to 10 billion.
- Once the AI+ car electronics ratio exceeds 30%, the overall Māori ratio will change qualitatively (from 6% to 8-10%).
- This critical point could be 2027-2028.

If the critical point is not reached (AI servers are growing less than expected), the company will be stuck in a "large but not strong" state for a long time.

# 4.3 Psychological model: narrative error

There are two extremes of the market-based Biadee:
- ** See multi-narrative**: "AI server plus robot plus car electronic three-wheel drive, revalued."
- **Calms**: "A substitute is a substitute, and the 6% Māori rate is not changed in essence."

Manger would remind us not to be kidnapped by any kind of narrative. Depending on the actual data — can the AI server really do 10 billion in 2026? What is the car ' s electronic Maori rate?

# 4.4 Economics Model: Uneconomical

The EMS industry has a "uneconomic of scale" phenomenon: revenue has increased from $17.7 billion to $17.95 billion (+1.2 per cent), while profits have declined from $4.27 billion to $3.52 billion (-17.6 per cent). This suggests that marginal income profits contribute to negative — new business is actually "deep" profitability.

Reason: In order to obtain orders from large clients, lower surrogate rates must be accepted. The increase in size does not represent an improvement in profits, but may worsen.

# VI. Business cycle judgement

# 6.1 Consumer electronic cycle

The 2023-2025 period was a low-end cycle for smart phones. The AI mobile phone switchover could drive a moderate recovery in 2026-2027, but it was difficult to return to its peak in 2016. For the e-consumption business in Yadi, it was "maintain" rather than "growth".

#6.2 Automobile Electronic Cycle

New energy vehicles are still in the prime of an upscaling rate (global penetration is about 25-30%) and Biadi is continuing to be released. Automobile electrons are in a rising cycle, with a high expected growth rate of 3-5 years.

# 6.3 AI Calculatory Cycle

2024-2026 is the period of the outbreak of AI infrastructure investment. But there are market concerns that the return on large model investments is uncertain and that AI server demand may fall cyclically if AI investment slows (like the contraction of Internet equipment following the 2000 Internet bubble).
