# BYD Electronics (0285.HK) Investment Research Final Report

> Study date: May 16, 2026
> Overall rating: ★★★☆☆ (neutral to cautious)
> Conclusion: It has structural features but is not a typical value investment target. It needs to wait for the verification of key variables.

---

## 1. Summary of investment thesis

### 1.1 One sentence summary

BYD Electronics is a large EMS company in a period of transformation: its main business (consumer electronics OEM) gross profit margin is only 6%, and revenue growth will not increase profits in 2025; but relying on the parent company's ecology (automotive electronics) and AI wave (server liquid cooling), it is trying to evolve from "low-end OEM" to a "high value-added manufacturing platform". The current valuation is reasonable but there is no obvious margin of safety.

### 1.2 Core Contradiction

| Bullish logic | Bearish logic |
|----------|----------|
| Deterministic growth of automotive electronics (+28%/year) | 84% of revenue comes from low-margin consumer electronics OEM |
| AI server guides 10 billion yuan in 2026 | Net profit drops 17.6% in 2025 |
| NVIDIA certification + liquid cooling technical barriers | Gross profit margin is only 5.99% and continues to decline |
| Stock price close to 52-week low | Heavy reliance on related-party transactions and poor independence |
| New robotics track layout | ROE is less than 10%, low return on capital |

## 4. Investment decision-making framework

### 4.1 What type of investors is it suitable for?

| Investor Type | Suitability | Reasons |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Value investing (Buffett style) | ❌ Not suitable | Low ROE, no pricing power, poor free cash flow |
| Growth investment | ⚠️ Cautiously suitable | AI+ automotive electronics has growth potential, but the valuation has been partially reflected |
| Event-driven | ✅ Suitable | Waiting for catalysts such as AI server order verification and quarterly gross profit margin recovery |
| Parent company beneficiary | ✅ Suitable | If you are optimistic about BYD Auto, electronics is an indirect beneficiary |
| Long-term holding | ❌ Not suitable | Governance structure issues make long-term holding risk-return ratio poor |

### 4.2 Buying conditions (if you want to consider opening a position)

At least 3 of the following conditions must be met:
1. ✅ The stock price fell below HK$25 (corresponding to about 11 times the 2026E PE)
2. ⏳ Q1/Q2 gross profit margin will rise to above 7% in 2026
3. ⏳ AI server revenue will reach more than 3 billion yuan in the first half of 2026 (verification of the full-year guidance of 10 billion yuan)
4. ⏳ Automotive electronics expands to at least one non-BYD external car company customer
5. ⏳ Management increases holdings or announces buyback plan

### 4.3 Sell/avoidance conditions

If any of the following occurs, you should avoid or leave the site:
1. Gross profit margin has been below 5% for two consecutive quarters
2. Apple significantly reduces its foundry share (such as being replaced by Luxshare)
3. The parent company introduces a related-party transaction plan that is obviously unfavorable to minority shareholders
4. AI server revenue in 2026 will be less than 3 billion yuan (seriously below guidance)
5. Management reduced holdings on a large scale

## 7. Final conclusion and action suggestions

### 7.1 Summary and Judgment

BYD Electronics is a target that "has a story but is not cheap enough":

**Front**:
- Automotive electronics is tied to the global leader in new energy vehicles, with high certainty of growth
- AI servers/liquid cooling/robots are in the right direction, and there is room for valuation reshaping once realized.
- Ranked seventh in global EMS, with outstanding scale and platform capabilities
- Share price near 52-week low, worst-case expectations may have been priced in

**Negative**:
- The nature of OEM determines that the dilemma of 6% gross profit margin is difficult to change in the short term.
- Increasing revenue without increasing profits in 2025 exposes the problem of diseconomies of scale
- The governance structure of parent and subsidiary companies is unfavorable to minority shareholders
- The guidance of 10 billion AI servers is too radical and carries high execution risks
- Management has low shareholding, low dividends, and inconsistent interests

### 7.2 Investment advice

| Suggestions | Content |
|------|------|
| Current Operations | **Wait and See/Small Position Tracking** |
| Reason | Reasonable valuation but no safety margin, need to wait for catalyst verification |
| Pay attention to time nodes | Interim report in August 2026 (verification of AI server, gross profit margin recovery) |
| Position trigger | Stock price falls below HK$25 + at least one fundamental catalyst confirmed |
| Position recommendations | Even if you open a position, it should not exceed 5% of the portfolio (the upper limit of the position is limited by governance risk) |

### 7.3 Continuous tracking of elements

1. **Quarterly focus**: Gross profit margin trend, AI server revenue progress
2. **Semi-annual attention**: Automotive electronics external customer expansion, related transaction announcement
3. **Annual Focus**: Management shareholding changes, dividend policy, strategic adjustments

## 8. Statement of research limitations

1. This report is based on public information and has not been communicated to the management.
2. The details of related-party transactions and the fairness of pricing cannot be independently verified.
3. The AI server business is still in its early stages, and data samples are not enough to make high-confidence judgments.
4. Hong Kong stock liquidity and market sentiment may cause stock prices to deviate from fundamentals for a longer period of time
5. Failure to consider macro factors (such as deterioration of Sino-US relations, global recession and other extreme scenarios)
