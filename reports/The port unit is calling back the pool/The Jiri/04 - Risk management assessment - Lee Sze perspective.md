# II. Risk matrix: a comprehensive look at potential threats

#2.1 Business risk

Risk one: price wars continue to erode profits
- ** Probability**: High (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: Medium and High (<unk> )
** Detailed analysis**: China’s automobile market was already in a fierce price war in 2025 and is expected to be even more brutal in 2026. By virtue of vertical integration of the cost advantage, Biadi continuously lowers prices, forcing all competitors to follow. The Quilli Maori rate, while stable, is 16.6%, but the increase in net interest rates is extremely difficult under constant price pressures.
- ** Mitigating factors**: $68.2 billion cash reserve, 3.2 million economies of scale, cost savings after integration

Risk two: Multi-brand integration failed
- ** Probability**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: High (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: The integration of Kirk and Gilly is the most complex organizational change in the company’s history. Risks include: core brain drain, cultural conflict, vague brand positioning, and confusion of clients.
- ** Mitigating**: management integration experience (Wolvo integration success case), clear organizational structure and personnel arrangements

Risk three: weak export growth abroad
- ** Probability**: Medium and High (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: In 2025, exports were only 420,000, or +0.4%, and were significantly behind competitors. The offshore market is a market of 5-10 percentage points higher profitability, which, if absent, would directly affect overall profitability.
- ** Mitigating factors**: target of 640,000 (+50% or more) for 2026, with a certain overseas brand base of the collar and the arcium

##2.2 Industry risk

Risk four: Smart competition lags behind
- ** Probability**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: Smart driving and smart cabins are becoming key factors in decision-making on car purchases. The Chinese department (inquirers, intellectuals) has developed a strong understanding of this, Tesla FSD continues to evolve, and Ping is leading in the field of intellectual driving.
- **mitigation**: large-scale R & D inputs (17.6 billion yuan/year), in cooperation with international suppliers

## Risk five: New energy subsidies fall and policy changes
- ** Probability**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: Medium and low (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: Government subsidy policies may be adjusted as new energy penetrations exceed 50%. The first mandatory standard of electric car consumption limits in 2026 could increase the compliance costs of car firms.
- **mitigation**: The new energy transition is relatively mature and dependence on subsidies is declining

#2.3 Financial risk

Risk six: worsening trend of less-than-gain revenue
- ** Probability**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: High (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: The growth in revenue in 2025 was 44%, but the net return on the mother ' s return was only 0.2%, and if this trend continued in 2026, the market would further lower expectations of guild profitability. In particular, the large-scale movement of Galaxy brands but low single-car profits could worsen the overall profit structure if high-end brands (alkryon, tectonics) failed to provide sufficient profit contributions.
- **mitigation**: cost savings from integration (R & D - 10~20%, procurement - 5~8%)

## Risk seven: Governance risks associated with the complex architecture of the group of shareholders
- ** Probability**: Low (<unk> )
- ** Impact**: Medium (<unk> )
- ** Detailed analysis**: GIG’s assets and operations are much larger than those of listed companies, with potential governance risks such as associated transactions, inequitable resource allocation, etc. Pricing and terms in the process of privatisation of the arcium, and distribution of intra-group resources between listed companies and non-listed operations are of concern to investors.
- ** Mitigating**: Port Authority regulatory requirements, independent director supervision

# Four: Reverse thinking: Under what circumstances does investment cost money?

This is the most important issue in investment decision-making. The following are the scenarios that could lead to a loss of investment:

# Scenario one: "Growing Traps"
- Sales continued to grow to 3.45 million, but net profits did not rise or fall
- The low price of the Galaxy brand erodes overall profit margins
- Price wars lasted for more than three years, and profits were long-term compressed
- ** Probability assessment**: Medium. This is the most critical risk of immediate alert.

# Scenario two: "Consolidation failed"
- The core of the Ultra-Krypton team is draining.
- The kryptonite and the collar brands are confused. Consumers' perceptions are falling.
- Integration would result in less cost savings than expected
- ** Probability assessment**: Medium and low. Management has successful experience with Volvo integration, but internal integration is more likely to cause friction than cross-border acquisitions

# Scenario three: "Competition crushes."
- Biadi keeps cutting prices with cost advantages, squeezes Gilly's space at the $10-2 million market.
- The Chinese system has created a dominant position in the field of intelligence, and the Ge Li has been marginalized in the middle and high-end markets
- Chiri's continued to lead overseas markets, and Gilly's export growth is not going to materialize.
- ** Probability assessment**: Low. The size and resources of the Girley are sufficient to avoid being crushed, but they may be "compressed."

# Scenario four: "Black Swan"
- Large-scale product quality issues
- Financial or governance scandal at the holding group level
- The international trade environment has changed dramatically (e.g., a total ban on Chinese car exports)
- ** Probability assessment**: very low, but very high impact

# V. Comprehensive assessment

# 5.1 Does management have a trust?

** Overall judgement: qualified trust.**

Li is one of China’s best entrepreneurs, and his strategic vision and ability to correct mistakes has been proven many times. The management team’s intergenerational transmission is orderly, and after 80 managers are growing up. But attention needs to be paid to:

- The complex structure of the holding group increases the uncertainty of governance
- Individually overly optimistic public commitments need to be discounted.
- The effect of "one good" integration requires at least two to three quarters of data to verify.

#5.2 Are the risks manageable?

** Overall judgement: Core risk is manageable, but profit margin pressure is the greatest uncertainty**

The biggest risk for Gilly is not "will it fail" -- $68.2 billion in cash and 320,000 in size are enough to ensure survival. The real risk is "can you make money" -- can the profit rate continue to rise from the current 4.2% (core net interest rate) under the triple pressure of price wars, consolidation costs, R&D inputs?

The answer to this question will become clear in the second half of 2026.
