# Geely Automobile (0175.HK) Team Lead Comprehensive Judgment

> Research date: May 16, 2026 | Currency unit: RMB (unless otherwise specified, it is Hong Kong dollar)

---

## Comprehensive rating

**★★★★☆ (4/5) — A "conditional buy" target worth tracking**

---

## 1. Core findings in four dimensions

After independent analysis in four dimensions: business model, financial valuation, industry competition, and risk management, we came to the following core findings:

### Nature of business (business model analysis)

Automobile manufacturing itself is not a good business, but Geely has achieved three things in it: a scale advantage of 3.02 million vehicles, a brand matrix covering RMB 70,000 to 500,000, and deep platform technology accumulation. The Galaxy brand's explosive growth of +150% year-on-year to 1.24 million units in 2025 proves Geely's product definition capabilities in China's largest market segment. However, insufficient brand power, weak export competitiveness, and intelligent shortcomings are clear shortcomings.

**Conclusion**: Not a good business, but a good player on a good track. Rating ★★★★☆

### Financial quality (financial valuation analysis)

With revenue of 345.2 billion yuan (+25%), core net profit of 14.4 billion yuan (+36%), and cash of 68.2 billion yuan (+46%), the quality of growth is excellent among large car companies. However, the net profit attributable to the parent company was only 16.85 billion yuan, a slight increase of 0.2%, which exposed the fluctuation of non-core profits and losses and the structural problem of "increasing revenue without increasing profits". The gross profit margin is solid at 16.6% but lower than BYD (22-25%), and the core net profit margin is only 4.2%. In terms of valuation, the current stock price is close to reasonable under the neutral scenario, and there is about 43% upside potential under the optimistic scenario.

**Conclusion**: Growth is strong but the quality of earnings needs to be improved. The valuation is not expensive but it is not a bargain. Rating ★★★☆☆

### Competitive Landscape (Industry Competition Analysis)

Geely is the second largest independent brand, but there is a structural gap (vertical integration capability) with BYD. The moat is "wide but not deep" - scale effect and platformization are real advantages, but it lacks a "killer" competitive barrier. Exports of 420,000 vehicles (+0.4%) were seriously behind Chery (1.29 million vehicles) and BYD (1.04 million vehicles). This is the biggest competitive shortcoming. The industry price war will not end in the short term, but Geely is likely to be "one of the 3-5 companies that survive."

**Conclusion**: The competitive position is stable but not leading. The biggest concern is the shortcomings in overseas expansion. Rating ★★★☆☆

### Management and Risk (Risk Assessment)

Li Shufu is one of China's best entrepreneurs, and his successful acquisition of Volvo fully proves his strategic capabilities. The "One Geely" integration is in the right direction and advancing rapidly. The growth of the post-80s management team is a positive sign. However, the complex structure of the holding group brings governance concerns, and overly optimistic remarks by individual management undermine credibility. The biggest risk is not "will it fail", but "whether it can make money" - the trend of profit margins under the triple pressure of price war + integration costs + R&D investment.

**Conclusion**: The management deserves conditional trust. The core risks are controllable but the profit margin is the biggest uncertainty. Rating ★★★★☆

## 2. Core investment logic

### Three reasons to be bullish

1. **Integration dividends are being released**: Jikrypton’s privatization has been completed, and the brand has been reduced from 6 to 4. It is expected that R&D savings will be 10-20%, procurement savings will be 5-8%, and management and marketing expense rates will both decrease. If the 2026 H2 earnings report validates these numbers, the market will reprice.

2. **New energy transformation leads the way**: New energy sales reached 1.69 million vehicles (+90%), accounting for over 55%. The Galaxy brand has successfully proved Geely’s competitiveness in the new energy track. When the industry elimination round ends, players who have completed the transformation early will get a larger share.

3. **Valuation Protection**: Static PE is about 6-14 times (depending on the caliber), 68.2 billion yuan in cash, and a dividend of HK$0.50 per share (+51.5%), which has certain protection in the downside scenario. 37 investment banks unanimously issued a buy rating, with an average target price of HK$27.93.

### Three reasons to be bearish

1. **Increased revenue without increasing profits may continue**: If the profit growth rate in 2026 continues to be significantly lower than the revenue growth rate, it means that Geely's growth is "growth without quality." Companies with larger scale and lower profit margins are not worth holding for the long term.

2. **Going overseas is a card that has not yet been played**: Exports of 420,000 units (+0.4%) were the lowest growth rate among all large independent car companies. Overseas markets have an incremental profit margin of 5-10 percentage points, and their absence means a ceiling for the profit structure.

3. **The outcome of the price war is uncertain**: If the price war continues for more than 3 years, Geely's 16.6% gross profit margin may be further compressed to 14-15%, and the core net profit margin may drop from 4.2% to below 3%, which will seriously damage investment returns.

## 3. Investment decision-making suggestions

### Key tracking indicators

Before making a purchase/increase decision, it is recommended to closely track the following indicators:

| Indicators | Current Levels | Positive Signals | Negative Signals |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Bicycle core net profit | ~5,200 yuan | Increased to more than 6,000 yuan | Declined to less than 4,500 yuan |
| Gross profit margin | 16.6% | Increased to above 17% | Declined to below 15.5% |
| Export volume | 420,000 vehicles/year | Quarterly growth of 30%+ year-on-year | Continued low single-digit growth |
| Integration cost savings | Just started to be implemented | Quantitative data on management’s fulfillment of promises | Integration costs exceed expectations |
| Ji Krypton + Lynk & Co sales | 570,000 vehicles | Advancing towards the 1 million vehicle target | Growth slowdown or brand confusion |

### Recommended Action

**Current stage**: Included in the key tracking list, there is no rush to build a position for the time being.

**Reason**: Geely Automobile is in the "integration and implementation period", and the financial report data of H1 in 2026 (expected to be released in August 2026) will be a key verification window. Until then, stories are more compelling than data.

**Trigger conditions**:

- **Buy trigger**: In 2026, H1 gross profit margin will increase to more than 17% + quarterly export growth rate will exceed 30% + bicycle net profit will increase, indicating that integration dividends and export increments are being realized
- **Strong Buy Trigger**: The stock price falls below HK$18 due to market sentiment (corresponding to about 10 times core PE). When the safety margin is sufficient, you can actively build a position.
- **Give up the trigger**: Two consecutive quarters of revenue growth without profit growth + Export growth continues to be sluggish + Integrated savings data is far less than expected

## 4. Final judgment

Geely Automobile is an investment target with "a good narrative and the data is still catching up."

The scale of 3.02 million vehicles, 68.2 billion yuan in cash, Li Shufu's strategic capabilities, and the integration direction of "One Geely" - these are all real positive factors. But the core net profit margin of 4.2%, the net profit growth rate attributable to the parent company of 0.2%, and the export growth rate of 0.4% - these figures remind us that a good story does not mean a good investment.

The core of value investing is not "buying a good company", but "buying a good company at a good price". Geely's current price is most likely reasonable, but it is not yet "obviously undervalued." The best strategy is to stay tuned and wait for data validation and emotional pullbacks to provide a better buying window.
