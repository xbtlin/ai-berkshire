# Geely Automobile (0175.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Business Nature and Competitive Advantage | Research Date: May 16, 2026
> Currency unit: RMB (unless otherwise specified as Hong Kong dollar)

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 The essence of business

Automobile manufacturing is a typical "asset-heavy, long-term, strong competition" business. Its essential characteristics are:

- **Capital Intensive**: A new car requires billions of yuan in R&D and mold costs from project establishment to mass production.
- **Scale driven**: Only by reaching a certain sales volume can fixed costs be diluted and supply chain bargaining rights obtained.
- **Limited brand premium**: Except for a few luxury brands (Porsche, Ferrari), the net profit margin of most car companies has been between 3-8% in the long term.
- **Fast technology iteration**: New energy and intelligence are reshaping the competition rules of the entire industry

Judging from Duan Yongping's "good business" standards, automobile manufacturing is naturally not an "easy money" business. But there are still structural opportunities in this track - when a company can simultaneously establish advantages in the three dimensions of scale, efficiency and brand, it can do "not bad business" in a "not so good industry".

### 1.2 Geely’s positioning

Geely Automobile is one of China's largest private car companies, with annual sales of 3.02 million vehicles in 2025, ranking among the top ten global passenger car companies. Its brand matrix covers the complete price range from economy to luxury:

| Brand | Positioning | Sales volume in 2025 | Price range |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| Geely Galaxy | Mainstream new energy | 1.24 million vehicles | 80,000-200,000 |
| Lynk & Co | Mid- to high-end | 350,000 vehicles | 150,000-300,000 |
| Ji Krypton | High-end pure electric | 224,000 vehicles | 200,000-500,000+ |
| Geely China Star | Mainstream fuel/hybrid | ~1.2 million units | 70,000-150,000 |

The logic of this brand matrix is clear: Galaxy is responsible for volume and new energy transformation, Lynk & Co and Jikry are responsible for upward breakthroughs to increase bicycle profits, while the China Star series maintains the cash flow of fuel vehicles.

## 2. User value: Are Geely’s products good?

### 2.1 Verification of product strength

The most direct indicators to judge whether a car company's products are good or not are sales growth and changes in market share.

- **Sales growth**: 3.02 million units in 2025, +39% year-on-year, far exceeding the industry average growth rate
- **Market Share**: Market share exceeded 10% for the first time in the first half of the year and continued to increase throughout the year
- **New energy penetration rate**: New energy sales volume was 1.69 million vehicles, accounting for over 55%, +90% year-on-year

Galaxy brand is the most illustrative case. Sales of the Galaxy brand will be approximately 500,000 vehicles in 2024 and soar to 1.24 million vehicles in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 150%. This explosive growth was achieved not by one or two models, but by the simultaneous increase in volume of multiple models such as the Galaxy E5, Galaxy L6, and Galaxy L7. This shows that Geely's product definition capabilities and cost-effective advantages are real in China's largest market segment of 100,000-200,000 yuan.

### 2.2 Product issues

But there is another side to the coin:

- **JiKr’s growth rate slows down**: JKr’s annual sales were 224,000 units. It faces fierce competition from Tesla, Wenjie, and NIO in the high-end pure electric market, and its growth momentum is not as good as that of Galaxy
- **Insufficient export competitiveness**: Exports for the whole year were 420,000 vehicles, an increase of only 0.4% year-on-year. There was a significant gap between Chery (1.29 million vehicles) and BYD (1.04 million vehicles).
- **Intelligent shortcomings**: Compared with Huawei and Xpeng, there is still a gap in intelligent driving capabilities. Management's statement that "intelligent driving will surpass Tesla FSD within the year" has been questioned by the market.

## 3. Where is the "moat" of the business model?

### 3.1 Scale advantage

The scale effect brought by the annual sales of 3.02 million vehicles is real:

- **Purchasing costs**: Large-scale procurement reduces the cost of parts and components. After the merger of Jikrpton and Lynk & Co, supply chain costs are expected to drop by another 5-8%.
- **Capacity utilization rate**: After integration, the capacity utilization rate is expected to increase by 3-5 percentage points
- **R&D Allocation**: R&D investment of 17.6 billion yuan is spread to 3.02 million units. The R&D cost of a single vehicle is about 5,800 yuan. This level is competitive in the industry.

### 3.2 Platform capabilities

Geely has done relatively well in terms of technology platformization:

- **CMA Platform**: jointly developed with Volvo, supporting Lynk & Co and some Geely models
- **SEA vast platform**: the basis of Ji Krypton and some Galaxy models
- **Thor Hybrid System**: covering Galaxy and China Star series

The advantage of platformization is that one underlying architecture can support multiple models, significantly reducing the cost and cycle of bicycle development. This is a key reason why Geely can quickly launch new models.

### 3.3 The value of brand

Frankly speaking, Geely's brand power is not strong enough. In the minds of consumers:

- Galaxy brand has value-for-money recognition, but has not yet established a clear brand premium
- Lynk & Co has a certain youthfulness and personalized awareness, but its popularity is not as good as competing products at the same price.
- Ji Krypton has a certain position in the high-end electric vehicle market, but its brand influence is not as good as Tesla and Wenjie

The lack of brand power means that Geely relies more on product power and price power to compete. This can quickly increase sales when the industry is booming, but in a price war environment, profit margins will be compressed.

## 4. "One Geely" integration - key strategic variables

### 4.1 Background of integration

In September 2024, Li Shufu issued the "Taizhou Declaration" in Taizhou, officially launching the internal integration of Geely Holding Group. The core reasons are:

- There is serious internal friction and duplication of resources among multiple brands (Jikrypton and Lynk & Co compete directly in the 200,000-300,000 yuan range)
- The independently listed JiKr performed poorly in the capital market (it was privatized within less than 600 days of listing)
- Competition in the industry has intensified, and "the market will not give room for error."

### 4.2 Progress of integration

As of early 2026, the integration has made substantial progress:

- The privatization of Jikrypton was completed (December 22, 2025) and it became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Geely Automobile
- Brands reduced from 6 to 4
- Consolidation of subsidiaries from three (Geely Automobile, Jike, Lynk & Co) to one
- Clear management structure: Gan Jiayue is appointed as CEO of Geely Automobile Group, which consists of Galaxy Business Group and Jikrypton Technology Business Group

### 4.3 Expected benefits of integration

Goals given by management:

- Reduce R&D investment by 10-20% (approximately 1.8-3.5 billion yuan)
- Supply chain costs reduced by 5-8%
- Both administrative expense ratio and marketing expense ratio decreased
- Capacity utilization increased by 3-5 percentage points
- Growth efficiency exceeds 5%

### 4.4 Risks of integration

But integration is never simple:

- **Culture Clash**: The Jikrypton team is accustomed to independent operations and entrepreneurial culture. Will there be a brain drain after being integrated?
- **Brand Positioning**: The customer groups of Jikrypton and Lynk & Co still overlap. How to achieve real differentiation under the "One Geely" framework?
- **Execution Difficulty**: Supply chain integration, channel integration, and system integration all require time and management bandwidth

## 5. Comprehensive judgment from the perspective of "good business"

### 5.1 Rating

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Business model | ★★★☆☆ | Car manufacturing itself is not a good business, but Geely has achieved scale advantage in it |
| User value | ★★★★☆ | Strong product strength and high cost performance, the Galaxy brand explosion proves market recognition |
| Moat depth | ★★★☆☆ | Scale and platform have advantages, but brand power and overseas competitiveness are insufficient |
| Growth space | ★★★★☆ | New energy transformation + overseas expansion + brand integration, space exists but execution is uncertain |

### 5.2 Core Conclusions

Geely Automobile is not a "good business to dream of", but it is transforming from an "ordinary manufacturing industry" to a "platform enterprise with differentiated competitiveness". The key variables are:

**The logic of bullishness:**
- The sales volume of 3.02 million vehicles has established a significant cost advantage
- If the "One Geely" integration is successful, it will release a large amount of resources wasted by repeated investment
- The penetration rate of new energy is still increasing rapidly, and Geely is leading the way in this round of transformation
- The target of 3.45 million vehicles in 2026. If achieved, the scale effect will be further amplified

**The logic of bearishness:**
- The price war in the automotive industry is far from over, and profit margins may continue to be under pressure
- Exports are an important source of incremental profits, but Geely lags behind in this regard.
- The path for the brand to make an upward breakthrough is still unclear, and more time will be needed to differentiate the positioning of Jikrypton and Lynk & Co.
- Increasing revenue without increasing profits (revenue +44%, net profit attributable to parent company only +0.2%) shows that the quality of earnings needs to be improved
