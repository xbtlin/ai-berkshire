### 1.2 Several noteworthy figures

**The difference between core net profit vs. net profit attributable to parent companies**

Core net profit attributable to the parent company was 14.41 billion yuan (+36% year-on-year), but net profit attributable to the parent company was 16.85 billion yuan, a slight increase of only 0.2%. The difference between the two is about 2.4 billion yuan, which mainly comes from non-recurring items such as foreign exchange gains and losses, asset impairment, and investment income. This means:

- Core operating capabilities are indeed improving
- But the quality and predictability of profits are significantly disrupted by non-core factors
- Investors cannot simply look at the net profit attributable to the parent company to judge the company's operating conditions

**Hidden worries about increasing revenue without increasing profits**

Another set of data deserves more attention: calculated by another caliber, revenue increased by 43.73% year-on-year, but net profit only increased by 1.32%. This means that for every RMB 1 increase in revenue, there is almost no increase in net profit. Possible reasons include:

- The Galaxy brand is gaining popularity, but its bicycle profits are low
- Terminal profit margin increases in price war environment
- One-time expenses incurred during the privatization process of JiKrypton
- High R&D investment (17.6 billion yuan)

## 2. Profitability analysis: Profit rate is the key battlefield

### 2.1 Gross profit margin

The gross profit margin in 2025 will be approximately 16.6%, which is at an upper-middle level among domestic car companies. For reference:

| Automobile companies | Gross profit margin (near term) |
|------|-------------|
| BYD | ~22-25% |
| Geely Auto | ~16.6% |
| Great Wall Motors | ~19-20% |
| Li Auto | ~20-22% |

Geely's gross profit margin is lower than that of BYD and Great Wall. The core reason is that the Galaxy brand wins with cost-effectiveness and the price of bicycles is lower; at the same time, Geely's vertical integration is not as good as BYD (BYD produces its own core components such as batteries, chips, and motors).

### 2.2 Net interest rate

Calculated based on the core net profit attributable to the parent company of 14.41 billion yuan / revenue of 345.2 billion yuan, the core net profit margin is approximately 4.2%. This level is lower than average among global car companies, but considering:

- Geely is in the new energy transformation investment period
- The Jikrypton brand will only turn a profit for the first time in 2025
- The industry as a whole is facing pressure from price wars

The core net profit margin of 4.2% is an acceptable starting point. The key is to see whether the trend can improve.

### 2.3 ROE

Judging from the user-provided ROE of 17.7%, this is a good number. This shows that even when profit margins are not high, Geely has maintained a good shareholder return rate through a high asset turnover rate and moderate leverage.

## 3. Balance Sheet: Is it Safe?

### 3.1 Cash adequacy

Total cash of 68.2 billion yuan (+46% year-on-year) is the biggest highlight of Geely's financial health. According to the operating scale in 2025, 68.2 billion yuan in cash is approximately equal to:

- Approximately 2.4 months of income
- Approximately 4.7x annual core net profit
- Far exceeding the cash reserve level of ordinary car companies

Abundant cash means that even if the industry continues to deteriorate, Geely will have enough "bullets" to survive the cold winter.

### 3.2 Asset Quality

As of Q3 2025, total assets are 274.4 billion yuan and shareholders' equity is 93.5 billion yuan. Rough calculation:

- Asset-liability ratio is approximately 66% ((2744-935)/2744)
- Equity multiplier approximately 2.93

For the automobile manufacturing industry, the asset-liability ratio of 66% is at a normal high level. What needs to be paid attention to is the structure of liabilities - if they are mainly operating liabilities (accounts payable, advance receipts, etc.), it is not a big problem; if the proportion of interest-bearing liabilities is too high, you need to be vigilant.

### 3.3 Credit Rating

Oriental Jincheng maintains a high credit rating for Geely Holding Group, which verifies Geely's financial soundness.

## 4. Cash Flow Analysis: The Gold Content of Profit

### 4.1 Operating cash flow

Bank balances and cash increased from 43.06 billion yuan at the beginning of the year to 56.22 billion yuan at the end of the year (another data showed total cash of 68.2 billion yuan, which may include short-term financial management), indicating that operating activities are indeed generating positive cash flow.

### 4.2 Capital Expenditure

The automotive industry is characterized by huge capital expenditures. Geely needs to continue to invest in the following aspects every year:

- New model research and development: 17.6 billion yuan/year
- Factory construction and renovation
- Sales network expansion
- Intelligent and autonomous driving technology

High capital expenditures mean free cash flow (operating cash flow - capital expenditures) may be significantly lower than net profit. This is a common problem in the automobile industry of "good profits but limited cash".

### 4.3 Dividends

The dividend per share is HK$0.50 (+51.5% year-on-year). Based on the total share capital of approximately 10.1 billion shares, the total dividend is approximately HK$5.05 billion (approximately 4.6 billion yuan), accounting for approximately 32% of core net profit.

- The dividend payout ratio of 32% is above average among Hong Kong car companies.
- Based on the current stock price of HK$22, the dividend yield is approximately 2.3%
- Dividend growth (+51.5%) significantly exceeded profit growth, reflecting management's confidence in the future

## 5. Valuation analysis: Is the current price lower than the value?

### 5.1 Relative valuation

Calculated based on the current stock price of approximately HK$22 and the total share capital of approximately 10.1 billion shares:

- **Market capitalization**: approximately HK$222.2 billion (approximately NT$205 billion)
- **PE (core net profit)**: approximately 14.2 times (2050/144.1)
- **PE (net profit attributable to parent company)**: approximately 12.2 times (2050/168.5)

The PE 5.9 times provided by users may be based on stock prices at different points in time or expected profits. There are also versions calculated based on Geely Holding's caliber (including more assets) in the market. But no matter which caliber, Geely's valuation is at a low level among global car companies:

| Automobile companies | PE (approximately) |
|------|----------|
| BYD | ~20-25 times |
| Tesla | ~50-80x |
| Toyota | ~10-12 times |
| Geely Automobile | ~6-14 times |
| Great Wall Motors | ~10-15 times |

### 5.2 Reasons for low valuation

Geely’s valuation has been low for a long time. Possible reasons include:

1. **Hong Kong stock discount**: The overall valuation of Hong Kong stocks is lower than that of A shares and US stocks
2. **Multi-brand complexity**: The market has a wait-and-see attitude towards the effect of the "One Geely" integration
3. **Profit quality concerns**: Increasing revenue without increasing profits has caused the market to discount future profitability.
4. **Pessimistic expectations for the industry**: The continuation of the price war has caused the market to give a lower valuation to the entire automobile sector.

### 5.3 Rough estimate of intrinsic value

**Scenario 1: Conservative estimate**
- Assuming core net profit increases by 15% to 16.6 billion yuan in 2026
-Gives 10x PE
- Reasonable market value of approximately 166 billion yuan (approximately HK$180 billion)
- The corresponding stock price is approximately HK$17.8 (currently there is a downside risk)

**Scenario 2: Neutral estimation**
- Assuming core net profit increases by 25% to 18 billion yuan in 2026
- Give 12 times PE (the integration effect is initially revealed)
- Reasonable market value of approximately NT$216 billion (approximately HK$234 billion)
- The corresponding stock price is approximately HK$23.2 (close to the current price)

**Scenario 3: Optimistic estimate**
- Assuming core net profit increases by 35% to 19.5 billion yuan in 2026
- Give 15 times PE (successful integration + overseas expansion)
- Reasonable market value of approximately NT$292.5 billion (approximately HK$316.8 billion)
- The corresponding stock price is approximately HK$31.4 (approximately 43% upside potential)

The average analyst consensus price target is HK$27.93, which is between neutral and optimistic scenarios.

### 5.4 Safety Margin Assessment

- The current stock price is HK$22, with almost no margin of safety in a neutral scenario
- ~43% upside under optimistic scenario
- About 19% downside risk under conservative scenario
- 68.2 billion yuan in cash provides certain "floor price" support

**Core conclusion:**

Geely Auto's financial situation can be summarized as "strong growth but earnings quality needs to be improved." Cash reserves of 68.2 billion yuan and sales volume of 3.02 million vehicles provide a solid safety cushion, but the core net profit margin of 4.2% and the almost zero growth rate of net profit attributable to the parent company expose the hidden danger of "volume for price".

From a valuation perspective, the current price is most likely not overvalued, but whether it is undervalued depends on two key assumptions:

1. Can the "One Geely" integration achieve profit margin improvement in 2026-2027?
2. Can overseas exports catch up from the current bottom position?

If both assumptions can be fulfilled, the current price has a good margin of safety; if integration fails to meet expectations or exports continue to lag, the current valuation may be "reasonable" rather than "cheap".
