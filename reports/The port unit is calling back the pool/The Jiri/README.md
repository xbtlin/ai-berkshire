## Core conclusion

**Comprehensive rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5) - Worth tracking, but needs to wait for integration verification**

Geely Automobile is currently one of the cheapest large car companies in Hong Kong stocks. In 2025, the annual sales volume will exceed 3.02 million vehicles (+39% year-on-year), the revenue will be 345.2 billion yuan (+25% year-on-year), the core net profit attributable to the parent company will be 14.4 billion yuan (+36% year-on-year), and the cash reserve will be 68.2 billion yuan (+46% year-on-year). Calculated based on the current stock price of about 22 Hong Kong dollars, the static PE is about 5-6 times and the ROE is about 17.7%, which is a typical "low valuation + high growth" combination.

**Core highlights:**
- The "One Geely" strategic integration is being implemented: the privatization of JiKrypton has been completed, the number of brands has been reduced from 6 to 4, and it is expected to save 1 billion yuan in R&D and billions of yuan in procurement.
- New energy transformation accelerates: New energy sales in 2025 will be 1.69 million vehicles (+90% year-on-year), accounting for more than 55%
- Galaxy brand exploded: 1.24 million units for the year (+150% year-on-year), exceeding the one million target
- Analysts are unanimously optimistic: 37 investment banks have given a buy rating, with an average target price of HK$27.93 (about 25% upside)

**Core Risks:**
- Hidden concerns about increasing revenue without increasing profits: Net profit attributable to the parent company only increased slightly by 0.2% to 16.85 billion yuan, which is lower than the growth rate of core net profit, indicating that there are fluctuations in non-recurring profits and losses
- The growth rate of overseas exports is at the bottom: 420,000 vehicles were exported throughout the year, a year-on-year increase of only 0.4%, far behind Chery (1.29 million vehicles) and BYD (1.04 million vehicles)
- The industry price war continues: competition is expected to become more intense in 2026, and profit margins will be squeezed
- Execution risks of multi-brand integration: The effect of integration still needs time to be verified

**Investment Judgment:** Geely Automobile has the conditions for "good price", but "good business" and "good management" still need to be observed after integrated operating data. It is recommended to closely track the three key indicators of gross profit margin, bicycle profit, and export growth in the first half of 2026.
