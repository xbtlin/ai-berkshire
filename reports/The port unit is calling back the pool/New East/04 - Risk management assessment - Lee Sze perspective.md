# New Oriental (9901.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: management quality, policy/system risks, long-term value creation capabilities, factors with Chinese characteristics

## 1. Management evaluation

### Yu Minhong: Entrepreneurship and Limitations

#### Core Advantages

1. **Resilience and Adaptability**: Yu Minhong is one of the most resilient entrepreneurs in China’s education and training industry. Under the "double reduction" policy, New Oriental's execution speed and decisiveness in refunding fees, layoffs, and transformation are second to none in the industry.

2. **Brand is personality**: Yu Minhong himself is the core asset of the New Oriental brand. Over the past 30 years, his personal image - from "godfather of overseas students" to "pioneer of live broadcast delivery" - has always been closely bound to the company.

3. **High public trust**: Among Chinese entrepreneurs, Yu Minhong's public image is relatively positive - his behavior of refunding tuition fees and relocating employees after the "double reduction" has won widespread respect.

#### Core Risks

1. **Excessive distraction**: Yu Minhong manages both the main business of education and live streaming e-commerce (although it has been divested), frequently expresses his views in public, publishes books, makes videos, and participates in events. Li Lu emphasized "focus", while Yu Minhong did not score high in this aspect.

2. **Successor issue**: Yu Minhong is nearly 64 years old. New Oriental's "post-Yu Minhong era" still lacks a clear succession path. The company's current strategic direction is highly dependent on Yu Minhong's personal judgment, and the institutionalized strategic decision-making mechanism is not yet complete.

3. **Dual sides of decision-making style**: Yu Minhong has both a quick-decision side (such as the transformation after "double reduction") and a hesitant side (such as Dongfang Selection's personnel management issues). In the anchor's resignation in April 2026, although his public apology was candid, it also exposed the shortcomings of his management capabilities.

### Management Team Depth

New Oriental's management team is among the best in the industry:
- The education business has a mature professional manager system
- Principals of local branch campuses have certain autonomy
- Relatively sufficient talent pool for middle management

But it is worth noting that executive mobility has increased after the "double reduction", and some core talents have flowed to AI education startups.

## 2. System and policy risk assessment

### Policy Risk: Unquantifiable "Gray Rhino"

Li Lu particularly emphasized "the importance of the institutional environment" when investing in Chinese companies. The policy risks faced by New Oriental are as follows:

#### The first level: Policy shocks that have occurred (digested)

- "Double reduction" in 2021: K-12 subject training is completely suspended
- Tighter review of teaching and training qualifications
- The capital market has tightened regulations on education stocks

#### Second level: Potential incremental policy risks (unquantifiable)

- Will quality education be included in the scope of supervision?
- Will the study abroad program be affected by changes in diplomatic relations?
- Will local governments increase their enforcement efforts on education and training institutions?
- Will the direction of "decapitalization" of education be further deepened?

#### The third layer: long-term institutional trends

- The Chinese government's positioning of education is that "public services" take precedence over "market goods"
- Under the narrative of common prosperity, educational equity is the core issue
- Population decline (birth rate continues to fall) will compress industry space from the demand side

### Difficulties in pricing policy risks

The core challenge of policy risk is that it is not a risk that can be quantified using a probability model, but a "binary" risk - either it happens and the impact is huge; or it doesn't happen and everything is calm.

**This is also the reason why the market gives New Oriental a lower PE** - not because of poor company quality, but because investors require additional risk compensation for "unquantifiable policy risks".

## 3. Capital market risks

### Structural discount of Chinese concept stocks

As a Chinese concept stock (listed on both US and Hong Kong stocks), New Oriental faces the following capital market-level risks:

1. **VIE Structural Risk**: Foreign investors hold shares through VIE agreements, and there is legal uncertainty.
2. **Delisting Risk**: Although the differences between China and the United States in audit supervision have eased, long-term uncertainty remains
3. **Liquidity discount**: The inflow and outflow of southbound trading funds may cause additional fluctuations
4. **Analyst coverage decreased**: Some foreign banks reduced their coverage of education stocks after the "double reduction"

### Exchange rate risk

New Oriental denominated its revenue in U.S. dollars, but most of its costs and revenue actually occurred within China (in yuan). Dollar fluctuations in reported revenue can distort non-operating profits.

## 5. Assessment of long-term value creation capabilities

### Li Lu’s review under the framework of “3.0 Civilization”

Li Lu emphasized investing in "high-quality Chinese enterprises in the process of modernization." Does New Oriental meet this standard?

**Front**:
- Education is the cornerstone of modernization, and high-quality educational services are always in demand
- New Oriental has unique advantages in the field of international education (connecting Chinese students with global educational resources)
- The company is laying out the intersection of AI + education

**Negative**:
- The education industry is regarded as a "quasi-public good" in China, with limited space for commercialization
- The company's growth ceiling is artificially lowered by policies
- Lack of clear path for global expansion

### Value judgment in the 10-year dimension

The most likely state of New Oriental in 10 years:
- **Best Scenario** (20% probability): Policies are relaxed, AI education layout is successful, it becomes the leader in education technology, and its market value doubles
- **Baseline Scenario** (50% probability): maintain current position, annualized revenue growth of 5-8%, steady profit growth, and slight upward market value
- **Worst scenario** (30% probability): Policies are tightened again or AI disruption accelerates, core business shrinks, and market value is cut in half

## 7. Core Conclusions

Starting from Li Lu’s investment philosophy, the core contradiction of New Oriental is:

**The Good**: This is a business that has survived extreme stress tests and remains profitable. The management has shown extraordinary adaptability, and the brand assets have relatively increased in value after the industry was cleared.

**The Difficult Aspect**: The core risk (policy) is completely outside management's control and cannot be hedged through diversification. Li Lu's typical investment targets - such as BYD - are companies with "policy tailwinds", while New Oriental is a company with "policy headwinds".

**Final judgment**: New Oriental is not in line with Li Lu's investment philosophy of "looking for value in certainty". The existence of policy risks makes it difficult to build a high-conviction investment thesis even if valuations look cheap. It can be considered as a small position for diversified allocation, but it is not suitable as a core position.
