#New Oriental (9901.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital return, free cash flow, safety margin

## 1. Profitability analysis

### Revenue growth trends

| Fiscal year | Revenue (100 million US dollars) | Year-on-year growth rate |
|------|---------------|----------|
| FY2023 | 35.6 | +30%+ (restore base after double reduction) |
| FY2024 | 43.2 | +21.5% |
| FY2025 | 49.0 | +13.6% |
| FY2026E | 51.5-53.9 | +5%-10% |

The growth rate has slowed down significantly - from 21.5% in FY2024 to 5-10% in FY2026E. This is in line with the natural decline of recovery growth after "double reduction", but it also means that valuations should not give a premium to high growth.

### Profit Quality

FY2025 Q4 net profit attributable to parent companies was US$7.1 million, a significant year-on-year decrease of 73.7%, mainly due to the impairment of goodwill of US$60.3 million. Excluding one-time factors, operating profit margins remained relatively stable.

**Front**:
- Education business pre-charge model brings high-quality cash flow
- New education business grew by 35% but has not yet dragged down overall profit margins
- The company's management guides profit growth of more than 10% in fiscal 2026

**Negative**:
- Goodwill impairment implies that the return on previous acquisitions/investments is not as expected
- Oriental Selection’s GMV continues to decline. Although it has been divested, it still has short-term impact.
- FY2026 Q1 growth rate is only 2-5%, a weak start

## 2. Capital return analysis

### ROE level

As an asset-light education services company, New Oriental should theoretically have a higher ROE. However, actual performance fluctuates greatly due to the following factors:

1. **"Double reduction" impact**: A large number of asset impairments occurred in 2021-2022, and ROE once turned negative
2. **Investment during the transformation period**: New business expansion puts pressure on profit margins
3. **Substantial Cash Reserves**: As of FY2025, there is ample cash on hand, which lowers ROE.

It is estimated that the current ROE is between 10-15%, which is lower than the ideal level of the asset-light service industry (20%+).

### Capital allocation

Buffett values management's capital allocation capabilities most. New Oriental's capital allocation record is as follows:

**reasonableness**:
- Maintain sufficient cash to cope with policy risks
- Continued stock buybacks (announced buyback plans multiple times)
- Oriental Selection recovered 1.5 billion yuan in cash after divestiture

**To be discussed**:
- Too many business expansion directions and scattered capital
- Goodwill impairment caused by certain investments/acquisitions
- No systematic capital allocation framework in sight

## 3. Free cash flow analysis

The cash flow characteristics of the education service industry are naturally high-quality:
- **Tuition Fees Collected in Advance**: Collect money first and then deliver services, operating capital is negative
- **Asset-light model**: Mainly leasing teaching venues, capital expenditures are relatively controllable
- **Low Inventory**: No physical inventory pressure

FY2025 operating cash flow is estimated to be approximately US$600-800 million, and free cash flow after deducting capital expenditures is approximately US$400-600 million. Based on the current market value of approximately US$8 billion, FCF Yield is approximately 5-7.5%, which is at a reasonable level.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation

| Indicators | Values | Evaluation |
|------|------|------|
| PE (TTM, US stocks) | ~20x | Medium |
| PE (Hong Kong stock standard) | ~13 times | Low |
| PS (TTM) | ~1.6x | Reasonably low |
| FCF Yield | ~5-7.5% | Attractive |

The difference in PE between Hong Kong stocks and US stocks mainly comes from the calculation caliber (Hong Kong stocks may use expected profits) and liquidity discount.

### Historical valuation comparison

- Before "double reduction" in 2021: PE 30-40 times
- The lowest point in 2022: PE not applicable (loss)
- Recovery period in 2023: PE 20-25 times
- Current: PE 13-20 times

The current valuation is in the mid-to-low range after the "double reduction".

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Conservative scenario assumed** (FY2026 net profit of US$300 million, no growth):
- 10 times PE = market value of US$3 billion (currently 8 billion, with about 60% downside)
- This scenario is too pessimistic and requires another substantial tightening of policy

**Assuming neutral scenario** (FY2026 net profit of US$400 million, 5% annual growth):
- 15 times PE = US$6 billion (approximately 25% off current price)
- The margin of safety is not sufficient

**Assuming an optimistic scenario** (FY2026 net profit is US$450 million, 10% annual growth):
- 20 times PE = US$9 billion (approximately 12% upside from current level)

## 5. Key issues in Buffett’s framework

### 1. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?

Partially available. The brand advantage is solid in the field of overseas study and training, but it has not yet formed an insurmountable barrier in new business areas (quality education, STEAM).

### 2. Is management honest and competent?

Yu Minhong is more than honest, but lacks strategic focus. Multiple public reflections and apologies reflect candor, but they also reveal management hesitation and trial and error.

### 3. Will this company be stronger or weaker in ten years?

This is the hardest question to answer. The policy environment of China’s education market remains the biggest variable. If the policy remains stable, New Oriental's brand and scale will be enough to support its continued leadership; but if the policy changes again, the company may once again face a test of survival.

### 4. Does the current price provide an adequate margin of safety?

The safety margin corresponding to 13 times PE of Hong Kong stocks is barely acceptable, but considering the "unquantifiable" characteristics of policy risks, Buffett's framework may require a larger safety margin (less than 10 times) before he is willing to take action.

## 7. Core Conclusions

New Oriental's financial situation has returned to a relatively healthy state after the "double reduction". The cash flow advantage brought by the prepaid model gives it a natural financial safety cushion. However, according to Buffett's standards, the following points have not yet reached the threshold of "significant undervaluation":

1. **Slowing growth**: 5-10% growth does not support high valuation multiples
2. **ROE is low**: A large amount of idle cash reduces capital efficiency.
3. **Policy risk cannot be priced**: This is the biggest "unknowable" factor in Buffett's framework
4. **Safety margin is not thick enough**: 13 times PE is not cheap after taking into account policy discounts

**Qualitative conclusion**: Unless there is a further decline below 10 times PE, or there is a clear positive signal in the policy environment, from the perspective of Buffett's pure value investment, New Oriental is currently in a position where "you can pay attention but don't have to rush to act".
