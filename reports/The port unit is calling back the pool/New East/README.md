## Company Profile

New Oriental Education Technology Group is China's largest private education service provider and was founded by Yu Minhong in 1993. After the "double reduction" policy in 2021, the company has undergone a major business transformation, divesting K-12 subject training business and turning to areas such as quality education, study abroad examinations, adult education, and live e-commerce (Oriental Selection).

## Core business structure (fiscal year 2025-2026)

1. **Education Business** (Core): Study abroad exams, quality education, intelligent learning systems
2. **Oriental Selection** (stripped off): Live broadcast e-commerce platform, GMV in the first half of fiscal year 2026: 4.1 billion yuan
3. **New education business**: 35% year-on-year growth, including study tours, STEAM, etc.
