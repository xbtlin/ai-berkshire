## One sentence summary

New Oriental is a high-quality company that has experienced extreme stress tests in policy-restrictive industries. It still has its brand and scale advantages, but the unquantifiable nature of policy risks makes it difficult for investors to establish a high-confidence investment thesis.

## Core investment arguments

### Reasons to be bullish

1. **The worst moment of the policy has passed**: The "double reduction" in 2021 is the darkest moment for the industry, and the surviving New Oriental has passed the extreme stress test
2. **Valuation is reasonably low**: 13 times PE of Hong Kong stocks corresponds to FCF Yield of 5-7.5%, which has a certain margin of safety
3. **Brand moat is effective**: The brand recognition and market share advantages in the field of study abroad and education are unshakable in the short term.
4. **Industry clearing bonus**: After a large number of competitors have withdrawn, the competitive environment for survivors is actually improving.
5. **Naturally high-quality cash flow**: The pre-charge model ensures strong operating cash flow

### Reasons to be bearish

1. **Policy risks cannot be quantified**: The long-term direction of "de-capitalization" of the education industry has not changed, and new restrictive policies may be introduced at any time.
2. **The growth ceiling has been lowered**: 5-10% growth is not worth the premium, and the core market (K-12) has been blocked
3. **Demographic structural headwinds**: China’s birth population continues to decline, and the demand for education market is shrinking
4. **AI Subversion Threat**: In the long term, AI may change the way educational services are delivered and weaken the value of offline institutions.
5. **Successor Risk**: Yu Minhong is 64 years old, his successor is unclear, and the company is overly dependent on the founder.

## Investment advice

### Conclusion: Observe, no action for now

**Reason**:
- The company's quality is above average, but the industry faces significant difficulties
- Valuations have a certain margin of safety, but compensation for policy risks may not be enough
- The expected rate of return is too low and the risk-benefit ratio is not attractive enough
- Lack of clear catalyst

### Trigger conditions (when to consider buying)

1. **The stock price fell below HK$30** (corresponding to about 10 times PE), the safety margin is large enough
2. **The policy has clearly turned to the positive**: For example, if the regulatory restrictions on quality education are relaxed
3. **AI education layout has made breakthrough progress**: forming a new growth curve
4. **Yu Minhong clarifies the succession plan**: Solve the "key person" risk

### Risk warning

- This report is based on public information and policy risks are inherently unpredictable
- New Oriental's valuation model highly relies on assumptions about the policy environment
- Long-term trends in the education industry (AI + population decline) may invalidate current valuation methods
