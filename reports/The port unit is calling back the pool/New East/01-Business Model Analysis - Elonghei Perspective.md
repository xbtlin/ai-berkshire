#New Oriental (9901.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: user value, business nature, moat depth, management level of "duty"

## 1. Business nature: What is the "user value" of this business?

### The essence of education business

The essence of New Oriental's core education business is to "help students improve their competitiveness." Whether it is study abroad exams, quality education or adult education, what users buy is not the course itself, but "better future expectations". This value proposition has naturally strong pricing power—parents’ willingness to pay for their children’s education is extremely high and price sensitivity is low.

Examined from the perspective of Duan Yongping's "doing the right thing": Do educational services create real user value? The answer is yes. The brand trust, teaching system and teacher reserve accumulated by New Oriental over the past 30 years are indeed helping millions of students achieve their academic goals.

### The essence of live broadcast e-commerce

The essence of Dongfang Selection's business model is "knowledge-based live streaming", which uses teachers' expressive skills and personal attractiveness to sell products. However, this has limited synergy with the education business and is more like an attempt at "capability migration".

**Key question**: Oriental Selection’s GMV in the first half of fiscal year 2026 has continued to decline from 570 million yuan (same period in 2024) to 480 million yuan (same period in 2025) to 410 million yuan. The loss of core anchors (Dong Yuhui left and the four major anchors resigned) exposed the fragility of the people-dependent business model.

## 2. Moat analysis

### Brand moat ★★★★☆

The New Oriental brand has 30 years of experience in the education industry. The three words "New Oriental" have a clear quality perception in the minds of Chinese parents. This kind of brand equity is difficult to be copied by new entrants in the short term.

**Front**:
- Ranked No. 1 in brand recognition in the field of study abroad exams and training
- Quality education brand migration has a higher success rate
- It can still survive after "double reduction", which proves the brand's resilience.

**Backside**:
- Risk of brand aging, Generation Z’s loyalty to traditional education brands decreases
- After the "double reduction", the value of the brand in the K-12 field has shrunk significantly.

### Scale effect ★★★☆☆

New Oriental has a large network of teaching centers across the country, and its scale effect is reflected in:
- Teacher training system can be reused
- Diminishing marginal cost of teaching and research content
- Customer acquisition cost is lower than the industry average due to brand effect

However, the localized nature of educational services limits the boundaries of scale effects—each city requires localized teachers and operations teams.

### Switching Cost ★★★☆☆

Switching costs for educational services are moderate. Once students adapt to the teacher's style and teaching system, they will not change easily in the short term. But this switching cost is based on teachers’ individual abilities rather than a systemic lock-in mechanism.

### Network Effect ★★☆☆☆

There are almost no network effects in the education business. Live streaming e-commerce has a certain platform effect, but Dongfang Selection, as a participant in the Douyin ecosystem (rather than the platform itself), does not have an independent network effect.

## 3. "Simplicity" assessment of business model

Duan Yongping emphasized "doing simple things". The complexity of New Oriental’s current business model is increasing:

**The easy part**:
- The core education business (study abroad examination and training) has clear business logic: enrollment → teaching → word of mouth → re-enrollment
- Healthy cash flow model: collect tuition fees in advance, collect money first and then provide services

**The complicated part**:
- Business lines are too scattered: quality education, study tours, STEAM, intelligent learning systems, international Chinese education... too many directions
- Although the divestiture of Oriental Selection reduced management complexity, it also lost a high-growth narrative
- Uncertainty in the policy environment greatly reduces the "certainty" of strategic planning

## 4. Assessment of the degree of "duty"

### Is management doing the "right thing"?

**Positive Signs**:
- Yu Minhong decisively laid off employees, refunded fees, and transformed after the "double cuts", demonstrating the entrepreneur's resilience and responsibility
- Focus on core education business in fiscal year 2026 and cut off non-core directions to reflect strategic focus
- The new education business grew by 35% year-on-year, indicating that the transformation is in the right direction

**Negative Signs**:
- Eastern Selection’s personnel turmoil exposed management’s lack of governance capabilities in new business areas
- Yu Minhong's personal energy is divided: he has to manage education and e-commerce, and he also frequently speaks publicly.
- Revenue growth guidance for fiscal year 2026 is only 5%-10%, which is much lower than the previous two years, indicating that the dividends of transformation are diminishing

### Is corporate culture "duty"?

New Oriental's corporate culture is centered on Yu Minhong's personal charm, which is both an advantage and a risk. Yu Minhong's "chicken soup culture" is effective in motivating employees and students, but it may also lead to the organization's over-reliance on individuals and the lack of an institutionalized management system.

## 6. Core Conclusions

New Oriental's business model has undergone a "destructive reconstruction" after the "double reduction". The user value of the core education business still exists, and the brand moat is still effective, but the following structural issues deserve attention:

1. **Growth ceiling**: Policies have restricted the largest K-12 market, and the remaining business (study abroad, quality education) market size is limited
2. **People dependence**: Whether it is education business or live broadcast e-commerce, they are highly dependent on "people" (famous teachers/anchors) rather than "systems"
3. **Insufficient strategic certainty**: Yu Minhong is still exploring what New Oriental in the 3.0 era looks like.

Judging from Duan Yongping's "certainty" and "simplicity" standards, New Oriental is currently not a "clearly visible" business, but if the price is cheap enough, the safety margin of brand equity is still worth considering.
