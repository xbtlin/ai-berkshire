# New Oriental (9901.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry ecological niche, competitive dynamics, reverse thinking, multidisciplinary model

## 1. Industry panorama: Structural changes in the education service industry

### Reshaping the industry ecology after "double reduction"

The "double reduction" policy in 2021 has completely changed the landscape of China's education and training industry. The policy is essentially the government's re-intervention in the allocation of educational resources - shifting K-12 subject training from market-driven back to the logic of public service.

**Munger's "reverse thinking" reminds us**: To understand New Oriental's competitive position, we should not just look at "what it can do", but also think about "what will destroy it".

Policy risk is the number one killer. But think about it from another perspective: If the policy has released the biggest negative impact in 2021, then "the worst may have passed" - at least in the field of K-12 subject training, the industry has been "cleared" by the policy, and the new policy risks faced by survivors are theoretically decreasing.

### Current market landscape

The education service market after the "double reduction" can be divided into several sub-tracks:

| Track | Market size | Competition intensity | New Oriental’s status |
|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Study Abroad Entrance Examination | ~30 billion yuan | Middle | First |
| Quality education | ~100 billion yuan | High | At the forefront but not absolutely leading |
| Adult education | ~50 billion yuan | High | One of the participants |
| International Education | ~20 billion yuan | Mid-low | Leading |
| Live e-commerce | Trillion level | Extremely high | Small and medium participants |

## 2. Sources and attenuation of competitive advantages

### Time value of brand equity

Munger often said, "A great brand is the friend of time." The time accumulation of the New Oriental brand is indeed considerable - with 30 years of deep experience in the education industry, "New Oriental" is almost equivalent to "education and training" itself in the minds of Chinese parents.

But Munger also emphasized that "brands will also depreciate over time":
- Brand loyalty is declining among Generation Z - young parents are more inclined to new forms such as online education and AI education
- After the "double reduction", the brand's application scenarios in the K-12 field were forcibly cut off by the policy
- Oriental Selection’s personnel dispute may cause indirect damage to the brand image

### Relativity of scale advantage

New Oriental's scale is unparalleled in the education industry: a nationwide network of teaching centers, tens of thousands of teachers, and hundreds of thousands of students. However, the advantages of scale are not as significant in the education industry as in the consumer goods industry:

- The degree of standardization of educational services is low, making it difficult to dilute costs through scale like consumer goods.
- The demand for localized operations is high, and profit growth cannot be simply linearly derived from scale growth.
- The quality of teachers is difficult to guarantee through scale - famous teachers are a scarce resource

### Competitive threats with AI education

This is the competitive variable worth watching over the long term. AI is reshaping the way education is delivered:
- Large models can provide personalized one-on-one tutoring
- Marginal cost approaches zero
- No geographical or time restrictions

**But think about the opposite**:
- The "human" factors in education—inspiration, companionship, and atmosphere—cannot be completely replaced by AI for the time being.
- Offline education scenarios are irreplaceable (especially for younger students)
- New Oriental is also deploying intelligent learning systems and may become a participant in AI education rather than a disruptor

### Direct competitors

### New Oriental's "ecological niche"

Use Munger's "ecological niche" concept to understand: New Oriental occupies the ecological niche of "high trust + offline services + comprehensive education". Features of this location are:
- High barriers to entry (triple barriers of brand + network + teachers)
- Limited growth space (policy capping + market saturation)
- The threat of substitutes is gradually rising (AI education)

## 4. Multidisciplinary model analysis

### Psychological Model: Anchoring Effect and Expectation Management

The market may have an "anchoring bias" towards New Oriental - investors may anchor its valuation at 30-40 times PE before the "double reduction", believing that the current PE of 13 times is "cheap". But the right approach is to break away from historical anchors and reassess intrinsic value based on current business quality and growth prospects.

### Biological Model: Survival of the Fittest

"Double reduction" is essentially a drastic change in the environment (similar to the "Ice Age"). The reason why New Oriental can survive is because:
1. Be large enough and have sufficient cash reserves (thick fat layer)
2. Business diversification provides alternative survival strategies (adaptive variation)
3. Yu Minhong’s quick decision-making ability (reaction speed)

But survival does not equal prosperity. Postglacial species may never return to their previous size and vigor.

### Economic model: beneficiaries of supply-side clearing

The "double reduction" policy eliminated a large number of small and medium-sized education and training institutions, and industry concentration increased passively. As a surviving leading company, New Oriental can theoretically enjoy the "industry clearing dividend" - but the upper limit of this dividend is subject to the policy's suppression of the total industry volume.

## 6. Munger’s conclusion

### "If I can only summarize it in one sentence"

New Oriental is a company with a "relative advantageous position" in a "policy-restricted industry" - it is better than its peers, but the industry itself is declining.

### Key judgments

1. Is the ** industry worth participating in? ** Barely. Rigid demand for education exists, but supply is controlled by policies and profit margins are under pressure.
2. **Is the competitive advantage sustainable? ** Sustainable in the medium term (3-5 years), but faces the threat of disruption from AI education in the long term.
3. **Does current valuation reflect industry distress? ** Partially reflected. 13 times PE includes a certain policy discount, but it may not be enough if the industry deteriorates further.

### What would Munger do?

Munger might say: "I would rather buy a good company in a good industry at a reasonable price than buy a good company in a difficult industry at a cheap price." New Oriental belongs to the latter - a good company, but the industry is difficult. Unless the price falls to the point of "picking up cigarette butts", it may not be in line with Munger's investment philosophy.
