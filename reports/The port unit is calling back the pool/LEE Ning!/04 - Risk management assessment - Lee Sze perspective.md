# Li Ning (2331.HK) risk management assessment

> Analysis dimensions: management quality, incentive mechanism, institutional risks, safety margin
> Core question: Is this company’s management trustworthy? What’s the worst case scenario?

---

## 1. Management evaluation

### Dual-headed structure: Li Ning himself + Qian Wei

**Li Ning (Founder/Executive Chairman)**
- Olympic gymnastics champion, founded the brand in 1990
- The soul of the brand and the biggest IP
- In recent years, more and more people have retreated behind the scenes and left operations to professional managers.
- but still the final decision-maker for the brand

**Qian Wei (Co-CEO)**
- Joined in 2019, coming from Uniqlo system (23 years of experience)
- Leading the transformation of DTC: from wholesale thinking to retail thinking
- Revenue increased from 13.8 billion to 29.6 billion during his tenure
- However, the proportion of revenue from direct sales channels has declined instead of rising, and the transformation effect is questionable.

### Dimensions of management evaluation

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Brand Protection | ★★★★☆ | Li Ning himself has emotional investment and long-term commitment to the brand |
| Operational efficiency | ★★★☆☆ | Qian Wei has improved the operating level but the growth has not outperformed the industry |
| Strategic Vision | ★★★☆☆ | DTC has the right direction but lacks second-curve thinking |
| Capital allocation | ★★★☆☆ | 20 billion cash idle, not active enough |
| Execution | ★★★☆☆ | DTC transformation has been promoted for 4 years, but direct sales have declined |

### The core contradiction of management

1. **Li Ning's personal feelings vs. capital efficiency**: Li Ning regards the brand as its "second life". This kind of sentiment ensures that the brand will not be ruined, but it may also lead to conservative decisions (unwilling to take risks).

2. **Qian Wei’s retail experience vs brand growth**: Qian Wei is good at optimizing operational efficiency (improving inventory management to the optimal level), but there does not seem to be a particularly effective answer at the brand strategy level (how to make the brand popular again).

3. **Single-brand persistence vs. multi-brand expansion**: The management insisted on a single-brand strategy and still did not follow up after Anta successfully proved the multi-brand model. This can be understood as "focus" or "conservatism".

### Incentives and benefits binding

- The Li Ning family holds approximately 10% of the company's shares (major shareholder status)
- The management team has option incentives
- The interests are basically the same, but the management has no strong urge to become bigger and stronger.

## 2. Institutional risk analysis

### Consumption environment risk ★★★☆ (medium)

1. **Consumption downgrade**: If the economic downturn causes consumers to pay more attention to cost performance, Li Ning's positioning of 300-800 yuan may be squeezed - the top is the discounted Nike Adidas, and the bottom is the cheaper Anta Xtep.
2. **The national trend is fading**: The national trend from 2018 to 2021 promoted Li Ning’s excess growth, but this wave of dividends has faded.

### Inventory cycle risk ★★☆☆ (lower)

- The current inventory-to-sales ratio is 3.6 months, at the healthiest level in history
- 0-6 months inventory accounts for 80%, and the inventory quality is good
- However, if the industry as a whole enters a destocking cycle, it may still face pressure to clear inventory through discounts

### Risk of channel change ★★★☆ (medium)

- The rise of new channels such as e-commerce live streaming has weakened the value of the traditional dealer system
- Short video platforms such as Douyin/Kuaishou have changed the rules of brand competition
- Li Ning’s layout in new channels needs to be continuously tracked

### Brand reputation risk ★★☆☆ (lower)

- Li Ning has a positive brand image and no major reputational risks
- But there is a risk of brand "aging" - if it cannot continue to attract young consumers

## 3. Li Lu type risk framework

### "What's the worst case scenario?"

**Scenario 1: Growth stagnates completely**
- Revenue stopped at 30 billion and stopped growing
- Profit maintained at 2.8-3 billion/year
- Result: Become a pure dividend stock
- Impact: PE may drop to 12-13 times, but supported by dividends and cash
- **Probability: 30%**

**Scenario 2: Market share eroded**
- Anta goes up (main brand upgrade), Nike goes down (counterattack in Greater China)
- Li Ning's market share dropped from 9.4% to 7-8%
- Result: Revenue may fall by 10-15%
- Impact: Profit decline, but not loss
- **Probability: 15%**

**Scenario 3: The consumption environment deteriorates**
- Economic recession led to a sharp decline in sportswear consumption
- The overall industry decline is 15-20%
- Result: Li Ning's revenue and profit fell simultaneously
- Impact: short-term pain but not fatal, with 20 billion in cash to cover the bottom line
- **Probability: 10%**

**Scenario 4: Major mistake by management**
- Blind internationalization or failed acquisitions consume a lot of cash
- Or serious improper brand strategy leads to loss of consumers
- Impact: Possible loss of 5-10 billion in cash
- **Probability: 5%**

### "What are the chances of surviving?"

**5-year survival probability: 99%+**
**Probability of maintaining profitability for 5 years: 95%+**
**Probability of maintaining current market share in 5 years: 75%**

Li Ning has almost no survival risk. Net cash of 19.97 billion means it won't go bankrupt even if it doesn't make money for a few years. This is a "safe but potentially mediocre" investment.

### Downstream protection

| Protective factors | Strength |
|---------|------|
| Net cash 19.97 billion | ★★★★★ |
| Brand value (decades of accumulation) | ★★★★☆ |
| Stable cash flow | ★★★★★ |

**Conclusion: The downside risk is extremely limited, which is Li Ning’s biggest investment advantage. **

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Conservative valuation (zero growth assumption):**
- Sustainable FCF 3.3 billion/year, discount rate 8% → operating value 41.3 billion
- Add net cash of HK$20 billion → total value of HK$61.3 billion
- Relative to current market value (about 50 billion) → safety margin of about 20%

**Neutral valuation (5% growth assumption):**
- FCF will grow at a rate of 5% in the next five years
- Sustainability after 5 years, discount rate 8%, terminal growth 2%
- Operating value of approximately HK$55 billion + net cash of HK$20 billion → Total value of HK$75 billion
- Relative to current market value → safety margin of about 50%

**Pessimistic Valuation (Negative Growth Assumption):**
- FCF decreased by 3% year-on-year
- Operating value of approximately HK$35 billion + net cash of HK$20 billion → Total value of HK$55 billion
- Relative to current market value → safety margin of about 10%

### Safety margin judgment

| Scenario | Valuation | Margin of Safety |
|------|------|---------|
| Conservative | 61.3 billion | +23% |
| Neutral | 75 billion | +50% |
| Pessimistic | 55 billion | +10% |

**Conclusion: Even under pessimistic assumptions, Li Ning's current price has a certain margin of safety. This is an asymmetric investment opportunity of "lose less and win more". **

## 6. Perspective of Civilization Evolution

### The role of sports brands in consumer society

As per capita income increases and health awareness increases, the proportion of sports consumption in overall consumption will continue to increase. This is a **long-term structural trend** that is not dependent on the economic performance of a single year.

The Li Ning brand has the unique label of "Chinese elements" and plays a certain role in the grand narrative of "the rise of Chinese brands". However, from the perspective of civilization evolution, the competition among sports brands is more of a matter of incremental allocation rather than a stock game - the industry pie is getting bigger, and even if the share declines slightly, the absolute scale can still be maintained.
