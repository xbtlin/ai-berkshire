# Li Ning (2331.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, competition pattern, multiple thinking models, reverse thinking
> Core question: What is the final outcome of competition in the sportswear industry? What position can Li Ning occupy in the final game?

---

## 1. Nature of the industry: branded consumer goods

### Use multiple thinking models to understand the sportswear industry

**Psychological model**: Sports brands are essentially operating "identity". What consumers buy is not just a pair of shoes, but a label of "what kind of person I am." Nike represents the sports spirit of "Just Do It", Anta represents "pragmatism", and what does Li Ning represent? "National trend"? "Professional sports"? Ambiguity in positioning is a big no-no for brands.

**Economic Model**: Sportswear is an industry with **differentiated competition**, not pure price competition. Consumers' willingness to pay a brand premium depends on brand power - this creates high gross margins (40-60%), but also means that when brand power declines, consumers will quickly switch to other brands.

**Biological Model**: Competition in the sports brand industry is similar to "niche differentiation" - each brand occupies a specific consumer mental space. Competition is most intense when the ecological niches of two brands overlap. The problem with Li Ning is that its ecological niche partially overlaps with Anta and Nike, but it is not the strongest in every overlapping area.

### Industry scale and growth

- China's sporting goods market size is approximately 500 billion yuan
- The annual growth rate is about 5-8% (driven by increased sports participation rate + consumption upgrade)
- Subcategories such as running, outdoor, and yoga are growing faster
- There is still room for 2-3 times improvement in penetration rate compared to Europe and the United States

## 2. Competitive landscape: three superpowers and many strong players

### Anta: Li Ning’s biggest rival

The comparison between Anta and Li-Ning is the key to understanding Li-Ning’s competitive position:

| Dimension | Anta | Li Ning |
|------|------|------|
| Strategy | Multi-brand matrix (ANTA/FILA/Arc'teryx/Descente) | Single brand |
| Revenue | 60 billion+ (2 times that of Li Ning) | 29.6 billion |
| Growth rate | 10%+ | 3% |
| ROE | 25%+ | 12% |
| Sinking | Extremely strong (Anta’s main brand) | Weak |
| High-end | Extremely strong (FILA/Archa'teryx) | Limited |
| Moat | Brand portfolio + channels + operational efficiency | Brand awareness + running product strength |

**Cruel reality**: Anta has built a brand matrix covering all price ranges through "buy, buy, buy". Li Ning's single-brand strategy is naturally limited by the growth ceiling.

### Nike: The ceiling of brand power

Nike still ranks first in China with a market share of 16.2%. Although it has declined in recent years (national trend substitution effect), the accumulation of brand power is far from catching up overnight.

## 3. Li Ning’s competitive position

### Ecological niche analysis

Position occupied by Li Ning:
- **Price range**: 300-800 yuan (below Nike, above Anta main brand)
- **Brand tone**: "higher" than Anta but "down to earth" than Nike
- **Core categories**: running (professional reputation), basketball (Wade series), training
- **Differentiated tags**: National trend (but the fever has subsided), Chinese elements, founder IP

### "Is the moat getting wider or narrower?"

**Narrower signals for more:**
- Market share decline from peak (2021 high)
- Growth rate is much lower than Anta
- Lack of new growth stories after the Guochao dividend fades
- Direct operation transformation is not effective
- Anta squeezes continuously at the upper and lower ends

**Factors that may widen:**
- Running shoe product strength continues to improve (26 million pairs sold)
- Gross profit margin remains stable at 49%, without being forced to cut prices
- Inventory management optimized to the best in history
- The 2026 Olympic cycle may bring brand exposure

## 4. Industry driving forces and trends

### Favorable trends

1. **Sports participation rate increases**: The running and fitness population continues to grow
2. **Consumption upgrade**: From "whatever you can wear" to "what you need to wear well"
3. **Domestic Substitution**: Young people’s acceptance of domestic brands continues to increase
4. **Category Expansion**: New categories such as outdoor, yoga, and skiing are growing rapidly

### Adverse Trend

1. **Growth slows down**: The industry enters a stable period from a period of high growth
2. **Hidden dangers of price war**: Brands may be forced to discount during the inventory cycle
3. **Channel Change**: The rise of e-commerce platforms has changed the rules of competition
4. **International brands fight back**: Nike/Adidas refocus on the Chinese market

## 5. Reverse thinking: Under what circumstances will Li Ning significantly outperform?

### Scenario 1: The 2026 Olympic cycle brings brand revival

The Paris Olympics/2028 Los Angeles Olympics provide Li Ning with a top brand exposure platform. If a hot product is launched (similar to the New York Fashion Week event in 2018), it may reignite consumer enthusiasm.

**Probability: Medium**. Sports competitions can indeed drive sports consumption, but the effect depends on the accuracy of brand marketing.

### Scenario 2: Anta encounters difficulties

If there are problems with Anta's multi-brand integration (such as FILA's growth peaking and Arc'teryx's Chinese market encountering obstacles), competitive pressure will be reduced.

**Probability: Low**. Anta's operational efficiency has always been strong.

### Scenario 3: International breakthrough

If Li Ning opens overseas markets (Southeast Asia, the Middle East, etc.), it may open up a new growth curve.

**Probability: Low**. Li Ning's many attempts at internationalization in the past were not very successful.

### Scenario 4: Management increases shareholder returns

If Li Ning uses 10 billion of its 20 billion net cash for repurchases/special dividends, the market may revalue it.

**Probability: Medium**. Management's willingness to allocate capital is a key variable.

## 6. Mangg's "list"

### Core questions that need to be answered when investing in Li Ning:

1. **Is brand power increasing or weakening? ** → At present, it seems to be stable and slightly weakened (market share has dropped slightly, and the national trend has subsided)
2. **Can the second growth curve be found? ** → Not visible at the moment. Internationalization and multiple brands are long-term explorations
3. **Will management change its capital allocation strategy? ** → Unknown, but this is the key to improving return on investment
4. Will there be disruptive changes in the ** industry? ** → Unlikely. Sportswear is a stable industry

### Possible comments from Munger

"Li Ning is a company that can be held but not necessarily bought. It will not make you lose a lot of money - the brand is valuable, the finances are healthy, and the valuation is reasonable. But it is also unlikely to give you excess returns - growth is slow, competitive position is not strong, and management is not aggressive enough. If you already hold it, you can continue to hold it and receive dividends; if you want to build a new position, you need a cheaper price or a clearer growth story."
