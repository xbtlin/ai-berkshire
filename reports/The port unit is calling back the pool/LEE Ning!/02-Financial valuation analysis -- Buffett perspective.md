## 2. In-depth analysis of profitability

### ROE analysis

Li Ning's ROE is about 12%, and the breakdown is as follows:
- Net profit margin: 9.9%
- Asset turnover rate: approximately 0.7 times
- Leverage multiple: approximately 1.7 times

**The ROE of 12% is mid-range in the consumer goods industry**. Comparison:
| Company | ROE | Description |
|------|-----|------|
| ANTA Sports | 25%+ | Multi-brand efficient operation |
| Li Ning | 12% | Single brand efficiency is average |
| Xtep International | 14% | Main brand + Saucony |
| Nike | 35%+ | Global Brand Premium |

**Reasons for Li Ning’s low ROE**:
1. Nearly 20 billion in net cash is "idle" on the balance sheet, which lowers ROE.
2. If excess cash is excluded, "operating ROE" may be close to 18-20%
3. The asset turnover rate is low, indicating that there is room for improvement in channel efficiency.

### Profitability Trends

| Year | Gross profit margin | Net profit margin |
|------|--------|--------|
| 2021 | 53.0% | 14.3% (peak) |
| 2022 | 48.4% | 10.6% |
| 2023 | 48.4% | 10.0% |

2021 is the peak of Li Ning's profit margin (national boom + consumption recovery), and then fell back and stabilized around 49%/10%. The stable gross profit margin shows that product pricing power has not been eroded, and the decrease in net profit margin is mainly due to the increase in expenses.

### Revenue Quality Analysis

- **Inventory health**: The inventory-to-sales ratio is 3.6 months, and inventory in 0-6 months accounts for 80%, which is the best level in history.
- **Accounts receivable**: There are less receivables under the dealer model, and direct sales and e-commerce mainly focus on immediate collection.
- **Operating cash flow/net profit = 1.65 times**: far more than 1 times, indicating that the profits are "real money"

## 3. Balance sheet quality

### Core Strengths: Extremely Healthy Balance Sheet

- **Net cash of 19.97 billion yuan**: It can be said to be "lying on a golden mountain"
- **Extremely low interest-bearing debt**: Financial leverage is conservative
- **Reasonable inventory**: No risk of overstocking
- **Moderate fixed assets**: light asset operating model

### Buffett-style thinking: Is net cash an asset or a waste?

19.97 billion net cash ÷ market capitalization (approximately HK$50 billion) ≈ 40%

This means: you buy Li Ning, which has a market value of HK$50 billion, of which 20 billion is cash. **The actual valuation of the operating assets purchased is only 30 billion. **

But the question is: Why isn’t this cash returned to shareholders?
- If used for buyback → Increase per share value
- If used for dividends → direct return
- If used for M&A → accelerate growth
- If you just leave it alone → capital inefficiency

**Management's attitude towards cash is a key indicator in assessing capital allocation capabilities. **

| Li Ning | ~17x | 12% | 3% |

Li Ning's PE of 17 times is in the middle among its peers. Consider:

- But cash flow quality is extremely high
- Net cash accounts for 40% of market capitalization

**After deducting net cash, the PE is about 10-11 times** - this valuation level is not expensive for a consumer goods company with stable cash flow and valuable brand.

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Method 1: DCF Valuation**
- Assuming that FCF is 3.5 billion/year and the growth rate is 5%/year in the next five years
- Terminal growth rate 2%, discount rate 8%
- Estimated intrinsic value: approximately HK$50-60 billion

**Method 2: Profit Valuation Method**
- Net profit in 2026 is expected to be 3.1-3.2 billion yuan
- Give 15 times PE → Market value 46.5-48 billion
- Plus net cash 20 billion → 66.5-68 billion

**Method 3: FCF yield method**
- FCF 3.5 billion/year, target FCF rate of return 6%
- Corresponding market value of approximately HK$58 billion

**Comprehensive valuation range: HK$50-65 billion**

The current market capitalization is approximately HK$50 billion, which is at the lower end of the valuation range and has a certain margin of safety.

## 6. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Management’s capital allocation capability ★★★☆☆

- **Dividend**: Maintain stable dividends, with a dividend rate of 3-4%, which is quite satisfactory.
- **Repurchase**: Occasionally repurchase but not with much intensity
- **M&A**: No major M&A, strategic focus maintained (this is good)
- **Investment in expansion**: Store expansion is prudent and no stores are opened blindly
- **Cash hoarding**: 20 billion cash "idle" is a manifestation of inefficient capital allocation

**The biggest problem**: 20 billion in net cash does not generate operating returns. If management does not actively buy back or increase dividends, it is equivalent to "wasting" shareholders' money.

### Comparison with Anta

Anta has converted excess cash into high-ROE brand assets by acquiring brands such as FILA and Arc'teryx. Li Ning deposited the cash in a bank account. This is the difference between the two capital allocation philosophies - Anta is more aggressive and Li Ning is more conservative.
