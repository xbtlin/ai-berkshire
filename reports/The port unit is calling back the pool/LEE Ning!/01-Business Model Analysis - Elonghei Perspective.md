# Li Ning (2331.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: user value, business essence, differentiated positioning, long-term competitiveness
> Core question: Is this company doing "the right thing"? Do users really need it?

---

## 1. What business is the company doing?

Li Ning is China's second largest local sports brand. Its main business is the design, manufacturing and sales of sports shoes, clothing and accessories**. Income structure:

- **Footwear**: 14.651 billion yuan (approximately 50%)
- **Apparel**: 12.327 billion yuan (approximately 42%)
- **Equipment and Accessories**: 2.621 billion yuan (approximately 8%, +12.7% year-on-year)

Channel structure:
- **Dealers**: 13.773 billion yuan (47%, +6.3%)
- **E-commerce**: 8.743 billion yuan (30%, +5.3%)
- **Direct operation**: 6.655 billion yuan (22%, -3.3%)

In essence, what Li Ning is doing is a **brand premium business** - through brand influence, product design and channel control, it obtains profits higher than manufacturing costs in the sports shoes and apparel market.

## 2. User value analysis: Who is buying Li Ning? Why?

### Core consumer portrait

- **Age**: Mainly 15-35 years old
- **Price range**: 300-800 yuan (main price), some co-branded/limited models exceed 1,000 yuan
- **Consumption motivation**: sports function + daily wear + recognition of national fashion

### Reasons why consumers choose Li Ning

1. **Brand Awareness**: As a national sports brand, Li-Ning has decades of brand accumulation.
2. **Cost-effectiveness**: 20-40% cheaper than Nike/Adidas, but product power is close
3. **National trend blessing**: The 2018 "China Lining" series detonated the concept of national trend
4. **Running professionalism**: 26 million pairs of running shoes are sold annually, and it has a good reputation in the professional running field.
5. **Design Upgrade**: Product design has been significantly improved in recent years, and its appearance is online.

### User pain points and shortcomings

1. **Blurred brand tone**: I want to do both professional sports and trendy fashion, but the positioning is not clear enough.
2. **The gap with Anta**: Anta covers a wider price range through multi-brand strategies such as FILA
3. **Insufficient sinking market**: Anta positions itself as a "cement killer" to harvest the sinking market, while Li Ning has insufficient coverage in third- and fourth-tier cities.
4. **Weak internationalization**: Almost all revenue comes from domestic sources and lacks overseas growth engines.

## 3. "Right" and "Wrong" business models

### Do the right thing ★★★★☆

**Right:**

1. **The essence of brand business**: Sportswear is a good business - high consumption frequency (purchased every year), high gross profit margin (49%), and moderate brand loyalty. People will always need to wear clothes and exercise, and the needs will never disappear.

2. **Product Power Improvement**: The sales volume of 26 million pairs of running shoes proves the product competitiveness. In the field of functional sports shoes (especially running), Li Ning has established a good professional reputation.

3. **Reasonable channel structure**: distribution + direct sales + e-commerce troika, e-commerce accounts for 30% and continues to grow, indicating good progress in online transformation.

4. **Strong cash flow**: Annual operating cash flow is 4.852 billion, net cash is close to 20 billion, and the financial situation is extremely healthy.

**Questionable aspects:**

1. **Weak growth**: Revenue will only grow by 3.2% in 2025, which is much lower than Anta. If the growth rate of a young-oriented brand lags behind that of the industry, it is a sign of declining brand power.

2. **Direct sales channels declined**: Qian Wei has promoted DTC transformation for many years, but direct sales revenue fell by 3.3%. The retail transformation has not been as effective as expected.

3. **National tide dividends are fading**: The national tide craze from 2018 to 2021 brought excess growth, but this wave of dividends has basically faded. After consumers return to rationality, Li Ning needs to use product power rather than stories to maintain growth.

4. **Positioning as "high or low"**: One level lower than Nike/Adidas, half a level higher than Anta/Xtep. This middle ground contains both opportunities (both sides can eat) and risks (both sides are trying to steal your customers).

## 5. Duan Yongping’s questioning

### "What will this business look like in ten years?"

- People will still buy sneakers and clothes ten years from now ✓
- China’s sports consumption penetration rate still has room for improvement ✓
- Li Ning brand will still exist in ten years ✓ (high probability)
- But can market share be maintained or even expanded? Not sure

### "What does Li Ning represent in the minds of consumers?"

This is Li Ning’s biggest problem – **The brand’s mind is not clear enough**:
- Nike = the world's top sports
- ANTA = King of cost performance (Volkswagen) + Fashionable and high-end (FILA)
- Li Ning = ?

In the past few years, Li-Ning has tried multiple labels such as "national trend", "professional sports", and "fashion trend", but none of them has been deeply rooted in the hearts of the people. In contrast, Duan Yongping's BBK/OPPO/vivo has a very clear brand positioning - "dare to be the queen of the world", but achieve the ultimate in the chosen field.

### "Is management doing what matters most?"

Qian Wei's retail transformation was in the right direction (from wholesale thinking to demand thinking), but the revenue from direct sales channels fell after four years of implementation, indicating that the implementation effect was not good enough. In the sports brand industry, product power and brand power are the most important things. Channels are amplifiers, but if products and brands are not differentiated, no matter how good the channel is, they will not be able to create excess returns.

## 6. Comprehensive assessment

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| User value | ★★★★☆ | Sports wear is a real demand, and Li Ning’s products are acceptable |
| Business logic | ★★★★☆ | Branded consumer goods business, high gross profit and high cash flow |
| Differentiated positioning | ★★★☆☆ | There is a brand but the positioning is not clear enough |
| Moat depth | ★★★☆☆ | The brand is well-known but consumer switching costs are low |
| Long-term certainty | ★★★★☆ | Industry demand is certain, and the company has a high probability of long-term survival |

**Overall Business Model Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Li Ning is a "good business" (branded consumer goods, high gross profit, strong cash flow), but it has shortcomings at the execution level (unclear brand positioning, weak growth, and DTC transformation is not smooth). Compared with Anta's multi-brand matrix strategy, Li Ning's single-brand strategy is naturally limited in terms of growth space. The good news is that even with slow growth, this is still a solid business that continues to generate cash flow.
