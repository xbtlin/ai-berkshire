## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **Extremely healthy finances**: Net cash of 20 billion, FCF/market capitalization of about 7%, reasonable returns even with zero growth
2. **Reasonable valuation**: PE after deducting net cash is only 10-11 times, with a clear margin of safety
3. **Brand is valuable**: A national sports brand with decades of accumulation will not disappear easily.
4. **High quality of cash flow**: Operating cash flow/net profit = 1.65 times, the "gold content" of profit is extremely high
5. **Downside risks are limited**: There is a margin of safety even under pessimistic assumptions
6. **Olympic Catalysis**: The 2028 Los Angeles Olympic cycle may bring brand improvement

### Bear Case

1. **Weak growth**: The 3.2% growth rate only slightly exceeds GDP. Such a low growth rate for brands targeting young people is not a good sign.
2. **Competitive pressure**: Anta’s multi-brand offensive continues, and Li Ning’s market share may continue to be eroded
3. **Blurred brand positioning**: Lack of new growth stories after the national trend subsides
4. **ROE is low**: An ROE of 12% can only be said to be "qualified", not "excellent"
5. **Management is not aggressive enough**: 20 billion cash idle, lack of positive awareness of shareholder returns
6. **DTC transformation is not smooth**: After 4 years of promotion, direct operation has been reduced, and the execution effect is questionable.

## Buying Conditions and Catalysts

### Buying conditions (can be considered if one of them is met)
1. PE fell back below 14-15 times (PE 8-9 times after deducting cash)
2. Management announces large buyback or special dividend plan
3. Revenue growth returns to 8%+ (proof of restarting growth)
4. 2026 guidance raised

### Judgment of current price (PE 17 times)
- Not expensive but not cheap either
- PE of 10-11 times after deducting cash provides a certain margin of safety
- As a long-term position, the current price is acceptable
- If you pursue a higher margin of safety, it is better to wait for a correction

### Potential Catalyst
- Successful marketing of the 2026 Olympic cycle
- Management increases dividend ratio or announces buybacks
- Make breakthroughs in sinking markets or internationalization
- Industry consolidation (such as being acquired by Anta and delisting at a premium)

### Sell/avoid signal
- Inventory deterioration (inventory-to-sales ratio exceeds 5 months)
- Gross profit margin continues to decline (falling below 45%)
- Management uses cash to make large, inefficient acquisitions
- Negative revenue growth for two consecutive years

## Final conclusion

**Current judgment: Can be included in the watch list, waiting for better buying opportunities or catalysts**

Li Ning is the "most suitable target for value investors" among the five companies:
- Highest financial quality (cash flow, balance sheet)
- Minimum risk (no possibility of bankruptcy, limited downside)
- Reasonable valuation (PE only 10-11 times after deducting cash)

But there are also clear shortcomings:
- Slow growth (may be a precursor to a "value trap")
- Management is not aggressive enough (20 billion cash idle)
- The competitive position is not strong enough (Anta is widening the gap)

**Ideal Buying Strategy**:
- If PE falls back to 14-15 times, actively build positions
- If management announces large buybacks/dividends, actively build positions
- The current price can be placed in small positions, waiting for verification
