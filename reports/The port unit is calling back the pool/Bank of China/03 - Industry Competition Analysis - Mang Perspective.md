# Bank of China (3988.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. China’s banking industry landscape

China's banking industry is the world's largest banking system, with total assets exceeding 400 trillion yuan. Pattern:

| Hierarchy | Representation | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Six major banks | Industrial, Construction, Rural Affairs, China Postal Service | Policy implementer, accounting for 40%+ of deposits and loans |
| Joint-stock banks | Merchants/Industrial/CITIC, etc. | Relatively market-oriented, differentiated development |
| City commercial banks | Bank of Ningbo/Beijing, etc. | Regional development |
| Internet banking | WeChat/online merchants, etc. | Technology-driven, small and micro customers |

Bank of China is positioned as an "international bank" among the six major banks, ranking fourth domestically (in terms of total assets), but ranking first in international business.

## 2. Competitiveness of Bank of China

### 1. Internationalization - unique advantages
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
- Overseas assets account for approximately 25%
- Branches in 60+ countries
- Leading the RMB cross-border settlement market
- The main force in financial services under "One Belt and One Road"

### 2. Retail Banking
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
- Over 700 million individual customers
- Retail AUM continues to grow
- But there is a clear gap with China Merchants Bank (retail benchmark)

### 3. Corporate banks
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
- Deep customer base of large state-owned enterprises
- Cross-border financial services are differentiated
- But competition in loan interest rates is fierce and bargaining power is weak

## 3. Key issues of Munger format

### "Are there differences among major Chinese banks?"
Frankly, the differentiation is minimal. The products, interest rates, and services of the four major banks are highly homogeneous. The only differentiation of Bank of China is its "internationalization" - if you are a cross-border enterprise, you may choose Bank of China first.

### "Will the net interest margin continue to decline?"
Most likely. Reason:
1. The central bank’s trend of interest rate cuts remains unchanged
2. LPR continues to decline
3. "Benefits to the real economy" policy
4. Deposit cost rigidity (residents’ deposit preference)

But the downward trend may slow down: the net interest margin of 1.26% is close to the "profit bottom line", and the central bank is unlikely to let major banks suffer systemic losses.

### "What is the biggest tail risk?"
Local government debt + real estate loans. If there were massive defaults in these two areas, all major banks would be affected. However, given that the country will take measures to prevent systemic risks, the probability of a complete collapse is extremely low. A more likely scenario is that risks can be resolved over time (provisions are gradually absorbed), at the expense of long-term suppression of profit growth.

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Bank of China ranks in the middle among major banks, with internationalization being its only differentiation. The industry as a whole is facing downward structural pressure on net interest margins. However, the "too big to fail" attributes and national credit endorsement of the six major banks provide a strong security guarantee.

Choose among the major banks: Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ROE up to 10%) > China Construction Bank > Bank of China (International) > Agricultural Bank of China. If you are optimistic about RMB internationalization/cross-border trade, Bank of China is a more accurate choice.
