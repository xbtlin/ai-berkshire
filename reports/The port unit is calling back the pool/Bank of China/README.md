# Bank of China (3988.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: Bank of China Co., Ltd.
- **Stock Code**: 3988.HK
- **Industry**: Large state-owned banks
- **PE(TTM)**: ~6x
- **ROE**：~9%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Operating income | 658.3 billion yuan | +4.48% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 243 billion yuan | +2.18% |
| Net interest margin | 1.26% | -14BP |
| Proportion of non-interest income | 33.06% | Improvement |
| Full-year dividend | $2.263 per 10 shares | - |

## Investment Highlights
1. PE is only 6 times - extremely undervalued large bank
2. ROE 9% - excellent among low PE companies
3. The most internationalized Chinese bank
4. Stable dividend returns

## Core Risks
1. Net interest margin continues to narrow (-14BP), and core profitability declines
2. Risks of real estate-related non-performing loans
3. Major state-owned banks are affected by policy tasks (contributing benefits to the real economy)
4. Profit growth is slow (+2.18%)
