## One sentence conclusion

Bank of China is the "most stable and safest high-dividend target" among the 13 companies - PE 6 times + ROE 9% + dividend rate 6-7%. It has the backing of national credit and is suitable for the bottom position configuration of the portfolio that "eats dividends and waits for recovery".

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. PE 6 times + ROE 9% - one of the cheapest large banks in the world
2. A dividend yield of 6-7% is extremely attractive in a low interest rate environment
3. National credit - it is almost impossible for the four major banks to fail
4. Central SOE market value management policies catalyze valuation restoration
5. RMB internationalization will benefit Bank of China’s overseas business in the long term

### Bearish logic
1. Net interest margin continues to narrow—the core profit logic is deteriorating
2. The hidden risk of real estate/local debt is low but not zero.
3. Profit growth is extremely slow (+2%) and lacks growth elasticity
4. Major state-owned banks are subject to policy constraints and have obvious profit ceilings
5. Serious homogeneity among major banks

## Buying conditions

1. **Currently has buying value**——PE 6 times, dividend rate 7%
2. **Better timing**: Net interest margin stabilizes + credit growth picks up
3. **Signal for adding positions**: Substantial progress has been made in the market value management of central enterprises

## Position suggestions

- **Current price**: 5-8% can be configured as the bottom position (pure interest collection logic)
- **Position limit**: 10% (need to diversify single risks in the banking industry)
- **Holding Period**: Long term (at least 3 years), patiently collect interest

## Keep track of key points

1. Quarterly net interest margin changes (the core indicator)
2. Non-performing loan ratio and provision coverage ratio
3. Growth of non-interest income
4. Changes in dividend policy
5. RMB internationalization/overseas business progress
