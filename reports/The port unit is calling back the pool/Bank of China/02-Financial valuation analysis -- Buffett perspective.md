# Bank of China (3988.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality

| Indicators | 2025 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 658.3 billion yuan | +4.48% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 243 billion yuan | +2.18% |
| ROE | ~9% | Reasonable among major banks |
| Net interest margin | 1.26% | Continued decline |
| Non-performing loan ratio | ~1.3% (estimated) | Controllable |
| Provision coverage ratio | ~190% (estimate) | Adequate |

**Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

ROE of 9% is a medium level among the world's large banks (JP Morgan is about 15%, HSBC is about 10%). Considering that the PE is only 6 times and the PE/ROE ratio is only 0.67, it is very cheap.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation
- PE: ~6x
- PB: about 0.5 times
- Dividend yield: about 6-7%
- PE/ROE: 0.67 (cheap)

### What does PE 6 times mean?
If you use 100 yuan to buy Bank of China stocks, you will earn about 17 yuan per year (net profit/market value), of which about 10 yuan will be distributed as dividends. This means:
- 6-7% dividend yield
- Even if profits do not grow, capital will be recovered through dividends in 6-7 years
- Any earnings growth is "extra income"

### Valuation comparison of major global banks
| Bank | PE | ROE | PB | Dividend rate |
|------|------|------|------|--------|
| Bank of China (3988) | 6x | 9% | 0.5x | 6-7% |
| ICBC (1398) | 5x | 10% | 0.5x | 7% |
| JP Morgan | 12x | 15% | 2x | 2% |
| HSBC (0005) | 8x | 10% | 1x | 5% |

The valuation of major Chinese banks is much lower than that of international peers. The discount comes from:
1. Hidden risks in China’s banking system (local debt, real estate loans)
2. Policy concessions lead to restricted profit growth
3. State-owned shareholders have low requirements for dividends
4. Foreign capital’s distrust of China’s financial system

### Will Buffett buy Bank of China?

Buffett bought H shares of PetroChina in 2003 and sold them after profiting several times. The conditions for him to choose Bank of China may be:
1. Convinced that the risk of non-performing loans is controllable ✓ (Adequate provisions)
2. ROE is sustainable △ (net interest margin is declining)
3. The valuation has a clear margin of safety ✓ (PE 6 times, PB 0.5 times)
4. The management is trustworthy △ (governance restrictions of central enterprises)

## 3. Core risk: "chronic bleeding" of net interest margin

Net interest margin will fall from 1.75% in 2021 to 1.26% in 2025 - a 50 basis point decrease in 5 years. If this trend continues:
- Net interest margin may fall to 1.15% in 2026
- May fall below 1.0% in 2028
- Profits will shrink significantly by then

Countermeasures:
1. The proportion of non-interest income increases (33% → 40%?)
2. Cost control (cost reduction and efficiency increase)
3. Credit costs fall (provisions release profits)
4. Asset structure optimization

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Bank of China is a defensive target with "high dividend + low valuation". The combination of PE 6 times + ROE 9% + dividend rate 6-7% has obvious cost performance among global bank stocks.

But investors need to answer a core question: **Do you believe that a systemic financial crisis will not occur in China? ** If the answer is "yes", then Bank of China at 6x PE is one of the cheapest large banks in the world. If the answer is "not sure", then this bargain may have "hidden costs".
