## Investment judgment: buy (open positions in batches)

**Judgment level:** Buy

**Confidence:** 70%

**Reason:**

AIA is a rare leader in the pan-Asian life insurance industry, and its business model quality ranks first among global life insurance companies. The current P/EV is about 1.0 times, which is not an extremely cheap "leave-in" price, but considering the 15% VONB growth, 15.8% ROEV and 6% total shareholder return, the expected return of long-term holding is considerable. The core bet is the fulfillment of the mainland China expansion story.

## Core investment logic

### Why buy? (Three core arguments)

**1. Asia’s only multinational independent life insurance leader with a scarce business model**

There is no other company in the world that has at the same time: 18 Asia-Pacific markets, MDRT’s global number one agent channel for 11 consecutive years, 58.5% NBV Margin, and pure life insurance business focus. AIA's positioning is unique in the industry - it is neither a comprehensive financial group like Ping An of China nor diversified into emerging markets like Prudential. This scarcity deserves a valuation premium.

**2. Mainland China expansion provides a clear growth path**

The 12 provinces cover only a small part of the mainland market. The pace of adding 1-2 provinces every year, the 40% compound growth target of new markets, and the momentum of NBV growth exceeding 20% ​​in the first two months of 2026 - these data depict a clear growth curve. AIA Life's premiums have increased sevenfold in ten years (a compound growth rate of over 20%), proving that the long-term potential of the mainland market is not empty talk.

**3. Capital return discipline provides downside protection**

75% net FSG payout ratio + continued buybacks + 10% year-over-year dividend growth = 6% total shareholder return. Even if the share price doesn't rise, the 6% annual return is better than most Asian fixed income assets. The capital adequacy ratio of 221% means that the return policy can be maintained in the long term.

### Why hesitate? (Three main concerns)

**1. P/EV 1.0x is not cheap enough**

This is not an opportunity where "the market is grossly mispriced." 1.0x P/EV is a reasonably low price, but it is not the "opportunity of the century" like 0.6x P/EV. If the growth rate of VONB slows down to single digits in the future, the current price may only obtain dividends + repurchases in return, lacking the flexibility to repair valuations.

**2. Uncertainty about mainland China’s growth rate**

Mainland NBV will only grow by 2% in 2025 - which is in contrast to the strategic positioning of the "most important market". Although it accelerated in the second half of the year and has strong momentum in early 2026, product attractiveness in a downward interest rate environment, maintenance of agent quality in new provinces, and competition with local giants are all ongoing challenges. The 40% compound growth target may be too optimistic.

**3. Overall suppression of the Hong Kong stock valuation system**

AIA is listed on the Hong Kong stock market, and factors such as the liquidity discount and geopolitical risk premium in the Hong Kong stock market may continue to suppress valuations. Even if the fundamentals continue to improve, repairing P/EV from 1.0 times to 1.5 times may require an improvement in the entire Hong Kong stock market environment, which cannot be controlled by a single AIA company.

## Key assumptions and triggering conditions

### Buying hypothesis (the premise for the core logic to be established)

| Assumptions | Current status | Verification method | Failure signal |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| VONB maintains double-digit growth | 2025: +15% | Interim report/annual report VONB data | VONB growth rate <5% for two consecutive reporting periods |
| Mainland NBV accelerates growth | H2 accelerates to 14%, >20% in early 2026 | Quarterly/half-year performance update | Mainland NBV has negative growth for two consecutive reporting periods |
| Capital returns continue | 6% total return | Dividend plan, buyback announcement | Dividend reduction or buyback cancellation |
| Stable management | Li Yuanxiang is in office | Announcement of executive changes | CEO suddenly leaves and succession is unknown |

### Sell trigger conditions

1. **VONB has experienced negative growth for two consecutive reporting periods** – the growth story is shattered
2. **Mainland’s NBV growth rate has been negative for a long time** - the core growth engine stalled
3. **Dividends are reduced or capital adequacy ratio falls below 150%** - capital security is compromised
4. **P/EV repaired to more than 1.5 times** - valuation repair completed, re-examining holding logic
