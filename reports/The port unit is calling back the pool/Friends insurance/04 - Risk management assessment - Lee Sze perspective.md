# II. Core risk assessment

# Risk I: Growth in the interior of the country is not as high as expected (important)

** Risk description:** The Chinese interior is the engine of growth that friendly management values most. The expansion targets for new provinces (40% NBV growth in 2025-2030) are ambitious, but the difficulty of implementation cannot be underestimated.

** Complex picture of data:**

- NBV grew by only 2% in 2025.
- Accelerated to 14% in the second half of 2025
- Over 20% growth in the first two months of 2026
- The new four provinces brought 100 million clients in the first half of the year alone.
- The new region has grown 36% since the first half of 2019.

** Positive arguments:** Growth is accelerating and management investment in the interior is increasing. Twelve provinces cover only part of the domestic market, with huge growth space. Friends ' life insurance revenues have for the first time broken the RMB 70 billion, and have grown by more than 20% over the 10-year composite growth rate, with a long-term growth trend unchanged.

** Reverse argument:** Only 2% of the growth rate throughout 2025 indicates that the inner market is not a "stale win" . The downside environment of interest rates puts pressure on the attractiveness of insurance products. The inner rivals (peace, life, euphoria) are accelerating pro-agent reform, and the differences in friendly countries are being eroded. Moreover, friendly states need to build agent teams and brand perceptions in new provinces from scratch – which takes time and a lot of input.

** Agents retain concerns:** There are reports that friendly agents have only about 20% long-term retention rates, and that high rates of elimination are by-products of elite routes. The key question is whether quality standards can be maintained in the course of the rapid expansion of new provinces (four new markets recruiting more than 1,700 agents in the short term).

# Risk two: interest rate down erosion of investment return (<unk> material)

** Risk description:** Interest rates in major Asian markets (China, Thailand, Korea) are on a downward trend and asset end-of-end rates of return for insurance companies are under long-term pressure.

** Friendship response:**

1. Companies with a product structure that is primarily safeguard-based and spread-dependent than savings-based products
2. The Hong Kong market has benefited from the policy of a ceiling on the return on investments in the risk of splitting (implemented in July 2025), and most of the products of friendly countries are covered by the new regulations
3. Multimarket layouts spread the impact of a single interest rate environment

** But note that:** Economic assumptions adjusted in 2025 due to low interest rate environments in the central and domestic areas have had some impact on VONB and EV. If interest rates continue to fall, more assumptions may be needed in the future to further affect the reliability of the underlying value.

# Risk three: Pillar Two (<unk> importance)

** Risk description:** The Pilar Two global minimum (15%) effective from 2025 may have a negative impact on the effective rates of friendly countries. Friends operate in several low-tax markets (Hong Kong, Singapore) and previously enjoyed lower combined rates.

** Impact assessment:** The exact magnitude of the impact is yet to be disclosed in the subsequent financial statements. But structurally, friendly countries, as multinational insurance groups, had a higher tax efficiency than before, and Pilar Two’s implementation would weaken this advantage. According to market expectations, effective tax rates could increase by 2-5 percentage points, corresponding to a decrease of about 3-7% in OPAT.

** But on the other hand:** This is a common industry risk, not exclusive to friendly countries. Procuring colleagues, like the others, will be equally affected. The market may have already partially priced this risk.

# # Risk IV: Volatility in Thailand and emerging markets (<unk> importance)

** Risk description:** Thailand’s capital market shocks in the first half of 2025 resulted in a $754 million investment loss. Uncertainty about the political and economic environment in emerging markets in South-East Asia is another aspect of the friendly diversity landscape.

**Assessment:** Thailand NBV 522 million (+35%) indicates that insurance operations remain strong and problematic at the end of the investment. The performance of friendly portfolio management in Thai markets needs follow-up. But, according to the NBV contribution, Thailand is less than 10% of the group VONB, even if Thailand is experiencing large fluctuations, with limited impact on the group as a whole.

# Risk five: Port risk of hot tides (<unk> importance)

** Risk description:** Hong Kong NBV 2256 million (+28%) in 2025, accounting for about 41% of the group VONB. The strong growth in Hong Kong’s market has been partly a result of the surge in cross-border insurance coverage by mainland customers. This growth engine could slow if the mainland tightens its supervision of cross-border insurance policies or if the currency of the renminbi changes negatively to the Hong Kong currency exchange rate.

** Positive arguments:** The long-standing trend of overseas asset allocation by Mainland residents has not changed, and Hong Kong’s role as a bridge between the Mainland and the global market is growing.

** Negative argument:** Regulatory policy changes are difficult to predict. The upper limit of the return on risk-sharing investment in 2025, although short-term friendly countries, is uncertain about the future. Moreover, 41% of VONB is concentrated in the single market, and the impact is significant if downward pressure arises.

## Risk management rating: <unk> (4/5)

** Core judgement: Friendship has one of the best management teams in the Asian insurance industry and risks are generally manageable**

** Why for four stars:**

1. ** Management performance was strong.** Since Li was appointed, VONB has recovered from the valley to its historical height, the quality of the agent channels has been continuously improved, capital returns have been highly disciplined. Clear strategy (focusing on interior expansion + maintaining agent quality) and implementation is in place (new provinces open, MDRT 11 running).

2. ** Capital management discipline is trustworthy.** 221% capital adequacy + 75% net FSG distribution + ongoing buy-back = management has achieved a good balance between growth and return. There is no blind expansion or over-bridling.

3. ** Risk fragmentation is better.** The layout of 18 markets provides a natural risk hedge. Even if a market is in trouble, it does not shake the firm ' s roots.

** Reason for deduction:**

1. The Chinese interior has only 2% of the NBV acceleration (in 2025) in contrast to management’s position on the most important market. The market has reason to question whether the 40% compound growth target is overly optimistic.

The controversy over the over-paying of the CEO can weaken market confidence, especially in those years when the growth in the interior is not optimal.

Succession planning is not yet transparent — a risk point that cannot be ignored for long-term investors.
