# SenseTime Group (0020.HK) Risk Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: management quality, incentive mechanism, institutional risks, safety margin
> Core question: Is this company’s management trustworthy? What’s the worst case scenario?

## 1. Management evaluation

### Core Management Team

**Dr. Xu Li (CEO)**
- Co-founder of SenseTime, Chairman of the Executive Board and CEO
- PhD from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, studying under Professor Tang Xiaoou
- Mainly responsible for vision strategy, business development and daily operations
- Managers with a technical background and high reputation in the AI academic circle

**Dr. Wang Xiaogang (CTO)**
- Co-Founder, Chief Technology Officer, Executive Vice President
- Responsible for the overall planning and management of the research team
- Strong academic background, with highly cited papers in the field of computer vision

**Historical Background**:
- Professor Tang Xiaoou, the founder of SenseTime, passed away in December 2023, which was a major loss to the company
- Xu Bing (co-founder) will resign as executive director in March 2025
- The core team is undergoing gradual changes

### Dimensions of management evaluation

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Technical vision | ★★★★☆ | The team has a profound background in AI research and strong technical judgment |
| Strategy execution | ★★★☆☆ | The transformation direction is right (generative AI), but the execution efficiency remains to be seen |
| Commercialization capabilities | ★★★☆☆ | Strong academic background but shortcomings in commercialization capabilities |
| Capital efficiency | ★★☆☆☆ | 40 billion in financing vs 5 billion in revenue, extremely low input-output ratio |
| Focus | ★★★☆☆ | Improving (peeling away shadow), but still scattered |

### Key Person Risk

1. **The impact of Tang Xiaoou’s death**: After the founder passed away, the company lost its most important spiritual leader and academic appeal. Although Xu Li has outstanding abilities, SenseTime in the "post-Tang Xiaoou era" may have weakened in terms of talent attraction and industry status.

2. **Core personnel turnover**: Xu Bing’s departure shows that the senior management team is adjusting. We need to pay attention to whether more core talents will be lost in the future.

3. **Academic vs. Commercial Management**: SenseTime’s management team mainly has an academic background, which is an advantage in the technology research and development stage, but may become a shortcoming in the commercialization competition stage. Writing a paper to a high level is a completely different ability than selling a product to a customer.

### Management incentives and interest binding

- After Tang Xiaoou’s death, the issue of ownership of his 6.9 billion shares has been settled
- The core management team’s shareholding ratio is reasonable
- But the W-share structure means the founding team retains voting control

**Assessment: The management's interest binding is moderate, but commercial execution is the core question. **

## 2. Institutional risk analysis

### Policy and regulatory risk ★★★★ (high)

1. **AI supervision is becoming stricter**: China’s supervision on AI-generated content, deep forgery and other fields is becoming increasingly strict, which may affect some of SenseTime’s businesses.
2. **US Sanctions Risk**: SenseTime was once included in the US Entity List. Although the main business is domestic, sanctions may affect GPU procurement (Nvidia chip export controls).
3. **Data Security Compliance**: The compliance of AI training data has received increasing attention, which may increase compliance costs.

### GPU supply chain risk ★★★★★ (extremely high)

This is SenseTime’s biggest institutional risk:

- The United States continues to tighten export controls on AI chips to China
- SenseTime relies on NVIDIA GPUs (H100/A100, etc.), and the new generation of high-end chips may not be available
- Some of the 54,000 GPUs may be reserved before sanctions, and subsequent expansion will face challenges.
- There is still a performance gap between domestic alternatives (such as Huawei Ascend)

**If it is unable to obtain the latest GPU, SenseTime’s computing power competitiveness will decrease over time. **

### Industry bubble risk ★★★☆ (medium)

- The valuation of the AI industry is generally high and there is a risk of bubble bursting
- If the market's expectations for AI are adjusted back, SenseTime will bear the brunt as a "pure AI concept stock"
- Lessons from the Internet Bubble in 2000: The hottest track does not mean that every participant can survive

### Financial Sustainability Risk ★★★☆ (Moderate)

- Losses narrowed but still burning cash
- If revenue growth slows down or the next round of financing becomes difficult, we may face capital chain pressures
- GPU depreciation is a rigid expense and will not decrease as income declines.

## 3. Li Lu type risk framework

### "What's the worst case scenario?"

**Scenario 1: The giants are completely crushed and SenseTime is marginalized**
- Alibaba/Byte/Tencent’s AI cloud service price war, SenseTime cannot compete
- Customers gradually flow to giant platforms
- Result: Revenue growth stagnated or even declined, and cash continued to be consumed
- Impact: Share price may drop 60-70%
- **Probability: 25%**

**Scenario 2: GPU supply is cut off and computing power competitiveness is lost**
- US sanctions are further intensified, completely cutting off the supply of high-end GPUs
- The performance of domestic alternatives is insufficient to support competitiveness
- Result: Computing infrastructure gradually falls behind and customers are lost
- Impact: The company needs a complete transformation
- **Probability: 15%**

**Scenario Three: AI Bubble Burst**
- The market found that the progress of AI commercialization is far less than expected
- Valuations of AI-related companies generally fell sharply
- Result: SenseTime's stock price plummeted, and its financing ability was damaged.
- Impact: Possible existential crisis
- **Probability: 15%**

**Scenario 4: Running out of cash and forced to raise funds**
- Losses continue and revenue growth slows
- Forced to raise funds under unfavorable conditions (additional issuance at a substantial discount)
- Result: severe dilution for existing shareholders
- Impact: 30-50% drop in per share value
- **Probability: 20%**

### "What are the chances of surviving?"

SenseTime has raised more than 40 billion yuan in total in its history and still has a certain amount of cash reserves. But continued losses are draining those reserves.

**3-year survival probability: 85%**
**Probability of maintaining independent public company status for 5 years: 65%**
**Probability of achieving sustained profitability in 5 years: 40%**

### Possible exit paths

1. **Independent profit**: The most ideal but the most difficult (30% probability)
2. **Acquired**: A giant acquires SenseTime’s technical team and GPU resources (25% probability)
3. **Business split**: Operate each X business independently, and the core business is independent (20% probability)
4. **Slow Decline**: Income stagnates, and the scale gradually decreases (25% probability)

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Method 1: Profit Valuation Method**
- Assuming net profit of 500 million yuan in 2027, give 40 times PE → market value of HKD 20 billion
- Assuming net profit of 1 billion yuan in 2028, give 30 times PE → market value of HKD 30 billion
- Current market capitalization is approximately HK$64 billion → **Severely overvalued**

**Method 2: Asset Replacement Method**
- Estimated market price of 54,000 GPUs: about 20-30 billion yuan
- Technology/patent/team value: about 10-20 billion yuan
- Net cash: approximately 5-10 billion yuan
-Total replacement value: approximately 35-60 billion yuan
- Current market capitalization is approximately HK$64 billion → **Basically reasonable but expensive**

**Method 3: PS Valuation Method**
- Estimated revenue in 2026 is 6.5 billion yuan, giving 8 times PS → market value of HK$52 billion
- Current 64 billion → **More than 20% more expensive**

### Safety margin judgment

| Valuation method | Reasonable value | Relative to current |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Profit method (conservative) | 20 billion | -69% |
| Asset Law | 45 billion | -30% |
| PS Law | 52 billion | -19% |

**Conclusion: There is almost no margin of safety at current prices, and most valuation methods point to overvaluation. **
