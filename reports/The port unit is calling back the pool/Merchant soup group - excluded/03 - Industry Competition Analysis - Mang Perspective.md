# SenseTime Group (0020.HK) Industry Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, competition landscape, multiple thinking models, reverse thinking
> Core question: In the AI industry dominated by giants, can SenseTime find room for continued survival?

---

## 1. Nature of the industry: AI infrastructure and platform

### Use multiple thinking models to understand this industry

**Ecological Analogy**: The competition in the AI industry is similar to the "hierarchical competition in the tropical rainforest" - the big trees (giants) at the top occupy the most sunlight (resources), and the plants (Shangtang) in the middle need to find living space in the limited gaps. The middle layer has a chance when a big tree falls, but most of the time it can only survive under cover.

**The Essence of Economics**: AI infrastructure and platforms are an industry driven by the dual drivers of **network effects + economies of scale**:
- More users → more data → better models → attract more users (network effect)
- The larger the scale → the lower the cost per unit of computing power → the more competitive the price (economies of scale)

Both of these characteristics point to one conclusion: the financial industry naturally tends to be concentrated, and the winner-takes-all effect is strong. **

**Historical Analogy**: Looking back at the development of the cloud computing industry, AWS/Azure/GCP account for 70%+ of the global share, and Alibaba Cloud/Huawei Cloud/Tencent Cloud account for 60%+ of the Chinese market. AI cloud services are likely to repeat this centralized path.

### Industry scale and growth

- The scale of China's AI industry is expected to exceed 500 billion yuan in 2025
- The annual growth rate of the AI computing power market is 40%+
- Generative AI application layer explodes rapidly
- But competitors are increasing simultaneously, and "involution" is intensifying

## 2. Competitive landscape: giants crushing them

### The competitor matrix faced by SenseTime

| Competitive dimensions | Main rivals | Relative position of SenseTime |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| AI Cloud/Computing Power | Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Byte Volcano Engine | Much smaller than the giants |
| Large model | Baidu Wenxin, Alibaba Tongyi, Byte Doubao, DeepSeek | Model ability is not dominant |
| AI application | Byte (AI office), Baidu (search + AI), Tencent (social + AI) | No C-side traffic entrance |
| Vertical fields | iFlytek (voice), Hikvision (security), Horizon (smart driving) | Not as focused as vertical players |

### The overwhelming advantage of giants

1. **Financial advantage**: Alibaba/Tencent/Byte have annual profits of tens of billions, so they can continue to invest in AI without feeling distressed; SenseTime loses money every year and consumes cash.
2. **Ecological advantages**: The giants bring their own customers (Alibaba’s e-commerce customers, Tencent’s social/game customers), and the cost of acquiring customers is extremely low.
3. **Talent advantage**: Top AI talents are more willing to go to giants with high salaries and high influence.
4. **Data advantage**: Giants have massive amounts of real user data for model training.
5. **Brand Trust**: Enterprise customers trust the stability and sustainability of large platforms more.

### SenseTime’s competitive position

**Frankly speaking, SenseTime is not the strongest in every dimension:**
- Computing power scale: 54,000 GPUs vs Byte/Alibaba’s hundreds of thousands of GPUs
- Model ability: Ririxin vs Tongyi Qianwen/Doubao/DeepSeek
- Customer resources: independent AI companies vs giants with their own ecosystems
- Financial strength: annual loss of 1.77 billion vs. annual profit of tens of billions

**The only difference** of SenseTime may be its "independent third-party" positioning - unlike Alibaba/Tencent/Byte, which is both a platform and an application, SenseTime can be a pure technology supplier. But the question is: is this positioning attractive enough?

## 4. Reverse thinking: Under what circumstances can SenseTime succeed?

### Scenario 1: Giants check and balance each other, independent platforms benefit

If corporate customers are worried about being locked in by a single giant (for example, they are afraid that Alibaba will see their data), they will choose independent third-party platforms such as SenseTime. This is similar to enterprises choosing a neutral cloud service provider instead of a competitor's cloud service.

**Likelihood: Moderate**. But the problem is that even if there is this demand, Huawei Cloud is a more powerful "neutral" option.

### Scenario 2: Overseas market breakthrough

In some markets that have demand for Chinese AI technology but cannot use US services (such as Southeast Asia and the Middle East), SenseTime may find a blue ocean.

**Likelihood: Medium-Low**. The cost of developing overseas markets is high, and it faces competition from Chinese giants going overseas.

### Scenario 3: A certain X business suddenly comes out

If "Yuanluobo" or other X businesses unexpectedly break out, it may open up new growth space for SenseTime.

**Likelihood: Low**. A small probability event.

### Scenario 4: The AI industry is reshuffled, and SenseTime becomes a survivor

If the AI bubble bursts and most small companies go bankrupt, SenseTime will become a minority survivor due to its larger scale and capital reserves, thereby gaining a larger market share.

**Likelihood: Moderate**. Similar to after the Internet bubble in 2000, survivors (such as Amazon) gained greater room for development.

| iFlytek | 2017-2019 | Stable revenue but thin profits | Commercialization dilemma of technology companies |
| Megvii/Yitu/Yuncong | 2018-2020 | Each has its own difficulties | The "Four AI Little Dragons" are collectively in trouble |

### Inspiration for Shangtang

History shows that it is difficult for AI technology companies to maintain an independent position after the entry of giants. Unless:
1. Be the absolute number one in a certain vertical field (such as Hikvision in security)
2. Acquired by a giant (exit strategy)
3. Betting on the right direction during a certain change in the technical route

## 6. Munger’s “Circle of Competence” Test

### The core questions that investors need to answer:

1. **Can you judge the position of SenseTime’s large model capabilities in the industry? ** → Extremely difficult, requiring a strong background in AI technology
2. **Can you predict the AI purchasing behavior of enterprise customers? ** → Unsure, the industry is still in its early stages
3. **Can you evaluate the real utilization and economic benefits of a 54,000 GPU cluster? ** → Information is not transparent
4. **Can you judge the long-term competitive outcome between SenseTime and the giants? ** → According to historical rules, it is difficult for independent small companies to maintain their share after the entry of giants.

### Possible comments from Munger

"I'm going to think about it the other way around - what's the reason why this company won't be crushed by giants? I can't think of a convincing answer. In the technology industry, when giants are determined to enter a certain field, independent small companies usually don't end well. Unless you can be irreplaceable in a certain dimension, you are in a race against time - build enough barriers before being crushed. I'm not sure SenseTime can win this race."
