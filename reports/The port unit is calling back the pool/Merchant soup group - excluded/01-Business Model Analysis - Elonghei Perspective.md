# SenseTime Group (0020.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: user value, business essence, differentiated positioning, long-term competitiveness
> Core question: Is this company doing "the right thing"? Do users really need it?

---

## 1. What business is the company doing?

SenseTime Group's business model has undergone a profound transformation, from an early computer vision (CV) solution provider to a **generative AI infrastructure and platform company**. Current business structure:

1. **AI Cloud Service (Core)**: Provides AI training and inference computing power services based on SenseCore large devices (54,000 GPUs)
2. **Large model platform (Ririxin SenseNova)**: Provides basic large model capabilities for enterprise customers
3. **Ecological Enterprise Matrix (X Business)**: Vertical applications such as smart car "Jueying", home robot "Yuanluobo", smart medical care, etc.
4. **Traditional CV business**: smart city, smart business, etc. (proportion continues to decline)

In essence, SenseTime wants to become the "AWS of the AI era" - providing AI services to thousands of industries through infrastructure and platform capabilities.

## 2. User value analysis: Who is paying for it? Why?

### Generative AI customers (accounting for 72.4%)

- **Enterprise Customers**: Medium and large enterprises that need large model capabilities but do not have self-built computing power and model capabilities
- **Developers**: The technical team that builds AI applications on the SenseTime platform
- **Government/State-owned Enterprise**: Institutions with requirements for data security and localization

### Reasons why customers choose SenseTime:

1. **Computing resources**: A large device composed of 54,000 GPUs, leading in scale among Chinese AI companies
2. **Domestic demand**: Compared with OpenAI/AWS, SenseTime is a domestic alternative.
3. **Full stack capability**: one-stop service from computing power to models to applications
4. **Cost-effectiveness**: Compared with building self-built AI infrastructure, it is more economical to use SenseTime Cloud services

### Key question: Why do customers have to choose SenseTime?

This is the biggest torture of SenseTime’s business model. In the field of generative AI:
- Alibaba has Tongyi Qianwen + Alibaba Cloud
- Baidu Youwenxinyiyan + Baidu Smart Cloud
- ByteDouBao + Volcano Engine
- Tencent has Hunyuan + Tencent Cloud

**The AI cloud services of these giants far surpass SenseTime in terms of funding, ecology, and customer resources. **

## 3. "Right" and "Wrong" business models

### Do the right thing ★★★☆☆

**Right:**

1. **The track is heading in the right direction**: Generative AI is a technological trend with a high degree of certainty, and the demand for AI computing power is growing explosively.
2. **Decisive transformation**: Transform from traditional CV to generative AI, and the revenue share of generative AI will increase from 64% in 2024 to 72% in 2025.
3. **Infrastructure first**: The computing power reserve of 54,000 GPUs is a scarce resource and has a certain value.
4. **Profitability improved significantly**: In the second half of 2025, EBITDA turned positive for the first time, and cash flow became positive for the first time, indicating that commercialization is improving.

**What’s wrong:**

1. **Lack of clear differentiation**: In the large model track, SenseTime is neither the strongest in computing power (not as good as Byte and Alibaba), nor the best model (not as good as DeepSeek, Tongyi Qianwen), nor the widest ecosystem (not as good as Baidu and Alibaba).

2. **The trap of "doing everything"**: AI cloud, large models, smart cars, robots, medical care... business lines are too fragmented and face competition from professional players in every field.

3. **To G/To B dependence**: Traditional business relies heavily on government and state-owned enterprise customers, with long billing period, project system and poor sustainability.

4. **Diseconomies of scale**: The maintenance cost of 54,000 GPUs is extremely high (electricity, depreciation, operation and maintenance). If the computing power utilization rate is not high, it is "burning money for heating".

## 4. Duan Yongping’s questioning

### "Do users really need SenseTime?"

**Honest answer**: For most enterprise customers, Alibaba Cloud/Tencent Cloud/Huawei Cloud are the more natural choices (more complete ecology and wider sales network). SenseTime’s target customers may be:
- Medium and large enterprises that do not want to be locked in by a single giant
- Customers who have special customization needs for AI capabilities
- Policy-sensitive customers (requires "pure AI companies" rather than Internet giants)

But is this market segment large enough to support SenseTime’s valuation? Doubtful.

### "What will SenseTime be doing ten years from now?"

**Optimistic Imagination**: Become China’s leading AI infrastructure provider, similar to the Chinese version of NVIDIA + Microsoft Azure
**Pessimistic imagination**: Being squeezed by giants and reduced to a small AI service provider in market segments, the scale cannot support continued R&D investment.

### "Where is the moat?"

Frankly speaking, SenseTime’s moat is **very thin**:
- Computing power? Giants can deploy more GPUs in 12 months
- Model? Open source models (such as DeepSeek) quickly democratize model capabilities
- Customer relations? Traditional CV’s government customer stickiness is limited
- Brand? In the field of AI, it does not have the brand appeal of giants.

**The only possible moat**: Positioning as an independent third-party AI platform (similar to Horizon's "not grabbing customer cakes" logic in the field of smart driving). But this requires reaching the extreme in a certain dimension, which we haven’t seen yet.

## 5. Comparison with the horizon

Both companies are "AI platform" companies, but Horizon's positioning is much clearer than SenseTime:

| Dimension | Horizon | SenseTime |
|------|--------|------|
| Focus | Extremely high (only smart driving chip platform) | Low (AI cloud + large model + car + robot...) |
| Customer relationship | Deep binding (chip embedded products) | Shallow level (can switch suppliers at any time) |
| Switching cost | High (chip ecosystem locked) | Low (AI cloud services are easy to migrate) |
| Differentiation | Clear (open Tier 2 positioning) | Fuzzy (compete on the same track as the giants) |

## 6. Comprehensive assessment

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| User value | ★★★☆☆ | AI capabilities are valuable, but there are too many alternatives |
| Business logic | ★★★☆☆ | The direction of AI infrastructure is right, but implementation is difficult |
| Differentiation of positioning | ★★☆☆☆ | Lack of clear moat among giants |
| Moat Depth | ★★☆☆☆ | Almost no sustainable barriers to competition |
| Long-term certainty | ★★☆☆☆ | The industry is certain, but the company’s position in the industry is uncertain |

**Overall Business Model Rating: ★★★☆☆ (2.5/5)**

SenseTime is doing things in the "right direction" - AI infrastructure and platforms. But in an industry filled with giants, SenseTime lacks convincing differentiated competitiveness. The state of "doing everything but not being the best at anything" makes it difficult to establish a moat for the business model. Unless real barriers are built in a certain vertical, the long-term prospects are bleak.
