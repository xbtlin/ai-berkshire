## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **The ceiling of the AI track is extremely high**: Artificial intelligence is the most certain technology trend
2. **Financial improvement is obvious**: losses narrowed by 58.6%, EBITDA turned positive for the first time, and cash flow turned positive
3. **Computing power slot**: The infrastructure of 54,000 GPUs leads the scale among Chinese AI companies.
4. **Transformation direction**: The revenue share of generative AI increased from 64% to 72%, and the focus is improving
5. **Independent third-party value**: Enterprise customers may not want to be locked in by giant platforms

### Bear Case

1. **Big giants crush**: Alibaba/Byte/Tencent completely crush in terms of capital, ecology, and customers
2. **Lack of moat**: There are no clear sustainable barriers to competition, and customers can switch at any time
3. **GPU supply cutoff risk**: US sanctions may cut off the supply of high-end chips and reduce the competitiveness of computing power
4. **Extremely Low Capital Efficiency**: 40 billion+ financing only yielded 5 billion in revenue, poor input-output ratio
5. **Valuation bubble**: PS is 12 times more than the profitable cloud service giant, and the margin of safety is zero
6. **Management Risk**: After the death of founder Tang Xiaoou, team cohesion and appeal declined.

## Final conclusion

**Current judgment: Do not open a position, keep observing**

From the perspective of value investment, SenseTime has too many fundamental questions that it cannot answer:
- How big is its long-term survival space in a market dominated by giants?
- Can RMB 40 billion in financing eventually be converted into sustainable profits?
- How to deal with the risk of GPU supply interruption?
- How much of the "AI bubble" does current valuation reflect?

**Under what conditions can it be reviewed again? **
1. Achieve positive operating cash flow for 4 consecutive quarters
2. Establish a clear first position in a certain vertical field
3. Valuation falls below 5 times PS (corresponding to a market value of approximately HK$25 billion)
4. Giants withdraw from the AI cloud market due to antitrust and other reasons

**Better alternative:**
- If you are optimistic about AI chips: Horizon (more focused, clearer differentiation)
- If you are optimistic about AI infrastructure: buy NVIDIA directly (the certainty is much higher than SenseTime)
- If you are optimistic about China’s AI applications: consider profitable application layer companies
