# SenseTime Group (0020.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: If you buy at the current price, can you get a reasonable return in the future?

---

### Interpretation of financial characteristics

3. **Cash circulation improvement**: The cash flow cycle dropped from 228 days to 129 days, which is strong evidence of improved management efficiency.
4. **EBITDA turned positive**: EBITDA of 380 million yuan turned positive in the second half of the year, which is still one step away from real profitability (depreciation and amortization are still huge).

## 2. In-depth analysis of profit quality

### Income structure analysis

| Business Segment | Revenue in 2025 | Proportion | Year-on-year |
|---------|-----------|------|------|
| Generative AI | 3.629 billion yuan | 72.4% | +51% |
| Traditional AI | About 1.386 billion yuan | 27.6% | Decline |

The high growth of the generative AI business partially masks the decline of the traditional CV business. Need to ask:
- How much of the generative AI revenue comes from computing power leasing (low gross profit) vs. high value-added models/application services?
- Will traditional AI business continue to shrink? If so, overall growth may slow.

### Uncertainty of profit path

EBITDA becomes positive ≠ net profit becomes positive. SenseTime faces huge **depreciation and amortization pressure**:
- Depreciation of 54,000 GPUs (depreciation based on 3-5 years, billions of yuan per year)
- Amortization of large upfront R&D investment
- There may still be a depreciation and amortization gap of 2-3 billion yuan between EBITDA and net profit.

**Key question**: Can revenue growth continue to cover depreciation and amortization, and achieve real net profit in 2026-2027?

## 3. Balance sheet analysis

### Key asset items

- **GPU cluster (fixed asset)**: This is the most important asset of SenseTime and the largest source of depreciation. The service life of GPUs and the risk of technology iteration are core concerns - if these GPU technologies fall behind in three years, a large number of assets will face impairment.
- **Intangible assets (technology/patents)**: A large amount of R&D is capitalized into intangible assets, and attention needs to be paid to whether there is a tendency for radical capitalization.
- **Cash Reserve**: SenseTime has raised more than 40 billion yuan in cumulative financing since its listing, but continued losses are consuming cash.

### Key Risks

1. **GPU asset impairment risk**: AI chip technology iterates extremely fast, H100 → H200 → B200 → GB200... If SenseTime’s GPU falls behind in technology within 2-3 years, it may face large asset impairment.
2. **Cash consumption**: Although losses have narrowed, cash is still being consumed every year. If the new round of financing is difficult, it may face financial pressure.
3. **Accounts receivable**: Although payment collection has improved, the account period of To G/To B business is still long.

## 4. Cash flow analysis

### Milestone: Operating cash flow positive for first time

In the second half of 2025, SenseTime's operating cash flow will achieve positive net inflow for the first time since its listing. This is an important turning point signal indicating:
- The quality of income is improving (not just "paper income")
-Improved payment efficiency
- Operating expenses are under certain control

But it needs to be seen whether this is sustainable - positive cash flow for half a year is not indicative of a long-term trend.

### Free Cash Flow

Considering the ongoing investment in GPU clusters (new models replacing old models), free cash flow may still be negative. AI infrastructure is an asset-heavy, high-investment business, which is fundamentally different from the asset-light SaaS model.

## 5. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

- **Market capitalization**: approximately HK$64 billion (data as of mid-2025, subject to change)
- **PS (TTM)**: approximately 12-13 times (based on revenue of 5.015 billion yuan)
- **PE**: meaningless (losing money)
- **PB**: about 1.5-2 times

### Valuation rationality assessment

**Optimistic scenario (turn from loss to profit in 2027, net profit of 500 million yuan): **
- 50 times PE → market value 25 billion yuan
- The current market capitalization of HK$64 billion means market pricing is well above earnings valuation

**What does this mean? ** The market is giving SenseTime a "dream valuation"—pricing is based on the long-term imagination of the AI ​​​​track, rather than recent profitability.

**PS Valuation Comparison:**

| Company | PS (TTM) | Growth rate | Profit status |
|------|----------|------|---------|
| SenseTime | ~12x | 33% | Losses narrowed |
| Alibaba Cloud | ~5x | 20% | Small profit |
| AWS | ~7x | 15% | High Margins |
| Kingsoft Cloud | ~3x | 25% | Loss |

Compared with the profitable cloud service giant, SenseTime PS’s valuation of 12 times is too expensive. The market premium comes from "pure AI concepts".

### Key Valuation Risks

1. **AI Concept Ebbs**: If the market’s enthusiasm for AI cools down, the valuation of the concept may shrink significantly.
2. **Profits are worse than expected**: If there is still no profit in 2027, the market may re-price
3. **Dilution Risk**: Continuous financing may dilute existing shareholders’ equity

## 6. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Management capital allocation ★★☆☆☆

- **Huge investment in R&D**: Sense of direction is improving (focus on generative AI), but historically investments have been spread across multiple directions
- **GPU Purchase**: The investment of 54,000 GPUs is a double-edged sword - if the utilization rate is high, it is a competitive advantage, and if the utilization rate is low, it is a sunk cost.
- **Business divestiture**: Begin to divest non-core businesses (such as smart car Jueying), and focus is improving
- **Dividends/Buybacks**: None. Loss-making companies currently have no ability to make returns

### Historical capital efficiency

SenseTime has raised more than 40 billion yuan since its listing, but its market value is only 64 billion yuan (the stock price has been sluggish for a long time). **Extremely low input-output ratio** - This is the most direct denial of capital allocation capabilities.

## 7. Core contradictions under Buffett’s framework

**Positive factors:**
- Revenue is growing, losses are narrowing, and the direction is right.
- The ceiling of the generative AI track is extremely high
- Operating cash flow begins to turn positive, which is an important signal
- Management begins to make subtractions (divesting non-core businesses)

**Negatives:**
- 40 billion+ investment only yielded 5 billion in revenue, extremely low capital efficiency
- The GPU-heavy asset model requires continuous huge investment, which is not a good business
- In a market dominated by giants, the sustainability of revenue growth is questionable
- Valuations fully or even over-reflect optimistic expectations
- Buffett will never touch this type of company - there is no certainty of profit and bottomless capital needs

From a value investing perspective, this is not a good risk-reward ratio.
