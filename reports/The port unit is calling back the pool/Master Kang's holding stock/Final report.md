## One sentence summary

Master Kong Holdings is a "cash cow" company with the strongest channel network in China's fast-moving consumer goods industry - the combination of 30% ROE, 5%+ dividend yield, and 16 times PE provides an attractive risk-return ratio, making it the choice with the highest comprehensive quality among the four Hong Kong stock targets.

## Core investment arguments

### Reasons to be bullish

1. **30% ROE - the top level among Chinese consumer goods**: It shows that the business model is extremely excellent and the capital efficiency is extremely high
2. **Channel moat cannot be copied**: millions of terminals, 30 years of accumulation, dual-category synergy, new entrants cannot copy
3. **Profit margin improvement trend**: Gross profit margin increased from 30.2% to 34.8%, there is still room for optimization
4. **High dividend and sustainable**: 5%+ dividend rate based on 3.5-4 billion FCF support
5. **Extremely high certainty**: There is a high probability that it will still be the number one in the industry in ten years, and there is no risk of being subverted.
6. **Benefits from downgrading consumption**: In times of economic downturn, instant noodles may benefit as a "cost-effective choice"

### Reasons to be bearish

1. **Growth Stagnation**: Revenue has basically had zero growth in the past four years, and the instant noodle market is shrinking
2. **Brand Aging**: Young consumers’ favorability towards the “Master Kong” brand is declining
3. **Sugar-free tea threat**: Traditional sugary tea beverages face structural substitution by sugar-free tea
4. **100% dividend payout ratio + high debt**: may overdraft the company’s future investment capabilities
5. **Limited room for profit margin improvement**: Gross profit margin cannot be increased indefinitely, and may currently be close to the ceiling
6. **Uncertainty of family management**: The new CEO has just taken over and his ability needs to be verified.
