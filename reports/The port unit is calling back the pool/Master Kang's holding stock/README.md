## Company Profile

Master Kong Holdings Co., Ltd. was founded in Tianjin in 1992 by Taiwan's Wei Yingzhou family and is one of China's largest instant noodles and beverage companies. The core brand "Tingyi" is well-known in China. In 2018, founder Wei Yingzhou retired, and his eldest son Wei Hongming took over as chairman of the board of directors; in January 2026, his third son Wei Hongcheng took over as CEO.

## Core Business Structure (2025)

1. **Beverage business**: revenue of approximately 50.1 billion yuan (accounting for 63%), including tea drinks, juice, and packaged water
2. **Instant noodle business**: revenue of approximately 28.4 billion yuan (accounting for 36%), including container noodles and bag noodles
3. **Others**: about 1%
