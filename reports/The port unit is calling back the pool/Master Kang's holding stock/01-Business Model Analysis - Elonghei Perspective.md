# Analysis of business model of Master Kong Holdings (0322.HK)

> Analysis dimensions: user value, business nature, moat depth, management level of "duty"

## 1. Business essence: "User value" of mass fast-moving consumer goods

### User value of instant noodles

The core value that instant noodles provide consumers is "a cheap, convenient, and delicious one-time meal solution." Here is one:
- **Price Sensitive** Demand: The core motivation for consumers to buy instant noodles is "cheapness"
- **Convenience-driven**: The characteristics of fast and no cooking determines the usage scenarios
- **Limited Flavor Differentiation**: Consumers have preferences for specific flavors but are not extremely loyal

### User value of beverages

The value provided by Master Kong’s beverages (ice tea, green tea, juice, etc.):
- **Instant thirst quenching + taste satisfaction**: a cost-effective choice in the cold drink scenario
- **Popular positioning**: Focus on the price range of 2-5 yuan, covering the widest range of consumer groups
- **Channel Convenience**: Can be found at any convenience store/commercial stand

From Duan Yongping's perspective: Master Kong provides "certain, widely accepted daily food and beverages." User value is real but not deep - consumers will not feel pain because of "the absence of Master Kong", and there are sufficient substitutes.

## 2. Moat analysis

### Channel moat ★★★★★

Tingyi’s greatest competitive advantage is its **unrivaled distribution network**:
- Covering millions of retail terminals in China
- Go deep into counties, towns and even rural canteens
- Beverages and instant noodles share a channel network to create synergy
- Cold chain logistics capabilities (required for cold drink products such as iced black tea)

This is a channel system that took decades and billions of capital to build, and is nearly impossible for new entrants to replicate. Even if Internet giants enter the food and beverage field, they cannot bypass the last meter of offline terminals.

### Brand moat ★★★★☆

"Master Kong" has a very high brand recognition in China:
- "The No. 1 Lenovo Brand" in instant noodles category
- Synonymous with the iced tea/green tea category
- 30 years of accumulation of brand equity

**But the brand has the risk of aging**:
- Young consumers may think "Master Kong = cheap/traditional"
- New consumer brands (Yuanqi Forest, etc.) are more attractive among young people
- The brand has limited scalability in terms of high-end development

### Scale effect ★★★★★

Master Kong is one of the companies with the strongest scale effect in China’s FMCG industry:
- Huge purchase volume → lower cost of raw materials
- National chemical plant layout → lower logistics costs
- Advertising fixed costs are diluted by a large number of products
- Shared channels for dual categories of instant noodles + beverages → higher output per unit terminal

### Pricing power ★★★☆☆

Master Kong’s pricing power is limited:
- Instant noodles are a price-sensitive category, and raising prices may lead to a decline in sales.
- Beverages face constraints from competitors of equal size such as Uni-President and Nongfu Spring
- With consumption downgrading, there is limited room for price increases.

However, recent data shows that gross profit margin will increase by 1.7pct to 34.8% in 2025, indicating that profitability is improving through product structure optimization (rather than direct price increase).

## 3. "Simplicity" assessment of business model

Master Kong’s business model is **textbook-level simplicity**:

1. **Purchase raw materials** (flour, palm oil, sugar, tea, etc.)
2. **Scale Production** (National Factory Network)
3. **Distribute goods through distribution network** (millions of terminals)
4. **Consumer purchase and consumption** (high-frequency repeat purchases)

There are no complex technology stacks, no new business models to understand, and no policy risks. This is Duan Yongping's favorite "understandable business" - even a primary school student can understand how Master Kong makes money.

## 4. Assessment of the degree of "duty"

### The "duty" of business

Master Kong is very "duty" in its business operations:
- Focused on the main food and beverage business for 30 years and has never significantly crossed over into other industries
- Not involved in real estate, finance and other fields unrelated to the main business
- Product innovation is conservative but steady - not chasing trends, but not falling too far behind either
- Continuous investment in channel construction without any reduction due to short-term profits

### The "duty" of capital allocation

A full-year dividend of 0.66 yuan per share will be distributed in 2024, with a cumulative cash dividend of 3.74 billion yuan and a dividend payout rate of approximately 100%. This reflects:
- Management acknowledges limited growth and returns all profits to shareholders
- Not retaining large amounts of cash for low-return expansion
- Respect for shareholders’ interests

**But there's controversy**: A 100% payout ratio means the company retains no profits to reinvest. If an opportunity arises that requires large investments (such as acquisitions, channel upgrades), the company may lack ammunition.

### Management compensation

In 2024, Wei Hongming's annual salary will be 9.72 million yuan, and Wei Hongcheng's annual salary will be 9.37 million yuan, a year-on-year increase of 25%+. As the second generation of the family, the salary growth rate is faster than the company's growth rate, so we need to pay attention to whether there is a tendency of "self-enrichment". But the absolute level is still within a reasonable range for a company with a market capitalization of 60 billion.

## 6. Core Conclusions

Tingyi Holdings is a **"great mediocre business"** - the business model is extremely simple, the moat (channels + scale) is deep and long-lasting, the cash generation capability is strong, and the management's operating style is honest.

Key judgments from Duan Yongping’s perspective:

**Is this a good business? ** Yes. The channel network and scale effect constitute a real competitive barrier. An ROE of 30% shows that capital efficiency is extremely high.

**Is it the responsibility of management? **Basic duties. Focus on the main business, pay a high proportion of dividends, and do not spend money randomly. However, the opacity of family management and the speed of salary growth require continued attention.

**What are the main concerns? ** Growth stalled. The instant noodle market is shrinking and the beverage market is growing slowly. Tingyi is more like "defending the city" than "attacking the city." This limits the upper limit of long-term compounding returns.

**Will Duan Yongping buy it? ** Might consider. This is in line with his standard of "looking forward to ten years" - Master Kong will most likely still be China's largest instant noodles and tea beverage company in ten years. But prices need to be cheap enough to make up for the lack of growth. 16 times PE may still require patience to wait for a better price.
