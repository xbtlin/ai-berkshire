# Master Kong Holdings (0322.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital return, free cash flow, safety margin

## 1. Profitability analysis

### Revenue and Profit Trends

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit attributable to parent companies (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Gross profit margin |
|------|-------------|------|-----------------|------|--------|
| 2022 | 787.2 | +6.3% | 32.5 | -27% | 30.2% |
| 2023 | 804.2 | +2.2% | 32.0 | -1.5% | 31.6% |
| 2024 | 795.5 | -1.1% | 37.3 | +16.5% | 33.1% |
| 2025 | ~791 | -0.6% | ~45 | +20.5% | 34.8% |
| 2026E | ~850-870 | +7-10% | ~47 | +4.4% | ~35% |

**Key Findings**:

1. **Income has basically stayed at the same level**: In the past four years, it has fluctuated between 79 billion and 80 billion, with almost zero growth.
2. **Profit growth comes from efficiency improvements rather than revenue growth**: Gross profit margin increased from 30.2% to 34.8%, and an improvement of 4.6 percentage points drove profit growth
3. **Is 2025 the "profit high point"? **: There is limited room for improvement in gross profit margin, and profit growth may slow down again in the future.

### Earnings Quality Assessment

**HIGH QUALITY SIGNAL**:
- Profit growth comes from real gross profit margin improvement (product structure optimization + cost control)
- Matching operating cash flow and net profit
- No large one-time gain/loss distortions

**Signs to watch out for**:
- Lack of revenue growth means there is a "ceiling" for profit growth - gross profit margin cannot be improved indefinitely
- Instant noodle sales are declining industry-wide (China's instant noodle consumption dropped from 47.8 billion packs to 43.8 billion packs in 2020-2023)
- Beverages face substitution pressure from sugar-free tea/healthy drinks

## 2. Capital return analysis

### ROE: A leader in China’s consumer goods industry

ROE will reach 30.8% in 2025, an increase of 4.2 percentage points from 2024. That's an **excellent** return on capital.

ROE breakdown (DuPont analysis):
- Net profit margin: about 5.7% (medium to low)
- Asset turnover rate: about 1.6x (very high - typical characteristics of light assets + high turnover)
- Financial leverage: approximately 3.4x (higher)

**The meaning of 30%+ ROE**:
- For every 1 yuan of capital invested, 0.3 yuan of profit is created every year
- Indicates that the company requires little additional new capital to maintain current profitability levels
- This is one of the indicators that Buffett values most - a sign of "good business"

### Why is ROE so high?

1. **Asset-light model**: Food and beverage manufacturing does not require a large amount of fixed assets (compared to heavy industry)
2. **High financial leverage**: The debt ratio is high, but mainly operating liabilities (accounts payable)
3. **High turnover**: The fast-moving characteristics of the product determine the high turnover speed
4. **Brand Premium**: Maintain brand recognition without large capital expenditures

### Capital allocation analysis

**2024 Capital Allocation**:
- Net profit: 3.73 billion yuan
- Cash dividend: 3.74 billion yuan (dividend payout rate 100%)
-Capital expenditure: about 1.5-2 billion yuan (maintenance + moderate expansion)
- Debt level: increased

**Buffett’s perspective**:
- A 100% dividend payout rate is reasonable for "mature companies" - since growth is limited, it is better to return it all
- But please note: the company's debt ratio is relatively high (about 65-70%). Does high dividend payout come at the expense of increased leverage?
- Some critics pointed out that this is a "empty dividend"-dividends that exceed net profit require the use of borrowings

## 3. Free cash flow analysis

### Cash flow structure

Master Kong’s cash flow characteristics:
- Operating cash flow: about 5-6 billion yuan/year (better than net profit - indicating high profit quality)
-Capital expenditure: about 1.5-2 billion yuan/year
- Free cash flow: approximately 3.5-4 billion yuan/year

Calculated based on the current market value of approximately 54 billion yuan:
- FCF Yield ≈ 35-40/540 ≈ 6.5-7.4%

### Cash Flow Sustainability

**Extremely High Sustainability**:
- The demand for instant noodles and tea drinks will not disappear in the short term
- Channel network maintenance costs are stable
- No need for large additional investments

**But there are downside risks in the long term**:
- If instant noodle sales continue to decline, working capital efficiency may decline
- If the beverage market share is eroded, the decline in terminal distribution rate will affect turnover

### The relationship between ROE and PE

How much PE is a company with a 30% ROE theoretically worth?

Use the perpetual growth valuation model:
- Assume g=3% (moderate growth), r=10% (required rate of return)
- Reasonable PE = (ROE-g)/(ROE*(r-g)) = (30%-3%)/(30%*(10%-3%)) = 12.9 times

- Assume g=5% (optimistic), r=10%
- Reasonable PE = (30%-5%)/(30%*(10%-5%)) = 16.7 times

The current PE of 16 times is within the theoretical reasonable range, which is neither obviously undervalued nor overvalued.

But considering a 30% ROE and a 5%+ dividend yield, this discount may be excessive.

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Conservative valuation** (perpetual annuity method):
- FCF 3.5 billion yuan, no growth, discount rate 8%
- Intrinsic value = 35/8% = 43.7 billion yuan ≈ 47 billion Hong Kong dollars
- Current market value of HK$58 billion → Premium of approximately 23%, **no margin of safety**

**Neutral Valuation** (Moderate Growth):
- FCF 3.8 billion yuan, 2% sustainable growth, discount rate 8%
- Intrinsic value = 38/(8%-2%) = 63.3 billion yuan ≈ 68 billion Hong Kong dollars
- Current market value of HK$58 billion → Discount of approximately 15%, **with a moderate margin of safety**

**Optimistic Valuation** (Profit improvement continues):
- FCF 4.5 billion yuan, 3% sustainable growth, discount rate 8%
- Intrinsic value = 45/(8%-3%) = 90 billion yuan ≈ 97 billion Hong Kong dollars
- Current market value of HK$58 billion → Discount of approximately 40%, **adequate safety margin**

## 5. Core issues of Buffett’s framework

### 1. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?

**Yes**. Tingyi's channel network and scale effect are one of the most enduring competitive advantages in China's FMCG industry. Even if the brand ages over time, the value of the channel network will only increase over time (the threshold for construction is getting higher and higher).

### 2. Is management honest and competent?

Medium. The Wei family has a stable management style and has focused on its main business for 30 years. However, the combination of 100% dividend payout ratio + high debt ratio has raised doubts about whether the company is overdrafting. We need to observe the strategic orientation of the new CEO Wei Hongcheng.

### 3. Will this company be stronger or weaker in ten years?

**Most likely similar to today, maybe slightly weaker**. The slow shrinking of the instant noodles market is a general trend, but the beverage business is likely to remain stable. Master Kong is unlikely to disappear, but it is also unlikely to become significantly larger. The value of the channel network may be maintained and the brand value may decline slightly.

### 4. Does the current price provide a margin of safety?

**Has a modest margin of safety**. The combination of 16 times PE + 30% ROE + 5% dividend rate can still provide an annualized return of approximately 7-9% (dividend 5% + retained earnings appreciation 2-4%) considering zero growth. Not "extremely cheap" but "reasonably low".

## 7. Core Conclusions

The financial performance of Master Kong Holdings is the best among the four Hong Kong stock targets: 30% ROE + 5% dividend yield + profit growth + stable FCF, forming a high-quality "cash cow" combination.

**Highlights that Buffett will pay attention to**:
- 30% ROE shows that the business itself is extremely good
- Channel network is the friend of time
- Product demand is changing slowly but will not disappear
- Management returns all profits to shareholders

**What Buffett would be worried about**:
- Stagnant growth puts a cap on long-term compounding
- Is 100% dividend payment + high debt overdrafting the future?
- Brand aging and industry shrinkage are long-term headwinds
- 16 times PE does not count as "breaking bones"

**Overall judgment**: Master Kong is the one "closest to Buffett's standards" among the four targets - good business (high ROE + strong moat), reasonable price (16 times PE), and predictable future (although growth is limited but high certainty). If the price falls back to the 12-13 times PE range, it will become very attractive.
