# Mengniu Dairy (2319.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: Is this company worth the current price? Where is the margin of safety?

---

## 1. Financial quality diagnosis

### 1.1 Income quality

| Year | Operating income (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| 2022 | 925.9 | +5.1% |
| 2023 | 986.2 | +6.5% |
| 2024 | 886.7 | -10.1% |
| 2025 | 822.4 | -7.3% |

Revenue will decline for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, with a cumulative contraction of approximately 16%. Main reasons:

1. Oversupply in the industry leads to intensified price competition
2. Management proactively shrinks inefficient channels and low-margin product lines
3. Growth of high-end products slows down amid consumption downgrade

**Income Quality Judgment**: The decline in income includes active optimization components and is not entirely a passive loss of share. In the second half of 2025, the year-on-year decline in revenue will narrow, with fresh milk, cheese, milk powder and other categories achieving double-digit growth, and the structure is improving.

### 1.2 Profit quality

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Gross profit margin | 37.2% | 39.9% | +2.7pct |
| Operating profit margin | ~5% | ~8% | +3pct |
| Operating profit (100 million yuan) | ~44 | 65.6 | +49% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company (100 million yuan) | ~1.5 (including large impairment) | Expected 30-35 | Significant recovery |

In 2024, due to Bellamy's goodwill impairment of 4.33 billion and Modern Dairy joint venture losses of about 800-900 million, net profit will almost return to zero. In 2025, one-time impairment provisions of 2.2-2.4 billion will continue to be made (idle production capacity and contract assets), but operating profits will improve significantly.

**Key Judgment**: If one-time impairment is excluded, the "normalized" net profit in 2025 will be about 5.0-5.5 billion yuan, corresponding to the current market value of HK$60 billion, and the normalized PE is about 11-12 times. The valuation is not expensive.

### 1.3 Cash flow quality ★★★★★

| Indicators | 2025 | Description |
|------|--------|------|
| Cash flow from operating activities | 8.75 billion | A record high |
| Free cash flow | 6.3 billion | A record high |
| Free cash flow/operating profit | ~96% | High profit content |

Cash flow is the biggest highlight of Mengniu's current finances. Operating cash flow significantly exceeds book profits, indicating:
- Impairment is a non-cash item and does not affect the actual hematopoietic ability
- Working capital management is improving (accounts receivable turnover is accelerating)
-Capital expenditure is shrinking (no longer blindly expanding production capacity)

**Buffett's judgment: This is a company with "ugly profits but good cash". ** Paper profits were distorted by impairments, but the authenticity and quality of free cash flow was high.

## 2. Capital Allocation Assessment

### 2.1 Historical capital allocation (2018-2024): ★★☆☆☆

Mengniu's capital allocation in the past six years can be called a "negative example":

- **Bellamy's Acquisition** (2019, HK$7.1 billion): Australian organic milk powder brand, China business has not yet started, and a goodwill impairment of 4.33 billion will be provided in 2024
- **Miao Ke Landuo Investment** (2022, approximately HKD 3 billion): cheese track layout, integration is currently in progress, Miao Ke Landuo's revenue in 2025 will be 5.6 billion, a year-on-year increase of 16%, which is considered to be an improvement
- **Modern Animal Husbandry Joint Venture**: Raw milk continues to be a drag in the downward cycle, and the joint venture will lose approximately 800-900 million in 2024

The common characteristics of these mergers and acquisitions are: the buying time is at the high level of the industry, the buying price is expensive, and the integration effect is slower than expected. The tendency of management to "spend other people's money without feeling bad" was the biggest capital allocation problem in the past.

### 2.2 Current capital allocation direction (2025-): ★★★★☆

After Gao Fei took office, the capital allocation strategy underwent a fundamental change:

- **Reduce capital expenditure**: No longer blindly expand factories, focus on improving the utilization rate of existing production capacity
- **Depreciation liquidation**: a one-time provision for historical baggage to prepare for the future with ease
- **Shareholder Return**: Launched a three-year dividend plan from 2025 to 2027, with annual dividends steadily increasing (0.52 yuan per share in 2025), while continuing to buy back
- **Focus on the main business**: No longer pursue large-scale mergers and acquisitions, focus on "one body and two wings" to become more sophisticated and in-depth

**The annualized shareholder return rate of dividends + buybacks is about 5-6%** (dividends are about 2 billion + buybacks continue), which is attractive in the current low interest rate environment.

### 2.3 ROE and ROIC analysis

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 (normalized) |
|------|--------|--------|-----------------|
| ROE | ~15% | ~1% (impairment drag) | ~12-14% (estimate) |
| ROIC | ~12% | ~3% (impairment drag) | ~10-12% (estimate) |

Normalized ROIC is about 10-12%, which is above average for a consumer products company. If expense ratio optimization continues, ROIC is expected to rise to 15%+, but it will take 2-3 years to verify.

### 3.2 Valuation comparison with Yili

| Indicators | Mengniu | Yili |
|------|------|------|
| Revenue | 82.2 billion | 115.6 billion |
| Market value | ~60 billion HKD | ~170 billion RMB |
| PE (normalized) | 11-12 times | 18-20 times |
| Market cap/revenue | 0.67x | 1.47x |

Mengniu’s valuation is significantly lower than that of Yili, reflecting the market’s three-tier discount to Mengniu:
1. Discount for the No. 2 player in the industry (gap in scale and efficiency)
2. Management uncertainty discount (turnaround ability has not yet been proven)
3. Hong Kong stock liquidity discount

### 3.3 Historical valuation range

Mengniu's historical median PE is about 25-30 times (in the past ten years), and the current normalized PE of 11-12 times is at a historically low level. Even if a discount should be given to consider the slowdown in growth, the current valuation has fully reflected the pessimistic expectations.

### 3.4 Calculation of margin of safety

**Pessimistic scenario** (revenue continues to decline by 5%, rates do not improve):
- Net profit in 2026 is about 3.5 billion, corresponding to 16 times PE, and a reasonable market value of HK$56 billion (approximately the current level)

**Neutral scenario** (flat revenue, slight improvement in rates):
- Net profit in 2026 is about 4.5-5 billion, corresponding to PE 15 times, with a reasonable market value of HK$67.5-75 billion (up 12-25%)

**Optimistic scenario** (revenue growth resumes by 3-5%, rates improve significantly):
- Net profit in 2026 is about 5.5-6 billion, corresponding to 17 times PE, and a reasonable market value of 93.5-102 billion Hong Kong dollars (up 55-70%)

## 6. Core Conclusions

From a financial perspective, Mengniu shows typical "difficulty reversal" characteristics:

**Pros**:
- Free cash flow of RMB 6.3 billion hit a record high, with extremely high profit content
- Normalized PE is only 11-12 times, P/FCF is less than 10 times, and valuation is at the historical bottom range
- The impairment burden has been basically cleared (4.3 billion in 2024 + 2.2-2.4 billion in 2025), and the 2026 financial statements will be significantly "good-looking"
- Dividends + buybacks provide a bottom line annualized shareholder return of 5-6%

**Risk Factors**:
- Revenue has declined for two consecutive years, with an inflection point yet to be confirmed
- The sales rate of 26.3% is much higher than that of Yili, and the efficiency disadvantage is obvious
- If industry price wars escalate, profit improvements may be interrupted
- The integration of Miokelando and Modern Dairy is still uncertain

**Buffett-style summary**: Mengniu's current price already implies quite pessimistic expectations. If you believe that the dairy duopoly will not be broken, that the raw milk cycle will bottom out in 2026, and that the Gao Fei team can continue to improve operating efficiency, then this is a position with better odds. But "cheap" in and of itself is not a reason to buy - you need to see real evidence of stabilizing revenues and improving rates.
