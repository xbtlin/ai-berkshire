## 2. Investment thesis (Bull Case)

### Core logic: dilemma reversal + valuation repair

1. **Impairment clearing completed**: Provision of 4.3 billion in 2024 + Provision of 2.2-2.4 billion in 2025. The cumulative historical burden of 6.5-6.7 billion has been basically cleared, and the 2026 financial report will be much "cleaner"

2. **The profit turning point is approaching**: Gross profit margin reached a new high of 39.9%, operating cash flow reached a new high of 8.75 billion, and free cash flow reached a new high of 6.3 billion. The operating quality has bottomed out, but it has been covered up by impairments.

3. **Extremely cheap valuation**: Normalized PE is 11-12 times, P/FCF is less than 10 times, which is at an extremely low level in history. Even if it is just mean reversion, there is still 50%+ room.

4. **Raw milk cycle assistance**: The industry is expected to rebalance supply and demand in 2026, and the relief of upstream pressure will reduce the drag on joint ranches

5. **Improved shareholder returns**: Dividends + buybacks annualized at 5-6%, providing the "interest" you are waiting for

## 3. Negative Paper (Bear Case)

### Issues that need to be faced honestly

1. **The gap with Yili may be irreversible**: The revenue difference is 33.4 billion, the market value difference is 3 times, and the fee rate difference is 7.7pct. These gaps may continue to expand in a low-growth environment.

2. **Risk of management change**: The history of six leaders shows that Mengniu’s governance structure has systemic flaws, and Gao Fei may be replaced at any time.

3. **Rate optimization is difficult**: Reducing the sales rate from 26% to 22% means reducing marketing investment by about 3 billion. It is difficult to "subtract" in a fiercely competitive environment.

4. **Uncertain income turning point**: Revenue will decline by a cumulative 16% for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. Whether it can stabilize in 2026 is unknown.

5. **Structural decline in the room temperature milk category**: The growth rate of low-temperature fresh milk far exceeds that of room temperature milk, and 75% of Mengniu’s revenue comes from liquid milk (mainly room temperature)

6. **Hong Kong stock liquidity discount**: As the target of Hong Kong stocks, the speed and extent of valuation recovery may not be as expected.

## 7. Final conclusion

### One sentence summary

**Mengniu is a dilemma reversal opportunity with "fair odds and medium winning rate". ** It's not a great company (moat not deep enough, management not strong enough), but the current price fully reflects these flaws.

### What type of investors is it suitable for?

- Suitable for: Value investors who have the patience to wait for confirmation of the profit turning point in 1-2 years, can accept the poor liquidity of Hong Kong stocks, and have the ability to track the dairy industry
- Not suitable for: Investors who pursue high growth, cannot tolerate short-term fluctuations, and need A-share liquidity

### Current action recommendations

**Included into the recall pool for observation, no positions will be established for the time being. **Reason:

1. The revenue inflection point has not yet been confirmed - wait for the interim results in August 2026 to see whether H1 revenue has stabilized.
2. Rate improvements require more data verification – improvements in one quarter do not constitute a trend
3. Valuation is cheap but lacks short-term catalysts - the next catalyst is 2026 interim results

**Trigger condition**: If the revenue in 2026H1 turns positive year-on-year and the sales rate continues to decline, you can consider establishing an initial position below HK$16.
