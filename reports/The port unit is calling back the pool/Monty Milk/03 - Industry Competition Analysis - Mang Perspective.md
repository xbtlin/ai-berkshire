# Mengniu Dairy (2319.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, competition landscape, competition outcome, reverse thinking
> Core question: What is the final outcome of competition in this industry? Where does Mengniu fit into the end game?

---

## 1. Industry structure analysis

### 1.1 Panorama of China’s dairy industry

| Indicator | Value | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Industry scale | About 500 billion yuan | Retail end in 2025 |
| Industry growth rate | 1-3% | Has entered a low growth stage |
| CR2 (Yili + Mengniu) | >50% | The two-super structure is stable |
| Per capita consumption | ~42kg/year | About 60% of the global average |
| Main categories | Room temperature milk, low temperature milk, yogurt, milk powder, cheese, ice cream | Room temperature milk accounts for the largest proportion |

### 1.2 Industry life cycle judgment

China's dairy industry has entered the "stock optimization period" from the "incremental expansion period":

- **2000-2015**: rapid growth of the industry, "horse racing to enclose land", Mengniu and Yili grew simultaneously
- **2015-2020**: Growth slows down, concentration increases, and duopoly pattern is established
- **2020 to present**: Growth has stagnated or even contracted, entering the stage of stock competition where "profit is king"

This means that the core competitive logic of the industry has changed from "who is growing faster" to "who is more efficient" - which is not very beneficial to Mengniu, because its efficiency has always been weaker than Yili.

### 1.3 The far-reaching impact of the raw milk cycle

Currently it is the bottom range of the raw milk cycle:

- This downward cycle has lasted for 5 years, far exceeding the historical average
- The number of dairy cows in 2024 will drop to 6.3 million, -4.55% year-on-year
- The average price of raw milk from January to October 2025 is 3.06 yuan/kg, down 16.4% from the ten-year average
- The exit of small and medium-sized ranches is accelerating, and the industry’s production capacity has been reduced and entered the deep water zone.
- **2026 is expected to usher in a rebalancing of supply and demand**

The stabilization of raw milk prices has a dual impact on Mengniu:
- **Positive**: The losses of upstream pastures are reduced, and the drag of joint venture modern animal husbandry is reduced.
- **Challenge**: The rising cost of raw milk may squeeze the gross profit of the processing end (but historically the leader can pass this on by raising prices)

## 2. In-depth analysis of competitive landscape

### 2.1 Yili vs Mengniu: What’s the gap?

| Dimension | Yili (600887) | Mengniu (2319.HK) | Gap |
|------|---------------|-----------------|------|
| 2025 revenue | 115.6 billion | 82.2 billion | 33.4 billion difference |
| Market value | ~170 billion RMB | ~55 billion RMB | 3 times difference |
| Sales rate | 18.6% | 26.3% | Difference 7.7pct |
| Marketing efficiency | 1 yuan of revenue: 5.37 yuan | 1 yuan of revenue: 3.81 yuan | Yili is 41% higher |
| Milk powder + cheese | ~36 billion | ~9 billion | 4 times worse |
| Ice cream | 9.8 billion | 5.4 billion | Nearly 1 time difference |
| Net profit margin | ~9-10% | ~4-6% (normalized) | Difference 4-5pct |

**Source of the gap**:

1. **Category structure difference**: Yili milk powder business is 32.7 billion (gross profit margin 41.6%), while Meng milk powder is only about 5 billion. The huge gap in high-margin categories leads to divergence in overall profitability.
2. **Channel efficiency difference**: Yili's terminal coverage is wider and deeper, especially in third-, fourth- and fifth-tier cities and rural markets, with stronger penetration.
3. **Management Continuity Difference**: Yili Pan has been at the helm for 20 years and has strong strategic consistency; Mengniu’s six leaders have been replaced frequently, and strategic swings have led to compromised execution.
4. **Difference in M&A burden**: Yili’s M&A is relatively prudent, while Mengniu’s M&A such as Bellamy’s and Yashili consume a lot of resources.

### 2.2 Second-echelon threats

| Company | Features | Threats to Mengniu |
|------|------|-------------|
| Bright Dairy | Low-temperature milk is strong, Shanghai base camp | Low-temperature track competition |
| New Hope Dairy | Regionalization + fresh milk, M&A integration | Partial market competition |
| Feihe | Baby powder leader | Milk powder track competition |
| Junlebao | Cost-effective positioning, fast growth | Mass price erosion |

The threat to Mengniu from the second tier is limited - the duopoly's brand and channel barriers are sufficient to withstand it. But in the segmented tracks (low-temperature fresh milk, infant formula), the second tier may be more focused than Mengniu.

### 2.3 New Entrants and Substitutes

- **Threat of new entrants**: Very low. Capital barriers (milk sources + factories + brands + channels) in the dairy industry are extremely high, making it almost impossible for new entrants to become competitive in the national market
- **Threat of Substitutes**: Moderate. Plant protein drinks (oat milk, etc.) can replace liquid milk to some extent, but the scale is still small. Functional drinks have a diversion to yogurt

## 3. Deduction of the final outcome of the competition

### 3.1 Ending Scenario 1: "One Super and One Strong" (50% probability)

Yili continues to expand its lead, while Mengniu remains the stable second player in the industry. The two form a pattern similar to Coca-Cola and Pepsi-Cola - the second one is doing well but there is always a gap between market value and profitability. Mengniu is worth 15-18 times PE in this scenario.

### 3.2 Ending Scenario 2: "Re-Catching Up" (Probability 25%)

Under the leadership of new management, Mengniu has successfully improved efficiency and narrowed the gap with Yili. If cheese (Microlando) and internationalization are successful, they may form differentiated growth poles. Mengniu is worth 20-25 times PE in this scenario.

### 3.3 Ending scenario three: "Continue to fall behind" (probability 25%)

Rate optimization fell short of expectations, revenue continued to shrink, and market share was lost to Yili and regional brands. Mengniu has become a mediocre company that is "big but not strong". In this scenario, it is worth 8-10 times PE.

### 3.4 Meng’s reverse thinking: Under what circumstances will Mengniu lose?

**Scenarios where Mengniu will lose:**
- Management once again made a large-scale acquisition mistake (low probability, high-flying style and prudent approach)
- Room-temperature liquid milk continues to be replaced by low-temperature fresh milk and Mengniu’s transformation is ineffective
-Channel changes (community group buying, instant retail) subvert the traditional distribution model
- The price war escalates and the industry enters vicious competition

**Situation where Mengniu will not lose (source of certainty):**
- The duopoly structure is extremely stable, with a huge gap between the third place and the top two
- Brand mentality has been established for 20 years, and consumer switching costs are not zero.
- Even if the efficiency is not as good as Yili, the absolute profitability is still far higher than the industry average
- The rigid demand attribute of dairy products ensures that demand will not disappear

### 4.2 Structural change trends

1. **Low Temperature**: The sales volume of low-temperature pure milk in Q3 2025 was +19.68% year-on-year, and the annual compound growth rate of low-temperature liquid milk was 7.2%, far exceeding that of normal temperature milk by 0.4%
2. **Healthy**: 0 additives, high protein, functional growth
3. **Premiumization**: Consumption stratification intensifies, and high-end and ultra-high-end products grow faster than mass price points
4. **Cheeseification**: The per capita consumption of cheese has grown rapidly from a very low base, and Mioclan has benefited greatly.

Mengniu's position in these trends: cheese has a Microland multi-position (plus points), but its low-temperature fresh milk layout is not as good as Guangming and New Hope (minus points), and room-temperature yogurt is facing downward pressure in the category (minus points).

## 6. Core Conclusions

Munger's summary:

The good news for the industry is that the dairy duopoly is extremely stable, with extremely high barriers to entry and long-term demand. This means that even if Mengniu does not do well enough, it will be difficult to be eliminated.

The bad news for the industry is that in a low-growth environment, the core of competition has changed from "grabbing for incremental growth" to "grabbing for efficiency," and efficiency is precisely Mengniu's shortcoming. As the industry leader, Yili enjoys a positive cycle of "winner takes all" (larger scale -> lower rates -> more profits -> stronger investment). It is very difficult for Mengniu to break this cycle.

**Contrary thinking**: The valuation discount given by the market to Mengniu (more than 40% discount compared to Yili's PE) has fully reflected the above disadvantages. If you don't believe that Mengniu will be eliminated (the probability is extremely low), then the pessimistic expectations implied by the current price may be overdone.

**Final judgment**: Mengniu is a company that "will never die, but it is also difficult to win". The logic of investing in it is not "it will become Yili", but "the current price has fully reflected its disadvantages. As long as it does not get worse, you can make money to repair the valuation." This is a mean reverting investment opportunity, not a growth investment.
