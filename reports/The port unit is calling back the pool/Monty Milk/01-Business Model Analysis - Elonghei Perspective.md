# Mengniu Dairy (2319.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business model essence, user value, differentiation, sustainability
> Core question: Is this a good business? Where is the moat?

---

## 1. The essence of business

### 1.1 User needs analysis

Dairy products are urgently needed consumer goods. China's per capita dairy consumption is about 42 kilograms per year, which is only 60% of the global average and one-third of that of developed countries. There is still room for long-term growth. However, in the short term, the market has entered the stage of stock game from incremental competition, and the overall industry growth rate will slow down to low single digits in 2025.

Mengniu's core user value is to provide safe, nutritious and convenient dairy products with reliable brand endorsement. This demand is real, lasting and irreplaceable - people will not stop drinking milk, but they may drink less or change brands.

### 1.2 Dismantling of business model

Mengniu's business model can be summarized as follows: upstream control of milk sources, midstream processing, downstream channels, and brand premiums. **

- **Income structure**: Liquid milk accounts for about 75% (mainly room temperature milk), ice cream about 7%, milk powder about 6%, cheese about 4%, and others about 8%
- **Gross profit margin**: 39.9% in 2025 (a record high), indicating that product pricing power is improving
- **Net profit margin**: The operating profit margin is about 8%. The net profit margin after deducting impairment is even lower. There is still room for improvement in efficiency.
- **Cash flow**: Cash flow from operating activities in 2025 will be 8.75 billion and free cash flow will be 6.3 billion, both reaching record highs.

This is a business that is asset-light but asset-heavy - brands and channels are asset-light, but milk sources and factories are asset-heavy. Mengniu controls the upstream through joint ventures (Modern Dairy, etc.) and builds its own factories for processing. It is essentially a branded consumer goods company with a certain depth of the industrial chain.

### 1.3 The logic of making money

The core logic for dairy companies to make money is: **scale effect + brand premium - channel investment. **

- The larger the scale, the lower the unit production cost (factory utilization, procurement negotiation)
- The stronger the brand, the higher the retail terminal price (deluxe is 50-100% more expensive than ordinary milk)
- But maintaining scale and brand requires continuous marketing investment and channel maintenance

Mengniu's sales expenses in 2025 are 21.6 billion, and the sales rate is 26.3%, which is higher than Yili's 18.6%. This shows that for every dollar of revenue Mengniu earns, it needs to spend more money on marketing and channels - the efficiency is inferior to Yili, which is the biggest weakness of the current business model. **

## 2. Moat assessment

### 2.1 Brand moat ★★★★☆

**Deluxe** is the largest single product in China's dairy industry and occupies an absolute leading position in the high-end white milk market. In the 20 years since the brand was founded in 2005, "Not all milk is called Deluxe" has penetrated deeply into the minds of consumers. Deluxe's ​​brand premium is solid and its position in the gift scene is hard to shake.

**Chunzhen** has strong recognition in the room-temperature yogurt market, but faces the threat of substitution from low-temperature yogurt and functional drinks.

Overall, Mengniu's brand matrix has broad consumer awareness in second- and third-tier cities, but amid the new trends of high-end and health-oriented, the speed of brand upgrades needs to be observed.

### 2.2 Channel moat ★★★☆☆

Mengniu has a nationwide dealer network and terminal channels, but this is not an exclusive resource – Yili’s channels have wider and deeper coverage. The channel barriers for dairy products are: cold chain logistics, terminal freezer placement, and dealer relationship maintenance, all of which require continuous investment rather than once and for all.

In recent years, the rise of new channels such as e-commerce and community group buying has impacted the traditional distribution system, diluting Mengniu’s channel advantages.

### 2.3 Scale moat ★★★☆☆

Mengniu has annual revenue of 82.2 billion and is China's second largest dairy company. Scale brings advantages in procurement negotiation and production capacity utilization, but compared with Yili's size of 115.6 billion, the scale advantage is not outstanding. In the core category of room temperature milk, Mengniu's market share is slowly losing.

### 2.4 Industrial chain moat ★★★☆☆

Mengniu controls some upstream milk sources through associated companies such as Modern Dairy (which holds about 27% of the shares), but this "double-edged sword" has brought huge impairment losses during the downward cycle of raw milk. The depth of the industrial chain is a long-term advantage, but a short-term burden.

## 3. Duan Yongping’s questioning

### 3.1 What would happen to users without this company?

Users will turn to other brands such as Yili, Guangming, and New Hope. The dairy market is not a "you have to" situation - user stickiness mainly comes from brand habits and channel convenience, rather than irreplaceable functional value. But Deluxe is irreplaceable in high-end gift-giving scenarios.

### 3.2 Will this company still be around ten years from now?

Most likely still there. The dairy duopoly structure is stable, and the possibility of Mengniu being eliminated as the second place is extremely low. But "still" and "doing well" are two different things - the key is whether we can narrow the gap with Yili, not just survive.

### 3.3 Is management doing the right thing or the easy thing?

After taking office, Gao Fei chose to liquidate impairments (not easy, not good-looking, but correct), control expense ratios (painful but necessary), and focus on profits rather than scale (going against Mengniu's past inertia). The overall direction is correct. But the implementation effect will take 2-3 years to be verified.

### 3.4 How about user reputation and repurchase?

Deluxe users have high loyalty and a stable repurchase rate. However, the quality perception of Mengniu's basic liquid milk products is not much different from that of Yili, and users may switch brands according to promotions and channel convenience. Chunzhen is facing pressure from the overall market contraction of Mengniu yogurt category.

## 5. Core Conclusions

The nature of Mengniu's business is good - the demand for dairy products is long-lasting, the branded consumer goods model is mature, and the duopoly structure provides basic profit guarantees. However, as the second largest company in the industry, the problem faced by Mengniu is: the radical mergers and acquisitions in the past decade have not brought about the expected synergies, but have instead lowered the overall operating efficiency.

Duan Yongping's core judgment: ** Mengniu is a "not bad but not the best" business. ** Its moat exists but is not deep enough - the brand is there but not as irreplaceable as Moutai, the channels are there but not as efficient as Yili, the scale is there but not as big as Yili. If you want to invest in the dairy industry, Mengniu is not the "only choice" but the "cost-effective choice" - provided that management can prove the sustainability of profit improvement.

**The biggest concern**: The huge gap between the sales rate of 26.3% and Yili's 18.6% shows that Mengniu's brand and channel efficiency still have systemic disadvantages. This is not a problem that can be solved in one or two years.

**Biggest Expectation**: If the Gao Fei team can reduce the sales rate to 22-23% while maintaining stable revenue, net profit will have significant elasticity.
