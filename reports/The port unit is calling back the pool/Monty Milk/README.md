## Research background

Mengniu is one of the duopoly companies in China's dairy industry (the other is Yili), with revenue of 82.24 billion yuan in 2025. The company is in a period of strategic adjustment after a management change: the burden of M&A impairment left by the predecessor (Bellamy's 4.33 billion goodwill impairment, Modern Dairy joint venture losses, etc.) is being cleared, and the new president Gao Fei is pursuing the "one body, two wings" strategy, shifting from scale expansion to profit quality improvement. The current PE is about 15 times, and the market is waiting for confirmation of the profit turning point in 2026.

## Core Contradiction

1. **When will the profit turning point be confirmed? ** Operating cash flow in 2025 will hit a record high (8.75 billion), but revenue will decline 7.3% year-on-year, and impairment clearing has not yet been completed.
2. Is the gap between ** and Yili reversible? ** The revenue gap has widened to 33.4 billion, and the market value gap is nearly 3 times
3. **Can the raw milk cycle help? ** The industry expects supply and demand to rebalance in 2026, which will be beneficial to downstream industries
4. **Do the management capabilities match? ** Gao Fei was born in "Old Mengniu", but has not yet proven his ability to turn around.
