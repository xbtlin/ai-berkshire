# Zijin Mining (2899.HK) Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: Is the money earned real money? Is the current price expensive?

## 1. Profitability analysis

### 1.1 Core data of income statement (2025 annual report)

| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Operating income | 349.079 billion yuan | 303.6 billion yuan | +14.96% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 51.777 billion yuan | 32.05 billion yuan | +61.55% |
| Gross profit margin | 27.73% | — | +36.09% (relative growth) |
| Net profit margin | 18.28% | — | +40.93% (relative growth) |
| Earnings per share | 1.95 yuan | 1.21 yuan | +61.16% |

**Key Observations**:

1. **Revenue increased by 15% and net profit increased by 62%**. The profit growth rate far exceeded the revenue growth rate, indicating that operating leverage was fully released. The core reason is that gold and copper prices have risen sharply (gold prices +25%, copper prices +10%), while the company's cost side has grown much slower than its revenue side.

2. **Strong performance in Q4 single quarter**: Q4 revenue was 94.88 billion yuan (+29.54% year-on-year), Q4 net profit attributable to the parent company was 13.914 billion yuan (+80.86% year-on-year), and growth accelerated in the fourth quarter.

3. **The Q1 performance forecast for 2026 is even more amazing**: Net profit attributable to the parent company is expected to be 20-22 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 100%-130%. If this pace is maintained throughout the year, net profit is expected to exceed 80 billion yuan in 2026.

### 1.2 ROE decomposition-DuPont analysis

The ROE in 2025 will be approximately 28%, ranking first among global metal mining companies. Its composition:

- **Net profit margin is about 18.3%** - the largest contribution, reflecting the strong profitability under high gold and copper prices
- **Asset turnover rate is about 0.72 times** - The mining industry is characterized by heavy assets. The turnover rate is low but at a reasonable level among peers.
- **Equity multiplier is about 2.13 times** - the leverage level corresponding to the asset-liability ratio of 53%, which is medium to high

**Comparison with global peers**:
- Freeport McMoRan (Freeport): ROE about 22-25%
- Southern Copper: ROE about 25-28%
- BHP Billiton (BHP): ROE about 15-20%

The core driving forces behind Zijin's world-leading ROE are **higher net interest rates** (low-cost advantage) and **moderate leverage**.

**Evaluation ★★★★★ (5/5) - Profitability is at the top level among global mining companies, and ROE has a high gold content. **

## 2. Cash flow quality

### 2.1 Operating cash flow

| Indicators | 2025 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|----------|
| Net operating cash flow | About 75 billion yuan (full-year estimate) | +54.31% |
| Operating cash flow per share | 2.84 yuan | +54.31% |
| Operating cash flow/net profit | About 1.45 times | — |

**Operating cash flow/net profit > 1**, indicating that the net profit has sufficient cash support and is not "paper profit". Operating cash flow in the first three quarters was 52.1 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 48.62%, and the growth rate matched the profit growth rate.

### 2.2 Free cash flow

- Capital expenditure plan for 2025 is approximately NT$15 billion
- Operating cash flow is about 75 billion yuan - Capital expenditure is 15 billion yuan = free cash flow is about 60 billion yuan

Free cash flow is abundant, but it should be noted that **M&A expenditures are not reflected in regular capital expenditures**. M&A expenditures in less than one quarter of 2026 will exceed 46.2 billion yuan. If M&A is regarded as capital investment, the actual free cash flow that can be distributed to shareholders will be greatly reduced.

**Evaluation ★★★★☆ (4/5) - The quality of operating cash flow is very high, but the high M&A expenditure needs attention. **

## 3. Balance sheet health

### 3.1 Core indicators

| Indicators | Data |
|------|------|
| Total assets | 483 billion yuan (September 2025) |
| Asset-liability ratio | 53.01% |
| Interest-bearing liabilities | RMB 169.7 billion |
| Monetary funds | 68.09 billion yuan |
| Trading financial assets | 9.8 billion yuan |
| Net interest-bearing liabilities | About 91.8 billion yuan |

### 3.2 Evaluation of asset-liability ratio

The asset-liability ratio of 53% is at a medium level in the mining industry. But please note:

**Front**:
- A decrease of 2.18 percentage points compared with the end of 2024, and the trend is improving
- Interest-bearing liabilities of RMB 169.7 billion correspond to annual net profit of RMB 51.8 billion. Interest-bearing liabilities/net profit is only 3.3 times, and debt repayment pressure is controllable.
- Monetary funds + financial assets are approximately 77.9 billion yuan, with ample short-term liquidity

**Requires Attention**:
- Part of the 110 billion yuan in mergers and acquisitions was financed with debt
- During the mining downturn, if profits are cut in half but liabilities remain unchanged, debt repayment pressure will rise rapidly.
- Global operations result in some assets being located in high-risk countries, and there is potential impairment risk in asset quality.

**Rating ★★★★☆ (4/5) - The current balance sheet is healthy, but we need to be vigilant about stress testing during the downturn of the cycle. **

## 4. Capital Allocation Assessment

### 4.1 Dividend Policy

- Historical cumulative dividends are 59.3 billion yuan, with a cumulative dividend rate of 37.89%
- In recent years, the cash dividend ratio has been 25%-30%
- Calculated based on 30% dividend in 2025, the dividend will be approximately 15.5 billion yuan

The dividend rate is not high, but considering that the company is still in the expansion period, the return rate of reinvesting profits in high-quality mineral resources (ROIC 15.5%) is much higher than depositing it in the bank or distributing it to shareholders. This strategy of "less dividends, more investment" is reasonable at the current stage.

### 4.2 ROIC vs WACC

- ROIC：15.5%
- Estimated WACC: 8%-9%
- ROIC/WACC = 1.7-1.9 times

ROIC is significantly higher than WACC, indicating that the return created by the company for every 1 yuan of capital invested far exceeds the cost of capital. **The company is creating value, not destroying value. **

### 4.3 Return on M&A Capital

The cumulative mergers and acquisitions from 2020 to 2026 will be approximately 110 billion yuan. To evaluate the success of these mergers and acquisitions, you need to look at:
- The current operating status of the acquired projects (the Kamoa copper mine has performed well and has become the fourth largest copper mine in the world)
- Bor Copper Mine significantly improved operating indicators after Zijin took over
- Some new M&A projects have not yet been produced and will take time to be verified.

**Evaluation ★★★★☆ (4/5) - The overall capital allocation is excellent, and ROIC far exceeds the cost of capital, but the large scale of mergers and acquisitions requires continuous tracking. **

### 5.5 Two sides of valuation

**Reasons for Cheap**:
- 21 times PE is lower than the historical average of 27 times
- Profit is expected to increase significantly in 2026, and the forward-looking PE is only 14-15 times
- ROE ranks first in the world, high-quality growth

**Reasons for not being cheap**:
- Low PE for mining stocks at the top of the cycle may be a "value trap"
- The current gold and copper prices are at historical highs. If prices fall back, profit growth expectations will be revised significantly downwards.
- PB 5.7 times is not low, implying that the market is fully pricing the sustainability of high ROE
- Historically, mining stocks have had their lowest PEs when commodity prices peaked, as the market expected profits to fall.

**Evaluation ★★★☆☆ (3/5) - The absolute valuation is not expensive, but it needs to be distinguished between "really cheap" and "fake cheap at the top of the cycle". **

## 7. Core financial judgment

Buffett focuses on three core issues when evaluating companies:

1. Is the money **earned real? ** --Yes. Operating cash flow/net profit > 1, cash flow quality is very high. ★★★★★

2. Can ** continue to make money? ** - Depends on two variables: commodity price (uncontrollable) and cost control (controllable). Zijin has done its best in the controllable dimensions, but the uncontrollable dimensions are highly volatile. ★★★★☆

3. **Is the current price reasonable? **——If you believe that gold and copper prices will remain high for a long time or continue to rise, 21 times PE is very cheap. If you believe that commodity prices will revert to the mean, the current valuation is only "reasonable" or even "expensive." ★★★☆☆

**Financial Valuation Overall Rating ★★★★☆ (4/5) - The financial quality is first-rate, but the safety margin of the valuation depends on your judgment of the commodity price cycle. **
