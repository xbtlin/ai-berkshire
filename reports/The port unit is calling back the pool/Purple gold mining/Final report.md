## 1. Core conclusion

**Zijin Mining is one of the best mining companies in the world, but "excellent company" does not mean "an investment worth buying at any price." **

The company's competitive advantages (low-cost system, resource reserves, M&A capabilities, production capacity growth) are real and sustainable. However, investment returns in mining stocks ultimately depend on two variables: the quality of the company's own operations and the direction of commodity prices. Zijin is at the top of the industry in terms of the first variable, but the second variable is beyond anyone's control.

## 4. Positive arguments—why Zijin deserves attention

### 4.1 Resource endowment continues to increase

Copper resource reserves are 120 million tons (top 3 in the world), and copper equity reserves have increased by 805% in the past ten years. Against the background that the global rate of new mine discovery continues to decline, Zijin’s resource base will only become more and more precious. Good mines are non-renewable assets. Once you get them, you get them.

### 4.2 High certainty of production capacity growth

2026-2028 is the period of concentrated release of Zijin’s production capacity:
- The second phase of Julong Copper Mine has been put into operation in January 2026, and copper output will increase from 190,000 tons to 300,000-350,000 tons.
- The third phase of the Kamoa copper mine was put into operation ahead of schedule, becoming the fourth largest copper mine in the world.
- Copper production target for 2028 is 40-47% higher than 2025
- Gold production target for 2028 is 44-56% higher than 2025

Even if commodity prices flatten, production growth can support results.

### 4.3 Low cost advantage provides downside protection

The project construction cost is more than 20% lower than that of international counterparts, and the construction period is shortened by 30%-50%. This means:
- When commodity prices fall, Zijin is the last mining company to lose money
- When commodity prices rise, Zijin has the greatest profit elasticity
- The low-cost capability is systematic (five-ring unified management model of ore flow) and will not disappear when an individual leaves.

### 4.4 Gold and copper prices have structural support

- Copper: Long-term incremental demand brought about by new energy transformation (electric vehicles, photovoltaics, power grids), Goldman Sachs predicts a copper price of US$12,200/ton in 2026
- Gold: The central bank de-dollarizes gold purchases, geopolitical hedging demand, and fiscal deficit monetization. Goldman Sachs predicts the average price in 2026 will be US$4,978 per ounce.
- Multi-mineral layout (gold + copper + zinc + lithium) provides cycle hedging

### 4.5 ROE ranks first in the world, with excellent capital efficiency

The ROE of 28% is not only far ahead among Chinese mining companies, but also ranks first among global metal mining companies. ROIC (15.5%) is significantly higher than WACC (8-9%), indicating that the company is truly creating value for shareholders.

## 5. Counterarguments—why you need to be cautious

### 5.1 The commodity cycle is the fate of mining stocks

No supercycle in history has lasted forever. Copper prices fell from US$10,000 in 2011 to US$4,300 in 2016 (-57%), with mining companies' profits and stock prices suffering a devastating blow. The current copper price of $12,000 is in the historically high area, and the same is true for gold price of $5,000.

**Key question**: Is the money you earn from "business operations" or the money from "commodity price increases"? If it’s the latter, will the money be sustainable?

### 5.2 Geopolitical risks are escalating

Zijin’s overseas layout is concentrated in geographically sensitive areas such as the Democratic Republic of the Congo, West Africa, and South America. Resource nationalism is spreading around the world (Ghana has raised royalties, the Democratic Republic of the Congo has demanded contract re-signing, and Argentina has boycotted environmental protection), and the game between China and the United States has further increased the overseas risks of Chinese mining companies.

These risks cannot be eliminated by diversification - a major political event (such as forced tax increases or nationalization of the Kamoa copper mine) could wipe out tens of billions in profits.

### 5.3 Uncertainty caused by founder’s retirement

Chen Jinghe is one of the best entrepreneurs in China's mining industry in the past 40 years. His retirement (January 2026) is the largest non-financial risk event in Zijin's history. Although the new chairman Zou Laichang has deep qualifications, he has not yet been tested in an environment where he can independently take charge of the overall situation, especially in the mining down cycle.

### 5.4 PE trap at the top of the cycle

Mining stocks tend to have the lowest PE at the top of the cycle - because profits are at their peak. 21 times PE does not seem expensive, but if profits fall back to 40 billion yuan next year, PE will become 24 times; if it falls back to 30 billion yuan, PE will become 32 times. ** Cheapness is just an appearance, the real risk is the decline in profits. **

### 5.5 The double-edged sword of M&A expansion

The scale of 110 billion yuan of mergers and acquisitions is eye-catching, but it is also worrying. The industry failure rate of mining M&A is about 50%. Even if Zijin's success rate is higher than the industry average, in such a large-scale merger and acquisition, it is inevitable that some projects will not meet expectations. More importantly, the large amount of funds used for mergers and acquisitions means that the dividends that shareholders can receive are relatively limited (only 25-30%).

## 6. Investment framework suggestions

### 6.1 Strategies in different scenarios

| Scenario | Your judgment on commodity prices | Investment strategy |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| Bullish on gold and copper | I believe that new energy transformation + de-dollarization will support the continued rise of gold and copper prices | You can configure it at the current price and enjoy the double-click of "capacity growth + price increase" |
| Neutral | Believe that gold and copper prices will maintain the current level or fluctuate slightly | You can wait for a correction of 10-15% before intervening, and the safety margin will be more sufficient |
| Bearish/uncertain | Unable to judge the direction of commodity prices | Wait for a clear downward cycle (when PE passively rises to 30-40 times) before considering counter-cyclical buying |

### 6.2 Position recommendations

Regardless of your view on the commodity cycle, mining stocks should not be used as core positions (it is recommended not to exceed 10-15% of the total position) for the following reasons:
- Commodity prices are so volatile that even the best mining companies cannot avoid them
- Geopolitical risks have "tail risks" - low probability but high impact
- Management transition increases short-term uncertainty

### 6.3 Signals that need to be tracked continuously

**Positive Signs**:
- Q1-Q2 output and profits in 2026 will continue to exceed expectations
- New management demonstrates independent judgment in M&A or operations
- Julong Copper Mine Phase II and Kamoa Phase III successfully reached production
- Gold and copper prices remain high or continue to rise

**Bearish Signal**:
- Copper prices fell below US$10,000/ton
- Congo (DRC) or other countries introduce policies that are unfavorable to Zijin
- Impairment on new acquisitions
- Production targets promised by management failed to be achieved
-The asset-liability ratio rose again to above 55%

---

## 7. One sentence summary

**Zijin Mining is the absolute king of the mining industry, but investing in mining stocks is investing in the commodity cycle - what you need to judge is not only "whether this company is good enough", but also "whether now is a good time to buy mining." **

If you believe the long-term supercycle in copper and gold is continuing, Zijin is one of the best carriers. If you do not have a strong directional judgment on the commodity cycle, it may be a safer choice to keep observing and wait for a better buying time.
