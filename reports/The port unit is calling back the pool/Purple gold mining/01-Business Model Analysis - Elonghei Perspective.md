# Zijin Mining (2899.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, moat, business model sustainability
> Core question: Is this business good? Can you continue to make money?

---

## 1. What is the essence of business?

The essence of Zijin Mining's business is to "look for ore, buy ore, mine and sell ore" - to obtain metal resources from the ground, process them into standardized commodities (copper concentrate, gold ingots, zinc ingots, etc.) and sell them at international commodity prices.

This is a typical **resource-based business** with several core features:

**1. Products are undifferentiated, and pricing power is in the hands of the market rather than the company**

Copper is copper and gold is gold. There is no situation where "Purple Gold Copper" is better than "BHP Billiton Copper". LME copper prices and COMEX gold prices determine the income ceiling of all mining companies. What enterprises can do is not to sell more expensively, but to dig cheaper**.

**2. Profit = commodity price - production cost, the essence is cost competition**

When the copper price is US$10,000 per ton, a mining company with a cost per ton of copper of US$4,000 earns US$6,000, a mining company with a cost of US$7,000 earns US$3,000, and a mining company with a cost of US$11,000 loses money. When the cycle goes down, low-cost mining companies survive and high-cost mining companies are eliminated.

**3. Asset-heavy, long-term, high capital intensity**

It usually takes 5-10 years for a mine to go from exploration to production, with an investment of billions or even tens of billions of yuan. The investment in the second phase of Julong Copper Mine exceeds 10 billion yuan, and the cumulative investment in Kamoa Copper Mine is astronomical. This determines that mining is a business of "spending big money first and then collecting it slowly".

**Comments: This is not an "easy" business. There is no brand premium, no network effects, and no switching costs. But if we can achieve the lowest cost in the entire industry, we can survive at the bottom of the cycle and make huge profits at the top of the cycle. **

★★★☆☆ (3/5)——The business model itself is average, but Zijin’s execution ability is better than it.

## 2. What did Zijin Mining do right?

### 2.1 "The five rings of ore flow are unified" - a low-cost systematic methodology

Zijin Mining’s core competitiveness is not a single point of technology, but a set of systematic low-cost methodologies**. The company regards the five links of geological exploration, mining, mineral processing, smelting and environmental protection as an "ore flow", and coordinates and optimizes the efficiency of each link.

The specific performance is:
- **The project construction period is 30%-50% shorter than that of international peers**: The third phase of the Kamoa Copper Mine was put into production half a year ahead of schedule, and the second phase of the Julong Copper Mine was completed in January 2026 as planned.
- **Construction cost is more than 20% lower than that of international counterparts**: Zijinhua spends less money to build the same copper mine
- **Low-grade ore turned waste into treasure**: The Zijinshan Gold Mine was initially considered a "tasteless" mine. The company built it into China's largest gold mine through its independently developed combined mining, processing and smelting process. This ability allows Zijin to see the value that others cannot see.

### 2.2 Countercyclical M&A - "Be greedy when others are fearful"

Zijin Mining’s M&A record is textbook-level:

- From 2020 to 2025, the cumulative spending on domestic and overseas mergers and acquisitions will be approximately 65 billion yuan
- In less than a quarter of 2026, new M&A funds will exceed 46.2 billion yuan
- The cumulative total amount of mergers and acquisitions reached approximately 110 billion yuan

The key is not how much you spend, but the timing and price of buying. Zijin is good at acquiring high-quality resources when the mining industry is in a downturn and asset prices are cheap. About 50% of the company's total resources come from independent exploration, and the other 50% comes from mergers and acquisitions, and the cost of mergers and acquisitions is generally lower than the industry average.

### 2.3 Dispersion of multiple mineral types – reducing the cyclical impact of a single variety

Gross profit structure in 2025:
- Gold: 40.89%
- Copper: 34.49%
- Zinc lead + others: 24.62%

Gold and copper often rise and fall out of sync (copper is an industrial metal and gold is a safe-haven asset), and the multi-mineral layout provides a certain degree of cyclical hedging. At the same time, the company is laying out lithium mines to reserve incremental reserves for the new energy era.

## 3. Moat assessment

### 3.1 Resource Barrier ★★★★☆

Mineral resources are natural monopoly assets. Zijin Mining currently owns:
- Total copper resource reserves are 120 million tons, ranking among the top 3 in the world
- Gold resources are approximately 3,973 tons, ranking among the top 6 in the world
- Zinc and lead resources amount to 12.98 million tons, ranking third in the world
- Lithium (LCE) resources are 17.88 million tons, ranking among the top 10 in the world

Over the past decade, Zijin's copper equity reserves have increased by 805% cumulatively, while the copper reserves of major global peers have remained flat or declined. **The growth rate of resources itself is a moat** - there are fewer and fewer good mines, and those who get them have more and more advantages.

### 3.2 Technical Barriers ★★★☆☆

Low-grade ore development technology, biometallurgy technology, refractory metallurgical ore recovery technology, etc., do constitute certain barriers. However, mining technology is more replicable than Internet technology and is not an absolute barrier in the long run.

### 3.3 Scale Barrier ★★★★☆

Mining is a typical industry with economies of scale. Zijin is already the fourth largest copper mine producer and the sixth largest gold mine producer in the world. The scale effect is reflected in many aspects such as procurement negotiation, technology research and development sharing, and financing costs.

### 3.4 Management and cultural barriers ★★★☆☆

The "Purple Gold Culture" brought out by Chen Jinghe - pragmatic, low-cost, and strong in execution - is a kind of soft barrier. But the founder has retired, and whether this culture can be passed on is a question mark.

** Comprehensive evaluation of moat ★★★★☆ (4/5) - Resources + scale constitute a strong barrier, but we need to be wary of the erosion of the commodity cycle. **

## 4. Sustainability of business model

### Positive factors

1. **Resource scarcity continues to increase**: There are fewer and fewer large-scale copper and gold mines discovered around the world, and the grade of existing resources is declining. Zijin’s resource reserves will become increasingly valuable as time goes by.

2. **Structural growth of copper demand**: The demand for copper from "green transformation" such as new energy vehicles, photovoltaics, wind power, and power grid upgrades is a long-term increase. Goldman Sachs predicts that LME copper prices will reach US$12,200 per ton in 2026.

3. **Production capacity is still growing**: The company plans to produce 1.5-1.6 million tons of mined copper in 2028 (an increase of about 40-47% from 1.09 million tons in 2025), and 130-140 tons of mined gold (an increase of about 44-56% from 90 tons in 2025). There is clear room for growth.

4. **Safe Hedging Demand for Gold**: Global geopolitical uncertainty has increased, and the central bank continues to increase its holdings of gold, supporting the long-term central rise in gold prices. Goldman Sachs expects the average price in 2026 to be US$4,978 per ounce.

### Negative factors

1. **Unpredictable commodity prices**: Gold and copper prices are at historically high levels. If the global economic recession causes copper prices to fall sharply, company profits will shrink sharply. This is the eternal fate of mining stocks.

2. **M&A integration risk**: Can a 110 billion yuan M&A scale produce expected returns? There are many historical lessons from large-scale mergers and acquisitions.

3. **Complexity of Overseas Operations**: The company operates in more than ten countries including the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Serbia, Argentina, and Mali. The political, legal, and community environments of each country are different, and the management radius is getting wider and wider.

**Sustainability evaluation ★★★★☆ (4/5) - Medium and long-term resource demand is improving, but cyclicality is a lingering shadow. **

## 5. List of key issues

1. **If the price of copper falls from US$12,000 back to US$7,000, how much will Zijin’s profits shrink? ** Historically, the price of copper fell from US$10,000 in 2011 to US$4,300 in 2016, and the profits of mining companies generally fell by more than half. How much decline can Zijin's low-cost advantage withstand?

2. **Does **countercyclical M&A ability rely on Chen Jinghe personally? ** Can new management maintain the same courage and judgment into the next mining winter?

3. What is the return on capital for the **110 billion merger and acquisition? ** Return on investment needs to be tracked on a project-by-project basis, not just at aggregate numbers.

4. Is **lithium mine layout an opportunity or a trap? ** Lithium carbonate equivalent output in 2025 is only 25,000 tons, with a target of 270,000-320,000 tons in 2028. The price of lithium has dropped from 600,000 yuan/ton to less than 100,000 yuan/ton. Is it the right time to expand production on a large scale?

## 6. Core judgment from Duan Yongping’s perspective

Look at it using Duan Yongping’s framework of “doing the right thing” and “doing things right”:

**Do the right thing**: Focus on the main business of gold and copper resources, not engage in diversification (Zijin has never been involved in real estate or finance), adhere to a low-cost strategy, and counter-cyclical mergers and acquisitions - these strategic choices are all right.

**Get things right**: The five-circle management system for ore flow, project construction efficiency, and cost control capabilities are indeed excellent at the execution level.

**But there is one thing that needs to be faced honestly**: Duan Yongping prefers "differentiated" businesses in the consumer category (such as Apple and Moutai), and is naturally cautious about "homogeneous" businesses in the resource category. Even if Zijin Mining achieves the best in the industry, its fate is still determined by commodity prices. **You can become the best mining company, but you can't escape the mining cycle. **

**Business Model Overall Review ★★★★☆ (4/5) - One of the best players in the mining circuit, but the circuit itself has a ceiling. **
