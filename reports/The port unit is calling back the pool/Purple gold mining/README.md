## Research background

Zijin Mining is China's largest metal mining company and one of the fastest growing mining companies in the world. The company takes gold, copper and zinc as its core, supplemented by silver, lithium, molybdenum and other minerals, and has important mining investments in more than ten countries around the world. In 2025, net profit attributable to parent companies will exceed 50 billion yuan for the first time, reaching 51.8 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 62%; ROE ranks first among global metal mining companies.

Research entry point: The company is in the triple overlapping period of "founder transition + production capacity expansion + high gold and copper prices". A valuation of 21 times PE may not seem expensive, but the cyclical nature of mining stocks requires a more prudent evaluation framework.

## Research Framework

This study adopts a four-dimensional parallel analysis method:

| Documents | Analysis Dimensions | Core Questions |
|------|----------|----------|
| 01-Business model analysis | Business essence and moat | Is this business good? Can it be sustained? |
| 02-Financial Valuation Analysis | Digital Verification and Valuation | Is the money earned real money? Is it expensive? |
| 03-Industry competition analysis | Competitive landscape and industrial chain | Who benefits from the industry structure? Where are the barriers? |
| 04-Risk Management Assessment | Risk and Human Factors | What is the biggest risk? Is the management trustworthy? |
| Final report | Comprehensive judgment | Is it worth holding for a long time? |
