# China Pacific Insurance (2601.HK) – Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Profit quality analysis

### Profit Structure (2025)

**Net Profit vs Operating Profit**:
- Net profit attributable to parent companies: 53.505 billion yuan (+19.0%)
- Operating profit attributable to parent companies: 36.523 billion yuan (+6.1%)
- The difference between the two mainly comes from investment income fluctuations (equity market fluctuations)

**Why is operating profit more important? **
- Operating profit excludes short-term investment fluctuations and can better reflect the "real profitability" of the insurance business
- About 17 billion of the 53.5 billion net profit comes from "excess returns" on the investment side, and its sustainability is questionable
- 36.5 billion operating profit is a more reliable foundation

### Profit-driven life insurance business
- New business value (NBV): 18.609 billion yuan (+40.1%) - this is the core "incremental value" indicator
- New business value rate: 19.8% (+3.2pct) - creating a value of 19.8 yuan for every 100 yuan of new premiums
- Embedded value of RMB 465.479 billion (life insurance) - the "locked" value of existing business

### Property and Casualty Insurance Business Profit
- Underwriting profit: 4.836 billion yuan (+81.0%)
- COR: 97.5% (down 1.1pct) - for every RMB 100 in premium collected, the compensation + expenses are RMB 97.5, leaving an underwriting profit of RMB 2.5
- Property and casualty insurance premiums increased slightly by 0.1% but profits surged by 81% - indicating that "quality is far better than quantity"

### Profit quality judgment ★★★★☆

**Front**:
- NBV +40% is a strong leading indicator - it means future profit growth is guaranteed
- Property and casualty insurance COR 97.5% with clear improvement trend
- Operating profit +6.1% is a good performance in a low interest rate environment

**Negative**:
- Net profit fluctuates greatly (highly dependent on capital market performance)
- Interest rate margins are compressing in a low interest rate environment
- Net profit of 53.5 billion is not as repeatable as operating profit of 36.5 billion

## 2. Embedded value analysis (core of insurance valuation)

### The meaning of embedded value (EV)
- Group embedded value: RMB 613.365 billion (+9.1%)
- Life insurance EV: 465.479 billion yuan (+10.2%)
- EV = Adjusted Net Assets + Effective Business Value (i.e.: the present value of profits that existing policies will generate in the future)

### Current PEV valuation
- The market value of H-shares is approximately HK$300-350 billion (estimate)
- Corresponds to about 0.5-0.6 times PEV
- That is: the market only prices half of the embedded value

### What does PEV 0.5x mean?
- The market believes that CPIC’s embedded value is “watery” – are the actuarial assumptions too optimistic?
- Or the market believes that in a low interest rate environment, the effective business value will shrink
- Or the market places a zero premium on "new business creation" capabilities

### If EV is real and reliable, 0.5x PEV means 100% upside potential
- This is the core game of insurance stock investment: Do you believe in the actuarial assumptions of EV?

## 3. PE valuation analysis

### Current PE level
| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| PE (based on net profit) | ~6-7x |
| PE (based on operating profit) | ~9-10x |
| Dividend Yield | ~3-4% |
| PEV | ~0.5-0.6 times |

### Valuation rationality judgment ★★★★☆

**PE 6-7x means**:
- Market implicit assumptions: profit growth is very low or profit quality is discounted
- For a company with ROE 12-13%, PE should be ≥10 times
- Reasons for the discount: (1) large profit fluctuations; (2) concerns about low interest rate spreads; (3) Chinese insurance stocks as a whole are not favored by the market

**Safety Margin Analysis**:
- 0.5x PEV is equivalent to "getting" all future new business value for free
- Even assuming that EV is "falsely high" by 20-30%, the actual PEV is still only 0.6-0.7 times
- Limited downside (PEV is difficult to stay below 0.4x for a long time)

## 4. Capital efficiency and dividends

### ROE analysis
- ROE ~12-13%: an excellent level in China’s insurance industry
- Significantly higher than major state-owned banks (9-10%)
- Close to but slightly lower than Ping An (13-15%)

### Dividend policy
- 1.15 yuan per share in 2025
- Dividends distributed approximately 11.063 billion yuan
- Accounted for 20.7% of net profit attributable to parent company
- Low payout ratio - insurance companies need to retain capital to maintain solvency

### Dividend Outlook
- A 20% dividend payout rate is normal in the insurance industry
- But compared to banks’ 30%+, insurance companies’ dividends are not generous.
- With the growth of embedded value and sufficient solvency, there is room for improvement in dividend rate

## 5. Cash flow and capital requirements

### The particularity of "cash flow" of insurance companies
- Insurance companies do not have stable cash flow of "net interest income" like banks
- Income is premium (collected in advance) + investment income (uncertain)
- Payouts are claims (lagged and indeterminate amounts)

### Float quality
- Life insurance float has a long term (10-30 years) and the cost is relatively fixed (predetermined interest rate)
- Property insurance float has a short term (1 year), but the cost is extremely low (negative cost when COR<100%)
- CPIC Property & Casualty's COR of 97.5% means that the cost of float is negative - this is the best state for the insurance business
