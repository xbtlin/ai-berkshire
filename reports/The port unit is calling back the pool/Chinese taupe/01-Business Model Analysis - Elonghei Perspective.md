#China Pacific Insurance (2601.HK) - Business model analysis

## 1. The essence of business

China Pacific Insurance is a **two-wheel drive comprehensive insurance group**:

**First round: Life insurance (China Pacific Life Insurance)**
- Sales of long-term life insurance/health insurance/annuity products
- Acquire customers through agent channel + bancassurance channel
- Core profit model: Collect premiums → Earn interest differentials from investment (interest differentials) + dead margins/fee differences in actuarial assumptions

**Second Round: Property Insurance (Pacific Insurance)**
- Mainly auto insurance + non-auto insurance (corporate property insurance, liability insurance, agricultural insurance, etc.)
- Profit model: underwriting profit (premium - compensation - expenses) + investment income

**Round 3 (Emerging): Asset Management**
- CPIC Asset Management 3.77 trillion yuan
- Investment management for life insurance/property insurance funds
- At the same time, undertake third-party outsourcing funds

### What is the essence of the insurance business?

Buffett said: "Insurance companies are a 'reverse bank' - they collect money first and pay later."

The core of CPIC is to obtain float at a lower cost and then earn excess returns through investment. The "predetermined interest rate" of life insurance is the cost of float, and the investment income exceeding the predetermined interest rate is the profit.

## 2. Moat Assessment ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Strengths:**

- **Agent channel barriers**: CPIC Life has hundreds of thousands of agents. Although the total number is shrinking, the quality is improving. Building and maintaining a fleet of agents takes decades
- **Brand Trust**: Insurance is a "trust business" - customers need to believe that the company will still be there and pay compensation 30 years from now. CPIC’s 30-year history provides this trust
- **Solvency Capital Requirements**: Insurance license + huge reserves + solvency regulation = extremely high barriers to entry
- **Scale effect of property and casualty insurance**: The third largest property and casualty insurance company, large enough to spread risks and reduce unit costs

**Weaknesses:**

- **Challenges facing the agent model**: The way the younger generation purchases insurance is changing (online trend)
- **The investment side is affected by market fluctuations**: interest rate decline + equity market fluctuations → investment returns are uncertain
- **Product homogeneity**: Life insurance product terms are similar, and differentiation among companies is limited.
- **Not as comprehensive as Ping An of China**: lags behind Ping An in terms of technology, medical and health ecosystem

## 3. "Simplicity" test of business model

The "simplicity" of the insurance business:
- **Positive**: The essence is simple - collect premiums, manage risks, and make investments. It was like this a hundred years ago, and it will still be like this a hundred years from now.
- **Negative**: The complexity of actuarial assumptions makes it difficult for outside investors to truly understand the source of profits. The splitting of interest margin, dead margin and fee margin requires professional knowledge.

Duan Yongping might say: "I understand the nature of insurance, but it is difficult to judge whether CPIC's actuarial assumptions are conservative or aggressive. This makes me uncomfortable."

**Conclusion**: The nature of business is simple, but the "black box" nature of profitability makes it difficult for ordinary investors to penetrate. New business value (NBV) is the closest metric to "true profitability".

## 4. User value and "differentiation"

Value created by CPIC for customers:
- **Long-Term Guarantee Promise**: The credibility of delivering on the promise of paying out decades later
- **Agent Service**: Face-to-face product explanation, claims assistance, policy management
- **Comprehensive Finance**: Life insurance + property insurance + asset management one-stop

**CPIC’s differentiated direction (from 2025)**:
- **Great Health Care Strategy**: Not just "selling insurance", but "insurance + retirement community + health management"
- **"AI+" Strategy**: Digitally empower agents and operational efficiency
- **Internationalization**: Expand overseas markets

**Comparison with Ping An of China**:
- Ping An is far ahead in technology and medical ecosystem
- CPIC is more "pure" - focusing on its main insurance business
- "Pure" is both an advantage (focus) and a limitation (lack of thickness of the second growth curve)

## 5. Long-term sustainability ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Pros:**
- China’s insurance penetration rate is still significantly lower than that of developed countries (premium/GDP ~4% vs. the United States ~11%)
- Aging trend drives demand for pension annuities and health insurance
- "Changhang Airlines Transformation" (the transformation of agents from quantity to quality) has achieved results: NBV growth rate is 40%+
- New business value rate of 19.8% indicates that the quality of new orders continues to improve

**Disadvantages:**
- The low interest rate environment suppresses investment returns (interest spread risk)
- A reduction in scheduled interest rates means the product becomes less attractive
- The long-term decline of the agent channel (although CPIC "compensates for quantity with quality", the overall trend is to decrease)
- Equity market fluctuations have a huge impact on net profit (the 19% increase in net profit in 2025 will largely depend on investment income)
