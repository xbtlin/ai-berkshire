## Research Summary

JD Health is China's largest pharmaceutical e-commerce and Internet medical platform. Relying on JD's self-built logistics system and quality trust endorsement, its revenue scale is far ahead. 23 times PE corresponds to a growth rate of 26%, PEG is less than 1, and the valuation is relatively reasonable. The core question is: Can high growth be sustained? How deep is the moat of Internet medical care?

## Core investment logic

**Reasons to be long:**
- Revenue growth rate of 26% + profit growth rate of 36%, strong and accelerating growth
- JD.com’s logistics and quality reputation constitute barriers to differentiation
- 23 times PE corresponds to high growth rate, PEG<1, reasonable valuation
- The penetration rate of pharmaceutical e-commerce is still increasing, and the industry dividend period is not over yet

**Reasons for shorting:**
- ROE is only 6%, capital efficiency is low
- The profit margin ceiling of the self-operated heavy model is not high
- Meituan’s instant delivery poses new threat
- Rely on JD.com Group Ecosystem
