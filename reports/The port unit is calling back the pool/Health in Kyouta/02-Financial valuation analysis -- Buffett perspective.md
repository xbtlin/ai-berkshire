# JD Health (6618.HK) – Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality

### 1.2 Income structure

| Sector | 2024 | 2025 | Growth rate |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Merchandise revenue | 48.8 billion | 60.9 billion | +24.8% |
| Service revenue | 9.4 billion | 12.6 billion | +34.1% |

The growth rate of service revenue (34%) was higher than that of goods revenue (25%), and its proportion increased from 16.1% to 17.1%. The direction is correct but the lifting speed is slow.

### 1.3 Analysis of growth acceleration

Revenue growth in 2025 will jump from 8.7% in 2024 to 26.3%. This "acceleration" deserves attention:
- Is there a one-time factor? (Such as a change in the rhythm of a major promotion)
- Is it sustainable? (Dual drive of increased penetration + share expansion)
- Can 20%+ be maintained in 2026 under a high base?

## 2. In-depth analysis of profitability

### 2.1 Breakdown of gross profit margin

| Sector | Gross profit margin | Proportion of revenue | Gross profit contribution ratio |
|------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Self-operated products | ~20% | 83% | ~69% |
| Service revenue | ~45% | 17% | ~31% |

Although service revenue only accounts for 17% of revenue, it contributes approximately 31% of gross profit. This explains why every 1 percentage point increase in the proportion of service revenue has a significant positive impact on the overall profit margin.

### 2.2 Operating leverage effect

The most eye-catching data in 2025 is that operating profit will surge from 1.47 billion to 3.8 billion (+158.5%). This means strong operating leverage:
- Revenue growth of 26% led to profit growth of 158%
- The dual effects of fixed cost dilution + gross profit margin improvement
- Indicates that the company is in the stage of "accelerated profit release"

### 2.3 ROE decomposition

| Factor | Value | Evaluation |
|------|------|------|
| Net profit margin | ~8.9% | Above average, continuing to improve |
| Asset Turnover | ~1.0x | Medium |
| Equity Multiplier | ~1.3x | Low Leverage |
| ROE | ~6% | Low, but in a rapid climbing period |

Reasons for low ROE:
1. After the IPO financing, a large amount of cash (tens of billions) was left in the account, which lowered the efficiency of the use of net assets.
2. Profit rates are still climbing and have not yet reached a steady state.
3. Low leverage operation (no need for borrowing)

**ROE improvement path:**
- Profit margin increased from 9% to 12%: ROE +2-3 percentage points
- Carry out buybacks/increase dividends (reduce idle cash): ROE +2-3 percentage points
- Potential steady-state ROE: 12-15%

### 2.4 Profit Quality

**Positive Signs:**
- Operating cash flow is strong and significantly improved
- The gap between Non-IFRS profits and IFRS profits is narrowing
- No large-scale non-recurring gains
- Net profit margin in 2025 will hit the highest level since listing

**Issues needing attention:**
- Share-based payment expenses are still the main item of Non-IFRS adjustment
- Inventory management and price drop risk of self-operated goods
- Related transactions with JD.com (logistics costs, etc.)

## 3. Capital allocation analysis

### 3.1 Cash Reserves

The company holds a large amount of cash and short-term investments (estimated at 30-40 billion) on its books, and these idle funds are currently not used efficiently.

**Potential uses of cash:**
1. Start a buyback plan (increase ROE and value per share)
2. Increase dividend ratio
3. Strategic mergers and acquisitions (offline pharmacies, medical institutions, etc.)
4. Continue to invest in infrastructure construction

### 3.2 R&D and technology investment

The company continues to invest in AI diagnosis and treatment, intelligent medication recommendation, supply chain optimization and other fields. Technology expenses account for about 3-4% of revenue, and the absolute amount is about 2.5-3 billion per year.

### 3.3 Shareholder Return

At present, dividends and buybacks are not strong. It makes sense for a high-growth company to reinvest profits, but not increasing shareholder returns even after growth slows is a governance issue.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 Relative valuation

| Company | PE | Revenue growth | Net profit margin | PEG |
|------|-----|----------|--------|-----|
| JD Health | 23x | 26% | 8.9% | 0.64 |
| Ali Health | 39x | 13% | 6.4% | 1.1 |
| Meituan | 25x | 22% | ~8% | ~1.1 |
| Pinduoduo | 10x | 58% | ~25% | 0.17 |

JD Health's PEG (0.64) is attractive among similar Internet companies, reflecting the market's relatively conservative pricing of its valuation.

### 4.2 Historical Valuation

When JD Health was listed (at the end of 2020), its PE exceeded 100 times, and it has since experienced a sharp correction. The current PE of 23 times is in the extremely low range in history, which has fully digested the "ebb of market enthusiasm for high-valued Internet medical care."

### 4.3 Thoughts on DCF valuation

Assumptions:
- Revenue CAGR in the next five years: 18-22%
- Net interest rate gradually increases to 11-12%
- Terminal growth rate: 5%
- Discount rate: 10%

Under neutral assumptions, DCF valuation has 20-30% upside potential from the current market capitalization. This shows that the current pricing is conservative and contains a certain margin of safety.

### 4.4 Key Valuation Drivers

1. **Sustainability of revenue growth**: Can it maintain 20%+ on a high base?
2. **Room for profit margin improvement**: The path from 9% to 12%
3. **Increase in service revenue ratio**: Change from 17% to 25%+
4. **ROE improvement**: driven by profit margin + buyback
5. **The market’s re-understanding of the “Internet Healthcare” valuation system**

### 4.5 Revaluation Catalysts

- Announced large-scale buyback plan (similar to JD.com)
- Net interest rate exceeded 10%
- Breakthrough in commercialization of AI medical products
- Included in Hang Seng Index constituent stocks

## 5. Financial risks

### 5.1 Risk of growth slowdown ★★★☆☆
The 26% growth rate in 2025 may have high base effects and one-time factors. If growth falls back to 15-18% in 2026, market expectations may need to be revised downwards.

### 5.2 Risk of profit margin peaking ★★☆☆☆
The net profit margin of 8.9% is already relatively high among self-operated e-commerce companies. If competition intensifies (Meituan/Alibaba price war), profit margins may stop improving or even fall back.

### 5.3 Related party transaction risks ★★☆☆☆
The scale of related-party transactions with JD Group in logistics, technology, traffic, etc. is large, and attention needs to be paid to whether pricing continues to be fair.

### 5.4 Management change risk ★★★☆☆
Jin Enlin resigned and Cao Dong took over. The new CEO comes from a financial background, and it is uncertain whether there will be major adjustments in strategic direction.
