## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **Strong and accelerating growth**: Revenue +26%, profit +36%, operating profit +158% in 2025, significant operating leverage effect
2. **Logistics barriers are difficult to copy**: JD Logistics’ self-built pharmaceutical warehouse distribution + cold chain system is the result of ten years of investment and cannot be copied by competitors in the short term.
3. **Reasonable Valuation**: 23x PE, PEG 0.64, attractive among high-growth Internet companies
4. **Profit margin is still on the rise**: The path towards moving from 8.9% to 12% is clear (the proportion of service revenue increases)
5. **Industry penetration rate is still low**: There is huge room for pharmaceutical e-commerce to grow from 15% to 30%+

### Bear Case

1. **ROE is low (6%)**: A large amount of idle cash on the account, and capital efficiency is not high
2. **Growth may slow down**: The 26% growth rate in 2025 may be driven by one-time factors, and high base pressure in 2026
3. **Meituan Threat**: Instant delivery model creates diversion in acute medication scenarios
4. **Management Change**: New CEO Cao Dong has yet to prove himself
5. **Self-operated model profit margin ceiling**: Is there room for significant improvement in the net profit margin of 8-9%?

## Valuation and buying conditions

### Current valuation judgment

23 times PE corresponds to 26% growth rate and 36% profit growth rate, PEG 0.64. From the perspective of growth stock investment, the current valuation is reasonably low, providing a certain margin of safety.

DCF valuations suggest 20-30% upside potential (neutral assumption).

### Buying advice

| Strategy | Conditions | Positions |
|------|------|------|
| Current price | PE 23x | Initial position can be established (3-4%) |
| Buy on callback | PE 18-20x | Increase position to 5-6% |
| Better than expected catalysis | Profit rate breaks through 10% | Hold and observe |
| Consider selling | PE >30x or growth rate <12% | Gradually reduce positions |

### Hold expectations

- Expected annualized return in 3 years: 15-20%
- Source of return: profit growth (20%+) + valuation maintenance/slight increase
-Downside protection: zero leverage + cash on account + rigid demand industries

1. The growth rate may drop from 26% to 18-20% in 2026, and expectations need to be adjusted
2. Meituan’s rapid growth in buying drugs may create a diversion in the OTC category
3. The new CEO’s strategic direction may be different from that of his predecessor.
4. If JD Group adjusts its resource investment in the health business
5. Uncertainty about Internet medical regulatory policies
6. Low ROE is an objective fact, and capital efficiency needs time to improve.

## Final judgment

JD Health is an investment opportunity with "good track + good location + good price":

- **Good track**: pharmaceutical e-commerce + Internet medical care, high certainty of growth
- **Good Location**: Absolute leader, deep logistics barriers, huge scale lead
- **Good Price**: 23x PE corresponds to 26% growth, PEG 0.64

It is not a perfect investment (ROE is low, management changes, profit margin ceiling needs to be broken), but it provides the best risk-return ratio among the Hong Kong stock medical and health sectors. For growth value investors pursuing annual returns of 15-20%, JD Health deserves to be taken seriously.
