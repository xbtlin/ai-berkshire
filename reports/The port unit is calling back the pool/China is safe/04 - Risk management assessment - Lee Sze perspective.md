**Quantified impact:**
- Combined investment return of 6.3 per cent in 2025 (over-expected), but 2026Q1 dropped sharply to 0.2 per cent (deannualized)
- The company's long-term investment return assumption of 4.5 per cent — if the actual long-term is below this figure, the value in it needs to be reduced
- Average combined investment return of 4.9 per cent over the past 10 years is above the 4.5 per cent assumption, but the trend is worsening

** Deductive factors:**
- Management clearly stated that there was no margin loss - there were a large number of high-yield long-term bonds in stock operations as "barrel stones"
