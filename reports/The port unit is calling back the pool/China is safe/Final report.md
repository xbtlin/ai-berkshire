## Investment Judgment: Buy

**Judgment level:** Buy (open positions in batches)

**Confidence:** 75%

**Reason:**

It's an asymmetric bet. On the downside, there is a bottom line of 6%+ dividend yield and extreme valuation protection of 0.6 times P/EV, and on the upside, there is room for NBV's 30% growth rate and valuation recovery. The weighted expected annualized return is approximately 11%, which is clearly attractive in the current asset shortage environment.

## Core investment logic

### Why buy? (Three core arguments)

**1. Valuations are at historical extremes and most risks have been priced in**

Hong Kong stocks' P/EV of 0.6 times means that the market believes that Ping An's embedded value needs to be discounted by 40% - which implies a pessimistic expectation of "permanent value destruction". But the fact is: the company's net assets exceeded one trillion for the first time, dividends have increased for 14 consecutive years, and NBV has experienced high growth for two consecutive years. There is a clear mismatch between valuations and fundamentals.

Even under the most pessimistic "Japanization" scenario (probability 15%), the stock price has room to fall by about 30-50%; while under the base scenario (probability 35%), there is room for growth of 40-80%. The odds are clearly in favor of the buyer.

**2. Dividends from life insurance reform are being realized at an accelerated pace**

This is the most certain catalyst in the medium term (2-3 years). The number of agents has been reduced from 1.2 million+ to 350,000, the per capita production capacity has increased significantly, and NBV has increased by nearly 30% for two consecutive years. NBV still maintained a growth rate of 20.8% in 2026Q1. The NBV of the bancassurance channel surged by 138%, opening up a new growth dimension. The customer penetration rate of the medical elderly care ecosystem continues to increase, further enhancing product differentiation and customer stickiness.

This is not a cyclical rebound, but a structural improvement - the efficiency gains brought about by reforms are irreversible.

**3. Comprehensive financial moat is the "only" one in China's financial industry**

13 core licenses, 251 million customers, 99% retention rate - no other company in China has such a comprehensive financial platform. The value of this platform is even more prominent in the era of low interest rates: when single product profit margins are compressed, cross-selling and customer lifetime value become key sources of differentiation.

### Why hesitate? (Three main concerns)

**1. Low interest rates are a real structural threat**

This is the most uncertain variable in this study. If China really moves towards long-term low interest rates (<2% ten-year treasury bonds), the investment yield of insurance funds will continue to be under pressure, and the embedded value may need to be lowered. The sharp drop in the comprehensive investment return rate in 2026Q1 to 0.2% (non-annualized) is already a warning sign.

**Rebuttal:** Management has proactively lowered its assumptions to 4.5%, and the more prudent assumptions have increased the credibility of EV. The transformation of products into participating insurance is also gradually transferring investment risks. The 4.9% average return over the past 10 years is still higher than assumed.

**2. Ma Mingzhe’s succession is pending**

The 69-year-old founder said that although the joint CEO system provides a framework, it has not yet been tested in practice. The succession history of Chinese financial companies is littered with failure cases.

**Rebuttal:** Ping An has the highest degree of institutionalized management among Chinese financial companies and is not a pure "one-person company." And Ma Mingzhe may still be in charge for quite some time.

**3. Financial transparency of insurance companies is naturally low**

Embedded value relies on actuarial assumptions, the risk exposure of the investment portfolio is not completely transparent, and the information disclosure of related party transactions needs to be improved. This reduces the accuracy of the valuation.

**Rebuttal:** This is a common problem in the industry, not unique to Ping An. Moreover, Ping An's information disclosure quality is among the best in the industry.

## Key assumptions and triggering conditions

### Buying hypothesis (the premise for the core logic to be established)

| Assumptions | Current status | Verification method | Failure signal |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| NBV maintains 15%+ growth | 2025: +29.3%, 2026Q1: +20.8% | Quarterly NBV data | NBV growth rate <10% for two consecutive quarters |
| Investment rate of return does not deteriorate significantly | Full year 2025 6.3% | Quarterly investment rate of return | Full year comprehensive investment rate of return <3.5% |
| Dividends continue to grow | 14 consecutive years of growth | Annual report dividend plan | Dividends reduce or stop growing |
| Stable management | Joint CEO system in operation | Announcement of executive changes | Sudden resignation of core executives |
| P/EV returns to the mean | Current 0.6x, historical average 1.0x | Stock price trend | P/EV continues to decline below 0.4x |

### Sell trigger conditions

If any of the following is true, the position needs to be re-evaluated:

1. **NBV has recorded negative growth for two consecutive quarters** - a sign that the dividends from life insurance reform have disappeared.
2. **Newly large impairments (>20 billion yuan)** —— Asset quality problems reappear
3. **Dividend reduction** - the hardest signal of deteriorating cash flow
4. **Ma Mingzhe suddenly resigned and his succession was not smooth** - governance risks broke out
5. **P/EV rises back to more than 1.2 times** —— Valuation repair is completed and no longer cheap
