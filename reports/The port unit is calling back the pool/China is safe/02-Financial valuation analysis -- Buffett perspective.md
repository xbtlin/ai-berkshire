## 2. Financial Trends in 10 Years

### Profit growth trajectory

| Stage | Period | Characteristics | Driving force |
|------|------|------|--------|
| Rapid growth period | 2015-2018 | Net profit increased from 54.2 billion to 107.4 billion yuan | Agent expansion + investment bull market |
| Peaking and falling period | 2019-2020 | Net profit remains at 140-160 billion yuan | Agent bonus peaks |
| Labor pain adjustment period | 2021-2022 | Net profit drops to RMB 70-100 billion | China Fortune Land Development impairment + life insurance reform + epidemic |
| Restoration and recovery period | 2023-2025 | Net profit rebounds from 85.6 billion to 134.8 billion yuan | Reform dividend + improvement in investment income |

**Key turning point:** 2021 is a watershed year. The explosion of China Fortune Land Development resulted in an impairment of approximately RMB 54 billion, coupled with a large-scale liquidation of agents in the industry (Ping An’s agents dropped from 1.2 million+ to approximately 350,000), resulting in a sharp decline in profits. But starting from 2023, the effect of "reducing staff and increasing efficiency" will begin to show - the per capita production capacity of agents has increased significantly, and NBV has returned to the growth track.

### NBV growth trend (key indicators)

NBV (New Business Value) is a leading indicator of a life insurance company's future profitability:

- 2020: NBV 49.5 billion yuan (high)
- 2021-2022: continued decline
- 2023: bottoming out
- 2024: NBV 28.6 billion yuan, +28.8%
- 2025: NBV 36.9 billion yuan, +29.3%
- 2026Q1: NBV 15.6 billion yuan, +20.8%

**Evaluation:** NBV has grown at a rate of nearly 30% for two consecutive years, and maintained a growth rate of more than 20% in 2026Q1. This is the most hard-core evidence of the effectiveness of life insurance reform.

## 3. Intrinsic value estimation

### Method 1: Embedded value method (P/EV)

Embedded Value (EV) is the core valuation anchor of insurance companies.

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Group EV in 2025 | About 1.5 trillion yuan (EV +5.7% year-on-year, life insurance EV +11.2% year-on-year) |
| Current market capitalization (Hong Kong stocks) | About RMB 840 billion |
| P/EV | About 0.56-0.60 times |
| Historical P/EV center | 1.0-1.2 times (last 5 years) |
| If P/EV returns to 0.8 times | the corresponding market value is approximately 1.2 trillion yuan, an increase of approximately 43% from the current level |
| If P/EV returns to 1.0 times | the corresponding market value is approximately 1.5 trillion yuan, an increase of approximately 79% from the current level |

**Key assumptions risk:** The calculation of EV relies on actuarial assumptions. Ping An has prudently lowered its long-term investment return assumption to 4.5% and risk discount rate to 9.5% in 2025. Assuming that the lowered EV is more reliable - this is actually a good thing, because it means that the "moisture" of the current EV has been squeezed out.

## 4. Judgment of safety margin

### Multi-dimensional safety margin test

| Dimension | Current value | Historical mean | Margin of safety |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| P/EV | 0.56-0.60x | 1.0-1.2x | Large safety margin |
| PE | 6-9x | 8-10x | There is a certain margin of safety |
| PB | ~0.75x | 1.2-1.5x | Large safety margin |
| Dividend Yield | ~6%+ | 3-4% | High dividends provide downside protection |

### Positive arguments (supporting adequate margin of safety):

1. **P/EV is in the historical extreme value area. ** 0.6 times P/EV means that the market believes that Ping An’s embedded value must be discounted by 40% to be worth buying - which implies the expectation of "permanent value destruction". As long as this expectation does not come true, there is room for repair in the valuation.

2. The dividend rate of **6%+ provides a "safety cushion". ** Even if the stock price does not rise, the dividend rate of more than 6% is far higher than the deposit interest rate and government bond yield. In the context of China's asset shortage, this yield is attractive.

3. **NBV’s high growth has not yet been reflected in the valuation. ** NBV has increased by nearly 30% for two consecutive years, indicating that the company's "new" value creation capabilities are recovering rapidly, but P/EV is still at a historically low level - this is a mismatch.

4. **The impairment of China Fortune Land Development has been basically absorbed. ** Of the RMB 54 billion impairment, RMB 43.2 billion has been completed, and the impact of the remaining RMB 10.8 billion on trillion-level net assets has been controllable.

### Negative arguments (the margin of safety may not be as large as it seems):

1. **Low interest rates may be "permanent." ** If China's long-term interest rates continue to fall (similar to Japan), insurance companies' investment yields will be under long-term pressure. The comprehensive investment return rate in 2026Q1 has dropped to 0.2% (non-annualized), a significant decrease of 1.1 percentage points year-on-year.

2. The credibility of **EV itself is controversial. ** The calculation of embedded value relies on a large number of actuarial assumptions. If the actual investment return rate is lower than the assumption of 4.5% for a long time, EV will need to be lowered - the "margin of safety" of P/EV is just a mirror image.

3. **Bank subsidiaries drag down valuations. ** Ping An Bank's net profit has continued to decline. If the bank's business continues to deteriorate, it may need to replenish capital at the group level and dilute shareholder returns.

4. **The difference between net profit vs operating profit. ** Net profit fell by 7.4% in 2026Q1 but operating profit increased by 7.6% - no problem in the short term, but if the investment side is under long-term pressure, the two will eventually converge.
