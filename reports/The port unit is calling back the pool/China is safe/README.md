## Core conclusion

**Overall Rating: ★★★★ (4/5)**

Ping An of China is China's largest comprehensive financial group, with 251 million individual customers, 13 core financial licenses, and 6.49 trillion yuan in insurance funds. In 2025, the net profit attributable to the parent company will be 134.8 billion yuan, and the equity attributable to the parent company will exceed one trillion yuan for the first time. The new business value (NBV) of life insurance increased by 29.3% year-on-year, and the dividends of the reform are being realized.

**Core contradiction: Valuation discount vs. operational improvement. ** Hong Kong stocks are currently trading at about 0.6-0.75 times P/EV (embedded value), static PE is about 5-9 times, and PB is about 0.75 times - this is a price that implies the expectation of "permanent value destruction". But the facts are: the company has increased dividends for 14 consecutive years, with cash dividends of 48.9 billion yuan in 2025; life insurance reform has achieved results, and NBV growth leads the industry; the management of spread loss risk has made it clear that it does not exist.

**Key judgment:** The current valuation is closer to an "opportunity of the century" than a "value trap", but it is necessary to pay close attention to the pressure on the investment side and the digestion of real estate exposure in the low interest rate environment. It is recommended to build positions in batches within the current valuation range. The core assumption is that life insurance reforms continue to be realized + valuations return from extremely low levels to the mean.

**Main risks:** Long-term low interest rates erode investment returns, residual risks from real estate exposure, and uncertainty during the management succession transition period.
