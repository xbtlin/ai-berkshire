## One sentence conclusion

Electric Power Industry is an overvalued "super dividend stock" - the combination of 4.6% dividend yield + 2% growth lacks a margin of safety at a PE of 22 times. If you want to configure public utilities in the Yangtze River system, Yangtze River Infrastructure is a better choice.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. Dividend payout ratio >90%, dividends are extremely certain
2. Global utility asset portfolio, excellent diversification
3. Guaranteed by experience in operation and management of the Yangtze River system
4. If merged with Cheung Kong Infrastructure, small shareholders will gain room for premiums

### Bearish logic
1. PE 22 times is obviously too expensive compared to ROE 5-6%
2. Profit growth is only 2%/year, with actual zero growth
3. There is a high degree of overlap with Yangtze River Infrastructure, and the value of independent investment is questionable.
4. The 4.6% dividend rate lacks relative attractiveness in a high interest rate environment
5. Revenue fell sharply by 16%, causing operational concerns
