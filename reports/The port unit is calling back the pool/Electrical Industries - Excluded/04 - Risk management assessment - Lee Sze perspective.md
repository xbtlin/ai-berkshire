# Electric Power Industry (0006.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

## 1. Core risks

### Risk Matrix

| Risk type | Severity | Probability of occurrence | Comprehensive rating |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|

### Special risks: The long-term dangers of stagnant growth
The profit growth of the electric energy industry is only 2%/year, which is close to the inflation level. This means real growth is zero. In the long run, if income cannot be increased through new investments, ROE will gradually decline under the erosion of inflation.

## 2. Management evaluation

The management of Electric Power Industry is handled by the CK Infrastructure team (because CK Infrastructure holds 75%).

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Front:
1. A very high dividend payout ratio of >90% reflects extreme honesty to shareholders
2. Excellent investment discipline (same as CK Infrastructure)
3. Global layout to spread risks

Negative:
1. Lack of independent growth strategy
2. High dependence on Yangtze River Infrastructure makes it lack of autonomy
3. Revenue fell by 16% with no clear response measures

## 3. Scenario analysis

### Optimistic scenario (probability 20%)
- Global interest rate cuts, valuation increases
- Merger of Cheung Kong Infrastructure and Power Industry (minority shareholders receive premium)
- New investment leads to growth
- Share price rises 15-25%

### Baseline scenario (probability 60%)
- Steady profit growth of 2%
- Dividend maintained at 4.6%
- Annualized return is about 6-7%
- Small fluctuations in share price

### Pessimistic scenario (probability 20%)
- Rising interest rates suppress valuations
- Impairment of gas distribution assets
- PE shrinks to 18-20 times
- Share price fell 10-15%

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Risk Management Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The risk characteristics of the electric energy industry are highly similar to those of Yangtze River Infrastructure - low risk, low return, and high certainty. However, its PE is higher (22 times vs 20 times), its growth is slower (2% vs 3%), and its price/performance ratio is not as good as that of Yangtze River Infrastructure.

From Li Lu's perspective: The electric energy industry is not an "exciting" investment opportunity. Its existence value is to provide an option for investors who pursue "highest dividend yield + lowest worries". But with the current PE of 22 times, the price paid is too high.
