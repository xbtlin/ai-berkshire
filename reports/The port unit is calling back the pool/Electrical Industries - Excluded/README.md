## Investment Highlights
1. Highly related to Yangtze River Infrastructure and stable asset quality
2. The dividend payout ratio is extremely high (>90%) and the certainty of interest collection is good
3. Global utility portfolio
4. High credit rating and low financing cost

## Core Risks
1. PE 22 times is extremely expensive compared to ROE 5-6%
2. Revenue fell sharply by 16%
3. Growth is approaching stagnation
4. Highly overlaps with Yangtze River Infrastructure and lacks independent investment value
