# Kingsoft (3888.HK) Investment Research

## Company Profile

Kingsoft Corporation Limited is China's leading software and Internet services company. It was founded by Qiu Bojun in 1988 and was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2007. The company's business covers three major sectors: office software, online games, and cloud computing. It is the parent company of WPS office software.

## Investment Paper

Kingsoft is a typical holding company discount target. The SOTP valuation of the core assets (Kingsoft Office + Kingsoft Cloud + Xishan Residence + Cash) is significantly higher than the current market value. Under the AI ​​wave, the rapid growth of WPS AI and Kingsoft Cloud AI business may become a catalyst for value revaluation. The continued advancement of the Xinchuang policy has provided structural growth momentum for the office business.
