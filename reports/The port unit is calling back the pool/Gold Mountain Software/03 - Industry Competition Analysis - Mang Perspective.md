# Kingsoft (3888.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Competitive Landscape and Reverse Thinking
> Core question: Under what circumstances will this investment fail?
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. First think clearly how you will lose.

Munger said: "I just want to know where I will die, and then I won't go there." Before analyzing Kingsoft Software, first list all the scenarios that may make this investment fail.

### 1.1 Fatal Risk List

**Risk 1: Microsoft’s counterattack in the Chinese market**

The core competitiveness of WPS is based on the policy dividend of "domestic substitution". If Sino-U.S. relations improve and Microsoft increases its investment in the Chinese market (such as slashing prices or launching a compliance program for Xinchuang), WPS's government and enterprise market share may be impacted.

*Probability of occurrence*: Low. The trend of technological decoupling between China and the United States will not be reversed in the short term, and the innovation policy is a decision at the national strategic level.
*Impact*: High. If this happens, WPS’s government-enterprise business growth logic will be weakened.

**Risk 2: AI subverts the office software paradigm**

If large model technology develops to the point where users no longer need traditional office software (such as directly using AI Agent to generate documents, presentations, and reports), the value of WPS as a tool layer software will be significantly weakened.

*Probability of occurrence*: Medium to low. In the short term, AI will enhance rather than replace office software, but the technological evolution in 5-10 years is unpredictable.
*Impact*: Very high. This is an existential risk for the office software industry.

**Risk 3: Game business continues to deteriorate**

"Unlocking Machine" has failed. If the popularity of "Goose and Duck" is unsustainable and the new game pipeline dries up, the game business may turn from a "drag" to a "black hole" - continuing to consume R&D resources but unable to create returns.

*Probability of occurrence*: Medium. The gaming industry itself is a high-risk industry, and Xishanju relies too much on a single IP.
*Impact*: Moderate. The gaming business accounts for 39% of the group’s revenue, but its profit contribution is even lower.

**Risk 4: The holding company’s discount will never narrow**

This is a classic situation of a "value trap". Even if SOTP's valuation is high, if management does not take actions to release value (privatization, large dividends, buybacks and cancellations, spin-off listings, etc.), the discount may exist for a long time, and investors' returns will only come from the numerator side (business growth) rather than the denominator side (valuation repair).

*Probability of occurrence*: Medium to high. Many holding companies in the Hong Kong stock market have been at a discount for a long time.
*Impact*: High. This directly determines the return on investment and time cost.

**Risk 5: Kingsoft Cloud becomes a "bottomless pit"**

Cloud computing is a money-burning business. Although Kingsoft Cloud will achieve profitability for the first time in Q3 of 2025, if the AI ​​computing power arms race escalates and competitors (Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, Tencent Cloud) significantly reduce prices, Kingsoft Cloud may fall back into losses.

*Probability of occurrence*: Medium. The competitive landscape of the cloud computing market is still being reshaped.
*Impact*: Moderate. Kingsoft Cloud has been independently listed. In the worst case, Kingsoft Software can reduce its holdings and exit.

## 2. Competitive landscape: different situations in the three battlefields

### 2.1 Office Software Track ★★★★☆

**Competitive Landscape: Duopoly**

The global office software market is essentially a duopoly between Microsoft and WPS. In the Chinese market, this pattern is even clearer:

| Dimensions | WPS | Microsoft Office |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic PC market share | Close to 50% | About 50% |
| Domestic mobile market share | ~90% | <10% |
| Government and enterprise market penetration rate | 78% | Xin Chuang was gradually replaced |
| Global market share | <5% | >90% |
| Subscription ARPU | Low | High |
| AI capabilities | WPS AI consonance | Copilot |

**Key Judgment**: In the Chinese market, WPS has established a competitive advantage that is close to irreversible. The Xinchuang policy makes the migration direction of government and enterprise customers one-way (from Microsoft to WPS), making reverse migration almost impossible. However, in the international market, WPS’s competitiveness is limited—the market share in Southeast Asia is only 12%, and that in Europe and the United States is less than 2%. CEO Zhang Qingyuan himself admitted that "the US market cannot be entered."

**Industry Growth Drivers**:
1. **Xinchuang penetration rate increases**: Xinchuang's "2+8+N" system will be fully implemented in 2026, and the demand for Office replacement in government affairs and state-owned enterprises will continue to be released
2. **AI payment increases ARPU**: WPS AI function becomes a value-added point for subscription, which is expected to increase user payment rate and customer unit price
3. **Enterprise collaborative office**: WPS 365 benchmarks against Microsoft 365, opening up a new growth curve in the enterprise SaaS market

### 2.2 Game track ★★☆☆☆

**Competitive Landscape: Veterans in the Red Sea**

China's game market is highly competitive, with Tencent and NetEase occupying the top two market shares. MiHoYo has emerged with global IPs such as "Genshin Impact" and "Star Iron". The position of Xishanju, a subsidiary of Kingsoft Software, in the industry:

- Not the top echelon (Tencent, NetEase, MiHoYo)
- Belongs to a mid-tier manufacturer, relying at its core on the single IP of "Jian Wang 3"
- The success rate of new products is not high, and the failure of "Unlimited Machine" is a typical case
- "Goose and Duck" is a rare bright spot, but the life cycle and commercialization ability of the social game category are questionable

**Key question from Munger's perspective**: If you can't be among the top three in the industry, why should you make money in this track for a long time? Xishanju did not answer this question well. The cruel thing about the game industry is that the survival status of "fourth place" is far worse than that of "third place" - there is no scale effect, no platform advantage, and complete reliance on product power.

### 2.3 Cloud Computing Track ★★☆☆☆

**Competitive landscape: Survivors squeezed by giants**

China's cloud computing market is dominated by the three giants Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud. Kingsoft Cloud's market share ranks between fifth and seventh.

| Cloud Vendors | Market Position | Competitive Advantages |
|---|---|---|
| Alibaba Cloud | First | Scale + Technology + Ecology |
| Huawei Cloud | Second | Government-enterprise relations + full stack capabilities |
| Tencent Cloud | Third | Social Ecology + Game Cloud |
| Kingsoft Cloud | Fifth-Seventh | Xiaomi Ecosystem + AI Computing Power |

The differentiation of Kingsoft Cloud lies in the "Xiaomi + Kingsoft" ecological synergy - the related transaction amount from 2025 to 2027 is as high as 11.3 billion yuan. This is both an advantage (a stable source of income) and a risk (over-reliance on related parties).

## 3. Reverse thinking: Are the market’s worries excessive?

### 3.1 Implicit assumptions in market pricing

The current stock price of HK$32.70 implies a very pessimistic combination of assumptions:
- The holding company’s discount of approximately 47-49% is permanent
- Gaming business will continue to drag down overall performance
- AI catalyst will not lead to valuation re-rating
- Management will not improve shareholder returns

### 3.2 What concerns are legitimate?

**Legitimate Concern**:
- Holding company discounts have a profound historical tradition in the Hong Kong stock market and may indeed exist for a long time.
- The volatility of the game business is an objective fact and will not change just because one or two new products are successful.
- Kingsoft Office’s A-share valuation of 65 times PE may itself be a bubble. If A-shares adjust, SOTP will also shrink.

**Potentially Excessive Concern**:
- The market does not seem to fully realize the commercialization potential of WPS AI. Kingsoft Office’s net profit in Q1 of 2026 is expected to increase by 402%-473% year-on-year. This growth rate shows that the revenue contribution of AI is accelerating.
- The existence of 26 billion in cash means a "hard bottom" in extreme situations - the company can be worth more than HK$20 billion in cash alone without doing any business

### 3.3 Under what conditions will the valuation be revalued?

1. **Management announces large special dividends or repurchases and cancellations**: This is the most direct catalyst for narrowing the holding company’s discount
2. **Game business stabilizes and rebounds**: If "Goose and Duck" and subsequent new products prove the richness of the pipeline
3. **Kingsoft Office continues to rise in A shares**: The numerator of SOTP becomes larger, and even if the discount rate remains unchanged, the absolute valuation will increase
4. **The overall recovery of the Hong Kong stock market**: Improved sentiment in Hong Kong stocks will systematically narrow the discount of holding companies

Kingsoft's advantage over NetEase lies in WPS, a more certain asset.

## 5. Comprehensive Rating

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Office software competitiveness | ★★★★☆ | One of the domestic duopoly, with deep barriers to innovation and innovation |
| Game competitiveness | ★★☆☆☆ | A mid-level manufacturer that lacks the ability to continue to hit sales |
| Cloud Computing Competitiveness | ★★☆☆☆ | Insufficient scale and reliance on related parties |
| Industry trend tailwind | ★★★★☆ | AI+ Xinchuang is a clear tailwind, gaming and cloud neutral |
| Excessive market pessimism | ★★★★☆ | The 47-49% discount may include overly pessimistic expectations |

**Comprehensive rating of industry competition: ★★★☆☆ (3.2/5)**

Kingsoft's competitiveness presents a pattern of "one strong and two weak" - the office software business is highly competitive, while the gaming and cloud computing businesses are not competitive enough. As an investment target, you need to believe that the value of WPS is enough to cover the uncertainties of the other two businesses, and that the market will eventually give a more reasonable pricing. If WPS is the soul of this holding company, then the soul is healthy - the question is whether the body (holding structure) can keep up with the soul.
