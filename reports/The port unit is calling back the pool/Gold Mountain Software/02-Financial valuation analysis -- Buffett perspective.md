## 2. Balance sheet analysis: Cash is king

Kingsoft's balance sheet is one of its biggest highlights.

**Asset Quality Assessment ★★★★★**

- Cash reserves of about 26 billion yuan, which is close to more than 60% of the company's own market value (about 45 billion Hong Kong dollars). In other words, when you buy Kingsoft Software, most of it is "buying cash."
-The asset-liability ratio is only 16.75%, which is far lower than the industry average (approximately 35%). This means that the company has almost no reliance on external financing and has extremely high financial security.
- There is no pressure on interest-bearing liabilities, and there is no risk of "not being able to repay the debt".

**Cash usage efficiency assessment ★★☆☆☆**

- The yield on 26 billion cash deserves asking: How much return does this money create?
- The dividend rate is extremely low (the final dividend is only HK$0.13 per share), and the buyback intensity is not large enough.
- A large amount of cash lying on the books without dividends or repurchases is one of the important reasons for the discount of holding companies.

### 3.2 Discount analysis

Kingsoft's current holding company discount is about 47-49%, which means that the market is pricing Kingsoft at about half of its asset value.

**Reasonable components of discount:**
- The governance efficiency of holding companies is naturally lower than that of independent operating companies
- The gaming business is highly volatile, increasing the uncertainty of valuations
- Large amounts of cash failed to be effectively deployed or returned to shareholders
- Discount on Hong Kong stock liquidity and market preference

**Part of the trade-in that may be excessive:**
- Even if a reasonable discount of 30% is given, SOTP should be HK$60-62 billion, corresponding to a stock price of about HK$44-45, which still has 35-38% upside compared to the current HK$32.70
- AI catalysts (acceleration of WPS AI commercialization + explosion of Kingsoft Cloud AI business) may narrow discounts

## 4. Profitability and growth trends

### 4.1 Changes in income structure

Kingsoft is undergoing a structural change - from the two-wheel drive of "games + office" to "office-based, games-assisted".

- The proportion of office business revenue in 2025 has exceeded 61% (5.929 billion/9.683 billion), a significant increase from previous years
- The proportion of gaming business dropped from approximately 50% to approximately 39%
- This shift is positive for valuations: Office software subscription revenue is more stable, more predictable, and valuation multiples are higher

### 4.2 Profit rate trend

- The group's overall gross profit margin is approximately 81%, remaining at a high level
- However, the operating profit margin (about 18%) is lower than that of pure software companies, mainly dragged down by the high R&D investment in the game business and the low profits of Kingsoft Cloud
- Kingsoft Office’s net profit margin is approximately 31% (1.836 billion/5.929 billion), which is at the level of a high-quality software company

## 5. Valuation and safety margin

### 5.1 Current Valuation Level

- **PE (TTM)**: about 15-18 times, at a mid-to-low historical level
- **PE considering net cash**: If net cash of approximately HK$23 billion is deducted, the PE of the actual operating business is even lower
- **PB**: below industry average

### 5.2 Safety Margin Assessment

**Optimistic scenario (SOTP discount narrows to 30%):**
- Target valuation: HK$60-62 billion
- Corresponding stock price: approximately HK$44-45
- Upside: approximately 35-38%

**Neutral scenario (discount maintained at 40%):**
- Target valuation: HK$51-53 billion
- Corresponding stock price: approximately HKD 37-39
- Upside: approximately 13-19%

**Pessimistic scenario (the game continues to deteriorate and the discount expands to 55%):**
- Target valuation: HK$38.5-40 billion
- Corresponding stock price: approximately HK$28-29
- Downside risk: approximately 11-14%

### 5.3 Key assumptions and sensitivities

The size of the safety margin is highly dependent on the following assumptions:
1. Is the valuation of Kingsoft Office A shares reasonable? (The current PE is about 65 times, and there is a premium for A shares)
2. Has the gaming business bottomed out? (If it continues to worsen, it will further lower NAV)
3. Will management increase shareholder returns? (If there is a large repurchase or special dividends, the discount will narrow quickly)

**Financial Valuation Comprehensive Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.6/5)**

From a financial perspective, Kingsoft's safety margin mainly comes from two aspects: first, abundant cash reserves provide downside protection, and second, SOTP discount provides room for value return. The main risk is that this discount may exist for a long time - if management does not take action (large buybacks, special dividends, simplifying the holding structure), the market has no reason to actively revalue. The distance between "cheap" and "worth buying" depends on whether a catalyst appears.
