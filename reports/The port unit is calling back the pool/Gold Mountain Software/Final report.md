## 2. Overview of investment thesis

**One sentence summary**: Kingsoft is an opportunity to "buy WPS at a discount", but whether the discount can be realized depends on whether a catalyst appears.

### 2.1 Reasons to be optimistic (bullish argument)

1. **SOTP significant discount**: Breaking it down, the value of Kingsoft Office’s stock holdings (~45.5 billion Hong Kong dollars) + the value of Kingsoft Cloud’s stock holdings (~12 billion Hong Kong dollars) + Xishanju (5 to 8 billion Hong Kong dollars) + net cash (~23 billion Hong Kong dollars) = 85.5 to 88.5 billion Hong Kong dollars. The current market value is only HK$45 billion, a discount of approximately 47-49%. Even if a reasonable discount of 30% is given, there is still 35-38% upside potential.

2. **WPS AI commercialization accelerates**: In Q1 2026, Kingsoft Office net profit is expected to increase by 402%-473% year-on-year, and WPS AI monthly active users exceed 80.13 million (+307% year-on-year). AI is changing from "concept" to "income".

3. **Xinchuang policy dividends continue**: In 2026, Xinchuang's "2+8+N" system will be fully implemented, and WPS will be the biggest beneficiary in the office software field. Xinchuang Software’s revenue increased by 51% year-on-year, and the growth rate is still accelerating.

4. **Cash provides hard bottom protection**: 26 billion yuan in cash and an asset-liability ratio of 16.75%, which means that even if all business returns to zero, the company still has a liquidation value of more than 20 billion Hong Kong dollars.

5. **Game business may hit bottom**: The most severe stage of decline in the game business in 2025 may have passed, and the performance of "Goose and Duck" with tens of millions of users in the first week provides a signal of marginal improvement.

### 2.2 Reasons to be bearish (short argument)

1. **The discount of holding companies may become permanent**: There are a large number of holding companies in the Hong Kong stock market that have been at a discount of 40-60% for a long time. If management does not take action (large buybacks, special dividends, simplified structure), there will be no incentive for the discount to narrow.

2. **Poor capital allocation by management**: 26 billion cash is lying on the account, and the dividend rate is less than 0.5% - the management's negative attitude towards shareholder returns is the biggest risk.

3. **Kingsoft Office’s A-share valuation is on the high side**: Kingsoft Office’s current A-share PE is about 65 times. If the A-share valuation is corrected, the numerator of SOTP will shrink. Even if the discount rate remains unchanged, the absolute valuation will also decline.

4. **Game business continues to be a drag**: After the failure of "Unlimited Machine", the recovery of the game pipeline will take time. If subsequent new products continue to fall short of expectations, the game business will continue to consume resources and management energy.

5. **Lei Jun’s energy is scattered**: Lei Jun’s core energy is on Xiaomi and building cars, Kingsoft software is not his first priority. Holding companies that lack strong leaders are more susceptible to strategic drift.

## 5. Investment advice

### 5.1 Conclusion

Kingsoft Software is a **moderately attractive** investment opportunity. The margin of safety comes from abundant cash and SOTP discounts, and the growth momentum comes from WPS AI and Xinchuang. However, there is no clear catalyst for the narrowing of the holding company's discount, and the negative attitude of management on capital allocation is the biggest risk factor.

### 5.2 What type of investors is it suitable for?

- **Suitable**: Patient value investors who can tolerate a holding period of 1-3 years and are willing to "wait for a catalyst"
- **Not Suitable**: Investors pursuing short-term returns, or investors who lack tolerance for holding company discounts

### 5.3 Position recommendations

- If you open a position, it is recommended to use it as a **small or medium-sized position** (3-5%) in the portfolio, rather than as a heavy position target.
- Reason: The margin of safety exists but the catalyst is uncertain, so it is not suitable for concentrated betting.
- Conditions for adding positions: Management announces large buybacks/dividends, or the stock price falls below HK$28 (larger safety margin)
- Stop loss conditions: Kingsoft Office A-share PE fell below 35 times, causing SOTP to shrink significantly

### 5.4 Comparison with direct investment in Kingsoft Office

A question worth thinking about: Why not just buy Kingsoft Office (688111.SH)?

| Dimension | Kingsoft (3888.HK) | Kingsoft Office (688111.SH) |
|---|---|---|
| PE | ~15-18 times | ~65 times |
| Margin of safety | Large (discount + cash) | Small (valuation already high) |
| Growth Purity | Low (Mixed Business) | High (Pure Office Software) |
| Volatility | Lower | Higher |
| Catalyst Dependence | High | Low |

The reason for choosing Kingsoft Software is "to use 15 times PE to indirectly hold assets of 65 times PE"; the reason for choosing Kingsoft Office is "purer growth and not being troubled by holding discounts". There are pros and cons to both, depending on investors' relative preference for valuation repair versus business growth.
