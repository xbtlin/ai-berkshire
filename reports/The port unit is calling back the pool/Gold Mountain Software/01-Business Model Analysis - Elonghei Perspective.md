# Kingsoft (3888.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Business Nature and Moat
> Core question: Is this a good business? Where is the user value?
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. The nature of business: the troika under the holding company

The business essence of Kingsoft Software is a holding technology group, with its three major business sectors each having its own logic:

**The first driver: Kingsoft Office (WPS) - the core asset with the highest certainty**

WPS is an office software product with a 36-year history. As of the end of 2025, its monthly active users have exceeded 618 million. Its domestic PC market share is close to that of Microsoft Office, and its mobile market share is as high as 90%. This is a typical good business with "clear user value, rigid demand, and high migration costs".

WPS's business model has undergone a transformation from selling software to selling services. The proportion of subscription revenue has increased to approximately 67% in 2025, with the three lines of personal subscription, enterprise SaaS (WPS 365) and Xinchuang Software growing in parallel. The subscription model brings predictable recurring revenue, which is one of the business characteristics that Duan Yongping values ​​most - "A good business model means that the money earned will still be there next year."

**Second driver: Xishanju (game) - a volatile cash flow business**

Xishanju is one of the oldest game studios in China. Its core IP is "Jian Wang 3" which has been in operation for 16 years. The full-year game business revenue in 2025 was 3.754 billion yuan, a sharp year-on-year decline (mainly because the operation of "Unlimited Machine" did not meet expectations). The game business is essentially a "big single product + hit-driven" model, and its revenue is extremely volatile.

But there are also bright spots: the "Goose and Duck Killing" mobile game launched in January 2026 added 5 million new users on the first day of public beta, and exceeded 10 million in 6 days, demonstrating Xishanju's potential in the social game category.

**The third driver: Kingsoft Cloud—the variable in the AI wave**

Kingsoft Cloud achieved adjusted net profit for the first time in Q3 2025 (28.73 million yuan). AI business revenue surged 228% year-on-year, and intelligent computing cloud became the core growth engine. However, cloud computing is an asset-heavy, high-investment business, and it is still at an inflection point of "moving from losses to profits."

## 2. User value analysis: What problems does WPS solve?

Duan Yongping's first criterion for judging the quality of a business is "user value" - what irreplaceable value does your product create for users?

**The user value of WPS is very clear:**

1. **For individual users**: Free + lightweight office solution, with AI support (WPS AI monthly active users exceed 80.13 million), it further improves the efficiency of writing, form processing, PPT generation and other scenarios. Users do not need to spend thousands of dollars to buy Microsoft Office to complete their daily office needs.

2. **For government and enterprise customers**: Domestic replacement is needed under the Xinchuang policy. WPS is the only domestic office software that can compete with Microsoft 365 at the functional level, with a penetration rate of 78% in the government and enterprise markets. This is not just a question of "easy to use", but a question of "must be used".

3. **For developers/organizations**: WPS 365 provides the capability of a one-stop AI collaborative office platform to help enterprises build a "digital knowledge base". In the first three quarters of 2025, WPS 365 revenue growth exceeded 60%, and in the third quarter it increased by 72% year-on-year.

**The user value of games** is more subjective and uncertain - user loyalty of entertainment products is naturally lower than that of tool products. The fact that "Jian Wang 3" has been operating for 16 years shows the vitality of the IP, but the success rate of new products is unpredictable.

## 3. Moat assessment

### 3.1 The moat of WPS ★★★★☆

- **Switching Cost**: Once enterprise customers deploy WPS and complete document migration and employee training, the cost of switching back to Microsoft Office is extremely high. Especially within the Xinchuang system, this lock-in effect is stronger.
- **Network Effect**: 618 million monthly active users form a powerful document format ecosystem. When everyone around you is using WPS, you also need to use WPS to ensure compatibility.
- **Policy Barriers**: Under Xinchuang's "2+8+N" system, WPS is almost the only choice for government affairs and state-owned enterprise office software. This is a moat built by policies that will be difficult to break in the short term.
- **AI first-mover advantage**: WPS AI 3.0 "Lingxi" is in a leading position in the field of domestic office AI, with knowledge base and document parsing capabilities built on 36 years of technology accumulation.

**Shortcomings**: Internationalization progress is slow, and overseas market share is less than 5%. CEO Zhang Qingyuan himself also admitted that "the US market cannot be entered" and the competitive battlefield lies in markets "outside China and the United States".

### 3.2 The moat of the game business ★★☆☆☆

- **IP Value**: "Jian Wang 3" has a stable core player group, but the aging of IP is an unavoidable fact.
- **No pricing power**: Competition in the game industry is fierce, user loyalty is low, and the life cycle of a single game is difficult to predict.
- **Dependence on popular products**: Performance relies heavily on the performance of single products. The failure of "Unlimited Machine" directly dragged down the annual performance in 2025.

### 3.3 Jinshan Cloud’s moat ★★☆☆☆

- **Ecological Synergy**: Backed by the Xiaomi + Kingsoft system, the related-party transaction limit will be as high as 11.3 billion yuan from 2025 to 2027.
- **AI capabilities**: Large model training and inference services account for more than 60% of AI revenue. However, Kingsoft Cloud's scale advantage is not obvious in front of Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud.

## 4. The "right side" and "left side" of the business model

**Right side (reasons to be optimistic):**

1. The subscription-based transformation of WPS has been successful, the proportion of recurring revenue continues to increase, and the business model is getting better and better.
2. AI is one of the most natural application scenarios for office software, and WPS AI has a clear commercialization path (subscription price increase, enterprise value-added services).
3. Innovation and innovation policies provide certainty for long-term structural growth.
4. The holding company holds approximately 26 billion yuan in cash, and its financial situation is extremely healthy, with an asset-liability ratio of only 16.75%.

**Left side (reasons for concern):**

1. There is too much uncertainty in the game business, and revenue will decline significantly year-on-year in 2025, dragging down the group's overall performance.
2. The holding company structure naturally brings about problems of loss of governance efficiency and information opacity.
3. The internationalization ceiling of WPS is obvious, with more than 600 million monthly active users mainly concentrated in the domestic market.
4. Although Kingsoft Cloud is becoming profitable, it does not have significant differentiated competitiveness on the cloud computing track.

## 5. Comprehensive Rating

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| User value clarity | ★★★★☆ | WPS user value is extremely clear, and games and clouds lower the overall score |
| Business model quality | ★★★★☆ | Subscription + Xinchuang provides high-quality recurring income |
| Moat strength | ★★★☆☆ | WPS moat is strong, but game and cloud business moats are weak |
| Management concentration | ★★★☆☆ | Too many business lines and scattered resources are hidden dangers |
| Predictability | ★★★☆☆ | WPS has high predictability, large game fluctuations, and overall average |

**Comprehensive rating of business model: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

The core problem of Kingsoft is this: it has a four-star asset (WPS), but its overall quality is dragged down by its holding company structure and two low-certainty businesses (games + cloud). If you just look at WPS, it's a good business; if you look at the entire holding company, it's a mixed bag. Investing in Kingsoft Software is essentially "buying WPS at a discount" - but whether this discount is large enough requires further analysis at the valuation level.
