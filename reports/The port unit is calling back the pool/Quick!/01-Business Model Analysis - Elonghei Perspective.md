# Kuaishou (1024.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user mentality, moat, corporate culture
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

Kuaishou is essentially a **traffic platform** - gathering user attention through short videos and live broadcast content, and then monetizing the attention into advertising revenue, e-commerce commissions and live broadcast rewards.

Income structure in 2025:
- **Online marketing services (advertising)**: 81.5 billion yuan, accounting for 57%, +12.5% year-on-year
- **Live streaming business**: 39.1 billion yuan, accounting for 27%, +5.5% year-on-year
- **Other services (e-commerce + Keling AI)**: 22.2 billion yuan, accounting for 16%, +27.6% year-on-year

From the perspective of revenue structure, Kuaishou has completed the transformation from "mainly live streaming rewards" to "mainly advertising revenue". This is a good change—advertising revenue is more stable, more predictable, and less dependent on top anchors than tipping revenue.

But we need to face a fundamental question: What exactly is Kuaishou’s user mind? **

### User Mind: From "Outside the Fifth Ring Road" to the Fuzzy Zone of "General Public"

Kuaishou’s early positioning was very clear—to record the lives of ordinary people. This positioning has allowed it to establish strong community stickiness in the sinking market. The "old iron" culture is a real emotional bond of the community. However, as commercialization deepens and content management tightens, Kuaishou attempts to expand to first- and second-tier cities and a wider user group, leading to blurred thinking among users.

Compared with Douyin's "efficient distribution + high-quality content + strong e-commerce", Kuaishou is more like a "community platform" - users not only watch the content, but also have a social relationship chain. This is both an advantage (user stickiness is stronger and the average daily usage time continues to grow), and a disadvantage (content distribution efficiency is not as good as algorithm-driven Douyin, and brand advertisers prefer Douyin’s crowd coverage capabilities).

**Evaluation: Kuaishou has user intelligence, but it is not sharp enough. It is not "the most beautiful content", nor is it "the cheapest product", but "the most intimate community". This kind of mentality is valuable, but the commercial realization efficiency is naturally lower than that of algorithm-driven platforms. **

## 2. Moat analysis

### 1. Network Effect ★★★☆☆

Kuaishou has a certain network effect - 741 million MAU and 410 million DAU constitute a huge content ecosystem. Creators can gain traffic by posting content on Kuaishou, and users can see rich content on Kuaishou, forming a bilateral network effect.

But this network effect is **waning**:
- Multi-platform distribution for creators has become the norm, and fewer and fewer top creators are exclusive to Kuaishou
- User growth has peaked (MAU growth dropped below 2%), and the "growth flywheel" of network effects has slowed down
- WeChat video accounts are eroding Kuaishou’s community advantage by relying on social relationship chains

### 2. Switching cost ★★☆☆☆

The conversion cost of short video platforms is naturally low. Users can use Douyin and Kuaishou at the same time, and there is no exclusivity. The only switching barrier is Kuaishou’s social relationship chain – some users have established following relationships and interaction habits on Kuaishou, and migrating to other platforms means losing these social assets.

But unlike WeChat's strong social relationships, Kuaishou's social relationships are more of "weak connections" - following an anchor does not mean establishing a deep social bond. Switching costs are therefore limited.

### 3. Brand value ★★★☆☆

The brand "Kuaishou" has high popularity and favorability in lower-tier markets. "Laotie" culture represents a real and down-to-earth community atmosphere. However, in the eyes of brand advertisers, Kuaishou’s brand tone is not as high-end as Douyin’s, which directly affects the ceiling of advertising unit price (CPM).

### 4. Data and algorithm barriers ★★★☆☆

Kuaishou has accumulated a large amount of user behavior data and content data, and its algorithm recommendation capabilities continue to iterate. However, the barriers to algorithm recommendation are no longer a decisive factor in the short video industry - Douyin and video accounts have powerful recommendation algorithms, which have become industry standards rather than differentiated advantages.

### 5. Keling AI – a potential new moat? ★★★★☆

Keling AI is Kuaishou’s most noteworthy moat variable at the moment. As the world's leading AI video generation model, Keling is in the first echelon of the industry in terms of technology ranking, user scale, and commercialization progress:
- There are more than 60 million users worldwide, and ARR will reach US$500 million in April 2026.
- KeLing 3.0 ranks among the best in the world in text generation video benchmarks
- Rumored spin-off valuation of $20 billion

However, it should be noted that the competition in the AI video generation track is fierce, and OpenAI Sora, Google Veo, ByteDream, etc. are all iterating rapidly. Whether Keling's first-mover advantage can be transformed into a lasting moat remains uncertain.

## 3. The "right" and "wrong" of business models

### Do the right thing

**1. Transformation from tipping to advertising**

In 2020, live broadcast revenue accounted for more than 60%, but by 2025 it had dropped to 27%. The proportion of advertising revenue increased from 30% to 57%. This is a healthy transition from "unstable income" to "stable income". Live streaming rewards are over-reliant on top anchors and users’ impulse consumption, with high regulatory risks and poor sustainability; advertising revenue is more diversified and predictable.

**2. Construction of e-commerce closed loop**

Kuaishou e-commerce has shifted from the external link model to the closed-loop model and established its own e-commerce infrastructure. E-commerce GMV will still maintain a growth rate of 15% in 2025. Although the growth rate is declining, the improvement of e-commerce infrastructure provides more possibilities for future monetization.

**3. Keling AI’s forward-looking layout**

On the AI video generation track, Kuaishou is one of the first Chinese companies to achieve large-scale commercialization. From its release in 2024 to 2026, ARR exceeded US$500 million, which is an astonishing speed. If Keling’s spin-off and listing is successful, it will create huge value for Kuaishou shareholders.

### Things you did wrong or things you need to be careful about

**1. Repeat in overseas markets**

Kuaishou's overseas expansion has gone through five major strategic adjustments. The phrase "five battles overseas" itself shows the lack of strategic focus. Although it has gained a firm foothold in Brazil, the performance in Southeast Asia (SnackVideo) is far less than expected, and markets such as Africa are also making slow progress. Compared with TikTok’s global expansion, the scale and growth rate of Kuaishou’s overseas business are not at the same level.

**2. Signal that GMV will no longer be disclosed**

Kuaishou announced that it will no longer disclose quarterly and annual GMV data separately starting from Q1 2026. This is generally not a good sign—companies tend to stop disclosing when a key metric is no longer growing. This deprives investors of an important window to judge the health of the e-commerce business.

**3. Structural problem of stagnant user growth**

The MAU growth rate dropped to 2%, and Q4 DAU lost 8 million month-on-month. For a traffic platform, the lack of user growth means that more value needs to be mined from existing users - this has an upper limit and will also affect the user experience.

## 4. Corporate culture and organizational capabilities

Kuaishou’s corporate culture has gone through several stages:

**Early Days (Geek Culture)**: Su Hua and Cheng Yixiao both have technical backgrounds. The company atmosphere is like a large technical team, with flat management and no ranks. This culture drove Kuaishou’s early product innovation.

**Before and after listing (acceleration of commercialization)**: After listing in 2021, Kuaishou faced commercialization pressure, introduced a large number of external executives, and the organizational culture shifted from "Buddhist" to "Wolf". This stage saw a lot of organizational friction and brain drain.

**Cheng Yixiao's single-core era (2022 to present)**: After Su Hua withdrew from daily management, Cheng Yixiao led a series of cost reduction and efficiency improvement measures, and organizational efficiency was significantly improved. The net profit margin will reach 14.5% in 2025, indicating that cost control has achieved effective results.

However, it should be noted that Cheng Yixiao is recognized as "not good at communication and rhetoric", and his presence in the public eye is extremely low. This is in contrast to the strong leadership styles of founders such as Byte Zhang Yiming and Pinduoduo Huang Zheng. A low-key CEO may be more pragmatic at the executive level, but when major strategic decisions need to be made (such as the spin-off of Keling), the leader's vision and courage are crucial.

## 5. Comprehensive assessment

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Business model clarity | ★★★☆☆ | The traffic platform model is clear, but users’ minds are not sharp enough |
| Moat strength | ★★★☆☆ | There is community stickiness but low switching cost, Keling AI is a variable |
| Doing the right thing | ★★★★☆ | Successful transformation of income structure, Ke Ling’s layout is forward-looking |
| Corporate Culture | ★★★☆☆ | From chaos to pragmatism, but leadership is questionable |
| **Overall review** | **★★★☆☆** | **A business that is okay but not good enough needs Keling AI to change the narrative** |

**Core conclusion**: Kuaishou is a business that "can make money but it is difficult to make big money". Its moat is not deep, its users are not mentally sharp, and its competitors are too strong. The only variable that can change this judgment is Keling AI - if Keling can really become the global leader in AI video generation and successfully go public independently, then the value of Kuaishou will be redefined. But betting on an AI business that has not yet been fully verified is inherently speculative.
