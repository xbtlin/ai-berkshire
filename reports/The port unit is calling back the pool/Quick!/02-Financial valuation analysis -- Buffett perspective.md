# Kuaishou (1024.HK) financial and valuation analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial health, earnings quality, capital allocation, intrinsic value
> Study date: May 16, 2026

## 1. Financial Panorama: Profits reach new highs, but growth story is fading

### 1. Revenue growth trend

| Annual | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate | Adjusted net profit (100 million yuan) | Net profit margin |
|------|-------------|---------|-------------------|---------|
| 2022 | 942 | -1.3% | Loss | - |
| 2023 | 1135 | 20.5% | 103 | 9.1% |
| 2024 | 1269 | 11.8% | 176 | 13.9% |
| 2025 | 1428 | 12.5% | 206 | 14.5% |

Revenue: Revenue in 2025 will be 142.8 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 12.5%. The growth rate is stable but narrowing - from 20.5% in 2023 to 12.5% ​​in 2025. Analysts are more pessimistic about revenue growth expectations in 2026, with Nomura lowering the growth rate to 4%.

Profit side: Adjusted net profit was 20.6 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 17%, and the net profit margin increased from 13.9% in 2024 to 14.5%. This is a beautiful set of numbers, but its origins need to be unpacked.

### 2. What is the source of profit growth—efficiency improvement or revenue driver?

The gross profit in 2025 will be 78.5 billion yuan, with a gross profit margin of 55%, basically the same as last year. This means that the improvement in profit margins mainly comes from the compression of expenses rather than the improvement of gross profit margins.

Specifically:
- Sales and marketing expenses as a percentage of revenue decreased
- R&D expenditure growth is lower than revenue growth
- Maintain discipline in administrative expenses

This shows that the profit growth in 2025 will be more "saved" rather than "earned". It is certainly a good thing to reduce costs and increase efficiency, but there are physical limits to cost compression. When the expense ratio has dropped to a relatively reasonable level, future profit growth can only rely on revenue growth - and revenue growth is exactly what the market is worried about.

### 3. Financial quality by business

**Advertising business (81.5 billion, +12.5%)**: Kuaishou’s core revenue source. The growth mainly comes from the improvement of AI-driven advertising efficiency and the growth of e-commerce advertising. However, the advertising business is highly dependent on the macroeconomic environment and may face pressure during economic downturns.

**Live broadcast business (39.1 billion, +5.5%)**: Slowing down the growth rate is an inevitable trend. Under the dual pressure of tighter supervision and changes in user consumption habits, live streaming tipping has reached its ceiling. A growth rate of 5.5% is basically equivalent to the level of inflation, which is essentially zero growth.

**Other services (22.2 billion, +27.6%)**: This is the fastest growing segment, including e-commerce commissions and Keling AI revenue. But it needs to be distinguished - the growth rate of e-commerce is declining (GMV growth rate is 15%), and the real bright spot is Keling AI. Keling's revenue in Q4 2025 is 340 million yuan, which is about 1 billion yuan for the whole year, accounting for less than 1% of total revenue. Although the growth rate is amazing, the absolute scale is still too small to change Kuaishou's overall growth curve.

## 2. Cash flow and capital allocation

### 1. Free cash flow

In 2025, Kuaishou's free cash flow will be approximately 12 billion yuan, and its capital expenditure will be approximately 15 billion yuan. Free cash flow is a good thing, but capital expenditures are growing rapidly—mainly used for the construction of AI computing infrastructure.

There is a core contradiction here: **Kuaishou requires huge capital expenditures to maintain the AI story, but the current revenue contribution of the AI business is minimal**. A considerable part of the 15 billion capital expenditures has been invested in GPU procurement and data centers. These investments have long return cycles and high uncertainty.

### 2. Return to shareholders

In 2025, Kuaishou will pay its first dividend (HK$0.69 per share, totaling approximately HK$3 billion). Together with the share repurchase of approximately HK$3.2 billion throughout the year, the total shareholder return will be approximately HK$6.2 billion. Based on the current market capitalization of approximately HK$290 billion, the shareholder return rate is approximately 2.1%.

This is a positive sign - the company is starting to give back to shareholders. However, it should be noted that compared with the shareholder return of HK$6.2 billion and the net profit of 20.6 billion yuan, the return ratio is only about 25%. A large amount of profits are retained for AI investment. Whether it can really create value will take time to verify.

### 3. Balance Sheet

As of the end of 2025, Kuaishou had 11.2 billion yuan in cash and cash equivalents. The financials are healthy considering the company's profitability and cash flow. There is no pressure from interest-bearing liabilities and the balance sheet is clean.

## 3. Valuation analysis

### 1. Relative valuation

| Company | PE (TTM) | Revenue Growth | Net Profit Margin |
|------|----------|---------|---------|
| Kuaishou | 11-12x | 12.5% | 14.5% |
| Tencent | 18-20x | ~10% | ~28% |
| Meituan | 15-17x | ~20% | ~8% |
| Pinduoduo | 8-10x | ~20% | ~25% |
| Bilibili | >50x | ~20% | Small profit |

Kuaishou's PE of 11-12 times is at a low level among the Hong Kong Internet sector. But the low valuation is not without reason:
- **Expectations of declining growth**: Market expectations are that revenue growth may drop to single digits in 2026
- **Profit quality concerns**: Profit growth mainly relies on cost compression, and sustainability is questionable
- **Uncertainty about AI investment**: Large capital expenditures are invested in AI, but the returns have not yet been realized.

### 2. Keling AI’s valuation contribution

This is the most imaginative part of current valuations. According to the latest rumors:
- Keling AI spin-off financing valuation is US$20 billion (approximately HK$156 billion)
- Kuaishou’s current total market value is approximately HK$290 billion
- If Keling’s valuation is true, it means that the implied valuation of Kuaishou’s remaining business (short video + live broadcast + e-commerce) is only about HK$134 billion

HK$134 billion corresponds to a net profit of 20.6 billion yuan, and the PE is about 5-6 times. At this price, it does seem cheap to buy a cash flow business with an annual revenue of 20 billion and 400 million daily active users.

But this algorithm has a major assumption: Is Keling AI’s $20 billion valuation reliable? **

Keling's ARR in April 2026 is US$500 million. To support a valuation of US$20 billion, a valuation multiple of 40 times ARR is needed. In an environment where competition in the AI ​​video generation field is becoming increasingly fierce and a large number of competing products are emerging, this valuation multiple is on the high side. If Kelin's ARR reaches US$1.3 billion in 2027 as expected, then US$20 billion corresponds to about 15 times ARR, which is more reasonable, but the premise is that revenue can continue to grow at a high speed.

### 3. Scenario analysis: How much is Kuaishou worth?

**Optimistic scenario (25% probability)**: Keling AI is successfully spun off and listed, with a valuation of more than US$20 billion; main business revenue maintains a growth of more than 10%; a reasonable valuation of approximately HK$450 billion (+55% upside potential)

**Baseline scenario (probability 45%)**: Keling AI maintains high growth but the spin-off progress is slower than expected; main business revenue growth rate drops to 5-8%; reasonable valuation is about HK$320 billion (+10% upside potential)

**Pessimistic scenario (30% probability)**: Competition in the AI ​​video generation track intensifies, and the growth rate of Keling slows down; the main business advertising revenue is dragged down by the macro; the reasonable valuation is about HK$200 billion (-30% downside risk)

Weighted expected value: approximately HK$310 billion, basically the same as the current market value.

## 4. In-depth interrogation of profit quality and sustainability

### 1. Can the 20.6 billion profit be sustained?

The sustainability of profits depends on three variables:
- **Can revenue continue to grow**: Advertising revenue is highly related to the macro economy, live broadcast revenue is close to the ceiling, and the growth rate of e-commerce is declining. The core variable is whether Keling AI can contribute substantial revenue.
- **Can the expense rate continue to be compressed**: Space is limited, and the expense rate in 2025 is already at a relatively low level.
- **Will AI investment eat up profits**: This is the biggest uncertainty. If AI computing power investment continues to increase (GPU depreciation, electricity costs) and Keling's revenue growth is less than expected, profit margins may be compressed.

### 2. A key accounting observation

Kuaishou’s operating profit in 2025 will be 20.6 billion yuan, but capital expenditure will be 15 billion yuan. If AI-related capital expenditures are regarded as "necessary inputs to maintain competitiveness" (similar to equipment renewal in the manufacturing industry), then the "real" profitability may be far lower than the reported figures.

Free cash flow of 12 billion yuan may be a better indicator of Kuaishou's true profitability than net profit of 20.6 billion yuan. Calculated based on free cash flow of 12 billion yuan and a market value of HK$290 billion (approximately 250 billion yuan), the free cash flow rate of return is approximately 4.8% - this rate of return is far from cheap for an Internet company with slowing growth and fierce competition.

### 3. Comparison with Pinduoduo - same cheap, but different quality

Pinduoduo’s PE is only 8-10 times, but Pinduoduo’s revenue growth rate is 20%, its net profit margin is 25%, and it requires almost no large capital expenditures. Kuaishou's PE is slightly higher (11-12 times), but its revenue growth rate is lower, profit margins are lower, and capital expenditures are higher.

The low valuation given by the market to Kuaishou is not that "the market is wrong", but that it prices the structural challenges faced by Kuaishou.
