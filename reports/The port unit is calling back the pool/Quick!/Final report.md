# Kuaishou (1024.HK) Investment Research Final Report

> Comprehensive analysis from four dimensions to provide investment conclusions
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. One sentence summary

**Kuaishou is a traffic machine with acceptable profits but peaked growth. Keling AI is the only variable that can change the valuation narrative. The current price of 11-12 times PE is roughly reasonable - it is neither a significantly underestimated "cheap picking" opportunity nor a value trap that needs to be avoided. **

---

## Summary of second and fourth dimensional analysis

### Business model (Duan Yongping’s perspective): ★★★☆☆

- Kuaishou is a traffic platform, and its income structure has shifted from being mainly based on rewards to mainly advertising. This is a healthy transformation.
- User minds (community/laotie culture) are valuable but not sharp enough, and the commercial realization efficiency is lower than Douyin
- The moat is not deep - the network effect is weakening, switching costs are low, and the brand tone is not as good as competing products
- Keling AI is the only source of new moats, but it has not yet formed a deterministic barrier

### Financial valuation (Buffett’s perspective): ★★★☆☆

- Revenue in 2025 is 142.8 billion, net profit is 20.6 billion, FCF is 12 billion, solid financial performance
- Profit growth mainly comes from expense compression rather than revenue growth, and sustainability is questionable
- PE 11-12 times is cheap on the surface, but considering the declining growth rate and high capital expenditure (15-26 billion/year), it is not deeply undervalued
- If the Keling spin-off is successful, the implied valuation of the main business is only 5-6 times PE, and there are opportunities for value release.

### Industry competition (Munger’s perspective): ★★★☆☆

- The short video industry is saturated with users and entering an era of stock competition.
- Kuaishou is clearly second in line, the gap with Douyin is widening, and the video account is catching up.
- The AI video generation track is leading in commercialization, but technical barriers are lowering and there are many competing products.
- Investing in Kuaishou is essentially betting that Keling AI can win.

### Risk management (Li Lu’s perspective): ★★★☆☆

- Core risks (peaking of user growth, uncertain return on AI investment) are structural
- Cheng Yixiao is a qualified CEO - more than pragmatic but not courageous
- Limited margin of safety – undervalued but not extremely undervalued
- The odds are average - the expected return is about 10-15%, and you need to be able to continue to exceed expectations.

## 3. The pros and cons of core investment logic

### The logic of looking long

1. **Keling AI is the global leader in AI video generation**. ARR has grown from US$240 million at the end of 2025 to US$500 million in April 2026, an astonishing growth rate. If this momentum is maintained and a successful spin-off and listing (with a valuation of US$20 billion), it will unlock huge value for Kuaishou shareholders.

2. **Stable cash flow from main business**. A business with annual revenue of 20 billion+ and FCF of 12 billion+ is valuable in itself. Even if growth stagnates, as a "cash cow" asset, PE 11-12 times is not expensive.

3. **Shareholder returns are just getting started**. First dividend + continuous repurchase, if management continues to increase returns (the current return ratio is only 25%), it will provide support for the stock price.

4. **Increase in overseas markets**. DAU in the Brazilian market increased by 9.7%. Commercialization has just begun, and there is room for long-term imagination.

### The logic of bearishness

1. **The growth narrative is broken**. The MAU growth rate dropped to 2%, and the GMV growth rate dropped to 15% and will no longer be disclosed. The low PE given by the market just illustrates the scarcity of growth - low valuation is not an opportunity, but pricing.

2. **AI investment may eat up profits**. 26 billion capital expenditure is a big gamble. If Kelin's growth rate slows down or competing products surpass it, these investments may become sunk costs, and profit margins will continue to be eroded by GPU depreciation.

3. The fate of the second child is difficult to break. In the main short video business, the gap between Kuaishou and Douyin will only grow wider. The rise of video accounts has made matters worse.

4. **Risk of Keling spin-off**. A valuation of US$20 billion corresponds to 40 times ARR, which is relatively high in an environment surrounded by competing products. If the spin-off valuation falls short of expectations or is delayed, the biggest catalyst will fall short.

5. **Potential pressure to reduce Tencent’s holdings**. As the largest external shareholder (15.7%), Tencent has been reducing its investment in Hong Kong stocks in recent years. The reduction of holdings by major shareholders will directly suppress the stock price.

## 5. Investment advice

### Overall rating: ★★★☆☆ (neutral to positive)

### Qualitative conclusion

Kuaishou is in a "neither up nor down" position:
- Not cheap enough to buy purely as a value investment (FCF yield of 4.8% is not attractive)
- There is not enough growth certainty to make growth investments (the growth rate is declining, and AI has not yet been verified)
- There is a highly uncertain "option" (Keling AI) that may lead to excess returns

### What type of investors is it suitable for?

**Fits**:
- Investors who have a deep understanding of the AI video generation track and believe it can build lasting barriers
- Patient capital willing to accept a holding period of more than 3 years and wait for the value of Keling AI to be realized
- Investors who need Hong Kong stock Internet allocation in the portfolio and already hold core positions such as Tencent/Pinduoduo (as satellite positions)

**Not suitable**:
- Conservative investors who pursue certainty and high winning rate (uncertainty is too high)
- Short-term traders (catalyst rhythm is uncontrollable)
- Investors who do not have in-depth knowledge of the AI ​​track (cannot judge the true value of Keling)

### Recommended operating framework

| Scenario | Trigger Condition | Action |
|------|---------|------|
| Actively buy | Keling renewal rate >80% confirms real demand + PE drops to 8-9 times | Establish core positions (5-8%) |
| Ogura Test | Current Valuation + Keling Split Expectation is Clear | Establish an Observation Position (2-3%) |
| Wait and see | Keling's growth rate slows down or spin-off is postponed | Not involved |
| Sell/Avoid | AI capital expenditures significantly exceed expectations + main business profits decline | Clear positions |

### Signals that need to be tracked continuously

1. **Keling AI monthly/quarterly ARR changes** - the most important leading indicator
2. **Keling user retention rate and ARPU** - verify the authenticity of demand
3. **Specific timetable for spin-off and listing** – Catalyst visibility
4. **Q1/Q2 main business income growth rate in 2026** - Verify whether it really drops to single digits
5. **Actual execution pace of capital expenditure** - Determining profit pressure
6. **Changes in Tencent’s shareholding**—major shareholder confidence indicator

## 6. Final judgment

**Kuaishou is not a "must-own" asset, but it is not a target that needs to be avoided. **

Its core value depends on a very specific question: How far can Keling AI go? If you don’t have an independent judgment on this issue that is higher than the market consensus, the best strategy is to stay on the sidelines and wait for Keling’s commercialization data (especially user retention and renewal rates) to be more verified before making a decision.

PE 11-12 times is not a "buy or lose" valuation level. There is a reason why the market gives this price - it accurately reflects the reality that the growth of the main business has peaked and the return on AI investment is uncertain. To get excess returns at this price, you need to believe in something that the market has not yet fully priced in—for example, Colin can become the Adobe of the AI ​​era, and Kuaishou Overseas can replicate the success of Douyin in China in Brazil.

These are all possible, but they are only possibilities. In investment, the distance between "possibility" and "high probability" is the margin of safety.
