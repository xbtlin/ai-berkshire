# Kuaishou (1024.HK) Industry and Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry ecology, competition landscape, reverse thinking, key variables
> Study date: May 16, 2026

## 1. Industry panorama: The golden age of short videos is ending

### 1. Current status of China’s short video industry

As of the end of 2024, the number of short video users in China has reached 1.026 billion, and the penetration rate is close to saturation. The industry has shifted from incremental competition to existing competition - it is not that there are fewer users, but that it is almost impossible to add new users**.

Douyin accounts for 50.8% of the total monthly usage time in the industry, with Kuaishou and other platforms dividing the remaining share. This distribution illustrates a cruel reality: the short video industry is not a "two heroes competing for hegemony", but a "one super, many strong" - Douyin is the clear industry leader, and Kuaishou is only the biggest challenger.

### 2. A new pattern of three pillars

In the past few years, the industry structure has evolved from the two strong players of "Douyin + Kuaishou" to the tripartite confrontation of "Douyin + Kuaishou + Video Account":

**Douyin**: DAU 600 million+, with the highest content distribution efficiency, largest e-commerce scale, and leading brand advertising share. ByteDance’s global presence (TikTok) and AI capabilities (i.e. Meng, Doubao) have further widened the gap with Kuaishou.

**Kuaishou**: DAU 410 million, with strong community attributes and obvious advantages in sinking the market, but it is not as good as Douyin in terms of brand advertising and penetration into first- and second-tier cities. Keling AI is the highlight of differentiation.

**WeChat Video Account**: Backed by the social relationship chain of WeChat’s 1.2 billion users, MAU has exceeded 800 million. The advantage of video accounts is low customer acquisition costs (natural traffic within the WeChat ecosystem), but the content richness and user duration are still not as good as Douyin and Kuaishou.

### 3. The competitive landscape of live streaming e-commerce

Live streaming e-commerce is Kuaishou’s second largest revenue contributor. In 2025, Kuaishou e-commerce GMV growth rate will drop to 15%, which is much lower than previous years. Looking at the entire industry:

| Platform | E-commerce GMV growth trend | Features |
|------|---------------|------|
| Douyin e-commerce | From high speed to medium speed | Branding, quality, covering all categories |
| Kuaishou E-commerce | Continued decline (15%) | Mainly white brands, sinking into the market, trusting e-commerce |
| Video account e-commerce | High growth stage | Social fission, private domain traffic |
| Pinduoduo | Stable growth | Ultimate cost-effectiveness, factory direct sales |

The core challenge facing Kuaishou e-commerce is: **It is neither the cheapest (Pinduoduo), nor the best quality (Douyin), nor the most social (Video Account)**. The positioning of "trust e-commerce" has certain differentiated value, but the moat is not deep.

## 2. In-depth analysis of competitors

### 1. Douyin/ByteDance - a giant that cannot be ignored

Douyin’s competitive pressure on Kuaishou is all-round:
- **User Duration**: Douyin DAU 600 million+ vs Kuaishou 410 million, the average daily duration of Douyin users is longer
- **Advertising Revenue**: ByteDance’s advertising revenue in 2025 will exceed 400 billion yuan, which is 5 times the advertising revenue of Kuaishou
- **E-commerce scale**: Douyin’s e-commerce GMV is 3-4 times that of Kuaishou
- **AI capabilities**: Byte's Dream AI and Doubao large models are also rapidly iterating
- **Globalization**: TikTok covers 150+ countries around the world, and Kuaishou Overseas (Kwai) is mainly in Brazil and Southeast Asia

What deserves more attention is Byte's advantages in organizational efficiency and talent density. The "context-based organization" (Context, not Control) established by Zhang Yiming allows Byte to fight on multiple battlefields simultaneously, while Kuaishou is difficult to match in terms of resources and talents.

### 2. WeChat video account - dimensionality reduction attack on social relationship chain

The threat of the video account is that it has almost no customer acquisition cost - the 1.2 billion WeChat users are its natural traffic pool. Although the content richness and user duration of the video account are currently not as good as Kuaishou, its growth trend is alarming:

- Video account live broadcast e-commerce growth rate exceeds 100%
-More and more creators use video accounts as an important distribution channel
- WeChat’s social fission capability is unmatched by any other platform

For Kuaishou, the rise of video accounts means that the "community" advantage in sinking markets is being eroded - many users in third- and fourth-tier cities spend more time watching video accounts on WeChat and less time watching Kuaishou.

### 3. Competition on the AI video generation track

This is the most imaginative track for Kuaishou, but the competition is also the most fierce:

| Products | Company | Technical Level | Commercialization Progress |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| Keling AI | Kuaishou | Top 3 in the world | ARR US$500 million (April 2026) |
| Sora | OpenAI | Advanced technology but slow commercialization | Mainly for professional users |
| Veo | Google | Integrated into YouTube ecosystem | Commercialization just started |
| Jimeng AI | ByteDance | Rapid iteration | Integrating with Douyin ecosystem |
| Happyhorse | Alibaba | Vincent Video leads the ranking | Early stage of commercialization |

The advantage of Keling is: **The fastest commercialization**. Looking at the ARR growth curve (240 million at the end of 2025 -> 300 million in January 2026 -> 500 million in April), the growth rate is astonishing. However, the technical barriers in this field are rapidly lowering - every few months, new models claim to surpass the previous generation. Whether the first-mover advantage can be transformed into a lasting advantage is a core question.

## 3. Reverse thinking: What might the market have overlooked?

### Market consensus

The current market consensus on Kuaishou is: "The growth of the main business has peaked, and Keling AI is the only bright spot, but there is uncertainty about its spin-off and listing."

### Factors that the market may underestimate

**1. The platform potential of Keling AI**

The market may simply understand Keling as an "AI video generation tool", but its potential may be greater. If it can be developed into an infrastructure platform for AI content creation - covering video generation, image generation, special effects production, advertising creativity and other aspects - then its TAM (total addressable market) is much larger than the current market pricing.

Adobe's annual revenue from creative tools exceeds US$20 billion. If Collin can capture a part of this market in the AI era, its valuation of US$20 billion may be an underestimate.

**2. Cash flow resilience of Kuaishou’s main business**

Even if growth slows down, Kuaishou's main business is still a stable cash flow machine - annual income of 20 billion and free cash flow of 12 billion. In an environment where Hong Kong stocks lack high-quality cash flow assets, this certainty is valuable. The market may focus too much on growth and ignore the value of stock.

**3. Long-term value of overseas markets**

Kuaishou’s success in Brazil is often underestimated. Brazilian DAU increased by 9.7% year-on-year, and user duration increased by 7.2%. Kwai has become a national-level application in Brazil. If it can be successfully monetized in the Brazilian market (advertising + e-commerce), this will provide Kuaishou with an incremental growth engine.

### Factors that may cause the market to be overvalued

**1. Commercial sustainability of Keling AI**

ARR grew from US$240 million to US$500 million, which is truly amazing. But we need to ask: How much of this revenue is one-time (users’ early adopter purchases)? What is the renewal rate? Can ARPU continue to increase? The user stickiness of AI video generation tools has not yet been verified - will users lose when competing products launch free or cheaper alternatives?

**2. The logic of value creation through spin-offs**

Keling’s spin-off valuation is US$20 billion, which is close to 70% of Kuaishou’s market value. But spin-off does not equal value creation - if Keling still relies on Kuaishou's computing infrastructure and data resources after becoming independent, independent operations may not be as efficient as integrated operations. Moreover, after the spin-off, Kuaishou lost the "growth story" of Keling, and the valuation of its main business may be further pressured.

**3. The sustainability of AI’s cost reduction and efficiency improvement**

Kuaishou promotes AI’s improvement in advertising efficiency and content recommendation efficiency, but these efficiency improvements will soon become industry standards. When all platforms use AI to optimize ad delivery, it will no longer be a competitive advantage but a survival necessity.

## 4. Key variables: What will determine the fate of Kuaishou?

### Variable 1: Can Keling AI establish lasting barriers?

If Kelingneng can stay ahead in technology and build a strong user ecosystem (tool chain, API ecosystem, developer community), it may become the "Adobe" of AI video generation - a platform product with high switching costs. However, if technical barriers continue to decrease and competing products continue to emerge, Keling may fall into a price war.

**Key observation indicators**: user retention rate, ARPU change trend, enterprise customer penetration rate

### Variable 2: Can the monetization efficiency of the short video industry continue to improve?

The room for improvement in advertising revenue per user (ARPU) determines whether Kuaishou's main business can continue to increase revenue without increasing user numbers. Referring to overseas markets - Meta's single-user ARPU is much higher than that of Chinese short video platforms, and theoretically there is room for improvement. However, advertising prices in the Chinese market are greatly affected by macroeconomics, and whether they can be realized depends on the degree of economic recovery.

**Key observation indicators**: Single DAU advertising revenue, number of advertisers and structural changes

### Variable 3: Can management find a balance between growth and efficiency?

Kuaishou needs to do two contradictory things at the same time: invest resources in developing new AI businesses (which costs money), while maintaining the profit margins and shareholder returns of its main business (which costs money). This balance tests the strategic determination and execution capabilities of management.

**Key observation indicators**: capital expenditure growth vs. revenue growth, changes in shareholder return policies

**Core conclusion**: The short video industry has shifted from incremental to existing, and the competitive pressure faced by Kuaishou as the second largest player will only increase. Douyin continues to expand its lead at the front, while video accounts are closing in behind. At the main business level, it is difficult for Kuaishou to reverse this pattern. Keling AI is Kuaishou's only opportunity to "change lanes and overtake" - if it can establish a platform status similar to Adobe's in the field of AI content creation, Kuaishou's value will be redefined. But the competition on this track is extremely fierce, and there is no clear winner. Investing in Kuaishou is essentially betting that Keling AI can win.
