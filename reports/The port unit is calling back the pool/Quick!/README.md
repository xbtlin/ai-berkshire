## Research background

Kuaishou is China’s second-largest short video platform, behind Douyin. The company achieved its best performance in history with revenue of 142.8 billion yuan and adjusted net profit of 20.6 billion yuan in 2025. However, the stock price plummeted 14% after the release of the annual report. The signal given by the market is very clear: **The quality of profits is not as important as the growth of profits**.

The core contradiction lies in:
- **User growth peaked**: MAU growth dropped below 2%, and 8 million DAU were lost in Q4.
- **E-commerce growth rate is declining**: GMV growth rate will drop from 78% in 2021 to 15% in 2025, and the company announced that it will no longer disclose GMV
- **Keling AI is the only bright spot**: ARR soared from US$240 million at the end of 2025 to US$500 million in April 2026, with a rumored spin-off valuation of US$20 billion
- **Cheap valuation but for a reason**: PE 11-12 times, lower than Douyin Group and Tencent, but growth narrative faces challenges
