## 1. Profitability analysis

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Indicators | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Revenue (billion yuan) | 265 | 238 | 219 | 208.6 |
| Net profit attributable to parent company (100 million yuan) | 46 | 30 | 10 | 8.45 |
| Net profit excluding impairment | 46 | 30 | ~14 | ~22-23 |
| Photovoltaic glass gross profit margin | ~25% | ~18% | 9.7% | 14.1% |

**Key Findings:**

Superficial data shows that the company is in a continuous decline in profits - revenue has shrunk from 26.5 billion to 20.9 billion, and net profit attributable to the parent company has dropped sharply from 4.6 billion to 845 million. But this data needs to be "peeled off the onion" to look at.

FY2025 net profit of 845 million yuan includes an impairment of fixed assets of 1.55 billion yuan (the after-tax impact exceeds 1 billion). If this non-recurring factor is excluded, the operating net profit will be approximately 2.2-2.3 billion yuan. Compared with approximately 1.4 billion yuan in FY2024 after excluding 390 million impairments, the operating profit has actually increased by approximately 57% year-on-year**.

This is an important signal: in the context of continued slight decline in revenue, operating efficiency has improved significantly.

### 1.2 Gross profit margin trend—has the turning point appeared?

The gross profit margin will jump to 24.8% in the second half of 2025, and the gross profit margin of photovoltaic glass will rebound to 14.1% from 9.7% in FY2024. The sharp improvement in the second half of the year mainly comes from:
- The proportion of overseas sales increased to 33.5%, and overseas products enjoyed a significant premium.
- Prices of raw material costs (especially soda ash) fall back
- Increase in capacity utilization due to the resumption of production of large kilns

**Does this mean a profit turning point has arrived? ** Requires careful judgment. The sustainability of overseas premiums depends on trade policies and the speed with which overseas production capacity can follow suit. But the improvement on the cost side is structural.

### 1.3 Profitability Rating ★★★☆☆

Currently at the bottom of the cycle, short-term profitability is weak. However, operating profits have improved, and the gross profit margin turning point signal is positive. Under normalized profitability (photovoltaic glass gross profit margin returns to 20-25%), the annualized net profit is estimated to be in the range of 2.5-3.5 billion yuan.

## 2. Balance sheet health

### 2.1 Debt level

By the end of 2025:
- **Net debt ratio after excluding Xinyi Energy: 2.8%** - extremely healthy
- Overall net debt ratio: 20% (including Xinyi Energy’s project loans)
- Xinyi Energy’s liabilities are mainly used for power plant project financing and are covered by stable cash flow

This is one of Xinyi Solar's biggest financial highlights. In the cold winter of the industry, the company has almost no net debt pressure. In contrast, many second- and third-tier photovoltaic glass companies have fallen into debt difficulties.

### 2.2 Asset Quality

**Focus: Impairment of fixed assets**

In FY2024, an impairment of 390 million will be provided, and in FY2025, an impairment of 1.55 billion will be provided (including 720 million for photovoltaic glass and about 800 million related to polysilicon). Large impairments mainly target:
- backward production capacity (small kiln production line)
- Polysilicon investment
- Adjustment of some overseas production capacity (the Malaysian production line will cease production in October 2024)

Looking at it positively, the management's choice to make concentrated provision for impairment during the trough period is a kind of financial prudent behavior of "taking a big bath", and profits in future years will benefit from reduced depreciation. Negatively, this reflects misjudgments in previous production expansion decisions.

### 2.3 Liquidity

The ratio of operating cash flow to current liabilities is approximately 20.3%, and the ratio of operating cash flow to profit is 122.5%. This means the company's profits are of higher quality - actual cash received exceeds paper profits.

### 2.4 Balance Sheet Score ★★★★☆

An extremely low net debt ratio is an important source of safety margin. In the most difficult moments of the industry, companies will not be forced to sell assets or raise funds at a low price due to financial pressure. This is especially important at the bottom of a cycle.

## 3. Cash flow analysis

### 3.1 Free Cash Flow

Photovoltaic glass manufacturing is a capital-intensive industry, with high maintenance capital expenditures (kiln cold repairs, daily maintenance) and even huge expansionary capital expenditures (new production lines).

At the bottom of the current cycle, the company has significantly slowed down its pace of production expansion:
- Domestic plans to resume production of 4 production lines (not new construction, but resumption of existing kilns)
- Two production lines are under construction in Indonesia and are expected to be put into production in Q1 of 2026.
- Overall capital expenditure has shrunk significantly compared with the peak period

Operating cash flow remains positive, and the cash flow profit ratio exceeds 100%, indicating that even in the trough period, the main business is still generating cash.

### 3.2 Capital Allocation

**Dividend policy:** No final dividend was paid in FY2024 due to losses in the second half of the year, which is a negative signal. But from a conservative financial management perspective, it makes sense to retain cash during trough periods.

**Investment Decision:** Polycrystalline silicon investment has made obvious strategic mistakes, and a large amount of impairment has been made. This suggests that management’s ability to make investment decisions outside of its main business is questionable.

**Xinyi Energy's shareholding:** The company holds 49.03% of Xinyi Energy's equity. This part is a high-quality asset-power station operation provides stable recurring income and cash flow.

### 3.3 Cash flow score ★★★☆☆

The quality of operating cash flow is good, but high capital expenditure pressure and unstable dividends are negative points.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 Current Valuation Level

Calculated based on the stock price of approximately HK$3.2 in May 2026:
- Market value: approximately HKD 28 billion (~25.5 billion RMB)
-Based on FY2025 statement net profit PE: about 30 times (distorted by impairment)
- PE based on FY2025 operating net profit: approximately 11-12 times
-Based on FY2026E net profit (2.1 billion yuan) PE: approximately 12 times
- PB: about 0.8-1.0 times

### 4.2 Historical valuation range

Xinyi Solar's historical PE range is approximately 8-25 times (based on normalized profits). The current operating PE of approximately 12 times is at a historically low mid-range level.

The historical PB range is approximately 0.8-3.0 times. Currently about 0.9 times PB is in the historical bottom area.

### 4.3 Normalized Profitability Estimation

Assuming that the photovoltaic glass industry returns to normalization (gross profit margin 20-25%), Xinyi Solar’s annualized net profit is estimated:
- Conservative scenario (gross profit margin 18%): approximately 2 billion yuan
- Neutral scenario (gross profit margin 22%): approximately 3 billion yuan
- Optimistic scenario (gross profit margin 25%+, overseas proportion further increases): about 4 billion yuan

Calculated based on a neutral scenario of 3 billion yuan in net profit, the current market capitalization of 25.5 billion yuan corresponds to approximately 8.5 times PE, and the valuation is somewhat attractive.

### 4.4 Intrinsic value range estimation

**Method 1: Normalized PE method**
- Neutral scenario net profit of 3 billion yuan x 12 times PE (reasonable valuation of manufacturing industry) = 36 billion yuan ≈ HKD 4.5/share
- Conservative scenario net profit of 2 billion yuan x 10 times PE = 20 billion yuan ≈ HKD 2.5/share

**Method 2: PB method**
- Net assets of approximately 28 billion yuan, 1.2 times PB (reasonable level for a manufacturing leader) = 33.6 billion yuan ≈ HKD 4.2/share

**Method 3: Segment Valuation**
- Photovoltaic glass business: normalized net profit 2.5 billion x 10 times = 25 billion
- Xinyi Energy holds 49% shares: Xinyi Energy’s market value is approximately HKD 8 billion x 49% = HKD 3.9 billion ≈ RMB 3.6 billion
- Total ≈ 28.6 billion RMB ≈ 3.6 HKD/share

Combining the three methods, the estimated intrinsic value range is about HK$3.5-4.5**, and the center is about HK$4.0. The current stock price of HK$3.2 has a potential upside of about 20-40%.

### 4.5 Valuation Rating ★★★★☆

Valuations at the bottom of the cycle are attractive, PB is at a historical bottom, and normalized PE is low. But you need to be aware of the risk of "value trap" - if the industry recovery is less than expected, low valuations may persist for a long time.

## 6. Financial risk reminder

1. **Impairment risk has not been fully released:** There may be subsequent impairment pressure on polysilicon investments
2. **Uncertainty about dividends:** The dividend policy may continue to be reduced during the trough period
3. **Exchange rate risk:** The proportion of overseas revenue increases, and fluctuations in the RMB exchange rate affect profits.
4. **Related transactions:** The fairness of Xinyi Group’s internal transactions needs to be paid attention to
5. **Goodwill and Investment:** Non-main business investments (such as polysilicon) have proven to have decision-making risks

## 8. Core Conclusions

From a financial perspective, Xinyi Solar shows the most typical bottom-of-cycle characteristics: revenue and profits are under pressure, asset impairments are concentrated, and dividends are reduced. But key "bottom signals" are also emerging: gross profit margins are turning, operating profits are improving, and net debt ratios are extremely low.

The most important judgment is: **This is not a company that may fail**. The net gearing ratio of 2.8% means that the company has sufficient financial resilience even if the industry continues to deteriorate for 1-2 years. When the industry recovers, as the lowest-cost leader, it will be one of the biggest beneficiaries.

Current valuations are in historically low ranges, providing patient investors with a certain margin of safety. But investors need to be patient about "when the recovery occurs," which could be 6 months or 2 years.
