# I. Full scan of key risks

# 1.1 Industry cycle risk (high)

** Risk description:** Overcapacity in the photovoltaic glass industry is not fully cleared and prices may hover for longer periods at the bottom.

**Exact performance:**
- Average sales of photovoltaic glass fell by about 50 per cent in 2024-2025 compared to 2021.
- Low utilization of overall industrial capacity
- Although the exit of the two-and-three-line business is accelerating, the clearance time may be longer than expected
- The pressure on the main chain (silicon, batteries) to transfer excess capacity to the auxiliary material chain has not yet been completely removed

** Worst-case scenario:** Industry valleys continued for another two to three years, companies suffered low profits or even losses for several years and stock prices were chronically depressed.

** Mitigation:** ** Shining Light is one of the lowest-cost producers, and even a continuing industry slump can sustain a positive cash flow to operate. The 2.8% net debt ratio means it has the ability to "get over".

##1.2 Technological Alternative Risks

** Risk description:** New generation photovoltaic technologies such as thorium mines may alter the demand characteristics for photovoltaic glass.

** Specific analysis:**
- The thorium cell is extremely sensitive to hydrooxygen and requires a very high containment technology.
- It might be necessary to replace the traditional pressure-conserving glass with the TCO conductor glass.
- If technological routes change, existing production lines may need to be overhauled on a large scale (investments of hundreds of millions to billions of yuan)
- Old lines may depreciate rapidly.

** Time dimension:** The sorghum mine is still in its mid-test phase, with commercial mass production expected to be 5-10 years later. This gives the time window for the transformation of the aluminum.

** Mitigation factors:** As industry leaders, Shinji light has the best R & D resources and funding to adapt to technological change. Historically, industry has successfully transformed its technological mix (from float to pressure, from 2 mm to 1.6 mm thinning).

# 1.3 Geopolitics and trade barriers risks

** Risk description:** In the face of globalization, policies to localize the photovoltaic supply chain in countries are both opportunities and risks.

** Double-edged saber effect:**
- ** Positive aspects:** Trade barriers create price premiums in overseas markets, and believing overseas production capacity (Malaysia, Indonesia) benefits directly
- ** Negative:** China may face greater restrictions on plant exports if trade barriers are further exacerbated; overseas plant operations are subject to policy uncertainty

** Specific risk points:**
- The constant change in the United States tariff policy on China ' s photovoltaic products
- South-East Asia "anti-circumvention" investigation into export routes that could affect overseas capacity
- Localization policies in such markets as India, the EU, etc. may require a higher proportion of local production
- New Indonesian factories may face operational risks such as local policy changes, labour issues, etc.

#1.4 Multi-investment risk of failure

** Risk description:** company investments in the multi-crystal silicon field have proven to be strategic miscalculations.

** Losses that have occurred:** Of the $1.55 billion in impairments projected by NY in 2025, about $800 million are related to multi-crystal silicon-related investments. This means that management has doubts about its ability to make investment decisions outside the main business.

**Follow-up risk:** If the company continues to expand its investments unrelated to the main industry, this may result in further value loss.

##1.5 Associated transactions and corporate governance risks

** Risk description:** There are a large number of related transactions between listed companies with a "faith system".

** Structural complexity:**
- Sin-Glass holds 23.23% of the Sight.
- Shiniyo holds 49.03% of the energy supply.
- The Lee Hyun-hyeon family is the actual control of the companies.
- Linked transactions between companies, such as raw materials procurement, equipment sharing, etc.

** Governance concerns:**
- Is the protection of minority shareholders ' interests adequate under the family control model?
- Is the transfer of power station assets between Sin-Energy and Shin-Sin-Sight justly priced?
- Is there a profit in the flow of purchase prices between the glass of faith and the light of faith?

** Mitigating factors:** There is no evidence of significant corporate governance problems. For many years, companies have been listed and audited by four major accounting firms.

# 1.6 Environmental and safety risks

The production of photovoltaic glass involves high-temperature kilns, which pose a risk to safe production. Gas use and emissions are subject to environmental compliance.

# III. Security margin assessment

#3.1 Downline Protection

** Balance sheet protection:** Net liability rate is only 2.8 per cent (excluding trusted energy), even if industry continues to be depressed for two to three years, and companies do not have financial crisis risks.

** Cost advantage protection:** As the least costly producer of the industry, Shinrikyo is the "last loss" company. When the profits of the industry are squeezed to the extreme, the two- or three-line firms withdraw from the loss and the long line is ultimately affected.

** Value protection of assets:** Currently, the PB is about 0.9 times closer to the level of net assets. Even as a "liquidation value" provides a certain bottom support (of course, the actual liquidation value of the kiln assets is much lower than the book).

** Trust energy holdings:** Holding 49% of the stake in the supply of philanthropy, which operates stable photovoltaic power stations and provides regular revenue.

#3.2 Upline Potential

** cyclical reversal potential:** If the price of photovoltaic glass is reversed from the current low of 20-30 per cent, the annual profits of the company's normalization could be $3-35 billion, corresponding to the current market value of only 7-8 times the PE.

The premium for overseas products can increase the overall profitability significantly.

** The market share of the industry has risen since the end of the cycle:** The market share of the oligarchs has been rising since the end of the cycle.

# 3.3 Security margin combined judgement

The current price (about $3.2) provides a certain margin of security, but not a "very cheap" level:
- about 10-20 per cent discounted against net assets
- E.E. about 8-12 times more profitable than normality
- Downside protection comes from a very low liability and cost advantage.
- But the duration of the end of the cycle is uncertain. Patience is necessary.

# IV. Scenario analysis

## Scenario I: Baseline scenario (40% probability)
- Industrial warming in the second half of 2026-2027
- Recovering of the gross gross profit rate of photovoltaic glass to 18-22%
- 2.5 to 3 billion yuan annual net profit
- Reasonable valuation: HK$3.5-4.5 (+10 to +40 per cent)

# Scenario two: optimistic (25% probability)
- Performance control policies are more effective than expected and demand and supply is tightened
- Over-expected growth in overseas markets and continued premium rates
- 3.5-4 billion yuan per annum net profit
- Reasonable valuation: HK$ 5.0-6.0 (+55 to +90 per cent)

# Scenario III: pessimism (25% probability)
- The industry continued to run down to 2028.
- Foreign trade policies tightened and premiums disappeared
- 1.5-20 billion yuan net annual profit
- Potential valuation: HK$ 2.0-2.5 (-20 to 35 per cent)

# # Scenario IV: extreme risk scenarios (at 10% probability)
- The breakthrough in the mining technology has accelerated and the need for light glass has fundamentally changed
- Loss of capacity abroad due to geopolitical conflicts
- The company continues to have a significant impairment.
- Potential valuation: HK$ 1.5-2.0 (-35% to 55%)

# VII. Core conclusions

Impartiality is essentially a logic of ** "buying the best companies at the bottom of the cycle."

** The premise underpinning this logic is:**
1. The photovoltaic glass industry is indeed at the bottom of the cycle (rather than in the structural recession)
2. Shinrikyo is indeed the best company in the industry (lowest cost, most financially sound)
3. Sectors eventually recover (rebalancing supply and demand)

** The logical element of the threat is:**
1. Bottom duration may be longer than expected
2. Technological route changes may change the fundamental logic of the industry
3. Possible management decisions that continue to be made outside the circle of competence

** Final judgement:** Risk is manageable and quantifiable. There is no risk of zero for the company. The core variable is time -- how long you need patience to wait for a cycle reversal. For long-term investors who are patient and disciplined, the current position has investment value. But the warehouse should not be too heavy to deal with the risk of "bringing down longer than expected".
