## 2. Investment thesis

### 2.1 Core bullish logic

**"Buy the leader at the bottom of the cycle":**

1. The industry is indeed at the bottom of the cycle. ** The price of photovoltaic glass has halved from its high point, second- and third-tier companies have continued to exit at a loss, and production capacity control policies have become stricter - these are typical characteristics of the bottom of the cycle.

2. **Xinyi Light Energy is indeed the best quality faucet. ** No. 1 in production capacity (23,200 tons/day), largest single kiln scale (average 1,009 tons), lowest cost (triple advantages of scale + location + resources), and most financially sound (net debt ratio 2.8%).

3. **The profit turning point signal has appeared. ** Gross profit margin jumped to 24.8% in the second half of FY2025, and the proportion of overseas sales rose to 33.5%. Operating profit after excluding impairments increased significantly by 57% year-on-year.

4. **Overseas layout opens up incremental space. ** The Indonesian production line will be put into operation in Q1 of 2026. Under the trend of anti-globalization, overseas production capacity enjoys a structural premium.

### 2.2 Core bearish logic

**Possibility of "value trap":**

1. **Cycle bottoms may last longer. ** The overcapacity in the photovoltaic main chain has not yet been completely cleared, and downstream component manufacturers are still squeezing profits from auxiliary materials. The bottom may take another 12-24 months.

2. **This is not a "good" business. ** Asset-heavy, cyclical, lack of pricing power, and technology iteration risks. There is a fundamental gap between the quality of business models of consumer goods or Internet companies.

3. **Management has a record of mistakes. ** The polysilicon investment loss was approximately 1 billion+, and the production line in Malaysia was suspended, indicating that the strategic decision was not perfect.

4. **Hong Kong stock liquidity discount. ** The photovoltaic sector in Hong Kong stocks overall lacks attention, and valuation restoration may take longer.

5. **Long-term threats to perovskites. ** If perovskites are commercialized on a large scale within 5-10 years, the value of existing production line assets may shrink significantly.

## 5. Investment Conclusions and Suggestions

### 5.1 Overall judgment

Xinyi Solar is a **"Conditional Investment Opportunity"**:

- Condition 1: You agree that the photovoltaic glass industry is indeed at the bottom of the cycle
- Condition 2: You have 12-24 months to wait patiently for the cycle to pick up
- Condition 3: You can accept the maximum retracement possibility of about 30% during the period
- Condition 4: Your investment portfolio is sufficiently diversified and there is no need to concentrate a large proportion of positions here.

If the above four conditions are met, the current position is worth opening a position.

### 5.2 Position recommendations

**Recommended position: Observe the position (1-3%), wait for the confirmation signal and then increase the position to the medium position (3-5%)**

**Reason:**
- The safety margin exists but is not sufficient, and heavy positions are not supported
- Uncertainty in timing of cycle bottom
- The quality of the business model is moderate and does not constitute an investment with "extremely high certainty"

### 5.3 Trigger conditions for adding positions

When more than two of the following signals appear, you may consider adding a position:
1. The quarterly average price of photovoltaic glass increased by more than 5% from the previous quarter.
2. The industry’s shutdown production capacity in a single quarter exceeds 3,000 tons/day
3. The company’s single-quarter gross profit margin rebounded to more than 20%
4. Indonesian production capacity was successfully put into operation and contributed profits
5. The stock price fell below HK$2.5 due to non-fundamental factors (such as the collapse of the Hong Kong stock market)

### 5.4 Reduce position/stop loss conditions

You should consider reducing your position or exiting when the following signals appear:
1. Management announced major non-core business investments (repeating the mistake of polysilicon)
2. A major breakthrough in commercialization of perovskite technology
3. The company’s net debt ratio has exceeded 30% for two consecutive half-year reports.
4. A vicious price war breaks out between the duopoly
5. A major corporate governance scandal occurred in the Xinyi Department

## 6. Fit with the research framework

### 6.1 Is this "buying a stock means buying a company"?

Partly yes. As an industry leader, Xinyi Solar's long-term competitive position is investable. But this is more of a "cyclical stock investment" than a "growth stock investment" or "value stock investment" - you need to buy and sell at the right cycle.

### 6.2 Is this an investment within the "circle of competence"?

The business model of photovoltaic glass is relatively simple and easy to understand: production of standardized industrial products, cost is the core competitiveness, and industry cycles determine profitability. This is an "understandable" investment that does not require judgment on complex technologies.

But timing industry cycles is difficult. If you are not good at judging cycles, the difficulty of this investment will increase.

### 6.3 Comparison with Buffett's "stocks not to look at" standard

Xinyi Lighting **does not meet** the standard of "you don't have to look at it after buying it". You need to continuously track industry supply and demand, price changes, and the pace of production capacity deployment. This is the natural attribute of manufacturing cyclical stocks and cannot be avoided.
