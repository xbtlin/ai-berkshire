## Research background

Xinyi Solar is the world's largest photovoltaic glass manufacturer. Together with Flat, they form a "duopoly" in the industry, with their combined market share exceeding 50%. The company is currently at the bottom of the industry cycle. From 2024 to 2025, profits will be under significant pressure due to falling photovoltaic glass prices and asset impairments. However, the cost advantage is outstanding, overseas layout is accelerating, and signs of tightening on the supply side of the industry are emerging.
