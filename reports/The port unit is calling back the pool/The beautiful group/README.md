## Company Profile

Midea Group is China's largest home appliance manufacturer, with its business covering smart home (air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, kitchen appliances, etc.) and commercial and industrial solutions (building technology, robotics and automation, new energy and industrial technology). The company has approximately 400 subsidiaries in more than 200 countries and regions around the world, and overseas revenue accounts for more than 40%.
