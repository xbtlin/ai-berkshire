## Investment thesis

### Core bullish logic

1. **One of the best manufacturing management teams in China. ** During his 13-year tenure, Fang Hongbo completed the transformation from a family business to a modern enterprise, the upgrade from a home appliance company to a technology group, and the leap from a domestic leader to a global player. This ability to continuously evolve is a scarce asset.

2. **100% of net profits will be returned to shareholders. ** Dividends of 32.4 billion in 2025 + repurchases of 11.6 billion = 44 billion, which is approximately equal to the annual net profit. At current valuations, the shareholder return rate is about 6%-7%, and even if the company grows at zero, it can obtain bond-like income while retaining the option value of growth.

3. **Two-wheel drive provides growth resilience. ** Smart home (299.9 billion, +11.3%) and commercial and industrial solutions (122.8 billion, +17.5%) walk on two legs. ToB business has fast growth, high profit margins, and strong customer stickiness, and is becoming an increasingly important growth engine.

4. **Globalization layout to build long-term competitiveness. ** Overseas revenue was 196 billion (+15.9%), accounting for over 40%. The realization of "production localization" in 63 manufacturing bases around the world is not only a source of growth, but also a strategic layout to deal with trade barriers.

### Core bearish logic

1. **The slowdown in the growth rate of the domestic home appliance market is structural. ** The average household ownership is close to saturation, the "trade-in" policy dividend is unsustainable, and domestic income growth may drop from double digits to mid-single digits.

2. **Tariffs and geopolitical risks are difficult to completely avoid. **Although the globalization layout is good, if trade protectionism further escalates, the profit margin and growth rate of overseas business will be affected.

3. **The successor has not been fully verified. ** Wang Jianguo’s appointment as CEO is a positive signal, but whether he can replicate Fang Hongbo’s strategic judgment and execution will require at least a 2-3 year observation period.

4. **Valuation is not extremely cheap. ** Although 13-14 times PE is at a historical low, it is not a "breaking price". If earnings growth slows to single digits, valuations could come under further pressure.

## Key variables that need to be continuously tracked

1. **ToB business profitability:** The revenue growth rate has been verified, but can the profit margin and payment quality keep up?
2. **Overseas OBM proportion:** The profit margin of independent brands overseas is much higher than that of OEM. How is the progress of this structural change?
3. **Wang Jianguo’s actual performance:** The executive president’s decision-making quality and management effectiveness are the core indicators for judging succession risks.
4. **The evolution of tariff policies:** Especially the policy trends of the United States and Europe towards Chinese manufacturing products.
5. **Accounts receivable and goodwill:** Changes in these two accounts reflect the true quality of ToB business and overseas mergers and acquisitions.

## Summary

Midea Group is a Chinese manufacturing leader with an excellent business model, first-class management, and excellent financial quality. At the current price of 13-14 times PE and 6%-7% total shareholder return, it provides an opportunity to "buy a high-quality company at a reasonable price."

The core attraction is not short-term price game, but: an excellent management team, on a track with high certainty, continues to create value in a shareholder-friendly way.

The core risks lie in: the structural trend of slowing domestic growth, policy uncertainty in the process of globalization, and variables in management transition. These risks are not enough to negate the investment thesis, but they determine the necessity of position management and continuous tracking.

**Summary in one sentence:** Midea Group is a company worthy of being put on the "priority attention list" - not the kind of super underestimated opportunity that makes people excited, but not a difficult target that requires harsh conditions to start. It is more like an option of "you can start building a position at any time and then patiently wait for time to compound interest".
