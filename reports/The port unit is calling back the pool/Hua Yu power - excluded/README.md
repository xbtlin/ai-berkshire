## Company Profile

China Resources Power is the flagship listing platform for the power business of China Resources Group. Its business covers thermal power generation, wind power, photovoltaic power generation, hydropower, distributed energy, electricity sales, comprehensive energy services and coal. By the end of 2025, the company's managed grid-connected installed capacity exceeded 100 million kilowatts, and renewable energy installed capacity accounted for more than 50%, marking a milestone in the company's strategic transformation from a traditional thermal power enterprise to a comprehensive clean energy operator.

## Core investment logic

**Reasons to be long**:
1. Restoration of thermal power profitability + capacity price mechanism to ensure basic income
2. New energy installed capacity is growing rapidly, and scale effects are gradually emerging.
3. China Resources New Energy spins off A-share listing (24.5 billion raised fund has been registered), releasing value
4. PE 7 times, dividend yield 5.7%, valuation has a margin of safety
5. Central enterprise background, obvious advantages in financing costs

**Reasons for shorting**:
1. Green electricity prices continue to fall, and new energy profit growth is under pressure
2. The rate of wind and light abandonment is rising, and consumption problems are becoming increasingly prominent.
3. Large-scale capital expenditures push up debt ratios and put free cash flow under pressure
4. The power market reform deepens and the uncertainty of electricity prices increases.
5. The risk of new energy subsidy arrears has not been completely eliminated.
