## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 The nature of the power industry

Electricity is a typical "necessity" business. Whether the economy is good or bad, the demand for electricity is there. But the problem is that electricity is an extremely homogeneous commodity. One kilowatt hour of electricity is one kilowatt hour of electricity. Users do not care whether it is produced by China Resources or Huaneng. This means that it is almost impossible for power companies to obtain excess profits through "product differentiation".

From Duan Yongping's perspective, the core criterion for a good business is: **Users need you, and it is difficult to find substitutes**. The electric power industry satisfies the first half of the sentence, but does not fully satisfy the second half. Power generation companies have limited say in the industrial chain - the upstream is subject to coal prices (thermal power) or weather (new energy), and the downstream is subject to power grid and government pricing. This is not an "easy money" business.

### 1.2 China Resources Power’s business structure

China Resources Power's current business can be divided into two major segments:

**Thermal Power Business**: Attributable installed capacity is approximately 39,000 MW, with core profit of HK$7.639 billion in 2025. The essence of thermal power is a "price difference business" - what you make is the difference between the price of electricity and the price of coal. Thermal power will make a lot of money during the downward coal price cycle (thermal power profit in 2025 is +64.7% year-on-year), but it may suffer losses when the coal price rises (a painful lesson from 2021-2022). This strong cyclicality means that thermal power profits are less predictable.

However, the adjustment of the capacity price policy in 2026 is a major variable - the recycling ratio is increased from 30% to no less than 50%, which is equivalent to setting a "guaranteed income" for thermal power. This has changed the profit structure of thermal power, turning it from pure price difference speculation to a "guaranteed + flexible" model.

**New Energy Business**: Attributable installed capacity is approximately 39,000 MW (wind power 2,555MW, photovoltaic 1,297MW, hydropower 44MW), and core profit in 2025 is HK$7.604 billion. The advantage of new energy is that the marginal cost is almost zero - when the wind blows and the sun shines, there is no need to buy coal. But the problem is: (1) fixed investment is huge and depreciation pressure is high; (2) on-grid electricity prices continue to fall; (3) the problem of wind and solar power abandonment is becoming increasingly serious.

The data in 2025 illustrates the problem: new energy profits fell by 17.6% year-on-year, the average wind power price dropped from 0.44 yuan to 0.35 yuan/kWh, and the average photovoltaic price dropped from 0.32 yuan to 0.28 yuan/kWh. Installed capacity is growing rapidly, but unit profitability is declining.

### 1.3 The logic of two-wheel drive

China Resources Power's strategic logic is the two-wheel drive of "thermal power + new energy". This combination has its rationality: thermal power provides stable cash flow and grid peak shaving capabilities, while new energy provides growth space and policy dividends. The two have a certain hedging effect in the economic cycle - when coal prices are high, new energy sources relatively benefit, and when coal prices are low, thermal power profits are restored.

But this "hedge" isn't perfect. The result in 2025 is: thermal power increased significantly by 64.7%, but new energy fell by 17.6%. After the two offsets, the overall profit increased by only 0.9%. The real effect of two-wheel drive is "stable" rather than "fast".

## 2. Moat analysis

### 2.1 Scale and resource barriers ★★★★☆

China Resources Power's managed grid-connected installed capacity has exceeded 100 million kilowatts, ranking among the top among Chinese power companies. Scale brings several benefits:
- **Low Financing Cost**: Central enterprises have AAA ratings, and bond interest rates are much lower than private enterprises.
- **Strong resource acquisition ability**: Obtaining wind and solar resource indicators requires government-enterprise relations and project operation capabilities. China Resources has unique advantages in central-regional cooperation.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Management experience and standardization capabilities for large-scale operations

However, the advantage of scale does not constitute an "insurmountable" barrier in the power industry. Each of the five major power generation groups (Huaneng, Datang, Huadian, State Power Investment Corporation, and National Energy) has a capacity of one million megawatts. China Resources does not have a crushing advantage in terms of size.

### 2.2 Identity and Policy Barriers of Central SOEs ★★★★☆

As the only power listing platform under China Resources Group (a central enterprise directly under the administration of the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council), China Resources Power enjoys:
- Innate advantages of policy acquisition (such as new energy indicator allocation)
- A foundation of trust for cooperation with local governments
- Priority status in industry restructuring

However, the status of a central enterprise also brings constraints: profits must be moderately distributed to benefit society, and there is limited room for electricity price increases; the flexibility of the system and mechanism is not as good as that of private enterprises; management replacement is affected by organizational arrangements.

### 2.3 Technology and efficiency barriers ★★★☆☆

The technical threshold of the power industry is lowering. Wind power and photovoltaic equipment are increasingly standardized, and technical barriers to construction and operation are limited. China Resources Power's advantages lie more in **project management capabilities** and **asset operation efficiency** rather than technology itself.

It is worth noting that China Resources New Energy's gross profit margin is close to 60%, which is outstanding among its peers, reflecting better project quality and operational efficiency. However, as high-quality resources are gradually divided up, the rate of return of new projects is facing downward pressure.

### 2.4 Brand and Pricing Power ★★☆☆☆

Electric power companies have almost no brand premium, and 1 kilowatt hour of electricity is 1 kilowatt hour of electricity. Under the general trend of power market reform, the pricing power of power generation companies is weakening rather than strengthening. This is a fundamental flaw in the power industry as an investment target.

## 3. Advantages and Disadvantages of Business Model

### 3.1 Advantages

**High demand certainty**: China's electricity consumption is still growing, and the electricity consumption of the whole society will increase by about 5% year-on-year in 2025. New demands such as AI data centers and new energy vehicles have provided incremental support for electricity consumption.

**The policy support is clear**: The capacity price system provides a guaranteed income for thermal power, and the weight of renewable energy consumption responsibilities provides demand protection for green power.

**Spin-off and listing to release value**: China Resources New Energy's 24.5 billion A-share IPO has received registration approval from the China Securities Regulatory Commission in May 2026, and is expected to be listed around July 2026. Spin-off and listing can not only provide independent financing channels for new energy businesses, but also release the valuation discount of the Hong Kong stock parent company.

### 3.2 Disadvantages

**Capital Intensive**: Both thermal power and new energy are asset-heavy businesses. The full-year cash capital expenditure in 2025 will be approximately HK$56.8 billion, of which 42 billion will be used for new energy construction. Continued large-scale investment means free cash flow will be under long-term pressure.

**Profit Volatility**: Thermal power is greatly affected by coal prices, and new energy is greatly affected by electricity prices and consumption. Although the overall profit after superimposing the two is relatively stable, it is difficult to achieve sustained high growth.

**The business model does not have a "flywheel effect"**: Unlike Internet platforms, the more users, the more data, the better the experience, and the more users. The power business is linear - the more installed capacity, the more power generated, the more revenue, but there will be no acceleration effect.

## 4. Looking at China Resources Power from the perspective of "doing the right thing"

Duan Yong usually says "do the right thing, do it right". China Resources Power has "got it right" in terms of strategic direction:

**Do the right thing**:
- Started the layout in the early stage of new energy trend, and the installed capacity of new energy has increased by 263% since 2019
- Spin off the new energy business and list it independently to unlock value
- Thermal power will not give up and use the capacity price policy to transform into a "peak shaving + guarantee" role

**Things to be wary of**:
- The "impulse for scale" of new energy - sacrificing project quality in order to complete installed capacity targets
- The rate of wind and light abandonment has increased rapidly (the rate of light abandonment has ranged from 1.52% to 12.73%), indicating that not all installed capacity can effectively generate electricity.
- The growth rate of capital expenditure far exceeds the growth rate of profits, and the risk of "increasing revenue without increasing profits" is accumulating

## 6. Summary

China Resources Power is a business that "can make money, but it's not easy to make big money." It has definite demand, the resource advantages of central enterprises and a reasonable strategic layout. However, due to the fundamental nature of "homogeneous products + policy pricing + heavy asset investment" in the power industry, it is difficult to become a "great business".

In Duan Yongping's words: This is not an opportunity that "you will miss if you don't do it", but at the right price, it is a target that "you won't make a big mistake if you buy it". The key is whether the price is cheap enough to make up for the shortcomings of the business model itself.
