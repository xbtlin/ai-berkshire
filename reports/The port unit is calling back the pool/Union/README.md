## Company Profile

Lenovo Group was founded in 1984 with investment from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. In 2004, it acquired IBM's global PC business and became a global enterprise. It is currently the world's largest PC manufacturer (market share 25.4%), and continues to expand in the server/infrastructure (ISG) and solution/service (SSG) fields.

Three major business segments:
- **IDG (Intelligent Device Business Group)**: PCs, tablets, mobile phones, etc., core profit sources
- **ISG (Infrastructure Solutions Business Group)**: servers, storage, AI infrastructure, rapid growth but not yet stable profitability
- **SSG (Solution Services Group)**: IT services and solutions, high profit margin (22% + operating profit margin)

## Research background

Global PC leader, PE 10-12 times, ROE about 20% (driven by high leverage), low profit margin assembly business. Under the AI ​​wave in recent years, ISG's server business has grown rapidly, with AI revenue accounting for 32%. The company has set the goal of revenue exceeding US$100 billion and net profit margin exceeding 5% within two years. It is worth in-depth study of the sustainability of its business model, the true value of AI transformation, and whether the current valuation fully reflects the fundamentals.
