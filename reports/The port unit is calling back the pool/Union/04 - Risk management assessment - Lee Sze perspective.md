# I. Management assessment: the work and the past of Yang Wonqing

# 1.1 Core person: Yang Wonqing

Yang Wonqing has been President of the United Nations Network of Vision CEOs since 2001 and has been in the process of leading the association for over 24 years. This is an extremely long term, enough to fully evaluate a manager.

♪ The "Wonking of Yang" ♪

1. **The implementation of globalization**: Yang Wonqing led the transition from China PC to the global technological hardware giant. In 2004, IBM PC was acquired with less than 3% of the global share; today it is the world ' s first (25.4%). Whatever the perception of the acquisition itself, this implementation has been successful.

** Organizational integration**: Integration after cross-border M&As is the most difficult - cultural conflicts, loss of people, loss of customers are the norm. It is a positive fact that the majority of the ThinkPad team and business clients were successfully retained after the acquisition of IBM PC.

3. **Strategic Patience**: The ISG operation (x86 server) has been in deficit for almost 10 years since its acquisition in 2014, and until 2025/26 it began to move closer to the balance of gains and losses. Yang won’s patience is respected if ISG eventually stabilizes the profits.

4. **Crisis response**: In a tariff storm, the multi-point manufacturing effort (India, Brazil, Hungary, Saudi Arabia) was linked to the pre-positioning of the market, demonstrating the maturity of the management of globalization.

♪ The Yang Won-kyung Pass ♪

** The profit rate has not increased for a long time**: 24 years of control, with revenues reaching nearly $100 billion from the grade of $10 billion, but net interest rates have remained at 2-3%. Income growth has not led to substantial improvements in the quality of profits. As long-term managers, this is an answer to the question — is the industry structure not allowed, or has management not found a way out?

2. **The digestiveness of the Motorola acquisitions**: The Motorola mobile was acquired for $2.9 billion in 2014 and the global share and profitability of mobile phone operations have been unsatisfactory to date. Mobile operations have long been close to the profit-and-loss balance line and have not become the "second engine of growth" of the idea. The acquisitions were at least not high in terms of financial return.

3. **Reservative R&D inputs**: The joint R&D costs are about 3% of the long-term revenue and are lower among major technology companies. Yuanqing Yang preferred the path of "acquisition technology through M&A" rather than "autonomous R&D innovation". This path is feasible in the PC era (since core innovation is done by Intel/Microsoft), but still is feasible in the AI era? If we think of changing from "assembler" to "resolver," 3% of R&D inputs are not enough.

4. ** High-pay dispute**: For fiscal year 2024/25, Yang Wonqing ' s total remuneration amounted to US$ 2.2386 million (approximately RMB 160 million), 65% of which was a long-term incentive. In the context of net corporate interest rates of only 2-3%, is the CEO ' s annual salary of US$ 160 million reasonable? In a horizontal comparison, Del CO Michael Dale ' s remuneration structure is more closely linked to the performance of the company ' s share price.

Yang Wonqing is a qualified executive manager – he made the connection first in scale under the industry constraints. But he is not a changer – the business model that he has been associated with has not changed fundamentally in his term of office. Higher pay is a small fraction.

- Is the conditions of tenure for long-term incentives challenging enough? If the threshold is too low (e.g., demand growth of revenue rather than profit growth), the incentive effect is reduced

# III. List of core risks

# Risk I: Geopolitics and tariffs (impact levels: <unk> )

This is the greatest external risk that the association would face.

The US tariff increases on Chinese electronics have affected the prices of the products they have been created. In extreme cases, if Chinese-American technology is decoupled, the sale of the United States may be substantially hit.
- ** Tariff response**: The association has established manufacturing bases in India, Brazil, Hungary, Saudi Arabia to avoid tariffs through "local production, local sales". But it takes time and cost to reconfigure the global supply chain.
- ** Sanctions risk**: Currently, the association is not listed as an entity, but as China’s largest PC enterprise, it cannot rule out the possibility of future sanctions. Once sanctioned (intel/AMD/Investda chips are not available), the association’s business will face fundamental challenges.

** Probability assessment**: Customs duties are determined on a continuous basis; sanctions are a tail risk with small probability but high impact.

# Risk two: ISG is not profitable for a long time (impact level: <unk> )

The organization wants to reorganize ISG in 2025 ($285 million in restructuring costs) with the goal of saving 200 million +/years over three years.

- **optimal **: ASI server demand continues to explode + L cool technology leads to variation + cost optimization works * ISSG achieved steady profitability in 2026-2027
- ** Pessimism**: ASI servers are competitive (Del, HPE, ultra-micro) + GPU costs are high + super-large-scale client self-research servers * ISG continues to struggle on the profit/loss balance line

If ISG continues to be unprofitable, the joint "AI transition story" lacks profit-end support -- there are growth-non-profit stories, capital markets do not pay long-term bills.

# Risk three: Down the PC market cycle (impact level: <unk> )

The PC market is clearly cyclical. It is currently in the upper stage of the AI PC re-routing cycle, but:
- The switch cycle usually lasts 2-3 years, and then it enters the next 2-3 years.
- Business and consumers delay PC procurement in the event of a global macroeconomic downturn
- The year 2018-2019 saw a round of the PC down-cycle, with a sharp contraction in profits.

** Impact on conjecture**: As IDG (PC operations) remains the main source of profits, the lower bounds of PC will directly impact group profits. Net interest rates of 2-3% have little buffer space.

# Risk four: high leverage + economic downturn combined risk (impact level: <unk> )

The joint high-leveraging model works well in normal circumstances, but in extreme cases it may be problematic:
- if PC demand falls off the cliff (as in the 2008 financial crisis), the revenue drop is 20-30%
- At the same time, interest costs on interest-bearing liabilities remain unchanged
- Operating liabilities (accounts payable) may be requested by suppliers to expedite payments
- Net interest rate could be directly reduced from 2% to loss.

The probability of this is low, but once it occurs, high leverage magnifies the loss.

# Risk five: risk of technological subversion (impact level: <unk> )

In the long term, would PC be replaced by other equipment (AR glasses, AI-associated devices, etc.)? It is unlikely for 5-10 years, but the 15-20 dimensions need to be considered. There is a lack of technological accumulation on the next generation of computing platforms — no self-research chips, no self-researching operating systems, no powerful AI software capabilities.

# IV. Culture and organization

# 4.1 Implementation culture

Conspicuousness is a typical "implementation-driven" organization - with emphasis on efficiency, process, KPI. This culture is appropriate for PC manufacturing operations (efficient, low cost), but not necessarily for innovative operations (needs error, tolerance for failure).

** Case**: The association performance in mobile (Motorola) and emerging operations reflects, to some extent, the limitations of the "implementing culture" in the area of innovation - the good practices of followers who are known to be in a race track, but the capacity to open up innovative products or new models is inadequate.

# 4.2 Culture of globalization

The United Nations is one of the most globalized Chinese enterprises – the internationalization of the executive team (from many countries), the working language English, and the global distribution of employees. This is a positive feature of the United Nations — it is not a "China-biased" company, but a real multinational corporation.

# 4.3 Talent issues

Recent management changes (February 2025, new CFO and Chief Justice) and ISG restructuring show that there are significant organizational adjustments. It is interesting to know whether ISG restructuring will lead to a loss of critical technical talent. What is the stability of the globalized team in a context of geopolitical tension?

# Evaluation: # # assessment: # # assessment: #

The implementation of a strong culture but inadequate innovative genes has led to the regulation of global governance but to organizational pressures brought about by geopolitical considerations.
