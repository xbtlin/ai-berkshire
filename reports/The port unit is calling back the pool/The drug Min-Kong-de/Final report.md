## 2. Core Contradiction

WuXi AppTec’s investment logic can be condensed into one core contradiction:

**Enterprise quality is top-notch, but pricing power and destiny are being partially handed over to Washington. **

### Strong side

- The world's leading CRDMO integrated platform, with revenue of 45.46 billion yuan in 2025, net profit attributable to parent companies of 19.15 billion yuan, and orders on hand of 58 billion yuan, all reaching record highs
- TIDES business (polypeptides + oligonucleotides) revenue of 11.37 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 96%, benefiting from the global wave of GLP-1
- The gross profit margin of the chemical business is 52.3%, ROE ~17%, ROIC ~14.5%, and the return on capital leads the global industry.
- 35.1 billion yuan in book cash, a "fortress-type" balance sheet, and strong risk resistance
- Revenue guidance for 2026 is 51.3-53 billion yuan (+18%-22%), with high certainty of growth

### Weak side

- The BIOSECURE Act was signed into law in December 2025, and OMB is required to publish a list of "Biotech Companies of Concern" by December 2026
- Revenue from US customers accounted for 68.7% (31.25 billion yuan), and any disturbance in the US market is a major event
- The actual controller team has reduced its holdings and cashed out a total of ~20 billion yuan since 2022. The consistency of interests between management and shareholders is questionable.
- The number of employees decreased by 14% year-on-year, R&D expenses have declined for three consecutive years, and the boundary between "efficiency optimization" and "overdrawing the future" is blurred
- Growth is highly dependent on the TIDES/GLP-1 track, and the risk of business concentration increases

## 3. Investment decision-making framework

### Currently not recommended to buy

The 25 times PE of Hong Kong stocks (based on 13.2 billion yuan in non-net profits in 2025) has fully reflected the optimistic expectations of 18-22% growth in 2026. As the BIOSECURE list has not been announced and management has not stopped reducing its holdings, the current price lacks a margin of safety.

### Catalysts and trigger points of concern

| Time nodes | Events | Impact on investment decisions |
|---------|------|----------------|
| December 2026 | OMB announces the list of "biotechnology companies of concern" | **The most critical node**: Whether WuXi AppTec is included will fundamentally change the valuation |
| Q2-Q3 2026 | Progress of the Delaware factory being put into production | Verifying the execution of the company's U.S. localization strategy |
| H2 2026 | Whether the actual controller’s shareholding reduction plan will continue | Determine the management’s true attitude towards the company’s prospects |
| 2027+ | BIOSECURE ban begins | Observing actual business impact vs. market expectations |

### Ideal buying conditions (re-evaluate if any one is met)

1. **Valuation falls**: Hong Kong stock PE falls back to 12-15 times (corresponding to a stock price of approximately HK$65-80). At this time, BIOSECURE risks have been fully priced
2. **Clear policy**: The OMB list is announced and WuXi AppTec is not included, leaving room for valuation repair.
3. **Management Signal**: The actual controller stops reducing its holdings or starts increasing its holdings, sending a positive signal
4. **Any two or more conditions are met at the same time**: significantly increase the investment winning rate

## 5. Final conclusion

### Qualitative judgment on WuXi AppTec

This is a company that ranks among the top in the world in terms of business quality. CRDMO integrated platform, 58 billion orders in hand, explosive growth of TIDES business, industry-leading return on capital - from a purely commercial perspective, WuXi AppTec is a benchmark for Chinese companies to go global.

But investment does not only look at the quality of the company, but also at price, risk and management. The current pricing of 25 times PE implies the optimistic assumption of "limited impact of the BIOSECURE Act + continued growth." If this assumption is true, investors can only obtain reasonable returns; if the assumption is not true, there is significant downside. This does not comply with the value investment principle of "buying something for one yuan with 40 cents".

Management's continued reduction in holdings is a warning signal. There is no doubt about the strategic capabilities and execution capabilities of Li Ge's team, but the behavior of "talking about being optimistic about the future while continuing to reduce holdings and cash out" makes it difficult for investors to fully trust the management.

### Operation suggestions

**Current action: Observe, do not take action. **

Put WuXi AppTec into the "Highly Concerned Recall Pool" and wait for any of the following opportunities:
1. Valuation falls below 15 times PE
2. Clarification after the announcement of the BIOSECURE list in December 2026
3. Management stops reducing its holdings

**One Sentence Summary**: WuXi AppTec is a good ship, but it is sailing through a reef area. It would be a wiser choice to wait until it has safely passed the reef, or when the ticket price has dropped enough to get on the boat.
