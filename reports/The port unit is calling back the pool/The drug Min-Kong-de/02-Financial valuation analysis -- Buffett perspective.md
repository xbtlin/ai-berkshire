#WuXi AppTec: Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Profit quality analysis: "Are you making real money?"

When Buffett reads financial reports, his first question is always: Is the profit real and sustainable, or one-time and artificial?

### 1. Income composition and growth quality

In 2025, WuXi AppTec achieved revenue of 45.46 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. Break down the income structure:

| Business segments | Revenue in 2025 | Year-on-year growth | Revenue proportion |
|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Chemical business (WuXi Chemistry) | 36.47 billion yuan | +25.5% | ~80% |
| Testing business (WuXi Testing) | 4.04 billion yuan | +4.7% | ~9% |
| Biology business (WuXi Biology) | 2.68 billion yuan | +5.2% | ~6% |
| Other/Divested Business | ~2.27 billion yuan | — | ~5% |

**Key Observations**:

- Growth is highly dependent on the chemical business, especially TIDES (polypeptides + oligonucleotides), with annual revenue of 11.37 billion yuan, +96% year-on-year. This growth is mainly due to the global outbreak of GLP-1 weight loss drugs (semaglutide, tilpotide, etc.), and WuXi AppTec is an important CDMO supplier of these drugs.
- **Risk Warning**: The high growth of TIDES business is highly related to the business cycle of GLP-1 drugs. If the growth rate of the GLP-1 track slows down or customers build their own production capacity, the sustainability of this part of the growth will need to be compromised.
- The testing and biology business grew at a single-digit rate, indicating that the company's growth engine is highly concentrated.

### 2. Profit authenticity test

The net profit attributable to the parent company in 2025 will be 19.15 billion yuan, +102.6% year-on-year. But this number needs to be broken down carefully:

**Excluding non-net profit of approximately 13.2 billion yuan**, +41% year-on-year - this can better reflect the true profitability at the operating level. The reasons why the net profit attributable to the parent company significantly exceeds the deduction include:

- Investment income generated from the sale of equity interests in subsidiaries (such as WuXi United, etc.)
- Gains from changes in fair value
- Government subsidies, etc.

The "Owner Earnings" that Buffett values most should be based on deducting non-net profits before adjusting capital expenditures:

**Owner's Earnings Estimate**:
- Excluding non-net profit: ~13.2 billion yuan
-Add back: depreciation and amortization of approximately 3 billion yuan (estimated)
- Less: Maintenance capital expenditure of about 4 billion to 5 billion yuan (of the total capital expenditure of 7 billion to 8 billion yuan in 2025, about half is expansionary)
- **Estimated owners' earnings: ~11.2-12.2 billion yuan**

### 3. Cash flow verification

| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Operating cash flow | ~16 billion yuan (estimate) | 12.4 billion yuan |
| Free cash flow | 4-5 billion yuan | 8.4 billion yuan |
| Capital expenditure | 7-8 billion yuan | ~4 billion yuan |
| Cash on Book | 35.13 billion yuan | ~18.3 billion yuan |

**Key Findings**:

- Operating cash flow covers net profit more than 1 times, indicating that profits are supported by cash and are not "rich on paper"
- Free cash flow in 2025 (4-5 billion yuan) will decrease compared with 2024 (8.4 billion yuan) due to a significant increase in capital expenditures - the company is investing heavily in Taixing peptide production capacity (solid-phase synthesis reactor over 100,000L), Delaware factory, etc.
- Book cash of 35.1 billion yuan nearly doubled year-on-year, and the balance sheet is extremely healthy

## 2. Analysis of return on capital: "How efficient is capital allocation?"

Buffett believes that ROE (return on equity) and ROIC (return on invested capital) are the core indicators for measuring the quality of a company.

### ROE and ROIC trends

| Year | ROE | ROIC (estimate) |
|------|-----|-------------|
| 2022 | ~18% | ~15% |
| 2023 | ~10% | ~8% |
| 2024 | ~12% | ~10% |
| 2025 | ~17% | ~14.5% |

**Analysis**:

- The sharp decline in ROE in 2023 is due to the severe winter in the industry and the impact of the BIOSECURE Act
- ROE will rebound to 17% in 2025, close to historical highs, mainly driven by the outbreak of TIDES and cost optimization
- ROIC 14.5%, which means that every dollar of capital invested by the company can generate 14.5 cents of after-tax operating profit, which is an **excellent level** in the manufacturing industry

**Benchmark comparison** (2025 consensus expectations):
- WuXi AppTec ROE ~17% (higher than the 6-16% range of global CXO peers)
- Lonza ROE ~12%
- Samsung Biologics ROE ~15%
- Asymchem ROE ~8%

WuXi AppTec leads the global industry in return on capital.

## 3. Financial health analysis: "Is the balance sheet strong enough?"

### Core indicators of balance sheet

| Indicators | 2025 |
|------|--------|
| Cash on Book | 35.13 billion yuan |
| Asset-liability ratio | ~30% (estimate) |
| Interest-bearing liabilities | Lower |
| Current ratio | >2 |

**Buffett-style evaluation**: This is a "fortress-type" balance sheet. The cash of 35.1 billion is enough to cover the capital expenditure plan in the next 3-4 years, and the company has almost no debt repayment pressure. Even if it encounters an extreme adverse scenario (such as being named in the BIOSECURE Act and losing some business), the company still has sufficient financial buffers.

### Shareholder Returns

The total cash dividend + repurchase in 2025 is 8.755 billion yuan, accounting for 45.7% of the net profit attributable to the parent company.

- Cash dividend: 6.755 billion yuan
- Share buyback and cancellation: ~2 billion yuan

The dividend rate is nearly 46%, which is relatively high among A-share technology/manufacturing companies. However, it should be noted that the actual controller reduced its holdings by 5.4 billion yuan during the same period and cashed out - from the perspective of "shareholders as a whole", the dividends were generous, but from the perspective of "actual controller's behavioral consistency", the amount of reduction was equivalent to the amount of dividends, and the signal was ambiguous.

## 5. Buffett-style "Circle of Competence" Test

### Can I understand this business?

WuXi AppTec’s business model (providing R&D and production outsourcing services to pharmaceutical companies) is understandable. But specifically:

- **Technical depth of chemical synthesis processes**: It is difficult for non-experts to assess whether WuXi AppTec’s technological leadership is sustainable
- **Cyclicity of TIDES business**: How long can the prosperity of the GLP-1 track last? There is an industry recognition threshold
- **Geopolitical Predictability**: Even Washington insiders find it difficult to accurately predict the final strength of the BIOSECURE Act.

Buffett usually requires a larger margin of safety for the parts he doesn't understand.

### Will this company be better or worse in ten years?

**Probably better**. Reason:
- There is still room for improvement in the penetration rate of global innovative drug R&D outsourcing (currently about 50%, and can reach 70%+ in the long term)
- The process complexity of new molecular types (polypeptides, oligonucleotides, ADCs, etc.) continues to increase, which is good for head CDMOs
- Although China’s engineer dividend has weakened at the margin, it will still have a cost advantage in the next 5-10 years

**But there are tail risks**: If the decoupling between China and the United States intensifies to the level of "total ban", WuXi AppTec's globalization model will face fundamental challenges.

## 6. Valuation conclusion

| Dimensions | Rating | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Profit quality | ★★★★☆ | Non-profit deductions are real, cash flow support is good, but growth depends on TIDES |
| Return on capital | ★★★★★ | ROE 17%, ROIC 14.5%, industry leading |
| Financial health | ★★★★★ | 35.1 billion in cash, low debt, fortress-shaped balance sheet |
| Valuation rationality | ★★★☆☆ | 25 times PE is at the upper end of the reasonable range, and the safety margin is insufficient |
| **General** | **★★★★☆(4/5)** | **Excellent financial quality, but the price is not cheap enough** |

**Buffett-style conclusion**: WuXi AppTec is a company with excellent financial quality - real profits, abundant cash flow, high return on capital, and a healthy balance sheet. If there are no geopolitical risks, below 20 times PE is a good buying point. However, the existence of the BIOSECURE Act means that additional "risk discounts" are needed. The ideal buying range is less than 15 times the PE of Hong Kong stocks (corresponding to a stock price of about HK$75). Only at this time will there be enough safety margin to compensate for the uncertainty.
