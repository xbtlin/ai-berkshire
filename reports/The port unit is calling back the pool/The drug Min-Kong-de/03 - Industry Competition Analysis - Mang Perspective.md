# Three, the moat's depth

Manger said, "We look for a company with a wide moat, the moat is growing. "How wide is the moat of the meds?

# Accompanied River I: integrated CRDMO platform (<unk> )

This is the competitive advantage of the drug Mingkang**, the core and uniqueest.

Most companies in the global CXO industry do only a "part" -- some only CRO (research outsourcing) and others only CDMO (production outsourcing). Physician Conduit is a very few platforms that can provide full chain services from "target spot to commercial production".

** Why does this make up the moat?**

- ** "Following Molecular" strategy**: Clients discovered a potential compound on the Pharmacene Condé platform, followed by process optimization, production of clinical test samples, commercial production, naturally prefer to remain on the same platform, as the transfer of suppliers implies a re-profiling of the process, transfer of technology and regulatory declarations, which takes 6-18 months.
- ** Data and knowledge deposition**: The drug Minconde accumulates project data for over 6,000 clients, and these know-how constitute the "invisible moats".
- ** Cross-sales capacity**: A customer may use both chemical and biological operations, and integrated services enhance client stickyness.

** Empirical data**: The contribution of the stock of clients in 2025 was over 90 per cent, with a very high retention rate.

Mang is also concerned about the erosion factor of the moat. The moat of the meddling condolence is threatened in three directions:

# Threat One: Geopolitical "institutional erosion" (several)

The BIOSECURE Act has been signed into law.

- OMB has to publish the list of "Biotech of Concern" by December 2026.
- If drug Mindcond is included, its operations related to US federal funds will be limited gradually after 2027

This is not a traditional sense of "competition", but a structural change in the rule level**, which is the greatest threat to the moat.

# Seven, Man format core judgement

** "If there is no BIOSECURE, Pharmacono Condé may be one of the best investment targets in the CXO industry around the world."**

It has a unique global integrated CRDMO platform, which has the leading position in a high-growth multi-billion-dollar market, with moats comprising customer stickyness, size advantages, technical barriers and regulatory compliance.

But the reality is that the BIOSECURE Act has been signed into law. Even if Ming Condé was not finally on the "list of concern" (which is the most optimistic scenario), the existence of the Act itself would constitute an "institutional discount" for the company's long-term competitive position – which forces some customers to add a layer of "policy compliance" to the selection of suppliers, which is an additional burden that no European or American competitor would have to face.

What would Manger do? He might say, "** It's a good boat, but it's in the waters full of reefs. If the reef is cleared, it's a good investment. If the reef is not cleared, any price can be high."**

The key catalyst is the list of "Biotech Enterprises of Concern", published by OMB in December 2026** — which will be a watershed in investment decision-making.
