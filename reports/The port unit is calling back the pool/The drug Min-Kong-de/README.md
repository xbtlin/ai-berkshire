## Core conclusion

**Comprehensive rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5) – A high-quality business, but governance and policy risks suppress valuation**

WuXi AppTec is one of the few "integrated CRDMO" platform companies in the world, with revenue of 45.46 billion yuan in 2025 (+15.8% year-on-year), net profit attributable to the parent company 19.15 billion yuan (+102.6% year-on-year), and orders on hand of 58 billion yuan, a record high. The chemical business accounts for more than 80%, and the TIDES business (polypeptide + oligonucleotide) annual revenue was 11.37 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 96%, benefiting from the wave of GLP-1 weight loss drugs. The company's ROE is about 17%, its gross profit margin is 46.6%, its cash on the books is 35.1 billion yuan, and its financial status is healthy.

**The core contradiction lies in**: (1) The U.S. BIOSECURE Act was signed into law in December 2025. Although WuXi AppTec was not named, OMB must publish a list of "biotechnology companies of concern" before December 2026. If it is included, the impact on U.S. federal business will be in 20 It gradually emerged after 27 years; (2) The actual controller Li Ge's team has reduced its holdings and cashed out nearly 20 billion yuan since 2022, and the consistency of the interests of management and shareholders is questionable; (3) R&D expenses have declined for three consecutive years, with a net reduction of 5,580 employees. Whether "cost reduction and efficiency increase" or "overdrawing the future" needs to be continuously observed.

**Buying conditions**: The PE of Hong Kong stocks falls below 15 times (corresponding to a stock price of approximately HK$75), or WuXi AppTec is clearly excluded from the BIOSECURE list. The current PE of 25 times has fully reflected optimistic expectations, and the safety margin is insufficient.
