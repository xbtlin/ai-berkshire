#WuXi AppTec: Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimension: Duan Yongping's "good business" perspective | Date: May 16, 2026 | Currency: RMB (unless otherwise noted)

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

WuXi AppTec is essentially an "infrastructure provider" for the innovative drug industry chain** - when global pharmaceutical companies develop new drugs, they need to go through a long chain from target discovery, compound screening, process development to clinical trials and commercial production. WuXi AppTec provides "one-stop outsourcing services" in this chain from beginning to end.

The company's own CRDMO model (Contract Research, Development and Manufacturing Organization) integrates the traditional separation of CRO (research outsourcing) and CDMO (production outsourcing) into an integrated platform. Customers can complete the entire process from "an idea" to "a bottle of medicine" on one platform.

**In the words of Duan Yongping**: Good business depends on "whether users will become better because of your products/services." WuXi AppTec's answer is yes - small and medium-sized Biotech companies around the world usually do not have the ability to build their own R&D and production systems. WuXi AppTec helps them advance their pipelines at a lower cost and faster speed. This is real value creation.

## 2. Four-dimension test of "good business"

### 1. Are user needs real and lasting? ★★★★★

Global innovative drug R&D outsourcing is an irreversible long-term trend. Several key drivers:

- **R&D costs continue to rise**: A new drug costs an average of US$2.6 billion from project approval to launch (Tufts CSDD data). Pharmaceutical companies have strong incentives to outsource non-core links to reduce risks and costs.
- **Biotech ecological prosperity**: There are about 6,000 Biotech companies in the world, and the vast majority do not have the ability or willingness to build their own production capacity. They are natural customers of the CXO industry.
- **Explosion of new molecule types**: Emerging molecules such as GLP-1 peptide drugs (semaglutide, etc.), ADCs, and oligonucleotides have extremely high requirements on process capabilities, and the demand for outsourcing is even more rigid. WuXi AppTec's TIDES business will generate revenue of 11.37 billion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 96%, which is a verification of this trend.

The demand for this business is not "optional consumption", but "rigid infrastructure" for the innovative drug ecosystem.

### 2. Does the business model have a "snowball" effect? ★★★★☆

WuXi AppTec's CRDMO model has an obvious "follow the molecule" strategy** - a molecule moves from early research to development to commercial production, with revenue and profit margins increasing step by step at each stage. This means:

- **Natural customer stickiness**: Once a molecule enters WuXi AppTec’s platform, the time and compliance costs of transferring it to other suppliers are extremely high. In 2025, existing customers will contribute more than 90% of revenue.
- **High revenue visibility**: Orders on hand are 58 billion yuan (+28.8% year-on-year), covering revenue in the next 1-2 years, giving strong certainty.
- **Scale effect**: The gross profit margin of the chemical business will increase from approximately 47% in 2024 to 52.3% in 2025. As TIDES's capacity utilization increases, marginal costs will decrease.

**Disadvantages**: This is not a "zero marginal cost" Internet business. CDMO is still a manufacturing industry in nature and requires continuous capital investment (capital expenditure of 7-8 billion yuan in 2025), and each project has customized attributes, and the scale effect has a ceiling.

### 3. What about pricing power? ★★★☆☆

WuXi AppTec's pricing power is at an "above average" level:

**Pros**:
- The switching cost of integrated platforms is high. Once customers are bound, their bargaining power decreases.
- Limited supply in high-barrier fields such as TIDES, strong pricing power in the short term
- The company's overseas revenue gross profit margin in 2025 is 51.7%, which is higher than domestically, indicating that it has a certain premium ability in the global market

**Disadvantages**:
- Competition on the CRO side (especially early-stage research) is fierce, with domestic companies such as Pharmaron and Tigermed following closely behind.
- Major customers (the top five are Johnson & Johnson, Merck, GlaxoSmithKline, Roche, and Pfizer) have strong bargaining power
- The existence of the US BIOSECURE Act essentially weakens the pricing confidence of Chinese CXO companies facing US customers.

### 4. Is management doing the "right thing"? ★★☆☆☆

This is the largest deduction item in WuXi AppTec’s business model analysis.

Duan Yongping repeatedly emphasized that "corporate culture determines the destiny of the company" and good management should "do the right things and do things right." WuXi AppTec's management has performed well in "getting things right" - CRDMO has a clear strategy, strong execution, and performance growth that exceeds expectations. But there is clear controversy over "doing the right thing":

- **Continuous large-scale reduction of holdings**: The actual controllers Li Ge, Zhang Zhaohui, and Liu Xiaozhong have reduced their holdings and cashed out nearly 20 billion yuan since 2022. The most recent time was from November 2025 to January 2026, when the holdings were reduced by 5.4 billion yuan, citing "self-funding needs." For a company with a market capitalization of RMB 300 billion, the continuous reduction of holdings by the actual controller sends a negative signal to the market.
- **Salary Controversy**: Li Ge's salary in 2024 is 41.8 million yuan, making him one of the highest-paid chairman of A-share companies. Although it is not extreme when compared to European and American MNCs, combined with the continued reduction of holdings, the market perception is not good.
- **Question on "self-circulation cash-out"**: Some market views believe that the company will increase profits by selling subsidiaries (such as WuXi United's equity), and then use outstanding performance to drive up the stock price, creating a window for the actual controller to reduce its holdings.

## 3. Long-term sustainability of business model

### Core advantage: "Shovel selling" business

WuXi AppTec is often compared to "the man selling shovels in the gold rush" - no matter which pharmaceutical company ultimately succeeds, as long as the research and development of innovative drugs continues, WuXi AppTec can benefit from it. The benefits of this "shovel selling" model are:

1. **Does not bear the risk of success/failure of drug research and development**: The pharmaceutical company's pipeline may fail, but WuXi AppTec charges a service fee
2. **Decentralized customer portfolio**: Provide services to more than 6,000 customers around the world in 2025, with the top five customers accounting for approximately 30% of revenue, and dependence on a single customer is controllable
3. **Cross-cycle capabilities**: Even if the industry is in a cold winter (such as the trough of Biotech financing in 2023-2024), the leading CXO can seize share with its cost advantage.

### Core risk: Geopolitical "structural discount"

The biggest challenge facing WuXi AppTec is not its competitors, but the "original sin of identity" caused by the game between China and the United States. The BIOSECURE Act was signed into law in December 2025. Although the final version does not directly name WuXi AppTec, OMB needs to publish a list of "Biotech Companies of Concern" by December 2026. Once listed:

- Contracts related to U.S. federal government funding will be gradually restricted after 2027
- May trigger preventive transfer of some conservative customers
- Although the "grandfather clause" protects existing contracts until 2032, it will be significantly more difficult to acquire new business.

The company is already coping: the Delaware factory is expected to be put into production in 2026, and the Singapore base is already operational. However, there is still uncertainty as to whether these measures can fully offset the impact of the bill.

## 5. Core judgment from Duan Yongping’s perspective

If we use Duan Yongping’s framework to summarize:

**Is this a good business? ** Yes. The CRDMO integrated platform's position in the global innovative drug industry chain is similar to TSMC's position in the semiconductor industry chain - an indispensable infrastructure. The demand is real, the stickiness is high, and there is scale effect.

**Is this a good company? ** Discounted. Execution at the business level is first-rate, but management's reduction in holdings is troubling. Duan Yongping once said, "Don't touch companies that are dishonest." WuXi AppTec's management is not dishonest, but its "words and deeds are consistent" score is not high - while saying they are optimistic about the company's future, they are reducing their holdings to cash out.

**Is it a good price now? ** The PE of Hong Kong stocks is 25 times, which corresponds to approximately 13.2 billion yuan in non-net profits in 2025, implying full expectations for 18-22% growth in 2026. Given the uncertainty surrounding the BIOSECURE Act and negative signals from management reducing their holdings, the current price lacks a margin of safety.

**Final conclusion**: WuXi AppTec is a good business that deserves continued attention, but it is not a target that should be "betted heavily" right now. Waiting for policy clarification or valuations to fall is a more prudent choice.
