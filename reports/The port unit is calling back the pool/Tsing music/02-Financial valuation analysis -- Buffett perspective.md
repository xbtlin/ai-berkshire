# Tencent Music (1698.HK) Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: Does this company make real money? Are the current prices reasonable?

---

## 1. Income structure and growth quality

### Annual revenue composition in 2025

| Business Segment | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate | Proportion |
|----------|-------------|---------|------|
| Online Music Subscriptions | 176.6 | +16.0% | 53.7% |
| Online Music Non-Subscription | 90.7 | +39.2% | 27.6% |
| Social entertainment and others | 61.7 | Decline | 18.8% |
| **Total Revenue** | **329.0** | **+15.8%** | **100%** |

### Revenue quality assessment

**Positive factors:**

1. **Subscription revenue accounts for more than half**: Subscription revenue of 17.66 billion yuan is the most stable source of cash flow, highly predictable, and consistent with the characteristics of "good business"
2. **Rapid growth of non-subscription business**: 39.2% growth rate comes from multiple channels such as digital albums, advertising, copyright licensing, etc., indicating that monetization capabilities are broadening
3. **Good revenue growth sustainability**: The compound growth rate of revenue from 2023 to 2025 is about 12-15%, not a flash in the pan

**Concerns:**

1. **Social entertainment business continues to shrink**: Revenue from products such as National Karaoke has declined. Social entertainment revenue in 2026Q1 has dropped another 11% year-on-year. This business may enter a structural decline.
2. **2026Q1 growth slowed down significantly**: Total revenue increased only 7.3% year-on-year (vs 15.8% for the whole year of 2025), music membership income growth dropped to 6.6%, and the market is worried about the weakening of growth momentum

### 2026Q1 data

| Indicators | Amount (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year |
|------|-------------|------|
| Total Revenue | 79.0 | +7.3% |
| Music related services | 65.1 | +12.2% |
| Music membership revenue | 45.7 | +6.6% |
| Non-member music revenue | 19.4 | +28.0% |
| Net profit | 20.91 | -51.3% |

The 51.3% year-on-year drop in Q1 net profit looks scary, but it needs to be dismantled: 2025Q1's associated companies containing 2.37 billion yuan are regarded as disposal income (one-time investment income). After excluding this item, the actual change in operating profit is not drastic. **The quality of operations cannot be denied just because of the disappearance of one-time gains. **

## 2. Profitability analysis

### Profitability Trends

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Net Profit Margin (GAAP) | ~25% | ~23% | 33.6% |
| Adjusted net profit margin | ~22% | ~28% | 30.2% |

The net profit margin will increase significantly to 33.6% in 2025, mainly because:
- Copyright costs as a proportion of revenue decline (scale effect)
- Social entertainment business losses narrowed
- Improved operational efficiency

**Streaming media platforms with a net profit margin of more than 30% are rare in the world. ** Spotify’s profit margins are still in the low single digits so far. This reflects the relatively low copyright costs in the Chinese market and the strong bargaining power of platforms.

### ROE and capital efficiency

| Indicators | Data |
|------|------|
| ROE（TTM） | 14.93% |
| Net Profit Margin (TTM) | 34.51% |
| Earnings per share (TTM) | About HK$3.86 |

ROE is about 15%, which is not outstanding for a cash-rich asset-light company. The reason is that the company holds a large amount of cash on its books (net cash of 24.3 billion yuan), which reduces capital efficiency. If management can be more active in buybacks or dividends, ROE will increase significantly.

## 3. Cash flow and balance sheet

### Cash Position

| Project | Amount |
|------|------|
| Net cash (end 2025) | 24.3 billion yuan (approximately US$3.5 billion) |
| Annual dividend | US$368 million (approximately 2.5 billion yuan) |
| 2-year repurchase plan | US$1 billion (slow progress, only US$65 million was repurchased in Q1) |

**This is a very healthy balance sheet. **24.3 billion in net cash means:
- The company has no debt repayment pressure
- Have sufficient funds to carry out strategic mergers and acquisitions (such as the US$2.9 billion acquisition of Himalaya)
- Ability to continue dividends and repurchases

### Shareholder Return Assessment

The dividend rate in 2025 is approximately 23% ($368 million/full-year net profit), and the dividend rate is approximately 2.4%. The buyback plan is US$1 billion over two years, but the execution pace is slow.

**Frank evaluation**: For a company with net cash accounting for more than 20% of its market capitalization, the current shareholder returns are conservative. If management truly believes the company is undervalued, it should pursue buybacks more aggressively. The slow execution of buybacks reflects that management is either reserving funds for acquisitions (proven - acquisition of Himalaya) or is not confident enough in its own valuation.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

| Valuation Metrics | Data |
|----------|------|
| Stock price | About HK$39 (May 2026) |
| Market capitalization | Approximately HK$122 billion |
| PE (TTM) | 10-12 times |
| 52-week high | HK$104 |
| 52-week low | HK$38.44 |

### Comparison with Spotify

| Metrics | Tencent Music | Spotify |
|------|----------|---------|
| Revenue (2025) | 32.9 billion yuan (approximately US$4.5 billion) | €17.1 billion (approximately US$18.5 billion) |
| Net profit margin | ~34% | Low single digits |
| Paying users | 127 million | 290 million |
| ARPU | 11.9 yuan/month (about 1.7 US dollars) | 4.76 euros/month |
| PE | ~10-12x | ~40-50x |

**Tencent Music’s profit margins far exceed those of Spotify, but its valuation is only a quarter of that. ** This valuation difference reflects:

1. **Growth Expectation Difference**: Spotify is still in the global expansion period, and Tencent Music MAU is declining
2. **Geopolitical Discount**: Chinese concept stocks are generally affected by this
3. **Competitive Risk Premium**: The threat from soda music is something Spotify does not face globally
4. **ARPU ceiling concerns**: How high can the ARPU of 11.9 yuan rise? Market doubts

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Conservative scenario** (stagnant growth):
- Assume that net profit will remain at 10 billion yuan in the next five years and will not grow.
- Gives 8-10 times PE
- Reasonable valuation: 80-100 billion yuan = approximately HK$90-110 billion
- Current market capitalization is HK$122 billion, slightly overvalued

**Neutral Scenario** (Moderate Growth):
- Assuming a compound growth rate of net profit of 8-10% in the next five years
- Net profit after 5 years is about 15 billion yuan, giving 12 times PE
- Reasonable valuation: 180 billion yuan = approximately HK$200 billion
- The current market value has about 60% upside potential

**Optimistic scenario** (Ximalaya synergy + price increase successful):
- Assuming revenue growth remains at 12-15%, profits will grow faster
- Net profit after 5 years is about 20 billion yuan, with a PE of 15 times
- Reasonable valuation: NT$300 billion = approximately HK$330 billion
- The current market value has about 170% upside potential

**Key variable**: The core difference in valuation is-can ARPPU continue to improve? When will the decline in MAU stabilize? Could Soda Concerts Become a Real Disruptor?

## 6. Key financial conclusions

1. **Real money is made**: 11 billion net profit, 30%+ profit margin, 24.3 billion net cash. This is real profitability, not paper wealth.
2. **Cheap for a reason**: 10 times PE seems cheap, but MAU has declined for 16 consecutive quarters, ARPPU growth has stagnated, and soda music has risen - these are real signs of fundamental deterioration. We cannot simply say "the market is wrong."
3. **The acquisition of Himalaya is the key variable**: If the integration of the 20 billion yuan acquisition is successful, it can open up a new space for the audio ecosystem; if it fails, it will be a waste of shareholders’ funds.
4. **The margin of safety exists but is not sufficient**: The stock price fell from HK$104 to HK$39 (a drop of more than 60%). The valuation has indeed been compressed a lot, but this does not mean that it has reached the level of "buying with eyes closed"
