## 2. Core findings

### The most outstanding advantages

1. **The ability to make real money**: Net profit in 2025 is 11.06 billion yuan, net profit margin is 34%, the best level in global streaming media. With net cash of 24.3 billion yuan, the balance sheet is extremely healthy.

2. **The valuation is indeed cheap**: The stock price fell from a 52-week high of HK$104 to HK$39 (a drop of more than 60%), and the PE was compressed from about 30 times to 10-12 times. If earnings can be stabilized, the valuation is attractive.

3. **Huge paying user base**: 127 million paying users are an asset that is difficult to replace, and the tiered strategy of 20 million super members has verified the feasibility of high-end development.

### The most worrying issue

1. **Monthly activity dropped for 16 consecutive quarters**: from about 650 million to 530 million, and management chose to stop disclosing this data. Almost all of the incremental users were intercepted by soda music.

2. **ARPPU growth stagnated**: RMB 11.9/month remained unchanged month-on-month. The core "price increase story" in the market is losing momentum. If the number of paid users also starts to decline, the double whammy will be very serious.

3. **The threat of soda music is structural**: free + Douyin traffic + algorithm recommendation. The combination of the three forms a dimensionality reduction attack. This cannot be solved by improving Tencent Music's products - competition at the business model level requires a response at the business model level.

4. **The acquisition of Himalaya consumes 20 billion yuan in cash**: If this money is used to buy back, it can buy back about 16% of the outstanding shares based on the current stock price. Management's choice of mergers and acquisitions rather than repurchases shows that they believe business expansion is more important than valuation repair, but it will take time to verify whether this judgment is correct.

## 3. Key variables and investment papers

### Do multiple papers (the following conditions need to be met)

1. ARPPU resumes growth, increasing from 11.9 yuan to 15 yuan+ (mid-term target)
2. The number of paying users has stabilized at more than 120 million and will not decline.
3. Himalayan integration brings revenue and profit increases
4. Soda music slows down expansion due to profit pressure

**If the above conditions are true**, the net profit will reach 15-20 billion yuan in 5 years, 12-15 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 200-300 billion Hong Kong dollars, and there is 60-150% upside space from the current position.

### Short selling paper (the following conditions need to be met)

1. Soda music continues to grow rapidly, and Tencent Music is forced to cut prices or launch a free version
2. AI music weakens copyright barriers and reduces willingness to pay
3. The integration of Himalaya fell short of expectations, and an investment of 20 billion yuan was impaired.
4. The social entertainment business is shrinking at an accelerated pace

**If the above conditions are true**, net profit may drop to 6-7.5 billion yuan, 8 times PE, corresponding to a market value of HK$50-60 billion, and there is still 45-55% downside from the current position.

## 4. Thoughts on comparison with Spotify’s valuation

| Metrics | Tencent Music | Spotify |

| PE | ~10x | ~40-50x |
| Net profit margin | ~34% | ~3-5% |
| Paid user growth rate | +5% | +12% |

Tencent Music's profit margin is 10 times that of Spotify, but its valuation is only one-quarter. This gap is not entirely "irrational" - Spotify has room for global growth, and Tencent Music faces declining MAU and free competition. But is the valuation gap of 4 times too large? If Tencent Music can prove the sustainability of profitability, there is indeed room for valuation repair.

## 5. Action suggestions

### Current judgment: Observe positions, do not rush to reposition

**Reason:**

1. It is a fact that the valuation is cheap, but there is a reason why it is cheap. Until the downward trend in MAU stabilizes and ARPPU growth recovers, it is impossible to confirm whether this is a value trap or an investment opportunity.

2. The Himalaya acquisition has just been approved, and it will take at least 2-3 quarters to make a preliminary judgment on the integration effect. Until then, this is a major uncertainty.

3. The trajectory of soda music remains to be seen. If its monthly active users exceed 200 million in 2026, it will mark a substantial change in the industry structure.

### Conditions for opening a position (meet any one)

-The number of paying users has stabilized or rebounded for two consecutive quarters
- ARPPU resumes sequential growth
- Quantifiable synergies emerge from Himalayan integration
- The growth rate of soda music has slowed down significantly (monthly activity growth rate dropped below 30%)

### Situations that need to be avoided

-The number of paying users begins to decline (not just MAU)
- Management continues to reduce disclosures
- Net profit fell by more than 20% year-on-year for two consecutive quarters (excluding one-time factors)

## 6. One sentence summary

Tencent Music is a Chinese online music leader that makes real money and has a cheap valuation, but it is standing at a critical crossroads - the free impact of soda music, the supply revolution of AI music, and the growth bottleneck of ARPPU. With the three challenges superimposed, "cheapness" itself does not constitute a reason to buy. You need to see actual evidence of stabilization of the competitive landscape or the launch of new growth engines before it is worth betting heavily.
