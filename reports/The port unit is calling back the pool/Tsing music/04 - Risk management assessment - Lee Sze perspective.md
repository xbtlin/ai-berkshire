# Tencent Music (1698.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: management capabilities, corporate governance, risk identification, safety margin
> Core question: Are these people trustworthy? How much will you lose in the worst case scenario?

## 1. Management evaluation

### Core Management Team

| Position | Name | Background |
|------|------|------|
| Executive Chairman | Peng Jiaxin | Responsible for long-term strategy and overall coordination |
| CEO | Liang Zhu | Taking office in April 2021, fully responsible for QQ Music, Kugou Music and Long Audio business |
| Other directors | Hu Min, etc. | — |

### Liang Zhu’s Governing Report Card

Liang Zhu took over as CEO in April 2021, which has been five years. Objectively evaluate his performance:

**Do the right thing:**

1. **Successful response to copyright antitrust**: After the exclusive copyright was broken in 2021, the company did not fall into chaos. Instead, it achieved continued growth in revenue and profits after losing exclusive barriers. This shows that management has the ability to adjust strategies in times of adversity.

2. **Promote payment rate increase**: The number of paying users increased from approximately 80 million to 127 million, and the payment rate increased from approximately 12% to approximately 20%. Super members have grown from zero to 20 million, which is an important source of incremental revenue.

3. **Profit margin continues to improve**: Adjusted net profit will increase from approximately 5 billion yuan in 2022 to 9.92 billion yuan in 2025, and the net profit margin will increase from approximately 20% to more than 30%.

4. **Strategic acquisition of Himalaya**: Although the results have yet to be verified, the expansion logic from "music" to "audio" is reasonable.

**Places that are not good enough:**

1. **Failed to effectively respond to the impact of soda music**: MAU has declined for 16 consecutive quarters, and management has yet to come up with an effective countermeasure. The discontinuation of disclosure of MAU data is even more unsettling to the market - ** Investors should remain vigilant when management chooses to hide unfavorable data. **

2. **Insufficient repurchase execution**: A two-year US$1 billion repurchase plan was announced, but only US$65 million was repurchased in Q1, and progress was slow. If management really believes the stock is undervalued, why not accelerate buybacks? This behavior of saying one thing and doing another damages market trust.

3. **Timing and price of acquiring Himalaya**: When its own stock price plummets by 60%, is it the optimal capital allocation to spend 20 billion yuan to acquire an audio platform that is "at a trough"? If this 20 billion is used to buy back its own shares, the effect may be better.

4. **Slow response to social entertainment business**: Social entertainment businesses such as National Karaoke have declined for several consecutive quarters, and management does not seem to have a clear stop loss or transformation plan.

### Management Rating ★★★☆☆

**Summary**: Liang Zhu is a qualified but not outstanding CEO. He stabilized the situation during the copyright crisis and promoted the payment process, but when faced with the structural competitive threat of soda music, he lacked a strong strategic response. The decision to stop disclosing MAU data is a particular demerit - good management faces problems head-on, not hides them.

## 2. Corporate governance analysis

### Equity Structure

The actual controller of Tencent Music is Tencent Holdings. This is both an advantage and a limitation:

**Advantages:**
- Tencent's brand and ecological resources (WeChat, QQ, Tencent Video) provide the synergy of traffic and joint membership for the music business
- Tencent’s technical capabilities (AI, cloud computing) can empower the music business
- The major shareholders are stable and there will be no "barbarian" governance risks

**Restrictions:**
- Management’s independent decision-making space may be limited by Tencent Group’s overall strategy
- Tencent Music may not be the highest priority in resource allocation
- Related transactions (such as copyright cooperation, traffic distribution) may involve conflicts of interest

### Information Transparency ★★☆☆☆

In March 2026, it announced that it would no longer disclose key operating data such as monthly activity on a quarterly basis. This is a major governance penalty item. For comparison:

- Spotify discloses MAU, paying users, ARPU, churn rate and other data in detail every quarter
- NetEase Cloud Music (unlisted) also lacks data transparency
- Soda Music (unlisted) has no disclosure obligations

**When a public company proactively reduces disclosures, it usually means those numbers are deteriorating. ** Investors should assume the worst-case scenario, which is that the MAU downward trend is accelerating.

### Second level: known but uncertain risks

| Risk | Potential Impact | Description |
|------|---------|------|
| AI music impacts copyright value | High | 7 million AI songs are produced daily, which may weaken copyright barriers in the long run |
| Ximalaya integration fails | Medium to high | 20 billion yuan acquisition, five regulatory restrictions weaken synergy space |

### The third level: the unknown unknown

- Will ByteDance significantly increase its investment in soda music? If Byte decides to seize the market "regardless of cost", Tencent Music will be almost unstoppable
- Will AI technology give rise to a new form of music consumption? For example, music created by personalized AI will be unique to everyone - this will completely subvert the existing platform model.
- Will hardware manufacturers such as Apple and Huawei enter the music market by taking advantage of pre-installation?

## 4. Worst case scenario analysis

### Stress Test: If everything goes wrong

Suppose the following situations occur simultaneously:
1. Soda music continues to grow, and Tencent Music MAU drops below 400 million
2. ARPPU does not rise but falls (forced to cut prices to cope with free competition)
3. Himalayan integration falls short of expectations
4. The impact of AI music has led to a decrease in copyright costs but also a decrease in willingness to pay.

**Worst case financial impact:**
- Revenue may shrink from 32.9 billion yuan to 28-30 billion yuan
- Net profit margin may be compressed from 34% to 20-25%
- Net profit may drop to 6-7.5 billion yuan
- Reasonable PE is given 8 times
- Corresponding market value is about 50-60 billion yuan = about 55-66 billion Hong Kong dollars
- The corresponding stock price is about HK$18-21

**Based on the current HKD 39, there is still about 45-55% downside in the worst-case scenario. ** This is not small.

### Safety Margin Assessment

| Source of safety margin | Reliability | Description |
|-------------|---------|------|
| Net cash 24.3 billion yuan | ★★★★★ | The highest certainty, but the acquisition of Himalaya will consume most of it |
| Current profitability | ★★★★☆ | High short-term certainty, medium-term competitive pressure |
| Dividend income | ★★★☆☆ | The 2.4% dividend rate provides some protection, but the dividend rate may fluctuate |
| Valuation discount | ★★★☆☆ | 10 times PE seems cheap, but if profits decline, PE will "passively" increase |

**Overall Margin of Safety Rating: ★★★☆☆**

The margin of safety is there but not thick enough. Net cash of 24.3 billion is the most reliable "floor", but after the acquisition of Himalaya, this buffer will be significantly reduced. If management can accelerate buybacks and increase the dividend rate, the margin of safety will be enhanced.

## 5. Key Personnel and Consistency of Interests

### Management shareholding

As a subsidiary of Tencent Holdings, Tencent Music’s management’s shareholding ratio is relatively limited. The compensation structure of managers such as Liang Zhu includes options and restricted stocks, but the specific amount is not transparent.

**Interest alignment score: ★★★☆☆**

As a major shareholder, Tencent ensures that management will not take actions that seriously harm the interests of shareholders (such as hostile takeovers and excessive dilution). However, the consistency of interests between management and small shareholders is not strong enough—specifically, poor implementation of buybacks and retrograde information disclosure.

## 7. Risk management suggestions

If you decide to invest, you need to set clear monitoring indicators and exit conditions:

### Core monitoring indicators
1. **Number of paying users**: If it declines for two consecutive quarters, it means that competitive pressure has been transmitted to core users
2. **ARPPU trend**: If it falls below 11 yuan/month, it means that the logic of price increase has been broken
3. **Soda Music Monthly Activity**: If it exceeds 200 million, it means that the "three pillars" pattern has been formed
4. **Ximalaya integration progress**: focus on user retention and revenue contribution after acquisition

### Exit signal
- Paid users declined for two consecutive quarters
- Management continues to reduce disclosures without reasonable explanation
- Soda Music launched a paid version and was successful (which shows that it can not only grab free users, but also pay users)
- Net profit fell by more than 20% year-on-year for two consecutive quarters (excluding one-time factors)
