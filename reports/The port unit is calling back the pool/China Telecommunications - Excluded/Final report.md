## One sentence conclusion

China Telecom is the one with the most "technological transformation potential" among the three major operators. Tianyi Cloud and AIDC business give it a growth narrative, but the 16 times PE has reflected the transformation expectations to a certain extent. The reward for buying now depends on whether the cloud business can re-accelerate growth.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. Tianyi Cloud + AIDC account for 60%+ of revenue, no longer a pure pipeline operator
2. The demand for computing power explodes in the AI era, and operators are natural infrastructure providers.
3. 75% high dividend payout rate, 5% dividend rate provides bottom-line returns
4. The peak of capital expenditure has passed (down 27%), free cash flow will improve
5. Satellite communication license layout opens up new growth space

### Bearish logic
1. Tianyi Cloud’s revenue growth rate plummeted from 50%+ to single digits, and the sustainability of growth is questionable.
2. ROE is only 7%, and PE 16 times is too expensive for such profitability.
3. Alibaba Cloud’s price-cutting competition will compress Tianyi Cloud’s profit margins
4. AIDC investment may face the risk of overcapacity
5. In the same industry, China Mobile has higher ROE (12%), lower PE (13 times), and better cost performance.

## Buying conditions

1. **PE drops to 12-13 times** (corresponding to a dividend rate of 6%+), providing more margin of safety
2. **Tianyi Cloud’s quarterly growth rate rebounded to 15%+**, verifying the recovery of growth
3. **AIDC business shows positive profits**, proving that the business model is feasible

## Position suggestions

- **Current Price**: Can be configured with small positions (within 3%), as an observation position for high dividends + technological transformation
