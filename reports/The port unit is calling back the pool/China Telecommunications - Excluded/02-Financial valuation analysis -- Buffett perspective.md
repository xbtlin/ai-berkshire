# China Telecom (0728.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 523.9 billion yuan | 520 billion yuan | +0.7% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 33.2 billion yuan | 33 billion yuan | +0.5% |
| ROE | ~7% | ~6.8% | Slow improvement |
| Net profit margin | ~6.3% | ~6.3% | Basically stable |

**Profitability Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

Although ROE of 7% is better than two barrels of oil, it is still lower than Buffett's preferred level of 15%+. The net profit margin of 6.3% reflects the operator's asset-heavy characteristics - large amounts of depreciation and amortization eroding profits.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- Operating cash flow is usually 2-3 times net profit (due to large depreciation)
- Free cash flow = operating cash flow - Capital expenditures, positive in 2025 (capex reduction)
- The ratio of capital expenditure to revenue dropped from 25%+ to below 20%, improving capital discipline

### Balance Sheet
- The asset-liability ratio is about 50%, with a healthy structure
- The proportion of interest-bearing liabilities is not high and interest expenses are controllable
- Depreciation of large fixed assets (network equipment) is the largest non-cash expense

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): ~16x
- PB: about 1.1 times
- Dividend yield: about 4.5-5%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 4x

### The core of the valuation disagreement
The market's valuation of China Telecom mainly revolves around one question: **Is it a "public utility" or a "technology company"? **

- If valued by public utilities: PE 10-12 times is reasonable, and the current 16 times is too expensive
- If valued according to "telecom + cloud": dividing the traditional business (PE 10 times) and cloud business (PS 2-3 times) into segments, there is still room.

### Segment Valuation Attempt
| Business | Valuation Methodology | Valuation |
|------|----------|------|
| Traditional communications (profit ~ 20 billion) | 10 times PE | 200 billion yuan |
| Tianyi Cloud (RMB 120.7 billion) | 2x PS | 240 billion yuan |
| Industrial digitalization (RMB 147.3 billion) | 1.5 times PS | 220 billion yuan |
| **Total** | | **~660 billion yuan** |

The current total market capitalization is approximately 530 billion yuan, and segment valuations show an upside potential of approximately 25%. But this depends on assumptions about the valuation multiples for the cloud business.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**Basically OK. ** Communication networks will not disappear; the demand for cloud computing and AI computing power is growing. However, there are uncertainties in the growth rate and competitive landscape.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Yes. ** License monopoly + network coverage + user base constitute a lasting barrier. But competitors (China Mobile, China Unicom) also have the same barriers.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
** Neutral to positive. ** The transformation direction is in the right direction, capital expenditure discipline has improved, and the high dividend payout ratio is shareholder-friendly.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**Limited. ** PE of 16 times is not cheap for a company with ROE of 7%, but the dividend payout ratio of 75% (dividend rate of 4.5-5%) provides certain downside protection.

## 4. Capital allocation efficiency

Capital expenditures will decrease significantly in 2025 (down 27% year-on-year), which is a positive sign:
1. The peak of 5G construction has passed and large-scale network investment is no longer needed.
2. More funds can be used for dividends and new business investment
3. There is huge room for improvement in free cash flow

A payout ratio of 75% means management returns the majority of profits to shareholders. The remaining 25% (approximately 8.3 billion yuan) is mainly invested in Tianyi Cloud and AIDC. If the return rate on these investments can reach 10%+, it will gradually increase the overall ROE.

## 5. Comparison of peer valuations

| Company | PE | ROE | Dividend rate | Cloud business proportion |
|------|------|------|--------|-----------|
| China Telecom (0728) | 16x | 7% | ~5% | ~25% |
| China Mobile (0941) | 13x | 12% | ~6% | ~15% |
| China Unicom (0762) | 15x | 5.7% | ~4% | ~20% |

China Mobile has the highest ROE, lowest valuation, highest dividend among the three major operators, and has the best overall cost performance. China Telecom's advantage lies in its deeper cloud business layout.

## 6. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

China Telecom is in a state of "reasonable valuation but not cheap". Its value depends on whether the transformation story delivers:
- If the growth of Tianyi Cloud and AIDC continues, ROE is expected to gradually increase from 7% to 10%, and the valuation will be re-rated
- If growth slows down (cloud revenue growth is already showing signs of slowing down in 2025), the current PE of 16 times lacks a safety margin

Buffett may prefer China Mobile (higher ROE, lower PE). China Telecom's investment logic is more about "transformation expectations" rather than "current value."
