# China Telecom (0728.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: China Telecom Co., Ltd.
- **Stock Code**: 0728.HK
- **Industry**: Fixed/Mobile Communications
- **PE(TTM)**: ~16x
- **ROE**：~7%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Operating income | 523.9 billion yuan | +0.7% |
| Service revenue | 485.4 billion yuan | +0.7% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 33.2 billion yuan | +0.5% |
| Tianyi Cloud revenue | 120.7 billion yuan | Rapid growth |
| Full-year dividend | RMB 0.272 per share | Dividend payout ratio 75% |

## Investment Highlights
1. Tianyi Cloud/AI data center has strong growth (AIDC revenue is 34.5 billion yuan)
2. High dividend payout ratio of 75% and considerable dividend yield
3. Transformation from traditional operators to "technology-based enterprises"
4. Low valuation + stable cash flow

## Core Risks
1. The growth rate of traditional communication services has peaked
2. Cloud business faces fierce competition from Alibaba Cloud and Huawei Cloud
3. Large-scale investment in 5G but limited ARPU improvement
4. ROE is only 7%, return on capital is low
