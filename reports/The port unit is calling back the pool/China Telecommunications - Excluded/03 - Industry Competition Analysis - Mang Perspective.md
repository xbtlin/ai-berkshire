# China Telecom (0728.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. Industry Ecology

### China’s communications industry landscape
China's communications market is a typical "three pillars + policy control" pattern:

| Operators | Mobile Users | Broadband Users | Core Advantages |
|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| China Mobile | 990 million | 300 million | Largest scale, strong rural coverage |
| China Telecom | 410 million | 190 million | Good fixed network foundation, strong government and enterprise customers |
| China Unicom | 340 million | 110 million | Flexible mixed-ownership reform, many cooperation with BAT |

The industry has entered a mature stage, the growth in the number of users is close to the ceiling, and the focus of competition has shifted from "grabbing users" to "increasing value."

### Industry Attraction
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Advantages:
- Monopolistic industry with only 3 license holders
- Stable cash flow and rigid downstream demand
- The demand for computing power in the AI era brings new growth points to the industry

Disadvantages:
- The policy of "increasing speed and reducing fees" suppresses pricing power
- Return on 5G investment falls short of expectations
- Three companies compete with each other and fall into a "Prisoner's Dilemma"

## 2. China Telecom’s competitive positioning

### Traditional communication field
China Telecom ranks second in traditional communications:
- Mobile users are about 410 million, lower than China Mobile’s 990 million, but higher than China Unicom’s 340 million
- There are about 190 million fixed-line broadband households, with traditional advantages in the southern region
- Leading the market share of government and enterprises (thanks to the government relations of the former post and telecommunications system)

### Cloud computing field-key battlefield
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

Tianyi Cloud’s competitive environment:

| Cloud Vendors | Market Share (IaaS) | Core Customers | Advantages |
|--------|-----------------|----------|------|
| Alibaba Cloud | ~33% | Internet enterprise | Leading technology, rich ecosystem |
| Huawei Cloud | ~15% | Large government enterprises | Technology + channels |
| Tianyi Cloud | ~12% | Government/Public | Security Compliance, Local Services |
| Tencent Cloud | ~10% | Game/Social | Scenario-driven |

Tianyi Cloud’s differentiated positioning:
1. **Government Cloud Market**: Governments and state-owned enterprises are more inclined to use clouds with state-owned assets background, and security compliance is the core demand.
2. **Localization capabilities**: Advantages of service outlets in third- and fourth-tier cities
3. **Network and Cloud Synergy**: Can package and provide a "network + cloud" integration solution
4. **AIDC (AI Data Center)**: Revenue of 34.5 billion yuan in 2025, seizing the demand for GPU computing power rental

### Disadvantages of Tianyi Cloud:
1. Technical depth and open source ecosystem are inferior to Alibaba Cloud
2. Public cloud PaaS capabilities are weaker than those of Internet cloud vendors
3. Innovation speed and product iteration are constrained by the central enterprise system
4. Tianyi Cloud’s revenue growth in 2025 has slowed down to single digits from 50%+ in the previous two years.

## 3. Key issues of Munger format

### "What is the essential contradiction when operators build a cloud?"
The genes of operators are "emphasis on assets, emphasis on operations, and emphasis on innovation", while cloud computing requires "rapid iteration, technology drive, and ecological construction." This is a conflict of organizational genes. Whether Tianyi Cloud can maintain Internet-style innovation speed within the central enterprise system is the biggest uncertainty.

### "Is the investment in 5G worth it?"
Frankly, the return on investment in 5G is far lower than expected:
- C-side: There is little difference in user perception, and ARPU has not been significantly improved by 5G.
- B-side: 5G to B applications (factories, mines, etc.) are still in the pilot stage, and it is still early to realize scale.
- Conclusion: 5G is more of a defensive investment that "must be invested but has limited returns"

### "Is AIDC a real opportunity or a new bubble?"
The explosion in AI computing power demand is real (large model training/inference requires a large number of GPUs), but you need to be vigilant:
1. GPU servers depreciate quickly (3-5 years)
2. Nvidia GPUs are in short supply and purchase costs are high
3. If the AI craze cools down, a large amount of computing power may be idle
4. There are many competitors (all operators + Internet cloud + third-party IDC are investing)

## 4. Global Benchmarking

| Indicators | China Telecom | AT&T | Verizon | NTT |
|------|----------|------|---------|-----|
| ROE | 7% | 15% | 20% | 10% |
| PE | 16x | 10x | 9x | 12x |
| Dividend yield | 5% | 5% | 6% | 3% |
| Cloud business proportion | 25% | 5% | 5% | 20% |

China Telecom's PE is significantly higher than that of its overseas peers, mainly because of: 1) A/H share premium; 2) The valuation premium given by the market to cloud business transformation. However, overseas operators have higher ROE and lower valuations. From a pure value perspective, their overseas counterparts are "cheaper".

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

China Telecom's competitive position in the traditional communications field is solid but there are no surprises. The real variables are cloud computing and AIDC. If it can continue to expand its share in the government cloud and AI computing power markets, it will evolve from a "low ROE operator" to a "medium ROE digital infrastructure provider." However, the signal of Tianyi Cloud's revenue growth slowing down is worthy of caution - if the growth story is falsified, the valuation will face compression.

Munger's summary: It is not a "long and wet snow road", but a "dry and wet snow road" - the traditional business is dry (low growth), and the cloud business is wet (high growth), but whether the length and humidity of the cloud business's snow road can be sustained remains to be seen.
