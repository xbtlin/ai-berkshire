## Research background

MTR Corporation is one of the few subway operators in the world that can achieve profitability in its railway business. Its original "Railway + Property" (R+P) model has been used as a benchmark case for sustainable operation of global public transportation by consulting organizations such as McKinsey. The company is controlled by the Hong Kong SAR government (74.4%) and operates in a commercial manner, combining the stability of a public utility with the profitability of a commercial enterprise.

**Research Triggers:**
- The R+P model is unique in the world and the business model is worthy of in-depth understanding.
- Static PE is about 10.7 times, PB is about 0.91 times, dividend rate is about 4.85%, and valuation is at a historically low range
- The investment in six major new railway projects is approximately HK$140 billion, and the next ten years will be a major capital expenditure cycle.
- Management change (Yang Meizhen will take over as CEO in January 2026), strategic direction deserves attention
- Overseas business is shrinking (UK, Sweden), and the business landscape is being reshaped
