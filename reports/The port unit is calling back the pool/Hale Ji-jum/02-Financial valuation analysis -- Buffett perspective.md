## 1. Financial quality physical examination

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Indicators | Full year 2025 | Year-on-year changes | 2026Q1 | Quarter-on-quarter trends |
|------|-----------|---------|--------|---------|
| Revenue | 302.347 billion yuan | +5.71% | 73.687 billion yuan | Q1 under pressure |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 19.553 billion yuan | +4.39% | 4.652 billion yuan | +24% month-on-month |
| Excluding non-net profit | 18.604 billion yuan | +4.5% | — | — |
| Gross profit margin | 26.7% | -1.1pct | — | — |
| Selling expense ratio | 11.2% | -0.6pct | — | — |

**Key Observations**:

1. **Revenue exceeds 300 billion but the growth rate slows down**: The growth rate in 2025 is 5.71%, which is far lower than the 10.2% in the first half of 2025 and the 9.98% in the first three quarters. This shows that there was a significant slowdown in growth in Q4, which is related to the weak recovery of the domestic white goods market and the extreme weather in North America.

2. **The decline in gross profit margin deserves vigilance**: The gross profit margin of 26.7% decreased by 1.1 percentage points year-on-year. Reasons include: copper price exceeding 95,000 yuan/ton, price increase of plastic particles such as ABS, and intensifying competition in the North American market. The good news is that the company has partially hedged the gross profit pressure through cost-side optimization (sales expense ratio decreased by 0.6 percentage points).

3. **2026Q1 profit improved month-on-month**: Despite year-on-year pressure, net profit attributable to the parent company of 4.652 billion yuan increased by 24% month-on-month, indicating that Q4 was a periodic low and operating resilience remains.

### 1.2 Balance sheet quality

| Indicators | End 2025 |
|------|---------|
| Total assets | 295.795 billion yuan |
| Net assets | 118.698 billion yuan |
| Asset-liability ratio | About 57.4% |

The asset-liability ratio of 57.4% is relatively high in the home appliance industry (approximately 66% for Midea and 72% for Gree). Haier's liability structure has a relatively high proportion of operating liabilities (accounts payable, advance receipts, etc.), which is a reflection of the bargaining power of suppliers in the home appliance industry, rather than a financial risk signal.

### 1.3 Cash flow - the most important financial indicator

| Indicators | 2025 |
|------|--------|
| Net cash flow from operating activities | 26.003 billion yuan |
| Net profit/operating cash flow ratio | 1.33 times |

**Operating cash flow is 1.33 times net profit, which is a very healthy sign. ** Description:
- The company's profits are of high quality and the money earned is real money
- No serious accounts receivable backlog problem
- Sufficient funds for dividends, buybacks and reinvestments

For comparison, if a company's net profit is very high but its operating cash flow is lower than its net profit, it often means that a large amount of accounts receivable or inventory is occupied in the profit, and the "quality" of the profit is questionable. Haier does not have this problem.

**Method 2: Discounted Free Cash Flow (DCF)**

Assumptions:
- Free cash flow baseline: 26 billion yuan (operating cash flow in 2025) - Capital expenditure of approximately 8 billion yuan = approximately 18 billion yuan
- Growth rate: 5% in the next 5 years, 3% thereafter
- WACC：9%

DCF estimates that the intrinsic value is about 260-300 billion yuan, corresponding to about 28-32 Hong Kong dollars per share (H shares).

**Method Three: Profitability Value (EPV)**

Assuming that the normalized net profit is 20 billion yuan, giving a PE of 12 times (the average level of the global home appliance industry):
- EPV = 200 x 12 = 240 billion yuan
- Corresponding to approximately HK$26 per H share share

## 4. Key financial risk points

### 4.1 Risk of continued downward trend in gross profit margin
Copper prices, plastic particles and other raw material costs are rising, coupled with intensified competition in the North American market, gross profit margins may continue to be under pressure. If the gross profit margin drops from 26.7% to below 25%, net profit growth will face substantial challenges.

### 4.2 Impact of exchange rate fluctuations
Overseas revenue accounts for more than 51%, and RMB appreciation will directly reduce the RMB-denominated amount of overseas profits. Every 1% appreciation of the RMB will affect profits by approximately 200-300 million yuan.

### 4.3 Goodwill and intangible assets
The goodwill and intangible assets formed by the acquisition of GE Appliances, Candy and other brands are relatively large. If overseas business continues to perform worse than expected, there is an impairment risk.

### 4.4 Growth of accounts receivable
Accounts receivable will increase in the first three quarters of 2025, and attention needs to be paid to the quality of collections and the aging structure.
