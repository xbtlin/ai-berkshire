# One, the nature of competition in the electricity industry

#1.1 Economic characteristics of the industry

China ' s white power industry is a mature industry that has ** undergone a high growth period and entered a competitive stock phase.

- ** Demand end**: Domestic urbanization dividends are weaker, real estate cycles are low, the domestic white power market declined by about 6.2 per cent in 2025 compared to the previous year, and growth is dependent on renewed demand and seaboarding.
- ** Supply end**: The industry is highly concentrated, with Hale, the American, and the Grecos occupying about 70% of the domestic white power market. The pattern of competition has been relatively stable, and new entrants are hard to shake.
- ** Profit characteristics**: Manufacturing attributes (raw materials + processing + channels), Māori 25-35 per cent, net interest rate 5-10 per cent, medium capital return.

** Key judgements**: This is a "good track but not the best track". Good for industry, stable competitive patterns, and cash flow; bad for growth ceilings, clear visibility, limited pricing rights, sensitivity to raw materials and real estate cycles.
