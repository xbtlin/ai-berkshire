## 2. Investment thesis

### Reasons to buy

1. **The scarcity of global home appliance leaders**. Haier is the only Chinese home appliance company that has its own brands and localized operating systems in major markets around the world. The seven major brands cover China, the United States, Europe, Australia, Japan, Southeast Asia, and South Asia, and the distributed layout of 163 manufacturing centers is unique among Chinese consumer goods companies. This strategic asset was accumulated over tens of billions of dollars and more than ten years, and is extremely difficult for competitors to copy.

2. **Valuation provides a margin of safety**. The PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 9 times, which is lower than Midea (13 times), international peers Whirlpool (12 times) and Electrolux (15 times). According to DDM and DCF estimates, the intrinsic value is approximately 24-32 Hong Kong dollars, and the current approximately 21 Hong Kong dollars provides a 15-30% safety margin.

3. **Shareholder returns continue to increase**. The dividend ratio will be increased from 48% in 2024 to 55% in 2025, and it is planned to be no less than 60% in 2028. Since 2021, A+H has repurchased approximately 8.5 billion yuan in total, and the latest launch is 3-6 billion yuan of A share repurchase + 30% repurchase and cancellation of D shares. The total annual shareholder return exceeds 10 billion yuan.

4. **Excellent cash flow quality**. The operating cash flow in 2025 is 26 billion yuan, which is 1.33 times the net profit. The profit content is high.

5. **Dominant position in the ice-wash category**. Domestic refrigerators and washing machines have an offline share of 47.7% and 47.4%, nearly half of the country. Casarte has a far leading share in the high-end market (78.7% of the market for washing machines priced at RMB 10,000+).

### Reasons not to buy

1. **North American tariff risk is a substantial threat**. If the United States revokes China's "permanent normal trade relations" status, even if GE Appliances is mainly manufactured locally, the China-dependent component of the supply chain will still be affected. North America is the largest overseas profit pool, and its profit fluctuations will directly affect overall performance.

2. **Profitability is not optimal**. The ROE of 17% is the lowest among the three white goods giants (Midea 23%, Gree 25%), indicating that there is room for improvement in the global asset-heavy model and the organizational efficiency of "Rentan Heyi".

3. **Insufficient domestic growth momentum**. Domestic revenue will only increase by 3.07% in 2025, and the white goods market will decline by 6.2% year-on-year. Population decline and a sluggish real estate cycle are long-term structural challenges.

4. **Gross profit margin downward trend**. The gross profit margin in 2025 will be 26.7%, a year-on-year decrease of 1.1 percentage points, with rising raw material prices and intensifying competition continuing to put pressure on the company.

5. **Ownership structure complexity**. Haier Group’s multi-layered and nested ownership structure increases the uncertainty of governance transparency.

## 3. Comprehensive judgment

**Rating: Worth in-depth tracking, you can gradually build a position on dips ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

Haier Smart Home is an investment opportunity of "good company + reasonably low price", but it is not a dream combination of "great company + extremely low price".

Its core attraction is: using 9 times PE to buy one of the most globalized Chinese home appliance leaders, while enjoying a 5-6% dividend rate and continuously increasing shareholder returns. In the current context of heightened uncertainty in global trade, Haier's globalization model of "localized manufacturing + independent brands" has demonstrated unique resilience - it does not rely on the fragile business model of "Made in China for export", but is truly rooted in markets around the world.

Its main risks are: the uncertainty of North American tariff policies (which has a short-term impact on profits), the weakness of the domestic white goods market (which limits overall growth), and low ROE (which limits the compound interest effect).

### Operation suggestions

- **position building strategy**: gradually build positions in the Hong Kong stock price range of 20-22 Hong Kong dollars, and increase efforts below 18 Hong Kong dollars.
- **Position recommendation**: The portfolio accounts for 3-5% (medium position), not suitable for heavy positions (ROE is not high enough and growth is not fast enough)
- **Holding Conditions**: Pay attention to quarterly North American business profit trends, domestic share changes, and dividend buyback execution status
- **Sell signal**: North American market share continues to decline, dividend promises are not fulfilled, and management has major integrity issues

| Metrics | Tracking frequency | Warning threshold |
