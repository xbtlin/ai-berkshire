# Haier Smart Home (6690.HK/600690.SH) Investment Research Framework

> Research date: May 16, 2026 | Currency unit: RMB (unless otherwise specified)

## Overview of research objects

Haier Smart Home is the most globalized Chinese home appliance company. In 2025, its revenue exceeded 300 billion yuan (302.347 billion yuan) for the first time. Its overseas revenue was 154.545 billion yuan, accounting for 51.1%, surpassing the domestic market for the first time. The company owns seven global brands: Haier, Casarte, Leader, GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel, Candy, and AQUA, and operates in 163 manufacturing centers and 126 marketing centers around the world. The current PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 9.1 times, ROE is 16.97%, and the dividend yield is about 5-6.5% (depending on H/A shares and calculation caliber).

## Core conclusion

**Comprehensive rating: Worth in-depth tracking, can gradually build a position ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

Haier Smart Home is one of the few Chinese manufacturing companies that has truly realized global independent brand operations. Its advantages include: a global seven-brand matrix covering diverse markets, a localized manufacturing system that effectively hedges geographical risks, a domestic share of nearly 50% in the ice and washing category that constitutes a strong barrier, solid cash flow (operating cash flow is 1.33 times net profit), and continued improvement in shareholder returns (the dividend ratio has been increased from 48% to 55%, and it is planned to be no less than 60% in 2028).

Major concerns include: the impact of uncertainty in North American tariff policies on GE Appliances' profits, the slowdown in growth momentum amid a weak recovery in the domestic white goods market, disputes over the organizational efficiency of the "Ren Dan He Yi" model, and the issue of discounted liquidity in Hong Kong stocks.

Buying a global home appliance leader with 9 times PE, the safety margin is relatively sufficient, but it is necessary to pay close attention to the evolution of North American tariffs and the recovery pace of domestic demand.
