#Haier Smart Home: Business Model Analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Judgment Criteria for Good Business - User Value, Business Logic, Sustainability
> Research date: May 16, 2026 | Currency unit: RMB (unless otherwise specified)

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 Basic business logic

The core business of Haier Smart Home is: **design, manufacture, and sell household appliances, and provide smart life solutions around home scenarios**.

Specific disassembly:
- **domestic market** (revenue of 146.036 billion yuan in 2025, accounting for 48.3%): Haier (popular), Casarte (high-end), and Leader (young) cover different consumer levels with three brands. The leading position in the ice and washing category is stable (offline refrigerator share 47.7%, washing machine 47.4%), and the air-conditioning category continues to catch up.
- **Overseas markets** (2025 revenue of 154.545 billion yuan, accounting for 51.1%): localized operations through GE Appliances (North America), Fisher & Paykel (Australia and New Zealand), Candy (Europe), AQUA (Japan/Southeast Asia) and other brands, ranking first in North American market share.
- **Smart home and scene solutions**: Provide whole-house smart solutions through the Triwing Bird brand. The Casarte "Connoisseur" series is equipped with AI technology, trying to transform from selling single products to selling scenes.

### 1.2 Do users need your product?

**Home appliances are just-in-demand consumer goods**, which is the biggest background of this business. No matter how the economic cycle fluctuates, people still need refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners. This gives the industry natural demand resilience.

But the question is: Do ** users definitely need "Haier" products? **

From the data, the answer tends to be yes:
- In the field of ice washing, Haier’s domestic share is close to 50%, indicating that consumers have strong awareness and strong preference for the Haier brand.
- In the high-end market, Casarte has a market share of 53% in refrigerators priced at RMB 15,000 and above, and 78.7% in washing machines priced at RMB 10,000 and above. Its brand premium capability has been verified.
- In overseas markets, GE Appliances’ share remains first while the price index in North America continues to increase, indicating that brand power is not driven by low prices.

### 1.3 Benchmark thinking: Checking the characteristics of a good business

## 2. What are the economic characteristics of this business?

### 2.1 Profitability Analysis

Full year data for 2025:
- Revenue: 302.347 billion yuan (+5.71% year-on-year)
- Net profit attributable to parent companies: 19.553 billion yuan (+4.39% year-on-year)
- Excluding non-net profit: 18.604 billion yuan (+4.5% year-on-year)
- Gross profit margin: 26.7% (-1.1 percentage points year-on-year)
- Sales expense ratio: 11.2% (optimized by 0.6 percentage points year-on-year)

**Core Observation**: The gross profit margin of 26.7% is a medium level for the home appliance industry (Gree is about 30%+, Midea is about 26%), which shows that Haier's pricing power is not the top in the industry. However, through the continuous optimization of the expense side (driven by digital transformation), the company has achieved an improvement in management efficiency with "gross profit margin declining and net profit margin remaining flat or slightly rising".

### 2.2 Cash flow quality

The net cash flow from operating activities in 2025 is 26.003 billion yuan, which is **1.33 times** the net profit attributable to the parent company. This is a very healthy indicator that:
1. Profits are "real money" rather than paper figures
2. Have certain bargaining power with upstream and downstream parties (accounts receivable management is acceptable)
3. Sufficient free cash flow to support dividends and buybacks

### 2.3 Return on Capital

ROE is about 17%, which is at a reasonable level in the home appliance industry. In comparison, Midea's ROE is about 23%+, and Gree's ROE is about 25%+. The core reason for Haier's low ROE is the heavy assets brought about by global expansion - 163 manufacturing centers and 126 marketing centers around the world. These heavy assets have lowered the net asset turnover efficiency.

**Here is a key judgment**: Haier's low ROE is not due to a poor business model, but the cost of its globalization strategy. If the global layout is successful, these "sunk" manufacturing and channel assets will bring continuous revenue and profit contributions in the future, and there is room for ROE improvement. If globalization is impeded (e.g., by tariff barriers), these assets become a drag.

## 3. Is globalization a good strategy or a burden?

### 3.1 The uniqueness of the globalization model

Haier's globalization path is very rare among Chinese companies. It is not OEM/ODM overseas, nor is it a simple brand export, but "independent brand + localized operation"**:

- Acquisition of local brands (GE Appliances US$5.58 billion, Candy, Fisher & Paykel)
- Retain local brand and management team
- Set up local factories, local R&D, and local sales
- Form a "Trinity" localization system

**Benefits of this model**:
1. Avoid tariffs and geopolitical risks (what is sold in the United States is the "Made in America" GE brand, not imported from China)
2. Close to local consumer needs and high product adaptability
3. High brand recognition and strong pricing power

**Cost of this model**:
1. Huge initial investment and long return cycle
2. Cross-cultural management is highly complex
3. Heavy assets lower return on capital

### 3.2 Verification of globalization results

In 2025, overseas revenue will be 154.545 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 8.15%, surpassing domestic revenue for the first time**. Specific areas:
- **North America**: GE Appliances continues to rank first in share, and the price index continues to increase
- **Europe**: The overall share of white goods ranks first among Chinese companies, and profitability continues to increase.
- **South Asia**: Pakistan ranks first for 10 consecutive years, and India ranks among the top three in market share
- **Southeast Asia**: Core countries such as Vietnam and Thailand lead sales

These data show that Haier's globalization has entered the "harvest period" from the "investment period", but it is still far from a "comprehensive harvest" - overseas profit margins are still lower than domestic ones.

### 3.3 The biggest current test: US tariffs

North America is Haier's largest overseas profit pool. Although GE Appliances is mainly manufactured in the United States, some parts and product lines still rely on imports. If the United States further increases tariffs on China or cancels its "Permanent Normal Trade Relations" status, the comprehensive tariffs may soar to about 61%, which will have an impact on supply chain costs.

The 2026Q1 data has already reflected this pressure: the overall industry demand in the North American market fell by about 10% year-on-year (partly due to the snowstorm). The company's revenue in the first quarter was 73.687 billion yuan, and the net profit attributable to the parent company was 4.652 billion yuan, which was under obvious short-term pressure.

## 4. Objective evaluation of the "Rentanheyi" model

### 4.1 The essence of the pattern

The core of "Rentanheyi" proposed by Zhang Ruimin in 2005 is to break up enterprises into numerous "micro-enterprises" (small entrepreneurial teams). Each micro-enterprise directly faces users and is responsible for its own profits and losses. This is a decentralized, platform-based organizational management experiment.

### 4.2 Positive reviews
- Inspire employees' entrepreneurial spirit and user-oriented awareness
- Incubated successful brands such as Casarte
- Certified by ISO as an international management standard (ISO 56012) and recognized by academic circles
- 14,885 maker partners have interests bound to the company

### 4.3 Controversies and Questions
- Decision-making efficiency issues: excessive decentralization may lead to high coordination costs and waste of resources
- Difficult to copy: Extremely high quality requirements for management and reliance on corporate culture inheritance
- Some investors believe that this is "management concepts outweighing actual results"
- The low ROE may be partly due to insufficient organizational efficiency.

### 4.4 Objective judgment

The "Rentanheyi" model has certain value in maintaining the innovative vitality of large enterprises, but it should not be overly deified. **The final test is financial results**: If this model is truly superior, it should be reflected in continued share gains and profit margin improvements. Judging from the data, Haier's share in the ice and washing category is indeed stable at a high level, but its profitability (ROE, net profit margin) still lags behind Midea, indicating that the efficiency advantage of the model has not yet been fully realized.

|------|------|------|
| Business quality | ★★★★☆ | There is strong demand for home appliances + brand barriers, but the industry growth rate is slowing down |
| Globalization Strategy | ★★★★☆ | One of the most successful globalization cases of Chinese companies, but the price is not low |

| Management model | ★★★☆☆ | "Rendanheyi" is innovative but its efficiency needs to be verified |
| User value creation | ★★★★☆ | High-end (Casarte) and smart sceneization have highlights |
| **Comprehensive** | **★★★★☆** | **Good business, but not the best kind** |

**Core Judgment**: Haier Smart Home is a "good business", but not a "great business". It has rigid demand attributes, brand barriers, and global layout, but it lacks network effects and extremely high pricing power. In the fiercely competitive field of the home appliance industry, Haier is one of the few Chinese companies that can continue to create user value on a global scale, which is valuable in itself.
