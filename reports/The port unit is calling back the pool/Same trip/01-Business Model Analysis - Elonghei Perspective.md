## 1. The nature of business: Is this a good business?

### 1.1 Business model overview

Tongcheng Travel is essentially a **travel service matching platform**, connecting C-side users and B-side travel service providers (airlines, railways, hotels, scenic spots, etc.), and making profits through commissions and advertising revenue.

Core revenue structure (2025):
- **Transportation business**: 7.93 billion yuan (accounting for about 41%), including air tickets, train tickets, and bus tickets
- **Accommodation business**: 5.45 billion yuan (accounting for about 28%), hotel booking
- **Other businesses**: approximately 6 billion yuan (accounting for approximately 31%), including scenic spot tickets, vacation products, advertising, etc.

### 1.2 User value proposition

The core user value of Tongcheng Travel lies in: **Providing convenient and low-threshold travel booking services to mass travelers in China's third, fourth and fifth-tier cities**.

What makes this value proposition unique is:
- WeChat mini program can be accessed with just one touch, no need to download independent APP
- The interface is simple and intuitive, suitable for the usage habits of users in the sinking market
- One of the best choices for price-sensitive users

### 1.3 Duan Yongping’s question: Why do users choose you?

**Positive answer:**
1. **Convenience of WeChat entrance**: Users can complete ticket booking in WeChat wallet, with zero learning cost
2. **First-mover advantage in lower-tier markets**: While Ctrip is focusing on business travelers, Tongcheng has been deeply involved in third-, fourth- and fifth-tier cities.
3. **Train tickets as a high-frequency entrance**: Train tickets are booked frequently and have low decision-making threshold, making them a natural customer acquisition tool.

**Consideration thinking:**
1. How high is the brand loyalty of users to Tongcheng? If the WeChat entrance is no longer exclusive, will users stay?
2. The ARPU of users in lower-tier markets is naturally limited. Can the value per customer be continuously increased?
3. The commission for the train ticket business is extremely thin, and it is more of a customer acquisition tool than a source of profit.

## 2. Moat analysis: How long can the competitive advantage last?

### 2.1 WeChat traffic moat ★★★★☆

**Moat description:**
Since 2014/2016, Tongcheng Travel has exclusive access to the two main entrances of train tickets/air tickets and hotels in WeChat wallet. About 80% of the average monthly active users come from WeChat mini programs. 69% of new paying users on the WeChat platform come from non-first-tier cities.

**Moat Strength Assessment:**
- Advantages: WeChat has over 1.3 billion monthly active users, making it the largest traffic pool in China; the entrance location is exclusive
- Risk: Tencent only holds about 20% of its shares and is slowly reducing its holdings; the exclusivity of the WeChat entrance essentially depends on Tencent's business decisions, rather than irreplaceable technical barriers.
- Sustainability: There is a high probability of maintenance in the medium term (3-5 years), but there is uncertainty in the long term

### 2.2 Sinking market network effect ★★★☆☆

**Moat description:**
There are 253 million annual paying users, 87% of which are from non-first-tier cities. Established supplier network and user habits in sinking markets.

**Moat Strength Assessment:**
- Advantages: The user base in the sinking market is large, and the entry cost of competitors is high
-Risk: Meituan and Douyin are entering strongly; switching costs for users in sinking markets are low
- Sustainability: It is being eroded, but there is still a first-mover advantage in the short term

### 2.3 Cross-selling ability ★★★☆☆

**Moat description:**
Acquire customers through high-frequency and low-price products such as train tickets, and cross-sell high-margin products such as hotels and tickets. In 2025, the revenue growth rate of the accommodation business (16.8%) is higher than that of the transportation business (9.6%), indicating that cross-selling is working.

**Moat Strength Assessment:**
- Advantages: Unique "Low Band High Frequency" mode
- Risk: The success of cross-selling depends on user stickiness to the platform
- Sustainability: the model is effective but not unique and can be copied by competitors

## 3. Who is the "enemy" of the business model?

### 3.1 Direct competitive threats

| Competitors | Threat Dimensions | Threat Level |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Ctrip | Penetrate downward into mid- and low-tier cities | ★★★☆☆ |
| Meituan | Local life scenes extend to travel | ★★★★☆ |
| Douyin | Content planting + live broadcast delivery model | ★★★★☆ |
| 12306/Airline direct sales | Disintermediation trend | ★★★☆☆ |

### 3.2 The biggest structural risk

**The impact of Douyin cannot be ignored. ** The full-year payment GMV of Douyin Life Services will exceed 850 billion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 59%, and the target growth rate in 2026 is 50%. Douyin has demonstrated strong customer acquisition capabilities in the cultural tourism scenic spots and resort hotel markets through short videos, live broadcasts and other content forms.

The threat to Tongcheng is that Douyin's content e-commerce model has changed the user's decision-making path - from "where should I go" to "this place looks good". As a search-based OTA, Tongcheng has a natural disadvantage in the "planting" process.

### 3.3 Disintermediation Risks of 12306

The railway department continues to optimize the 12306 experience, and the proportion of direct sales of train tickets continues to increase. Although Tongcheng’s train ticket business is an important customer acquisition tool, its commission space is being compressed.

## 4. Long-term perspective: What will this company be like in ten years?

### 4.1 Optimistic scenario

- Successfully upgraded from "sinking OTA" to "national travel platform"
- International business contributes more than 20% of revenue
- AI agents reshape travel decisions, and Cheng Xin’s big model becomes the industry standard

### 4.2 Neutral Scenario

- Maintain the advantage of sinking market, but growth tends to be stable
- Internationalization has achieved small results but has not yet become a reality.
- Profit grew moderately in line with the industry's broader market

### 4.3 Pessimistic scenario

- The advantages of WeChat entrance are weakened or canceled
- Douyin + Meituan are squeezing the sinking market
- Growth has completely stalled and valuations have further compressed

## 5. General comments from Duan Yongping’s perspective

### Is this a business that I can understand? ★★★★☆

The OTA platform model is clear, revenue sources are transparent, and the financial structure is not complicated. The core logic is "traffic entrance + travel service matching", and the business model is easy to understand.

### Do users really need you, or can they leave at any time? ★★★☆☆

**Key question:** Do users choose Tongcheng because "Tongcheng is good" or because "WeChat is convenient"? If the answer is towards the latter, then the sustainability of the moat will be compromised. Judging from the number of user complaints (60,000+ complaints from Black Cat), user experience is not Tongcheng’s strength, which means that user stickiness comes more from convenience than brand loyalty.

### Is management doing the right thing? ★★★☆☆

The management is promoting "upward growth" (expanding the high-end market) and internationalization. The direction is correct, but the execution will take time to verify. The AI ​​layout (Cheng Xin’s large model + DeepSeek) reflects the foresight of technology. But at the same time, the proportion of marketing expenses is relatively high, indicating that acquiring customers still relies on "paying money to buy traffic."

### Comprehensive business model rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)

Tongcheng Travel is a "okay" business - it has a unique traffic entrance advantage, a clear market positioning for sinking, and its profitability is improving. But this is not a "great" business: user loyalty is questionable, the moat is being eroded, and the core competitiveness (WeChat entrance) is in the hands of others.

Duan Yongping might say: This business "can be understood", but he is not sure whether he can "see far". The WeChat entrance is given by others, not earned by oneself - this makes people feel uneasy.
