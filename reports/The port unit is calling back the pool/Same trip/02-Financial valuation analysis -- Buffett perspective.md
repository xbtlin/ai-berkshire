## 1. Profitability: What is the quality of the company’s money?

### 1.1 Revenue growth trend

| Period | Revenue | Year-on-year growth rate |
|------|------|----------|
| 2023 | About 15.5 billion | High-speed recovery |
| 2024 | 17.34 billion | +11.6% |
| 2025 | 19.4 billion | +11.9% |
| 2026E (consensus expectations of brokers) | About 21.5-22 billion | +10-13% |

**Core Observation:** Revenue growth will fall from the high base recovery period in 2023 (100%+) to 11.9% in 2025, entering steady state growth. From a quarterly perspective, Q1 in 2025 will grow by 13.2%, Q2 by approximately 10%, and Q3 by approximately 10.4%, with the growth rate basically stabilizing in the 10-13% range.

### 1.2 Evolution of profit margins

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Adjusted net profit | About 2.2 billion | 2.785 billion | 3.403 billion |
| Adjusted net profit margin | Approximately 18.5% | 16.1% | 17.5% |
| Adjusted EBITDA margin | — | Approximately 21% | Approximately 23.5% |

**Core Observation:** The decline in profit margin in 2024 is mainly due to increased marketing investment. In 2025, the profit margin will rise to 17.5%, and the EBITDA profit margin will reach 23.5%, indicating that operating leverage is being released. The profit growth rate (22.2%) is higher than the revenue growth rate (11.9%), indicating that the company is improving operational efficiency.

### 1.3 ROE analysis

ROE is about 13%, which is at the level of "not bad but not outstanding":
- Compared with Ctrip’s ROE of about 20%+: there is still a gap with Tongcheng
- Comparing Meituan's overall ROE: Tongcheng is more efficient in OTA business
- ROE breakdown: Net interest rate is about 17.5% x asset turnover rate x equity multiplier ≈ 13%

**Buffett's perspective:** ROE 13% is not outstanding. Buffett prefers companies with a sustained ROE of more than 20%. However, considering that Tongcheng is still in the growth stage and is investing in internationalization and expanding hotel management, there is room for improvement in ROE in the future.

## 2. Balance sheet: How is the financial security?

### 2.1 Cash and Debt

| Metrics | Mid-term 2025 | Assessment |
|------|-----------|------|
| Cash and equivalents | 7.27 billion yuan | Sufficient |
| Operating cash flow (half year) | 3.04 billion yuan | Health |
| Investment cash flow (half year) | -3.56 billion yuan | Large investment |

**Key findings:** Tongcheng's cash flow generated from operating activities in the first half of 2025 was 3.04 billion yuan, but there was a net outflow of 3.56 billion yuan from investment activities (mainly used for the acquisition of Wanda Liquor Management, Elong Hotel Technology, etc.), resulting in some cash consumption. However, the overall cash reserve of 7.27 billion is still healthy.

Comparison: Ctrip's cash reserves in 2025Q1 exceeded 56.3 billion yuan - completely different in magnitude, reflecting the size gap of Tongcheng as the "little brother".

### 2.2 Capital Allocation

Management’s capital allocation direction in recent years:
1. **Hotel Management M&A**: Acquired Wanda Liquor Management and Elong Hotel Technology to expand asset-light business
2. **International investment**: outbound tourism and international hotel supply chain construction
3. **AI technology research and development**: Cooperation between Chengxin Model and DeepSeek
4. **Shareholder Return**: Certain dividends and buybacks

**Assessment:** The direction of capital allocation is reasonable, but investing in multiple directions at the same time may lead to resource dispersion. If the integration of the hotel management business goes smoothly, it will be a catalyst for revaluation.

## 4. Valuation analysis: Is the current price expensive?

### 4.1 Current Valuation Level

| Valuation Metrics | Current Value | Historical Median | Valuation |
|----------|--------|-----------|------|
| PE (TTM) | About 12-13 times | About 18-20 times | Low |
| PS (TTM) | About 2.3 times | About 3-4 times | Low |
| EV/EBITDA | About 8-9 times | — | Reasonably low |
| FCF Yield | About 7-8% | — | Attractive |

### 4.2 Comparison with peers

| Company | PE | Growth | PEG |
|------|-----|------|-----|
| Same trip | 12-13x | 10-15% | 0.9-1.2 |
| Ctrip | 18-22x | 15-20% | 1.0-1.2 |
| Meituan (travel business) | — | — | — |

**Observation:** Tongcheng’s PE (12-13 times) has a significant discount compared to Ctrip (18-22 times). Part of the reasons are:
1. The growth rate of the same journey is slow
2. The ARPU ceiling in sinking markets is lower
3. The moat is not as strong as Ctrip
4. Small market capitalization and discounted liquidity

### 4.3 DCF Valuation Framework

**Assumptions:**
- Revenue growth rate from 2026 to 2028: 12%, 10%, 8%
- Long-term steady-state net profit margin: 18-20%
- Sustainable growth rate: 3%
- Discount rate (WACC): 10%

**Valuation range:**
- Pessimistic scenario (growth slows to single digits): corresponds to 10 times PE → current price is reasonable
- Neutral scenario (maintaining 10-12% growth): corresponding to PE 14-16 times → 20-30% room for growth
- Optimistic scenario (internationalization + breakthrough in wine management): corresponding to PE 18-20 times → 50%+ upside potential

### 4.4 Safety Margin Assessment ★★★★☆

**The margin of safety at current valuations is adequate:**
- PE 12-13 times to buy a platform company that is still growing (10%+), with limited downside risk
- FCF yield 7-8% provides bottom support
- Even if the future growth rate drops to single digits, the current PE has partially reflected this expectation

**But be wary of valuation traps:**
- If there are changes to the WeChat entrance, current profitability may not be sustainable
- If industry competition intensifies and profit margins decline, low PE may be "falsely cheap"
- The liquidity discount of Hong Kong small-cap stocks may exist for a long time

## 5. What does Buffett think of this business?

### 5.1 Aspects that meet Buffett’s standards

- **Easy to understand**: OTA platform model is clear ✓
- **There is a certain moat**: WeChat entrance + first-mover advantage in sinking market △
- **Profitability improving**: rising profit margins ✓
- **Inexpensive valuation**: PE 12-13 times, FCF yield 7-8% ✓

## 6. Comprehensive score of financial valuation

### Profitability ★★★☆☆
Profit margins continue to improve, but ROE is low and there is still a gap between "excellent" and "excellent".

### Financial Security ★★★★☆
Cash flow is healthy, balance sheet is solid, and there are no obvious financial risks.

### Valuation attractiveness ★★★★☆
PE 12-13 times and FCF yield 7-8% provide a margin of safety, but we need to be wary of the risk of "value trap".

### Capital Allocation ★★★☆☆
The direction is reasonable but the execution effect needs to be observed. Wine management mergers and acquisitions are the key variables.

### Comprehensive financial rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)

The current valuation of Tongcheng Travel is on the low side and there is a certain margin of safety. If you believe that: (1) WeChat entrance will not change in the short term; (2) profit margins can continue to improve; (3) wine management/internationalization can contribute incrementally, then 12-13 times PE is a reasonable buying price. The risk is that growth could continue to slow, causing current valuations to be "reasonable" rather than "cheap."
