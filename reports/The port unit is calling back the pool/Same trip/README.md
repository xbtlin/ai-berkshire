## Investment background

Tongcheng Travel is the third largest OTA platform in China (approximately 15% market share based on GMV). It has exclusive access to WeChat Wallet for train tickets/air tickets and hotels, and has over 87% of users in non-first-tier cities. The company is transforming from a "lower market OTA" to a "full-scenario travel platform" while promoting internationalization and AI intelligent layout.
