# Tongcheng Travel (0780.HK) Investment Research Final Report

> Comprehensive analysis of the four masters | Research date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. Company portrait

Tongcheng Travel is the third largest OTA platform in China (GMV market share is about 15%). It has exclusive access to WeChat Wallet for train tickets/air tickets and hotels. 87% of its users are from non-first-tier cities. It is the leader in online travel in the sinking market. In 2025, it will achieve revenue of 19.4 billion yuan (+11.9%), adjusted net profit of 3.4 billion yuan (+22.2%), and annual service visits of more than 2 billion.

## 3. Core investment logic

### 3.1 Reasons to be bullish (Bull Case)

1. **Sufficient margin of safety in valuation**: PE 12-13 times, FCF yield 7-8%, which is low for a platform company that is still growing.
2. **WeChat traffic moat is stable in the short term**: Exclusively owns the two major entrances to WeChat wallet, and the customer acquisition cost is far lower than the industry average
3. **Profit margins continue to improve**: The adjusted net profit growth rate (22%) is significantly higher than the revenue growth rate (12%), and operating leverage is being released
4. **The wine management business has potential for revaluation**: If the integration of Wanda Wine Management + Elong Hotel Technology is successful, it will open a second growth curve
5. **There is still room for improvement in travel penetration in lower-tier markets**: The structural growth of travel demand in third-, fourth- and fifth-tier cities has not yet ended

### 3.2 Reasons to be bearish (Bear Case)

1. **The downward shift of the growth center is a definite trend**: from 117% → 12%, the post-epidemic dividend has been fully digested
2. **Core competitiveness is in the hands of others**: The survival of the WeChat portal depends on Tencent’s business decisions
3. **Founder’s shareholding is extremely low (0.76%)**: The interests of management and shareholders are insufficiently bound
4. **Multiple competition and siege**: Ctrip’s downward penetration, Meituan’s scene extension, and Douyin’s content interception
5. **Low ARPU ceiling in sinking markets**: There is limited room for improvement in unit price per customer, and scale growth is also slowing down.
6. **M&A expansion may divert resources**: Dalian Sunya’s acquisition triggered market doubts

## 6. Dialogue with the Master: The intersection of four perspectives

### What would Duan Yongping think?

> "This business is understandable, but not very reassuring. The WeChat entrance is given by others, not earned by oneself. A good business should be something that 'no one else can take away even if they want to', rather than 'it's yours for the time being because others won't take it away'."

**Duan Yongping's core concern:** Do users choose Tongcheng because "Tongcheng is good" or "WeChat is convenient"? If it is the latter, the sustainability of this business will be greatly compromised.

### What would Buffett think?

> "PE is 12-13 times, FCF yields 7-8%, and the price is not expensive. But the ROE is only 13%, which is not excellent. This is an 'ok' company - it can make money and is growing, but it has not proven that it can continue to make 'big money'. I will put it on the watch list and wait for a better price or clearer competitive advantages."

**Buffett's core concern:** The ROE is not high enough, the moat is not deep enough, and it is not a typical "reasonable price for a great company" target.

### What would Munger think?

> "To understand this company, you have to understand the ecological niche of China's OTA industry. Tongcheng occupies a unique position - sinking market + WeChat entrance - but this ecological niche is being eroded. The rise of Douyin is a structural change, not a cyclical fluctuation. The biggest risk is not a certain competitor, but a change in the underlying logic of the industry."

**Munger's core concern:** The structural trend of the industry changing from "search OTA" to "content + recommendation".

### What would Li Lu think?

> "China's travel market is a good track, with high certainty of long-term growth. Tongcheng has its own position in this track. But two problems make me hesitate: first, the founder's shareholding is too low, and second, the core resources are not under control. Farming on other people's land, even if the harvest is good, is not as good as owning your own land."

**Li Lu's core concerns:** Insufficient binding of management interests and "ownership" issues in core competitiveness.

## 7. Investment advice

### Overall rating: Moderately attractive ★★★☆☆

Tongcheng Travel is a "conditionally good target" - worthy of attention at a certain price, but its structural limitations need to be accepted.

### What kind of investors is it suitable for?

- **Suitable**: Investors looking for low-valuation growth targets in Hong Kong stocks, optimistic about the recovery of China’s travel consumption, and able to accept the narrative of a sinking market
- **Not suitable**: Investors who pursue "great companies", value the depth of the moat, and have high requirements for the binding of management interests

### Key questions to answer before buying

1. Do you believe that the WeChat portal will be maintained in the next 3-5 years? If you are not sure, the core logic of this target is not valid.
2. Do you accept 10-12% revenue growth as the norm? If you're expecting high growth, you won't find it here
3. Do you think that 12-13 times PE has reflected the expectation of "slowing growth + intensifying competition"? If you think it has not been fully reflected, you may need a lower price.

### Potential Catalyst

- The integration of the wine management business exceeded expectations, and profit margins jumped
- A shares were successfully listed and the valuation system was switched
- Profitable scale from international business
- AI agents bring qualitative changes to user experience
- Tencent increases its holdings or increases ecological support

### Potential risk events

- WeChat entrance is weakened or shared
- Tencent accelerated the reduction of its holdings to less than 15%
- Industry competition has led to a sharp decline in profit margins
- Failure in mergers and acquisitions integration leads to waste of resources
- Macro consumption continues to be weak
