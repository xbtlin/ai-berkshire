## 1. Industry Panorama: Competitive Landscape of China’s OTA Market

### 1.1 Market size and structure

In 2025, China's online travel market will exceed 1.5 trillion yuan. Market share distribution by GMV:

| Platform | GMV share | Core advantages | Main battlefield |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Ctrip | ~56% | Brand + business travel + internationalization | first- and second-tier cities + outbound travel |
| Tongcheng Travel | ~15% | WeChat entrance + sinking market | Third, fourth and fifth tier cities |
| Meituan | ~13% | Extension of local life scenes | Hotels + surrounding tours |
| Fliggy | ~8% | Alibaba Ecosystem + Young Users | Vacation + Overseas Hotels |
| Douyin | ~3% (rapid growth) | Content planting + live broadcast | Scenic spots + resort hotels |

### 1.2 Industry Characteristics

Munger emphasized the need to understand the "ecological niche" of the industry. China’s OTA industry has several key characteristics:

1. **Winner-take-all tendency**: Ctrip dominates with 56% share, and OTA is a typical industry with economies of scale.
2. **But not one company**: Unlike search or social networking, travel decision-making is diversified, allowing differentiated players to exist
3. **The threat of new entrants is real**: Douyin uses a new model to enter, proving that the industry is not monolithic
4. **From commission war to efficiency war**: The industry has passed the period of extensive competition and entered the stage of refined operations.

## 2. Tongcheng’s competitive positioning: third place in the gap

### 2.1 Dislocation competition strategy

The core survival strategy of Tongcheng Travel is **dislocation competition** - avoiding the head-on confrontation with Ctrip in the high-end market and deeply exploring the sinking markets that Ctrip "disdains to do" or "does not do well".

| Dimension | Ctrip | Tongcheng | Meituan |
|------|------|------|------|
| Target users | Business + high-end | Mass + sinking | Local life |
| Core products | Air tickets + high-star hotels | Train tickets + mid- to low-star hotels | In-hotels |
| Customer acquisition methods | Brand + search | WeChat entrance | Takeaway traffic diversion |
| ARPU | High | Medium Low | Medium |
| Gross profit margin | High (32%+) | Medium (about 70% gross profit margin) | Medium |
| Net profit margin | High (25%+) | Medium (17.5%) | Low (OTA part) |

### 2.2 Comparative advantages of Tongcheng

**Compare Ctrip’s advantages:**
- Stronger ability to reach lower-tier markets (zero threshold for WeChat mini program)
- Higher market share in the train ticket/bus ticket field
- Lower customer acquisition costs (relying on WeChat natural traffic)

**Compare the advantages of Meituan:**
- The travel scene is more vertical and professional
- Transportation business (air tickets + train tickets) is Meituan’s shortcomings
- Better coverage of long-distance travel scenarios

**Advantages compared to Douyin:**
- Stronger ability to satisfy deterministic needs (when the user has a clear travel plan)
- The transportation ticketing supply chain is highly mature
- The after-sales service system is relatively complete

## 3. Competition dynamics: What are the parties doing?

### 3.1 Ctrip’s “sinking”

Ctrip has clearly listed the sinking market as a strategic direction, through:
- Launch low-priced product lines other than "Ctrip Travel"
- Increase local promotion and advertising investment in third- and fourth-tier cities
- Leverage brand potential to attract lower-tier hotel suppliers

**Impact on Tongcheng:** Ctrip's sinking is a "dimension reduction strike", and its brand power and supply chain integration capabilities are better than Tongcheng's. However, Ctrip's sinking is the "second curve" rather than its main business, and its investment intensity and sustainability are questionable.

### 3.2 Meituan’s “travelization”

Meituan extends from local life to travel. Its advantages are:
- Ultra-high frequency takeout/in-store traffic
- Natural "surrounding travel" scene extension
- The hotel business has formed a scale (especially mid- to low-star hotels)

**Impact on Tongcheng:** Meituan directly competes with Tongcheng in the field of mid- and low-star hotels, and Meituan’s traffic is larger and more frequent. However, most of Meituan's travel users are "short-distance travel around the area", and long-distance travel scenarios are still Tongcheng's advantage area.

### 3.3 Douyin’s “subversion”

Douyin’s life service GMV will exceed 850 billion in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 59%:
- Change users’ decision-making paths through expert store visits and scenic spot live broadcasts
- Develop unique advantages in destination marketing and resort hotels
- Target growth rate of 50% in 2026, continuing rapid expansion

**Impact on Tongcheng:** Douyin may have a greater impact on Tongcheng than on Ctrip. The reason is: Tongcheng's users in the lower-tier market happen to be Douyin's core user group - third-, fourth- and fifth-tier cities, which are price-sensitive and whose decisions are easily affected by content. Douyin does not need to "steal" users from Tongcheng, it only needs to intercept traffic at the upstream (information acquisition stage) of users' travel decision-making.

### 3.4 12306 direct sales with airlines

Railway 12306 and various airlines continue to optimize direct sales channels:
- The functions of 12306 are increasingly perfected and the user experience is improved.
- Airlines directly reach users through their own APPs and mini-programs
- The intermediate value of OTA in the transportation field continues to be compressed

## 4. Munger’s reverse thinking: Under what circumstances will Tongcheng fail?

### 4.1 "Reversal" thinking - Suppose Tongcheng fails, what will be the reason?

1. **WeChat entrance has been canceled or weakened**: Tencent has adjusted its strategy and no longer gives Tongcheng exclusive entrance.
2. **Saturation of the lower-tier market**: User penetration in third-, fourth- and fifth-tier cities has peaked, and space for growth has been exhausted.
3. **Douyin reshapes the decision-making link**: Users no longer make decisions through the OTA platform, but complete the entire process from planting to placing orders on the content platform
4. **Price war restarts**: Industry competition intensifies, all parties return to money-burning mode, and profit margins collapse
5. **Technological changes**: AI completely changes the travel booking model, and traditional OTAs lose their existence value

### 4.2 Probabilistic assessment of the above risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Comprehensive |
|------|------|------|------|
| WeChat entrance change | Low (20%) | Extremely high | ★★★☆☆ |
| Saturation of the sinking market | Medium to high (60%) | Medium | ★★★★☆ |
| Douyin interception | Medium (40%) | High | ★★★★☆ |
| Price war restarts | Low (15%) | High | ★★☆☆☆ |
| AI subverts OTA | Low (10%) | Extremely high | ★★☆☆☆ |

### 4.3 "If I couldn't own this company, how would I attack it?"

If I were Meituan/Douyin, I would:
- Create a travel portal within the WeChat ecosystem (Meituan already has a WeChat mini program)
- Use more frequent scenarios (takeaway/short video) to continuously reach users on the same journey
- Give higher commission ratios to hotel suppliers and poach suppliers from the same process
- Use content (short video/live broadcast) to intercept traffic upstream of decision-making

**How much can Tongcheng defend? ** Some of them can be defended (the exclusivity of the WeChat entrance, the depth of the train ticket supply chain), but it is more difficult to defend in the hotel and resort fields.

## 5. Industry evolution: What will happen in the next 3-5 years?

### 5.1 Munger’s “Worldly Wisdom” Application

**Trend 1: AI reshapes travel decision-making**
- In the future, users may complete travel planning and booking through AI assistants
- Tongcheng's "Cheng Xin Big Model" is a response, but competition barriers in the AI era may be reshuffled
- Key question: In the AI era, will the user entrance change?

**Trend 2: Content e-commerce erodes search e-commerce**
- Travel decision-making shifts from "search-based" to "recommended-based"
- Content platforms such as Douyin/Xiaohongshu have become sources of travel inspiration
- Traditional OTA needs to be upgraded from "shelf" to "content + shelf"

**Trend 3: The sinking market switches from incremental to stock**
- Dividends from post-pandemic travel recovery fade
- The growth of users in lower-tier markets has slowed down, and competition has shifted to increasing ARPU for existing users.
- Tongcheng needs to prove that it can win in the stock competition

**Trend 4: Asset-light wine management has become a new track**
- Tongcheng acquires Wanda Liquor Management + Elong Hotel Technology to lay out hotel brand management
- If successful, it will expand from the OTA commission model to the hotel brand management fee model
- This is the direction Ctrip is also taking, and competition will not be easy

### 5.2 Tongcheng’s position in industry evolution

**Pros:**
- There is still room for improvement in travel penetration in lower-tier markets (vs. first-tier cities are already saturated)
- The growth rate of the mass tourism market is still higher than that of the high-end tourism market
- The traffic cost of WeChat ecosystem is much lower than that of acquiring customers in the open market
- If the wine management business is successfully integrated, it will provide a new profit growth pole

**Disadvantages:**
- The natural slowdown in industry growth is a major trend
- Competitors are planning to enter the lower market
- The impact of content platforms on traditional OTAs is structural

## 6. Comprehensive rating of industry competition

### Stability of competitive landscape ★★★☆☆

The overall OTA industry structure is stable (Ctrip is the dominant player), but competition in Tongcheng's market segment (sinking + mid- to low-end) is intensifying. The entry of Douyin and Meituan has brought about structural changes.

### Tongcheng’s competitive position ★★★☆☆

The third place position is relatively stable (it is unlikely to be surpassed in the short term), but there is a huge gap from the first place. Faced with the "sandwich" dilemma of being dominated by Ctrip at the top and being besieged by Meituan/Douyin at the bottom.

### Long-term attractiveness of the industry ★★★★☆

Travel is a long-term development. As per capita income increases and leisure demand escalates, the long-term growth of the industry is highly certain. However, the value proportion of the OTA link may be compressed due to the trend of disintermediation and contentization.

### Tongcheng’s coping ability ★★★☆☆

The management is actively responding (AI, internationalization, wine management), but with limited resources and multi-front operations, the implementation effect remains to be seen.

### Comprehensive industry competition score: ★★★☆☆ (3.0/5)

Munger might say: This is a "third place in a pretty good industry." The industry itself has the certainty of long-term growth, but Tongcheng’s competitive position is not unshakable. The biggest risk is not a single competitor, but a change in the underlying logic of the industry - when users' travel decisions change from "search-based" to "recommended", the moat of traditional OTAs will be redefined.
