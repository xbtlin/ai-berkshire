##2.2 Maximum single risk: Micro-Investment Dependence

** Description of risk:**
About 80% of the monthly users of the same trip come from micro-telephone programs, and the "train ticket" and "hotel" entrances in the microtouch are the core source of traffic for the same journey. The presence of this portal depends on commercial decisions about the tweak.

** Why is this a "survival" risk:**
- If the troupe opens the portal to competition with OTAs, the cost of the same trip will rise sharply.
- If the tweaking of the travel service or the porting of the entrance, the same process may face a user-slashed fall
- Low user ratio of stand-alone APPs of the same process, which indicates insufficient independent access to clients after graduation from micro-letters

** Mitigation:**
- Tsing still holds 20% of the shares and has the incentive to defend the interests of the same process.
- The micro-mail portal has been in operation for 10 years, and the ecology is stable.
- The same process is strengthening independent APP and other channels
- As a major shareholder, it's unlikely that we'll be able to steal the same way.

** Risk assessment:** Short-term (1-2 years) is extremely unlikely and medium-term (3-5 years) requires continuous observation of telecommunications attitudes.
