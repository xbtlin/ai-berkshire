## Research background

Sunny Optical is the leader in China's optical industry and ranks first in global shipments of mobile phone lenses and camera modules. We are currently at a critical juncture in the transformation from mobile phone optics to automotive optics and AR/XR optics. 2026 is regarded by the market as a "year of transition" - the old kinetic energy (mobile phones) is under pressure, and the new kinetic energy (car, AR) has not yet fully expanded.

**Key Catalyst:**
- Automotive optics subsidiary (Sunny Intelligent Optoelectronics) plans to spin off and list on the Hong Kong stock market
- The AI glasses industry chain will enter the stage of tens of millions of shipments in 2026
- Strategic cooperation with Goertek Optics to enter the global AR supply chain such as Meta
- Increased volume of high-end products such as glass-plastic hybrid lenses and periscope lenses

**Key Risks:**
- Global smartphone shipments may drop by 15% year-on-year in 2026 (Monopoly forecast)
- Rising storage prices are squeezing the production of mid- to low-end mobile phones and affecting demand for optical modules
- Risk of "time lag" in switching between old and new kinetic energy
- Uncertainty over the spin-off and listing of the automotive business
