# Sunny Optical Technology (2382.HK): Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Financial Quality and Intrinsic Value Assessment
> Core question: Is it real money earned? Is capital allocation reasonable? Do current prices provide a margin of safety?

---

## 1. Profit quality: Are you making real money?

### Revenue and Profit Trends

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit rate |
|------|-------------|------|----------------|------|--------|
| 2021 | 313.4 | +2.4% | 30.2 | -26.5% | 9.6% |
| 2022 | 280.6 | -10.5% | 16.8 | -44.4% | 6.0% |
| 2023 | 316.9 | +12.9% | 11.0 | -34.5% | 3.5% |
| 2024 | 382.9 | +20.9% | 27.0 | +145.5% | 7.1% |
| 2025 | 432.3 | +12.9% | 46.4 | +71.9% | 10.7% |

A few important observations:

**1. Profit volatility is much greater than revenue volatility. ** From 2022 to 2023, revenue only fell by about 10% and then recovered, but net profit plummeted from 3.02 billion to 1.1 billion, a drop of more than 60%. This shows that Sunny's operating leverage is very high - as a heavy-asset manufacturing company, fixed costs account for a large proportion, and small fluctuations in revenue will cause large changes in profits. This is typical of manufacturing and not a bad thing, but investors need to understand this resilience.

**2. There is a one-time factor in profit recovery in 2025. ** The net profit of 4.64 billion includes approximately 919 million yuan of investment income generated from the transfer of equity interests in subsidiaries. After deducting this part, the recurring net profit is approximately 3.7 billion yuan, corresponding to a net interest rate of approximately 8.6%. This level is an improvement from 2024, but is still far from the 2021 high.

**3. The improvement trend of gross profit margin deserves recognition. ** Gross profit margin in 2025 is 19.7%, an increase of 1.4 percentage points from 18.3% in 2024, reflecting the effectiveness of the high-end product structure - the proportion of high-end lenses and modules has increased, and the average selling price has increased.

### Cash flow status

Manufacturing companies must pay attention to cash flow quality. Sunny's operating cash flow has been generally healthy in recent years, with free cash flow in 2024 ranging from approximately 4.1 to 7.3 billion yuan (different calibers). It should be noted that Sunny has approximately 2-3 billion in capital expenditures every year for capacity expansion and equipment updating.

**Key question: Are high capital expenditures sustainable? **

The update cycle of optical manufacturing equipment is about 5-7 years, and the payback period of production line investment is about 3-5 years. Sunny is currently expanding its automotive optics production capacity, AR optics production line and mobile phone high-end production line at the same time, and the capital expenditure pressure is not small. If the mobile phone market declines as expected in 2026, the payback period for some production capacity investments may be lengthened.

## 3. Analysis of return on capital

### Dismantling of ROE

In 2025, ROE will be approximately 13% (partly including one-time income), and approximately 11% after deducting one-time income. Historically, Sunny's ROE reached more than 20% during the peak period of 2018-2019. The main reasons for the decline in recent years are:

1. **Net profit margin decline**: Competition in the mobile phone industry intensifies, coupled with initial investment in new businesses
2. **Asset turnover rate declines**: Large-scale capital expenditures have led to the expansion of total assets, but revenue growth has not kept pace.
3. **Financial leverage is basically stable**: no excessive leverage

ROE dropped from 20%+ to 13%, indicating that Sunny's capital efficiency is declining. This is a common phenomenon for companies in transition—new businesses require upfront investment, but have yet to generate returns that match the investment. The key question is: after the in-vehicle and AR business reaches scale, can ROE rise back to above 15%?

### Comparison with competitors

| Company | ROE (recent years) | Gross profit margin | Net profit margin |
|------|------------|--------|--------|
| Sunny Optical | ~13% | ~20% | ~11% |
| Largan | ~20% | ~65% | ~45% |
| Qiutai Technology | ~5-8% | ~8-10% | ~2-3% |

Sunny is in the middle: not as good as Largan's ultra-high profitability (Largan has almost a monopoly on high-end lenses), but much better than Qiu Titanium (which mainly does low-value-added module packaging). This positioning reflects the characteristics of Sunny's business model of "full chain but not the most high-end".

## 4. Capital allocation: How does management spend money?

### R&D investment

R&D expenses will exceed 2.9 billion yuan in 2024, accounting for approximately 7.5% of revenue, maintaining high growth for many consecutive years. Main directions of R&D investment:
- Glass-plastic hybrid lens (HLS): revenue is expected to grow by more than 150% in 2026
- Periscope lenses: revenue expected to grow 40% in 2026
- Car HD camera: 17MP ADAS camera, etc.
- AR optical waveguide and light engine

R&D investment is in the right direction, but the question lies in the input-output ratio - when can these new technologies be converted into scale profits?

### Strategic Investment

In December 2025, Sunny exchanged shares for 1.903 billion yuan to acquire Goertek and became the second largest shareholder. The logic of this investment is very clear: Goertek is an AR/VR module supplier for brands such as Meta, Pico, and Xiaomi, and it can quickly enter the global AR supply chain through its investment.

From the perspective of capital allocation, this is a reasonable investment - using equity instead of cash to obtain a ticket to enter a new track.

### Dividends and buybacks

Sunny's dividend ratio has historically been about 20-30%, which is not high. For a business that is in an expansion phase and requires significant capital expenditures, a conservative dividend strategy makes sense. But from the perspective of shareholder returns, if new business investments fail to generate expected returns, low dividends mean that capital is being used inefficiently.

## 5. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

Based on market data in May 2026:

| Valuation indicators | Current levels | Historical range | Judgment |
|----------|---------|----------|------|
| PE (TTM) | ~16 times | 15-40 times | In the historically low range |
| PB | ~2 times | 2-6 times | Close to historical bottom |
| PS | ~1.7x | 1.5-5x | Low |

Judging from historical valuations, 16 times PE is indeed at the low level of Sunny's valuation. But two possibilities need to be distinguished:

**Scenario A: Undervalued, with large room for mean reversion. ** If the vehicle and AR business expands as expected, profits will re-enter a period of rapid growth from 2027 to 2028, and the current 16 times PE purchase will enjoy the "Davis Double Click" (profit growth + valuation improvement).

**Scenario B: Valuations reasonably reflect changes in fundamentals. ** If the mobile phone business continues to be under pressure and the new business volume is lower than expected, Sunny will change from a "high-growth technology stock" to a "cyclical manufacturing stock", and 15-18 times PE may become the new normal.

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Conservative scenario (mobile phone decline + new business slowly increasing volume):**
- Recurring net profit in 2026: 3.5-3.8 billion yuan
- Give 15 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 52.5-57 billion yuan
- Equivalent to approximately HK$48-52 per share

**Neutral scenario (stable mobile phone + steady growth of new business):**
- Recurring net profit in 2026: 4.0-4.3 billion yuan
- Give 18 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 72-77.4 billion yuan
- Equivalent to approximately HK$66-71 per share

**Optimistic scenario (mobile phones exceed expectations + rapid increase in vehicle/AR volume): **
- Recurring net profit in 2026: 4.5-5 billion yuan
- Give 22 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 99-110 billion yuan
- Equivalent to approximately HK$91-101 per share

### Valuation impact of spin-off and listing

The spin-off and listing of the automotive optics subsidiary (Sunny Intelligent Optoelectronics) is an important catalyst. Analysts have valued the automotive business at approximately HK$35.66 per share. If the spin-off is successful, it is expected to release "hidden value", but it is also necessary to pay attention to the risk that the spin-off may lead to an adjustment in the parent company's valuation.

## 6. The particularity of the "transitional year" of 2026

Market concerns for 2026 focus on:

1. **Mobile phone shipments decline**: Morgan Stanley predicts that global mobile phone shipments will decline by 15% year-on-year, and the Android camp will decline even more
2. **Storage price increase transmission**: Memory price increases have led to production cuts in mid- to low-end mobile phones, and pressure on optical module demand
3. **High base effect**: Net profit in 2025 includes one-time income of 919 million, and the year-on-year growth rate in 2026 is under pressure
4. **Management guidance cautious**: Revenue and operating profit growth guidance for 2026 is only "not less than 7%"

Sunny's own guidance - growth of "not less than 7%" - is a fairly conservative number, reflecting management's cautious judgment of the market environment in 2026.

Sunny Optical's current financial situation is in the "cycle bottom recovery" stage. The profit recovery in 2025 is a positive sign, but there will be more uncertainties in 2026. 16 times PE provides a certain margin of safety, but it is not absolutely cheap - the key depends on the judgment of profit growth in 2027 and beyond. If you believe that automotive and AR can become a new growth engine in 2027-2028, the current valuation is attractive; if you believe that the switch between old and new driving forces will last longer, the current valuation is only "reasonable" rather than "cheap".
