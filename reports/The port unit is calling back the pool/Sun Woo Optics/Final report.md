## 1. One sentence summary

** Sunny Optical is the absolute leader in China's optical manufacturing industry, with solid competitive barriers and an excellent management team. However, 2026 is in a "transition year" when new and old driving forces are switching, with short-term performance under pressure and long-term direction clear. The current PE of about 16 times provides a certain margin of safety, but it does not constitute an "obvious bargain" - a judgment needs to be made on the timing of the ramp-up of the automotive and AR businesses. **

## 3. Core investment arguments

### Reasons to go long

**1. The "water seller" logic of the optical industry**
Regardless of mobile phones, cars, AR or robots, as long as the device needs to "see", it needs an optical system. As the world's largest optical lens and module manufacturer, Sunny is an infrastructure supplier in the intelligent era. Downstream application scenarios continue to expand, and the value of stand-alone optics continues to increase.

**2. Deterministic growth of automotive optics**
In 2025, the vehicle business revenue will be 7.33 billion yuan (+21.3%), and the global market share will exceed 30%. Driven by the triple driving force of increasing penetration rate of autonomous driving + increasing number of bicycle cameras + pixel upgrade, vehicle-mounted optical TAM is rapidly expanding. A spin-off and listing will release the independent value of the automotive business.

**3. Option value of AR/XR**
By investing in Goertek Optics, it has entered the global AR supply chain such as Meta, and developed its own optical waveguides and optical engines. The CEO predicts that 50 million units of AI glasses will be shipped within 5 years. Even with conservative estimates, the explosion of AR optics in 2028-2030 will provide Sunny with significant incremental space.

**4. Management quality**
The de-family "elite co-governance" model, the strategic determination to increase investment in R&D counter-cyclically, and the pragmatic capital allocation (swapping shares to acquire Goertek Optics) all reflect the long-term business philosophy. In China's manufacturing industry, such management teams are scarce.

**5. Valuations at historically low levels**
~16 times PE, ~2 times PB, both are in the low range since Sunny was listed. If the new business ramps up in 2027-2028, the current price is expected to enjoy a "Davis double-click".

### Reasons for shorting

**1. 2026 is a definite "headwind year"**
Global mobile phone shipments may drop by 15% (Mono Morgan predicts). Storage price increases are squeezing demand for mid-to-low-end mobile phones. The high base in 2025 (including one-time income of 919 million) will put great pressure on year-on-year growth in 2026. Management's own guidance is only "not less than 7%."

**2. Mobile phone dependence is still too high**
The mobile phone business accounts for 63% of revenue and is the core fundamentals. Even if the "upgrading of regulations" can partially offset the decline in shipments, Sunny's revenue and profits will still be substantially impacted in the context of a sharp shrinkage in the mobile phone market.

**3. The time for increasing the volume of new business is uncertain**
Although the automotive business is growing rapidly, it will only account for 17% of revenue in 2025 and is not large enough to support it independently. The AR/XR business is in its early stages and will have limited contribution in 2026-2027. The "time gap" between old and new momentum may last 2-3 years.

**4. Manufacturing profit margin ceiling**
The gross profit margin is ~20% and the net profit margin is ~11%, which is a huge gap from Largan's 70%/45%. Sunny's business model essentially relies on scale and efficiency to make money, not on irreplaceable technology premiums. Limited protection during industry downturns.

**5. The valuation may be a "repricing" rather than an "underestimation"**
If Sunny changes from a "high-growth technology stock" to a "cyclical manufacturing stock", 15-18 times PE may become the new normal rather than a temporary underestimation.

## 6. Matching with investment standards

### Eligible conditions

- **Clear leadership position in the industry:** No. 1 in the world for mobile phone lenses and modules, and No. 1 in the world for automotive lenses
- **Moat can be identified:** Scale advantage + full chain capability + certification barriers
- **Excellent management:** De-family governance, counter-cyclical investment, clear strategic vision
- **Valuation is not expensive:** PE is about 16 times, which is at a historical low
- **Has long-term growth drivers:** Automotive optics + AR/XR expands long-term growth space

### Ineligible conditions

- **This is not a "lay and make money" type of business:** The manufacturing industry requires continuous investment, and profit margins are greatly affected by industry cycles.
- **Limited pricing power:** Intermediate parts supplier, subject to upstream and downstream attacks
- **High uncertainty about short-term performance:** 2026 may be a year when profit growth slows down or even declines
- **High dependence on a single industry:** Mobile phones still account for 63% of revenue

## 7. Final judgment

### Investment attractiveness grade: B+ (worth tracking and waiting for better buying points)

**Reason:**

Sunny Optical is a company that is "good" but not "excellent" in all aspects - the moat is solid but not insurmountable, the management is excellent but faces the test of transformation, the valuation is low but not to the point of "picking up cigarette butts", the long-term direction is clear but the short-term headwinds are obvious.

**Is 16 times PE a sufficient margin of safety? ** Depends on tolerance for "worst case scenario". Under the pessimistic scenario, each share may fall to HK$41-45, corresponding to a downside of approximately 20-25%. Under a neutral scenario, the intrinsic value is about HK$59-65. If the current stock price is within this range or below, the margin of safety is not large but it is not expensive either.

**A more ideal buying time may be:**
1. In Q2-Q3 of 2026, if the mobile phone market really declines sharply and performance falls short of expectations, valuations will be further reduced.
2. The spin-off listing of the vehicle was delayed due to market reasons, causing pessimism.
3. PE falls back to the 12-14 times range, providing a more adequate margin of safety

**Situations where buying is not recommended:**
1. The mobile phone business has experienced a sharp decline in shipments for many consecutive months, and the increase in regulations cannot be hedged.
2. Management lowers full-year guidance to sub-single-digit growth
3. The growth rate of vehicle business has slowed down significantly (falling below 15%)
4. Negative signals appear at the governance level

## 8. An honest ending

Sunny Optical is a company that makes people "respect but hesitate".

Respect, because it has demonstrated rare qualities in China's manufacturing industry - de-family governance, counter-cyclical R&D investment, and sober strategic positioning. The story of Wang Wenjian starting from a 60,000 yuan loan and building a 100 billion enterprise is admirable. The concept of "money scattered and people gathered together" is especially rare in the face of interests.

Hesitation, because of the fate of the manufacturing industry - you must keep running, keep investing, and keep up with technological iterations in order to maintain your current position. Sunny is not the kind of business that you can "buy and leave alone for ten years." It requires investors to continue to track the cycle of the mobile phone industry, the penetration rate of automotive optics, and the technical roadmap of AR, and then continuously update their judgments.

If you are willing to do such tracking and can take decisive action when the market offers a better price, Sunny Optical is a target worthy of long-term attention. If you're looking for ultimate certainty and simplicity, you may want to keep looking.
