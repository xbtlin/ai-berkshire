# NetEase (9999.HK) Investment Research Overview

> Research date: May 16, 2026 | Hong Kong stock code: 9999.HK | US stock code: NTES

---

## Company Profile

NetEase is China's second largest Internet gaming company, founded by Ding Lei in 1997. The company takes self-developed high-quality games as its core competitiveness, and owns long-life cycle IP matrices such as "Fantasy Westward Journey", "Westward Journey", "Eternal Calamity", "Onmyoji" and "Egg Boy Party". In addition to games, NetEase also operates diversified businesses such as NetEase Cloud Music, Youdao Education, and email. In 2025, the annual revenue will be 112.6 billion yuan and the net profit will be 33.8 billion yuan.

## Core conclusion

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

**Conclusion: Actively pay attention and wait for a better buying price**

NetEase is a good business with a high degree of certainty. The gross profit margin of the game business exceeds 64%, net profit margin is 30%, ROE is 22%, free cash flow is abundant, and net cash of 163.5 billion yuan accounts for a very high proportion of market value. These financial indicators are among the top among global game companies. "Fantasy Westward Journey" has been in operation for more than 20 years and still reaches new online highs, proving the stickiness of the IP and the depth of the community moat.

**Pros:**
- The characteristics of gaming cash cows are clear and free cash flow continues to be abundant.
- PE is only 15 times, and the actual PE after deducting net cash is even lower
- Ding Lei returns to the front line, organizational reform focuses on quality products
- New game reserves ("Sea of Forgotten" and "Infinite") provide incremental options
- Continuous repurchases + dividends, increasing shareholders’ awareness of returns

**Risk Factors:**
- The governance structure in which Ding Lei makes decisions alone has the risk of "rule by man"
- Uncertainty about regulatory policies in the gaming industry
- The progress of going overseas is not as good as expected, and the proportion of overseas revenue is still low.
- Limited profit contribution from non-game businesses (Cloud Music, Youdao)
- The disruptive impact of AI on the gaming industry is still unclear

**Buying strategy:** The current price of about HK$180 corresponds to a PE of 15 times. The valuation is reasonable but not cheap. If the stock price falls below HK$150 (corresponding to a PE of about 13 times) and the PE drops to single digits after deducting net cash, it will constitute a clear margin of safety, and you can consider opening a position at that time.
