## 2. Analysis of financial trends in 10 years

### 2.1 Revenue growth trajectory

| Year (recent years) | Total revenue | Year-on-year growth |
|--------------|--------|----------|
| 2020 | ~73.7 billion | +24% |
| 2021 | ~87.6 billion | +19% |
| 2022 | ~96.5 billion | +10% |
| 2023 | ~103.5 billion | +7% |
| 2024 | ~105.3 billion | +2% |
| 2025 | 112.6 billion | +7% |

**Trend Interpretation:** NetEase's revenue growth has gradually returned to mid-single digits from high growth. The growth rate will slow down to 2% in 2024 and rebound to 7% in 2025, mainly due to the return of double-digit growth in the game business (+11%). This is in line with the growth characteristics of a mature company - no longer pursuing explosive growth, but pursuing stable and quality growth.

### 2.2 Profit growth trajectory

NetEase's profit growth rate continues to be higher than revenue growth rate, which is due to:
- The operating efficiency of old games continues to improve (marginal costs decrease)
- Cuts in non-core items lower expenses
- Gradual optimization of R&D expense rates under scale effect

Net profit in 2025 will be 33.8 billion yuan, +14% year-on-year, significantly higher than the revenue growth rate of 7%, indicating that "the quality of profits is improving."

### 2.3 Long-term trend judgment

From a 10-year perspective, NetEase has gone through three stages:
1. **High-speed growth period (2015-2018):** Mobile games explode in dividends and revenue doubles
2. **Growth speed shifting period (2019-2022):** Tightening of industry supervision + cold winter of version number, growth rate will decline.
3. **Stable operation period (2023 to present):** Focus on quality products, profit growth exceeds revenue growth

**Key judgment:** NetEase has passed the rapid growth stage, and in the future it is more likely to be a model of **mid-single-digit revenue growth + double-digit profit growth**, similar to mature consumer goods companies.

## 4. Safety Margin Analysis

### 4.1 Current Valuation Level

As of May 2026, NetEase's stock price is approximately HK$180, which is in the lower middle of the reasonable valuation range (HK$160-210).

**Key Valuation Metrics:**
- Full-bore PE: ~15x (based on 2025 profits)
- Net cash PE: approximately 12x
- 52-week price range: HK$137-248
- From 52-week low: +31%
- From 52-week high: -27%

### 4.2 Safety Margin Assessment

**The safety margin required by Buffett is usually above 30%. **

- Based on DCF’s conservative valuation of HK$152: there is currently no margin of safety at HK$180
- Based on the segment valuation of HK$206: the current discount of HK$180 is about 13%, which is not sufficient
- Judging from the PE after deducting net cash: 12 times PE is a reasonably low valuation for a company with 22% ROE.

**Core Contradiction:** NetEase's "book safety margin" relies heavily on 163.5 billion net cash. If this cash sits on the account forever and is not distributed, its actual value will be reduced. If management can increase buybacks or increase dividends, the margin of safety will be significantly thicker.

## 5. Summary of financial and valuation assessment

### Core Discovery
1. **Extremely high financial quality:** High gross profit, high net profit, high ROE, low leverage, abundant cash, and almost no shortcomings
2. **Growth has slowed:** From high growth to mid-single digit growth stage, but profit growth is still flexible
3. **Reasonable valuation but not cheap:** PE 15 times may not seem expensive, but after deducting net cash, PE 12 times is indeed attractive
4. **Insufficient margin of safety:** Current prices lack obvious "bargain" opportunities

### Financial Health Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)
### Valuation attractiveness score: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)

**Summary:** NetEase's financial quality is impeccable, and it is a good company financial report at the textbook level. But a good company does not equal a good price. At the current price of 180 Hong Kong dollars, it’s not a loss, but it’s hard to say that it’s an advantage. The ideal buying range is below HK$150 (the PE after cash is reduced to below 10 times), by which time the safety margin will be more adequate.
