# NetEase (9999.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: good business judgment, free cash flow quality, customer stickiness and pricing power

---

## 1. Is this a good business?

### 1.1 The essence of business model

NetEase's core business is very simple: to develop high-quality games and continue to operate and monetize them. ** The business model of the game industry has a unique advantage-marginal cost is close to zero. After the development of a game is completed, the incremental cost of one more player is almost zero, which makes successful game products naturally have extremely high profit margins.

NetEase’s business model can be broken down into three layers:

**The first level: Self-developed game cash cow (core). ** The "Fantasy Westward Journey" series has been in operation for more than 20 years. In November 2025, the computer version exceeded 3.58 million online at the same time, and the monthly activity of the mobile game hit a two-year high. Products such as "Westward Journey", "Onmyoji", "Everlasting" and "Egg Boy Party" form a multi-category matrix. The characteristics of these long-life-cycle products are: R&D costs have been sunk, operation and maintenance costs are relatively controllable, and most of the cash flow generated is "real money."

**Level 2: New game development pipeline (growth options). ** NetEase will invest 17.7 billion yuan in R&D in 2025 and has 28 games in reserve. "Sea of ​​Forgotten" (nautical open world) is planned to be launched in Q3 2026, and "Infinite" (urban RPG) is being optimized. Every new game is an "option" - a new cash cow if it succeeds, and limited losses if it fails.

**The third level: non-game business (supplementary ecology). ** NetEase Cloud Music (7.8 billion revenue), Youdao Education (5.9 billion revenue, profitable for the first time), Mailbox, etc. These businesses form ecological synergies strategically, but their profit contribution is relatively limited.

### 1.2 Characteristic test of good business

| Features | NetEase Performance | Evaluation |
|------|----------|------|
| Asset-light operation | The main investment is talent and R&D, without a large amount of fixed assets | ★★★★★ |
| High gross profit margin | 64%, higher in game business | ★★★★★ |
| High net profit margin | 30% | ★★★★★ |
| Predictability | The revenue of long-term operating games is stable, but the success or failure of new games is uncertain | ★★★★☆ |
| Pricing power | The pricing of virtual items is flexible and players are willing to pay | ★★★★☆ |
| Scale effect | R&D costs are fixed, the more users, the higher the profits | ★★★★★ |

**Judgment: NetEase is a good business. ** It meets the core criteria of a good business - making "real money", not requiring large amounts of capital reinvestment to remain competitive, and having high and sustainable profit margins.

## 2. Free cash flow analysis

### 2.1 Cash flow quality

Free cash flow is the "gold standard" for judging corporate value. NetEase performs well in this regard:

**Key figures for 2025:**
- Cash flow from operating activities: approximately 24.9 billion yuan
-Capital expenditure: approximately 17.1 billion yuan (including large one-time investment)
- Free cash flow: approximately 7.8 billion yuan (conservative basis)

It should be noted that a large proportion of capital expenditures in 2025 will be investment in infrastructure such as data centers, which are non-routine expenditures. Normalized capital expenditure is about 5 billion to 8 billion yuan, corresponding to free cash flow of about 17 billion to 20 billion yuan.

**Net cash reserves:** As of the end of 2025, NetEase's net cash is approximately 163.5 billion yuan (cash + short-term investments - interest-bearing liabilities), which is an astonishing number - accounting for approximately 28% of the market value. In other words, if you buy NetEase for a market value of 100 yuan, about 28 yuan of it is cash lying in the bank.

### 2.2 "Moat indicator" of cash flow

One of the hallmarks of a good business is the **consistency and predictability** of cash flow. NetEase has unique advantages in this regard:

**Deferred revenue:** As of the end of Q4 2025, NetEase's contract liabilities (deferred revenue) are approximately 20.5 billion yuan, an increase of 5% from the previous quarter. Deferred revenue represents the amount that players have paid but has not yet been recognized as revenue, and is a "reservoir" for future revenue. This number continues to grow, indicating that players’ willingness to pay is increasing.

**Mainly operating liabilities:** NetEase's total liabilities are about 53.5 billion yuan, but they are mainly operating liabilities such as accounts payable and contract liabilities, and the proportion of interest-bearing liabilities is very low. This means that the company hardly needs to borrow money to operate, which reflects its strong self-generation ability.

### 2.3 How to use cash?

A good business must not only be able to make money, but also be able to spend money. NetEase’s cash allocation strategy:

- **Stock Repurchase:** US$5 billion repurchase plan, approximately US$2 billion has been repurchased as of Q3 2025, and the validity period is extended to 2029
- **Continuous dividend payment:** Quarterly dividend system, dividend per share in Q4 2025 is US$0.232
- **R&D investment:** 17.7 billion yuan/year, maintaining innovation capabilities
- **Cash Retention:** Substantial cash reserves provide strategic flexibility

**Evaluation:** NetEase has improved in terms of shareholder returns, but 163.5 billion in net cash still seems "too conservative." If repurchases can be increased or the dividend ratio can be increased, shareholder value will be more fully released.

## 3. Customer stickiness and user value

### 3.1 "Fantasy Westward Journey": "Moutai" in the gaming industry

"Fantasy Westward Journey" is the best sample to understand NetEase's customer stickiness. This game, launched in 2003, still set a new high in the number of people online at the same time in 2025 (3.58 million), which is extremely rare in the global game industry.

**Analysis of stickiness sources:**

**Social relationship chain (core moat). ** "Fantasy Westward Journey" has established mature social systems such as gangs, masters and apprentices, and marriage. The interpersonal network accumulated over the past 20 years cannot be replicated by any competitor. As NetEase itself concluded: "A strong sense of humanity is the biggest moat." This is highly consistent with Duan Yongping's understanding of good business - users stay not because they have "no choice", but because they are "reluctant to leave."

**Virtual asset precipitation. ** Players have invested a lot of time and money in the game to accumulate virtual assets (equipment, pets, characters, etc.). These sunk costs constitute extremely high switching costs. Fantasy Westward Journey even has an active virtual item trading market, which further enhances the "realism" of the assets.

**Continuous content updates. ** NetEase's continued investment in old games - "Changwan Server" hit four consecutive new online highs after its launch - proves that old users can be reactivated.

### 3.2 Multi-product matrix reduces dependency risk

NetEase doesn't rely on just one game:

### 3.3 Pricing power verification

The pricing power of game companies is reflected in players’ willingness to pay. The ARPU (average revenue per user) of NetEase users is at a relatively high level in the industry, and revenue growth can be achieved by relying on the growth of user scale and increase in payment penetration rate without significantly raising prices. This is a kind of "moderate pricing power" - it does not rely on price increases to make money, but relies on the attractiveness of the product to make users actively pay.

## 4. Summary of business model evaluation

### Advantages
- Game cash cow + light asset model = super high return on capital
- Long life cycle IP matrix provides a stable cash flow basis
- The "soft moat" formed by social relationship chains is extremely difficult to subvert
- Net cash of 163.5 billion provides a strong safety cushion

### Disadvantages
- The game industry is naturally facing the "risk of blockbuster hits", and the success rate of new games is uncertain.
- Revenue is highly dependent on gaming business (82%) and lacks diversification
- Non-game business has not yet formed an independent profit pillar
- Net cash allocation efficiency is low, shareholder returns can be more positive

### Business model rating: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5)

NetEase is a good business with high certainty. It's not the sexiest growth stock, but it's the kind of business that "you can understand, you can settle the accounts, and you can sleep peacefully." The cash flow quality of the game business is extremely high, the IP matrix of long-term operations provides a stable fundamentals, and the huge net cash provides an additional margin of safety. The only shortcomings are over-reliance on the gaming business and low cash allocation efficiency.
