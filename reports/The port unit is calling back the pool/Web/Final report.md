## Core investment logic

### Why is NetEase worth paying attention to?

**1. Games are an excellent business model. ** 64% gross profit margin, 30% net profit margin, 22% ROE - NetEase's profitability is among the best in the world. The game's triple characteristics of "zero marginal cost + virtual consumption + social stickiness" give it a naturally high return on capital.

**2. The characteristics of cash cows are established. ** Net profit in 2025 is 33.8 billion yuan, and normalized free cash flow is 17-20 billion yuan. The "Fantasy Westward Journey" series has still hit new online highs after 20 years of operation, proving the long-term monetization ability of old games. Deferred revenue was 20.5 billion and increased sequentially, providing visibility for future revenue.

**3. The balance sheet is extremely safe. ** Net cash is 163.5 billion yuan and almost zero interest-bearing liabilities. Even if revenue falls sharply in the worst-case scenario, the company is not at risk of financial crisis. This 163.5 billion net cash is equivalent to 28% of the market value - 28 yuan of the market value of buying 100 yuan is cash.

**4. The oligarch’s position is stable. ** Tencent and NetEase together account for 78% of the Chinese game market, and the "duopoly" pattern will not change in the short term. NetEase has established deep social moats and brand barriers in categories such as turn-based MMOs and action sports.

### What are the main concerns?

**1. The structural risks of Ding Lei’s “one-man governance”. ** 45.5% shareholding + iron-fist management style makes NetEase highly dependent on Ding Lei’s personal judgment. The accelerated departure of core producers and recent graduates taking up important positions have triggered concerns at the governance level.

**2. Limited room for growth. ** China's game market has entered the stage of stock competition, and revenue growth has dropped to mid-single digits. Overseas expansion fell short of expectations (studio layoffs/shutdowns in Japan and Canada), and it will be difficult to open up the second growth curve in the short term.

**3. Valuation is reasonable but not cheap. ** HKD 180 corresponds to a PE of about 15 times, and the PE after deducting net cash is about 12 times. For a company with a ROE of 22%, the valuation is not expensive, but there is no obvious opportunity to "pick up bargains".

**4. The possibility of "value trap" for huge amounts of cash. ** If the 163.5 billion net cash continues to be idle and not distributed to shareholders, its actual value will gradually shrink due to the time cost of funds.

## Investment Conclusions and Recommendations

### Conclusion: Actively pay attention and wait for better prices

**Current HKD 180 = reasonable valuation, not ideal buying price. **

NetEase is a company with extremely high financial quality, excellent business model, and solid industry position, meeting most of the criteria for a "good company." But a good company also needs a good price. The current valuation is in the middle of the reasonable range and lacks a sufficient margin of safety.

### Buying Strategy

| Above HKD 200 | >14x | Don’t buy, may be overvalued |
| HKD 170-200 | 11-14x | Reasonable range, small positions can be opened |
| HKD 150-170 | 9-11x | There is a margin of safety and positions can be opened normally |
| Below HKD 150 | <9x | Obviously undervalued, you can increase your position |

### Key variables that need to be continuously tracked

1. **New game performance:** "Sea of Oblivion" (Q3 2026) and "Infinite" launch and revenue data
2. **Shareholder Returns:** Whether the repurchase progress and dividend ratio have increased
3. **Talent changes:** Will the loss of core producers affect the product pipeline?
4. **Progress in going overseas:** Changes in the proportion of overseas income
5. **AI strategy implementation:** AI’s actual improvement in R&D efficiency and product experience

### Catalyst

- Substantially increase buyback efforts or increase dividend ratio (release cash value)
- New games are launched online and explode (such as "Sea of Forgotten" becoming a hit)
- The market plunge caused the stock price to fall below HK$150 (providing a margin of safety)
- Breakthrough progress in overseas business

### Risk warning signs

- The number of online users of core products (Fantasy Westward Journey, etc.) continues to decline
- Multiple new games performed poorly in a row
- Ding Lei made major irrational decisions (such as large-scale unrelated acquisitions)
- Significant adverse changes in regulatory policies

## One sentence summary

**NetEase is an efficient game cash generator with a very solid financial foundation and a deep moat. However, the "Ding Lei one-man decision-making" governance model and the current slightly "neither up nor down" valuation make it more suitable to buy when the market panics, rather than chasing highs in a reasonable valuation zone. **
