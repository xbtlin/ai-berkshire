# NetEase (9999.HK) Industry and Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, moat depth and width, competitor comparison

---

## 1. China’s gaming industry landscape

### 1.1 Market size and structure

China's game market is one of the largest single game markets in the world. Data for the first half of 2025 shows that the industry pattern presents a significant "duopoly + long tail" structure:

| Echelon | Representative company | Revenue scale | Market share |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Super leaders | Tencent (197.7 billion), NetEase (105.3 billion) | >100 billion | CR2≈78% |
| First-line | MiHoYo, Century Huatong, 37 Interactive Entertainment | 10-50 billion | ~15% |
| Long Tail | Hundreds of small and medium-sized game companies | <10 billion | ~7% |

**Key data:** In the first half of 2025, Tencent's market share will be approximately 50%, NetEase's market share will be approximately 28%, and the two companies total 78%. Although it is slightly lower than 86% in 2024, the duopoly pattern remains stable.

### 1.2 Industry Characteristics

**The era of stock competition. ** The user penetration rate of China's game market is close to the ceiling, and the increase mainly comes from the competition for user time and paid in-depth mining, rather than the increase in the number of users.

**The trend of quality products is irreversible. ** After the version number was tightened, a large number of "reskinned" low-quality games were eliminated, benefiting leading companies with R&D capabilities. NetEase will invest 17.7 billion yuan in R&D in 2025. This R&D barrier makes it increasingly difficult for small and medium-sized companies to compete.

**Globalization has become a new battlefield. ** After the growth rate of the domestic market slows down, going overseas has become the consensus of leading companies. Among the top ten game companies in the world, China accounts for three (Tencent at 2/25.5 billion US dollars, NetEase at 4/11.5 billion US dollars, and MiHoYo at 9/3.8 billion US dollars).

### 1.3 Industry Attractiveness Assessment

| Porter's Five Forces | Strength | Impact on NetEase |
|----------|------|-----------|
| Threat of new entrants | Low | High R&D investment + version number threshold creates barriers |
| Existing competitors | Medium to high | Tencent is the strongest competitor, but the tracks are different |
| Threat of substitutes | Medium | Short videos steal user time |
| Bargaining power of suppliers | Low | Mainly self-researched, not dependent on external IP |
| Buyer’s bargaining power | Medium | Players can choose not to pay in free mode |

## 2. Analysis of NetEase’s moat

### 2.1 Moat Type 1: Social Network Effect

**This is NetEase’s deepest moat. **

Take "Fantasy Westward Journey" as a typical case: the social systems such as gangs, masters and apprentices, and marriage in the game have formed an extremely complex interpersonal network after 20 years of accumulation. For a player to leave this game, it means leaving behind gang brothers, apprentices and masters, and even "virtual marriage" relationships.

The characteristics of this social moat are:
- **Time is the biggest barrier:** The social relationships accumulated over 20 years cannot be replicated by new games in a short time.
- **User voluntary maintenance:** Players are "bound" to each other and can retain users without the company spending money.
- **Intensify over time:** Social networks become more and more popular as they are used, similar to the network effects of products such as search engines.

**Verification data:** The PC version of "Fantasy Westward Journey" exceeded 3.58 million online concurrently in November 2025. This is the fourth time that Changwan Server has reached a new high since its launch. A 20-year-old game is still hitting new online highs, which is the most powerful proof of the social moat.

### 2.2 Moat Type 2: Switching Cost (Virtual Asset Precipitation)

Players have accumulated a large number of virtual assets in the game - equipment, pets, characters, achievements, etc. These assets represent a substantial investment of time and money and constitute extremely high sunk costs. "Fantasy Westward Journey" even has a complete virtual item trading market (such as the "Treasure Pavilion"), which gives virtual assets "real value" and further increases the conversion cost.

### 2.3 Moat type three: brand and IP accumulation

NetEase has established strong brand recognition in specific game categories:
- **Turn-based MMO:** "Fantasy Westward Journey" and "Westward Journey" are the absolute category kings
- **Action Competition:** "Everlasting" has established a differentiated positioning in the field of PC fighting competition.
- **Asymmetrical Competition:** "Identity V" has a loyal player base around the world

### 2.4 Moat Type 4: R&D Capability Barriers

NetEase's self-research gene is its core difference from Tencent. NetEase has 28 games in reserve, covering multiple categories such as two-dimensional, martial arts, and open world. R&D investment of 17.7 billion yuan per year has formed a multi-dimensional accumulation of talents, technology, tool chains, etc. This cannot be quickly established by burning money.

### 2.5 The fragility of the moat

**The shortcomings of the moat must be faced honestly:**

1. **The moat of a single game will decrease. ** Although "Fantasy Westward Journey" has reached new highs, games have a life cycle after all. The key question is not "how long can old games last?" but "can new games build a new moat?"
2. **The moat out to sea is very weak. ** NetEase's moat is mainly built in the Chinese market, and overseas markets are still in the "siege" rather than "defend" stage.
3. **AI may change the rules of the game. ** If AI significantly lowers the threshold for game development, small and medium-sized companies may narrow the research and development gap with NetEase.

## 3. In-depth comparison of competitors

### 3.1 Tencent Games: The Strongest Rival

| Dimension | Tencent | NetEase |
|------|------|------|
| Game revenue | ~197.7 billion | ~92.1 billion |
| Global ranking | No. 2 (USD 25.5 billion) | No. 4 (USD 11.5 billion) |
| Game Reserve | 35 models | 28 models |
| Core advantages | Social distribution (WeChat/QQ) | Self-developed quality |
| Track layout | The widest | Focus on quality products |
| Overseas proportion | ~30% | ~15% (estimate) |

**Relationship positioning:** Tencent is "full category coverage + traffic distribution", while NetEase is "high-quality self-research + in-depth operation". Although the two compete, it is more of a misplaced competition. Tencent's social traffic barrier is insurmountable by NetEase, but NetEase's self-research quality and deep cultivation of specific categories are also difficult for Tencent to match.

**Analogy:** If China's gaming industry is compared to the soft drink market, Tencent is Coca-Cola (channel is king, full category), and NetEase is Pepsi-Cola (differentiated positioning, premium route). Second place is secure, but catching up to first is nearly impossible.

### 3.2 MiHoYo: The most noteworthy challenger

| Dimension | MiHoYo | NetEase |
|------|--------|------|
| Global Ranking | No. 9 (USD 3.8 billion) | No. 4 (USD 11.5 billion) |
| Core products | "Genshin Impact" and "Star Railway" | Multi-product matrix |
| Product quantity | Few but refined | Multi-category matrix |
| Overseas capability | Extremely strong (global server) | Under construction |
| New travel reserve | 2 models | 28 models |

**Threat of MiHoYo:** MiHoYo has proved the possibility of "a company can enter the top ten in the world with 1-2 products." If MiHoYo continues to launch hit games, it may erode NetEase’s share in categories such as ACG.

**But limitations of MiHoYo:** The product line is too concentrated (mainly relying on the two-dimensional track), with only 2 new games in reserve, and the sustainability of growth is questionable. In contrast, NetEase's multi-category matrix is ​​more robust.

### 3.3 Other competitors

- **Sanqi Interactive Entertainment:** Good at volume marketing, but product quality and IP accumulation are far inferior to NetEase
- **Century Huatong:** With Shanda Games (Legendary IP) as the core, the track has low overlap with NetEase
- **Global 3A manufacturers:** As the Chinese market opens up, Sony, Microsoft, etc. may increase investment in China

## 4. Key variables of the industry

### 4.1 Version Number Policy

Game version number is a unique variable in the Chinese market. Version number approval has recovered after going through the "freeze period" in 2021-2022, but policy uncertainty always exists. The impact on NetEase is two-sided - tightening the version number has inhibited the pace of new game launches, but it has also eliminated a large number of low-quality competitors and strengthened the market position of leading companies.

### 4.2 The impact of AI on the gaming industry

NetEase has deeply integrated AI into the research and development process and built an "AI new quality productivity system" in 2025. The impact of AI on the gaming industry needs to be considered from two directions:
- **Benefits:** AI improves research and development efficiency, reduces content production costs, and enhances player experience
- **Risk:** If AI significantly lowers the development threshold, it may weaken the R&D barriers of major manufacturers.

### 4.3 Progress in going overseas

NetEase CEO Ding Lei once proposed a goal of increasing the proportion of overseas revenue to 40-50%, but the actual progress has not been as expected. In 2024, Japan's Sakura Studio will undergo major layoffs and the Canadian studio will cease operations, showing that the road to overseas is not smooth. This is a key variable to keep track of.

## 5. Summary of Industry and Competition Assessment

### Core Discovery
1. **The duopoly structure is stable:** CR2=78%, NetEase ranks second and will not be overturned in the short term
2. **The moat is mainly based on social networks and switching costs:** Sufficient depth but limited width (mainly in the Chinese market)
3. **Misplaced competition with Tencent:** The second place position is safe, but the ceiling is relatively clear
4. **Going overseas is a key variable for growth:** However, the current progress is not as good as expected and needs to be viewed with caution.

### Industry pattern rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)
### Moat Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Summary:** NetEase is in a good industry with oligopolistic competition and occupies a solid second place. Its moat is deep enough in the domestic market (social relationship chain + virtual assets + brand IP), but its moat in the global market is still under construction. The biggest uncertainties in the industry come from regulatory policies and changes in AI technology. These factors are both risks and opportunities.
