# ZhongAn Online (6060.HK)—Industry Competition Analysis

## 1. Internet Insurance/Insurtech Industry Pattern

### Industry development stage

China's Internet insurance is in a critical period of transformation from "traffic-driven" to "value-driven":

- **2013-2018**: Traffic bonus period, return insurance/flight delay insurance exploded, and pioneers such as Zhongan expanded rapidly
- **2018-2022**: Tightening supervision + rising traffic costs + intensified competition among short-term insurance homogenization
- **2023-2026**: Refined operation period, underwriting profit > premium scale, AI empowerment becomes a new variable

### Competitive Landscape

| Player Categories | Representatives | Strengths and Weaknesses |
|----------|------|--------|
| Internet-native insurance | Zhongan Online | Strong technology + fast innovation, but limited scale |
| Traditional insurance companies go online | Ping An Good Doctor, China Pacific Insurance Online | Brand + capital + customer base, but slow transformation |
| Platform intermediary | Ant Insurance, WeInsurance (Tencent) | Huge traffic, but no risk coverage |
| Pure technology company | Shuidi, Huize | Asset-light, but no underwriting capability |

### Zhongan’s unique positioning
- **The only "full stack" Internet insurance company**: It has an insurance license (can underwrite), a technology platform (can export), and a banking license (Zhongan Bank)
- This means ZhongAn can do more than any single category competitor
- But it also means: the front is longer and resources are more dispersed

## 2. Zhongan’s competitive position ★★★☆☆

### Core Competitive Advantages

**1. Technical capabilities**
- The AI ​​middle platform "Zhongyoulingxi" was called 2 billion times throughout the year
- Industry-leading claims automation rate
- R&D investment of 398 million yuan (accounting for ~1% of revenue)
- Application of large models in underwriting, claims settlement, customer service, etc.

**2. Ecological cooperation network**
- Ant Group (the largest shareholder) brings huge traffic entrance to Alipay
- Tencent (the second largest shareholder) brings WeChat ecosystem
- Cooperate with 150+ large-scale sports events to expand the brand
- This "shareholder = channel" structure is difficult for other companies to copy

**3. Product innovation speed**
- The cycle from demand insight to product launch is extremely short
- Ranked first in the industry in terms of development capabilities for fragmented and scenario-based products
- Pioneer of new categories such as pet insurance and sports insurance

**4. Data accumulation**
- Behavior data + claims data of hundreds of millions of users
- More data → more accurate pricing → better risk control → lower loss ratio
- This is a forward cycle

### Competitive disadvantages

**1. Dependence on third-party platforms**
- ~79% of premiums come from third-party channels
- If Ant/Tencent introduces more competition from insurance companies, ZhongAn’s share may be diluted
- Or the platform increases cooperation rates and compresses profits

**2. Insufficient brand awareness**
- Users know "Insurance on Alipay" but may not know that it is "ZhongAn Insurance"
- Brand mental building is still in the early stages
- The 21% share of self-operated channels is increasing but still low.

**3. The scale effect is not as good as that of traditional large insurance companies**
- 35.7 billion premium vs Ping An of China’s trillion level
- In standard products such as auto insurance, large companies have more obvious advantages in pricing and claims network

**4. The sustainability of technological output needs to be verified**
- Technology output business is still losing money
- Are insurance companies really willing to buy core systems from competitors?
- Mid- to long-term business model has not yet been verified

## 3. Analysis of multiple thinking models

### Platform Economics Perspective
ZhongAn is trying to build an **insurtech platform**:
- Upstream: docking traffic platforms (Ant, Tencent, JD.com, etc.)
- Midstream: own underwriting/risk control/claims engine
- Downstream: Insurance products are exported to terminal C and terminal B

If this platform is established, ZhongAn’s value will be far greater than that of an insurance company. But the problem is:
- ZhongAn's position in the platform ecosystem is "chosen by the platform" rather than "choosing the platform"
- It is part of the ant ecology, not the ant ecology is built around it

### "Moat widening" or "moat narrowing"?
- **Broadened signal**: Increase in the proportion of self-operated channels (15% → 21%), accumulation of AI capabilities, and brand investment
- **Narrowing signals**: Traditional insurance companies are accelerating online, platforms are introducing more competition, and products can be imitated.

**Current Judgment**: The moat is **slowly widening**, but not fast enough. Competitors are catching up.

### "Killer App" Test
Does ZhongAn have a core product/capability that competitors cannot copy?
- "Exclusive e-Life" million-dollar medical insurance: It was once a killer product, but now it has been imitated by many companies
- AI claims automation: has technical advantages but is not "irreplaceable"
- **Conclusion**: There is no clear "killer barrier" yet

## 4. Industry Trends and Zhongan’s Adaptability

### Trend 1: AI deeply changes the insurance value chain
- AI-based entire chain from underwriting to claims settlement
- ZhongAn AI calls 2 billion times per year, leading the industry
- **Adaptability: ★★★★★**——This is Zhongan’s biggest differentiation direction

### Trend 2: Internet-based auto insurance
- Potential for UBI (usage-based insurance)
- New energy vehicle insurance requires new risk control models
- Zhongan auto insurance growth rate 30%+
- **Adaptability: ★★★★☆**——Actively occupying

### Trend Three: Health Management + Insurance Integration
- From "post-event compensation" to "pre-event health management"
- Requires integration capabilities of data + technology + services
- **Adaptability: ★★★★☆**——"Exclusive e-Life" has a health management ecology

### Trend 4: Tighter supervision
- Standardization of Internet insurance sales
- Increased requirements for automatic renewal, commission transparency, etc.
- **Adaptability: ★★★☆☆** - Rising compliance costs are a challenge

## 5. "50-Year Perspective"

Will ZhongAn Online still be there in 50 years? **uncertain. **

- The insurance industry must exist - yes
- Internet insurance must exist - yes
- But whether the brand "Zhongan" is still there - not sure
- If the moat is not wide enough, it may be replaced by a larger platform (Ant does its own insurance?) or more efficient new entrants

This is very different from banks, which have license barriers and systemically important protections. Insurers live or die by underwriting capacity and customer base, and these can be eroded.
