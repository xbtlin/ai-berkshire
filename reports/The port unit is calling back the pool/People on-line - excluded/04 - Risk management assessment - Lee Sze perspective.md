**The game between the three major shareholders**:
- Ant Group (~15%): a source of traffic and a potential competitor
- Tencent (~5-10%): Provides WeChat ecosystem but limited investment
- Ping An Insurance (~5%): It is a collaborator and the most direct competitor

**Risk**: If the conflict of interests between shareholders intensifies (such as Ant building its own insurance capabilities), Zhongan may lose its core traffic source.

## 2. Risk Assessment

### Risk 1: Platform dependence risk ★★★★★ (highest)

**Specific performance**:
- ~79% of premiums come from third-party platforms (mainly Ant and Tencent Ecosystem)
- If Ant launches its own insurance brand or introduces more competitors
- If the platform increases cooperation rates or changes traffic allocation algorithms

**Extent of impact**: fatality - if the Ant channel is lost, premiums may be cut in half

**Release factors**:
- Ant is a shareholder of Zhongan and has consistent interests
- The proportion of ZhongAn’s self-operated channels is increasing (15% → 21%)
- Multi-platform cooperation (not just relying on Ant)

**Probability judgment**: The probability of complete breakage is <5%, but the probability of deterioration of cooperation conditions is 20-30%

### Risk 2: Risk of intensified competition ★★★★☆

**Specific performance**:
- Traditional insurance companies (Ping An, China Pacific Insurance, PICC) vigorously develop online channels
- These companies have stronger brands, more capital, and wider service networks
- In terms of standard products such as auto insurance, Zhongan’s competitive advantage is not obvious

**Extent of impact**: May compress profit margins and market share growth

**Release factors**:
- ZhongAn still has advantages in innovative products and technical efficiency
- Large companies may not be willing to make fragmented and scenario-based products (the scale is too small and the management cost is high)

### Risk 3: Regulatory policy risk ★★★☆☆

**Specific performance**:
- Regulation of Internet insurance sales continues to tighten
- Increased requirements for automatic deductions, complaint handling, and information disclosure
- May restrict how certain innovative products can be sold

**Impact**: Increases compliance costs but does not fundamentally change the business model

### Risk 4: Uncertain returns on technology investment ★★★☆☆

**Specific performance**:
- Technology output business continues to suffer losses (although losses narrowed by 32%)
- R&D investment of 398 million yuan per year is an ongoing cost
- AI ROI takes time to verify

**Influence**: If technology output cannot be profitable in the long term, it will drag down overall returns.

### Risk 5: Natural disaster/large compensation risk ★★★☆☆

**Specific performance**:
- As a property insurance company, we face catastrophic risks
- Although covered by reinsurance, extreme events may still impact profits

**Extent of impact**: May cause significant fluctuations in profits in a single year

- **Evaluation**: ★★★☆☆

- **Evaluation**: ★★★★☆

- But individual policies may restrict innovation
- **Evaluation**: ★★★★☆

### Fair playing field

- **Evaluation**: ★★★★☆

## 4. Investment risk and return assessment

### Worst case scenario (10% probability)
- Ant reduces cooperation + intensifies competition + worsens overall cost ratio
- Profits fell back to 500 million to 1 billion, and PE was compressed to 15 times
- Market value shrunk to HK$10-15 billion
- Downside: -40-50%

### Pessimistic scenario (20% probability)
- Growth rate slows to 5-8% + technology output continues to suffer losses
- PE compressed to 15-18 times
- Downside: -20-30%

### Baseline scenario (45% probability)
- Premium growth rate of 10-15% + combined ratio continues to improve
- Profit increased from 1.8 billion to 2.5-3 billion yuan
- PE maintained at 18-22 times
- Annualized total return: 15-25%

### Optimistic scenario (25% probability)
- Auto insurance + health insurance two-wheel drive + AI significantly reduces costs
- Profit reaches 3.5-4 billion yuan
- The market gives technology a premium, PE 25-30 times
- Annualized total return: 30-50%
