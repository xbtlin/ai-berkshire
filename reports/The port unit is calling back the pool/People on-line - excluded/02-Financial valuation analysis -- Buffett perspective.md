# ZhongAn Online (6060.HK) – Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Profit quality analysis

### Revenue structure (2025)
- Total premiums: 35.735 billion yuan (+6.9% YoY)
- Health insurance: the largest sector
- Digital life insurance: including sports insurance, pet insurance and other innovative products
- Auto insurance: fastest growing (+30%+)
- Consumer financial insurance: slowest growth/decline

### Profit Trend
- Adjusted net profit attributable to parent companies: 1.800 billion yuan (+198.3% YoY)
- Underwriting profit: positive for fifth consecutive year
- Combined ratio: ~95-96% (continuous improvement)
- ZhongAn Bank’s first profit: HK$17.27 million (symbolic significance is greater than actual significance)

### Profit quality judgment ★★★★☆

**Front**:
- Profit increased by 198% - although there is a low base effect, the trend is improving
- The underwriting side has been profitable for five consecutive years, indicating that pricing capabilities and risk control levels are improving.
- The combined ratio dropped from 97%+ to 95-96%, and every percentage point improvement is worth real money.
- The AI platform has been called 2 billion times, indicating that technology investment is beginning to yield efficiency.

**Negative**:
- The absolute profit is still not high: 1.8 billion yuan corresponds to a market value of 30 billion Hong Kong dollars = ROE is only 6-8%
- Technology export business is still losing money
- Profits are highly volatile - this is naturally the case in the insurance industry, which is compounded by fluctuations in investment returns
- Years that rely on investment income to fill underwriting profit shortfalls (such as 2020-2022)

## 3. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| PE (TTM) | ~21x |
| PB | ~1.5-2.0 times |
| Market capitalization | ~25-30 billion HKD |
| ROE | ~6-8% |

### Valuation rationality judgment ★★★☆☆

**What does PE 21 times mean? **
- Much higher than traditional insurance companies (China Pacific Insurance 6-7 times, China Life 7-8 times)
- The market is priced at a "growth + technology" premium
- If profits can grow at a growth rate of 30-40% for 2-3 years, 21 times PE can be digested
- But if the growth rate slows down to 10-15%, 21 times PE will be too expensive

**Logic compared with traditional insurance**:
- Traditional insurance PE is 6-7 times because growth rate is low + ROE has reached its peak
- ZhongAn PE 21 times because profits are still growing rapidly + technology attribute premium
- Key question: How long can ZhongAn’s growth rate last? Is the technology premium justified?

**Critical path to growth realization**:
- If the profit from 2026 to 2028 is from 1.8 billion → 2.5 billion → 3.5 billion → 5 billion yuan
- Then the PE will fall back to 6-8 times in 2028 (corresponding to a market value of 30 billion), converging with traditional insurance
- This requires 40%+ annual profit growth - challenging but not impossible

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Downside Risks**:
- If the growth rate does not meet expectations (premium growth rate drops below 5%), PE may be compressed to 12-15 times → -25-40% downward
- If Ant/Tencent reduces cooperation, premiums may increase at zero or even decline
- Strong supervision in the insurance industry may limit the space for innovation

**Upside Flexibility**:
- If the two-wheel drive of auto insurance + health insurance achieves 25% + premium growth
- Combined ratio continues to be optimized to below 94%
- Technology output business turned around losses
- PE can maintain 20-25 times → annual return 20-30%

## 4. Capital efficiency and dividends

### Capital status
- As a company in the growth stage, ZhongAn does not pay large-scale dividends yet.
- Capital is mainly used for: business expansion + technology research and development + solvency maintenance
- Solvency adequacy ratio meets regulatory requirements

### Dividend prospects
- Large dividends unlikely in the short term (1-2 years)
- In the medium term (3-5 years), if profits grow steadily to 3 billion to 5 billion, dividends may be started
- Dividends are not currently suitable as a source of return

### ROE improvement path
- The current ROE of 6-8% is low
- If profits double (from 1.8 billion → 3.6 billion) and net assets grow more slowly, ROE can rise to 10-12%
- This is the key to the transition of valuation from "growth stocks" to "value growth stocks"
