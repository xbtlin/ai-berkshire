# ZhongAn Online (6060.HK) - Investment Research Final Report

## Overall rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.0/5.0)

## One sentence summary

ZhongAn Online is a pioneer in China's Internet insurance track. Five consecutive years of underwriting profits have proven the feasibility of the business model. AI empowerment and the growth of auto insurance have provided growth momentum. However, the 21 times PE needs sustained high growth to digest it. High dependence on third-party platforms is the biggest structural risk.

## Core investment logic

### Reasons to be bullish
1. **Profit turning point confirmed**: From years of losses/meager profits to 1.8 billion profits (+198%), the trend is clear
2. **AI empowered cost reduction**: The comprehensive cost ratio continues to improve, and 2 billion AI calls bring real efficiency improvements.
3. **Auto insurance is the second growth curve**: 30%+ growth rate, new energy vehicle insurance is a structural opportunity
4. **Industry penetration rate is still low**: There is still room for significant improvement in the proportion of Internet insurance in China
5. **Increase in the proportion of self-operated channels**: from 15% to 21%, which is the right direction to reduce platform dependence

### Reasons to be bearish
1. **Platform dependence is a fatal weakness**: 79% of premiums come from third parties, and your destiny is not entirely in your own hands.
2. **21 times PE requires sustained high growth**: once the growth rate slows down, valuations will face severe compression
3. **The moat is not wide enough**: Products have no patent protection and innovations can be quickly imitated.
4. **Technology output is still losing money**: It is uncertain when the breakeven will be
5. **ROE is low (6-8%)**: capital efficiency is not high enough

## Differences in positioning from other targets this time

Zhongan Online is the only "growth" target among the six companies this time, and the other five companies all follow the logic of "interest collection + value restoration". This means:
- ZhongAn's return source is "profit growth + valuation maintenance" rather than "dividends + valuation repair"
- ZhongAn’s risk-return characteristics are completely different from the other five companies
- In the combination, Zhongan plays the "offensive" role, and bank stocks play the "defensive" role.

## Conclusion and suggestions

ZhongAn Online is an investment opportunity that is **in the right direction but lacks certainty**. The core contradiction lies in:

- The business model is in the right direction (Internet + Insurance + AI), and the trend is on Zhongan’s side
- But the moat is not wide enough - the first-mover advantage is shrinking and platform dependence is increasing
- 21 times PE is not cheap - it takes 3-5 consecutive years of 20%+ profit growth to be "worth the price"

**Suitable Investment Strategy**:
- If you have strong confidence in the Internet insurance track, you can allocate a small amount of positions (5-10%)
- Closely track: Proportion of self-operated channels, comprehensive cost rate, Ant cooperation dynamics
- If growth slows below 10% and valuations do not compress → consider exiting

ZhongAn is not a target to "buy with eyes closed", but a "conditional investment" that requires continuous verification.
