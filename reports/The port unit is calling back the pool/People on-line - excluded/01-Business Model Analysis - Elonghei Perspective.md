# ZhongAn Online (6060.HK) - Business model analysis

## 1. The essence of business

ZhongAn Online’s business model can be broken down into three layers:

**Level One: Internet Insurance Distribution**
- Sell fragmented and scenario-based insurance products through the traffic portals of Ant, Tencent and other platforms
- Return freight insurance, flight delay insurance, broken screen insurance, etc. - small amount, high frequency, embedded
- Financial services that are essentially "traffic realization"

**Second level: self-operated insurance products**
- Health insurance (million-dollar medical insurance, "Exclusive e-Health", etc.)
- car insurance
- Digital life insurance (pet insurance, sports insurance, etc.)
- From scene embedding to private brand

**Third level: Technology output**
- Export the insurance core system to other insurance companies
- AI platform (all-in-one) empowers industries
- ZA Bank - virtual banking business

**Core Cognition**: ZhongAn tries to be an "insurance company" and a "technology company" at the same time. This dual identity is both an advantage and a confusion.

## 2. Moat evaluation ★★★☆☆ (3/5)

**There is a certain moat but not wide:**

**Strengths:**
- **Ecological Embedding**: In-depth cooperation with super platforms such as Ant and Tencent makes the customer acquisition cost much lower than that of traditional insurance companies
- **Product Innovation Capability**: Able to quickly develop new insurance products (such as sports insurance, pet insurance) and respond to market demand
- **Technical capabilities**: The AI platform "Zhongyoulingxi" has been called 2 billion times throughout the year, and has accumulated experience in claims automation, risk control models, etc.
- **First mover advantage**: Pioneer in the Internet insurance industry, with high brand recognition

**Weaknesses:**
- **Dependence on platforms**: About 79% of premiums come from third-party platforms, and only 21% are self-operated. If Ant/Tencent changes their cooperation strategy, the impact will be huge
- **Product replicability**: Insurance products have no patent protection and competitors can quickly follow up
- **Low switching cost**: Users’ loyalty to insurance brands is much lower than their loyalty to platforms. "I bought insurance from Alipay" rather than "insurance from ZhongAn"
- **Technology output has not yet verified the scale effect**: Technology business is still losing money (although the loss narrowed by 32%)

## 3. "Simplicity" test of business model

**This is not a simple business**:

- **Positive**: The essence of insurance is simple - collecting premiums, managing risks, and paying compensation - Zhongan has achieved the ultimate through Internet channels
- **Negative**:
  - Doing insurance + technology output + virtual banking at the same time, the front is too long
  - Dependence on traffic platforms makes its destiny partly out of its own hands
  - The valuation logic of "insurtech" is vacillating - the market sometimes values based on insurance and sometimes based on technology.

Duan Yongping may ask: "Is ZhongAn an insurance company or a technology company? If it is both, what is your core competitiveness?"

**Conclusion**: The pattern is attractive but not simple and focused enough. The dual identity of "insurance + technology" not only brings valuation premium, but also brings strategic ambiguity.

## 4. User value and "differentiation"

The core values ZhongAn creates for users:

**1. Ultimate convenience**
- Online insurance, underwriting in seconds, and quick claims settlement
- No need to go to the insurance company or meet with the agent
- Embed in shopping/travel/life scenes, "senseless purchase"

**2. Innovative products fill gaps**
- Small-amount, fragmented products that traditional insurance companies are unwilling/unable to produce
- Pet insurance, sports insurance, return insurance, etc. meet the "long tail demand"
- "Exclusive e-life" million-dollar medical insurance has changed the health insurance market structure

**3. Value for money**
- Internet distribution eliminates agent commissions
- Some products are priced lower than traditional channels

**Differentiated vulnerability**: The above advantages are being imitated by traditional insurance companies. Ping An, China Pacific Insurance, etc. are all increasing investment in online services. ZhongAn’s first-mover advantage is shrinking.

## 5. Long-term sustainability ★★★☆☆ (3/5)

**Pros:**
- China’s insurance penetration rate is still low, and Internetization is an inevitable trend
- There is huge market space for health insurance and auto insurance
- AI empowerment can continuously reduce operating costs
- The increase in the proportion of self-operated channels (21%) helps reduce platform dependence

**Disadvantages:**
- Platform traffic costs may rise (Ant/Tencent may raise the threshold for cooperation or introduce more competitors)
- Homogenization of insurance products - profit margins may be compressed by competition in the long run
- The technology output business is still losing money, and it is uncertain when it will break even.
- Tighter regulatory requirements for Internet insurance sales
