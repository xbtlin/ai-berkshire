## 3. Investment thesis

### 3.1 Core investment logic

The essence of investing in CLP is to buy a "quasi-utility bond" with a guaranteed return rate of 8% and a duration of more than 15 years at a reasonable price, with a dividend rate of about 4.3% and a moderate growth of about 2%. **

The prerequisites for this logic to hold:
1. Hong Kong’s regulatory framework will remain basically stable in 2033 and beyond
2. Hong Kong’s electricity demand will not shrink significantly
3. Management does not make major capital allocation errors
4. The buying price provides an adequate margin of safety

### 3.2 Bull market arguments

1. **Unshakable monopoly position**: 2.83 million customers, 99.999% reliability, natural monopoly barriers
2. **Profit Guaranteed**: 8% allowed rate of return is the profit "floor"
3. **Steady dividend growth**: Maintained or increased for many consecutive years, the 4.3% yield is attractive in a low interest rate environment
4. **Decarbonization investment = profit growth**: New fixed assets also enjoy an 8% return rate
5. **Electrification trend is positive**: New demands such as data centers and electric vehicles drive growth

### 3.3 Bear market argument

1. **Valuation is expensive**: 18.6 times PE lacks a margin of safety
2. **Australian business is a "bleeding point"**: Profit will plummet 85.6% in 2025, and structural problems remain unresolved
3. **Risk of reduction in allowed rate of return**: Renewal in 2033 may drop from 8% to 7-7.5%
4. **Low growth ceiling**: Mature market + fixed rate of return = limited growth space
5. **Interest rate sensitivity**: Changes in the global interest rate environment may compress valuations

## 7. Final conclusion

### 7.1 Overall judgment on CLP Holdings

CLP Holdings is a **"good but not good enough"** investment target:

**"Good" lies in**:
- The quality of Hong Kong’s electricity monopoly business is extremely high
- Regulatory framework provides profit certainty
- Management integrity and governance standards
- Financially sound and reliable dividends

**"Not good enough" lies in**:
- Overseas diversification lowers overall return on capital
- Australian business is a strategic baggage to be resolved
- Current valuations do not provide an adequate margin of safety
- Limited growth space, annualized return expected to be only 5-6%

### 7.2 Investment decision-making suggestions

| Decision | Suggestion |
|------|------|
| Open a position at the current price (HKD 74)? | **Not recommended**. Lack of safety margin, waiting for better prices. |
| Put on the watch list? | **Suggestion**. High-quality assets deserve continued tracking. |
| Ideal buying range? | HKD 58-65 (corresponding to 5% + dividend rate) |
| What type of investors is it suitable for? | Long-term investors who pursue stable cash flow and accept low growth |
| Position suggestions? | Even at ideal prices, it should not exceed 10% of the portfolio (limited growth) |

### 7.3 One sentence summary

**CLP Holdings is a utility stock with a "bottom at the bottom and a top at the top" - it won't let you lose big money, but it will also hardly let you make big money. The most appropriate investment strategy is to patiently wait for a reasonable price (near HK$60) when the market panics, buy at a 5%+ dividend rate, and then hold it for a long time to collect interest. **
