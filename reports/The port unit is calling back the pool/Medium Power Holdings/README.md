## Research background

CLP Holdings is one of the two major power suppliers in Hong Kong, supplying power to approximately 2.83 million customers in Kowloon, the New Territories and most outlying islands, with power supply reliability reaching 99.999%. Hong Kong’s electricity business is protected by the Scheme of Control Agreement, with a permitted rate of return of 8% of the net fixed asset value, and the agreement is valid until 2033.

The company's business covers Hong Kong, Mainland China, Australia, India and Southeast Asia, and it is a leading power company in the Asia-Pacific region. The controlling shareholder is the Kadoorie family, which holds about 35% of the shares. The family has been in business for more than a century.
