# Baidu Group: Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business nature, user value, moat depth, corporate culture
> Core concept: A good business model should be simple and sustainable, with clear and identifiable user value

---

## 1. What is Baidu’s business model?

### 1.1 Traditional model: the “toll booth” of search advertising

Baidu's traditional business model is extremely clear - China's largest search engine charges advertisers through bidding rankings. This model was perfect before 2015: users use search for free, advertisers pay for traffic, and Baidu collects tolls.

But the core assumption of this model is: **Users need to "search" to obtain information**.

The reality of 2025 is that this assumption is being shattered. Douyin has an average daily search volume of 5.3 billion, WeChat has an average daily search volume of over 1 billion, and Xiaohongshu has 600 million searches. The average daily search volume of Baidu App has dropped to 300 million times, and the mobile search market share is about 30%. More importantly, the information acquisition habits of young users have shifted from "active search" to "passive recommendation" - watching short videos, watching social content, and searching is only an occasional supplementary behavior.

**Assessment: The moat of the traditional search advertising business is fading fast. ★★☆☆☆**

### 1.2 New model: Commercialization of AI full-stack capabilities

Baidu is building a new business model: with the large model as the bottom layer, it supports the three monetization layers of AI cloud (enterprise level), AI application (consumer level), and autonomous driving (travel level).

- **AI Cloud**: Revenue in 2025 will be approximately 28 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 34%. AI computing power subscription revenue in Q4 increased by 143% year-on-year. Essentially, it is selling Baidu’s AI infrastructure capabilities to enterprise customers.
- **AI native marketing**: Revenue in 2025 will be 2.8 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 262%. Upgrade traditional search advertising to AI-generated marketing.
- **Autonomous Driving**: Luobo Kuaipao completed 3.4 million services in Q4 of 2025, and Wuhan has achieved bicycle breakeven.
- **Kunlun Core**: AI chip business, with revenue expected to be 5 billion yuan in 2025, has launched a spin-off and listing.

**Assessment: New business growth is strong, but a closed-loop profit model has not yet been formed. ★★★☆☆**

## 2. User value analysis: What does Baidu mean to users?

### 2.1 Search users: value is losing

A key test criterion is: if Baidu search disappears tomorrow, how much pain will users feel?

Frankly, the pain is decreasing. For most daily information needs, Douyin search, WeChat search, and Xiaohongshu can already satisfy it. Baidu search still has unique value in professional information queries, long-tail questions, academic materials, etc., but the commercial value of these scenarios is limited.

What is even more worrying is the quality of Baidu’s content ecosystem. The content quality of Baijiahao is uneven, and the user search experience is diluted by excessive advertisements and low-quality content, which further weakens user stickiness.

### 2.2 AI Cloud Customers: Value is Growing

Baidu Intelligent Cloud ranks first in AI large model solutions with a market share of 16.6%, which shows that Baidu does provide valuable products in the field of enterprise-level AI services. Wenxin Big Model's capabilities in Chinese scenarios and Baidu's accumulation of AI infrastructure are of practical value to corporate customers.

However, it should be noted that the enterprise-level AI market is extremely competitive. Alibaba Cloud's Tongyi Qianwen, Huawei's Shengteng Ecosystem, and Byte's Volcano Engine are all catching up quickly, and each has greater ecological advantages.

### 2.3 Autonomous driving users: value is being verified

Feedback from users of Luobo Kuaipao in cities such as Wuhan is generally positive - the price is cheap, the experience is novel, and safety is constantly improving. However, self-driving travel is currently more of a "supplement" than a "replacement." To become a truly in-demand travel method, a larger scale of vehicle deployment and coverage in more cities are needed.

## 3. Moat assessment

### 3.1 Technical moat ★★★★☆

Baidu’s accumulation of AI technology is real:

- Cumulative R&D investment from 2016 to 2025 exceeds 100 billion yuan
- The official version of Wenxin 5.0 has 2.4 trillion parameters and has entered the first echelon in the world in multiple benchmark tests.
- Leading the world in self-driving technology, tied for first place with Waymo
- Kunlun Core ranks third in domestic AI chips (after Huawei Ascend)

Technology accumulation is Baidu’s deepest moat. But technology itself does not constitute a business moat – the key lies in whether the technology can be transformed into irreplaceable products and services.

### 3.2 Data Moat ★★★☆☆

Baidu has the largest search data set in China, as well as a large amount of road test data in the field of autonomous driving. This data is valuable for training AI models, but as synthetic data and other data sources become more abundant, the barriers to exclusive data are lowering.

### 3.3 Network Effect ★★☆☆☆

The network effect of search itself is weak - the more people who use it, the better it is. Baidu is far inferior to WeChat (social network effect) and Douyin (content creator network effect) in this regard. The ecological stickiness of the AI ​​cloud business is being established, but it is still in its early stages.

### 3.4 Brand Moat ★★☆☆☆

"Just click Baidu and you'll know" was once a national-level brand recognition, but in recent years Baidu's brand image has been significantly damaged. The long-term impact of the Putian Hospital incident, the decline in search content quality, and the negative impression of bidding rankings have made Baidu less trustworthy in the minds of consumers.

In terms of brand recognition in the AI field, Wen Xinyiyan is well-known, but Doubao and Qianwen have surpassed Baidu in terms of user growth.

### 3.5 Switching Cost ★★☆☆☆

The switching cost of search is extremely low - users can switch to other search engines or information platforms at any time. The switching cost of AI cloud business is relatively high (enterprises' deployed solutions have migration costs), but the industry standardization trend may lower this barrier.

## 4. Corporate culture and “doing the right thing”

### 4.1 The double-edged sword of technology supremacy

Baidu's internal R&D personnel account for more than 60%. This is a typical "engineer culture" company. This culture is an advantage in technology research and development, but it has shortcomings in productization and commercialization.

A key fact is: **Baidu has invested more than 100 billion yuan in research and development in the past ten years, but it has never been able to incubate a phenomenal C-side application**. Wenxinyiyan has 200 million MAU, but it is far behind Doubao (315 million) and Qianwen (203 million). On the contrary, the MAU of Baidu Wenku, Baidu Netdisk and other products is declining.

This shows that there is a deep problem in Baidu's culture: **good at technological innovation, not good at user insights**.

### 4.2 History of strategic swings

Baidu has repeatedly swung between "platformization" and "productization" throughout its history. From O2O (Baidu Waimai/Baidu Nuomi), to information flow, to autonomous driving, to large models, huge investments are made in each round, but many end up giving up halfway or being surpassed by competitors. This strategic swing reflects the company's uncertainty about what to do.

### 4.3 Current cultural change signals

From 2025 to 2026, Baidu will carry out intensive organizational structure adjustments:

- TPG is split into basic model department and application model department
- MEG established a "search and push integration" team
- New PSIG focuses on personal AI applications
- The rank system has been overhauled, with T/P/E merged into a numerical system for levels 5-12
- Introducing young backbones into key technical positions

These changes are in the right direction - more focused, flatter, and more focused on product implementation. However, the implementation effect still needs to be verified.

---

## 5. General evaluation of business model

### Core Contradiction

The core contradiction faced by Baidu is: **The cash flow of the old business (search advertising) is declining, and the investment in the new business (AI cloud/autonomous driving/large model applications) is increasing. However, when the new business can generate enough profits to replace the old business, the timetable is uncertain**.

### Positive factors

- Deep AI technology reserves and full-stack capabilities (chip + framework + model + application) are unique in China
- AI cloud business has strong growth and is competitive in the enterprise market
- Leading the world in commercialization of autonomous driving
- Net cash of RMB159 billion provides ample transition buffer

### Negative factors

- The search advertising base continues to shrink, declining for 6 consecutive quarters
- Surpassed by Byte and Alibaba in the C-side AI application competition
- The ability to transform technology accumulation into commercial realization is weak.
- Insufficient brand trust and network effects

### Comprehensive rating of business model: ★★★☆☆

Baidu is not a "simple and sustainable" business model. It is in the midst of a difficult transition from an old model to a new one, with the transition in the right direction but facing huge competitive pressures to execute. For investors, this means having to make accurate judgments about how quickly new business will grow and old business will decline—and that’s precisely what’s most difficult to predict.
