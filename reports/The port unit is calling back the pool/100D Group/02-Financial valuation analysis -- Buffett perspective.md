#Baidu Group: Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, return on capital, intrinsic value estimation, margin of safety
> Core philosophy: Buy great companies at reasonable prices rather than mediocre companies at cheap prices

---

## 1. Financial quality physical examination

### 1.1 Revenue Trend: Switching of Growth Engines

| Year | Total revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate | Remarks |
|------|--------------|---------|------|
| 2022 | 1237 | -0.7% | Impact of the epidemic |
| 2023 | 1346 | +8.8% | The initial appearance of recovery superposition AI |
| 2024 | 1331 | -1.1% | Search advertising declines sharply |
| 2025 | 1291 | -3.0% | AI growth fails to fully compensate for search decline |

Total revenue has declined for two consecutive years, which is a signal that cannot be ignored. Although the AI ​​business is growing rapidly (AI business revenue in 2025 will be approximately 40 billion, a year-on-year increase of 48%), search advertising is shrinking even faster. The online marketing business has declined for six consecutive quarters starting from Q2 in 2024, with Q3 falling 18% year-on-year in a single quarter.

**Key question: When will the growth of new AI businesses exceed the shrinkage of search advertising in total? **

Extrapolating based on current trends: If search advertising declines by 10-15% per year and AI business grows by 30-40% per year, it will take approximately 2027-2028 to achieve total revenue growth again. This means investors may have to endure 2-3 years of stagnant or even declining revenue.

### 1.2 Profit quality: the difference between appearance and substance

The full-year data for 2025 shows two sides:

- **GAAP Perspective**: dragged down by an impairment loss of 16.2 billion yuan on the core asset group, an operating loss of 5.8 billion yuan was recorded for the full year. This was primarily a one-time impairment, but the impairment itself reflected that certain business units were worth less than book value.
- **Non-GAAP perspective**: Net profit attributable to Baidu is 18.9 billion yuan, indicating that recurring business is still profitable.

Non-GAAP net profit in Q4 was 3.9 billion yuan in a single quarter and 18.9 billion yuan in the whole year, which means that the profitability of Q4 was lower than the average of the first three quarters (about 5 billion/quarter). This is partly because Q4 is the peak period for AI infrastructure investment.

### 1.3 Cash Flow: A Test of Real Money

Baidu's free cash flow has historically been relatively healthy, and operating cash flow in 2025 is estimated to be in the range of 20-25 billion yuan. However, continued growth in capital expenditures needs to be deducted - investment in AI infrastructure (computing centers, chip research and development, etc.) is accelerating.

**Positive**: Cash flow from operating activities remains positive and substantial.
**Negative**: Capital expenditures are on the rise and free cash flow rates may come under pressure.

## 2. Return on capital: Baidu’s biggest “flaw”

### 2.1 ROE analysis

| Annual | Approximate ROE | Evaluation |
|------|---------|------|
| 2022 | ~5% | Very low |
| 2023 | ~7% | Low |
| 2024 | ~4% | Very low (including impairment effects) |
| 2025 | ~3-5% | Very low (including impairment effects) |

ROE fluctuates between 3-7%, which is substandard for a technology company. For reference:

- Tencent ROE is about 20-25%
-Alibaba ROE is about 10-15%
- Google ROE is about 25-30%

There are two fundamental reasons for Baidu’s low ROE:

**Reason 1: Net profit margins have been compressed by high R&D expenditures**. Baidu invested more than 21 billion yuan in R&D in the first three quarters, accounting for more than 20% of revenue. A large amount of investment is flowing into directions that cannot be realized in the short term, such as autonomous driving and large models.

**Reason 2: There are a large number of inefficient assets on the asset side**. Net cash of 159 billion yuan accounts for about 40% of the company's market capitalization. The return generated by this cash lying on the account is far lower than the return rate on operating assets. If you calculate operating ROE after deducting net cash, the number will look much better (estimated to be in the 10-15% range), but this just illustrates a problem-management is not efficient in capital allocation.

### 2.2 DuPont Analysis Perspective

- **Net profit margin**: Non-GAAP caliber is about 14-15%, which is pretty good.
- **Asset turnover rate**: On the low side, a large amount of cash reduces asset efficiency
- **Equity Multiplier**: Low, debt ratio is not high, leverage is used conservatively

**ROE score: ★★☆☆☆** – This is the weakest link in Baidu’s investment case.

## 3. Balance sheet: hidden safety cushion

### 3.1 Net Cash Analysis

As of mid-2025, Baidu's net cash is approximately 155.1 billion yuan (cash and equivalents + short-term investments - total debt). It is expected to be approximately 159 billion yuan by the end of 2025.

This is an astonishing number. Baidu's current market value in Hong Kong is approximately HK$390 billion (approximately RMB 360 billion), and net cash of RMB 159 billion means:

- **Net cash accounts for approximately 44% of market capitalization**
- After excluding net cash, the market’s pricing for Baidu’s operating business is only about 200 billion yuan
- Corresponding to Non-GAAP net profit of 18.9 billion yuan, the implied PE of operating business is only about 10.6 times

This net cash size provides a significant safety cushion. Even if the business outlook is uncertain, the net cash of 159 billion itself constitutes support for the lower limit of value.

### 3.2 Investment assets

Baidu also holds a range of investment assets, including:

- iQiyi (holding approximately 53% of shares): iQiyi’s current market value is approximately US$3 billion
- Ctrip shares: value to be confirmed
- Kunlun Core (valued at approximately 21 billion yuan before spin-off)
- Other strategic investments

The value of these investment assets may be in the range of 20-40 billion yuan, further thickening the safety cushion.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 PE Valuation Method

| Valuation method | Calculation | Conclusion |
|----------|------|------|
| Apparent PE (Non-GAAP) | 360 billion/18.9 billion ≈ 19 times | Moderately high |
| PE after net cash | 200 billion/18.9 billion ≈ 10.6 times | Low |
| Forward-looking PE (2026E profit 21.6 billion) | 200 billion/21.6 billion ≈ 9.3 times | Low |

If we only look at the apparent PE of about 19 times, it is not cheap for a company with declining revenue. However, after deducting net cash, it is only a little over 10 times, which is somewhat attractive considering the growth potential of the AI ​​business.

### 4.2 Segment valuation method (SOTP)

| iQIYI’s shareholding | Market value × shareholding | 100-120 |

The current market capitalization is approximately RMB 360 billion, which is at the low end of segment valuation. If the AI ​​cloud and autonomous driving business continue to maintain high growth, the upper limit of segment valuation may be 450-500 billion yuan.

### 4.3 DCF simple estimation

- Sustainable growth rate of 3% after 2030

## 5. Return to shareholders: belated but sincere

### 5.1 Buyback Plan

The US$5 billion buyback plan, accounting for about 10% of the market value, is very powerful among global technology companies. In comparison, Meta and Apple's repurchase efforts typically range from 2-5% of market capitalization. If implemented effectively, it will have a significant accretive effect on per share value.

### 5.2 Dividend Policy

The first dividend will be paid in 2026. Although the specific amount has not been determined, for a company with $159 billion in net cash, starting a dividend is the right direction.

### 5.3 Kunlun Core Spin-off

Kunlun Core launched dual listing guidance on the Science and Technology Innovation Board + Hong Kong stocks, with a valuation of approximately 21 billion yuan. A spin-off will unlock hidden value and provide Baidu with an additional source of capital.

**Shareholder return score: ★★★★☆** - With strong buyback efforts and the launch of dividend policy, the spin-off of Kunlun Core is expected to release value.

## 6. General evaluation of financial valuation

### Core conclusion

Baidu's financial situation presents a contradiction: **The asset side is very strong (net cash 159 billion + investment assets), but profitability is weak (ROE only 3-7%)**.

To use a metaphor: Baidu is like a middle-aged man with a lot of cash. His past main business income is declining, and new skills are being cultivated but have not yet been fully realized. He does not lack money to survive, but whether he can find a high-paying job (a high-ROE business) again determines his long-term value.

### Positive factors

- Net cash of RMB159 billion provides a safety cushion of approximately 44% of market capitalization
- Implied PE after deducting net cash is just over 10 times
- US$5 billion in buybacks + first dividend, clear improvement in shareholder returns
- AI cloud business grew by 34% and is expected to become a new profit engine
- The spin-off of Kunlun Core is expected to release more than 20 billion in value

### Negative factors

- ROE is only 3-7%, return on capital is extremely low
- Total revenue has declined for two consecutive years, and earnings growth prospects are uncertain
- High R&D investment (more than 20 billion per year) has a long return cycle
- GAAP profits fluctuate greatly due to impairment
- Free cash flow may be under pressure due to investment in AI infrastructure

### Comprehensive score of financial valuation: ★★★☆☆

The current valuation is inexpensive and net cash provides a safety cushion, but low ROE and declining revenue trends are substantial risks. Investors are essentially betting that AI transformation will significantly improve profitability and return on capital within 2-3 years - this is not a high-certainty bet.
