# Baidu Group: Industry Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: competitive landscape, industry trends, multiple thinking models, reverse thinking
> Core philosophy: Think the other way around, always think the other way around. When understanding the competitive landscape, look at who will lose, not just who will win

---

## Panorama of competition on one and three fronts

Baidu is fighting on three battlefields at the same time: search/information services, AI cloud/large models, and autonomous driving. This in itself is food for thought—a company fighting three battles at once rarely wins every one of them.

---

## 2. The first front: search and information services

### 2.1 The industry is being redefined

The boundaries of the traditional search engine industry have been blurred. The way users obtain information has evolved from "people looking for information" (search box) to:

- **Short Video Recommendation**: Douyin’s average daily searches are 5.3 billion, 17 times that of Baidu
- **Social Search**: WeChat searches exceed 1 billion times per day
- **Community Search**: Xiaohongshu averages 600 million times a day, with an annual growth rate of over 100%
- **AI dialogue**: ChatGPT/Doubao/Qianwen and other AI assistants answer questions directly

The average daily search volume of Baidu App is only 300 million times, and the PC market share is 30.15% (even lower than Bing’s 50.99%). This is not a battle for market share, but the decline of the entire category.

### 2.2 Competitor Analysis

| Competitors | Advantages | Threat to Baidu |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Douyin/Byte | User duration, content ecology, recommendation algorithm | ★★★★★ The biggest threat |
| WeChat search | Social relationship chain, closed-loop ecology | ★★★★☆ |
| Xiaohongshu | Consumption decision-making scenarios, young users | ★★★☆☆ |
| AI search (Perplexity, etc.) | Give answers directly, no need to click on links | ★★★★☆ Long-term threats |

### 2.3 Baidu’s AI search counterattack

Baidu has reconstructed more than 20% of its search results pages with generative AI and launched AI-native marketing services (revenue of 2.8 billion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 262%). But this faces a fundamental contradiction:

**The better the AI search is, the less users need to click on advertising links**. Generative AI directly gives answers, which naturally conflicts with the business model of bidding ranking. This means that even if Baidu is technologically leading in AI search, it may still face the dilemma of "self-subversion".

**Search Front Rating: ★★☆☆☆** - This is a losing war, and the AI search transformation may delay but cannot reverse the trend.

## 3. The second front: AI cloud and large models

### 3.1 Industry Structure

China's AI cloud market is Baidu's most competitive battlefield. But the competition is also the fiercest:

| Manufacturer | AI Cloud Positioning | Large Model | Differentiation Advantages |
|------|---------|--------|-----------|
| Alibaba Cloud | Integrated cloud + AI | Tongyi Qianwen | The largest cloud infrastructure and the most complete ecosystem |
| Huawei Cloud | Government and enterprise market | Pangu | Shengteng chips are independently controllable |
| Baidu Intelligent Cloud | AI-first | Wenxin | No. 1 in AI large model solution share |
| Byte Volcano Engine | Internet Scenario | Doubao | C-side traffic feeds back to B-side |
| Tencent Cloud | Social + Game Scenario | Hunyuan | WeChat Ecological Entrance |

### 3.2 Baidu’s ranking

Baidu Intelligent Cloud ranks first in the field of AI large model solutions with a market share of 16.6%, but only ranks fourth or fifth in the overall cloud computing market. This shows that Baidu has an advantage in the vertical segmentation of AI, but its comprehensive cloud capabilities are not as good as Alibaba and Huawei.

The growth of AI cloud is certain (year-on-year growth of 34% in 2025, Q4 computing power subscription revenue growth of 143%), but the market structure is far from stable:

- Alibaba Cloud's Qwen ranks first in the average daily calls of enterprise-level large models with 32.1% share
- Huawei Ascend accounts for 23% of domestic computing power replacement
- The user data and scene understanding accumulated by ByteDoubao on the C side can feed back to the B side

### 3.3 Failure of large model application layer

An uncomfortable fact: Baidu has fallen behind in the competition at the AI application layer.

Global AI application MAU ranking in February 2026:
1. ChatGPT: 956 million
2. Beanbao (bytes): 315 million
3. Qianwen (Alibaba): 203 million
4. Wen Xin Yi Yan (Baidu): about 200 million

Baidu Wenxinyiyan’s MAU is about 200 million, lagging behind Doubao and Qianwen. What is even more worrying is the growth trend - Doubao's daily active users have exceeded 100 million, with a growth rate of 87.38%, while Baidu Netdisk MAU fell by 23.4% and Baidu Wenku fell by 10.06%.

Some analysts pointed out that the competition for AI native applications "has become a game between Byte, Alibaba and Tencent" - Baidu is not even included in the first echelon. This is a serious problem for a company whose life is based on AI.

**AI cloud/large model front score: ★★★☆☆** - The enterprise level has advantages but is not strong, and C-side applications have fallen behind.

## 4. The third front: autonomous driving

### 4.1 Current status of the industry

Autonomous driving is Baidu’s biggest and longest bet. Globally, the Robotaxi industry is still in the early stages of commercialization, with major players including:

| Company | Operating City | Stage | Valuation/Market Cap |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| Waymo (Alphabet) | Multiple cities in the United States | Large-scale operations | Latest financing valuation exceeds US$45 billion |
| Luobo Kuaipao (Baidu) | 26 cities in China + overseas | Large-scale operation | Not separately valued |
| Tesla FSD | Global | Assisted Driving → Robotaxi | Implied in Tesla’s market capitalization |
| Pony.ai | China + Middle East | Early commercialization | Listed market value approximately US$3 billion |
| WeRide | China + Overseas | Early Commercialization | Listed with a market value of approximately US$3 billion |

### 4.2 The competitive advantage of Carrot Run

The data of Carrot Run is real:

- 3.4 million travel services will be completed in Q4 of 2025, a year-on-year increase of 209%
- Cumulative services exceed 20 million times
- Access to 26 cities around the world (including overseas: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Seoul)
- From February 2025, 100% fully driverless operation will be achieved in China
- Wuhan has achieved bicycle breakeven
- Obtained Dubai's first autonomous driving test license

In the global Robotaxi field, Baidu and Waymo are tied for the first echelon. Robin Li claimed that the cumulative number of orders placed by Carrot Kuaishou has exceeded that of Waymo.

### 4.3 Profit dilemma and long-term prospects

However, the commercialization of autonomous driving faces structural challenges:

- **Single city profit to overall profit**: Wuhan bicycle breakeven is good news, but Wuhan is one of the cities with the most supportive policies. Replicating this model in other cities will take time and policy coordination.
- **Capital Intensive**: Although the cost of each autonomous vehicle is declining, large-scale deployment still requires huge amounts of capital.
- **Policy Risk**: Regulatory policies for autonomous driving vary greatly from place to place and may be tightened due to safety incidents.
- **Intensified competition**: Tesla is accelerating the promotion of driverless taxis, and Pony.ai and WeRide are also expanding in the Chinese market.

**Autonomous driving front score: ★★★★☆** - The global leadership position is clear, but there is still a distance from commercialization to full profitability.

## 5. Chip Front: Potential and Challenges of Kunlun Core

### 5.1 Market structure

China GPU/AI chip market (2025):
- NVIDIA: 70% share
- Huawei Ascend: 23%
-Kunlun core: ranked third in China

Kunlun Core's revenue will exceed 1 billion yuan in 2024 and is expected to reach 5 billion yuan in 2025. JP Morgan predicts that it will reach 8.3 billion yuan in 2026. This growth rate is very fast, but the volume is still small.

### 5.2 The significance of spin-off listing

Kunlun Core launched dual listings on the Science and Technology Innovation Board + Hong Kong stocks, with the latest valuation of about 21 billion yuan. The significance of the spin-off is:

- Standalone valuation may be higher than current implied value within Baidu Group
- Attract professional investors in the semiconductor field
- Provide additional funding to Baidu

However, it should be noted that in the context of the U.S.’s chip export controls to China, the development of domestic AI chips not only benefits from the policy dividend of “domestic substitution”, but also faces the challenge of technological generation gap.

**Chip Front Rating: ★★★☆☆** - Fast growth but small size, ranking low in the market structure.

## 6. Reverse thinking: Under what circumstances will Baidu lose?

Using reverse thinking, the biggest risk scenarios include:

**Scenario 1: Search advertising collapses faster than expected**. If search advertising revenue declines by more than 20% per year (instead of 10-15%), the growth of new AI businesses may not be able to make up for it, causing total profits to continue to deteriorate.

**Scenario 2: AI cloud market share is squeezed**. If Alibaba Cloud and Huawei Cloud take away Baidu's AI cloud customers with their greater ecological advantages, Baidu's leading position in the enterprise market may not be sustainable.

**Scenario 3: Autonomous driving will not be able to make large profits in the long term**. If regulatory restrictions and intensified competition prevent Luobo Kuaipao from being profitable in more cities, it will become a black hole that continues to consume cash.

**Scenario 4: The battle for the C-side AI entrance is completely defeated**. If Doubao and Qianwen continue to expand their leading advantages, Baidu may lose its C-end entrance position in the AI ​​era, thus affecting the competitiveness of its entire AI ecosystem.

## 7. Overall review of industry competition

### Core judgment

Baidu is in an awkward position: ** has certain competitiveness in every battlefield, but it is not the strongest ** in every battlefield.

- Search: Behind Douyin and WeChat
- AI Cloud: Leading in the enterprise segment, but lagging behind Alibaba and Huawei in comprehensive cloud capabilities
- Large model application: behind Byte Doubao and Alibaba Qianwen
- Autonomous driving: leading the world, but commercialization is still early
- AI chip: third in China, very small in size

This pattern of "doing everything, loosening everything" is exactly what we need to be most vigilant about in investment. It is difficult for a company to win in multiple battlefields at the same time, especially when each battlefield has opponents with richer resources than itself.

### Positive factors

- The world's first echelon of autonomous driving, leading in commercialization progress
- AI cloud temporarily leads the enterprise solution market
- Full-stack AI capabilities (chip + framework + model + application) are unique in China
- AI native marketing has strong growth rate (262%)

### Negative factors

- The search base is being cannibalized by multiple platforms
- Being left behind by Byte and Alibaba in the C-end AI application competition
- Face opponents with greater resources on every battlefield
- Technology leadership may not necessarily translate into product and market share leadership

### Comprehensive rating of industry competition: ★★★☆☆

Baidu is a strong player in terms of technology, but it faces severe challenges in terms of products and market. Autonomous driving is the area most likely to establish lasting advantages, but has the longest realization cycle. AI cloud is the most realistic growth engine in the short term, but the competitive landscape is unstable. The decline of search advertising is the most certain trend.
