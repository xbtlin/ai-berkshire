# III. Risk analysis

#3.1 Operational risk

** Risk I: Search advertising decline accelerated (probability: high)**

The search for advertising revenue has declined for six consecutive quarters, with a 18% decline in the year-on-year rate of Q3 in 2025. If the recession accelerates to over 20% annually, it will put a substantial strain on 100% cash flow.

Trigger: The share of dithered/micro-scanning further encroaches; the advertising mode is not working after the AI search is popular.

** Risk II: AI cloud competition share squeezed (probability: medium)**

100 degrees are now leading the AI model solution market, but Aliun (a few asked about the 32.1% call by business) and Hwa-chan are fast catching up. There is uncertainty about whether the share of the medium- and long-term will be sustained without Ali’s eco- and Chinese-oriented political relations.

Triggers: Ali cloud price reduction strategy; China's ecological expansion; and the pervasiveness of corporate client cloudy strategies.

** Risk three: Automated driving policy tightening (probability: medium and low)**

The regulatory environment for autopilots is generally liberalized, but policy may shift quickly in the event of a major security incident. The 2024 Mayan carrot run triggered protests by Internet drivers, and policy sensitivities persist.

Triggers: major security incidents; change in attitude of local governments; policy interventions under employment pressure.

** Risk 4: Large models are losing their game (probability: medium and high)**

The MAU is worth about 200 million, but the speed of the increase has been lost by the bean bag (315 million, 87% faster) and the Quick Start. If 100% of the competition is marginalized at the C-EAL entry, it will affect the competitiveness and liquidity of the entire AI ecology.

Triggers: Byte bean packs continue to grow at high rates; open source models (DeepSeek, etc.) are technical barriers to lowering the 100%; new phenomenon-level AI applications are emerging and do not come from 100%.

# Four: A certainty analysis: What can be trusted?

Among the many uncertainties, search for credible certainty factors:

##4.1 High-determinate factors

** Net cash is real**. Whatever business prospects, this cash constitutes a lower limit on value. Even if all AI investments fail, 100% of cash and investment assets can support a significant portion of the current market value.

** Search ads will continue to decline ** It is not cyclical, but structural change. The trend is irreversible in the distribution of mobile Internet information from search to recommendation.

The Chinese firm AI has just begun to land, and the demand for AI's computing power will continue to grow at a high rate in the next three to five years. The percentage of the centile share is uncertain, but industry growth is in itself certain.

**The $5 billion buyback plan has a high degree of credibility**. The board has approved it and has sufficient cash to implement it. This will significantly increase the value per share over the next three years.

##4.2 Moderately determinative factors

** Autopilots are gradually commercialized**. Technology has reached a level of availability, policy is gradually being liberalized, but the pace and scale are uncertain.

** 100 degrees of ACI technical capability is in the top three** of the country. Although competition has lost at the application level, it is still one of the strongest in China in terms of modeling capacity and total layout.

##4.3 Low-determinate factors

** Whether AI operations will fully offset the downside of the search within 2-3 years** ** The timetable is uncertain.
** Whether ROE could be raised from 3 to 7 per cent to more than 15 per cent** - a fundamental improvement in the business model is needed.
** Whether management performance can be significantly enhanced** - Organizational change takes time to validate.

# VI. Risk management overview

# Core judgment

100% of management has the right strategic direction, but implementation has been poor. Current organizational change and improved capital allocation are positive signals, but it will take time to validate.

From a risk perspective, the biggest uncertainty is:** the race between the rate of decline in old business and the rate of growth of new business.** Net cash of $159 billion provides an adequate buffer, but it cannot hide indefinitely the lack of profitability.

# Positive factor

- Lee's A.I. strategy is correct for the long term.
- $5 billion buyback + first-time pie, improved shareholder payback concept

- Net cash provides sufficient security buffers.

# Negative factor
