## 2. Investment Discussion

### 2.1 Bullish discussion (reasons to be bullish)

**Net cash value is understated**. The net cash of 159 billion accounts for 44% of the market value. After deducting the net cash, the implied PE of the operating business is only 10.6 times. The market's pricing for Baidu's new AI business barely gives any premium - if you believe that the AI ​​business is worth tens of billions, then the current price is cheap.

**The turning point of AI business is approaching**. AI cloud revenue will grow by 34% in 2025, Q4 computing power subscription revenue will grow by 143%, and AI native marketing will grow by 262%. AI business’s total revenue has reached 43%, and is expected to exceed 50% in 2026. If this trend continues, Baidu's total revenue may grow again starting in 2027.

**Shareholder returns improved significantly**. US$5 billion repurchase (accounting for 10% of market value) + first dividend payment + Kunlun Core spin-off. Taken together, these three items may release more than 50 billion yuan in shareholder value in the next three years.

**The value of autonomous driving is ignored**. Luobo Kuairun ranks first in the world, with a total of 20 million trips, and Wuhan has broken even. With reference to Waymo's latest financing valuation of over US$45 billion, Luobo Kuaipao is worth more than US$20 billion even at a 50% discount - while Baidu's current overall market value is just over US$50 billion.

**What does it mean if the stock price rises 70% in one year**? The market has begun to re-recognize Baidu, but the revaluation may be far from over.

### 2.2 Short-selling argument (reasons for short-selling)

**The irreversible decline of search advertising**. It has declined for 6 consecutive quarters, with the latest single quarter -18% year-on-year. The search volume of Douyin is 17 times that of Baidu, and young users have become accustomed to not using search engines. This trend cannot be saved by AI - even AI search itself is contradictory to the advertising model.

**ROE is extremely low and difficult to improve in the short term**. An ROE of 3-7% means that the company cannot effectively convert assets into profits. The idle net cash of 159 billion is the superficial reason, and the deeper reason is that the profitability of the business model is not high. Even if the AI ​​business grows, high R&D investment (20 billion+ per year) will continue to compress profit margins.

**C-end AI application competition has been lost**. Wenxinyiyan lags behind Doubao and Qianwen, while Baidu Wenku and Wangpan MAU are declining. An AI company is marginalized in the competition at the AI ​​application layer. This is an extremely dangerous sign. Byte’s Doubao has over 100 million daily users, with a growth rate of 87%. Baidu’s gap on the C side may become wider and wider.

**Management execution is worrisome**. In the past 10 years, RMB 100 billion invested in R&D has not hatched a single phenomenal application; O2O has burned tens of billions in vain; the turnover rate of senior executives is 38%. Whether organizational change works is a matter of belief, not analysis.

**Valuation is not extremely cheap**. The apparent PE is 19 times, which is not cheap for a company with declining revenue. 10 times PE after deducting net cash may seem low, but if profits do not grow or even decline in the next few years, 10 times PE will not be attractive.

## 3. Key Assumptions and Scenario Analysis

### Scenario 1: Optimism (probability 25%) - AI transformation successful

- AI business will maintain a growth rate of 30%+ from 2026 to 2028
- The decline in search advertising is gradually narrowing (AI native marketing substitution effect is good)
- Carrot Run will be profitable in 10+ cities in 2027
- Kunlun Core's Hong Kong stock listing releases a valuation of 30 billion+
- ROE increased to above 10%

→ Target price in 2027: HK$220-260 (corresponding to about 50-80% of the current upside)

### Scenario 2: Neutral (50% probability) - transformation is slow but in the right direction

- AI business grows by 20-30%, but search advertising continues to decline by 10-15%
- Total revenue will be basically flat in 2026-2027
- Non-GAAP profit maintained at 20-22 billion yuan
- Buybacks and dividend payments are realized, and the value per share gradually increases
- ROE improved slightly to 6-8%

→ Target price in 2027: HK$150-180 (corresponding to the current upside of approximately 5-25%)

### Scenario 3: Pessimism (25% probability) - transformation blocked

- The decline of search advertising is accelerating (-20% or more per year)
- AI cloud market share is squeezed by Alibaba and Huawei
- Large model competition falls further behind
- Autonomous driving is slowly advancing due to policy or competition
- ROE remains at a low level of 3-5%

→ Target price in 2027: HK$90-120 (corresponding to the current downside risk of approximately 15-35%)

## 4. Comparison with investment principles

### 4.1 Is it a good company?

Baidu is a company with top-level technology accumulation, but it is not a "good company" in the traditional sense:
- ROE too low
- The moat is receding
- Management's execution ability is not strong enough
-Business models are undergoing transformation and uncertainty is high

If we strictly follow the standards of a "good company" (high ROE, strong moat, excellent management, simple and sustainable business model), Baidu currently does not meet the standards.

### 4.2 Is it a good price?

From an asset perspective, the current price has a certain margin of safety:
- Net cash of 159 billion accounts for 44% of market capitalization
- PE after deducting net cash is about 10 times
- $5 billion buybacks provide buying support

But from a profit perspective, if profits do not grow in the next 2-3 years, the current price is only "reasonable" rather than "extremely cheap."

### 4.3 Do you understand?

Baidu's business portfolio is highly complex - search advertising, AI cloud, autonomous driving, large model applications, AI chips - each direction requires independent judgment. This is not a "simple" investment target.

## 5. Final conclusion

### Investment advice: Observation/Hold small positions

Baidu is currently in a state where the valuation is reasonable but the certainty is not high. Net cash provides significant downside protection, the growth trends in the AI ​​business are real, and management's attitude toward rewarding shareholders is clearly improving. However, low ROE, the structural decline of search advertising, and the competitive disadvantage of C-end AI applications are all substantial risks.

**Suitable investor profile**:
- Able to accept uncertainty for 2-3 years
- Optimistic about the long-term development of China’s AI industry
- Think net cash + buybacks provide adequate safety cushion
- Have some confidence in management's improvement trends

**Unsuitable investor portrait**:
- Pursue high certainty and stable growth
- Value high ROE and strong moat
- Strict requirements on management's execution ability
-Shorter investment horizon

### Key indicators that need to be continuously tracked

1. **Online marketing revenue year-on-year changes** - whether the decline rate of search advertising has slowed down
2. **AI cloud business quarterly revenue and growth rate** - whether it can maintain 30%+ growth
3. **Luobo Kuaipao monthly order volume and number of profitable cities** - Commercialization progress
4. **Wen Xinyiyan/Baidu AI Application MAU**——C-side competitiveness
5. **Repurchase execution progress** - whether the promise is fulfilled
6. **Non-GAAP profit trend** – whether profitability has bottomed out and recovered
7. **Kunlun core listing progress** - the rhythm of value release

### Conditions for triggering additional positions

- The stock price fell below HK$120 (PE is about 7-8 times after net cash)
- AI cloud quarterly revenue exceeded 10 billion yuan
- Carrot Run is profitable in 5+ cities
- Total revenue returned to year-on-year growth

### Conditions that trigger position reduction/sell

- Search advertising fell more than 25% year-on-year quarterly
- AI cloud market share fell out of the top three
- The execution of the buyback plan was significantly lower than expected
- Major personnel turmoil in management

## 6. One sentence summary

> Baidu is a company with excellent AI technology reserves and a huge cash safety cushion. It is in the midst of a difficult but right-directed transformation process. The current valuation provides a certain margin of safety, but the low ROE and execution uncertainty under fierce competition make it more suitable as a moderate allocation of the AI ​​theme rather than a heavy position.
