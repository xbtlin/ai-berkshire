## Research background

Baidu is at a key strategic turning point: its traditional search advertising business continues to decline, and new AI businesses (intelligent cloud, autonomous driving, large models) are growing rapidly but have not yet completely filled the gap. The company has net cash of 159 billion, launched a US$5 billion buyback plan and first dividend policy, and promoted the spin-off and listing of Kunlun Core.

The core contradiction is: this is a company with top-level AI technology reserves, but it has been surpassed by Byte and Alibaba in the C-end application competition, its search base has been cannibalized by Douyin/WeChat/Xiaohongshu, and its ROE has been sluggish for a long time. What investors need to judge is whether the AI ​​transformation can be realized into sustainable profit growth in the next 2-3 years.

## Quick overview of key data

**Business structure (2025):**
- Online marketing services (search advertising): approximately 77 billion yuan, down approximately 15% year-on-year
- AI cloud business: approximately 28 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 34%
- Autonomous driving and other AI: about 12 billion yuan
- Total revenue from AI business accounted for 43% in Q4

**AI Competition Ranking (May 2026):**
- AI large model application: Wenxinyiyan has about 200 million MAU, lagging behind Doubao (315 million) and Qianwen (203 million)
- AI cloud market: Baidu Intelligent Cloud ranks first in AI large model solutions with a 16.6% share
- Autonomous driving: Luobo Kuaipao’s cumulative orders exceed 20 million, ranking first in the world
- AI chip: Kunlun Core ranks third in domestic production (after Huawei’s ascension), launching dual listings on the Science and Technology Innovation Board + Hong Kong stocks

**Shareholder Return:**
- USD 5 billion buyback plan (approximately 10% of market capitalization), valid until the end of 2028
- First dividend payment in 2026
- Kunlun Core was spun off and listed (valued at approximately 21 billion yuan)
