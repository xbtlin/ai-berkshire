## 3. Key investment assumptions

The investment judgment in this study is based on the following assumptions. If any assumption is falsified, it needs to be re-evaluated:

### The core assumption of bullishness

1. **Milwaukee’s brand power continues to increase**: Maintain mid-to-high single-digit growth in the next 3-5 years, and the brand’s mind share among professional users will not be eroded
2. **Gross profit margin continues to increase slightly**: from 41.2% to 43-45%, driven by brand premium and product mix optimization
3. **Tariff impact is controllable**: The strategy of transferring production capacity to Vietnam and Mexico is effectively implemented, and the net impact of tariffs is controlled within 50-100 basis points of profit margins.
4. **New CEOs don’t make big mistakes**: Steven Richman at least maintains the strategic direction and execution standards established by his predecessor
5. **Industry structure remains stable**: The oligarchy pattern will not be subverted, and Chinese brands will not be able to pose a substantial threat to the North American professional market in the short term.

### Reversal signals to be wary of

1. **Milwaukee’s growth rate fell below 3% for two consecutive quarters**—which may mean that the brand power is declining or the market is peaking
2. **Gross profit margin has declined for two consecutive quarters** - This may mean a loss of pricing power or a larger-than-expected tariff impact
3. **Free cash flow shrinks significantly to less than $800 million** - may mean inventory accumulation or out-of-control capital expenditures
4. **Massive selling of shares by management** – could mean insiders are losing confidence in prospects
5. **Home Depot terminates exclusive agreement with Ryobi** - will directly impact the fundamentals of the consumer business

## 4. Comparative perspective with other Hong Kong stocks

In the "recall pool" of Hong Kong stocks, the unique value of Chuangke Industrial lies in:

- **Highest degree of globalization**: 75% of revenue comes from North America. It is a truly global consumer industrial brand, rather than relying on the "overseas concept" of the Chinese market.
- **Best profit quality**: 41% gross profit margin, 20% ROE, net cash, almost no rivals in the Hong Kong stock manufacturing sector
- **Highest certainty**: Power tool demand is tied to infrastructure maintenance and home renovation, making it more resilient than consumer electronics.
- **But the valuation is also the most sufficient**: The market has long recognized the high quality of TTI, and 23 times PE is a premium level in the Hong Kong stock manufacturing industry.

## 5. Final conclusion

Chuangke Industrial is a company that I am willing to hold for the long term. It has one of the best competitive positions I've ever seen in manufacturing - the right industry, the right brand strategy, the right platform model, the right financial discipline. Milwaukee's battery ecological lock-in effect is its core competitive advantage. This advantage is strengthened every time a new tool is sold, forming a positive feedback loop.

But a good company does not equal a good investment. The current price of HK$124.5 basically reflects the company's intrinsic value, and the margin of safety is insufficient. Considering the uncertainty of tariffs, the need for observation during the CEO handover period, and the fragility of the global macro environment, I tend to remain patient and seriously consider opening a position below HK$100**.

If the following scenarios occur, you can take action in advance:
- The tariff policy is clear and its impact is controllable → Build a light position at HKD 110-120
- Macro panic leads to a systematic undervaluation of Hong Kong stocks → Build a mid-term position at HKD 90-100
- Economic recession + tariffs double-kill profits → Build heavy positions below HK$80

**Summary in one sentence: Good company confirmation, good price still needs to wait. Put it on the watch list and wait for the wind to come. **
