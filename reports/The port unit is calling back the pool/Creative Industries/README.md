## Core conclusion

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

Chuangke Industrial is one of the rarest global manufacturing leaders in Hong Kong stocks. The Milwaukee brand is positioned in the professional market, forming a flywheel effect of "battery platform locking + continuous new product expansion"; Ryobi covers the consumer market and has grown steadily through Home Depot's exclusive channel. The company's revenue in 2025 is US$15.3 billion, free cash flow is US$1.4 billion, net cash is US$700 million, and the balance sheet is extremely healthy. Gross profit margin has steadily increased from 35.7% in 2015 to 41.2% in 2025, reflecting the continued enhancement of the brand's premium capabilities.

The main risks are: (1) Uncertainty in U.S. tariff policy. Although the transfer of production capacity for China's exports to the United States has basically been completed, the situation is still changing; (2) The impact of cyclical fluctuations in the U.S. housing market on demand; (3) New CEO Steven Richman has been in office for less than two years, and his execution ability needs to be observed.

The current stock price is approximately HK$124.5 (May 2026), corresponding to a PE of approximately 23 times in 2025. Considering the company's leading position, continued margin improvement and strong free cash flow, the valuation is reasonable but far from cheap. It is recommended to wait and see for better prices, or actively buy below HK$100**.

**Investment advice: wait and see (waiting for safety margin)**
