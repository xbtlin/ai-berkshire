## 3. Intrinsic value estimation

### 3.1 Discounted free cash flow method (DCF)

**Basic Assumptions**:

| Assumptions | Conservative | Neutral | Optimistic |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Revenue CAGR in the next 5 years | 4% | 6% | 8% |
| Terminal growth rate | 2.5% | 3% | 3% |
| Free Cash Flow Rate | 8.5% | 9% | 10% |
| Discount rate (WACC) | 9% | 8.5% | 8% |

**Conservative Scenario** (4% growth + 8.5% FCF rate):
- Free cash flow from 2026 to 2030: approximately US$1.40 → US$1.66 billion
- Terminal value: approximately US$28 billion
- Intrinsic value: approximately US$34 billion
- Equivalent to approximately **HK$186** per share

**Neutral Scenario** (6% growth + 9% FCF rate):
- Free cash flow from 2026 to 2030: approximately US$1.49 → US$1.93 billion
- Terminal value: approximately US$38 billion
- Intrinsic value: approximately US$44 billion
- Equivalent to approximately **HK$241** per share

**Optimistic scenario** (8% growth + 10% FCF rate):
- Free cash flow from 2026 to 2030: approximately US$1.68 → US$2.33 billion
- Terminal value: approximately US$52 billion
- Intrinsic value: approximately US$58 billion
- Equivalent to approximately **317 HKD per share**

> Note: The above estimate is based on a total share capital of approximately 1.83 billion shares, and the Hong Kong dollar/US dollar exchange rate is calculated at 7.8. Estimates are for reference only and are highly sensitive to assumptions.

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### 4.1 Implicit assumptions in current valuations

The implicit assumption of the current stock price of HK$124.5 (market value of approximately HK$228 billion) is:
- Maintain annual revenue growth of 6-7% in the future
- Margins continue to improve slightly
- No major tariff shock or economic recession

These assumptions are reasonable but not conservative.

### 4.2 Safety margin calculation

| Scenario | Intrinsic value (HKD) | Current share price | Margin of safety |
|------|-----------------|----------|----------|
| Conservative DCF | 186 | 124.5 | +49% (with room) |
| Neutral DCF | 241 | 124.5 | +94% (Abundant) |
| PE 20 times (conservative) | 102 | 124.5 | -18% (overvalued) |
| PE 25 times (reasonable) | 128 | 124.5 | +3% (basically reasonable) |

**Key Judgment**:
- If you believe DCF analysis, the current price has sufficient margin of safety
- If you pay more attention to short-term PE valuation, the current price is in the "reasonable but not cheap" range
- If the economic recession causes profits to fall by 20%, 23 times PE will become 29 times, indicating significant short-term pressure

## 5. Dividends and shareholder returns

### 5.1 Dividend history

The company maintains a stable dividend policy, with a full-year dividend of HK$2.57 per share in 2025, with a dividend rate of approximately 2.1%. Considering that the company is still in the growth stage, this dividend ratio is reasonable - it not only gives shareholders a certain cash return, but also retains sufficient funds for research and development and expansion.

### 5.2 Buyback Plan

The 18-month US$500 million buyback plan announced in March 2026 represents approximately 2.2% of the current market capitalization. This is management's first large-scale buyback and is a positive sign - indicating that management believes the current share price is attractive.

## 6. Conclusion

**Financial Valuation Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

TTI's financial performance is textbook level: its 10-year revenue CAGR exceeds 11%, gross profit margin continues to increase by 550 basis points, ROE remains at 19-20% with almost no leverage, free cash flow is abundant and it is in a net cash position. This is a coveted financial characteristic for value investors.

In terms of valuation, the current PE (2025) of 23 times is in the "reasonable but not cheap" range. DCF valuation shows considerable upside potential, but the safety margin is insufficient from a short-term PE perspective. Taking into account tariff uncertainty and macroeconomic risks, the ideal buying range is below HK$100 (corresponding to 20 times PE or lower), when the safety margin will be more abundant.

17 analysts unanimously agreed on "strong buy", with an average target price of HK$137.45 (source: Investing.com), with a maximum of HK$175.87 and a minimum of HK$109.64.
