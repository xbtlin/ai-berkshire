# China Resources Land (1109.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial health, earnings quality, balance sheet safety, valuation rationality
> Core question: Are the book numbers credible? Is there a sufficient margin of safety at current prices?

---

## 1. Profitability analysis: two curves of ice and fire

### 1.1 Overall profit overview

China Resources Land's financial statements in 2025 show a "split" state:

| Indicators | 2025 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|---------|
| Comprehensive turnover | 281.44 billion yuan | +0.9% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 25.42 billion yuan | -0.45% |
| Core net profit | 22.48 billion yuan | -11.4% |
| Core net profit from recurring operations | 11.65 billion yuan | +13.1% |
| Comprehensive gross profit margin | 21.2% | -0.4ppt |
| Development business gross profit margin | 15.5% | Continued decline |
| Investment property gross profit margin | 72.9% | Year-on-year increase |

**Key findings:** Core net profit fell by 11.4%, but net profit attributable to parent companies fell by only 0.45%. This gap mainly comes from "subsidies" for non-recurring items such as changes in the fair value of investment properties. Investors should focus on core net profit, as it more truly reflects the quality of operations.

### 1.2 Development business: "bleeding" of profit margins

The gross profit margin of the development and sales business has dropped from 42.9% in 2018 to 15.5% in 2025, a drop of 27.4 percentage points in seven years. This is not a problem unique to China Resources Land, but a common feature of the entire industry - land costs remain high, while selling prices are suppressed by regulation and market downturns.

What does a development gross profit margin of 15.5% mean? After deducting marketing expenses, administrative expenses, financial costs and taxes, the net profit margin of the development business is already very thin. If it falls further to 12-13%, the development business may be close to breakeven.

**This is one of the biggest financial risks:** Although the development business contributed 84.6% of revenue, its profit contribution has been less than half, and profit margins are still declining.

### 1.3 Recurring business: the real "profit engine"

Recurring business behaves very differently:

- **Shopping mall rental income is 21.9 billion yuan**, +13.3% year-on-year, and the operating profit margin is 63.1%, a record high
- **Revenue from operating real estate rental collection business was 25.44 billion yuan**, +9.2% year-on-year, and core net profit was 9.87 billion yuan, +15.2% year-on-year.
- The light asset management business (China Resources Vientiane Life) also maintained steady growth

The core net profit contribution from recurring businesses increased to 51.8%, which means that China Resources Land already has a "basic market" with high profit margins and stable growth.

## 2. Balance sheet analysis: Stable but not without worries

### 2.1 Debt structure

| Indicators | Values | Evaluation |
|------|------|------|
| Total loans | 281.47 billion yuan | Huge scale |
| Cash on hand | 116.99 billion yuan | Sufficient |
| Net debt ratio | 39.2% | Excellent level in the industry |
| Weighted financing cost | 2.72% | Best echelon in the industry |
| Asset-liability ratio after excluding advances received | 56.1% | Reasonable |

**Positive interpretation:** The net debt ratio of 39.2% is at a very healthy level in the real estate industry. The extremely low financing cost of 2.72% means that China Resources Land's financial cost burden is much smaller than that of its peers. With cash on hand of 117 billion yuan, we have no worries about short-term debt repayments.

**Prudent Interpretation:** Total borrowings reached 281.5 billion yuan, and the net debt ratio increased by 7.3 percentage points year-on-year. While the absolute level is still safe, the direction is worth paying attention to. If the net debt ratio continues to rise, it means that the expansion rate may exceed its own hematopoietic capacity.

### 2.2 Inventory quality

The book value of inventory properties was 398.09 billion yuan, a decrease of 9.8% from the beginning of the period. The management admits that the elimination of existing projects faces certain pressure.

Inventory is the largest "black box" in a real estate company's balance sheet. The key question is: How much can this 398.1 billion inventory be sold for at the current market price? If discounts and promotions need to be eliminated, profit margins will be further pressured.

### 2.3 Investment Property Value

China Resources Land holds a large number of investment properties (shopping malls, office buildings, etc.), which are carried on the books at fair value. Changes in the fair value of investment properties have a direct impact on the income statement.

There is a risk of an "accounting illusion": if investment property valuations are revised downwards, profits will be eroded; but if valuations are revised upwards, profits will be inflated. Investors should focus on core net profit (excluding changes in fair value) rather than paper net profit.

## 3. Cash flow analysis: focus on "real money"

The income statement of a real estate company is easily beautified by accounting treatments, and the cash flow statement is the mirror that shows the evil.

China Resources Land’s cash flow characteristics in 2025:
- Operating cash flow benefited from the steady growth of rental income and improved quality
- Development business repayment depends on sales progress and repayment rate
- Investment cash flow expenditure is relatively large - both land acquisition and shopping mall construction require large amounts of capital investment

**A contradiction worthy of attention:** China Resources Land is transforming into an asset-light company while investing heavily in new projects (equity land acquisition in the first half of 2025 +76% year-on-year). This shows that "asset-light" is still in its early stages and capital expenditures will not decrease in the short term.

### 4.2 Segment valuation ideas

The valuation dilemma of China Resources Land is that the market often uses the low valuation of real estate developers to price the entire company, but the value of recurring business is seriously underestimated.

If you do a simple segment valuation:

**Recurring Business Valuation:**
- Core net profit 11.65 billion yuan
- Refer to the valuation of REITs and give 15-20 times PE
- Valuation is approximately 174.8-233 billion yuan

**Development Business Valuation:**
- Core net profit is approximately 10.8 billion yuan (224.8-116.5)
- Give 5-6 times PE (reasonable valuation for real estate development)
- Valuation is approximately 54-64.8 billion yuan

**Total segment valuation:** Approximately 228.8-297.8 billion yuan (i.e. approximately 228.8-297.8 billion Hong Kong dollars)

The current market capitalization is approximately HK$273.8 billion, which is in the middle range of segment valuations. This means: **At the current price, the market has partially recognized the value of recurring operations, but has not yet given a REIT-level premium. **

### 4.3 Comparison with historical valuation

China Resources Land's stock price has increased by more than 100% since the low in September 2024. It recently hit a record high of HK$38.40 (May 11) and once approached HK$40. From an absolute valuation perspective, the current price is no longer "extremely undervalued."

Bank of America Securities gave a target price of HK$43, corresponding to approximately 8 times PE in 2026. If recurring business continues to grow and the REIT platform continues to expand, the long-term valuation center is expected to move higher.

### 4.4 Safety Margin Assessment

**Pros:**
- Dividend yield 4.7%, providing some downside protection
- High certainty of recurring business growth, providing valuation support
- Net debt ratio is 39.2%, and the balance sheet is safe

**Disadvantages:**
- The stock price has doubled, and the short-term margin of safety has narrowed
- The decline in development business profit margin has not yet bottomed out
- The absolute scale of total borrowings of 281.5 billion yuan cannot be ignored

## 5. Shareholder return analysis

### 5.1 Dividend policy

China Resources Land maintains a dividend payout ratio of 37% based on core net profit, with a full-year dividend of RMB 1.166 per share in 2025. The cumulative dividends in the past five years have exceeded HK$56 billion.

The dividend payout ratio of 37% is above average in the real estate industry. Considering that the company is still investing heavily in expansion (new shopping malls, acquiring land), it makes sense to retain 63% of profits for reinvestment.

### 5.2 Additional returns brought by REIT exit

The expansion of China Resources REIT platform will bring triple value to shareholders:
1. Realize revaluation when assets are injected into REITs
2. The withdrawn funds can be used for new investments or dividends
3. Management fee income increases profits from asset-light business

## 7. Core Conclusions

China Resources Land's financial statements tell a story of "two worlds": the financial performance of the recurring business is excellent - high profit margins, steady growth, and predictable cash flow; while the development business faces continued downward pressure on profit margins.

**The key judgment of valuation is:** What valuation system do you think the market should use to price China Resources Land?

- If you use the standards of real estate developers (5-6 times PE), the current price is too expensive
- If we use the standard of "development + asset operation" hybrid (8-10 times PE), the current price is reasonable
- If we use REIT/asset operator standards (15-20 times PE), the current price still has room for upside

China Resources Land's revaluation journey has begun (the share price doubled), but it is not over yet. Whether the valuation can be further improved in the future depends on whether the proportion of recurring business profits can continue to rise to 60% or even 70%. For investors pursuing a margin of safety, the current price needs to wait for a better buying opportunity; for investors who are optimistic about long-term transformation, the current valuation is still not expensive in a historical dimension.
