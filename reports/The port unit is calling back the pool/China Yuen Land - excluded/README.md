## Research background

China Resources Land is the most "REIT-like" target among the central real estate enterprises in Hong Kong. The company is transforming from a traditional residential developer to an "urban investment, development operator", and its recurring business profit contribution has exceeded 50%. During the period of deep adjustment in the industry, China Resources Land has demonstrated its ability to ride through cycles by relying on the credit of central enterprises, low financing costs, and a high-quality commercial asset portfolio. The PE is about 6-8 times, and the dividend rate is about 4.7%. It is one of the few targets in the real estate sector that has both growth and defensive properties.
