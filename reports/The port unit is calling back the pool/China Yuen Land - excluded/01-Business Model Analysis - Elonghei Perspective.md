# China Resources Land (1109.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business model essence, user value, moat, long-term sustainability
> Core question: What is the essence of this business? Why do users choose you? Is the moat widening?

---

## 1. The essence of business: paradigm shift from "selling houses" to "collecting rent"

China Resources Land is undergoing a profound business model transformation. To understand this company, you must first understand that it is changing from one kind of business to another.

**Old model - development and sales business:** Acquire land, build houses, and sell houses. This is a high-leverage, high-turnover, and strong-cyclical business. The profit margin depends on the timing of acquiring the land and the selling price. It is essentially a one-time transaction that "bets on the appreciation of land value". The core problem of this business is that every income requires a huge investment of capital to obtain it, and there is no compound interest effect.

**New model - recurring business:** Hold high-quality properties such as shopping malls and office buildings, and collect rent and management fees. This is a low-leverage, high-certainty, anti-cyclical business. Income is recurring and predictable, and as property quality improves and brand premiums accumulate, rents will naturally grow.

The key turning point in 2025: core net profit from recurring operations is 11.65 billion yuan, accounting for 51.8% of the total core net profit, exceeding half for the first time in history. This means that China Resources Land is no longer a company that "sells houses and collects rents", but a company that "collects rents and sells houses."

**Judgment of the nature of the business:** The real value of China Resources Land lies in its recurring business, especially the shopping mall combination with MixC/MIXC as its core. If the development business is regarded as a "cash flow generator" to feed the commercial real estate portfolio, the logic of this business model is self-consistent.

## 2. User value: "Quality Monopoly" of Vientiane Business

### 2.1 Value on the consumer side

Wanxiang City is positioned as a high-end shopping mall, while Wanxianghui is positioned as a regional shopping mall. As of the end of 2025, China Resources Land owns and operates 98 shopping malls, with retail sales reaching 239.2 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 22.4%.

This data is worth pondering: in the context of consumption downgrade, the retail sales of Vientiane shopping malls have increased significantly. What does this mean?

1. **Extremely strong site selection ability. ** Almost all of China Resources Land's shopping malls are located in the core areas of first- and second-tier cities, which naturally enjoys the advantage of foot traffic.
2. **Operation capabilities are verified. ** The annual rental income of Shenzhen Mixc City is 1.603 billion yuan and Shenyang Mixc City is 1.375 billion yuan. These figures show that the profitability of individual projects is extremely strong.
3. **Brand aggregation effect. ** High-end brands are willing to settle in Vientiane City, forming a positive cycle of "good brands attract good customer flow, and good customer flow attracts good brands".

### 2.2 The value of brand tenants

For luxury brands and high-end retailers, MixC is one of the preferred platforms to enter Chinese cities. The operating profit margin of shopping malls reached a record high of 63.1%, which shows that China Resources Land has a strong position in tenant negotiations - it can make tenants pay high rents while maintaining extremely high profit margins.

### 2.3 The value of urban areas

China Resources Land's "investment, development, construction and operation" integrated model (investment-development-construction-operation) is essentially a one-stop area development solution for cities. This gives it a unique advantage in land acquisition - local governments tend to hand over core land parcels to developers who can "do a good job in the entire area" rather than simply to the developer with the highest bid.

## 3. Moat analysis

### 3.1 Moat 1: Credit and financing cost advantages of central enterprises ★★★★★

The weighted average financing cost in 2025 is only 2.72%, a decrease of 39 basis points from the previous year. what does that mean?

- Financing costs for private real estate developers (if they can still raise funds) are usually above 6-10%
- China Resources Land’s financing costs are lower than those of many local urban investment companies
- In the spread game of "return on assets - financing cost", China Resources Land naturally occupies a huge advantage

This moat widened during the industry downturn. When private enterprises are hit by thunderstorms and financing channels tighten, the low-cost funding advantage of central enterprises becomes more prominent.

### 3.2 Moat 2: Commercial real estate operation capabilities ★★★★☆

Operational capabilities are a "soft moat" that cannot be clearly quantified like brand patents, but are extremely difficult to copy. The operational experience, brand resource accumulation, and membership system establishment of 98 self-owned shopping malls all take time to accumulate.

The annual rents of benchmark projects such as Shenzhen Mixc City and Hangzhou Mixc City continue to hit new highs, indicating that operating capabilities are constantly improving. The operating profit margin of the shopping center increased from approximately 55% to 63.1%, which also confirmed the results of scale effects and operational optimization.

But be careful: the operating capacity moat is not as wide as the financing cost moat. Competitors such as China Shipping and Longfor are also actively deploying commercial real estate, and the gap may narrow in the long run.

### 3.3 Moat 3: Land reserves and resource acquisition capabilities ★★★★☆

China Resources Land's equity land acquisition amount in the first half of 2025 increased by 76% year-on-year, and the proportion of investment in first- and second-tier cities was as high as 94%. During the period of industry contraction, most real estate companies were unable to acquire land. Instead, China Resources Land bucked the trend and expanded its high-quality land bank. This is a classic "be greedy when others are fearful".

The total land reserve is approximately 48.95 million square meters, including 7.76 million square meters for investment properties. This means that a large number of shopping malls will be opened in the future, and the growth pipeline of recurring revenue is clearly visible.

### 3.4 Moat 4: REIT platform and capital exit mechanism ★★★☆☆

China Resources Land has successfully issued China Resources Consumer REIT and completed the expansion. This builds an asset management closed loop of "investment-cultivation-injection into REIT-fund withdrawal-reinvestment".

The value of the REIT platform lies in:
- Provide exit channels for mature assets and release accumulated capital
- Retain operating rights to obtain management fee income
- Achieve separation of light and heavy assets and improve ROE

But this moat is still in its early stages. The scale of the REIT platform is limited, and it will take time to verify whether it can truly succeed in large-scale "asset-lightization". The implementation of the commercial real estate REIT pilot in 2026 will be an important catalyst.

## 4. Long-term sustainability of business model

### Positive factors

1. **The proportion of recurring revenue continues to increase. ** Increased from less than 30% to 51.8%, the trend is clear.
2. **The shopping mall has abundant pipelines. ** About 15 new commercial projects will enter the market in 2026, and the end target of the "15th Five-Year Plan" is 30 billion yuan in rental income from operating properties.
3. **Structural opportunities of urbanization. ** The supply of high-quality commercial space in China's first- and second-tier cities is still insufficient, and the trend of consumption upgrading has existed for a long time.
4. **REIT system dividends. ** The commercial real estate REIT pilot program is expanded to provide institutional guarantees for the realization of assets.

### Negative factors

1. **The development business is still huge. ** Development business revenue will still account for 84.6% of total revenue in 2025. Although the profit proportion has dropped to less than half, the transformation of the revenue structure is far from complete. The gross profit margin of the development business fell from 42.9% in 2018 to 15.5% in 2025, dragging down overall profits.
2. **E-commerce and changes in consumption habits. ** The impact of online shopping on physical commerce is long-term. Although high-end shopping malls are less affected, they cannot be ignored.
3. ** Heavy assets and slow returns. ** It usually takes 3-5 years for a shopping mall to mature from opening to maturity, and the initial investment is huge.
4. **The marginal benefits of scale expansion are diminishing. ** Core areas in first- and second-tier cities are limited, and as the layout approaches saturation, the quality of new projects may decline.

## 6. Core Conclusions

China Resources Land's business model is in a critical "transformation period." To sum it up in one sentence: **This is a company that is shifting from cyclical business to recurring business. The transformation direction is correct and the progress is substantial, but the tail of the old model is still very long. **

The most noteworthy signal is when the proportion of recurring business profits can stabilize above 60%. If the rental income target of RMB 30 billion can be achieved by the end of the "15th Five-Year Plan" and the gross profit margin of the development business stabilizes, then China Resources Land will truly complete its transformation from a "real estate developer" to an "urban asset operator" - this will be a qualitative change.

The core question that investors need to judge is: Do you believe that this transformation can be completed? If you believe it, the current valuation is an opportunity for you to buy "asset operators" at "developer prices".
