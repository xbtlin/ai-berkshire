## 2. Summary of investment thesis

### 2.1 Core investment logic

The investment value of China Resources Land does not lie in the cyclical game of "real estate recovery", but in the superposition of the following structural changes:

1. **Qualitative change in business structure. ** The proportion of core net profit from recurring business exceeded 50% (51.8%) for the first time, and the company is changing from a "developer" to an "asset operator".
2. **Industry concentration increases. ** With the liquidation of private enterprises and the dominance of central enterprises, China Resources Land's market share and pricing power are increasing.
3. **REIT system dividends. ** The commercial real estate REIT pilot program has been expanded to provide institutional support for China Resources Land's "big asset management" model.
4. ** Financing cost advantage. ** The weighted financing cost of 2.72% naturally dominates the interest rate spread game of "return on assets - financing cost".

### 2.2 Core Contradiction

**Contradiction 1: The high quality of new business vs. the drag of old business. ** The recurring business profit margin is 72.9%, which is excellent; but the development business gross profit margin is only 15.5% and is still declining. Investors are buying the "future", but the "present" books are still weighed down by old business.

**Contradiction 2: Revaluation vs Margin of Safety. ** The stock price has doubled from a low to HK$38.40 (a record high), and the market has begun to recognize the value of recurring business. However, for investors pursuing a margin of safety, the downside protection at the current price level is obviously insufficient.

**Contradiction 3: Asset-light strategy vs. asset-heavy expansion. ** While the company is shouting "asset-light", its equity land acquisitions have increased by 76% year-on-year. This shows that the transformation is still in its early stages, and in the short term it will still be a "heavy asset + high investment" model.

## 4. Objective evaluation of pros and cons

### Reasons to be bullish

1. **Recurring business growth is highly certain. ** 98 shopping malls are in operation, and about 15 new projects will enter the market in 2026. The growth pipeline of rental income is clearly visible.
2. **The valuation still has room for revaluation. ** If the proportion of recurring business profits rises to 60-70%, the market may give a higher valuation multiple (from 8 times PE to 12-15 times PE).
3. **Attractive shareholder returns. ** With a dividend yield of 4.7% + REIT exit dividend, even if the stock price does not rise, the return on holding is good.
4. **The credit of central enterprises is even more precious during the industry’s liquidation period. ** While its peers are in trouble, China Resources Land is gaining market share and high-quality land reserves.
5. **Policies are favorable. ** Policy directions such as the commercial real estate REIT pilot, urban renewal, and affordable housing are all beneficial to China Resources Land.
6. **Sales in the first four months of 2026 increased by 49.6% year-on-year. ** It shows that the market stabilization signal has appeared, and the company's sales side has taken the lead in recovering.

### Reasons to be bearish

1. **The stock price has doubled, and the margin of safety is insufficient. ** The increase has exceeded 100% from the low in September 2024 to now, and the risk of catching up at the current price is high.
2. **The profit margin of development business has not bottomed out. ** The gross profit margin of 15.5% may continue to decline, and 398.1 billion inventories are at risk of impairment.
3. **The total debt is 281.5 billion yuan. ** Although the net debt ratio is controllable, the absolute scale is huge, and changes in the interest rate environment or sales collections that are less than expected will bring pressure.
4. **Management changes frequently. ** The chairman and CFO will be replaced at the same time in 2025, and strategic continuity is questionable.
5. **The consumption environment is uncertain. ** With economic growth slowing down and residents’ income expectations declining, the high growth of high-end shopping malls may be unsustainable.
6. **The REIT market is immature. ** China's public REITs are still in the early stages of development, and there is uncertainty about whether the "big asset management" model can be successful.

## 7. Final conclusion

China Resources Land is one of the few "good companies" in China's real estate industry. Its strategic direction is correct (from development to operation), its competitive advantages are clear (central enterprise credit + operational capabilities + high-quality assets), and its financial fundamentals are solid (low debt ratio + low financing costs + high dividend rate).

But "good company" does not equal "good investment". A good investment also requires a "good price". The current stock price of HK$38.40 has reached a record high, which to a considerable extent reflects the market's recognition of the above advantages. Investors pursuing a margin of safety should wait for better buying opportunities.

**To summarize in one sentence: China Resources Land is the "right company", but it may not be the "right price" currently. Wait patiently for the market to give you a better opportunity. **
