## One sentence conclusion

CRRC is one of the 13 companies with the best balance of "growth certainty and valuation cost-effectiveness" - global rail transit monopoly + 100 billion breakthrough in new industries + overseas acceleration, with a PE of only 8-9 times. If the net interest rate can be increased from 4.8% to 6%+, it will usher in a "Davis double-click".

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. Ranked first in global rail transit equipment, with a market share of 50%+, unchallenged
2. The new industry exceeded 100 billion (+19.4%), and the second growth curve took shape
3. Overseas revenue increased by 23% and internationalization accelerated.
4. PE 8-9 times is obviously cheap for global leading + growth stocks
5. The trend of carbon neutrality is a long-term structural benefit
6. Newly signed orders amounted to RMB 346.1 billion, exceeding annual revenue. The future is guaranteed.

### Bearish logic
1. Net profit margin is only 4.8% - profit is as thin as a razor blade
2. The peak of domestic railway investment has passed
3. Overseas expansion is disrupted by geopolitics
4. Urban rail business fell by 7.4%
5. The governance efficiency of central enterprises needs to be improved
6. The hidden concern of increasing revenue without increasing profits (revenue +10.8% vs profit +6.4%)
