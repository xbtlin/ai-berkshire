# Analysis of business model of CRRC (1766.HK)

## 1. The essence of business

CRRC's core business is the manufacturing and sales of rail transit equipment** - high-speed rail EMUs, urban rail subway vehicles, locomotives, trucks, etc. It is the world's largest rail transit equipment manufacturer and is close to a monopoly in the Chinese market (market share is 90%+).

Important changes in recent years: New industry business (wind power equipment, new energy equipment, semiconductors, etc.) revenue exceeded 100 billion yuan, becoming the second growth curve.

From Duan Yongping's perspective, this is an "order-driven manufacturing industry" - the advantage is that it has technical barriers and scale advantages, but the disadvantage is that the profit margin is thin and it is highly dependent on a large customer (China Railway Group).

## 2. Moat assessment

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

### Powerful moat:
1. **Technical Barrier**: Only a few companies in the world have the design and manufacturing capabilities for high-speed rail EMUs (speed 350 km/h+)
2. **Scale Monopoly**: Domestic railway equipment market share is 90%+, with almost no competitors
3. **Customer locking**: China Railway Group is the largest customer, and the cooperative relationship is deep and stable
4. **Full industry chain capabilities**: full life cycle services from design → manufacturing → overhaul → maintenance
5. **Advantages of going overseas**: China’s high-speed rail brand power leads the world

### Moat defects:
1. Limited bargaining power - China Railway Group, the largest customer, is also a state-owned enterprise, and the purchase price is affected by policies.
2. Overseas markets are subject to geopolitical resistance - some countries exclude Chinese equipment due to security concerns
3. The nature of manufacturing determines the net profit rate ceiling (~5%)

## 3. Dual-engine growth structure

### The first engine: rail transit equipment (123.6 billion yuan, +11.9%)
- High-speed rail EMU: domestic demand is stable (maintenance of existing lines + construction of new lines)
- Urban rail vehicles: down 7.4% in 2025 (domestic subway approvals tightened)
- Overseas exports: strong growth (+22.88%), Southeast Asia/Middle East/Africa markets

### Second engine: new industries (103.1 billion yuan, +19.4%)
- Wind power equipment (blades, towers, etc.)
- New energy equipment
- Power semiconductors (IGBT, etc.)
- This sector exceeded 100 billion for the first time - marking that CRRC is no longer just a "train maker"

From the perspective of "growth sustainability" pursued by Duan Yongping, the 19.4% growth rate and RMB 100 billion scale of the new industry sector are positive signals - it proves that CRRC's engineering and manufacturing capabilities can be transferred to adjacent fields.

## 4. User value and differentiation

### Domestic market:
- China Railway Group is the "only customer" - CRRC has almost a monopoly on supply
- Product differentiation is not an issue (since there are no competitors)
- The key lies in whether it can continue to improve operational efficiency and reduce delivery costs

### Overseas markets:
- Competitors: Siemens, Alstom, Bombardier (acquired by Alstom)
- CRRC’s advantages: 30-50% lower price + fast delivery
- CRRC’s weaknesses: brand trust, geopolitical resistance, localization capabilities

## 5. Whether management is "doing the right thing"

### The right strategy:
1. Develop new industries as the second growth curve – avoid sole dependence on railways
2. Increase overseas market expansion—diversify geographical risks
3. Double revenue and net profit for three consecutive years - good execution
4. Autonomization of core components such as IGBT - improving technological content

### Doubts exist:
1. Net profit margin is only 4.8% - a common but disturbing problem in the manufacturing industry
2. Are the profit margins of new industries higher than those of traditional businesses?
3. Countermeasures for overseas expansion affected by geopolitical interference
4. Urban rail business fell by 7.4% - is it temporary or a trend?

## 6. Is it a good business?

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

From Duan Yongping’s standard:
- There are technical barriers and monopoly status ✓
- Growth potential (new industry + overseas) ✓
- But the net interest rate is low (4.8%) △
- Heavy asset manufacturing model △
- Highly dependent on big customers and policies △

It's "more interesting" than oil companies and operators - because there are real technical barriers and growth stories. However, thin profit margins limit its rating as a "good business."

## 7. Core Conclusions

CRRC is a "growing manufacturing leader" - global rail transit monopoly + new industry breakthroughs + overseas acceleration. PE 8-9x is cheap for this kind of growth. The core focus is: whether the profit margin of the new industry can be higher than that of the traditional equipment business, and whether overseas revenue can sustain high growth.
