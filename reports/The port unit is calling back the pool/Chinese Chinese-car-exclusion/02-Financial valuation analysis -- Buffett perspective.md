# CRRC (1766.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality

| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 273.1 billion yuan | 246.5 billion yuan | +10.8% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 13.18 billion yuan | 12.39 billion yuan | +6.4% |
| Net profit margin | 4.8% | 5.0% | Slight decrease |
| ROE | ~8% | ~7.5% | Improving |
| New orders signed | 346.1 billion yuan | - | Exceeded full-year revenue |

**Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

ROE of 8% is mid-range. Revenue growth of 10.8% is good, but net profit growth is only 6.4% (profit growth is slower than revenue), indicating that profit margins are declining. The net profit margin of 4.8% is typical for the manufacturing industry but on the low side.

### Order Coverage——Important Signal
New orders signed in 2025 will be 346.1 billion yuan, exceeding annual revenue of 273.1 billion yuan. The order/revenue ratio is >1.2 times, indicating that revenue growth is guaranteed in the next 1-2 years. This is a strong positive sign.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation
- PE (TTM): 8-9 times
- PB: about 0.7-0.8 times
- Dividend yield: about 3%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 6x

### Is 8-9 times PE reasonable?

Comparison:
- Revenue growth of 10.8% + overseas growth of 23%
- ROE 8% and improving
- New industries + overseas provide structural growth

PE 8-9 times is **low** for such growth. The market may give a discount because:
1. The overall valuation of the manufacturing industry is low
2. Valuation discount of central enterprises
3. Geopolitical uncertainty in overseas business
4. Net interest rate is low

### Valuation comparison of global peers
| Company | PE | ROE | Revenue Growth |
|------|------|------|----------|
| CRRC (1766) | 8-9x | 8% | 10.8% |
| Siemens Mobility | 15-20x | 12% | 5% |
| Alstom | 20-25x | 8% | 3% |

CRRC's valuation is only 1/2 to 1/3 of its European peers, but its growth rate is faster. Even taking into account the "China discount", there is still room for improvement in the current valuation.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**More confident. ** Long-term demand for rail transit (global urbanization + carbon neutrality drives rail investment). CRRC’s position as a global leader will not be shaken in 10 years.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Yes. **Technical barriers + scale monopoly + full industry chain capabilities form a lasting moat. Overseas competitors (Siemens + Alstom) will not be able to compete with CRRC on price after the merger.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
** Neutral to positive. ** Double growth for three consecutive years, new industries exceeding RMB 100 billion, and overseas expansion going smoothly.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**There is a certain margin of safety. **PE 8-9 times is low for global rail transit leaders + growth stocks. 0.7-0.8 times PB is not expensive either.

## 4. Growth value assessment

If the CRRC can maintain:
- Annual revenue growth of 8-10%
- Net profit margin maintained at 5%
- ROE gradually increased to 10%

Then the net profit will reach 17-18 billion yuan in three years. Based on 10 times PE valuation, the market value will be about 170-180 billion yuan, which has 40-50% upside compared to the current situation.

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

CRRC is one of the 13 companies with the "best balance between growth and valuation value"** - PE 8-9 times, revenue +10.8%, overseas +23%, new industry +19.4%. It does not have policy risks like Sands, nor does it have stagnant growth like banks, nor does it rely on weather like oil companies.

The core risk lies in low net interest rates - if new industries and overseas businesses can bring higher profit margins, ROE and valuation will increase simultaneously. This is a key assumption worth tracking closely.
