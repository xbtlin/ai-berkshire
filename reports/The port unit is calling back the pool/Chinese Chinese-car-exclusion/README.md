## Investment Highlights
1. Global leader in rail transit equipment - No. 1 in market share in the world
2. New industry revenue exceeds 100 billion - the second growth curve takes shape
3. Overseas revenue increased by 23%—accelerating internationalization
4. PE is only 8-9 times – cheap for growth

## Core Risks
1. The peak of domestic railway investment has passed
2. Net profit margin is low (~4.8%) and profits are thin
3. Overseas business faces geopolitical headwinds
4. Urban rail business fell by 7.4%
