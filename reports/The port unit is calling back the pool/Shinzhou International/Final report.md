## 2. Core investment logic

### Look at the long logic

1. **One of the deepest manufacturing moats in the world**: Fabric R&D capabilities + vertical integration + 20 years of customer relationship accumulation, almost impossible for competitors to copy
2. **Valuation is at historical bottom**: PE 10.7 times, dividend yield exceeds 4%, the market seems to have priced in the "worst case scenario"
3. **Increase in industry concentration is a definite trend**: 3.8% market share still has room for significant improvement, and the expansion of leading share will continue for several years
4. **Profit is expected to recover in 2026**: The ramp-up of the new factory is completed + the impact of tariffs is digested. Brokerages predict that net profit can reach 7.4 billion yuan (corresponding to about 8.5 times PE)
5. **Extremely excellent management**: long-termism, high shareholding, low-key and pragmatic, employee-oriented

### Bearish logic

1. **After all, it is an OEM business**: No matter how high-end it is, the bargaining power is limited to the downstream brands and they cannot set their own prices.
2. **Profit decline in 2025 may be structural**: If gross profit margin of 26.3% becomes the new normal rather than a short-term low, the current valuation is not cheap
3. **Customer concentration is too high**: 80% of revenue comes from four customers, and a single customer strategy adjustment can cause a significant impact
4. **Uncontrollable geopolitical risks**: Sino-US game, tariff policy, Southeast Asian political situation - production capacity is distributed in three sensitive areas
5. **The growth ceiling is approaching**: The scale of the global sportswear OEM market is limited, and Shenzhou is already the largest. The growth rate will gradually slow down in the future.
6. **The market valuation of Hong Kong stocks has been low for a long time**: Even if the fundamentals are excellent, the liquidity discount of Hong Kong stocks may suppress valuations in the long term.

## 7. Summary

**Shenzhou International is a manufacturing leader with top moats, top management, and top financial quality. Its current valuation is at the bottom of history. **

**Front**:
- Fabric research and development barriers are a unique competitive advantage in the world
- ROE 15-18%, low leverage, strong cash flow - excellent financial quality
- PE 10.7 times + dividend rate above 4% - attractive valuation
- Management ownership of 53%, employee turnover rate of 3% - excellent governance and culture
- The trend of increasing industry concentration is confirmed, and there is room for improvement in the market share of 3.8%

**Negative**:
- The profit decline in 2025 needs to be confirmed whether it is a short-term factor or a structural turning point
- 80% of customer concentration is ongoing risk exposure
- Geopolitical/tariff risks are uncontrollable
- The ceiling of the foundry model will eventually come
- Hong Kong stock liquidity discount may exist for a long time

**Core Judgment**: If the profit decline in 2025 is indeed due to the superposition of short-term factors (new factory ramp + tariff impact) rather than the deterioration of the business model, then the current 10.7 times PE provides a good buying window for long-term investors. The core indicator that needs to be closely tracked is whether the gross profit margin can rise back to above 28% in 2026 - this is the watershed for judging whether to "pick up a bargain" or "to catch a flying knife".
