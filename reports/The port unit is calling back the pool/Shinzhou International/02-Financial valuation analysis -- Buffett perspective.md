# Shenzhou International (2313.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, capital allocation and valuation safety margin
> Core question: How much is this company worth? Do current prices provide a sufficient margin of safety?

---

## 1. Financial quality physical examination

### 1.1 Profitability: Excellent but volatile

**Historical net interest rate trend**

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Net profit margin | Gross profit margin |
|------|------------|--------------|--------|--------|
| 2021 | 238.8 | 51.1 | 21.4% | 27.6% |
| 2022 | 243.3 | 38.5 | 15.8% | 23.8% |
| 2023 | 249.5 | 45.6 | 18.3% | 24.3% |
| 2024 | 286.6 | 62.4 | 21.8% | 28.1% |
| 2025 | 310.0 | 58.3 | 18.8% | 26.3% |

2024 will be a highlight year - revenue will increase by 14.8%, profits will increase by 36.9%, and net profit margin will return to a historical high of 21.8%. However, there will be a significant decline in 2025, with gross profit margin falling by 1.8 percentage points to 26.3%, and net profit falling by 6.7% year-on-year.

**Reason breakdown**:
- Rigid increase in employee salaries
- The new factory in Cambodia is in the production capacity ramp-up period, and the efficiency has not yet reached the standard.
- Cost sharing brought about by the U.S.’s additional tariffs (Vietnam’s tariffs on the U.S. have basically been implemented)
- Increase in volume but decrease in price - Sales volume increased by approximately 9%, but unit price decreased by approximately 0.9%

**Key Judgment**: The profit decline in 2025 is more due to the superposition of short-term factors (new factory ramp + tariff impact), rather than a signal of deterioration in the business model. However, we need to be vigilant. If the gross profit margin cannot rise back to above 28% in 2026, it means that the increase in costs is structural.

### 1.2 ROE analysis: long-term excellence

| Year | ROE (weighted average) |
|------|---------------|
| 2020 | ~15% |
| 2021 | ~17% |
| 2022 | ~12% |
| 2023 | ~14% |
| 2024 | ~18% |
| 2020-2024 average | 15.94% |
| 2026E | 18.6% |

Shenzhou's ROE has remained in the 15%-18% range for a long time. It fell to a low point in 2022 due to the impact of the epidemic and then recovered quickly. This level is very good for the manufacturing industry - it means that every 100 yuan of shareholder capital invested can create a net profit of about 16-18 yuan per year.

More importantly, this ROE is achieved under **low leverage** conditions. The asset-liability ratio is only 32%, and there are almost no interest-bearing liabilities, indicating that profitability is "real money" and does not rely on financial leverage.

### 1.3 Cash flow quality: high profit content

Operating cash flow in 2024 is 5.27 billion yuan, net profit in the same period is 6.24 billion yuan, and the cash flow/net profit ratio is about 84%. This ratio is healthy considering working capital fluctuations.

Indicators that deserve more attention: **Operating cash flow/revenue ratio** The long-term average reaches 19.21%, which is significantly higher than the industry level of 12%. This means that nearly 20 yuan of every 100 yuan of income is converted into operating cash flow, and the operating quality is first-rate.

The company has no major accounts receivable risk (customers are all top global brands), inventory turnover is normal, and the possibility of cash flow fraud is extremely low.

## 2. Capital Allocation Assessment

### 2.1 Capital expenditure: restrained but forward-looking

| Year | Capital expenditure (100 million yuan) | Proportion of revenue |
|------|----------------|---------|
| 2023 | 10.6 | 4.2% |
| 2024 | 17.1 | 6.0% |

Capital expenditures will increase significantly to 1.71 billion yuan in 2024, mainly for the construction of new bases in Cambodia. For the manufacturing industry, it is reasonable to maintain the capital expenditure/revenue ratio at 4%-6% - maintaining the pace of capacity expansion without over-investment.

Compared with TSMC’s capital expenditures, which account for more than 30% of revenue, Shenzhou’s capital needs are significantly lower. This is the natural advantage of textile manufacturing over semiconductor manufacturing – it does not require continuous huge equipment upgrades.

### 2.2 Shareholder Return: Generous Dividends

| Year | Full-year dividend payment (HKD/share) | Dividend payout rate |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| 2023 | ~2.03 | ~60% |
| 2024 | 2.53 | 55.8% (about 60% after excluding one-time income) |
| 2025 | ~1.95 (including final 1.20) | ~60.9% |

The company maintains a dividend payout ratio of approximately 60%. Based on the current stock price of HK$47.6, the dividend rate will be approximately 5.3% in 2024 and approximately 4.1% in 2025. For a company with an ROE of 15%-18% and still room for growth, this dividend payout level is very sincere - it not only retains the necessary funds for expansion, but also gives shareholders considerable cash returns.

### 2.3 Balance Sheet: Extremely Stable

- Asset-liability ratio: approximately 32%
- Interest-bearing liabilities: minimal
- Cash reserves: ample

This is a "risk-resistant" balance sheet. During cyclical downturns (such as 2022), companies do not need to worry about financing pressure and can buck the trend and expand production. This financially conservative style is very consistent with the concept of long-term value creation.

The historical PE center of Shenzhou International is about 20-25 times, and the current TTM PE of 10.7 times is at a significant historical low. Reasons why the market gives such a low valuation may include:

1. Profit decline in 2025 triggers market concerns about sustainability of profitability
2. Sino-US trade friction and tariff uncertainty
3. The overall valuation of the Hong Kong stock market is low
4. Concerns about slowing global consumer demand

### 3.2 Multi-angle valuation calculation

**Method 1: PE Valuation Method**

| Scenario | 2026E Net Profit | Grant PE | Reasonable Market Value | Corresponding Stock Price (Hong Kong Dollar) |
|------|-----------|--------|---------|---------------|
| Conservative | 6.5 billion yuan | 12 times | 78 billion yuan | ~58 |
| Neutral | 7.46 billion yuan | 15 times | 111.9 billion yuan | ~83 |
| Optimistic | 8 billion yuan | 18 times | 144 billion yuan | ~107 |

**Method 2: DCF simplified estimation**

Assumptions:
- Net profit CAGR 8% in the next five years (resuming growth from 5.83 billion in 2025)
- Terminal growth rate 3%
- Discount rate 10%
- Sustainable management

Under these assumptions, the intrinsic value of Shenzhou International is approximately in the range of HK$70-90 per share.

**Method Three: Dividend Discount**

Based on the current dividend payment level of approximately HK$2/year, assuming a future dividend growth rate of 5% and a discount rate of 10%:
- DDM valuation = 2 / (10% - 5%) = HKD 40
- If the dividend growth rate increases to 7%: DDM valuation = 2 / (10% - 7%) = HK$67

Dividend discount models show that current stock prices largely reflect lower growth expectations.

## 4. Review of historical valuation cycles

Shenzhou International's stock price hit a historical high of about HK$180 in 2021, which corresponded to a PE of more than 30 times. Experienced since then:

1. **Plummet in 2022**: The epidemic has caused production capacity to be restricted, profits have fallen sharply, and stock prices have halved.
2. **Rebound in 2023**: Performance recovers, stock price rebounds
3. **Surge and fall in 2024**: Performance exceeds expectations, but tariff concerns suppress valuations
4. **Continuous decline in 2025-2026**: Profit decline + Hong Kong stock market weakness, PE compressed to historical lows

Current valuation levels may reflect "worst case expectations" - the market views the profit decline in 2025 as a trend rather than a cyclical one. If profits resume growth to more than 7.4 billion yuan in 2026 as expected, there is significant room for repair in the current valuation.

---

## 5. Financial risk reminder

1. **Whether the gross profit margin can recover is the key**: If the gross profit margin of 26.3% becomes the new normal rather than a short-term low, then the expectation of profit recovery in 2026 may be disappointed.
2. **Exchange rate risk**: Revenue is mainly in US dollars and Hong Kong dollars, costs are mainly in RMB and Vietnamese dong, exchange rate fluctuations affect profits
3. **Customer Bargaining Pressure**: If the unit price decrease trend of 0.9% continues, it will continue to erode profit margins
4. **Tariff Variables**: The U.S.’s trade policies towards Vietnam and Cambodia are still uncertain
