# CATL: Business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: good business judgment, business model, free cash flow, customer stickiness
> Study date: May 16, 2026

## 1. Is this a "good business"?

Duan Yongping's core criteria for judging "good business": **doing the right thing (whether the business model itself creates value) + doing the right thing (whether the execution level is excellent)**.

### 1.1 The essence of business

The essence of CATL's business is: **Provide core components for the global electrification transformation-power batteries and energy storage batteries**.

This business has several naturally good features:

**(1) Demand side: Electrification is an irreversible trend**

The global penetration rate of new energy vehicles will increase from approximately 4% in 2020 to approximately 25% in 2025, and the penetration rate in the Chinese market will exceed 50% in 2025. The battery accounts for 30-40% of the cost of an electric vehicle and is the most core component. At the same time, the energy storage market is exploding - CATL's energy storage battery sales in 2025 will be 121GWh, a year-on-year increase of 29%. This is not a business that needs to "create demand", but rides on the historic wave of the energy revolution.

**(2) Supply side: Technology and scale thresholds are extremely high**

Battery manufacturing is not a simple assembly, but involves the intersection of materials science, chemical engineering, precision manufacturing, thermal management and other disciplines. Billions of yuan are invested in a battery production line, and it takes 1-2 years for the yield rate to increase. CATL's 772GWh production capacity and 60,000+ patented technology accumulation cannot be easily copied by latecomers.

**(3) Customer switching cost is high**

The battery is the "heart" of electric vehicles. After car companies select a battery supplier, they need to go through 18-24 months of joint development, testing and verification, and production line debugging. The hidden costs of switching vendors are extremely high—not just money, but also time and security risks.

## 2. In-depth dismantling of the business model

### 2.1 Income structure

CATL's revenue sources are mainly divided into three major sectors (2025 annual report data, unit: RMB):

| Business segments | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Gross profit margin | Year-on-year changes |
|----------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Power battery system | About 280 billion | About 26% | Growth |
| Energy storage battery system | 62.44 billion | 26.71% | +8.99% |
| Battery materials and recycling | About 80 billion | Lower | Growth |

Key findings:
- **Power batteries are still the basic market**, but the growth rate is slowing down;
- **Energy storage business is the second growth curve**, with a global market share of 30.4%, ranking first in the world for five consecutive years;
- **The gross profit margin of overseas business (31.44%) is much higher than that of domestic business (24%)**, and overseas expansion has brought about an increase in profit margins.

### 2.2 Is it profitable to "make batteries"?

This is the core question in judging good business. The answer is: **very profitable and getting more and more profitable**.

- In 2025, gross profit margin will be 26.3% and net profit margin will be 18.1%, both reaching new highs in the past five years.
- ROE has exceeded 24% for 4 consecutive years, and the weighted ROE in 2025 will be 24.91%
- Net profit attributable to parent companies was 72.2 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 42.28%

Compared with the average ROE of 10-15% in the manufacturing industry, CATL's profitability is close to that of consumer goods companies, which is extremely rare in the manufacturing industry.

### 2.3 Position in the value chain

CATL is at the "throat" of the new energy vehicle industry chain:

```
Upstream (lithium ore/positive and negative electrode materials) → CATL (cell + module + Pack) → downstream (car companies / energy storage operators)
```

**To the upstream**: CATL is the world’s largest purchaser of battery materials and has strong bargaining power. The company also realizes its upstream layout by investing in lithium mines (such as the acquisition of Canada's Millennium Lithium Industry, etc.) to reduce the impact of raw material fluctuations.

**For downstream**: The 2025 annual report shows that contract liabilities (advance receipts) are as high as 49.2 billion yuan, indicating that downstream customers need to pay in advance to lock in production capacity - this is a reflection of extremely strong bargaining power.

## 3. Free cash flow analysis

Duan Yongping repeatedly emphasized: **"Free cash flow is the most important indicator to measure the true value of a company."**

### 3.1 Cash flow performance

| Indicator (RMB) | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---------------|--------|--------|--------|
| Cash flow from operating activities | About 83 billion | About 97 billion | 133.2 billion |
| Capital expenditure | About 50 billion | About 45 billion | About 40 billion (estimate) |
| Free cash flow | About 33 billion | About 52 billion | About 93 billion (estimate) |

Operating cash flow in 2025 will increase by 36.2 billion yuan, or 37.35%, compared with the previous year, mainly due to the growth in sales scale and improvement in payment collection.

### 3.2 Cash flow quality evaluation

**(1) Matching degree of net profit and operating cash flow**

In 2025, the net profit attributable to the parent company will be 72.2 billion yuan, the operating cash flow will be 133.2 billion yuan, and the cash flow/net profit ratio will be approximately 1.84 times - this means that for every dollar of profit the company earns, there will be an inflow of nearly two dollars of real money. This is extremely high quality profit.

**(2)Capital Expenditure Trends**

As the peak period of capacity construction has passed (772GWh capacity has been built), the proportion of capital expenditures in revenue is declining, and free cash flow is accelerating.

**(3) Shareholder return**

- Dividends: 50% of the net profit attributable to the parent company has been distributed as cash dividends for three consecutive years, with the cumulative dividends approaching RMB 100 billion.
- Repurchase: A 4-8 billion yuan repurchase plan will be launched in 2025, and 1.551 billion yuan has been repurchased by the end of the year
- At the end of the period, monetary funds and trading financial assets totaled RMB 392.5 billion.

This shows that the company has entered a virtuous cycle of "making money - returning shareholders" and no longer needs to retain a large amount of profits for expansion.

## 4. In-depth analysis of customer stickiness

### 4.1 Core customer binding

CATL has established in-depth cooperative relationships with global mainstream car companies:

| Customers | Depth of Cooperation | Cooperation Model |
|------|----------|----------|
| Tesla | Core supplier | Long-term supply agreement, Shanghai factory supporting |
| BMW | Strategic cooperation | Joint development of next-generation batteries |
| Volkswagen | Deep binding | Joint venture to build a factory (Europe) |
| Geely/Ideal/Nio | Main suppliers | Long-term cooperation + customized development |
| Hyundai/Stellantis | Expanding | Signing a long-term supply agreement |

### 4.2 Why can’t customers leave CATL?

**(1) "You have to be" brought about by technological leadership**

The third-generation Kirin battery has an energy density of 280Wh/kg and can achieve a range of 1,000 kilometers; the Shenxing superchargeable battery is fully charged in 6 minutes at room temperature. These are indicators that peers cannot catch up in the short term. In order to make their products competitive, car companies must use the best batteries.

**(2) Cost competitiveness brought by scale advantage**

The scale effect brought by the 772GWh production capacity gives CATL a significant advantage in cost. The cost of second-tier battery factories is often 10-20% higher at the same technical level.

**(3) Deep binding formed by joint development model**

CATL's relationship with car companies is not a simple buyer-seller relationship, but joint development begins from the model definition stage. This deep cooperation model makes switching costs extremely high.

### 4.3 Customer concentration risk

It should be noted that BYD, as the former largest customer of CATL, has fully shifted to self-developed batteries. This reminds us: **When customers themselves have technical capabilities, their dependence on CATL may be reduced**. But at present, most car companies do not have the ability and willingness to develop their own batteries.

## 5. The pros and cons of "good business" judgment

### Positive arguments

1. **Extremely certain demand**: Two-wheel drive with electrification and energy storage, high demand growth for at least the next 10 years
2. **Excellent profitability**: ROE>24%, and improving; net profit margin>18%, close to consumer goods companies
3. **Extremely abundant cash flow**: Accelerated release of free cash flow, increased dividend repurchase efforts
4. **Deep technical barriers**: 60,000+ patents, continued high R&D investment (22.1 billion yuan in 2025)
5. **High customer stickiness**: High switching costs, 49.2 billion yuan in contract liabilities prove that downstream companies are queuing up to grab production capacity

### Counterarguments

1. **The essence of manufacturing remains unchanged**: No matter how good the manufacturing industry is, it still faces the pressure of technological iteration and price competition.
2. **The impact of lithium price fluctuations**: Although the decline in lithium prices from 2023 to 2025 is beneficial to costs, a rebound in lithium prices in the future may compress profits.
3. **Second-tier manufacturers are catching up**: China Airlines, Guoxuan Hi-Tech, Yiwei Lithium Energy, etc. are accelerating their rise. The combined market share of the "two kings" will fall below 70% in 2025.
4. **Solid-state battery changes**: If there is a disruptive breakthrough in the all-solid-state battery technology route (not the CATL route), existing production capacity may depreciate.
5. **Geopolitics**: Although the actual impact of the U.S. CMC list is limited, it may affect the purchasing decisions of European and American customers.

---

## 6. Conclusion

**Good Business Rating: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5)**

There is no doubt that CATL is a "good business". It has the core elements that Duan Yongping values: powerful differentiated products, extremely high customer stickiness, abundant free cash flow, and a simple and easy-to-understand business model.

The only deduction items are: **It is still essentially a manufacturing industry** and faces long-term risks of technological route changes and intensified industry competition. Unlike Moutai's consumer products that "never go out of style", battery technology has the possibility of being subverted (although the probability is unlikely).

In Duan Yongping's words: **"This is a good business, but we need to continue to pay attention to whether it is still 'doing the right things'." ** At present, CATL is heading in the right direction and its execution is first-rate.
