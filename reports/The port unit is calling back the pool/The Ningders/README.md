## Core conclusion

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

**Judgment: Wait and see, waiting for a better buying price**

CATL is the absolute leader in the global power battery field, with revenue of 423.7 billion yuan in 2025, net profit attributable to the parent company of 72.2 billion yuan, ROE exceeding 24% for 4 consecutive years, and operating cash flow reaching 133.2 billion yuan. The company has built a very deep moat in three dimensions: technology (60,000+ patents), scale (772GWh production capacity), and customers (tied to global mainstream car companies). In Q1 of 2026, it returned to its peak with a domestic market share of 50.1%.

**Core Advantages**: Leading technology (Kirin battery, Shenxing Supercharger, sodium-ion battery), significant scale effect, strong customer stickiness, and abundant free cash flow (annual dividend rate 50% + repurchase).

**Core Risks**: Zeng Yuqun's "key person dependence" (the successor has not yet been determined), geopolitical uncertainty caused by the US CMC list, risks of battery technology route changes (solid-state batteries), and industry overcapacity pressure.

**Valuation Judgment**: The current A-share PE is about 29 times (corresponding to 2025 profits). If calculated based on the expected net profit of 96-98 billion yuan in 2026, the dynamic PE is about 19-20 times, which is within a reasonable range. However, taking into account the geopolitical risks and intensified industry competition, the ideal buying price should be 15-17 times the dynamic PE, which is the range of 350-380 yuan for A shares, which corresponds to a discount of about 15-20% for Hong Kong stocks.
