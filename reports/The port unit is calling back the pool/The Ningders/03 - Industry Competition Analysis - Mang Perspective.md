As previously analysed, the cost of switching the battery supplier to the vehicle includes:

This ecological layout has transformed the Ninder era from "parts and components supplier" to "energy solution provider" and has raised the dimension of the barriers to competition.

# IV. Sectoral trends: What happens in the next 5-10 years?

##4.1 Determinable trends

** (1) Global electro-reversibility**
- China ' s new energy vehicle penetration rate is over 50 per cent, Europe is about 20 per cent and the United States is about 10 per cent
- Global power cell demand is expected to increase from about 1,200 GWh in 2025 to about 3,000 GWh in 2030
- About 20% of the annual compound increase.

**(2) Market eruptions in storage capacity**
- Global energy storage is growing rapidly, with Ningde-era operations growing by 29 per cent in 2025.
- The storage capacity is expected to be the second largest market to run alongside power cells.

**(3) Industry concentration may decrease and rise**
- The combined "double king" fell 70% in 2025.
- But the 2026Q1 Ningde era returns 50% (long-term concentration) and the industry will be stronger after the shuffling.

# 4.2 Uncertainty Trends

**(1) Solid-state batteries: the largest technological variable**

Manger says: ** "The biggest risk is not volatility, but you don't know what you're doing." ** Solid-state batteries are the biggest known unknown in the battery industry.

- Ningdé Plan 2027, small batch production, 2030 scale
- Toyota, Samsung and others are on the layout.
- If a firm takes the lead in breaking the whole solid mass product, it may re-create the pattern.

But it is important to note that the Ningde era has a global share of over 30 per cent of solid-state battery patents, and that even with technological changes, the Ningde era is probably still a lead.

**(2) Geopolitics: second-largest variable**

The US CMC list, the European Battery Code, India’s localization requirements, etc. are all driving the regionalization of the battery supply chain. The Nender era was a response in Germany and Hungary, but additional overseas capacity investments could affect short-term returns.

**(3) Technical route differentiation**

The three-bit lithium vs sodium vs. ion vs. SOLID, which is used in different technical routes, is used in different scenarios. The Ningde strategy is "full-range coverage," but this also means that research and development is fragmented.
