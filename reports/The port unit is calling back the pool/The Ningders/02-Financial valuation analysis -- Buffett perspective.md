## 2. Ten-year trend: transformation from growth to maturity

### 2.1 Growth trajectory

The development of the Ningde era can be divided into three stages:

**Phase 1 (2011-2018): Entrepreneurship and Rise**
- In 2011, it became independent from ATL and focused on power batteries.
- A-share listing in 2018, with revenue of approximately 30 billion yuan

**Phase 2 (2018-2023): Rapid expansion**
- Revenue increased from 30 billion to 400.9 billion, an increase of 13 times in 5 years
- Capacity expansion from approximately 50GWh to 772GWh
- Capital expenditures are huge at this stage and free cash flow is small

**The third phase (2024 to present): profit release period**
- The peak period of production capacity construction is basically over
- Capital expenditures as a share of revenue decline
- Accelerated release of free cash flow
- Start large-scale dividend distribution (50% profit dividend) and buyback

Buffett prefers companies that have entered the third stage: **Growth continues, but the need for capital expenditures decreases, and free cash flow begins to be released in large quantities**.

### 2.2 Stability of ROE

What is the significance of ROE>24% for 4 consecutive years?

Assuming that a company's ROE continues to maintain 25% and 50% of profits are retained and reinvested (the other 50% is distributed as dividends), then its intrinsic value will increase by approximately 12.5% per year. If it can be maintained for 10 years, the intrinsic value will increase to 3.25 times its current value.

CATL's current ROE level and dividend policy fit this model exactly.

## 3. Intrinsic value estimation

### 3.1 Method 1: Discounted Free Cash Flow (DCF)

**Core Assumptions** (Conservative Scenario):

| Parameters | Assumptions | Basis |
|------|--------|------|
| Net profit attributable to parent company in 2026 | 96 billion yuan | Consensus expectations of brokers |
| Net profit growth rate from 2027 to 2030 | 12%→8% | Industry growth slows down |
| Net profit growth rate from 2031 to 2035 | 5% | Growth in mature period |
| Perpetual Growth Rate | 3% | Inflation + Basic Growth |
| Discount rate | 10% | Includes China market risk premium |
| Free cash flow/net profit | 80% | Steady state after capex decline |

**Estimated results**:

In the next 10 years, the discounted present value of free cash flow is about 850-950 billion yuan, the discounted sustainable value is about 600-700 billion yuan, and the total intrinsic value is about 1450-1650 billion yuan.

The current market value of the A-share market is approximately 1.86 trillion yuan (calculated at 423.6 yuan/share), and the market value of the Hong Kong stock market needs to be calculated separately.

**Safety Margin**: The current A-share price is approximately 1.1-1.3 times the intrinsic value, **Insufficient Safety Margin**.

### 3.2 Method 2: Profit rate method

A simple method commonly used by Buffett: treat the company as an "equity bond" and compare the earnings yield (E/P) with the risk-free interest rate.

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Net profit attributable to parent company in 2025 | 72.2 billion yuan |
| Current A-share market value | ~1.86 trillion yuan |
| Static earnings yield (E/P) | 3.88% |
| Expected net profit in 2026 | ~96 billion yuan |
| Dynamic profit yield | 5.16% |
| China’s 10-year government bond yield | ~1.7% |

The dynamic earnings yield of 5.16% is approximately three times the treasury bond yield of 1.7%. From this perspective, **CATL provides reasonable risk compensation**.

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### 4.1 Buffett’s margin of safety principle

Buffett requires a margin of safety of at least 25-30%. Let's calculate:

| Scenarios | Assumptions | Reasonable Valuation | Current Price Comparison |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| Optimistic | Net profit of 98 billion in 2026, 25 times PE | Total market value of 2.45 trillion | 31% premium |
| Neutral | Net profit of 96 billion in 2026, 20 times PE | Total market value of 1.92 trillion | 3% premium |
| Conservative | Net profit of 90 billion in 2026, 17 times PE | Total market value of 1.53 trillion | Discount of 18% |
| Pessimistic | Net profit of 80 billion in 2026, 15 times PE | Total market value of 1.2 trillion | 35% discount |

**Conclusion**: Under the neutral assumption, the current A-share price is basically reasonable and lacks a margin of safety. To obtain the 25% safety margin required by Buffett, the price needs to fall back to the level of a conservative scenario - **A shares are about 350 yuan or less**.

## 5. Will Buffett buy CATL?

### 5.1 Aspects that meet Buffett’s standards

- **ROE continues to be excellent** (>24%, 4 consecutive years) - in line
- **Abundant operating cash flow**, high profit content - in line with
- **Industry leading position**, global market share 39.2% - in line with
- **Outstanding management ability** (more details later) - basically in line
- **Started to give back to shareholders on a large scale** (50% dividend + buyback) - compliant

### 5.2 Aspects that do not meet Buffett’s standards

- **Technological Change Risk**: Buffett avoids industries with rapid technological changes. New technologies such as solid-state batteries and sodium-ion batteries may change the competitive landscape - not consistent
- **Geopolitical Risk**: Uncertainties such as CMC list, Sino-US game - not consistent
- **Cyclicality of manufacturing**: Fluctuations in battery prices and lithium prices bring profit uncertainty - not consistent
- **Lack of Margin of Safety at Current Valuation**: Buffett will not buy without a margin of safety - Not compliant

### 5.3 Comprehensive judgment

There is a high probability that Buffett will pay attention to but will not buy CATL at the current price. He will put it in the list of "Good company, waiting for good price". He might say:

> "CATL is an excellent company with an excellent economic moat and management team. But I need a better price to get a sufficient margin of safety. If the stock price falls below 15 times PE due to market panic, I will seriously consider it."
