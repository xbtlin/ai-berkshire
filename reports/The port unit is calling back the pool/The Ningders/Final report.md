## 2. Investment Judgment

### Conclusion: Wait and see, waiting for a better buying price

**CATL is undoubtedly a great company, but the current price is not the best time to buy. **

The core logic is as follows:

**Why is it a good company? **

1. The absolute leader in global power batteries, with a market share of 39.2%, and domestically reached 50.1% in 2026Q1
2. ROE has been >24% for 4 consecutive years, net profit margin is 18.1%, and profitability is close to that of consumer goods companies rather than manufacturing companies.
3. Operating cash flow/net profit >1.8 times, the profit content is extremely high
4. Deep technology moat (60,000+ patents), high scale barriers (772GWh), and strong customer stickiness (49.2 billion yuan in advance receipts)
5. Entering the profit release period - capital expenditures slow down, free cash flow accelerates, and dividend repurchases increase

**Why not buy now? **

1. The current A-share price is about 424 yuan, which corresponds to a dynamic PE of about 19 times in 2026, lacking a safety margin of more than 25%.
2. The growth rate may slow down from 40%+ to 12-15%, and the PEG is about 1.3-1.6, which is not cheap.
3. Geopolitical uncertainty (CMC list) has not yet been fully digested
4. The succession issue is an unresolved "grey rhino"
5. Overcapacity in the industry may lead to periodic profit pressure in 2026-2027

## 6. Investment Thesis

**Summary in one sentence**: CATL is the "Coca-Cola" of the global power battery industry - it has the deepest moat, the strongest profitability, the best management team, and occupies the most advantageous position in the wave of the energy revolution. The only problem right now is the price, not the company itself.

**Full paper**:

1. **Demand certainty**: Global electrification + energy storage two-wheel drive, high certainty of growth for at least 10 years. As the absolute leader, CATL will enjoy most of the dividends from industry growth.

2. **Moat deepening**: The triple barriers of technology (60,000+ patents, Kirin/Shenxing/sodium ion) + scale (772GWh) + customer stickiness (bound to global mainstream car companies) continue to deepen. The market share returning to 50% in 2026Q1 shows that the strong will always be strong.

3. **Profit release period**: The peak of production capacity construction has passed, and the release of free cash flow is accelerating. From the "investment period" to the "harvest period", the intensity of dividend repurchases will continue to increase.

4. **Reasonable valuation range**: 15-17 times dynamic PE (corresponding to 300-350 yuan for A shares) provides sufficient safety margin. Although the current PE of 19 times is not expensive, it lacks a safety margin.

5. **Key tracking variables**: Successor progress, CMC list changes, solid-state battery technology evolution, quarterly market share data.
