# Sands China (1928.HK) Industry Competition Analysis

## 1. Macau gaming industry structure

### Six major gaming operators

| Operator | Positioning | Number of properties | Core advantages |
|--------|------|----------|----------|
| Sands China | High-end resort + MICE | 5 | The largest complex and the leader in exhibitions |
| Galaxy Entertainment | High-end + Asian style | Galaxy Integrated Resort City | Strong brand premium, expansion in progress |
| MGM China | High-end luxury | 2 | Highest brand positioning |
| Wynn Macau | High-end boutique | 2 | Fine service, customer loyalty |
| Melco Resorts | Diversification | 3 | Strong innovation, good entertainment experience |
| SJM | Traditional Mass | Grand Lisboa/Shanghai Lisboa | Deep local roots |

### Industry characteristics
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

1. **Oligopoly**: 6 license holders form a highly concentrated market
2. **Extremely high barriers to entry**: New licenses are almost impossible to obtain
3. **Excellent cash flow**: Gambling business is "settled daily" and there are no accounts receivable
4. **High profit margin**: EBITDA profit margin 25-35%
5. **Growth drivers are clear**: China’s middle class consumption upgrade + Macau tourism recovery

## 2. Sands China’s competitiveness

### 1. Scale advantage
**Rating: ★★★★★ (4.5/5)**

Sands China is Macau’s largest integrated resort operator:
- 10,829 rooms (the most in Macau)
- Approximately 1.5 million square feet of convention and exhibition space (largest in Asia)
- 5 major properties covering different areas of Cotai
- Cost sharing and cross-selling advantages brought by scale

### 2. Non-Gaming Income Capacity
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

Sands China’s non-gaming strategy is at the core of differentiation:
- Market leader in MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions)
- High-end retail shopping (Four Seasons stores, etc.)
- Rich catering brands
- Entertainment performances and experience projects

The value of this strategy is to reduce reliance on gaming revenue while attracting a wider customer base.

### 3. Midfield business competitiveness
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

The Macau gaming industry is undergoing "de-VIPization" - the transformation from VIP rooms to mass gaming. Sands China is well positioned in this trend:
- Plenty of mass-market gaming tables and slot machines
- The comprehensive experience of hotel + shopping + dining attracts middle-class customers
- No more over-reliance on a few big gamblers

## 3. Key issues of Munger format

### "The long-term future of the gaming industry?"
- **Positive**: The human desire for gambling is eternal; China’s middle class is still expanding
- **Negative**: The penetration of online gambling may divert some customer groups; anti-corruption policies restrict VIP rooms
- **Conclusion**: The "experience" of offline integrated resorts cannot be replaced by online ones, but the growth rate will slow down.

### "Macau vs Singapore vs Southeast Asia?"
- Sands Casino Singapore (operated simultaneously by LVS): high-end positioning, complementary to Macau
- Japan IR (Integrated Resort): Slow progress, small short-term impact
- Philippines/Cambodia: low-end positioning, not directly competing with Sands
- Conclusion: Competition in Southeast Asia will divert some customer groups, but Macau's "brand premium" and "convenience to mainland China" cannot be replaced

### "How to price policy risk?"
After the gambling license renewal crisis in 2022, the market has fully priced policy risks (PE has been compressed from 30 times + to 15-18 times). The new gambling license is valid until 2032, providing a confirmation window of at least 6 years.

However, policy risks that require vigilance include:
1. Further limit the number of gaming tables
2. Increase gaming tax rates
3. Demand more non-gaming investments
4. Limit the frequency of mainland residents traveling to Australia

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

Sands China has the strongest comprehensive competitiveness among the six gaming operators in Macau - it has the largest scale, clear MICE advantages and smooth mass transformation. Industry barriers are extremely high (license monopoly) and profit margins are excellent.

Munger's summary: This is a "good fish in a good pond" - the gaming industry is one of the few industries that can consistently generate 20%+ ROE, and Sands China is the leader in this industry. The only unsettling thing is that the "pond level" (the regulatory environment) may be artificially adjusted. If the policy is stable, this is a very good investment target.
