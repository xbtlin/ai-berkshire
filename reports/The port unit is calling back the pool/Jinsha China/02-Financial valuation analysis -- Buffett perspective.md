# Sands China (1928.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Net Income | ~$7.8 billion | ~$7.5 billion | +4% |
| Net Profit | ~$1 billion | ~$1.05 billion | -3.3% |
| EBITDA margin | ~29-33% | ~32% | Slightly volatile |
| ROE | 20-25% | 25%+ | Industry leading |

**Profitability Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

ROE 20-25% is the highest among the 13 companies, reflecting the ultra-high return on capital in the gaming industry. The EBITDA profit margin of around 30% is also an excellent level. The only flaw is that profits will drop slightly by 3.3% in 2025.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- The gaming industry has excellent cash flow characteristics: instant payment from customers, no accounts receivable
- Large EBITDA (approximately US$2.3 billion/year)
- But capital expenditures are also large (property renovation/construction), and free cash flow is limited

### Balance Sheet
- Medium debt level (with certain leverage, property loans)
- Ample liquidity
- The core assets are the use rights and equipment of the five integrated resorts

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): 15-18 times
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 10-12 times
- Dividend yield: ~1.6%
- PB: about 3-4 times

### Reasonable judgment of valuation

PE 15-18 times is **significantly cheaper** for companies with ROE 20-25%.

Comparison:
- In the case of ROE 20%+, growth companies usually give 30-40 times PE
- Sands China only gives 15-18 times, indicating that the market has given a huge discount to policy risks

Is this "policy discount" reasonable? If you believe that Macau’s gaming policy will be basically stable for the next 10 years, then the current valuation is significantly underestimated; if you are worried that the policy may change drastically at any time, then this discount is reasonable.

### EV/EBITDA comparison
| Company | EV/EBITDA | ROE |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Sands China | 10-12x | 20-25% |
| MGM China | 8-10x | 15-20% |
| Wynn Macau | 9-11x | 15-20% |
| LVS (parent company) | 12-14x | 20%+ |

Sands China's valuation is moderately high among its Macau gaming peers, but it also has the highest ROE.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**Partially OK. ** People will continue to gamble and travel, that’s for sure. What is uncertain is: How will Macau’s regulatory policies evolve? How much passenger traffic will be diverted from competition in Southeast Asia?

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Yes. ** The monopoly barrier of 6 licenses will be almost impossible to break through in the next 10 years. The physical assets of the top 5 resorts cannot be replicated either.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
**Front. ** LVS’s global operating experience makes Sands China’s resort management level leading in the world. However, the dividend policy is conservative.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**There is a certain margin of safety. **PE 15-18x is cheap for ROE 20-25%. But the calculation of the margin of safety depends on your judgment of policy risks.

## 4. Buffett and Gambling-An Interesting Paradox

Buffett himself does not invest in gambling companies (he believes gambling is unethical), but from a purely financial perspective:
- High ROE ✓
- Monopoly barriers ✓
- Excellent cash flow ✓
- Strong predictability ✓

In terms of financial indicators, Sands China is one of the companies that most "qualifies" under Buffett's framework. Policy risk is the only reason for it to be "discounted".

## 5. Dividend policy and value return

The dividend per share in 2025 is 3.7 cents, and the dividend rate is only 1.6%. That's on the low side for value investors. Reason:
1. The company is still investing heavily in property renovations
2. Parent company LVS has its own capital needs
3. Management prefers to use profits for capital expenditures rather than dividends

If management increases the dividend payout ratio to 50%+ (corresponding to a dividend yield of 4-5%), the valuation may be significantly re-rated.

## 6. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Sands China is the best among the 13 companies in terms of financial quality - ROE 20-25%, EBITDA profit margin 30%, and strong monopoly barriers. PE 15-18 times is a significant discount compared to such profitability, and the discount comes from policy risks.

Buffett-style judgment: If you are confident that Macau’s gaming policy will remain basically stable in the next 10 years (the new gambling license has been extended to 2032), Sands China’s current valuation is attractive. This is an investment opportunity with "good company + reasonable price + need to judge the policy".
