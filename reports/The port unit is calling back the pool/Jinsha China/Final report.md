## One sentence conclusion

Sands China is the target with "the best business quality and the most attractive valuation" among the 13 companies - the combination of ROE 20-25% + PE 15-18 times is extremely attractive under the value investment framework. The core decision point is: are you willing to bear volatility for policy risks?

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. ROE 20-25% is the highest among the 13, with excellent return on capital
2. PE 15-18 times is a significant discount for such profitability.
3. License monopoly + property barriers form a deep moat
4. The new gambling license will be issued until 2032, with a 6-year window to determine
5. Midfield business growth + MICE differentiated positioning
6. If policy risks subside and consumption recovers, the upside potential will be 60%+

### Bearish logic
1. Gambling regulatory policies are the uncontrollable "Sword of Damocles"
2. China’s economic slowdown suppresses tourism consumption in Macau
3. Dividends are low (1.6% dividend yield) and lack of immediate returns
4. Profit will fall by 3.3% in 2025, and growth momentum is questionable
5. Capital intensive – property renovations continue to consume cash flow
6. Parent company LVS’s priority over the interests of minority shareholders is uncertain
