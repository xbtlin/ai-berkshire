## Investment Highlights
1. ROE 20-25% - the highest among 13 companies
2. Macau gaming license monopoly (one of 6)
3. Good news for tourism/consumption recovery
4. Global operating experience of parent company LVS

## Core Risks
1. Risks of sudden changes in gambling regulatory policies
2. China’s economic slowdown suppresses VIP room revenue
3. Singapore/Southeast Asia competition diversion
4. Capital intensive – property maintenance/renovation requires ongoing investment
