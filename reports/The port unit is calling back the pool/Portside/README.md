## Research background

The Hong Kong Stock Exchange is a textbook-level monopoly business—the only exchange group in Hong Kong. It is also the core connection node between mainland China and the global capital market. The interconnection mechanism (Shanghai-Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect) puts it in an irreplaceable position in the general trend of global capital allocation of Chinese assets.

In 2025, the performance of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange reached a record high: IPO financing volume returned to the first place in the world (286.9 billion Hong Kong dollars, +227% year-on-year), the average daily turnover of the spot market reached 231.5 billion Hong Kong dollars (+89.5% year-on-year), and the derivatives and commodity markets simultaneously set records.

The core question is: Is the price reasonable for buying a monopoly asset with a growth center of 10%-15% at 30 times PE?
