# ZTO Express (2057.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business nature, user value, long-term competition barriers
> Study date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 The essence of business

The essence of the express delivery industry is a "scale-driven network service industry." What ZTO Express does is very simple - transport packages from point A to point B. But behind this seemingly simple business is a huge logistics network covering the entire country, as well as the operational capacity to handle hundreds of millions of packages every day.

From Duan Yongping's perspective of "doing the right thing", the express delivery industry has several core characteristics:

**First, demand is certain. ** China’s e-commerce penetration rate continues to increase, and the industry-wide express delivery business volume will exceed 170 billion items in 2025. As long as e-commerce is growing, the demand for express delivery will be growing. This is a business that doesn’t need to create demand, just satisfy it.

**Second, the scale effect is extremely significant. ** Express delivery is a typical industry with economies of scale. The larger the piece volume, the lower the single-ticket sorting cost and transportation cost. ZTO will handle 38.52 billion parcels in 2025. The cost advantage per ticket brought by its scale advantage is a real moat.

**Third, winners don’t need genius. ** Express delivery is not a technology-driven industry and does not require continuous disruptive innovation. It is more like "hard work" - whoever has a denser network, higher efficiency, and lower costs will live better.

### 1.2 Zhongtong’s position in this business

ZTO Express is the "profit king" in China's express delivery industry. In 2025, revenue will be 49.1 billion yuan, net profit will be 9.24 billion yuan, and adjusted net profit will be 9.51 billion yuan. Ranked first in terms of business volume for ten consecutive years, with a market share of approximately 19.4%.

More importantly, ZTO is not only the largest, but also the most profitable. Among the Tongda series (Zhongtong, Yuantong, Yunda, and STO), Zhongtong has a long-term lead in single-ticket profit. In 2025, the net profit per ticket will be approximately 0.24 yuan, which is much higher than that of its peers.

## 2. Business model analysis

### 2.1 Mixed model of "terminal franchise + sorting direct operation"

ZTO's business model can be summarized as "a combination of light and heavy":

- **Asset-heavy end (direct operation):** The national sorting center is directly operated by the headquarters, ensuring the uniformity of sorting efficiency and service standards. As of 2025, ZTO will have nearly 100 self-operated transshipment centers.
- **Asset-light terminal (franchise):** The terminal outlets adopt a franchise system, and more than 30,000 franchise outlets across the country are responsible for collection and delivery. The franchise system reduces capital expenditure pressure and achieves rapid expansion.

The essence of this model is to firmly control the links that most affect service quality (sorting, trunk transportation) in your own hands, and leave the links that most require local resources and flexibility (end collection and delivery) to franchisees.

### 2.2 Income structure

ZTO’s revenue mainly comes from two parts:

1. **Express service revenue:** Accounts for the vast majority of total revenue and comes from transfer fees and transportation fees collected from franchisees.
2. **Parts business:** It has grown rapidly in recent years. In the fourth quarter of 2025, the average daily parts business volume reached 9.8 million pieces, a year-on-year increase of over 38%, and the annual parts business volume increased 46% year-on-year. This part of the business has higher profit margins and is an important engine for future growth.

### 2.3 Cash flow characteristics

The cash flow characteristics of this business are very good:
- Cash flow from operating activities in 2025 will be approximately 12 billion yuan, far exceeding net profit
-Capital expenditure is relatively controllable, about 6-7 billion yuan for the whole year
- Abundant free cash flow and the ability to continuously return high returns to shareholders

## 3. "User" value analysis

### 3.1 Who are the "users" of ZTO?

There are three types of "users" in ZTO's business chain:

1. **E-commerce merchants (core customers):** They choose express delivery companies mainly based on price and timeliness. ZTO's cost advantage enables it to offer competitive prices while maintaining reasonable margins.
2. **Consumer (terminal recipient):** Although consumers usually do not choose express delivery companies directly, service experience will affect merchants’ choices. ZTO has continued to invest in terminal services in recent years.
3. **Franchise (network partner):** The stability of the franchisee directly determines the quality of the network. Zhongtong's culture of "building together and sharing" and its reasonable profit distribution mechanism make its franchisee system relatively stable.

### 3.2 How is user stickiness?

Frankly speaking, user stickiness in the express delivery industry is not strong. For e-commerce merchants, the cost of switching express delivery companies is very low, and the price difference is often the decisive factor. This is also the fundamental reason for the recurring price wars in the industry.

But ZTO has two comparative advantages:
- **Network coverage advantages brought by scale:** More than 30,000 outlets across the country mean wider coverage and faster timeliness
- **Brand effect of component business:** In the scenario of personal shipping, consumers have certain brand preferences.

## 4. Assessment of competition barriers

### 4.1 Cost advantage ★★★★☆

Zhongtong's core barrier is its cost advantage. Through economies of scale and refined management, ZTO’s cost per ticket has been lower than that of its peers for a long time. In the first half of 2025, the total cost of single ticket sorting and transportation will drop by 4 cents. This cost lead may seem small, but multiplied by the business volume of 38.5 billion pieces, it is a huge profit gap.

Sources of cost advantage:
- Scale effect: the largest unit volume, the largest dilution of fixed costs
- Own fleet: trunk transportation costs are controllable
- Sorting automation: continuous investment in automation equipment to improve efficiency
- Management efficiency: Lai Meisong emphasizes "fairness, efficiency and results"

### 4.2 Network Effect ★★★☆☆

The express delivery network has certain network effects: the more outlets, the wider the coverage, and the better the timeliness, the more packages can be attracted, forming a positive cycle. But this network effect is not as winner-take-all as that of Internet platforms—competitors can build similar networks.

### 4.3 Brand Barriers ★★☆☆☆

In the field of e-commerce parcels, express delivery brands have limited differentiation. There is little difference in consumers' perceptions of the "Tongda Series" brands. Only SF Express has formed a clearly differentiated positioning in terms of brand (high-end express delivery).

### 4.4 Switching Cost ★★☆☆☆

As mentioned before, the cost for merchants to switch courier companies is very low. This is the biggest structural weakness of the business.

---

## 5. Key issues and concerns

### 5.1 The root causes of price war have not been eliminated

The root causes of the price war in the express delivery industry are: product homogeneity + low switching costs + overcapacity. Although the State Post Bureau will promote the "anti-involution" policy in 2025 and prices have stabilized, the sustainability and effectiveness of the policy are questionable. Once industry growth slows down, price wars may return.

### 5.2 Dependence on e-commerce platforms

Zhongtong's shipment volume is highly dependent on e-commerce platforms, especially Taobao. Although Alibaba is a shareholder of ZTO, Cainiao controls the distribution rights of e-commerce products, and ZTO does not dominate this relationship. The rise of Pinduoduo has brought about growth, but it has also been accompanied by lower single ticket prices.

### 5.3 The double-edged sword of franchise system

The franchise system has helped Zhongtong achieve low-cost and rapid expansion, but it has also brought about the problem of service quality control. The complaint rate at terminal outlets and the service attitude of couriers are not entirely within the control of the headquarters.

---

## 6. Overall evaluation

### Business model rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Advantages:**
- Demand is highly certain, and e-commerce express delivery is a rigid demand
- The scale effect is significant and the cost advantage is a real moat
- Excellent cash flow and abundant free cash flow
- The "combination of light and heavy" model takes into account efficiency and flexibility

**Disadvantages:**
- Product homogeneity is serious and space for differentiation is limited
- User switching costs are low, and the risk of price wars always exists
- Strong reliance on e-commerce platforms and limited bargaining power
- The end of the industry is unclear and integration has not yet been completed

**Core judgment:** ZTO is a "best player in a good business". The express delivery industry itself is not an outstanding business (products are homogeneous and switching costs are low), but in this track, ZTO has established a relatively solid competitive position by virtue of its scale and cost advantages. The key question is: whether the price war in the industry will truly shift from a "price war" to a "value war", and whether ZTO can continue to maintain its lead in this transformation.
