## 1. Financial Overview

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Trends |
|------|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 38.4 billion | 44.3 billion | 49.1 billion | Steady growth |
| Net profit | 8.89 billion | 8.89 billion | 9.24 billion | Slight growth |
| Adjusted net profit | 10.08 billion | 10.16 billion | 9.51 billion | Slight decline |
| Package volume | 30.2 billion pieces | 34 billion pieces | 38.5 billion pieces | Rapid growth |
| Single ticket income | ~1.27 yuan | ~1.30 yuan | ~1.28 yuan | Basically the same |

A few key observations:

**First, there is the hidden worry of "increasing revenue without increasing profits". ** Revenue will grow by 10.9% in 2025, but net profit will only increase by 3.9%, and adjusted net profit will even drop by 6.3%. This shows that part of the growth in unit volume has been swallowed up by price competition and rising costs. Margins are compressing.

**Second, there are signs of improvement in income quality. ** In 2025, Q4 single ticket revenue will increase by 2.9%, reflecting the effect of the industry's "anti-involution" policy. If price stabilization trends continue, profit elasticity will emerge.

**Third, the component business is a bright spot. ** The annual spare parts business volume increased by 46%, with an average daily volume of 9.8 million pieces in Q4. The single-vote profit of spare parts is higher than that of e-commerce parts, and this increase is a substantial improvement in the profit structure.

### 1.2 Profitability Analysis

| Indicators | Values | Industry comparisons |
|------|------|----------|
| ROE | ~17% | Highest in Tongda system |
| Gross profit margin | ~25% | Industry leading |
| Net profit margin | ~18.8% | Highest in the express delivery industry |
| Operating leverage | Medium | — |

ROE of about 17% is a number worthy of attention. This level indicates that ZTO's return on capital is good, but not outstanding. Compared with consumer goods or Internet companies, which often have an ROE of 30%+, the return on capital of the express delivery industry reflects its asset-heavy nature.

But if combined with ZTO's low leverage (the asset-liability ratio is only about 22-28%), the gold content of this ROE is high - it is not piled up by adding leverage, but real operating efficiency.

## 3. Shareholder return analysis

### 3.1 Buyback Plan

ZTO’s buyback intensity ranks among the top among Chinese concept stocks:

- **Cumulative repurchases from 2020 to 2025:** About US$1.22 billion (approximately RMB 8.8 billion), 50.54 million ADSs have been repurchased, and 18.25 million shares have been cancelled.
- **New Authorization 2026:** $1.5 billion buyback program (March 2026-March 2028)
- **Cumulative repurchase authorization:** Total approximately US$3.5 billion before and after

The sincerity of the repurchase is reflected in two aspects: first, the amount is large (a total of more than 2 billion US dollars), and second, it is truly written off (it is not repurchased and left there to look good). The cancellation of shares directly increases earnings per share and net assets per share.

### 3.2 Dividend policy

- Dividend in 2024: US$0.35 per ADS/common share, dividend payout rate approximately 40%
- 2025 dividend: $0.39 per ADS/common share
- New policy starting from 2026: shareholder return (dividends + buybacks) shall not be less than 50% of the adjusted net profit of the previous year

The dividend rate was increased from 40% to 50%, coupled with large-scale buybacks, the shareholder return structure continued to be optimized. Based on current market capitalization and profit levels, the comprehensive rate of return from dividends + buybacks can reach 6-8%.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 Current Valuation Level

| Valuation indicators | Values | Comparison |
|---------|------|------|
| PE (TTM) | ~13 times | Asian logistics industry average ~16 times |
| PE (2026E) | ~11-12 times | — |
| Market value | About HK$140 billion | — |
| EV/EBITDA | ~7-8x | — |

13 times PE is not expensive for a leading company with an ROE of 17%, the largest market share, and abundant cash flow. Compared with the average PE of 16 times in the Asian logistics industry, ZTO has a discount of about 20%.

### 4.2 Reasons for valuation discount

**Reasonable discount factors:**
1. **Price war risk premium:** The market is worried that the industry will once again fall into vicious competition and compress profits.
2. **Discount of Chinese concept stocks:** VIE structure and geopolitical risks bring systematic discounts
3. **Expected growth slowdown:** The growth rate of China’s e-commerce is slowing down, and the growth rate of express delivery volume will gradually decline.

**Risk of possible over-valuation:**
1. The "anti-involution" policy is changing the logic of industry competition, and the most intense period of price wars may have passed.
2. The high growth of the spare parts business provides new profit growth points
3. Repurchase and cancellation continue to increase the value of each share

### 4.3 Intrinsic value estimation

**Method 1: Discounted Free Cash Flow (DCF)**

Assumptions:
- Free cash flow will increase by 5-8% annually over the next five years
- Sustainable growth rate 3%
- Discount rate 10%
- Current free cash flow is approximately 5.5 billion yuan

A conservative estimate of the intrinsic value is about HK$160-200 billion, corresponding to an upside of 15-40% of the current market value.

**Method 2: Profit multiple method**

- Estimated adjusted net profit in 2026: 10.0-10.5 billion yuan
- Give 15 times PE (reasonable industry level): 1500-157.5 billion yuan
- Grant 12 times PE (conservative): 120-126 billion yuan
- Current market capitalization: approximately HK$140 billion

Under the 15 times PE assumption, the valuation is basically reasonable and slightly underestimated; under the 12 times PE assumption, the current price has fully reflected the pessimistic expectations.

### 4.4 Safety margin judgment

The current combination of 13 times PE, 17% ROE, and 22% debt ratio provides a certain margin of safety. The downside risk mainly comes from the resumption of price wars, leading to a sharp decline in profits. If the industry returns to the fierce price war of 2020-2021, profits may drop by 20-30%, the corresponding PE will rise to 16-18 times, and the safety margin will be consumed.

## 5. Financial risk points

### 5.1 Profit margin compression risk ★★★★☆

The adjusted net profit in 2025 will fall by 6.3% year-on-year, which is a warning sign. If the growth in unit volume cannot offset the decline in single-ticket profits, profit margins will continue to be under pressure.

### 5.2 Capital expenditure expansion risk ★★★☆☆

Industry competition forces companies to continue investing in automation and network construction. If capital expenditures continue to be higher than expected, free cash flow will be eroded.

### 5.3 Exchange rate risk ★★☆☆☆

ZTO is dual-listed on U.S. and Hong Kong stocks. Buybacks and dividends are denominated in US dollars, but revenue and costs are denominated in RMB. Currency fluctuations can affect the real value of shareholder returns.

---

## 6. Overall evaluation

### Financial Quality Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Advantages:**
- Excellent cash flow quality, cash conversion rate continues to be >100%
- Clean balance sheet and low debt ratio
- Strong shareholder returns, with a comprehensive return rate of repurchases + dividends of 6-8%
- Profitability leads the industry

**Disadvantages:**
- Profit growth is slowing down, and we need to be wary of the trend of "increasing revenue without increasing profits"
-Capital expenditure is still in the expansion cycle and has not yet entered the harvest period
- Single ticket profit is suppressed by industry competition

### Valuation Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

The current PE is about 13 times, which is reasonably low for a leading express delivery company. If the "anti-involution" policy continues to be effective and the component business maintains high growth, there is room for the valuation to recover to 15-16 times. But if the price war resumes, the current valuation’s margin of safety is not that thick.

**Core judgment:** Zhongtong's financial quality is "excellent but not outstanding". It has the best profit margins and cash flow in the express delivery industry, but the business itself has limited profit elasticity. The valuation is not expensive, but it is not a bargain either - it requires the cooperation of the industry's competitive environment to achieve an increase in valuation.
