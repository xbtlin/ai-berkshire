## Core Discovery

### Positive factors

1. **The king of profits in the express delivery industry. ** Net profit in 2025 is 9.24 billion yuan, and adjusted net profit is 9.51 billion yuan. The profitability is far ahead in the Tongda system. Ranking first in terms of business volume for ten consecutive years, scale barriers are real.

2. **Excellent cash flow and shareholder returns. ** Operating cash flow is approximately 12 billion yuan, and the cash conversion rate continues to be >100%. The cumulative repurchases exceeded US$2 billion and were cancelled, and the dividend + repurchase ratio was increased to 50% of the adjusted net profit. Comprehensive return rate is 6-8%.

3. **Anti-involution policies bring turning point signals. ** In Q1 of 2026, industry revenue growth exceeded business volume growth for the first time, and single ticket prices rebounded substantially. If the trend continues, profit elasticity is significant.

4. **The spare parts business opens the second curve. ** In 2025, the business volume of spare parts will increase by 46% year-on-year, with an average daily volume of 9.8 million pieces in Q4. The profit margin of bulk parts is higher than that of e-commerce parts, and it does not rely on platform distribution.

5. **The quality of management is trustworthy. ** Lai Meisong's "co-build and share" structure, low-debt financial discipline, and strategic determination to ensure profits make him the best manager in the Tongda Department.

### Negative factors

1. **The risk of price war is a sword hanging over the head. ** The structural conditions for industry competition (homogeneous products, low switching costs, overcapacity) have not been eliminated. The administrative binding force of the anti-involution policy is limited. Once growth slows down further, the price war may restart.

2. **The declining trend in market share requires vigilance. ** From 22% to 19.4%, although Q4 has recovered, Jitu’s offensive continues. If the share drops below 17%, the cost advantage will be significantly eroded.

3. **The hidden concern of increasing revenue without increasing profits. ** Revenue in 2025 is +10.9% but adjusted net profit is -6.3%, indicating that the quality of unit volume growth is declining.

4. The ** industry landscape is far from clear. ** Six or seven companies have coexisted for a long time, and the integration speed is much slower than expected. Investing in ZTO is essentially betting on the improvement of the industry structure, rather than simply betting on the company itself.

5. **The safety margin is not sufficient. ** The downside protection corresponding to 13 times PE is limited. If profits fall by 25%, PE will rise to 17 times, and the attractiveness of valuation will disappear.

---

## Investment Conclusion

**ZTO Express is the best company in the express delivery industry, but the express delivery industry is not the best industry. **

The current valuation of 13 times PE is in the middle zone of "not expensive but not cheap either". Looking upward, if the anti-involution policy continues to be effective, the component business maintains high growth, and the industry structure improves, there is room for the valuation to be repaired to 15-16 times (up about 20-25%). Looking downward, if the price war restarts, profits may fall by 25-30% and valuations will be compressed to less than 10 times (a decline of about 30-40%).

**Suitable investors:**
- Able to tolerate industry fluctuations and willing to hold for more than 3 years
- Focus on cash returns (dividends + buybacks combined 6-8%)
- Moderately optimistic about the improvement of the industry structure

**Not suitable for investors:**
- Pursue high certainty and thick margin of safety
- Unable to withstand the sharp fluctuations in profits caused by the resumption of price wars
- Expect to see significant valuation improvement within 1-2 years

**Key Tracking Metrics:**
1. Change trend of single ticket revenue (reflecting the intensity of price war)
2. Changes in market share (reflecting competitive position)
3. Growth rate of component business (reflecting the progress of the second curve)
4. Industry policy trends (continuity of anti-involution policies)
5. Jitu’s strategic trends (the biggest competitive variable)

## Cross-validation with other studies

The case of ZTO provides an investment issue worthy of repeated consideration: **A good company is in a highly competitive industry, how should you price it? **

13 times PE, 17% ROE, abundant cash flow - if this is the data of a consumer goods company or an Internet company, it is undoubtedly an underestimate. But because it is in the express delivery industry, the predictability and sustainability of profits are compromised. How much of this discount you apply depends on your judgment of the industry's eventual outcome. This is not an easy question to answer, and precisely because it is not easy to answer, the market gives a price that is neither high nor low.
