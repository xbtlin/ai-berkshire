## Research background

ZTO Express is the company with the largest business volume and the strongest profitability in China's express delivery industry. It has maintained the leading market share for ten consecutive years. The company adopts the "terminal franchise + sorting direct operation" model to build competitive barriers with cost advantages. The current PE is about 13 times, ROE is about 17%, and a cumulative repurchase plan of US$3.5 billion has been launched. The core concern lies in the persistence of price wars in the express delivery industry and the fluctuation of market share.
