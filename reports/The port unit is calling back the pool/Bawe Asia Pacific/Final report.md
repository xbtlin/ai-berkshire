## One sentence summary

Budweiser Asia Pacific has excellent brand equity and a simple business model, but the governance structure flaws under the control of the parent company, the continued loss of market share in China, and the valuation that lacks a margin of safety make it ineligible for value investment standards.

## Core investment arguments

### Reasons to be bullish

1. **Strong brand equity**: Global brands such as Budweiser and Corona still have high recognition in the Asia-Pacific market
2. **Indian market growth**: High-end product portfolio grew by 20%+, with great long-term potential
3. **Higher Dividend Yield**: The dividend yield of about 5.5% is attractive to income investors
4. **The nature of the beer business is good**: high repurchase, brand premium, good cash flow
5. **Valuation has fallen back to historical lows**: PE dropped from 40 times to 18-20 times

### Reasons to be bearish

1. **China’s share continues to lose**: from 50% to 40%, the trend continues
2. **Profits continue to decline sharply**: Net profit in 2025 will be almost halved compared with 2022
3. **The parent company is seriously bleeding**: 153% dividend payout rate consumes company assets
4. **Valuation is still not cheap enough**: 18-20 times PE is too high for companies with negative growth
5. **The national trend is irreversible**: The structural premium of foreign brands is shrinking
6. **Management does not serve minority shareholders**: The governance structure determines the unfair distribution of benefits.

## Investment advice

### Conclusion: Not recommended to buy

**Core reasons**:
- Fundamental flaws in the governance structure cannot be fixed by time or management changes
- Competitive headwinds + valuations not cheap enough = negative expected returns
- There are better beer industry targets than Budweiser Asia Pacific (such as China Resources Beer)

### At what price level will become attractive?

- When the stock price is 5-6 Hong Kong dollars (corresponding to 12-14 times expected PE), the margin of safety begins to appear
- But even if the price is in place, governance structure issues are still a flaw

### Alternative target suggestions

If you are optimistic about the high-end trend of Chinese beer:
- **China Resources Beer (0291.HK)**: rising share, improving governance, lower valuation
- **Tsingtao Beer H (0168.HK)**: Strong brand power, low valuation, benefiting from the national trend

## Risk warning

- The unexpected recovery of China's economy may lead to a rebound in high-end consumption, and Budweiser, as a high-end leading brand, may benefit in the short term
- The new CEO may bring about strategic changes that exceed expectations
- The Indian market may grow faster than expected to make up for the decline in China
- If the parent company reduces its "blood draw" due to its own financial improvement, Budweiser Asia Pacific's capital allocation may improve
