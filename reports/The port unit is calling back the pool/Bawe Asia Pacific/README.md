## Company Profile

Budweiser Asia Pacific is the Asia-Pacific business platform of AB InBev, the world's largest beer group. It was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2019. The company operates multiple beer brands such as Budweiser, Corona, Harbin, and Fujia, and its business covers Asia-Pacific markets such as China, South Korea, India, and Vietnam.

## Core Business Structure (2025)

1. **Asia Pacific West (including China)**: Revenue accounts for about 65%, and China is the largest single market
2. **Asia Pacific East (South Korea, Japan)**: Revenue accounts for about 15%
3. **South Asia (India, Vietnam)**: Revenue accounts for about 20%, fastest growing
