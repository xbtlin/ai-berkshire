# Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (1398.HK) - Investment Research Final Report

## Overall rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.0/5.0)

## One sentence summary

ICBC is the world's largest bank and the backbone of China's financial system. Its 5-6 times PE + 6-7% dividend rate provides a safe interest return, but its ROE is lower than the cost of capital and its governance structure limits value creation. It is essentially more like a financial utility than a high-quality investment target.

## Core investment logic

### Reasons to be bullish
1. **Extremely low valuation provides safety cushion**: 0.5 times PB, 5-6 times PE, implying extremely pessimistic expectations
2. **High dividends and sustainable**: 6-7% dividend rate, dividend rate is only 30%, with room for improvement
3. **"Too big to fail"**: The country will not let anything happen to ICBC, the ultimate safety guarantee
4. **Policy Catalyst**: "China Special Valuation" encourages central enterprises to increase dividends and valuations
5. **Non-performing ratio continues to improve**: 1.31% has dropped for four consecutive years, and asset quality is controllable

### Reasons to be bearish
1. **ROE is lower than the cost of capital**: 9.5% ROE cannot create excess value for shareholders
2. **Net interest margin continues to narrow**: 1.28% and trending downward, profit growth is limited
3. **Structural Issues in Governance**: Serving the country takes precedence over serving shareholders
4. **Hidden risks are difficult to assess**: The true exposure of local debt and real estate cannot be penetrated by the outside world
5. **No solution to "giving profits to the real economy"**: Policy-based profit transfer is a long-term trend

**Conclusion**: The two are highly convergent. China Construction Bank's PE is lower but its ROE is slightly higher. On the margin, CCB's performance-to-price ratio is slightly better, but the actual difference is not significant.

## Suitable investor types

- **Interest-earning investors**: 6-7% dividend rate is the core attraction, suitable for replacing deposits/bonds
- **Investors who "believe in the national destiny"**: If you believe that China's economy can continue to grow, the quality of bank assets will be fine
- **Not suitable**: Long-term investors pursuing compound interest growth (ROE does not support high compound returns)
- **Not suitable**: Investors with high requirements for governance quality

## Conclusion and suggestions

ICBC is a safe but mediocre investment target**. Its safety comes from "too big to fail" and extremely low valuation, and its mediocrity comes from insufficient ROE, limited growth, and discounted governance.

**The essence of investing in ICBC is a "bet"**: You are betting that the Chinese economy will not experience systemic risks + dividends will gradually increase + "mid-special valuation" will bring about valuation restoration. If all three are realized, annual returns of 10-15% can be expected. If you only cash out the first item, it will be a "bank deposit substitute" of 6-7%.

For value investors who pursue "good company + good price", ICBC may not be "good enough" - it is "not a bad company + cheap price".
