# Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (1398.HK)—Business Model Analysis

## 1. The essence of business

ICBC is essentially China's largest financial intermediary and payment settlement infrastructure. But unlike general commercial banks, it also assumes "quasi-policy" functions:

1. **Deposits-Lending**: Use the country’s widest network of outlets (15,000+) to absorb deposits and issue loans
2. **Payment and Settlement**: Provide infrastructure for the operation of the national economy
3. **Policy Tools**: Cooperate with national macro-control - serve the real economy, support infrastructure, and stabilize employment

**Core Cognition**: ICBC is not a commercial entity that purely pursues maximizing shareholder returns. It is the backbone of the country's financial system first and listed companies second. This determines all its business decisions.

## 2. Moat Assessment ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Strengths:**

- **License Barrier + National Credit Endorsement**: Large commercial bank license + state-owned background = implicit government guarantee. Depositors will not worry about the collapse of ICBC. This is the strongest "trust moat"
- **Scale cannot be copied**: 53.48 trillion yuan in total assets, 15,000 outlets, hundreds of millions of customers - no new entrant can copy this scale
- **System Importance**: "Too big to fail" itself is a barrier
- **Low-cost deposits**: A large number of corporate settlement accounts and salary payment accounts provide low-interest demand deposits

**Weaknesses:**

- **Not a "selective" moat**: ICBC's barriers come from scale and system, not product differentiation or brand preference. Customers often choose ICBC because of "convenience/safety" rather than "better products"
- **Homogeneous competition**: The products of the six major banks are highly similar and lack differentiation except for scale.
- **Policy Compliance**: Part of the moat comes from policy grants, and policies can also erode returns (such as benefiting the real economy)

## 3. "Simplicity" test of business model

**Is this a business that Duan Yongping would invest in? Most likely not. **

Reason:
- **Management is not selected by the market**: CEO is appointed by the organization, not selected by the board of directors based on ability
- **Business objectives are not single**: not only maximizing shareholder returns, but also taking into account employment, stability, and policy guidance
- **Limited pricing power**: Deposit and loan interest rates are guided by LPR and cannot be freely set
- **It is difficult to judge "doing the right thing"**: For ICBC, is the "right thing" to maximize the interests of shareholders or the country? The two often conflict

**But the model itself is simple**: absorb savings - lend - charge, unchanged for a hundred years. The complexity is not in the model, but in the governance.

## 4. User value and "differentiation"

Value created by ICBC for customers:
- **Corporate customers**: The largest credit line capacity (single loan can be tens of billions), national settlement network
- **Retail customers**: the most outlets, the most complete product lines, and "a sense of security"
- **Government Clients**: Cooperate with fiscal fund allocation and local bond issuance

**Extremely weak differentiation**: Compared with China Construction Bank, Agricultural Bank of China, and Bank of China, ICBC’s products and services are almost indistinguishable. Choosing ICBC was more due to inertia than preference.

## 5. Long-term sustainability ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Pros:**
- The Chinese economy still needs large banks as credit intermediaries, and this role will not disappear.
- "Too big to fail" provides certainty of survival
- Dividends of RMB 110 billion per year provide stable returns
- The defective rate continued to improve at 1.31%

**Disadvantages:**
- Net interest margin is 1.28% and continues to narrow (-14bp), profit growth is limited
- "Benefits to the real economy" may continue to suppress ROE
- Fintech (WeChat Pay, Alipay) cannibalizes retail payment fees
- Hidden local government debt risks - ICBC may be required to "extend" rather than "recover"

## 6. General comments
