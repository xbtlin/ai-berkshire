# Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (1398.HK) – Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Profit quality analysis

### Revenue structure (2025)
- Operating income: 838.270 billion yuan
- Net interest income: approximately 580-600 billion yuan (accounting for ~70%)
- Net fee and commission income: 111.171 billion yuan
- Other non-interest income: investment income, etc.

### Profit Trend
- Net profit: 370.766 billion yuan (daily average 1.005 billion yuan)
- Profit growth: low single-digit growth (matching revenue growth)
- Profit before provision may better reflect true operating capabilities

### Profit quality judgment ★★★☆☆

**Front**:
- The income is huge and stable, and will not fluctuate significantly.
- Fee income of RMB111.1 billion provides a non-interest-rate sensitive income base
- Provision coverage ratio is 213.60%, and there is a "reservoir" to smooth profits

**Negative**:
- Net interest margin was 1.28% and decreased by 14bp year-on-year - this is the biggest profitability pressure
- Reasons for the decline in net interest margin: LPR continues to decrease + deposit competition costs increase
- Profit growth is highly dependent on asset scale expansion (total assets +9.5%) rather than efficiency improvements
- The "price based on quantity" model is unsustainable - capital consumption is accelerating

## 2. Capital efficiency and return

### Key Capital Indicators
| Indicators | 2025 | Description |
|------|--------|------|
| ROE | ~9.5% | Below cost of capital (~10-12%) |
| ROA | ~0.75% | Typical level in the banking industry |
| Capital adequacy ratio | 18.76% | Far exceeding regulatory requirements |
| Dividend rate | ~30% | Gradually increased in recent years |

### Capital Efficiency Assessment ★★★☆☆

**Core question: ROE < COE (cost of capital)**

-ICBC's ROE is about 9.5%, assuming the cost of equity capital is 10-12% (emerging market risk premium)
- This means that for every 1 yuan of equity invested by ICBC, the value created is less than 1 yuan.
- Theoretically, PB should be <1 times (this is also true in practice)
- **This is a common dilemma faced by all major Chinese banks**

**Why is ROE low? **
1. Net interest margin continues to narrow (policy benefits)
2. Excessive capital (18.76% CAR) and too large denominator
3. Credit growth must match national economic goals rather than pursue optimal ROE.

## 3. Balance sheet health

### Capital Adequacy
- Capital adequacy ratio 18.76%: far exceeding the regulatory lower limit of 8%
- Have adequate capital buffers to deal with any shocks
- But it also means that capital utilization is not efficient

### Asset Quality ★★★★☆
-Non-performing loan ratio is 1.31%, falling for four consecutive years
- Provision coverage ratio 213.60% - for every 1 yuan of non-performing loans, 2.13 yuan of provisions are covered
- Reasonable overdue loan/non-performing loan ratio
- **Potential Risk**: The true quality of local government’s implicit debt is difficult to judge from financial reports

### Implied asset quality risk
- "Extension without downgrade" in local urban investment bond/implicit debt restructuring
- Exposure to the real estate industry (although it has been reduced, the margin is still large)
- The vulnerability of SME loans to economic downturns
- These risks may be temporarily masked by the "time for space" strategy

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| PE (TTM) | ~5-6x |
| PB | ~0.5-0.6 times |
| Dividend Yield | ~6-7% |
| ROE | ~9.5% |

### Valuation rationality judgment ★★★★☆

**What does PE 5-6 times mean? **
- Market implicit assumption: ICBC’s future profit growth is close to 0% or the quality of profits is questionable
- A dividend yield of 6%+ means that even with zero growth, holding returns are acceptable
- PB 0.5-0.6 times means that the market believes that there are "hidden losses" that are not reflected in the net assets

**Why so cheap? **
1. ROE < COE → PB < 1 is reasonable
2. Market pricing of "hidden bad"
3. Discount on state-owned enterprise governance
4. Discount for lack of growth

**Is it a "value trap"? **
- If the net interest margin continues to narrow and profits start to grow negatively - the current PE may be higher (due to falling profits)
- If local debt/real estate risks are concentrated - substantial provisions will be required
- But if the above risks do not explode - 6-7% dividend rate + occasional slight increase in profits = annualized return of 8-10%

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Downside Protection**:
- 0.5x PB already implies quite pessimistic assumptions
- The state will not let anything happen to ICBC - the implicit guarantee provides the ultimate safety cushion
- 6-7% dividend yield provides the returns you have been waiting for

**Upside Flexibility**:
- If ROE rises back to 12%+, PB should be repaired to 0.8-1.0 times (+30-60%)
- If the dividend rate is increased from 30% to 50%+ (there is a precedent: ICBC’s 2025 dividend rate has been increased)
- If "Zhongte Valuation" (valuation system with Chinese characteristics) continues to promote the revaluation of state-owned enterprises

## 5. Cash flow and shareholder returns

### Dividend performance
- Dividends for the whole year of 2025 will be approximately RMB 110.593 billion
- 0.1689 yuan per share for A shares (tax included)
- Dividend ratio is about 30%
- Calculated based on H share price, the dividend yield is about 6-7%

### Dividend trend
- The dividend rate has increased from 25% to 30% in recent years
- Policy orientation (the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission requires central enterprises to increase dividends) to promote further improvement
- Target: The dividend rate may gradually increase to 35-40%

### Shareholder Return Sustainability ★★★★☆
- The current dividend payout ratio of 30% is very conservative and there is huge room for improvement
- Provision coverage ratio of 213% means there are "profit reserves" to release
- Constraints: capital adequacy ratio needs to be maintained → limits the maximum dividend payout space
