# VI. Methodological description and limitations

1. ** Data source**: Data from public sources, mainly company annuals, GuruFocus, Investment.com, almonds, Eastern wealth, co-pollus, voucher research, etc.
2. **ROE calculation**: Using a weighted average net asset rate of return, 10 years refers to financial year 2015-2024
3. ** Free cash flows**: net cash flows from operating activities - capital expenditures
4. ** Interest coverage multiple**: EBIT/ Interest cost
5. ** Limitations**:
- Some company data are estimates and actual accuracy is required to be consulted in the full annual report
- The property, gambling and gambling industries were severely affected by the 2020-2022 epidemic and were cyclically distorted
- ReIT-type companies (exhibits) account for profits by revalued property, which is not entirely comparable to the average business
- Semiconductor manufacture (mid core, rainbow) is in the national strategic input period, and FCF is responsible for its reasonableness
** Determination principle**: Strict implementation of the red line standard and preference for exclusion in border cases (preferably by mistake)
