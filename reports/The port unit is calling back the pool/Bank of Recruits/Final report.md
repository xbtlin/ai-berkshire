## 2. Core investment logic

### Look at the bull case

1. **China's most high-quality retail bank**: The retail AUM of 17.08 trillion yuan is more than three times that of the second joint-stock bank. The non-performing rate of 0.94% is the only joint-stock bank below 1%. The provision coverage rate of 388% is a thick safety cushion. These moat figures will not be tied anytime soon.

2. **Valuation has reflected pessimistic expectations**: The implicit assumption of Hong Kong stock PB of 0.92 times is that China Merchants Bank will destroy value in the long term (ROE < COE), which is seriously inconsistent with the reality of ROE of 13.4%. The market is pricing in a "worst-case scenario," but the probability of the worst-case scenario happening is limited.

3. **Significant dividend returns**: 6.5% dividend rate + annualized growth of intrinsic value of about 8%. Even if the valuation is not restored, the long-term annualized return can reach 10%+. In the low interest rate environment of Hong Kong stocks, this rate of return is attractive.

4. **Revenue turning point may occur**: 2026Q1 revenue growth rate is 3.81%, and net interest income growth rate is 4.99%, which is the first significant improvement after three consecutive years of zero growth. If the trend continues, market expectations may revise.

### Bear Case

1. **The bottom of the net interest margin is not yet confirmed**: Management said that "it is expected to stabilize in the second half of the year", but this is only an expectation and not a fact. If LPR continues to decrease, the interest rate spread may further fall below 1.5%, and ROE will drop to around 10%.

2. **Retail credit risk is on the rise**: The credit card non-performing rate increased from 1.74% to 1.9%, and management admitted frankly that it is "still on the rise." If consumer confidence continues to be sluggish, this number may exceed 2.5%.

3. **Coach change increases uncertainty**: Wang Xiaoqing has just taken office, and the strategic direction and execution ability have not yet been verified. The honeymoon period for a new officer can last 6-12 months, during which it is difficult to judge the quality of management.

4. **Macro tail risk**: Although the probability of the "Japanization" of China's economy, real estate and local debt systemic risks is not high, once it occurs, PB may fall below 0.5 times.

## 3. Investment advice

### Current judgment: **Observe the position / wait for a better opportunity**

**Reasons not to rush into large positions**:

1. The turning point of net interest margin is still unclear, and improvement in 2026Q1 will need to be confirmed in at least 2-3 quarters.
2. The new management is still in the transition period, and its strategic direction and execution capabilities have not been verified.
3. PB 0.92 times has a safety margin, but it is not an extreme underestimation of "you can make money even by throwing darts"
4. Uncertainty in the global macro environment remains high

## 4. Final conclusion

China Merchants Bank is the target closest to the standard of "good company" in China's banking industry. 17 trillion retail AUM, a non-performing ratio of 0.94%, and a provision coverage ratio of nearly 400%. These data form a solid fundamental base. The valuation of the Hong Kong stock market with a PB of 0.92 times and a dividend yield of 6.5% already contains quite pessimistic expectations.

But "good company" does not mean "buy now." The continued narrowing of net interest margins, rising retail credit risks, and the transition period for management changes—these three uncertain factors add up to make this not the best time to enter the market.

**To summarize in one sentence: China Merchants Bank is a "candidate position" worthy of long-term tracking, and is currently in the "observation range" rather than the "sale range". Be patient and wait for opportunities when valuations become more extreme or when fundamental inflection points become clearer. **

If forced to make a choice at current prices:

- **No touching at all? ** Too conservative. With a combination of 6.5% dividend yield + 13% ROE, you won’t lose a lot of money in the long run.
- **Buy heavy position? ** Too aggressive. Many uncertainties have yet to be lifted.
- **Small position observation? ** Reasonable choice. Use small positions to establish tracking discipline and wait for better prices or clearer signals.
