## Core conclusion

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5) — High-quality assets, waiting for better prices**

China Merchants Bank is a rare target with "good business" characteristics in China's banking industry. Retail AUM exceeded 17 trillion yuan, private banking customers exceeded 190,000, the non-performing rate continued to remain at an industry low of 0.94%, and the provision coverage rate was nearly 388%. These data form a solid fundamental base.

But the challenges are equally clear: the net interest margin has narrowed from 2.15% in 2023 to 1.83% in 2026Q1, revenue has been nearly zero growth for three consecutive years, the credit card business is under pressure, and the strategic uncertainty caused by the coaching change remains to be seen. The PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 6.5 times, the PB is about 0.92 times, and the dividend yield is about 6.5%. The valuation has reflected a considerable degree of pessimistic expectations.

**Core Judgment**: China Merchants Bank is the "right company", but currently it is not the "perfect price". The signal that the net interest margin has bottomed out is still unclear, and the management transition period has increased short-term uncertainty. It is recommended to continue to follow up and focus on observing: (1) the inflection point of net interest margin stabilization; (2) the strategic direction of the new management; (3) the evolution of credit card and retail credit risks. If the PB of Hong Kong stocks falls below 0.8 times (corresponding to a stock price of approximately HK$44), the margin of safety will be significantly increased.
