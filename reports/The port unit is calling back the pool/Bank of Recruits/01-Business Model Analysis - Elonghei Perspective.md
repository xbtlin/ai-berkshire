# China Merchants Bank: Business Model Analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Good Business Judgment | Research Date: May 16, 2026 | Currency: RMB

## 1. Core question: Is China Merchants Bank a "good business"?

Duan Yongping's core criterion for judging a good business is very simple: **Can this business make money in the long term? Can it get better and better? Can it benefit both consumers and enterprises? ** In his words, good business must have a "culture of the right" - do the right thing and do it right.

Banks are essentially risky businesses, and most banks can hardly be called "good businesses" because of their high leverage, serious homogeneity, and strong cyclicality. But China Merchants Bank is a special entity in China's banking industry - it has spent twenty years developing a differentiated retail banking path, which is rare in the global banking industry.

**Conclusion first: China Merchants Bank is the closest target to "good business" in China's banking industry, but the inherent limitations of the banking industry prevent it from meeting the "good business" standard at the consumer goods level. Rating ★★★★☆. **

## 2. Dismantling of business model

### 2.1 The nature of China Merchants Bank’s business

China Merchants Bank's business model can be summarized as follows: **With retail customers as the cornerstone, wealth management as the engine, and technology as the driver, we build a positive cycle of low-cost liabilities + high-quality assets. **

Specifically:

1. **Low-cost liability side**: As of the end of 2025, CMB’s retail customers will exceed 200 million, with retail AUM reaching 17.08 trillion yuan. The huge retail customer base brings a large amount of demand deposits and financial management funds, making China Merchants Bank's debt cost lower than its peers in the long term. In 2025, CMB’s deposit interest rate will be 1.52%, which is among the lowest among joint-stock banks.

2. **High-yield asset side**: The pricing power of retail loans (mortgages, consumer loans, credit cards) is higher than that of corporate loans, and they have good dispersion, small single amounts, and controllable risks. In 2025, China Merchants Bank's non-performing rate will be only 0.94%, making it the only institution among joint-stock banks to remain below 1%.

3. **Intermediary business income**: Light capital businesses such as wealth management, asset management, and custody contribute a large amount of fee income. Net non-interest income will reach 121.9 billion yuan in 2025, accounting for approximately 36% of revenue. The stock premium of insurance agency sales exceeded 1 trillion yuan.

### 2.2 Does the "flywheel effect" exist?

There is a clear flywheel in CMB’s business model:

> High-quality services → Attract high-quality customers → Low-cost funds → Higher interest margins → More profits invested in services and technology → Better services

Key evidence for this flywheel:

- **Extremely strong customer stickiness**: The monthly active users of China Merchants Bank App and Mobile Life App have reached 123 million, and digital services cover all scenarios such as deposits, loans and foreign exchange, financial management, and life payment. 13.84% of retail customers have achieved diversified asset allocation, which means that customers not only save money, but also use CMB as the main platform for wealth management.

- **The Matthew Effect is obvious**: There are more than 190,000 private banking customers and nearly 3.4 trillion yuan in assets under management. Once a high-net-worth customer forms a trusting relationship, migration costs are extremely high. The AUM of CMB’s mid-to-high-end private banking customers accounts for 31.28%, which is much higher than that of its peers such as CITIC, Everbright, and Industrial Bank.

- **Continued investment in technology**: China Merchants Bank’s annual technology investment exceeds 4% of revenue. Its leading edge in financial technology enables its digital experience to continue to lead the industry.

## 3. Highlights of "Good Business"

### 3.1 Structural advantages of the liability side

China Merchants Bank's greatest competitive advantage lies on the liability side. While other banks are still relying on high interest rates to attract deposits, China Merchants Bank has attracted a large amount of low-cost funds through its services and brand. This was not established overnight, but the result of twenty years of continuous investment.

The data speaks for itself: China Merchants Bank’s retail AUM has grown from approximately 7 trillion in 2018 to 17.08 trillion in 2025, an increase of nearly 1.5 times in seven years. This growth does not depend on interest rate competition, but on service quality and customer trust. This is a typical embodiment of what Duan Yongping calls "doing the right thing".

### 3.2 Transformation from "deposit and loan spread" to "wealth management"

China Merchants Bank is transforming from the traditional "earning interest rate differential" model to the "earning management fee" model. Intermediary businesses such as fund sales, insurance, and financial management do not consume capital and are truly asset-light businesses. The stock of agency insurance premiums has exceeded 1 trillion yuan. If this direction continues to expand, the quality of China Merchants Bank's business model will be further improved.

### 3.3 Risk control culture

The life and death line of a bank is risk control. China Merchants Bank's non-performing ratio has remained below 1% for a long time, and its provision coverage ratio is close to 400%, which shows that the management is disciplined in risk management. Duan Yongping said, "Don't do the wrong thing." China Merchants Bank's restraint in risk control - such as proactively shrinking the high-risk credit card customer group - reflects this philosophy.

## 4. The hidden worries of "good business"

### 4.1 Inherent flaws in the banking industry

No matter how good CMB is, it is still a bank. Problems inherent in the banking industry include:

- **High Leverage**: Banks operate with about 10 times leverage. Once asset quality deteriorates, shareholders' equity may be quickly damaged.
- **Strong Supervision**: Interest rates, capital adequacy ratios, loan investments, etc. are all subject to strict supervision, and operating freedom is limited.
- **Procyclical**: When the economy goes down, banks' asset quality and profitability will come under pressure.

This is the fundamental reason why Duan Yongping rarely invests in bank stocks - banks are not "simple" enough and have too many variables.

### 4.2 Structural pressure for narrowing net interest margin

This is the biggest negative factor currently. China Merchants Bank's net interest margin continued to narrow from 2.15% in 2023 to 1.83% in 2026Q1 (a year-on-year decrease of 8 basis points). Although management stated that it is expected to stabilize in the second half of 2026, the general direction of interest rate liberalization means that the downward movement of the interest rate center is a long-term trend.

Every 10 basis points decrease in net interest margin will affect net interest income by approximately 12 billion yuan. If the interest rate spread drops below 1.5% in the next few years, China Merchants Bank's profitability will face a substantial test.

### 4.3 Rising retail credit risks

2026Q1 data shows that credit card loan balances decreased by 38.6 billion yuan compared with the end of the previous year, and the credit card non-performing rate increased from 1.74% to 1.9%. Management has made it clear that "market-wide retail credit risks are still on the rise." This is consistent with the current macroeconomic background of insufficient consumer confidence and rising employment pressure.

### 4.4 Revenue growth stagnated

Revenue in 2025 will be 337.5 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of only 0.01%—basically zero growth. Although the revenue growth rate rebounded to 3.81% in 2026Q1, revenue has remained almost unchanged for three consecutive years, indicating that the growth engine has temporarily stalled. For a "good business", stagnant growth is a sign that requires great attention.

## 5. General evaluation of business model

### Overall rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

China Merchants Bank's position in China's banking industry is similar to Apple's position in the mobile phone industry - it is not the largest, but it has the strongest brand power, the highest customer loyalty and the best earnings quality in the market segment.

**Summary using Duan Yongping’s framework**:

- **Do the right thing**: Choosing the direction of retail banking twenty years ago was a correct strategic choice, and it is still enjoying dividends today.
- **Do things right**: Continuous investment in technology, services, and talents, the flywheel effect has been formed
- **Corporate Culture**: CMB has a strong "customer-oriented" culture, which is not common in the Chinese banking industry

**But we must be soberly aware**: Banks are not consumer goods after all, and their business model complexity and risk exposure are much higher than Duan Yongping’s preferred businesses such as OPPO/vivo or Moutai. China Merchants Bank is a good business in the bank, but it is not the kind of "good business" that Duan Yongping would take a heavy position in. Investors need to remain disciplined about prices while recognizing their quality.
