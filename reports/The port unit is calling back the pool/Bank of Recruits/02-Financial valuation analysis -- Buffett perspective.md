# China Merchants Bank: Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Value Investment Judgment | Research Date: May 16, 2026 | Currency: RMB (Hong Kong stock prices are in Hong Kong dollars)

## 1. Core question: Does the current price provide a sufficient margin of safety?

Buffett has three iron rules for investing in banking stocks: ** (1) ROE is excellent in the long term; (2) Management operates prudently; (3) Buy at a reasonable price. ** When he invested heavily in Wells Fargo in 1990, it was the time of the U.S. savings and loan crisis, and bank stocks were generally sold off. However, he saw that Wells Fargo's ROE and asset quality were far better than those of its peers. He bought it at 5 times PE and held it for more than 20 years.

China Merchants Bank's situation today is similar to Wells Fargo's back then: the financial industry as a whole is pessimistic and valuations are at historically low levels, but the company's fundamentals are far better than those of its peers. ** The question is – to what extent are they similar? Is the safety margin enough?

**Conclusion first: CMB's financial quality is among the best in China's banking industry, but the current valuation (Hong Kong stock PE 6.5 times, PB 0.92 times) only reflects part of the pessimistic expectations and has not yet reached the level of "significant underestimation". Rating ★★★★☆. **

## 2. Financial quality analysis

### 2.1 Profitability: ROE breakdown

Buffett's most important banking indicator is ROE. China Merchants Bank’s ROE performance:

| Year | Weighted ROE | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|---------|
| 2021 | 16.96% | — |
| 2022 | 17.06% | +0.10pct |
| 2023 | 16.22% | -0.84pct |
| 2024 | 14.49% | -1.73pct |
| 2025 | 13.44% | -1.05pct |

ROE dropped from 17% to 13.4%, and the trend is downward. This is an indisputable fact. But even the ROE of 13.4% is still at the forefront of China's banking industry, and the ROE of most joint-stock banks has dropped below 10%.

**DRIVING FACTORS FOR ROE DECLINE**:

1. **Narrowing of net interest margin**: from 2.15% in 2023 to 1.87% in 2025, directly compressing net interest income
2. **Intermediary business income declines**: Net non-interest income will decrease by 3.38% year-on-year in 2025, affected by capital market fluctuations and declining rates.
3. **Capital adequacy ratio improvement**: The denominator (net assets) grows faster than the numerator (net profit), and ROE naturally declines

Key judgment: The main reason for the decline in ROE is the macro environment (lowering interest rates, economic slowdown), not the deterioration of China Merchants Bank's own operations. In the cold winter of the industry, it is not easy to maintain an ROE of 13%+.

### 2.2 Asset quality: NPL ratio and provisions

Buffett said: "When the tide goes out, you will know who is swimming naked." Asset quality is the ultimate criterion for distinguishing good banks from bad banks.

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026Q1 |
|------|------|------|------|--------|
| NPL ratio | 0.95% | 0.95% | 0.94% | 0.94% |
| Provision coverage ratio | 437.68% | 411.98% | 391.79% | 387.76% |
| Proportion of special-mention loans | 1.20% | 1.31% | — | — |

A few key observations:

1. **Non-performing rate is as stable as a rock**: It has been maintained at 0.94%-0.95% for many consecutive years, which is extremely rare in an economic downturn. During the same period, the non-performing ratios of most banks were rising.

2. **Although the provision coverage ratio has dropped, it is still extremely high**: 387.76% means that for every 1 yuan of non-performing loans, there is 3.88 yuan of provision coverage, which is sufficient "ammunition". It's also a profit reservoir - if asset quality improves in the future, provision write-downs will unlock significant profits.

3. **Credit cards are the biggest concern**: The credit card non-performing rate increased from 1.74% to 1.9%, and the loan balance decreased by 38.6 billion yuan. Management is actively "exchanging quantity for quality" - shrinking scale to control risks. This is the right approach, but it will have a negative impact on revenue in the short term.

### 3.2 PB Valuation Framework

For bank stocks, PB is a more reasonable valuation anchor. Theoretically, PB = ROE / (Ke - g), where Ke is the cost of equity and g is the sustainable growth rate.

**Current Implied Assumptions**: PB 0.92 times means that the market believes that CMB's future ROE will be lower than the shareholder required rate of return in the long term - in other words, the market is saying "CMB is destroying value."

**Is this assumption reasonable? **

- China Merchants Bank’s current ROE is 13.4%. Even assuming it drops to 10% in the future, it is still higher than the required return on equity of most investors (usually 8-12%)
- PB < 1 implies that the market is either overestimating risks (such as large-scale bad loans) or overreacting to short-term interest margin pressures
- If the net interest margin stabilizes in the second half of 2026, ROE is expected to be stable in the 12-13% range, and the corresponding reasonable PB should be 1.0-1.2 times

### 3.3 Dividend Perspective

China Merchants Bank's 2025 dividend plan has not yet been announced, but with reference to the 2024 cash dividend per share of 1.972 yuan (tax included), the dividend rate of Hong Kong stocks is about 6.5%.

A few key points:

- The dividend payout rate is approximately 35%, maintaining a stable dividend while maintaining a high capital adequacy ratio.
- The compound growth rate of dividends in the past five years is approximately 10%, and dividend growth is sustainable
- The dividend yield of 6.5% is very attractive among the blue-chip Hong Kong stocks

Buffett's logic: If a company can continue to operate at 13% + ROE, distribute 35% of profits to shareholders, and reinvest the remaining 65% with the same efficiency, then the intrinsic value will increase by approximately 8.7% per year (13.4% x 65%), plus the dividend rate is 6.5%, and the annualized total return can reach about 15%.

But the premise is that ROE does not fall further sharply.

### 3.4 Safety Margin Assessment

**Conservative scenario (net interest margin continues to decline, ROE drops to 10%)**:
- A reasonable PB is about 0.8-0.9 times, and the current valuation basically reflects conservative expectations
- The corresponding stock price of Hong Kong stocks is about 43-48 Hong Kong dollars

**Neutral scenario (net interest margin stabilizes, ROE stabilizes at 12-13%)**:
- A reasonable PB is about 1.0-1.2 times, corresponding to a Hong Kong stock price of about HK$54-65
- There is 7%-29% upside potential relative to the current situation

**Optimistic scenario (economic recovery, interest rate spread rebounds, ROE rebounds to 14%+)**:
- A reasonable PB is about 1.3-1.5 times, corresponding to the Hong Kong stock price of about 70-81 Hong Kong dollars
- 39%-60% upside potential

## 5. Key financial risk points

### 5.1 Where is the "floor" of net interest margin?

The experience of the Japanese banking industry shows that in a long-term low interest rate environment, banks’ net interest margins can be reduced to less than 1%. If China becomes Japanized, China Merchants Bank's profit center will move further downwards. However, the economic structures of China and Japan are different, and the central bank is also working hard to avoid falling into the zero interest rate trap. The probability of complete Japanization is not high, but it is not impossible for the net interest margin to fall to about 1.5%.

### 5.2 Tail risks of real estate and local debt

Although China Merchants Bank's real estate loan exposure is relatively controllable (retail housing loans are mainly retail loans and development loans account for a small proportion), as a systemically important bank, systemic risks cannot be completely avoided. The good news is that the work to ensure the delivery of housing is progressing smoothly (the delivery rate is 99%), and real estate risks are gradually being resolved.

### 5.3 The "sustainability" issue of profits

China Merchants Bank's net profit has increased slightly in recent years, mainly through cost reduction and release of provisions. If revenue continues to not grow, the room for profit growth will become increasingly narrow. The revenue growth rate of 3.81% in 2026Q1 is a positive signal, but it needs more quarterly verification.

## 6. Valuation summary

### Overall rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Conclusion**: China Merchants Bank's financial quality is second to none in China's banking industry - low non-performing ratio, high provisions, stable dividends - these are the qualities that Buffett values most in bank investments. The valuation of Hong Kong stocks with PB of 0.92 times, PE of 6.5 times, and dividend yield of 6.5% already contains quite pessimistic expectations.

**But where is the missing "one star"? ** Although the margin of safety exists, it is not extreme enough. When Buffett bought Wells Fargo, it was 5 times PE, and when he bought Bank of America, it was far less than 0.5 times tangible PB. The current 0.92 times PB of China Merchants Bank means that the market is only "moderately pessimistic" rather than "extremely panicked". If the Hong Kong stock market PB falls to 0.7-0.8 times (which requires further systemic panic in the market), the safety margin will be more sufficient.

**Questions investors should ask themselves**: Do you believe that China Merchants Bank’s average ROE can remain above 10% in the next 10 years? If the answer is "yes", then the current price has a high probability of not losing money in the long run. But if you want "significant excess returns," you may need to wait for a better price.
