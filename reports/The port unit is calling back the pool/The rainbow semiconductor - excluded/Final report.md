# Hua Hong Semiconductor (1347.HK) - Investment Research Final Report

> Research date: May 16, 2026 | Stock price: approximately HK$141 | Market value: approximately RMB 220 billion

## 1. Core investment thesis

### Multi logic
1. Mainland China’s leading OEM for distinctive craftsmanship, with high certainty of demand for domestic substitution
2. After the FAB9 production capacity ramp is completed, huge profit elasticity will be released (fixed depreciation + revenue growth = profit margin jump)
3. The power semiconductor market is exploding (new energy vehicles, photovoltaics, energy storage), and Huahong is the biggest OEM beneficiary
4. The new president Bai Peng (former global vice president of Intel) brings technical depth and international perspective
5. U.S. sanctions have strengthened Huahong’s irreplaceability for customers in mainland China

### Short logic
1. PE 52 times + ROE 1-2% = typical "paying a high premium for forward expectations"
2. Geopolitical risks materialize: The United States has cut off the supply of equipment to some Huahong factories
3. Mainland China’s mature process production capacity is concentrated, and the risk of ASP decline and overcapacity is rising.
4. Frequent changes in management (change of chairman and president within one year), uncertainty in strategy execution
5. Even if profits are fully restored, the ROE ceiling of mature process OEMs is limited (12-15%)

## Summary of research on the second and fourth dimensions

### Dimension 1: Business model (see report 01 for details)

| Project | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Nature of business | Featured process wafer foundry, focusing on mature process power/analog/storage devices |
| Moat | Medium - technology accumulation + customer switching cost + differentiated positioning |
| Pricing power | Weak - gross profit margin is only 12%, lower than industry average |
| Sustainability | Better - Domestic substitution trend determined + structural growth in demand for power devices |

**Key findings**: Huahong has chosen a correct differentiation path (avoiding head-on competition with advanced processes), but it has not proven that it can continue to earn excess profits on this path. The high proportion of consumer electronics (64%) is an important reason for the current weak profitability. The transformation to automotive/industrial is the key to improving ASP and profit margins.

### Dimension 2: Financial valuation (see 02 report for details)

| Project | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Revenue growth | Strong (20%+), clear production capacity-driven logic |
| Profitability | Extremely weak (ROE 1-2%), huge depreciation pressure |
| Free cash flow | Negative, still burning cash |
| Valuation level | High (PE 52x, PB 5.86x) |
| Margin of safety | Shortcomings - high valuation + low profit + uncertainty |
| Comprehensive rating | ★★☆☆☆ |

**Key findings**: Huahong is currently in a typical expansion period of "rapid revenue growth but extremely low profits". When investors buy at 52 times PE, they are essentially prepaying for the profit recovery in 2-3 years. If earnings recovery falls short of expectations (for example, due to overcapacity or delayed equipment supply), current valuations will lack support.

### Dimension three: Industry competition (see 03 report for details)

| Project | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Industry status | The sixth largest foundry in the world and the second largest foundry in mainland China |
| Competitive positioning | Focus on unique processes, differentiation from TSMC/SMIC |
| Industry trends | Domestic substitution + growing demand for power devices (favorable) |
| Competitive risks | Overcapacity of mature processes + TSMC’s dimensionality reduction attack (unfavorable) |
| Comprehensive rating | ★★★☆☆ |

**Key findings**: Huahong's position in the industry is "distinctive but not strong enough" - it is not irreplaceable (SMIC can also do most of its business), but it has indeed established a leading advantage in the field of power device foundry (the world's largest 12-inch power device foundry base). The overall industry trend is favorable, but the risk of overcapacity in 2026-2028 requires vigilance.

### Dimension 4: Risk management (see 04 report for details)

| Project | Assessment |

| Management capabilities | Bai Peng has a strong technical background and a good global perspective |
| Management stability | Poor - double coaching changes within one year |

**Key Findings**: The biggest risk facing Huahong is geopolitics, which is beyond the company's control. Although the equipment supply cutoff in April 2026 mainly affected advanced process lines, it sent a very negative signal-the scope of US sanctions is expanding from "advanced processes" to "any possible advanced capabilities."

### 3.2 Investment Conclusion

**Rating: Wait and see (not recommended to buy yet)**

Core reasons:
1. **Valuation lacks safety margin**: 52 times PE corresponds to 1-2% ROE. Even considering profit recovery, the risk-return ratio is not ideal.
2. **Unpredictable geo-risks**: The biggest risk is exogenous and uncontrollable, and is worsening
3. **The profit turning point has not yet been confirmed**: gross profit margin is improving but has not reached the "safe area" (20%+)
4. **There are better alternatives**: There are investment targets with higher ROE and lighter assets in the same track.

### 3.3 Under what conditions can I buy?

| Trigger condition | Description |
|---------|------|
| Valuation correction | Stock price fell 30%+ to less than 35 times PE |
| Profit turning point | Gross profit margin exceeds 18% for 2-3 consecutive quarters |
| Geopolitical relaxation | A clear signal from the United States to relax restrictions on Huahong equipment |
| Capacity verification | FAB9 utilization rate exceeds 80% and order visibility is high |

**Ideal Buying Scenario**: Consider establishing an initial position when at least two of the above conditions are met.

### 3.4 If held, key tracking indicators

1. **Gross profit margin per quarter** (most important): target 18%→20%→25%
2. **FAB9 capacity utilization**: target 80%→90%→95%
3. **automotive/industrial revenue ratio**: target increased from 30% to 45%+
4. **US Sanctions Policy Updates**: Whether to expand to power device production lines
5. **Industry ASP Trend**: Is there a price war signal?
6. **Management execution**: Is Bai Peng’s strategy being implemented?

## 6. Final summary

Hua Hong Semiconductor is a company on the right track - specialty process foundry has irreplaceable strategic value in China's semiconductor industry. But "good track" does not equal "good investment". The current core contradiction is:

> **The market has paid a higher price (52 times PE) for Huahong's "future", but the path to realizing the "future" faces multiple uncertainties (sanctions, overcapacity, and the pace of profit recovery). **

Until uncertainty is not significantly reduced or valuations do not provide a sufficient margin of safety, it is more prudent to remain on the sidelines. Put it into the "recall pool" and continue to track it, waiting for a better buying opportunity.

---

### Core sentence

Hua Hong Semiconductor is a "good company but not a good price" - the strategic position is valuable, but the current valuation has fully reflected optimistic expectations, and the risk-benefit ratio is not ideal. Wait for profit inflection points to be confirmed or valuations to fall back before taking action.
