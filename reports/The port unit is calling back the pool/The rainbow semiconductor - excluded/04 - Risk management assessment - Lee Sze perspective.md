##2.2 Risk I: Geopolitics and equipment supply failures (maximum risk)

** Status**:
In April 2026, the United States Department of Commerce sent letters to application materials, the Panforest Group, and the Cobb, requesting a suspension of equipment supplies to Fab6 and Fab8a in the rainbow, on the grounds that the rainbow was prevented from acquiring the capability to produce 7 nm chips.

** Deep logic**:
The US sanctions logic is the "Major Wall" -- a precision strike on an enterprise that might break through advanced processes. Waron was previously considered "safe" (because of the focus on maturity), but FAB6's 28/22nm process and its 7nm plan for a flavour microelectronics have led it to the U.S. radar range.

** Impact assessment**:
- Short-term: FAB6 and FAB8a affected, but FAB7/FAB9 (power-based) is not limited
- Medium-term: possible limitations on the supply of spare parts for equipment, affecting production line rates and capacity
- Long term: replacement by forced national production, but uncertainty over the transition period of 3-5 years

** Worst-case scenario**: The extension of sanctions to all production lines in the rainbow has resulted in equipment not being maintained or updated and production capacity declining. Although the probability is low (because of the higher alternative to mature process equipment), it cannot be completely excluded.

**Quantified impact**:
- Direct impact: the relative share of income associated with FAB6 is about 15-20%
- Indirect impact: higher investor risk premium leads to valuation pressure
- Replacement costs for national production equipment: projected increase of 10-20 per cent in procurement expenditure

#2.3 Risk two: Overcapacity and price warfare

** Industry supply side data**:
- 4 new plants in China-Crystal International by 2026, 340,000 new units/during 12 inches.
- 83,000 new units/months in the rainbow FAB9
- Other small and medium plants (e.g., crystals, silicon, etc.) are also expanding.
- More than 500,000 additional units per month of capacity added to the maturity process in mainland China

** Demand side**:
- downstream demand growth of about 15-20 per cent per year
- ASP will be under downward pressure if supply increases continue to exceed demand.

**The rainbow response**:
- Transition to the High ASP Car Code and Industry
- Avoiding pure price competition through technological differentiation
- But consumer electronics still account for 64% of the revenue, which is the most vulnerable to price warfare

#2.4 Risk III: FAB9 Capacity to climb the slope

** Current progress**:
- The primary capacity target is met.
- Q3 plan 2026, with full planned capacity (83,000 units/month)
- About 20 to 40 thousand pieces of monthly production are still climbing.

** Risk point**:
1. A good climb rate: A good new line rate is usually 12-18 months to reach an economic level
2. Client import progress: Is the production capacity available but the order still valid?
3. Equipment disruption: some equipment may be affected by the impact of sanctions on its ability to maintain it
4. Persistence of depreciation pressure: if the slope is slower than expected, depreciation will be prolonged to the profit erosion

**Quantified**: FAB depreciations of approximately $170-$180 million in 1999. If capacity utilization is below 70 per cent for a long period, the line will continue to lose.

# VII. Core conclusions

From a risk management perspective, the China Rainbow Semiconductor faces a fundamental contradiction:

** The largest source of value for the company (substitute demand for national production) is on the other hand the most risky source (geopolitic sanctions) is the same coin.**

It is because of the US technological blockade that IC Designs on mainland China need more indigenous sub-plants like Huaiyong. At the same time, US sanctions are directly threatening the Chinese supply of equipment and technology upgrading routes.

** The core judgements that investors need to make are:**
1. Believing that national production will be effectively replaced within 3-5 years?
Or is it a fear that the escalating sanctions have rendered the production line unworkable?

** Management evaluation**: White pelican is a capable technical leader whose intel background brings internationalized management experience and technological depth to the Wallow. The challenge is enormous – pushing up productivity and profit improvements under external sanctions, while completing the client structure shift from consumer electronics to car regulation.

** Final Recommendation **: The China Rainbow Semiconductor is a high-uncertain investment target. It is not suitable for conservative investors who pursue a certain return, and is more suitable for investors who have a deep faith in China’s semiconductor industry and are able to withstand short-term fluctuations.
