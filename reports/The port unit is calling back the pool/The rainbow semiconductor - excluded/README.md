## Company Profile

Hua Hong Semiconductor is the world's leading pure-play wafer foundry with specialty technologies, the second largest pure-play wafer foundry in mainland China and the sixth largest in the world. The company focuses on the characteristic processes of mature processes of 55nm and above, and provides foundry and supporting services for diversified characteristic process platforms such as embedded/stand-alone non-volatile memory, analog and power management, logic and radio frequency, and power devices.

## Research background

- A leader in wafer foundry with unique technology, differentiated positioning to avoid head-on competition with TSMC/SMIC
- The current PE is about 52 times (dynamic), ROE is at a low level (about 1-2%), and it is in the production capacity ramping period.
- The large-scale production expansion of FAB9 (Wuxi 12-inch line) brings depreciation pressure and short-term profits are under pressure
- In April 2026, the U.S. Department of Commerce requested to suspend the supply of equipment to some Huahong factories, and geopolitical risks intensified.
- Management change: Former Intel Global Vice President Bai Peng will take over as president in January 2025

## Research conclusion preview

Hua Hong Semiconductor is a specialty process foundry enterprise in a critical transition period. Its differentiated positioning has strategic value, but the current valuation implies higher growth expectations. Investors need to weigh the contradiction between the certainty of domestic substitution and geopolitical risks, depreciation pressure, and the pace of profitability recovery.
