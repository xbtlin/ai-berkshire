# One, the crystal round industry #

#1.1 Nature of the industry

Crystal Circle Generations is a typical "Smart Economy Plus Technical Barrier" industry:
- ** Capital-intensive**: $1 billion to $2 billion in investment in an advanced 12-inch line
- ** Technology-intensive**: process development cycle long, improvement requires considerable experience
- ** High sticky client**: Chip flow validation cycle 12-24 months, high conversion cost
- ** very cyclical**: highly associated with semiconductor climate cycle and high demand volatility

#1.3 Maturity vs Advanced

The crystal circle is dividing into two distinct worlds:
- ** Advanced production (under 7 nm)**: power supply/tristar double-olive, ASP extremely high, client concentration ( apples, English Weida, etc.) and investment thresholds very high
- ** Maturity process (28nm and above)**: there are a large number of competitors, ASP is relatively low, and clients are dispersed, but demand is high and stable

The mature/characterized process area of the rainbow is developing a two-track competition pattern for "continent expansion, Taiwan profit preservation".

# III. Long-term trends in the industry

#3.1 Trends favourable to the rainbow

**1. Structural demand for national production replacement (very certain)**

The rise of IC Designs on mainland China is the most definitive trend. In 2025, China’s IC Design Industry collected more than RMB 500 billion, which needed local surrogate capacity.

Projections are that China will have the largest global surrogate centre in China by 2030, with a 30 per cent share of global crystal round manufacturing capacity going beyond Taiwan (23 per cent).

**2. Power semiconductor demand outbreak**

The demand for IGBT, MOSFET, Sic devices in the areas of new energy vehicles, photovoltaics, storage, charge posts and so on has continued to grow at a high rate:
- The power of a single new energy vehicle is 5-10 times the capacity of a conventional fuel vehicle
- The global power semiconductor market is projected to exceed $18.9 billion in 2025
- The rainbow is the main substitute in this field.

**3. Automobile electronics ratio increase**

Cars "new quadrupling" (electricization, intelligence, network connectivity, sharing) drive the demand for car chips. With 20 years of driving experience and AEC-Q100 certification, the rainbow is expected to share this increase.

# 3.2 Unfavourable trends towards the rainbow

**1. Risk of excess capacity for mature process capacity (medium probability)**

China is expanding its capacity through a mature production process that has never been seen before:
- 4 new plants in China-Chole International, 340,000 new units per month by 2026
- 83,000 units/months planned by the Fab9
- Other small and medium-sized plants are expanding.

If demand increases are not matched by supply growth, the ASP downward pressure will erode the profitability of all players. This is a classic risk for cyclical industries.

**2. Challenges of technological upgrading**

Even in mature areas, clients demand better performance and lower costs.
- The layout of the third generation semiconductor (SiC, GaN)
- Accompaniment of advanced containment technology
- Development of new-generation power devices

**3. "Down-wire blow" from a battery**

After the advanced process earns excess profits, the power has the potential to take over the mature process market at lower prices. The expansion of 28nm production capacity in 2024-2025 poses a potential threat to mature process players.

# IV. Geopolitics and sanctions impact

# 4.1 April 2026 incident

The United States Department of Commerce sent letters to companies with United States chip equipment such as application materials, the Panforest Group, and the Cordeaux, requesting a suspension of the supply of equipment to two plants (FAB6 and FAB8a) in China on the grounds that it could be used to produce China ' s most advanced chips.

** Direct impact**:
- FAB6 (28/22nm advanced process line): equipment maintenance and extension limited
- FAB8a (under construction): construction may be delayed
- FAB7 and FAB9 (power devices dominated): not directly affected for the time being

**Indirect effects**:
- Signal meaning: the United States sanctions on the Chinese semiconductor are expanding.
- Investor confidence: rising geo-risk premiums
- Domestication pressure: forced to accelerate replacement of domestically produced equipment

# 4.2 Progress in the national production of equipment

- Erosion equipment: Sino-Mico, North China Creative Substitute
- Light engraved machines: Shanghai microelectronics can meet needs above 90 nm
- The membrane deposition: North China, Takutech is breaking through.
- Measurement: Accurate electron, medium-flying layout

The Chinese government has been working on the issue of the import of domestic production equipment.

# Five, reflect backwards: What could the market be wrong?

# 5.1 Markets may underestimate

1. ** Profit elasticity after depreciation peak**: FAB9 depreciation is essentially fixed, but income increases with utilization. When utilization is raised from 50 to 90%, marginal costs are almost zero and profit elastic.
2. ** Long-term value of car regulation clients**: The two- to three-year cycle of Car regulation certification implies that imported customers are a stable source of income for the next 5 to 10 years.
3. ** Geo-risks accelerate national production substitution**: United States sanctions are short-term and may reinforce the irresistible nature of the rainbow in the long term.

# 5.2 The market may be overestimated

1. **The "premise"** for national production is policy-driven and does not mean that the rainbow is overprofitable. Clients may choose the rainbow out of security demand rather than higher prices.
2. ** Sustainability of capacity utilization**: the current utilization rate of 106 per cent partially benefits from a concentration of short-term orders and stock replenishment cycles, which does not represent a long-term steady state.
3. ** Positive impact of management changes**: new management needs time to co-excuse and uncertainty about strategy implementation.

# Eight, core conclusion

The Manger Model of Multi-Specific Thinking is a way to look at the rainbow semiconductor:

** Enabling mental model**:
- **Likey Market Theory**: Hua-Rong has established a good position in the niche market of specialty, avoiding positive competition with giants
- **Scaling effect**: Once the FAB9 climb is completed, the fixed cost amortization will significantly improve profitability
- **Inreversible trends**: National production substitution as an irreversible trend driven by both political and economic forces

** Unadverse mental model**:
- **Competition disruption**: An arms race in productive capacity in mature processes could cause "a thousand self-destructive"
- **Lustrative risk**: Dependence on overseas equipment is a fundamental vulnerability and not a short-term solution
- ** mediocre return trap**: Even if everything goes well, the long-term ROE of mature-scale workers may be just "fine" (12-15%) rather than "excellent" (20%+)

** Final judgement**: Huayung is a medium-competitive firm on the right track. It will not disappear (national capital background + demand for national production replacement), but investors will need to think:** ** Is there a better way to participate in the trend of national production substitution of China’s semiconductor?** e.g., IC Design (lighter assets, higher ROE) or equipment (benefiting from accelerated national production).
