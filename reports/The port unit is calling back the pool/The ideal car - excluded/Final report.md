## 1. Core conclusion

### Overall rating: ★★½☆☆ (wait and see)

Li Auto is a company with first-class product definition capabilities, but it operates in an extremely competitive industry with razor-thin profits. The current valuation requires multiple optimistic assumptions to be justified at the same time, and the downside risk cannot be ignored although it is backed by cash.

**Summary in one sentence:** Good team, difficult to do business, expensive price, waiting for opportunities.

## 4. Positive arguments (why it may be worth investing in)

### 4.1 Strong cash moat
- 101.2 billion in cash, No. 1 among domestic new forces
- Even if you continue to lose money, you can "burn" for 3-5 years
- Gives the company ample time to make mistakes and correct them

### 4.2 Industry-leading product definition capabilities
- L series has established strong recognition in the family SUV market
- Cumulative orders for pure electric i6 exceeded 100,000, preliminary market verification
- Li Xiang’s product intuition is still the company’s core asset

### 4.3 There is still value in the transitional period of program extension
- Charging infrastructure in China is still imperfect
- Extended range is still a necessity in long-distance family travel scenarios
- L series replacement (L9 Livis, etc.) is expected to boost sales

### 4.4 Valuation has a "safety cushion"
- Net cash per share is approximately HK$51, current stock price is HK$70
- Only HK$19/share was actually paid for the automobile business (approximately HK$40 billion)
- Limited downside (30-40% to around net cash)

### 4.5 Signs of sales recovery in 2026
- Q1 monthly average of more than 35,000 vehicles
- Pure electric i6 production capacity is ramping up
- The new L9 will be delivered in the second half of 2026

---

## 5. Negative arguments (why it may be a trap)

### 5.1 Profitability is extremely fragile
- Net profit margin is only 1%, and sales may fluctuate by 10%, resulting in losses
- Gross profit margin continues to decline due to price war
- The pure electric business is "price-for-volume" and no profit will be seen in the short term.

### 5.2 The moat does not exist
- The range extension market has been invaded by competitors
- Brand premium is eroded by large discounts
- Product definition capabilities cannot be institutionalized and replicated
- No cost advantage (not as vertically integrated as BYD)

### 5.3 Management strategy fragmentation
- The direction of "embodied intelligence" deviates from the main automotive business
- Self-developed chips consume huge resources and the returns are uncertain
- The organizational structure was adjusted twice within half a year, indicating that the direction is still swinging

### 5.4 The competitive environment is extremely harsh
- Huawei competes head-on in the industry and has unlimited resources
- BYD is squeezing from below and has the ability to crush costs
- Zero Pao has surpassed its ideals and become the best-selling new force
- Xiaomi Auto’s rapid rise diverts budget

### 5.5 The risk of pure electric transformation is high
- MEGA recalls all quantities (coolant problem), quality control is questionable
- Initial i8 sales fell short of expectations
- There is no first-mover advantage in the pure electric market
- It is necessary to maintain the extended range + pure electric product lines at the same time

## 6. Key Assumptions and Scenario Analysis

### Scenario 1: Optimism (probability 20%)

**Assumptions:** The monthly delivery of the pure electric i-series will be stable at 30,000+, and the extended-range L series will remain at 40,000+. The total delivery volume will exceed 800,000 units in 2027, and the net profit margin will return to 5%.

**Result:** Profit in 2027 is 6 billion-8 billion, giving 20 times PE, market value 120-160 billion. The stock price target is HK$100-130 (+43%~+86%).

### Scenario 2: Neutral (50% probability)

**Assumptions:** Pure electric vehicles will slowly climb, with a slight loss of range extension share. The total delivery volume in 2027 will be 550,000-650,000 vehicles, with a net profit margin of 2-3%.

**Result:** Profit in 2027 is 2-4 billion, with a PE of 15 times and a market value of 30-60 billion. The stock price target is HK$45-70 (-36%~0%).

### Scenario 3: Pessimism (30% probability)

**Assumptions:** Pure electric vehicles continue to suffer losses, the extended range market is eroded by Wenjie/BYD, and annual delivery volume stagnates at 350,000-450,000 vehicles, continuing to suffer slight losses.

**Result:** The market values the stock based on net assets/net cash, with a stock price target of HK$35-50 (-29%~-50%).

### Expected value calculation

Weighted expected stock price = 115×20% + 57.5×50% + 42.5×30% = 23 + 28.75 + 12.75 = **64.5 Hong Kong dollars**

The current stock price is HK$70, and the implied expected return is negative. **Indicates that the current price has reflected the more optimistic expectations. **

## 8. Investment advice

### 8.1 Current operation suggestions

**Rating: Wait and see (no buy, no short)**

Reason:
1. The valuation has not reached the "significantly undervalued" range (needs to fall below HK$50)
2. The success or failure of pure electric transformation is not yet clear (needs to observe for another 2-3 quarters)
3. The competitive landscape is still deteriorating (Qianjie continues to grab share)
4. Management’s strategic direction is still not focused enough

### 8.2 Conditions that trigger buying

If 2-3 of the following conditions are met, you can consider opening a position:
1. The stock price falls below HK$50 (the safety margin is sufficient)
2. Monthly delivery volume has stabilized at more than 50,000 vehicles for 2 consecutive quarters
3. Vehicle gross profit margin rebounded to over 20%
4. Monthly delivery of pure electric i-series exceeded 30,000 units and gross profit margin turned positive
5. Management clearly focuses on building cars and stops or reduces investment in AI/embodied intelligence.

### 8.3 Conditions that trigger sell/give up

If the following signals appear, it is recommended to be removed from the watch list:
1. Net losses for 4 consecutive quarters
2. Cash reserves dropped below 60 billion (burning money too fast)
3. Monthly delivery volume continues to be less than 30,000 vehicles
4. Management reduced holdings on a large scale
5. Major quality incident (similar to MEGA recall but larger scale)

## 9. Final summary

Li Auto is an interesting observation sample of China's new energy automobile industry. It shows the ceilings and bottlenecks of a "product-driven company":

**Ceiling:** Relying on strong product definition capabilities, we have achieved amazing growth and profitability in 2023-2024.

**Bottleneck:** But when competition intensifies and technology routes change, product companies that lack a structural moat will quickly lose their advantage. One person’s product intuition cannot be scaled, nor is it guaranteed to be right every time.

**For value investors:** The ideal is not a "buy and hold for ten years" target. It is more suitable as a "contrarian investment opportunity under specific conditions" - when the market is extremely pessimistic and the stock price falls near net cash, it provides an asymmetric opportunity with "limited downside and considerable upside". But it's not that moment yet.
