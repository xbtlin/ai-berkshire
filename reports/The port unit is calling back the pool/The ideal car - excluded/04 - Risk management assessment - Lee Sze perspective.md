# 3.2 The biggest single risk: "Two Lines of Battle"

The greatest risk to the ideal is not a single event, but a structural dilemma of "two lines of action":

** Left hand:** Defence in the incremental market - in the face of new entrants such as the question world, deep blue, need to be invested in maintaining the L series's competitiveness on a continuous basis
** Right hand:** Attack on the pure electricity market - i series requires a lot of research and development and marketing inputs to build user awareness

The simultaneous fighting on both lines means:
- Dispersed R & D resources
- Double the marketing budget.
- It's complicated.
- Management attention diluted.

Historically, "two lines of combat" have led to more failures than successful ones.

#3.3 Warning of the recall of MEGA

In November 2025, the ideal recall of all 11,411 MEGAs was due to inadequate cooling fluid preservative performance. This incident revealed several deep-seated problems:

1. **Quality control shortboard**: Full recall of the first pure electric flagship, indicating inexperience in quality management of the pure power platform
2. ** Financial shock**: One-time loss of approximately 1.1 billion, with significant impact on profits
3. ** Brand injury**: Quality problems with high end pure electric flagships, undermining users ' confidence in ideal pure electricity products
4. **Secure of the front car**: If similar problems arise in the i series, the impact will be greater (higher delivery)
