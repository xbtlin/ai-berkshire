## Investment background

Li Auto is China's leading new energy vehicle company. It started with extended-range electric SUVs and will achieve rapid growth in 2023-2024 with its precise product definition and home user positioning. However, it encountered major setbacks in 2025: MEGA's pure electric flagship failed, competition in the extended-range market intensified, full-year delivery fell by nearly 19% year-on-year, and net profit plummeted by 86%. In 2026, the company is in the transition period of "extended range + pure electric" two-wheel drive, facing the dual challenges of fierce competition and profitability restoration.
