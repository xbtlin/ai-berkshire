#Xpeng Motors (9868.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, corporate culture, long-term sustainability
> Analysis date: May 16, 2026

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 Business model overview

Xpeng Motors' business model is evolving from "simply selling cars" to a triple model of "cars + technology licensing + physical AI platform":

**The first level: vehicle sales (basic plate)**
- Delivery of 429,445 vehicles in 2025, with revenue of 76.72 billion yuan
- Products cover the price range of 100,000-400,000 yuan: MONA series (100,000-150,000 yuan), P series (150,000-250,000 yuan), G series (250,000-400,000 yuan)
- MONA M03 and P7+ together contributed more than 65% of sales, showing the characteristics of "hot-selling driven"
- Entering the extended range market in 2026, launching 3 super extended range products in Q1

**Second level: Technology licensing (new growth pole)**
- Signed a strategic cooperation framework agreement on electronic and electrical architecture (EEA) technology with Volkswagen
- Will be used in electric models produced by Volkswagen in China from 2026
- Expanded to Volkswagen fuel and hybrid models in 2027
- Xpeng’s second-generation VLA model is fully open source, and Volkswagen is the first strategic partner
- Self-developed Turing AI chip has been designated by the public

**Third layer: Physical AI platform (long-term imagination)**
- Robotaxi: 3 models will be launched in 2026 and will be put into trial operation in Guangzhou and other places
- Humanoid robot: R&D team of over 1,000 people, mass production by the end of 2026
- Flying car "land aircraft carrier": mass production and delivery in 2026

### 1.2 Core question: What is the essence of this business?

In essence, Xpeng Motors is in the business of "implementing AI technology in the physical world". Cars are the largest carrier and source of cash flow for AI technology. Technology licensing is the commercialization path to verify the value of technology. Physical AI is the ultimate vision.

This is different from pure manufacturing logic - Xpeng's core assets are not factories and production capacity, but AI algorithms, data accumulation and engineering capabilities.

## 2. User value analysis

### 2.1 Why do users buy Xpeng?

Xpeng's core user value proposition is "get the best smart driving experience at the lowest price":

- **Smart Driving Experience**: XNGP is available nationwide without restrictions on cities, routes, and road conditions. It has been verified by real vehicles in 2,595 cities, and the cumulative test exceeds 7.56 million kilometers.
- **Value for money**: MONA M03 starts at 119,800 yuan and is equipped with high-end smart driving, achieving "equal rights in smart driving"
- **Iteration Speed**: A total of 5 full releases and 35 version iterations within 70 days, surpassing the mobile phone industry

### 2.2 User reputation and satisfaction

Front:
- MONA M03 rolled out over 10,000 units within 22 days of its launch, setting a record for the fastest innovation power
- P7+ Dading exceeded 30,000 units in the first week
- Smart driving user activity leads the industry

Concerns:
- Brand recognition is weaker than Weilai (service) and Ideal (family scene)
- Quality and delivery problems of early G3/P5 models affected the reputation of some old users
- The product line is expanding too fast and the brand positioning is unclear (the price range of 100,000-400,000 is too large)

## 3. Business model sustainability assessment

### 3.1 Positive factors

**Leverage effect of technology licensing model**

Cooperation with Volkswagen is of benchmark significance:
- Prove that Xpeng’s technology is internationally competitive
- The marginal cost of technology licensing income is close to zero, and once it reaches scale, the profit margin is extremely high
- Volkswagen is one of the world's largest car companies, and the depth of cooperation continues to increase (from EEA to VLA to chips)
- Expanded to fuel vehicles in 2027, TAM will be significantly expanded

**The scale effect is beginning to appear**

After doubling delivery volume in 2025:
- Gross profit margin improves to 18.9% from 14% in 2024
- Operating cash flow changed from net outflow to net inflow of 8.26 billion yuan
- Q4 achieved profit in a single quarter for the first time

**Technology reuse reduces marginal costs**

The same AI technology system is simultaneously reused in cars, robots, and flying cars, and the output ratio of R&D investment increases with the increase in application scenarios.

### 3.2 Negative factors

**Complete vehicle sales are still a cyclical and highly competitive business**

- The price war in the automobile industry continues, and Xpeng’s delivery volume in Q1 2026 will drop by approximately 30% year-on-year.
- The extended course already has strong enemies such as Ideal and Wenjie
- Overseas markets face tariff barriers (the EU imposes up to 36%)

**The proportion of technology licensing revenue is still low**

At present, the contribution of technology licensing to revenue has not yet reached a large scale. Volkswagen's cooperation models will not be mass-produced and launched in 2026, and real revenue recognition will take time.

**There is great uncertainty in the commercialization of physics AI**

Robotaxi, humanoid robots, and flying cars are currently in the investment stage, and the commercialization path is not yet clear:
- Robotaxi policy and regulatory restrictions
- Humanoid robots still have a long way to go before large-scale application
- Airworthiness certification and infrastructure support for flying cars

## 4. Corporate culture and "doing the right thing"

### 4.1 He Xiaopeng’s business philosophy

He Xiaopeng is the founder of UC Browser and has experience in successful entrepreneurship and cash out. His business style:
- Strong belief in technology and willing to bear short-term losses for long-term investment
- Strong strategic adjustment ability - decisively adjust the organizational structure in 2023, shifting from "G/P/X series separation" to a unified platform
- Courage to admit mistakes publicly (have publicly reflected on mistakes in product definition many times)

### 4.2 Cultural concerns

- The battle front is too long: cars + technology licensing + Robotaxi + robots + flying cars, and management energy is scattered
- Talent flow: Frequent turnover of senior executives, with many VP-level executives leaving in the past few years
- "Want everything" mentality: We must be number one in every direction. Is it consistent with the "subtraction" business philosophy?

---

## 5. The deeper significance of cooperation with the public

This deal deserves separate analysis because it could change the nature of Xpeng’s business model:

**Value to Xpeng:**
1. Verify the value of technology—selected by the world’s largest car company
2. Diversification of income sources - from "making money selling cars" to "making money selling technology"
3. Feedback from scale – using public data and scenarios to reversely train Xpeng’s model
4. Brand Endorsement - Cooperate with the public to enhance brand credibility

**Potential risks:**
1. Volkswagen may develop its own technology after digesting it (refer to the paths of Huawei and Cyrus)
2. The exclusivity and pricing power of technology licensing are unclear
3. Collaborative R&D takes up engineer resources

### Key judgments

Xpeng's business model is in a critical window period of "transforming from manufacturing to technology platform." This is the evolution of a good business—from selling hardware to selling software and technology, from linear growth to exponential growth. But the success of this transformation depends on:

1. Can mass cooperation be transformed from "model room" to "batch copy"
2. Can vehicle sales maintain scale in a price war?
3. Among the three lines of physics AI, at least one can contribute meaningful income within 3 years.

**Bottom line thinking**: Even if technology licensing and physical AI all fail, Xpeng, as a car company that delivers 430,000 vehicles annually and has revenue of 76.7 billion, will still have its fundamentals. It's not an all-or-nothing bet.
