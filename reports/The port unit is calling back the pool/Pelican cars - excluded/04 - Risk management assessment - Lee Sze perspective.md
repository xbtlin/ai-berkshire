**The 2023 major organizational restructuring**: The decision to merge the G/P/X three product lines into a unified platform to reduce competition for internal resources. The rapid roll-out of the 2024-2025 — MONA M03 and P7+ — benefited from the platform.

2. ** The decisive launch of the market sinking of the Mona brand**: As the competitors were still in the 200,000+ price range, Pyramid opened up the incremental space through the MONA M03 (1198,000) and maintained a reasonable Maori rate.

3. ** Embrace public cooperation**: Technical cooperation with the public is a step-by-step "profit and fame" and cooperation is continuing to deepen.

3. ** Global expansion of 8,000 **: The large-scale recruitment in an industry price war and efficiency priority environment is in conflict with the concept of "savory business".

# Core judgment

** Buying pelican = buying pelican's judgment and enforcement.**

He is a continuous entrepreneur with a successful exit experience, with a technical belief and a long-term perspective. The challenge he faces today is unprecedentedly complex:

- To open three new fronts of physical AI when the auto industry is not yet stable.
- Increase research and development investment significantly during the most intense phase of the Chinese price war
- Targeting 60 countries from the beginning of globalization

It's not about doing the right thing vs did the wrong thing, but about going too fast in the right direction.

** Final Judgement**: If Pyramid can prove that the "technical authorization model can be replicated" (signing 2-3 OEM clients) within the next two years, all current risks and inputs will prove worthwhile. If not, pylons may return to a normal electric car company with a limited value ceiling.

This is an investment subject of "asymmetric bet" - downside risks are limited (47.6 billion cash plus positive cash flows) and up-front space depends on whether the technology platform model works.
