> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, intrinsic value

## 1. Financial panoramic scan

### 1.1 Revenue and Growth

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|--------|--------|----------|
| Total revenue | 30.68 billion | 40.87 billion | 76.72 billion | +87.7% |
| Deliveries | 141,601 vehicles | 190,068 vehicles | 429,445 vehicles | +125.9% |
| Average bicycle price | ~217,000 | ~215,000 | ~179,000 | -16.7% |

Key observations:
- The doubling of revenue growth is mainly driven by delivery volumes rather than price increases
- The average price of bicycles dropped by about 36,000 yuan, reflecting the increase in sales proportion of MONA M03 (starting at 119,800 yuan)
- The "price-for-volume" strategy is obvious: M03 and P7+ contributed more than 65% of sales
- There is a signal of slowdown in growth in Q1 of 2026: delivery volume will drop by about 30% year-on-year, and revenue is expected to drop by 16-23%

### 1.2 Evolution of profitability

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 Q4 | 2025 Full Year |
|------|--------|--------|----------|----------|
| Gross profit margin | 1.5% | 14.0% | 21.3% | 18.9% |
| Net profit | -10.38 billion | -5.84 billion | +380 million | -1.14 billion |

The triple jump in gross profit margin is very critical:
- The gross profit margin in 2023 will be only 1.5%, almost selling cars at a loss
- Jump to 14% in 2024, with improved scale effect and product structure
- It will further increase to 18.9% in 2025 and reach 21.3% in Q4

**Gross margin improvement drivers**:
1. Scale effect - doubling the delivery volume dilutes fixed costs
2. Improvement of supply chain bargaining power
3. Platform-based cost reduction—multiple vehicle models share technology platform
4. Technology licensing income (extremely low marginal cost)

### 1.3 R&D investment

| Year | R&D expenses | Proportion of revenue | AI related investment |
|------|----------|----------|------------|
| 2023 | ~5.2 billion | 16.9% | - |
| 2024 | ~6.5 billion | 15.9% | - |
| 2025 | 9.49 billion | 12.4% | 4.5 billion |
| 2026 (Planned) | ~11 billion+ | - | 7 billion |

The cumulative R&D investment in the three years was 21.23 billion yuan. Although the absolute value of R&D expenses continues to grow, the ratio of revenue to revenue decreases with the expansion of scale - this is a healthy trend, indicating that revenue growth outperforms R&D expenditure growth.

Special investment in AI will increase from 4.5 billion to 7 billion in 2026, an increase of 55.6%. This is a huge bet, and the bets include: end-to-end intelligent driving models, Robotaxi, humanoid robots, and self-developed chips.

### 1.4 Cash Flow and Balance Sheet

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Cash flow from operating activities | -2.01 billion | +8.26 billion |
| Free Cash Flow | Negative | ~+5 billion |
| Cash and equivalents | ~40 billion | 47.66 billion |

This is a fundamental shift:
- Operating cash flow turned from negative to positive, with an improvement of more than 10 billion yuan
- Free cash flow is close to 5 billion yuan, which means that the company has the ability to "self-generate blood"
- Cash reserves of 47.66 billion yuan, which can last for more than 40 years based on the loss rate in 2025 (although this is not a correct calculation, it shows that there is no risk of fund rupture in the short term)

## 2. Is the profit turning point established?

### 2.1 Positive argument: The turning point has arrived

1. **Q4 single-quarter profit has structural significance**: The 380 million net profit is not a one-time income, but the result of continued improvement in gross profit margin.
2. **Operating leverage appears**: Revenue doubled but expense ratio (sales + management) did not increase year-on-year
3. **Free cash flow turns positive**: This is a more reliable indicator of earnings quality than net profit
4. **Technical licensing contribution**: The marginal cost of Volkswagen cooperation income approaches zero

### 2.2 Opposition arguments: still need to be verified

1. **Poor Q1 forecast**: Deliveries in Q1 of 2026 are expected to drop by 30% year-on-year, and revenue will drop by 16-23%. Is this seasonal or a trend?
2. **The average price of bicycles continues to decline**: from 217,000 to 179,000. If it continues to decline, gross profit margin will be under pressure
3. **R&D investment will increase significantly**: AI investment will increase to 7 billion in 2026, which may devour profit improvements
4. **Fierce competition in the extended-range market**: Ideal, Wenjie, and Leapmotor already have the first-mover advantage

### 2.3 Judgment

The direction of the earnings turning point is certain, but there is uncertainty about the slope and persistence. The slowdown in Q1 in 2026 requires close attention - if Q2/Q3 can pick up, it means that it is just a phased fluctuation in the switching of new and old models; if it continues to weaken, it means that competitive pressure exceeds expectations.

### 3.3 Segment valuation attempt

**Vehicle Business Valuation**

Assumptions:
- Deliveries will resume growth to 500,000 vehicles in 2026
- The average price of a bicycle is 170,000 yuan
- Revenue of approximately 85 billion yuan
- Refer to Ideal/BYD’s 1.3-1.4x PS

**Technology Licensing Business Valuation**

Assumptions:
- Volkswagen cooperation will contribute 1 billion to 3 billion yuan in revenue in 2026-2027
- Gross profit margin 70%+
- If copied to more OEMs in the future, revenue can reach 10 billion+
- Reference Software Company 10x PS
- Technology licensing valuation: RMB 10-30 billion (current stage)

**Physics AI Business Valuation**

At present, Robotaxi, robots, and flying cars are all in the investment stage and are difficult to value using traditional methods. It can be used as a "free call option" - if it succeeds, the value may exceed the entire vehicle business; if it fails, the sunk costs of research and development that have been invested need to be absorbed.

**Sum of parts: RMB 120-150 billion**

## 4. Capital Allocation Assessment

### 4.1 Is the money spent correctly?

**Do the right thing:**
- Concentrate resources to create two popular models, MONA M03 and P7+, instead of spreading investment widely
- Exploration of technology licensing model - light assets, high gross profit
- Overseas localized production (Magna cooperation, Malaysian factory) to avoid tariffs

**Things worth debating:**
- AI investment will surge from 4.5 billion to 7 billion in 2026. Is the increase too fast?
- Open three physical AI product lines (Robotaxi/Robot/Flying Car) at the same time, with resources dispersed
- Global recruitment expansion of 8,000 people, which seems radical in the context of declining Q1 delivery volume

### 4.2 Shareholder Return

As a growing company that is still losing money, Xpeng has no plans for dividends or buybacks. Shareholder returns depend entirely on stock price increases, and stock price increases depend on earnings realization.

---

## 5. Conflict with traditional value investment framework

Frankly speaking, Xpeng Motors is not a "value investment target" in the traditional sense:

1. **No sustained profit record**: Only a single quarter of profit in Q4, but still a loss for the whole year
2. **No moat certainty**: technological leadership may be caught up
3. **High Capital Intensity**: Automobile manufacturing + AI R&D double money burning
4. **No history of shareholder returns**: No dividends, no buybacks
5. **Valuation Depends on Assumptions**: Growth expectations for the next 3-5 years dominate the current valuation

But there are also differences from traditional loss-making enterprises:
- Operating cash flow has turned positive and external financing is no longer needed
- If the technology licensing model works, the profit margin will be much higher than that of the manufacturing industry
- Cash reserves of 47.6 billion provide ample safety cushion
