## 1. One sentence summary

Xpeng Motors is a company that is transforming from "selling cars" to "AI technology platform". It has industry-leading intelligent driving technology and Volkswagen, a heavyweight technology authorized customer. Fundamentals are improving (Q4 is profitable for the first time, cash flow has turned positive), but in the short term it faces execution risks of slowing delivery volume and multi-front operations. The current stock price is close to a 52-week low, and the market is paying almost no premium to the technology platform.

## 3. Investment thesis

### 3.1 Bull Case

1. **Technology licensing model verification**: Volkswagen cooperation continues to deepen, from EEA to VLA to chips to fuel vehicles. If it can be expanded to more OEMs, Xpeng will transform from a manufacturer to a technology platform provider, and the valuation logic will completely change
2. **Profit turning point established**: Q4 is the first single-quarter profit, and operating cash flow has turned positive. With the expansion of scale and cost reduction, full-year profits can be expected.
3. **Physical AI option value**: Robotaxi/robots/flying cars currently have almost zero pricing in the market value, and any breakthrough is a huge increase.
4. **Globalization Increment**: Overseas market share is only 10%, with huge room for growth. Smart driving may be more competitive overseas.
5. **Valuation bottom**: The stock price is close to the 52-week low, and the PS 1.4x is in the historically low range.

### 3.2 Bear Case

1. **Q1 delivery volume fell by 30%**: It may not be a simple seasonal fluctuation, the competition in the extended range market is more intense than imagined
2. **The moat is narrowing**: Huawei, BYD and others are rapidly catching up in the smart driving field, and end-to-end large models have lowered technical barriers.
3. **Excessive R&D investment**: 7 billion investment in AI in 2026, which seems radical in the context of Q1 slowdown
4. **The battle front is too long**: cars + technology licensing + Robotaxi + robots + flying cars + 60 overseas countries. It is difficult for any management team to balance it all.
5. **Insufficient brand power**: Lack of clear brand positioning within the broad price range of 100,000-400,000
6. **Reliance on a single technology customer**: Volkswagen is the only technology authorized customer

## 7. Operation suggestions

### 7.1 What type of investors is it suitable for?

- Acceptable holding period of 3-5 years
- Believe in the long-term trend of AI being implemented in the physical world
- Tolerant of short-term stock price fluctuations and quarterly performance uncertainty
- Positions are controlled within 5-10% of the portfolio

### 7.2 What type of investors is it not suitable for?

- Pursue stable dividend income
- Unable to accept the uncertainty of a loss-making enterprise
- The holding period is less than 2 years
- Lack of psychological preparation for the price war in the automobile industry

### 7.3 Recommended observation strategies

**Current judgment: Mainly wait and see, waiting for catalytic confirmation**

Reason:
1. The 30% drop in Q1 deliveries in 2026 is a signal that needs to be verified - is it a switch between old and new or is the trend weakening?
2. The sales data of new models with extended range (3 models launched in Q1) will take 2-3 months to verify.
3. Volkswagen’s cooperation models will be launched in the second half of the year, and the true value of technology licensing can be verified by then

**Potential entry opportunities:**
1. Q2 monthly delivery volume recovered to 40,000+ vehicles - confirming that Q1 is just a phased fluctuation
2. Monthly sales of Volkswagen's cooperative models exceeded 10,000 units after their launch - verifying the value of technology licensing
3. Sign the second technical licensing OEM - Confirmation of model replicability
4. The stock price fell below 55 HKD - a more adequate margin of safety

**Potential give-up signals:**
1. Delivery volume fell year-on-year for 2 consecutive quarters
2. Gross profit margin dropped below 15%
3. Public cooperation is delayed or reduced in scale
4. Operating cash flow turns negative again

## 8. Core Conclusions

Xpeng Motors is a target with "correct direction, unclear path, reasonable current stock price, and the need for catalytic confirmation".

The positive elements are already in place: leading technology, public endorsement, early profit turning point, and abundant cash. But whether it can truly transform from "a company that sells cars" to an "AI technology platform company" is the core question that determines its long-term value - there is currently no definite answer to this question.

**Final rating: Included in the recall pool, continue to track, wait for catalytic signals and then choose the opportunity to open a position. **

Prioritize indicators of concern:
1. Monthly Delivery Volume Trends
2. Performance of Volkswagen’s cooperative models after their launch
3. The emergence of the second technology licensing customer
