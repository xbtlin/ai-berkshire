# China Hongqiao (1378.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: profitability, asset and liability quality, cash flow, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Analysis date: 2026-05-16

---

## 1. Profitability analysis

### 1.1 Revenue and profit scale

China Hongqiao's key data for the whole year of 2025:
- **Operating income**: 162.354 billion yuan (+4.0% year-on-year)
- **Net profit**: 22.636 billion yuan (+1.2% year-on-year)
- **Net profit margin**: approximately 13.9%

Income structure split:
- Aluminum alloy product revenue: approximately 106.1 billion yuan (+3.6% year-on-year), accounting for approximately 65%
- Alumina product revenue: approximately 38.8 billion yuan (+4.0% year-on-year), accounting for approximately 24%
- Other income (including aluminum processing products, etc.): approximately 17.5 billion yuan

It is worth noting that the full-year net profit growth rate in 2025 is only 1.2%, which is significantly lower than the performance in the first half of the year (H1 net profit is 12.361 billion yuan, +35% year-on-year). This means that **performance in the second half of the year dropped significantly**, mainly due to the correction of alumina prices from high levels, which dragged down the profits of the alumina external sales business. This reflects that although the company's main business is electrolytic aluminum, fluctuations in alumina prices have a significant impact on profits.

### 1.2 Earnings Quality Assessment

**ROE level: about 22%**

This ROE level is excellent in cyclical industries. Compare to peers:
- Chalco (2600.HK): ROE about 8-12%
- Yunnan Aluminum Co., Ltd. (000807.SZ): ROE about 15-18%
- Tianshan Aluminum (002532.SZ): ROE about 12-15%

China Hongqiao's ROE continues to be higher than its peers, mainly due to three factors:
1. Higher net profit margin (brought by cost advantage)
2. High asset turnover rate (capacity utilization is extremely high, close to full production)
3. Moderate financial leverage (asset-liability ratio 59%)

**Gross profit margin analysis:**
- The gross profit margin of H1 in 2025 is approximately 25.7%, of which the gross profit margin of alumina products is as high as 35.4%
- Full-year gross profit margin declined due to the fall in H2 alumina prices
- Gross profit margin of electrolytic aluminum core business remains relatively stable

### 1.3 Cyclical Characteristics of Profitability

As a commodity company, profits are significantly affected by aluminum prices. The average selling price of aluminum alloys in 2025 is about 17,853 yuan/ton (about 20,600 yuan/ton including tax), and management expects that the price of electrolytic aluminum in 2026 is expected to reach an average of about 23,000 yuan/ton (including tax), an increase of more than 2,000 yuan/ton compared with 2025.

If this expectation is realized, it means that the price increase of electrolytic aluminum alone in 2026 will bring about an incremental profit of approximately 13 billion yuan (6.545 million tons x 2,000 yuan/ton). Adding the contribution of alumina and Simandou iron ore, brokers predict that the net profit in 2026 will reach approximately 34.8 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of approximately 54%.

**Profitability Rating: ★★★★☆**
ROE is excellent, and net profit margin is outstanding in the resource industry, but it is necessary to pay attention to the sensitivity of profits to aluminum prices and alumina prices.

## 2. Balance sheet quality

### 2.1 Asset-liability ratio

The asset-liability ratio in 2025 will be approximately 59%. This level:
- Medium to high in the electrolytic aluminum industry
- Higher than Chalco (approximately 50%) and Yunnan Aluminum Holdings (approximately 45%)
- But it has improved from the company's historical high (which once exceeded 65%)

**Note**: The company has passed the peak period of concentrated debt repayment and has no concentrated debt maturity risk. As of November 2025, there are no non-performing loans or overdue records.

### 2.2 Related party transactions

This is an area that needs to be focused on.

As of March 2025:
- Long-term payables to related parties (China Hongqiao and Hongqiao Leasing) are approximately RMB 32.53 billion
-Other receivables from related parties are approximately RMB 99.12 billion

The amount of transactions between related parties is relatively large. Although they all have commercial substance (raw material procurement, asset leasing, etc.), the amount is relatively high compared to the company's net profit, and investors need to continue to track it.

### 2.3 Asset Structure

The company's assets are mainly fixed assets (electrolytic aluminum tanks, alumina production lines, self-owned power plants, etc.), which is a typical asset-heavy operation model. The good side is:
- Extremely high capacity utilization (97%+) and excellent asset efficiency
- Core equipment has a long life and maintenance capital expenditures are relatively controllable
- Overseas mineral resources (Guinea bauxite, Simandou iron ore) are "hidden assets" that are not fully reflected in the statements

**Asset and liability quality score: ★★★☆☆**
High debt ratio and large amounts of related transactions are points deduction items, but there is no concentrated debt repayment risk.

## 3. Cash flow analysis

### 3.1 Operating cash flow

As a low-cost manufacturer operating at full capacity, China Hongqiao has strong operating cash flow generation capabilities. Operating cash flow in H1 in 2025 will be good, supporting high dividends and buybacks.

Positive signs for cash flow quality:
- The company can continue to pay a high proportion of dividends (the dividend rate in 2025 is about 65%), indicating sufficient cash flow
- Management announced that it would not rule out further buybacks and would also need sufficient cash support.
- The final dividend in 2025 is HK$1.65 per share, higher than the full-year total of HK$1.61 per share in 2024

### 3.2 Capital Expenditure

The company has passed the period of large-scale production capacity construction. Current capital expenditures are mainly used for:
- Green energy projects in Yunnan base (photovoltaic power station construction)
- Overseas projects in Guinea and Indonesia
- Investments related to Simandou Iron Ore
- Equipment maintenance and technical upgrades

Due to capacity ceiling policy restrictions, the company is unable to build large-scale electrolytic aluminum production capacity, which is conducive to the release of free cash flow - it does not require large capital expenditures to "catch up with production capacity", but can return more cash to shareholders.

### 3.3 Free Cash Flow Assessment

Judging from the intensity of dividends and repurchases, the company's free cash flow has been abundant in recent years. Since 2006, the cumulative net profit has been 117.116 billion yuan, the cumulative dividends have been 43.482 billion yuan, and the historical dividend rate is approximately 38.9%. The dividend rate has increased to 60-65% in the past two years, indicating that management's confidence in cash flow is increasing.

**Cash flow rating: ★★★★☆**
Operating cash flow is stable, capital expenditures are controllable, free cash flow is good, and high dividends are sustainable.

## 4. Capital allocation capability

### 4.1 Dividend Policy

The dividend rate in 2025 is approximately 65%, with a final dividend of HK$1.65 per share. Based on the current stock price of approximately HK$35, the dividend yield is approximately 4.7%. If the net profit in 2026 increases to 34.8 billion yuan as expected and the dividend rate is maintained at 60-65%, there is room for further increase in dividends.

### 4.2 Buyback

Management has stated that it will not rule out further buybacks based on market conditions. Buybacks show confidence in a company's value and are friendly to long-term shareholders.

### 4.3 Investment Decision

- **Guinea Bauxite**: The strategy is correct, ensuring long-term raw material security, and the winning alliance model is mature.
- **Indonesia Alumina**: production capacity of 2 million tons, diversifying regional risks and reasonable overseas expansion
- **Simandou Iron Mine**: Penetration holds 21.36% of the shares and will be put into production by the end of 2025. After full production, the annual production capacity will be 60 million tons. This investment deviates from the main aluminum industry, but iron ore is also a scarce commodity resource in the world and may bring considerable returns in the long run.
- **Yunnan Clean Energy Transformation**: 2GW of PV has been built and 4GW is under construction, which not only reduces costs but also complies with the trend of carbon neutrality

**Capital Allocation Rating: ★★★★☆**
Generous dividends, focused investment, and active buybacks. Simandou Iron Mine's deviation from its main business is a minor flaw.

## 5. Valuation analysis

### 5.1 Current Valuation Level

Based on a net profit of 22.636 billion yuan and a market value of approximately HK$350 billion (approximately RMB 310 billion) in 2025:
- **PE(TTM)**: about 12-13 times
- **PB**: about 2.5-3 times
- **Dividend Yield**: About 4.5-5%

### 5.2 Valuation comparison with peers

| Company | PE (TTM) | ROE | Dividend Yield |
|------|-----------|-----|--------|
| China Hongqiao | 12-13x | ~22% | ~4.7% |
| Chalco | 15-18x | ~10% | ~2% |
| Yunnan Aluminum Shares | 12-15x | ~16% | ~3% |
| Tianshan Aluminum | 10-12x | ~13% | ~3% |

China Hongqiao's ROE and dividend rate are significantly better than its peers, but its PE is not lower than its peers, reflecting that the market has given a certain "leading premium" and "dividend premium".

### 5.3 Dynamic Valuation Outlook

Brokerages unanimously expect net profits from 2026 to 2028 to be 34.8 billion yuan, 37 billion yuan, and 39.3 billion yuan respectively. If calculated based on 34.8 billion yuan in 2026:
- The current market value is approximately RMB 310 billion, corresponding to approximately 8.9 times the 2026E PE
- If 10 times PE is given, the corresponding reasonable market value is about 348 billion yuan (about 393 billion Hong Kong dollars)
- If 12 times PE is given, the corresponding reasonable market value is approximately RMB 417.6 billion (approximately HKD 471.8 billion)

### 5.4 "Safety Margin" of Valuation

**Front:**
- 12-13 times PE is not expensive for a company with ROE 22%
- If the expected increase in aluminum prices in 2026 is realized, the current valuation has a margin of safety
- High dividends provide the "reward you've been waiting for"

**Caution:**
- Commodity companies usually have the lowest PE when profits are high and the highest PE when profits are low. This is the "PE trap"
- 12-13 times PE is based on profits in high-prosperity years, and needs to take into account the scenario of a fall in aluminum prices
- The Hong Kong stock market usually gives valuation discounts to family businesses and resource stocks

### 5.5 Stress Test

If the aluminum price falls back to 18,000 yuan/ton (tax included), assuming that the cost of aluminum per ton is 14,500 yuan (tax included), the profit per ton is only 3,500 yuan:
- Electrolytic aluminum profit: 6.545 million tons x 3,500 yuan = 22.9 billion yuan
- Excluding alumina profit reduction and other factors, net profit may fall back to 15-18 billion yuan
- Corresponding to the current market value PE is 17-21 times

This shows that the current valuation implies an expectation that aluminum prices will remain high. If aluminum prices fall back sharply, the "margin of safety" in valuations will be significantly narrowed.

**Valuation Rating: ★★★☆☆**
The current valuation is reasonable but not "significantly undervalued." There is a certain margin of safety based on optimistic profit expectations for 2026, but we need to be wary of the "PE trap" at cycle highs.

### Core conclusion

China Hongqiao's financial characteristics are a combination of "high ROE + high dividends + moderate leverage", which is superior in cyclical industries. The core contradiction in valuation is: if you believe that aluminum prices will remain high or continue to rise, the current PE of 12-13 times is cheap; if you believe that the current price is at a cycle high, then this PE may be a "sweet trap." Investors need to make their own judgments on the medium-term trend of aluminum prices. The high dividend (about 4.7%) provides some downside protection, but is not enough to fully hedge against a sharp drop in aluminum prices.
