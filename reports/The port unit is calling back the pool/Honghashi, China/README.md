## Company Profile

China Hongqiao is one of the world's largest electrolytic aluminum manufacturers. It was founded by Zhang Shiping, the "richest man in Shandong", and is now helmed by his son Zhang Bo. The company has built a complete integrated industrial chain from bauxite mining, alumina production, self-generated power to electrolytic aluminum smelting, with an electrolytic aluminum production capacity of approximately 6.46 million tons and alumina production capacity of approximately 21 million tons. In 2025, it will achieve revenue of 162.354 billion yuan and net profit of 22.636 billion yuan.

## Core investment thesis

- **One of the lowest-cost electrolytic aluminum producers in the world**: The cost of aluminum per ton in 2024 is about 13,232 yuan, far lower than the industry average
- **Capacity ceiling policy confers scarcity**: China’s electrolytic aluminum production capacity has an upper limit of 45 million tons, and its operating capacity has reached 44.1 million tons, with very little room for new additions.
- **Structural growth on the demand side**: New energy vehicles, photovoltaics, energy storage, and AI data centers bring new aluminum demand
- **High dividend attributes**: The dividend ratio in 2025 is about 65%, and the final dividend is HK$1.65 per share.
- **Strong overseas resource reserves**: Guinea bauxite + Simandou iron ore + Indonesian alumina
