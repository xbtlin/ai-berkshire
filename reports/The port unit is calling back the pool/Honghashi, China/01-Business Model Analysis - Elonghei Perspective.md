# China Hongqiao (1378.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: essence of business model, depth of moat, user value, long-term competitive advantage
> Analysis date: 2026-05-16

---

## 1. What kind of business is this?

### 1.1 Nature of business

Electrolytic aluminum is a typical commodity industry - the products are highly homogeneous, and companies cannot obtain premiums through branding or differentiation, and can only rely on cost control to make money. In this type of industry, the core competitiveness of long-term winners is not "selling expensive" but "doing cheap". This is completely different from the logic of the consumer goods industry.

China Hongqiao's core business model can be summarized as: **A low-cost electrolytic aluminum manufacturer integrating the entire industry chain**.

The company has formed a vertically integrated industrial chain from upstream bauxite mining (Guinea's Bocai mining area, proven resources of 600 million tons), to midstream alumina production (domestic 17.5 million tons + Indonesian 2 million tons, a total production capacity of 19.5 million tons), to self-produced power (installed capacity of 9.92 million kilowatts, power self-sufficiency rate of about 50%), and finally to core product electrolytic aluminum smelting (capacity of about 6.46 million tons).

### 1.2 Value creation chain

```
Guinea Bauxite → Alumina Production → Own Power → Electrolytic Aluminum Smelting → Aluminum Alloy/Aluminum Processing Products
   (raw materials) (intermediate products) (energy) (core) (extension)
```

Full year data for 2025:
- Electrolytic aluminum output 6.545 million tons
- Alumina production of 20.1378 million tons (self-sufficiency rate exceeds 160%, and the excess is sold externally)
- Aluminum alloy product revenue is approximately 106.1 billion yuan, and alumina product revenue is approximately 38.8 billion yuan
- Total revenue was 162.354 billion yuan, net profit was 22.636 billion yuan

### 1.3 The "simplicity" of the business model

From Duan Yongping's perspective of "doing the right thing", electrolytic aluminum is an "understandable" industry. Product demand has grown steadily in the long term, and the technology route is mature and there is no risk of being subverted. Aluminum will not be replaced – its physical properties (light, conductive, corrosion-resistant) are unique in the world of lightweighting.

But "simple" does not mean "easy". The difficulty of this business lies in the extreme management of scale and costs.

## 2. Where is the moat?

### 2.1 Cost advantage moat ★★★★☆

This is the core moat of China Hongqiao. In 2024, the company's electrolytic aluminum ton cost will be approximately 13,232 yuan/ton, a year-on-year decrease of 3%, and is in the lowest range of the global electrolytic aluminum cost curve.

Three major sources of cost advantage:

**(1) Electricity cost**
Electricity accounts for approximately 34% of electrolytic aluminum production costs. The company has its own power plant in Shandong (installed capacity: 9.92 million kilowatts), with an electricity self-sufficiency rate of approximately 50%. Self-generated electricity has significant cost advantages over purchased electricity. At the same time, the company has deployed hydropower aluminum production capacity in Yunnan and has built approximately 2GW of photovoltaic power stations. It will also complete 4GW of photovoltaic projects in the future to further reduce electricity costs.

**(2) Self-sufficiency in alumina**
Alumina is the core raw material of electrolytic aluminum, accounting for about 30-35% of production costs. The company's approved alumina production capacity reaches 21 million tons. Calculated based on the electrolytic aluminum production capacity of 6.46 million tons, the self-sufficiency rate exceeds 160%. In 2024, the gross profit margin of alumina products will reach 35.4%, a year-on-year increase of 24.3 percentage points. Not only are self-use costs controllable, but external alumina sales can also contribute considerable profits.

**(3) Scale effect**
As one of the world's largest electrolytic aluminum producers, it has economies of scale in procurement, logistics, management and other aspects. The production bases are concentrated in Zouping, Shandong and Yunnan, with a significant cluster effect.

### 2.2 Policy supply barriers ★★★★★

This is a **unique and extremely rare** moat.

Since the supply-side reform in 2017, China's electrolytic aluminum production capacity has been strictly limited to a total cap of 45 million tons. As of 2025, domestic operating production capacity has reached approximately 44.1 million tons, with a capacity utilization rate of 97.55%, almost at full production. Newly built production capacity must be replaced by equal or reduced amounts, and must meet strict energy consumption and environmental protection requirements.

This means: **Existing production capacity is a scarce resource**. China Hongqiao has an electrolytic aluminum production capacity of 6.46 million tons, accounting for approximately 14.3% of the country's total. It is one of the largest holders of electrolytic aluminum production capacity by a single company in the world. These capabilities cannot be easily replicated.

### 2.3 Resource Barriers ★★★★☆

Bokai Bauxite Mine in Guinea: Developed through the "Win Alliance" model, the proven resources are about 600 million tons and the annual production capacity is about 60 million tons, ensuring the long-term supply of upstream raw materials.

Simandou Iron Mine: Penetration holds 21.36% of the shares and will be officially put into production in November 2025. After full production, the annual production capacity will be 60 million tons of iron ore, which will become a new profit growth point. Although the correlation between the core businesses of iron ore and aluminum is limited, they provide the company with diversified resource-based profits.

### 2.4 "Water level" assessment of the moat

**Positive factors:**
- The production capacity ceiling policy will not be relaxed in the short term, and supply-side barriers are "hard constraints"
- The alumina self-sufficiency rate exceeds 160%, and it has a stronger ability to resist risks amid alumina price fluctuations.
- The layout of overseas mineral resources is far-reaching, and long-term raw material costs are guaranteed

**Factors to pay attention to:**
- The cost advantage relies heavily on the self-supply power policy. If the self-supply power policy is adjusted, the cost structure may be affected.
- The Shandong base is dominated by thermal power, and carbon emission costs may rise in the future.
- The commodity industry's moat is essentially "cost-based" rather than "pricing-based", and profits will still be under pressure when aluminum prices fall sharply

## 3. Economic characteristics of this business

### 3.1 Cyclicity and anti-cyclicality

Electrolytic aluminum is a strong cyclical industry, but China Hongqiao has stronger anti-cyclical capabilities than its peers:

- **Aluminum price rising period**: The company benefits from the rising aluminum price like its peers, but due to lower costs, the profit per ton of aluminum is higher
- **Aluminum price decline period**: When aluminum prices fall close to the industry's marginal cost, high-cost production capacity is forced to reduce or even suspend production. However, China Hongqiao can still maintain profitability because its cost line is far lower than the industry average.

In 2025, the average selling price of the company's aluminum alloy products is about 17,853 yuan/ton, while the cost per ton is about 13,000-13,500 yuan/ton, and the profit per ton of aluminum is about 4,000-4,800 yuan. Even if aluminum prices fall by 20% to 14,000 yuan/ton, the company still has room for profit.

### 3.2 Capital intensity

Electrolytic aluminum is a typical heavy asset industry. The company's asset-liability ratio is about 59%. Although it is not extremely high, it requires continued attention among resource-based companies. The good thing is that the company has passed the period of concentrated capital expenditures and is currently focusing on maintenance capital expenditures.

### 3.3 Cash flow characteristics

Due to extremely high capacity utilization and controllable costs, the company has good cash flow generation capabilities. The dividend ratio in 2025 is about 65%, with a final dividend of HK$1.65 per share, showing that management is willing to share profits with shareholders.

## 4. In the long run, is this business "right"?

### 4.1 The perspective of "doing the right thing"

**What you did right:**
- Globally deploy bauxite resources to ensure the safety of upstream raw materials
- Completed production capacity accumulation before the introduction of the production capacity ceiling policy, occupying an unreplicable first-mover advantage
- Adhere to the integration strategy and do not engage in diversified expansion (basically focusing on the aluminum main line except Simandou Iron Mine)
- High dividend policy establishes credibility in the capital market

**Questionable points:**
- The governance structure of a family business with frequent related-party transactions (see risk assessment document for details)
- The Shandong base relies heavily on thermal power. Is the transformation fast enough under the general trend of carbon neutrality?
- Investment in Simandou Iron Mine deviates from its main business, and the "discipline" of capital allocation needs to be observed

### 4.2 Long-term certainty on the demand side

The demand growth drivers for aluminum are undergoing structural changes:
- **New energy vehicles**: The amount of aluminum used in a single vehicle exceeds 200kg, which is much higher than that of traditional vehicles. It can contribute to a million-ton increase by 2026
- **Photovoltaic and energy storage**: Photovoltaic frames and energy storage battery compartments use a large amount of aluminum
- **AI and power infrastructure**: The core material of UHV transmission lines is aluminum (copper is too heavy), and the expansion of AI data centers drives power investment
- Traditional real estate demand has declined (annual decrease of 600,000-800,000 tons), but is far exceeded by emerging demand (annual increase of 3-3.3 million tons)

From 2025 to 2027, China's electrolytic aluminum supply and demand gaps are expected to be 1.82 million tons, 2.13 million tons, and 2.86 million tons respectively, and import dependence will continue to increase.

### Core conclusion

China Hongqiao's business model is not the "best business" - the commodity industry naturally lacks pricing power and brand premium. But in the field of commodities, it is the representative of "good business": the world's lowest cost, policy-based supply barriers, and demand-side structural growth. The three superpositions form a strong long-term competitive advantage. Key risks are family governance, energy transition pressures and cyclical fluctuations in aluminum prices.
