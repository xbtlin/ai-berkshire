## 2. Core investment thesis

### Reasons to be bullish

1. **The leading company with the lowest range of the global electrolytic aluminum cost curve**. The cost of a ton of aluminum in 2024 is about 13,232 yuan, and it has strong anti-cyclical capabilities.
2. **The capacity ceiling policy confers scarcity**. The 6.46 million tons of electrolytic aluminum production capacity is a "franchise" that cannot be copied. The operating capacity has reached 44.1 million tons (the upper limit is 45 million tons), and there is almost zero room for new additions.
3. **Structural growth on the demand side**, new energy vehicles, photovoltaics, energy storage, and AI power infrastructure have brought new aluminum demand of more than 3 million tons per year, and the supply and demand gap will continue to expand from 2025 to 2027 (182 → 213 → 2.86 million tons)
4. **High dividend return**, the dividend rate in 2025 is 65%, and the dividend rate is about 4.7%, providing compensation for waiting.
5. ** Deep overseas resource reserves **, Guinea bauxite (600 million tons) + Simandou iron ore (already in production) + Indonesian alumina (2 million tons), long-term raw material safety is guaranteed

### Reasons to be bearish/cautious

1. **Family business governance risks**, insufficient independence of the board of directors, large scale of related transactions (nearly 100 billion yuan receivable from related parties), and information transparency needs to be improved
2. **PE trap at cycle highs**, the current PE of 12-13 times is based on high boom profits. If the aluminum price drops sharply to 18,000 yuan/ton, the net profit may shrink to 15-18 billion yuan, and the PE will rise to 17-21 times
3. **Uncertainty of the self-supply power policy**. The company’s cost advantage of about 50% of its power self-sufficiency rate partially relies on the self-supply power policy. If the policy is adjusted, it will directly affect the cost structure.
4. **Carbon emission transformation pressure**, Shandong base is mainly thermal power, green transformation requires time and a lot of investment
5. **Has been questioned by short-selling institutions**. Although there is no real evidence, it suppresses the valuation of Hong Kong stocks.

## 3. The gap with "great companies"

China Hongqiao is an "excellent cyclical company", but there is still a gap between it and a "great company":

- **Lack of pricing power**: Commodity companies are unable to set prices independently, and profits are dominated by aluminum price fluctuations
- **Lack of brand premium**: Aluminum is a standardized product with no differentiated competitive advantages
- **Insufficient governance structure**: High degree of family control, far from global best governance practices
- **Limited room for growth**: The capacity ceiling is both a moat and a ceiling - the company cannot significantly expand electrolytic aluminum production capacity

It is more like a "cash bull asset" - stably producing cash flow and giving back to shareholders through high dividends, but it is difficult to achieve exponential growth in profits.

## 6. Final conclusion

China Hongqiao is the "cost king" in the electrolytic aluminum industry. It occupies the most favorable competitive position in an industry landscape where supply is locked by policies and demand is driven by new energy sources. ROE is 22%, dividend rate is 65%, and dividend rate is about 4.7%. The financial indicators are excellent in cyclical industries.

**But investors need to face two core questions honestly:**

**First, can you accept the cost of family governance? ** The large scale of related-party transactions, insufficient board independence, and short-selling issues—these problems will not disappear just because the company’s performance is good. A certain "governance discount" is already included in 12-13 times PE, but whether this discount is sufficient depends on your subjective assessment of these risks.

**Second, what is your judgment on the price of aluminum? ** Current valuations imply expectations that aluminum prices will remain high. If you think aluminum prices will further rise to 23,000 yuan/ton in 2026, the current price has a safety margin; if you think it is already a cycle high, then you need to wait for lower prices.

**In summary, China Hongqiao is a target worthy of being put into the observation pool - "good assets, average governance, reasonable valuation". ** The best time to buy may occur when a correction in aluminum prices causes market panic, when the safety margin brought by the low-cost advantage will truly appear.
