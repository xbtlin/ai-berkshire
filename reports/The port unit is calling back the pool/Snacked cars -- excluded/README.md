## Core investment thesis

**Bull argument:**
- The cost advantage brought by self-developed in the whole field (self-developed parts account for 65% of the total vehicle cost)
- Profit for the first full year in 2025, validating the business model
-Strategic cooperation with Stellantis opens up overseas markets
- The new force has the best-selling status and the scale effect continues to be released
- Target 1 million vehicles/5 billion net profit in 2026

**Short argument:**
- Q1 gross profit margin in 2026 dropped to 9.4%, with a net loss of 390 million
- The profit from bicycles is extremely thin (only 905 yuan will be earned from bicycles in 2025)
- The goal of 1 million vehicles is aggressive and the execution pressure is high
- Price war continues, competition from BYD/Geely and others intensifies
- Intelligent driving ability is relatively behind the first echelon
