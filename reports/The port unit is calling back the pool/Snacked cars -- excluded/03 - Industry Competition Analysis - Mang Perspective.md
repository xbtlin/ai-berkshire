# The fundamental characteristics of the industry

** This is not an easy business to make money.** The reasons are as follows:

1. ** Product differentiation limited**: $100,000-$20 million SUV, with consumer perception of the difference becoming smaller and smaller
2. ** Technology spread fast**: today ' s leading technology is followed tomorrow by competition
3. ** Price war normalization**: Biadi continued to wage price war with a large advantage, and all participants were forced to follow suit
4. ** High capital intensity**: Factories, molds, R & D require billions of dollars per year
5. ** Low brand loyalty**: The proportion of new energy car owners changing brands is much higher than the proportion of traditional fuel trucks

From the perspective of Mange, “Don’t look for good companies in bad industries,” the new energy automobile industry as a whole is an industry with a low return**. But there is a structural division within the industry – firms with cost advantages or brand premiums still get over-returns.
