## 2. Summary of investment thesis

### Bull Thesis (Why It Might Be Worth Buying)

1. **Structural cost advantage of global self-development**: Self-developed parts account for 65% of the total vehicle cost, with a gross profit margin of 14.5% (2025) at an average price of 100,000 yuan, which is unique in the industry

2. **The flywheel of scale is starting**: Sales volume will increase from 140,000 in 2023 → 290,000 in 2024 → 600,000 in 2025, which verifies the model.

3. **High certainty of overseas growth**: Stellantis cooperation has produced substantial results (Q1 overseas accounted for 37%), the Spanish factory will be put into production soon, and the two parties will further expand cooperation in May 2026

4. **Abundant cash and no shortage of ammunition**: 37.88 billion cash reserves and 7.8 billion free cash flow. We will not run out of ammunition in the price war.

5. **Relatively cheap valuation**: 0.85x PS is the lowest among new forces. If the profit target is reached, PE will only be 11 times

### Short Thesis (Why It Might Not Be Worth Buying)

1. **Profits are extremely fragile**: Bicycle profit was 905 yuan, Q1 gross profit margin plummeted to 9.4% and turned into a loss of 390 million yuan

2. **BYD’s shadow**: The same logic of vertical integration + cost leadership, but BYD’s scale is 8 times that of Lingpao, and its cost advantage is stronger

3. **Smart driving is obviously lagging behind**: With smart driving becoming the core competitiveness, Leapmotor is in a catching-up position

4. **The 1 million vehicle target is too aggressive**: Only 110,000 vehicles (11%) were completed in Q1, and it is extremely difficult to meet the target for the whole year.

5. **Weak brand power**: In a market characterized by consumption upgrades and brand differentiation, "cheapness" does not constitute sustainable differentiation.

6. **The industry itself has low returns**: New energy vehicles are a typical "growth trap" industry - high growth but difficult to make profits

## 4. Valuation Judgment

### Scenario analysis

| Scenario | Sales volume in 2026 | Net profit | Reasonable market value (RMB) | Reasonable stock price |
|------|-----------|--------|-------------------|----------|
| Optimistic (Meeting Standard) | 1 million vehicles | 5 billion | 100-150 billion | HKD 80-120 |
| Neutral (20% off) | 800,000 vehicles | 1.5-2 billion | 40-60 billion | HKD 33-50 |
| Pessimistic (40% off) | 650,000 vehicles | 0-500 million | 25-35 billion | HKD 20-29 |

### Current valuation judgment

The current price of HK$49.4/approximately HK$60 billion roughly corresponds to a "neutral to optimistic" scenario. The market has given a certain growth premium, but it is not extremely optimistic.

- If you believe that 1 million vehicles can be achieved: the current price is very attractive
- If you think 800,000 vehicles is a more pragmatic expectation: the current price is basically reasonable, and there is no obvious safety margin
- If you are worried about continued pressure on gross profit margin: the current price is too expensive

## 5. Investment decision-making framework

### Conditions suitable for buying

1. The Q2 financial report shows that the gross profit margin has rebounded to more than 12% (confirming that Q1 is a seasonal fluctuation)
2. Monthly sales are stable at more than 70,000-80,000 units (the target of one million units has entered the credible range)
3. The stock price falls back to the range of HKD 35-40 (providing sufficient safety margin)
4. If two of the above three conditions are met, you can consider opening a position.

### Conditions suitable for waiting and watching (current status)

1. The nature of the sharp drop in gross profit margin in Q1 is still unclear - is it seasonal or structural?
2. The credibility of the million-unit target requires data verification in Q2-Q3.
3. The current price corresponds to the neutral scenario and the margin of safety is insufficient.

### Signals that must be avoided

1. Gross profit margin <10% for two consecutive quarters
2. Management lowers full-year guidance
3. Stellantis reduces its holdings or reduces cooperation
4. Monthly sales continue to be less than 60,000 units

## 6. Relationship with investment portfolio

### If you consider configuring zero-running, questions that need to be answered

1. **Do you already own BYD? ** If so, Leapao is highly logically overlapping with BYD (both are cost-leading), and it needs to be assessed whether it constitutes repeated exposure.
2. **What is your judgment on the industry? ** If you believe that new energy vehicles are ultimately a "winner takes all" approach, you should hold BYD instead of Leappo; if you believe that multiple companies can coexist, Leapao is a good "second choice"
3. **Position size? ** Considering the high risk (fragile profits and fierce industry competition), even buying should be controlled within 3-5% of the portfolio
4. **Holding period? ** It will take at least 2-3 years to verify whether the scale effect of one million vehicles can be achieved.
