# Leapmotor (9863.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, balance sheet health, free cash flow, valuation rationality
> Core question: Are you financially healthy? Is the money earned real money or paper profits? Are the current prices reasonable?

## 1. Income analysis: What is the quality of growth?

### Revenue growth trajectory

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate | Delivery volume (10,000 units) | Average bicycle price (10,000 yuan) |
|------|-------------|----------|---------------|----------------|
| 2023 | ~167 | +35% | 14.4 | ~11.6 |
| 2024 | ~321 | +92% | 29.4 | ~10.9 |
| 2025 | 647.3 | +101% | 59.66 | ~10.9 |
| Q1 2026 | 108.2 | +8% | 11.02 | ~9.8 |

**Key Findings:**

1. **Volume increases and price stabilizes, volume increases and price decreases**: From 2024 to 2025, the ideal state of "volume and price soaring" was achieved. However, the average price of a bicycle in Q1 of 2026 has dropped to about 98,000 yuan, indicating that new models (such as the A series) have lowered the average price, and at the same time, the pressure of price wars has emerged.

2. **Q1 growth slows down significantly**: The growth rate of 101% in 2025 will be only 8% in Q1 of 2026. This is mainly because: a) the Q1 base in 2025 itself is not low (10 billion); b) the Spring Festival factor; c) new models (A10, D19) have not yet fully expanded in Q1. The record-breaking performance of 71,400 vehicles in April indicates that a recovery is expected in the future.

3. **The proportion of overseas revenue has increased rapidly**: 40,900 vehicles were delivered overseas in Q1, accounting for 37%. This is an important structural change.

### Revenue quality assessment

Almost 100% of Leapao's revenue comes from vehicle sales, which is both an advantage (simplicity and transparency) and a risk (over-reliance on a single source of income). Compared with Lili (which already has income from charging services) and Tesla (which has income from software subscriptions), Leapmoon’s income is less diversified.

## 2. Profitability: The gold content of 540 million net profits

### Income statement dismantling

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 Q1 |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Gross profit margin | ~10% | 14.5% | 9.4% |
| R&D expense ratio | ~10% | ~7.5% | ~9% |
| Sales and administrative expense ratio | ~8% | ~6% | ~8% |
| Net profit margin | Loss | 0.83% | -3.6% |

**Gross Profit Roller Coaster**

The gross profit margin rising to 14.5% in 2025 is the key to full-year profitability. However, the gross profit margin in Q1 of 2026 plummeted to 9.4%, a decrease of 5 percentage points. Reason analysis:

1. New models are priced aggressively at the initial stage of their launch (the starting price of the A10 starts at 79,800 yuan)
2. Rising raw material costs (lithium carbonate prices rebound)
3. Amortization of fixed costs during the production ramp-up period
4. Capacity utilization decreased during the Spring Festival

**Extremely low bicycle profits are the core concern**

Net profit in 2025 is 540 million yuan / 596,600 units = **Net profit of bicycles is only 905 yuan**. This means that Lingpao's profit is a "balance on the tip of a knife" - any small fluctuations in costs or fine-tuning of selling prices may turn profits into losses. Q1 2026 has proven this.

### Comparison with peers’ gross profit margin

| Automobile companies | Gross profit margin in 2025 | Average bicycle price | Remarks |
|------|-------------|----------|------|
| BYD | ~22% | ~150,000 | Vertical integration + scale effect |
| Li Auto | ~20% | ~300,000 | Brand premium + high price |
| Xpeng Motors | ~14% | ~200,000 | Technology premium |
| Leapmotor | 14.5% | ~109,000 | Global self-research + low price |

Achieving a gross profit margin of 14.5% at an average price of 100,000 yuan indeed reflects the cost advantage of self-research. However, the 22% gap with BYD shows that there is still much room for improvement in scale effects.

## 3. Balance sheet health

### Cash and Liabilities

| Indicators | End 2025 |
|------|----------|
| Cash and equivalents | 37.88 billion yuan |
| Operating cash flow (full year) | 12.62 billion yuan |
| Free cash flow (full year) | 7.82 billion yuan |
| Asset-liability ratio | About 60-65% |

**Abundant cash reserves**: 37.88 billion yuan of funds on hand, which can support operations for 3-4 years even if it does not make money. This provides sufficient strategic buffer for Zero Run, so that it will not run out of ammunition and food in the price war.

**Operating cash flow far exceeds net profit**: Operating cash flow in 2025 is 12.62 billion, while net profit is only 540 million. The difference mainly comes from: depreciation and amortization (R&D capitalization part), increase in accounts payable (increased bargaining power with suppliers), and increase in advance receipts. This shows that Lingpao's "cash earning ability" is much better than the figures presented in the income statement.

**Free cash flow of 7.82 billion is the key highlight**: After deducting capital expenditure, there is still 7.8 billion free cash flow, indicating that Leappo has the ability to "self-generate" and no longer relies on financing to continue its life.

### Asset Quality

Items that need attention:
- **Inventories**: The inventories of the automobile industry are mainly raw materials and finished vehicles. If sales are lower than expected, there is a risk of impairment.
- **Accounts receivable**: The government subsidy payment cycle is long, which may affect the rhythm of cash flow.
- **R&D Capitalization**: You need to pay attention to the capitalization ratio of R&D investment. Excessive capitalization will inflate profits.

## 4. Free cash flow analysis

### The "truth" of the cash flow statement

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 |

| Cash flow from operating activities | About 5 billion | 12.62 billion |
| Capital expenditure | About 3 billion | About 4.8 billion |
| Free cash flow | About 2 billion | 7.82 billion |

The substantial growth in operating cash flow and free cash flow is the most solid foundation for the investment value of Lingpao. **The income statement can be embellished, but cash flow does not lie. **

But please note:
1. If we want to sprint to 1 million vehicles in 2026, capital expenditures will increase significantly (new factory expansion, new model molds, etc.)
2. The collection cycle of accounts receivable in overseas markets may be longer than in China.
3. If the gross profit margin level in Q1 continues, the quality of operating cash flow will decline significantly.

### Several ways of thinking about valuation

**Scenario 1: Achieve 5 billion net profit target in 2026**

If Leapao really achieves a net profit of 1 million vehicles/5 billion, based on the current market value of 55 billion yuan, the forward-looking PE for 2026 will be only 11 times. For a high-growth tech company, that's extremely cheap. But - Q1's loss of 390 million has cast a shadow on this goal. It is extremely difficult to reach the target by earning 5.4 billion in the remaining three quarters.

**Scenario 2: Actual net profit in 2026 is 1.5-2 billion**

If the annual sales volume is 800,000-900,000 vehicles and the gross profit margin returns to 12-13%, the net profit may be in the range of 1.5-2 billion. The corresponding PE is about 28-37 times. For a company that is still growing rapidly, the valuation is basically reasonable.

**Scenario 3: Continued losses in 2026**

If the gross profit margin continues to be low (<10%), coupled with overseas expansion expenses and R&D investment, there may be a loss or small profit for the whole year. At this time, valuation can only be based on PS (price-to-sales ratio). PS of 0.85x is not expensive in the automotive industry, but it is not cheap either.

### Core conclusion

Lingpao's financial statements present a contradictory picture: **Cash flow is healthy, but profits are fragile. **37.8 billion in cash and 7.8 billion in free cash flow indicate that this company will not fail; but the profit of 905 yuan per bicycle and the sharp drop in Q1 gross profit margin indicate that the profit model is not yet stable.

In terms of valuation, if it is believed that Leappo can stabilize its gross profit margin at more than 13% and annual sales of 800,000+ in 2026-2027, the current market value of HK$60 billion is significantly underestimated. But if the gross profit margin continues to be under pressure, the current valuation does not have much of a margin of safety.

**Key Tracking Indicators**: Whether Q2-Q3 gross profit margin can return to above 12% is a decisive signal. If it can, it means that Q1 is a seasonal fluctuation; if it cannot, the profit target of 5 billion in 2026 will become empty talk.
