# Analysis of business model of Leapmotor (9863.HK)

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, differentiation barriers, long-term sustainability
> Core question: Is this a good business? Where is the moat? Can it be sustained in the long term?

---

## 1. What is the essence of business?

The essence of Leapmotor's business is to provide highly cost-effective smart electric vehicles in the mainstream price range of 100,000-200,000 yuan with its global self-developed vertical integration capabilities. **

Take it apart:
- **Income model**: Selling cars (accounting for more than 95% of revenue) + a small amount of accessories and services
- **Value Proposition**: The highest configuration at the same price, and the high-priced functions are reduced to the mass price
- **Core capabilities**: Self-developed electric drive, battery PACK, smart cockpit, smart driving system, thermal management and other core components
- **Cost structure**: Self-developed parts account for about 65% of the total vehicle cost, eliminating supplier profit margins

Compared with traditional car companies and other new forces, Leapmo's business model is closer to BYD's vertical integration route, but its scale is much smaller than BYD. Unlike new forces such as Ideal and NIO that pursue brand premiums, Lingpao takes the route of "cost leadership + technology equality".

## 2. User value analysis

### What value does Leapao create for users?

1. **Extreme cost-effectiveness**: C11, C16 and other models provide configuration levels (lidar, large battery, high computing power chip) that far exceed the same price range at the price of 150,000-200,000.
2. **Technological equality**: Decentralize smart driving and smart cabin functions that are only available in the price range of 200,000 to 300,000 to the 100,000 to 150,000 level
3. **Good product consistency**: Because core components are self-developed, supply chain stability and quality control are relatively controllable.

### User portrait

The core users of Leapao are pragmatic consumers who are price-sensitive but pursue technology. They are concentrated in second- and third-tier cities, aged 30-45 years old, and mainly need family cars. This user group is large, but brand loyalty is relatively low - they buy "value" rather than "brand".

### The strategic significance of price bands

RMB 100,000-200,000 is the largest price range in China’s passenger car market, accounting for more than 40% of total sales. Zero Run chose this battlefield. The ceiling was very high, but the competition was also the fiercest. BYD, Geely, Changan, and Chery are all fighting here.

## 3. Global self-research—moat or burden?

### Positives: Structural advantages brought by self-research

1. **Cost advantage**: The self-developed electric drive system saves about 10% of the cost compared with external procurement; the self-developed battery PACK saves CATL’s intermediate profits
2. **Iteration speed**: Self-developed electronic and electrical architecture, OTA upgrade is not restricted by suppliers
3. **Differentiation**: CTC battery-chassis integration technology and Clover central domain control architecture are both self-developed and unique
4. **Gross profit protection**: When the industry engages in price wars, self-developed companies have more room to reduce prices.

Leapmotor's gross profit margin will reach 14.5% in 2025, which is already outstanding among car companies with an average price of 100,000 yuan. As a reference, the gross profit margin of Xpeng (average price is 200,000+) is only 14-15%.

### Opposite side: risks and challenges of self-research

1. **Sustainability of R&D investment**: More than 5 billion R&D investment is required every year. Once sales fall below expectations, R&D amortization will erode profits.
2. **Technology is broad but not refined**: Researching everything in-house means that investment in each field is limited. Smart driving indeed lags behind Huawei and Xpeng
3. **Scale Threshold**: When the scale is small (<300,000 vehicles/year), the efficiency of the self-developed model is not as good as that of external procurement. Only by increasing the scale can the cost be diluted.
4. **Talent bottleneck**: 5,000 engineers need to cover all fields such as battery, electric drive, smart driving, cockpit, chassis, etc., with a high degree of dispersion

### Key differences with BYD vertical integration

BYD's vertical integration is based on annual sales of more than 4 million vehicles, and batteries (Fudi), semiconductors, etc. can be supplied externally to earn additional profits. The vertical integration of Leapao currently only serves itself, and the scale effect has not yet been fully released. If Leapao can achieve annual sales of more than 1 million vehicles, this model will truly show its advantages.

## 4. Stellantis cooperation - the second growth curve?

### Cooperation mode

Stellantis holds a 20% stake in Leapao, and the two parties established Leapao International Joint Venture, with Stellantis responsible for the overseas sales and service network of Leapao products. The subtlety of this model is:

- Lingpao does not need to build its own overseas channels (save huge fixed cost investment)
- Leverage Stellantis’ 6000+ dealer network in Europe
- Localized production in Spanish factories to avoid tariff risks
- Stellantis’ brand endorsement lowers the trust threshold of overseas consumers

### Progress Assessment

- Export 67,000 vehicles in 2025, ranking first among new forces
- In Q1 2026, 40,900 vehicles were delivered overseas, accounting for 37% of the total, with rapid growth
- The Spanish factory is expected to start production in 2026, with the first model B10

### Risk

- Stellantis has its own operating difficulties (profit will drop sharply in 2024), and the sustainability of the cooperation is in doubt
- European electric vehicle subsidy policy is uncertain
- Geopolitical risks (tariff barriers, countervailing investigations)

## 5. Sustainability Assessment of Business Model

### Forward circulation (flywheel effect)

Larger scale → Dilution of self-research costs → More competitive prices → Further growth in sales → More investment in R&D → Improved product strength

This flywheel has initially started to turn in 2024-2025: sales doubled from 290,000 to 600,000, gross profit margin increased from about 10% to 14.5%, and profitability was achieved for the first time.

### Negative risk (possibility of flywheel stalling)

- If the target of 1 million units is not reached (for example, only 700,000-800,000 units are sold), the R&D amortization pressure will increase significantly.
- The continued price war has led to a decline in gross profit margin (gross profit margin has dropped to 9.4% in Q1 2026)
- If the performance of new models (A10, D19, etc.) does not meet expectations, the existing models will face aging again

### Thoughts on the long-term outcome

China's new energy vehicle market may eventually have only 5-8 major players left. Could Zero Run be one of them?

**Favorable factors**: The break-even point has been passed, the self-research system is complete, overseas channels have been established, and cash reserves are sufficient (37.88 billion)

**Unfavorable factors**: Brand power is weaker than BYD/Ideal/Huawei, bicycle profit is extremely thin (905 yuan/vehicle), and it is in a catching-up position in the smart driving arms race

### Core conclusion

The logic of Leapao's business model is self-consistent - self-research in all areas → cost leadership → cost-effective products → scale and volume → positive cycle. But this model is highly dependent on "scale". 600,000 vehicles in 2025 has proven that the model is feasible, and whether it can reach 1 million vehicles in 2026 will be the decisive verification. If the volume can continue to increase to 1 million+, this will be an excellent business; if it is stuck in the range of 600,000-800,000 units, the profit margin will continue to be squeezed by R&D investment and price wars.

**Summary in one sentence**: Good direction, but it is not yet at the stage where we can say "good business". The moat is under construction and has not yet been completed.
