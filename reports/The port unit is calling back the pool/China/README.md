## Company Profile

China Shenhua Energy Co., Ltd. (1088.HK / 601088.SH), affiliated to China Energy Group, is the world's largest listed coal company. The company has built a six-in-one closed-loop industrial chain of "coal-electricity-road-port-navigation-chemical" and is the absolute leading enterprise in China's energy industry.

## Major event: Hundreds of billions of assets reorganization comes to an end

From August 2025 to April 2026, China Shenhua completed the largest central enterprise asset reorganization in recent years - acquiring the equity of 13 companies under the National Energy Group for a consideration of 133.6 billion yuan, covering coal, electric power, chemicals, shipping and other fields. After the reorganization, the company's coal production capacity increased by 57%, power installed capacity increased by 28%, and coal reserves almost doubled.

## Research background

Coal, as the ballast stone of China's energy security, cannot be replaced in the short term. China Shenhua is regarded by the market as a "bond-like" asset due to its entire industrial chain layout, extremely low asset-liability ratio and stable dividend policy. However, the coal industry faces structural challenges such as peaking of long-term demand, new energy substitution, and carbon neutrality policies. This study attempts to answer: In the context of energy transition, is China Shenhua still a good investment?
