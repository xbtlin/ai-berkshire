# China Shenhua (1088.HK) Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Financial Quality and Intrinsic Value Assessment
> Core question: What is the true profitability behind the financial statements? Do current prices provide a margin of safety?
> Data cutoff: May 2026

---

## 1. Profitability analysis: the real water level after the ebb of the cycle

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Year | Operating income (100 million yuan) | Net profit attributable to parent company (100 million yuan) | Excluding non-net profit (100 million yuan) | Net profit rate |
|------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|--------|
| 2021 | 3352 | 503 | 493 | 15.0% |
| 2022 | 3445 | 697 | 693 | 20.2% |
| 2023 | 3431 | 593 | 570 | 17.3% |
| 2024 | 3396 | 558 | 601 | 16.4% |
| 2025 | 2949 | 528 | 486 | 17.9% |
| 2026Q1 | 704 | 107 | 107 | 15.2% |

Revenue will drop by 13.2% in 2025, but net profit will only drop by 5.3%, indicating strong cost control capabilities. Net profit after non-recurring fell 19.2%, and the gap with net profit attributable to the parent company mainly came from non-recurring income (including income related to asset restructuring).

**Key observation**: 2022 is the peak of the coal super cycle. Since then, coal prices have fallen year by year, but Shenhua’s net profit has always remained above 50 billion yuan. Even during the industry downturn, the company still showed strong profitability resilience. This is the value of the integrated business model - when coal prices fall, the profit release from the power business partially offsets the decline in the coal business.

### 1.2 ROE analysis

| Year | ROE | Net profit margin | Total asset turnover | Equity multiplier |
|------|-----|--------|-------------|---------|
| 2021 | 12.8% | 15.0% | 0.65 | 1.31 |
| 2022 | 16.3% | 20.2% | 0.63 | 1.28 |
| 2023 | 13.0% | 17.3% | 0.60 | 1.25 |
| 2024 | 14.0% | 16.4% | 0.57 | 1.30 |
| 2025 | ~11.0% | 17.9% | ~0.47 | ~1.30 |

ROE will drop to about 11% in 2025, mainly due to the substantial increase in net assets caused by the reorganization of hundreds of billions of assets and the denominator becoming larger. If the impact of restructuring is excluded, the core business ROE is still in the 13-14% range.

**Core findings from DuPont analysis**:
- Net interest rate remains in the 15-20% range, very stable
-The total asset turnover rate is declining year by year, reflecting that the expansion of asset scale is faster than the growth of revenue
- The equity multiplier is extremely low (1.25-1.30), indicating that there is little need for leverage

This is a "high profit margin, low leverage, medium turnover" business model. The upper limit of ROE is limited by low leverage, but the lower limit is also protected. It won't lose money when the industry is at its worst.

### 1.3 Historical profitability stability

In the ten years from 2015 to 2025, China Shenhua only experienced a significant profit decline in 2015-2016 on the eve of the most drastic supply-side reform in the coal industry (net profit in 2016 was approximately 22.7 billion yuan), but it has never suffered a loss. Since then, ROE has steadily recovered from 5.5% in 2015 to 14% in 2024.

**Key Judgment**: Even in the most extreme industry downturn, Shenhua can maintain profitability. This is a company that "won't die".

## 2. Balance sheet analysis: a "cash fortress"

### 2.1 Capital structure

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Asset-liability ratio | 23.4% | 23.3% |
| Interest-bearing liabilities | ~30 billion yuan | ~35 billion yuan |
| Monetary funds | ~70 billion yuan | ~60 billion yuan |
| Net cash (cash - interest-bearing liabilities) | ~40 billion yuan | ~25 billion yuan |

The asset-liability ratio is 23.3%, which is extremely rare in the coal industry (the industry average is about 55%). The cash on the company's books far exceeds interest-bearing liabilities, and it is a true "net cash" state.

**Key judgment**: This balance sheet is so clean that it is almost "luxury". In a downward interest rate environment, low leverage means that the company has huge room to increase leverage if necessary, but it also means that capital efficiency is suboptimal. For interest-earning investors, low debt is a kind of safety guarantee; for investors pursuing high ROE, this is a kind of "conservative excess".

### 2.2 Asset Quality

The number of projects under construction at the end of the first quarter of 2026 is approximately 34 billion yuan, mainly including:
- Well 1 and 2 in Taigemiao Mining Area, Xinjie (each with an annual production capacity of 8 million tons)
- Hangjin Energy Tarangole Mine (10 million tons/year production capacity)
- Multiple power projects

The retained coal resources are 41.4 billion tons (before reorganization) and 68.49 billion tons after reorganization. The recoverable reserves are 34.5 billion tons (after reorganization), which can be recovered for 67 years based on the annual output of 512 million tons.

**Key judgment**: Asset quality is excellent. Coal reserves are sufficient, projects under construction are progressing in an orderly manner, and there is no obvious risk of asset impairment.

## 3. Cash flow analysis: Is the profit real money?

### 3.1 Cash flow quality

| Year | Operating cash flow (100 million yuan) | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Cash/profit ratio | Capital expenditure (100 million yuan) | Free cash flow (100 million yuan) |
|------|-------------------|---------------|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 2022 | 1080 | 697 | 1.55 | 310 | 770 |
| 2023 | 1039 | 593 | 1.75 | 337 | 702 |
| 2024 | 933 | 558 | 1.67 | 335 | 598 |
| 2025 | 751 | 528 | 1.42 | 447 | 304 |

The operating cash flow/net profit ratio is greater than 1.4 for a long time, indicating that the profit quality is extremely high. For every 1 yuan of profit earned, 1.4-1.7 yuan of cash can be recovered. This is the top level among A-shares and Hong Kong stocks.

Free cash flow will drop significantly to 30.4 billion yuan in 2025, mainly due to an increase in capital expenditures to 44.7 billion yuan (restructuring-related investment). Capital expenditure is expected to drop to 38 billion yuan in 2026, and free cash flow is expected to rebound.

**Key Judgment**: This is "real money" profit, not paper profit based on accumulation of accounts receivable and inventory. Operating cash flow far exceeds net profit and is one of the most important indicators for judging financial quality.

**Key Judgment**: There is no doubt about the sustainability of the dividend. Even if coal prices fall further, with Shenhua's cash flow capabilities and low debt levels, there is no pressure to maintain a dividend ratio of 65-75%. Annual dividends of more than 40 billion yuan are one of the most stable cash returns in the Hong Kong stock market.

## 4. Valuation analysis: Is the current price expensive?

### 4.1 Static valuation

Calculated based on the net profit attributable to the parent company in 2025 of 52.8 billion yuan (Hong Kong stocks are about 45 Hong Kong dollars per share, corresponding to A shares are about 45 yuan):
- PE (TTM): about 12-13 times
- PB: about 1.3-1.4 times
- Dividend yield: approximately 4.9% (Hong Kong stocks)

Horizontal comparison:
| Company | PE | PB | ROE | Dividend Yield |
|------|-----|-----|------|--------|
| China Shenhua (Hong Kong) | 12-13x | 1.3x | 11% | 4.9% |
| Shaanxi Coal | 12x | 2.0x | 17% | 5.5% |
| China Coal Energy | 8x | 0.8x | 10% | 6.5% |
| Yankuang Energy | 9x | 1.0x | 12% | 5.0% |

Shenhua's PE is at a medium to high level in the industry, and its PB is also higher than that of China Coal and Yankuang. The market gives a "certainty premium."

### 4.2 DCF valuation ideas

Assumptions:
- Net profit will remain in the range of 48-55 billion yuan in the next five years (taking into account the moderate decline in coal prices + the increase in asset restructuring)
- Profits dropped by an average of 2-3% annually over the next 10 years (coal consumption peaked and then declined slowly)
- The final value is estimated based on sustainable zero growth and 10 times PE.
- Discount rate 8%

Under this set of conservative assumptions, the intrinsic value is approximately 0.9-1.1 times the current market capitalization. That is, **the current valuation is basically reasonable and is neither cheap nor expensive**.

### 4.3 Dividend discount model (DDM)

Assumptions:
- Dividend 40 billion yuan every year, with the dividend ratio maintained at 75%
- Dividend growth rate 0% (conservative assumption)
- Investors require a rate of return of 8%

DDM valuation = 400 / 8% = 500 billion yuan

The current market value of the Hong Kong stock market is about 450-500 billion Hong Kong dollars, and the value of the A-share market is about 900 billion yuan (the total value of A+H is about 1100-1200 billion yuan). DDM valuation suggests that the current price is basically within a reasonable range.

### 4.4 Liquidation value perspective

If Shenhua is viewed as a company that "slowly mines coal and then closes down" (extremely pessimistic assumption), its liquidation value includes:
- Net cash of approximately NT$25 billion
- Coal reserves of 34.5 billion tons (calculated based on resource value of 1-2 yuan per ton, approximately 34.5-69 billion yuan)
- The residual value of fixed assets such as railways, ports, and power plants is hundreds of billions of yuan.
- Cumulative distributable profits (assuming cumulative dividends of 600-800 billion yuan in the next 20 years)

The liquidation value far exceeds the current market cap, providing an ample margin of safety.

## 6. Core Conclusions

**China Shenhua's financial statements are the "textbook" for the coal industry. ** Extremely low debt, extremely high cash flow quality, extremely stable dividends - it has all the financial characteristics that value investors like.

But "good company" and "good price" are two different things. The current PE of 12-13 times reflects the market's recognition of its "bond-like" attributes, which also means that most of the good news has already been included in the price.

The core logic of buying Shenhua should not be "how much will it rise", but "even if coal prices fall by 20%, I can still get a 4-5% dividend every year, and this dividend is very safe." This is an investment with a high degree of certainty but a capped rate of return.

**Safety Margin Assessment**: The safety margin of current prices mainly comes from high dividends and low debt, not low valuations. If the PE of Hong Kong stocks falls back below 10 times (corresponding to a dividend rate of above 6%), it will provide a more attractive buying opportunity.
