# China Shenhua (1088.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis Dimension: Business Nature and Moat Depth
> Core question: What is the nature of this business? Can it be done for a long time? Why do users (customers) have to buy from you?
> Data cutoff: May 2026

---

## 1. The nature of business: What kind of business is this?

The essence of China Shenhua's business can be summarized in one sentence: **dig out the coal in the west, transport it to its own port by its own railway, load it on its own ship, and send it to the power plant in the east to burn it to generate electricity, or burn it directly at the pit mouth to generate electricity**.

This is a vertically integrated business "from the ground to the socket." Take it apart and look at:

**Coal Mining** (Profit Core): Commercial coal output in 2025 is 327 million tons, and the cost of self-produced coal is only 179 yuan/ton, which is the lowest level in the industry. This cost advantage comes from the resource endowment of the Shendong mining area - thick coal seams, shallow burials, and good mining conditions, which are God's blessings.

**Railway transportation** (the second profit pole): 2,155 kilometers of self-operated railways, and the core assets are Shenshuo Railway and Shuohuang Railway. Railways are characterized by large initial investment and extremely low later marginal costs. The more transportation is carried out, the more profitable it is. The gross profit margin of railway transportation in 2025 will be approximately 42%, which is an extremely high level in the transportation industry.

**Port Shipping** (Logistics Closed Loop): Huanghua Port is one of the world's largest coal loading ports, with a shipping volume of approximately 250 million tons in 2025. Ports and shipping transport coal from the place of production to the place of consumption, completing a closed logistics loop.

**Power business** (hedging tool): The installed power generation capacity is 47,600 MW (60,900 MW after restructuring), which is a natural coal price hedging tool. When coal prices are high, coal earns more and electricity earns less; when coal prices are low, electricity profits are released. This structure smoothes out the fluctuations in overall profits.

**Coal chemical industry** (incremental business): Businesses such as coal-to-olefins are high value-added utilization directions of coal. The current volume is small but of strategic significance.

The essence of this business is **low-cost resource monopoly + logistics network control + downstream consumption guarantee**. It is not simply "mining coal and selling coal", but a self-contained energy infrastructure platform.

## 2. Moat analysis: Why can’t others do it?

### 2.1 Resource endowment moat ★★★★★

China Shenhua's core mining area is located in the Shendong mining area in the Ordos Basin, which is one of the best thermal coal mining areas in China and even the world. Coal seams are thick (5-8 meters), shallowly buried, and have simple geological conditions, making them naturally suitable for large-scale mechanized mining. The cost of self-produced coal is 179 yuan/ton, which is 30-40% lower than the industry average.

After the reorganization is completed, the retained coal resources will reach 68.49 billion tons and the recoverable reserves will be 34.5 billion tons. Based on the current production capacity of 512 million tons/year, the mine can be mined statically for 67 years. These resources are non-renewable, and new entrants simply cannot obtain resources of the same quality.

**Key Judgment**: Resource endowment is the hardest moat. It does not depend on management capabilities, but on geological conditions and history. This is "the card given by God".

### 2.2 Infrastructure moat ★★★★★

There are 2,155 kilometers of self-operated railways and the annual shipping capacity of Huanghua Port exceeds 300 million tons - the cost of duplicating these infrastructures is extremely high. Even if someone is willing to invest tens of billions to build a railway, the approval, land acquisition, and construction cycle will take at least 10 years. More importantly, the road network effect makes the unit transportation cost decrease as the transportation volume increases.

Shenhua's transportation costs are 15-20% lower than external logistics, which means that even if coal prices fall, Shenhua can still maintain profitability, while small and medium-sized coal companies that rely on external transportation have already lost money.

**Key judgment**: Railways and ports are the second layer of moats. This is not just an asset, but also a "stuck position" - your coal must pass through my railway and port before it can go out.

### 2.3 Economies of scale moat ★★★★☆

The annual output of coal is 512 million tons (after restructuring), which is about 2.5 times that of Shaanxi Coal and about three times that of China Coal Energy. The advantage of scale is reflected in many aspects: procurement bargaining power, equipment utilization, management expense dilution, R&D investment capacity, etc.

However, the moat effect of economies of scale in the coal industry is not as obvious as in the technology industry - coal is a homogeneous commodity, and large scale cannot bring about differentiated pricing capabilities. So deducted one star.

### 2.4 Policies and the moat of central enterprises ★★★★☆

As the core listing platform of China Energy Group, China Shenhua enjoys natural advantages at the policy level:
- Output guarantee under the coal supply guarantee policy
- Price stability under the Changxie coal pricing mechanism (about 87% of sales volume is Changxie coal)
- Priority beneficiaries of major asset injections
- Policy buffer period in the transition to carbon neutrality

However, the status of a central enterprise also means that it must assume social responsibilities: price limits to ensure supply, investment in safety production, environmental compliance, etc., which will erode some profit margins.

## 3. Customer stickiness: Why does the power plant buy your coal?

The answer to this question is simple: **not because Shenhua’s coal is better than others, but because buying Shenhua’s coal is the easiest, most stable and cheapest**.

1. **Logistics closed-loop lock**: If the power plant is connected to Shenhua’s railway and port system, the switching cost will be very high. You can't build a new railway just to save a few dollars on coal prices.

2. **Changchang Xie Price Lock Guarantee**: 87% of sales are locked through the Changchun Xie, the power plant obtains price certainty, and Shenhua obtains sales volume certainty. Both parties benefit.

3. **Coal-power integration**: Shenhua’s own power plants consume a large amount of self-produced coal, and there is no problem of customer loss in this part. After restructuring, the installed power generation capacity is 60,900 MW, and self-consumption has further increased.

4. **Quality consistency**: The thermal coal produced in the Shendong mining area is of uniform quality, with a stable calorific value of around 5,500 kcal. There is basically no need for adjustment after the power plant boiler is adapted.

**Key Judgment**: Customer stickiness does not come from "brand loyalty", but from "infrastructure locking" and "minimum transaction costs". This lasts longer than brand stickiness.

## 4. "Changes" and "unchanges" of business models

### The unchanged part

- China's demand for thermal coal will not decline significantly in the next 5-10 years (even if new energy grows rapidly, the demand for existing thermal power is still huge)
- The scarcity of high-quality coal resources will not change
- The location advantages of infrastructure such as railways and ports will not change.
- The hedging logic of "coal and electricity integration" will not change

### Changed parts

- Coal consumption is expected to peak around 2028, and then enter a long-term slow decline channel
- The replacement of thermal power by new energy sources continues to accelerate, with new energy power generation increasing by more than 20% year-on-year in the first quarter of 2026.
- Tightening carbon emission policies may increase operating costs
- Integration and digestion risks brought about by the reorganization of hundreds of billions of assets - the management level and profitability of the 13 companies are uneven

## 5. Core torture from Duan Yongping’s perspective

**"If you can't explain the business in one sentence, you shouldn't invest in it."**

China Shenhua's business can be explained in one sentence: **Deliver the cheapest coal to the places where it is most needed in the cheapest way**.

**"Does it have pricing power?"**

No complete pricing power. Coal prices are affected by both market supply and demand and policy regulation, and Changxie coal prices are basically anchored by policies. But Shenhua has "cost immunity" - even if coal prices fall below 500 yuan/ton, it can still make money from its self-produced coal. This is more reliable than pricing power.

**"Is management doing the right thing or the easy thing?"**

The reorganization of hundreds of billions of assets is a difficult but correct thing to do. Injecting the group's high-quality assets into listed companies not only enhances the company's resource base, but also fulfills its commitment to minority shareholders. But the restructuring also brings short-term integration risks.

**"Will this company still be around in ten years?"**

Must be there. The strategic needs of national energy security determine the existence value of China Shenhua. But whether it can maintain its current level of profitability ten years from now depends on how quickly coal consumption declines and the effectiveness of the company's transformation.

## 7. Core Conclusions

China Shenhua is a "good business" but not a "great business".

"Good" lies in: low cost, full industry chain, high dividends, and strong certainty. In the current energy landscape, it is the coal company least likely to have problems.

"Not great" lies in the fact that coal is a sunset industry with no growth potential and only shrinking expectations. No matter how well Shenhua is managed, it cannot change the long-term trend that coal consumption will eventually peak and decline.

The logic of investing in Shenhua is not "growth", but "earning interest" - as the final winner in the gradual shrinkage of the coal industry, continue to pay dividends to shareholders. This is essentially a "bond-like + liquidation value" investment logic.
