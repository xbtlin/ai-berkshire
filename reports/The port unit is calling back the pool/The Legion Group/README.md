## Research background

Ctrip is the absolute leader in China's online travel industry, with a domestic OTA market share of about 56%, far exceeding the second place Tongcheng Travel (15%) and Meituan (13%). The company has four major brand matrices: Ctrip, Qunar, Trip.com and Skyscanner, covering four major business sectors: accommodation booking, transportation ticketing, travel and vacation, and business travel management.

**Current core contradiction:**

1. **Anti-monopoly investigation is pending**: On January 14, 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation opened an investigation for suspected abuse of market dominance. The investigation is still ongoing, and the fine may be between 6-6.5 billion yuan.
2. **Whether the valuation has fully reflected the risks**: The PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 14 times, which is a discount from the historical center, but it needs to be judged whether this is a value trap or a buying window
3. **Can internationalization open up the second curve**: Overseas business growth rate is 60%, and inbound tourism has doubled, but whether it can continue is doubtful
4. **Can AI reconstruction solidify the moat**: Can TripGenie and other products maintain competitive barriers under the impact of traffic from Douyin and Meituan?
