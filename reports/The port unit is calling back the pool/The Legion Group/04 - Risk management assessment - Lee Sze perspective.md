# Risk II: Flow-to-flow migration (Several: <unk> )

This is not a "spill" risk, but a "hot water boiling frog" long-term trend.

The Quescob brigade’s market share, although only 3%, is growing fastest. If it raises the rate of write-off from its current level to close to OTA, the journey will face a real loss of share.

The deeper problem is:** Travel policy habits of younger generations of users have changed**. They trust more in "the people recommend" than "the platform rank" and are more used to decision-making on content platforms. This intergenerational change is irreversible.

# Risk three: Internationalized input returns are uncertain (Severity: <unk> )

The internationalization of the process is the right strategic direction, but the risks are:

The rate of growth in the economy is 17% in 2025, but the sustainability of growth depends on the macro-environment. If the rate of growth falls below 10% in 2026, the market’s valuation of the process may further decrease.

# Risk five: Technological subversion (Several: <unk> )
