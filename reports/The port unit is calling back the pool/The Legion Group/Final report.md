## 3. Pros and cons of investment thesis

### Long view (why it may be worth buying)

**1. The industry’s leading position cannot be shaken in the short term**
56% market share, 26 years of supply chain accumulation, and the brand mentality of mid-to-high-end users. Even if antitrust penalties are implemented, the market position of the leading company will not fundamentally change based on the precedents of Alibaba and Meituan.

**2. Financial quality is top-notch**
81% gross profit margin, net cash position, and abundant operating cash flow. Even in the face of the most severe fine (6.5 billion yuan), the company's financial security will not be substantially threatened.

**3. Internationalization and inbound tourism are real growth engines**
In 2025, international OTA bookings will increase by 60%, and inbound tourism will double. The expansion of China’s visa-free policy creates unique opportunities for Ctrip that competitors will be hard-pressed to replicate.

**4. Valuations partially reflect pessimistic expectations**
14-17 times PE is low for a company with an annual growth rate of 15% and a gross profit margin of 80%+. If the negative effects of antitrust are exhausted, there is room for valuation restoration.

**5. Management is actively responding**
The advancement of the AI strategy (TripGenie), the adjustment of the organizational structure (new CPO and chief scientist), and the US$5 billion repurchase plan all show that the management is actively seeking changes.

### Bearish argument (why you probably shouldn’t buy)

**1. Antitrust investigations are uncontrollable "black boxes"**
The amount of fines (6-6.5 billion yuan) and the severity of behavioral restrictions are completely unpredictable. If asked to significantly reduce commission rates or do away with a core business tool (price adjustment assistant), the ceiling on long-term profitability would be lowered.

**2. The structural migration of traffic entrance is irreversible**
Douyin and Meituan are changing users’ travel decision-making paths. Ctrip may degenerate from "user's first touch point" to "back-end supply chain service provider", which will erode its pricing power and brand premium.

**3. The two co-founders resigned at the same time, which was a poor signal**
Fan Min and Ji Qi both resigned more than a month after the antitrust case was filed. Regardless of the actual reasons, this is not a positive signal in the eyes of the market. As Baidu continues to reduce its holdings, the actions of insiders are disturbing.

**4. Internationalization return cycle is long and uncertain**
Competing with Booking and Expedia on their home turf may require far more investment than expected. If internationalization does not progress as expected, high investment may drag down profit margins.

**5. The 81% gross profit margin may not be sustainable**
Regulatory pressure + intensified competition + supplier resistance are three factors that may depress gross profit margins in the medium term. Meituan and Douyin’s low commission strategies are also forcing the industry’s commission rates to fall.

## 4. Key Assumptions and Catalysts

### Positive catalysts (potentially driving the stock price higher)
1. Antitrust penalties are mild (fines <3 billion, behavioral restrictions are mild), and all the bad news is gone
2. International business achieves break-even or profitability
3. AI products such as TripGenie significantly improve conversion rates and user stickiness
4. Continued execution of large-scale repurchases

### Negative catalysts (could push stock price lower)
1. Severe antitrust penalties (fines >5 billion + strict behavioral restrictions)
2. Accelerated growth in share of Meituan or Douyin Wine & Travel
3. Macroeconomic deterioration beyond expectations
4. Baidu further significantly reduces its holdings

## 5. Investment strategy suggestions

### Current conclusion: wait, don’t take action

**Core logic**: The antitrust investigation is an event with a clear time window (results are expected in the second half of 2026). Before the results are announced, investors are faced with a "gamble" with an extremely wide probability distribution - it may be a big profit or a big loss. This is not how value investing works.

### Trigger conditions: Under what circumstances is it re-evaluated?

**Trigger conditions for purchase consideration:**

| Conditions | Description |
|------|------|
| The antitrust results were announced and moderate | The fine was <3 billion yuan, and the behavioral restrictions did not involve a significant reduction in the commission rate |
| The stock price fell below HK$350 | Corresponding to about 10-12 times operating PE, with sufficient safety margin |
| Management demonstrates crisis management capabilities | Quickly adjusts strategies and stabilizes supplier relationships after antitrust |
| International business data continues to shine | International OTA booking growth rate >40% for 2-3 consecutive quarters |

**Conditions for continuing to wait and see:**

| Conditions | Description |
|------|------|
| Antitrust penalties are severe | Fines > 5 billion yuan, or required to significantly reduce commission rates |
| Management turmoil continues | More key executives leave |
| Meituan/Douyin share accelerated growth | OTA landscape has undergone substantial changes |
| Gross profit margin began to decline | From 81% to below 75% |

## 8. Final judgment

Ctrip is a company that is most worthy of being put into the observation pool and continuously tracked. The quality of its business model, financial health and industry status are all excellent, and there is no doubt that it is a "good company".

But "good company" does not mean "buy now." The hanging sword of the antitrust investigation has not yet fallen. This is a risk event that can wait and has a time window. The cost of waiting is that you may miss a rebound, but the benefit of waiting is to avoid betting at the moment of greatest uncertainty.

**Operation suggestion: Put it into the recall pool and wait for the antitrust results to be clarified before re-evaluating. If the penalty is mild and the stock price falls below HK$350, you can consider opening a position. **
