## Research background

**Why pay attention to Sun Hung Kai Properties? **

1. **Extreme discount**: PB is about 0.32 times, which is equivalent to buying net assets at about 30% discount, which is rare in history
2. **Extremely conservative finances**: The net debt ratio is only 13.5%, far lower than the industry average, and there is almost no financial risk
3. **Stable Cash Flow**: Annual rental income exceeds HK$12 billion, providing downside protection
4. **Industry leader**: Hong Kong’s largest real estate developer, with a land reserve of approximately 57.3 million square feet in Hong Kong
5. **Macro turning point**: Hong Kong’s property market recovers after the recession, the interest rate cut cycle starts, and industry fundamentals improve

**Core question:**

- Is deep discounting a value trap or a value depression?
- How long can Hong Kong’s property market recovery last?
- How to assess mainland business risks (corruption incidents, pressure on commercial real estate)?
- Do family governance and succession issues pose long-term risks?
