## 1. The nature of Hong Kong’s real estate industry: a zero-sum game about land

### 1.1 Hong Kong real estate from the perspective of institutional economics

To understand Sun Hung Kai, we must first understand the underlying institutional logic of Hong Kong’s real estate industry:

**Land supply monopoly**: The Hong Kong government is the only land supplier. This single-source supply side, combined with the physical constraint that only about 25% of the 1,100 square kilometers is developed land, creates a naturally scarce market.

**Interest chain of land price policy**: The government relies on land sales revenue to maintain low tax rates, developers rely on land scarcity to maintain high profit margins, and owners rely on rising property prices to maintain wealth effects—the three parties have formed a solid community of interests.

**Oligopoly structure**: The four major families, Sun Hung Kai, Cheung Kong, Henderson, and New World, together account for 40-50% of new home sales. This level of concentration is extremely rare in real estate markets in major cities around the world.

What does this mean? Competition in Hong Kong’s real estate industry is not free market competition, but a game among oligarchs. The barriers to entry are extremely high (scarcity of land + financial threshold + relationship network), and the barriers to exit are also extremely high (sunk costs + binding family interests).

### 1.2 Industry cycle position: from "withdrawal" to recovery

Hong Kong's comprehensive "removal of spicy food" in February 2024 is an important policy turning point:

| Time node | Event | Impact |
|----------|------|------|
| February 2024 | Comprehensive withdrawal of spicy food | Stamp duty for non-local buyers reduced from 15% to 4.25% |
| September 2024 | The Federal Reserve begins to cut interest rates | Hong Kong mortgage interest rates follow the downward trend |
| 2025 | 20,000 first-hand residential transactions | A new high after the "first-hand new case" |
| January 2026 | Private residential price index 301.4 | Rising for eight consecutive months |
| 2026 Forecast | Property prices expected to rise 15% | Goldman Sachs raises forecast to +12% |

Current judgment: The Hong Kong property market is in the early to mid-term recovery stage. The withdrawal of spicy food has released pent-up demand, interest rate cuts have reduced purchasing costs, and "high-talented" policies have brought in incremental buyers. But the sustainability of the recovery still depends on interest rate trends and economic fundamentals.

## 2. In-depth dismantling of the competitive landscape

### 2.1 Comparison of the four major families: each has its own strengths

| Dimension | Sun Hung Kai | Cheung Kong Holdings | Henderson Land | New World Development |
|------|--------|----------|----------|------------|
| Core strengths | The strongest comprehensive, land bank + rent collection | The strongest finance, global diversification | The most farmland, deep cultivation in the New Territories | The strongest innovation, cultural tourism + K11 |
| Financial style | Extremely conservative | Ultra-conservative | More conservative | Relatively aggressive |
| Rent collection scale | Largest (annual 12 billion+) | Large (but globally dispersed) | Medium | Small |
| Residential development | Most active | Optional participation | Active | Slowing down |
| Management generational transition | Second generation → third generation transition | The third generation has taken over | Second generation → third generation transition | The third generation has taken over |
| Current valuation (PB) | ~0.32x | ~0.35x | ~0.25x | ~0.15x |

**Key Insight**: The PB ranking of the four major families (Cheung Kong > Sun Hung Kai > Henderson > New World) is highly consistent with their financial soundness ranking. The market's logic for pricing "a sense of security" is very clear.

### 2.2 Sun Hung Kai’s positioning in competition

Sun Hung Kai's competitive positioning can be summarized as an "all-rounder":

- **Residential Development**: No. 1 in market share and highest brand recognition
- **Shopping mall operation**: holds the largest property area and the most core locations
- **Office Building**: Top assets such as ICC
- **Data Center**: Became the largest data center operator in Hong Kong through SUNeVision
- **Telecommunications**: SmarTone provides stable income

This strategy of "doing everything without being bad" has the advantage of strong risk resistance and diversified income; the disadvantage is that it is difficult to establish an overwhelming advantage in a certain segment.

### 2.3 Where do competitive threats come from?

**Competition from mainland developers has weakened**: In the past few years, the debt crisis of mainland real estate companies (Evergrande, Country Garden, etc.) has caused a sharp contraction in mainland capital that had previously actively entered the Hong Kong market. In the short term, mainland developers will not pose a real threat to Hong Kong's Big Four.

**Competition from institutional capital is increasing**: Global institutional capital such as Blackstone and BlackRock are increasingly interested in Hong Kong’s core assets. However, they are more likely to buy ready-made assets rather than develop them from scratch, and their direct competition with Sun Hung Kai is limited.

**Challenges from alternative models**: The impact of REITs on the traditional development + holding model deserves attention. REITs such as Link can operate commercial properties with higher efficiency, but they cannot compete with the Big Four in terms of development capabilities and land acquisition.

## 3. Multidimensional mental model analysis

### 3.1 Psychological Model: Anchoring Effect and Mean Regression

The market’s pricing of Hong Kong property stocks is affected by a strong anchoring effect:

- **Downward Anchoring**: Investors are anchored to the long-term downward trend from 2019-2023 and lack confidence in the recovery
- Psychological anchoring of **Family Governance Discount**: The market has discounted family holding companies for a long time, but whether this discount is reasonable requires detailed analysis.
- **Mean Reversion Expectation**: PB fell from the historical median of 0.55-0.65 times to 0.32 times. The power of mean reversion will work sooner or later, but the time is uncertain

### 3.2 Physics model: potential energy and kinetic energy conversion

Compare Sun Hung Kai to a heavy object hanging high:

- **Potential energy (potential value)**: 600 billion net assets, 12 billion annual rent, 57.3 million square feet of land reserve
- **Momentum (release catalysts)**: Property market recovery, interest rate cut cycle, valuation repair
- **Resistance (Inhibiting Factors)**: Family governance discount, low ROE, mainland risks

The current state is: there is plenty of potential energy but kinetic energy has just begun to accumulate. The withdrawal of spicy food and interest rate cuts in 2024 are the starting point for the release of momentum, but whether it can be transformed into a sustained stock price increase requires more catalysts.

### 3.3 Ecological model: ecological niche and adaptability

In the "ecosystem" of Hong Kong real estate, Sun Hung Kai occupies the ecological niche of the "top predator":

- Top of the food chain, almost no natural enemies
- But it also means that growth is limited by the total size of the ecosystem (Hong Kong’s economic size and population)
- Strong adaptability (two-wheel drive can switch strategies in different environments)
- But evolution is slow (organizational inertia of large family businesses)

### 3.4 Historical Model: The Cycle of Empires

From a historical perspective, family business empires generally face the "three-generation curse":

- **First Generation (Guo Desheng)**: Entrepreneurship, starting from scratch
- **Second generation (Guo Binglian, etc.)**: Defend the status quo and maintain the empire
- **The third generation (Guo Jihui, Guo Jihong)**: a critical period of transformation or decline

It is currently in the critical stage of handover from the second generation to the third generation. Historical experience shows that the risk at this stage is not the sudden collapse of the enterprise, but the slow marginalization caused by insufficient innovation.

## 4. Industry trends and long-term prospects

### 4.1 Favorable trends

1. **Interest rate cut cycle**: The Fed's interest rate cut trend continues, and Hong Kong mortgage interest rates fall, which directly benefits real estate sales and asset valuations.
2. **North Water Goes South**: Under the interconnection mechanism, mainland funds continue to flow into Hong Kong stocks, and real estate stocks are the first choice for low-value areas.
3. **Tightening supply**: Pre-sale approvals will slow down after August 2025, and the supply of new properties will tighten. The competition for 1 buyer for 2 units will increase to 1.1 people for 1 unit.
4. **Talent introduction**: The "Gaocaitong" plan continues to introduce high-net-worth talents to provide incremental demand for the high-end residential market
5. **Data center demand**: The AI wave promotes the explosion of data center demand, and SUNeVision directly benefits.

### 4.2 Adverse trends

1. **Demographic structure**: Hong Kong’s birth rate continues to decline, and long-term housing demand growth slows down
2. **Office building glut**: The vacancy rate of Grade A office buildings has increased, and downward pressure on rents has continued; quality differentiation has intensified.
3. **Retail format changes**: E-commerce penetration and consumption downgrade will put long-term pressure on traditional shopping malls
4. **Geopolitics**: The game between China and the United States and changes in Hong Kong’s status as an international financial center may affect foreign investment confidence
5. **Mainland Economy**: Weak consumption in the mainland and excess commercial real estate affect Sun Hung Kai’s mainland business

### 4.3 Scenario analysis

| Scenario | Probability | Core Assumptions | Impact on Sun Hung Kai |
|------|------|----------|---------------|
| Strong recovery | 25% | Interest rate cut exceeds expectations + Mainland economy picks up + Property market volume and price rise | Property sales profits increase significantly, PB rebounds to 0.5x+ |
| Moderate recovery | 45% | Gradual interest rate cuts + Stable increase in property market volume + Stable rents | Profitability improves steadily, PB slowly rebounds to 0.4x |
| Sideways shock | 20% | Interest rate cuts are suspended + property market volume and price are sideways + office buildings continue to be under pressure | Profit remains flat, PB maintains 0.3-0.35x |
| Second bottom | 10% | Interest rate hike or geopolitical shock + property market declines again | Profits decline but not loss, PB may fall below 0.3x |

## 6. Core Conclusions

From the perspective of industry competition, Sun Hung Kai Properties enjoys triple protection:

1. **Industry Barrier Protection**: Oligarchic structure + land scarcity + capital threshold, new entrants can hardly threaten its position
2. **Differentiated Protection**: The two-wheel drive business model has the strongest comprehensive strength among the four major companies and is leading in risk resistance.
3. **Cycle position protection**: We are currently in the early and middle stages of recovery, with tailwinds rather than headwinds

But there are also three structural challenges:

1. **The growth ceiling is clear**: Hong Kong’s land area of 1,100 square kilometers and population of 7.5 million determine the ceiling
2. **Structural pressure on office buildings and retail**: not a cyclical issue, but a trend issue
3. **Intergenerational handover risk**: It remains to be seen whether the third generation can maintain the empire’s competitiveness.

Taken together, Sun Hung Kai occupies the most advantageous position in an industry that is "good but not better". The core bet of investing in Sun Hung Kai is not rapid industry growth, but stable industry returns + valuation restoration. This is closer to the logic of "picking up cigarette butts" than the logic of "buying a great company."
