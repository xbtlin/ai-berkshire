A few points worth paying attention to:

**Positive interpretation:**
- Property sales revenue nearly doubled, reflecting Hong Kong's strong recovery in post-pandemic residential market
- Underlying profit increased by 16.7%. After excluding the impact of revaluation of investment properties, core profitability remains solid.
- Total revenue growth of 32% was the strongest half-year performance in recent years

**Crudent interpretation:**
- Rental income remained flat or even slightly decreased, indicating that the rent collection business has not yet returned to the growth track.
- Net rental income fell by 1%, and operating costs experienced upward pressure
- Part of the high growth in property sales comes from the low base in the previous period, and sustainability needs to be observed

## 2. Balance sheet analysis

### 2.1 Financial Security: Fortress Level ★★★★★

| Indicators | Sun Hung Kai | Industry Average | Rating |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| Net debt ratio | 13.5% | 40-60% | Extremely conservative |
| Asset-liability ratio | ~25% | 50-70% | Far lower than peers |
| Current ratio | 3.73 times | 1.5-2.0 times | Super safe level |
| Interest coverage ratio | 8.7 times | 3-5 times | High security |
| Total assets | ~HK$796.4 billion | - | Largest in the industry |
| Total debt | ~HK$196.7 billion | - | Very low debt |
| Net worth | ~HKD 600 billion | - | Solid heritage |

This balance sheet is almost textbook sound. Among the world's major real estate companies, there are only a handful of large developers that can maintain such a low debt ratio.

**Key meaning:**
- In a rising interest rate cycle, low debt means extremely low interest burdens
- During a property market downturn, there is no risk of being forced to sell assets at low prices to repay debts
- There is sufficient financial space to expand against the trend during the industry downturn
- Provides a solid guarantee for continued dividend payment

### 2.2 Free cash flow

During 2020-2024, Sun Hung Kai continues to generate positive free cash flow:

- 2020: approximately HK$44.688 billion
- Decreases year by year but is always positive
- 2024: approximately HK$19.895 billion

Free cash flow continues to be positive, indicating that the company can maintain operations and investments without resorting to debt, which is one of the most hard-core financial quality indicators.

### 2.3 Asset Quality Warning

While the balance sheet is overall solid, the following areas deserve attention:

- **Investment property revaluation risk**: Net book assets include a large number of investment properties, whose fair value may shrink due to market downturns
- **Mainland Asset Quality**: Mainland land reserves are approximately 64.6 million square feet, and some projects in second-tier cities may face sales pressure
- **Agricultural land conversion uncertainty**: The realization of the value of a large amount of agricultural land reserves depends on the progress of government approvals

## 3. Valuation analysis

### 3.1 Price-to-book ratio (PB) valuation

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Current stock price | ~130 HKD |
| Estimated net assets per share | ~HK$207 (book value) |
| Estimated NAV per share | ~HK$214 (Haitong International) |
| Current PB | ~0.32-0.35 times |
| NAV discount | ~40-60% |
| Historical PB median | ~0.55-0.65 times |
| Historical PB bottom | ~0.30 times (extreme low in 2022) |

**Interpretation:**

The current PB of about 0.32 times means that the market’s pricing for Sun Hung Kai’s 600 billion net assets is only about HK$200 billion. In other words, the market believes that Sun Hung Kai's net assets are nearly 70% "watery" or discounted.

**Is this discount reasonable? Arguments from both sides: **

**The discount has certain reasonable reasons:**
- Book values of investment properties are based on appraisals and may overestimate actual realizable value
- Uncertainty over the conversion timing and final value of agricultural land reserves
- Under the family holding structure, it is difficult for minority shareholders to influence asset allocation decisions
- A large amount of profits are retained but the return on reinvestment is not high (ROE is between 5-8% in the long term)
- The long-term prospects of Hong Kong real estate are subject to an aging population and a single industrial structure

**Reason for excessive discount:**
- The net debt ratio is only 13.5%, and the possibility of asset moisture is greatly reduced by low leverage
- The actual value of core investment properties (ICC, IFC, etc.) may be underestimated
- Even if the property market continues to slump, the annual rental income of 12 billion + provides a hard bottom
- The Hong Kong property market is in the early stages of recovery, and property sales profits are highly elastic.
- The 4.9% dividend yield already provides attractive holding returns

### 3.2 Price-to-earnings ratio (PE) valuation

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Interim earnings per share | HK$3.54 |
| Annualized earnings per share | ~HK$7-8 (taking into account seasonality in the second half of the year) |
| Current PE | ~16-18 times |

PE valuation does not look cheap, but you need to pay attention to:

- The profits of real estate companies fluctuate greatly, and the reference value of PE in a single year is limited.
- Current profits are in the recovery stage at the bottom of the cycle, and PE may decline in the future due to profit growth.
- If calculated using normalized earnings (mean reversion), PE may be 12-14 times

### 3.3 Dividend discount valuation (DDM)

Assumptions:
- Annual dividend: approximately HK$3.75 (mid-term 0.98 + estimated final period 2.77)
- Sustainable growth rate: 2%
- Discount rate: 8%

DDM valuation = 3.75 / (8% - 2%) = HKD 62.5

**This result implies**: If viewed purely from the perspective of dividend cash flow, the current stock price of HK$130 has fully reflected the value of the dividend. In other words, the market is giving an additional premium to Sun Hung Kai's asset value (albeit still significantly below NAV).

### 3.4 Safety Margin Assessment

| Scenario | Valuation | Margin of Safety |
|------|------|----------|
| 30% discount on NAV (conservative scenario) | ~HK$150 | About +15% |
| NAV discount 40% (neutral scenario) | ~128 HKD | About -2% |
| NAV discount 50% (pessimistic scenario) | ~107 HKD | About -18% |
| NAV discount 60% (extremely pessimistic) | ~86 HKD | About -34% |
| Historical PB median return | ~124-135 Hong Kong dollars | About the same |

Shares are currently trading around a neutral scenario. If you believe that the NAV discount will return to the historical median from the current extreme level, there is some room for upside; but if the discount continues to expand, the downside risk cannot be ignored.

## 6. Core Conclusions

From a financial perspective, Sun Hung Kai Properties presents a set of contradictory signals:

**MAXIMUM CERTAINTY**: This is a company that is almost impossible to go bankrupt. With a net gearing ratio of 13.5%, interest coverage of 8.7 times, and continued positive free cash flow, Sun Hung Kai can survive even if the Hong Kong property market drops another 30%.

**The biggest uncertainty**: When will the deep discount narrow? This depends on: (1) the sustainability of Hong Kong's property market recovery; (2) the direction of the fair value of investment properties; (3) whether management is willing to unlock value through buybacks or increased dividends.

**Safety Margin Judgment**: Buy at the current price, the downside risk is limited (rental income + low debt provides a hard bottom), and the upside depends on valuation repair. If PB rises from 0.32 times to the historical median of 0.55 times, the stock price will have an implied increase of about 70%. But no one can accurately predict the timing and magnitude of this return.

For investors who pursue "not losing money", Sun Hung Kai has an asymmetric risk-return ratio at the current price - bottomed on the downside and open on the upside. But for investors pursuing efficient capital allocation, low ROE and conservative capital return strategies may be a continuing trouble.
