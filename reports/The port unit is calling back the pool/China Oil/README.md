## Company Profile

CNOOC Limited is China's largest offshore oil and gas producer and one of the world's largest independent oil and gas exploration and production companies. The company is mainly engaged in the exploration, development, production and sales of offshore crude oil and natural gas, with its business covering Chinese waters and many overseas countries and regions. The parent company is China National Offshore Oil Corporation (a central enterprise directly under the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council).
