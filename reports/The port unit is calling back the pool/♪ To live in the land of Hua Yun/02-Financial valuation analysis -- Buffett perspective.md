# China Resources Vientiane Life (1209.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital efficiency, cash flow, balance sheet health, valuation rationality
> Core question: Are you making real money? Is capital allocated efficiently? Is it worth it at the current price?

---

## 1. Profit quality: the gold content of profits

### 1.1 Revenue growth: slowing down but structural optimization

| Year | Operating income (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate | Core net profit (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth rate |
|------|-----------------|---------|-------------------|---------|
| 2023 | 147.6 | +24.5% | 29.2 | +28.0% |
| 2024 | 170.4 | +15.4% | 35.1 | +20.1% |
| 2025 | 180.2 | +5.7% | 39.5 | +13.7% |

Revenue growth dropped from 24.5% to 5.7%, but core net profit growth remained at 13.7% - indicating that the quality of growth is improving. The slowdown in revenue growth was mainly due to the fact that the property channel only grew by 1.1%, but the commercial channel still maintained a double-digit growth of 10.1%, and the gross profit margin jumped from 33.0% to 35.5%, and profitability continued to improve.

Key signal: The profit growth rate continues to be higher than the revenue growth rate, which shows that the company has remained an exception to the common industry problem of "increasing revenue without increasing profits" and is truly committed to reducing costs and increasing efficiency.

### 1.2 Gross profit margin trend: increase against the trend

| Year | Overall gross profit margin | Commercial channel gross profit margin | Property channel gross profit margin |
|------|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| 2023 | 31.2% | 58.5% | About 15% |
| 2024 | 33.0% | 60.2% | About 16% |
| 2025 | 35.5% | 63.1% | Improving |

Commercial Channel's gross profit margin of 63.1% is among the top levels in the property management industry. This is no accident - high-end shopping malls have strong rental pricing power, and the rigid demand from brand merchants for MixC supports high gross profits.

Although the gross profit margin of Property Channel is low, it is continuing to improve through organizational streamlining, cost reduction through centralized procurement, and digital system empowerment. The company will reduce its workforce significantly in 2025 (mainly in property management), and will release profits through efficiency improvements.

### 1.3 The "touchstone" of earnings quality: cash flow verification

**Operating cash flow/core net profit = 103.3% (107.2% after restoring the impact of mergers and acquisitions)**

This is one of the most important indicators of earnings quality. A ratio of more than 100% means that for every 1 yuan of profit earned, more than 1 yuan of actual cash flows in. This means:
- Profits are not generated by piles of accounts receivable
- No large capitalized expenses to "beautify" profits
- The company's profits are real money

It has maintained a cash flow coverage rate of more than 100% for many consecutive years, which is among the first-class levels in the property management industry, far better than those peers with high accounts receivable and bleeding cash flow.

### Assessment: Earnings Quality ★★★★★

The profit growth rate is higher than the revenue growth rate, the gross profit margin has increased against the trend, and the cash flow fully covers the profit. This is high quality profit.

## 2. Capital efficiency: Is the money used well?

### 2.1 ROE analysis

The market generally quotes China Resources Vientiane Life's ROE as approximately 20%, which is an excellent level in the asset-light service industry. Take it apart:

- **Net Profit Margin**: Core Net Profit/Revenue = 39.5/180.2 = 21.9%. The net profit rate exceeds 20%, which is unique in the property management industry.
- **Asset turnover rate**: The asset-light model determines that the asset scale is small and the income/asset ratio is high.
- **Financial Leverage**: The company has almost zero interest-bearing debt and extremely low leverage.

High ROE comes from high net interest rate rather than high leverage - this is the healthiest ROE structure.

### 2.2 Capital allocation: Dividends are king

The dividend per share in 2025 is 1.731 yuan, with a core net profit dividend payout rate of 100%, and full dividends have been paid for three consecutive years. This sends a clear signal:

1. **Management has confidence in the quality of earnings**: Dare to pay 100% dividends, indicating that profits are real and sustainable
2. **Don’t spend money indiscriminately**: No large-scale mergers and acquisitions in pursuit of scale
3. **Shareholder Friendly**: In the Hong Kong stock market, there are very few companies that can maintain a 100% dividend payout ratio.

Based on the current stock price of approximately HK$40, the dividend yield is approximately 4.3%. For a company that's still growing, with a 4.3% dividend yield and double-digit profit growth, total return expectations are attractive.

### 2.3 Cash on hand: plenty but need to pay attention to usage efficiency

As of the end of 2025, cash on hand was 15.8 billion yuan and time deposits were 5.1 billion yuan. While such abundant cash provides a safety cushion, it also raises a question: With so much money on the books, is there a problem of inefficient capital allocation?

On the positive side: Abundant cash provides ammunition for asset-light M&A (acquisition of third-party project management rights).
Negative view: If you cannot find good investment targets in the long term, holding a large amount of low-yielding cash will drag down ROE.

### Assessment: Capital Efficiency ★★★★☆

About 20% of ROE is excellent, and the 100% dividend payout ratio reflects shareholder orientation. The penalty point is that it remains to be seen how efficiently the large amount of cash on hand is used.

## 3. Balance sheet health

### 3.1 Liability structure

China Resources Vientiane Life has almost no interest-bearing liabilities, and its balance sheet is extremely clean. This is almost unique in the real estate related industry. The main liabilities are mainly operating liabilities such as advances from customers and accounts payable - this is a source of "free" funds generated from normal operating activities.

### 3.2 Accounts receivable risk

A common problem in the property management industry is the difficulty in collecting accounts receivable. China Resources Vientiane Lifestyle relies on its central enterprise background and high-quality customer structure (mainly projects related to China Resources Land), and the quality of its accounts receivable is better than the industry average. However, we still need to pay attention to the changing trend of accounts receivable after the increase in third-party projects.

### 3.3 Goodwill and intangible assets

The company has generated a certain amount of goodwill due to historical mergers and acquisitions. During the reshuffle period in the property management industry, goodwill impairment is an industry risk (Country Garden Services has made substantial provision for goodwill impairment). The quality of China Resources Vientiane Life's M&A targets is relatively controllable, but this is a risk point that requires continuous monitoring.

### Assessment: Balance Sheet ★★★★★

Almost zero interest-bearing debt, plenty of cash, and a clean balance sheet. It is a top allocation in the Hong Kong property sector.

## 4. Valuation analysis: Is it expensive?

### 4.2 What is the reasonable range of valuation?

**Horizontal comparison:**

China Resources Vientiane Life’s PE is among the highest in the Hong Kong property sector. Country Garden Services is about 8-10 times PE, and Wanwu Cloud is about 12-15 times PE. China Resources Vientiane Life's valuation premium of more than 20 times reflects the market's recognition of the high quality and high certainty of its commercial management business.

**Vertical comparison:**

After the listing of China Resources Vientiane Life, the PE fluctuation range is roughly between 15-40 times. The current level of about 24 times is below the historical median, and the peak in 2021 was more than 40 times.

**DCF logic:**

Assuming that the core net profit compound growth rate is 10-12% in the next five years (driven by double-digit growth in commercial channels), the sustainable growth is 3% from the 6th year, and the discount rate is 8-9%, the reasonable valuation is roughly in the 20-28 times PE range. The current 24 times is in the middle of the reasonable range.

### 4.3 Pros and cons of valuation

**Reasons supporting high valuation:**
1. The commercial management business has a gross profit margin of 63% and a light-asset model, so it deserves to enjoy a higher valuation than ordinary property management.
2. 100% dividend payout rate + 4.3% dividend rate, which is a rare product in Hong Kong stocks
3. Asset-light expansion opens up space for long-term growth
4. State-owned enterprise background + industry leading position provide a certain premium

**Risk of downside valuation:**
1. Revenue growth has dropped to 5.7%. If profit growth also drops to single digits, PE may face contraction pressure.
2. The overall valuation of the Hong Kong stock market is low, and trust in the property sector has been damaged (country garden services and other thunderstorm cases)
3. The downturn in Chinese property stocks may suppress valuations through sentiment transmission
4. If macro consumption continues to be weak, the high growth assumption of commercial shipping lanes may be falsified

### 4.4 Investment return from dividend perspective

Assuming that it is held for 5 years and the annual dividend rate is 4-5% (dividends grow as profits grow), the cumulative dividend return in 5 years is about 22-25%. Coupled with the valuation increase brought about by profit growth (assuming that PE remains unchanged and profits increase by 10% annually), the total return in five years is expected to be approximately 70-85%, with an annualized rate of approximately 11-13%.

This level of return is reasonably favorable among Hong Kong stocks, but it is not an extremely underestimated "cheap picking" opportunity.

### Assessment: Valuation ★★★☆☆

The current PE of about 24 times is within a reasonable range, neither expensive nor cheap. The dividend yield of 4.3% provides a certain safety cushion, but lacks a clear margin of safety.
