# China Resources Vientiane Life (1209.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, competition barriers, long-term sustainability
> Core question: What is the nature of this business? How deep is the moat? Can it be done for a long time?

---

## 1. The essence of business: an operation service provider that connects space and people

The business essence of China Resources Vientiane Life is to transform physical spaces (shopping malls, office buildings, residential communities) into service carriers that continuously generate cash flow through professional operating capabilities. This is not an asset-holding-heavy business, but an asset-light service business that "exchanges capabilities for income."

The company adopts the "2+1" business model:

- **Commercial Channel** (shopping mall + office building operation): revenue in 2025 is 6.91 billion yuan, accounting for 38.3%, and gross profit margin is as high as 63.1%. This is the company's true core competitiveness and profit engine.
- **Property Channel** (residential and urban space property management): revenue in 2025 will be 10.85 billion yuan, accounting for 60.2%. Large scale but relatively low gross profit margin, providing a stable cash flow base.
- **Ecosystem Business** (Large Membership System): The revenue is about 260 million yuan, which is in the cultivation stage, but the ecological value of 72 million members far exceeds the current revenue.

In terms of income structure, property channels contribute 60% of revenue, but commercial channels contribute far more than 60% of profits. This model of "property foundation and business management to make money" is the fundamental difference between China Resources Vientiane Life and ordinary property management companies.

### Assessment: The Essence of Business ★★★★☆

Commercial operation services are characterized by light assets, high gross profit, and strong cash flow, making it a good business. However, the property management segment is facing industry-wide slowdown in growth and intensifying competition, which has lowered the overall score.

## 2. User value: What value is created for whom?

The "users" of China Resources Vientiane Life are multi-level:

**First level: Brand merchants (B-side core customers)**

The shopping mall provides brand merchants with a one-stop business solution of "customer flow + scene + operation". The 97.1% occupancy rate shows that merchants voted with their feet and recognized this value. Among the 135 shopping malls, 53 ranked first in the local market in terms of retail sales, and 104 ranked among the top three in the local market. This means that brand merchants opening stores in Mixc/Mixiang Hui have a high probability of achieving the best business performance in the city.

**Second layer: Consumer (C-side traffic source)**

The 72 million Vientiane Star members are the company’s traffic assets. The number of consumption and repeat purchase members increased by more than 30% year-on-year, and the total number of points issued was 590 million yuan, which shows that consumer stickiness is increasing rather than decreasing. Vientiane City targets mid-to-high-end consumption and accurately targets the urban middle class group with the most spending power in China.

**Third level: Property owners (property management customers)**

The 426 million square meters of area under management covers various business types such as residential buildings, office buildings, and urban public spaces. China Resources Vientiane Lifestyle provides owners with not only basic services such as cleaning and security, but also promotes the transformation of "urban space operation service providers" - urban space revenue will reach 2.04 billion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 12%.

### Evaluation: User Value ★★★★★

The company has established clear user value in the field of high-end commercial operations. Merchants are willing to move in (97% occupancy rate), consumers are willing to come back (membership growth is 32%), and owners are willing to renew their contracts - the satisfaction of the three parties constitutes a positive cycle of business.

## 3. Moat analysis: superposition of multiple barriers

**(1) Brand barriers: Positioning advantages of Vientiane City**

Vientiane City is one of the synonyms of high-end shopping malls in China. 14 luxury projects (introducing top luxury brands such as Hermès and Louis Vuitton) constitute the industry's most high-end commercial matrix. In the field of high-end commercial operations, brand effect means: luxury brands give priority to opening stores in Vientiane City, forming a flywheel effect in which "strong brands attract high-quality merchants, and high-quality merchants strengthen their brands."

This brand barrier is a function of time. China Resources has been in business operations for more than 20 years. The brand potential accumulated by MixC since its first store in Shenzhen in 2004 cannot be replicated by latecomers by spending money.

**(2) Scale and network effects**

There are 135 shopping malls in operation, covering 26 key cities, and many cities have implemented the "one city, multiple hubs" layout. Scale brings threefold advantages:

1. **Negotiating power with brand merchants**: When national brands open stores in multiple cities, they tend to cooperate with the same operator to reduce communication costs.
2. **Supply chain and centralized procurement advantages**: A unified centralized procurement system reduces operating costs.
3. **Talent and knowledge reuse**: The operational experience of mature projects can be quickly copied to newly opened projects.

**(3) Related party resource barriers**

China Resources Group is one of China's largest state-owned enterprises, and China Resources Land is China's leading real estate company. This relationship brings two irreproducible advantages:

1. **Certainty of project sources**: China Resources Land continues to develop new projects to provide a stable increase in management area for Vientiane life. 82% of the shopping mall area under management comes from related parties of China Resources Land.
2. **Brand Credit Endorsement**: A central enterprise background has natural advantages in government bidding, urban space operations and other fields.

But it’s also a double-edged sword – see the risks section for details.

**(4) Barriers to digital operations**

The store management system coverage rate is 100%, and the Liangjia mini program tenant-end coverage rate is 98%. Digitalization is not a gimmick, but actually improves operational efficiency: through data analysis, it optimizes merchant portfolios, adjusts rental strategies, and improves square footage efficiency. Once this digital capability is built, it will form a continuous accumulation of data assets.

### Assessment: Moat Depth ★★★★☆

Brand barriers and scale advantages have been established, and digitalization creates differentiation. The reason for deducting one star is that a considerable part of the moat relies on China Resources Land's related resources. Once the relationship between related parties changes, the moat will be reduced.

## 4. Sustainability of business model

**The logic of bullishness:**

1. **The long-term trend of consumption upgrading remains unchanged**: The demand for quality consumption among China’s middle class is still growing, and offline high-end business experience is irreplaceable.
2. **Asset-light expansion opens up the ceiling**: Third-party projects accounted for 27.4% (37 buildings), and revenue accounted for 33.1%. The asset-light model means that China Resources Land does not need to develop new projects to grow.
3. **Operational dividends in the era of inventory**: The inventory of shopping malls in China exceeds 500 million square meters, and a large number of projects are poorly operated and require professional management to take over. As an industry benchmark, China Resources Vientiane Lifestyle is the preferred partner for stock renovation.
4. **Cost reduction and efficiency increase continue to release profits**: The gross profit margin increased from 33.0% to 35.5%, and there is still room for "improving quality and efficiency" in the property channel.

**The logic of bearishness:**

1. **The growth rate of property channels has almost stagnated**: In 2025, the revenue of property channels will only increase by 1.1%, and the area growth rate is also slowing down. This accounts for 60% of revenue.
2. **The continued impact of e-commerce and live streaming on offline businesses**: Although high-end businesses are less affected, mid-range Wanxianghui projects are not completely immune.
3. **Risk of dependence on related-party transactions**: If China Resources Land’s own development slows down, Vientiane Life’s project increment will be limited.
4. **Intensified competition in asset-light expansion**: Wanda, Longfor, Joy City and other competitors are vying for third-party management projects, and the blue ocean is turning into a red ocean.

### Assessment: Sustainability ★★★★☆

The commercial channel is highly sustainable (high-end consumption is irreplaceable), but the property channel faces growth bottlenecks. The overall business model can be sustained, but the growth rate may drop from double digits to single digits.

## 5. "Do you dare to marry your daughter to him?" - General evaluation of business quality

If China Resources Vientiane Life is compared to a person, his portrait is like this:

- **Good background**: Central enterprise background, abundant resources
- **Strong ability**: 20 years of business operation experience, first echelon in the industry
- **Healthy income structure**: The proportion of high-margin business management business is increasing
- **There is room for growth**: Asset-light expansion has just begun
- **But there are hidden worries**: Deep dependence on the "family" (China Resources Land), self-hematopoietic ability needs to be proven

It's a good business, but it's not a perfect business. The good thing is that high-end commercial operations are scarce capabilities, and the asset-light model is scalable; the imperfection is that the property management sector is mediocre, and dependence on related parties is a structural risk.
