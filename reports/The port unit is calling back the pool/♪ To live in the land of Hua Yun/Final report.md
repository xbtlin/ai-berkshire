## 2. Investment thesis: pros and cons

### Positive argument (long view)

1. **The best consumer operation platform for Hong Kong stocks**
   - Commercial waterway has a gross profit margin of 63%, unique in the property management industry
   - 53 of the 135 shopping malls are the city’s retail sales champions
   - Brand barriers + scale effect + bilateral network effect superposition

2. **Extremely high quality of earnings**
   - Operating cash flow/net profit > 100%, profits are real money
   - Gross profit margin increased for three consecutive years (31.2% -> 33.0% -> 35.5%)
   - Core net profit growth rate (13.7%) is much higher than revenue growth rate (5.7%), and profitability efficiency is improving

3. **Strong certainty of shareholder returns**
   - 100% core net profit dividend payment for three consecutive years
   - 4.3% dividend yield + double-digit profit growth = potential return of 15%+ annualized
   - 15.8 billion in cash on hand, almost zero interest-bearing liabilities

4. **Asset-light expansion opens up the second curve**
   - 37 third-party projects, accounting for 33.1% of revenue, are accelerating
   - A large number of projects in China's 500 million square meter shopping mall stock require professional operators to take over
   - The story of growth without relying on China Resources Land is coming true

5. **The scarcity of central enterprise background**
   - In the crisis of trust in the property management sector, the governance of central enterprises is the biggest differentiating advantage
   - Government urban service outsourcing gives priority to central enterprise partners

### Negative arguments (short)

1. **Growth slows down like a cliff**
   - Revenue growth dropped from 24.5% to 5.7%, a trend that cannot be ignored
   - Property channel (60% revenue) almost stagnated, growing only 1.1%
   - 24 times PE pricing is "high growth". If growth disappears, the valuation may shrink to 15-18 times

2. **Related party dependence is difficult to fundamentally change**
   - 82% of the shopping mall area comes from China Resources Land
   - The chairman of the board of directors is also the chairman of China Resources Land, so conflicts of interest naturally exist.
   - China Resources Land's core net profit will fall by 11.4% in 2025, and the parent company itself is also facing pressure

3. **Insufficient safety margin**
   - 24 times PE is not cheap in Hong Kong stocks
   - Under the pessimistic scenario (PE shrinks to 18 times), the downside is about 25%
   - Compared with peers Country Garden Services (8 times) and Wanwu Cloud (13 times), the valuation premium needs to be continuously proven

4. **Industry Challenges**
   - The real estate downturn cycle is not over yet, and sector sentiment suppresses valuations
   - E-commerce/live streaming continues to impact offline consumption
   - Asset-light expansion faces strong competition from Wanda, Longfor and others

5. **Signs of personnel turmoil**
   - 5 personnel changes in the first half of 2024
   - The Chief Strategy and Operations Officer resigned six months after taking office
   - Large-scale layoffs (thousands of people) reflect the pain of business adjustment

## 6. Final conclusion

### One sentence summary

China Resources Vientiane Life is the highest-quality asset in the property management sector of Hong Kong stocks. The commercial management business has long-term competitiveness, but the current valuation lacks a clear margin of safety. It is suitable to build a position during a correction rather than chasing at the current price.

### What kind of investors is it suitable for?

- **Suitable**: Long-term investors who pursue stable dividends + moderate growth; investors who are optimistic about China's high-end consumption; investors who need high-quality allocation of Hong Kong stocks
- **Not suitable**: Investors pursuing highly elastic returns; Investors with zero tolerance for related party transactions; Investors who need clear catalysts in the short term

### Action recommendations

1. **Current (HKD 40)**: Enter the watch list, don’t rush to buy
2. **If it pulls back to HK$33-35 (PE 19-20 times)**: You can start to open a position, and the dividend rate is about 5% at this time
3. **If it continues to fall below HK$30 (PE <17 times)**: Actively add positions, sufficient safety margin
4. **Continuous Tracking**: Pay attention to the growth rate of commercial waterways, progress of third-party projects, and stabilization of property waterways every quarter

### Risk warning

- Related party dependence is an unavoidable structural risk
- If revenue growth continues to slow to low single digits, PE shrinking to 18x means a -25% downside
- Liquidity risks and policy risks in the Hong Kong stock market always exist
