## Core conclusion

**Comprehensive rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5) - has investment value but needs to be discounted to compensate for country risks**

China Molybdenum has transformed from a Henan molybdenum mining company into a global resources giant: the world's largest cobalt producer and one of the top ten copper producers. Net profit attributable to parent companies in 2025 is 20.3 billion yuan (+50% year-on-year), and net profit in the first quarter of 2026 is 7.8 billion yuan (+97% year-on-year). The copper-gold bipolar strategy is clear, with copper production capacity targeting 800,000-1 million tons in 2027-2028. The global copper supply and demand gap continues to expand (S&P Global estimates the gap will reach 10 million tons in 2040), and the company is on the best track.

**Core Contradiction**: More than 70% of profits come from the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and country risk is a flaw. It has been forced to pay a settlement of US$800 million in 2023. The Congolese government's mining policies (mandatory 10% share conversion, cobalt export quota system) continue to pose uncertainties. PE of 11-13 times seems cheap, but it partly reflects the rationality of geographical discount.

**Key judgment**: If copper prices remain high and the situation in Congo is stable, the current valuation is attractive; if a geopolitical black swan occurs, the downside risk cannot be ignored. It is recommended to pay attention but be patient and wait for a greater safety margin.
