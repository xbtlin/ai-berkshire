## 1. Financial quality physical examination

### 1.1 Profitability: Explosive growth, quality needs to be verified

**Revenue and Profit Growth**

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026Q1 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Operating income | 186.2 billion | 213 billion | 206.7 billion | 66.4 billion |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 6.6 billion | 13.5 billion | 20.3 billion | 7.8 billion |
| Net profit growth rate | — | +105% | +50% | +97% (year-on-year) |
| Weighted ROE | ~10% | ~18% | ~27% | 9.06% (single quarter) |

China Molybdenum has shown an astonishing growth trajectory in the past three years: its net profit has increased from 6.6 billion to 20.3 billion, tripling in three years. The net profit in a single quarter of 2026Q1 was 7.8 billion yuan, an annualized rate of more than 30 billion yuan, and according to brokerage estimates, it is expected to reach 32-35 billion yuan for the whole year.

**Examination from Buffett’s Perspective**:

The driving force for profit growth needs to be dismantled - how much comes from **volume growth** (increased production), and how much comes from **price increases** (rising copper prices)?

- Volume: 650,000 tons of copper in 2024 -> 740,000 tons in 2025, an increase of 14%
- Price: LME copper prices will rise from an average price of approximately US$8,500/ton in 2024 to an average price of approximately US$9,500/ton in 2025, an increase of approximately 12%
- Increase in volume and price combined with operating leverage drove 50% profit growth

**Core question**: If the copper price drops back to US$8,000/ton, how much profit can be maintained? A rough estimate is that even if copper prices fall by 15-20%, the company can still maintain a net profit level of more than 15 billion, because production growth and cost dilution can be partially offset. But if copper prices fall back to 6,000-7,000 US dollars per ton (2020 levels), profits may shrink significantly to 5-8 billion yuan.

### 1.2 ROE dismantling: Leverage optimization drives ROE jump

The weighted ROE in 2025 will reach 27%, which is among the top levels among global mining companies. But it is necessary to disassemble the DuPont three factors:

- **Net profit margin**: 20.3 billion/206.7 billion = 9.8% (the net profit margin of the mining business is much higher than this, diluted by IXM's low gross profit trading income)
- **Asset turnover rate**: Driven by trade business, the turnover rate is relatively high
- **Equity Multiplier**: Asset-liability ratio drops from approximately 59% in 2024 to 50.3% in 2025, with moderate reduction in leverage

The healthy signal of rising ROE is: **Profit growth is faster than asset growth, and leverage is declining**. This means that the quality of earnings is improving, rather than relying on increased leverage to increase returns.

### 1.3 Cash flow quality: greatly improved, verifying the authenticity of profits

| Indicators | 2024H1 | 2025H1 | 2026Q1 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Operating cash flow | 10.78 billion | 12 billion | 11.3 billion |
| Capital expenditures | — | — | Expected full year <8 billion |
| Free cash flow | — | — | Expected to be >12 billion for the full year |

Operating cash flow in 2026Q1 was 11.3 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 762% (only 1.3 billion yuan in the same period last year), mainly because the net inflow of operating activities of IXM's trading business increased significantly.

**Buffett’s most important metric**: free cash flow. The company expects capital expenditures to fall back to less than 8 billion yuan in 2026. If operating cash flow maintains a level of 35-40 billion yuan (annualized in Q1), free cash flow can reach 27-32 billion yuan. This is a very impressive number.

### 1.4 Balance Sheet Health

- **Asset-liability ratio**: 50.3% (end of 2025), a year-on-year decrease of approximately 9 percentage points, which is the healthiest level in recent years
- **Current Ratio**: 1.57, leading in the industry, with strong short-term solvency
- **Interest-Bearing Debt**: Net debt continues to decline as operating cash flow improves

Buffett prefers companies with low debt. An asset-liability ratio of 50% is a reasonably low level for a mining company (peers such as Zijin Mining are about 55-60%). Considering that the company has just gone through a period of large-scale mine construction, the downward trend in debt ratio is a positive sign.

3. A-H stock price difference (A-share premium is about 20-30%), H-shares bear the liquidity discount of Hong Kong stocks

### 2.3 Buffett-style valuation: Owners’ earnings method

**Owner's earnings = Net profit + Depreciation and amortization - Maintenance capital expenditures**

Rough estimate (2026):
- Net profit: approximately 32-35 billion yuan
- Depreciation and amortization: about 6 billion to 8 billion yuan
- Maintenance capital expenditure: about 4 billion to 5 billion yuan (total capital expenditure of 8 billion yuan, expansionary capital expenditure of about 3 billion to 4 billion yuan)
- **Owner's earnings: approximately 33-38 billion yuan**

Calculated based on a market value of HK$200 billion (approximately RMB 185 billion):
- **Owner's earnings yield = 330-380/1850 = 17.8%-20.5%**

This means that if you buy at the current price, the annualized return will be 18-20%, far exceeding the 10-15% threshold required by Buffett.

**But there is an important premise**: This is based on the assumption that copper prices will maintain their current high levels. If the central copper price of the cycle (about 8,500 US dollars/ton) is used to re-estimate, the owner's profit may drop to 20-25 billion yuan, and the yield will drop to 11-13.5%. There is still a certain margin of safety but the space will narrow.

### 2.4 DCF scenario analysis

| Scenario | Assumption | Net profit in 2026 | Reasonable market value (RMB) |
|------|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Optimistic | Copper price $12,000/ton+ | 40 billion+ | 400-500 billion |
| Benchmark | Copper price $10,000/ton | 32-35 billion | 280-350 billion |
| Conservative | Copper price 8,000 US dollars/ton | 18-22 billion | 180-220 billion |
| Pessimistic | Copper price $6,500/ton | 8-12 billion | 80-120 billion |

The current market value is approximately RMB 185 billion, which is within the valuation range of conservative scenarios. If copper prices remain above the benchmark, there is 50-90% upside; if copper prices fall sharply back to the pessimistic scenario, there is 30-40% downside risk.

## 4. What would Buffett think?

### Factors that will attract Buffett:

1. **Adequate safety margin**: 11-12 times PE, 18-20% owner earnings yield, cheap valuation under base scenario
2. **Free cash flow improvement**: After the construction period and entering the harvest period, the quality of cash flow is greatly improved
3. **Balance Sheet Health**: Debt ratio is on a downward trend and liquidity is sufficient
4. **On a good track**: Copper is one of the most certain commodities

### Factors that would make Buffett hesitate:

1. **Valuation trap of cyclical stocks**: Buffett has warned many times that cyclical stocks are often at the top of the cycle when their PE is lowest. Copper prices are near historical highs. Whether 11 times PE is really cheap needs to be examined from a cyclical perspective.
2. **Geopolitics is uncontrollable**: Buffett emphasizes the "circle of competence". The political risks in the Democratic Republic of the Congo are beyond the judgment of most investors.
3. **Undifferentiated commodities**: Buffett prefers companies with pricing power. Commodity manufacturers naturally lack this trait.
4. **Merger-driven growth**: Successive large-scale mergers and acquisitions increase integration risk and the possibility of capital allocation errors
