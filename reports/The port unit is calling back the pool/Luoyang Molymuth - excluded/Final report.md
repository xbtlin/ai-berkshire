## 2. Investment thesis: why it is worth paying attention to

### Core logic

1. **Copper is the most certain commodity in this era**. Driven by the triple drivers of electrification, AI data centers, and defense spending, S&P Global predicts that the copper supply and demand gap will reach 10 million tons in 2040. As one of the top ten copper producers in the world, China Molybdenum has directly benefited from this super trend.

2. **Capacity expansion is at its best stage**. TFM+KFM has been fully put into production, with a production capacity of 740,000 tons of copper in 2025 and a target of 800,000-1 million tons in 2028. The peak of capital expenditure during the construction period has passed and it has entered the harvest period - the "sweet spot" of operating leverage release + free cash flow improvement.

3. **Valuation has a margin of safety**. The PE of H shares is 11-12 times (based on profits in 2025), and the dynamic PE in 2026 is only 7-8 times. Owner's earnings yield is 18-20%. There is a significant discount to global peers (Freeport 20-25x).

4. **The bipolar strategy of copper and gold opens up a new growth curve**. After completing the acquisition of the Brazilian gold mine in 2026, the gold business will start from scratch with an annual output of 6-8 tons. Gold mines provide a profit buffer at the trough of the copper cycle.

### Data support

- Net profit attributable to parent companies in 2025 is 20.3 billion yuan, +50% year-on-year
- Net profit in 2026Q1 was 7.8 billion yuan, +97% year-on-year
- Weighted ROE 27%, asset-liability ratio reduced to 50%
- Operating cash flow in 2026Q1 was 11.3 billion yuan, +762% year-on-year
- 32% share of global cobalt quota, top ten in copper production in the world

## 3. Core risk: why discount is needed

### The first major risk: Congo (DRC) country risk

This is the only risk that may result in permanent loss of capital. More than 70% of profits come from the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and the Congolese government has shown a continued tendency to "squeeze wool":

- 2023: US$800 million settlement (approximately 12% of net profit for that year)
- 2025: Require mining companies to transfer 10% of their equity to the government
- Export quota system: Government control over mining continues to increase

These are not one-off events, but a pattern. It is almost certain that new "wool harvesting" incidents will occur every 2-3 years in the future, and each time may bring a cost of billions of yuan.

### The second biggest risk: the trap at the top of the cycle

Copper prices are near historical highs (about 10,000 US dollars per ton), and the "cheapness" of 11 times PE may be an illusion. If the copper price falls back to US$8,000, the PE will automatically rise to 18-20 times; if it falls to US$6,500, the company may be close to breaking even. The rule of "the lowest PE is the selling point" for cyclical stocks cannot be ignored.

### The third major risk: the pace of mergers and acquisitions

From 2025 to 2026, the total of three consecutive gold mine acquisitions exceeded US$1.5 billion, and they occurred at historically high gold prices. Are management's "empire-building" tendencies accelerating? Can capital allocation discipline be maintained? Continuous monitoring is required.

## 4. Team Lead decision-making

### Current judgment: Pay attention but don’t rush to take action

**Reason**:
1. The margin of safety exists but is not generous enough - only a discount of 8-35% under conservative valuation, which is narrow for assets facing country risks.
2. Copper prices are at a high level and may be in the middle and late stages of the cycle
3. The management has just experienced a change of core executives and needs to observe the performance of the new team.

### Ideal buying conditions

Re-evaluate when any of the following conditions occur:

1. **H-share PE falls below 8 times** (corresponding to a stock price drop of approximately 25-30%), and the safety margin becomes sufficient
2. **Copper prices have fallen back to 8,000-8,500 US dollars/ton**, and it is safer to buy at cycle lows
3. **Congo risk clearing**: If there is a one-time negative (such as a new round of policy shocks leading to a sharp drop in stock prices), but it does not affect mining rights and production capacity, it is a reverse buying opportunity

### Situations to avoid

1. When copper prices further surge to 12,000+ US dollars/ton, chase the high price and buy
2. Impulsively jump on the bandwagon when companies announce new major acquisitions
3. "Gambling at the bottom" when the political situation in Congo has deteriorated significantly.

## 7. Summary

China Molybdenum is a global mining upstart that is on the right track, owns top-notch assets, and is run by a capable management. From the "global cobalt king" three years ago to the "copper-gold bipolar" today, the company's strategic upgrade is impressive.

But the key to investment decisions is not "whether the company is good or not" but "whether the price is cheap enough to compensate for the risk." The country risk of the Democratic Republic of the Congo cannot be eliminated through operating efforts. It needs to be compensated through a low enough purchase price.

There is a safety margin of 11-12 times PE, but for a company with 70% of its profits exposed to Congolese political risks, this safety margin is not thick enough. Stay tuned and wait for better prices. If the price enters a range below 8 times PE, this will become an extremely attractive contrarian investment opportunity.
