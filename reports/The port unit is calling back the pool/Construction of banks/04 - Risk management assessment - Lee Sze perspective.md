## 2. Risk Assessment

### Risk 1: Housing loan concentration risk ★★★★☆

**Specific performance**:
- China Construction Bank is the largest personal housing loan bank in China
- Housing mortgages account for a higher proportion of total loans than other major banks
- If the property market continues to decline or there is a systemic default

**Key data**:
- China’s mortgage default rate is historically <0.5%
- But in the current environment: property prices fell by 25-30% + residents’ income expectations weakened + a wave of early loan repayments
- Mortgage asset quality may change from "best quality" to "concerns"

**Influence Analysis**:
- Even if the mortgage default rate rises to 1% (extremely pessimistic), the loss to CCB’s total assets will be within control.
- Because LTV is generally <70%, property prices need to drop by 50%+ to cause "insolvency"
- The more realistic risk is "prepayment" - reduced interest income rather than bad debts

**Release factors**:
- Chinese mortgages are full recourse loans (unlike the United States where you can "walk away")
- High down payment ratio (usually 30%+)
- Policies continue to stabilize the property market

### Risk 2: Local government debt ★★★★★

Consistent with ICBC. China Construction Bank also faces the risk of "extension and interest rate reduction" of urban investment loans.

### Risk three: Narrowing of net interest margin ★★★★☆

Consistent with ICBC. CCB NIM is also in a downward channel.

### Risk 4: Governance and policy risks ★★★★☆

Consistent with ICBC. However, the strategic autonomy of CCB’s management may be slightly stronger (market-oriented initiatives such as CCB Financial Group have gained more space).

### Risk 5: Risks unique to H shares ★★★☆☆

**Specific performance**:
- The valuation of H shares is lower than that of A shares in the long term (AH premium)
- Foreign capital continues to reduce its holdings in Chinese banks
- Southbound trading funds are the main marginal buyers

**Impact**: H-share investors are exposed to additional liquidity discount and exchange rate risks

## 5. Investment risk and return assessment

### Worst case scenario (5% probability)
- Systemic collapse of the property market + widespread defaults on local government bonds
- PB fell to 0.3x
- Downside: -40%

### Pessimistic scenario (20% probability)
- NIM continues to decline + early loan repayment impact + negative profit growth
- PB maintained at 0.4-0.5 times
- Annualized return on dividends alone: 6-7%

### Baseline scenario (55% probability)
- NIM stabilized + mortgage defaults under control + dividend rate increased to 35%
- PB repaired to 0.6-0.7 times
- Annualized total return: 10-15%

### Optimistic scenario (20% probability)
- Stabilizing property market + promotion of special appraisal + dividend rate to 40-50%
- PB repaired to 0.8-1.0 times
- Annualized total return: 20-30%
