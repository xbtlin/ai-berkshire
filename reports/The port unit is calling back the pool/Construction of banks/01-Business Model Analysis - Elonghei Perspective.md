# China Construction Bank (0939.HK)—Business Model Analysis

## 1. The essence of business

China Construction Bank and Industrial and Commercial Bank of China both belong to the "big state-owned banks" camp, and their business models are highly consistent in nature:

1. **Deposit-Lending**: Use the nationwide network to absorb low-cost deposits and issue loans at higher interest rates
2. **Intermediary business**: agency insurance/fund sales, bank card handling fees, investment banking business
3. **Policy Implementation**: Cooperate with national macro-control and serve infrastructure construction (the name "Construction Bank" itself reflects its historical mission)

**Core differences with ICBC**:
- Historical genes: CCB originated from financial allocations and loans, and has the deepest relationship with infrastructure construction.
- Housing finance: China Construction Bank is the largest personal housing loan bank in China, with the largest mortgage market share
- Strategy in recent years: differentiated attempts in housing leasing, inclusive finance, and financial technology

## 2. Moat Assessment ★★★★☆ (4/5)

Similar to ICBC, the moat comes from system and scale:

**Strengths:**
- **Leading position in the field of housing finance**: The balance of personal mortgages ranks first in the country. "If you want to buy a house, go to China Construction Bank" is a deeply rooted understanding among the people.
- **Infrastructure Loan Advantages**: Deeply tied to national infrastructure construction
- **Leading the industry in digital transformation**: CCB Financial is the most successful financial technology subsidiary in market-oriented operations among the six major banks.
- **Scale and license barriers**: 45.63 trillion total assets, 14,000+ outlets nationwide

**Weaknesses:**
- The same structural problems as ICBC: homogeneity, policy compliance, and limited pricing power
- Advantages of housing loans may turn into risk exposure during property market downturn
- Growth is also subject to NIM compression

## 3. "Simplicity" test of business model

**Consistent with ICBC’s conclusion**: The model itself is simple (deposit and loan interest rate spread), but governance and policy constraints make it unsuitable for Duan Yongping’s investment framework.

**Marginal difference**: CCB has a more distinct brand positioning in the "housing finance" segment. Compared with ICBC's "big and comprehensive", CCB at least has a clear "main battlefield".

## 4. User value and "differentiation"

CCB’s differentiation is mainly reflected in:

**1. Housing Finance Ecosystem**
- Mortgage, provident fund loan, housing rental platform ("Jianrong Home")
- Full process service from "wanting to buy a house" to "moved in"
- This is the area where CCB is closest to its "ecological" competitive advantage

**2. CCB Financial’s market-oriented output**
- CCB Financial not only serves CCB itself, but also exports its technical capabilities to the outside world
- This enables CCB’s investment in the financial technology field to have a higher asset reuse rate

**3. Inclusive Finance**
- The "Hui understand you" platform is differentiated in the field of small and micro enterprise loans
- But profit contribution is limited

## 5. Long-term sustainability ★★★★☆ (4/5)

The judgment is almost consistent with that of ICBC:
- 100% certainty of survival
- Earnings growth limited by NIM compression
- Increased dividends are the main source of returns
- Leadership in housing finance remains valuable in the long term

**Marginal advantage**: ROE 10.04% is slightly higher than ICBC's 9.5%, which means that CCB's capital use efficiency is slightly better.
