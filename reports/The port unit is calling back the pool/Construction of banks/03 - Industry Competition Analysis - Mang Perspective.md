### Positioning of CCB: "housing + infrastructure + technology" trinity

CCB attempts to establish differentiation in the following areas:

**1. Full ecology of housing finance**
- Ranked first in the country in terms of mortgage loan balance
- Housing rental platform "Jianrong Home" - extending from "buying a house" to "renting a house"
- Provident fund services, housing provident fund loans

**2. Traditional advantages of infrastructure loans**
- The name "Construction Bank" comes from the national infrastructure investment system
- Decades of experience in infrastructure project evaluation and post-loan management
- But this advantage is weakening as the growth rate of infrastructure investment slows down

**3. Fintech output**
- CCB Financial is the first independently operated financial technology subsidiary among the six major banks
- Exporting technical capabilities to the outside world creates sources of income outside the banking system.
- But the scale is still small and the impact on overall profits is limited

### Competitive advantage analysis

**Advantage 1: The "stock lock-in" effect of housing finance**
- The average duration of mortgage loans issued is 15-20 years
- Customers are unlikely to refinance just because the interest rate difference is 0.1% (high handling costs)
- This constitutes a stable pool of interest income

**Advantage 2: Slightly higher ROE reflects better asset allocation**
- China Construction Bank 10.04% vs ICBC 9.5% - the difference is small but reflects the difference in operating quality
- Possible source: Low NPL ratio of mortgage loans contributes to better asset quality

**Advantage 3: First-mover effect of digital transformation**
- CCB Financial was established earlier and has demonstration projects in the fields of government affairs, housing and construction, etc.
- External technology export brings marginal income and brand effect

### Competitive disadvantages

**Disadvantage 1: Housing concentration is a double-edged sword**
- A high mortgage ratio means: a good real estate market → the most benefited, a poor real estate market → the most hurt
- The current property market is still in an adjustment period, and this "advantage" is a burden for the time being.

**Disadvantage 2: Corporate business is not as strong as ICBC**
- The share of super-large state-owned enterprise customers (such as "Three Barrels of Oil", Power Grid, etc.) is lower than that of ICBC
- The profit contribution of public banks is weaker than that of ICBC

## 3. Analysis of multiple thinking models

### "Comparative Advantage" Perspective
Reasons to invest in CCB among the six major banks:
- The highest ROE (10.04%) → the best capital efficiency
- The lowest PE (4.4 times) → the cheapest valuation
- The combination of the two means: among similar targets, CCB may have the highest "cost-effectiveness"

### "Mean Regression" Thinking
- The valuations of the six major banks have historically been highly correlated and have converged over the long term.
- If CCB’s current PE of 4.4 times is significantly lower than ICBC’s 5-6 times PE, and its fundamentals are not worse
- Then there is a "mean reversion" valuation repair opportunity

### "Anti-fragile" analysis
- Home loans look vulnerable in housing downturn
- But China’s mortgage LTV restrictions + high down payment ratio + historically low default rate
- In fact, mortgage assets may be the most "antifragile" asset class in the Chinese banking system
- Only extreme scenarios (50% drop in property prices + large-scale supply cuts) will cause real losses

## 4. Industry trends and CCB’s adaptability

### Trend 1: The housing market shifts from "incremental" to "stock"
- New home sales decline, but financial demand for existing housing stock (renovation loans, second-hand housing transactions) remains
- Housing rental is a new growth point
- **Adaptability: ★★★★☆**——"Jianrong Home" has been laid out

### Trend 2: Infrastructure investment shifts from traditional to new infrastructure
- The growth rate of traditional railway and public infrastructure is slowing down
- New infrastructure (5G, data centers, new energy) requires different evaluation capabilities
- **Adaptability: ★★★☆☆**——Traditional advantages are being transformed

### Trend 3: Bank digitalization and open banking
- Banks are gradually shifting from "branch services" to "embedded finance"
- CCB Jinke’s technology output capability is a source of differentiation
- **Adaptability: ★★★★☆**——Leading among the six major industries

### Trend 4: Dividend rate increasing trend
- Policies encourage central enterprises to improve shareholder returns
- The dividend rate of the six major banks will be from 25% to 30%, and the next step may be to 35-40%
- **Adaptability: ★★★★★** - direct benefit

## 5. Sustainability of the moat

Judgment consistent with ICBC: "100% will exist, but excess returns are uncertain"

**The marginal advantages of CCB are**:
- The brand mentality in the housing finance field is difficult to replace
- A slightly higher ROE means better capital efficiency
- Fintech deployment may bring long-term marginal returns

**50-year perspective**: China Construction Bank will be one of the top three banks in China - confirmed. But whether ROE can recover to 12%+ - not sure.
