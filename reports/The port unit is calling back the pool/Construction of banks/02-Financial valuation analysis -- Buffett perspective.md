# China Construction Bank (0939.HK) – Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Profit quality analysis

### Revenue structure (2025)
- Operating income: 740.871 billion yuan (+1.69% YoY)
- Net interest income: approximately 520-540 billion yuan (accounting for ~72%)
- Non-interest income: handling fees + investment income

### Profit Trend
- Net profit: 339.790 billion yuan (+1.04% YoY)
- Profit growth basically matches revenue growth
- Profit growth rate in the past three years: low single-digit growth of 1-3%

### Profit quality judgment ★★★☆☆

**Front**:
- Total assets grew by 12.47%, far exceeding profit growth by 1% - indicating that the profit growth rate is "real" and not inflated by expanding the balance sheet
- The cost-to-income ratio of 29.44% is at a moderate to excellent level among major banks.
- Provision coverage ratio is good and profits are not overly dependent on provision release

**Negative**:
- Continuous compression of NIM is the core profit pressure
- Assets increased by 12.47% but profits only increased by 1% - indicating that the rate of return on new assets is declining
- The "quantity makes up for the price" model is failing - quantity can no longer make up for the price.

## 2. Capital efficiency and return

### Key Capital Indicators
| Indicators | 2025 | vs ICBC |
|------|--------|---------|
| ROE (weighted) | 10.04% | 9.5% |
| ROA | 0.79% | 0.75% |
| Capital adequacy ratio | 19.69% | 18.76% |
| Cost-to-income ratio | 29.44% | ~27% |
| Dividend rate | 30% | 30% |

### Capital Efficiency Assessment ★★★☆☆

- ROE 10.04% is slightly higher than ICBC and close to the lower limit of capital cost
- The capital adequacy ratio of 19.69% is also considered "over-capitalization"
- But compared to ICBC, CCB's ROE is closer to the threshold of "creating value" (10-12%)

**Key question**: Is ROE in a declining channel (caused by NIM compression), or can it maintain 10%?
- If it can be maintained at 10% - PB≈1 times is reasonable
- If reduced to 8-9% - PB should be <0.8x

## 3. Balance sheet health

### Capital Adequacy
- Capital adequacy ratio 19.69%: extremely sufficient
- Asset growth of 12.47% consumed some capital, but still far exceeded regulatory requirements

### Asset Quality ★★★★☆
- The non-performing loan ratio is 1.33%, basically the same as ICBC (1.31%)
- Need to pay attention: CCB has a higher proportion of mortgage loans. If the property market risk is transmitted, the impact will be more direct.
- Robust provision coverage

### Special Considerations for Home Loans
- China Construction Bank is the largest personal housing loan bank in China
- The historical mortgage default rate in China is extremely low (<1%)
- However, property prices continue to fall + income expectations weaken, and the migration of special-mention loans needs to be monitored

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| PE (TTM) | ~4.4x |
| PB | ~0.5-0.55 times |
| Dividend Yield | ~7% |
| ROE | 10.04% |

### Valuation rationality judgment ★★★★☆

**PE 4.4 times is among the lowest among the Hong Kong banking sector**

Compared with ICBC’s PE of 5-6 times, CCB’s PE is lower, but ROE is higher. This means:
- CCB may be the "best value for money" target among the six major banks
- 4.4 times PE implicit assumption: zero profit growth or even negative growth
- A 7% dividend yield means that even without growth at all, dividends alone are a good return

**Why is China Construction Bank cheaper than ICBC? **
- Possible reasons: H-share liquidity differences, investor structure differences
- It is also possible that the market views CCB’s housing loan concentration as an additional risk
- But from a fundamental perspective, the difference between the two is minimal

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Downside Protection**:
- 0.5x PB extremely pessimistic
- 7% dividend yield is a strong return on holding
- Even if ROE drops to 8%, there is still 6-7% dividend coverage at 0.5x PB

**Upside Flexibility**:
- ROE maintained at 10% → Reasonable PB is about 0.8-1.0 times → +40-80% upside potential
- Dividend rate raised to 40% → Dividend rate approximately 9-10% → Revaluation
- Catalyzed by "China Special Appraisal" → Reference A-share CCB PB is about 0.7 times

## 5. Cash flow and shareholder returns

### Dividend performance
- Full-year dividend in 2025: 101.684 billion yuan
- 3.887 yuan (tax included) for every 10 shares
- Among them, the interim dividend is 1.858 yuan and the final dividend is 2.029 yuan.
- Dividend rate 30%

### Dividends improve prospects
- Similar to ICBC, policies encourage central enterprises to increase dividends
- An increase of 30%→40% will bring the dividend rate from 7%→9%+
- This is the most realistic positive catalyst

### Shareholder Return Sustainability ★★★★☆
- 30% dividend payout ratio is extremely conservative
- Capital adequacy ratio of 19.69% far exceeds demand
- Plenty of room to increase dividends or buybacks
- Constraints: capital consumption (asset expansion) and regulatory expectations
