# China Construction Bank (0939.HK) - Investment Research Final Report

## Overall rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.3/5.0)

## One sentence summary

China Construction Bank is the "best value for money" target among the six major banks - it has the highest ROE (10.04%), the lowest PE (4.4 times), and the highest dividend rate (~7%). If you can only choose one among the major state-owned banks, CCB may be the best choice, but it still faces the NIM compression and governance discount problems common to major banks.

## Core investment logic

### Reasons to be bullish
1. **The best value for money among the six major banks**: highest ROE + lowest PE + highest dividend rate
2. **7% dividend rate provides high certainty of return**: Even if there is zero growth, the return on holding is considerable
3. **Anti-fragility in housing finance**: Historically low mortgage default rates and high LTV limits protect asset quality
4. **There is huge room for improvement in dividend rate**: 30% → 40% can lead to revaluation
5. **CCB Financial provides marginal differentiation**: It may become a new growth point in the long term

### Reasons to be bearish
1. **NIM narrowing trend remains unchanged**: profit growth is weak
2. **Housing concentration is a burden during the property market downturn**: Although it is not a systemic risk, it suppresses market confidence.
3. **Reasonable governance discount**: The attributes of state-owned enterprises limit value creation
4. **H share liquidity discount**: AH premium exists for a long time

## Final comparison with ICBC

**If you have to choose between ICBC and CCB:**
- Reasons for choosing China Construction Bank: lower PE (4.4x vs 5-6x), higher ROE (10% vs 9.5%), differentiated housing finance
- Reasons for choosing ICBC: larger scale (No. 1 in the world), better liquidity, and stronger access to large corporate customers
- **My judgment**: CCB is marginally better. But the difference between the two is very small, and the core return drivers are exactly the same (NIM trend + dividend rate + valuation revaluation).

## Conclusion and suggestions

China Construction Bank is the "top student" among the six major banks - under the same state-owned enterprise governance framework, its ROE is higher, its valuation is lower, and its strategic awareness is stronger. The dividend rate of 4.4 times PE + 7% is an extreme value in the global banking industry.

**Core Judgment**: If you believe that a systemic financial crisis will not occur in China, then CCB’s current valuation provides a sufficient margin of safety, and a 7% dividend rate + potential valuation repair can provide an annualized return of 10-15%. If you have strong concerns about systemic risks in the Chinese economy, all Chinese banks should be out of the portfolio.

CCB is not an "exciting" investment target, but it may be one of the best choices for "allowing you to collect interest with peace of mind."
