## Research background

CSPC Pharmaceutical Group is China's leading comprehensive pharmaceutical company and is in a critical stage of transformation from generic drugs to innovative drugs. The company's performance has declined for three consecutive years (net profits attributable to the parent company from 2023 to 2025 were 5.873 billion, 4.328 billion, and 3.882 billion yuan respectively), mainly due to the impact of the centralized procurement policy on the traditional generic drug business. The current valuation is at a historical low, with a PE of only 8.7 times, and market pricing has fully reflected the pessimistic expectations.

## Core Contradiction

**Bull logic:**
- Valuation is extremely low, PE 8.7 times is the historical bottom area
- The innovative drug pipeline has entered the cash-out period, ranking 15th in global pipeline scale
- BD licensing explodes, with cumulative contract value exceeding US$28.2 billion in 2025
- Expected performance inflection point in 2026 (brokerage forecasts net profit +47% year-on-year)
- Generic drug of semaglutide is about to be approved, opening up the weight loss track

**Short-selling logic:**
- Performance has declined for three consecutive years, and fundamentals have not yet stabilized.
- Management turmoil, CEO Hei Yongjiang resigned after only 1.5 years in office
- The second generation Cai Lei takes over, and there are doubts about whether he can handle the complex transformation.
- The risk of contract renewal in centralized procurement is still there, and core varieties such as NBIPU continue to be under pressure
- Commercialization capabilities of innovative drugs have not yet been fully verified
