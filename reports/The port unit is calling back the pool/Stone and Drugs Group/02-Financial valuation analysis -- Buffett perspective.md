# CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, safety margin
> Core question: Do current prices provide a sufficient margin of safety?

---

## 1. Financial panorama: the truth about three years of decline

### Revenue and Profit Trends

| Year | Operating income (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit attributable to parent companies (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year |
|------|-----------------|------|-------------------|------|
| 2022 | 310.16 | +13.5% | 60.34 | +1.9% |
| 2023 | 315.68 | +1.8% | 58.73 | -2.7% |
| 2024 | 290.09 | -8.1% | 43.28 | -26.3% |
| 2025 | 260.06 | -10.4% | 38.82 | -10.3% |

**Key Observations:**
- Revenue has declined for two consecutive years and profits have declined for three consecutive years.
- Profits are declining faster than revenue, indicating that gross profit margin is compressing (from 70% to 65.6%)
- However, the profit decline in 2025 (10.3%) is smaller than that in 2024 (26.3%), and the decline shows signs of narrowing.

### Changes in business structure

Revenue from the patent medicine business in 2025 will be 20.584 billion yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 13.3%. But if the impact of centralized purchasing is excluded:
- Innovative drug revenue accounts for approximately 48% of the patent drug business, and the proportion continues to increase
- BD licensing income is 1.789 billion yuan, becoming a new source of income
- R&D expenses were 5.809 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 11.9%, accounting for 28.2% of patent medicine revenue

## 2. Profit quality: What is the "gold content" of profits?

### Gross profit margin and net profit margin

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Gross profit margin | 72.5% | 70.0% | 65.6% |
| Net profit margin | 18.6% | 14.9% | 14.9% |
| R&D expense ratio | 16.5% | 17.9% | 22.3% |

**Analysis:**
- The decrease in gross profit margin was mainly affected by the price reduction of centralized purchasing products, which dropped from 72.5% to 65.6%.
- The net profit margin is stable at about 15%, indicating that the company has partially hedged the decline in gross profit through expense control.
- The R&D expense rate increased significantly to 22.3%, reflecting the strategic choice of "exchanging profits for the future"

### Cash flow quality

CSPC Pharmaceutical Group's operating cash flow is generally healthy, but it should be noted:
- One-time cash flow from BD licensing revenue is unsustainable
- Increased R&D investment will continue to consume cash
- Capital expenditures (capacity construction, etc.) remain at a high level

From Buffett's perspective, the "real" level of free cash flow for a company that requires continuous heavy investment to remain competitive is often lower than its book profit. A considerable part of CSPC's R&D investment of 5.8 billion is essentially "maintenance capital expenditure" - if it is not invested, it will be eliminated.

## 3. Capital allocation efficiency

### ROE analysis

| Year | ROE | Analysis |
|------|-----|------|
| 2022 | About 19% | Peak profit period |
| 2023 | About 17% | Start to decline |
| 2024 | About 12% | Profits drop sharply |
| 2025 | About 10% | Continue to be under pressure |

ROE fell from 19% to 10%, reflecting a material deterioration in profitability. If profits resume growth in the future, ROE is expected to rebound, but it will be difficult to reach a level above 15% in the short term.

### Dividends and buybacks

CSPC Pharmaceutical Group maintained a certain dividend ratio, but during the period of declining performance:
- The dividend ratio is maintained at around 30-40%
- Based on the current stock price, the dividend yield is about 4-5%
- No large-scale buyback actions have been seen yet

**Buffett's Perspective:** A company that is investing heavily in research and development while maintaining high dividends needs to question the rationality of its capital allocation. If the expected return on R&D projects is higher than the capital cost, dividends should be reduced and investment increased; otherwise, returns to shareholders should be increased. Shi Yao's approach is more like "don't want to give up on both ends".

## 5. Safety margin analysis

### Where is the bottom line to support valuation?

1. **Asset perspective**: PB is about 1.5-2 times, not particularly cheap. The assets of pharmaceutical companies are mainly intangible assets (pipelines), and the book value has limited reference significance.

2. **Dividend perspective**: Calculated based on a 4-5% dividend rate, it provides a certain holding cost compensation, but it is not enough to constitute a major safety margin.

3. **Liquidation perspective**: CSPC owns physical assets (factories, API production capacity), but the liquidation value of pharmaceutical companies is far lower than the going concern value, so this perspective is of little significance.

4. **Acquisition Perspective**: With a current market value of approximately HK$60 billion and a pharmaceutical company platform ranking 15th in the global pipeline, it may be attractive to multinational pharmaceutical companies. But the possibility of actual acquisition is extremely low (state-owned enterprise background, family control).

### Sensitivity of key assumptions

## 6. Buffett-style summary

### Financial Health Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)

**Front:**
- No significant debt risk, relatively healthy balance sheet
- Positive operating cash flow and no short-term liquidity problems
- Dividend yield provides some defensiveness

**Negative:**
- Profitability continues to deteriorate (ROE drops from 19% to 10%)
- Free cash flow is eroded by high R&D investment
- The gross profit margin trend is downward, indicating that pricing power is weakening

### Valuation attractiveness score: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

**Front:**
- PE 8.7 times is at the bottom of history, and the downside space is relatively limited
- If the performance inflection point is realized, there is considerable room for valuation repair
- Dividend rate provides a certain safety cushion

**Negative:**
- Low PE may be a sign of a "value trap"
- There is great uncertainty in realizing the performance turning point
- The overall valuation of the Hong Kong pharmaceutical sector is low, and individual stocks are difficult to repair independently.

### Core conclusion

The current PE of 8.7 times has been priced in quite pessimistic expectations. If performance does reach an inflection point in 2026 (net profit growth of more than 30%), the current price provides a reasonable margin of safety. But this safety margin is based on the premise that "the performance turning point must be realized" rather than the absolute safety margin of "even if the performance is not good, you will not lose money."

**This is more like an investment opportunity with "good odds but uncertain winning rate" rather than the "high winning rate and reasonable odds" investment that Buffett prefers. **
