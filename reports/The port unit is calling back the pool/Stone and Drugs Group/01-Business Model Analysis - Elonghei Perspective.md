# CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business nature, user value, competition barriers, business model sustainability
> Core question: Is this a good business? Can it be done for a long time?

---

## 1. Nature of business: How does this business make money?

CSPC Pharmaceutical Group is essentially a comprehensive pharmaceutical company that combines generic drugs with innovative drugs. It is in the transition period from a cash cow of generic drugs to a growth engine of innovative drugs.

**Revenue composition (2025):**
-Pharmaceutical business: 20.584 billion yuan (accounting for 79%), of which innovative drugs and innovative preparations account for approximately 48% of patent drug revenue
- API business: approximately 5.4 billion yuan (accounting for 21%)
- BD licensing revenue: 1.789 billion yuan (included in patent medicine business)

**Profit model dismantling:**
1. **Generic drug business**: It relies on scale and cost advantages to earn manufacturing profits, but the centralized procurement policy makes it a "price taker"
2. **Innovative drug business**: Earn excess profits by relying on patent protection and clinical value, but the commercialization cycle is long
3. **BD Authorization**: Obtain one-time income by realizing the value of pipelines. The total contract value in 2025 will exceed US$28.2 billion.

The essential contradiction in this business is that generic drugs contribute to cash flow but are shrinking, while innovative drugs contribute to imagination but have not yet been fully realized.

## 2. User value: Is the product irreplaceable for users?

### Core product analysis

| Product | Domain | Position | Substitutability |
|------|------|------|----------|
| Enbipu (butylphthalide) | Nervous system | Once an exclusive variety | Patent expiration, generic drugs entered |
| Domeisu | Tumor | Domestic liposomal doxorubicin leader | Has been included in centralized procurement, competition intensifies |
| Jinyouli | Tumor auxiliary | The main force in the field of long-term whitening | Already included in centralized purchasing |
| Keali (albumin-paclitaxel) | Tumor | First domestic imitation | Many competing products |
| Mingfulue | Cardiovascular | Thrombolytic field | Differentiated advantages |

**Key judgment:** CSPC’s traditional core products are losing their irreplaceability. The expiration of NBIPU's patent and the entry of Duomeisu and Jinyouli into centralized procurement means that the barriers to competition in the past have been systematically weakened by policies. This is not a problem of CSPC alone, but a structural dilemma of the entire Chinese generic drug industry.

### User value of innovative drug pipeline

CSPC has more than 200 innovative drugs under research and more than 160 clinical trials are ongoing, but most of them have not yet been commercialized. Among them worthy of attention:
- **SYH2086 (oral GLP-1)**: In the field of weight loss, it has been authorized overseas, with a potential transaction value of approximately US$2 billion.
- **SYSA-1801 (tumor immunity)**: in Phase III clinical trials, expected to submit NDA in 2026
- **Semaglutide biosimilar**: Two marketing applications have been accepted

## 3. Barriers to competition: Where is the moat? How wide?

### Positive evaluation

1. **R&D Scale Barriers**: Annual R&D investment is 5.8 billion yuan, ranking 15th in global pipeline scale, and 113 active R&D projects. This kind of large-scale investment creates a deep pipeline, which cannot be easily copied by small and medium-sized pharmaceutical companies.

2. **Full industry chain capabilities**: Vertical integration capabilities from raw materials to preparations give it a natural advantage in cost control. Although the profit margin of the API business is low, it provides a cost moat for the preparation business.

3. **Technology platform accumulation**: We have a layout in cutting-edge technology platforms such as nano-preparations (liposomes, albumin nanoparticles), ADC, and mRNA to provide technology reuse capabilities for subsequent pipelines.

4. **Sales Network**: With a nationwide sales team and channel capabilities, new products can be quickly ramped up after they are launched.

### Negative evaluation

1. **No “killer” product**: Unlike Hengrui’s camrelizumab and BeiGene’s zanubrutinib, CSPC lacks a blockbuster innovative drug that truly defines the brand. Although there are many pipelines, there is a lack of sharp knives.

2. **Low switching cost**: The switching cost of the pharmaceutical industry itself is not high (doctors can easily change prescriptions), and CSPC has not established strong brand loyalty.

3. **The two-sided nature of BD licensing**: Although licensing a large number of overseas products brings revenue, it also means giving up the long-term value of the product in overseas markets. This is essentially a strategy of trading space for time.

---

## 4. Business model sustainability: Can it continue in the long term?

### Positive factors

1. **The transformation direction is correct**: Shifting from generic drugs to innovative drugs is the only way for Chinese pharmaceutical companies, and there is no problem with the direction of CSPC. R&D investment increased by 11.9% against the trend, showing the determination of transformation.

2. **The pipeline is about to enter the harvest period**: It is expected that by the end of 2028, more than 50 new drugs or new indications will be applied for listing, which if realized will bring significant revenue increases.

3. **Diversified layout**: covering six major fields: oncology, neurology, cardiovascular, immunity, metabolism, and anti-infection, plus the expansion of weight loss and rare diseases, reducing the risks in a single field.

### Negative factors

1. **The pain of transformation continues**: The shrinkage rate of the generic drug business may be faster than the growth rate of the innovative drug business, resulting in a longer time window for "green and yellow".

2. **Uncertainty in R&D success rate**: Among 200 projects under development, only 20-30 may eventually be successfully commercialized. There is a risk of "sinking" in large investments in R&D.

3. **Competition is becoming increasingly fierce**: Hengrui and BeiGene are already one step ahead in the field of innovative drugs, while CSPC is facing the situation of being a catch-up.

## 5. Duan Yongping’s summary

### Is this a good business?

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The pharmaceutical industry itself is a good business - high gross profit, strong demand, and long life cycle. However, CSPC is currently in the embarrassing stage of "the old moat has been weakened and the new moat has not yet been built."

If measured by Duan Yongping’s standards:
- **Easy to understand? ** Not completely. The complexity of the pharmaceutical industry (R&D success rate, policy changes, competitive landscape) makes it difficult to predict.
- **Free cash flow good? ** Currently OK, but increased investment in R&D is eroding free cash flow.
- **Has pricing power? ** Generic drugs do not (price is determined by centralized procurement), innovative drugs do (but real clinical differentiation is required).
- **Is management doing the right thing? ** The direction is right, but the execution is questionable (management turmoil is a warning sign).

### Key observation points

1. Can performance in 2026 truly achieve an inflection point (brokerage predicts net profit +47%)
2. Commercialization conversion rate of innovative drug pipeline (how many of the 50 applications can actually contribute revenue)
3. Sustainability of BD licensing income (one-time income vs. ongoing income)
4. Management stability (can Cai Lei stabilize the team and promote transformation)

**Conclusion:** CSPC is an enterprise that is transforming from a "mediocre business" to a "good business", but the transformation has not yet been completed, and the results are highly uncertain. At present, it is more suitable as the target of "observation + small position testing" rather than the object of heavy position holding.
