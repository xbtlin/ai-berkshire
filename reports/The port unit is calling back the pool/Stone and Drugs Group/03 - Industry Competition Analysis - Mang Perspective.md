# CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Industry Competition Analysis

## 1. Industry panorama: Structural changes in China’s pharmaceutical industry

### What is the industry going through?

China's pharmaceutical industry is in the midst of a "big reshuffle", with the core driving force being the combination of policy reshaping and technological progress:

1. **Normalization of centralized purchasing**: The first ten batches of centralized purchasing have covered more than 500 varieties. The profits of generic drugs have been systematically compressed, and the era of "making money by relying on generic drugs" is completely over.

2. **Policy Support for Innovative Drugs**: The 2026 Government Work Report lists biomedicine as an "emerging pillar industry" for the first time, and the policy has shifted from "cost control as the main priority" to "cost control + encouragement of innovation" in parallel. The catalog of innovative drugs for commercial health insurance was released and a multi-level payment system was established.

3. **Going overseas has become a new growth pole**: There will be 157 BD licensing transactions for China’s innovative drugs in 2025, with a total amount exceeding US$135.6 billion. Chinese pharmaceutical companies have moved from "imitation import" to "innovation export".

4. **Patent cliff for multinational pharmaceutical companies**: A large number of MNC’s blockbuster drugs are facing patent expiration, which provides a window of opportunity for Chinese pharmaceutical companies’ biosimilars and differentiated innovation.

### "Economic Characteristics" of the Industry

In Munger’s words, to understand an industry we must first look at its economic characteristics:

- **Long R&D cycle**: It usually takes 10-15 years from discovery to launch of an innovative drug, with billions of yuan invested.
- **Low success rate**: The success rate from clinical phase I to final marketing is about 10-15%
- **Winner takes all**: For the same target/indication, the first to market will occupy the majority of the market share
- **Strong policy dependence**: Medical insurance access, centralized procurement rules, and review speed all directly affect commercialization results
- **Limited scale effects**: R&D does not have obvious scale effects like manufacturing. Large companies are not necessarily more efficient than small companies.

### Competitive position analysis of CSPC

**Comparison with Hengrui:**
Hengrui is the "benchmark" for innovative drugs in China. Its advantages include: rich experience in successful commercialization of innovative drugs (camrelizumab and many other blockbuster varieties), high pipeline conversion efficiency, and strong sales capabilities. CSPC's pipeline scale is no less than that of Hengrui, but there is a clear gap in commercialization capabilities. Hengrui's innovative drug revenue has exceeded 60% and is growing; CSPC has just approached 50% and is still in the process of transformation.

**Comparison with Baekje:**
BeiGene is a representative of "global innovation", with global sales of zanubrutinib exceeding 10 billion. Although CSPC is also promoting internationalization, it is mainly through BD authorization (selling pipelines) rather than going overseas (selling products) independently. Baekje’s model is riskier but also has a higher ceiling; CSPC’s model is more robust but has a limited upper limit.

**Comparison with China Biopharma:**
The two are most similar - both are a combination of imitation and innovation, both are affected by centralized purchasing, and both are in the process of transformation. CSPC’s R&D investment is higher and its pipeline is richer, but China Biopharmaceuticals has brand advantages such as Chia Tai Tianqing. This is the most direct competitor.

## 3. Differentiated advantages and disadvantages of stone medicine

### Differentiation Advantages

1. **Diversity of technology platforms**: CSPC has layouts in multiple cutting-edge platforms such as nanoformulation (liposome technology), ADC (antibody drug conjugate), mRNA, and AI drug discovery. This kind of multi-platform capability is relatively rare among Chinese pharmaceutical companies.

2. **Pipeline Depth**: 113 active R&D projects, 21 Phase III clinical trials, and 34 Phase II clinical trials. This depth of pipeline means that even if some projects fail, the overall probability of success is still high.

3. **Manufacturing Capability**: The capability of the entire industrial chain from raw materials to preparations gives it a cost advantage in the CMC (chemistry, manufacturing and control) link, especially in the field of complex preparations.

4. **BD Liquidity**: The total amount of BD contracts in 2025 will exceed US$28.2 billion, indicating that large overseas pharmaceutical companies recognize the quality of CSPC pipelines. This is an endorsement from a "professional buyer".

### Differentiation Disadvantages

1. **Lack of global commercialization infrastructure**: Unlike BeiGene, which has a complete commercialization team in the United States, CSPC’s overseas capabilities mainly remain at the research and development and licensing levels.

2. **Low brand awareness**: In the capital market and pharmaceutical industry, CSPC’s brand power is weaker than that of Hengrui and Baekje. For pipelines of the same quality, CSPC’s valuation premium is lower.

3. **The burden of "generic drug genes"**: The market's perception of CSPC still remains as "generic drug companies in transition" rather than as "innovative drug companies". This cognitive gap will take time and performance to fill.

4. **Talent attraction**: In the competition for innovative drug talents, CSPC Pharmaceuticals (Shijiazhuang) is not as attractive as Hengrui (Lianyungang/Shanghai) and Beiji (Beijing/Cambridge) in terms of geographical location and brand appeal.

## 4. Impact of industrial trends on stone medicine

### Favorable trends

1. **The impact of centralized procurement tends to be blunted**: After 8 years of centralized procurement, CSPC’s generic drug business has "cut most of the flesh." The marginal impact of subsequent centralized procurement on the company will be diminishing - most of the varieties that can be centralized purchased have already been centralized.

2. **Construction of a multi-level payment system**: The launch of the commercially insured innovative drug catalog is expected to provide a better payment environment for CSPC’s innovative drug products and reduce reliance on price reductions negotiated by medical insurance.

3. **MNC Patent Cliff**: A large number of blockbuster drugs around the world are about to have their patents expire. CSPC’s generic drug capabilities (especially the ability to imitate complex preparations) can seize the opportunity of biosimilar drugs. Semaglutide is a typical example.

4. **AI empowered drug research and development**: CSPC has deployed an AI drug discovery platform, and its cooperation with AstraZeneca involves AI target discovery. If AI can effectively improve R&D efficiency, CSPC’s pipeline output efficiency is expected to increase.

### Adverse Trend

1. **Intensified involution**: China’s innovative drugs are severely homogenized (there are too many players in popular tracks such as PD-1 and ADC), and price competition may spread from generic drugs to innovative drugs.

2. **The threshold for going overseas has increased**: FDA’s recognition of China’s clinical data is tightening, and the overseas strategy faces higher regulatory barriers.

3. **The financing environment in the capital market is tightening**: Hong Kong stocks and A-share IPOs are tightening, and Biotech has difficulties in financing. However, this has limited impact on companies like CSPC that have the ability to self-generate, and may even be acquisition opportunities.

## 5. Key competitive issues

### Can CSPC "catch up" on the innovative drug track?

This is the core competition issue. Shiyao's situation is similar to that of a "latecomer":

**Advantages:**
- The scale of the pipeline is large enough, and there is a probability that there will be successful varieties
- Funds obtained from BD authorization can continue to support research and development
- Diversification of technology platforms reduces the risk of "betting in the wrong direction"

**Disadvantages:**
- Hengrui and Baekje are already 1-2 positions ahead, and their brand and talent advantages are self-reinforcing
- Lack of overseas independent commercialization capabilities limits the value ceiling of innovative drugs
- Management turmoil may affect the coherence of R&D strategy

## 6. Summary of Mang style

### Industry Attractiveness Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

China's innovative drug industry is in a "painful but correct" transition period. The policy has shifted from "stuck" to "supportive", and going overseas has opened up new growth space. The MNC patent cliff has provided a historic opportunity. This is an industry worth participating in, but competition is fierce and success rates are uncertain.

### Competitive Position Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)

CSPC is at the "end of the first echelon" or the "1.5 echelon" of Chinese pharmaceuticals. The scale of the pipeline is large enough, but the commercialization capabilities and brand power are not as good as those of the leaders. There is no lag behind in difficult situations, but there are no obvious signs of "overtaking on corners".

### Key conclusions

Use Munger's "reverse thinking" to analyze: What conditions are needed for CSPC Group to fail? **

1. Large-scale failure of innovative drug pipelines (low probability, pipelines are dispersed enough)
2. Centralized procurement will worsen further than expected (low probability, almost over)
3. The BD licensing market collapses (medium probability, affected by the global Biotech financing environment)
4. Continued management turmoil leads to the collapse of strategic execution (medium probability, the risk that requires the most attention at the moment)

** CSPC is unlikely to "completely fail", but to be "a big success" it requires the commercialization of innovative drugs to exceed expectations. ** This is a pattern of "little loss but uncertain win". For investors who buy at a low valuation, the odds are reasonable.
