## 1. Investment Thesis

### Core logic

CSPC Pharmaceutical Group is a typical candidate for "dilemma reversal":

1. **The dilemma is known**: The impact of centralized procurement has led to three consecutive years of decline in performance, and the market has fully reflected the pessimistic expectations (PE 8.7 times)
2. **Possible reversal**: Innovative drug pipeline enters the cash-out period, BD licensing contribution increases, and the impact of centralized procurement decreases
3. **Catalytic time**: 2026 is generally regarded as the turning point year for performance by brokerages (forecast net profit +47%)

### But this is not a "certain" opportunity

Different from the classic dilemma reversal, CSPC is not facing a simple "return to normal from cyclical lows", but requires "successful model transformation" to realize the reversal. This increases uncertainty.

## 2. Summary of positive arguments

### Valuation and margin of safety

- PE 8.7 times is at an absolute low in history, with limited downside space
- Dividend yield of 4-5% provides holding period returns
- Very low risk of permanent capital loss (healthy balance sheet, no major debt issues)
- If the performance turning point in 2026 is realized, 15x PE corresponds to 42% upside potential

### Positive fundamental signals

- The total number of BD licensing contracts exceeds 28.2 billion US dollars, and it is highly recognized internationally.
- Ranked 15th in global pipeline scale, with sufficient pipeline depth
- R&D investment has grown against the trend, reaching 5.8 billion yuan in 2025, accounting for 22% of revenue
- The impact of centralized purchasing is almost over, and the marginal impact is diminishing
- Semaglutide similar drug is about to be launched, opening up the weight loss track
- The policy environment shifts to support innovation (multi-level payment system, industrial policy support)

### Industry Opportunities

- MNC patent cliff provides Chinese pharmaceutical companies with a window to go overseas
- AI empowers drug research and development to improve efficiency
- Biomedicine will be listed as an "emerging pillar industry" in 2026

---

## 3. Summary of negative arguments

### Management and Governance Risks

- The second generation Cai Lei took over less than half a year ago, and his ability has not yet been verified.
- Executive President Hei Yongjiang resigned after only 1.5 years in office, implying internal differences in line.
- The media reported on "when will the personnel turmoil stop?", which damaged the industry's reputation.
- The family control structure lacks effective error correction mechanisms

### Fundamental concerns

- Performance has declined for three consecutive years, with net profit in 2025 being only 3.882 billion (a 36% decrease from the high in 2022)
- The commercialization ability of innovative drugs has not yet been verified - there are many pipelines but lack of "blockbuster" products
- Huge investment in R&D but uncertain success rate (how much revenue can 200 ongoing projects ultimately contribute?)
- BD licensing income has a one-time character and cannot be used as evidence of ongoing operating capabilities.
- The gap with Hengrui and Baekje may continue to widen in terms of brand power and talent attraction

### Competitive landscape pressure

- Involution of the innovative drug industry is intensifying, and homogeneous competition may compress profits
- Lack of overseas independent commercialization capabilities and limited value ceiling
- A relatively weak chaser position in the first echelon

## 5. Investment decision-making framework

### Scenario analysis

**Scenario A: Successful transformation (30% probability)**
- Performance will clearly reverse in 2026, with net profit increasing by more than 40%
- Management is stable and innovative drug pipeline continues to deliver
- Target valuation: 15-18x PE, corresponding to a market value of HK$90-100 billion
- Expected return: +50% to +70%

**Scenario B: Moderate recovery (40% probability)**
- Performance will recover moderately in 2026, with net profit increasing by 15-30%
- Part of the pipeline realization has been implemented, but it is not as good as expected.
- Target valuation: 10-13x PE, corresponding to a market value of HK$50-70 billion
- Expected return: -15% to +15%

**Scenario C: Continued deterioration (30% probability)**
- The performance turning point is delayed and management turmoil intensifies
- Confidence collapsed due to failure of centralized procurement contract renewal or new varieties
- Target valuation: 6-8x PE, corresponding to a market value of HK$30-45 billion
- Expected return: -25% to -50%

**Expected return calculation:**
- 0.3 × 60% + 0.4 × 0% + 0.3 × (-35%) = 18% + 0% - 10.5% = +7.5%

The expected return is positive but not huge, and the variance of the distribution is large.

## 6. Investment advice

### Conclusion: "You can pay attention" if conditions permit, but don't rush to take a heavy position.

**Current Action:**
- Included in the observation pool and set the 2026 mid-term report as the key verification node
- You can establish an observation position of 1-2% at the current price
- It is not recommended to establish a position of more than 5% before the performance turning point is verified.

**Conditions for adding a position (at least 2 must be met at the same time):**
1. Net profit in the 2026 interim report will increase by more than 30% year-on-year
2. There will be no major personnel changes in the management team in the next 6 months.
3. A new blockbuster innovative drug is approved or a major BD contract is signed
4. The stock price has not increased significantly from the current level (still within 10x PE)

**Exit conditions (meet any one):**
1. The 2026 interim report performance is less than expected (net profit growth <10%)
2. Another core executive resigned, increasing management turmoil.
3. Major pipeline failure (poor phase III clinical readout)
4. Major governance issues arise (related transactions, financial doubts, etc.)

## 7. Differences from classic dilemma reversal targets

| Features | Classic dilemma reversal | CSPC Pharmaceutical Group |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Reasons for difficulties | Cyclical/one-time | Structural (business model transformation) |
| The path to recovery | Going back to the past | Becoming something new |
| Certainty | High (the cycle will inevitably turn around) | Medium (the transformation may fail) |
| Time Frame | 1-2 years | 2-3 years |
| Valuation elasticity | High (return to normal valuation) | Medium-high (need to prove new story) |

The reversal of CSPC's dilemma is not simply "waiting for the cycle to return", but needs to be driven by "the successful transformation of innovative drugs". This makes it more like a "value stock with option attributes" - the room for decline is limited (valuation is already low), but a catalyst is needed for growth (performance inflection point is realized).

## 8. Final judgment

**Summary in one sentence:** CSPC Pharmaceutical Group is in the position of "bottom of valuation + eve of business turnaround", the odds are reasonable but the winning rate has not yet been confirmed. It is suitable as a candidate target in the dilemma reversal observation pool. Participate with a small position and wait for verification signals before deciding whether to increase the position.

**The biggest risk is not losing a lot of money, but not rising for a long time after buying, and the opportunity cost is too high. **

**The biggest chance is that if the performance inflection point in 2026 is realized and management is stable, the current price will be a clear low in retrospect. **
