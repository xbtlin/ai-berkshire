# ENN Energy (2688.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial health, earnings quality, intrinsic value estimation
> Core question: Is the money the company makes real? How much is it worth? Is there a margin of safety at current prices?

---

## 1. Income statement: dismantling of earning power

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Indicators | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Turnover (billion yuan) | 1,078 | 1,054 | 1,099 | 1,119 |
| Gross profit (100 million yuan) | 121 | 131 | 134 | 133 |
| Gross profit margin | 11.2% | 12.4% | 12.2% | 11.9% |
| Net profit attributable to parent companies (100 million yuan) | 56.5 | 59.0 | 59.9 | 59.0 |
| Core profit (100 million yuan) | 62.7 | 64.5 | 66.3 | 67.4 |

**Positive interpretation ★★★☆☆**

Revenue has maintained steady growth at the 100 billion level, with a four-year compound growth rate of approximately 1.3%. Core profit has steadily increased year by year, from 6.27 billion to 6.74 billion, indicating that after excluding one-time gains and losses, the profitability of the main business is improving. This is in line with the characteristics of utility companies: slow but stable growth.

**Reverse view**

Net profit attributable to the parent company has been "stagnant" at around 5.9 billion yuan for three consecutive years, and will even drop 1.39% year-on-year in 2025. Against the backdrop of slight revenue growth, profit stagnation means cost pressure is eroding profit margins. Gross profit margin will decline from 12.4% in 2023 to 11.9% in 2025, mainly due to pressure on both natural gas retail and engineering installation businesses.

What is even more alarming is the continued shrinkage of **engineering and installation business** - this was originally the company's highest gross profit business segment, with revenue and gross profit falling by 17.9% and 12.3% respectively. The impact of the real estate downturn on gas companies is not a one-time event, and the number of new home connections may continue to be sluggish.

## 2. Balance Sheet: Financial Health

### 2.1 Asset Structure

ENN Energy is a typical asset-heavy company, with a large amount of capital deposited in fixed assets such as pipeline networks and equipment. The asset-liability ratio in 2025 will be approximately 49%, which is a reasonable level in the utility industry.

### 2.2 Liability quality

**Signs worthy of attention:** Net current liabilities surged from 7.4 billion yuan to 13.2 billion yuan in the first half of 2025, an increase of nearly 78%. This shows that short-term debt repayment pressure is increasing. While net current liabilities are not unusual for utilities with stable cash flows (because they can be rolled back through operating cash flow), the magnitude of the increase warrants attention.

**The bigger concern comes from the consolidated statements after privatization. ** If ENN Co., Ltd. successfully privatizes ENN Energy, the post-merger debt ratio may rise from 54% to 67%. High leverage is a profit amplifier during economic upturns, but it becomes a risk amplifier during economic downturns.

### 2.3 Accounts receivable

ENN Co., Ltd.'s accounts receivable in 2025 will be relatively large, which is a signal that needs attention in the natural gas distribution industry. If the payment collection cycle of downstream users (especially industrial and commercial customers) is lengthened, it will occupy the company's operating funds.

## 3. Cash flow statement: profit content

### 3.1 Operating cash flow

| Net operating cash flow | 9.61 billion | 10.29 billion | 5.57 billion |
| Net Profit to Cash Ratio | ~1.6 | ~1.7 | - |

**This is the most satisfactory indicator for ENN Energy. ** Operating cash flow continues to be higher than net profit, and the net profit to cash ratio is stable at 1.6-1.7 times, indicating that profit is "real" cash earned, not an accounting numbers game.

What Buffett values most is this - whether the profits earned can be turned into real money. ENN Energy performs well in this area.

### 3.2 Free cash flow

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Free cash flow | 2.13 billion | 3.56 billion |
| Free Cash Flow/Net Profit | 36% | 59% |

Free cash flow is improving, increasing from 2.1 billion in 2023 to 3.6 billion in 2024, mainly due to lower capital expenditures. As pipeline network construction enters a mature stage, capital expenditures are expected to continue to decline and free cash flow will be further released.

**But there is a contradiction here:** If the company wants to maintain growth, it needs to continue to invest in capital expenditures; if it reduces investment, free cash flow will look good, but future growth may be limited. For ENN, which has covered more than 250 cities, the trade-off of "growth vs. return" is becoming increasingly realistic.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 Relative valuation

| Indicators | ENN Energy | China Resources Gas | Kunlun Energy | China Gas |
|------|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| PE（TTM） | ~9.9x | ~12x | ~10x | ~8x |
| PB | ~1.3x | ~1.5x | ~0.9x | ~0.5x |
| Dividend Yield | ~5.7% | ~4.5% | ~5% | ~6% |
| ROE | ~15% | ~13% | ~9% | ~6% |

**Positive interpretation ★★★★☆**

Among the four major gas companies, ENN Energy has the highest ROE (15%), but its PE is not the highest (9.9 times vs. China Resources' 12 times), which means that the market has given ENN a "private enterprise discount"**. If you look at ROE and PE combined, ENN's price/performance ratio (PEG or ROE/PE) is the best among its peers.

The dividend yield of 5.7% is quite attractive in a downward interest rate environment, and the dividend ratio is promised to be no less than 50% in 2026, which means there is still room for dividend growth.

**Reverse view**

China Gas has a lower PE (about 8 times) and a higher dividend rate (about 6%), but its quality is obviously not as good as ENN. A low valuation doesn't necessarily mean it's cheap and may reflect higher risk. In the same way, ENN's "private enterprise discount" is not without reason - private enterprises do face policy uncertainty and governance risks.

### 4.2 Absolute valuation (DCF simplified estimation)

**Key assumptions:**
- Free cash flow growth of 5% annually over the next five years (conservative)
- Sustainable growth rate 2%
- Discount rate 9% (taking into account the risk premium of Hong Kong private companies)
- Free cash flow base in 2025 is approximately 3.8 billion yuan

| Year | Free cash flow (100 million yuan) |
|------|-------------------|
| 2026E | 40 |
| 2027E | 42 |
| 2028E | 44 |
| 2029E | 46 |
| 2030E | 49 |
| Sustainable value | 713 |

**DCF valuation = present value of cash flow in the next 5 years + present value of sustainable value ≈ 170 + 463 = 63.3 billion yuan**

Corresponding to about 56 yuan per share (about 49 Hong Kong dollars), the current stock price is about 63 Hong Kong dollars, which is not cheap from a DCF perspective**.

However, if the discount rate is reduced to 8% (reflecting a more optimistic risk assessment), the valuation will rise to approximately 78 billion yuan, corresponding to approximately HK$61 per share, which is still lower than the current share price.

**DCF’s conclusion is: the current valuation is basically reasonable, and there is no significant margin of safety. **

### 4.3 Anchoring effect of privatization consideration

The privatization consideration given by ENN is HK$80 per share, including HK$24.5 in cash and 2.94 H shares of ENN. If the privatization is successful, the current share price of HK$63 implies an upside of approximately 27%.

**But this "margin of safety" is conditional:**
1. Privatization must be completed successfully (regulatory approval is required and is still in progress)
2. The value of 2.94 ENN H shares depends on the listing price of ENN H shares
3. After privatization, the debt ratio of the merged entity may rise to 67%, and the integration risk cannot be ignored.

**It is dangerous to think of the going private consideration as a "safety cushion"** - it is not a permanent value, but an event-driven option.

## 5. Return to shareholders

### 5.1 Dividend policy

The company's 2023-2025 dividend plan: the annual increase in cash dividends will not be less than 0.15 yuan/share, and the cash dividend ratio will not be less than 30% of core profits. The final dividend in 2025 is HK$2.35 per share. Starting from 2026, the dividend ratio is promised to be increased to no less than 50%.

### 5.2 Buyback

The company has sporadic share repurchases in recent years, but the intensity is not large and no sustained and systematic repurchase plan has been formed.

**Buffett's standard:** At the ROE level of 15%, is the return on reinvestment of the company's retained profits higher than the opportunity cost of shareholders' own investment? The answer is not entirely certain - if a large amount of capital is invested in an unproven ubiquitous energy business, the return rate may not reach 15%. From this perspective, it is wiser to increase the dividend ratio than blind expansion, and the company is indeed moving in this direction.
