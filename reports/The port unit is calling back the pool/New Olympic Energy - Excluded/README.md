## Research background

ENN Energy is a benchmark private enterprise in the gas sector of the Hong Kong stock market. The combination of PE 9.9 times and ROE 15% is at a high level among public utilities. The biggest variable at present is the privatization acquisition initiated by ENN Corporation (600803.SH): a consideration of HK$80 per share (HK$24.5 in cash + 2.94 ENN H shares), and the total transaction size is approximately HK$59.9 billion. Whether privatization is completed or not and the integration effect after completion will profoundly change the company's investment logic.

Based on the principle of "both sides, being objective and not presupposing", this study dissects the four dimensions of business model, financial valuation, industry competition, and risk management one by one.
