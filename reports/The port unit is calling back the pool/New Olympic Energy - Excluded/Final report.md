## 2. Core investment thesis

### Reasons to be bullish

**1. Valuation is attractive**
PE 9.9 times, ROE 15%, dividend yield 5.7% - this combination is at a high-quality level in the entire market. In a downward interest rate environment, the 5.7% dividend yield itself is a strong downside protection. The dividend ratio is committed to increase to more than 50% in 2026, and there is still room for dividend increases.

**2. Cash flow quality is the real "hard currency"**
Operating cash flow was 10.3 billion yuan, and the net profit to cash ratio was 1.7 times. This is not a profit made through accounting techniques, but actual cash received. Free cash flow increased from 2.1 billion to 3.6 billion, and the trend is improving.

**3. Industry demand growth is highly certain**
China's natural gas consumption will increase by 4%-6% annually, reaching 550-600 billion cubic meters in 2030. The "coal-to-gas" policy continues to advance, and the status of natural gas as a transitional energy source will not be shaken in the next 10 years.

**4. Privatization provides additional upside**
If the consideration of HK$80 is realized, the current HK$63 means an upside of about 27%. Even with a discount on H-share pricing using conservative assumptions, there are still substantial returns.

**5. "Scarce assets" in private enterprises**
In the Hong Kong public utility sector, there are only a handful of private companies with an ROE of 15%. ENN's operating efficiency, long-term LNG cooperation layout, and comprehensive energy service exploration make it stand out among similar companies.

### Reasons to be bearish

**1. Privatization uncertainty suppresses valuation**
The restructuring resolution has been postponed for 12 months, and the two key prerequisites (Approval by the Stock Exchange and Filing with the Securities Regulatory Commission) have not yet been met. If it fails, the stock price may fall back to the HK$50-55 range, implying a 15-20% downside risk.

**2. Profit growth has stalled**
Net profit attributable to the parent company has remained unchanged at around 5.9 billion yuan for three consecutive years. The engineering and installation business continues to shrink, retail gross profit margins decline, and value-added business has yet to fill the gap. If profits still do not grow in the next 2-3 years, 9.9 times PE is not cheap.

**3. The long-term threat of "the state advances and the people retreat"**
Kunlun Energy jumped from third to first, and the advantages of central enterprises in the incremental market have become increasingly obvious. ENN's franchise is safe during its lifetime, but every renewal is a point of risk.

**4. High leverage after privatization**
After the merger, the debt ratio may rise to 67%. During the economic downturn, combined with rising interest rates, high leverage may change from a profit amplifier to a risk amplifier.

**5. Long-term substitution risk is real**
Photovoltaics + energy storage + heat pumps are gradually eroding the use scenarios of natural gas. The time window of 10-20 years is not too far for long-term investors.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

### Optimistic scenario (30% probability): privatization successful + performance improvement

- The privatization will be completed in 2026. After the merger, the PE in 2026 will be 6.6 times and the dividend rate will be 7.6%.
- The synergies of integration are gradually released, and the combined ROE is increased to 16-17%
- The proportion of residents paying the same price has increased to more than 85%, and the retail gross profit margin has stabilized and rebounded.
- **Corresponding valuation: HKD 80-90 (+27% to +43% on current basis)**

### Baseline scenario (45% probability): privatization is delayed but ultimately successful, with stable performance

- Privatization will be completed in the first half of 2027, during which the stock price fluctuated between 55 and 70 Hong Kong dollars.
- Annualized returns mainly come from 5-6% dividend + 10-15% privatization premium realization
- Maintain the status quo at the operating level, with profits increasing slightly by 2-3%
- **Annualized total return: approximately 12-15% (including dividends)**

### Pessimistic scenario (25% probability): privatization failure or major delay

- The stock price fell back to the range of HK$50-55
- But the company's fundamentals will not deteriorate, and the 5.7%+ dividend yield provides downside protection
- After losing the privatization catalyst, return to the investment logic of "low valuation of public utilities + high dividends"
- **Annualized total return: approximately 5-8% (mainly dependent on dividends)**

## 6. Investment advice

### What kind of investors is it suitable for?

**Suitable for:**
- Investors who pursue stable cash returns and are willing to accept lower capital appreciation
- Investors who have the ability to research and judge privatization events and are willing to bear event risks
- Investors with an investment period of 3-5 years and who do not require rapid liquidity

**Not suitable for:**
- Investors pursuing high growth and flexible returns
- Investors who cannot bear a short-term drawdown of more than 20%
- Investors who have no awareness or tolerance for the governance risks of Hong Kong private companies

| HKD 58-65 | 9.8-11x | Average | Reasonable valuation range, needs privatization catalyst |
| Above HK$65 | >11x | Insufficient | More reliance on privatization premium, lower risk/reward ratio |

### Final judgment

ENN Energy is a company with excellent operating quality but lacks a significant margin of safety in valuation**. The current price of HK$63 is in the "reasonably expensive" range - not outrageously expensive, but not a cheap price that makes people feel confident and bold to buy it.

**The core contradiction is that most of the company's "goodness" has been reflected in the price, while the "badness" (privatization risk, profit stagnation, policy uncertainty) has not yet been fully priced. **

If you look purely at fundamentals (ignoring privatization), this is a bond-like target with "5-6% dividend + 2-3% growth" and an annualized return of 7-9%. It is a good choice among Hong Kong public utilities.

If privatization expectations are superimposed, the return elasticity is greater, but event risks are also introduced. Success in privatization is "the icing on the cake", while failure is "removal of a flower but the foundation remains".

**The ideal buying time is when the stock price falls below HK$55 (PE<9.3 times). At this time, even if the privatization fails, pure fundamental valuation can provide a sufficient margin of safety. **
