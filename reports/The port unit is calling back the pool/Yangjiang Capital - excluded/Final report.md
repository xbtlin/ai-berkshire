## One sentence conclusion

Cheung Kong Infrastructure is the asset closest to "perpetual bonds" in Hong Kong stocks - 28 consecutive years of additional distribution, regulated monopoly, and global diversification. However, PE of 20 times is too expensive for ROE of 6%. Currently, you can only get 6-8% annualized return by buying it, which lacks a safety margin.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. 28 consecutive years of increasing dividends—one of the strongest dividend growth records in Hong Kong stocks
2. Regulated infrastructure assets provide predictable cash flow for 10-20 years
3. The Li family’s capital allocation capabilities are world-class
4. The global interest rate cut cycle will benefit infrastructure valuations
5. Inflation hedging – fees adjust with inflation

### Bearish logic
1. PE 20 times is too high for ROE 6%
2. The UK’s asset concentration ratio is 42% and faces the risk of regulatory tightening
3. The 4% dividend rate lacks relative attractiveness in a high interest rate environment
4. Growth can only rely on acquisitions, and organic growth is almost zero.
5. Low return ceiling

## Buying conditions

1. PE dropped to 15-16 times (corresponding to dividend rate 5.5%+)
2. The world may clearly enter an interest rate cutting cycle
3. UKPN sales funds have clear purpose

## Position suggestions

- **Current Price**: Small position allocation (2-3%), as the "stabilizing anchor" of the portfolio
- **Ideal buying range**: Hong Kong stocks 45-48 yuan (corresponding to PE 15-16 times)
- **Position limit**: 5%
