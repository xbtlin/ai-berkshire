## Investment Highlights
1. 28 consecutive years of dividend growth—one of the longest consecutive growth records for Hong Kong stocks
2. Global infrastructure asset portfolio to diversify geographical risks
3. Regulated assets provide predictable and stable returns
4. Li Ka-shing family’s long-term management experience

## Core Risks
1. PE 20 times is too expensive compared to ROE 6%
2. The proportion of British assets is too high (42%), Brexit/policy risks
3. Rising interest rates increase financing costs
4. Risks of changes in overseas regulatory policies
