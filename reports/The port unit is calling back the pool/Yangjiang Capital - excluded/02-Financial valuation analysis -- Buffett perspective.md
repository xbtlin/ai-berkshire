#Cheung Kong Infrastructure (1038.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

| Indicators | Values | Evaluation |
|------|------|------|
| Operating income | ~HK$4.8 billion | Small scale (profit comes from associated companies) |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | ~HK$8.6 billion | Steady growth |
| ROE | ~6% | Low |
| Continuous dividend growth | 28 years | Extremely rare |
| Credit Rating | A/A2 | Investment Grade |

**Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (2.5/5)**

ROE 6% is not considered excellent under Buffett's framework. Profit is mainly reflected in "share of profits of associates/joint ventures", and assets include long-term infrastructure investments and interests in associates.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE: ~20x
- PB: about 1.2 times
- Dividend yield: about 4%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 12-14 times

### Is 20 times PE reasonable?

Why does the market give 20 times PE to a company with ROE of only 6%? The answer is the certainty premium: 28 years of continuous distribution increases, regulated assets, global diversification, and the credit of the Li Ka-shing family.

But rational assessment: a 4% dividend yield lacks a comparative advantage in the current high interest rate environment. As a "pure dividend instrument", its pricing should be as sensitive to interest rates as a bond.

### Reasonable valuation range
| Scenario | Reasonable PE | Corresponding dividend rate |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Low interest rate environment (US debt below 3%) | 22-25x | 3.5-4% |
| Neutral interest rate environment (U.S. bonds 3-4%) | 18-22x | 4-5% |
| High interest rate environment (US debt 4%+) | 15-18x | 5-6% |

The current U.S. bond yield is about 4%+, and the theoretical reasonable PE should be 15-18 times. A PE of 20 times means that the market has implicit expectations for an interest rate cut.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

1. Can you understand the next 10 years? ——Very certain, infrastructure assets are highly predictable
2. Lasting competitive advantage? ——Yes, natural monopoly + regulatory barriers
3. Is the management honest and capable? ——Extremely positive, the additional dispatch in 28 years proves everything
4. Margin of safety? ——Limited, PE 20 times is too expensive compared to 6% ROE

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Cheung Kong Infrastructure is a good company but not a good price. PE 20 times is too expensive in the current interest rate environment. If the PE drops to 15-16 times (corresponding to a dividend rate of 5-5.5%), the investment value will be significantly improved. It is most suitable for investors such as retirees and insurance funds who have extremely high requirements for certainty.
