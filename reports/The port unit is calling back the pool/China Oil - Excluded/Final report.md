## One sentence conclusion

PetroChina is a "buy oil price" proxy target - if you are bullish on oil prices, it is worth holding. If you have no judgment on oil prices, there is no special reason to buy. In the same industry, CNOOC's price/performance ratio is better than that of PetroChina.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. PE 10-11 times is relatively low among large energy companies, and the valuation has a margin of safety
2. Natural gas business growth is confirmed (accounting for 65%+ of domestic production), providing a growth engine
3. The dividend rate is 54.7% and the dividend rate is ~5%, which is attractive in a low interest rate environment.
4. PB is only 0.5 times, and is supported by asset bottom in extreme cases
5. Geographical conflicts may push up oil prices and bring profit flexibility

### Bearish logic
1. ROE is only 5%, far lower than the cost of capital, and value continues to be destroyed.
2. Oil prices are unpredictable, and profits will have dropped by 4.5% year-on-year in 2025.
3. Under the general trend of global energy transformation, fossil energy assets face the risk of "stranding"
4. The governance efficiency of central enterprises is low, and it is difficult to improve the organizational efficiency of 1 million+ employees.
5. CNOOC (ROE 15%+, PE 7-8 times) in the same industry is obviously better
