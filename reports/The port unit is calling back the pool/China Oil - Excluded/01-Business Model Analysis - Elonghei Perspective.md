# PetroChina (0857.HK) business model analysis

## 1. The essence of business

PetroChina is a comprehensive energy company with upstream oil and gas exploration and development as its core business. Different from Sinopec's "refining + terminal" positioning, PetroChina's core competitiveness lies in "finding and extracting oil". This is a typical resource-based business - whoever owns high-quality oil and gas resources can make excess profits when oil prices are high.

From Duan Yongping's perspective, PetroChina's business logic is "depending on the weather" - performance is highly dependent on international oil prices, and the impact of management efforts on profits is far less than the impact of oil price fluctuations.

## 2. Moat assessment

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

### Existing moat:
1. **Resource Barriers**: The largest oil and gas fields in China (Daqing, Changqing, Tarim, etc.), these resources cannot be copied
2. **License Barrier**: Oil and gas exploration and development require national franchise rights
3. **Scale advantage**: Daily oil and gas production exceeds 5 million barrels, ranking among the top in the world
4. **Pipeline Network Assets**: Although the pipeline network has become independent (national pipeline network), PetroChina still has a large number of gas storage and other infrastructure

### Moat defects:
1. Oil and gas are commodities and there is no brand premium.
2. Some oil fields have entered a depletion period, requiring continued high capital expenditures to maintain production.
3. The quality of domestic oil and gas resources is average, and the cost of extraction is higher than that in the Middle East
4. The downstream refining and sales sectors are less competitive than Sinopec

## 3. Core business structure

| Sector | Revenue proportion | Profit contribution | Evaluation |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| Exploration and production | ~25% | ~60% | Core profit source, highly dependent on oil prices |
| Refining and chemicals | ~55% | ~15% | Large scale but thin profits |
| Sales | ~15% | ~10% | Gas station network weaker than Sinopec |
| Natural gas and pipelines | ~5% | ~15% | Future growth points |

Key Insight: PetroChina's profit quality is highly dependent on its exploration and production segment, which is entirely driven by oil prices.

## 4. User value and differentiation

PetroChina's "users" are mainly:
1. Industrial gas companies (natural gas) - have a certain degree of stickiness because the conversion cost after pipeline connection is high
2. Gas station owners - low brand loyalty, no different from Sinopec
3. Crude oil trading counterparties—price competition

From the perspective of "differentiated products" and "user mentality" that Duan Yongping pursues, PetroChina hardly has them. No consumer will say "I particularly like PetroChina's natural gas."

## 5. Whether management is "doing the right thing"

### Positive review:
1. The natural gas business continues to expand and conform to the trend of energy structure transformation.
2. Capital discipline has improved in recent years, and we no longer blindly pursue output growth.
3. Increase the dividend ratio to 54.7%, which is more friendly to shareholders

### Negative comments:
1. History of acquiring assets overseas at high prices (some of which have been impaired)
2. The refining and chemical sector has been inefficient for a long time but continues to invest.
3. The organization is huge (1 million+ employees) and management efficiency is limited

## 6. Is it a good business?

**Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

The core characteristics of upstream oil and gas exploration are:
- High capital intensity - continued need for significant investment in exploration and development
- Natural decline in production - the production of developed oil fields is declining year by year, and new projects are needed to supplement it.
- Profit depends entirely on oil prices - management has little control over profits
- Natural gas is the bright spot - China's natural gas penetration rate is still increasing, and demand certainty is strong

Duan Yongping's possible evaluation is: This is not a business where people can "sleep well at night" because you never know whether the oil price next year will be 50 US dollars or 90 US dollars.

## 7. Core Conclusions

The investment value of PetroChina is essentially an "oil price option" - buying PetroChina means being bullish on oil prices. Without a clear judgment on oil prices, just from the perspective of business quality, it is not a "good business", but the dividend rate of about 5% and the relatively undervalued PE (10-11 times) provide certain downside protection. Growth in the natural gas business is the only visible structural positive.
