# PetroChina (0857.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 2,864.5 billion yuan | 2,938 billion yuan | -2.5% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 157.3 billion yuan | 164.7 billion yuan | -4.5% |
| ROE | ~5% | ~5.5% | Continuously low |
| Net profit margin | ~5.5% | ~5.6% | Basically stable |

**Profitability Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**

ROE of about 5% means that shareholders can only get a return of 5 yuan for every 100 yuan invested, which is lower than the average level of most industries. This is mainly because:
1. Although upstream assets are profitable, the book value of old oil fields measured using the cost method is high.
2. The refining and chemical sector has huge assets but meager profits.
3. The overall asset turnover rate is low

### Cash Flow Analysis
As an upstream company, PetroChina's operating cash flow is usually better than net profit (depreciation and amortization are large). This is a positive factor, indicating that actual cash generation ability is stronger than book profits.

### Balance Sheet
- Moderate asset-liability ratio (about 45%)
- The scale of interest-bearing liabilities is controllable
- Book net assets exceed 3 trillion yuan, PB is about 0.5 times (Hong Kong stocks), and there is a book discount

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation
- PE (TTM): 10-11 times
- PB: about 0.5 times
- Dividend yield: about 5%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 4-5 times

### Key issues in valuation

**Why is PB only 0.5 times? **
1. The market believes that the actual value of some assets (especially aging oil fields) is lower than the book value
2. Valuation discount of central enterprises (lack of governance premium)
3. ROE continues to be lower than the cost of capital (~8%), which should theoretically break the net

**Is PE 10-11 times cheaper? **
Compare to global peers:
- ExxonMobil: PE 12-14 times, ROE 15-20%
- Saudi Aramco: PE 14-16 times, ROE 25%+
- CNOOC: PE 7-8 times, ROE 15%+

PetroChina's PE is lower than that of international comprehensive oil companies, but its ROE is even lower. From the perspective of "ROE/PE" cost performance, CNOOC > Exxon > PetroChina.

### Normalized earnings valuation
Assuming that the long-term center of Brent crude oil is 65-75 US dollars / barrel:
- Normalized annual profit of approximately 140-160 billion yuan
- 10 times PE corresponds to a market value of 1.4 trillion-1.6 trillion yuan
- The current market value is approximately 1.5 trillion yuan (total A+H), which is basically reasonable

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**Partially OK. ** Energy demand will remain and natural gas demand growth is certain. But oil prices are unpredictable, which is the biggest uncertainty.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Resource monopoly is an advantage. ** However, the resource advantages will weaken as the oil fields are depleted, requiring continued investment in exploration.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
** Neutral to positive. ** Improved capital discipline and increased dividends in recent years are good signs. But there is a history of poor overseas acquisitions at high prices.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**Limited margin of safety. ** PB 0.5 times provides an asset base, and PE 10-11 times is a reasonable level for cyclical stocks. But an ROE of 5% means that even if the valuation remains unchanged, organic growth will be extremely slow.

## 4. Capital allocation efficiency

PetroChina’s annual capital expenditure is approximately 250-300 billion yuan, which is mainly used for:
1. Maintain production in old oil fields
2. Exploration and development of new blocks
3. Natural gas production capacity construction
4. Refining and chemical plant maintenance

A dividend payout ratio of 54.7% means retained profits of approximately 86 billion yuan per year. The question is: Does the return on reinvestment of this 86 billion yuan exceed the cost of capital? Based on an ROE of 5%, the answer is no. In theory, the dividend ratio should be further increased.

Selection logic: Choose oil if you are bullish on oil prices, choose petrochemicals if you value stability, and choose CNOOC if you pursue optimal cost performance.

## 6. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

PetroChina is not an ideal target under Buffett's framework: ROE is too low, earnings are volatile, and capital intensive. But 10-11 times PE and 5% dividend rate provide certain "price protection". It is essentially a combination of oil price call options and 5% fixed income - if the oil price rises, there is profit elasticity; if the oil price is stable, it can obtain a 5% annual return through dividends. There is no exciting long-term growth logic.
