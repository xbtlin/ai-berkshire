# PetroChina (0857.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: China National Petroleum Corporation
- **Stock Code**: 0857.HK
- **Industry**: Oil and gas exploration/refining
- **PE(TTM)**: 10-11 times
- **ROE**：~5%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Operating income | 2,864.5 billion yuan | -2.5% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 157.3 billion yuan | -4.5% |
| Final dividend | 0.25 yuan per share | Dividend payout ratio 54.7% |

## Investment Highlights
1. China’s largest oil and gas producer with strong upstream resource endowment
2. The rapid growth of natural gas business is in line with the direction of energy transformation.
3. Valuation is relatively cheap (PE 10-11 times)
4. Stable dividends, with a dividend rate of about 5%

## Core Risks
1. Falling oil prices affect upstream profits
2. The refining and chemical sector’s profitability is weak
3. ROE is low (~5%) and capital efficiency is not high
4. Overseas assets face geopolitical risks
