# CITIC Limited (0267.HK) Business Model Analysis

## 1. The essence of business

CITIC Limited is one of the largest comprehensive groups in China, with its business spanning five major sectors: comprehensive finance, advanced intelligent manufacturing, advanced materials, new consumption, and new urbanization. It is more like a "Chinese version of Berkshire" - creating value through a combination of finance and industry.

Core profit sources: CITIC Bank (accounting for about 60% of profits), CITIC Securities (about 15%), and the financial sector contributes a total of about 75% of profits.

From Duan Yongping's perspective: CITIC Limited faces a similar "consolidated group" problem as CK Hutchison - its business is too fragmented and there are more professional competitors in each field. But CITIC has a key difference: ** Its core financial business (CITIC Bank + CITIC Securities) itself is an industry leader. **

## 2. Moat assessment

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

### Existing moat:
1. **Financial License**: Controls both large commercial banks (CITIC Bank) and leading securities firms (CITIC Securities + CITIC Construction Investment), and the license combination is scarce
2. **Brand Reputation**: 40 years of central enterprise brand, the word "CITIC" plays an important role in China's financial industry
3. **Policy Resources**: As a central enterprise, it has advantages in obtaining policy resources and projects.
4. **Synergy**: Fully licensed financial closed loop of banking-securities-insurance-trust

### Moat defects:
1. The banking industry itself is a homogeneous competition (interest rate control + consistent regulatory requirements)
2. The industrial sector lacks unique advantages
3. Consolidated Group Valuation Discount
4. No consumer brand premium

## 3. Business structure analysis

| Sector | Revenue proportion | Profit contribution | Evaluation |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| Comprehensive Finance | ~35% | ~75% | Core Profit Source |
| Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing | ~20% | ~10% | Manufacturing profits are thin |
| Advanced Materials | ~15% | ~5% | Resource Cyclicity |
| New consumption | ~15% | ~5% | Fierce competition |
| New urbanization | ~15% | ~5% | Real estate-related pressure |

Key insight: CITIC is essentially a "financial company + grocery store" - finance contributes most of the profits, and other businesses are more like "incidental".

## 4. The rationality of ROE 8-9%

Among the 13 companies, CITIC’s ROE is at an upper-middle level (8-9%), which is mainly due to:
- CITIC Bank ROE is about 9-10%
- CITIC Securities ROE is about 10.6%
- The leverage effect of the financial sector increases overall ROE

But it was also dragged down by:
- Industrial sector ROE is low
- Some loss-making or inefficient businesses erode overall returns

## 5. Whether management is "doing the right thing"

### Front:
1. The "Five Five Three" strategic framework is clear (five major sectors + five major positioning + three steps)
2. Reform of central enterprises promotes efficiency improvement
3. CITIC Securities acquires CITIC Construction Investment to promote financial integration
4. Effective cost control

### Negative:
1. Profit growth is extremely slow (+0.9%) and lacks a growth engine
2. The integration of industrial sectors has not progressed significantly.
3. Should some non-core businesses be divested?

## 6. Core Conclusions

CITIC Limited is a "wrongfully killed comprehensive financial + industrial group" - a combination of PE 5-6 times and ROE 8-9% is attractive under the value investment framework (PE/ROE ratio is only 0.6). It does not have an extremely low ROE (2-3%) like CK Hutchison, but it has a reasonable return on capital. But conglomerate discounts and stagnant profit growth are core obstacles. If it can focus on its financial business and divest itself of inefficient industries, the valuation is expected to be revalued.
