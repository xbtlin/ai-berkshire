## One sentence conclusion

CITIC Holdings is the "most cost-effective" in-depth value target among the 13 companies - the combination of PE 5-6 times + ROE 8-9% + dividend rate 5-6% is extremely rare. The comprehensive discount of 40% is both a risk (which may continue) and an opportunity (a catalyst for the reform of central enterprises).

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. PE/ROE ratio is only 0.6 – extremely cheap among the world’s largest financial groups
2. The scarcity value of fully licensed financial holdings (banks + securities dealers + trusts + insurance)
3. Central SOE reform + market value management = clear policy catalyst
4. 5-6% dividend rate provides waiting cost compensation
5. PB 0.4 times, comprehensive discount 40%, huge room for value release

### Bearish logic
1. Profit growth rate is only 0.9%, lacking growth momentum
2. The narrowing of banks’ net interest margins is a structural pressure
3. Consolidated group discounts may exist for a long time
4. The industrial sector drags down overall ROE
5. Governance of central enterprises limits the speed of value release

## Buying conditions

1. **The current PE of 5-6 times is already worth buying** (provided that comprehensive discounts and low growth are accepted)
2. **Substantial progress in the reform of central enterprises** (such as divesting industries/increasing dividends) is a signal to increase positions
3. **Bank net interest margin stabilization** is a signal of improving fundamentals

## Position suggestions

- **Current price**: 3-5% position can be opened (deep value + high dividend allocation)
- **Ideal conditions for adding positions**: The catalyst for the reform of central enterprises is clear
- **Position limit**: 8% (need to wait for confirmation of fundamental improvement)

## Keep track of key points
