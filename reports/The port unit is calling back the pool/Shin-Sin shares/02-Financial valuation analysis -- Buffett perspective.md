**Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Although ROE 8-9% is not outstanding, it is a reasonable level among low PE companies. The problem is that profit growth has almost stagnated (+0.9%), indicating that the existing business structure has no growth momentum.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation
- PE: 5-6 times
- PB: about 0.4-0.5 times
- Dividend yield: about 5-6%

### PE 5-6 times cost performance
Compared with ROE of 8-9%, the **PE/ROE ratio is only 0.6** - which is a "cheap" range in value investing (usually 1.0 is reasonable, and less than 0.8 is cheap).

The comprehensive discount is about 40%, which is lower than CKH Holdings' 50% but still significant.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### Benchmarking Buffett’s Investment Banking Stocks
Buffett has invested heavily in Bank of America and Wells Fargo. His logic for investing in banking stocks:
1. Low valuation (PE < 10 times)
2. Reasonable ROE (10%+)
3. Stable dividends
4. Management is honest and prudent

CITIC Limited basically meets the standards under these conditions (lower PE, close ROE, and dividends), but there are additional discount factors of a "consolidated group".

### Safety Margin Assessment
- PB 0.4-0.5 times provides asset bottom
- 5-6% dividend yield provides immediate returns
- Financial license portfolio has scarcity value
- Central SOE reform may become a valuation catalyst

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

CITIC Holdings is one of the "most cost-effective" targets among the 13 companies** - the combination of PE 5-6 times + ROE 8-9% + dividend rate 5-6% is very attractive under the value investment framework. It does not have a low ROE like CK Hutchison, nor does it have policy uncertainty like Sands.

The core risk is: Will the conglomerate discount persist in the long term? Can profit growth resume? If the reform of central enterprises and market value management promote the return of valuation (PB returns from 0.4 to 0.7), the upside potential will be about 75%.
