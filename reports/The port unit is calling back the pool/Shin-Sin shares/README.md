# CITIC Limited (0267.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: CITIC Corporation of China Limited
- **Stock Code**: 0267.HK
- **Industry**: Comprehensive Group (Finance + Industrial)
- **PE(TTM)**: 5-6 times
- **ROE**：8-9%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Revenue | 769.3 billion yuan | +3.0% |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 58.7 billion yuan | +0.9% |
| Final dividend | RMB 0.385 per share | - |

## Investment Highlights
1. PE is only 5-6 times—tied with Cheung Kong for the lowest valuation
2. ROE 8-9% - relatively good among low PE companies
3. CITIC Bank + CITIC Securities + CITIC Construction Investment = Strong Comprehensive Finance
4. Policy dividends (central enterprise reform + market value management)

## Core Risks
1. The comprehensive group suffers serious discounts
2. The net interest margin of the banking industry continues to narrow.
3. The business is too fragmented (finance + real estate + manufacturing + resources, etc.)
4. Profit growth is extremely slow (+0.9%)
