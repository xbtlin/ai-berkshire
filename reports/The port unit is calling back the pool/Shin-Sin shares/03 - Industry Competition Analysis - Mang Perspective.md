# CITIC Limited (0267.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. Competitive landscape of comprehensive finance

CITIC’s core competitiveness lies in the financial sector:

| Subsidiaries | Industry rankings | Core strengths |
|--------|----------|----------|
| CITIC Bank | Top 5 joint-stock banks | Strong corporate business, internationalization |
| CITIC Securities | No. 1 among securities firms | Leading in investment banking + brokerage + asset management |
| CITIC Construction Investment | Top 10 brokers | Strong investment banking capabilities |
| CITIC Trust | Leader in the trust industry | Large scale of asset management |

**Full-license financial capabilities** is CITIC's most unique competitive advantage - there are very few groups in China that control large banks + leading securities firms at the same time.

## 2. Competitiveness of the industrial sector

The industrial sector is CITIC's "short board":
- Advanced intelligent manufacturing (CITIC Heavy Industries, etc.): fierce competition in the manufacturing industry and low profit margins
- Advanced materials (CITIC Special Steel, etc.): highly cyclical and affected by commodity prices
- New consumption (CITIC Publishing, Dah Chong Hong, etc.): Each sub-industry has stronger professional opponents
- Urbanization (CITIC Real Estate, etc.): Real estate market downturn

Munger's judgment: The industrial sector is a "drag" rather than a "help". If the industrial sector is separated, the ROE of the financial sector may increase to 10-12%.

## 3. Catalyst for the reform of central enterprises

The "market value management of central enterprises" policy is a potential benefit for CITIC:
1. The State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission requires central enterprises to improve their awareness of ROE and market value management.
2. CITIC Securities’ acquisition of CITIC Construction Investment—accelerating the integration of the financial sector
3. May promote divestiture of non-core assets
4. Increase dividend ratio

If the reform of central enterprises is implemented, CITIC will have the greatest room for revaluation - because it has the lowest PE, the most assets, and the deepest discount.

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

CITIC has strong competitiveness in the financial field (full license combination of CITIC Bank + Securities + Trust), but the industrial sector has dragged down the overall performance. If it can focus on finance and divest itself of inefficient industries, it will become a high-quality financial holding company with an ROE of 10%+ and a PE of 5-6 times - a very attractive valuation level globally.

The catalyst is: Can the reform of central enterprises promote substantial structural optimization?
