## 2. In-depth analysis of financial quality

### 2.1 Revenue quality: high visibility, but structure deserves attention

**Positives**:
- Orders on hand are US$23.7 billion (approximately 170 billion yuan), nearly 8 times annual revenue
- Visible orders of US$4.5 billion within 3 years, providing strong short-term certainty
- 209 new comprehensive projects will be added in 2025 (a record high), and the funnel continues to expand
- Production service revenue increased by 26.4%, and back-end monetization accelerated

**Risk Factors**:
- Of the $23.7 billion in orders, $12.2 billion are "potential milestone payments" - which are dependent on the clinical success rate of the customer's drug and are not guaranteed revenue
- The geographical composition of orders on hand is not fully disclosed. If the proportion of US customers is too high, the impact of the BIOSECURE Act may be underestimated.
- COVID-19-related income has basically been cleared, and the company's deliberately excluded "continuing operations" growth rate of 20%+ is more referenceable

### 2.2 Profitability: The improvement trend is clear, but the absolute level still has room for improvement

**Drivers for gross profit margin improvement**:
1. Increase in production capacity utilization (new factories turn from losses to profits)
2. Increase in the proportion of revenue from high-profit production services
3. Improve operational efficiency (digital WBS system)
4. The proportion of complex biopharmaceutical projects increases (higher pricing)

**But ROE is still low**: ROE of about 10.5% is not outstanding among high-quality companies. The reason is:
- Heavy asset model, low total asset turnover rate
- Capital precipitation during the construction period of large production capacity
- Conservative equity multiplier (low leverage)

**ROE Dismantling (DuPont Analysis Framework)**:

| Factor | Estimate | Evaluation |
|------|--------|------|
| Net profit margin | ~25-30% | Good |
| Asset turnover rate | ~0.35-0.40 | Low (asset-heavy characteristics) |
| Equity Multiplier | ~1.1 | Conservative |
| **ROE** | **~10.5%** | **Below Excellent Standard** |

If capacity utilization continues to increase, asset turnover is expected to improve, and ROE has room for upside. Management's guidance for 34 PPQ projects in 2026 indicates that capacity utilization will continue to increase.

### 2.3 Free cash flow: inherent shortcomings of the asset-heavy model

This is WuXi Biologics' biggest financial weakness.

Capital expenditures in 2024 will be approximately 4.8 billion yuan, and free cash flow will be only approximately 1.3 billion yuan. This means that for every 100 yuan of profit earned, only about 24 yuan is actually turned into freely disposable cash. The rest is "left in cement and steel" - building bioreactors and expanding production capacity.

**Comparison with asset-light model**:

| Indicators | WuXi Biologics | Typical asset-light technology company |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| Free Cash Flow/Net Profit | ~24% | 80-100% |
| CapEx/Revenue | ~26% | 5-10% |
| Actual cash distributable to shareholders | Limited | Abundant |

**Positive side**: As the main production capacity construction cycle is coming to an end (global layout is basically completed), the growth rate of capital expenditure is expected to slow down in the future, and the logic of improvement in free cash flow is established. But this will take time to prove.

## 3. Valuation analysis

### 3.1 Current Valuation Level

Estimate based on the stock price of approximately HK$40 in May 2026:

| Valuation Metrics | Data | Evaluation |
|----------|------|------|
| TTM PE | ~29x | Lower than historical center |
| Forward PE (2026E) | ~23 times | If you can achieve 20% growth, it is not expensive |
| EV/EBITDA | ~17x | CDMO industry medium to low |
| EV/FCF | ~68x | Extremely high, reflecting weak free cash flow |
| PB | ~3 times | Corresponds to 10.5% ROE, which is reasonable |

### 3.2 Historical valuation comparison

WuXi Biologics’ historical valuation range fluctuates greatly:

- High point in 2021: PE exceeds 100 times, market value exceeds HK$500 billion
- Low point in 2024: PE about 15-20 times, stock price as low as HK$10.14
- Current position: PE is about 29 times, at a mid-to-low historical level

**Based on historical valuations, the current price is not expensive**. However, it should be noted that the 100 times PE in 2021 is itself a bubble and cannot be used as a reference benchmark.

### 3.3 Comparison of peer valuations

| Company | PE (TTM) | Gross profit margin | Market capitalization |
|------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Lonza | ~35x | ~40% | ~CHF65 billion |
| Samsung Biologics | ~50x | ~45% | ~50 trillion won |
| WuXi Biologics | ~29x | ~46% | ~HK$170 billion |

**Wuxi Biologics has the lowest valuation among its peers**. This partly reflects geopolitical discounts - concerns about the impact of the BIOSECURE Act have suppressed valuations.

### 3.4 Intrinsic value estimation (DCF simplified framework)

Key assumptions:

| Assumptions | Values | Basis |
|------|------|------|
| Revenue CAGR from 2026 to 2030 | 15% | Supported by orders on hand + industry growth |
| Steady-state net profit margin | 25% | Scale effect + product structure optimization |
| Steady-state free cash flow/net profit | 50% | Slowing capex cycle assumption |
| Discount rate | 10% | Account for geopolitical risk premium |
| Sustainable growth rate | 3% | Long-term growth of the biopharmaceutical industry |

Under the above assumptions, a rough estimate of the intrinsic value range is **40-55 HKD**. The current stock price is about HK$40, which is at the lower end of the valuation range.

**But DCF is extremely sensitive to assumptions**: If the BIOSECURE Act leads to a loss of 10-20% of US customers, the revenue growth assumption needs to be lowered, and the intrinsic value may drop to the HK$30-40 range.

## 4. Shareholder Return Analysis

### 4.1 Dividends and buybacks

- The company approves a buyback plan of up to US$600 million in 2023
- Buyback intensity will increase in 2025
- But the dividend policy is not stable and generous enough, and free cash flow limits the dividend space

### 4.2 Equity dilution risk

- Employee equity incentives continue (H share purchase of HKD 2.5 billion in 2025 will be used for incentives)
- Need to pay attention to the degree of dilution of shareholders' equity by equity incentives every year

### 4.3 Founders reduce holdings

This is a serious negative sign:

- Li Ge and related parties’ shareholding dropped from 73.5% at the time of listing to approximately 12%
- Cash out of approximately HK$6.8 billion in the first half of 2025
- The company explained that it was repaying early privatization debt, but the market interpreted it as lack of confidence

**In the framework of financial analysis, a significant reduction in holdings by founders means that the people who know the company best are continuing to sell**. Even if there is a reasonable explanation, this at least shows that insiders do not judge that the current valuation is "too cheap to sell".

---

## 5. Key financial risks

1. **Free Cash Flow Trap**: If the utilization rate of new capacity is less than expected, large capital expenditures become sunk costs
2. **Order Cancellation Risk**: Milestone payments in the US$23.7 billion order depend on the clinical success of the customer’s drug, and the clinical success rate of biologic drugs is only about 15-20%.
3. **Exchange rate risk**: Revenue is mainly denominated in US dollars, and costs are mainly denominated in RMB. RMB appreciation will compress profit margins.
4. **Customer concentration**: If the top few customers transfer orders due to policy reasons, the impact will be very concentrated.

One dollar of profit is not worth as much as 29 dollars in an uncertain environment.
