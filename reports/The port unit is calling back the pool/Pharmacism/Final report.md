## 2. Core investment thesis

### Bull Case

**1. The global biopharmaceutical CDMO industry is one of the few long-term deterministic tracks**

The trend of biological drugs replacing small molecule chemical drugs is irreversible. CDMO outsourcing rate continues to increase. Industry CAGR 10%+. This isn’t an industry trend that needs to be predicted—it’s already happening, and it’s here to stay.

**2. Backlog of $23.7 billion provides strong revenue visibility**

There are 945 projects in hand, including 74 Phase III and 25 commercial projects. 34 PPQs are scheduled for 2026. This is a "loaded cannon" - revenue growth in the next 3-5 years is supported by tangible projects.

**3. The performance turning point has been confirmed**

In 2025, revenue will grow by 16.7%, gross profit margin will jump to 46%, and adjusted net profit will increase by 22%. The COVID drag has cleared. Complex biological drugs (bisantibodies, ADCs) accounted for more than 50%, driving up both unit price per customer and profit margin.

**4. Valuation is at historical and industry lows**

TTM PE is about 29 times, which is much lower than Samsung Biologics (~50 times) and Lonza (~35 times). If geopolitical risks ease, the narrowing of the valuation gap alone could contribute 20-30% to returns.

**5. Technical barriers to complex biopharmaceuticals increase customer stickiness**

There are 196 projects for bisAbs and 252 projects for ADCs - the process transfer of these complex biopharmaceuticals is more difficult than that of traditional monoclonal antibodies, and customers are even less willing to take the risk of switching to CDMOs. This hedges against the impact of the BIOSECURE Act to a certain extent.

### Bear Case

**1. Geopolitical risks are systemic and uncontrollable**

The BIOSECURE Act is just the beginning. The general direction of the technological game between China and the United States is decoupling rather than coupling. No matter how good a company is, it cannot change the political environment. This risk cannot be hedged, diversified, or predicted.

**2. Samsung Biologics is executing a clear "replacement of WuXi" strategy**

With a production capacity of 845,000 liters (WuXi 580,000 liters), US$4.1 billion in new orders, 45% operating profit margin, and zero geographical risk—Samsung is catching up or surpassing it in every dimension. This is not a "possible" threat, but a strategy in action.

**3. The founder’s continued substantial reduction in holdings is the strongest negative signal**

From 73.5% to 12%. In the first half of 2025, HK$6.8 billion will be cashed out. The people who know the company best are continuing to sell. No matter how reasonable the public explanation may be, this fact alone sends a clear signal.

**4. ROE 10.5% indicates mediocre return on capital**

The asset-heavy model determines that it is difficult for WuXi Biologics to become a high ROE company. A large amount of profits were invested in production capacity construction, and free cash flow accounted for only 24% of net profit. The real money received by shareholders is far less than the reported profits.

**5. Valuation discount may be reasonable rather than undervalued**

WuXi Biologics is cheaper than Samsung and Lonza, but it also faces far greater political risks than Samsung and Lonza. 28-29 times PE may not be "undervalued", but the market's reasonable pricing of political risks.

## 4. Investment Conclusion

### 4.1 Overall judgment

**WuXi Biologics is a good company, in a good industry, but faces systemic headwinds that transcend business. **

Its business model is clear, its customers are sticky, and its performance is accelerating—these are real. But the BIOSECURE Act, the game between China and the United States, and founders’ reduction of holdings—these risks are also real, and they are more difficult to quantify and predict.

The current valuation of 29 times PE is neither obviously undervalued (taking into account risk) nor overvalued (taking into account growth). It's closer to a "reasonably low" price - assuming geopolitical risks remain at current levels and don't worsen.

### 4.2 What kind of investors is it suitable for?

**Fits**:
- Has its own framework for judging the direction of Sino-US relations, and is optimistic
- Able to withstand short-term fluctuations of 30-50%
- Investment cycle is more than 3-5 years
- Positions are controlled within 5-10% of the portfolio

**Not suitable**:
- Investors seeking certainty and stable returns
- Unable to withstand severe fluctuations caused by geopolitical events
- Expect clear returns within 1-2 years
- Investors who prefer "simple, predictable and understandable" investment targets
