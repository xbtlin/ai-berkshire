## Quick overview of core investment logic

**Reasons to be long**:
1. The long-term growth certainty of the global biopharmaceutical CDMO industry is high (CAGR 10%+)
2. The "Follow and Win Molecules" strategy continues to be validated, with US$23.7 billion in orders on hand providing strong revenue visibility
3. Complex biological drugs (dual antibodies, ADCs) account for more than half, and technical barriers continue to increase
4. The performance inflection point in 2025 is confirmed, revenue growth returns to double digits, and gross profit margin improves significantly.

**Reasons for shorting**:
1. The U.S. Biosafety Act (BIOSECURE Act) will be signed in December 2025, restricting cooperation between the U.S. government and Chinese CDMOs
2. Founder Li Ge continued to reduce his holdings in large amounts (shareholding dropped from 73.5% to approximately 12%), which damaged market confidence.
3. Samsung Bio’s aggressive expansion of production capacity (845,000 liters vs. WuXi’s 580,000 liters) worsens the competitive landscape
4. ROE is only 10.5%, return on capital is low, and inherent challenges of the asset-heavy model
