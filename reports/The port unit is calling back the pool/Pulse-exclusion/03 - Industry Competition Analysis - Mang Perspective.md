# BeiGene (6160.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. Industry Panorama: Competitive Landscape of BTK Inhibitor Market

### 1.1 Market size and growth

BTK (Bruton's tyrosine kinase) inhibitors are a core class of drugs for the treatment of B-cell malignancies. The global market size will be approximately US$10-11 billion in 2025, is expected to reach US$15-18 billion in 2030, and may reach US$29 billion in 2034.

Core factors driving growth:
- The number of CLL (chronic lymphocytic leukemia) patients is large and continues to grow
- Expansion of indications for BTK inhibitors from late-line to first-line treatment
- Combination therapy (BTK+BCL-2, etc.) expands usage scenarios
- Increased penetration in emerging markets

### 1.2 Three pillars: main competitors

| Products | Company | Global Sales in 2025 ($100 million) | Market Position |
|------|------|------------------------|----------|
| Imbruvica | AbbVie/Johnson & Johnson | ~35 (continued decline) | First mover, losing share |
| Zanubrutinib (Brukinsa) | BeiGene | ~39 | The fastest growing rate, surpassing acalabrutinib |
| Acalabrutinib (Calquence) | AstraZeneca | ~35 | Steady growth, new patient share leader |

**Important changes**: 2025 is the year when the competitive landscape will undergo qualitative changes. With global sales of approximately US$3.9 billion, zanubrutinib surpassed acalabrutinib (approximately US$3.5 billion) for the first time, leading in sales for five consecutive quarters. Sales of ibrutinib continue to shrink due to safety disadvantages and the upcoming patent cliff (the core patents in the United States and the European Union will expire in 2026).

### 1.3 Competitiveness comparison

| Dimension | ibrutinib | zanubrutinib | acalabrutinib |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Selectivity | Low (many off-target effects) | High | High |
| Cardiac safety | Poor (high risk of atrial fibrillation) | Better | Better |
| Head-to-head data | Lost to zanubrutinib in ALPINE | Defeated ibrutinib | No head-to-head data vs zanubrutinib |
| Breadth of indications | Widest (first mover advantage) | Rapidly expanding | Medium |
| Price | Highest | Medium | Medium |
| Patent Expiration | 2026 (US/EU) | Mid-2030s | Early 2030s |

## 2. Source of competitive advantage: Why does BeiGene?

### 2.1 Clinical data advantages ★★★★☆

Zanubrutinib is the only drug to beat a first-generation BTK inhibitor in a head-to-head phase 3 clinical trial. The results of the ALPINE trial have been written into multiple international clinical guidelines (NCCN, etc.), which is the most convincing competitive weapon.

However, it needs to be pointed out objectively: there is no direct head-to-head data between zanubrutinib and acalabrutinib. Both have their own advantages and disadvantages in terms of security, and they also have their own characteristics in terms of selectivity. Some physicians still prefer acalabrutinib because of its oral convenience (once-daily vs. twice-daily zanubrutinib). Zanubrutinib was also recently approved for once-daily dosing, which could help close this gap.

### 2.2 Global commercial execution ★★★★★

This is BeiGene’s most impressive capability. As a biotechnology company originating from China, it is an extremely rare achievement to be able to build its own team, promote independently, and continuously increase its share in the United States, the world's largest oncology drug market.

Specific performance:
- Sales team of 500+ people in the US market
- U.S. sales of zanubrutinib were 20.2 billion yuan (approximately US$2.8 billion), +45.5% year-on-year
- The growth rate of new patient prescriptions continues to lead
- Self-built teams in major European markets, sales increased by 66.4% year-on-year

Behind the execution is heavy investment - sales and administrative expenses of US$2.081 billion. But this input-output ratio is positive: for every US$1 invested in sales expenses, approximately US$2.5 is generated in revenue.

### 2.3 Combination therapy layout ★★★★☆

The future of BTK inhibitors does not lie in single-drug competition, but in combination therapy. BeiGene has a first-mover advantage in this regard:

- **Zarubrutinib + Sotoclax (BCL-2)**: This combination is expected to become the new standard regimen for first-line treatment of CLL. CELESTIAL-TNCLL global phase III trial has completed enrollment. If the data is positive, it will directly benchmark AbbVie's Venclexta + ibrutinib/acalabrutinib combination.
- BeiGene is the only company that has both self-developed BTK inhibitors and BCL-2 inhibitors. It does not need to rely on external partners and has complete autonomy in the development and commercialization of combination therapies.

This strategic layout is well thought out, and if executed successfully, it will build deeper competitive barriers than a single drug.

## 3. Dynamic evolution of industry competition

### 3.1 Recent threats (1-3 years)

**Ibrutinib patent cliff**: The core patent of ibrutinib will expire in the United States and the European Union in 2026, and the price of generic drugs will drop significantly. This has a two-fold impact on zanubrutinib:
- Positive: Accelerates transition of ibrutinib patients to next-generation BTK inhibitors
- Negative: Cheaper generics may compete with branded products in price-sensitive markets

**Acalabrutinib Strikes Back**: AstraZeneca has a stronger global commercialization infrastructure and a longer oncology brand history. Acalabrutinib is still competitive in terms of new patient market share, especially in terms of certain doctor habits and hospital access.

### 3.2 Mid-term threats (3-5 years)

**Non-covalent BTK inhibitors**: Lilly/Loxo’s pirtobrutinib represents a new generation of BTK inhibitors. It is effective in patients who are resistant to covalent BTK inhibitors, including zanubrutinib. Although it is currently mainly used for back-line treatment, if it shows advantages in first-line head-to-head trials, it will directly threaten zanubrutinib.

**The cannibalization of CAR-T therapy**: In some indications (such as MCL, DLBCL, etc.), CAR-T therapy is showing deeper relief and potential cure. Although the application of CAR-T in CLL is limited, technological advances may change this situation.

**Bispecific antibodies**: Bispecific antibodies such as Roche's glofitamab and AbbVie's epcoritamab have shown strong efficacy in B-cell lymphoma and may compete with BTK inhibitors for the market for certain indications.

### 3.3 Long-term pattern (5 years+)

The BTK inhibitor market may evolve from a "battle for single drugs" to a "battle for protocols." The competition in the future is no longer which BTK inhibitor is better, but which company can provide the best combination treatment plan. In this dimension, BeiGene (BTK+BCL-2 own combination) and AbbVie (Venclexta ecosystem) are the two most competitive players.

## 4. A broader competitive perspective: BeiGene’s position in global Pharma

### 4.1 The leap from Biotech to Pharma

BeiGene is at a critical stage of transformation from "large Biotech" to "mid-sized Pharma". With an annual revenue of US$5.3 billion, it ranks around 40-50 among global pharmaceutical companies, far from the first echelon (the top 20 Pharmas have annual revenues of over US$20 billion).

To truly become a global pharmaceutical company, BeiGene needs to:
- At least 2-3 products with annual sales exceeding US$1 billion
- Successful breakthroughs in the field of solid tumors (currently mainly relying on blood tumors)
- Stronger BD (Business Development) capabilities to supplement the pipeline through introduction or acquisition

### 4.2 Benchmarks and limitations of China’s innovative drugs overseas

BeiGene is one of the most successful cases of globalization of innovative drugs in China. But its success has special characteristics:
- The founder, John Oyler, is an American, and the company has had global genes from the beginning.
- Foreigners occupy a core position in the management team
- Listing on NASDAQ in the United States provides funding and brand endorsement
- These advantages are not easily copied by other Chinese pharmaceutical companies

### 4.3 Competition in the Chinese market

In the Chinese market, BeiGene faces a different competitive landscape:
- Medical insurance negotiations lead to continued pressure on drug prices
- Local leaders such as Hengrui Medicine have more advantages in the field of solid tumors
- The sales growth rate of zanubrutinib in China (33.1%) is lower than overseas (48.8%), reflecting the ceiling effect of the domestic market

**Overall Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.8/5)**

BeiGene is in a favorable position in the competition in the BTK inhibitor market - leading clinical data, excellent commercialization execution, and far-sighted combination therapy layout. However, the final outcome of the competition has not yet been determined: the patent cliff of ibrutinib, the rise of non-covalent BTK inhibitors, and the impact of new technological pathways such as CAR-T/bi-antibodies may rewrite the current competitive landscape. If BeiGene's biggest strategic bet - the combined plan of zanubrutinib + sotocla - succeeds, it will significantly strengthen its competitive barriers in the next five years; if it fails, the company may face the dilemma of insufficient growth momentum. This is a competition in which the probability of "winner takes all" is low, but the probability of "the strong will always be strong" is high.
