## Core Business

BeiGene is a global biotechnology company focused on the research, development, production and commercialization of innovative oncology drugs. The core product zanubrutinib (BRUKINSA) has become the global BTK inhibitor market leader, with global sales of approximately US$3.9 billion in 2025. The company also owns a number of self-developed products such as tislelizumab (Belizumab), and acts as an agent for products authorized by Amgen.

## Research background

BeiGene is a benchmark enterprise in the globalization of innovative drugs in China. Achieving full-year profitability for the first time in 2025 marks a historic shift from "burning money on R&D" to "commercially making money". Zanubrutinib has surpassed acalabrutinib to become the second largest BTK inhibitor in the world, and continues to catch up with ibrutinib in terms of new patient market share. The company is listed on three places (Hong Kong Stock Exchange, Science and Technology Innovation Board, and Nasdaq), has more than 10,000 employees worldwide, and its pipeline covers 30 clinical-stage products and 96 clinical trials.

This study strives to be objective, does not presuppose any position, and fully discusses both pros and cons.
