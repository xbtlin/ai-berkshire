# 1.3 Management ' s Fear

** Overdependence on Founder**

The genes of globalization in the state of Chismago depend heavily on the abilities, connections, and reputation of Orejian individuals. If the core person is lost for any reason (health, retirement, policy change), corporate globalization strategies may face major challenges.

** Potential friction in the "Both Managing Directors" architecture**

While dual-headed management is theoretically complementary, in practice it may lead to less efficient decision-making or strategic divisions. In particular, in the context of the growing complexity of global operations, it is important to be clear who has the ultimate decision-making power over key decisions.

** Brain drain risk**

The core competitiveness of innovation companies is talent. The state of Chistom faces pressure from large multinational drug companies, especially in the US market.
