## 2. Investment thesis: Why can you buy it?

### Bull Market Narrative

1. **The winner in the global BTK inhibitor market**: With the advantage of ALPINE’s head-to-head data, zanubrutinib is taking over the throne of the world’s largest BTK inhibitor from ibrutinib. Sales of US$3.9 billion in 2025 are just the starting point. The global BTK market is expected to reach US$15-18 billion in 2030, and zanubrutinib is expected to capture 40-50% of the market share.

2. **The profit margin inflection point has been reached**: From consecutive years of losses to the first full-year profit in 2025, operating leverage is being released. The R&D expense rate has dropped from 125% to 40%. If it continues to drop to 25-30% (mature Pharma level), the operating profit margin can reach 20-25%, corresponding to annual profits of US$1.5-2.5 billion in 2028-2030.

3. **The second growth curve can be expected**: The first-line CLL regimen of sotoclax (BCL-2 inhibitor) combined with zanubrutinib has the potential to replicate the successful path of Venclexta (Venclexta’s annual sales are approximately US$2.5 billion). BeiGene is the only company that has both self-developed BTK and BCL-2 inhibitors.

4. **The irreplaceability of China's innovative drugs in globalization**: Against the background of "de-Sinicization" and "Chinese innovation overseas", BeiGene is the most globally competitive company among Chinese biotech companies. This scarcity commands a premium in certain market circumstances.

## 3. Investment thesis: Why can’t you buy it?

### Bear Market Narrative

1. **Single product dependence is a fatal flaw**: 73% of revenue comes from zanubrutinib. No pharmaceutical company in the world with a market value of over US$30 billion has such a high degree of dependence on a single product. This is not "concentration", but "fragility". Any negative news about zanubrutinib—safety incidents, competitive product breakthroughs, patent challenges—will have a disproportionate impact on the company.

2. **Growth slowdown is on the horizon**: Revenue guidance for 2026 is 16%-20%, which is significantly lower than the 40%+ growth rate in the past three years. The market is shifting from a "high growth narrative" to a "profit realization narrative", and the current valuation of 44 times PE still implies quite high growth expectations. Once the growth rate of zanubrutinib further slows down to single digits, the valuation may face a "Davis double kill".

3. **Geopolitics is an unhedgeable tail risk**: No matter how well the New Jersey factory is built, BeiGene’s Chinese identity is a risk factor in extreme scenarios. The United States accounts for 72% of zanubrutinib sales. If Sino-US relations turn to the worst scenario, the company's core revenue source will face an existential threat.

4. **There is no margin of safety in valuation**: The PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 44 times. Even if it drops to 30+ times after taking into account the profit growth in 2026, it is still at a premium compared with global mature Pharma (15-25 times PE). Investors buy based on the belief that growth can continue to absorb valuations - but this in itself is an assumption that needs to be continuously verified.

5. **SBC dilution continues to consume shareholder value**: The annual equity incentive of US$600-700 million is not an item that "is not important if it is not cash". Based on current market value, the annual dilution rate is about 2%, and the cumulative dilution in five years exceeds 10%.

## 5. Final conclusion

### What is BeiGene?

BeiGene is the most successful case in the globalization of China's innovative drugs and is at a historic turning point from "high growth and loss" to "profit and volume". It has a first-class management team, the world's leading BTK inhibitor products, and a potential second growth curve (BCL-2 inhibitors).

### What is BeiGene not?

BeiGene is not a low-risk "certainty" investment target. It is essentially still a "single product company" trying to transform into a "platform company", facing multiple uncertainties such as product concentration, geopolitics, and pipeline fulfillment. Current valuations already price in a considerable degree of optimism.

### Investment Action Suggestions

**Conclusion: High-quality companies need to wait for better prices or stronger catalysts. **

The current adjusted PE of Hong Kong stocks is about 44 times, which corresponds to a "fair but expensive" valuation level. For value investors, the ideal buying time may occur in the following scenarios:

1. **Price Retracement**: When the valuation of Hong Kong stocks returns to the 30-35 times PE range (corresponding to a retracement of about 20-30%), the margin of safety is more sufficient
2. **Pipeline Catalyst**: The positive reading of the first-line and third-phase data of Sotokela CLL will substantially reduce the risk of "single product dependence". Even if the valuation does not decline, it will be worthy of re-evaluation.
3. **Geopolitical Clarity**: If China and the United States reach some kind of consensus or exemption framework in the field of biomedicine, the largest tail risk will be eliminated

**Position suggestions**:
- If you already hold it, you can continue to hold it and track the above key variables
- If you do not hold it, it is recommended to add it to the observation pool and wait for a better time to buy.
- Position control: Considering the dependence on single products and geopolitical risks, even if you buy it, it is not suitable to be a core heavy position. It is recommended to control it within 3-5% of the portfolio.

### One sentence summary

BeiGene has proven that Chinese innovative pharmaceutical companies can win in the world's top competitions, but there is still a "reasonable price" gap between "can win the competition" and "worthy of investment".
