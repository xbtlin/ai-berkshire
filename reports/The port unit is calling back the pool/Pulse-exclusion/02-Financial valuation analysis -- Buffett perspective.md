# BeiGene (6160.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality and intrinsic value
> Core question: Is the current price reasonable? How much is this company worth?
> Study date: May 16, 2026

## 1. Financial quality physical examination

### 1.1 Income quality: Is high growth sustainable?

| Year | Revenue (100 million U.S. dollars) | Year-on-year growth | Proportion of zanubrutinib |
|------|---------------|----------|-------------|
| 2022 | ~18.1 | — | ~50% |
| 2023 | ~25.5 | +41% | ~58% |
| 2024 | ~38.1 | +49% | ~68% |
| 2025 | 53.43 | +40.2% | ~73% |
| 2026E | 62-64 | +16%-20% | ~75%+ |

**Positive**: Revenue growth of 40%+ for three consecutive years is extremely rare among large biotech companies. Zanubrutinib is still in the stage of increasing its share (the global BTK inhibitor market is about US$10 billion, and zanubrutinib accounts for about 35%-40%), and its growth is far from peaking.

**Cons**: Growth is slowing. The 2026 revenue guidance of 16%-20% growth has dropped significantly from the previous 40%+. More importantly, revenue concentration continues to increase - zanubrutinib accounts for more than 73%. Once the growth rate of this product slows down or encounters the impact of competing products, the overall revenue will be directly under pressure.

### 1.2 Profit quality: Are profits real?

Key profit indicators in 2025:

| Indicator | Value | Description |
|------|------|------|
| GAAP net income | $287 million | First full-year profit |
| Non-GAAP operating profit | — | Higher profit excluding SBC |
| Gross profit margin | ~80% | Typical level of innovative drugs |
| GAAP operating margin | ~5-6% | Still in the early stages of profitability |
| 2026 GAAP operating profit guidance | US$700-800 million | Operating profit margin improved to 11%-13% |

**Key Details – Impact of Equity Based Compensation (SBC)**:

High equity incentives are common among innovative pharmaceutical companies. The profit gap between BeiGene's GAAP and Non-GAAP mainly comes from SBC. Non-GAAP operating profit guidance is US$1.4-1.5 billion, while GAAP operating profit guidance is only US$700-800 million. The difference is approximately US$600-700 million due to non-cash expenses such as SBC. This means:
- Half of Non-GAAP profits come from "add-back" equity incentives
- Although SBC is a non-cash expense, it represents a real dilution to shareholders
- When assessing true profitability, more attention should be paid to GAAP profits and free cash flow

### 1.3 Cash flow quality: How is the hematopoietic ability?

| Indicators | 2025 |
|------|--------|
| Cash flow from operating activities | Turned positive for the first time, approximately 13.7 billion yuan |
| Free cash flow | $942 million |

The turning from negative operating cash flow is a milestone signal. Free cash flow of US$942 million was higher than GAAP net profit of US$287 million, indicating that the quality of profits was acceptable - because non-cash items such as depreciation, amortization and SBC contributed to the difference. However, it should be noted that a large part of R&D expenditures is expensed in the income statement. If treated as capitalized, the actual "investment expenditures" are much higher than the capital expenditures.

### 1.4 Balance Sheet Health

In its history, BeiGene has raised more than US$10 billion through multiple rounds of financing (IPO, additional issuance, convertible bonds). After years of "burning cash", the company's balance sheet is characterized by:
- A high proportion of intangible assets (drug rights, patents, etc.)
- Sufficient cash reserves and no short-term liquidity risk
- Interest-bearing liabilities are controllable
- But the accumulated losses are large and retained earnings are negative

Overall judgment: The balance sheet has shifted from the "blood transfusion model" to the "hematopoietic model", but there is still a gap between the financial thickness of large multinational pharmaceutical companies.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### 2.1 Relative valuation: Who to compare with? How to compare?

This is the core problem of BeiGene's valuation - it is in an embarrassing position of "misaligned benchmarking":

| Benchmark company | 2026E PE | 2026E PS | Market value (100 million US dollars) |
|----------|----------|----------|---------------|
| BeiGene (Hong Kong stocks) | ~44x (adjusted) | ~5.5x | ~340 |
| AbbVie | ~14x | ~4.5x | ~3,500 |
| AstraZeneca (AZN) | ~22x | ~6x | ~2,800 |
| Regeneron | ~18x | ~8x | ~1,100 |
| Hengrui Medicine | ~50x | ~15x | ~500 |

**Valuation Dilemma**:
- In domestic benchmarking, BeiGene is the "big brother in medicine", with the A-share market value exceeding 500 billion yuan (September 2025)
- In global benchmarks, BeiGene's PE is significantly higher than mature Pharma, but lower than some high-growth Biotech
- The valuation of Hong Kong stocks and US stocks is significantly lower than that of A shares - as of March 2026, the value of A shares is 360 billion yuan, Hong Kong stocks are 264 billion yuan, and US stocks are 223 billion yuan.
- The arbitrage space among the three places reflects the market’s differences in their identity positioning

### 2.2 Absolute valuation: DCF thinking framework

Do a simplified DCF thinking (non-exact calculation):

**Key Assumptions**:
- 2026 revenue of $6.3 billion, GAAP operating profit of $750 million
- Revenue compound growth rate in the next five years is 12%-15% (zanubrutinib growth rate slows down + contribution from new products)
- Operating profit margin will increase to 20%-25% after 5 years (scale effect + expense rate decrease)
- Terminal growth rate 3%, discount rate 10%

**Optimistic Scenario**: Sotokla is successfully launched + solid tumor pipeline breakthrough, revenue of 12 billion + US dollars and operating profit of 3 billion + US dollars in 2031. The corresponding valuation is approximately US$50-60 billion.

**Neutral Scenario**: Steady growth of zanubrutinib + incremental contribution from Sotokra, revenue in 2031 of US$9-10 billion and operating profit of US$2-2.5 billion. The corresponding valuation is approximately US$35-40 billion.

**Pessimistic scenario**: Zanubrutinib's growth rate slows down significantly + pipeline failure, revenue in 2031 is US$7-8 billion, and operating profit is US$1-1.5 billion. The corresponding valuation is approximately US$20-25 billion.

The current market value of the Hong Kong stock market is approximately US$34 billion, which roughly corresponds to a neutral to optimistic scenario.

### 2.3 Safety Margin Assessment

| Dimensions | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Is PE 44 times reasonable? | For companies that are making profits in the first year and experiencing rapid growth in profit margins, static PE has limited significance. We should pay more attention to the profit level after 2-3 years |
| Can growth absorb valuations? | If GAAP profits reach US$700-800 million in 2026, the corresponding PE will drop to approximately 45x; if profits reach US$1.5-2 billion in 2028, PE will drop below 20x |
| How big is the downside risk? | Hong Kong stocks already have a 30%+ discount compared to A shares, which provides a certain safety cushion; but if the growth rate of zanubrutinib is lower than expected, the valuation may be further compressed |

**Margin of Safety Judgment**: The current valuation of Hong Kong stocks does not have an obvious margin of safety, but it is not seriously overvalued either. Investors will need to remain confident in zanubrutinib's continued growth and pipeline delivery to support current prices.

## 3. Key risks at the financial level

### 3.1 Single product dependency

Zanubrutinib contributed 73%+ of revenue. If the growth rate of this product in the U.S. market drops to single digits from the current 45%, the company's overall growth rate will fall sharply even if other products maintain growth.

### 3.2 Equity incentive dilution

The $600-700 million per year SBC is the true cost to shareholders. Based on current market capitalization, the annualized dilution rate is approximately 2%. This means that shareholders need the company's market value to grow by at least 2% annually to avoid being diluted.

### 3.3 Uncertainty of R&D investment

Annual R&D expenses are US$2.146 billion, accounting for 40% of revenue. These investments are necessary to maintain long-term competitiveness, but the returns are highly uncertain. Among the 30 pipeline products, there may not be more than 2-3 that can ultimately contribute more than US$1 billion in annual sales.

### 3.4 Exchange rate and cross-border risks

68.5% of revenue comes from overseas (mostly denominated in US dollars), but a large number of costs are incurred in RMB. RMB appreciation will compress profit margins. In addition, the complex structure of listing in three places also increases the difficulty of financial management.

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.2/5)**

BeiGene is at an inflection point from "high growth and loss" to "profit and volume". The improvement trend of financial data is clear - revenue continues to increase, profit margins increase rapidly, and cash flow turns positive. However, the current valuation has priced in a considerable degree of optimistic expectations, and the margin of safety is insufficient. For value investors, this is a classic case of "a good company, but you need to wait for a good price." The discount of Hong Kong stocks compared to A shares provides a certain buffer, but it is still necessary to pay close attention to the inflection point of zanubrutinib's growth rate and pipeline milestones.
