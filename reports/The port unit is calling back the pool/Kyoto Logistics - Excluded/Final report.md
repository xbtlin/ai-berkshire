## One sentence conclusion

JD Logistics is the fastest growing target among the 13 companies (revenue +18.8%). The supply chain barriers are real and reliable, but the 3.5% net profit margin makes it vulnerable to the economic cycle. The current valuation is reasonable but not cheap. The best point to intervene is when there is a definite improvement in profit margins.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. Revenue growth rate is 18.8%, which is a scarce high-growth asset in Hong Kong stocks.
2. The integrated supply chain model creates a real barrier to competition
3. The proportion of external customers continues to increase (67%), and the de-JDization process is successful.
4. Automation + AI is expected to gradually improve profit margins
5. PE 15 times is reasonable for 12% ROE and 18% growth rate

### Bearish logic
1. The net interest rate is only 3.5%, and the ability to resist risks is extremely weak.
2. The rigid increase in labor costs for 660,000 employees is inevitable
3. The signal that revenue will increase but profits will not increase in 2025 is worrying.
4. The express delivery business faces a continued price war between Tongda and Tongda.
5. Reliance on related-party transactions with JD.com

## Buying conditions

1. **Net profit margin increased to 5%+ and maintained for 2 quarters** - Proving the improvement of the profit model
2. **PE drops below 12 times** - providing more safety margin
3. **Clear signal of economic recovery** - Reduce the risk of shrinking demand
4. Currently can be used as an observation warehouse (within 3%)

## Position suggestions

- **Current Price**: Small positions can be allocated (3%), but changes in profit margins need to be closely tracked
- **Ideal buying range**: After the net interest rate is confirmed to increase, the position can be increased to 5-8%
- **Stop Loss Condition**: Net interest rate is less than 3% for 2 consecutive quarters

## Keep track of key points

1. Changes in quarterly net profit margin/gross profit margin (the most core)
2. Number of external integrated supply chain customers and single customer revenue
3. Labor cost growth rate vs. revenue growth rate
4. Automated warehouse advances progress
5. JD Group’s changes in strategic positioning of logistics
