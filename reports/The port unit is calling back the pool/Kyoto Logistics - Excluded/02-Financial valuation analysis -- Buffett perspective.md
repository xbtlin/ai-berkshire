# JD Logistics (2618.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 217.1 billion yuan | ~182.8 billion yuan | +18.8% |
| Adjusted net profit | 7.71 billion yuan | ~7.9 billion yuan | Basically flat |
| Gross profit margin | ~9-10% | 10.2% | Slight decrease |
| Net profit margin | ~3.5-4% | ~4.3% | Under pressure |
| ROE | ~12% | ~10% | Improving |

**Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

ROE of about 12% is significantly better than the several central enterprises analyzed earlier (5-7%), close to Buffett's preferred level. But please note: the net profit margin is only 3.5-4%, which shows that profits are extremely dependent on scale - if you are not careful, you may slip into losses.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- Good operating cash flow: The logistics company’s model of collecting payment first and then shipping/transporting is conducive to cash flow
- But the capital expenditure is relatively large (warehouse construction, equipment purchase, vehicle purchase)
- Free cash flow is improving (operating leverage from revenue growth)

### Key Financial Characteristics
1. **Fast revenue growth but slow profit growth**: Revenue in 2025 will be +18.8% but profits will be almost the same, indicating that labor costs are growing faster than revenue growth
2. **Labor costs exceed 100 billion yuan/year**: accounting for about 50% of revenue, it is the largest cost item
3. **Mixture of light and heavy assets**: self-owned warehouse + vehicles (heavy), but does not own land (most warehouses are leased)

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): ~15x
- PS: ~0.5x
- PB: ~1.8x
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 8-9 times

### Judgment of rationality of valuation
PE 15 times corresponds to ROE 12%, and the "PE/ROE ratio" is 1.25, which is within a reasonable range (1-1.5 is reasonable).

Key question: Can this ROE be sustained or even improved?
- If the scale effect continues to appear (revenue growth > cost growth), ROE is expected to be close to 15%, and the valuation is reasonable
- If labor costs continue to increase rigidly, profit margins will be compressed and ROE will fall below 10%

### Peer valuation comparison
| Company | PE | ROE | Net profit margin | Revenue growth rate |
|------|------|------|--------|----------|
| JD Logistics (2618) | 15x | 12% | 3.5% | 18.8% |
| SF Holdings (002352) | 18x | 10% | 3.5% | 8% |
| ZTO Express (2057) | 16x | 18% | 15% | 12% |

Comparison found:
- ZTO's "online franchise model" has a much higher profit margin than JD Logistics' "self-operated model" (15% vs 3.5%)
- But JD Logistics’ revenue is growing faster and the barriers to supply chain services are deeper
- The valuation of JD Logistics is slightly lower than that of SF Express. Considering the higher growth rate, it has a comparative advantage.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**More confident. ** Logistics is the infrastructure for e-commerce and manufacturing, and demand is only increasing. The penetration rate of supply chain services is still increasing.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Conditionally yes. ** Warehousing and distribution network + supply chain capabilities + service brand constitute a barrier, but this barrier requires continuous investment to maintain (it is not a "natural" barrier like Coca-Cola).

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
**Front. ** The JD Logistics team has strong execution capabilities and has developed from JD’s own logistics to a social platform with a clear strategy. However, attention needs to be paid to JD.com’s impact on it (such as allocating manpower to support the takeaway business).

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**Neutral. ** PE 15 times is not expensive for growth companies, but the net profit margin of only 3.5% means that it may lose money in an economic recession. The margin of safety comes from growth potential, not low valuation.

## 4. Path to increase profit margin

The key path for JD Logistics to improve its profit margin in the future:
1. **Automation replaces labor**: Each automated warehouse can replace 50-70% of labor, but it requires a large amount of initial investment.
2. **Scale effect**: After the revenue exceeds 200 billion, the management expense rate/operating expense rate is expected to continue to decrease.
3. **Product Structure Optimization**: From low-margin express delivery to high-margin supply chain services
4. **Price Strategy**: Supply chain services can charge a premium, and there is no need to participate in express price wars

If the profit margin increases from 3.5% to 5%, the corresponding net profit will increase from 7.7 billion yuan to 10 billion+, and the PE will fall below 12 times.

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

JD Logistics is currently the target with the strongest growth rate among the 13 companies - its revenue growth rate of 18.8% is unique in this pool. PE 15 times is reasonable or even low for such a growth rate.

But the risk is: the extremely thin net interest rate (3.5%) makes it weak in resisting risks. Once the economy downturns or price wars intensify, profits may fluctuate significantly. It is more suitable for "growth" investors rather than "stable" investors.

Buffett's possible attitude: respect its moat and growth, but dislike the 3.5% net profit margin and labor-intensive model. If the net profit margin can be increased to 6-7% and proven sustainable, the evaluation will be significantly changed.
