# JD Logistics (2618.HK) Business Model Analysis

## 1. The essence of business

The essence of JD Logistics' business is **integrated supply chain services** - not just "sending packages from A to B", but helping customers manage the entire chain of warehousing, sorting, transportation, distribution and after-sales. It is positioned as a "supply chain solution provider" rather than a simple express delivery company.

From Duan Yongping's perspective, this is a business that is "hard work but with barriers":
- Hard work: 660,000 employees, delivering tens of millions of orders every day, extremely complex operations
- There are barriers: The construction of a warehouse and distribution network requires years of investment and accumulation, making it difficult for latecomers to quickly replicate

## 2. Moat assessment

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

### Existing moat:
1. **Integrated warehouse and distribution network**: 1,600+ warehouses across the country, covering almost all counties and districts. The construction of this network took more than 10 years and tens of billions of dollars were invested, forming a huge physical barrier.
2. **Supply chain capabilities**: Not just transportation, but helping customers with inventory management, demand forecasting, and warehousing layout. These abilities require long-term accumulation
3. **Service Quality Brand**: User mentality of "JD Logistics has fast delivery and good service"
4. **Technology investment**: tens of billions of R&D per year (automated warehousing, drones, algorithm optimization)

### Moat defects:
1. Heavy reliance on manpower - 660,000 employees are a barrier and a cost burden
2. Dependence on JD.com – although declining, it still accounts for 33% of revenue
3. The express delivery business has a high degree of homogeneity - there is no essential difference from SF Express and Tongda in the standard express delivery field.

## 3. User value and differentiation

### Value to B-side customers:
- Integrated supply chain customers exceed 73,000, with average annual consumption of 239,000 yuan
- Help brand owners increase inventory turnover by 20-30%
- Provide a one-stop solution of "warehouse + distribution + technology"

This is the core differentiation of JD Logistics: it is not just about "shipping things", but helping customers "sell things better."

### Value to C-side users:
- "211 Limited Time Delivery" (orders placed before 11 a.m. will be delivered on the same day)
- The courier comes to pick up and deliver the goods, and the service attitude is good
- This part of the brand has strong mentality, but the moat is not deep (SF Express can also provide similar services)

### Duan Yongping’s evaluation:
JD Logistics has the product strength of "users will recommend" - B-side customers will indeed recommend it to their peers because of the improvement in supply chain efficiency. This is a positive sign.

## 4. Strategic Progress of "De-Jingdongization"

The most important strategic direction of JD Logistics is to reduce dependence on JD Group:
- External customers account for 54% of revenue at IPO in 2021
- Revenue from external customers will account for 67.1% in the first half of 2025
- The trend is clear: from "JD.com's logistics department" to "socialized supply chain platform"

This is of great significance to investors: the higher the proportion of external income, the more competitive JD Logistics’ capabilities are in the market, rather than relying on related transactions.

## 5. Whether management is "doing the right thing"

### Correct decision:
1. Adhere to the self-operated model and service quality - although the short-term cost is high, brand barriers have been established
2. Vigorously expand external customers - verifying the transferability of capabilities
3. Invest in automation and AI - cost reduction and efficiency increase are the long-term direction
4. Coordinated development of express delivery business and supply chain business

### If you have any doubts:
1. Is 660,000 employees too large? Labor costs exceed 100 billion yuan annually
2. The gross profit margin is only 9-10%, the profit margin is extremely thin, and the ability to resist risks is weak.
3. How efficiently does JD.com take advantage of its logistics network? Is it synergy or consumption?
4. Can the premium be maintained in the price-sensitive express delivery market?

## 6. Is it a good business?

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

**Positive Features:**
- Strong revenue growth (18.8%) and confirmed market demand
- Supply chain services are sticky (customer switching costs are high)
- Scale effects gradually emerge (revenue growth is faster than cost growth)
- The industry has huge space (China’s logistics market exceeds 10 trillion)

**Negative Traits:**
- Asset-heavy + manpower-heavy model, capital intensive
- The gross profit margin is extremely low (9-10%), and the profit is as thin as a razor blade
- Rigid rise in labor costs (minimum wage, social security pressure)
- It is not the "light assets and high gross profit" model that Duan Yongping likes

## 7. Core Conclusions

JD Logistics is a company that "does well but not easily". It has established real barriers and user value in the supply chain logistics field and has strong growth momentum. But it is not an "easy money" business - every penny of profit needs to be squeezed out through extreme operational efficiency.

Duan Yongping might say: This is a business that "I respect but don't want to own" - the complexity is too high, the profit margin is too thin, and the requirements for management capabilities are too demanding. But if the valuation is right (15x PE is reasonable for 12% ROE), it still has value as an allocation in the portfolio.
