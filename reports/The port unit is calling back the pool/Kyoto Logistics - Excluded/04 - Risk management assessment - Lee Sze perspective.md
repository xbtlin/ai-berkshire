### Detailed explanation of key risks

**1. Human cost - the biggest deterministic risk**
The annual labor cost of 660,000+ employees exceeds 100 billion yuan. China's minimum wage is rising every year and social security payments are becoming more standardized. Labor costs will only rise, not fall. If revenue growth cannot continue to cover cost growth, profits will be squeezed.

The data for 2025 already has warning signs: revenue is +18.8% but profits are almost flat, indicating that gross profit margin is declining (from 10.2% to ~9%) and manpower is expanding too fast.

**2. Net interest rate vulnerability**
A net profit margin of 3.5% means that if revenue drops by 10%, the company may directly lose money. During the economic downturn, e-commerce order volume may decline, and logistics demand will decrease accordingly, but fixed costs (labor, warehouse rent) will still remain.

**3. Risks associated with JD.com**
- JD Group still contributes about 33% of revenue
- Jingdong’s food delivery business may deploy logistics human resources
- JD Group’s strategic adjustment may affect the pace of logistics investment
- However, the continued increase in the proportion of external income is a positive sign to reduce this risk

## 2. Management evaluation

### Execution
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

JD Logistics management team has demonstrated excellent execution capabilities:
- From JD.com’s own logistics to an independent company and then to listing, the transformation path is clear
- Remarkable results in external customer expansion (proportion from 54% to 67%)
- Achieve scale expansion while maintaining service quality
- Technical investment is continuous and fruitful (automated warehouse efficiency improvement)

### Strategic Clarity
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Front:
1. "Integrated supply chain" has a clear positioning and is differentiated from pure express delivery companies
2. Firm implementation of external customer expansion strategy
3. Technology-driven cost reduction and efficiency increase are in the right direction

Negative:
1. The path to profit margin improvement is not clear enough—when will the state of increasing revenue without increasing profits end?
2. The international layout lags behind that of Rookie
3. The management of 660,000 people is extremely complex, and any management mistakes will be costly.

### Relationship with JD.com
Although JD Logistics has been listed and operates independently, Liu Qiangdong and JD Group still have huge influence:
- JD.com is the largest shareholder
- Management personnel are affected by JD Group
- The strategic direction needs to be coordinated with the JD Group’s grand strategy
- This is both a risk (may be forced to serve the group’s interests) and an advantage (JD ecological support)

## 3. Li Lu’s perspective of "civilization progress"

### Improvement of supply chain efficiency and progress of civilization
Li Lu emphasized "the progressive direction of civilization". From this perspective:
- The improvement of logistics efficiency reduces the transaction costs of the whole society
- Intelligent supply chain reduces inventory waste
- Fast delivery improves consumer experience
- China’s logistics cost as a share of GDP has dropped from 18% to 14%, and JD Logistics is one of the promoters

JD Logistics stands in the direction of civilizational progress of "efficiency improvement", which is a long-term positive factor.

### But you need to be careful:
- Is "fast" too much? If it reaches 30 minutes the next day, the marginal value will decrease.
- Has the excessive pursuit of delivery speed resulted in unnecessary consumption of resources?
- The protection of labor rights of 660,000 couriers is an ESG concern

## 4. Scenario Analysis

### Optimistic scenario (30% probability)
- Demand for supply chain services explodes, and external customer revenue growth maintains 20%+
- The effect of automated cost reduction is evident, and the net profit rate increases to 5%+
- ROE increased to 15%+
- PE maintains 15 times, profit growth drives stock price to rise 40-60%

### Baseline scenario (probability 45%)
- Revenue growth maintained at 10-15%
- Net profit margin maintained at 3.5-4%
- ROE remains around 12%
- Stock price rises moderately by 10-15%/year with profit growth

### Pessimistic scenario (probability 25%)
- Economic downturn suppresses demand
- Labor cost growth exceeds revenue growth
- Net interest rate drops below 2% or even loses money
- Share price fell 30-40%

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Risk Management Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The core contradiction of JD Logistics is: **Excellent business model vs fragile profit margin**. Its supply chain barriers are real, its growth momentum is strong, and its management is capable -- but 3.5% net margins make everything fragile. Any economic downturn, price war, or out-of-control costs could turn small profits into losses.

From Li Lu's "long-term holding" perspective:
- If you believe in the long-term growth of China's supply chain services market and believe that automation can gradually increase profit margins, JD Logistics is worth holding.
- If you are concerned about the long-term sustainability of a labor-intensive model, you should wait for clear signs of improving margins before jumping in

The current PE price of 15 times gives you a "reasonable" buying cost - neither expensive nor cheap. The real buying signal is when the net interest rate increases to 5%+ and lasts for more than 2 quarters.
