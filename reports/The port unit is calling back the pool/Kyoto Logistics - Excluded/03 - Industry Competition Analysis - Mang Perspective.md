# JD Logistics (2618.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. Panorama of China’s logistics industry

### Industry scale and structure
The total scale of China's logistics market exceeds 10 trillion yuan, including:
- Transportation and logistics: about 6 trillion yuan (mainly road transportation)
- Warehousing and logistics: about 2 trillion yuan
- Supply chain management: about 2 trillion yuan

JD Logistics’ revenue of 217.1 billion yuan accounts for about 2% of the 10 trillion market, and its penetration rate is extremely low, indicating huge room for growth.

### Competitive landscape - different logics for different tracks

| Track | Major Players | Competition Intensity | JD Logistics Positioning |
|------|----------|----------|------------|
| E-commerce Express | ZTO/YTO/Yunda/STO | Extremely high (price war) | High-end differentiation |
| Time-sensitive express delivery | SF Express/JD.com | Medium to high | Direct competition |
| Integrated supply chain | JD Logistics/Cainiao | Medium | Core areas |
| Cold chain/bulky/cross-border | Fragmentation | Medium and low | Active layout |

## 2. Competitiveness assessment of JD Logistics

### 1. Integrated supply chain (core competitiveness)
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

This is JD Logistics’ “killer feature”:
- Not simple transportation, but "helping customers place their goods in the warehouse closest to consumers"
- Through data prediction + distributed warehousing, you can achieve the ultimate experience of "place an order and it will be by your side"
- Competitors (Tongda Department) lack warehousing capabilities and cannot copy
- Rookie is the closest competitor, but Rookie prefers the "platform model"

### 2. Delivery network
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

- 1,600+ warehouses nationwide
- Own delivery team, service quality controllable
- Disadvantages: The density of terminal outlets is not as good as that of the Tongda system (the Tongda system has hundreds of thousands of express delivery outlets)

### 3. Technical ability
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

- Annual R&D investment exceeds 10 billion yuan (a total of more than 30 billion yuan in three years)
- Automated sorting center, unmanned warehouse, and unmanned vehicle pilot
- Intelligent supply chain brain (demand forecast, route optimization)
- Technology leadership is the key to long-term cost reduction and efficiency improvement

### 4. Service quality
**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

- Industry-recognized delivery speed and service attitude
- Own staff model ensures service standardization
- "JD Express" brand has strong mentality
- Disadvantages: Premium model lacks competitiveness among price-sensitive customers

## 3. Key issues of Munger format

### "What is the endgame for the logistics industry?"

The logistics industry will move toward **polarization**:
1. **Extreme Efficiency School**: Such as Zhongtong, through the franchise model, the cost of each order is reduced to an extremely low level, and huge profits are earned.
2. **Extreme Value School**: For example, JD Logistics creates value-added through supply chain services and earns higher profits from fewer customers.

JD Logistics chose the second path. The end result of this road is: becoming China's "supply chain version of Amazon" - not only transporting things, but also helping customers with full-chain services such as inventory management, demand forecasting, and returns processing.

### "Self-operated model vs franchise model, who wins?"
| Model | Representative | Net profit margin | Service quality | Expansion speed |
|------|------|--------|----------|----------|
| Self-operated | JD Logistics/SF Express | 3-5% | High | Slow |
| Join | Zhongtong/Yunda | 12-18% | Medium | Fast |

From Munger's perspective: the franchise model has "won" in the express delivery field (ZTO's profit margin is 4 times that of JD Logistics). JD Logistics’ counterattack strategy is: instead of fighting bloody battles in the red ocean of express delivery, it will open up new horizons in the blue ocean of the supply chain. ** Whether this strategy is correct can be verified by looking at the growth rate of external supply chain customers.

### "How can labor-intensive companies continue to improve returns?"
660,000 employees is a double-edged sword:
- Negative: Labor costs exceed RMB 100 billion annually, accounting for 50%+ of revenue
- Positive: Every front-line employee is a "customer contact point" and carries service quality

In the long term, automation is the inevitable direction. However, full automation is still not realistic in the "last mile" delivery field (regulatory, technical, cost constraints). In the next 3-5 years, JD Logistics will most likely be "semi-automated" - highly automated in the warehouse + continued reliance on manual labor at the end.

## 4. Rookie - the most threatening competitor

Cainiao Network (Alibaba) is the most direct competitor of JD Logistics:
- Also working on integrated supply chain
- Have huge traffic entrance from Taobao/Tmall
- The model is more "light" (platform + self-operated hybrid)
- Cainiao has led JD Logistics in the field of overseas logistics

Competitive focus:
1. Competition for domestic supply chain customers
2. Cross-border logistics capacity building
3. Automation technology competition
4. Who can realize "smart supply chain" faster?

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

JD Logistics has a real competitive advantage in the field of integrated supply chain - the barriers formed by warehouse distribution network + technical capabilities + service quality are not easy to be copied. But it faces two structural challenges: 1) the profit margin ceiling of the labor-intensive model; 2) Rookie’s continued catch-up in the supply chain field.

Munger concluded: JD Logistics has chosen a "difficult but correct" path - establishing barriers through heavy investment and creating value through supply chain services. The question is: Can profit margins along this path be improved to a level that satisfies shareholders? The current net interest rate of 3.5% shows that it is still on the way from the "investment period" to the "harvest period".
