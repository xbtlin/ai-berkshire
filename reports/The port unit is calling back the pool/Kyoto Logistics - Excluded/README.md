# JD Logistics (2618.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: JD Logistics Co., Ltd.
- **Stock Code**: 2618.HK
- **Industry**: Supply Chain Logistics
- **PE(TTM)**: ~15x
- **ROE**：~12%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Operating income | 217.1 billion yuan | +18.8% |
| Adjusted net profit | 7.71 billion yuan | Significant growth |
| Integrated supply chain revenue in the first half of the year | 50.1 billion yuan | +19.9% |
| Proportion of revenue from external customers | 67.1% | Continuous improvement |
| Number of front-line employees | Over 660,000 | - |

## Investment Highlights
1. Revenue growth rate is strong (18.8%), exceeding 200 billion yuan
2. The integrated supply chain model creates barriers to competition
3. The proportion of revenue from external customers continues to increase, de-Jingdong
4. AI/automation has huge room for cost reduction and efficiency improvement

## Core Risks
1. Labor-intensive enterprise, 660,000+ employees, cost rigidity
2. Gross profit margin is low (~9-10%) and profit is thin
3. Reliance on related-party transactions with JD.com
4. Price war risks in the express delivery industry
