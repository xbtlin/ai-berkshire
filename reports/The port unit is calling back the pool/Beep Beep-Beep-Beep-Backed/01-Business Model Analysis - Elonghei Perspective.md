#bilibili: Business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: product essence, user value, business model sustainability
> Core question: Are Bilibili’s products good? Are users really inseparable? Right way to make money?

---

## 1. Product essence: What exactly is Station B?

To understand the business model of Bilibili, we must first understand what product it is. On the surface, Bilibili is a video platform; in essence, it is a young people’s interest community using videos as a carrier.

This definition is crucial because it determines that Bilibili’s competitors are not exclusively Douyin and Kuaishou, but also include community products such as Zhihu, Douban, and Xiaohongshu. The core asset of Bilibili is not the video content itself, but the community relationship formed around the video - barrage interaction, comment area culture, and the chain of trust between UP owners and fans.

As of Q4 2025, Bilibili’s MAU reached 366 million, DAU 113 million, and users’ average daily usage time was 107 minutes. This duration data illustrates the problem: users do not come to watch a few short videos and then leave, but consume medium and long video content immersively. 86% of users are under 35 years old, with an average age of 26, which means that Bilibili has targeted a generation with rising consumption power in China.

## 2. User value: Do users really need Bilibili?

The simplest criterion to judge whether a product is good or not is: if it disappears, will users feel uncomfortable?

For the core users of Station B, the answer is most likely "yes". There are three reasons:

**First, the irreplaceability of content. ** There is a large amount of medium and long video content on Bilibili that cannot be found on other platforms: in-depth science popularization, hard-core technical analysis, game strategies, animation creations, and learning tutorials. The production of these contents requires professional ability and time investment, which cannot be replaced by 60-second short videos on Douyin. More than 120 million people consume AI-related content on Bilibili every month, and the playback time of AI content increased by 53% year-on-year, indicating that Bilibili’s content supply in emerging fields is still attractive.

**Second, the scarcity of community atmosphere. ** Danmaku culture is a unique interactive form of Bilibili. The meme culture in the comment area and the ritual sense of "one click and three consecutive" form the user's sense of belonging. Once this kind of community atmosphere is formed, the cost of migration is very high. Douyin can poach UP owners, but it is difficult to replicate the community atmosphere.

**Third, the network effect of UP’s main ecosystem. ** Station B has nearly 100,000 active AI-related UP owners, and the overall scale of active creators is larger. UP owners have accumulated fans and content assets at Station B, and their fan charging revenue has doubled year-on-year, which means that creators have financial incentives to stay.

But we must also look at the other side of the coin: Bilibili users’ willingness to pay is relatively low. The penetration rate of large members is only 6-7% (about 25 million), which is far lower than the subscription rate of iQiyi and Tencent Video of about 20%. The 32 million monthly paying users account for less than 9% of MAU. This shows that although users like Bilibili, the proportion of them who are willing to pay for it is limited - the community attribute makes users feel that "free is what it should be".

## 3. Income structure: Walking on four legs, but at different paces

In 2025, Bilibili’s total revenue will be 30.35 billion yuan, with revenue coming from four sectors:

**Value-added services (39%, 11.93 billion yuan, +8%):** Including big membership, live streaming rewards, fan charging, etc. This is the most stable source of income, but growth is slowing. The growth of large members relies on the expansion of user scale rather than the increase in penetration rate, and there is limited room for ARPU value improvement. Over 80% are annual subscription or automatic renewal users, and retention is good, but the ceiling is looming.

**Advertising business (33%, 10.06 billion yuan, +23%):** This is the fastest growing engine, exceeding 10 billion for the first time throughout the year. Bilibili advertising is divided into three categories: fireworks advertising (UP hosts advertising), performance advertising (oCPX), and brand advertising. The growth logic of advertising is clear: Bilibili’s user profile is accurate (young, highly educated, and highly willing to spend), which is attractive to advertisers, especially game and AI application advertisers. Q4 advertising revenue was 3.04 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 27%. However, the advertising loading rate of Station B is still much lower than that of Douyin and Kuaishou, which is both a disadvantage (low commercialization efficiency) and potential (there is still room for improvement).

**Game business (21%, 6.40 billion yuan, +14%):** Games were once the largest source of revenue for Bilibili, but later the proportion continued to decline. There are two products supporting the market in 2025: "Three Kingdoms: Conquering the World" has performed stably, and the self-developed "Escape from Duckkov" has sold more than 3 million copies globally on Steam, with a praise rate of 96%, ranking first in the annual domestic game sales list in 2025. This shows that Bilibili’s game self-development capabilities are improving. However, the problem with the game business is that it is highly volatile and highly dependent on hot hits, making it unsuitable as a core support for valuation.

**IP derivatives and others (6%, 1.97 billion yuan, -3%):** Including e-commerce and IP peripherals, the volume is the smallest and declining, and no effective growth model has yet been found.

## 4. The core contradiction of the business model

The core contradiction of Bilibili’s business model lies in the tension between community attributes and commercial efficiency. **

The community atmosphere of Station B is its moat, but it is also a shackles to commercialization. Users have low tolerance for advertising, and UP owners have complicated attitudes towards commercialization. The platform must walk a tightrope between user experience and commercial monetization. In contrast, Douyin has been designed for commercialization from the first day - information flow advertising, live e-commerce, local life, every step is directed towards monetization. Bilibili first establishes a community and then finds ways to make money. This sequence determines that its commercialization must be gradual.

The good news is that AI is changing the equation. Chen Rui defined AI in the 2026 financial report as "an era of reshaping content creation, community experience and business efficiency." AIGC tools have been used to automatically generate advertising materials. More than 50% of effective advertising materials are completed with AIGC; multi-modal large language models are used to improve recommendation accuracy. If AI can improve advertising efficiency without destroying the community atmosphere, the commercialization ceiling of Bilibili may be broken.

## 5. Moat Assessment

| Moat Type | Assessment | Description |
|-----------|------|------|
| Network effect | ★★★★☆ | UP owner-user bilateral network effect is obvious, but the increase is slowing down |
| Switching costs | ★★★☆☆ | The sense of community belonging constitutes psychological switching costs, but there are many substitutes at the functional level |
| Brand awareness | ★★★★☆ | Brand awareness is extremely high among Generation Z, and "Bilibili" has become a cultural symbol |
| Cost advantage | ★★☆☆☆ | High content cost, high bandwidth cost, no obvious cost advantage |
| Scale effect | ★★★☆☆ | User scale brings advertising bargaining power, but it has not yet been converted into profit margin advantage |

**Comprehensive moat rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

Station B has a moat, but the width and depth of the moat are not as wide as those of platform-level companies like Tencent and ByteDance. Its moat comes more from the soft barrier of "community culture" than from the hard barrier brought about by technology or scale. This kind of moat is strong when user loyalty is high, but if the community atmosphere is damaged (such as excessive commercialization or content quality declines), the moat can quickly collapse.

## 6. General evaluation of business model

**Advantages:**
- Has the largest young people video community in China, with clear and valuable user profiles
- The advertising business is growing rapidly, and efficiency is expected to continue to improve with AI empowerment.
- The self-development ability of the game has been improved, and "Escape from Duck Cove" has proved the strength of the team
- User time is 107 minutes, extremely sticky

**Disadvantages:**
- Commercialization efficiency is far lower than that of peers, and there is a gap in advertising loading rate and paid penetration rate
- Each of the four business lines faces their own growth bottlenecks
- Community attributes limit the space for radical commercialization
- High content and bandwidth costs, thin profit margins

**Business Model Sustainability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)**

Bilibili’s business model is sustainable, but not outstanding. It is a classic case of "good product but not good business" - users like it, but it is not efficient in making money. Whether the future can turn from a "good product" to a "good business" depends on whether the advertising business can continue to grow at a high speed without harming the community, and whether AI can truly change the efficiency equation of commercialization.
