## 2. Core investment thesis

### Reasons to be bullish (Bull Case)

**1. China’s largest in-depth content community for young people, irreplaceable for mindshare**

366 million MAU, 113 million DAU, 107 minutes of average daily usage, and 86% of users are under 35 years old. In China's Internet, there is no other platform that can meet these three conditions at the same time: large scale, young users, and high depth of use. The long-term value of this user asset may be undervalued by the market.

**2. Profit rates are in the early stages of an upward trend**

The gross profit margin will increase from approximately 28% in 2023 to 36.6% in 2025, and the trend is clear. The increase in the proportion of advertising (high gross profit business), the improvement of AI-enabled operational efficiency, and the release of operating leverage are three factors that drive the improvement in profit margins. If the adjusted net interest rate can increase to 12-15% in 2028, corresponding to a profit of 4-5.5 billion, the current valuation will be cheap.

**3. The growth space of advertising business is far from being released**

Station B’s advertising revenue in 2025 is 10.06 billion, with each DAU contributing approximately 89 yuan. Referring to Douyin’s approximately 430 yuan per DAU and Kuaishou’s approximately 200 yuan per DAU, Bilibili’s advertising monetization efficiency still has room for improvement by 2-4 times. Guosen Securities predicts that the growth rate of game advertising at Bilibili will exceed 30% in 2026, and AI application advertisers are also increasing investment.

**4. Game self-development ability verified**

"Escape from Duck Cove" has sold more than 3 million copies globally and has a 96% positive rating, proving that Bilibili's game team is not only engaged in intermodal transportation and agency, but also has the ability to create blockbuster hits. Although the game business is volatile, once a hit occurs, it will have a significant boost to profits.

**5. Abundant cash and positive shareholder returns**

Cash reserves of 23.5 billion yuan (accounting for about 35% of market value), operating cash flow of 7.1 billion, and a repurchase plan of up to 700 million US dollars. The company is not short of money and is willing to return money to shareholders.

### Reasons to be bearish (Bear Case)

**1. Commercialization efficiency is much lower than that of peers, which may be structural rather than temporary**

The community attribute of Bilibili limits the advertising loading rate and commercialization space. Users are sensitive to advertising, and UP owners are resistant to excessive commercialization. The platform has to maintain a balance between monetization and experience. This is not a problem of "not yet ready", but rather a structural bottleneck of "never reaching the level of peers".

**2. First-time profit does not mean sustained high-quality profit**

GAAP net profit in 2025 is 1.19 billion, and the net profit rate is only 3.9%. Even using the adjusted 2.59 billion, the net interest rate is only 8.5%. Compared with Tencent's 20%+ and NetEase's 25%+ net profit margins, the gap is huge. If the steady-state net interest rate is only 8-10%, 28 times PE is expensive.

**3. Growth is slowing**

Revenue growth in 2025 is 13%, which has slowed down significantly compared to 30%+ in previous years. MAU grew by 8% and DAU grew by 10%, both of which are slowing down. The great growth trend of Internet users in China has passed. As a platform that has penetrated 60% of young people, Bilibili has limited room for user growth in the future.

**4. Competitive threats persist**

The penetration of Douyin into medium- and long-form videos, the videoization of Xiaohongshu, and AI lowering the threshold for content creation may all erode Bilibili’s differentiated advantages. Although the community moat at Station B exists, it is not omnipotent.

**5. Game revenue cannot be linearly extrapolated**

"Escape from Duckkov" and "Three Kingdoms: Conquering the World" are the two biggest hits in 2025, but the game industry is characterized by "the alternation of big and small years." If there are no products of the same magnitude in 2026, game revenue may decline.

**6. AI investment increases short-term suppression of profits**

Management announced an increase in AI R&D investment of 500 million to 1 billion in 2026. If output falls short of expectations, this expenditure will directly drag down profit performance.

## 3. Key Assumptions and Scenario Analysis

### Optimistic scenario (25% probability): advertising + AI driven profit margin breakthrough

- Advertising revenue will increase by 20% annually from 2026 to 2028, reaching 15-18 billion
- AI empowerment promotes significant improvement in advertising efficiency, with gross profit margin reaching 40%+
- Adjusted net profit margin increased to 12-15%
- Market value in 2028 could reach HK$120-150 billion (corresponding to stock price of HK$300-380)

### Baseline scenario (50% probability): Steady improvement but not amazing

- Annual revenue growth of 10-13% from 2026 to 2028
- Adjusted net interest rate slowly increases to 10-12%
- Adjusted net profit in 2028 is 3.5-4.5 billion yuan
- 20 times PE + net cash, market value is approximately HK$90-110 billion (corresponding to stock price of HK$230-280)

### Pessimistic scenario (25% probability): Growth stalls and profit margins peak

- Advertising growth rate dropped below 10%, and there were no new hits in the game business
- The deterioration of community atmosphere leads to a decrease in user duration and DAU
- Adjusted net profit margin remains at 8-9%
- The market value in 2028 is approximately HK$50-70 billion (corresponding to a stock price of HK$130-180)

## 5. Suggested action plan

### Reasons why buying is not currently recommended

1. **Insufficient safety margin. ** The current price corresponds to an adjusted PE of 28 times. The upside is about 40% under the base scenario, but it may lose 10-20% under the pessimistic scenario. The risk-reward ratio is not attractive enough.
2. **Profit quality needs more verification. ** Only one year of earnings data, and margins are still thin. Watch the profit margin trends for at least another 2-3 quarters.
3. **Uncertainty in the game business. ** We need to see whether profits can still maintain growth without a hit product in 2026.

### Buy trigger conditions

When 2-3 of the following conditions are met, you can consider opening a position:

1. **The stock price fell back below HK$150** (corresponding to approximately 23 times adjusted PE), and the margin of safety is more sufficient
2. **Advertising revenue growth has maintained 20%+ for 2 consecutive quarters**, confirming the sustainability of the advertising engine
3. **Adjusted net profit margin increased quarter-on-quarter for two consecutive quarters**, confirming the improvement trend in profit margins
4. **DAU/MAU continues to grow and the average daily usage time does not decrease**, confirming the ecological health of the community
5. **AI investment begins to show ROI** (such as increased advertising ARPU or improved operational efficiency indicators)

## 6. Final summary

Bilibili is a company with unique assets (a deep community of 366 million young users) but an inefficient business model. The first profit in 2025 is an important milestone, but there is still a way to "prove that this is a good business."

For investors, the core bet is: How high can **Bilibili’s profit margin increase from 8.5%? ** If the answer is 12-15%, the current price is reasonable or even cheap; if the answer is 8-10%, the current price is too expensive.

Before there is more data to confirm the profit margin improvement trend, it is more prudent to stay on the sidelines and wait for better prices or stronger signals. Station B is worth putting on the watch list, but it is not worth buying heavily at the current price.
