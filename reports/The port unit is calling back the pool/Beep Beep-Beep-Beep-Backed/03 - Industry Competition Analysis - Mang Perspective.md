#bilibili: Industry and Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry ecology, competition landscape, competitive advantages and disadvantages, and long-term evolution
> Core question: Can B hold its position under the attack of Douyin, Kuaishou and Xiaohongshu? Where is the long-term ecological niche?

---

## 1. Industry Ecology: The Pattern of China’s Online Video and Content Communities

To understand the competitive position of Bilibili, we need to first draw a panoramic view of China’s online content consumption.

The current Chinese Internet content platform can be divided into several levels:

**First echelon - super platform (DAU 500 million+):**
- Douyin (ByteDance): DAU exceeds 700 million, integrating short videos, live broadcasts, e-commerce, and local life, with advertising revenue exceeding 300 billion
- WeChat Video Account (Tencent): Relying on the WeChat ecosystem, DAU is about 400-500 million, and commercialization is accelerating

**The second tier - large platforms (DAU 100-400 million):**
- Kuaishou: DAU is about 400 million, deeply engaged in the sinking market and live broadcast e-commerce
- Xiaohongshu: about 150 million DAU, pictures, texts + short videos, lifestyle community
- Bilibili: 113 million DAU, medium and long videos + community

**The third echelon - vertical platform:**
- Long video platforms such as iQiyi, Youku, and Mango TV

Station B is in the second echelon, with the same size as Kuaishou and Xiaohongshu, but much smaller than Douyin and Video Account.

## 2. Competitive landscape: It is not a zero-sum game, but the overlap is increasing

Many people simply put Bilibili and Douyin in opposition. This is a misunderstanding. They serve different user needs: Douyin satisfies "fragmented entertainment" (you can leave after a few minutes of browsing), while Station B satisfies "immersive content consumption" (watching a 30-minute in-depth video). The competition between the two is more of a competition for users' time than a direct replacement.

But it should be noted that the boundaries of competition are blurring:

**Douyin penetrates into medium and long videos:** Douyin is already pushing 5-15 minute video content to encourage creators to make longer videos. Although Xigua Video failed to operate independently, Douyin’s own medium and long video content is growing. If Douyin can use algorithm recommendation + traffic subsidy to attract medium and long video creators, the content supply side of Station B will be affected.

**Videoization of Xiaohongshu:** Xiaohongshu has an obvious trend of shifting from a picture and text community to a video community, and has a high penetration rate among young female users. The gender ratio of users at Bilibili is skewed toward men, while Xiaohongshu is skewed toward women. The two are currently more complementary than competitive, but if Xiaohongshu further expands its video categories, the overlap will increase.

**Steady-state competition of Kuaishou:** Kuaishou has advantages in live broadcasting and lower-tier markets, and has low user overlap with Bilibili. But Kuaishou’s advertising efficiency is higher (PE is about 15 times). If the capital market uses Kuaishou’s commercialization efficiency to measure Bilibili, the valuation premium of Bilibili will be under pressure.

## 3. Competitive advantages of Bilibili

**1. Community cultural moat**

This is the core competitive advantage of Bilibili and the most difficult to replicate. Barrage culture, comment area ecology, and the trusting relationship between UP owners and fans cannot be established by throwing money or algorithms. The community at Bilibili has a "self-purification ability" - low-quality content will be voted out by users, while high-quality content will naturally come up. This mechanism does not exist on Douyin and Kuaishou. Those platforms rely entirely on algorithms to determine distribution.

**2. Content barriers for medium and long videos**

Bilibili is the only large-scale PUGV (professional user-generated video) platform in China. For in-depth videos on knowledge, technology, and games, creators prefer to publish them on Station B because the audience here is willing to take the time to watch and interact with a 30-minute video. It is difficult for this kind of content to get traffic on Douyin—Douyin’s algorithm prefers short, fast, and highly stimulating content.

**3. The mind of young users**

Bilibili’s brand awareness among Generation Z is extremely high. The penetration rate of users aged 5-35 reaches 60%, which means that 6 out of 10 Chinese young people are using Bilibili. Once this mental position is formed, it is difficult for competitors to shake it. Young people "studying at Bilibili" have become a cultural phenomenon.

**4. Diversified income sources**

Compared with platforms with a pure advertising model, Station B has four revenue lines: advertising, value-added services, games, and IP. Although each line is not strong, the combination reduces dependence on a single business. Especially in the game business, "Escape from Duck Cove" has sold more than 3 million copies globally on Steam and has a 96% praise rate, proving that Bilibili has the ability to develop its own hit hits.

## 4. Competitive disadvantages of station B

**1. There is a huge gap in commercialization efficiency**

This is the core question. Let the numbers speak:

- The loading rate of Douyin ads is about 15-18%, while that of Bilibili may only be 3-5%.
- Douyin’s advertising revenue will exceed 300 billion in 2025, while Bilibili will only have 10.06 billion
- Douyin’s commercialization efficiency of 700 million DAU: each DAU contributes about 430 yuan in advertising revenue
- The commercialization efficiency of Station B’s DAU of 113 million: each DAU contributes about 89 yuan in advertising revenue

The advertising revenue contributed by each user of Bilibili is only about one-fifth that of Douyin. This gap is partly because users at Bilibili are more sensitive to advertising (due to community culture), and partly because Bilibili’s advertising infrastructure is not mature enough.

**2. Gap in algorithm capabilities**

ByteDance's recommendation algorithm is the world's top and has huge advantages in content distribution and accurate advertising. Although Bilibili’s recommendation system is making progress (with the introduction of multi-modal LLM), the gap with Byte still exists. Algorithm capabilities directly affect advertising conversion rate and user duration, which is a hard power in competition.

**3. High content cost**

Station B needs to share revenue from UP (fireworks business order), purchase copyrighted content, and provide a high-quality playback experience for medium and long videos (bandwidth cost). In comparison, the production cost of UGC short videos on Douyin is extremely low, and the platform does not need to pay for most of the content. Station B’s content supply model determines that its cost structure is naturally high.

**4. Disadvantage of scale**

Bilibili’s 366 million MAU vs. Douyin’s 800 million + MAU. The scale gap brings disadvantages in many aspects: advertisers are more inclined to prioritize large platforms; the data volume gap affects the speed of algorithm optimization; insufficient scale effect leads to higher fixed cost sharing.

## 5. Long-term evolution of the industry: Where is the ecological niche of Station B?

In the long term, China’s online content industry may evolve into the following pattern:

**Douyin/Video account:** Occupying the central position of "fragmented entertainment + social commerce", it is the absolute overlord of traffic.
**Xiaohongshu:** Become a "lifestyle decision-making platform" and irreplaceable in consumer product marketing.
**Bilibili:** Become an "in-depth content community for young people", similar to the Chinese version of YouTube.
**Long video platform:** Continue to shrink or transform into streaming media.

Bilibili's long-term ecological niche is "deep content community" - this positioning is valuable, but the ceiling is lower than that of "super platform". The key question is: What is the commercial value of this niche?

The experience of benchmarking YouTube is of reference significance. YouTube's annual advertising revenue is approximately US$35 billion, corresponding to approximately 2.5 billion MAU, with an advertising value of approximately US$14 per MAU. Station B has 366 million MAU. If calculated at 30% of YouTube’s efficiency (taking into account the lower price in the Chinese advertising market), the potential advertising revenue is about 15-20 billion yuan. The current advertising revenue of Bilibili is 10.06 billion, which means there is still 50-100% room for improvement. But it will take years.

## 7. Comprehensive assessment of industry competition

**The competitive situation of Bilibili can be summed up in one sentence: it can defend market segments, but it cannot attack the mass market. **

The advantages are: community culture is truly differentiated, medium and long video content is irreplaceable, and young users have strong minds. These ensure that Station B will not be eliminated or marginalized in the foreseeable future.

The disadvantages are: commercialization efficiency is much lower than that of competitors, there is a gap in scale and algorithm capabilities, and the cost structure is relatively high. These determine that it is difficult for Bilibili to become a "super profitable" company, and it is more likely to be a "stable profit but limited profit margin" company.

From an investment perspective, the key is not whether Bilibili can survive (it can), but to what level its profitability can be improved. If the ad loading rate and efficiency can gradually increase to 50-60% close to the industry average (instead of the current 20-30%), there will be a lot of room for improvement in Bilibili's profits. But this process may take 3-5 years and needs to be completed without destroying the community atmosphere.

**Industry Competition Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Bilibili has a unique ecological niche and certain competition barriers, but in an industry dominated by super platforms, its competitive strength is limited. Not the best business, but not the worst situation either.
