#bilibili: Risk and Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: key risk identification, management capabilities and integrity, corporate governance, long-term uncertainty
> Core question: How far can Chen Rui take Station B? What risks could invalidate an investment thesis?

---

## 1. Management evaluation: Chen Rui

### Background and Qualifications

Chen Rui is the soul of Bilibili and serves as the chairman of the board and CEO. He is not the founder of Bilibili - Bilibili was founded by Xu Yi ("⑨bishi") in 2009. Chen Rui was originally an angel investor and officially joined as chairman in 2014 and leads the company's operations.

Chen Rui's professional background: He graduated from Chengdu University of Information Science and Technology in 2001 and joined Kingsoft Software. In 2006, he served as the general manager of Kingsoft Internet Security Division. He founded Shell Security in 2008, which was acquired by Kingsoft in 2010 and became the co-founder of Kingsoft Network (later renamed Cheetah Mobile). In 2014, Cheetah Mobile withdrew after its U.S. stock market listing and fully devoted itself to Bilibili.

There are several noteworthy points about this background:

**Advantages:** Chen Rui has profound experience in Internet products (from Jinshan University and is an old friend of Lei Jun), has experience in entrepreneurship and management of listed companies, and is an in-depth user of Bilibili - he was an active user of Bilibili before joining. Understanding products, understanding users, and having management experience are a good combination among Chinese Internet CEOs.

**Hidden concerns:** Chen Rui is not a technically genius founder. There is a gap between B-station and ByteDance in terms of algorithm and technology. He is more like a "product manager CEO" who is good at understanding user needs, but his ability in large-scale commercial operations still needs to be proven.

### Management’s execution records

Evaluate management based on what they say and do.

**Things done:**

1. **Turn losses into profits. ** This is Chen Rui’s core commitment for many years, and it will finally be fulfilled in 2025. From the huge losses in consecutive years after listing in 2018, the losses will be significantly narrowed in 2024, and the first full-year GAAP profit will be made in 2025. This turnaround was hard-won, which shows that management has the willingness and ability to control costs and improve efficiency.

2. **User growth. ** Station B’s MAU has increased from approximately 100 million when it was launched in 2018 to 366 million in 2025, and its DAU has increased from approximately 30 million to 113 million. Achieving this growth in an already crowded market validates management's product capabilities.

3. **Advertising business exceeded 10 billion. ** Advertising has grown from an insignificant business line to a core engine with annual revenue exceeding 10 billion, with a year-on-year growth rate of 23%, indicating that the commercialization system is gradually established.

4. **A breakthrough in self-research of the game. ** The success of "Escape from Duckkov" (global sales of more than 3 million copies, Steam praise rate of 96%, and the top sales of domestic games in 2025) proves the self-research capabilities of Bilibili's game team, not just through joint transportation and agency.

**Things not done well:**

1. **Commercialization efficiency has been low for a long time. ** It took Bilibili 7 years from its listing in 2018 to 2025 to achieve profitability. Cumulative losses during this period exceeded 20 billion yuan. Although the commercialization of community platforms does take time, the seven-year loss period is too long and capital efficiency is not high.

2. **Live broadcasting and e-commerce have repeatedly failed. ** Station B has tried many times to conduct live e-commerce and member purchase e-commerce, but the results were not good. The IP derivatives business will even experience negative growth in 2025. This illustrates management's limited ability to find growth outside of gaming and advertising.

3. **Short video response is slow. ** When Douyin emerged, Bilibili was slow to respond. It was only in recent years that it began to pay attention to the integration of short video forms. This may divert the attention of some users.

### Management Integrity

Watch for a few signals:

- **Repurchase Action:** The cumulative repurchase from 2024 to 2025 will exceed US$116 million, and an additional US$500 million repurchase plan will be added in early 2026. Expressing confidence with real money is a positive signal.
- **Salary Reasonability:** Chen Rui’s salary is not excessive among Chinese Internet CEOs and has not caused obvious controversy.
- **Information Disclosure:** The quality of information disclosure in financial reports and performance meetings is above average. Management is willing to discuss difficulties and challenges, and not only reports good news but not bad news.
- **Related transactions:** There are no significant related transactions that harm the interests of small shareholders.

**Management Rating: ★★★★☆ (3.5/5)**

Chen Rui is a qualified CEO - he understands products, has the ability to execute, and has basic integrity. But he is not an excellent CEO - commercialization is slow, the efficiency of taking 7 years to make a profit is not high, and he is lackluster in new business development.

## 2. Corporate Governance

### Equity Structure

Station B adopts a WVR structure. Chen Rui and Xu Yi hold Class B shares and have more than 50% of the voting rights. This means that small shareholders have little say in major company decisions.

This structure is very common among Chinese concept stocks and is reasonable - it protects management from short-term capital pressure and focuses on long-term strategies. But it also means that if management makes mistakes, small shareholders lack a correction mechanism.

### Institutional shareholders

The main institutional shareholders of Bilibili include Tencent (holding about 8-9% of the shares), Alibaba (which has reduced most of its holdings), etc. Tencent's shareholding has strategic significance for Bilibili - there is synergy in terms of game interoperability and content cooperation. However, Tencent may also strategically "frame" Bilibili and limit its development space in certain fields.

**Corporate Governance Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

WVR structure and major shareholder influence are standard risk items. The overall governance level is medium among Chinese concept stocks.

## 3. List of key risks

### Risk 1: Community culture dilution risk (probability: medium | impact: high)

The core competitiveness of Bilibili is community culture, but as the number of users increases from 100 million to 366 million, the "purity" of the community is inevitably diluted. Old users have always complained that "Bilibili has changed." If the ad loading rate is further increased for commercialization, or a large amount of low-quality content is introduced for growth, the deterioration of the community atmosphere may accelerate.

This is a chronic risk that will not break out overnight, but if not handled properly, it will erode the core assets of Station B like a "frog boiling in warm water".

### Risk 2: Risk of slowdown in advertising growth (Probability: Medium | Impact: High)

Advertising is the core engine of current growth. The advertising growth rate in 2025 is 23%. If the growth rate drops below 15% in 2026, market expectations will be hit. Advertising growth depends on: macroeconomics (advertising is a cyclical industry), advertising budgets in the gaming and AI industries (short-term dividends may fade), and improvements in advertising system efficiency (technical investment takes time).

### Risk 3: Game business fluctuation risk (probability: high | impact: medium)

The gaming business is highly dependent on hit hits. In 2025, "Three Kingdoms: Conquering the World" and "Escape from Duckkov" will be two hit hits, but this kind of luck cannot be replicated every year. If no new games of the same magnitude are launched in 2026-2027, game revenue may fluctuate significantly.

### Risk four: regulatory and policy risks (probability: low-medium | impact: medium-high)

China continues to tighten regulations on Internet content. As a content platform, Bilibili faces: content review costs (manpower + AI review), copyright compliance costs, impact of minors protection policies, and possible new regulatory requirements. Although there are currently no major regulatory actions against Bilibili individuals, uncertainty in the policy environment exists for a long time.

### Risk five: Sino-US relations and VIE structural risks (probability: low | impact: high)

Station B is dual-listed in the United States and Hong Kong and adopts a VIE structure. If Sino-US relations deteriorate further, or the Chinese government takes restrictive measures on the VIE structure, it may have an impact on the company's listing status. However, Bilibili has been listed for the second time in Hong Kong, which has hedged the risk of delisting of US stocks to a certain extent.

### Risk 6: Risk of intensified competition (probability: medium-high | impact: medium)

The penetration of Douyin into medium- and long-form videos, the homogenization of content due to AI lowering the threshold for content creation, and the competition for UP owners on other platforms may all erode Bilibili’s competitive advantage.

### Risk 7: Uncertainty of AI investment (Probability: Medium | Impact: Medium)

Management announced an increase in AI R&D investment of 500 million to 1 billion in 2026. If AI investment fails to effectively improve advertising efficiency and user experience, this expenditure will drag down profits. On the other hand, if AI empowerment is successful, it may become a catalyst for a jump in profit margins.

## 5. Conditions for invalidation of investment papers

The triggering of any of the following conditions means that the investment thesis needs to be re-examined:

1. **Advertising revenue growth rate is less than 10% for two consecutive quarters:** This shows that the momentum of commercialization has weakened and the core logic of profit margin improvement has been broken.
2. **MAU or DAU has declined year-on-year:** This indicates that the user growth story is over and the community may be deteriorating.
3. **The average daily usage time drops below 100 minutes:** User stickiness is the core indicator of Bilibili, and a decline means losing ground in competition.
4. **Large-scale management reduction or resignation of senior executives:** Insiders are not optimistic, and outsiders should not be optimistic.
5. **Adjusted net profit fell sequentially for two consecutive quarters:** The trend of profit margin improvement reversed
