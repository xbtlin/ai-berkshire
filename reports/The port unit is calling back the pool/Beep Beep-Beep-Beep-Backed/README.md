## Research background

Bilibili is a video community platform for Generation Z in China, with Danmaku culture and PUGV (Professional User Generated Video) content as its core. It will achieve full-year profitability for the first time in 2025, but there is still a big gap in commercialization efficiency compared with Douyin and Kuaishou. The current PE is about 30 times (adjusted net profit caliber). The market disagreement is: Is the community attribute of Bilibili a ceiling for commercialization, or is it a gold mine of value that has not yet been released?
