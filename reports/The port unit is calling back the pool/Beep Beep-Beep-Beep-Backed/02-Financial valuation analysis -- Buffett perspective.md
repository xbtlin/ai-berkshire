#bilibili: Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, intrinsic value
> Core question: After the first profit, can this "making ability" be sustained? Are current prices cheap or expensive?

---

## 1. Financial Overview: Transition from Huge Loss to Meager Profit

Bilibili will achieve full-year GAAP profitability for the first time in 2025, which is a milestone event. But we need to look at this "profit" calmly - it is a small profit, far from "strong earning power".

**Key Financial Data 2025:**

| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|--------|---------|
| Total revenue | 30.35 billion | 26.83 billion | +13.1% |
| Gross profit | 11.11 billion | 8.77 billion | +26.7% |
| Gross profit margin | 36.6% | 32.7% | +3.9pp |
| GAAP net profit | 1.19 billion | Loss | Turnaround |
| Adjusted net profit | 2.59 billion | ~540 million | + approximately 380% |
| Operating cash flow | 7.1 billion | ~5.5 billion | +about 29% |

Judging from the data, Bilibili’s financial situation is indeed improving rapidly. The gross profit margin increased from 32.7% to 36.6%, indicating that the quality of income is improving - the proportion of advertising business has increased (advertising is a high-margin business) and cost control has taken effect. However, the GAAP net profit of 1.19 billion corresponds to the revenue of 30.35 billion, and the net profit rate is only 3.9%. Even using the adjusted caliber of 2.59 billion, the net interest rate is only 8.5%. Compared with the 20-30% net profit margin of mature Internet companies such as Tencent and NetEase, there is a huge gap.

## 2. Income quality analysis

**Sustainability of Revenue Growth:**

Revenue growth in 2025 is 13.1%, which has slowed down significantly compared to the high growth rate of more than 30% in previous years. Breaking it down:

- **Advertising (+23%):** The fastest growing rate and is the core driving force. However, the size of 10.06 billion is still small in China’s Internet advertising market (Douyin’s advertising revenue exceeds 300 billion). There is huge room for growth but fierce competition.
- **Value-added Services (+8%):** The growth rate is slowing down, and it is difficult to increase the penetration rate of major members. It is difficult for this sector to become a growth engine in the future.
- **Games (+14%):** Benefited from "Three Kingdoms: Conquering the World" and "Escape from Duckkov", but game revenue is highly volatile and cannot be linearly extrapolated.
- **IP Derivatives (-3%):** is shrinking and no effective model has been found yet.

A structural change worthy of attention is that the proportion of advertising revenue will increase from approximately 28% in 2023 to 33% in 2025, while value-added services will decrease from approximately 43% to 39%. This means that Bilibili's revenue structure is shifting from "charging users" to "charging advertisers", which is a more sustainable direction - because advertisers are willing to pay to reach high-value young users, while users' willingness to pay is relatively limited.

**Earning Quality Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Revenue is growing and the structure is optimizing, but the growth rate is slowing down, the game business is volatile, IP derivatives are declining, and the overall revenue quality is medium.

## 3. Profit rate and cost structure

The gross profit margin of 36.6% is relatively low among Internet companies. The main cost components include:

- **Income sharing cost:** The sharing to UP hosts and anchors is the largest cost item. As the creator ecosystem grows, this part of the cost will become rigid.
- **Content cost:** Investment in self-made content and copyright procurement.
- **Bandwidth and server costs:** The inherent cost of the video platform increases with the growth of users.

The good news is that gross profit margin is improving rapidly (about 28% in 2023, 32.7% in 2024, and 36.6% in 2025), indicating that Station B has made progress in controlling costs. The application of AI technology (more than 50% of effective advertising materials are generated by AIGC) is expected to further reduce operating costs.

**Operating leverage:** Station B has passed the period of large-scale investment. The growth rate of R&D and sales expenses is lower than the revenue growth rate, and operating leverage has begun to be released. Management stated that it will invest an additional 500 million to 1 billion in AI research and development in 2026, which may suppress profit margins in the short term, but if the output is effective, it will help improve efficiency in the medium and long term.

**Margin Trend Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

The trend of profit margin improvement is clear, but the absolute level is still low and requires continued tracking.

## 4. Cash flow and balance sheet

**Cash flow:**

Operating cash flow in 2025 is 7.1 billion yuan, which is a more convincing figure than net profit. The operating cash flow of 7.1 billion means that Bilibili’s business is truly generating cash and does not rely on accounting adjustments. Free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capital expenditures) should also be positive, which is an important sign of financial health.

**Balance Sheet:**

As of Q3 2025, Bilibili holds a total of 23.49 billion yuan (approximately US$3.3 billion) in cash, time deposits and short-term investments. Considering that the company's market capitalization is approximately HK$73.5 billion (approximately US$9.4 billion), and cash accounts for approximately 35% of the market capitalization, this is a very thick cash cushion.

Station B still has debts such as convertible bonds, but the overall debt level is controllable. What's important is that the company has money in hand - no refinancing is needed, and it won't dilute shareholders.

**Share repurchase:** A US$200 million buyback plan was approved in November 2024, and US$116.4 million has been repurchased as of Q3 2025. In May 2025, HK$783 million was repurchased in a single day. In addition, in early 2026, management announced a new buyback plan of up to US$500 million, and Hong Kong stocks once rose by more than 10%. A positive sign of buybacks is that management believes the stock price is undervalued and is willing to use real money to reward shareholders.

**Balance Sheet Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

It has abundant cash, controllable liabilities, active shareholder return policy, and high financial safety margin.

## 5. Valuation analysis

**Current Valuation Level:**

| Valuation Metrics | Data |
|----------|------|
| Hong Kong stock price | ~176.60 Hong Kong dollars |
| Market capitalization | ~HK$73.5 billion / ~US$9.4 billion |
| Adjusted PE in 2025 | ~28 times (HKD 73.5 billion / adjusted net profit of HKD 2.59 billion) |
| 2025 GAAP PE | ~62 times (HK$73.5 billion / net profit of 1.19 billion) |
| PS (price to sales ratio) | ~2.4 times (HKD 73.5 billion / revenue of 30.35 billion) |
| EV after net cash | ~HK$50 billion |
| EV/Adjusted Net Profit | ~19x |

Note: The above is a rough estimate, the exchange rate is calculated as 1 US dollar = 7.8 Hong Kong dollars.

**Valuation Discussion:**

The adjusted PE of 28 times is not outrageous for an Internet company that has just achieved profitability and its profit margin is still rising, but it is not cheap either. The key question is: to what extent can profits grow in the future?

If we use a more optimistic assumption - adjusted net profit will increase by 40-50% to 3.6-3.9 billion yuan in 2026, the corresponding forward-looking PE will be about 19-20 times. Including the 23.5 billion in cash on hand, the EV/profit after deducting cash is about 14-15 times. This valuation has certain appeal among Chinese Internet companies.

But if profit growth is less than expected (for example, the game business slows down, advertising growth slows down), PE may look expensive. The median target price given by analysts is about HK$242 (+37% upside), and Goldman Sachs' target price is HK$240, with most giving it a "buy" rating.

**Comparison with peers:**

| Company | PE (adjusted) | PS | Growth rate |
|------|-------------|-----|------|
| Bilibili | ~28x | ~2.4x | 13% |
| Kuaishou | ~15x | ~2.5x | 12% |
| Tencent | ~20x | ~7x | 8% |
| NetEase | ~14x | ~3.5x | 7% |

In comparison, Bilibili's PE is higher than Kuaishou and NetEase, mainly because the market gives a premium to its room for profit margin improvement. But if the growth rate cannot be maintained above 15%, this premium will be difficult to sustain.

## 6. Intrinsic value estimation

Use the simplified DCF idea to make a rough estimate:

**Assumptions:**
- Revenue growth rate from 2026 to 2028: 15%, 12%, 10%
- Adjusted net profit margin in 2028: 12% (continuous improvement from 8.5% in 2025)
- Adjusted net profit in 2028: approximately 4.7 billion yuan
- Give 20 times PE, corresponding to a market value of approximately 94 billion yuan
- Including net cash of approximately NT$20 billion, the total value is approximately NT$114 billion
- Discounted to the present (8% discount rate, 2.5 years), approximately 95 billion yuan
- Corresponding to approximately HK$240 per share

That's close to the median analyst price target. However, this estimate is based on the optimistic assumption that profit margins will continue to increase, and any deviation in execution will significantly affect the results.

**Conservative scenario (margin improvement blocked):**
- Adjusted net profit margin in 2028: 9%
- Net profit in 2028: approximately 3.6 billion yuan
- 15 times PE + net cash, with a total value of approximately 74 billion yuan
- Corresponding to approximately HK$190 per share

**Valuation Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The current valuation is neither expensive nor cheap, and is above a reasonable range. If you are optimistic about the story of continued improvement in profit margins, there is 30-40% upside potential; if you are cautious, the margin of safety is insufficient.

For value investors, the biggest question is not whether Bilibili can make money - it has proven that it can - but how much money it can make and where is the ceiling of profit margins. If the final steady-state net interest rate is only 8-10%, the current valuation is not cheap; if it can be increased to 12-15%, there is room for better returns.
