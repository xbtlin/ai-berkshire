## 2. Investment thesis

### Core bullish logic

**1. A scarce target for global IP design retail. ** Globally, there is almost no other company that can simultaneously possess "150+ global IP authorization network + thousands of Chinese supply chains + 8,485 global stores + the ability to launch 550 new SKUs per month". Miniso has a first-mover advantage in a new industry that is being formed.

**2. Overseas growth space is far from peaking. ** 3,213 stores overseas and 503 stores in the United States in 2025, while management’s long-term goal is 40,000 stores worldwide. Even with a 50% discount (20,000 stores), the current number of stores is only 42% of the ultimate goal. The brand's premium capability and growth rate in overseas markets (same-store growth in mid-to-high double digits in North America) far exceed those in China.

**3. The financial quality of the main business can withstand inspection. ** 45% gross profit margin, 25% ROE, 1.58 billion yuan of free cash flow, 65.8% shareholder return - behind these numbers is a truly profitable business.

**4. Valuation is a discount to growth expectations. ** It is about 14 times PE based on adjusted profits and about 10 times forward PE based on expected profits in 2026. The valuation is not expensive for a company with a growth rate of 20%+. The discount given by the market mainly reflects the concerns of Yonghui Investment.

### Core bearish logic

**1. Yonghui Supermarket is a dangerous non-core asset. ** The 6.27 billion yuan acquisition of a traditional supermarket that continued to lose money caused borrowings to surge 12 times (<600 million to 7.5 billion), the asset-liability ratio doubled (43% to 62%), and reported profits were cut in half. This investment has no obvious synergy with Miniso’s core competency circle, and there are few successful global examples of traditional supermarket transformation.

**2. The founder’s tendency to take risks is troubling. ** Yonghui acquisition, 80% store reopening plan, TOP TOY spin-off and listing - every step taken by Ye Guofu is bold, but with 63% absolute control and lack of effective governance checks and balances, "boldness" is both an advantage and a risk.

**3. The ceiling of the domestic market is looming. ** In Q1 of 2025, 111 net domestic stores were closed, and the growth rate (16.8%) was significantly lower than overseas (29.3%). Miniso has entered a mature stage domestically, and its future growth is highly dependent on overseas markets - and overseas growth faces uncontrollable factors such as geopolitics.

**4. Over-reliance on IP strategy. ** Management has set the IP share target at more than 80%, which means that the fate of Miniso is highly bound to "IP consumption popularity." Once IP consumption cools down or licensing costs rise significantly, the economics of the entire business model will be challenged.

## 4. Investment advice

### Overall qualitative judgment

Miniso is a company whose main business is of excellent quality but comes with significant non-core risks**. If you only look at Miniso's main IP design retail business, it is a good business worth holding for the long term - it has huge space for globalization, a deep moat, and strong profitability. However, Yonghui Supermarket’s investment has cast a shadow of uncertainty over the entire investment project.

### Operation suggestions

**It is currently suitable to be included in the close watch list and wait for a better opportunity to build a position. ** Specifically:

**Ideal buying conditions (meet 2-3 of them):**
1. Yonghui Supermarket will turn around or significantly reduce losses in 2026
2. The stock price falls below 10 times the adjusted PE due to short-term negative effects (such as fluctuations in single-quarter performance)
3. The asset-liability ratio begins to fall
4. Quarterly same-store sales in overseas markets continued to maintain double-digit growth

**Ideal Selling/Avoidance Conditions:**
1. Yonghui Supermarket continues to suffer heavy losses and Miniso needs additional investment
2. Overseas market growth plummeted to single digits
3. Ye Guofu launches new large-scale non-core investment
4. Systemic policy risks emerge in the U.S. market

### Position suggestions

- **If you choose to open a position:** Considering the risk factors, it is recommended to tentatively open a position with a small position (2-3%), and then decide whether to add a position after the key variables become clear.
- **If you choose to wait:** Set price reminders (adjusted PE drops below 10 times) and event reminders (Yonghui's 2026 semi-annual report, Miniso's 2026 interim report).

## 6. Summary

Miniso is a company that is transforming from "China's 10-yuan store" into a "global IP design retail group". This change is in the right direction, and the execution so far is good - 8,485 stores worldwide, revenue of 21.4 billion, gross profit margin of 45%, and ROE of 25%. These data tell the story of a good business.

But the decision to acquire Yonghui Supermarket for 6.27 billion yuan added a discordant footnote to this story. It not only caused reported profits to halve and the asset-liability ratio to double, but also exposed the founder’s tendency to take risks when investing outside his circle of competence. In a company where the founders own 63% of the shares and lack effective governance checks and balances, this tendency is itself a risk that needs to be priced in.

**Final judgment: Good business, good location, good growth, but not good enough capital allocation and not safe enough. ** Add to watch list and wait for catalysts or better prices before taking action.
