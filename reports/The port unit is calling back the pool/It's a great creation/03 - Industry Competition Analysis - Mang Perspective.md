# I. Definition of industry: which track exactly is the first prize?

The industry of the best is not easy to attribute. It cuts across at least three overlapping markets:

**1. Global Dwellings Retail Market**
Global retail sales of living goods are trillions of dollars, usually growing at low-digit rates. In this market, the best-known matches include Japan’s Big Bang (Daiso), the US Dollar Tree/Five Below, and Northern Europe’s Flying Tiger.

**2. Global IP-authorized derivatives market**
Global retailing of authorized commodities totals over $300 billion, with IP derivatives being the fastest growing segment. In this market, the competitors for the best are Disney Homes, Hot Topic, BoxLunch, etc.

**3. China Tide Market**
In 2023, China’s tidal market was about 60 billion yuan, and is expected to reach 110 billion yuan in 2026, an annual increase of more than 20%. The main players in this market are Bubble Matt (first-tier), TOP TOY (second-tier) and 52 TOYS, X11, Brooke (third-tier).

** The unique feature of the original is that it does not belong entirely to any track, but rather opens its own place at the intersection of the three tracks.** This is both an advantage (differentiated competition, avoiding front-loading) and a challenge (no ready-made model of success).

# II. Sources of competitive advantage: five force analysis

The competition for the best in the name of the "Port Five Power Model" is being dismantled:

##1. Vendor bargaining power: weak (in favour of branding excellence)

With the size of the procurement and the digital supply chain management system, the branded excellence has a strong bargaining power on suppliers. The speed of entry of about 550 new SKUs per month means that the cost of supplier replacement is extremely low.

##2. Buyer bargaining power: medium

Consumers buy low amounts (about $30-50 per unit) and change costs almost zero. But the best is the difference created by IP products — you cannot buy the same three-gull cups that you designed elsewhere.

#3 New entrants Threat: Medium Low

The entry threshold does not seem to be high (it is not difficult to open a living department store), but it is almost impossible to replicate the full range of capabilities (150+IP enabling relationships, 1,000 supplier networks, operating systems for 8,485 global shops, and new systems for 711 products) in the short term.

##4. Alternative threat: medium height

If consumers value only "favour", the advantage of "favourable" is diminished. If consumers value "scrowing experience plus IP design immediately," the alternative is limited.

5. Intra-industry competition: intense

Domestic markets: faced with multiple competitions from other IP retail brands, tidal brands (bubbles Matts), and online channels. The net closure of 111 shops in Q1 in 2025 suggests that competitive pressures are increasing.

Overseas markets: competition is relatively relaxed. The "China Supply Chain + Global IP+ Low Price" combination, which is a great success, is almost non-direct against the target in most overseas markets.

# Four, Reverse Thinking: When the investment argument fails

This is the most important part. Think about negative scenarios without prejudice:

Scenario one: loss of IP heat

The entire transformation strategy of the original good is marked by "IP consumption growth." High Maori and brand premiums for the original good will be hit if young consumers are less enthusiastic about IP-based products – such as the US fatigue caused by the proliferation of IPs or the economy’s downside return to purely functional purchases.

** Probability assessment: ** Low. Global IP consumption trends have been going on for decades (from Disney to Hello Kitty to Poco Dream) and are permeating towards a wider range of products. But the risk of IP strategy implementation remains.

Scenario two: globalization is blocked

If Chinese-American relations deteriorate further, leading to policy barriers to the United States market for outstanding excellence (like TikTok’s) or to a significant increase in tariff escalation, the engine of growth for the most outstanding could be out. The US is now the most important market for the most outstanding overseas (503 shops at the end of 2025), with any systemic risk having a significant impact.

** Probability assessment: ** Medium. ** Geopolitical risks are real and unpredictable. But the political sensitivity of the product of the best (life goods) is much lower than that of the technologically advanced product, and the probability of being targeted is relatively low.

# Scenario three: Never-failed supermarket becomes a bottomless hole

If the market continues to lose, it will not only continue to consume profits from its famous creation, but also drain management’s energy and distract attention from its main business. Worse still, if the prize requires additional investment to save it, capital allocation will deteriorate further.

** Probability assessment: ** medium-high. There are few success stories in the transformation of traditional supermarkets worldwide, with Inguo losing $2.55 billion in 2025. The 2026 Q1 twist is a positive sign, but one quarter does not explain the problem.

# Scenario four: collapse of the union system

If the upgrading of the door shop results in a significant increase in inputs but does not yield the expected returns, a large number of the partners will withdraw from the losses, which could trigger a chain reaction. The net closure of 111 doors in Q1 in 2025 may be an early signal.

** Probability assessment: ** Low. The branded excellence of the union system has been operating for over a decade, with some risk resistance. Management is also actively adjusting (closing down inefficient shops, upgrading high-product shops), which is a healthy optimization process. But continuous monitoring of sales and union satisfaction data is required.

# Five, the final business

** What is the final end of the IP design retailing track for the next 5-10 years?**

** Most likely scenario:** The global formation of two to three niche IP design retail platforms, one of which is one of the first excellence. These platforms have three main elements of IP-authorized network plus supply chain plus global door, and form a winner-takes pattern. The goal of the world's first IP design retail group is achievable in this scenario.

** Support logic:**
1. IP Authors are more willing to work with the larger platform (distribution is efficient)
2. Cost advantages of supply chain scale effects
3. Global door-to-door network constitutes a barrier to access
4. Sustained consumer demand for IP consumption

** The potential for subversion of online channels should also be considered.** If future IP consumption is shifted mainly to the line (e.g., through AR/VR technology to achieve immersion IP experience), the value of the underline shop may be diminished.
