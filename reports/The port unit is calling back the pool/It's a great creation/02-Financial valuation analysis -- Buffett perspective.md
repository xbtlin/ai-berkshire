#MINISO (9896.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital efficiency, cash flow, financial health, valuation rationality
> Core question: Do the financial data reflect a good business that actually makes money? Do current prices provide a margin of safety?

---

## 1. Revenue growth: speed and quality

**Full-year revenue in 2025 will be 21.444 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 26.2%. ** From the perspective of growth rate, this is a company still in the high growth stage.

**Split Growth Source:**

| Business Segment | Revenue in 2025 | Year-on-year growth rate |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Miniso China | ~10.9 billion yuan | +16.8% |
| Miniso Overseas | ~12.34 billion yuan | +29.3% |
| TOP TOY | ~1.92 billion yuan | +94.8% |

A few key observations:

1. **Overseas income has exceeded domestic income. ** Overseas revenue will account for more than 57% in 2025, which means that Miniso is no longer a story of "Chinese companies ordering goods overseas", but a truly global enterprise.
2. **Domestic growth has slowed but remains healthy. ** The growth rate of 16.8% is at an upper-middle level in the current consumption environment, but the net closure of 111 domestic stores in Q1 is a signal worthy of attention.
3. **TOP TOY has an amazing growth rate but a small base. ** The growth rate of 94.8% is very impressive, but the volume of 1.92 billion yuan accounts for less than 10% of the group, and its contribution to the total market is limited.

**Q1 data in 2026 is even more eye-catching:** The overall revenue growth rate is no less than 25%, China Miniso GMV has increased by more than 25% year-on-year, and North American GMV has increased by more than 50% year-on-year. The full-year guidance given by management for 2026 is "high-teens growth", that is, 15-19%, which is relatively conservative.

### Rating: ★★★★☆

The growth rate is healthy, and overseas contributions continue to increase, but the trend of domestic growth slowdown needs to be continuously tracked.

## 2. Profitability: The jump in gross profit margin and the differentiation of net profit margin

**The continued improvement in gross profit margin is one of the most important financial stories of Miniso. **

| Year | Gross profit margin | Change |
|------|--------|------|
| 2022 | 34.9% | - |
| 2023 | 41.2% | +6.3pct |
| 2024 | 44.9% | +3.7pct |
| 2025 | 45.0% | +0.1pct |

The gross profit margin will increase from 35% in 2022 to 45% in 2025, an increase of 10 percentage points in three years, which is a very significant improvement. Driving factors include: an increase in the proportion of IP products (the gross profit margin of IP products is higher than that of ordinary products), an increase in the proportion of overseas revenue (the gross profit margin of overseas products is higher than that of domestic products), and the continued optimization of supply chain efficiency.

**However, there is a clear differentiation in net profit margin:**

- **Adjusted net profit:** 2.898 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 6.5%, and the adjusted net profit rate is approximately 13.5%
- **Reported net profit:** 1.210 billion yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 54.1%

**The core reason for the difference: Yonghui Supermarket’s investment losses. ** In 2025, Miniso will confirm a loss of approximately 813 million yuan due to its investment in Yonghui Supermarket. In addition, the large amount of new borrowings for the acquisition of Yonghui Supermarket led to a significant increase in interest expenses.

**How do you view this differentiation? ** If Yonghui Investment is regarded as the impact of a "one-time" decision, the profitability of Miniso's main business is actually continuing to improve. But Yonghui Investment is not a one-time impact - it is an ongoing balance sheet burden that will continue to impact cash flow and income statements for years to come.

### Rating: ★★★☆☆

The profitability of the main business is excellent and continues to improve, but the negative impact of Yonghui Investment cannot be ignored. The huge difference between adjusted profits and reported profits requires investors to judge for themselves which one better reflects true profitability.

## 3. Capital efficiency: ROE and capital allocation

**ROE is about 25%, which is an excellent level in the retail industry. ** Sources of high ROE:

1. **Asset-light model:** Mainly franchise, low proportion of fixed assets
2. **High gross profit + fast turnover:** The average selling price of the product is low but the turnover is fast, and the gross profit margin continues to increase.
3. **Moderate leverage:** The asset-liability ratio will be approximately 43% in 2024, rising to 62% in 2025 due to Yonghui’s acquisition

**The pros and cons of capital allocation:**

| Capital allocation direction | Amount/proportion | Evaluation |
|-------------|----------|------|
| Shareholder return (2025) | 1.907 billion yuan (65.8% of adjusted net profit) | Excellent, demonstrating shareholder friendliness |
| Among them: dividends | 1.358 billion yuan | Dividend rate is about 3% |
| Among them: buyback | 549 million yuan | record high |
| Yonghui Supermarket Investment | 6.27 billion yuan | Extremely controversial |
| Store expansion/upgrade | - | Core investment direction |

**Yonghui Investment is the biggest failure in capital allocation. ** The investment of 6.27 billion yuan not only confirmed a loss of 800 million in 2025, but also caused borrowings to soar from less than 600 million to 7.5 billion, and the asset-liability ratio rose from 43% to 62%. This investment severely consumes capital that could have been used for the expansion of the main business or shareholder returns.

**But we must also see the positive side:** In Q1 2026, Yonghui Supermarket achieved a net profit of 287 million yuan, a year-on-year increase of 94.4% (although the base is low). If Yonghui can turn around its losses, the drag on Miniso will gradually be reduced.

### Rating: ★★★☆☆

The main business has excellent capital efficiency and generous shareholder returns. However, Yonghui Investment seriously lowered its overall capital allocation score.

## 4. Cash flow: real money or paper profits

**Operating cash flow in 2025 is 2.578 billion yuan, and free cash flow is 1.580 billion yuan. ** This is a very important positive sign.

**Quality Analysis of Cash Flow:**

1. **Operating cash flow/adjusted net profit = 89%,** indicating that the profit is supported by real cash flow
2. **Free cash flow/adjusted net profit = 55%,** there is still sufficient free cash flow after deducting capital expenditures
3. **Cash flow advantages of the franchise model:** Franchisees bear store investment, and the capital expenditure of the headquarters is relatively light

**But the balance sheet changes are concerning:**

| Indicators | End 2024 | End 2025 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Long-term and short-term borrowings | <600 million yuan | 7.51 billion yuan |
| Asset-liability ratio | 42.85% | 62.23% |

It only took one year to go from an "almost zero debt" company to a "highly leveraged" company. Although this is mainly caused by Yonghui's acquisition, high leverage means reduced financial flexibility and a weakened ability to deal with risks.

### Rating: ★★★★☆

The cash flow quality of the main business is excellent, but the balance sheet has deteriorated significantly due to the acquisition of Yonghui.

## 5. Valuation analysis: Is it expensive?

There is a "perspective difference" in the valuation of Miniso:

| Valuation method | Numerical value | Reference significance |
|----------|------|----------|
| PE (reported profit) | ~29x | Including Yonghui's losses, on the high side |
| PE (adjusted profit) | ~14x | Excluding one-time effects, more reference |
| Forward PE (2026E) | ~10x | Based on consensus market expectations |
| Dividend yield | ~3% | Above average |
| PEG | <1 | Reasonable valuation after considering growth rate |

**Comparison to comparable companies:**

| Company | PE (TTM) | Growth | ROE |
|------|----------|------|-----|
| Miniso (adjusted) | ~14x | ~26% | ~25% |
| Bubble Mart | ~30x | ~50% | ~30% |
| Dollar Tree | ~15x | ~5% | ~15% |
| Five Below | ~20x | ~10% | ~18% |

**Valuation Judgment:**

If it is believed that the impact of Yonghui Investment is temporary and Miniso's main business will return to normal profit growth in 2026-2027, then the current growth rate of approximately 14 times adjusted PE + 25% ROE + high teens, the valuation is attractive. Forward PE of about 10 times means that the market has given a large discount, implying concerns about Yonghui's risks and slowing growth.

If you believe that Yonghui Investment will continue to be a drag and Miniso's high growth is difficult to maintain, then a PE of 29 times reported profit is not cheap.

**Key assumptions:** Whether the valuation is attractive ultimately depends on two judgments - whether Yonghui Investment can stop the bleeding, and whether overseas high growth can be sustained for 3-5 years.

### Rating: ★★★★☆

In terms of adjusted profits, the valuation is reasonably low, but the premise is that Yonghui's impact is believed to be limited and overseas growth is sustainable.

**One sentence summary:** The financial quality of Minso's main business is excellent - high growth, high gross profit, high ROE, and strong cash flow. However, the 6.27 billion yuan acquisition of Yonghui Supermarket was a major capital allocation error, which caused reported profits to be cut in half and the asset-liability ratio to double. What investors need to judge is whether the negative impact of this investment will be absorbed over time or whether it will become a long-term drag. If the answer is the former, current valuations offer a decent margin of safety.
