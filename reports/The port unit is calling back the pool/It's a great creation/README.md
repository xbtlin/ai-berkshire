## Core Observation

**Positive factors:** Strong momentum of global expansion, remarkable results in IP strategic transformation, profound supply chain advantages, high ROE + high shareholder return.

**Negative factors:** Yonghui Investment dragged down reported profits, the asset-liability ratio rose sharply due to acquisitions, domestic same-store growth was under pressure, and the franchise model was controversial.
