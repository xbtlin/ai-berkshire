## 2. Core investment logic

### Look at the bull case

**1. Extremely undervalued and sufficient margin of safety**

Link currently trades at a discount to NAV of 35%+ and a dividend yield of 6.6%+, which are extreme positions in its 20-year listing history. Before 2019, Link traded at a premium to NAV all year round. Current pricing already implies the following pessimistic assumptions:
- Hong Kong’s retail market permanently shrinks
- NAV will continue to decline
- Management changes may cause problems

If any of these concerns prove to be overly pessimistic, the stock price has significant room to recover upward.

**2. Direct beneficiaries of the interest rate cut cycle**

REITs are interest rate sensitive assets and benefit from interest rate cuts through two channels:
- Declining financing costs increase distributable income (down 7.3% in 2025/2026H1)
-Capitalization rate compression drives NAV recovery

**3. Bottom line protection for necessities consumption**

About 60% of Link's rent comes from necessities such as wet markets, supermarkets and restaurants. This part of demand will not disappear due to consumption going north or the penetration of e-commerce. This resilience is demonstrated by an occupancy rate of 97.6% and a rent-to-sale ratio of 13.0%.

**4. Incremental catalysis of interconnection**

If REITs are included in the Shanghai-Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect, Link REIT, as the largest REIT in Asia-Pacific, will be one of the preferred targets for mainland funds. The influx of incremental funds could quickly narrow the NAV discount.

**5. Growth contribution from overseas portfolio**

The operating data of Australia (occupancy rate 98.1%, lease renewal +16.3%) and Singapore (occupancy rate 99.8%, lease renewal +12.9%) are much better than those in Hong Kong. As the overseas share increases, the overall quality of the portfolio is expected to improve.

### Bear Case

**1. Hong Kong’s retail sales may be experiencing a long-term structural decline**

Going north for consumption is not a short-term phenomenon. The Shenzhen-Hong Kong high-speed railway, the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge, and the popularity of digital payments—infrastructure are making cross-border consumption more and more convenient. If this trend accelerates, Link's non-essential retail tenants may face more serious operating pressure, and the renewal rent adjustment rate may be negative for a long time.

**2. NAV may not have bottomed out yet**

S&P forecasts that property valuations will suffer another moderate loss of 2-4% per year in fiscal years 2025-2027. If the capitalization rate continues to expand, NAV may further drop from HK$63.30 to HK$55-58. A 35% NAV discount seems safe, but if NAV itself is shrinking, the margin of safety is changing dynamically.

**3. The management window period increases risks**

The joint CEO model, the permanent CEO is undecided, and whether Hongchoy's strategic legacy can be fully inherited - these uncertainties may last 6-12 months. During this period, if the external environment deteriorates, Link, which lacks strong leadership, may miss the window to respond.

**4. Mainland property is a drag that cannot be ignored**

Lease renewal -16.4% is a big number. There are no signs of a complete solution to the problems of oversupply and weak consumption in mainland commercial real estate in the short term. Mainland properties (accounting for 13.8%) may continue to drag down overall performance in the next 1-2 years.

**5. Dividend growth may stall**

For investors relying on DPU growth, Link may not be attractive enough. DPU growth has slowed from +5.4% in 2023/2024 to +3.7% in 2024/2025, and may remain flat or even decline slightly in 2025/2026.

## 5. Comprehensive research and judgment

### Investment attribute positioning

Link is a defensive high-interest-earning asset, not a growth investment target.

Its investment appeal mainly comes from:
- Dividend yield of 6.6%+ provides impressive cash returns
- 35%+ NAV discount provides downward safety cushion
- Rate-cutting cycle and potential catalysts offer potential for valuation repair

Its investment risks mainly come from:
- The structural downturn in Hong Kong retail sales may continue
- Stagnant DPU growth leaves it lacking a "growth premium"
- Management changes increase short-term uncertainty

### What type of investors is it suitable for?

| Investor Type | Suitability | Rationale |
|-----------|----------|------|
| Pursuing stable cash returns | ★★★★★ | 6.6%+ dividend rate, stable dividend payment record |
| Pursuing valuation restoration | ★★★★☆ | NAV discount is at a historical extreme, but the restoration time is uncertain |
| Pursuing rapid capital appreciation | ★★☆☆☆ | Lack of growth drivers and limited stock price elasticity |
| Pursuit of high certainty | ★★★☆☆ | Management changes and retail decline have increased uncertainty |

### Suggested rating

**Accumulate on Weakness**

| Dimensions | Judgment |
|------|------|
| Business model | Passed - deep moat, bottom line protection for necessities consumption |
| Financial Quality | Passed - Strong balance sheet, excellent credit rating |
| Valuation levels | Providing a margin of safety through – NAV discount and dividend yield |
| Management | TBD - Uncertainty during the transition period requires more time to observe |
| Industry Trends | Neutral - Headwinds and tailwinds coexist |

**Reason for not giving a "Buy Now" rating**: Management change is a real risk, and the selection of a permanent CEO and strategic direction have not been clear yet. Until this critical uncertainty is lifted, a healthy dose of caution is reasonable.

**Reason for not giving an "Avoid" rating**: The NAV discount of 35%+ and the dividend rate of 6.6%+ have provided sufficient compensation for the risk. If the stock price falls further to the HK$35-37 range (near the 52-week low), the margin of safety will become more substantial.

## 7. One sentence summary

** Link is a high-quality defensive asset priced "on the ground" by the market - the NAV discount of 35%+ has reflected most of the bad news, and the dividend rate of 6.6%+ provides the rewards that have been awaited. If you have the patience to wait for catalysts (interest rate cuts, interconnection, new CEO appointment), the current price is a range worthy of gradually building a position; if you pursue certainty and quick returns, it is recommended to wait for more uncertainties to be eliminated. **
