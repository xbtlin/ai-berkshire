## Core positioning

The largest real estate investment trust in Asia Pacific, with Hong Kong community retail malls as its core, supplemented by retail, office and logistics assets in mainland China, Australia, Singapore and the United Kingdom. The geographical distribution of the property portfolio is: Hong Kong 74.4%, Mainland China 13.8%, and overseas 11.8%.
