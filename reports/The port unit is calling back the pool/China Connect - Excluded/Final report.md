## One sentence conclusion

China Unicom is the weakest choice among the three major operators - the lowest ROE, smallest scale, smallest dividend, and slowest growth. Unless the valuation is extremely cheap (PE < 10 times), China Mobile should always be preferred among the same industry.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic
1. 700 million Internet of Things connections + 65% share of Internet of Vehicles, which has differentiated value in the era of Internet of Everything
2. PB < 1 provides a certain level of asset protection
3. 5G private network growth rate is 51%, with potential for vertical industry applications
4. Institutional flexibility brought about by mixed-ownership reform (theoretically)

### Bearish logic
1. ROE 5.7% is the lowest among the three major operators, and the return on capital is poor
2. Revenue and profit growth rate is close to zero, lacking growth momentum
3. The dividend payout rate and dividend rate are the lowest among the three companies
4. Scale disadvantage is a structural shortcoming in capital-intensive industries
5. Peer selection (mobile and telecom) is superior in almost all dimensions

1. **PE drops below 10 times** (corresponding dividend rate 6%+)
2. **Internet of Things/Internet of Vehicles revenue doubled**, proving the value of differentiation
3. **Management increases dividend payout ratio to 70%+**
