# China Unicom (0762.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

## 1. Financial quality assessment

### Profitability
| Indicators | 2025 | 2024 | Evaluation |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Operating income | 392.2 billion yuan | 389.5 billion yuan | +0.7% |
| Service revenue | 347.7 billion yuan | 346 billion yuan | +0.5% |
| Total profit | 25.3 billion yuan | 25.1 billion yuan | +0.8% |
| Profit attributable to shareholders | 20.8 billion yuan | 20.6 billion yuan | +1.0% |
| ROE | 5.7% | 5.6% | Slight improvement |

**Profitability Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**

ROE of 5.7% is the lowest among the three major operators (China Mobile 12%, Telecom 7%). This means that China Unicom has the worst capital utilization efficiency - every 100 yuan of net assets can only create 5.7 yuan in profit. Net profit growth was only 1%, which was close to stagnant.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- Similar to telecommunications, operating cash flow is significantly higher than net profit (large depreciation)
- But free cash flow is suppressed by high capital expenditure
- The capital expenditure plan for 2026 is reduced to 50 billion yuan, which is expected to improve

### "Virtual and Real" of Profit Structure
China Unicom's "profit attributable to shareholders" on the basis of Hong Kong stocks is 20.8 billion yuan vs. "net profit attributable to shareholders" on the basis of A shares is 9.13 billion yuan. There is a huge difference. The main reason is the difference in accounting treatment (different consolidation standards). Investors need to pay attention to using a consistent caliber for valuation.

## 2. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level
- PE (TTM): ~15 times (based on Hong Kong stock caliber 20.8 billion yuan)
- PB: about 0.8-0.9 times
- Dividend yield: about 4%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 3.5x

### Historical valuation range
The historical PE of Unicom Hong Kong stocks fluctuates between 10-20 times, and the current 15 times is at the middle level.

### Horizontal comparison
| Operator | PE | ROE | Dividend Yield | PB |
|--------|------|------|--------|------|
| China Mobile | 13x | 12% | 6% | 1.5x |
| China Telecom | 16x | 7% | 5% | 1.1x |
| China Unicom | 15x | 5.7% | 4% | 0.85x |

China Unicom's valuation is "sandwiched in the middle", but its profitability is the weakest. From the perspective of ROE/PE, Mobile has the highest cost performance.

## 3. Buffett-style investment test

### 1. Can you understand the next 10 years?
**Basically OK. ** The structure of the communications industry is stable, and the coexistence of the three oligarchs will not change. However, China Unicom's relative position among the three may continue to weaken.

### 2. Does it have a lasting competitive advantage?
**Weaker than peers. ** Same license barriers, but user scale, network coverage, and financial strength are not as good as those of China Mobile and Telecom. The only differentiation is in the Internet of Things.

### 3. Is the management honest and competent?
**Neutral. ** The mixed-ownership reform introduced private capital and Internet thinking, but the actual effect of the system reform was limited. Slow profit growth suggests that execution needs to be strengthened.

### 4. Does the price have a margin of safety?
**Limited. **PE 15x is not cheap for a company with ROE 5.7%. PB of 0.85 times provides a certain level of asset protection, but ROE lower than PB means that value is slowly being lost.

## 4. Capital allocation efficiency

The core capital allocation problems faced by China Unicom:
- Maintaining network competitiveness requires continued high capital expenditures
- But high capital expenditure suppresses free cash flow and dividend capabilities
- Capital expenditure will be reduced to 50 billion yuan in 2026 (computing power accounts for 35%+), which is the right direction

Dividend policy:
- The dividend payout ratio is about 50%, lower than Telecom (75%) and China Mobile
- The dividend yield is about 4%, the lowest among the three
- This further reduces the attractiveness of China Unicom as a dividend stock

## 5. "Will Buffett buy it?"

Almost certainly: **not**.

Reason:
1. Not No. 1 in the industry - Buffett prefers industry leaders
2. ROE is too low – 5.7% is far lower than Buffett’s threshold of 15%+
3. Lack of pricing power – homogeneous competition among three companies
4. Growth is close to stagnant - revenue and profit growth is less than 1%
5. There is a significantly better peer choice - China Mobile

## 6. Core Conclusions

**Overall Rating: ★★☆☆☆(2/5)**

China Unicom has little investment appeal under Buffett's framework. It is not the cheapest (PE is not lower than China Mobile), not the most profitable (lowest ROE), not the fastest growing (revenue growth is the same as Telecom but with thin profits), nor is it the best dividend payer (lowest dividend rate).

Among the three major operators, China Unicom ranked last. The only possible buying logic is: if the Internet of Things/Internet of Vehicles business explodes beyond expectations, or if management significantly increases the dividend payout ratio, these are currently not baseline scenarios.
