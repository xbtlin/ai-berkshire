# China Unicom (0762.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: China United Network Communications Co., Ltd.
- **Stock Code**: 0762.HK
- **Industry**: Mobile Communications
- **PE(TTM)**: ~15x
- **ROE**：~5.7%

## 2025 core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Operating income | 392.2 billion yuan | +0.7% |
| Service revenue | 347.7 billion yuan | +0.5% |
| Net profit attributable to shareholders | 9.13 billion yuan (A shares) / 20.8 billion yuan attributable to shareholders (Hong Kong shares) | +1.0-1.1% |
| Return on equity | 5.7% | Stable |
| 5G private network revenue | 12.3 billion yuan | +51% |

## Investment Highlights
1. The dividends of mixed-ownership reform still exist, and we have in-depth cooperation with BAT
2. Rapid growth of 5G private network/Internet of Things/Internet of Vehicles
3. Increase the proportion of computing power investment to 35%+
4. Valuation is relatively reasonable among the three major operators

## Core Risks
1. ROE is only 5.7%, the lowest among the three major operators
2. The smallest number of mobile users (340 million), the scale disadvantage is obvious
3. Revenue growth almost stagnated (+0.7%)
4. Core profits are thin and growth momentum is insufficient.
