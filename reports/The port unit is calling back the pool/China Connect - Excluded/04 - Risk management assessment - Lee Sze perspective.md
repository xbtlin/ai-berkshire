# China Unicom (0762.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

## 1. Core risk identification

### Risk Matrix

| Risk type | Severity | Probability of occurrence | Comprehensive rating |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| The scale gap continues to widen | High | High | ★★★★☆ |
| Capital expenditure pressure (the arrival of 6G) | High | High | ★★★★☆ |
| Profit growth continues to stagnate | Medium | High | ★★★☆☆ |
| Pressure on investors to withdraw from mixed-ownership reform | Medium | Medium | ★★★☆☆ |
| Internet of Things commercialization falls short of expectations | Medium | Medium | ★★★☆☆ |
| Acceleration of C-side user churn | Medium | Low | ★★☆☆☆ |

### Detailed explanation of key risks

**1. Vicious cycle of scale disadvantage**
The smallest scale → the highest unit cost → the thinnest profit → the weakest investment ability → further lagging network quality → users are more likely to choose mobile/telecom. This cycle is difficult to break.

**2. Technology iteration investment burden**
Each generation of communication technology upgrades (5G → 5.5G → 6G) requires hundreds of billions of investments. For China Unicom, which has the thinnest profits, this is continued financial pressure. Although capital expenditures will drop to 50 billion yuan in 2026, they will still account for 12%+ of revenue.

**3. The underlying reasons for stagnant growth**
Revenue growth was 0.7% and profit growth was 1%, which is actually negative growth in the context of inflation. The underlying reasons are:
- C-end user growth peaked
- The increase in ARPU value is subject to the "speed increase and fee reduction" policy
- New businesses (Internet of Things, computing power) are not large enough

## 2. Management evaluation

### Strategic judgment
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Front:
1. Clearly focus on the four major tracks (connectivity, computing power, service, security)
2. Establish differentiated positioning in the field of Internet of Things/Internet of Vehicles
3. Obtain a satellite mobile communications license and deploy mobile phones to directly connect to satellites
4. The proportion of computing power investment is increasing in the right direction

Negative:
1. Extremely slow profit growth indicates that strategy execution is not efficient.
2. Mixed ownership reform has been going on for many years but the release of institutional vitality is limited.
3. The "four major tracks" are highly overlapping with telecommunications/mobile and lack differentiation.

### Mixed ownership reform governance evaluation
**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The mixed reform has introduced Tencent, Alibaba, Baidu, etc. as strategic investors, which should theoretically bring:
- Internet thinking and user operation capabilities
- Market-oriented management mechanism
- Technical collaboration and business cooperation

Actual effect:
-User acquisition really helps (internet packages)
- But deep governance changes are limited (core management is still appointed by the Organization Department)
- BAT's actual participation is weakening (China Unicom's strategic priority for it has declined)

### Capital Return Awareness
**Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

- The dividend payout ratio is about 50%, lower than Telecom (75%) and China Mobile
- The dividend yield is about 4%, the lowest among the three companies
- Management seems more interested in retaining funds to invest in new businesses than in rewarding shareholders
- This shows that management is too optimistic about the return on reinvestment when ROE is only 5.7%

## 3. Li Lu’s “asymmetry” analysis

### Downside protection factors
1. **Oligopoly status**: Three license companies have a monopoly and will not be eliminated
2. **PB < 1**: About 0.85 times PB provides asset bottom
3. **Stable operating cash flow**: Even if profits do not grow, cash flow can still be maintained
4. **Policy Protection**: The country will not allow the gap between operators to be too large

### Upside drivers
1. The value of the Internet of Things/Internet of Vehicles jumps from connection to service
2. Management significantly increases dividend payout ratio
3. The explosion of computing power business
4. Industry integration (possibility of China Unicom + Telecom merger - extremely unlikely)

### Asymmetry evaluation
- **Downside**: Limited (oligopoly protection + asset bottom), but low ROE means "chronic blood loss"
- **Upside**: Depends on the realization of IoT and improvement in profit margins. The possibility is currently medium.
- **Overall judgment**: The asymmetry is not attractive enough - the downside is protected but not completely safe (ROE < cost of capital means value is being consumed), and the upside lacks a clear catalyst

## 4. Scenario Analysis

### Optimistic scenario (probability 20%)
- Explosive growth of IoT/Internet of Vehicles business
- ROE increased to 7%+
- Management increased dividend payout ratio to 60%+
- Stock price increased by 30%+

### Baseline scenario (probability 55%)
-Business maintains low growth (1-3%)
- ROE maintained at 5.5-6%
- Investors receive 4% dividend return
- Share price fluctuates at current levels

### Pessimistic scenario (probability 25%)
- The gap with mobile/telecom continues to widen
-Capex pressure erodes profits
- ROE fell below 5%
- Stock price fell 15-20%

## 5. Core Conclusions

**Risk Management Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2.5/5)**

China Unicom is the "least worthy of investment" choice among the three major operators. From Li Lu’s framework:
1. **Not the best choice**: China Mobile in the same industry is better in almost all dimensions
2. **Lack of safety margin**: The combination of PE 15 times / ROE 5.7% is not cheap
3. **Lack of growth engine**: Core business is stagnant and new business does not contribute enough
4. **Management is not generous enough to shareholders**: 50% payout ratio and 4% dividend rate

If it must be configured in the communications industry, the priority should be: China Mobile >> China Telecom > China Unicom. China Unicom is only worth considering when its PE drops below 10 times.
