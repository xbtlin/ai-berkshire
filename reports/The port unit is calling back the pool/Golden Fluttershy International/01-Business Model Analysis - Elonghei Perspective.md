# Kingdee International (0268.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business model quality, user value, pricing power, sustainability
> Core question: Is this a good business? Do users really need it?

---

## 1. Overview of business model

Kingdee International's business model is in the midst of a fundamental transformation from the traditional software license model to the cloud subscription (SaaS) model. This is a "self-revolutionary" transformation - giving up one-time high revenue in exchange for a predictable recurring revenue stream.

**Product matrix stratified by customer size:**

| Product Line | Target Customers | 2025 Subscription Revenue | Year-over-Year Growth |
|---------|----------|----------------|----------|
| Cangqiong + Xinghan | Large/super large enterprises | 530 million yuan | +35.4% |
| Starry Sky | Growth medium-sized enterprise | 1.52 billion yuan | +19.3% |
| Xingchen + Jingdou Cloud | Small and micro enterprises | 1.14 billion yuan | +22.7% |

This hierarchical structure covers the entire life cycle of the enterprise. In theory, customers can upgrade from low-end products to high-end products as they grow.

## 2. User value analysis

### Positive factors

**1. ERP/financial software is the "rigid need among enterprises"**

An enterprise's financial accounting, supply chain management, and human resources management are basic operational needs and cannot be used without them. Once the system goes online and accumulates a large amount of business data, the cost of migration is extremely high. This provides Kingdee with natural user stickiness.

**2. Xinchuang’s domestic substitution brings structural opportunities**

The process of replacing central state-owned enterprises Xinchuang is accelerating, and foreign-owned ERPs such as SAP and Oracle are facing pressure to be replaced. As of the end of 2023, Kingdee has helped 221 companies complete localization replacement. The Xinchuang market size is expected to exceed 100 billion yuan in 2025 and reach 146.2 billion yuan, and is expected to reach 322.8 billion yuan in 2027. This is a huge incremental market.

**3. Upgrade from "Tools" to "Platform"**

As a PaaS platform, Kingdee Cloud not only provides standardized SaaS applications, but also supports customers and partners to conduct personalized development on the platform. Xinghan provides 260+ standard applications and 1000+ industry applications for large enterprises. Platformization means ecological lock-in effect.

**4. AI overlay brings new value layer**

The company launches AI suite products, using natural language as the entry point for interaction, and the underlying SaaS capabilities are accumulated into Skills and Agents. The AI ​​contract amount in 2025 is 356 million yuan, and 230 million yuan has been signed in 2026Q1, with a full-year target of 1 billion yuan. AI is not a gimmick, but an incremental value that actually improves user efficiency.

### Negative factors

**1. The inherent vulnerability of SME customers**

Kingdee's basic market - small micro and growth enterprises - have short life cycles, are sensitive to economic cycles, and have large fluctuations in renewal willingness. This results in the company needing to continue to acquire new customers to supplement losses, and sales expenses remain high (sales expenses in 2025 are 2.726 billion yuan, accounting for 38.9% of revenue).

**2. The short-term "pain" caused by subscription conversion has not been completely eliminated**

Although it has turned a profit in 2025, its adjusted net profit is only 232 million yuan, and its net profit margin is about 3.3%, which is far lower than the 10-20% level of mature SaaS companies. Low profit margins during the transition period mean that the company's "earning power" has not yet been fully demonstrated.

**3. Product complexity and implementation difficulty**

The implementation cycle of ERP in large enterprises is long and there are many customization requirements, which to some extent contradicts the ideal of SaaS standardization. To gain a foothold in the large customer market, Xinghan needs a large amount of industry Know-How and implementation resources.

## 3. Analysis of pricing power and switching costs

### Switching Cost Assessment ★★★★☆

Once the ERP system is deployed, it involves all business processes, historical data, and employee usage habits of the entire company. The direct and opportunity costs of replacing a system are extremely high. The NDR (net revenue retention rate) data of Kingdee customers confirms this:
- Xinghan (large enterprise): 103% (users not only renew, but also expand their use)
- Starry Sky (Growth Enterprise): 96%
- Stars (small and micro enterprises): 94%

Xinghan's NDR exceeding 100% is a positive sign, indicating that major customers are continuing to purchase more. However, the starry sky and stars are lower than 100%, which means there is a certain shrinkage or loss of customers.

### Pricing Power Assessment ★★★☆☆

Kingdee's current pricing power is medium to weak. The reason is:
1. Head-on competition with UFIDA limits the room for price increases
2. Small and medium-sized enterprises are price sensitive
3. Xinchuang’s alternative market is still in the “horse race” stage, with fierce price competition.

But as customers migrate to cloud platforms and accumulate data, long-term pricing power is expected to increase.

## 4. Sustainability of economic model

### Unit Economics Model (Unit Economics)

Judging from the subscription ARR of 4.09 billion yuan and a growth rate of approximately 19%, the company's recurring revenue base is constantly expanding. But the key question is:

- **LTV/CAC (customer lifetime value/customer acquisition cost)**: The company's sales expense ratio is as high as 38.9%. If it is assumed that the average customer life cycle is 5-7 years, LTV/CAC may just exceed the healthy line of 3 times.
- **Gross profit margin**: The gross profit margin of 67.1% is relatively low for a SaaS company (the world's leading SaaS companies are usually 75-85%), indicating that the company still has a lot of implementation services and customization costs.
- **Operating Leverage**: As the revenue scale expands, the leverage effect of R&D and administrative expenses begins to appear (R&D expenses -6% year-on-year), but sales expenses are still growing (+8.9%), indicating that the customer acquisition cost has not yet reached an inflection point.

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow was 1.097 billion yuan (+17.6%), significantly better than the net profit attributable to shareholders of 93 million yuan, indicating:
1. Advance receipts under the subscription model (contract liabilities of 3.773 billion yuan, +20.7%) have brought good cash flow
2. The company's "real earning power" is stronger than what the income statement shows.

## 6. Key conclusions

**Is this a good business? **

In the long term, enterprise SaaS is a good business - recurring revenue, high switching costs, scalable platform effects. But Kingdee is currently in a stage where "good business has been seen, but has not yet been fully realized."

**Core Conflict:**
- The good side: ARR continues to grow, cash flow is healthy, Xinchuang and AI bring structural opportunities
- The bad side: Weak profitability, high customer acquisition cost, high churn rate of small and medium-sized customers, consumption due to struggle with UF

**Duan Yongping's judgment: **"Do the right thing" - Kingdee's choice to transform to the cloud and embrace AI is right, and user value exists. But "doing things right" still needs time to verify: can sales efficiency be improved? Can big customers continue to make breakthroughs? Can AI truly bring differentiation? The answers to these questions will determine whether the business model can be upgraded from "okay" to "excellent."
