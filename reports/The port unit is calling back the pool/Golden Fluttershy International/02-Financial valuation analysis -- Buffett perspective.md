> Core question: Is the margin of safety sufficient when buying at the current price?

**Revenue Quality Analysis:**

Front:

- Traditional ERP business continues to shrink, dragging down overall growth
- The "other" part of cloud services other than subscriptions (including implementation, customization, etc.) has low profit margins

## 2. Profitability analysis

### Key Indicators of Income Statement

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Trends |
|------|--------|--------|--------|----------|
| Total revenue | 5.689 billion | 6.255 billion | 7.006 billion | Continuous growth |
| Gross profit margin | 64.2% | 65.1% | 67.1% | Steady improvement |
| Sales expense ratio | ~42% | 40.0% | 38.9% | Improving year by year |
| R&D expenses | — | 1.516 billion | 1.425 billion | Starting to decline |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | -360 million | -142 million | 93 million | Turn loss into profit |
| Adjusted net profit | -185 million | -007 million | 232 million | Turn loss into profit |
| Operating cash flow | — | 933 million | 1.097 billion | +17.6% |

**Analysis points:**

1. **Gross profit margin is recovering year by year**: It has rebounded from the low of 61.6% in 2022 to 67.1%, reflecting the positive impact of the increase in the proportion of cloud subscriptions on gross profit margin. However, there is still a gap between 75-85% of the world's outstanding SaaS companies.

2. **Inflection point in cost control**: R&D expenses fell year-on-year for the first time (-6.0%), and the sales expense rate continued to decline. This is a signal that operating leverage is beginning to be released. However, administrative expenses bucked the trend and increased by 20.1%, which requires attention.

3. **The absolute value of profit is still meager**: The adjusted net profit of 232 million yuan corresponds to 7 billion revenue, and the net profit rate is only about 3.3%. The net profit attributable to the parent company is 93 million yuan, corresponding to a market capitalization of 29.3 billion Hong Kong dollars, and the static PE is as high as approximately 300 times (converted in Hong Kong dollars).

4. **Cash flow is significantly stronger than profit**: Operating cash flow of 1.097 billion yuan is nearly 12 times the net profit attributable to the parent company, indicating that the subscription model has a strong cash recovery capability.

| Debt-to-equity ratio | 0% | No debt pressure |

**Balance Sheet Analysis:**

Kingdee's balance sheet is a clear bright spot. Zero interest-bearing debt means the company is not relying on debt to survive even during a transition period that has seen years of losses. The contract liabilities of 3.773 billion yuan are essentially subscription fees paid in advance by customers and are a "reservoir" for future revenue. This shows that although the company's cloud transformation is time-consuming and labor-intensive, it has maintained good financial discipline.

## 4. R&D Capitalization Issues

There are capitalization issues in R&D expenses that need attention:
- R&D capitalization rate in 2025: 31.3% (31.9% in 2024)
- Of the total investment in R&D of 1.425 billion yuan, approximately 450 million yuan was capitalized and included in intangible assets.
- If all R&D is expensed, adjusted net profit will shrink significantly from 232 million yuan

This means that the company's "real" profitability may be weaker than the reported numbers. However, for software companies, moderate R&D capitalization is industry practice. The key is to see whether the capitalized R&D actually generates commercial value - judging from the continued growth of ARR, this is basically true.

## 8. Key conclusions

**Safety Margin Judgment:**

From a PE perspective, Kingdee's margin of safety is almost non-existent - even the adjusted net profit caliber exceeds 100 times PE. This is not Buffett's target of "buying good companies at reasonable prices" in the traditional sense.

But from the perspective of PS and EV/ARR, 3.9 times PS and 6.6 times EV/ARR are not expensive for a SaaS company with a revenue growth rate of 15-20%. The core bet is whether profit margins can increase from 3% to more than 15% - if so, the current price is reasonable; if not, it is a "revenue growth but shareholders do not make money" story.

**Core Risks:**
1. Profit margins are improving slower than expected
2. R&D capitalization masks the lack of real profitability
3. AI investment may prolong the profit ramp-up cycle

**Core Opportunities:**
1. After the scale effect of subscription revenue is released, profit margins are expected to increase non-linearly
2. If the AI kit can be successfully monetized, it will open up a new growth curve
3. Zero debt provides a strong financial safety cushion
