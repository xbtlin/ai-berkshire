## Research background

Kingdee International is one of the leading companies in China's enterprise-level SaaS field. It has experienced three strategic transformations from financial software to ERP to cloud SaaS. The company turned a profit for the first time in 2025, marking the harvest period of cloud transformation. The current market value is approximately HK$29.3 billion, corresponding to a PE of approximately 150 times + net profit attributable to the parent company in 2025. The company has proposed three core strategies of "AI first, subscription first, and globalization", with an AI suite revenue target of 1 billion yuan in 2026.
