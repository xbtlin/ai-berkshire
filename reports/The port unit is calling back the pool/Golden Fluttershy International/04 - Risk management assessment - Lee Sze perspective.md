# Kingdee International (0268.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

> Analysis dimensions: management quality, corporate governance, key risk factors, protection of minority shareholders’ interests
> Core question: Is management trustworthy? What are the risks that could make an investment completely fail?

---

## 1. Management evaluation

### Core figure: Xu Shaochun

| Project | Content |
|------|------|
| Position | Founder, Chairman and CEO |
| Age | About 62 years old (born in 1964) |
| Education | Senior Economist, State Council Special Allowance Expert |
| Entrepreneurship History | Borrowed 5,000 yuan to start a business in 1991, 30+ years of business management experience |
| Shareholding | One of the largest shareholders of the company |
| Salary | Annual salary is about 10-12.5 million yuan (slightly lower during the period of loss) |

### Management advantages

**1. Strategic vision and transformation courage ★★★★★**

Xu Shaochun's most admirable quality is strategic courage. He led three major transformations during Kingdee’s development:
- Switched from DOS to Windows in 1994 (seizing technology trends)
- Shift from financial software to ERP (expanding business boundaries) in the 2000s
- Full bet on cloud computing in 2012 (subvert yourself)

Although the series of behaviors of "smashing the computer, smashing the boss's chair, and smashing the ERP" were performative in nature, they did convey the firm determination to transform. The third cloud transformation has caused the company to suffer losses for many consecutive years (accumulated losses of more than 1.4 billion yuan from 2020 to 2024). Few founders are willing to bear such great short-term pressure to pursue long-term correctness.

In 2025, it proposed an "AI first" strategy, quickly connected to DeepSeek and launched an AI suite, once again demonstrating its sensitivity to technology trends.

**2. Founder’s long-termism ★★★★☆**

Xu Shaochun has been in charge of Kingdee for more than 30 years and has never "cash out and leave". As one of the company's largest shareholders, his personal wealth is deeply tied to the company's destiny. This consistency is beneficial to minority shareholders.

He has publicly expressed his vision of "letting China's management model rise globally" many times. Although the path to realization is still far away, it shows a pattern that goes beyond short-term interests.

**3. Product intuition ★★★★☆**

From early financial software to today's AI Xinghan, Kingdee has always had a better reputation than UFIDA in terms of product experience. This shows that the founder has a pursuit of product quality, and it is not a pure "relationship and sales" model.

### Management Weaknesses and Concerns

**1. Risk of founder centralization ★★★☆☆**

Xu Shaochun serves as both chairman of the board and CEO and has been in charge of the company for more than 30 years. The risks of this "top leader" model are:
- Lack of effective checks and balances and challenge mechanisms
- Succession planning is unclear
- There may be blind spots in decision-making

Although the company has independent non-executive directors (Zhou Junxiang, Bo Lianming, Xin Rong, etc.), in China's corporate governance environment, the binding force of independent directors on founders is generally limited.

**2. Salary does not match performance ★★☆☆☆**

During the company's three consecutive years of losses (2021-2023), Xu Shaochun's annual salary still exceeded 10 million yuan (12.49 million, 12.53 million, and 10.57 million yuan in 2020-2022 respectively). Although it has been reduced, it is questionable whether this salary level is reasonable for a loss-making company.

**3. The risk of "big thunder but little rain" in strategy execution ★★★☆☆**

Grand narratives such as "enterprise management AI", "globalization", "AI revenue will account for 30% in 2030" are exciting, but:
- Globalization has basically made no real progress at present
- Although AI suite contracts are growing rapidly, its proportion of total revenue is still small (about 5%)
- In the past, it took more than 10 years for "cloud transformation" to become truly profitable.

A distinction needs to be made between "preaching the vision" and "actual execution".

## 3. Key risk assessment

### Risk 1: The impact of macroeconomic downturn on small and medium-sized enterprise customers ★★★★☆ (high risk)

**Risk Description:**
Kingdee's customer base is China's small and medium-sized enterprises and growth enterprises. If the economy continues to decline:
- Enterprise IT budgets have shrunk, and willingness to renew has declined.
- Large-scale closure of small and medium-sized enterprises, direct loss of customers
- Fewer new customers and rising customer acquisition costs

**Quantified Impact:**
The average life cycle of small and medium-sized enterprises may be shortened from 5-7 years to 3-5 years, resulting in a 20-30% drop in LTV. If NDR falls below 90% from the current 94-96%, ARR growth may stagnate.

**Buffering Factor:**
- Large enterprise customers (Xinghan) are less affected by the macro economy
- The demand driven by Xinchuang policy is relatively rigid
- A debt-free balance sheet provides a survival safety cushion

### Risk 2: Profit rate improvement is less than expected ★★★★☆ (high risk)

**Risk Description:**
The market's expectations for Kingdee are based on the assumption of "rapid climb in profit margins." Brokerages predict net profit CAGR of 70% from 2025 to 2027. if:
- Competition prevents price increase
- AI investment continues to consume profits
- Sales expense ratio cannot be reduced
Then profit margins may stay at low levels for a long time.

**Quantified Impact:**
If the net profit rate reaches only 5% in 2027 (instead of the 8-10% expected by the market), the corresponding profit will be about 400 million yuan, and the current market value will correspond to about 70 times PE, and the valuation pressure will be significant.

### Risk three: AI investment "burns money" but it is difficult to realize it ★★★☆☆ (medium risk)

**Risk Description:**
The company’s AI suite revenue target for 2026 is 1 billion yuan, but:
- AI functions may become "icing on the cake" rather than "must be paid for"
- Customers’ willingness to pay for AI is still unclear
- Rapid iteration of large model technology, investment may need to be repeated
- Competitors are also promoting AI, making it difficult to form lasting differentiation.

**Buffering Factor:**
- In 2026Q1, 230 million yuan of AI suite contracts were signed, and the annual target of 1 billion is expected to be achieved.
- AI suite is a value-added service built on existing SaaS products, with low marginal cost
- Kingdee has a large amount of enterprise management scenario data, which is the core asset for training vertical AI

### Risk four: Protracted war of attrition with UF ★★★☆☆ (medium risk)

**Risk Description:**
The 30-year battle shows that neither company can "eat" the other. Continuous competition leads to:
- Lowering each other’s prices in Xinchuang’s bidding process
- Repeat investment in key customer markets
- Industry profit margins have been suppressed for a long time

**Buffering Factor:**
- The two companies gradually formed a misaligned competition pattern
- Kingdee Cloud takes the lead in transformation + takes the lead in profitability, and its competitive position is improving
- Under the trend of industry consolidation, leading companies benefit from tail clearing

### Risk five: founder succession and governance risks ★★★☆☆ (mid-term risk)

**Risk Description:**
Xu Shaochun is 62 years old and still full of energy, but the issue of succession within the 5-10-year dimension cannot be avoided. Kingdee's transformation is highly dependent on Xu Shaochun's personal vision and decisiveness. If the successor does not have the same ability, the company may fall into strategic confusion.

At present, no clear succession training plan has been publicly disclosed. CEO Zhang Yong and other senior executives do not have high public exposure.

## 4. Scenario Stress Test

### Worst case scenario ("Black Swan")

If the following factors occur simultaneously:
- China's economy has a hard landing, with GDP growth falling below 3%
- Large-scale collapse of small and medium-sized enterprises, Kingdee ARR has been experiencing negative growth for two consecutive years
- AI has huge investment but cannot be monetized at all
- UFIDA has achieved breakthrough leadership in the field of large models

In this extreme scenario:
- The company may return to losses
- Market value may fall to HK$10-15 billion (stock price HK$3-4)
- But since there is zero debt, the company will not go bankrupt

### Best case scenario

- Xinchuang's replacement is fully accelerated, and large customers sign contracts in batches
- AI suite becomes industry standard, doubling ARPU value
- The small and medium-sized enterprise market is recovering, and NDR has increased to more than 100% across the board.
- Net profit will reach 700-800 million yuan in 2027

In this optimistic scenario:
- Market value may reach HK$50-60 billion (stock price HK$15-18)
- Kingdee is established as the absolute leader in Chinese enterprise SaaS

## 6. Key conclusions

**Is management trustworthy? **

Xu Shaochun is a respectable founder - he has persisted for 30 years, transformed three times, and dared to revolutionize himself. He is not a "make quick money and leave" kind of person. But he is not a perfect manager: the disconnect between compensation and performance, the centralized governance model, and the gap between grand narrative and actual execution are all issues that need attention.

**Li Lu type judgment framework:**

"Good management" is a necessary but not sufficient condition. The quality of Kingdee's management is at an above-average level among Chinese technology companies, but the external risks (macroeconomic, industry structure, technological changes) faced by this company are also great.

Investing in Kingdee is essentially making the following judgments:
1. Can China’s enterprise SaaS industry grow into a market worth hundreds of billions? There is a high probability that it can, but the pace is uncertain.
2. Can Kingdee maintain its top two position in this market - the probability is currently relatively high
3. Whether profitability can be substantially improved within 3-5 years - this is the biggest uncertainty

**Bottom line judgment:** The company will not die (zero debt + positive cash flow), but it may make investors "painful to wait." If your investment horizon is less than 3 years, Kingdee may not be suitable for you. If you're willing to hold on for more than 5 years and accept fluctuations along the way, management's quality and direction hold up.
