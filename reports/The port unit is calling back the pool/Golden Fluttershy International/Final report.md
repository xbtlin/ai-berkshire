## 2. Investment thesis (Bull Case)

If you are bullish on Kingdee, the core logic chain is:

```
Innovation policy driven + AI monetization → Subscription ARR continues to grow by 20%
                       ↓
            Release of operating leverage → Net profit margin increased from 3% to 10%+
                       ↓
            Net profit in 2027 is 500-600 million yuan → PE compression from 100 times to 50 times
                       ↓
            Davis Double Click → Stock price doubling potential
```

**Supporting factors:**
1. Subscription ARR was 4.09 billion yuan (+19%), which continued to increase to 4.22 billion yuan in 2026Q1, and the growth momentum has not diminished.
2. The contract for AI suite in 2026Q1 is 230 million yuan, and the full-year target is 1 billion yuan. If achieved, it will contribute approximately 14% to the revenue increase.
3. Zero debt + 1.097 billion operating cash flow, extremely thick financial security cushion
4. Be the first to make profits vs. UFIDA is still losing money, and its competitive position is improving
5. NDR for major customers is 103%, indicating that the product is recognized in the large enterprise market
6. The Xinchuang market is expected to reach 322.8 billion yuan in 2027, and Kingdee’s benefits are highly certain

## 3. Bear Case

If you are bearish on Kingdee, the core logic chain is:

```
Low willingness to pay for SaaS in China + fierce competition → long-term suppression of profit margins
                               ↓
            Net interest rate is difficult to exceed 5% → PE remains high for a long time
                               ↓
            "Increasing revenue without increasing profits" → Unable to realize shareholder value
```

**Supporting factors:**
1. The 30-year-old industry battle between the two giants has no end in sight, and profits continue to be consumed.
2. The sales expense ratio of 38.9% shows that the customer acquisition efficiency is low, unlike a business with a strong moat.
3. R&D capitalization is 31.3%, and the real profitability may be weaker
4. The fundamentals of small and medium-sized enterprises are fragile, and the churn rate will accelerate during the economic downturn.
5. The "1 billion AI suite goal" may be another grand narrative that will take 5-10 years to be realized.
6. The Hong Kong stock market has given a long-term discount to Chinese SaaS, and there is limited room for valuation compression.

## 5. Valuation Judgment

### Is the current valuation reasonable?

**Viewed from different angles:**

| Perspective | Conclusion |
|------|------|
| PE perspective | Extremely expensive. An adjusted PE of 116 times requires rapid profit growth for many years to digest |
| PS perspective | Not expensive. 3.9x PS is lower than the global average for a SaaS company with a growth rate of 20% |
| EV/ARR perspective | Reasonable. 6.6 times the valuation of global SaaS in the 5-10 times range |
| DCF Perspective | Depends on the assumption of the final state profit rate. If the final net interest rate is 15%, the current price is attractive |

**Core contradiction:**Currently we are in a stage where "PS is not expensive but PE is very expensive". Buying Kingdee is essentially buying an option that "profit margins will increase significantly."

### Reasonable buying range estimation

| Scenario | Bid Price (HKD) | Logic |
|------|---------------|------|
| Ideal buying price | 6.0-7.0 | Corresponds to about 5 times PS in 2026, providing a better safety margin |
| Acceptable buying price | 7.0-9.0 | Corresponding to 6-7 times PS in 2026, most optimistic assumptions need to be realized |
| Too high price | >12.0 | Overdrawn growth expectations for more than 2 years |

## 7. Final conclusion

### Investment Qualitative: Conditional Opportunities

Kingdee International is a company that is "in the right direction and on the right path, but has not yet reached its destination". It is not a target that can give a clear "buy" or "don't buy" conclusion at the moment, but an observation object that requires setting conditions and continuous tracking.

### Action recommendations

| Investor Types | Recommendations |
|------------|------|
| Conservative value investors | Not suitable yet. PE is too high and profitability has not been verified |
| Growth value investors | You can pay attention. Wait for the stock price to fall back below HK$7 to open a position |
| Trend Investor | Pay attention to the progress of monetization of AI suites and quarterly ARR growth rate |
| Long-term holders (5 years +) | You can observe the current price with small positions, add positions and other right-side signals |

### Core tracking node

1. **2026 interim report (expected to be August 2026)**: Look at whether AI suite revenue is on track, NDR trends, and profit margin improvement speed
2. **2026 full-year results (estimated in March 2027)**: See whether the full-year profit margin increases from 3.3% to more than 5%
3. **Competitive Situation**: When will UF make a profit? Who will win the big deal from Xinchuang?

### One sentence summary

> Kingdee International is one of the companies with the most correct direction and the healthiest financials in the Chinese enterprise SaaS track, but "good company" does not mean "good investment" - the current valuation has partially reflected optimistic expectations, and real buying opportunities may appear when the market's confidence in its growth is shaken.
