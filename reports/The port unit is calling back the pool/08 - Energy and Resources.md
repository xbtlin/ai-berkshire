# A moat of the river

The mooring river of the Shinji Glass is a process of industrial chain integration and cost control. The supply of silicon alluvials + natural gas has reduced the cost of raw materials, and the large kilns (the single-cook-scale leading industry) have reduced the cost of production per unit.
