## 3. Investment thesis

### 3.1 Core bullish logic

1. **The transformation from imitation to innovation has been basically completed, and innovation dividends are being realized. ** Innovative drugs accounted for 82.2% of revenue, with a growth of 30%+ for three consecutive years. The growth engine has been decoupled from the risks of generic drug policies. This is not a story of "still in transformation", but a "harvest period after successful transformation".

2. **The financial safety margin is almost unrivaled among Chinese pharmaceutical companies. ** 27.1 billion in cash, zero debt, 90% gross margin, 6.2 billion in free cash flow. Even if it encounters a pipeline failure or an industry downturn, the company has ample ammunition and buffers.

3. **License-out cooperation verifies the international competitiveness of the innovation pipeline. ** When the world's top pharmaceutical companies such as GSK, Regeneron, and Merck are willing to pay hundreds of millions of dollars in down payments, this is the most powerful market verification of Hansoh's R&D capabilities.

4. **2026-2028 is the "harvest period" of the pipeline. ** Multiple core pipelines (B7-H3 ADC, GLP-1/GIP, autoimmune pipeline, etc.) have entered Phase III or are about to submit marketing applications. Milestone payments and new product launches are expected to bring a new round of growth.

5. **Valuation is not expensive compared to peers. ** The PE of 32 times is lower than Hengrui Pharmaceuticals (50-60 times) and lower than Innovent Biologics, and the PEG based on 20%+ growth rate is about 1.3-1.6 times, which is within a reasonable range.

### 3.2 Core bearish logic

1. **Amitinib dependence is too high. ** The annual sales of a single product of 5.5 billion is too large. Intensified competition or price reductions in medical insurance may lead to an inflection point in growth. If subsequent large-scale products fail to be delivered in time, there may be a "break" in performance.

2. **Insufficient globalization capabilities. ** There is a clear gap compared with BeiGene’s global layout. Although License-out reduces risks, it also transfers the greatest value - global commercial profits.

3. **Family governance and intergenerational inheritance risks. ** 75.66% Family holding, chairman and CEO, and Hengrui's "mom and pop shop" label - these governance structure problems will not disappear just because of good performance. Zhong Huijuan is over 60 years old, and the uncertainty of intergenerational inheritance will increase year by year.

4. **32x PE reflects considerable growth expectations. ** If growth falls short of expectations or there are major setbacks in the pipeline, the risk of valuation contraction cannot be ignored.

5. **The "involution" of China's innovative drug industry is intensifying. ** With the clustering of targets, price wars, and competition for talent, the honeymoon period of the industry is ending. Hansen's ability to stay ahead of increasingly crowded competition is an ongoing challenge.
