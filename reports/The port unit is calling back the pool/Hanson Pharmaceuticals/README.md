# Hansen Pharmaceutical (3692.HK) Investment Research

## Company Profile

Hansoh Pharmaceutical Group Company Limited, Hong Kong Stock Exchange code 3692.HK, is China's leading innovation-driven pharmaceutical company. The company was listed in Hong Kong in June 2019. It was founded by Zhong Huijuan and serves as chairman and CEO.

**Core business areas:** Five major therapeutic areas including anti-tumor, central nervous system, anti-infection, metabolic diseases, and autoimmunity.
