# Cheung Kong Group (1113.HK) Risk and Management Assessment

## 1. Core risks

| Risk type | Severity | Probability of occurrence | Comprehensive rating |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| The real estate market continues to be depressed | High | High | ★★★★★ |
| Significant impairment of property assets | High | Medium | ★★★★☆ |
| ROE continues to be sluggish without improvement | Medium | High | ★★★★☆ |
| Mainland project delivery risk | Medium | Low | ★★☆☆☆ |
| Geopolitics (for Hong Kong companies) | Low | Low | ★☆☆☆☆ |

### Core security factors
Even if it faces many risks, Changshi has a strong "survival bottom line":
- 5% debt ratio - almost "no leverage" in the real estate industry
- A/A2 credit rating - smooth financing channels
- Diversified income - aircraft leasing and infrastructure investment provide non-real estate cash flow
- Ample cash – can wait for the cycle to reverse

## 2. Management evaluation

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Front:
1. Extremely conservative financial management - staying safe from industry turmoil
2. Precisely sold a large number of mainland assets before 2017
3. Diversified investment reduces single dependence

Negative:
1. Over-conservatism leads to extremely low ROE - a debt ratio of 5% means that a large amount of net assets are "idle"
2. Lack of big moves to "attack against the trend" in the down cycle
3. Profits continue to decline but no clear countermeasures are in place to stop the decline.

## 3. Scenario analysis

### Optimistic scenario (probability 25%)
- Real estate market bottoms out
- Property sales accelerate and profits resume growth
- PB rebounded from 0.3x to 0.5x
- Share price rises 60-70%

### Baseline scenario (50% probability)
- Market downturn lasts 2-3 years
- Profits remain low or decline slightly
- Dividend maintenance (4-5%)
- Stock price fluctuates at low levels

### Pessimistic scenario (probability 25%)
- House prices continue to fall by 20%+
- Substantial impairment of property value
- Profits fall further
- Stock price drops 15-25% (but PB protects downside)

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Risk Management Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Cheung Kong is an "extremely safe but uncertain return" deep value stock. PB of 0.3 times provides strong asset bottom protection, and a 5% debt ratio ensures that it will not go bankrupt. But ROE 3-4% means that if there is no cycle reversal, your capital is "dormant" here rather than "growing".

From Li Lu's perspective: Changshi is not a target that can be "forget about it and get 10 times in 10 years." It's more of a "waiting for catalyst" deep value opportunity - if you believe Hong Kong/Mainland real estate will eventually recover, PB 0.3x offers extremely high odds. But the time cost cannot be ignored.
