## One sentence conclusion

Cheung Kong Group is the "safest deep value stock" in Hong Kong stocks - PB 0.3 times + debt ratio 5% + A-level credit rating, making it almost impossible to suffer permanent capital losses. However, ROE of 3-4% and declining profits mean that the return on investment depends on when the industry recovers, and the time cost is uncertain.

## Investment thesis

### Look at the long logic (reverse + value regression)
1. PB 0.3 times - even if assets are significantly impaired, there is still a huge margin of safety
2. Debt ratio 5% - the only large-scale developer in the industry that is "unlevered"
3. Low-cost land bank - acquire it early and still make profits even if the price drops
4. Huge elasticity when the cycle reverses (PB returns to 0.5 times = rises by 67%)
5. Diversified investments provide non-real estate cash flow

### Bearish logic (value trap)
1. ROE 3-4% - almost no capital appreciation
2. Profits continue to decline (-26.75%)
3. The real estate cycle reversal time is uncertain (maybe 3-5 years+)
4. Excessive conservatism - a large amount of capital "sleeping"
5. Changes in the long-term logic of the industry (population reduction, housing glut)

## Buying conditions

1. **If you have approved the "Deep Value + Contrarian" strategy**: The current PB of 0.3 times already meets the buying conditions
2. **Safer confirmation signal**: Hong Kong housing prices stabilize and rebound + monthly transaction volume picks up
3. **Catalysts**: Special dividends, large-scale repurchases, asset disposals

## Position suggestions

- **Current price**: 3-5% reverse position can be established (provided that the real estate industry is not overly exposed in the portfolio)
- **Conditions for adding positions**: Signs of improvement in real estate fundamentals
- **Stop loss consideration**: No need to stop loss (PB 0.3 times + 5% debt ratio, almost never return to zero)
- **Time planning**: 3-5 years of patience required

## Keep track of key points

1. Hong Kong/Mainland housing prices and transaction volume trends
2. Changes in global interest rates (interest rate cuts are good for real estate)
3. Property sales progress and profit margin
4. Management capital operation (buyback/special dividend/new investment)
5. Changes in property valuation
