# Cheung Kong Group (1113.HK) Investment Research

## Basic information
- **Company Name**: Cheung Kong Holdings Limited
- **Stock Code**: 1113.HK
- **Industry**: Real Estate/Investment
- **PE(TTM)**: 5-6 times
- **ROE**：3-4%

## Core financial data
| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes |
|------|------|----------|
| Net profit in the first half of 2025 | HK$6.3 billion | -26.75% |
| Revenue recognized from property sales (first half of the year) | HK$7.37 billion | +58.9% |
| Net debt/total capital | ~5.0% | Very low in the industry |
| Credit Rating | A/Stable (S&P), A2 (Moody's) | - |

## Investment Highlights
1. PE is only 5-6 times - the lowest among the 13 companies
2. The debt ratio is extremely low (5%) and the finances are extremely safe.
3. Large amounts of low-cost land bank (early access)
4. Diversified layout (real estate + infrastructure + hotel + aircraft leasing)

## Core Risks
1. ROE is only 3-4% - extremely low return on capital
2. Hong Kong/Mainland real estate market continues to slump
3. Profits fell sharply by 26.75%
4. Property revaluation decreases
