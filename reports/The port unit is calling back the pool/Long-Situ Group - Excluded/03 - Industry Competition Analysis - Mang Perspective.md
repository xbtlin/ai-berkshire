# Cheung Kong Group (1113.HK) industry competition analysis

## 1. Current situation of Hong Kong/China real estate industry

In Munger's words: "You're fishing in a not-so-good pond right now."

### Industry dilemma:
1. Hong Kong house prices have fallen by about 25-30% from their 2021 highs
2. Mainland real estate has experienced in-depth adjustments after the "three red lines"
3. High interest rate environment suppresses demand for home purchases
4. Population decline/aging reduces long-term housing demand
5. The policy shifts from "stabilizing housing prices" to "not speculating on housing"

### The relative advantages of Changshi
In this "poor" industry, Cheung Kong is the "safest" player:
- 5% debt ratio - when peers face a break in the capital chain, Changshi is as safe as a rock
- Acquire land at low cost early - there is still room for profit even if the price is reduced
- Does not rely on high leverage - there will be no "Evergrande-style" thunderstorms

## 3. Key issues of Munger format

### "How long will the winter in the real estate industry last?"
Can't judge accurately. But what is certain is:
- High interest rates + depopulation + oversupply = recovery will be very slow
- It may take 3-5 years to digest the inventory
- Longshi's low debt allows it to "survive the winter"

### "How much is Changshi's land bank worth?"
Key question: book value vs market value. If house prices continue to fall, the land bank may need to be written down. However, long-term land is mostly acquired at an early stage (at a very low cost), and even if the value is significantly reduced, there is still a safety cushion.

### "Why not distribute cash to shareholders?"
Cheung Kong holds a lot of cash and low debt, which it could theoretically return to shareholders through special dividends or buybacks. But management may be waiting for the opportunity to "buy the dip" - acquiring high-quality assets at low prices when the industry is at its worst. This is a reasonable capital allocation strategy, but it will take time to verify.

## 4. Core Conclusions

**Industry competitiveness rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Changshi is the player who "wears the thickest cotton-padded jacket" in a "winter" industry. It won't freeze to death, but it won't enjoy the warmth either. Investing in Changshi is betting that "spring will come" - when the industry recovers, Changshi with low debt + low-cost land bank + abundant cash will be one of the biggest beneficiaries. But "when spring will come" is uncertain.
