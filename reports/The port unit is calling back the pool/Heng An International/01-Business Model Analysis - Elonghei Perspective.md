# Hengan International (1044.HK) Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: user value, business nature, moat depth, management level of "duty"

## 1. Business essence: "user value" of daily consumer goods

### The nature of consumption of paper towels and sanitary products

Hengan's products (paper towels, sanitary napkins, diapers) are "indispensable daily consumables". User value is very straightforward:
- **Tissues**: A basic need for wiping and cleaning, with almost no substitutes
- **Sanitary Napkins**: Women’s monthly rigid demand, high brand loyalty
- **Diapers**: a rigid expenditure for families with infants and young children

From the perspective of Duan Yongping's "user value": Hengan provides "basic health protection" - demand is not affected by the economic cycle, and paper towels will not be used less because of poor economic conditions. This is one of the most robust consumer categories.

### The essence of "boring" is precisely the advantage

Duan Yongping likes "simple" business. Hengan’s business is simple enough:
- Buy pulp → process into paper towels/sanitary napkins → sell to consumers through channels
- No risk of technological disruption
- No worries about policy restrictions
- Products will not become obsolete

"Boring" means that it is not easy to make big mistakes, will not be subverted, and the competitive landscape is relatively stable.

## 2. Moat analysis

### Brand moat ★★★☆☆

Hengan's brand has a certain degree of recognition in China:
- "Xinxiangyin" is a well-known brand in the tissue category
- "Seven Dimensions" has about 10% market share in the sanitary napkin category

**But the brand moat is not deep enough**:
- Low brand loyalty in the tissue category - consumers are easily attracted by promotional prices to switch brands
- Low brand concentration in the industry (the four major companies only have a 30% share in total)
- Lack of absolute mental occupation of "brand = category"

### Scale effect ★★★☆☆

As a leading company in the industry, Hengan has certain scale advantages in procurement and production:
- Bargaining power for bulk purchases of pulp
- National production base layout
- Logistics and distribution efficiency

However, the scale effect is not extremely significant in this industry - small and medium-sized enterprises can also maintain competitiveness through regional operations.

### Channel Network ★★★☆☆

Hengan has a nationwide distribution network, including:
- Traditional KA stores and supermarkets
- E-commerce channels (the proportion is increasing, about 30%)
- New retail channels

Channel advantage is medium - e-commerce has lowered the channel threshold, making it easier for new brands to reach consumers directly.

### Switching cost ★★★☆☆ (sanitary napkins) / ★★☆☆☆ (paper towels)

- There is a certain switching cost for sanitary napkins: once female consumers find a comfortable brand, they are unwilling to change it frequently
- Paper towels have almost no switching costs: consumers can easily switch brands based on price

## 3. "Simplicity" assessment of business model

Hengan’s business model is highly simple and predictable:

**The easy part**:
- Product demand is stable and not affected by economic cycles and technological changes
- The manufacturing process is mature and the technical barriers are low but scale barriers exist.
- The channel model is traditional and controllable
- Clear cash flow model: production → wholesale → retail → payment collection

**The complicated part**:
- Raw material (pulp) price fluctuations have a great impact on profit margins
- The price war in the industry is fierce and gross profit margin is under pressure
- Continuous brand investment is required to combat white label/new brand competition

## 4. Assessment of the degree of "duty"

### Historical performance of management

Hengan has a history of corporate governance controversies:
- In 2018-2019, short-selling institutions questioned the authenticity of related transactions and cash.
- Historically, the proportion of non-operating transactions is relatively high
- Strong family color (jointly controlled by Shi Wenbo family + Xu Lianjie family)

**But there are positive changes**:
- Hired international consulting companies three times in 2001, 2008 and 2014 to implement management changes
- De-family reform is gradually advancing, and many family members have been persuaded to leave management
- Although Xu Lianjie’s son Xu Qingchi and Shi Wenbo’s son Shi Huangjian have joined the board of directors, they already have a professional manager system

### "Duty" rating

| Aspects | Evaluation |

## 6. Core Conclusions

Hengan International is a "good but not excellent" business. Evaluation from Duan Yongping’s perspective:

**Aspects that meet the criteria of "good business"**:
- Product demand is rigid and not afraid of economic cycles
- The business model is extremely simple and understandable
- Does not require large capital expenditures and has good cash generation capabilities
- Will not be disrupted by technology

**Aspects that do not meet the standards of "Business Excellence"**:
- The moat is not deep enough - brand loyalty is low (especially for paper towels) and easily eroded by price wars
- Lack of pricing power - the industry is severely involved and can only follow market pricing
- Limited growth - the total industry volume is growing slowly, and it is difficult to significantly increase the share
- Management has historical doubts - although it is improving, trust will take time to rebuild

**Duan Yongping's possible judgment**: This is a company suitable as an "interest-earning" investment - 6% dividend rate, stable operations, and low valuation. But it is not a target that can "see multiple times in 10 years". If you are pursuing "certain and reasonable returns" rather than "high growth", Hengan can be considered.
