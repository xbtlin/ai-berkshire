## Company Profile

Hengan International Group was founded by Shi Wenbo and Xu Lianjie in Jinjiang, Fujian in 1985. It is China's leading manufacturer of sanitary products and tissue paper. The main brands include "Seven Dimensions" (sanitary napkins), "Xinxiangyin" (tissue for daily use), and "Anerle/Q·MO" (diapers). Listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 1998.

## Core business structure

1. **Household paper** (Xinxiangyin, etc.): revenue accounted for approximately 58%, gross profit margin approximately 22%
2. **Sanitary Napkins** (Seven Dimensions, etc.): Revenue accounts for about 30%, gross profit margin is about 60%+
3. **Diapers** (Anerle/Q·MO): Revenue accounted for about 10%, gross profit margin is medium
