# Hengan International (1044.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry ecological niche, competitive dynamics, reverse thinking, multidisciplinary model

## 1. Industry panorama: China’s daily necessities market

### Market Structure

China's household paper and personal hygiene products market is a mature market with a scale of approximately 200 billion yuan, with a growth rate of only 1-3%. Industry characteristics:

- **Low concentration**: The total share of the four major companies (Hengan, Vinda, Jinhongye, and Jierou) is only about 30%
- **Excess Capacity**: New production capacity will be 1.43 million tons in 2024 (+10.77%), and demand will only increase by 0.61%
- **Intense price war**: Platform e-commerce and live streaming have intensified price transparency and competition intensity
- **White Label/New Brand Emergence**: Low entry barriers enable new brands to continue to enter

### The "unattractiveness" of the industry

Munger will first examine "whether the industry itself is worth participating in." The problem with the household paper industry is:

1. **No differentiation**: It is difficult for paper towels to make differentiation that consumers perceive.
2. **Low switching cost**: It is easy for consumers to switch brands based on promotions/prices
3. **Low entry barrier**: A large number of white brands can compete at lower prices
4. **E-commerce amplifies competition**: Online channels make it easier for consumers to compare prices

**The sanitary napkin category is relatively better**:
-Higher brand loyalty (consumers are more cautious when it comes to physical contact)
- Differentiated space existence (comfort, absorbency, material)
- Moderate switching costs

## 2. In-depth dismantling of the competitive landscape

### Comparison of the four major companies

| Enterprise | Revenue Scale | Core Categories | Competitive Strategy | Current Situation |
|------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| Hengan International | ~22.7 billion | Paper towels + sanitary napkins | Steady operation, brand upgrade | Stagnant revenue |
| Vinda International | ~20 billion | Tissue | Acquired and privatized by Essity | Exited from Hong Kong stocks |
| Golden Hongye | ~18 billion | Tissue | Unlisted, low-key operation | Stable |
| Zhongshun Jierou | ~8 billion | Tissues | Decline after founder's turmoil | Falling behind |

### Hengan’s competitive position

The uniqueness of Hengan lies in its **dual-category layout**:
- Tissue (Xinxiangyin) provides scale and channel coverage
- Sanitary napkins (seven dimensions) provide profit margins and brand stickiness

This structure makes Hengan's comprehensive profitability better than that of pure tissue companies (such as Jierou), but it also means that the competitiveness of single product categories may not be as good as that of focused rivals.

### Impact of industry price war

Hengan President Shi Huangjian once publicly pointed out the "malignant involution" of the industry. Specific performance:
- Online platform promotions continue to increase
- Competitors seize share at low prices
- The average price of paper towels continues to decline

Hengan’s response strategy:
- Launched high-end product line (Yunsen Soft Skin Series)
- Increase the proportion of self-operated e-commerce
- Control promotion efforts to protect profit margins

## 3. Munger’s “reverse thinking”

### "Under what circumstances will Hengan become significantly better?"

1. **Pulp prices have dropped significantly**: Raw materials account for a high proportion of costs, and pulp price reductions directly increase profit margins.
2. **Competitors withdraw**: If price wars lead to the exit of small and medium-sized enterprises, industry concentration will increase
3. **Premiumization successful**: If consumers are willing to pay a premium for branded tissues
4. **Sanitary napkin share increase**: breakthrough in high-profit categories

### "Under what circumstances will Hengan become significantly worse?"

1. **Pulp prices soar**: Costs are rising but cannot be fully passed on to consumers
2. **E-commerce white brands are further eroded**: Pinduoduo/Live e-commerce’s low-priced paper towels seize more shares
3. **Population decline is accelerating**: The core user groups of sanitary napkins and diapers (women of childbearing age, infants and young children) are decreasing
4. **Governance issues erupt again**: Affecting investor confidence and valuation

### Probability evaluation

The probability of "getting better" is medium - the cyclical decline in pulp prices is a high probability event, but high-endization and share increase will take time.
The probability of "going bad" is also medium - the competitive pressure of e-commerce white brands is a long-standing structural problem.
**Net conclusion**: Hengan will most likely maintain the status quo - it will not get significantly better or worse.

## 4. Multidisciplinary model analysis

### Game theory model: Prisoner’s dilemma

The price war in the household paper industry is essentially a "prisoner's dilemma":
-Every business knows that price wars are harmful to the industry
- But no one dares to take the lead in raising prices (for fear of losing share to rivals)
- As a result, everyone is locked into low-price competition

**Possible ways to break the dilemma**:
- Industry consolidation (leading companies acquire competitors)
- Differentiated innovation (so that consumers no longer purely compare prices)
- Tacit cooperation (tacit agreement in the industry to raise prices)

Can Hengan break this dilemma? No clear path is currently in sight. After Vinda was acquired and privatized by Essity, there was a precedent for industry consolidation, but it is not clear whether Hengan has the ability/willingness to carry out large-scale mergers and acquisitions.

### Evolutionary Model: Survival of the Fittest vs Boiled Frog in Warm Water

The changes in the competitive environment faced by Hengan are gradual - the rise of e-commerce channels, the rise of white brands, and the strengthening of consumer price comparison behavior. This kind of "boiling frog in warm water" type of change is more difficult to deal with than a sudden shock because:
- The annual changes are not large enough to trigger a sense of crisis
- But after 10 years of accumulation, it may be found that the competitive position has been significantly weakened
- Hengan’s conservative operating style may result in it being passive in this gradual change

### Accounting Perspective: Profit vs Cash Flow

Although Hengan's profits fluctuate, its cash flow has been stable. This means:
- Profit fluctuations are mainly at the accounting level (impairments, provisions, etc.) rather than at the cash level
- A company's actual profitability may be better than reported profits
- High FCF guarantees the sustainability of dividends

## 7. Munger’s conclusion

### Core judgment

The industry in which Hengan International operates is "not good enough" - low differentiation, low growth, and fierce price wars. But the company itself is "good enough" in the industry - leading position, dual-category layout, and stable cash flow.

**Possible comments from Munger**:

"This is an investment that 'won't lose money but won't make a lot of money'. The industry itself is unattractive, but the company's valuation has reflected this. If you are pursuing a stable 6% dividend income plus some appreciation, this is acceptable. But if you are pursuing capital appreciation and compound interest effects, you should find a better industry."

Hengan's "boring" is both a protection (no big losses) and a restriction (no big profits). The key question is: Are you satisfied with the expected annual return of 8-10% (dividends + moderate appreciation)? If you are satisfied, Hengan's valuation is reasonable; if you are pursuing higher returns, you should look for better targets.
