## 1. One sentence summary

**Zhongsheng Holdings is the leading company in the automobile dealer industry that is most likely to go through the cycle, but the industry itself faces structural challenges in the new energy era, and the current valuation reflects a "survival discount" rather than a "growth premium." **

---

## Summary of second and fourth dimension analysis

### Business Model Analysis (Comprehensive Rating: ★★★☆☆)

**Core findings**:
- Dealers are a "middleman" business. New car sales are no longer profitable (gross profit margin -3%), and after-sales service is the only profit pillar.
- New energy has a double impact on the dealer model: channel model change (direct sales vs. distribution) + reduced after-sales demand (electric vehicles are easy to maintain)
- Industry cooperation proves that the dealer model will not be completely eliminated, but the value chain is narrowing

**Key Judgment**: The business model is at a structural inflection point and is not a "good business" in the traditional sense.

### Financial Valuation Analysis (Comprehensive Rating: ★★★☆☆)

**Core findings**:
- Net loss in 2025 is 1.673 billion yuan, but after excluding 2.291 billion yuan in goodwill impairment, it is close to breakeven
- Operating cash flow of 9.4 billion yuan far exceeds net profit, and the real hematopoietic ability is strong
- 20.4 billion cash + 11 billion after-sales gross profit provides a solid financial safety cushion
- The current market capitalization is approximately HK$50 billion, and the valuation corresponding to the neutral scenario is basically reasonable.

**Key Judgment**: Financial security but profitability is at a low level, valuation needs to be verified by earnings recovery

### Industry Competition Analysis (Comprehensive Rating: ★★★☆☆)

**Core findings**:
- The industry is undergoing an unprecedented reshuffle, with 58% of dealers losing money, Guanghui delisting, and Wanjia 4S stores closing.
- Zhongsheng is the strongest candidate for "last man standing" - with the most cash, the strongest after-sales service, and the fastest transformation
- Cooperation with the industry signifies new energy brands’ recognition of the value of dealer channels
- But the premise of "last standing" is that the dealer model itself is not completely eliminated

**Key Judgment**: The leading position is stable but there are major uncertainties in the industry prospects

### Risk Management Assessment (Comprehensive Rating: ★★★☆☆)

**Core findings**:
- The founders hold a total of 49% of the shares, and their interests are highly bound.
- Excellent execution: cash flow improved significantly despite adversity, and Wenjie stores were quickly launched
- The biggest risk is structural (disruption of the dealer model), not financial or managerial
- The margin of safety exists but is not sufficient - the liquidation value is about 30-40 billion yuan to provide bottom support

**Key Judgment**: The management is excellent but industry risks exceed personal capabilities.

## 4. Investment thesis

### Core arguments (if you want to buy, what are the reasons?)

1. **Industry clearing dividend**: After the weak players exit in large numbers, the market share of the leading players will naturally increase, and profitability will eventually be restored.
2. **New Energy Transformation Catalyst**: With the implementation of the cooperation between the world and the expansion of Geely, the new energy business may become the second growth curve
3. **After-sales service safety cushion**: 11 billion gross profit provides stable cash flow, and the company can survive even if new cars continue to make losses
4. **Valuation has reflected extremely pessimistic expectations**: The market values businesses other than after-sales business at close to zero

### Core Risks (If you don’t buy, what are your concerns?)

1. **The dealer model may be eliminated by history**: This is not the bottom of the cycle, but the beginning of the end of the industry
2. **Electric vehicle after-sales erosion**: The number of fuel vehicles will peak in 3-5 years, and the "last fortress" of after-sales service will also be shaken.
3. **Endless price war**: If brands continue to pass on losses to dealers, cash reserves will eventually be exhausted
4. **No dividend return**: Investors cannot get cash returns while waiting for the transformation process

## 6. Comparison with the classic value investment framework

### Eligible conditions

- The industry is extremely pessimistic and market sentiment is low -- in line with the prerequisites for contrarian investment
- A leading company with the strongest competitive position -- in line with the principle of "buying the best company"
- Safe balance sheet and abundant cash -- meeting the basic requirements of safety margin
- Management's interests are bound - meets the criteria of "buying people"
- Valuations at historically low levels -- qualifying as "reasonably priced or undervalued"

### Ineligible conditions

- The business model faces structural challenges -- it does not meet the standards of "good business"
- No pricing power -- does not meet the core definition of "moat"
- The outcome of the industry is unclear -- does not meet the requirements of "predictability"
- No dividends -- does not meet "shareholder return" expectations
- The path to profit recovery is uncertain -- does not comply with the investment principle of "certainty"

## 7. Final conclusion

### Investment judgment: wait and see, no buying yet

**Reason**:

1. **Not cheap enough**: If this is a confirmed good business, the stock price of HK$21 is very cheap. But in the face of such great industry uncertainty, current valuations do not provide a sufficient margin of safety. The real "golden pit" may be in the range of HK$10-15.

2. **Uncertainty**: There is currently no definite answer to the impact of new energy on the dealer model, the sustainability of industry cooperation, and the long-term trend of after-sales revenue—these core variables. When uncertainty is high, more evidence should be awaited.

3. **Lack of Catalysts**: There are no catalysts in sight that can significantly improve earnings expectations in the short term. The 2026 semi-annual report (expected to be released in August) may be the first observation window-if the gross profit margin of new cars turns positive and the contribution to the industry is significant, it can be re-evaluated.

### If you want to open a position, under what conditions will your judgment change?

1. **The price drops to HK$12-15**: At this price, the liquidation value can provide a sufficient margin of safety
2. **The 2026 semi-annual report shows a significant recovery in profits**: new car gross profit margin is close to 0%, and the contribution of the industry exceeds expectations.
3. **Industry clearing is accelerating**: More competitors have withdrawn, and Zhongsheng’s market share has increased significantly.
4. **Resumption of dividends**: Management releases confidence in the future by restoring dividends
5. **Large-scale increase in holdings by founders**: Not a symbolic few million shares, but an increase in holdings of hundreds of millions of yuan

## 8. Final Thoughts

The case of Zhongsheng Holdings raises an investment philosophy question worth pondering: **Is the combination of "good management + poor industry" worth investing in? **

Buffett's answer is clear: "When a management with a reputation for great management encounters an industry with a reputation for poor fundamentals, without exception, it is the industry that retains the reputation."

But Li Lu might add: "In China, the speed and magnitude of industry change may exceed expectations—for better or worse."

Zhongsheng Holdings is not a "buy on the table" opportunity, but it deserves continued attention. Buying the best companies during an industry's darkest moments has historically produced huge returns many times. The question is: are we already in the "darkest hour" or is the night just beginning?

The 2026 semi-annual report will be a key observation window.
