## Core Business

Zhongsheng Holdings is one of the largest luxury car dealer groups in China. It mainly operates:

1. **New car sales**: luxury brands such as Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, and Lexus, as well as mid-to-high-end brands such as Toyota, Honda, and Nissan; new energy brands such as AITO/Hongmeng Zhixing will be added at the end of 2024
2. **After-sales Service**: Repair and maintenance, spare parts sales, extended warranty service
3. **Second-hand car business**: Second-hand car transactions and replacements
4. **Auto Finance and Insurance**: Car loan commission, insurance agency

## Research background

- **Industry drastic changes**: The penetration rate of new energy vehicles has exceeded 50%, and traditional luxury brand dealers have encountered price inversion and profit collapse.
- **Performance Turning**: A net loss of RMB 1.673 billion was recorded for the first time in 2025, of which goodwill impairment was RMB 2.291 billion.
- **Valuation depression**: 2026E PE is about 4.3 times, and the market’s valuation of the traditional dealer model is seriously discounted
- **Critical period of transformation**: Accelerate the layout of new energy brands such as Huawei and Geely, with the goal of doubling the number of new energy stores by the end of 2026
