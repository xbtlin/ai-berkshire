# Zhongsheng Holdings (0881.HK) Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, balance sheet safety, cash flow, intrinsic value estimation
> Core question: Is 4.3 times PE a value trap or a gold pit?

---

## 1. Income statement: the surface and depth of losses

### 2025 full year results

| Projects | 2025 | 2024 | Year-on-year changes |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Operating income | 164.4 billion yuan | 168.1 billion yuan | -2.2% |
| New car sales revenue | 138.3 billion yuan | 140.7 billion yuan | -1.7% |
| After-sales service revenue | 22.9 billion yuan | 22 billion yuan | +4.1% |
| Used car revenue | 12.4 billion yuan | 15.4 billion yuan | -19.4% |
| Net profit | -1.673 billion yuan | +3.2 billion yuan | From profit to loss |
| New car sales | 497,000 units | 485,000 units | +2.5% |
| Used car sales | 221,200 vehicles | 226,000 vehicles | -2.2% |

### Loss dismantling

The net loss in 2025 will be 1.673 billion yuan, but this number needs to be dismantled carefully:

**1. Impairment of goodwill and intangible assets: 2.291 billion yuan**

This is the largest non-cash loss. Zhongsheng has accrued goodwill impairment for some underperforming stores (mainly traditional luxury brands). This means that the premium paid when acquiring these stores is no longer worth that much in the current market environment.

From an investment perspective, impairment has two sides:
- The bad side: Indicates that past acquisition decisions were not wise enough in hindsight
- The good side: clear the burden at once, and the future profit base will be cleaner

**2. Gross loss of new car sales expands**

The overall gross profit margin of new cars is -3%, further deteriorating from 2024. Gross loss increased by approximately 70% year-on-year. This reflects an industry-wide problem: the pressure from OEMs to cut prices is transmitted to dealers, who are forced to sell new cars at a price lower than the purchase price to maintain sales.

**3. Financial commissions plummet**

Auto finance commission income was 2.574 billion yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 38.7%. This is mainly the result of tighter regulation and increased competition, rather than a temporary fluctuation.

**If the non-cash impairment of 2.291 billion yuan is excluded, Zhongsheng's operating level in 2025 is actually close to breakeven. ** This is an important criterion for judgment - the company's "blood-forming ability" has not been completely lost.

## 2. Balance sheet: How thick is the safety cushion?

### Cash and Liquidity

- Cash balance: 20.438 billion yuan
- Completed the repayment of convertible bonds and 2026 bonds
- Highly liquid vehicle inventory + cash can cover all long- and short-term liabilities

This is the most reassuring thing about Zhongsheng Holdings. Even in a loss-making year, the company had more than 20 billion in cash and had paid off its major debt. Compared with China Grand Automobile, which was forced to delist due to the debt crisis, Zhongsheng's balance sheet is several orders of magnitude safer.

### Operating cash flow

In 2025, the net cash inflow from operating activities will be 9.405 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 5.966 billion yuan.

**This is the most critical indicator. ** Net profit can fluctuate significantly due to non-cash items such as impairment and depreciation, but operating cash flow represents the company's actual cash generation ability. Description of operating cash flow of 9.4 billion:

1. After-sales service and daily operations are indeed generating positive cash flow
2. Inventory management has improved (no longer hoarding large quantities of unsaleable vehicles)
3. The company has the ability to self-sustain operations and does not need to rely on external financing.

### Asset Quality

Risk points that need attention:
- Inventory impairment risk: As the price war continues, the market value of inventory vehicles may further shrink.
- Accounts receivable: mainly rebates receivable from OEMs. If the brand owner has operational difficulties, payment may be postponed.
- Remaining goodwill: After accruing 2.291 billion yuan, how much goodwill is left on the account? If the industry continues to deteriorate, there may be subsequent impairments

## 3. Profitability analysis

### Profit quality of each business segment

| Business segments | Revenue | Gross profit margin | Trend judgment |
|----------|------|--------|----------|
| New car sales | 138.3 billion | -3% | Difficult to turn regular in the short term, relying on brand subsidies |
| After-sales service | 22.9 billion | ~48% | Steady growth, core profit source |
| Used cars | 12.4 billion | Low single digits | Shrinking scale, falling bicycle prices |
| Financial commissions | 2.574 billion | High profit margins | Tightening of regulations and continued decline |

**After-sales service is the company's "profit cornerstone". ** The after-sales gross profit of 11.05 billion yuan can cover the company's entire operating costs. This "zero service absorption rate" is at the leading level in the industry. In other words, even if the new cars don't make a penny, the company can survive by relying on after-sales.

### Key variables for profit recovery

1. **When will the gross profit margin of new cars turn positive? ** If the price war eases in 2026 (there are signs that car companies will start to increase prices in early 2026), the gross profit margin of new cars may rise from -3% to around 0%, which is a profit improvement of about 4 billion yuan.

2. **New contribution from Wenjie**: Each Wenjie store is expected to contribute an annual net profit of approximately 20 million yuan. If the number of Wenjie stores reaches 80-100 by the end of 2026, the profit contribution will be about 1.6-2 billion yuan.

3. **Is the impairment still there? ** If the biggest burden has been cleared in 2025, the amount of impairment in 2026 should be significantly reduced.

## 4. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

| Market value | About HK$50 billion (about RMB 46 billion) |

| 2026E PE | About 9-13 times |
| Dividend rate (TTM) | 0% (no dividend will be paid in 2025) |

Note: If we use the statement "2026E PE is about 4.3 times", it corresponds to a net profit expectation of about 10.7 billion yuan. This may be a more optimistic profit assumption (including comprehensive profits of new energy stores + positive new car gross profit margin + no impairment), or it may be based on different stock price and expected combinations. Taking into account the large differences in expectations among various brokerages, it is more prudent to conservatively estimate the 2026E PE in the range of 9-13 times.

### Historical valuation comparison

The historical PE center of Zhongsheng Holdings is about 10-15 times. At the high point in 2021, the PE is about 20 times, the stock price exceeds HK$70, and the market value exceeds HK$170 billion. The current high market value has evaporated by more than HK$150 billion.

### Intrinsic value estimation

**Scenario 1: Pessimism (the dealer model continues to shrink)**
- Assuming that net profit will be stable at 2-3 billion yuan in the next five years (after-sales support)
- Give 8 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 16-24 billion yuan
- The converted stock price is about HK$7-10
- There is room for a 50-65% downside relative to the current stock price

**Scenario 2: Neutral (the transformation has initial results)**
- Assuming net profit returns to 3.5-5 billion yuan in 2026-2027
- Give 10 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 35-50 billion yuan
- The converted stock price is about HK$15-21
- Basically the same as the current stock price

**Scenario 3: Optimism (new energy + after-sales two-wheel drive)**
- Assuming net profit reaches 5-7 billion yuan after 2027 (close to 2023 level)
- Give 12 times PE, corresponding to a market value of 60-84 billion yuan
- The converted stock price is about HK$26-36
- 22-69% upside potential relative to current stock price

## 5. Valuation comparison with peers

The overall valuation of the car dealer sector is at a historically low level:

- Yongda Auto: also turned from profit to loss, with greater valuation pressure
- Meidong Auto: Smaller but with higher after-sales gross profit margin
- China Grand Automobile: Delisted in 2024, lessons learned from past mistakes

As the industry leader, Zhongsheng's financial status is significantly better than that of its peers. In the major reshuffle of the industry, if the weak ones withdraw (Guanghui has delisted and Zhengtong has operational difficulties), the market share of the leader is expected to naturally increase.

## 6. Thinking about discounted cash flow (DCF)

For a company with huge profit margin fluctuations and an industry facing structural changes, the certainty of DCF valuation is low. But there is a simplified idea:

- Zhongsheng’s after-sales service generates approximately 11 billion in gross profit every year, and contributes approximately 5 to 6 billion in profit after deducting expenses.
- This part of the income is relatively stable and can be regarded as "annuity-like"
- If only the after-sales service part is valued, calculated based on 10 times PE, the value is about 50-60 billion yuan
- This means that the market’s current valuations for new car sales, used cars, finance and other businesses are close to zero or even negative

**This may be where the "margin of safety" lies - the market has already priced in the most pessimistic expectations. **

## 8. Summary

The financial situation of Zhongsheng Holdings shows the characteristics of "ice and fire":

**The "Fire" Side**: 20.4 billion in cash, 9.4 billion in operating cash flow, 11 billion in after-sales gross profit - these figures show that the company's core operating capabilities are still strong, and it is far from an "endangered enterprise."

**The "ice" side**: Net loss of 1.673 billion, new car gross profit margin -3%, financial commissions cut in half, and dividends cancelled--these signals indicate that the traditional profit model has been broken, and a new profit model has not yet been established.

In terms of valuation, the current market value of approximately HK$50 billion corresponds to a "transition period valuation" - the market neither believes that the company will go bankrupt (otherwise the stock price will be lower), nor does it believe that the company can recover quickly (otherwise the PE will not be so low).

**Key Judgment**: If investors believe that profits can return to the level of 3 billion to 5 billion yuan in 2026-2027, the current stock price has a certain margin of safety. However, if the industry deteriorates beyond expectations and the new energy transformation fails to meet standards, valuations may continue to decline. This is not an opportunity to "buy with eyes closed", but a value investment target that requires continuous tracking of the progress of profit recovery.
