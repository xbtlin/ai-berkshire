# Galaxy Entertainment (0027.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, capital allocation, intrinsic value estimation, margin of safety

## 1. Financial quality analysis

### 1.1 Core financial data

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Trends |
|------|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Net income (HKD 100 million) | 353 | 434 | 492 | Steady growth |
| Adjusted EBITDA (HKD billion) | 104 | 122 | 145 | Strong growth |
| Net profit attributable to shareholders (HKD 100 million) | 72 | 88 | 107 | Accelerated growth |
| EBITDA profit margin | 29.5% | 28.1% | 29.5% | Stabilizing and recovering |
| Net profit margin | 20.4% | 20.3% | 21.7% | Steady improvement |
| ROE | ~10% | ~11.5% | ~13-14% | Continuous improvement |

**Positive interpretation:**
- Double-digit revenue growth for three consecutive years, reflecting the strong recovery of Macau’s tourism industry
- EBITDA growth (19%) is faster than revenue growth (13%), reflecting operating leverage
- Net profit growth rate (22%) is the fastest, reflecting that non-operating items (financial expenses, etc.) are also improving
- ROE will increase from about 10% in 2023 to about 13-14% in 2025, and capital efficiency is recovering

**Worth Attention:**
- EBITDA margin of 29.5% is still lower than pre-pandemic levels of approximately 32-33%
- During the peak period of capital expenditure in the fourth period, depreciation and amortization will increase in the future.
- Revenue growth is expected to slow down to 8-9% in 2026-2027

### 1.2 Balance Sheet—Best in the Industry

| Indicators | Data | Industry Comparison |
|------|------|----------|
| Cash and liquid investments | HK$36.3 billion | Highest in the industry |
| Interest-bearing debt | Close to zero | The only net cash in the industry |
| Net cash | About HK$35 billion | Extremely conservative |
| Total assets | Approximately HK$94.6 billion | — |
| Total liabilities | About HK$14.8 billion | Very low debt ratio |

This is Galaxy Entertainment’s most prominent advantage. Among the six major gambling companies, Galaxy is the only company that does not have bank loans**. The other five companies all borrowed heavily during the epidemic and are still repaying them today.

**What does net cash mean? **
- Zero interest expense, all EBITDA translates directly into shareholder value
- Huge financial flexibility to expand during industry downturns
- Strong ability to withstand black swan events (the three-year epidemic has proven this)
- Ability to continue to increase dividends without worrying about debt repayment pressure

### 1.3 Cash Flow Analysis

| Indicators | 2025 Estimates |
|------|-----------|
| Operating cash flow | About HK$13-14 billion |
| Capital expenditure | About HK$5-6 billion (Phase 4 construction) |
| Free cash flow | About HK$7-8 billion |
| Dividend payment | About HK$5.2 billion (based on 1.20/share) |
| Free cash flow payout ratio | About 65-75% |

Capital expenditures are expected to peak in 2026 (the fourth phase of construction), which may suppress free cash flow. However, after the completion of the fourth phase in 2027, capital expenditures will drop significantly and free cash flow is expected to jump.

## 2. Capital allocation evaluation

### 2.1 Dividend Policy

| Annual | Dividend per share (HKD) | Dividend payout ratio | Trend |
|------|-----------------|--------|------|
| 2023 | 0.85 | ~52% | Recovery |
| 2024 | 1.00 | ~50% | Improvement |
| 2025 | 1.20 (including the recommended final period of 0.80) | ~49% | Continue to improve |

CLSA expects Galaxy's dividend payout ratio to gradually increase to 60% of net profit, especially after the peak of capital expenditures in the fourth period. Based on the current stock price of HK$34.9 and a dividend of HK$1.20 in 2025, the dividend rate is approximately 3.4%.

### 2.2 Buyback

Galaxy Entertainment has historically been less likely to engage in large-scale buybacks, preferring to reward shareholders through dividends. This is consistent with its family holding structure - the majority shareholder has a high shareholding ratio (about 50%+), and dividends are more beneficial to the majority shareholder.

### 2.3 Reinvestment

Main reinvestment directions:
- The fourth phase of the project (estimated total investment is tens of billions of Hong Kong dollars): focusing on non-gaming, expected to be completed in 2027
- Hengqin project (approximately RMB 10 billion): slow progress, uncertain return schedule
- Overseas market exploration (Thailand, etc.): initial stage

**Capital Allocation Rating: ★★★★☆**
The conservative balance sheet management is excellent and dividends continue to grow, but the capital efficiency of the Hengqin project and overseas expansion needs to be verified.

## 3. Valuation analysis

### 3.1 Relative valuation

| Indicators | Galaxy Entertainment | Sands China | Wynn Macau | MGM China |
|------|----------|----------|----------|-----------|
| 2026E PE | ~13x | ~14x | ~10x | ~10x |
| 2026E EV/EBITDA | ~10.1x | ~10.8x | ~6.8x | ~8.3x |

**Interpretation:**
- Galaxy's PE and EV/EBITDA are higher than those in the industry, reflecting the market's premium on its financial security
- The industry's overall EV/EBITDA is approximately 8.5 times, compared with the average of 12.8 times over the past 15 years. There is still room for valuation repair
- If net cash (about HK$35 billion, accounting for about 23% of the market capitalization) is excluded, Galaxy's "operating business PE" is actually only about 10 times

### 3.2 Historical Valuation

Galaxy Entertainment’s historical PE range:
-Trough period (epidemic, policy impact): 8-10 times
- Center: 13-16 times
- Peak period (2017-2018): 20-25 times

The current PE of 13-14 times is at a historically low position.

### 3.3 Intrinsic value estimation (DCF idea)

**Assumption:**
- Net profits from 2026 to 2027 will be HK$11.6 billion and HK$12.5 billion respectively (consensus expectations among brokers)
- Entering a period of stable growth from 2028, with a growth rate of 5-7% (contribution from the fourth phase of production)
- Discount rate 10%
- Terminal growth rate 3%

**Rough estimate:**
- Operating business value: approximately HK$160-180 billion
- Add: Net cash approximately HK$35 billion
-Total intrinsic value: approximately HK$195-215 billion
- Corresponding share: approximately HKD 45-50
- The current stock price is HK$34.9, implying a discount of approximately 25-35%

### 3.4 Analyst consensus

20 analysts unanimously rated "buy", with an average target price of HK$48.67, a minimum of HK$39, and a maximum of HK$55.4. The current share price implies about 39% upside potential.

## 4. Safety Margin Assessment

### 4.1 Favorable factors

1. **Net cash protection**: HK$35 billion in net cash accounts for 23% of market capitalization, providing hard bottom support
2. **Inexpensive valuation**: Operating PE after excluding net cash is about 10 times, a historical low
3. **Growth certainty**: The fourth phase will be put into production in 2027, which will bring certainty to the increase.
4. **Dividend increase**: The dividend payout ratio is expected to increase from 50% to 60%
5. **License until 2032**: At least 6 years of operating certainty

### 4.2 Disadvantages

1. **Slowing growth**: Revenue growth is expected to drop to 8-9% in 2026-2027, and the market may give a lower valuation
2. **Capital expenditure peak**: capex will be the largest in 2026, free cash flow may temporarily decline
3. **EBITDA profit margin has not recovered**: 29.5% vs 32-33% before the epidemic, indicating that the cost structure has changed
4. **Macro Risk**: China’s economic slowdown may inhibit gamblers’ willingness to spend

### 4.3 Safety margin judgment

| Scenario | Valuation | Value per share | Current discount |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Pessimistic (PE 10x + Net Cash) | 142 billion | ~HK$33 | -5% (basically flat) |
| Neutral (PE 13x + Net Cash) | 174.1 billion | ~HKD40 | 13% discount |
| Optimistic (PE 16x + Net Cash) | 206.2 billion | ~HK$48 | 27% discount |

The current stock price has basically no margin of safety under pessimistic scenarios, but there is some room under neutral and optimistic scenarios.

Galaxy Entertainment's financial quality is unrivaled in the gaming industry, and its net cash position provides a strong safety cushion. But the current price of HK$34.9 is not an "obvious bargain" - there is almost no margin of safety in a pessimistic scenario. For value investors pursuing a margin of safety, a more ideal buying price may be in the range of HK$28-32 (corresponding to PE 10-11 times).
