## 2. Summary of investment thesis

### 2.1 Core arguments

Galaxy Entertainment is a company that occupies a dominant position in a high-barrier industry, has the best balance sheet in the industry, and has a deterministic growth engine (Phase 4)**.

**Why it’s worth paying attention to:**
1. License monopoly - Macau only has 6 licenses, valid until 2032
2. Zero debt—the only net cash company in the industry (HK$35 billion)
3. Share increase - from 20% to 21.7%, the trend is improving
4. Growth is confirmed - the fourth phase will be put into production in 2027 to bring incremental growth
5. Increasing returns to shareholders – the dividend payout ratio is expected to increase from 50% to 60%

## 6. Research summary

Galaxy Entertainment is a **good but not great** company:

**Advantages:** The best balance sheet in the industry, license monopoly barriers, solid management, clear growth path, and increasing shareholder returns.

**Disadvantages:** Industry growth will slow down, EBITDA profit margin has not been restored, policy risks are uncontrollable, dependence on a single region, and customer brand loyalty is limited.

**Final Verdict:** This is a stock that is "worth owning but requires patience to wait for a good price". The current price of HK$34.9 provides a modest margin of safety, but is not the extreme undervaluation of "buy with eyes closed". If one day it falls to the HKD 28-30 range due to short-term negatives (such as quarterly results that are worse than expected, macro panic, etc.), it will be a significantly better buying opportunity - the PE will drop below 10 times, and the PE excluding net cash is only 7-8 times, and you can also lock in a 4%+ dividend rate.
