## Research background

Galaxy Entertainment is one of the six major gaming licensees in Macau and is known for its financial health - zero net debt and ample cash reserves (HK$36.3 billion in cash and liquid investments). The company is controlled by the Lui Che-woo family. After the death of founder Lui Che-woo in November 2024, his son Lui Yiu-tung took over as chairman of the board of directors.

**Research Triggers:**
- Macau’s most financially sound gaming stocks
- PE 13-14 times, at a low position in the center of historical valuation
- ROE 13-14%, stable and with room for improvement
- License monopoly (Macau only has 6 gaming licenses, valid until 2032)
- Completion of Phase 4 project in 2027 will bring growth
