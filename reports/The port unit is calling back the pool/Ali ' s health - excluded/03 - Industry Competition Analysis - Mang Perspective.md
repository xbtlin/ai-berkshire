#Alibaba Health (0241.HK)—Industry Competition Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, competition landscape, moat evolution, long-term industry trends

## 1. Industry panorama

### 1.1 Overview of China’s pharmaceutical e-commerce industry

After nearly ten years of development, China's pharmaceutical e-commerce industry has moved from the stage of "can I buy medicine online?" to the stage of competition of "which platform is better to buy on?"

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Online drug retail market size in 2025 | ~300 billion yuan |
| Pharmaceutical e-commerce penetration rate | ~15-18% |
| The proportion of online prescription drugs | ~30% and rising rapidly |
| Expected growth rate | 15-20%/year |

### 1.2 Industry drivers

**Growth Drivers:**
1. **Prescription drug outflow**: Hospital prescriptions outflow to retail/online channels
2. **Chronic disease management needs**: follow-up consultation + prescription refill + online medication management
3. **Young user habits**: Generation Z is more inclined to purchase drugs online
4. **Policy Promotion**: The policy for online sales of prescription drugs is gradually relaxed

**Constraints:**
1. Some drugs (acute diseases) still need to be purchased offline
2. Cold chain pharmaceutical logistics restrictions
3. Imperfect access to medical insurance payment
4. Patients’ trust in online drug purchase still needs to be cultivated

### 1.3 Industrial chain structure

```
Pharmaceutical companies → Distributors → Pharmaceutical e-commerce platforms/retail pharmacies → Consumers/patients
                            ↑
                      Online consultation/prescription
```

Pharmaceutical e-commerce is in the "retail terminal" position of the industry chain. It has certain bargaining power facing the upstream (large platforms have large purchasing volumes), and is in fierce competition facing consumers.

### 2.3 The threat of Meituan

Meituan’s purchase of drugs is an emerging threat to Alibaba Health:
- Core advantage: The immediacy of "30-minute home delivery" surpasses next-day delivery in acute disease medication scenarios
- Strong scene entrance: Meituan itself is a super entrance to local life, and users open it frequently
- Mature delivery network: Relying on Meituan’s rider network, delivery cost margins are extremely low

The entry of Meituan has escalated the competition among pharmaceutical e-commerce companies from "next-day delivery" to "half-hour delivery", which poses a direct threat to Alibaba Health's self-operated model.

### 2.4 Judgment of competitive trends

- **Short term**: JD Health and Alibaba Health duopoly, Meituan is catching up quickly
- **Mid-term**: It is possible to form a "three-legged alliance" (JD.com + Alibaba + Meituan), each focusing on different scenarios
- **Long-term**: The winner depends on who can establish the strongest "medicine + medicine + data" closed loop

## 3. In-depth analysis of moat

### 3.1 Objective assessment of moats

**Moat claimed by Alibaba Health:**
1. Alibaba ecological traffic
2. Big data and AI capabilities
3. Merchant Ecology
4. Physician resources

**Actual moat strength:**

| Moat elements | Declared strength | Actual strength | Reasons |
|-----------|----------|----------|------|
| Traffic entrance | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Alibaba’s e-commerce share is declining |
| Supply chain | ★★★☆☆ | ★★☆☆☆ | Far inferior to JD.com |
| Data capabilities | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Capable but not fully realized |
| User stickiness | ★★★☆☆ | ★★☆☆☆ | Extremely low switching cost |
| Physician resources | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Large quantity but quality and depth need to be verified |

### 3.2 The core weakness of the moat

**Why is it said that the moat is "not deep"? **

1. **Lack of exclusivity**: Any consumer can use multiple platforms to purchase drugs at the same time
2. **Lack of network effect**: increasing the number of users will not make other users’ experience better
3. **Lack of differentiation**: The same medicines are sold, and the difference in service experience is limited.
4. **Dependence on mother body**: Without the Alibaba ecosystem, independent survival ability is questionable

### 3.3 Shen Difan’s differentiation strategy

New CEO Shen Difan tried to avoid head-on competition with JD Health:
- **Avoid logistics duel**: Don’t try to compete with JD/Meituan on delivery speed
- **Focus on industrial services**: Cooperate with pharmaceutical companies to provide "digital patient support" and "chronic disease management"
- **Platform Connector Role**: Give full play to Alibaba's ability as a "super platform" to connect pharmaceutical companies and patients

Whether this strategic direction can be successful or not will take time to verify.

## 4. Long-term trends in the industry

### 4.1 Trend with higher certainty

1. **Pharmaceutical e-commerce penetration rate continues to increase**: moving from 15% to 30%+
2. **Expansion of online sales of prescription drugs**: Policies continue to be liberalized
3. **Instant delivery becomes standard**: Meituan’s model will force other platforms to increase delivery speed
4. **AI+Medical Deep Integration**: Intelligent diagnosis and treatment, medication recommendation and other scenarios

### 4.2 Uncertainty factors

1. **Will the final competitive landscape be "winner takes all" or "multiple companies coexist"? **
2. Will **medical insurance payment be connected to online channels on a large scale? **
3. **Will the online transformation of traditional pharmacy chains divert incremental revenue? **
4. **Where is the profit margin ceiling for pharmaceutical e-commerce companies? **

## 5. Alibaba Health’s long-term positioning

### 5.1 Best case scenario

If Alibaba Health successfully transforms into a "health management platform":
- Integrate medication data + consultation records + health monitoring to form a closed loop
- Form "health management dependence" on users instead of "drug purchase channel dependence"
- Profit margin increased to 10%+, PE revalued to 30x (reasonable)

### 5.2 Neutral Scenario

Maintain the positioning of "the pharmaceutical retail entrance of Alibaba Ecosystem":
- Revenue growth of 10-12%/year
- Net interest rate remains stable at 7-8%
- But valuation returns from 39x to 25x

### 5.3 Worst case scenario

Alibaba’s e-commerce overall declines + JD/Meituan competition intensifies:
- Revenue growth drops to single digits
- Margins are compressed
- Valuation fell to 15-20x

It lags behind JD Health in most dimensions and faces emerging threats from Meituan. The biggest problem is that the moat is not deep - if the moat is not deep, then 39 times PE will be difficult to justify.
