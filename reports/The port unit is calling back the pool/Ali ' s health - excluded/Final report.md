### One sentence summary

Alibaba Health is in a good track (pharmaceutical e-commerce), but its competitive position is not strong, its moat is not deep, and its PE of 39 times lacks a safety margin—in the same track, JD Health is a better choice.

---

## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **Profit turning point confirmed**: FY2025 net profit increased by 62%, adjusted growth of 36%, profit margins continued to improve
2. **Pharmaceutical e-commerce penetration rate increases**: moving from 15% to 30%+, industry growth is confirmed
3. **Differentiation Strategy**: Shen Difan focuses on industrial services and digital patient support, avoiding head-on logistics
4. **Alibaba Ecological Value**: Huge traffic pool and data capabilities are natural resources

### Bear Case

1. **Not deep moat**: low user switching cost, lack of exclusivity and network effect
2. **Lagging behind JD Health in all aspects**: Scale, growth rate, profit margin, and valuation are all inferior to JD Health.
3. **39 times PE lacks safety margin**: Corresponding to 8% ROE and 13% growth rate, the pricing is too expensive
4. **Depend on Alibaba Ecosystem**: The decline in Alibaba’s e-commerce share will directly affect the traffic base
5. **Meituan Threat**: Instant delivery model may change the competitive landscape

## Core variable tracking list

1. **Can revenue growth maintain double digits**: Changes in Alibaba’s ecological traffic are the key
2. **Profit margin improvement trajectory**: Is the path from 6.4% to 8-10% visible?
3. **Is the gap with JD Health expanding or narrowing**
4. **The effect of Shen Difan’s differentiation strategy**: changes in the proportion of industrial service revenue
5. **Meituan’s market share growth rate**: degree of diversion from Alibaba Health
6. **Alibaba Group’s resource investment in the health sector**: Whether it continues to give strategic priority

---

## Valuation and buying conditions

### Current valuation judgment

PE of 39 times is too expensive given the current growth rate (13%) and profitability (ROE 8%). More importantly, it is difficult to find sufficient reasons to buy Alibaba Health when JD Health offers better growth and profit margins at a PE of 23 times.

### If I have to buy it, what is the reasonable price?

- PE is around 25x (current stock price is discounted by more than 60%) to have a reasonable margin of safety
- Or wait until the profit margin increases to 10%+ and ROE to 12%+, proving that the moat has been established

### Position suggestions

- Current price: **Not recommended to buy**
- If it must be configured in the pharmaceutical e-commerce track, **priority will be given to JD Health**
- If Alibaba Health PE pulls back below 25x, it can be re-evaluated

## Comparison with JD Health’s investment decisions

This is the most critical investment decision issue. The conclusion of the comparison between the two companies in the same track is clear:

| Decision Factors | Alibaba Health | JD Health |

| Margin of safety | None | There is a certain margin of safety |

**Conclusion: If you are optimistic about the pharmaceutical e-commerce track, JD Health is a clear and better choice. **

---

## Conditions under which Alibaba Health may "counterattack"

Although it is not optimistic at present, if the following conditions occur, it needs to be re-evaluated:

1. **AI Health Management Platform Breakthrough**: If Alibaba’s AI technology can create a true closed loop of health management
2. **The proportion of platform business has increased significantly**: the profit margin has therefore jumped to 10%+
3. **Alibaba’s e-commerce share stabilizes and rebounds**: The traffic base is re-established
4. **Medical insurance payment is fully accessible online**: Alibaba has the advantage of Alipay on the payment side

---

## Risk warning

1. Competition among pharmaceutical e-commerce companies may be more intense than expected
2. The decline in Alibaba’s overall e-commerce share will directly affect Alibaba’s health
3. The high valuation of 39 times PE faces the risk of correction
4. Management strategies may take 2-3 years to take effect, and they face uncertainty during this period.
5. If Alibaba Group adjusts the priority of its big health strategy, it may affect resource investment

---

## Final judgment

Alibaba Health is a target with "good track, average players, and expensive pricing". In the pharmaceutical e-commerce track, it is neither the strongest competitor nor the cheapest target. For investors pursuing the optimal risk-return ratio, JD Health provides better investment opportunities in the same track.

The core issue of Ali's health is not "can it survive", but "can it build a real moat". If the answer is no, then it is an "online pharmacy with medium profit margins" and is not worth 39 times PE. If the answer is yes (through AI health management, digital industry services, etc.), then the current valuation may be reasonable - but it will take time to verify.
