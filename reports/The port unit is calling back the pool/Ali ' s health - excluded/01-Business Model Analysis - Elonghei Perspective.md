#Alibaba Health (0241.HK)—Business Model Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: nature of business model, user value, competition barriers, long-term economic characteristics

## 1. What is the essence of business?

Alibaba Health's core business is "pharmaceutical e-commerce + health service platform", which relies on the traffic ecological operation of Alibaba Group.

Business composition:
- **Pharmaceutical self-operated** (main source of income): direct sales of medicines and health products through Tmall Pharmacy
- **Platform Business**: Third-party merchant commissions and advertising revenue from Tmall Medical Center
- **Medical and health services**: online consultation, physical examination appointment, vaccine appointment, etc.
- **Digital Services**: Digital marketing and patient management services for pharmaceutical companies

In essence, what Alibaba Health does is "use the Internet to sell medicine + provide health services."

### Mode selection of self-operated vs. platform

Alibaba Health faces a strategic dilemma:
- **Self-operated**: high income but low gross profit margin (~20%), essentially an "online pharmacy"
- **Platform**: low income but high profit margin (commission + advertising), the essence is "traffic monetization"

At present, the company is mainly self-operated, which means: high income, low profit margin, and emphasis on operation. This is fundamentally different from Alibaba Group's traditional "light platform" model.

## 2. User value analysis

### Consumer side

The value Alibaba Health provides consumers:
1. **Convenience**: Buy medicines without leaving home, especially for privacy needs.
2. **Rich variety**: 1.23 million+ self-operated SKUs, far exceeding that of independent pharmacies
3. **Price Transparency**: Comparable prices, some varieties have price advantages
4. **Health Services**: Convenience of online consultation and health consultation

**User stickiness evaluation: ★★☆☆☆**

This is the core problem - Alibaba Health’s user stickiness is not strong:
- Consumers’ purchase of medicines is “demand-driven” rather than “brand-driven”, so go wherever it’s cheaper.
- User switching costs between pharmaceutical e-commerce companies are extremely low (just install another App)
- There is no strong social relationship or content ecology to bind users
- Limited differentiation from JD Health and Meituan Pharmacy

### Merchant side

Platform value provided to pharmaceutical companies and retailers:
1. **Traffic Acquisition**: Tmall/Taobao’s massive user base
2. **Digital Marketing**: Accurate user portraits and marketing tools
3. **Channel Expansion**: Supplement the online reach of offline channels
4. **Data Insight**: Consumer behavior data assists decision-making

## 3. Barriers to competition (moat)

### 3.1 Traffic entry barrier ★★★☆☆

Relying on the huge traffic pool of Taobao/Tmall, Alibaba Health has obtained low-cost user acquisition channels. But this barrier has two sides:

- **Advantages**: No need to acquire customers independently, low marginal cost of traffic
- **Risk**: Over-reliance on group traffic and lack of independent customer acquisition capabilities; if Alibaba’s e-commerce share declines, Alibaba Health will also be affected

In recent years, Alibaba’s e-commerce share has been under pressure in competition with Pinduoduo and Douyin, which poses an indirect threat to Alibaba’s healthy traffic base.

### 3.2 Supply chain barriers ★★☆☆☆

Alibaba Health’s supply chain capabilities are significantly weaker than JD Health:
- No self-built logistics system (relying on third-party logistics and Cainiao)
- Cold chain capabilities are not as good as JD Logistics
- Delivery timeliness and certainty are not as good as JD.com’s self-operated

This is one of Alibaba Health’s biggest shortcomings—pharmaceutical products have extremely high logistics requirements (cold chain, timeliness, safety), and Alibaba Health does not have an advantage in this regard.

### 3.3 Data and technical barriers ★★★☆☆

- Relying on Alibaba Cloud's technical infrastructure
- Accumulation of user health data (drug purchase records + consultation records)
- AI-assisted diagnosis and treatment capabilities are under construction

However, these capabilities are also being rapidly built by JD Health and Meituan and do not constitute strong barriers to differentiation.

### 3.4 Compliance/License Barriers ★★★☆☆

- Internet hospital license
- Online drug sales qualification
- Nearly 240,000 contracted doctors/pharmacists

Compliance qualifications are a barrier to entry but not a barrier to differentiation—competitors have them too.

### 3.5 Brand Barriers ★★☆☆☆

"Tmall Pharmacy" has a certain brand recognition, but in the pharmaceutical field, consumers trust "drug brands" more than "channel brands". When buying medicines, what consumers care about is the medicine itself, not which platform to buy it from.

## 4. Economic characteristics of business model

### Revenue Predictability ★★★☆☆

- The overall growth trend of pharmaceutical e-commerce is confirmed (penetration rate increases)
- However, the specific growth rate is affected by changes in Alibaba’s ecological traffic, competitive landscape and policies.
- Platform business (commission + advertising) is relatively stable

### Profit rate characteristics

| Indicators | FY2024 | FY2025 | Trends |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| Gross profit margin | ~22% | ~24% | ↑Improvement |
| Adjusted Net Margin | ~5.3% | ~6.4% | ↑Improvement |
| Statement net profit margin | ~3.3% | ~4.7% | ↑Improvement |

Margins improving but still low:
- The essence of self-operated business is retail, and the profit margin ceiling is not high
- Platform business has high profit margin but limited revenue share
- Customer acquisition and operating costs remain high

### Capital efficiency ★★☆☆☆

ROE is about 8%, which is low for an Internet company. Reason:
1. Self-operated business requires a large amount of working capital (inventory + receivables)
2. It has not yet entered the stage of accelerated profit release.
3. Net assets include more cash and investments

### Free Cash Flow ★★★☆☆

Operating cash flow is positive and improving, but the self-operated model requires continued investment in inventory and logistics.

## 5. Core questioning from Duan Yongping’s perspective

**"Where is the moat of this business?"**

Frankly speaking, Ali's healthy moat is not deep. Its core asset is the traffic entrance to the Alibaba ecosystem, but this entrance is not exclusive:
- Consumers can use JD.com, Meituan and Alibaba to buy medicines at the same time
- No strong locking effect
- Unlike WeChat/Alipay, which has strong binding of payment habits

It is more like a "channel" than a "platform" - a channel is characterized by high substitutability, and a platform is characterized by strong network effects.

**"What will happen in ten years?"**

Two paths:
1. **Become a real health management platform**: If a closed loop of "data + AI + doctors" health management can be established, the stickiness will be greatly improved. But the completion of this closed loop is not yet visible.
2. **Maintain the positioning of "online pharmacy"**: It is a retail business with low profit margins and fierce competition, and is not worth 39 times PE.

**"How much is this company worth without Alibaba's traffic?"**

This is the most critical question. If the traffic support of Alibaba Ecosystem is stripped away, the independent value of Alibaba Health will be greatly reduced. This is not a company that can "exist independently", but an accessory to the Alibaba ecosystem.

**Overall Rating: ★★★☆☆ (2.8/5.0)**

The core weakness of the business model is that the moat is not deep. In a field where "anyone can do it", it is difficult for companies that rely on parent traffic to establish real independent value. Unless differentiated barriers can be established in the field of health services, it will only be an "online pharmacy with medium profit margins" in the long run.
