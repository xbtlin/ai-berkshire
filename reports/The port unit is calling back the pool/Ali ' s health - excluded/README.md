## Research Summary

Alibaba Health is a comprehensive health platform under Alibaba Group. It operates pharmaceutical e-commerce business relying on the traffic portals of Tmall and Taobao. The company's revenue exceeded 30 billion but profit margins are still low. The 39 times PE reflects the market's expectations for growth but also implies high valuation risks. The core question is: How deep is Alibaba’s healthy moat?

## Core investment logic

**Reasons to be long:**
- The penetration rate of pharmaceutical e-commerce is still increasing, and the industry space is broad
- Alibaba’s ecological traffic entrance is a natural advantage
- The profit turning point has been reached, with net profit increasing by 62% year-on-year
- Service-oriented transformation (online consultation, chronic disease management) opens up the second growth curve

**Reasons for shorting:**
- The PE valuation of 39 times is too high, corresponding to the current ROE of 8%
- The moat is not deep, JD Health wins in multiple dimensions
- Relying on the Alibaba ecosystem and having limited independent value
- Pharmaceutical e-commerce competition is fierce and profitability ceiling is not high
