### 1.3 Profit quality analysis

**The "gold content" of profits deserves vigilance:**

1. **High proportion of government subsidies**: SMIC receives a large amount of government subsidies every year (about 2-3 billion yuan in 2025). If the subsidies are deducted, the actual operating profit will be even lower.
2. **Non-net profit is more realistic**: In 2025, non-net profit will increase by 55.9% year-on-year. Although the growth rate is faster, the absolute value is still not high.
3. **Depreciation eats up profits**: Depreciation corresponding to capital expenditures of US$8.1 billion is rising year by year, and Q4 gross profit margin has dropped to 19.2% due to increased depreciation.

**Financial Quality Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**

Reason: Revenue growth is good, but profits are thin, reliance on subsidies is high, and depreciation pressure continues to increase.

## 2. Capital return analysis

### 2.1 ROE dismantling (DuPont analysis)

| Components | SMIC | TSMC | Gap |
|----------|----------|--------|------|
| Net profit margin | 7.4% | 38% | 5 times |
| Asset turnover rate | 0.25 | 0.55 | 2 times |
| Equity Multiplier | 1.6 | 1.5 | Close |
| **ROE** | **About 3%** | **About 30%** | **10 times** |

SMIC’s low ROE mainly comes from:
1. Very low net profit margin (depreciation + low gross profit)
2. Low asset turnover rate (huge fixed assets have not yet fully generated income)

### 2.2 ROIC (return on invested capital)

Estimated ROIC: operating profit after tax / invested capital ≈ 5 billion / 300 billion+ ≈ **less than 2%**

This figure means: For every 100 yuan of capital invested by the company, it can only earn back less than 2 yuan a year. ** Well below the cost of capital (usually assuming 8-10%), in a state of **value destruction**.

### 2.3 Free cash flow

| Indicators | 2025 Estimates |
|------|-----------|
| Operating cash flow | About 20 billion yuan |
| Capital expenditure | About NT$59 billion |
| **Free cash flow** | **About -39 billion yuan** |

The company has had negative free cash flow for many consecutive years, and its capital expenditures will not decrease in 2026 (still exceeding US$8 billion). This means:
- The company cannot cover investment needs through its own operations
- Requires ongoing debt or equity financing
- No cash returns for shareholders in the foreseeable future

**Capital Return Rating: ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**

Reason: ROE 3%, ROIC less than 2%, free cash flow continues to be negative, and no dividends - this is an extremely poor investment from the perspective of capital return.

## 3. Valuation analysis

### 3.1 Current Valuation Level

| Valuation indicators | Values | Historical quantiles | Judgment |
|----------|------|----------|------|
| PE(TTM) | About 216 times | Extremely high | Far beyond the reasonable range |
| PE(2026E) | About 128 times | Extremely high | Implied extremely high growth expectations |
| PB | About 5.3 times | High | ROE 3% cannot support 5 times PB |
| PS (price to sales ratio) | About 12 times | Extremely high | Compared to TSMC, about 10 times |
| EV/EBITDA | About 30 times | High | - |

### 3.2 Expectations Implicit in Valuation

The current market value is 800 billion, corresponding to a net profit of about 5 billion, and the PE is about 160 times.

If it is assumed that the reasonable PE is 30 times (growth manufacturing industry), the market's implicit "final net profit" expectation is:
- 800 billion / 30 = **Net profit of about 26.7 billion yuan**

This means that net profit needs to increase from 5 billion to 26.7 billion, an increase of more than 5 times**.

To achieve a net profit of 26.7 billion yuan, assuming a net profit margin of 15% (close to the industry average), revenue needs to reach approximately 180 billion yuan (2.7 times the current 67.3 billion yuan).

**Is this goal realistic? **
- Optimistic assumptions: revenue will increase by 15% annually and reach approximately 135 billion yuan in five years; net profit margin will increase to 15%, and net profit will be approximately 20 billion yuan
- Even so, it is still less than the 26.7 billion implied by the market.
- What's more, it is difficult to improve net profit margin during the peak period of depreciation.

### 3.3 PB-ROE framework

Under the classic PB-ROE framework:
- PB = 5.3 times
- ROE = 3%

Reasonable relationship: If ROE maintains 3% for a long time, reasonable PB should be 0.5-1 times (lower than net assets)
If ROE can be increased to 15% in the future, the reasonable PB will be about 2-3 times

The current ROE expectation implied by 5.3 times PB is **more than 20%** - which is almost impossible for SMIC to achieve within 5 years.

### 3.4 Valuation comparison with global peers

| Company | PE | PB | ROE | Gross profit margin |
|------|-----|-----|-----|--------|
| TSMC | 25 times | 7 times | 30% | 55% |
| UMC | 12 times | 1.8 times | 15% | 30% |
| GF | 20x | 1.5x | 8% | 25% |
| Huahong | 80 times | 2.5 times | 3% | 12% |
| **SMIC** | **160 times** | **5.3 times** | **3%** | **21%** |

SMIC's valuation multiple far exceeds that of all global peers, which is a typical "domestic substitution premium" pricing.

**Valuation Rating: ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**

Rationale: Current valuations are extremely expensive and implied growth expectations are nearly impossible to achieve. Even if the company's fundamentals continue to improve, current prices have overstretched growth over the next 5-10 years.

## 4. Financial risks

### 4.1 Balance Sheet Risk

-The asset-liability ratio is about 40%, which is acceptable
- But the scale of interest-bearing liabilities continues to increase (debt is needed to fill the free cash flow gap)
- A large number of fixed assets are exposed to depreciation risk (if capacity utilization decreases, depreciation remains unchanged but revenue decreases)

### 4.2 Cash flow risk

- Free cash flow continues to be negative
- Capital expenditures will not decrease in 2026 and cannot be improved in the short term
- If the industry goes down (similar to 2023), cash flow pressure will increase sharply

### 4.3 Profit Cyclicity

The semiconductor industry is highly cyclical. From the high point in 2022 to the low point in 2023, SMIC's revenue fell by 13%. In the next cycle of downturn, small profits may turn into losses in an instant.

**Financial Risk Score: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
