## Research background

SMIC is the largest and most technologically advanced integrated circuit wafer foundry company in mainland China, with sales revenue of US$9.327 billion (67.3 billion yuan) in 2025 and a global wafer foundry market share of approximately 4.8%, ranking second in the world (after TSMC). The company is under U.S. Entity List sanctions and is benefiting from the wave of domestic substitution of Chinese semiconductors. The current valuation is at a historically high level, and the market gives a large "domestic substitution premium."

## Core Contradiction

1. **Huge gap between valuation and profitability**: PE exceeds 100 times, ROE is only 3%, and market pricing implies extremely high growth expectations
2. **Contradiction between strategic value and financial return**: National strategic assets vs. The actual return to shareholders is zero
3. **Contradiction between the necessity of expansion and capital efficiency**: annual capital expenditure of US$8 billion vs. net profit of US$5 billion
4. **Technological breakthrough narrative and commercial reality**: 7nm yield breakthrough vs. the high cost of DUV multiple exposure
