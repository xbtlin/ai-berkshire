## Core conclusion

**Anta Sports is a rare "platform" company in China's consumer goods field. ** Its core capability is not a single brand, but a set of proven system capabilities of "brand acquisition - localized operation - large-scale profitability". From the successful transformation of FILA to the outbreak of Arc'teryx in China, Anta has proven the replicability of this methodology.

**Positive factors:**
- Revenue in 2025 is 80.2 billion yuan, including Amer Group’s total revenue exceeding 127.8 billion yuan, ranking third among global sports brands
- The multi-brand matrix covers all price ranges from mass to ultra-high-end, with strong anti-cyclical capabilities
- Gross profit margin 62%, ROE approximately 25%, operating cash flow 16.7 billion yuan, excellent financial quality
- PE is about 14 times, valuation is at a historically low range, and dividend yield is 3.2%

**Main concerns:**
- The growth rate of Anta's main brand slowed to 3.7%, and FILA's growth of 6.9% faced a ceiling.
- M&A spending in 2025 will be nearly 15 billion yuan (including 12.2 billion yuan to acquire 29% of Puma’s equity), and the pace of expansion will be aggressive
- Puma's performance is sluggish, and acquisitions with high premiums pose the risk of "value traps"
- The complexity of family governance + globalization management has increased sharply, and there is doubt whether the management radius is too long.

**Comprehensive rating: It is worth tracking in depth and waiting for a better buying opportunity. ** Anta is an excellent business, but it is currently in a period of radical expansion. The results of Puma's integration are unclear, and the main brand's growth is weak. It is recommended to continue to pay attention but remain patient. If the valuation falls below 12 times PE or there are positive signals from Puma integration, you may consider building a position.
