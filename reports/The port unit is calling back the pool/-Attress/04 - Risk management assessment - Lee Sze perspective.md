# Four, "irreversible" risk vs "reversible" risk

Recillation emphasizes the distinction between the "irreversible" risk, which, if it occurs, is irreversible, and the "reversible " risk, which, while painful, can be restored.

# Irreversible risk (needs high vigilance)

1. **The huge amount of asset impairment resulting from the Boma acquisition**: If the BMW continues to deteriorate, the investment of $12.2 billion may face substantial impairments and the money will never return.
2. ** Brand reputation collapse**: Brand assets may be permanently damaged in the event of a major quality accident or moral scandal.
3. ** Critical brain drain**: If Tin Shi Trung leaves suddenly for health or other reasons, the group as a whole may lose its strategic direction.

# Reverse risk (need not fatal)

1. **FILA slowdown**: brand cyclical fluctuations, recovery through product innovation and channel adjustment.
2. ** Inventory increase**: Short-term inventory pressure can be absorbed through discounts and normality can be restored for 6 to 12 months.
3. ** Stock price falls**: Stock price re-allocation may be an opportunity to buy, as long as the basics are healthy.
