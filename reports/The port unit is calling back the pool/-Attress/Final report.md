## One sentence conclusion

**ANTA Sports is one of the best consumer product platform companies in China, with a proven multi-brand operating system and strong financial fundamentals. However, it is currently in a period of radical expansion (Puma acquired 12.2 billion, Wolfpaw acquired 2.1 billion), the growth of the main brand has slowed to 3.7%, and multiple uncertainties are superimposed. At a valuation level of 14 times PE, the margin of safety is not sufficient. It is recommended to include it in a deep tracking pool and wait for a better buying window. **

---

## Core investment logic

### Reasons to be long (Bull Case)

1. **Brand operation capabilities are "real skills":** FILA has gone from loss to 28.5 billion in revenue, and Descente has gone from obscurity to tens of billions of dollars - Anta has repeatedly proven its system capability of "acquisition + transformation", which is extremely difficult to copy.

2. **Structural dividends of Arc'teryx + outdoor track:** China's outdoor sports are in the early stages of explosive growth. Anta has the world's strongest outdoor brand matrix (Arc'teryx, Salomon, Peak Performance, etc.) through Amer, which is a long-term growth engine for the next 5-10 years.

3. **Valuation is at a historical low:** 14 times PE, 3.2% dividend yield, and ROE of approximately 25%. For a consumer leader with annual growth of 13% and free cash flow exceeding 13 billion, this valuation is not expensive. Analyst consensus target price is HK$112.5 (+37%).

4. **Strategic position as the third largest sports brand in the world:** With total revenue of Amer Group exceeding 127.8 billion yuan, Anta is already the third largest sports brand group in the world. If the integration is successful, Puma will further consolidate its global position.

### Reasons for short selling (Bear Case)

1. **Puma's acquisition may be a "value trap": ** 12.2 billion yuan to acquire a brand with deteriorating performance at a 60% premium (net loss of 247 million euros in the first half of 2025). The 29% minority stake cannot control operations, but losses will drag down Anta's profits through the equity method.

2. **Management radius is too long:** 10+ brands, covering mass to luxury, spanning China to the world - this is an extreme challenge for any management team. Past success does not guarantee future success, especially when the nature of the challenge changes from "Chinese market operations" to "global brand management."

3. **Risk of growth engine switching:** Anta’s main brand (43% of revenue) grew by 3.7% and nearly stagnated, while FILA (36% of revenue) grew by 6.9% and slowed significantly. The group's growth is overly dependent on "other brands" which account for only 21%. If the high-end outdoor craze cools down, the growth pillar may be shaken.

4. **Hidden concerns about Arc'teryx's "FILA-ization":** The outbreak of Arc'teryx in China has a strong "fashion/social currency" attribute, similar to FILA's status in 2019-2021. FILA's growth rate later dropped from 40%+ to single digits. Will this cycle repeat itself for Archaeopteryx?

## Comparison with other targets

If you can only choose one Chinese sports brand, Anta is still the first choice. Its comprehensive strength - brand matrix, operational capabilities, financial quality - far exceeds that of its competitors. But "best company" does not equal "best investment" - the key is price.

If you buy Anta at the current 14 times PE, you will most likely get positive returns in the long term (3-5 years), but the annualized return may only be moderate (10-15%). If you wait until below 12 times to buy, the potential return will be significantly improved (annualized 15-25%), and the downside risk will be smaller.

ANTA Sports is an excellent company that deserves long-term attention, but it is not an investment opportunity that must be bought immediately.
