## Research background

China Feihe is the leader in the domestic infant milk formula market, with the largest market share for many years. The company faces structural headwinds as the birth population continues to decline. In 2025, revenue will be 18.11 billion yuan (-12.7% year-on-year) and net profit will be 1.94 billion yuan (-45.7% year-on-year), which is the worst performance in the past six years. The current PE is about 13-15 times, the dividend rate exceeds 6%, and it is in the "high dividend + strong repurchase" stage. Core question: Can the second curve absorb the decline of the main business? Is a low valuation a value trap or an opportunity?
