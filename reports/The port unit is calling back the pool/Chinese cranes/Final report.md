## 3. Core investment thesis

### Reasons to be bullish (Bull Case)

1. **Extremely strong financial safety cushion**: 7.5 billion in cash, zero interest-bearing liabilities, and positive free cash flow. This company will not "die"
2. **Beneficiaries of increased industry concentration**: Small and medium-sized brands are accelerating their exit, and leading players are expected to gain share
3. **6%+ dividend rate provides bottom support**: Even if the stock price does not rise, 6% annual cash return
4. **If the birth population stabilizes**: Feihe, as the number one brand, will be the biggest beneficiary
5. **Super high-end upgrade capability has been verified**: The success of the Zhuorui series proves that the product strength is still there

### Reasons to be bearish (Bear Case)

1. **Structural headwinds are irreversible**: The number of births dropped from 17.86 million to 9 million, with no rebound in sight
2. **Profit margin continues to deteriorate**: Net profit margin fell from 40% to 10.7%, and the trend has not stopped.
3. **Yili is surpassing**: the market share gap has narrowed from 5% to 1.2%, and may complete the overtake in 2027
4. **The second curve has not been passed yet**: The gross profit margin of adult milk powder is only 1%, which is far from profitability.
5. **Management conflict of interest**: Excessive dividends + high salary, no adjustment even when profits are cut in half
6. **Low PE may be a value trap**: If profits continue to decline, PE will become expensive again

## 5. Investment decision-making suggestions

### 5.1 Current judgment: **Wait and see, waiting for the turning point signal**

Reasons why it is not recommended to open a position at this time:
- The downward trend in profit margins has not yet bottomed out
- The second curve has yet to prove itself
- The threat of Yili's transcendence is real
- Valuation is not extremely cheap (limited margin of safety in neutral scenario)

### 5.2 Under what conditions can you consider buying?

**Trigger conditions (can be considered if 2-3 are met)**:

1. Quarterly revenue showed positive year-on-year growth (signal of stabilization)
2. Net profit margin rebounded for two consecutive quarters (cost optimization took effect)
3. Birth population data shows signs of stopping the decline
4. The stock price falls below HK$2.5 (providing a more adequate margin of safety)
5. The gross profit margin of the second curve is increased to 15%+ (business model verification)

### 5.3 If buying, position and strategy recommendations

- Position limit: 3-5% of the portfolio (industries with structural headwinds are not suitable for heavy positions)
- Strategy: Use dividend income as the main source of return and treat reversal as option income
- Holding period: 3-5 years (it takes time for industry clearing)
- Stop loss line: food safety incidents or market share decline for 4 consecutive quarters

## 8. Final conclusion

**China Feihe is an investment target that is "understandable but difficult to be excited about". **

It has clear advantages (brand, cash, dividends) and clear disadvantages (shrinking industry, increased competition, management motivations). The current price of HK$3.4 is neither extremely cheap nor obviously overvalued.

For value investors, Feihe’s correct strategy may be:

> Put it into the "watch pool", set clear trigger conditions, and take action when profit margins bottom out or stock prices fall to a level with a safer margin. While we wait, birth data and second curve progress are the most important leading indicators to watch.

**One sentence**: A good company is in a bad industry, and the price is reasonable but not cheap. Mainly wait and see, waiting for a better strike zone.
