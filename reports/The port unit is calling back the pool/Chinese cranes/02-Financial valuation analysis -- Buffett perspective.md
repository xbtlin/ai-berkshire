#China Feihe: Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, changes in profitability, capital allocation, intrinsic value and margin of safety

---

## 1. Financial Panorama

### 1.1 Revenue and Profit Trends

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Net profit rate |
|------|-------------|------|---------------|------|--------|
| 2020 | 185.9 | +35.5% | 74.4 | +89.0% | 40.0% |
| 2021 | 227.8 | +22.5% | 68.7 | -7.7% | 30.2% |
| 2022 | 213.1 | -6.4% | 49.5 | -28.0% | 23.2% |
| 2023 | 195.3 | -8.4% | 33.9 | -31.5% | 17.4% |
| 2024 | 207.5 | +6.2% | 36.5 | +7.7% | 17.6% |
| 2025 | 181.1 | -12.7% | 19.4 | -46.8% | 10.7% |

**Key Observations**:
- Revenue has dropped by 20% from the high point in 2021, and net profit has plummeted from 7.44 billion to 1.94 billion (a 74% drop)
- The "resumption of growth" in 2024 will only last for one year, and will fall sharply again in 2025
- Net profit margin fell from 40% to 10.7%, indicating that profits fell much faster than revenue - the problem of cost rigidity is serious

### 1.2 Profitability Analysis

**Gross profit margin**: 66.3% in 2024, and expected to be about 65% in 2025 - still very high, indicating that the product pricing ability is still there.

**Sales expense ratio**: 34.6% (approximately 7.2 billion yuan) in 2024 - this is Feihe's largest "profit black hole". During the industry downturn, Feihe did not dare to cut marketing investment (for fear of losing share), resulting in severe squeeze on profit margins.

**Change path of net interest rate**: 40% → 30% → 23% → 17% → 17% → 10.7% - the trend is clearly downward.

**ROE**: Neutral forecast is 15-20%, but considering that net profit will be cut in half in 2025, the actual ROE may have dropped below 10%.

### 1.3 Balance sheet quality

**Big highlight – plenty of cash**:

- Cash and equivalents at the end of 2025 will be approximately RMB 7.5 billion
- Almost zero interest-bearing debt
- The financial structure is very healthy and there is no debt repayment pressure

**Hidden concerns - biological assets**:
- The entire industrial chain means holding a large number of biological assets such as cows
- Against the background of falling milk prices and overcapacity, biological assets are facing impairment pressure
- In Q2 of 2025, there was an abnormal situation where the impairment of biological assets deviated from the trend of milk prices.

## 2. Cash flow analysis

### 2.1 Operating cash flow

Feihe has historically had good operating cash flow quality, with the cash content of net profit usually exceeding 100%. But here’s what to pay attention to in 2025:

- Is the 1.2 billion milk powder subsidy (trade-in, etc.) included in sales expenses but actually a cash outflow?
-Whether the dealer's purchase/return situation affects the real operating cash flow
- Whether accounts receivable turnover has deteriorated

### 2.2 Capital expenditure

The full industry chain model means ongoing maintenance capital expenditures (pasture maintenance, factory upgrades). During the period of industry contraction, the amortization pressure on these fixed costs increases instead of decreasing.

### 2.3 Free cash flow

Despite lower profits, Feihe still generated positive free cash flow, mainly due to:
- Cash recovery capabilities for high-margin products
- Peak capital expenditure period has passed
- But if the second curve continues to burn cash, the quality of free cash flow will decline

## 3. Capital Allocation Assessment

### 3.1 Dividend Policy

| Year | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Dividends (100 million yuan) | Dividend rate |
|------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| 2023 | 33.9 | 22.9 | 67.6% |
| 2024 | 36.5 | 27.2 | 74.5% |
| 2025 | 19.4 | 20.3 | 104.8% |

Dividends exceed net profit in 2025 - this sends two signals:
- **Positive Interpretation**: Management is confident in future cash flow and is willing to give back to shareholders
- **Negative Interpretation**: Excessive dividend distribution in a year when profits were cut in half may be to maintain the stock price/please the major shareholders (Leng Youbin holds 43% of the shares, and the biggest beneficiary of the dividend is himself)

### 3.2 Buyback

Since 2026, a total of approximately HK$1 billion (236 million shares) has been repurchased, with a repurchase price of approximately HK$3-4. The intensity of buybacks is not small, but the effect is limited in the context of continued decline in stock prices.

### 3.3 Capital Allocation Score

- Dividend: ★★★★☆ (generous but questionable motives)
- Repurchase: ★★★☆☆ (with action but limited effect)
- Investment discipline: ★★★☆☆ (The ROI of the second curve investment has not yet been verified)

## 4. Valuation analysis

### 4.1 Relative valuation

| Indicators | Feihe | Yili | Mengniu | International dairy company average |
|------|------|------|------|-------------|
| PE (static/2025) | 12-13x | 18-20x | 15-17x | 18-20x |
| PE (dynamic/2026E) | 15-16x | — | — | — |
| Dividend yield | 6%+ | 3-4% | 2-3% | 2-3% |
| PB | ~1.5x | ~3x | ~1.5x | 3-4x |

Feihe’s static PE looks cheap, but you need to pay attention to:
- If profits continue to decline, "low PE" may be a value trap
- Dynamic PE 15-16x (based on expected profit recovery in 2026) is not cheap
- The 6% dividend yield is attractive, but only if the dividend is sustainable

### 4.2 DCF valuation ideas

**Key Assumptions**:

Optimistic scenario:
- Revenue stabilizes from 2026 to 2028 (recovery of birth population + second curve contribution)
- Net interest rate returns to 15%
- Long-term growth rate of 2%
- Discount rate 10%
- Intrinsic value: approximately HK$40-50 billion (corresponding to stock price of HK$5-6)

Neutral scenario:
- Revenue will fall by 3-5% annually over the next five years
- Net profit margin maintained at 12-15%
- Long-term growth rate 0%
- Discount rate 10%
- Intrinsic value: approximately HK$25-30 billion (corresponding to stock price of HK$3-3.5)

Pessimistic scenario:
- Market share was surpassed by Yili, and revenue declined at an accelerated pace
- Net interest rate drops below 10%
- Intrinsic value: approximately HK$15-20 billion (corresponding to stock price of HK$2-2.5)

### 4.3 Safety margin judgment

The current stock price is about HK$3.4, corresponding to a market value of about HK$30 billion:
- Relatively optimistic scenario has 30-50% upside potential
-Basically reasonable pricing relative to the neutral scenario
- Relatively pessimistic scenario still has 20-30% downside risk

**Conclusion**: Current prices do not provide a Buffett-esque "extremely cheap" margin of safety. The apparent low PE is offset by the downward trend in profits.

---

## 5. Key financial risks

### 5.1 Profit margins continue to deteriorate

If revenue continues to decline and sales expenses remain rigid, the net profit margin may further drop from 10.7%. Feihe faces the dilemma of "losing share if you don't advertise, losing profits if you advertise".

### 5.2 Cash consumption

7.5 billion in cash seems abundant, but if there are excess dividends (2 billion) + buybacks (1 billion) + second curve investment every year, and profits are only about 2 billion, the cash reserves will be consumed year by year.

### 5.3 Asset impairment risk

Biological assets and factory production capacity throughout the industry chain face impairment when the industry shrinks. This is a "ticking time bomb" in the financial report, which may lead to large non-cash losses in a certain year.

## 6. What would Buffett think?

### What you will like:
- Branded consumer goods, high gross profit, urgent demand
- Good cash flow and very low debt
- High dividends and buybacks, strong shareholder return awareness
- Industry leader, concentration is still increasing

### Areas of hesitation:
- The market cake is shrinking - Buffett prefers "long slopes with thick snow", Feihe's snowballs are melting
- Profit rates continue to decline, with no turning point in sight
- Insufficient certainty in the second curve
- Management’s capital allocation motives are questionable (excessive dividends + high salaries)

### Buffett-style conclusion:

> Between "buying a great company at a reasonable price" and "buying a mediocre company at a cheap price", Feihe is currently in an awkward middle ground - a once excellent company is becoming mediocre, and the price is not extremely cheap. "
