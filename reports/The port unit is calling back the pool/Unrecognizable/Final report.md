## 1. Investment Thesis

### See Bull Case

NIO is experiencing the most important business turning point in its 11 years of existence:

1. **First quarterly profit in Q4 2025** - net profit of 283 million yuan, proving that the business model can be profitable after increasing scale.
2. **Three-brand matrix increases sales** - Ledo sold 107,000 vehicles in the first year, Firefly ranked first in market share, and NIO’s brand was stable. The target is 456,000-489,000 vehicles in 2026.
3. **Upgrading the battery swap network to a platform** - Open Alliance introduces Changan and Geely, and external vehicles are expected to contribute 30% of battery swap orders in 2026.
4. **Undervaluation** - PS is only 1.18x, far lower than Ideal (2.1x) and Xpeng (2.5x). If profits are confirmed, there is plenty of room for valuation repair.
5. **Strong binding of management interests**——Li Bin’s 248 million share incentive plan is linked to profitability and market value.

The target price under the Bull Case: If the full-year profit in 2026 is 2 billion to 4 billion yuan, a PE of 30 to 50 times will be given, corresponding to a market value of 60 billion to 200 billion Hong Kong dollars, and a stock price of 26 to 85 Hong Kong dollars. The target price is approximately HK$60 (+27%) under the neutral assumption.

### Bear Case

NIO’s core problems have never been fundamentally solved:

1. **Profit promises repeatedly failed** - from 2024 to 2025 and then to 2026, why is this time different?
2. **Q4 profit has seasonal factors** - 48,000 vehicles were delivered in a single month in December during the peak season, which increased the full quarter data, and there is a high probability of returning to losses in the Q1 off-season.
3. **Current ratio is lower than 1** - short-term debt repayment pressure exists, and the continuous capital expenditure of the power swap station will not stop.
4. **Supercharging technology threatens the foundation of battery replacement** - 800V supercharging can replenish energy for 400km in 15 minutes, and the time advantage of battery replacement is shrinking.
5. **The competitive landscape continues to deteriorate** - BYD’s 4.6 million vehicles are crushing it, and Huawei/Xiaomi are grabbing high-end shares.

Target price under Bear Case: If it fails to make a full-year profit in 2026, market confidence collapses, and PS falls back to 0.8 times, corresponding to a market value of approximately HK$70 billion and a stock price of approximately HK$30 (-37%).

## 2. Key decision variables

The final decision to invest in NIO depends on the evolution direction of the following three variables:

### Variable 1: Can the full-year profit in 2026 be realized?

This is the most important variable. Need to monitor:
- Q1 financial report (expected to be released in June 2026): Is the loss controllable?
- Q2/Q3 delivery volume: Can it reach the monthly average level of 40,000+?
- Gross profit margin trend: Can it stabilize at 17%+ and move towards 20%?

**Judgment threshold:** If the Q1 loss is controlled within 3 billion, and the Q2 delivery volume returns to 100,000+ levels, the probability of profit for the whole year is relatively high.

### Variable 2: Substantial progress of the Power Exchange Open Alliance?

The power swap alliance is still mainly based on "signing contracts", and the actual external vehicle battery swap data has never been disclosed by NIO. Need attention:
- When will Changan and Geely's battery-swap models be mass-produced and delivered?
- Can the proportion of external power exchange orders really reach 30%?
- Single site utilization and profit and loss data (Nio may disclose it for the first time in 2026)

**Judgment threshold:** If the number of external power swap orders exceeds an average of 10,000 times per day by the end of 2026, the logic of the power swap platform will be initially verified.

### Variable three: Can Letao and Firefly stand firm in their respective price bands?

The success or failure of the three-brand strategy determines NIO’s scale ceiling.
- Ledo's 2026 target of about 150,000-200,000 vehicles, can it be achieved under the attack of BYD and Tesla?
- Can Firefly turn its market share advantage into sustained sales growth?

**Judgment threshold:** The average monthly delivery of Ledao is 15,000+, and the average monthly delivery of Firefly is 8,000+, indicating that the multi-brand strategy is steadily advancing.

## 3. Investment advice

### Current qualitative judgment: wait and see, waiting for Q1-Q2 verification

Weilai is currently in a critical transition period of "from loss to profit". The first profit in Q4 is a positive sign, but a single quarter is not enough to confirm the trend. It is not advisable to place heavy positions before the following conditions are met:

1. Q1 losses are controllable (<3 billion)
2. Q2 delivery volume recovered to 100,000+
3. Gross profit margin maintained above 17%

### Position suggestions

| Investor type | Recommended positions | Reasons |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Conservative | 0% | Profitability has not been continuously verified, risk-return asymmetry |
| Balanced type | 1-2% | Observe the position and wait for Q1 data to be verified before deciding to add or subtract |
| Aggressive | 3-5% | Betting profit turning point confirmation + valuation repair |

### Key Catalyst (Positive)

- 2026 Q1 financial report exceeds expectations (loss narrows significantly or becomes profitable again)
- The Power Exchange Alliance has a major new partner joining
- Monthly delivery volume continues to hit new highs (average monthly 40,000+)
- Breakthrough progress in overseas markets

### Key Risk Triggers (Negative)

- Q1 loss exceeded 5 billion
- Month-on-month delivery volume continues to decline
- Price war forced price cuts to damage gross profit margins
- CYVN holding reduction or governance conflict
- A major breakthrough in overcharging technology weakens the value of battery swapping

## 4. Comparison with the classic value investment framework

### Does it meet the standards of "good company"?

| Standard | Judgment | Explanation |
|------|------|------|
| Sustained profitability | No | Profitable only for one quarter, need to be continuously verified |
| High ROE | No | Negative ROE |
| Strong cash flow | Part | Free cash flow has just turned positive and is not yet stable |
| Wide moat | Part | There are barriers to power exchange but the economic model has not been verified |
| Excellent management | Part | Strong ability but execution discipline needs to be proven |
| Reasonable valuation | Yes | PS 1.18 times, low |

**Conclusion:** According to strict value investment standards (sustained profitability + high ROE + strong cash flow), NIO is currently unqualified. It is more suitable to be evaluated as a "dilemma reversal" target - that is, betting that it can change from "unqualified" to "qualified".

### Where is the margin of safety?

NIO's current margin of safety does not come from "low valuation" - because if it cannot make profits, any valuation will not be low enough. The margin of safety comes from:
1. Replacement cost of power replacement network (5.5-7.4 billion+)
2. Brand + intangible asset value of millions of car owners
3. Technical assets (self-developed chips, OS, platforms)
4. Management re-incentives and interest binding

These are "soft margins of safety" - not as reliable as bank deposit-style "hard margins of safety", but provide a certain floor in extreme circumstances.

---

## 5. Summary

NIO is a company at a critical turning point. Its business model design is far-sighted, its power exchange network has unique barriers, its management is making up for its shortcomings in execution, and its valuation is relatively low.

But it also has obvious uncertainties: profitability sustainability has not been verified, the competitive landscape is severe, overcharging technology poses a long-term threat to battery replacement, and management has historical credit issues.

**Summary in one sentence: Weilai is a "high odds, medium risk" dilemma reversal target. If the full-year profit is confirmed in 2026, the current price will be a good buying point in retrospect; if the profit is postponed again, it will continue to bottom out and consume patience. **

For investment decisions, the most sensible approach is to establish a small observation position, closely track the Q1-Q2 financial report data, and add positions after the profit trend is continuously verified. Don’t bet heavily before a turning point—let facts speak for themselves, not predictions.
