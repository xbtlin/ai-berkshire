## Company Profile

NIO is a high-end smart electric vehicle brand in China and currently operates three major brand matrices:

- **NIO**: Positioned as a luxury smart electric vehicle with an average price of more than 300,000 yuan
- **ONVO**: Focusing on the mainstream market of 200,000-300,000 yuan, 107,808 vehicles will be delivered in the first full delivery year of 2025
- **Firefly**: Focusing on the high-end small car market above RMB 100,000, ranking first in market share for 7 consecutive months

The core differentiated competitiveness lies in the power exchange system (BaaS model) and user community ecology.

## Core Contradiction

NIO is one of the most controversial companies in China's new energy vehicle industry. Bulls believe that its battery swap ecosystem, user community and multi-brand strategy have created unique barriers; bears point out that its cumulative losses exceed 100 billion, its business model is asset-heavy, and its competition landscape is deteriorating. Whether the first quarterly profit in Q4 2025 is an inflection point or a flash in the pan is the core question of this study.
