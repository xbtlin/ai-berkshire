#Nio (9866.HK) business model analysis

> Analysis dimensions: business essence, user value, business logic, long-term competitiveness
> Core question: Is NIO a good business?

---

## 1. The nature of business: What exactly is NIO doing?

NIO is a car company on the surface, but if you only understand it in terms of "making cars", you will miss its core logic. NIO’s business model can be broken down into three layers:

**The first level: sell cars. ** This is the most basic source of income. The full-year revenue in 2025 will be 87.488 billion yuan, most of which will come from vehicle sales. The three brands cover the price range of more than 100,000 yuan to more than 500,000 yuan - Weilai brand is for high-end cars (300,000+), Ledo is for mainstream cars (200,000-300,000 yuan), and Firefly is for high-end small cars (100,000+).

**Second level: selling services. ** The BaaS (battery as a service) model allows users to "buy a car without buying a battery." Taking NIO ES6 as an example, choosing the BaaS model directly reduces the purchase price of 70,000 to 80,000 yuan, and pays a few hundred yuan in rent every month. The proportion of BaaS users has exceeded 60%. This means that NIO has turned one-time revenue into long-term recurring revenue - which is rare in the automotive industry.

**The third level: selling ecology. ** The battery swap network is open to brands such as Changan and Geely, and external vehicles are expected to contribute 30% of battery swap orders in 2026. NIO House and user community have built strong brand stickiness.

From a business perspective, Weilai is trying to upgrade from "selling products" to "selling services + selling ecology". This direction is right - Apple's transformation from selling hardware to selling services has proven the value of this path. But the question is: Does NIO have Apple’s pricing power and scale effect? **

## 2. User value: Why do users buy NIO?

The core reasons why NIO users choose NIO, ranked by priority:

1. **Battery replacement experience. ** Replace the battery in 3 minutes to solve charging anxiety. As of the beginning of 2026, NIO has built more than 3,680 battery swap stations, covering more than 700 cities, and completed the layout of more than 80 highly popular routes. This is infrastructure that no competitor will be able to replicate anytime soon.

2. **User community. ** NIO House, NIO Day, etc. have built a sense of identity in the luxury car circle. The repurchase rate and recommendation rate of NIO car owners are leading in the industry, which shows that the user value is real.

3. **Product power. ** The NT3.0 platform is equipped with the self-developed Shenji NX9031 chip (dual-chip 2000TOPS computing power), Tianshu SkyOS global operating system, and 800V high-voltage architecture. The technical parameters are in the first echelon.

**Positive judgment:** NIO has created real and differentiated user value, especially in the two dimensions of battery exchange and community. This is not the result of marketing, but the result of real money invested in infrastructure.

**Reverse question:** Is user value equal to business value? The construction cost of each power swap station is about 1.5-2 million yuan, and 3,680 stations means at least 5.5-7.4 billion yuan of fixed asset investment. The target is 4,600 seats by the end of the year, and additional investment is needed. If the battery utilization rate is not high enough, these assets are sunk costs.

## 3. Multi-brand strategy: expanded coverage or dispersed resources?

NIO’s three-brand strategy is a key move:

| Brand | Price Band | Delivery 2025 | Positioning |
|------|--------|-----------|------|
| NIO | 300,000+ | ~150,000 units (estimated) | High-end flagship |
| Letao | 200,000-300,000 | 107,808 vehicles | Mainstream volume |
| Firefly | 100,000+ | Rapidly growing | High-end cars |

**Positive logic:** Multiple brands are the only way for NIO to break through the sales ceiling. Only high-end, the ceiling is too low - China's annual sales of more than 300,000 pure electric vehicles are only a few hundred thousand. Ledo exceeded 100,000 units in its first year on the market, proving the feasibility of the brand's further expansion. Firefly continues to rank first in the market share of high-end small cars and has found an entry point for differentiation. The three brands share the battery exchange network, supply chain and technology platform, which can theoretically dilute fixed costs.

**Reverse risks:** Multi-brand operations require extremely high management capabilities. Volkswagen Group and General Motors have both been in trouble due to too many brands. Weilai has integrated the supply chains of the three brands into one, and the channels are also being integrated - this shows that the early spin-off did cause efficiency losses. More importantly, the competition intensity faced by Ledo and Firefly in their respective price ranges is much higher than that of the NIO brand - 200,000-300,000 yuan is the main battlefield of BYD and Tesla, and 100,000 yuan is the most involved market for Chinese cars.

## 4. The deep logic of the power swap model: moat or baggage?

Battery swapping is NIO’s most controversial strategic choice, and it needs to be examined from two dimensions.

**Power exchange as a moat:**
- Network effect: The more battery swap stations there are, the better the user experience will be, attracting more users, and more users will support more battery swap stations. So far, 3,680 stations have formed a preliminary network density.
- The Open Power Swap Alliance has introduced Changan, Geely and other car companies, and it is expected that external vehicles will contribute 30% of the unit volume in 2026. If the alliance grows bigger, NIO's power exchange network will become an infrastructure platform - similar to Telaidian and Star Charging in the charging field.
- The BaaS model allows more than 60% of users to choose battery leasing, creating continuous recurring revenue.

**Power exchange as a burden:**
- Supercharging technology is advancing rapidly. Under the 800V architecture, 15 minutes of charging can replenish energy for 400 kilometers, and the time advantage of battery replacement is shrinking.
- Battery swap station utilization is a key financial indicator, but Weilai has never disclosed detailed data. If the average number of daily services per site is less than a certain threshold, you will lose money.
- The standardization of battery replacement means that NIO's model design is subject to more restrictions - the chassis must reserve a standard battery pack position.
- Over 1,000 new stations will be built in 2026, and continued capital expenditure pressure cannot be ignored.

**Key judgment:** The depth of the moat for power exchange depends on the progress of the open alliance. If there were only NIO’s own battery swapping stations for vehicles, the utilization rate would never be high enough, and battery swapping would be a burden. But if the battery-swapping models of Changan, Geely and other brands really increase in volume, the battery-swapping network may enter a positive cycle. This is a bet where "success is a platform, failure is a burden".

## 5. Core contradictions of business models

There is a fundamental contradiction in NIO's business model: **It attempts to be an automobile company, an energy infrastructure company, and a user service company at the same time. **Each identity requires a huge investment, and together it is an astronomical amount of money consumption.

This explains why Weilai has accumulated losses of more than 110 billion yuan in the 11 years since its establishment - it is not doing one business, but three businesses at the same time.

Comparison:
- **Tesla** focuses on car manufacturing + autonomous driving, and the supercharging network is an auxiliary, not the core business model.
- **BYD** focuses on car manufacturing + battery vertical integration, achieving ultimate scale effect.
- **NIO** Car manufacturing + battery exchange network + user community, every line is very important.

If this "full stack" model succeeds, the barriers will be extremely high - because competitors will need to copy three layers of capabilities at the same time. But if it fails, it will be "everything is accessible and everything is loose" caused by over-dispersion of resources.

## 7. Core Conclusions

NIO's business model design is far-sighted. If the three-layer architecture of "car + service + ecology" is fully realized, it will build a very deep moat. However, this model has extremely high requirements for execution and capital, and Weilai's performance in the past 11 years shows that: **The vision is beautiful, but the road from vision to profitability is long and expensive. **

If measured by "doing the right things", NIO has done a good job in creating user value. If measured by "doing things right", Weilai still has a lot of room for improvement in business efficiency - it will lay off more than 10,000 people in 2025, cut off more than 20 VPs, and implement a CBU operating system, which shows that the management is also aware of this problem.

The most critical uncertainty: whether the Power Exchange Open Alliance can become bigger. This variable determines whether Weilai's business model is a "platform enterprise" or a "cash-burning idealism."
