# I. Management assessment: Li Binqi

# Start-up experience

Li Bin is a continuous entrepreneur in the internet and automobile industry in China:
- Launch of the limousine (listed at the New York Post), one of China ' s largest car Internet platforms, in 2000
- Started a car in 2014.
- Also involved in investment/foundation of various projects such as the Mobbi, the capital.

** Positive evaluation:** Li Bin was rarely entrepreneurial. In 2019, he nearly closed down (in cash only hundreds of millions), selling a home, mortgaged shares financing, and eventually survived 7 billion dollars in compost government investment. In 2023, he faced a financial crisis, introducing $2.2 billion in Abu Dhabi. Two death attempts showed that he was extremely well-funded and crisis-management.

** Negative question:** The same experience can be interpreted as: a recurring financial crisis, which indicates that management is not well equipped to control cash flows and that strategic expansion is too radical. A healthy company should not be "dead" repeatedly.

# Strategic decision-making capacity

The most critical strategic decisions of Li Bin are** the continuation of the power switching model** and** the multi-brand strategy**.

** Switching of power:**
- When you decide to switch power in 2017, industry agrees that charging will win. Li Bin will do it against consensus.
- Tens of billions of dollars were invested in power-exchange networks, which were never shaken by long-term losses.
- 2023, Open Power Exchange Alliance, strategic upgrade to "Infrastructure Platform".

This decision has not been fully validated so far – the power switch has not yet been profitable alone. It has indeed created a divergence in the way competitors cannot reproduce in the short term. This "long-termism" is a good or a bad one, depending on the final outcome.

** Multi-brand decision-making:**
- The launch of two sub-brands, " Lecter and Fireflies " , 2023-2024.
- Short-term increases in management complexity and financial consumption.
- The delivery of 107,000 vehicles in 2025, the first year of the Happy Book, attests to the correct direction of decision-making.

# Enforcement and discipline

** Positive:**
- In 2025, he was significantly cut off by more than 10,000 people (-23%), cut off 20+VP and introduced CBU independent accounting. This shows that he has the courage to make painful decisions.
- R & D costs have been reduced from 13 billion to 10.6 billion, controlling the "R & D boom."
- Integration of the three brands supply chain, integration of channels and improvement of organizational efficiency.

** Reverse:**
- The profit schedule has been postponed several times. 2022 says 2024, 2025, 2025, 2026. This repeated "destination" undermines management credit.
- Early organizational inflation (ceiling 456,000 people) was itself a managerial failure — without subsequent painful layoffs if the benefits were retained from the outset.
- Do multi-line operations (electric + three brands + overseas + self-research chips + self-research OS) go beyond the organizational capability boundaries?

# The interest is tied

This is a strong positive sign:
1. A statement of the Board ' s recognition of Li Bin ' s leadership

348 million shares at current stock prices of approximately HK$11.7 billion, with a very strong incentive

But note that Li Bin currently holds only 7.9% of the shares (36.7% of the vote), which are continuously diluted in multiple rounds of finance. Equity incentives can be understood as compensation for past dilution.

# III. List of core risks

Risk one: profit-making is not as good as expected

The profit targets for the year 2026 require:
- 45.6-480,000 vehicles sold (+40-50%)
- Māori rate is 20%
- The cost rate continues to decline.

Q1 is usually a mild season (spring season effect), and if Q1 loses more, the pressure for profit will be concentrated throughout the year on Q2-Q4. Do the historical profit commitments fail again and again?

** Trigger: ** Q1 loss of over 3 billion / Q2 delivery below expectations / price war caused the Maori rate to fall
** Impact:** likely 20-30% return on share prices and further depletion of market trust

# Risk two: price wars increase # (high probability medium impact)

By default, the price of Biadey’s cost advantages allowed it to fall down. By 2026, Biadi would have been the first to wage a new price war between 200,000 and 300,000.

** Trigger:** Biadei launched 200,000 class flagship pure electricity / Tesla China significantly reduced prices
** Impact:** Leisure Māori rate is under pressure and overall profit targets are blocked

# Risk three: power switching mode is being turned over by technology #

800V supercharge + solid cell is a potential subversive in the switch mode. If the charge time is reduced to 5-10 minutes, the battery life span is extended to the same age as the vehicle, the core value proposition (short time, battery upgrade) of the switch will be significantly weakened.

** Trigger conditions:** 5-minute full battery/supercharged technology production / solid-state battery cost reduced to lithium level
**Time window:** within 3-5 years
** Impact:** significant impairment of core differentiated asset values

# Risk four: Cash flows break up # (low probability impacts)

Although the probability of a significant decrease in the 2019 and 2023 years (Q4 has earned 42.2 billion in cash), the liquidity ratio is below 1, the ongoing capital expenditure on power switching stations and the likely volume of sales below expectations may still lead to cash flow strains.

** Trigger:** Significant losses for two consecutive quarters + financial inability to finance due to deteriorating capital market environment
** Extent of impact:** Corporate survival risk in extreme cases

# Risk five: Geopolitics and seaboard obstructions

The country is scheduled to enter 40 countries in 2026. But policy risks such as China-US decoupling, EU tariffs on China’s electric vehicles, etc., may limit the speed and profitability of overseas expansion.

** Trigger:** EU/US additional tariff/technology export restrictions
** Impact:** Limited contribution from overseas without affecting the basic domestic base

# Risk six: decentralized management # (medium probability medium impact)

Li Bin manages three brands, power switching networks, self-research chips, overseas expansion, and organizational change simultaneously. Any management oversight lapses can cause problems.

# Four, security margin analysis

# Value the security margin

- The current market value is about 103 billion yuan.
- 87.5 billion in 2025, PS 1.18 times.
- If we collect 120 billion + a year in 2026, the PS will drop to 0.86 times.

From the PS perspective, it is much cheaper than ideal (2.1x) and pelican (2.5x). But a low PS may reflect "market distrust of its profitability" rather than "undervalued".

# Assets secure margin

- Cash reserve: $42.2 billion
- Power exchange network 3,680+ (replaced cost 5.5-74 billion+)
- Technology assets like self-research chips, OS are not quantifiable but valuable
- Brand assets: millions of cars with a home plate plus high loyalty community

If liquidated, is the value of its tangible assets + technical assets + brand assets supporting the current market value? This is a security margin in extreme cases. The answer is uncertain — automobile companies usually have a high discount in liquidation.

# Business security margin

The three brands matrix provides some business diversification. Even if one brand does not perform well, others can be replaced.

# V, negative argument: The core argument of the emptier

1. ** "As long as it is a cake."** What reason does the profit schedule have to be carried forward from 2024 to 2025 and 2026?
2.** "The power switch is a false demand."** After the overcharge, no one would pay tens of thousands of dollars for the three-minute switch.
3. ** "Multiple brands are dispersing resources."** Three brands are not strategic, and they're not even standing.
4. ** "Li Bin is not a good cost manager."** He is a visionary, but not an optator. What is needed is a CEO that can control costs to the extreme COO, not a CEO that always thinks about the next big ideaa.
5. ** "Competition patterns do not permit."** The scale effect of Biadi would crush all medium and small players, with 300,000 to 500,000 vehicles in total capacity and no cost competitiveness in front of 4.6 million.

# Six, positive arguments: a multi-person core argument

1. ** "Q4 has proved it."** The first quarterly profit was not a matter of luck, but a combination of scale effects plus efficiency gains plus product strength.
2. ** "The power exchange is the final asset."** Battery can be upgraded as the power change is faster than the power change. When open alliances are large, a positive cycle will arise.
3. ** "The valuation is too cheap."** PS is only 1.18 times more than the value of the restoration space if the year 2026 is recognized as a full year of profit.
4. ** "Li Bin ' s ability to turn the tide of adversity has been repeatedly proven."** This is a better face than any other time, in 2019 and 2023, when he was nearly dead.
5. ** "The Three Brands are the only way."** There is no scale effect on just being high, but multi-branding is difficult but must be done. Success in music has proved that.

# Eight, core conclusion

Li Bin is a visionary, resilient, and profit-bound founder of CEO. His strengths lie in strategic layout and financing, and the short-term in cost control and discipline.

On risk: the current risk has been downgraded from "life risk" to "profit risk". This is a major advance. But "profit risk" is still important -- market patience could be exhausted if 2026 is not fully profitable.

The investment is essentially two things:
1. Can Li Bin be transformed from "visionary" to "visionary + oprator"
2. Whether the Open Alliance for Power Change can move from concept to real infrastructure platform

The answers to these two questions will be gradually revealed in 2026-2027.
