# NIO (9866.HK) financial valuation analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial health, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: After hundreds of billions of losses, has Weilai’s financial turning point really arrived?

---

## 1. Financial Panorama: How much money did you burn in 2011?

From its establishment in 2014 to the end of 2025, Weilai's cumulative net loss exceeded 128 billion yuan (approximately 113.1 billion at the end of 2024 + 14.9 billion in 2025). Cumulative financing exceeded 115 billion yuan. Looking at these two figures together, it means that Weilai has basically burned all the money it raised.

**Annual loss trajectory:**

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Net loss (100 million yuan) | Delivery volume (10,000 vehicles) |
|------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| 2022 | 492.7 | 145.6 | 12.25 |
| 2023 | 556.2 | 211.5 | 16.00 |
| 2024 | 657.3 | 224.2 | 22.18 |
| 2025 | 874.9 | 149.4 | 32.60 |

It is worth noting that there will be an important turning point in 2025 - while revenue will increase significantly, losses will narrow significantly by 33.3%. This is the first time that Weilai has shown a trend of "increasing revenue and reducing losses".

**2025 Q4 Milestones:**
- Revenue of 34.65 billion yuan, +75.9% year-on-year, +59% month-on-month
- Net profit of 283 million yuan - the first quarterly profit since its establishment
- Vehicle gross profit margin is 18.1%, comprehensive gross profit margin is 17.5%
- 124,800 vehicles delivered

Is this single-quarter profit a true turning point signal, or is it a one-time manifestation of a seasonal peak? This is the core issue.

## 2. Gross profit margin analysis: What is the quality of earnings?

Gross profit margin is a key indicator for judging the sustainability of Weilai's profitability.

| Period | Vehicle gross profit margin | Comprehensive gross profit margin |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 2023 | 11.9% | 5.5% |
| 2024 | 12.3% | 9.9% |
| Q1 2025 | 10.0% | -- |
| Q2 2025 | 12.9% | -- |
| Q3 2025 | 14.7% | 13.9% |
| Q4 2025 | 18.1% | 17.5% |
| 2026 Q1 Guidance | ~18% | -- |
| 2026 target | 20%+ | 20% |

The trend of continued improvement in gross profit margin is clear. From 11.9% in 2023 to 18.1% in Q4 of 2025, it has increased by 6.2 percentage points in three years. Drivers include:

1. **Release of scale effect. ** Q4 delivered 124,800 vehicles in a single quarter, significantly increasing production capacity utilization and diluting fixed costs.
2. **Supply chain integration. ** The supply chain of the three brands has been merged, and the purchasing and pricing power has been enhanced.
3. **Reduce costs and increase efficiency. ** More than 10,000 people were laid off, more than 20 people were cut off at the VP level, and CBU independent accounting was implemented.
4. **Product structure optimization. ** Ledo's mass-market models contributed incrementally, and the gross profit of Firefly's high-end small cars was acceptable.

**Positive judgment:** If sales increase by 40-50% to 450,000-490,000 vehicles in 2026 as planned, the scale effect will be further released, and the 20% gross profit margin target is likely to be achieved.

**Contrary question:** Q4 is usually the peak season for car sales, with 48,000 vehicles delivered in a single month in December being the annual peak. The Q1 off-season delivery volume fell by about 35% month-on-month (guidance is 80,000-83,000 vehicles), the scale effect has weakened, and it is doubtful whether the gross profit margin can be maintained at 18%. In addition, the price war is still ongoing, and Weilai’s pricing space is not generous.

## 3. Fee structure: Where is the money spent?

NIO’s operating losses come not only from insufficient gross profit, but also from high expenses.

**2025 Fee Overview:**

| Expense items | Amount (100 million yuan) | Proportion of revenue | Year-on-year change |
|----------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Research and development expenses | 106 | 12.1% | -18.7% |
| Selling and administrative expenses | ~130 (estimate) | ~14.9% | Declining |
| Total Operating Expenses | ~236 | ~27.0% | Improvements |

R&D expenses will drop from 13 billion in 2024 to 10.6 billion, mainly from layoffs (reducing R&D personnel by 40%) and efficiency improvements. However, the annual R&D investment of 10.6 billion is still very high - for reference, Li Auto’s R&D expenses in 2025 will be approximately 10 billion, but Li Auto has already made profits throughout the year.

Key question: Is NIO’s high R&D investment worth it?

NIO's full-stack self-research scope is very wide: self-developed chip (Shenji NX9031), self-developed operating system (Tianshu SkyOS), self-developed electric drive, and self-developed battery pack management. This full-stack self-research strategy will significantly increase R&D expenses in the short term, but if the technology is reused on a platform, it can reduce marginal costs in the long term. The NT3.0 platform has covered the three brands of Weilai, Letao and Firefly, and the platform effect has begun to show.

## 4. Cash flow and balance sheet: How long can it last?

**Cash Reserves:**
As of the end of Q3 2025, NIO’s cash reserves were 42.2 billion yuan. After achieving quarterly profit for the first time in Q4, the cash position should improve.

**Assets and liabilities:**
- Current liabilities 78.58 billion yuan > Current assets 76.63 billion yuan
- The current ratio is lower than 1, indicating short-term debt repayment pressure.
- Free cash flow will turn positive for the first time in Q3 2025

**Financing dependence:**
NIO has historically relied heavily on external financing. In 2023, it received a total investment of approximately US$2.2 billion from CYVN (Abu Dhabi), and in March 2025, it received additional financing through additional issuance. If full-year positive operating cash flow cannot be achieved in 2026, financing will still need to continue.

**Positive Signs:** Free cash flow is one of the most important financial signals. If sales increase by 40%+ and gross profit margin increases to 20% in 2026, Weilai may achieve positive operating cash flow for the whole year in 2026, fundamentally changing the "burning money" narrative.

**Reverse risk:** A current ratio lower than 1 means that Weilai's short-term liquidity is not ample. More than 1,000 new power swap stations will be built in 2026, and the demand for capital expenditure will not decrease. If Q1-Q3 fails to turn a profit or approaches breakeven, cash consumption will continue.

## 5. Bicycle Economic Model

To understand Weilai’s profit prospects, we need to dismantle the bicycle economic model.

**Q4 2025 (earnings quarter) estimate:**
- Delivery volume: 124,800 vehicles
- Vehicle revenue: approximately 31 billion yuan (estimated)
- Average bicycle price: about 248,000 yuan (including Ledao and Firefly lowering the average price)
- Vehicle gross profit: approximately 5.6 billion yuan (18.1% gross profit margin)
- Bicycle gross profit: about 45,000 yuan
- Single-quarter R&D expenses: approximately 2.5 billion yuan
-Single quarter sales and management expenses: approximately 3 billion yuan (estimated)
-Single quarter net profit: 283 million yuan

This means that under the current expense structure, Weilai needs to deliver more than 120,000 vehicles in a single quarter to achieve breakeven. The guidance for Q1 in 2026 is 80,000-83,000 units, which is lower than this threshold - so Q1 may return to a loss-making state.

To achieve full-year profits, Q2-Q4 will require a significant increase in volume. The full-year target set by management is 456,000-489,000 vehicles, which means that in the next three quarters, an average of 120,000-130,000 vehicles will be delivered per quarter. The pressure is not small but not impossible.

From a PS perspective, NIO has the lowest valuation among several new forces. This reflects the market's doubts about the sustainability of its earnings.

**If full-year profitability is achieved in 2026:**
Assuming revenue in 2026 of 120 billion yuan (+37%) and net profit of 2-4 billion yuan, the corresponding PE is 25-50 times. Among new energy vehicle growth stocks, this valuation is not expensive. But this is all based on the premise that "it can really be profitable for the whole year in 2026."

**If not profitable in 2026:**
NIO will face further erosion of market trust. In the past few years, Li Bin has set profit timetables many times, but failed to fulfill them each time (the early target was to make profits in 2024, which was later postponed to 2025, and then to 2026). If it fails again in 2026, the stock price may come under greater pressure.

## 7. Analyst target price

Institutions such as SPDB International have given the target price of HK$59.9, which has about 33% upside potential from the current price of about HK$47. However, analysts’ target price is based on the full-year profit assumption of 2026. If the assumption does not hold true, the target price will be lowered.

## 9. Core Conclusions

NIO’s financial situation is undergoing the most important turning point since its establishment. The first quarterly profit in Q4 of 2025, gross profit margin continues to improve, and free cash flow becomes positive - these are real and positive signals.

But from a prudent perspective, three key uncertainties deserve vigilance:

1. **Profitability sustainability. ** Q4 is the peak season, and there is a high probability that Q1 will return to losses. Full-year profits require continued volume growth in the next three quarters.
2. **Cash flow safety margin. ** The current ratio is lower than 1, the battery replacement station continues capital expenditures, and the safety margin is not thick enough.
3. **Management credit. ** The profit schedule has been postponed many times. Whether the "2026 full-year profit" can be realized is the ultimate test of management's credit.

At the current valuation of 1.18 times PS, if Weilai can really achieve full-year profits in 2026, it does have a certain margin of safety. But if profits are delayed again, this "margin of safety" will be a trap. Investing in NIO is essentially betting on the certainty of a profit turning point.
