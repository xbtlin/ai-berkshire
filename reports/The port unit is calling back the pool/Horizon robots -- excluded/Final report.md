## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **High track certainty**: Intelligent driving is a definite trend from optional to standard, and the penetration rate is accelerating.
2. **Leading market position**: China’s own brand ADAS market share is 47.7%, and it cooperates with 42 car companies
3. **Volume and Price Rise**: The proportion of AD chips increased to 45%, the average unit price increased by 75%, and the revenue structure was optimized.
4. **Excellent management**: Yu Kai is one of the best founders in China’s AI chip field, with strategic focus and restraint
5. **The profit turning point is approaching**: Brokerages predict that profits will be achieved in 2027, and losses are continuing to narrow.

### Bear Case

1. **Continuous huge losses**: R&D expenses exceed revenue, with a loss of 2.8 billion yuan in 2025
2. **Valuation is not cheap**: PS is about 12 times, which already implies high growth expectations, and the safety margin is insufficient.
3. **Strong Enemies Surrounded**: Both Nvidia and Huawei have the ability to erode Horizon’s market share
4. **Technology iteration risk**: The failure of one generation of products in the chip industry may lead to a large-scale loss of customers
5. **Geopolitical Risk**: Relying on TSMC for manufacturing, the supply chain may be blocked in extreme cases

## Buying Conditions and Catalysts

### Buying conditions (need to be met at the same time)
1. Valuation falls below 8-10 times PS (providing a safety margin)
2. Revenue growth maintained at 40%+ for 2 consecutive quarters
3. Losses narrowed as expected, and R&D expense rates began to decline.
4. No major customer churn signals

### Potential Catalyst
- Achieve operating breakeven in a quarter
- The new generation of journey chips has gained major recognition
- Breakthrough of major overseas customers
- Industry penetration data exceeds expectations

### Sell/avoid signal
- Market share declined for two consecutive quarters
- Core major customers (such as BYD) switch suppliers
- The release of next-generation chips is delayed or the performance is not as good as expected
- Yu Kai resigned or the core team was lost

---

## Final conclusion

**Current judgment: Observe, do not open a position yet**

Horizon is a great company, on the right track, and run by great people. But from the perspective of value investing:
- Profitability not yet proven → Business model not fully verified
- Valuation is adequate → insufficient margin of safety
- The outcome of the competition is undecided → there is not enough certainty

**Suitable strategy**: Continue to track and wait for valuation correction or profit realization before making decisions. If PS falls back below 8 times, or profits are confirmed soon in 2027, you can consider opening a position.
