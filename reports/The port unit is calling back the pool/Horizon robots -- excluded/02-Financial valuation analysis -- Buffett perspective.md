# Horizon Robotics (9660.HK) Financial Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: financial quality, profitability, capital allocation, valuation rationality
> Core question: If you buy at the current price, can you get a reasonable return in the future?

---

## 1. Financial panoramic scan

### Core data for 2025

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Revenue | 2.38 billion yuan | 3.76 billion yuan | +57.7% |
| Gross profit | About 1.7 billion yuan | 2.43 billion yuan | +about 43% |
| Gross profit margin | About 71% | 64.5% | -6.5pct |
| R&D expenses | 3.15 billion yuan | 5.15 billion yuan | +63.3% |
| Adjusted net loss | About 1.8 billion yuan | 2.81 billion yuan | Losses widened |
| Cash reserves | — | Over 20 billion yuan | — |

### Interpretation of financial characteristics

1. **High Growth**: Revenue growth rate is 57.7%, mainly coming from the increased volume of mid-to-high-end smart driving solutions. AD chip shipments accounted for 45%, contributing more than 80% of revenue.
2. **High gross profit**: The gross profit margin of 64.5% is much higher than that of general chip companies (30-50%), and is close to the high-end levels of design chip companies such as Qualcomm and MediaTek. This shows that the product has high added value and the competitive landscape is acceptable.
3. **Huge investment in R&D**: R&D expenses are 1.37 times of revenue. This is understandable in the early stages of a chip company, but means it won't be profitable in the short term.
4. **Abundant cash**: Cash reserves of over 20 billion yuan, which can last for more than 3-4 years at the current cash burn rate. IPO financing provides the company with sufficient "war capital."

## 2. Profitability and path analysis

### Dismantling of loss structure

Horizon's losses do not come from operating losses of "sell one and lose one", but from **R&D-driven losses**:
- Gross profit 2.43 billion yuan (positive)
- R&D expenses 5.15 billion yuan (main expenditure item)
- Operating loss ≈ Gross profit - R&D - Other expenses

This means that as long as revenue continues to grow at a high rate and R&D investment growth slows down, the company can naturally move toward profitability.

### Profit forecast path

Brokers unanimously expected:
- 2025: Net loss of approximately 1.8 billion yuan
- 2026: Net loss of approximately 1 billion yuan (loss narrowed by 45%)
- 2027: Net profit of approximately 1.1 billion yuan (first profit)

**Key Assumptions**: Revenue maintains a growth rate of 40%+, and R&D expense rates gradually decrease.

### Benchmarking analysis

| Benchmark company | Time from loss to profit | Current status |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| NVIDIA (early stage) | About 6 years | Has become the dominant player in AI chips |
| AMD | Turnaround after multiple losses | PE 30+ |
| Mobileye | Profitable about 3 years after listing | Valuation falling |
| Horizon | Expected in 2027 | Losses narrowing |

## 3. Balance sheet quality

### Advantages:
- **Net cash exceeds 20 billion yuan**: no short-term funding pressure
- **Asset-light model**: Chip design companies do not need to invest heavy assets in wafer factories (manufactured by TSMC, etc.)
- **Accounts receivable are controllable**: Automotive OEM customers have good credit

### Risk:
- **Equity Dilution**: Listed financing and option incentives continue to dilute shareholders’ equity
- **R&D Capitalization Issue**: Need to pay attention to whether R&D expenses have a tendency to be aggressively capitalized
- **Customer Concentration**: Pay attention to the proportion of receivables from large customers such as Volkswagen Group

## 4. Cash flow analysis

As a loss-making company with high investment in R&D, negative operating cash flow is expected. Key observations:
- **Free cash flow**: Continued to be negative, completely dependent on financing cash flow support
- **Capital Expenditure**: Relatively low (Fabless model), mainly R&D personnel salary
- **Cash consumption rate**: Net cash consumption of approximately 2-3 billion yuan per year

**Buffett-style concern**: A company that needs to continue to invest a lot of capital but cannot generate free cash flow is essentially "consuming value" rather than "creating value." Only when economies of scale emerge and revenue significantly exceeds expenses can the business model be verified.

## 5. Valuation analysis

### Current valuation level

- **PS (TTM)**: approximately 12 times (based on 2025 revenue of 3.76 billion yuan)
- **Market capitalization**: approximately HK$45 billion (estimate)
- **PE**: meaningless (losing money)
- **PB**: about 2-3 times (mainly IPO cash)

### Valuation rationality assessment

**Optimistic scenario (profit of 1.1 billion yuan in 2027):**
- If 50 times PE is given → market value is 55 billion yuan
- If 30 times PE is given → market value is 33 billion yuan
- The current market value of HK$45 billion already implies higher growth expectations

**Neutral scenario (profit of 800 million yuan in 2027):**
- 40 times PE → market value 32 billion yuan
- There is a certain downside compared to the current situation

**Pessimistic scenario (profits delayed until after 2028):**
- Loss of share due to technological iteration or intensified competition
- Market value may fall by 30-50%

### PS Valuation Comparison

| Company | PS (TTM) | Profit status | Growth rate |
|------|----------|---------|------|
| Horizon | ~12x | Loss | 58% |
| Mobileye | ~8x | Small profit | 15% |
| NVIDIA | ~25x | Huge profits | 100%+ |
| Cambrian | ~40x | Loss | 40% |

Compared with domestic AI chip companies (such as Cambrian), Horizon's valuation is relatively reasonable; but compared with similar global companies (such as Mobileye), considering the uncertainty of profit timing, PS 12 times is not cheap.

## 6. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Management’s capital allocation capability ★★★☆☆

- **R&D investment**: The direction is right (focusing on high-end smart driving), but the efficiency has yet to be verified
- **Mergers and Acquisitions**: No large-scale mergers and acquisitions yet, maintaining strategic focus
- **Dividend/Repurchase**: No dividend during the loss period, understandable
- **Financing Decision**: The timing of the IPO was well chosen (October 2024), and sufficient funds were locked in

### Shareholder Return Outlook

Short term (1-2 years): No dividends, stock price depends on growth expectations and market sentiment
Medium term (3-5 years): If profits are made in 2027, shareholder returns may start to be considered
Long-term (5 years+): If we become the industry leader, we are expected to generate substantial free cash flow.

## 7. Core contradictions under Buffett’s framework

**Positive factors:**
- High gross margin indicates pricing power and competitive barriers
- The asset-light model means that once profitable, ROE can be high
- The industry ceiling is extremely high, and the penetration rate of smart driving is still in its early stages
- Abundant cash, no existential crisis

**Negatives:**
- Unable to generate free cash flow, currently just "consuming capital"
- Valuation already implies high growth expectations, with limited safety margin
- The technology of the chip industry is iterating rapidly, and today’s lead does not guarantee tomorrow.
- Buffett himself will most likely not invest in this type of company - he cannot see the certainty of profits in five years.

**Overall Financial Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Financially, Horizon is a "high-growth, high-loss" technology company. It has bright spots in terms of financial quality (high gross profit, abundant cash), but its core shortcoming is its inability to generate positive cash flow. PS's 12x valuation isn't excessive, but it doesn't provide a sufficient margin of safety either. Investing in such companies is essentially "betting on the arrival of a profit turning point" and requires extremely high confidence in industry trends and the company's competitiveness.
