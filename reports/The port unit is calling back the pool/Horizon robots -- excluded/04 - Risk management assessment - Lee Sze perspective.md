### Core Management Team

Yu Kai has a profound technical and industry background and is one of the top founders in the AI field in China. But please note:

**Advantages:**

## 2. Institutional risk analysis

### Political and geopolitical risks ★★★★ (high attention)

1. **China-U.S. technology decoupling**: Horizon uses TSMC as its foundry. If geopolitical conflicts escalate and the foundry is restricted, this is a fatal risk. However, the process requirements of Zhengcheng chip (16nm/7nm) are relatively low, and domestic OEM substitution is feasible.

2. **Policy Risk**: The progress of autonomous driving policies affects the pace of the industry. The Chinese government generally supports the development of intelligent driving, and the policy trend is favorable.

3. **Data Security Compliance**: Autonomous driving involves maps and driving data, and there are data regulatory risks. However, as a chip/platform supplier (rather than an operator), Horizon’s direct risks are relatively small.

### Industry cycle risk ★★★☆ (medium)

1. **Cyclicity of the automobile industry**: Automobile sales fluctuate cyclically. During economic downturns, the decline in new car sales directly affects chip shipments.
2. **Technology Intergenerational Risk**: Each new generation of chips is an "All-in" bet. If a certain generation of products is not competitive enough, it may lose a large number of customers within 2-3 years.
3. **Customer concentration**: If large customers such as Volkswagen and BYD switch suppliers, revenue will be significantly impacted in the short term.

### Financial risk ★★☆☆ (lower)

- Net cash exceeds 20 billion yuan, no short-term financial pressure
- No interest-bearing liabilities and no liquidity risk
- The main risk is that long-term losses consume cash, but at the current pace 3-4 years will not be a problem

### Valuation Risk ★★★☆ (Medium)

- The current PS of 12 times already implies higher expectations
- Valuations could be significantly revised downwards if growth slows or earnings are delayed
-Tech stocks typically fall more when markets panic

## 3. Li Lu type risk framework

### "What's the worst case scenario?"

**Scenario 1: Increased competition leads to decline in share**
- Nvidia cuts prices + Huawei opens up → share drops from 47% to less than 30%
- Result: Revenue growth slowed down and profit timing was delayed.
- Impact: The stock price may fall by 40-50%, but the company will not die
- **Probability: 20%**

**Scenario 2: Loss of core customers**
- BYD/Volkswagen, etc. turn to self-development or switch suppliers
- Result: Revenue is under short-term pressure, and new customers need to be found to fill the gap.
- Impact: Share price may drop 30-40%
- **Probability: 15%**

**Scenario 3: Next-generation chip development fails**
- The performance of Journey 7 or subsequent products is not as good as expected
- Result: Loss of technological leadership within 2-3 years
- Impact: The stock price may drop by 60%+, and the company faces survival challenges
- **Probability: 10%**

**Scenario 4: Geographical conflict affects supply chain**
- TSMC’s foundry is limited and domestic alternatives are immature
- Result: Product delivery severely hampered
- Impact: The company may face a 2-3 year window period
- **Probability: 5-10%**

### "What are the chances of surviving?"

With 20 billion+ cash reserves and a light-asset model, Horizon has almost no bankruptcy risk in the next 3-5 years. Even if business suffers setbacks, there are enough resources to readjust.

**5-year survival probability: 95%+**
**Probability of remaining among the top three in the industry for 5 years: 70%**
**Probability of achieving profitability in 5 years: 75%**

## 5. Perspective of civilization evolution (Li Lu-style long-term thinking)

### The position of intelligent driving in human civilization

Autonomous driving technology represents an important step in humanity's transformation from "manual control" to "machine intelligence". If steam engines liberate physical labor and computers liberate mental labor, then autonomous driving will liberate "attention labor" - this is a civilization-level change.

**Long-term view (10-20 years)**:
- Intelligent driving will become standard, just like ABS and airbags
- The value of computing platforms will continue to grow
- But who is the final winner is not 100% sure now

### Horizon’s role in this trend

If intelligent driving is compared to the "discovery of the New World", then Horizon is like an "early colonizer" - starting early and occupying a good position, but the final ownership of the New World is still in competition.
