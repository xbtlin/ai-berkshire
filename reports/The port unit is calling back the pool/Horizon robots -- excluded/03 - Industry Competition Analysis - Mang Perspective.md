# Horizon Robotics (9660.HK) industry competition analysis

> Analysis dimensions: industry structure, competition landscape, multiple thinking models, reverse thinking
> Core question: What is the final outcome of competition in this industry? What place can Horizon occupy in the endgame?

## 1. Nature of the Industry: Intelligent Driving Computing Platform

### Use multiple thinking models to understand this industry

**Biological Analogy**: The intelligent driving chip industry is in the "Cambrian Explosion" stage - there are many species (players), but they will eventually undergo large-scale elimination, leaving only a few of the most adaptable species.

**Physics Analogy**: The chip industry has a strong "scale gravity" - the greater the shipment volume, the lower the unit cost, the more complete the ecology, attracting more customers, forming a positive feedback loop. The ultimate winner will act like a black hole and absorb most of the industry's profits.

**Essence of Economics**: This is an industry with **high fixed costs and low marginal costs**. The one-time investment in chip design is huge (several hundred million yuan per tape-out), but once mass-produced, the marginal cost of each additional chip sold is extremely low. This means that industries naturally tend to be concentrated.

### Industry scale and growth

- China's ADAS market penetration rate will increase rapidly in 2025
- L2+/L3 intelligent driving is penetrating from high-end models to mid-range models
- It is expected that by 2030, China’s intelligent driving chip market will reach tens of billions of yuan
- Sources of increase: (1) Increase in the standard configuration rate of new cars; (2) Increase in unit price of solutions (from L2 to L3); (3) Chinese car companies going overseas

## 2. Competitive landscape: three strong forces standing together

### Comparison of major players

| Players | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses | Market Share |
|------|------|------|------|---------|
| **NVIDIA** | High-end full stack | Computing power ceiling, CUDA ecosystem, global brand | High price, high power consumption, weak local service | High-end market dominance |

| **Horizon** | Open platform | Cost-effectiveness, local services, open ecology, mass production verification | Absolutely inferior computing power to NVIDIA, insufficient brand globalization | Independent brand market 47.7% |
| **Mobileye** | Closed plan | Deep accumulation in the era of rules | Slow transition to AI driving, Chinese market decline | Rapid decline |
| **Black Sesame** | Chaser | Have certain technical reserves | Scale is much smaller than Horizon | <5% |

### Manganese analysis of industry structure

**Porter's Five Forces:**

1. **Competition intensity of existing competitors** (high): Nvidia, Huawei, and Horizon compete fiercely, each with its own niche.
2. **Threat from new entrants** (medium): The threshold for chip design is high, but self-developed chips by major car companies (such as BYD and NIO) pose potential threats
3. **Threat of substitutes** (low): Pure software solutions cannot replace dedicated hardware, and general-purpose chips are difficult to match in efficiency.
4. **Bargaining power of suppliers** (middle): Foundries such as TSMC have a certain say in the production capacity
5. **Customer Bargaining Power** (medium-high): Large car companies such as Volkswagen and BYD have strong bargaining power, but switching costs restrict their willingness to switch.

## 3. Horizon’s competitive position

### Ecological niche analysis

Horizon occupies a **precise and defensible ecological niche**:
- Not the highest end (occupied by NVIDIA)
- Not the most full stack (occupied by Huawei)
- Rather, it is the preferred smart driving platform for Chinese independent brand car companies**

The value of this location is:
1. China’s own brands are rapidly replacing joint venture brands, and their market share continues to increase.
2. Chinese car companies’ overseas expansion will bring incremental growth (Horizon can follow suit overseas)
3. The volume of mid-to-high-end solutions is the largest, and the profit pool is also expanding.

### "Is the moat getting wider or narrower?"

**Evidence of widening:**
- Market share increases from 9.3% in 2023 to 15.4% in the first half of 2024
- Cooperating car companies from few to many (42), 290 designated models
- The proportion of AD chips has increased, indicating that high-end customers are increasing
- The HSD solution has been market-proven, with an 83% top-end option rate

**Possible risk of narrowing:**
- Nvidia cuts prices (Thor chips target the mid-range market)
- Huawei begins to develop pure Tier2 solutions (changing competition model)
- Large car companies develop self-developed chips (BYD, NIO, etc.)
- If next-generation architecture lags behind, it may be quickly replaced

## 4. Reverse thinking: Under what circumstances will Horizon fail?

### Scenario 1: NVIDIA slashes prices

If NVIDIA decides to trade price for volume to enter the mid-range market, it may erode Horizon's share with its brand and ecological advantages. **Probability Assessment: Moderate**. Nvidia is currently focusing on high-margin markets, but does not rule out penetration through low-end product lines.

### Scenario 2: Automobile companies successfully develop self-developed chips

If major customers such as BYD and Ideal successfully develop their own chips, Horizon's largest customers may become competitors. **Probability Assessment: Moderately Low**. The threshold for chip design is high, and most car companies may take the dual-track route of "self-research + external procurement" rather than complete replacement.

### Scenario 3: Subversion of technical route

If a new computing architecture (such as optical computing, brain-like computing) emerges, the traditional AI chip architecture will become obsolete. **Probability Assessment: Low**. Disruptive substitution is unlikely to occur in the short term (5 years).

### Scenario 4: Intelligent driving commercialization is hindered

If autonomous driving regulations progress slowly, consumer acceptance is less than expected, and safety accidents occur frequently. **Probability Assessment: Low**. L2+ assisted driving is a definite trend and does not rely on the complete realization of L4/L5.

## 5. Industry Final Deduction

### The most likely outcome (probability 40%): Three-legged confrontation

NVIDIA occupies the global high-end market, Huawei occupies China's high-end closed-loop market, and Horizon becomes China's largest open platform. Similar to the Qualcomm/Apple/MediaTek pattern of mobile phone chips.

### Optimistic ending (25% probability): Horizon becomes the global leader

Relying on the scale advantages and overseas opportunities in the Chinese market, Horizon will eventually occupy a dominant position in the global mid-range smart driving market.

### Pessimistic ending (20% probability): squeezed

NVIDIA's dimensionality reduction attack + Huawei's open ecosystem, Horizon has been squeezed at both the upper and lower ends, and it has become a market segment player.

### Extremely pessimistic (probability 15%): falling behind in technology and being eliminated

The next-generation chip architecture failed to keep up, and core customers were lost, repeating Mobileye's mistakes in the Chinese market.

## 6. Munger’s “Circle of Competence” Test

### Core questions that need to be answered when investing in this company:

1. **Can you judge the quality of chip technology routes? ** → Most investors do not have this ability
2. **Can you predict the speed of smart driving penetration? ** → The trend is certain but the rhythm is difficult to judge
3. **Can you assess the technology gap between Horizon vs Nvidia? ** → Requires deep industry knowledge
4. **Are you confident about the shape of this industry in five years? ** → To be honest, the variables are still huge

### Possible comments from Munger

"This is an interesting company in an exciting industry, but a good track doesn't mean every player will win. The history of the chip industry tells us that the distance between leaders and laggards can reverse within one product generation cycle. I need more certainty before I can make a bet."
