### One sentence summary

China National Biopharmaceuticals is a leading pharmaceutical company that is undergoing the critical transformation of "from generic to innovative". It has the right direction and rich pipeline, but the transformation results have not yet been fully realized, and the safety margin of 24 times PE is limited.

---

## Core investment thesis

### Look at the bull case

1. **The inflection point of innovation and transformation has reached**: Innovative products account for close to 50% of revenue, with an annual growth rate of 26%+, the impact of centralized procurement is close to being cleared, and profits are accelerating.
2. **Intensive pipeline catalysts**: Nearly 20 new products are to be approved from 2025 to 2027, with many blockbuster varieties with peak sales exceeding 2 billion.
3. **Room for repair of valuation discount**: Compared with Hengrui Pharmaceuticals (55x PE), the company’s 24x PE has a significant discount. If the pipeline is realized, it may trigger a valuation re-rating.
4. **Family interests are deeply bound**: 55% of family holdings ensure that the interests of management and small shareholders are consistent and will not do anything that harms long-term value.

### Bear Case

1. **ROE continues to be low (8%)**: Capital utilization is not efficient, and the goodwill accumulated from historical mergers and acquisitions drags down the return on net assets.
2. **Uncertainty about pipeline fulfillment**: The success rate of innovative drug research and development itself is not high, and commercialization also faces fierce competition.
3. **The "involution" of the industry intensifies**: There are many popular target drugs, and even if they are approved, they may face price wars
4. **Young management has not been verified**: Although CEOs born after 1995 have academic qualifications, they lack experience in going through cycles.

## Core variable tracking list

Key variables to keep track of when investing in this company:

1. **Innovative product revenue growth**: Whether it can maintain 25%+ is the core of valuation support
2. **Approval progress of blockbuster varieties**: TQB3616 (CDK2/4/6), TQB2102 (HER2 ADC), etc.
3. **ROE improvement trajectory**: Is the path from 8% to 12%+ visible?
4. **Centralized procurement and clearing progress**: Whether the generic drug sector can really stop the bleeding
5. **International breakthrough**: progress in overseas licensing or overseas sales
6. **Management Execution**: The performance of young teams at key decision-making nodes

## Valuation and buying conditions

### Current valuation judgment

24 times PE corresponds to earnings in 2025, which is in the "reasonable but not cheap" range. PEG is about 0.8, indicating that the growth rate can basically match the valuation, but it does not provide a sufficient margin of safety.

### Ideal buying conditions

- PE pulled back to 18-20 times (stock price fell 15-20%)
- The approval of a blockbuster pipeline may lead to an upward earnings revision, causing the forward PE to drop below 18 times
- Or there is a clear turning point in ROE (rising to 10%+), proving that the transformation has been transmitted from "income" to "return"

### Position suggestions

- Current price: observation position (0-2%), waiting for a better safety margin
- PE 18-20x: Initial position can be established (2-3%)
- Catalyst + callback for blockbuster varieties approved: positions can be increased to 5%

## Comparative analysis

### Differences from Hengrui Medicine

Hengrui is the "benchmark" for innovative drugs in China, with innovative drugs accounting for 58%, ROE 18%, and PE 55x. China Biopharmaceuticals is comparable in scale, but there is still a clear gap in the purity of innovation and capital efficiency. The current valuation discount (24x vs 55x) partly reflects this quality difference and partly reflects the market's pricing of "HK stock discount" and "transition uncertainty".

### Differences from CSPC Pharmaceutical Group

CSPC is more conservative and stable, with a PE of only 10x and an ROE of 15%. If you pursue certainty and a margin of safety in valuation, CSPC may be a better choice; if you are optimistic about the acceleration of transformation and pipeline explosion, China Biopharmaceuticals will be more flexible.

## Risk warning

1. The R&D failure rate in the pharmaceutical industry is high, and pipeline expectations may fail.
2. Medical insurance negotiations and centralized purchasing policies continue to put pressure on drug prices
3. Family business governance structure may limit company development
4. Liquidity discount in Hong Kong stock market
5. The systemic risk of an overall correction in the valuation of the innovative drug industry

---

## Research limitations

- This research is based on public data and seller research reports, and does not involve management interviews
- Pipeline value assessment requires more professional knowledge of the pharmaceutical industry
- Each variety under development has not been evaluated individually
- It is recommended to supplement professional analysis of core pipelines before making investment decisions.
