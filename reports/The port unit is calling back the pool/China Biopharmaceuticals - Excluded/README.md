## Research Summary

Sinopharm is China's leading pharmaceutical company and is at a critical stage of comprehensive transformation from generic drugs to innovative drugs. The company is helmed by the third/fourth generation of the Xie family. In 2025, innovative products will account for nearly 50% of revenue, and the impact of centralized procurement on the generic drug business is almost cleared.

## Core investment logic

**Reasons to be long:**
- Innovation and transformation have achieved remarkable results, with revenue from innovative products increasing by 26%+ annually.
- Nearly 20 innovative products are to be approved from 2025 to 2027, including many blockbuster varieties
- The impact of centralized procurement is close to being cleared, and the profit turning point has arrived
- Valuation is at a significant discount to Hengrui Pharmaceuticals

**Reasons for shorting:**
- ROE is still low (8%), capital efficiency needs to be improved
- 24 times PE corresponding to current profitability is not cheap
- Competition for innovative drugs is fierce, and there is uncertainty about pipeline fulfillment
- Family business governance structure, young management with limited experience
