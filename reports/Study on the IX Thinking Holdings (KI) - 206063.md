The need for Chinese bulk investors to “studies, want to be explained, want tools, and be tempted to pledge their earnings” persists. The nine-way business is precisely that: turning complex market information into courses, live broadcasts, tools, indicators, and accompanying clients and an AI assistant.

But its competitors are not single companies, but a set of different types of alternatives:

1. Integrated financial information platforms: Eastern wealth, homogenousness, snowball and other sources of flows, data, communities and tools.
Internet and traditional voucher dealers: wealthy, tiger, Huatai, and the popularizing of trading accounts, license plates and customer asset deposition.
3. Free content ecology: short video, public number, station B, large amounts of content on live platforms dilute the willingness to pay.
4. Universal AI Assistant: Basic interpretation services are rapidly commercialized as large model financial question and answer capabilities increase.
5. Regulation per se: The more strong marketing shifts, the more pressure regulation is likely to slow growth models.

The comparative advantage of the Qingjin is that of a system of high-pricing unit products, more investment and securities practitioners, private sector operating experience, cash flow subscriptions and continuous research and development inputs. Weakness is that the cost of client switching is low, product effectiveness is difficult to prove, and access and sales compliance have a significant impact on profit elasticity.

My judgment is that the Nine-Spint-Senses has a staged brand, product mix and operational capability, but there is not enough evidence of long-term moats. It can be a good business, but it may not be a natural business of "no looking for a decade."

# Critical risks

First, the regulatory risk. The three-month suspension of new clients in 2026 has shown that regulation is not abstract.

Second, the cost of access. Sales and marketing costs were calculated as a proportion of income in the second half of 2025, which is about 56.5 per cent, well above the 31.5 per cent rate in the first half of the year. If the conversion rate declines after the correction, the profit rate may be lower for the long term than it was in the whole year of 2025.

Third, the customer value proves risk. The cost of renewal of high-cost single-incurred services depends ultimately on whether the product is considered useful by the customer.

Fourth, AI is a commercialized risk. AI can improve efficiency, but common models and financial platforms can accelerate catch-up. If nine intellectual investments do not turn AI into a lower service cost, higher retention and higher unit cost, it is difficult to sustain high valuations by concept alone.

Fifth, the fluctuations in the returns on financial assets. The profits from fair value financial assets in 2025 increased profits, but such gains were cyclical and market fluctuations and should not be considered as stable operating profits.

Sixth, governance risks are concentrated. The founder is also the chairman and CEO, with a higher shareholding among the concerted actors. Governance may be more efficient, but small and medium-sized shareholders need to track whether the distribution, buy-back, associated transactions, acquisitions and equity incentives actually add value per share.

# Track the list

In the next six to twelve months, the following indicators will be highlighted:

3. Whether the sales and marketing costs/income ratio will be able to recover from the high levels in the second half of 2025.
4. Whether the number of user fees, the Gross Billings, refunds, renewal rates are disclosed or indirectly improved.
5. Whether products such as AI stock machines and nine shares contribute to higher unit or lower service costs.
6. Whether the regulatory reform has ended and whether there are additional penalties, complaints or negative media.
7. Whether the company continues to buy back and write off at low cost or uses inventory shares for incentive or refinancing.
8. Whether the return on investment in financial assets in 2026 was a reverse drag on profits.

The most critical of these are the first three: contractual liabilities, sales cost rates, and the recovery of new clients after supervision. As long as the three are not repaired, the nine-way investment should not be valued at high value.
