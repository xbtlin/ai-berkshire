## Conclusion: What to think?

### Ant Group

**Positioning: mature cash cow + global options + AI uncertainty. **

The current valuation is about US$88 billion, and the probability-weighted value in five years will be about US$143 billion (1.03 trillion yuan), with an annualized return of about 6.5%. Not too cheap, not too expensive either. The biggest upside catalyst is the success of Ant International's IPO (which may boost the overall valuation by 15-35%); the biggest downside risk is the continued decline in payment share + the shelving of the financial holding license.

If you are an employee option holder of Ant Group: The options are valuable but don’t expect to get rich suddenly. An annualized rate of 6-8% is a reasonable expectation.

### Ant Mathematics

**Positioning: Small but beautiful financial technology ToB + RWA option. **

The current valuation is about 40 billion, and the weighted value after 5 years will be about 72 billion, with an annualized rate of about 10%. The overall growth rate is faster than that of Ant Group, but the certainty is lower. RWA and AI are two variables that may open up the ceiling, but the scale of the track is relatively small (BaaS~1.9 billion/private computing~1 billion), and dependence on related-party transactions is a flaw in IPOs.

If you are an employee option holder of Ant Digital: the realization cycle is much longer than that of Ant International (after 2028), but if the RWA volume increases, the valuation upside will be greater.

### The relationship between the two

Ant Digital is the carrier of Ant Group’s technological capabilities. In the short term (1-2 years), it still needs blood transfusion and brand endorsement from the parent company. In the medium term (3-5 years), as the proportion of external revenue increases and profits are realized, it is expected to have independent viability. But "independence" and "listing" are two different things - an IPO requires reducing related-party transactions to an acceptable level, which will take at least 2-3 years.
