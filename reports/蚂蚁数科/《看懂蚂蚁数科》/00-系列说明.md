> 4 main articles that break Ant Group Digital Technology down layer by layer and reveal its true structure.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Ant Group Digital Technology is not a company that has been widely researched - it is one of the companies in China's tech scene that is **frequently mentioned but rarely truly understood**.

- The bulls say it is the core externalization vehicle of Ant Group's technology, a dual leader in blockchain and privacy computing, and one of the few players in the world that has AI, blockchain, privacy computing, and regulatory licenses all at once
- The bears say it is the leader of a niche sector, a related-party-transaction black box, a fintech subsidiary spin-off with no successful IPO precedent, and AI dependence on the group-wide BaiLing large model

**Both sides are talking about the same company, but their answers to "what kind of species is Ant Group Digital Technology?" are completely different.**

This series aims to **first explain what it is** - not to hand you a conclusion about whether it is worth following.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - only the most reliable sources are used, with no fake precision
4. **Mark all estimates as "inferred" or "estimated"** - unverified data is never disguised as fact
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer

---

## How This Series Differs From "Understanding PDD" and "Understanding Tencent"

This is our first study of an **unlisted company**.

If you are used to the Tencent series style of data precision down to the decimal point, please lower your expectations. Ant Group Digital Technology does not even publicly disclose revenue, and its core financial data has not been independently audited, so the confidence level is only medium-low (about 55%). This series is more like **"identifying a silhouette in the fog"** than "examining details under a magnifying glass."
