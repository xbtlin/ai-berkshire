# Advertising wheels: five links

Meta's advertising wheels have five key links, each of which is driven by AI:

# Part one: User size - 398 billion months of attention

By the beginning of 2025, the monthly users of the Meta family applications (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenge) had reached **3.38 billion **, the daily life **3.58 billion ** (average for December 2025) (source: Meta 2025). This covers about half the world ' s population.

Key features of this user pool:
- ** Multiple scene coverage**: socialization (Facebook), pictures/screech (Instagram), newsletter (WhatsApp/Messenge)
- **Globalization distribution**: Unlike China, where Meta uses a wide variety of markets around the world.
- **Sunday/Mentime ratio about 90%**: indicates that users are not registered as invalids but are using them every day

Inverse: User growth is slowing. Q1,2026 days of living on the same day increased by only 4%, close to the ceiling of the global Internet population. The user size is limited in the incremental space of the flying wheel.

Response: The growth in the number of users is a fact at best, but it is important to note that **The duration of stay and liquidity efficiency of each user is still increasing**. Growth in the total number of users can slow, and flying wheels can continue to turn as long as the value of one user increases.

# Part two: Content Recommendations - AA The Core Fields of Algorithm

Meta's AI referral system determines what users see on Facebook and Instagram. This is not a support function -- ** this is the most important product of Meta**.

Since 2023, Meta has significantly upgraded the recommended algorithm:
- **Applied Recommendations**: AI can optimize what it sees on Facebook based on user behaviour on Instagram
- ** Engines found**: More and more content comes from creators who are not of interest to users (like TikTok's Discovery Mode)
- ** Stay time increase**: Management has confirmed on several occasions at the FMT that the increased content relevance of the AI recommendation directly increases the user ' s stay time

Better recommendation = longer stay = more available slots. This is the second ring of the flying wheel.

# # Part three: Advertising efficiency -- the power of Advantage+

Advantage+ is the Meta automated advertising placement system. Its core value is:** advertisers no longer need to manually set their audience, creativity, timing -- AI fully automated and more effective**.

Key data:
- Advantage+ automated processing over **60 billion United States dollars** annual advertising expenditure (source: Meta Q1,2026 telephone conference)
- The advertising unit price increases on a year-on-year basis **12% ** - the advertiser is willing to pay higher prices for higher investment efficiency

Advantage+ means more than just increasing the effectiveness of advertising, but more importantly,** reducing the threshold for advertising. Only large brands had a team of professionals to do the exact pitch, and now SMEs use Advantage+ to do the same.

Inverse: Advantage+ may be efficient in the short term. When all advertisers use AI to optimize their placement, competition pushes up advertising costs, and SMEs may have lower output than they could have, leading to the withdrawal of some advertisers.

Response: This risk does exist, but Meta’s advertising auction system is itself a price discovery mechanism – if the price of an ad post is pushed too high, the input-output-lower advertiser will naturally withdraw until the price is new. Historically Google has undergone a similar evolution, and it has not collapsed.

# # Part four: Reels -- from "The Diluent" to "The incremental engine"

This is one of the most important transformations of the last two years.

In 2022, Reels (Small Video) was seen as a burden on the market for Meta: users spent more time watching short videos, but the advertising rate for short videos was much lower than the flow of information — Meta in "Hunging on its own."

The 2026 Reels are completely different:
- **Reels annualized advertising revenue has reached about $50 billion** (source: Gotrade analysis)
- ** Realignment efficiency close to information flow advertising**
- ** became the core driver of Instagram user growth and stay **

The success of Reels illustrates one thing:** Meta has the ability to later rise up on new formats invented by others (a short video created by TikTok) and commercialize**. This "learning-catch-up" is a competitive advantage in itself.

It's a landmark event. Google ruled the digital advertising market for 20 years, and Meta for the first time went beyond it, which means that the advertising industry's focus is shifting from "search intent matching" to "AI recommended matching"**.

The logic behind it:
- The core of Google is search ads - users actively search, ad ads are precisely matching intent. But AI's assistant (ChhatGPT, Google's own AI Overview) is eroding the flow of traditional searches.
- The core of Meta is the recommended ad - passive consumer content, AI predicts interest and pushes advertising.** The stronger AI, the more effective this model is**

The counter-view: eMarketer’s projections are based on the extrapolation of current trends, and actual implementation may not be as expected. Google is also investing heavily in AI and will not be left to die.

Response: Google is really fighting back, but it is facing a structural dilemma -- AI is also enhancing its product and eroding its core business model (search ads). Meta has no problem -- AI will only sell Meta's ads more expensive.

# WhatsApp: 2 billion users' unchanged cash mines

WhatsApp is Meta's biggest hidden card in his hand.

Basics:
- ** Over 2 billion moon users worldwide**
- Emerging markets such as India, Brazil, Indonesia are de facto communication infrastructure
- It's been a long time since it's been realized.

Realisation action 2025-2026:
1. ** Click on whatsApp ad**: Post on Facebook/Instagram, and then the user jumps directly to whatsApp to talk to the business. This has become the preferred format for SMEs to receive.
2. ** Commercial API New pricing**: January 2026: shift from "to dialogue" to "to bar" ** to clearer commercial path
3. ** Channel-paying subscription**: creators can set up monthly fees **0.99-9.99**

The potential of whatsApp is that it's not just a chat tool, it's a **business portal. When you talk to a businessman on whatsApp, you pay a bill, it becomes a micro-trusted commercial ecology, which is almost blank on the planet.

Inverse: The commercialization of micro-credit in China has been successfully based on payment infrastructure (wet payment), and WhatsApp has no similar payment infrastructure in most markets. Without payment closures, the commercialization of WhatsApp may remain forever in the "chatting tool + simple advertising" phase.

The commercialization of WhatsApp is a legitimate concern. It is far from credible, and payment infrastructure varies widely across countries. But even if it is not complete, it is only "click on whatsApp" advertising and commercial API, WhatsApp can contribute billions of dollars in incremental income each year.
