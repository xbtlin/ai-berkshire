# Why does Meta have a discount on valuation?

Forward PE 18.4 times vs Inter-operative 29 - Meta's valuation discounts are close to **37%**. A company with 33% income growth, net interest rates above 30%, ROIC 35% must have a reason for trading in this valuation.

The market discount for Meta comes from three main concerns:

# Worry one: AI, capital spending could get out of control

Capital expenditure in 2026 was 54-63 per cent of income in the amount of $1250-1450 billion. The market is concerned that this figure will continue to climb, reducing free cash flows and profit margins over the long term.

While management has committed to operating profits in 2026 that will exceed 2025, it has warned that the cost increase is "manifestly faster" than the revenue increase.

** The rationale for this concern is 7/10**. Capital spending is disturbing, but Meta's advertising cash flow is strong and, even with higher capital spending, free cash flows are expected to exceed **100 billion** in 2026.

# Worrying about two: the gambling of Zuckerberg

The 83.6 billion-dollar front-car history is evident. If AI input does not eventually deliver the commensurate scale of the return, Meta is repeating the mistakes.

** The rationality of this concern: 5/10**. There is a difference between the wager of the metacosm and the AI's wager - AI has already produced a clear return on advertising (Q1 income + 33%) and the metacosm has never produced a decent income. The analogy is not entirely valid.

# Worrying about three: whether the acceleration is sustainable

Q1,2026 Revenues are rising at an alarming rate of 33%, but it contains a $8.3 billion one-time tax benefit boost to profits, and a Reels liquidity catch-up of the information stream’s phased dividend. If the acceleration returns to below 20% in the next few quarters, the current valuation’s appeal will also be compromised.

** The rationality of this concern: 4/10**. Even if the income growth fell to 20%, it would still be healthy for a trillion-dollar-market company. And whatsApp is just beginning to commercialize, providing incremental space.

# Perspective two: The alignment of growth with valuation

A simple frame:** If a company increases its income at an X per cent rate, a reasonable Forward PE is about between X and 2X** (PEG ratio 1-2).

Meta 's income grew at 33 per cent (Q1 2026) and forward PE 18.4 times.

- **PEG ratio = 18.4 / 33 = 0.56**

PEG below 1 is generally considered to be "growth is not fully priced". PEG 0.56 means **Meta is increasing at almost twice the rate implied by the valuation**.

This means using a more conservative 20% acceleration expected: PEG = 18.4 / 20 = 0.92, still below 1.

Inverse: The PEG ratio is an overly simplified framework that does not take into account the impact of capital expenditure on free cash flows. If used as EV/FCF (31.8 times), Meta is not cheap.

Response: The EV/FCF 31.8 times is not low, but this includes the effect of a significant increase in capital spending in 2026. If capital spending slows down in 2027-2028 (management suggests that it may be nearing its peak in 2026), free cash flows will improve significantly, and EV/FCF will decline.

# Situation analysis

Not giving precise target prices - the exact target price is "false accuracy". But scenarios can be used to understand what valuation means under different assumptions.

Scenario I: AI cashing in on the return of input (optimism)

Assumptions:
- 2026-2028 income CAGR 20% +
- Capital spending 2027, which started slowing down, gradually returning to 30-35% of the income ratio.
- Reality Labs, down from 19 billion to less than 10 billion.
- WhatsApp Commercialization contribution $100-20 billion a year

Outcome: Net profits in 2028 amounted to **90-100 billion**, in 25 times PE, with market value of approximately **2.2-2.5 trillion United States dollars** and corresponding share price of approximately **870-990**, up from the current level of **45-66 per cent**.

# Scenario II: status quo (neutral)

Assumptions:
- The income rate is going down to 15%.
- Capital expenditure maintained at 40-50 per cent of income
- Reality Labs. No loss.
- WhatsApp, commercial slow progress.

Outcome: Net profits in 2028 amounted to approximately $750-85 billion**, in 20 times PE, with market value of approximately $1.5-1.7 trillion** and equity value of approximately $590-670**, which was roughly equal to or slightly higher than current stock prices**.

# Scenario III: AI Arms Race Upgrade (pessimistic)

Assumptions:
- AI competitions led to a continuous rise in capital spending, reaching over 180 billion in 2027
- Revenue growth has fallen to 10-12%, but competition has forced investment to keep going.
- Profit margins are squeezed by capital spending, net profits stagnating or falling
- Zuckerberg's betting on another direction.

Outcome: Net profits in 2028 may be only **50 billion-60 billion**, in 15 times PE, with market value of approximately **7500-900 billion** and corresponding share price of approximately **300-360**, which is a decline from the current level of **40-50 per cent**.
