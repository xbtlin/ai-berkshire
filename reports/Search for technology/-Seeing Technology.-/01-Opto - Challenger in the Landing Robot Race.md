## A company that people "love to hate"

At the beginning of 2026, Chumi Technology CEO Yu Hao announced to an employee group of 1,000 people that Chumi will become "the first billion-dollar company ecosystem in human history." The employee's reply was: "Took too many drugs? More than NVIDIA in one year?"

This scene almost accurately describes the entire contradiction of this company: the technology is indeed strong, but even his own family members don’t believe what the CEO says. **

- Bulls say: **200,000-rpm high-speed motor leads the world**, Europe ranks first in sweeping machine shipments, revenue has grown by over 100% for 6 consecutive years, and "China Dyson" is just around the corner.
- Bearishists say: The founder claims to build a "hundred-billion-dollar company ecology**" while doing cleaning appliances, building cars, mobile phones, chips, and space. He is becoming more and more like LeTV.

**What both factions say is true. This is where the pursuit becomes most interesting. **

Open the public information of Zhuimi Technology as of May 2026, and you can see a set of extremely contradictory data:

| Indicators | Data | Signals |
|------|------|------|
| Revenue in 2024 | **~15 billion yuan** | Amazing growth rate (Source: DoNews, Brand Ark and other media) |
| 2025 revenue (claimed) | **40 billion+** | But the caliber is questionable - does it include car pre-sale orders? (Source: Securities Star, Oriental Fortune) |
| Net profit in 2025 (claimed) | **5.55 billion yuan** | The calculation method has been questioned by many parties including TMTpost (Source: TMTpost) |
| Global sweeper market share | **14.4% (second)** | Overtaken Ecovacs (Source: IDC 2025 full-year data) |
| European sweeper market share | **27.6% (first)** | Sweden exceeds 62%, Poland and Denmark exceed 50% (Source: GfK) |
| Primary market valuation | **45-50 billion yuan** | Nearly 4 times from 13 billion yuan in the past 2 years (Source: Hurun Unicorn List) |
| Automobile business valuation | **64 billion yuan in financing** | The equity of the main car manufacturer has been frozen (Source: Sina Finance, May 2026) |
| Product categories | **30+** | Expanding from sweepers to cars, mobile phones, chips, drones, and astronomical telescopes |
| Listing status | **Unlisted** | Plan for Science and Technology Innovation Board + "Batch IPO" in many places around the world |

Putting these together - **A company with annual revenue of 15-40 billion (different calibers), the world's second largest sweeper brand, and a valuation of 50 billion, is also building cars, mobile phones and chips. The CEO claims to be a company with a billion-dollar ecosystem and unaudited financial data. **

If you think it's the "next Dyson" - you're probably only seeing half of it.
If you think it is "the next LeTV" - you have probably only seen half of it.

What this series wants to do is not to draw conclusions for you, but to break apart Chai Mi layer by layer so that you can judge for yourself.

## What is chasing after all?

Most people's understanding of Zhui Mi remains as "a sweeping robot". This perception is both correct and incorrect.

Zhuimi Technology was founded in 2017. Its founder, Yu Hao (born in 1987, Tsinghua Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics, recommended for the Physics Olympiad), accumulated high-speed digital motor technology while doing drone research in Tsinghua's "Sky Factory" laboratory. In 2015, inspired by Dyson, we decided to commercialize aviation-grade motor technology. In 2017, it received **14 million yuan** angel investment from Xiaomi Heshunwei Capital, joined the Xiaomi ecological chain, and started manufacturing vacuum cleaners for Mijia.

**One sentence definition of Zhuimi**: With high-speed digital motors as the core technology platform, it started as an OEM for Xiaomi and is radically expanding into an intelligent hardware company with a "borderless technology ecosystem".

The evolution of Chuimi’s product line clearly reflects its ambitious expansion trajectory:

| Stage | Time | Core Actions | Product Line |
|------|------|---------|--------|
| OEM period | 2017-2019 | Mijia OEM, making handheld vacuum cleaners | 1 category |
| Independent period | 2020-2022 | Launch of the Chuanmi brand and focus on sweeping machines | 3-5 categories |
| Expansion period | 2023-2024 | Floor scrubbers, hair dryers, lawn mowing robots | 10+ categories |
| Out of focus period? | 2025-2026 | Car manufacturing, mobile phones, chips, space, trendy toys, tea drinks | **30+ categories** |

From 1 category to 30+ categories, it only took 8 years. This is the most exciting and unnerving part of the hunt.

## Three common misunderstandings about chasing

### Misunderstanding 1: "Zhunmi is a sweeping robot"

Not entirely true. Sweeping robots are indeed Chuimi’s most important source of income (estimated to account for **40-45%**), but Chuimi’s business territory has far exceeded cleaning appliances:

| Product Category | Revenue Contribution (Estimated) | Market Position |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| Sweeping robot | ~40-45% | Second in the world (IDC, 2025) |
| Floor scrubber | ~20-25% | Ranked first in the global market in multiple regions |
| Cordless vacuum cleaner | ~15-20% | Domestic share 5.9% (Dyson 51.6%) |
| Hair dryer | ~5% | Niche high-end positioning |
| Lawn mowing robot | <5% | Overseas emerging category, growing rapidly |
| Car (Starry Sky Project) | Not yet delivered | 64 billion valuation financing, planned to debut in 2027 |
| Mobile phones, chips, etc. | Not yet contributing revenue | Exploration stage |

**Note**: Chaimi is unlisted. The above revenue structure is an estimate based on industry reports, and the confidence level is medium to low.

A more accurate statement is: Zhuimi is a company with sweeping machines and floor washing machines as its core cash cows, high-speed motors as its technical base, and is currently opening up more than ten new fronts**.

### Misunderstanding 2: "Zhuimi is a Xiaomi ecological chain enterprise"

It used to be the case, but it is basically not the case now.

| Time | Proportion of Mijia OEMs | Proportion of private brands |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 2017-2019 | >90% | <10% |
| 2020-2021 | ~70% | ~30% |
| 2022-2023 | ~30% | ~70% |
| 2024-2025 | ~15-20% (estimate) | ~50%+ (Chasing Brand) |

Zhuimi has basically completed the "de-Xiaomiization" process. The gross profit margin of OEM is about **10-15%**, and the gross profit margin of private brand can reach **35-50%**. This transformation direction is undoubtedly the right one. But Xiaomi is still a shareholder of Chui Mi - both a capital supporter and a potential competitor. This dual identity has always been a subtle existence.

**Opposite view**: Some people think that pursuing "de-Xiaomi" is too hasty and has lost the traffic dividend of Xiaomi channels, resulting in domestic market share (12.2%) being far lower than Ecovacs (25.4%) and Shitou (21.6%). There is some truth to this view - part of the cost of pursuing domestic weaknesses does come from the need to build its own brand recognition after breaking away from the Xiaomi system.

### Misunderstanding 3: "The core competitiveness of Chumi is low price"

Totally wrong. The strategic positioning of Chuimi is precisely **high-end**, not cost-effectiveness.

- The average customer price of Chai Mi’s global sweepers is **627 USD**, and in the North American market it is close to **800 USD**
- Chase China CEO Guo Renjie publicly declared that "the goal is to surpass Dyson in vacuum cleaners and hair dryers."
- The flagship sweeper X40 Ultra is priced at over **5,000 yuan**, and is comparable to the flagship products of Ecovacs and Roborock.

The real core competitiveness is not low price, but **high-speed digital motor technology** - the world's first mass-produced **150,000 rpm** high-speed motor, with reserves of **180,000 rpm and 200,000 rpm** technologies. Globally, only Zhuimi and Dyson have similar capabilities.

But here’s a tough question: **Does technological leadership = commercial success? ** Sony's technology has long been ahead of Samsung, but its market value is far surpassed by Samsung. The commercial value of technical barriers depends on whether it can be converted into brand premium and pricing power. Chaimi still has a long way to go at this point.
