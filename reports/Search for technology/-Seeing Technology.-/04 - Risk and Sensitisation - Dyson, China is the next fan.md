# Nine, wrap it up

The most profound thing I've ever felt in this series is ** "Pity."**

Unfortunately, there is a risk that the search for high-speed motor technology with the best global skills, the impressive rate of overseas expansion, and long-sampling ramps. If Yon Ho can focus – focus on the land sweeper and the land washer to be the world’s absolute first, then carefully expand to powerful related categories such as lawn mowers – it could well be Dyson in China, even beyond Dyson.

But the reality is that Yu Ho has chosen a very different path: millions of dollars, 30+ items, car-making, chip-making, space. This way, it's a clear way forward.

** The pursuit of technology is respectable, but the strategic discipline of the founders is not trustworthy**

Until Yu Ho thinks clearly, "do nothing", pursuit is not a suitable target for investors seeking certainty.

But remember -- this is probably wrong. If Yu Ho is really the next Mask, all questions today will be proven to be "unimaginable" after 10 years. That's the hardest part of the investment:** the distinction between "crazy genius" and "uncontrollable adventurer," often takes time to answer.**
