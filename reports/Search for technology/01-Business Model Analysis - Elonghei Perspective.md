# IX. Overall conclusion

## Business model score: <unk> (3/5)

# Core judgment

** The pursuit of technology is a company with a truly technical barrier to clean appliances, but is being brought into high-risk orbit by the founder's "landless expansion" strategy.**

**Enaps**:
- High-speed digital motor technology leading the world, forming a three- to five-year technology moat
- The growth of overseas markets has been remarkable, and the European market has gained a leading position
- The harvest grew rapidly, and the mini-mei workers successfully transformed their brand
- There's a certain advantage in the product's strength in the competition.

** Disadvantage**:
- The founder's "million dollar eco-vision" vision is out of touch.
- Expansion of the cleaning appliances to 30+ class (car/cell) to manage the radius beyond the power boundary
- Brands are vague. "Seek =?" No clear answer.
- Growth in revenue but declining profits, new business burning money
- Unlisted, low financial transparency, real profitability questionable

** Final judgement of the Iongping perspective**:

If it is able to track the core of the clean appliances track**, the deep-deep floor sweeper plus the ground washer, and continuously strengthens brand names and technical barriers, it has the opportunity to become a Chinese "Dyson" — a good business worth long-term ownership.

But if the search continues to expand without boundaries along the current route of Car-building plus Phone+UAV+Astronomy Telescope, it could be the next "Functioning" instead of the next "Dyson".

** To sum up in the words of the Aryan Ping: ** "This company has the genes for good products but lacks the discipline to do things. Technical skills are first-rate, but strategic forces fail. If I'm going to vote, I have to wait until the day when the founder thinks clearly what he's not doing."
