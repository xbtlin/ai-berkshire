## Series Positioning

NetEase is not a company that nobody has heard of - it is one of the most **extremely profitable yet least seriously discussed** companies in Chinese internet.

- The bulls say: 33% net margin, RMB 49.6 billion in free cash flow, RMB 163.5 billion in net cash equal to 31% of market cap, and only 12x forward P/E - absurdly undervalued
- The bears say: gaming accounts for 82% of revenue, overseas only contributes 10% and lags Tencent far behind, Marvel Rivals simultaneously online players dropped 85%, and seven overseas studios were closed - the growth story does not add up

**Both sides have data support, but they reach completely opposite conclusions.** That means the issue is not who is right and who is wrong; it is that they are looking at different slices of the same company.

This series aims to **lay out each slice clearly** so that you can decide whether it is worth buying, rather than having us decide for you.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Use plain, direct language** - avoid mixed-language clutter
4. **Do not write "I think" or "I feel"** - use "the data shows" and "the evidence suggests"
5. **Do not reference major investors by name** - with one exception for a quoted famous investor, which is labeled generically as "a famous investor"
6. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
