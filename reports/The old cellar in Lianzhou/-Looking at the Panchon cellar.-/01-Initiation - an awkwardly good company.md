** Second, the location of the physical and mental moats.** The 453 cellars, 1,619 centenary cellars, and the 100th century pantry of the industry ** 91.3% ** The physical moats are almost non-robable. But the consumer's heart is ranked as the "Magaba > Five Grains > National Caves" - the mind moats are clearly weak than the physical moats.

** Third, the valuation is extremely low and uncertain.** PE 12-13 is at 10 years of split, with a rate of 5.8% near the highest in history -- valuations have taken into account extremely pessimistic expectations. But industry is still adjusting, cellar prices are still shaking, management is facing a change in tenure in three years -- "favour" does not necessarily mean "the end."

This triple error is the root cause of the huge market divide over the old cellars in the state of Chenzhou.
