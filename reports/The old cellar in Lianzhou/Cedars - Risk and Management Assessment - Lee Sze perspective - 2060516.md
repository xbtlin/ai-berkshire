# 8.1 Is this a good business?

White wine – especially the high-end, strong-coloured wine with unreplicated vault assets – is a small Chinese business of "bankmarking." Net interest rate of 40% +, ROIC 30% +, hardly requiring capital spending to remain competitive. But the old cellar in Chenzhou is "two good business" and not "one good business".

# 8.2 Does management count?

Liu Xiao is a competent manager (marketing origin, 10 years of company leadership, from 69 billion to 31.2 billion), but not a brilliant entrepreneur (strategic commitments are often frustrated and cycle judgement is not sensitive). The system of state enterprises determines that management changes are not dependent on shareholder willingness, and this risk requires a discount in valuations.

# 8.3 Is the price good?

** Need sufficient security margin**. Taking into account all the above risks, the reasonable valuation area of the Penang cellar is 12-18 times PE.
