# Wrap it up #

1. ** Large differences in size**: by income and GMV, Temu/PDD is about 1/12 of Amazon and market value is only 1/21.

2. ** The valuation gap is equally large but in the opposite direction**: PDD is only 10 times more than PE and 32 times more than Amazon. The market gave Amazon a higher valuation mainly because of AWS and two high-profit growth engines for advertising, while the market was cautious about Temu’s sustainability and tariff risks.

3. ** The profit gap is relatively small**: PDD Group net profit is about $14.4 billion, or one quarter of Amazon. This is mainly due to the high profit margin (over 40% profit margin) in many domestic main stations, but Temu is still losing abroad.

4. **Temu ' s unique advantage**: to cover the 80+ market, the very low price belt (a large number of goods below $5), and the supply chain to Chinese factories, but faced significant uncertainty about the United States tariff policy (de Minimis cancellation).

**Amazon Haul responded**: Amazon has spent 12 months expanding Haul to 25 markets, with 400 per cent growth in selections, three times more visits, and a positive face to the low price market of Temu.

** Key differences**: Amazon is a multi-engineer enterprise (Cunt+Advert + Electric + Subscription), with AWS and advertising contributing the vast majority of profits. PDD is essentially an electric power company and is moving from a "profit harvest" to "redisposal".
