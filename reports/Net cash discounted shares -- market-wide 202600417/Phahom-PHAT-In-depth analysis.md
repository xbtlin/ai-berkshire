- ** Faster: first dose inhibits stomach acid, PPI accumulates 3-5 days
- ** Stronger and more sustained: no acid breakthroughs are significantly less dependent on acid activation
- ** Higher rate of hysteria hysteria hysteria **: 3 stages of clinical vs lansoprazole, neither healing nor maintenance period is poor or effective

The product has been approved by the FDA for adaptive disorders:
- The cure of decomposition Gerd + maintenance (November 2023)
- Decomposition of non-thinistic GERD heart fever (July 2024, extended adaptive)
- H. pylori eradication (Triple Pak/ Dual Pak with antibiotics)

** Key moat extension**: 2024 FDA confirmed 10 years of exclusive possession of new chemical entities** to 3 May 2032** (base patent expires in November 2030, but exclusive period locked to 2032).

# VI. Feedback on the ai-berkshire process

The greatest finding of the study was not that PHARE itself was judged, but that it was rather that ** exposed the methodological issues of the last round of net cash discount scans**:

1. ** Scanners define "cash" too broadly**: "Cash + Short-term investments" as molecules, but without "Long-term debt + Revenue internest financing liability". This is a fatal error for biomedical companies - many biotech have heavy debt + structured financing liabilities.

2. ** Proposed amended definition of "net cash discount"**:
- Molecular = cash + short-term investments — total debt ** collectable equity financing liabilities ** non-operational liabilities (e.g. litigation reserves)**
- denominator = fully diluted market value
- The margin is <unk> 1.0.

3. **PHAT as amended definition = ~0.31 (net liability) should be removed from Tier 1**

4. ** It is proposed to rerun a net cash discount scan**, using a stricter definition of Net-Net Cash, Graham, which is likely to become very scarce - a valuable finding in itself (the United States dollar does not currently have a large net cash discount.
