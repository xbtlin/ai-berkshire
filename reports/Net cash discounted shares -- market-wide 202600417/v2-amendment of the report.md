#2. Methodological lessons (most important output in the current round)

# # Lessons 1: The definition of net cash must be strictly defined (hard constraints)

**v1 Easy caliber (Error):**
```
Any company with "Handy Cash on the books vs. Low Market Value" is in Tier 1.
```

**v2 Strict caliber (amended)**:
```
Net cash = cash + short-term investments + financial management
- Interest-bearing bank borrowing (long-term and short-term)
- Bonds payable
- Transferable debt
- Revenue Interest Finance for Biotech
- quasi-debts for mortgages/concessions
- Finance lease liabilities (manufacturing industry, as the case may be)

Screening conditions: net cash / market value * 1.0

Add a separate column for catering/heavy assets:
Rigid caliber 2 = net cash above - IFRS 16 lease liability
```

** For four**: at the strict caliber of v2,** none of the Tier 1 candidates are still valid**

# # lesson 2: "Data timeliness" of third party list-keeping can be fatal

** The case of the Permanent Letter of Letter**: v1 quoted as "cash 597 million/current assets 22.25 billion/market value 10.23 billion" is ** 2018 data**. By 2025, cash had fallen to 199 million and borrowing had risen to 1.1 billion - ** Structural reversals of 7 years**.

** Apocalypse**: Any candidate for scanning output must re-check the latest financial report (T-2 quarter)**. Data over 2 years are always invalidated.

# # Lessons 3: Fundamental facts must be verified, not by "labelling"

** The case of the Hentenian letter**: v1 classifies it as "family holding" -- actually a subsidiary of the UMG (shareholding 55.94%). This error directly affects the judgement of the "shareholder payback motive" — the motives of the company's listing platform are completely different from those of the family business.

** Apocalypse**: The nature of the controlling shareholder (family / interstate / local state enterprise / agency / management) is a hard verification item ** prior to decision-making ** and cannot be inferred from industry or stock prices.

# The lesson 4: The history evidence chain of management's capital allocation is more important than the current action

** 99% case**: surface split + buyback, but:
- 2022-12 Founder holds 888 million Hong Kong dollars (high)
- 2026-01 to over 100x PE to buy Big Way Hot Pot (irrationally)
- HK$200 million buyback authorization is only 7.7% (symbol)
- Retrieval shareholder: Burning in Extension = 1:7

** Apocalypse**: We can't just see if there's a red or no buy back.
- The size of the buyback**, vs, the scale of expansion
- Did management ** have a high-level reduction**
- Whether the value of the acquisition is reasonable**
- Do the high rate of spreads be stubble from the small molecules?

# # lesson 5: Biotech's "cash discount" is almost all a trap (unless it's commercialized)

** PHARS Case** Commercialization fundamentals are indeed good (Voquezna 2025 + 217%, 2026 Guidance + 90%, Q3 2026 Operational Balances are valid), but:
- Revenue Interest Finance 10% (total 600M top)
- Patent cliff 2032, only 6 years of cashing window
- Single drug company.

** Apocalypse**: The "cash-rich" nature of biotech is "the unrealized future cash-out" and not the Buffett-style "free cash." Even if technology and commercialization succeeds, shareholders’ rights are extracted on three levels (authorization + Revenue Interest investor + creditor).

# # Lessons 6: Theme Unit window closes very fast

** Case of Power Science**: From the market value of 700,000 million at the end of 2023 to the market value of 107 billion at the end of August 2025 (+1290%), less than two years. When AI scans based on data from 2023, windows** are already closed**.

** Apocalypse**:
- The frequency of scans must be higher (monthly vs year)
- Beware of "historical low data" -- often backsliding after windows close.
- It should be "average on the day of the 30th day" instead of using old data.

#3. Revised screening process (for future scanning)

## Step 0: Data time-bound gates
- Financial data must be disclosed within **T-2 ** Quarterly
- All data over 2 years are invalidated
- Market value is locked at the average price of the last 30 days.

## Step 1: Strict net cash measurement (the above formula)
- All forms of interest-bearing/admissible liabilities must be deducted
- Biotech must separately list the liability of the Revenue Interest Finance
- Catering/heavy assets company labels "incl./not IFRS 16"

## Step 2: Stockholder nature verification (hard)
- Family / interstate / local state enterprise / management / financial investor
- The physical profile and history of the subject.

## Step 3: Management capital allocation evidence chain (over three)
- Total Reds for the past 5 years vs Total capital expenditure on new/acquisitions
- Was there a high-level reduction in the founder/supervisor over the past 5 years?
- Number of units/units/drawn-up financings available over the past 5 years
- Repurchase authorization implementation rate (level of authority vs actually implemented)

## Step 4: Business quality initial screening
- Main occupation 5 years ROE average (deductible)
- Protect the city by type identification (web effects/ brand / size / patent / conversion costs)
- Blast-type restaurant / single drug biotech / cycle commodity <unk> Default downgrade

## Step 5: Event-driven vs Value Investment Classification
- Privatization arbitrage / reorganization incident <unk> Independent classification
- Liquidation value arbitrage — independent classification
- Hold long-term value <unk> Tier 1/2/3
