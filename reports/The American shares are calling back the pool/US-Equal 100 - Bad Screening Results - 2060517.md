# Four, key findings

1. **SaaS plate wide area phase-out**: 13 only passed. The core issue is that the early SBCs caused GAAP losses to fall by 10 years of ROE average. This does not represent a "bad" for these companies, but rather that the framework requirements for "10 years of average" are not naturally favourable to high-growth early-deficit enterprises.

2. ** The highest rate of medical + financial pass**: mature business model, stable profitability, low dilution, high cash flow conversion.

3. ** "Raad immunity" requires attention**: Walmart (Exemption C), McKesson (Exempt C), Airbnb (Exempt A), GE Aerospace (Exempt A), Amgen (Expense Coverage Edge), ExxonMobil (net interest rate margin).

4. **Countered but business model high-quality companies**: Servicenow, CrowdStrick, Palantir, Costco, TransDigm — the phase-out of these companies is more a limitation of the indicator system than an imperfectity of the company itself.
