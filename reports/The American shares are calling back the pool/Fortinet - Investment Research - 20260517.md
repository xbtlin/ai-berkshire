# Fortinet (FTNT) Investment Research Report

**Study Date: May 17, 2026 | Stock Price: Approximately $122.78**

---

## 1. Business model analysis

**Summary in one sentence:** Sell self-developed chip-driven hardware firewalls into corporate networks, and then rely on operating system subscriptions and security services to continue to charge high-profit annual fees.

### Income structure

- Hardware products (firewalls, etc.): ~35%
- Service Subscription (FortiGuard+FortiCare+SASE): ~65% (a real cash cow)

### Moat Assessment

| Type | Strength | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Switching costs | ★★★★★ | Migration costs are extremely high after FortiGate is embedded in the corporate network |
| Proprietary technology | ★★★★☆ | The performance of the self-developed FortiASIC chip is 5-10 times that of competing general-purpose CPUs |
| Platform Ecology | ★★★★☆ | FortiOS unified management of 40+ products |
| Scale | ★★★☆☆ | Ranked first in global firewall unit share (55%) |

### Moat Trend: Strengthening

- SASE business +18% year-on-year, SSE business growth rate exceeds 110%
- Q1 2026 bill growth rate 31%
- FortiOS 8.0 has built-in AI native security controls

### Management

Founder Xie Xiaoling holds ~15% of the shares, and his interests are bound. Rule of 45 for 7 consecutive years. FCF in 2025 hit a record $2.21 billion. Additional $3 billion repurchase authorization.

## 4. Risk and Management

### Three major risks

1. **Growth collapse in the second half of the replacement cycle (high probability/medium impact)**: Dividends fade in 2027-2028
2. **Security vulnerability incident (low probability/high impact)**: CVE-2026-35616 has been exploited
3. **Competitors’ platform-based catch-up (medium probability/medium impact)**: Palo Alto uses discount strategy to grab customers

### Management: high integrity and high ability

The founder has not sold a large amount of shares in 25 years, creating an underpromise-overdeliver culture. The only concern: hidden dangers in the governance of the family management structure.

### Worst case scenario

End of generation change + SASE not as good as expected + vulnerability events superimposed: stock price $75-85 (down 30-38%)

### 10 years later: Stronger, but limited in strength

The demand for network security is irreversible, and FortiASIC’s unique advantages cannot be replicated within a few years. Constraints: Limited space for volume growth, and completion of cloud-native transformation is weaker than competing products.

---

## Overall rating: Good

**Summary in one sentence:** Fortinet is a high-quality company with a real moat, excellent profits, and credible management. However, the current $122 has fully priced the good news, and the safety margin is insufficient. It is suitable for bargain hunting at $95-105.
