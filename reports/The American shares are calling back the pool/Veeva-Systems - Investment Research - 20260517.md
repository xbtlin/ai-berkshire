# Veeva Systems (VEEV) Investment Research Report

**Study date: May 17, 2026 | Share price: ~$173**

---

## 1. Business model analysis

**Summary in one sentence:** The vertical cloud software monopoly in the life sciences industry charges highly sticky subscription fees to pharmaceutical companies, covering the entire chain from research and development to commercialization.

### Income structure

- Subscription revenue accounted for 84%+ (FY2026 subscription revenue $2.684 billion, +17%)
- Typical high-margin SaaS model

### Moat Assessment

| Type | Strength | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Switching costs | ★★★★★ | The pharmaceutical company's Vault system involves FDA compliance and historical data migration, and replacement will take several years |
| Industry Barriers | ★★★★☆ | Deeply embedded in the GxP regulatory system, general cloud vendors cannot quickly copy |
| Network effect | ★★★☆☆ | Industry data standardization creates certain effects |

### Moat trend: strengthening

- Vault CRM migration: Migrated customers from Salesforce to its own platform, 125+ customers have been online
- Target to cover 14 top 20 pharmaceutical companies by the end of FY2027

### Management

CEO Peter Gassner has been in office for 19 years and holds 7.51% of the shares (~$2.1 billion), with his interests fully bound. A $2 billion buyback plan was announced in March 2026. No impulse for mergers and acquisitions, focusing on organic growth.

## 3. Industry competition landscape

### Industry structure: oligopoly, dominated by Veeva

- Veeva market share 26.8% (first)
- Salesforce 16.4%，Oracle 11.2%

### Key competition

| Competitors | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Salesforce Life Sciences | Large investment in AI, wide platform ecology | Insufficient depth of compliance |
| IQVIA (OCE) | Strong data assets | Weak CRM functionality |
| Oracle | Enterprise-grade foundation | Non-cloud native |

**Salesforce is a real threat but has not shaken the foundation yet. ** 9 of the top 20 pharmaceutical companies chose Vault CRM, while Salesforce only chose 3.

### Industry Trends

- Acceleration of pharmaceutical digitalization
- AI empowers clinical research (Veeva has deployed AI agents)
- Regulatory compliance complexity continues to increase

---

## 4. Risk and Management

### Three major risks

1. **Vault CRM migration execution risk (medium probability/high impact)**: real pressure in 2027-2029
2. **Salesforce competition intensifies (medium probability/medium impact)**: Agentforce AI capabilities actively compete for customers
3. **M&A consolidation in the pharmaceutical industry compresses IT budgets (low probability/high impact)**

### Management: Excellent

Gassner has been in charge since 2007, holding $2.1 billion in shares, with no governance scandals, and $2 billion in buybacks reflecting his judgment on intrinsic value.

### Worst case scenario

Massive loss of Vault CRM + customer acquisition by Salesforce: growth rate dropped to single digits, stock price ~$120-150

### 10 years later: stronger

Life science regulatory compliance is a long-term structural need, and 20 years of industry knowledge accumulation cannot be replicated. Target revenue of $6 billion in 2030.

---

## Overall rating: Excellent

**Summary in one sentence:** Veeva is the de facto digital infrastructure for the life sciences industry, with a wide moat + high profit margins + long-term growth certainty. It is currently falling to a historically low valuation due to market panic (forward PE 20x vs historical 76x), which is a rare opportunity for high-quality assets to be mispriced.
