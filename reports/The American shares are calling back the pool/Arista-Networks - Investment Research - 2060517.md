# Four simulations of analytical perspectives

> ** Business nature perspective**: "Arista's EOS is a truly rare asset in the network -- an operating system that allows customers to integrate so deeply that 'replace 'it' is more expensive than 'retention'. This locking does not depend on monopoly clauses, but is embedded in the client's daily workflow. The problem is not whether the business is good or not, but whether the price is not reasonable."

> ** Capital allocation perspective**: "Arista has done little extra-delegation and acquisition over the past five years, focusing on organic growth and R & D, while repurchased stocks. ROE over 32% and FCF conversion rates over 120% of net profits. Such capital allocation discipline, coupled with light asset models, is a rare example of self-regulation by management. Jayshree Ullal is not a personal charm CEO, but an operator."
