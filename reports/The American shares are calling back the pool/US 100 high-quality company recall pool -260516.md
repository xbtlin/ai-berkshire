# Six, risk hints

1. ** Data limitations**: All data are searched from open sources, and some of the "estimate" data are approximate and may be biased from the exact values
2. ** Valuation judgement is qualitative**: not an investment recommendation based on a combination of subjective judgements based on historical PE ranking, speed matching, and the strength of the moat
** Periodical factor**: Energy company negative 3-year CAGR is due to the high oil price base in 2022, not deterioration of base
**GAAP vs Non-GAAP**: SAAS (CrowdStike, Snowflake, etc.) GAAP loss but may be profitable for Non-GAAP, PE is not true
**AI foam risk**: The overall valuation of semiconductors and software plates was pushed up by the AI heat, and caution was required against the expected downturn risk
** ROIC caliber differences**: different calculations from different sources (GuruFocus, StockAnalysis, AlphaSpread) and possibly more different ROIC from the same company
7. ** Fiscal year inconsistency**: companies end the fiscal year at different times (both January-December) and data comparability is limited
