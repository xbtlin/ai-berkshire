## 1. The essence of business model ★★★☆☆

### Core business definition

The essence of Ant Digital is "an enterprise-level technology service provider that grew out of Ant Group's financial technology practice" - it will support the underlying technical capabilities (blockchain, database, privacy computing, AI, mobile development, etc.) of 1 billion Alipay users and export them to external corporate customers in a productized manner.

In Duan Yongping's words, the starting point of this business is "the spillover realization of self-used technology", which is similar to the path AWS grew from Amazon's e-commerce infrastructure, but Ant Digital focuses on the vertical track of **financial-level technology stack**.

In March 2024, Ant Digital operated independently from Ant Group. CEO Zhao Wenbiao defined the strategy as **"ABC" three major business lines**:

| Business Line | Full Name | Core Meaning |
|--------|------|----------|
| **A** | AI (artificial intelligence) | Agent development platform Agentar, industry large model, pay-per-effect |
| **B** | Blockchain (Blockchain/Web3) | Ant Chain BaaS, RWA asset on-chain, ZAN Web3 platform |
| **C** | Cloud-native (cloud native technology service) | mPaaS mobile development, SOFAStack financial cloud, ZOLOZ security technology |

### Breakdown of income structure (calculation)

| Business lines | Main products | Estimated revenue proportion | Confidence | Basis for estimation |
|--------|----------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Cloud native technology services (C) | mPaaS, SOFAStack | ~35-40% | 🔴Low | mPaaS serves more than 60% of the Top 50 financial institutions; SOFAStack serves 130+ financial institutions |
| AI service (A) | Agentar intelligent platform, industry large model, intelligent risk control | ~25-30% | 🔴Low | CEO said revenue increased by 50%, and AI is the main growth engine |
| Blockchain/Web3 (B) | Ant Chain BaaS, ZAN, RWA | ~15-20% | 🔴Low | Blockchain patents rank first in the world, but commercialization is slow |
| Privacy Computing | Moss Platform, Linguistic Framework | ~5-10% | 🟡Medium | IDC reported a market share of 36.7%, with a total market size of only 980 million yuan |
Overseas benefits of the above programs ~10-15% of 300% in 2022-2023

**Total revenue scale estimation**: It is comprehensively estimated that Ant Digital's total revenue in 2025 will be approximately **3 billion to 6 billion yuan** (excluding independent OceanBase), and the confidence level is low.

## 2. Product/Platform Flywheel Effect ★★★☆☆

### Flywheel model

```
Ant Group Financial Practical Scenario (Alipay 1 Billion User Verification)
        ↓
  Technology productization (mPaaS/SOFAStack/Ant Chain/Privacy Computing)
        ↓
  Adopted by external financial institutions → Accumulating industry know-how
        ↓
  AI agent platform (Agentar) → Pay-per-effect lowers customer decision-making threshold
        ↓
  Xinglan Plan (300+ ecological partners) → 100+ joint solutions
        ↓
  Accumulation of more industry data and scenarios → more accurate models → more customers
```

### Flywheel Verification

| Flywheel elements | Is it true | Evidence |
|----------|----------|------|
| Uniqueness of technology source | ✅ Establishment | Actual verification of Alipay’s 1 billion users, non-laboratory product |
| Deeply bound to the financial industry | ✅ Partially established | Serving 200+ financial institutions, but has not yet formed an "irreplaceable" dependency |
| Ecological partner network effect | ⚠️ Early stage | 300 partners, 13,000 corporate customers, self-reinforcement effect has not yet formed |
| AI Pay-Per-Effect Flywheel | ⚠️ To be verified | Innovative model but unknown scale effect |
| Collaboration across business lines | ⚠️ To be verified | ABC three lines theoretically collaborate, but whether 1+1>2 actually remains to be seen |

**Conclusion**: The flywheel frame has been built, but it is still in the early stages of "pushing the flywheel" and is far from reaching the state of rotation.

## 3. Moat analysis ★★★☆☆

| Moat Type | Rating | Analysis |
|-----------|------|------|
| **Brand** | ★★★☆☆ | The "Ant" brand has high recognition in the financial technology circle, but it is not as good as Huawei and Alibaba Cloud in the To B market |
| **Switching Cost** | ★★★★☆ | **One of the Strongest Moats**. After financial institutions adopt SOFAStack/mPaaS for core system transformation, the migration cost is extremely high |
| **Network Effect** | ★★☆☆☆ | Weak. To B enterprise software naturally lacks C-side network effects |
| **Scale Effect** | ★★★☆☆ | Technology research and development can be invested once and reused many times, but financial To B needs to be deeply customized, which limits the scale effect |
| **Technical Barriers** | ★★★★☆ | Most blockchain patents in the world; No. 1 in privacy computing market share for three consecutive years with 36.7%; No. 1 in local deployment of OceanBase distributed database |

**Summary from Duan Yongping's Perspective**: There are real technical barriers and switching cost moats, but the durability in China's To B market is questionable - government customers tend to diversify suppliers in procurement, and Huawei and Tencent are also actively deploying. **The moat "is there but not wide enough"**.

## 4. Customer value analysis ★★★★☆

| Customer Type | Unique Value | Evidence |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Bank/Financial Institution** | Alipay-level distributed architecture empowers small and medium-sized banks, and AI agents help business growth | mPaaS serves more than 60% of the Top 50 financial institutions; a certain bank’s financial transactions increased by 20% |
| **Government/Government Affairs** | Judicial evidence storage, data element circulation, and government affairs digitization | 3.3 billion pieces of evidence storage data on the judicial blockchain of the People's Court |

The "pay-for-performance" model** is the core highlight - customers pay based on the actual results of business growth, which shows that Ant Digital has enough confidence in the customer value it creates.

## 5. Duan Yongping’s “Good Business” Standard Evaluation

| Dimensions | Rating | Assessment |
|------|------|------|
| Differentiation | ★★★★☆ | Financial-grade technology stack is the core differentiation, non-universal cloud competition logic |
| Pricing power | ★★☆☆☆ | The overall pricing power of China’s To B market is weak, which is the biggest structural challenge |
| Sustainable competitive advantage | ★★★☆☆ | There is a high probability that it will still be there in 10 years, but whether it will be stronger depends on whether AI can be scaled up |

**Duan Yongping’s possible judgment**:

> "Ant Digital is a company that has the right direction but has not yet established a deterministic moat. It has real technical barriers and unique customer value. However, the structural problems of China's To B market - weak pricing power, high customer acquisition costs, and long payment cycles - are the 'original sin' of the entire track. If AI agents can really help customers make quantifiable money, this model may change from a 'bitter business' to a 'good business'. But it is too early to judge now. **This is not within my circle of competence.**"
