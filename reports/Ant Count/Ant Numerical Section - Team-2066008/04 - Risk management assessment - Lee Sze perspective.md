## 2. Regulatory risks

### 2.1 Ant Group’s rectification status
- **Completed**: A fine of 7.123 billion yuan was paid in July 2023, special rectification was completed, and normalized supervision was entered
- **Unfinished**: The financial holding company license is still being applied for; the personal credit reporting business restructuring plan is still being adjusted
- **Impact on Ant Digital**: After independent operation, the direct connection with financial rectification has weakened, but the brand and resource allocation are still affected indirectly.
- **Influence level: Medium | Occurrence probability: Partially occurred, remaining medium**

### 2.2 Data Security Law/Personal Information Protection Law
- The "Network Data Security Management Regulations" will be implemented on January 1, 2025, and platforms with more than 10 million monthly active users will have stricter requirements.
- Ant Digital’s security technology products involve a large amount of personal identity verification data and are a focus of supervision
- **Impact level: medium to high | Probability of occurrence: high (already in implementation)**

### 2.3 Innovation policy (positive impact)
- Document No. 79 requires that 100% of central and state-owned enterprises be replaced by Xinchuang by 2027
- OceanBase directly benefits from being the leader in domestic distributed databases
- However, private enterprise background may be limited in some government affairs scenarios.
- **Net Impact: Positive | Certainty: Medium-High**

## 4. Business risks

### 4.1 Dependence on related party transactions (maximum risk)
- Ant Digital was born out of Ant Group, and the proportion of internal customers in the initial stage is expected to be extremely high
- Undisclosed proportion of revenue from internal and external customers - this is a key indicator of independent commercialization capabilities
- If Ant Group’s internal contribution exceeds 50% of revenue, the logic of independent valuation is questionable
- **Influence level: High | Probability of occurrence: High (almost certain to have a high proportion of related-party transactions)**

### 4.2 Technical route risks

**Can blockchain be commercially used on a large scale? **
- The BaaS market is only 1.89 billion yuan, which is extremely small
- RWA is the latest direction (charging pile RWA 100 million yuan, Brazilian photovoltaic green certificate 220 million reais), but the scale is still small
- Honest judgment: PMF for blockchain enterprise-level applications is still being verified
- **Impact: High | Probability of occurrence: Medium**

**Can AI business support growth? **
- Newly established "Large Model Technology Innovation Department" and launched Agentar
- Enterprise-level AI competition is extremely fierce (Baidu, Alibaba, Huawei, and Byte are all doing it)
- **Impact level: medium to high | Probability of occurrence: medium**

### 4.3 International business risks
- Overseas revenue increased by 300% but from a small base. The main markets are Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Brazil.
- Geopolitical risks, local competition, compliance costs
- **Impact: Medium | Probability of occurrence: Medium**

## 5. Governance structure risks

### 5.1 Equity Structure
- In January 2023, Jack Ma gave up actual control and his voting rights dropped from 53.46% to 6.2%
- 10 natural persons independently exercise the voting rights of shares
- Positive: Eliminates the political risk of "one person at the helm"; Negative: May weaken the efficiency of strategic decision-making

### 5.2 Independent listing plan
- Ant International has been rumored to be preparing for a Hong Kong listing
- Ant Digital CEO said in September 2024 that "there is no specific timetable"
- OceanBase will complete financing in 2024, and the new shareholders will highly overlap with Ant Group, which is interpreted as a pre-IPO signal
- **Judgment**: Ant Digital may be listed on the market in 2027-2028 at the earliest

### 5.3 Related transactions and conflicts of interest
- There are a lot of technical and business synergies between Ant Digits, Ant International, and OceanBase
- The fairness of transfer pricing and profit distribution needs to be handled carefully before listing
- **Influence level: Medium to high | Structural presence**

## 6. Long-term deterministic analysis (Li Lu’s perspective)

### What could disrupt its business model?

1. **AI native application reconstructs enterprise services**: AI agent directly replaces traditional SaaS/PaaS
2. **Mature public chain ecology**: Enterprises may not need alliance chain/BaaS services
3. **Large manufacturers' dimensionality reduction crackdown**: Huawei and Alibaba Cloud are bundled with stronger ecological capabilities
4. **Regulatory Black Swan**: A new round of regulatory actions against Ant Group

### Is it in the right direction of "civilization evolution"?

**Partly yes, partly not sure. **

Right direction: Irreversible digital transformation, deterministic direction of enterprise-level AI empowerment, and structural opportunities for Xinchuang’s domestic substitution.

Uncertain direction: It is undecided whether blockchain is a "necessity" or an "icing on the cake" in enterprise-level applications; the regulatory prospect of RWA/stablecoins in China is unclear.

## Overall conclusion

**Ant Digits currently does not meet the "certainty" requirements in Li Lu's investment framework. **

1. **Insufficient understanding** (C-level information): not listed, no public financial reports, core operating data is not transparent
2. **Not sure enough**: The commercial prospects of blockchain are uncertain, the competitive landscape of AI is uncertain, and the dependence on related-party transactions is uncertain.
3. **Management is capable but not excellent**: Strong technical capabilities, but lacks long-term independent To B operation verification

**Advice**: Keep tracking but don’t rush to bet. Key observation indicators:
- Changes in the proportion of revenue from external customers
- Whether the proportion of overseas income reaches 20%+
- Can the RWA/stablecoin business scale exceed 1 billion?
- Whether to start the IPO process (detailed data will be disclosed in the prospectus at that time)

**Comprehensive score of risk and management dimensions: ★★★☆☆**
