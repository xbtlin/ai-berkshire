### What would Buffett think?

> "I would rather be vaguely correct than precise wrong." Ant Digital's "fuzzy correctness" is that it has the first position in multiple market segments, is backed by the Ant ecosystem, and benefits from the two major trends of AI and domestic substitution. But the "precise risk" is that China's To B technology companies have difficulty making profits as a whole, and valuation realization is highly dependent on IPOs.
>
> Buffett will most likely say: "I don't understand the exact profitability of this company five years from now, so I choose to skip it."
