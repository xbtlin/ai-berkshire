1. ** Different legal entities**: Level A is a financing interest in "Ant Chain (Shanghai) Digital Technology Limited"; Level B is a stake in "Ant Technology Group Inc."; Level C is the subject of a future reorganization of IPOs (presumably a new Port Holding Company).
