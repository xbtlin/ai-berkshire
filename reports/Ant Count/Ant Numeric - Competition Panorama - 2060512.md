** Not technology, but "The dirty and labor of the financial scene".**

- Technical barriers (patent/arithmetic) can be pursued - China has the capacity to build a fully fledged prison, and the brain spectrum has the self-research model

1. **Fung is the top of the cloud** (high probability/high impact): Hwa is already the financial Iaas height and if the extension to PaaS/Applied Layer continues, the ants market can be gradually eroded by a "one cloud" strategy and customer relations

3. ** Core track ceiling** (high probability/impact): BaaS ~ 1.9 billion + privacy calculations ~ 1 billion + share of safe wind shield = core market of less than 4 billion = does not support high valuation narratives
