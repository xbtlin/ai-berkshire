## Series Positioning

Luzhou Laojiao is not a company that "nobody studies" - it is the **most awkwardly positioned** company in the baijiu industry, despite being studied a great deal.

- The bulls say: P/E of **~12.5x** (6th percentile over 10 years), gross margin **87%**, net cash **RMB 25 billion**, and dividend yield **5.8%** - absurdly cheap
- The bears say: the premium Guojiao 1573 price is inverted, Fenjiu has overtaken it and pushed it to fourth place, the strong-aroma segment is shrinking, and management turnover is coming within 3 years

**Both sides are looking at the same company and reaching totally different conclusions.** The interesting part is that both sides have a point.

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and then explain what has changed**, so that you can decide whether it is worth buying.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - some key Luzhou Laojiao metrics (such as Guojiao 1573 single-product revenue and regional revenue mix) are not publicly disclosed, so this series only uses the most reliable sources and does not force in fake precision
4. **Do not invent data** - all numbers are sourced, and estimates are clearly marked as "estimated"
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
