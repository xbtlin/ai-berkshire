** Fatal risk - 93% of the monolithic product:** "If a company earns 90% of its income from a product, it is essentially a product, not a company."

# Barfitt perspective - Finance and valuation

1. ** "155 times increase" is primarily a base effect:** in 2023, income was only 58.3 million, which is 5.8 times the real point, 2024 ff 2025 - should not be confused by surprising figures.

** Cash flow was under pressure:** Cash on hand as at January 2025 was only 380 million, current liabilities were 3,314 million - IPO fund-raising will improve significantly
4. **IPO pricing is not cheap:** 24 x PE has given high expectations to a company that has only been profitable for one year
** Three scenario valuations:** optimism + 176%, neutrality + 60%, pessimism - 49% - risk returns are more general

# Manger perspective - Industry and competition

1. **Calculatory:** Global distributed light storage market CAGR~30%, stackable breakdown ~60%
2. ** "Breaking First" is misleading:** 28.6% is the share of the stackable one machine segment, and only 1.3% is wide-calibre — 18% is the share of the Chinese
3. **Was the elephant in the room:** Once the stacking track is large enough, the Chinese will counter-attack, and the technology/brand/channel/money will run out of place.
4. **AI enabling is a double-edged sword:** SigenOS is valuable but not unique, and data barriers are disadvantageous (by far less than Wa and Tesla)
5. **Australasia product quality wave:** ACCC released SigenStor safe recall (AC plug overheating/fire), which, although remedied, exposed the risk of product control during the rapid expansion period

# Eight, wrap up

Sigg New Energy is one of the most topical IPOs in 2026. The first-ever break-up in the world from China to three years for smart photovolt CEOs, SigenStor, does represent a better user experience and business model innovation. 50% Māori, 35% net interest rate, 176 times overpurchase, and Leats/Golden/Golden endorsement - all short-term indicators are screaming "buy-in."

But the Four Masters framework reminds us that this is a company that ** has only been profitable for one year,**93% of income is dependent on a single product,** ** 50% of the Māori rate is subject to competition ** **, ** the giant who is the former homeowner + 14 times the market share may be able to fight back at any time** **, ** the tariff barriers on North American markets are almost sealed**. 80 billion HKD market value corresponds to 24x PE, and the market has priced a lot of good news.

** Final recommendation: short-term excess gains are feasible (probability > 70%), but medium- and long-term holdings are required to wait - for certification of performance after at least two quarters of listing, for confirmation of stability of the Māori rate, for the second growth curve to be fulfilled and for the Chinese attitude to be clarified.** This is more appropriate as a transaction than as an investment before.
