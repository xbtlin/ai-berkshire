# 01 Business model analysis – Duan Yongping’s perspective

> "Do the right thing, do it right." —— Duan Yongping

---

## 1. What is the core business?

The core business of SIG New Energy is **distributed photovoltaic storage integrated solution** - integrating photovoltaic power generation, energy storage, charging, and energy management into a "plug and play" product, which is sold to overseas (mainly Europe and Australia) households and industrial and commercial users.

**Business model breakdown:**

| Level | Content | Revenue proportion |
|------|------|---------|
| Hardware layer | SigenStor five-in-one optical storage machine | ~93% (2025) |
| Software layer | SigenOS AI energy management system | Included in hardware |
| Service layer | Installation, operation and maintenance, cloud services | Very few (currently) |

**SigenStor's "five-in-one" product integrates: **
1. Photovoltaic inverter
2. Energy storage converter (PCS)
3. Energy storage battery (can be stacked 5-100kWh)
4. DC fast charging module (EV charging)
5. AI energy management system (SigenOS)

The core value of this design: **Turn things that originally required 5 devices, 5 brands, and 5 installations into 1 device, 1 installation, and 1 APP. ** This is like Apple integrating an MP3 player, mobile phone, and camera into an iPhone.

---

## 2. Duan Yongping’s “good business” standard evaluation

### 2.1 Are user needs real? ★★★★★

Real and rigid. The global energy transformation is an irreversible trend, and distributed optical storage is the general direction:
- European electricity prices are high (0.3-0.5 Euro/kWh), and the energy storage investment payback period is only 3-5 years.
- Australia has plenty of sunlight and strong government subsidies
- "Five-in-one" is 20-30% cheaper than scattered purchasing, and the installation time is shortened by 50%+
- Users are indeed voting with their feet: In the first three quarters of 2024, global stackable optical storage all-in-one machine shipments will be accounted for by SiGe, which accounts for 28.6%.

### 2.2 Is it a "simple" business? ★★★☆☆

**Not simple enough. ** Duan Yongping's favorite business is the kind that "any fool can run". SIG's business has the following complexities:
- The technical threshold is high (power electronics + electrochemistry + software + AI + charging piles), requiring continuous R&D investment
- Complex supply chain management (core components such as battery cells, IGBTs, and PCBs)
- Overseas channel construction and maintenance costs are high (85 countries, 172 distributors)
- Localized service network is required for installation and after-sales service

**Duan Yongping may say:** "This business is much more complicated than OPPO/vivo, and it is more like what Huawei is doing - but Xu Yingtong originally came from Huawei."

### 2.3 Is there differentiation? ★★★★☆

**Yes, and the differentiation is strong:**
- **Product level:** The world's first "five-in-one" stackable design, most competing products are still split
- **Experience level:** One-stop purchasing + installation + APP management, saving trouble for both installers and users
- **Brand level:** It has established a "high-end optical storage" positioning in the European, American and Australian markets, similar to Apple in the consumer electronics field

**But differentiation is not lasting enough:** Five-in-one integration is not an insurmountable technical barrier. Huawei, Tesla, Enphase, etc. are all capable of similar integration. Differentiation comes more from **first-mover advantage + channel network + brand recognition** rather than from a technical moat.

### 2.4 What about pricing power? ★★★★☆

**Currently strong pricing power:**
- Gross profit margin in 2025 is 50.1%, much higher than peers 20-30%
- SigenStor sells for about 2.2 yuan/Wh, higher than the industry average
- Distributors are willing to promote this brand (high profit, simple installation, less after-sales service)

**Duan Yongping will remind:** "High gross profit margin will attract competition. 50% gross profit margin is unsustainable in the manufacturing industry and will be pulled down sooner or later. The key is to see how long it can be maintained."

### 2.5 Channel moat? ★★★★☆

SiGe’s channel network construction speed is astonishing:
- Covered 85 countries and 172 distributors within 2 years
- Ranked first in energy storage shipments in the Australian market (surpassing Tesla Powerwall)
- Signed a 1GWh MOU with Lawrence & Hanson (the leading distributor in Australia)
- Rapid market penetration in Europe (UK, Sweden, Netherlands)

**Huawei's genes are most vividly reflected in channel construction** - Xu Yingtong led Huawei's photovoltaic inverter to be the first in the world, and its core competency is channels.

## 3. The biggest hidden danger of "good business": single product dependence

> "If 90% of a company's revenue comes from one product, then it is essentially a product, not a company." —— Duan Yongping

**This is SIG’s biggest structural risk:**

| Annual | SigenStor Revenue Proportion |
|------|:----------------:|
| 2023 | 96.4% |
| First three quarters of 2024 | 90.5% |
| 2025 | ~93% |

Single product dependency means:
1. **Any factor that affects SigenStor = affects the entire company** (technology iteration, substitution of competing products, tariff barriers)
2. **No "Second Curve"** - If SigenStor's growth slows, the company stagnates
3. **High concentration of supply chain** - The supply security of core components such as batteries is the lifeblood

**Comparison reference:** Apple’s iPhone revenue accounts for about 50% and is considered to be highly dependent. SIG's 93% is an extreme level.

## 4. General comments from Duan Yongping’s perspective

### Rating: ★★★★ 4.0/5

**Advantages:**
- Strong product capabilities, real user needs, and obvious differentiation
- First-class channel building capabilities (Huawei gene)
- Strong pricing power, gross profit margin far exceeds peers
- Strong team execution (revenue from 0 to 9 billion in 3 years)

**Disadvantages:**
- Not a "simple" business, with high technical and supply chain complexity
- Reliance on single product exceeds 90%, which is a fatal risk
- 50% gross profit margin will inevitably lead to fierce competition
- Founded only 3 years ago, its business resilience has not been tested

**Duan Yongping's possible conclusion:** "This product is indeed good, and the team is very capable. But the business model is not simple enough and relies too heavily on single products. It is impossible to maintain a 50% gross profit margin for a long time in China's manufacturing industry. I will pay close attention, but I will not take a heavy position in the IPO - let the bullets fly for a while."
