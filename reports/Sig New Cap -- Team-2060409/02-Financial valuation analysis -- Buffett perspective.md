## 3. Buffett-style review

### 3.1 The "gold content" of revenue growth

> "If it takes two years for a tree to grow from 1 meter to 150 meters, the question you should ask is not 'How tall can it grow?' but 'Is this normal?'"

**Teardown of 155x Growth:**
- Established in May 2022, large-scale shipments will only begin in 2023 - extremely low base
- Revenue in 2023 is only 58.3 million RMB (approximately US$8 million), the size of a medium-sized installer
- The truly meaningful growth data is: 5.8 times growth from 2024 to 2025
- **Base effect dominates the growth story – don’t be fooled by “155x”**

### 3.2 Gross profit margin of 50% - pie in the sky?

**The gross profit margin of 50.1% is extremely rare in the optical storage industry and needs to be dismantled for the following reasons: **

| Factors | Impact | Sustainability |
|------|------|---------|
| Five-in-one integration premium | Users are willing to pay a premium for a one-stop solution | Medium - the premium shrinks after competing products catch up |
| Channel pricing strategy | Selling through distributors in high-end markets (Europe, the United States and Australia) | Medium - the price war has spread from domestic to overseas |
| Early market dividends | There are few competitors for stackable optical storage all-in-one machines | Low - Huawei, GoodWe, etc. are quickly following up |
| Software value-added | SigenOS improves product added value | High - software barriers are relatively durable |
| The scale effect is beginning to show | Nantong factory is put into operation, production costs drop | High - continue to benefit from the expansion of scale |

**Buffett would say:** "50% gross profit margin is not a problem, the question is how long you can maintain it. In China's manufacturing industry, high gross profit margin is like honey - it will attract every bear."

### 3.3 Cash Flow Analysis

**Key concerns disclosed in the prospectus:**
- As of September 30, 2024, total liabilities were RMB 744 million and current liabilities were RMB 702 million.
- Cash and cash equivalents are only RMB 294 million
- **IPO raising HKD 4.19 billion (~3.68 billion RMB) will significantly improve the balance sheet**
- However, working capital needs are huge in the high growth stage (inventory, receivables)

### 3.4 Safety Margin Assessment

**Buffett’s Margin of Safety Test:**

| Scenarios | Assumptions | Valuation |
|------|------|------|
| **Optimistic** | 2026 revenue of 18 billion, net profit margin of 35%, 35x PE | Market value of HKD 220.5 billion (+176%) |
| **Neutral** | 2026 revenue of 15 billion, net profit margin of 30%, 25x PE | Market value of HKD 127.7 billion (+60%) |
| **Pessimistic** | 2026 revenue of 12 billion, net profit margin of 20%, 15x PE | Market value of HKD 40.9 billion (-49%) |

**Safety margin judgment: There is 60% upside potential under the neutral scenario, but the decline is close to 50% under the pessimistic scenario. For a three-year-old company, this risk-benefit ratio is not particularly attractive. **

## 4. General Comments from Buffett’s Perspective

### Rating: ★★★☆ 3.5/5

**Advantages:**
- The growth rate is astonishing, and a net profit of 2.9 billion has been achieved in 2025
- 50% gross profit margin and 36% net profit margin stand out in the industry
- IPO PE ~24x, not outrageous for high-growth companies
- Balance sheet will improve significantly after IPO fundraising

**Disadvantages:**
- "155 times growth" is mainly a base effect and should not be linearly extrapolated
- There is a high probability that the 50% gross profit margin will be lowered by competition - the question is whether it is fast or slow
- It has only been established for 3 years, and the financial data sample is too small to judge the profitability after "normalization"
- Operational cash flow and inventory management require continuous tracking
- The valuation at the time of IPO was not cheap - there was no obvious opportunity to "pick up bargains"

**Buffett's possible conclusion:** "I don't invest in IPOs - never. The growth of this company is indeed amazing, but I need to see at least 3-5 years of stable operating data to evaluate its 'normalized' profitability and the durability of the moat. 24 times PE for a company with only 3 years of history, the market has given high expectations."
