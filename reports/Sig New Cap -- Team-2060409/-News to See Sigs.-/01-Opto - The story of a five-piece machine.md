It was oversubscribed 1,102 times, doubled on the first day, and the market value exceeded HK$160 billion - and then fell 24% in a month.

The same company allows those who are new to the market to make a lot of money and those who pursue high prices to lose money. Let the bulls talk about the "next CATL era" and the bears talk about the "IPO bubble." Both factions have factual basis, but their conclusions are completely opposite. That's not normal - it usually means the market hasn't seen the company yet for what it is.

Specific figures: On April 16, 2026, SIG New Energy (06656.HK) was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, opening at HK$580 (79% higher than the IPO price of HK$324.20), and closing at HK$659.50, a 103% increase on the first day. It then fell back from the highest point of HK$669, to about HK$519 as of mid-May 2026, a 22% retracement from the high, but still 60% higher than the issue price. This company was only established in May 2022. In less than 4 years, it has gone from zero to a market value of more than 100 billion Hong Kong dollars.

Before you label it, look at it for what it is.

## What exactly is SIG New Energy?

In a sentence: **Sige New Energy is a company that provides distributed photovoltaic and storage integrated solutions - integrating photovoltaic power generation, energy storage, charging, and energy management into a "plug and play" product, which is sold to overseas households and industrial and commercial users. **

Its core product is called SigenStor, which is a "five-in-one" machine that turns what originally required 5 devices, 5 brands, and 5 installations into **1 device, 1 installation, and 1 APP**:

| Function module | Traditional solution | SigenStor |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Photovoltaic inverter | Purchase separately + install | Built-in |
| Energy storage converter | Purchased separately + installed | Built-in |
| Energy storage battery | Purchased separately + installed (5-100kWh can be stacked and expanded) | Built-in |
| DC fast charging module (electric vehicle charging) | Purchase separately + install | Built-in |
| AI Energy Management System | Purchased separately or without | Built-in (SigenOS) |

The value of this design: **20-30%** cheaper than scattered procurement, and the installation time is shortened by more than **50%**. For the installer, installing one SigenStor is much easier than installing five devices; for users, one APP can manage the energy of the whole house.

**Analogy:** If Apple's integration of MP3, mobile phones, and cameras into iPhone is considered an integrated innovation, Sigen's integration of inverters, energy storage, charging piles, batteries, and energy management into SigenStor is a similar attempt in the field of optical storage.

## Three questions that need to be clarified

Before launching into detailed analysis, let’s break down three easily confused perceptions.

### Question 1: What does 155 times growth mean?

155 times sounds amazing, but break it down:

- 2023 revenue of $58.3 million - the size of a small installer
- Revenue of 1.330 billion in 2024 - large-scale shipments will really begin
- Revenue of 9.001 billion in 2025 - explosive growth

**The truly meaningful growth figure is 5.8x from 2024 to 2025**, not "155x". 155x is the base effect - starting from close to zero, any growth will generate a staggering multiple.

**Counter argument:** Even if the base effect is excluded, the annual growth of 5.8 times is still extremely strong, indicating that the product has indeed hit the market pain point. The question is not whether growth was fast in the past, but whether it can be sustained in the future.

### Question 2: Is 50% gross profit margin normal?

Gross profit margins of peers: Goodwe ~30%, Jinlang Technology ~30%, Enphase ~45%, SolarEdge ~23%, Huawei FusionSolar ~25% (estimated). SIG's **50.1%** is the highest in the industry.

**Reasonable explanation:** Five-in-one integration reduces the cost of intermediate links, but the selling price remains at the total price of the split model; high-end market positioning (Europe, the United States and Australia) users are not price sensitive; there are few early market competitors.

**But be wary:** 50% gross profit margin is extremely rare among Chinese manufacturing companies. High gross margins will inevitably attract competitors. In fact, the average selling price of SigenStor has been declining - from **3.17 yuan/watt-hour** in 2023 to **2.69 yuan/watt-hour** in 2024, and further dropped to **2.22 yuan/watt-hour** in the first three quarters of 2025 (Source: Yema Finance). The selling price is falling, but the gross profit margin is rising - indicating that cost control and scale effects are at work, but the trend of falling selling prices itself is worthy of attention.

### Question 3: How valuable is "Segment No. 1"?

SiGe holds a **28.6%** share in the "stackable distributed optical storage all-in-one" market segment, ranking first in the world. Sounds amazing.

But looking one level up:

| Caliber | SIG share | Main competitors |
|------|:-------:|-----------|
| Stackable optical and storage all-in-one machine (segment within a segment) | **28.6%** | Competitors scattered |
| Distributed optical storage market (wide caliber) | **1.3%** | Huawei 18%, Tesla 6.4% |
| Global energy storage system market | **0.2%** | Dominated by CATL and BYD |

**28.6% is a share of an approximately $3 billion market. Huawei accounts for 18% of the US$30 billion wide-diameter market - 14 times the size of Sigurd. **

This is not to say that Sige is not good. To be No. 1 in a segment with a growth rate of 60% really demonstrates product strength and execution capabilities. But there is an order of magnitude difference between "first in segmentation" and "first in industry".

## An unavoidable new variable: Huawei patent litigation

This series is based on a research manuscript written on April 9, 2026. A big thing happened after that:

**Huawei submitted a patent infringement dispute settlement request to the local Intellectual Property Office on April 3, 2026**, claiming that SIG New Energy was suspected of infringing on two patents - "Inverter bus voltage adjustment method and device" and "Global maximum output power determination method and device". According to reports, Huawei may claim more than 10 potentially infringing patents, which mainly involve SiGe's core products - photovoltaic and storage integrated machines, photovoltaic and storage inverters and industrial and commercial photovoltaic inverters (Source: Jiemian News, 36 Krypton).

The timing of the incident — just before SEG’s IPO — and Xu Yingtong’s 23-year relationship with Huawei make the dispute far more than legal. Chapters 03 and 04 will expand on this in detail.
