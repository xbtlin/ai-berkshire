* Data source: caller book*

** Comparative reference: ** Apple iPhone earns about 50%, which is considered highly dependent by the market. 93% of Sig is extreme.

Mono-dependence means:

1. ** Any factors affecting SiganStor = affecting the whole company.** Technological overlaps, substitution of competing goods, tariff barriers, quality accidents — any one of them could destabilize the whole picture.

2. ** There is no "second curve" buffer.** The company has introduced SigenStack, a business storage energy product, but its revenue contribution is currently minimal. If SigenStor slows down, corporate growth as a whole will stagnate.

3. ** Supply chain risk concentration.** The safety of supply of core parts (icet, IGBT, PCB) is the lifeblood of the company.

** Reverse argument:** Many great companies started as monolithic drivers -- Tesla started with Model S/3 and Apple with iPhone. Mono-dependent is not fatal in itself, and the key is whether the product matrix can be expanded during the window period. Sig's SigenStack and charge-post operations are starting, and if the SiganStor ratio is reduced to below 80% in 2026, this risk will be mitigated.

** The opposite of the counter-argument:** Tesla only expanded from Moder S to Mode 3/Y+ Energy+Auto-Driving. Apple only expanded from iPhone to App Store+ iPad+ Services in six years. Sigg only has four years of history, product matrix expansion takes time, and the competitive environment may not give it much time.
