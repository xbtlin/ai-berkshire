# Where's the growth ceiling?

** Short term (1-2 years): ** Growth may be high. 60% of the stackable one-stop race speed increases, and there is still room for improvement in Sig City. The doubling of income to $1.5 billion in 2026 is a neutral expectation for most analysts.

** Mid-term (3-5 years):** Growth is bound to slow down.

1. ** Percentage growth naturally slowed after the base figure had grown.** From 9 billion to 18 billion is doubled, and from 18 billion to 36 billion is much more difficult.
2. ** Competition has increased the compressed share and the Maori rate.** After the entry of Huaihua, Gudwe and others, the Sig may have declined from 28.6 per cent.
3. ** Discreet track ceiling.** The stackable one-stop market was ~15 billion dollars even in 2027, and Sig could not eat it all.

** Long term (5-10 years): ** Depends on Sig evolving from "a product" to "a platform" -- similar to Tesla expanding from Moder S to the entire energy ecosystem. This requires successful expansion of SiganStack, charge-posting, virtual power plants, etc.
