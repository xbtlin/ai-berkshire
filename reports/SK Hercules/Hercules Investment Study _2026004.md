# 4. Summary of the analysis of dimensions

# 4.1 Business model and moat river (Long-Ping perspective, <unk> )
- HBM has brought real progress: spot auctions, order orders plus advance payments, 2026 capacity sold out, orders placed in 2027, and first storage history in 40 years.
- But the Q1 surge is almost entirely due to the "price" (DRAM ring ratio + 65%, NAND + 75%), and the volume of the shipment is flat or falling - typical top characteristics of the cycle.
- The pricing authority test was not passed: the HBM4 increase of 50-70 per cent was reduced from 62 per cent to 56.4 per cent; and British Weida pulled all the Samsung Lights into the supply list — the “price increase but the share to be lost” was a shortfall in bargaining, not a low-cost pricing authority.
- The moat is a "genuine": the MR-MUF process has been retested from generation to generation, the HBM4 generation Samsung has been able to reach within a quarter; and the HBM4 base nub is given to the power replacement, and the "smart" part of the value chain is beginning to be drawn away by the logical camp.
- Vigilance signal: the company chose to make a record increase of about $29.4 billion in ADRs to NASDAQ at the highest profit point.

# 4.2 Finance and valuation (Buffett perspective, <unk> )
- The inter-cyclical real ROE is about 16 per cent, with 61 per cent currently "Cyber readings"; capital expenditure accounts for 29.3 per cent of revenue over five years, and two thirds of operating cash flows are consumed by equipment.
- **PB cross-check indisputable**: currently 10.45 times vs 0.82-3.73 times between zones 2021-2025; normalizing PE 49 times net 35 trillion calibres and 5-year average 131 times.
- Three scenarios (based on financial_rigor.py tools): optimism (a three-year increase of 25 per cent plus 15 times PE); neutral (standard cycle regression) **-73.2 per cent; pessimism - 94.2 per cent is highly negative.
- Inherent value measurement: Normalization profit method <unk> 638,854 shares, optimistic <unk> 1,104,758 shares, historical PB central <unk> 463,904 shares — currently 120 per cent premium on the most generous calibre.
- Highlights: the strongest balance sheet ever (32.5 trillion net cash and 0.13 debt) was sufficient to cover a standard down-cycle cycle.

# 4.3 Industry and competition (Mange perspective, <unk> )
- A full rise in the price of the forty-one-accelerator: the storage market forecast for 2026 was raised from $551.6 billion to $889.3 billion over a six-month period; AI consumed about 20 per cent of the global DRAM crystal yield.
- ** Market consensus is lagging some 18 months**: Samsung HBM4 has been certified and supplied with prices (no more discounting 30%), led by Google TPU 2026 HBM supply, HBM4 sales have broken by $1 billion; and the share of Light HBM has been levelled.
- Hercules held the British Weidelberg Rubin in about two thirds + the multi-year agreement, leading in 2026-27; however, structural changes in the "single three equals" have occurred and pricing has reached a peak.
- CXMT long-term platinum is underestimated: the price increase cycle has allowed it to earn 70 per cent of its operating profits, the industry is giving blood transfusions to Chinese substitutes, and is concocted with the scripts of the tablet industry in the east of Kyoto; and the threat of 5-10 years.
- Historical observation post: Hercules took the initiative in June 2026 to slow the climb of HBM4, giving priority to the profits of the DDR5 — the same logic that "the cash cow logic overtakes the technological input on the edge" that Samsung fell in the HBM.

#4.4 Risk and Management (Li Sze perspective, <unk> 2)
- Management 4x: Engineer CEO Guo Lu is exercising pragmatic restraint; organizational patience in 2019 for not disbanding the HBM team is real; Kioxia invested approximately 32 times the return on home base; withholding of points at the top of the cycle, and the implementation of the largest capital expenditure plan in the history of 11 trillion won at the top.

- Periodical position: price indicators not at the top, but ** behavioural indicators at the top of the scale ** — 30 months up the road to a record record capital expenditure, cap increases, Kioxia shares at 18 months 50 times.
