# Tencent (0700.HK) Portfolio Fair Value Estimate

**ai-berkshire Tencent portfolio analyst | 2026-04-22**

## 1. Core Conclusion

As of 2025-12-31 (Tencent's latest annual-report basis), Tencent's investment portfolio had a total fair value of about **RMB 1,035.8 billion** (roughly **USD 144 billion**): **RMB 672.7 billion of listed-investment fair value** (about USD 93.5 billion) and **RMB 363.1 billion of unlisted-investment book value** (about USD 50.5 billion). Compared with RMB 1,146.0 billion as of 2025-09-30 (listed 800.8 + unlisted 345.2), the Q4 listed portion pulled back by about RMB 128.1 billion. That equals about **24%** of Tencent's current market cap of roughly HKD 4.67 trillion.

## 4. Major Reduction Timeline (Revised)

### 2021-12 to 2022-03: JD distribution milestone
- Distributed 958 million JD Class A shares (worth USD 16.4 billion / HKD 127.7 billion)
- Stake fell from 17% to **2.3%**
- JD moved from an associate (equity method) to FVTPL

### 2022-11 to 2023-03: Meituan distribution
- Distributed 958 million Meituan Class B shares (worth USD 19.8 billion / HKD 159.4 billion)
- Stake fell from 17% to **about 1.5%**

### 2024-2025: more moderate trimming
- No JD/Meituan-scale distributions
- New investments fell from 300+ per year to 20-40 per year
- In 2025, "disposals exceeded investments by more than USD 1 billion"
- Capital was mainly redirected toward AI capex (RMB 18 billion in 2025, doubling in 2026)

## 5. Prosus Reverse Holding

- Prosus holds about **22.99%** of Tencent as of 2026-04
- Since 2022-06, Prosus has run an open-ended buyback: selling Tencent daily and buying back its own shares
- Cumulatively reduced from 28.8% to 22.99% (a drop of 5.8 percentage points)
- **Tencent's 2025 buybacks of HKD 80 billion plus dividends of HKD 41 billion offset the selling pressure from Prosus**
