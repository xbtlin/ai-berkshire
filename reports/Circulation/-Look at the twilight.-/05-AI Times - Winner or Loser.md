# Eleven, a perspective on restraint

The most accurate summary of the AI path of the tweak is actually two words: ** Restraint**

- Not involved in the "arms race" -- Capex 110-1300 billion, clearly below bytes.
- No dead mix-- Yuanbao, get it straight to DeepSeek.

This restraint is not a lack of progress, but a recognition of its endowment**.

What is the gift? 1.4 billion micro-user, 14 games of age, the world's largest distribution, the deepest payment scene in China, and the public signage of eight years of sedimentation.** Micro-letter and QQQ are the world's top C-end products - the ability to make C-ends.**
