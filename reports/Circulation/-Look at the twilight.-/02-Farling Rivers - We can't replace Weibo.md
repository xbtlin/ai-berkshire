# A reverse mind experiment

And then with a reverse, end of the show:

** If AGI really comes, will the micro-mail disappear?**

And on the other hand, may it be more enlightening -- when AGI comes along, ** will the micro-trust be bypassed or more dependent**?

AGI ** can only run on "over and above" and not "over and around" ** -- because:

1. AI assistants must send messages to the receiving place (= micromail);
2. AI assistants must pay for the payment channel (= 60% for micro-mail payments and 36% for payment treasures);
3. AI assistants must access micro-programme ecology (= 4.3 million businesses here) to reach services;
4. AI assistants are required to see the user ' s preferences and to see where the user is (= 1.4 billion in micromail).

The stronger AGI, the deeper the reliance on entry and compliance; and the more the entry and compliance**, which is just as unique as the `Micro-Secret', is.

If this reasoning were to be established, **AGI would not kill MSG, but would make it more valuable**.

But to be honest with this logic, the ** boundary conditions** -- it assumes that AI Agent still has the bottom of the "App+Smartphone" ecology. If the AI is actually in its original interactive form, the chain is bypassed. It lists at least three potential paths:

- ** New hardware replaced mobile phones**: AI glasses, AI Pin, brain interfaces etc. to remove the "open App" action and re-create the entry logic;
- **OS Level Agent + General Payment Agreement**: ASI assistants at the operating system level call directly to API, in conjunction with stable currency, central bank digital currency or more open payment agreements, bypassing the micro-trust payment/payment of the treasures;
- ** Trans-App Relationship Agreement**: Similar interconnection regulation drives the chain of relationships to be forced to open or partially relocated by new protocols (e.g. decentralised IM for end-to-end encryption).

The probability of these three paths occurring in the next 10 years is not high (combined scale of 10-15%), but ** does exist. It is the farthest and most alarming tail risk of the micro-trust moat -- and why "AGI makes it worth more."
