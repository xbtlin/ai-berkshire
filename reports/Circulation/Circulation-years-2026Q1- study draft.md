**May 13, 2026 | Stock price HK$462.60 | Market value ~ HK$418 billion**

---

## 1. Conclusion in one sentence

> Revenue is slightly lower than expected but profit is slightly higher than expected. The core business's ability to print money continues to increase (FCF +20%, gross profit margin 56.7%). Advertising +20% is the biggest highlight. The real change is: management is exchanging deterministic shareholder returns (55% reduction in buybacks) for uncertain AI bets (investment doubled to 36 billion+), while Tencent is clearly lagging behind in the AI ​​application competition (Yuanbao MAU is only 57 million vs. Doubao 340 million). The investment thesis remains unchanged, but the conversion efficiency of AI investment needs to be closely tracked.

---

## 2. The three most important changes in this issue

**1. Advertising business accelerated to +20%, becoming the number one growth engine**
Advertising revenue was 38.2 billion yuan, and the growth rate accelerated from 17% in Q4 to 20%, outperforming the industry average (~10%) and all major competitors. The core driver is not "spending more money to buy volume", but the efficiency improvement brought about by AI upgraded advertising models - advertisers use less money to get better conversion rates, so they are willing to invest more budget. This is evidence that the moat is widening.

**2. Big shift in capital allocation priorities: from shareholder returns to AI armament**
Q1 repurchases were only HK$7.6 billion (17.1 billion in the same period last year), with an annualized rate of about 30 billion vs. 80 billion last year. At the same time, capital expenditure is 31.9 billion (+39%), and the full-year AI investment will exceed 36 billion. Management made it clear that "repurchases are being reduced to fund AI investment." This is a clear signal: Tencent is exchanging certainty for uncertainty.

**3. AI application competition lags behind and the model level is catching up**
Yuanbao’s MAU is only 57 million, while Doubao’s MAU is 340 million (a difference of 6 times). However, Hunyuan Hy3 has reached the top position in OpenRouter (although there are limited promotion factors), and its model capabilities have entered the forefront of the world. Management created a new caliber of "operating profit +17% after excluding new AI products" - this not only shows that the core business is strong, but also implies that AI investment is dragging down the report.

## 5. Four-dimensional depth discovery

### 5.1 Nature of business

1. **Advertising is the first battlefield for AI monetization** - AI upgrade recommendation model increases advertising ARPU (advertising +20% and users only +2%). This is "efficiency-driven price increase" rather than "burning money to buy volume"
2. **Video account duration +20%**, mini programs have 800 million daily users, WeChat has evolved from a "chat tool" to a "life operating system", and its irreplaceability is increasing
3. **Game revenue actually increased by more than ten%**, which is much higher than the +6% reported in the report. The Spring Festival time difference is noise not a signal
4. **Social Networks -2% is the only sign of deterioration** - Music/Video paid membership growth is weak
5. **Gross profit margin continues to expand at 56.7%** - the quality of income is improving, the proportion of high-gross profit advertising increases + cost efficiency improves

### 5.2 Financial Quality

1. **Operating cash flow is 76.9 billion, cash flow/profit = 113%** - excellent profit quality
2. **GAAP vs Non-GAAP gap narrowed to ~17% (22% last year)** - "moisture content" reduced
3. **Operating profit +17% after excluding new AI products** - The core business’ money printing capabilities are accelerating
4. **Net cash 90.2 billion (+63%)** – extremely healthy balance sheet
5. **Repurchases reduced by 55%** - from annualized 80 billion to ~30 billion Hong Kong dollars, EPS growth will be structurally dragged down

### 5.3 Competitive Landscape

1. **Advertising +20% outperforms the industry and all opponents** - but Byte’s absolute scale is still 3 times that of Tencent
2. **Video account e-commerce GMV~800 billion, growth rate 50%+** - return rate only 7% (vs. Douyin 30-50%), higher quality
3. **Cloud services +22% are the best in recent years** - but Alibaba Cloud +36% and share 33% are far ahead
4. **AI application is seriously lagging behind**: Yuanbao has 57 million MAUs vs. Doubao 340 million (a difference of 6 times). It has 1.4 billion WeChat users but has extremely low penetration rate.
5. **Hunyuan Hy3 is among the top three models** - but the top OpenRouter part is limited and free of promotions

### 5.4 Risk Signals

1. **Management created a "shadow income statement"** - "+17% after excluding new AI products" is a new non-standard indicator with obvious intention to guide expectations.
2. **Yuanbao data is avoided** - the management is unwilling to give specific DAU, and vaguely uses "early stage" to pass it off
3. **The promise fulfillment rate is about 67%** - Hunyuan 3.0 was released on time (✅), but the deployment progress of Yuanbao was lower than expected (❌)
4. **The sudden drop in buybacks is the biggest hidden signal**——Capital allocation shifts from "shareholder returns" to "AI arms race"
5. **The U.S. White House’s review of Tencent’s overseas gaming investments is pending**

## 6. Intersection and contradiction of four perspectives

### Consensus point (highest credibility)
- Core business (advertising + gaming + payment) money printing capabilities continue to increase
- Excellent free cash flow quality and sufficient net cash
- AI application competition is indeed lagging behind
- Repurchase reduction is a price investors must accept

### Contradictions (the most valuable analysis)
- **Business essence says "AI investment is pragmatic and walks on two legs"** vs **Risk signals say "stimulating demand at full speed is an arms race mentality"** - Who is right? They may all be right: the management's attitude is indeed more restrained than that of its peers (Ma Huateng "can't just grab territory"), but the execution level is still burning money quickly.
- **The nature of business says that "management's product intuition is good"** vs **Risk signals say that "Hunyuan reaches the top by promotion"** - Both make sense: Yuanbao's product design embedded in WeChat is indeed smart, but Hunyuan's user volume data is really not good-looking
- **Financial quality says "+17% excluding AI is healthy"** vs **Risk signals say "This is a shadow income statement created by management"** - The number itself is fine, but the way it is presented shows that management knows the market will be worried about slowing profit growth

## 7. Conclusion

**1. Exceeded expectations/Meeted expectations/Below expectations? **
Structurally exceeded expectations. Revenue was slightly lower (-1.5%), but profit slightly exceeded expectations. More importantly, profit quality (FCF+20%) and core business efficiency (gross profit margin +0.9ppt, advertising +20%) both exceeded expectations.

**2. Investment Thesis Impact: Strengthening**
The thesis on Tencent as "China's digital economic infrastructure" is strengthened. The moat is widening (advertising acceleration, video account penetration, small program closed loop), and profitability is improving.

**3. The next catalyst**
- Short term: Q2 game revenue (delayed release during the Spring Festival) + performance of "Glory of Kings"
- Mid-term: Can Yuanbao MAU exceed 100 million within the year?
- Long-term: ROI of AI investment - continuous improvement in advertising efficiency + cloud AI revenue growth

**4. Operation suggestions**
- Holders: continue to hold. The core business has strengthened and the valuation is reasonable (PE 16.6x, below the historical center and close to the 52-week low)
- On the sidelines: HKD 462 is close to the 52-week low (454), with an improved margin of safety. If Q2 game revenue increases significantly due to deferred releases, it may be a short-term catalyst
- Risk warning: The conversion efficiency of AI investment needs to be verified in 2-3 quarters, and the reduction of repurchases will have a structural drag on EPS growth.
