The seven risks listed above are listed. But risk analysis is not a deterrent -- it is a matter of knowing what you're buying.

** Most of the above risks can be absorbed if the following conditions are established:**

1. **AI input began to yield a visible return in 2027.** MEU breakthroughs 200 million, AI advertising revenue contribution of more than 30%, blends become the country ' s Top 3 big model - any signal is delivered, the market revalues AI input.

2. **CFIUS closed the case with "risk mitigation" instead of "forced stripping".** As long as Riot Gomes remains in hand, the growth narrative of the overseas game is complete.

3. ** Repurchase returned to HK$150-$20 billion per quarter in the second half of 2026.** Management said "This is a good time" and if words and deeds are consistent, repurchases are re-applied as anchors for valuation.

4. ** Central American relations maintain the "competitive but manageable" tone.** Do not move towards full decoupling.

** If you believe that these four conditions are likely to be established, the Hong Kong dollar is paying a high "fear premium" for the risks. If you're not sure, it's a safer option to lower the position or build the warehouse in lots.**
