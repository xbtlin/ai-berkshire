# Panoramic review of Tencent’s investment empire (2010-2026)

**ai-berkshire Tencent Investment Layout Specialist | 2026-04-22**

## 1. Core conclusion

Tencent's investment territory** spans 15 years, 800+ companies, covering China, the United States, Europe and Southeast Asia**. Starting from defensive investment after the "3Q War" in 2011, to global strategic expansion (Riot, Supercell, Epic, Sea, Snap) from 2015 to 2020, to contraction and focus under antitrust pressure after 2021, it finally evolved into the "dividend reduction + shareholder return" model from 2024 to 2026**. As of the end of 2025, the fair value of the **investment portfolio is approximately RMB 1.04 trillion** (listed RMB 672.7 billion + unlisted RMB 363.1 billion), accounting for approximately 24-29% of the total market value. The narrative changes from "Tencent Inc." to "China's version of Berkshire".

## 2. Historical evolution of Tencent Investment Department

### Phase One: Defensive Investment (2010-2013)
- After the 2010-11 "3Q War", Ma Huateng announced an "open" shift
- 2011 Established "Tencent Industry Win-Win Fund" (5 billion) + James Mitchell served as CSO
- No more "one family takes it all", "half life is given to partners"
- **Milestone**: 2014-03 Exchange e-commerce assets + US$215 million for 15% of JD.com

### Phase 2: Strategic Ecological Investment (2014-2017)
- Chip Lau + James Mitchell dual-core driver
- Minority equity + traffic empowerment + WeChat entrance
- **Milestones**:
  - 2014 Meituan-Dianping merger continues to increase
  - 2015 Didi-Kuaidi merger
  - **2015 Riot Games 100% wholly owned** (cumulative USD 2.3 billion)
  - **2016 Supercell US$8.46 billion acquisition 84.3%** (jointly with Prosus/Kingsoft)
  - 2016 Pinduoduo Series B investment (later increased to 14-17%)

### Phase 3: Globalization + Financialization (2018-2020)
- Annual investment 150-200+ projects
- Tesla 5%（2017）
- Snap 12%
- Spotify 9.1% (swap with Tencent Music)
- **Epic Games ups the ante to ~40%** (2020)
- Invested in 800+ companies in 2019

### Phase 4: Contraction Period (2021-2023)
- Alibaba’s 18.2 billion fine warning
- **2021-12: Dividend reduction in JD.com** (USD 16.4 billion, 17% → 2.3%)
- 2022-01: Reduce holdings in Sea Limited (USD 3 billion)
- **2022-11: Dividend reduction in Meituan** (USD 19.8 billion, 17% → 1.5%)
- New investment scale dropped by 70% year-on-year

### Phase Five: Dividend Reduction + Focus on AI (2024-2026)
- 2024 buyback **HK$112 billion** (history high)
- 2025 buyback 80 billion + dividend 41 billion = **121 billion HKD shareholder return**
- 2025 final dividend increased by 18% to HKD 5.30 per share
- New investment directions:
  - Overseas game mergers and acquisitions (new in Techland 2024)
  - **AI New Focus**: 2025-11 Jointly led by IDG to invest USD 600 million in Moonshot (Kimi)
  - Investment in China is basically frozen
- **2026-03 New Risk**: The Trump administration in the United States initiates a CFIUS review of Tencent in Epic/Riot/Supercell

## 6. Three important turning points in investment strategy

### Turning point one: 2011 "3Q War" → Open Ecosystem
- Stop doing all business
- Institutionalization of the Investment Department

### Turning point two: Antitrust in 2021 → Focus on contraction
- Alibaba’s 18.2 billion fine warning
- Actively reduce holdings in JD.com, Sea, and Meituan
- New investment dropped by 60%+ annually

### Turning point three: 2024-2026 dividend reduction → shareholder friendly
- From "holding ecology" to "releasing value"
- 2024 buyback 112 billion + 2025 121 billion shareholder return
- **"China Berkshire" Narrative**
