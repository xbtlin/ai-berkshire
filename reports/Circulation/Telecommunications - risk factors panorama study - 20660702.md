# The greatest structural advantage of the tweaking

The social chain of 1402 million MAU is not replicable. In the migration from PC to mobile, the chain allows the tether to be transformed from QQ to Q. In the AI era, the value of the platform on which the chain is carried will not disappear due to a change in the technical paradigm. # Three risks that deserve attention

1. **CFIUS review (high risk):** If the Riot Gomes is forcibly removed, the Circumvention will lose two major global IPs, the Heroes Alliance and the Valorant. The case has been delayed by five years + and may be concluded in the second half of 2026. This is a "low probability, high impact" event that will directly change the pattern of international game business once it occurs.

2. **AI competition lags behind (high risk):** Mabau only 1/6 of the bean bag, 7% of the cloud-end API market share, and capital expenditure only a third-to-fourth of bytes. The hedge strategy of the Quest (investment in all head AI companies plus the 1.4 billion MAU portal) partially mitigates risks, but if AI becomes the next generation computing platform, its own capacity will likely lead to a downward shift in the value chain.

3. ** Chip supply chain (high risk):** United States sanctions continue to tighten, capital expenditure has fallen short of expectations in 2025, overseas leasing programmes face policy uncertainty and self-research chips cannot yet replace training in GPS. This is the biggest external bottleneck that constrains the pace of development of AI and is not under the control of the RTL itself.

# The other side of the risk

It is worth noting that the tsong has shown remarkable resilience in several dimensions:
- 14 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth in advertising revenue, which has been expanding in reverse in weak consumption
- 42% growth in game revenue from 2022-2025, internationalization of Piti to 32%, effective hedge against domestic risk
- The worst period of domestic regulation has passed, and the number has returned to its highest in almost seven years.
- Net profits of Non-IFRS increased robustly by $259.6 billion, with profits of much better quality than major competitors
- South is a record influx of funds, PE 15 times vs 30 times the price of US stock.
