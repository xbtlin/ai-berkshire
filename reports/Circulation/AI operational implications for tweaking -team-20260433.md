#8. Summary

The biggest asset of the AI era** is not the hybrid model, but the "Build portal + compliance network" of 1.4 billion micro-letter users**. When the universal model is homogenized, the real scarcity is the "distribution + payment + compliance" package -- which is unique to us. The "AI in All" strategy of the telecommunications management is the best solution that is in keeping with its own endowment: a refusal to enter the arms race, a pragmatic access to DeepSeek, and a multiplier of the AI into existing operations.

** The severe fragmentation of the business** was the most important finding of the study: the game is built on AI to deepen the moat, advertising is the fastest-moving dividend curve, reading is the biggest beneficiary of the seriously underestimated AI; but the tungsten cloud has been structurally cut off, the music is exposed to the risk of copyright scarcity being diluted, video numbers cannot be held up in algorithmic competition. This differentiation requires investors** to evaluate the tungsten as an "AI concept unit" and to decomposition as "AI winner's block (game + ad + entry + paper) + AI loser block (coon + music + video)"**.

From an investment perspective, tweaking is consistent with Lee Szee's "Long-term certainty + security margin" framework: the core business model has a 10-year survival probability of 85 per cent +, AI Net Positive expectations + 50 per cent valuation (5 years), and the current PE 15-18x is conservative in pricing AI risks and has limited access to room for downside risks.** tweaking is not the biggest winner in the AI era, but may be the most robust winner** — which is itself a quality benchmark consistent with Buffet/Li Sze investment philosophy.

> **ning-a-long ending**: Buying shares is buying companies. The most valuable feature displayed in the A.I. era is not a technological lead, but rather the restraint and determination of **Pony** - the place where the money is burned (micro-economy au Auction), the place where the money is set, the place where the money is set free (mixed access to DeepSeek), the place where the hold is held (a long-term strategy of games). This "A.A.A." pragmatism is the most important ability to cross the AI bubble cycle.

#9. Methodology Redisk

The study is a "theme-specific" application of the four-play parallel investment framework -- not a full corporate valuation analysis, but a focus on the theme "AI impact." Several tests are available:

1. ** Value of thematic-specific studies**: 4 analysts cut from different business plates and eventually aggregated the core insight of "serious fragmentation of business" - a conclusion that cannot be derived from a single panorama report
2. **AI dimensions require "operational particle size"**: the message is lost as an AI unit and must be individually evaluated for each piece of game/advertisement/cloud/music
**AI influences AI capabilities**: TsengAI technical capabilities (mixed) are not the first in the country, but TsengAI operations may have benefited the highest of the BATs - because it has the strongest application scenes
4. ** Data sources are graded**: 3 of 4 analysts have real data for 2025-2026 (fiscal plus third parties) and 1 is only known publicly.
