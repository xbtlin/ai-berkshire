1. **Text is mandatory only at 5% shareholding** <5% in position (e.g. Roblox, Reddit, AI startup) low confidence
2. ** Unlisted valuations are not naturally transparent**: single-stock ratios are never made public, inter-zone estimates
3. ** Round valuations <unk> fair value**: First-market valuations often include priority rights to liquidation, bet clauses, with a book-to-real real real real realized value gap of 30-50 per cent
4. ** bytes beat stock holding to estimate**: the exact figures are available only for Treasury and byte shareholder rosters
** Valuation of education **: no active market after double-down policy and it is not clear whether the telecommunications book is impaired
6. ** Equity fluctuations**: The data in this table are 2026-05-01 hours, with monthly fluctuations of ~10 per cent
