## A1: Core paper

I bought Tencent Holdings at **496 HKD** because:

1. **The essence of this business is**: a super platform with WeChat’s 1.385 billion MAU social relationship chain as the core and monetized through four major sectors: games (32%), financial technology and corporate services (31%), advertising (19%), and social networks (17%). I understand its way of making money - users' social relationship chains lock traffic, and then extract value through multiple channels such as in-game purchases, payment commissions, advertising displays, and cloud services.

2. **Its moat is**: WeChat’s 1.385 billion MAU social network effect + extremely high switching costs (social relationship chains cannot be migrated), the moat is **stable but wide** - video accounts (DAU 400 million+) and the mini-program ecology continue to deepen the moat, but competition for AI entrances (Yuanbao vs. Doubao) brings uncertainty.

3. **Management**: Ma Huateng + Liu Chiping are the best technology management combination in China. The reason why they are trustworthy is that the "self-revolution" from QQ to WeChat proves strategic judgment. The annual repurchase of HK$80-112 billion + dividend of 41 billion proves the shareholder-friendly capital allocation ability. The AI capital expenditure of 79.2 billion proves the forward-looking investment courage.

4. **The current price is equivalent to about 25% off the intrinsic value** (DCF intrinsic value ~ HK$658). The margin of safety comes from: PE 17.6x, a 35% discount to the 10-year median of 27x; gross profit margin of 56%, a record high; net cash of 107.1 billion provides downside protection.

5. **Even if I am wrong, the downside risk is controllable** because: net cash of 107.1 billion + annual FCF of 182.6 billion means that the company is not short of money; repurchases continue to write off equity to support the stock price; the diversified income structure makes the decline of a single business not fatal; even based on the pessimistic assumption (zero growth + 14x PE), the stock price is still supported by ~HK$455 (32.5×14).
