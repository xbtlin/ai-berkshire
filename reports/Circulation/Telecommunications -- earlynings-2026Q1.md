# Tencent Q1 financial report: Profit exceeded expectations, but the repurchase price of 80 billion was reduced to 30 billion. Where did the money go?

**One sentence conclusion: The core business is stronger than it looks, but management is betting shareholders' money on AI. **

Tencent’s 2026 first quarter report was released today. Revenue was 196.5 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 9%, slightly lower than the market consensus of 199.4 billion yuan (Bloomberg consensus estimate). But there was a surprise on the profit side - adjusted net profit was 67.9 billion yuan, an increase of 11%, exceeding consensus expectations of 67.8 billion yuan. Free cash flow was 56.7 billion, a year-on-year increase of 20%.

Behind the numbers, three changes deserve attention.

---

## Advertising has become the most powerful growth engine

Looking at different businesses, the most eye-catching thing is not the games, but the advertising.

| Business | Revenue | Year-on-year | Last quarter growth rate |
|------|------|------|-----------|
| Value-added services | 96.1 billion | +4% | +14% |
| Advertising | 38.2 billion | **+20%** | +17% |
| Fintech and corporate services | 59.9 billion | +9% | +8% |

Advertising revenue was 38.2 billion, and the growth rate accelerated from 17% in the previous quarter to 20%. It accelerated for two consecutive quarters, ranking first among the three major sectors.

What is more critical is the quality of growth. The number of WeChat users only increased by 2%, but advertising revenue increased by 20% - the value of advertising per user increased significantly. The reason behind this is that AI has upgraded the advertising model, improved conversion efficiency, and advertisers can get better results with less money, so they are willing to increase their budget.

Looking at the industry, the advertising growth rate of the entire industry is about 10% (data from China Daily), and Tencent outperformed it by double. This is an efficiency dividend driven by technology, not earned by burning money.

---

## Buybacks plummet, money turns to AI

The most noteworthy signal in this financial report is hidden in the capital allocation.

| Indicators | 2026Q1 | 2025Q1 | Year-on-year change |
|------|--------|--------|---------|
| Repurchase amount | ~HK$7.6 billion | ~HK$17.1 billion | **-55%** |
| Capital expenditures | ~31.9 billion | ~23 billion | **+39%** |

Last year, Tencent’s annual repurchase scale was approximately HK$80 billion, making it the largest repurchase machine in Hong Kong stocks. In the first quarter of this year, buybacks plummeted to HK$7.6 billion, an annualized rate of only about HK$30 billion.

The money went to AI. Management made it clear at the 2025 annual report conference call that AI-related investment will exceed 36 billion yuan in 2026, more than double last year’s 18 billion yuan. Almost all the money saved by the reduction of buybacks will be transferred to AI infrastructure.

The essence of this choice is: trade deterministic shareholder returns for uncertain technical bets. **

For holders, this means that the important forces that have supported the stock price over the past two years are weakening. Has management given any guidance on the pace of buybacks in the second half of the year? Not yet.

---

## AI: two sides of the same coin

The impact of AI on Tencent needs to be viewed at two levels, which are completely different in nature.

**Level 1: AI empowers existing businesses—already making money. **

The 20% increase in advertising revenue is a direct result of AI empowerment. AI has upgraded the delivery model and improved conversion efficiency. In addition, AI-related revenue in cloud services grew by 22%. Management disclosed an intriguing figure: after excluding investment in new AI products, operating profit increased by 17% and profit margin was 43%. The money printing capacity of the core business is accelerating.

**Second level: AI as an independent product - still burning money. **

Yuanbao’s monthly active users are only 57 million. Compare Doubao with 340 million (a difference of 6 times) and Tongyi Qianwen with 170 million. There are 1.4 billion WeChat users, but the penetration rate is less than 1%. Management avoided daily activity data during the conference call and was only willing to mention monthly activity.

There are bright spots in the model layer: Hunyuan Hy3 performed well in third-party platform evaluations after its release. However, it should be noted that the top ranking is partly due to the usage caused by the limited-time free promotion (36Kr report), and is not purely product power.

Looking at the two levels together: **AI is already helping Tencent make more money, but it has not yet helped Tencent win a new battlefield. ** The investment of 36 billion yuan has already seen returns on the first layer, but the second layer is still burning.

---

## Several details that cannot be ignored

**The money you make is real money. ** Operating cash flow is 76.9 billion, which is 113% of profit. Not only was it earned, but it was recovered. The profit adjustment range narrowed from 22% last year to 17%, and the water content is decreasing. Net cash was 90.2 billion, a year-on-year increase of 63%.

**The game's "false slowdown". ** Domestic game revenue was 45.4 billion, an increase of 6%, but the actual game revenue growth rate reached more than ten percentage points. The difference comes from accounting revenue deferral—virtual items purchased by players during the Spring Festival require revenue to be recognized in installments over the period of use. This part will be released in Q2, which is not bad news.

**Video account ecological acceleration. ** User duration increased by 20%, and mini program daily activity exceeded 800 million. The annual GMV of video account e-commerce is expected to exceed 800 billion, with a growth rate of more than 50%, and a return rate of only 7% - far lower than the average level of 30-50% in the live broadcast e-commerce industry.

**Social networks declined slightly. ** Revenue was 31.9 billion, down 2% year-on-year. Music and video paid membership growth was weak. This is one of the few business lines with negative growth. However, the social relationship chain itself has not been damaged. This is more a matter of competition in content payment.

## Risk list

- The intensity of buybacks has weakened, and its role in supporting the stock price has declined.
- There is a huge gap between AI applications and top players, and the catching-up window is narrowing
- The return schedule for the 36 billion AI investment is unclear
- U.S. review of Tencent’s overseas gaming investments pending
- Management is highly transparent in its core business, but tends to beautify its AI narrative.

---

## Valuation: Not expensive, but you have to think clearly about why it is cheap

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Share price | HK$462.60 (near 52-week low of 454) |
| Price-to-earnings ratio (TTM) | 16.6 times |
| Net cash | 90.2 billion yuan |
| P/E ratio after net cash | About 14 times |
| Free cash flow yield | About 5.4% |
| Analyst consensus target price | HK$726 (Bloomberg, +57%) |

A company with free cash flow growth of 20% and net cash of nearly 100 billion has a price-to-earnings ratio of 14 times after deducting cash. The price level is not expensive.

But there is a reason behind the cheapness: the market is worried that AI investment will eat up profit growth and the reduction of buybacks will weaken the support of stock prices. These concerns are not unfounded - management is indeed making a large investment with an uncertain payback period.

---

## So what?

This earnings report means different things to different investors:

**If you value cash flow and shareholder returns** - The signals this quarter are on the negative side. If buybacks are reduced by 55% and AI investment is doubled, short-term profit growth will be suppressed.

**If you value business competitiveness and long-term value** - the signal is positive. Advertising is accelerating, gross profit margin is expanding, the video account ecosystem is taking shape, and the moat of the core business is widening. Buying for HKD 462 is equivalent to giving away an AI option.

**Next signal to track**: In the Q2 financial report (1) whether game revenue has increased significantly due to deferred release; (2) whether Yuanbao MAU can exceed 100 million within the year; (3) whether the repurchase rhythm has picked up.
