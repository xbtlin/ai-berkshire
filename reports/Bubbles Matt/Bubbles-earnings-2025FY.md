As of April 3, the cumulative buyback of $11.12 million was made.

# 4.5 The price of hidden money has fallen sharply - demand or supply?

The hidden price of the blind box reportedly fell sharply by 87% (from $4,465 to $565), and the monthly capacity was expanded to 50 million. This may reflect: (1) the dilution of scarcity by oversupply; and (2) the retreat of speculative demand.

#6.3 Core conclusions

**1. Over-expected/complyed/under-expected?**

** Income and profits were significantly higher than expected, but the 2026 growth rate was less than market expectations.** This is a "turn back to perfection, to the question of going forward." 37.1 billion revenues are slightly lower than the market forecast of 38 billion, but net profits of 12.8 billion and net interest rates of 35 per cent are far above expectations.

**2. Impact on investment papers**

The core paper, Global IP Operating Platform, has been fully validated. But it needs to add a risk variable: **LABUBU heat cycle.** The increase in concentration from 23% to 38%, combined with the sudden fall in hidden money prices and excess capacity signals, makes the probability of a "heat retreat" unmindable. 20% acceleration guidelines mean that management itself is also paving for a "decompression".

**3. Next catalyst**

- ** Short term (1-2 quarters): ** 2026Q1 operational data, confirming 20% "breathing" or "slate"
- ** Mid-term (3-6 months):** Labubu 4.0 launch effect, expansion of the United States gate to 100+ households
- ** Potential negative catalyst:** LABUBU hot spots, China-US trade friction escalation, stock turnover continues to deteriorate

**4. Recommendations for warehousing/holding/reducing**

** Hold, awaiting confirmation of signal.**

Reason:
- ** Not expensive:** PE 13.1x corresponds to an increase of even 20%, PEG only 0.66, which is low in consumer goods
- ** But not in a position to be radical:** LABUBU concentration, speed change, triple risk of deterioration in inventory turnover has not been resolved
- **Best loading points: ** (1) data showing a faster rate of increase than 20% of the guidelines; (2) LABUBU 4.0 successful relay; (3) non-LABUBI new explosion
- **Lowing signal: ** (1) stock turnover continues to deteriorate to 150 days +; (2) the growth rate of overseas business with a negative impact; (3) the rate of repurchase by members has declined significantly

** The recent change in attitude in Yongping is noteworthy:** On March 30, the **Yongping publicly stated "Return the story of Bubble Matt" and endorsed its IP hatching closed-ring system and management capacity. But he also said earlier that "it is hard to understand why people need this thing" – a paradox of "watching the company but not understanding the demand" – is precisely the core tension of Bubble Matt’s investment paper.
