## 1. Company profile and latest developments

Bubble Mart International Group (9992.HK) is the world's leading trendy toy company. It was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in December 2020. It has grown from a single-category Chinese blind box company to a global IP operation platform covering multiple categories such as plush, figures, building blocks, and home furnishings.

**Major events since 2026:**

- **March 19**: LABUBU officially announced that it will become the protagonist of the movie, cooperating with Sony Pictures, directed by "Paddington Bear" director Paul King, and extending its IP to film and television content
- **March 25**: Released full-year results for 2025, with revenue of 37.12 billion (+185%) and adjusted net profit of 13.08 billion (+285%). Management has given conservative guidance of "growth rate not less than 20%" in 2026
- **March 25-28**: The stock price plummeted by more than 30% in two days, and the market value evaporated by nearly 100 billion. The company repurchased approximately HK$1.4 billion within 6 trading days
- **Mid-April**: Duan Yongping publicly confirmed the purchase, and then liquidated all China Shenhua stocks he had held for more than 15 years and exchanged them for Bubble Mart.
- **Mid-May**: Released 2026 Q1 operating data, overall revenue increased by 75%-80% year-on-year, far exceeding the conservative guidance of 20%
- **Intensive launch of new IPs**: Since the beginning of the year, three new IPs have been officially announced: "merodi after school", "SUPER TUTU" and "Key A"

## 3. The essence of business model and moat

The essence of Bubble Mart is not "selling toys", but an **IP operation platform** - "a record company in the trendy toy industry". Signed contracts with 200+ artists around the world and is responsible for IP commercialization. A plush that costs 15 yuan sells for 99 yuan, and consumers pay a 6 times premium for the "cuteness".

The fourth dimension of the moat:
1. **Brand Mind**: Bubble Mart = trendy toy, this category’s synonymous status is difficult to replicate
2. **Upstream supply**: Sign a large number of top trendy artists around the world to form an "artist ecology"
3. **Global channels**: 630 directly operated stores + 2637 robot stores, online + offline DTC closed loop
4. **Management Team**: Wang Ning, 39 years old, founder of the three-in-one "product intuition + business rationality + global vision"

Note: The moat is more of a "systemic capability" than a structural monopoly. The entry barrier for the trendy toy industry is not high.

## 7. Risks and Catalysts

### Core Risks

1. **IP life cycle**: LABUBU’s domestic popularity has obviously subsided, and second-hand prices have collapsed by 80%+
2. **IP Concentration**: 38.1% is too high. If the fever is accelerating and the new IP is not picked up, there will be a growth gap.
3. **Management reduction**: Collective reduction of over HK$1.7 billion in October 2024
4. **Tariffs and Geography**: Price increases in the US market + supply chain policy uncertainty
5. **Inventories soared by 259%**: Historically, out-of-control consumer goods inventories were a precursor to collapse.

### Potential Catalyst

1. **Q2/half-year report exceeded expectations**: 75-80% of Q1 far exceeded guidance. If it continues, it is expected to promote valuation restoration.
2. **LABUBU Movie**: Sony Pictures cooperates to advance and open up long-term content space
3. **Xingxingren and other new IP verification**: Every successful new IP is evidence that "platform capabilities can be replicated"
4. **Duan Yongping Effect**: Clearance of Shenhua’s full-stock Bubble Mart, arousing widespread market attention
5. **Continuous large-amount repurchases**: exceeded HK$1.4 billion during the year, reflecting management confidence

---

## 8. Investment Conclusion

**Comprehensive judgment**: Bubble Mart's current valuation (12-15x PE) has been significantly adjusted back to a reasonably low range, and the actual Q1 growth rate of 75-80% far exceeds conservative guidance. For investors who can withstand the fluctuations in the IP cycle, HK$152.50 provides a better risk-reward ratio.

The core bet is "whether Bubble Mart can evolve from a hit-driven consumer goods company to a true IP platform." The filmization of LABUBU, the rise of Star People, and the continued expansion of the global store network are all positive evidences that are accumulating. However, LABUBU’s domestic cooling, management’s reduction of holdings, and inventory surge are also risk signals that cannot be ignored.

**The probability of evergreen is about 30-40%**, depending on whether it can successfully "de-LABUBU" and add narrative depth in the next 3-5 years.
