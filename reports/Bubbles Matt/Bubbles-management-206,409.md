# 2.1 Strategic vision for IP operations

Wang Ning’s understanding of the IP industry is at the top of China’s consumer goods founders.

**1) Positioning of the "Record Company" model (2016 - 2017)**
Long before the boom, Wang Ning had named Bubble Ma a "stergress of artists" instead of a blind box retailer. This position determined that the company's core competitiveness was signed and operated on IP, not on channels. It proved to be a very forward-looking judgement — when the blind box concept dropped, Bubble Matt was able to survive the 2022 valley with IP assets.

**2 Decision-making on signing of the LABU (2019)**
The decision to sign the book of the Sanctuary of Hong Kong artist, Laboubu IP, was not visible at the time. The beauty style of the labubu is very different from that of traditional Molly, which means that Wang Ning has the aesthetic tolerance to jump out of comfort zones.

**3 (Victo-feathers leaping from blind boxes to wool (2022)**
The low-vale division of the IP operation in 2022 led to a series of gel fur. The wool class rose from zero to $18.7 billion in 2025, becoming the first class of products, exceeding hand-made. The timing and implementation of the switch was impressive.

**4) Data driver + horse racing**
