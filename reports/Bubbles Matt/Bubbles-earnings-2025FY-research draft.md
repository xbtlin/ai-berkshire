**The 13 billion that Bubble Mart will earn in 2025 is genuine real money (operating cash flow/net profit = 1.007), but 2026Q1 revealed a structural turning point: domestic doubling growth (+100%) has supported the situation, but overseas has plummeted from three digits to double digits (Asia-Pacific only +25%). ## 2. The three most important changes in this issue

### Change 1: Overseas growth rate cliff—from 292% to 25-60%

In 2025, the overseas growth rate will be 292%, in 2026Q1 the Asia-Pacific region will only be 25-30%, and the Americas will be 55-60%. This cannot be fully explained by the "high base effect" - COO Shi De personally admitted that "a large number of new users have insufficient understanding of the trendy culture, and the sales retracement is relatively large after the traffic ebbs." The Asia-Pacific region is where LABUBU first broke out and where it cooled down first.

### Change 2: Domestic unexpectedly strong - online +150% becomes a new engine

The growth rate in China in Q1 2026 is 100-105%, of which online channels are +150-155%. While the overseas market stalled, the domestic market once again became the main force of growth. The number of members increased from 46.08 million to 72.58 million (+57%), and the repurchase rate increased from 49.4% to 55.7% - new users did not try it once and then leave, but actually stayed.

### Change three: The profit quality is extremely high but the inventory is red.

Operating cash flow of 12.865 billion and net profit of 12.776 billion almost matched 1:1, and the turnover days of accounts receivable were compressed from 11 days to 7 days. However, the inventory growth rate of 246% far exceeded the revenue growth rate of 185%, and the turnover days worsened from 21 days to 123 days. Whether the 5.473 billion inventory can be digested healthily is the biggest financial suspense in 2026.

## 5. In-depth analysis of each dimension

### The essence of business (3 core findings)

1. **Plush category subverts the category structure**: 18.7 billion (+561%), accounting for 50.4% from 15%. Bubble Mart has transformed from a "blind box company" to a "plush + figurine dual engine", and its category expansion capabilities have been verified. However, the production barriers for plush are lower than for figures, and long-term competition may be more intense.

2. **Member economy takes shape**: 72.58 million members contribute 93.7% of sales, and the repurchase rate is 55.7%. This set of data shows that Bubble Mart is already a "quasi-membership" business - scalpers and speculators withdrew after the collapse of LABUBU second-hand prices, and real consumers are settling down. This is actually a good thing for long-term value.

3. **Overseas enters the "brand building" stage from "traffic dividend"**: Asia-Pacific growth rate dropped from 157% to 25-30%, and the Americas dropped from 748% to 55-60%. Management admitted that it lacked ten years of cultural accumulation overseas. After making the "easy money", you have to rely on your real skills.

### Financial quality (3 core findings)

1. **Net profit of 13 billion is almost 100% converted into cash**: operating cash flow is 12.865 billion, OCF/net profit = 1.007 times. The difference between GAAP and Non-GAAP is only 2.38% (300 million), and the company does not embellish the numbers. The turnover of accounts receivable is 7 days - the bigger you get, the faster you collect payments.

2. **Inventory is the only red light**: 5.473 billion inventory, a growth rate of 246% exceeding the revenue growth rate of 185%, and the turnover days worsened from 21 days to 123 days. If LABUBU continues to cool down, the risk of inventory impairment is 1.1-2.2 billion yuan (8-17% of net profit).

3. **Free cash flow was 9.88 billion, but the conversion rate dropped from 147% to 77%**: Growth is "eating" cash. Capital expenditures are expected to double to 1.5-2 billion in 2026 (parks, overseas flagship stores, Vietnam factories), and the FCF rate may be further pressured.

### Competitive landscape (3 core findings)

1. **The leading position is unshakable but the growth gap is narrowing**: Bubble Mart 37.1 billion vs TOP TOY 3.6 billion vs 52TOYS 630 million, the size difference is 10:1:0.17. However, Bubble Mart’s 2026 guidance is only 20%+, while TOP TOY’s 2025 guidance is +88%. Mean reversion in growth brings pursuers closer in speed.

2. **Funko’s recovery is an unexpected variable**: Gross profit margin in 2026Q1 was 44.2%, a new high since the IPO, and revenue was +5.3% year-on-year. The local overlord in North America has returned to its blood, and with its Western IP matrix such as Marvel/Disney, it is Bubble Mart's most powerful competitor in the Americas.

3. **The biggest competition does not come from peers, but from the mean return of category popularity**: The trendy toy industry has concentrated IPOs (TOP TOY, 52TOYS, Suplay, card games, etc. are queuing up for listing), and capital has shifted from the "investment period" to the "harvest period" - a classic signal that the industry's popularity has peaked.

### Risk signals (3 core findings)

1. **Wang Ning was absent from the first Q1 conference call - the biggest red flag**: The company added a quarterly conference call for the first time, but the CEO himself did not appear. Regardless of the reason, being absent from a company's first quarterly call is a negative sign.

2. **"Contradiction between words and deeds" is the pattern that needs the most attention**: Talking about anxiety (70 points from the organization), but cashing out (3 billion Hong Kong dollars). They say "no less than 20% growth" but lower the dividend rate (35% → 25%). Wang Ning's candor is commendable in itself, but actions speak louder than words.

3. **Combined risk of inventory + LABUBU ebb**: How much of the 5.473 billion inventory is related to LABUBU? If LABUBU accounts for 38% of revenue but a higher proportion of inventory (capacity is expanded from 10 million to 50 million/month), once the popularity drops and the inventory is overstocked, the impairment impact may exceed expectations.

## 9. Conclusion

1. **Exceed/meet/low expectations? ** The 2025 annual report exceeded expectations (+185% vs. guidance 50%+), 2026Q1 was in line with expectations (+75-80%), and the 2026 full-year guidance was lower than the market’s inertial expectations for high growth.
2. **Impact of investment thesis:** It will be slightly weakened in the short term (overseas slowdown, LABUBU ebb), but the mid- to long-term core logic (IP platform + globalization + emotional consumption trend) has not been destroyed.
3. **Next Catalyst:** 2026Q2 data (expected to be released in August) - If the overseas growth rate stabilizes at 30%+, the paper will be strengthened, if it continues to deteriorate to below 20%, the paper will be shaken.
4. **Operation suggestions:** Holders should continue to hold but control their positions; those on the sidelines can gradually build positions in the forward PE 12-15x range (corresponding to a stock price of 150-200 Hong Kong dollars); conservatives will wait for Q2 verification.
