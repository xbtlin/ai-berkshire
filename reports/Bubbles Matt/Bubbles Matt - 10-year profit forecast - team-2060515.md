# AI study limitations statement

1. ** Level A of information abundance does not amount to high certainty**: Bubbles Maat coupons have a wide coverage and data base, but the ten-year fate of consumer brands is unpredictable in nature - Beanie Babies looks equally "sure" at the peak.
2. **AI 's understanding of "emotive consumption" is limited**: Why is the fire in LabubU? Why is it this image, not another? Such questions relate to human emotions and cultural resonance, and the AI analytical framework is difficult to touch the essence.
3. **Inherent defect of extrapolation of growth **: The 10-year projection model is based on the annual decline in growth assumptions, but non-linear changes are possible in the real world (new IP outbreak/industry collapse/black swan event)
** Survivors' deviation**: Lego and Man-Dyun were cited extensively in this report as positive, but in practice most consumer brands will decline in 10 years' dimension - except that the declining brands will not be discussed
