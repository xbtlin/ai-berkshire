# Bubble Mart made 13 billion real money, but the overseas "ebbing tide" signal has already come on

## Let’s talk about the conclusion first

Bubble Mart's 2025 annual report is a beautiful report card: revenue of 37.1 billion, net profit of 13 billion, and a growth rate of 184%. But looking at the data for the first quarter of 2026, the picture has changed dramatically—overseas growth plummeted from 292% to 25%. The management themselves said that "last year was based on luck." The core contradiction of this financial report is: ** has the strongest earning power in history, but its largest growth engine is stalling. **

---

## The 3 most important changes

**First, overseas growth has jumped off a cliff. **

Overseas revenue will soar by 292% in 2025, and LABUBU is selling like crazy in Southeast Asia. However, in the first quarter of 2026 (actual data announced), the growth rate in the Asia-Pacific region is only 25-30%, and the growth rate in the Americas is 55-60%. The overall overseas growth rate has dropped from three digits to double digits. COO Sid said something very honest during the Q1 conference call: "A large number of new users do not have enough understanding of the trendy culture, and the sales retracement will be relatively large after the traffic ebbs."

To put it simply, LABUBU’s first wave of popularity overseas is over. The real test will be whether we can retain people next.

**Second, domestic accidents doubled. **

While the overseas market is cooling down, the domestic market is unexpectedly strong. In the first quarter, the growth rate in China exceeded 100% (announced data), and the online channel soared to more than 150%. The number of members increased from 46.08 million to 72.58 million, and the repurchase rate increased from 49% to 56%.

After the scalpers quit, users who really like trendy games are settling down. The second-hand price of LABUBU dropped from 1,100 yuan to 200 yuan, but the official price is still affordable. This is not good for short-term growth, but good for long-term value.

**Third, the profit is real money, but the warehouse is full of goods. **

Operating cash flow is 12.865 billion, which is almost one-to-one with net profit of 12.776 billion - every dollar earned can be found in corresponding cash on the account. The turnover of accounts receivable only takes 7 days, and the more goods sold, the faster the money is collected.

However, inventory surged from 1.5 billion to 5.5 billion, a growth rate of 259%, far exceeding the revenue growth rate of 185%. Turnaround days worsened to 123 days. LABUBU's monthly production capacity has expanded from 10 million to 50 million pieces. If the heat continues to cool down, the 5.5 billion inventory is the sword of Damocles hanging over the head - the impairment impact may reach 1.1 to 2.2 billion yuan, equivalent to 8% to 17% of net profit.

> **Summary:** The 2025 Bubble Mart is like a racing car sprinting at full speed, and it will be in the pits in 2026. Domestic operations have taken over from overseas markets and become the new engine, but inventory risks need to be closely tracked.

## The background of business is changing

Bubble Mart is changing from a "blind box company" to a "plush empire".

Plush category revenue was 18.7 billion, a year-on-year increase of 561%, accounting for half of the total revenue. Two years ago, figures were the soul, but now plush holds up half the sky. The ability to expand categories has been proven, but the production threshold for plush is far lower than that of figures - in the long run, there will be more imitators.

Who will take over after LABUBU? The current IP matrix is ​​in a "one super, many strong" pattern: THE MONSTERS (LABUBU) 14.16 billion accounts for 38%, SKULLPANDA 3.54 billion accounts for 9.5%, CRYBABY 2.93 billion accounts for 7.9%, and MOLLY 2.9 billion accounts for 7.8%. In 2025, 5 IPs have exceeded 1 billion, and 57 new IPs have been released (29 in the previous year). Q1 management revealed that "Starman" performed well, but the newly launched SUPER TUTU and Merodi were not successful. IP creation is not an industrial assembly line, and the success rate cannot be guaranteed.

Stories overseas are also taking a turn. From the "traffic dividend period" to the "brand building period", a slowdown in growth is inevitable. The management admitted that there was a lack of ten years of cultural accumulation overseas and that the "easy money" had been made. The key question is: What level of slowdown is considered "steady state"?

> **Summary:** Business is getting better (user quality has improved, category expansion has been successful), but the "easiness" of growth is getting worse. This is a typical feature of moving from the outbreak stage to the maturity stage.

## Financial quality: a good hand, a hidden danger

The good news first.

Net profit is 13 billion, operating cash flow is 12.9 billion, and the conversion rate is close to 100%. In contrast, the ratio in 2024 will be as high as 1.59 times - mainly because of the excess cash inflow caused by large changes in advances from customers and accounts payable that year. Returning to 1.0 times in 2025 is normalization, not deterioration. The difference between GAAP and non-GAAP profits is only 2.38%, so there's no numbers game. 17.2 billion in cash on hand and zero bank borrowings.

Now about the bad news.

5.5 billion in inventory, a growth rate of 259%, which is 1.4 times the revenue growth rate of 185%. If LABUBU accounts for 38% of revenue, but inventory accounts for a higher proportion, once the popularity declines and the inventory backlog accumulates, the impairment impact may reach 1.1 to 2.2 billion yuan.

Free cash flow is also under pressure. Free cash flow in 2025 is 9.88 billion, accounting for 77% of net profit. Although the absolute value is considerable, the ratio in 2024 is 147%. Growth "eats" cash. Capital expenditures are expected to double to 1.5 to 2 billion in 2026 (the second phase of the park, overseas flagship stores, Vietnam factories), and cash flow will be further diluted.

> **Summary:** You are making real money, and there are more places to spend it. Inventory turnover days (123 days) is the single most worthy of monitoring in the next six months.

## Competition: The leader is firmly seated, but the track is cooling down

Bubble Mart 37.1 billion, TOP TOY 3.6 billion, 52TOYS 630 million. The difference in size is 10:1:0.17, and the leading position is unshakable.

But two changes are worth noting.

First, the pursuers are accelerating. Bubble Mart's guidance for 2026 is only more than 20% growth, while TOP TOY's growth rate in 2025 is 88%. The absolute gap is still widening, but the growth rate gap is narrowing.

Second, real rivals have emerged in North America. Funko's gross profit margin hit a new high since its IPO (44.2%) in the first quarter of 2026. Backed by a matrix of Western IPs such as Marvel and Disney, it is the most powerful obstacle to Bubble Mart in the Americas.

The biggest risk actually does not come from your opponents, but from the track itself. TOP TOY, 52TOYS, Suplay, Card Games, etc. are all lined up to be listed on the market, and capital is shifting from the investment period to the harvest period - this is a classic signal that the popularity of the industry has peaked.

> **Summary:** Bubble Mart's leading position is not a worry in the short term, but the entire trendy entertainment track is changing from a "trendy outlet" to a "normal industry." The era of big water and big fish may be passing.

## "Contradiction between words and deeds" of management

The most intriguing thing about this financial report is not the numbers, but the people.

CEO Wang Ning was absent from the company’s first quarterly conference call. Regardless of the reason, missing a "milestone" occasion is never a good sign.

What is more subtle is a set of contrasts: talking about anxiety, giving the organization and management a score of 70; while reducing holdings, executives cashed out HK$3 billion. He said "no less than 20% growth"; he lowered the dividend rate from 35% to 25%.

The fulfillment of the promise is also worthy of review: the growth rate guidance for 2025 is more than 50%, and the actual delivery is 185%, which is a substantial excess. However, not only was the promise of "IP diversification" not fulfilled, LABUBU's share increased from 23% to 38%. The promise of "improving organizational capabilities" also failed - Wang Ning's self-evaluation dropped from 80 to 70.

Management's candor is worthy of respect - 99% of CEOs of listed companies cannot say the words "I relied on luck last year". But honesty is one thing; whether it can bring the organization from 70 to 90 is another matter.

> **Summary:** The management’s words can be trusted, but it depends more on what they do next.

## What it means for investment decisions

**Already held:** The nature of the business has not deteriorated, the quality of users is improving, 13 billion real money, 17.2 billion cash, zero debt - there are good reasons to continue to hold. However, management reduction and inventory risks are real, and it is recommended to adjust to a position that allows you to sleep well.

**For those who haven’t bought yet:** No rush. Based on the neutral forecast of net profit of 16.4 billion, total equity of approximately 1.33 billion shares, and EPS in 2026 of approximately 12.3 yuan (equivalent to approximately 13.2 Hong Kong dollars), the current stock price of 163 Hong Kong dollars corresponds to a forward PE of approximately 12.3 times, which is not expensive among consumer goods. However, whether overseas growth can be stabilized and whether inventories can be digested in a healthy manner, the answers to these two questions will not be clear until the second quarter data. Wait a minute, you won't miss much.

**The next key time point:** Second quarter data of 2026 (expected to be released in August). If the overseas growth rate stabilizes above 30%, it means that brand precipitation is working and the thesis is strengthened; if it continues to deteriorate below 20%, the growth story needs to be re-examined.
