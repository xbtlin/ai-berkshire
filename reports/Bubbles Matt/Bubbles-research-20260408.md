# 4. Summary of the analysis of dimensions

# 4.1 Business model and moat river (Long-Ping perspective)

- ** Core Position**: Bubbles Matt is essentially "Consumables Company with its own IP as a core asset, its emotional value as an internal product, its global retailing as a cash-as-usual route", not Blind Box Company
- ** The fly-wheel effect has been activated**: the positive cycle of IP contracting, full distribution of product design, and fan community, value added of IP is well functioning, but the lack of cultural content (immotion/vision/ narrative) is a long-term risk
- **Cave validation**: brand (<unk> ), scale effects (<unk> ), stronger IP barriers; weak conversion costs (<unk> ) and virtually non-existent network effects (<unk> )
- ** Non-consensual point 1**: Blind boxes are not moats. The type of stuff that was super-handled (18.7 billion) in 2025 became the first class, and the true competitiveness is in IP itself.
- ** Non-consensual view 2**: There are doubts about brand loyalty - are consumers following Laborubu or Bubble Matt? If the former, the brand moat is at a deep discount.

# 4.2 Finance and valuation (Buffett perspective)

- **Excellent financial quality**: Māori 72.1% close to the level of AME, zero interest liabilities, OCF/net profit > 1.0, free cash flow over 13 billion in 2025
- ** Profitability tested**: Rapid rebound after 2022 "to Dark Time" (net profit - 44%), demonstrating business model resilience
- ** Valuation judgement**: The current HK$141.8 corresponds to about 13-15 times the PE in 2025 and is in the lower range of reasonable areas (port 130-217). The equivalent of the famous PE (14.6x) is 30 percentage points higher and the increase is many times higher.
- ** Core concern**: Management took the initiative to reduce the 2026 acceleration to 20%, from 184.7% to the risk of a "Davis double-killing". Super-profit capacity in 2025 contains a one-time factor in the LABUBU boom.
- **DCF valuation range**: Conservative HK$112 / Neutral HK$164 / Optimistic HK$238

#8. Summary

Bubble Matt is an impressive company – an enterprise that grew from a small crowd shop to the top of the world's market for money in five years, with 37.1 billion profits and 13 billion net profits in 2025, with 72% of the Māori directly pursuing luxury goods. Its "contracting designer-incubation IP-global retail" business model is clear and efficient, and overseas expansion is being implemented with the highest capacity among China's consumer companies.

However, from the four masters' comprehensive framework, BubbleMart faces a structural paradox:** good business models vs uncertain IP life cycle**. The THE MONSTERS (LABUBU) account for 38% of the concentration of revenues, stock growth far exceeding the income growth rate, management's high-level decline — three signals that are combined to suggest that investors need more patience to wait for validation than to rush to build a warehouse.

The current price of HK$141.8 (PE 13-15x) is not expensive, but it is not enough to call it a "clear margin of security" as Buffett has demanded.** The most prudent strategy is to engage small warehouses and postpone the decision to rehousing until after the release of the first half of 2026, when the question of LABUBU dependency and stocks will be answered clearly**.

* "Erecise when others are greedy, Greed when others are afraid." -- Warren Buffett. Bubble Matt is moving from "Extra-Grovernance" to "Mighty Fear" (141.8 HK$, PE 13x). The question is: * Is fear enough?*
