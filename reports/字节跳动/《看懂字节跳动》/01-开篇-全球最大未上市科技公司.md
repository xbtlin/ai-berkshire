## A Set of Numbers That Reframes ByteDance

Among the world's most valuable technology companies, one has no publicly traded stock, no financial statements, and not even quarterly reports: ByteDance. In May 2026, its secondary-market valuation had reached **$550 billion**, on par with Tencent and nearly twice Alibaba's value.

The company is more opaque than almost any technology giant you know, yet it is growing faster than most listed companies. Both statements are true, and that is the starting point for understanding ByteDance.

Put it in context:

| Company | Market cap / valuation (May 2026) | Listed? |
|------|---------------------|---------|
| Apple | ~$3.2 trillion | Listed |
| Microsoft | ~$3.0 trillion | Listed |
| Meta | ~$1.7 trillion | Listed |
| Tencent | ~$550 billion | Listed |
| **ByteDance** | **~$550 billion** | **Private** |
| Alibaba | ~$330 billion | Listed |
| PDD | ~$140 billion | Listed |

**A private company is valued on par with Tencent, at roughly 1.7x Alibaba and nearly 4x PDD.** Its founder, Zhang Yiming, topped China's rich list with a net worth of **$69.3 billion** (source: 2026 Forbes World's Billionaires list).

Outsiders can only piece together its financial data from media reports and secondary-market transactions. There are no annual reports, no investor calls, and no publicly audited quarterly filings.

## What ByteDance Actually Is

In one sentence:

> **ByteDance = the world's largest algorithmic content-distribution company, monetized through advertising and e-commerce, and now rebuilding everything around AI.**

Breaking that down:

### Revenue Structure (2025 Estimate)

| Business | Estimated revenue | Share (est.) | Growth (est.) |
|------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Domestic advertising (Douyin feed + search) | ~$58 billion (~RMB 420 billion) | ~31% | ~10% |
| Domestic e-commerce (Douyin e-commerce commissions) | ~$35 billion | ~19% | ~20% |
| Domestic local services | ~$12 billion | ~6% | ~50% |
| TikTok advertising + e-commerce | ~$50 billion+ | ~27% | ~50% |
| Volcano Engine / Doubao AI | ~RMB 15 billion (~$2 billion) | ~1% | >100% |
| Other (Lark, gaming, CapCut, etc.) | ~$30 billion | ~16% | Mixed |
| **Total** | **~$186 billion** | **100%** | **~20%** |

(Note: The figures above combine media reports and analyst estimates. Individual line items come from different sources, so the subtotal and total may not reconcile exactly. ByteDance does not publicly disclose its revenue breakdown. Sources: 36Kr, The Information, Bloomberg.)

**A few key numbers:**

- **2025 total revenue was about $186 billion**, exceeding Meta for two consecutive quarters (source: MIT Technology Review China)
- **Overseas revenue exceeded 30% for the first time**, up sharply from 25% in 2024 (source: exclusive reporting by 36Kr)
- **Monthly active users across its products exceeded 4 billion**, roughly the same order of magnitude as Meta (source: in-depth Zhihu analysis)
- Global headcount was about **110,000-150,000** (source: data from multiple recruitment platforms, with inconsistent methodologies)

### Profit: A Reversal Story

ByteDance's 2024 net profit was about **$33 billion**. At one point, the market expected 2025 net profit to surge to **$50 billion**.

But the actual result was that **2025 net profit fell by more than 70% year over year**.

The reason was not business deterioration. It was ByteDance's aggressive AI spending ramp in the second half of 2025:

- 2025 capital expenditure of about **RMB 150 billion**
- 2026 AI budget of about **$23 billion**, including **RMB 85 billion** earmarked for AI chip procurement

(Sources: Sina Finance, Securities Times, 36Kr.)

Li Liang, vice president of Douyin Group, responded that the 70% decline included factors such as changes in preferred-share and option costs under international accounting standards, and therefore did not fully reflect operating fundamentals.

**Even after adjusting for that caveat, the conclusion is unchanged: ByteDance is trading current profit for an AI future. This is an active choice, not passive deterioration.**

Counterpoint: The sharp profit decline may also indicate execution risk in management's pacing of AI investment. The payback period and certainty of returns from such large capital expenditure remain unproven.

## Zhang Yiming: The Invisible Presence

To understand ByteDance, you cannot avoid Zhang Yiming.

| Dimension | Facts |
|------|------|
| Birth | 1983, Longyan, Fujian |
| Founder journey | Jiujiufang → Neihan Duanzi → Toutiao → Douyin → TikTok |
| Stepped down as CEO | May 2021, handing the CEO role to co-founder Liang Rubo |
| Current role | Actual controller, with about 21% economic interest and **over 60% voting power** under a dual-class share structure |
| Wealth | China's richest person in 2026, with about **$69.3 billion / RMB 550 billion** |
| Recent status | Long-term travel between Singapore and Beijing; personally involved in AI strategy (source: Tencent News) |

(Sources: Tide News, Hurun Rich List, Forbes.)

After Zhang Yiming stepped down as CEO in 2021, outsiders once thought he had "retired." In reality, **he remains ByteDance's ultimate controller**. Through the dual-class share structure and concert-party arrangements, he holds final veto power over major corporate decisions.

Current CEO Liang Rubo was Zhang Yiming's college roommate and co-founder in two ventures. This arrangement lets Zhang "semi-retire": **he does not manage day-to-day operations, but he controls strategic direction and key personnel decisions**.

Unlike PDD founder Colin Huang's near-total disappearance from public view, Zhang Yiming is more like an "invisible presence": **you do not see him, but his shadow sits behind every major turn the company makes**.

Counterpoint: History has many examples of founder-led companies stumbling when the founder stayed off the front line and managed remotely. No one can guarantee that Zhang Yiming's control will continue to translate into the right decisions in the AI era.

## Comparison with the Understanding Tencent and Understanding PDD Series

If you have read the first two series, ByteDance is a third, entirely different type of asset:

| Dimension | Tencent | PDD | ByteDance |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| Asset quality | WeChat + gaming, with an extremely deep moat | Algorithmic advertising machine with very high efficiency | Algorithmic distribution + globalization, with the fastest growth |
| Transparency | High (listed company with detailed financials) | Low (listed but does not communicate much) | **Extremely low (private, no public financials)** |
| Valuation | ~$550 billion, 14.5x PE | ~$140 billion, 10.5x PE | **~$550 billion, no public PE** |
| Core risks | Regulation + China-U.S. relations | Temu political risk + management trust | **TikTok geopolitics + returns on AI investment + no listing** |
| Investability | Directly available via Hong Kong stocks | Directly available via U.S. stocks | **Not directly available to ordinary investors** |

Tencent is like a castle whose full shape you can see: solid, transparent, and possible to value.
PDD is like a profit machine, but the door to the control room is locked.
**ByteDance is like a skyscraper in dense fog: you know it is tall, but you cannot see how many floors it has or whether the structure is safe.**

The goal of this series is to **clear as much of that fog as possible**.
