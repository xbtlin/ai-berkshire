# The Douyin Empire: A Three-Stage Rocket of Advertising, E-Commerce, and Local Services

## What Business Is Douyin Really In?

In 2025, Douyin alone generated more advertising revenue than Baidu and Tencent Advertising combined; its e-commerce GMV closed in on PDD, ranking third in Chinese e-commerce; and its local-services GTV grew 59%, directly challenging Meituan. All three things happened inside the same app.

Douyin's business is not "letting you scroll videos." **It is China's largest algorithmic advertising engine, while also becoming China's second-largest e-commerce platform and second-largest local-services platform.** In ByteDance's revenue structure, **Douyin contributes the vast majority of domestic revenue**, and domestic revenue accounts for nearly **70%** of total revenue.

Stacked together, the three businesses form a three-stage rocket:

| Business layer | 2025 scale (estimate) | Growth (est.) | Benchmark |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|------|
| **Advertising** (feed + search + brand) | Advertising revenue of about **RMB 420 billion** (Douyin is the majority) | ~10% | Google, Meta |
| **E-commerce** (Douyin e-commerce GMV) | GMV exceeded **RMB 4 trillion** | ~20% | Taobao, PDD |
| **Local services** (in-store + at-home) | GTV exceeded **RMB 850 billion** | ~59% | Meituan |

(Sources: Eastmoney, 36Kr, Securities Times.)

**In 2025, Douyin's advertising revenue alone reached RMB 420 billion, accounting for 46.6% of ByteDance's total revenue.** What does that number mean? Compare it with peers:

| Company | 2025 advertising revenue (est.) |
|------|---------------------|
| Douyin (including ByteDance domestic advertising) | ~RMB 420 billion (~$58 billion) |
| Baidu | ~RMB 120 billion |
| Tencent Advertising | ~RMB 110 billion |
| Alimama | ~RMB 260 billion |

(Sources: company filings, Eastmoney.)

**Douyin alone generates more advertising revenue than Baidu + Tencent Advertising combined.** This is not merely a "short-video platform." It is the clear leader in China's digital advertising market.

## Stage One: Advertising, an Algorithm-Fed Money Printer

### Why Douyin Advertising Is So Profitable

There is one answer: **the precision of algorithmic recommendation determines ad conversion efficiency, and conversion efficiency determines how much advertisers are willing to pay.**

The logic of traditional internet advertising is "people looking for information": you search "running shoes," and the search engine shows running-shoe ads. That is the Baidu model.

Douyin's logic is "information looking for people": you have not searched for anything, but the algorithm knows you recently watched three running videos and spent 8 seconds on a shoe-review clip, so the next item it serves is a running-shoe ad. **Before you even realize you might want to buy shoes, the ad has already arrived.**

The advantages of this model:

| Dimension | Search ads (Baidu model) | Feed ads (Douyin model) |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| User intent | Explicit (active search) | Latent (captured by algorithm) |
| Audience reach | Users who search | **Everyone scrolling videos** |
| Ad format | Text links | **Native short-video content** |
| Conversion path | Search → click → redirect → purchase | **See → want → order (closed loop)** |
| Advertiser ROI | High but capped | Volatile, but with a very high ceiling |

**Douyin's killer feature is that it controls both ends of the chain: attention and transaction.** Users see an ad, develop purchase intent, and place an order inside Douyin without leaving the platform. That is something Baidu and Weibo can never fully replicate.

### Risks in Advertising

Growth is slowing. In 2025, domestic advertising revenue growth fell to about **10%** for a simple reason: **China's internet advertising market is already near its ceiling**.

- China's digital advertising market was about **RMB 1.2 trillion** in 2025 (source: iResearch), and Douyin alone captured more than one-third of it
- User time spent has plateaued: Douyin's average daily usage is stable at about **120 minutes**, making further increases difficult
- Ad load is already high, and pushing it higher would damage user experience

Counterpoint: Slowing ad growth is not the same as business deterioration. Meta's ad growth is also slowing, but it remains one of the world's most profitable advertising companies. The key question is whether ARPU, or revenue per user, still has room to rise.

## Stage Two: E-Commerce, Interest-Based Commerce at RMB 4 Trillion GMV

### From Zero to RMB 4 Trillion in Just 5 Years

Douyin e-commerce officially launched in 2020. In 2025, full-year Douyin e-commerce GMV exceeded **RMB 4 trillion** (sources: Eastmoney, 36Kr), closing in on PDD and far surpassing JD.com.

Put in context:

| Platform | 2025 GMV (estimate) | Market share |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| Taobao Tmall | ~RMB 8 trillion | ~35% |
| PDD | ~RMB 4.5 trillion | ~20% |
| **Douyin e-commerce** | **~RMB 4 trillion** | **~18%** |
| JD.com | ~RMB 3.5 trillion | ~15% |

(Sources: Eastmoney, company filings.)

**In 5 years, it went from zero to China's No. 3 e-commerce platform, nearly catching the No. 2 player.** In e-commerce history, only PDD has grown at a comparable speed.

### How Is "Interest-Based E-Commerce" Different from Traditional E-Commerce?

| Dimension | Traditional e-commerce (Taobao / JD.com) | Interest-based e-commerce (Douyin) |
|------|---------------------|-----------------|
| User behavior | "I want to buy X" → search → compare prices → order | **"I am scrolling videos" → get influenced → impulse order** |
| Traffic distribution | Search ranking + store operations | **Algorithmic recommendation + content quality** |
| Merchant core capability | Operations + customer service + supply chain | **Content production + livestreaming capability** |
| Return rate | 15-20% | **30-40%** (pain point) |
| Repeat purchase | High (search-driven purchases) | Lower (impulse-driven purchases) |

The essence of Douyin e-commerce is **merging browsing and buying**. Users do not come to buy things; they come to watch videos. But while watching, they end up buying.

The genius of the model is that **it turns e-commerce traffic cost into zero**, because users were already scrolling videos, and ads or commerce content are "disguised" as ordinary content.

### E-Commerce Concerns

1. **Return rates remain elevated**: Douyin e-commerce return rates are about **30-40%**, far above traditional e-commerce. High returns are the cost of impulse consumption. This directly erodes merchant profit, and some merchants have already started "leaving Douyin" (source: 36Kr).

2. **GMV growth is slowing**: Growth fell from ~60% in 2023 to ~20% in 2025, as base effects combined with intensifying competition.

3. **Pain from the shift to shelf-based e-commerce**: Douyin is aggressively pushing "shelf e-commerce" (search + marketplace), trying to make users "search and buy" instead of only "scroll and buy." But that means fighting Taobao on Taobao's home turf, and the odds are uncertain.

Counterpoint: High return rates are a structural feature of interest-based e-commerce and do not necessarily have to worsen. As algorithms become more precise and user habits mature, return rates may gradually converge. On a **RMB 4 trillion** GMV base, even a slowdown to 10-15% growth would still generate a striking absolute increase.

## Stage Three: Local Services, Meituan's Biggest Challenger

### RMB 850 Billion GTV, Growing 59%

In 2025, Douyin local-services paid GTV exceeded **RMB 850 billion**, up **59%** year over year (sources: 36Kr, Zhihu industry analysis). The cumulative number of active-selling stores reached **15.198 million**, with **3.99 million** newly onboarded merchants.

Compared with Meituan:

| Dimension | Meituan | Douyin local services |
|------|------|-------------|
| 2025 GTV | ~RMB 2 trillion (in-store + at-home) | **~RMB 850 billion** |
| Growth | ~15% | **~59%** |
| Model | Search + reviews + delivery | **Short-video influence + group-buying redemption** |
| Strongest scenarios | Food delivery, at-home services | **In-store dining, leisure, and entertainment** |
| Fulfillment capability | Extremely strong (rider network) | Weaker (no owned delivery network) |

Douyin's local-services playbook is the same as e-commerce: **use content and algorithms to create demand, then use low-price group-buying deals to drive traffic**. You see a short video about a hotpot restaurant, tap in, find a 50%-off group-buying voucher, and immediately buy and redeem it.

### Can Douyin Disrupt Meituan?

**In the short term, that is difficult.** The reason is that the core barrier in local services is fulfillment, not traffic:

- Food delivery requires a rider network, which Douyin does not have
- At-home services require service-provider management, which Douyin does not have
- User habit: "I want food delivery" → open Meituan, not Douyin

**But in the "in-store" scenario, Douyin is already a material threat.** In categories that do not require delivery, such as in-store dining and leisure entertainment, Douyin is rapidly taking share with its traffic advantage and low-price strategy.

Douyin's 2026 local-services GMV growth target remains **~50%** (source: Sina Tech), indicating that management is still on the offensive.

Counterpoint: Local services is a heavily operational business. It is hard to build a lasting advantage on traffic and subsidies alone. Meituan's operating depth on the merchant side, including its review system, business tools, and delivery capability, cannot be replicated by Douyin in the short term. Once subsidies fade, merchants and users may flow back.

## The Three Stages Combined: How Deep Is Douyin's Moat?

Putting the three businesses together, Douyin's competitive-advantage structure looks like this:

| Moat dimension | Depth | Explanation |
|-----------|------|------|
| **Algorithmic recommendation** | Deep | Data flywheel from **700 million+** DAU and 120 minutes of average daily usage; almost impossible for new entrants to replicate |
| **Content ecosystem** | Medium-deep | Tens of millions of creators + MCN agencies, though creator loyalty is limited |
| **User scale** | Deep | **700 million+** DAU, second only to WeChat among Chinese internet apps |
| **Merchant ecosystem** | Medium | Large e-commerce + local-services merchant base, but merchants "go wherever traffic is" |
| **Brand mindshare** | Medium | The mental association "scroll videos = Douyin" is established, but "buy things = Douyin" is not yet established |
| **Network effects** | Medium | Strong content network effects, but transaction network effects are weaker than Taobao's or Meituan's |

**Overall judgment: Douyin's domestic moat is "medium-to-deep." Algorithms and user scale create hard barriers, but the competitive advantages in e-commerce and local services are still being built and have not yet reached the depth of Taobao's or Meituan's moats.**

The largest risk is not competitors, but **migration in user attention**. If the next generation of young users stops spending time on short videos, just as earlier generations moved away from Weibo and blogs, Douyin's traffic foundation would be shaken.

For now, this risk is not large over a 3-5 year horizon. But over a 10-year horizon, no content platform can guarantee that it will hold user attention forever.
