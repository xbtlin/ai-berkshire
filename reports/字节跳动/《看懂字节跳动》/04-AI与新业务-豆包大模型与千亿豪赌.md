## The Underlying Logic of ByteDance's AI Push

In 2025, ByteDance's net profit fell by more than 70% year over year. Not because the business deteriorated, but because of an active choice: trading profits for algorithms, computing power, and the future of AI.

That year, ByteDance's capital expenditure was about RMB 150 billion, and its 2026 AI budget rose further to around $23 billion. There are few global precedents for a company with annual revenue of $186 billion betting on AI with this level of intensity.

To understand this choice, start with ByteDance's DNA. When Toutiao launched in 2012, its core selling point was "algorithmic news recommendations." Douyin's rise also depended on recommendation algorithms. **ByteDance is fundamentally a company that uses algorithms to distribute content and monetize that distribution.** The arrival of the large-model era simply upgrades "algorithms" from recommendation systems to general intelligence.

In 2024-2025, ByteDance's AI investment escalated from "normal R&D" to a "strategic all-in bet":

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| 2025 capital expenditure | ~**RMB 150 billion** |
| 2026 AI budget | ~**$23 billion** (~RMB 165 billion) |
| AI chip procurement within that | ~**RMB 85 billion** |
| 2025 net profit decline | **More than 70%** (mainly due to AI investment) |

(Sources: Sina Finance, Securities Times, 36Kr)

## Doubao: China's Largest AI Application

### From Zero to More Than 100 Million DAU

Doubao is ByteDance's AI chat product and was officially released in May 2024. Its growth speed has been astonishing:

| Time | Milestone |
|------|--------|
| May 2024 | Doubao officially launched |
| October 2025 | Integrated with Douyin Mall |
| December 2025 | DAU around **70 million** (APP channel only) |
| January 2026 | **DAU surpassed 100 million**, China's first AI application to reach 100 million DAU |
| February 2026 (Spring Festival) | Peak DAU reached **145 million** |
| February 2026 | MAU reached **315 million**, up 87% MoM |

(Sources: Sina Tech, TMTPost, PC Home)

**What does 315 million MAU mean?** It equals the **combined** MAU of DeepSeek, Yuanbao, Ant A-Fu, and Qianwen, the No. 2-5 AI applications (source: Sina Tech).

### Why Doubao Became No. 1

Three reasons:

**1. ByteDance's traffic distribution capability**

Douyin's **700 million+** DAU is Doubao's largest user acquisition channel. ByteDance can promote Doubao "for free" inside Douyin's feed. Other AI companies have to spend real money buying traffic; ByteDance only needs to "place an entry point inside its own App."

During the 2026 Spring Festival, Doubao partnered with the CCTV Spring Festival Gala, driving explosive user growth.

**2. Multi-scenario integration**

Doubao is not just a chatbot. ByteDance has integrated it into:
- Douyin (video generation, search enhancement)
- Jianying/CapCut (AI video editing)
- Lark (AI office assistant)
- Douyin Mall (AI shopping recommendations)
- A Doubao AI phone launched jointly with ZTE

**3. Aggressive pricing**

Doubao large model 2.0 pricing:

| Version | Input price | Output price |
|------|---------|---------|
| Pro | **RMB 3.2/million tokens** | **RMB 16/million tokens** |
| Lite | **RMB 0.6/million tokens** | — |

ByteDance claims inference costs have fallen to **1/10** of the industry benchmark, a **90%** reduction (sources: Caixin, Xinhua). This "price for volume" strategy is consistent with the playbook ByteDance used in Douyin e-commerce: first win market share with low prices, then talk about profitability.

Counterargument: a large user base does not equal large commercial value. How much of Doubao's MAU comes from "free traffic injection"? Once ByteDance reduces promotional support, will retention collapse? China's AI application market is currently highly commoditized, and Doubao's user stickiness and paid conversion rate remain unproven.

## Doubao Large Model 2.0: How Good Is the Technology?

On February 14, 2026, ByteDance released Doubao large model 2.0 (Doubao-Seed-2.0), the first major version upgrade since the 2024 release.

### Product Matrix

| Model | Positioning |
|------|------|
| Doubao-Seed-2.0 **Pro** | Flagship general-purpose model |
| Doubao-Seed-2.0 **Lite** | Lightweight, omni-modal understanding (video + image + audio + text) |
| Doubao-Seed-2.0 **Mini** | On-device deployment |
| Doubao-Seed-2.0 **Code** | Programming optimized |

On May 6, 2026, ByteDance further released its **first omni-modal understanding model**, Doubao-Seed-2.0-lite, supporting native unified understanding of four modalities: video, image, audio, and text (sources: IT Home, Volcano Engine).

### Objective Assessment of the Technology Level

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Chinese-language understanding | First tier (alongside DeepSeek and Qianwen) |
| English/multilingual | Second tier (behind GPT-4 and Claude) |
| Code generation | Upper-middle |
| Multimodal | Significant progress, but still behind GPT-4o |
| Inference cost | **Among the lowest in the industry** |

**ByteDance's large-model strategy is not "build the strongest model," but "build the cheapest and easiest-to-use model."** This is completely different from OpenAI's path toward AGI.

Counterargument: the endgame of large-model competition remains unclear. If the future is winner-take-all, similar to search engines, then "second-tier" models may have little value. ByteDance's foundation in basic research is far weaker than Google DeepMind, OpenAI, and Anthropic. Whether "application-layer innovation" can offset a "foundation-layer gap" remains unknown.

## Volcano Engine: The Ambition to Become China's AWS

Volcano Engine is ByteDance's cloud computing and AI infrastructure platform, benchmarked against AWS/Azure.

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| 2024 revenue (est.) | **RMB 11-12 billion** |
| Large-model invocation market share | **49.2%** (No. 1, source: IDC) |
| 2031 revenue forecast in research working papers | **RMB 120 billion** |
| Forecast CAGR | **50-70%** |

(Sources: research working papers, IDC)

Volcano Engine's advantage lies in **Doubao large model + ByteDance's own businesses (Douyin, TikTok) = enormous internal demand**. This is the same logic that drove AWS's early growth through Amazon's own e-commerce business.

But Volcano Engine's share of China's cloud computing market remains far behind Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud. **Cloud computing is a "strong get stronger" business, making it extremely difficult for latecomers to catch up.**

Counterargument: Volcano Engine's core competitiveness is not traditional IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service), but **MaaS (Model as a Service)**. Its 49.2% share of the large-model invocation market shows that it is already leading in the "cloud computing for the AI era" track. That may matter more than traditional cloud market share.

## Other Businesses: Lark, Games, and Jianying

### Lark: Far from Success

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| ARR (annual recurring revenue) | ~**$300 million** (2024 estimate) |
| Ranking in China's enterprise collaboration market | **No. 3** (far behind DingTalk and WeCom) |
| 2031 ARR forecast in research working papers | ~$700 million |

Lark is one of ByteDance's largest B2B investments, but progress has fallen short of expectations. Network effects in the enterprise collaboration market are extremely strong. If your partners use DingTalk, you have to use DingTalk. Breaking through will be difficult for Lark.

### Games: Strategic Retrenchment

ByteDance once entered the gaming industry aggressively, acquiring studios at a furious pace in 2021-2022. But starting in 2023, it sharply cut back the gaming business (Nuverse), which is now **marginalized**.

**This is one of ByteDance's rare examples of "large investment, low return."** It illustrates one thing: ByteDance's algorithmic distribution capability cannot be copied into every field. Games require content creativity and long-term operations, not problems algorithms can solve by themselves.

### Jianying/CapCut: The Global No. 1 Video Editor

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| 2024 revenue (est.) | ~**$1.3 billion** |
| 2031 revenue forecast in research working papers | ~**$4.0 billion** |
| Global market position | **No. 1 video editing App** |

Jianying/CapCut is one of ByteDance's most underestimated assets. As demand for AI video editing explodes, its growth runway may exceed expectations. **But CapCut's U.S. business has been included in the USDS joint venture**, diluting ByteDance's economic interest in that portion of growth.

## The Risks and Returns of the AI Bet

### Payback Cycle for ByteDance's AI Investment

| Stage | Timeline | Expected return |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Current (2025-2026)** | Large-scale investment period | Negative: profits materially eroded |
| Medium term (2027-2028) | AI application commercialization | Volcano Engine + Doubao begin contributing revenue |
| Long term (2029-2031) | AI embedded into all businesses | Higher ad efficiency, higher e-commerce conversion, new revenue sources |

**Core question: can ByteDance afford to wait?**

The answer is **probably yes**. Reasons:

1. **ByteDance is not listed**, so it has no quarterly earnings pressure. Management can sacrifice short-term profit without facing shareholder revolt
2. **Core businesses (Douyin advertising + e-commerce) have extremely strong cash flow**, and even with profit down 70%, the company is not short of cash
3. **Zhang Yiming remains the absolute controller** and will not be removed by the board

But the counterargument is equally strong: **historically, the failure rate of big bets that "trade profits for the future" is far higher than the success rate.** Yahoo burned money on search, Microsoft burned money on mobile, and Meta burned money on the metaverse. The "strategic losses" of large companies are often a polite name for "strategic waste." Whether ByteDance's AI investment can generate sufficient returns should become clear within 5 years.
