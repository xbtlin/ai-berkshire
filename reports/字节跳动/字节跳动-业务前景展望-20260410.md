## TikTok Ban Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Probability | Impact |
|------|------|------|
| **Base case (USDS operation)** | 70% | Divestiture completed in January 2026; ByteDance retains a 19.9% stake plus algorithm licensing fees, while losing 40-60% of the U.S. economic interest |
| Bull case (meaningful licensing fees) | 15% | Overseas business remains the largest growth engine; overseas revenue share reaches 35-40% in 5 years |
| Bear case (other countries copy the divestiture model) | 15% | Overseas revenue growth falls below 20%, and the growth engine shifts back to China |

## What ByteDance Could Look Like in 5 Years

**One of the world's highest-revenue internet companies, evolving from an "advertising company" into a three-engine platform built on e-commerce, AI, and advertising.**

- Revenue of ~$300-350 billion, approaching or even exceeding Meta
- E-commerce (Douyin + TikTok Shop) contributing 40%+ of revenue
- Volcano Engine / AI contributing 10-15% of revenue
- The largest risk is not competition, but geopolitics
- Potential partial listings of selected businesses in 2027-2029, likely in Hong Kong
