** GO-2 Core innovation**: Introduction of "**action thinking chain**" - "thinking one step" before robots do the action, translating semantic understanding into action planning.

For example, the traditional way to make robots "put the cup on the table" is to directly export the joint angle sequence (explicit failure). The GO-2 idea is to understand "where the cup is and what kind of track it needs to be held" and then to execute it.

** Positive evaluation**: This idea is solid in academia. The lead scientist, Sergei Levine, is head of the Berkeley Robotic Learning Laboratory and a leading scholar in the field of intelligence. The GO-2 design is consistent with the current academic frontier.

** Reverse view**: Tesla Optimus takes the **end-to-end** route -- no matter "brain" -- direct from sensor input to action output, trained in big data violence. History has shown that end-to-end programs often crush stratification when the data are large enough. Tesla has the world's largest plant data acquisition capability -- if its route runs, the stratification of the brain can turn into "excessive engineering".

** It is not possible to judge who is right or wrong.** The race of routes like Waymo (Story Perception-Decision-Control) vs. Tesla in the autopilot field -- 10 years of undecided debate.

** Why data and simulations matter?** Physically intelligent models require big amounts of "rotator-physical world interaction" data. But collecting these data is very slow and expensive in the real world (a robot grabs objects over time for several minutes at a time). Simultaneous platforms can be generated with virtual environment acceleration data -- theoretically faster than the real world **1,000 times more than **.

The strategy of the wise-minded is "**real data + analog data double-wheel drive**":
- Real data: recovery of real interactive data from the deployed 10,000 robots
- Simulation data: Large-scale generation of training data in Genie Sim

** Positive evaluation**: 10,000 deployed robots form a natural "data-collection network" that is an advantage that competitors cannot replicate. The greater the data, the better the model; the better the model, the better the product; the better the product, the more the product is used -- it's a flying wheel.

** Anti-conversation**: Sim-to-real gap between the simulation and the real world is one of the biggest unresolved problems in the field of intelligence. A model that is well trained in simulation, which is often "twitched" or even completely disabled in real environments. This gap is not being fully resolved by any company.

# The ultimate pursuit of the technology

By stretching the horizon to 10 years, Ji-won faces a fundamental technical problem:

** What's the ultimate decision about "intelligence" of human robots? Hardware? Software? Data?

If the answer is ** data** (comparable to the conclusions in the area of large language models - ultimately the amount of data and the arithmetic determines the level of intelligence), Tesla may win — it has the largest network of plants and the big amount of data accumulated by FSD worldwide.

If the answer is **the software architecture** (like iOS vs Android dispute - the architecture design determines the ceiling for experience), there may be opportunities for fine model architecture for think-tank and Figure AI.

If the answer is ** Hardware** (the robot's physical ability determines what it can do), the current leader (Jiwon, Woo-ki) has a pre-emptive advantage.

** No one knows the answer at this point.** That is why this track attracts both extreme optimism and extreme pessimism — because before the technology's path is down, everyone is "bringing."
