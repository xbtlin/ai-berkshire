# China's five-year outlook for the car market: better or worse?

> Analysis date: 16 June 2026
> This document does not constitute any investment proposal.

---

# Core conclusion

** The Chinese automobile market will be "better" in five years -- but "good" is a small number of winners and "bader" for most participants.**

It's not a simple question of "good or bad." The answer is more accurate:

- ** Total number of industries**: summit, 34 million to 36 million ceilings have been touched
- **New energy**: from 47% penetration to 80%, very certain
- ** Out of the sea**: from 8.32 million to 10 million plus, second-growth pole
- **The knockout game**: 50+ brands down to about 15, joint brands worst.
- ** Profitability**: Tighting bottom (3.2%), 2026Q3 may start repair
- **Investment Opportunities**: Parts and components (Ninger Age) > The whole of the car (Biadey) > Platform (Carhouse/Creating Autonomy)

** Word: Total platform period, big structural changes. China's automobile industry is completing a qualitative shift from "big" to "strong" - at the cost of the loss of most players.**
