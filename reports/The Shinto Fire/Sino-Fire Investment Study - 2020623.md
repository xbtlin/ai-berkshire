# 4. Summary of the analysis of dimensions

# 4.1 Business model and moat

** Business nature**: The fire is not pure coal stock or a full integrated aluminium business tap, but a cycle resource unit for “low-cost electrolysis aluminium profit core + coal resource chassis + aluminium silt extension”.
2. ** Core moat**: Not brand, customer sticky or network effects, but compliance with the scarcity of aluminium production, the cost advantage of coal and aluminium in Xinjiang, the low carbon and location advantage of green aluminium in Yunnan.
3. ** Integration requires a discount**: Xinjiang base is more integrated, but Yunnan relies on electricity grids and water for water, aluminium oxide is still mainly outsourced, and the company does not go from bauxite to aluminium oxide to full closed rings of electrolytic aluminium.
4. ** Coal is not the current moat**: 2025 Coal Maori rate is only 7.61 per cent, and the mine assets are significantly impaired, and coal is more like a resource options and historical bottom plate.
** Paragraph Eongping concluded** that it is not a top business that “user is indissociable and can continuously raise prices”; it is better suited to be a good asset candidate when prices are cheap enough and cycles are suitable.

# 4.2 Finance and valuation

1. ** Balance sheet repairs are significant**: asset liability ratio dropped from 73.13 per cent in 2021 to 42.19 per cent in 2025 and interest-bearing liabilities from about $26.3 billion to about $9 billion.
2. ** High quality cash flow**: 2025 Operating cash flow of $8,043 million, simplifying free cash flow of approximately $7,882 million, significantly covering dividends and buybacks.
** High profit cycle**: Net profit return to mother in 2022, 7578 million yuan, falling to $4.05 billion in 2025; no longer value can be estimated with a high-scenario value.
4. ** Values are not expensive but not cheap: 2025 static PE about 12.70 x, PB about 2.1 x; there is a lack of sufficiently deep discounts for the Resource Cycle Unit.
** Three scenario valuation**: based on $2025 EPS 1.81, optimistic after five years, approximately $27.3, neutral about $18.1 and pessimistic about $9.8. Current prices are higher than neutral values.

# 4.3 Industry and competition

1. ** The electrolytic aluminium industry has better supply ends**: China ' s electrolytic aluminium is subject to energy replacement, energy consumption, double carbon and approximately 45 million tons of grade-based energy ceilings, and the inventory compliance capacity has been upgraded.
2. ** Demand-side growth is not unilateral**: New energy vehicles, photovoltaics, grids, packaging, etc., are downside of land-related hedges, but are still in general cyclical materials and not high-speed growth tracks.
3. ** Cost curve is a negative hand**: Electricity and aluminium oxide are the key to the cost of tons of aluminium.
4. ** Comparable position**: Macro Bridge, Aluminum in size and whole chain; Aluminum in green purity; Aluminum in Tianshan in aluminium oxide; South Mountain in high end processing; Fire of God is a low-cost second line flexible.
** Aluminum sulfon does not warrant overestimation**: Aluminium/ battery sulfon barriers are derived from customer certification, good rates, processing fees and capacity utilization, not solely from upstream aluminium costs.

##4.4 Risk and management

1. ** Governance of State capital is stable but subject to discount**: the realist is the Commissariat of State for the City of Comú and is more stable; however, the boundaries of the related transactions, local industrial responsibility and the interests of minority shareholders need to be followed up over time.
2. ** Cash returns were positive**: 10th in 2025, with a cash share of approximately 1,787 million yuan; and repurchase of about $255 million for equity incentives.
3. ** Reorganization of associated transactions is a warning**: the annual report discloses the termination of some of the association with the Fire Group in accordance with the requirements of the On-Site Inspection and Reorganization of the South Bank; it is not a negative vote, but it is tracked as a red line of governance.
4. ** Core risk variable**: Aluminium price falls, aluminium oxide rebounds, sub-electricity/electricity increases, coal and carbon costs in Xinjiang, coal mine continues to lose value, and safety and environmental accidents.
** Ten-year certainty**: Low-cost aluminium production can be of long-term value, but carbon costs, recycled aluminium, real estate needs, water and policy will continue to reshape profit pools.

#5. Investment argument: Bull vs Bear

# # # look at how logical

1. ** The electrolytic aluminium supply end discipline**: the energy ceiling and replacement system have limited the additional supply and the inventory compliance performance value has increased.
2. ** The fire is on the left side of the cost curve**: Coal aluminium and Yunnan green aluminium together constitute a ton of aluminium cost advantage.
3. ** Significant improvement in balance sheet**: The past few years have seen continuous leverage, with financial risks well below 2021.
4. ** Cash flows and dividends are better**: 2025 FCF about 78.82 billion yuan, covering a significant 1.787 billion yuan cash share.
**2026Q1 shows profit elasticity**: net return to the mother, 22.90 billion yuan, a significant increase in the same year, indicating strong resilience in improving aluminium prices/costs.
**Cycles have a shareholder sense of return**: dividends, buy-backs, equity incentives all reflect a certain degree of capital discipline.
** Low-carbon aluminium has long-term structural opportunities**: Yunnan Green Aluminum may have added value in carbon footprints and customer low-carbon procurement.

# # look at the logic #

1. ** There is no active pricing power**: The prices of electrolytic aluminium and coal are determined primarily by the market, and companies earn price differentials rather than brand premiums.
**2026Q1 Non-mechanical annualization**: One-season profits are derived from high-silent windows such as higher aluminium prices and lower aluminium oxide and cannot be considered as permanent profits.
3. **Aluminum outsourcing shortboards**: Not fully integrated, and once aluminium oxide rebounds, tons of aluminium profits are rapidly compressed.
4. **Unnan base is affected by water and electricity prices**: Green aluminium advantage is associated with the risk of water-time electricity, upswing electricity and fluctuations in production.
** Xinjiang coal-fired aluminium has carbon cost risks**: low cost from coal-fired electricity, subject to long-term double carbon, CBAM, green electricity certification and ESG discounts.
6. ** Risk of drag and impairment of coal operations**: 2025 Low coal Maori rate and the impairment of multiple coal mines exposed historical asset quality problems.
7. ** Current price security margin**: $22.99 is higher than the neutral valuation of $18, and risk returns are not sufficiently comfortable.
