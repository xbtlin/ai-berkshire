#AI full track stock screening: investment opportunity candidate pool

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Method:** 20 groups of research teams scanned in parallel, covering all aspects of the AI industry chain, Hong Kong stocks/A shares/US stocks/Taiwan stocks/Korean stocks
**Goal:** Discover all good AI investment opportunities in the market and build a candidate pool

---

## Filter criteria

From the perspective of value investment, a "good opportunity" needs to meet the following requirements at the same time:
1. **Clear moat** — monopoly/oligopoly/high switching costs/network effects
2. **Strong Pricing Power** — Gross profit margin is high and stable or rising
3. **AI growth rate is determined** — revenue growth rate >30% or AI purity is high
4. **Valuation is not outrageous** — PE/PS is within an acceptable range (growth stocks are allowed to have a premium but not bubbles)
