## 4. Summary of analysis of each dimension

### Business model (Duan Yongping’s perspective)
- Meituan is essentially a local life super platform with "high-frequency takeaways as the traffic entrance and instant delivery as the infrastructure"
- The moat is "wide but shallow": network effect ★4, scale effect ★4, brand ★4, but switching cost is only ★2
- Duan Yongping's judgment: Meituan does things that "others don't want to do" rather than "others can't do" - once JD.com/Alibaba are willing to do it, the moat will loosen
- The flywheel is still spinning but has changed from efficiency-driven to cost-burn driven

### Finance and Valuation (Buffett’s Perspective)
- Profit fluctuation in 2025 is 59.2 billion (profit 35.8 billion → loss 23.4 billion), the root cause is the subsidy war
- Stable balance sheet: Net cash of 116.1 billion accounts for 25% of market capitalization, sufficient downside protection
- PS 1.29x and EV/Revenue <1x are the lowest since launch
- The SOTP segment is valued at approximately HK$191, implying 119% upside potential (prerequisite: profit normalization)

### Industry and Competition (Munger’s Perspective)
- Douyin’s daily orders for food delivery are only 10,000-20,000 (Meituan’s 70 million+), and the threat is seriously overestimated.
- Real threat: Alibaba’s share increased from 27% to 42%, making it the most comprehensive opponent
- JD.com spent RMB 30 billion in half a year to reach 15% share, but its sustainability is questionable
- The largest deterministic increase is in real-time retail: breaking one trillion in scale in 2026

### Risk and Management (Li Lu’s Perspective)
- Wang Xing has top-notch strategic vision (group buying → takeaway → in-store → retail every step is correct), but the discipline of capital allocation is a shortcoming (preferred to burn 100 billion, Mobike devalued by 30 billion)
-Three core risks: worsening competition in food delivery, social security for riders (potential annual increase of 35-56 billion), and new business burning cash
- Ten-year test: Meituan is likely to exist, but it may become a low-profit delivery company rather than a high-profit platform

## 5. Investment thesis (Bull vs Bear)

### 🟢 Look at the long logic
1. PS 1.29x is the lowest in history. Even if the profit returns to zero, there is still a net cash base of 116.1 billion.
2. The takeout subsidy war is unsustainable (regulations are already halting it), and profits will eventually return
3. On the trillion-dollar real-time retail track, Meituan leads the way in card slots (100,000 flash warehouse plans)
4. The daily peak of 150 million orders proves that demand is not a problem, it is just a temporary concession.
5. The valuation of the SOTP segment is HK$191, which is a 54% discount to the current HK$87.60.
6. Hotels and hotels are still cash cows with high and stable profit margins
7. Overseas KeeTa is a forward option

### 🔴 Bearish logic
1. A loss of 23.4 billion in 2025, and the time and magnitude of profit recovery are highly uncertain
2. The moat has been proven to be filled by big capital (Alibaba’s share increased from 27% to 42%)
3. No pricing power: There is limited room for increasing the commission rate, and prices must be reduced as soon as competition comes.
4. The social security policy for riders is the Sword of Damocles hanging over the head (potential annual cost increase of 35-56 billion)
5. The plan to burn RMB 70 billion in four years has been falsified, and the discipline of capital allocation is worrying.
6. Fighting on three sides (JD.com + Alibaba + Douyin) consumes resources
7. It may forever become a low-profit delivery company rather than a high-profit platform.

## 8. Summary

Meituan is a good company in its "darkest moment". The three-sided war in 2025 will cause nearly 60 billion in profits to evaporate, but 116.1 billion in net cash provides an adequate safety cushion. PS 1.29x is the cheapest moment since its listing. The core contradiction is: Is this a temporary price war (like JD.com in 2015), or a permanent deterioration of the competitive landscape?

Our judgment is biased towards the former - the subsidy war is unsustainable, supervision is already intervening, and JD.com's model of burning 30 billion yuan in half a year is unsustainable. But the confidence level of this judgment is only 60-70%. If you believe that local life is "whoever has the delivery network wins the world", Meituan's current price provides very good odds; if you are worried that the moat has been permanently weakened, then waiting for clearer signals is a safer choice.

**Duan Yongping would say**: "If you are not sure, don't take action."
**Warren Buffett would say**: "Be greedy when others are fearful, but make sure the object of your greed is worth it."
**Munger would say**: "Think about it the other way around - if Meituan really fails, can you afford to lose 87 yuan?"
**Li Lu would say**: "Put it on the watch list and wait until the competitive landscape becomes clear before making a decision."
