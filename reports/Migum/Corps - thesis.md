# Meituan (3690.HK) investment thesis

**Establishment date**: 2026-04-09
**Current stock price**: HKD 87.60
**Market capitalization**: HKD 535 billion (RMB 470.5 billion)
**Exchange rate**: 1 HKD = 0.8795 RMB

---

## A1: Core paper

I bought Meituan at **87.60 HKD** because:

1. **The essence of this business is** - China's largest local life service platform, connecting 690 million annual transaction users and millions of merchants, and building an instant delivery network through 7.45 million riders. It is essentially an "infrastructure tax for local life".
2. **Its moat is** - the largest instant delivery network in the country (covering almost all cities and counties in the country) + the stickiness of user consumption habits + high switching costs on the merchant side. The share of food delivery GTV still exceeds 60%, despite the fierce attack by Alibaba/JD.com in 2025, proving that the moat is still effective although it has been eroded.
3. **Management** - Wang Xing has first-class strategic vision (Meituan Selection, overseas Keeta, AI layout), but capital allocation discipline is a shortcoming, and he has burned money for expansion many times in history. Currently fully embracing AI transformation, R&D investment increased by 23% to 26 billion yuan.
4. **Current Price** - PS is only 1.29x, the lowest in history, and net cash of 116.1 billion yuan (about 25% of market capitalization) provides downside protection. In 2026, analysts expect a return to profit of approximately RMB 19.6-22.2 billion, corresponding to a forward PE of approximately 24-27x. If takeout competition eases, profit elasticity will be huge.
5. **Even if I'm wrong** - a business with net cash of 116.1 billion + annual revenue of 364.9 billion will not go to zero. In the worst-case scenario, the takeaway share drops to 50%, but instant retail (flash sales/grocery shopping) and overseas business provide growth hedges. PB 3.15x is not expensive.

## A5: Key Risks and Investment Discipline

### Maximum uncertainty

The duration of the takeout war. After Alibaba’s Taobao flash sale was integrated with Ele.me, the number of riders exceeded 2 million, and the daily order volume reached 83% of Meituan’s. If Alibaba continues to invest, Meituan’s profit recovery will be significantly delayed.

### Duan Yongping’s perspective

> "A business without pricing power is not a good business no matter how big it is." Meituan is currently in a state of forced "involution" and has no pricing power in the short term. However, local life is a typical scale effect + network effect business, and it is highly likely that it will eventually return to rational competition. Key question: How much share will Meituan have left when it returns?

### Munger’s perspective

> "If you are going to compete in an industry where you must win to survive, you'd better make sure you can win." Meituan is still No. 1 in the field of food delivery, but the competition has changed from "one super" to "three pillars". Instant retail is an incremental market worthy of more attention.

### Buffett’s perspective

>116.1 billion net cash = "safety cushion" of HK$19 per share. The enterprise value after deducting cash is only HK$418.9 billion, corresponding to EV/Revenue 1.0x. Even if you continue to lose money for 2 years, you will not run out of cash.

### Li Lu’s perspective

> China’s 1.4 billion population has only reached less than half of the digitalization of local life services. If Meituan can survive this round of price wars, its long-term compound return rate will still be considerable.
