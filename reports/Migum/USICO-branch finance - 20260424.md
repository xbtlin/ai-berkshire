# I, the core conclusion

1. ** Official disclosure of only two branches** - "Core Local Commerce" and "New Business". Financial data from the sub-branch of the takeout, the liquor store, the flash purchase are no longer disclosed separately since 2022Q1, and the outside judgement of the sub-business income/profits is essentially based on the seller's calculations,** and should not be cited as facts**.

2. ** Three-year trend: from "Biennium-Short-Short" to "Bio-Short-Square-Square"**. In 2024, the profits were high (CLC operating profit of 52.4 billion, losses on new business of 7.3 billion, total company operating profit of 36.8 billion) and in 2025, as a result of the immediate retail price battle (CLC running by 6.9 billion, losses on new business expanded to 10.1 billion and losses on company operations by 17 billion).

** New business income continued to increase, but the cycle of burning money re-emerged** — three years of new business income CAGR + 22 per cent (6998 874 — 1004 billion), a significant loss in 2024 and a further increase in loss in 2025 due to the expansion of the Elephant Supermarket + Keeta.

4. ** The "subsidy deduction" for core local businesses resulted in a loss of income data** - the same decline in the income from distribution services in 2025, but the increase in the volume of orders indicates that the subsidy is deducted directly from total income** the real GMV is increasing at a significantly faster rate than the reported income increase**.

# VII. Data limitations and disclaimers

** Official disclosure of only two divisions** - core local business vs new business. Sub-business income and profit data for takeout/tourist/stellar, elephant/Keeta/optimal** are external calculations** and there are 20-40% differences between vendors and results under different methodologies.

2. ** New operating income in 2024 was available on two calibres** - the official performance bulletin disclosed $87,344 million (used in the present report), and earlier media reports reported 83.9 billion versions, with differences arising from "other" sub-classification adjustments.

** Partially extrapolated for 2025** - Data such as adjusted net profit, single-quarter splits, etc. are not disclosed article by article in the main annual report, and items marked "estimate" or "deferated" in this report are reported by third parties and not officially disclosed by the Panel.

4. ** The annual paper revealed a change of calibre** — Since 2022, the United States Corps has combined the former "Dave and Restaurant" and "Go to the Liquor Hotel" branches into "Core Local Commerce" and transferred the "Staff Purchase" from the new business to the CLC.** The "Extending Revenue" from 2021 to 2023 will be lost** in direct comparison.

**The 2025 statement of income is affected by the subsidy deduction** - The United States Group's 2025 large user/roper subsidy was deducted directly from the distribution services income (IFRS 15 requirement),** the real GTV acceleration > statement of income acceleration**. A clear view of the true scale of the company's 2025 business requires cross-checking with operational indicators such as GTV or volume.
