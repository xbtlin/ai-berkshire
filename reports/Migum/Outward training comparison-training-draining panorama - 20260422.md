# Panoramic review of "double reduction" in education and training: the political economy of an industrial destruction

**ai-berkshire Education and Training Double Reduction Review Specialist | 2026-04-22**

## 1. Core conclusion

Double reduction is not an isolated industrial policy, but a systematic political and economic action integrating population strategy (to deal with the collapse of the fertility rate), educational equity (to set the tone for common prosperity), and capital consolidation (to prevent disorderly expansion). The intensity of the attack is rare in the history of the global industry: the market value of leading companies generally evaporated by more than 90% (TAL went from ~US$50 billion to less than US$2 billion), the scale of the K12 subject training market shrank by 80%+, and millions of practitioners were forced to change jobs. The most profound lesson for investors is: **In China, when an industry hits the three red lines of "exacerbating injustice, creating anxiety, and capital fanaticism" at the same time, no matter how good the fundamentals are, they will be reset to zero under a piece of paper**.

## 2. In-depth analysis of antecedents (5 levels)

### Level 1: Educational equity - the hardest political reason
- "Haidian Mom" and "Shunyi Mom" phenomenon, families invest 100,000-300,000 yuan per year
- The narrative of class solidification that it is difficult to produce a noble son from a poor family
- High-quality educational resources are monopolized by the middle and upper classes through capital increases
- **"Common prosperity" is officially established as a core political and economic theme in 2021**

### Level 2: Fertility crisis—the lowest level of strategic anxiety
- 12 million births in 2020 (peak value in 2016: 17.86 million)
- Further decline to 10.62 million in 2021, negative population growth in 2022
- **The essence of double reduction is to reduce the cost of raising children and coordinate with the population strategy**

### Level 3: Capital frenzy creates anxiety—the most angry trigger point for public opinion
- 2018-2021 Education and training industry financing **>100 billion US dollars**
- Yuanfudao’s 2020 Series G valuation is US$17 billion
- Zuoyebang’s E+ round valuation reaches US$11 billion
- **Capital drives anxious industrialization → is judged as "Ponzi expansion"**

### Level 4: Outflow of teacher resources - institutional pain points
- Key public teachers were poached (pay gap 2-5 times)
- Multiple warnings from the Ministry of Education are ineffective
- **The foundation of public education has been eroded**

### Level 5: Strategic shift in culture and technology
- Sino-US technology decoupling → Talent training shifts to physics/chemistry/engineering
- Double cuts to make room for non-subjects (STEM/arts/vocations)
- **The public welfare attribute of compulsory education cannot be alienated by capital**
