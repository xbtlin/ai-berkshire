#Will AI bypass Meituan?

> The impact of AI and Douyin on Meituan’s moat · Chapter 01
> Reading time approx. 7 minutes

---

Many people worry about one thing:

In the future, if users no longer open Meituan and only say "order lunch for me" or "book a hotel for me" to the AI assistant, will the entrance value of Meituan be lost?

This concern sounds reasonable.

But local life is different from ordinary information search. AI can help you make decisions, but it cannot deliver meals to your door in person; AI can recommend hotels, but it cannot complete contract signing, inventory, pricing, and contract fulfillment for the platform.

For Meituan, AI is more like a sharper tool than someone who can directly tear down walls.

The real question is not "will AI change the entrance", but:

**After the entrance changes, who still knows the real supply and real performance of the contract. **

---

## Takeaway is not an information business, it is a contract fulfillment business

Takeaway is ostensibly an app.

The user opens it, Meituan displays nearby merchants, the user places an order, and the rider delivers.

But when you take apart the chain, the real difficulty is not in the first half of "displaying information", but in the second half of "delivering certain things to a certain place at a certain time."

| Link | What AI can do | What AI cannot directly replace |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Demand understanding | Know what you want to eat, what your budget is, and what you should avoid | There is no guarantee that nearby businesses can deliver meals stably today |
| Merchant recommendations | Sorting according to preferences, explaining ratings, and combining packages | Cannot replace merchant coverage and evaluation system |
| Route planning | Optimize rider paths and estimated delivery times | Unable to change rider, road and instant delivery density |
| After-sales processing | Automatic customer service, refund diversion, complaint classification | Unable to eliminate real performance failures |

The barrier to takeaway is in the physical world.

Merchant density, rider scale, scheduling system, user habits, and after-sales capabilities, these things cannot be copied by a large model calling interface.

If the AI assistant really becomes the entrance, it will still have to call a platform with merchants and riders.

It can ask Meituan, Ele.me, or Taobao flash sales.

But it cannot perform in a vacuum.

This determines that the impact of AI on the food delivery platform is more likely to be “redistributing entrances” rather than “eliminating the platform.”

---

## AI may instead strengthen the head platform

The smarter the AI, the more data it needs.

The data in the takeout scenario is not a static web page, but changes in real time.

Which store is open today?

Which restaurant is slow to deliver food?

Which rider is closer to the business district?

How much longer will the delivery time be after it rains?

What is the difference between what the same user wants to eat at noon on Tuesday and on Friday night?

The richer the data, the more accurate the recommendations and scheduling will be.

As the largest player, Meituan naturally has more scenario data and contract performance feedback. AI here does not distribute capabilities evenly, but amplifies existing data advantages.

This is a bit like the search engine era: the algorithm itself is important, but what really widens the gap is the data scale, feedback closed loop and commercialization system.

Therefore, the first impact of AI on Meituan is efficiency improvement:

| Scenario | Possible help for Meituan |
|------|------------------|
| Scheduling | More accurately predict rider arrival, merchant delivery, and user waiting time |
| Supply and demand matching | Allocate riders in advance during the afternoon peak to reduce overflow and empty rides |
| Merchant Operations | Help merchants automatically design packages, place ads, and make inventory forecasts |
| Customer service after-sales | Reduce labor customer service costs and increase dispute resolution speed |
| User recommendations | From "nearby popular" to more personalized consumption suggestions |

This is not to say that AI poses no threat to Meituan.

Of course there are threats.

If the AI assistant becomes a unified entrance in the future, Meituan will have to give up part of the traffic entrance value to the AI assistant; if the AI assistant is biased towards price sorting, Meituan's brand mentality may be weakened; if merchants directly reach users through AI, the platform's advertising revenue will also be affected.

But these threats are more like profit distribution issues than life and death issues.

As long as contract fulfillment relies on the platform, Meituan will not be bypassed by AI with just one click.

---

## Liquor travel is more likely to be impacted by AI than takeout

Compared with takeout, wine travel is more like an information matching business.

Where the user wants to go, how many nights to stay, what the budget is, whether to bring children, and how far away from the attractions, these questions are very suitable for AI to understand and plan.

Therefore, AI will have a greater impact on the entrance of wine hotels than takeaways.

In the past, users might search for hotels on Ctrip, Meituan, or Fliggy; in the future, they might directly ask the AI:

“Help me find a weekend family hotel in Shanghai with a budget of less than 1,500, close to Disneyland, and don’t rate it too low.”

AI can break down demand into very fine details and quickly compare prices and reviews on different platforms.

But wine travel is not a pure information business.

There is a lot of dirty work on the supply side of the hotel:

| Supply-side capabilities | Why AI is not easily replaceable |
|------------|----------------------|
| Hotel signing | Requires offline BD, negotiated price, account period and partnership |
| Inventory management | Room availability changes in real time, and holiday prices and inventory are more complex |
| Price system | Coordination among platforms, hotels, memberships, packages, and promotions |
| After-sales capabilities | Cancellation, rescheduling, no room available at the store, negative reviews and disputes all need to be borne by the platform |

AI can be a planner, but it does not naturally own the supply chain.

Ctrip is strong in the wine and travel supply chain, and Meituan is strong in high-frequency local life diversion. AI will change the way users make decisions, but it will not automatically replace these supply-side capabilities.

For Meituan Jiujiu, the real opponent is still Ctrip, not AI itself.

---

## What will Meituan lose after the AI entrance becomes stronger?

A more realistic impact is that the entrance value is repriced.

One of the values of the platform in the past was that users took the initiative to open the app.

If the AI assistant becomes a super portal, users may no longer remember which platform they finally placed an order from, and will only care about "whether the AI recommended results are cheap, fast, and reliable."

This will bring about three changes.

### First, brand mentality becomes weak

What users used to think was “open Meituan and order takeout”.

In the future, it may become "let AI help me find the fastest and most cost-effective takeaway nearby."

This undermines the sanity of the platform as the first point of entry.

### Second, platforms may be forced to compare prices more transparently

AI is best at comparison.

Once the user authorizes the AI to view prices on multiple platforms at the same time, subsidies, delivery fees, and membership rights will all be spread out on the table.

This is not a good thing for the platform’s pricing power.

### Third, merchants’ advertising budgets may be redirected

If the AI assistant masters the entrance to consumption decisions, merchants may shift from "buying platform advertising rankings" to "optimizing AI recommendation results."

This will affect the long-term monetization of Meituan’s in-store and takeaway advertising.

However, these changes will take time and rely on AI assistants to truly become the entrance to high-frequency consumption.

Until then, Meituan’s most important assets remain its merchants, riders and fulfillment network.

---

## Conclusion of this article

AI will change Meituan, but it will not easily bypass Meituan.

Takeaway is not a web search. The core is not who answers smarter, but who can deliver the meal stably.

Liquor travel is more likely to be changed by AI, but supply-side contracting, inventory, pricing and after-sales still require a platform.

For Meituan, AI is more like two things stacked together:

On one side are efficiency tools that help it optimize scheduling, recommendations, customer service and merchant operations;

On the one hand, there are entrance variables, which may weaken the app’s intelligence, compress advertising value, and intensify cross-platform price comparisons.

Therefore, the judgment of Meituan’s moat should be divided into layers:

**AI will impact the entrance, but it is difficult to replace contract fulfillment; AI will redistribute profits, but it does not mean to eliminate the platform. **

The real danger is not when users ask AI to order takeout.

The real danger is that one day users no longer care who sends it, and the only competition between platforms is price, speed and subsidies.
