## 2. Segment Valuation

### 2.1 Core local business (subject value)

**Core question**: Actual loss in 2025 is 6.9 billion, current profitability = 0. Valuation must determine when/if recovery can occur.

**Three ideas of normalized profits**:

| Ideas | Basis | Normalized CLB OP |
|------|------|--------------|
| A. Optimism (the war will end in H2 in 2026) | 52.4 billion in 2024 is the real profitability | **45-50 billion** |
| B. Neutral (OP rate permanently lowered to 15%) | Competition normalized, share maintained but profit margin reduced | **39-42 billion** |
| C. Pessimism (long-term losses or low single-digit OP rate) | Douyin + JD + Pinduoduo continue to attack | **10-20 billion** |

**Historical OP curve reference**:
- 2022: 29.5 billion → 2023: 38.7 billion → 2024: 52.4 billion → 2025: **-6.9 billion**
- The growth curve of CAGR 33% in the past three years has been directly interrupted

This report adopts A/B/C assumptions in the three scenarios respectively.

**PE multiple basis**:
- DoorDash EV/EBITDA 25x, Uber PE 30x, JD.com PE 10-12x
- Meituan’s moat is stronger than JD.com’s but its growth rate has been broken; Hong Kong stocks are valued at a discount
- Give **PE 12-15x** (reasonable multiple of post-war recovery period)

## 5. Counterarguments

1. **The food delivery war is not a "one-off" but a "new normal"** (30% probability): If JD.com and Douyin continue to subsidize for 2-3 years, the OP rate may be stuck below 15% for a long time, and the normalized profit will need to be significantly reduced.
2. **In-store is structurally eroded by Douyin** (probability 25%): The closed loop of Douyin’s short video planting + group buying is a dimensionality-reducing blow to Meituan’s in-store, and the revenue from dimensional in-store may decline by 20-30% in 5 years.
3. **Hong Kong stock valuation system is suppressed for a long time** (probability 40%): Even if the fundamentals recover, the PE multiple may stay at 10-12x for a long time
4. **New business valuation may be too high** (30% probability): Meituan Youxuan continues to lose blood, and Keeta’s expansion outside the Middle East has not been verified
5. **AI/Unmanned Delivery Capital Expenditure Devours FCF** (Probability 20%): Wang Xing has stated that he will continue to invest heavily in AI, and FCF may not be as expected from 2026 to 2028.

## 6. Conclusion

### Reasonable value range (weighted based on three scenarios)

- **Weighted Center**: **131 HKD**
- **Range**: 68-187 HKD (the span is very large, reflecting the high uncertainty of the war trend)

### Buy and sell price

- **Buy price**: **90-100 HKD** (safe margin range close to neutral scenario)
- **Heavy position price**: **70-80 HKD** (close to a pessimistic scenario, with limited downward space)
- **Selling price**: **Above 170 HKD** (close to optimistic scenario)

### What do you think of the current stock price (85.75 HKD)?

- Market capitalization 529 billion − Net cash 193.8 billion = **EV 335.2 billion HKD = 274 billion RMB**
- **What are these 274 billion EV buying**:
  - A business with a loss of 17 billion in 2025
  - But it is also a business with a profit of 45 billion in 2024 (CLB 524 - new business 73)
- **Implied Profitability**: If given 15x PE, the market implies "normalized annual profit ≈ 18 billion" - only 40% of the 2024 level

### Duan Yongping type judgment
- **Core question**: Is this food delivery war "cyclical" or "structural"?
- **Reason for cyclical bias**: JD.com and Douyin’s takeaway investment requires financial reporting, and it is impossible to burn money indefinitely; all O2O subsidy wars in history will eventually determine the winner (Didi, Ele.me, Pinduoduo vs. Taobao are all examples)
- **Structural reasons**: Douyin’s traffic is free (it comes with the short video ecosystem), and it can last longer than Meituan; in-store business is hard to recover after being hit by dimensionality reduction
- **Conclusion**: What you buy at the current price is not the "business itself", but the option of "when will the war end". If you can't understand this option, you can't take a heavy position.
