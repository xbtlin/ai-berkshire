# The whole conclusion

The history of shareholder returns since the United States Corps was listed can be divided into three stages:

** Phase I (2018-2020)**: During expansion period, sustained loss but understandable; the acquisition of Mobba is the main reduction.

** Phase II (2021-2022)**: The most intense period of strategic failure. The historical surge, the hundreds of billions of dollars in losses on community buy, antimonopoly fines, withdrawal, multiple profit accumulations, stock prices fell from a peak of HK$ 460 to about HK$80, and market values evaporated by over 90%. This is the most serious period of damage to the interests of the American shareholders.

** Phase III (2023-2024)**: Rationale period. Substantial reduction in losses, core profits at an all-time high, and first-time repurchase. This was the most friendly period in the history of the United States for shareholders, with stock prices rebounding from low to around HK$180 (more than three times higher).

** 2025 to date (phase IV): ** External shocks. The new round of take-out wars has led to a sharp deterioration in profits, with a projected significant loss throughout the year, and there is still a debate as to whether it is strategic or passive defensive or passive blood loss.
