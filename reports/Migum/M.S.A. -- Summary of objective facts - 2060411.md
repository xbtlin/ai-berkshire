## 5. New business status

### 5.1 Meituan Selection (Community Group Buying)
- June 2025: Suspended in many places, only Guangdong and Zhejiang remain
- December 17, 2025: Nationwide shutdown
- Cumulative investment: over 100 billion yuan

### 5.2 KeeTa (overseas business)
- Covering: Hong Kong, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, United Arab Emirates (5 countries)
- Hong Kong: first profitable month in October 2025, with market share of approximately 44%
- Saudi Arabia: 10% share in 5 months after launch, 20 cities, 13,000 restaurants, 15,000 riders
- Brazil: Plans to invest US$1 billion in 5 years

### 5.3 Little Elephant Supermarket/Meituan Grocery Shopping
- Covering 39 cities, plans to expand to all first- and second-tier cities
- 70% of Meituan’s preferred warehouses have been transformed into Xiaoxiang Supermarket front-end warehouses

### 5.4 Flash Sale/Instant Retail
- Flash warehouse target: more than 100,000 in 2027, with a scale of 200 billion yuan
- Daily orders for non-catering categories exceeded 18 million
