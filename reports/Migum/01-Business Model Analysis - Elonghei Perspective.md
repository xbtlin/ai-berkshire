# Three, the moat's analysis

##3.1 Network effects <unk> (4/5)

** The trilateral network effect does exist:** The more businesses choose the more orders they take, the more efficient the riders they deliver, the faster the users are satisfied.

** But the network effects have geographical boundaries:** The American family network effects are "local" and not "national". Shanghai's business density has no meaning for Beijing users. This means that competitors can break down across cities.

** Decrement factor:** The vibration has created an independent network effect (content creators, users, traders) in the shop business, bypassing the American regiment’s distribution network barrier. The GMV service was over $850 billion in 2025, increasing by nearly 60%, and the network effect is emerging.

#3.2 Conversion costs

** Side: very low. ** The cost of downloading an App is almost zero. The takeout user "what subsidy is more than what" is normal. The US membership system has a locking effect, but it is far less expensive than the cost of switching apple ecology.

** Business side: medium and low.** Catering houses have almost zero costs for simultaneous access to multi-platform platforms. Businesses that rely partly on American data analysis and operations tools have a certain switching cost, but do not constitute a strong barrier.

** Side of the rider: extremely low.** The rider can run multiple platforms simultaneously without any exclusive constraints.

The Eternity of the Eternity is the most concerned with the cost of conversion -- "The user cannot leave you, the real moat." The Corps is weak in this respect.

##3.3 Scale effect<unk> (4/5)

** The distribution density advantage is the strongest moat:** The United States Mission has established an instant distribution network in 2,800+ cities across the country, connecting an average of 3.36 million riders per month (2024 data).

2. Reduced rider rate

** But the advantage of scale is being eroded:** Ali is integrated through beebirds and poofs, and has reached sufficient order density in some cities. Kyoto, though small (7% per day) is a small volume, is more rapidly built on the infrastructure of Tokington logistics than expected.

#3.4 Brand mind #4/5

** The user of the "Extale = The American Corps" remains strong:** The Order maintains an absolute advantage in the market for the highest-middle-class single-priced meals. The share of the GTV market has stabilized at over 60%.

** But the mind is divided:**
- The new mind of "Steal to the shop = The sound" is emerging, especially among young users.
- The mind of "Affordable takeout = Price for Platforms" is replacing "Extreat = Legion."
- "Instant retail" is not stable.

#3.5 Technical barriers

** The dispatch algorithm is indeed leading:** The USS distribution system has been optimized for a decade, processing 150 million peak day orders, with an average of 34 minutes. R&D invested $26 billion in 2025, an increase of 23%.

** But technical barriers are overestimated: **
- The core of algorithms is the amount of data, not the algorithm itself. When the number of orders is large enough, the algorithm divides quickly narrows.
- The large model capabilities of the AI era are rapidly spreading and the threshold of the dispatch algorithms is lowering
