## 3. Detailed explanation of the three most critical dimensions

### Dimension 1: Social value/externality (biggest difference)

**Education and Training: Selling Anxiety, High Negative Externalities**
- Arms race ("Haidian Mama" phenomenon)
- Parents have been robbed for a long time
-Indirect suppression of fertility rate
- People's Daily has repeatedly named "creating anxiety" since 2019

**Takeaway: People’s livelihood convenience, positive externalities**
- Employment reservoir (13 million riders, 6 times prefecture-level city civil servants)
- User convenience (600 million rigid needs)
- Empowering small and medium-sized businesses (millions of restaurants go online)
- New real-time retail formats (supporting the 30-minute economic circle)

**Conclusion**: The existence of education and training itself is regarded as negative, and the existence of take-out itself is regarded as positive. This determines that the direction of supervision is very different - the former is to eliminate, and the latter is to regulate.

### Dimension 2: National strategic status

**Three red lines are violated in education and training (hard violations)**:
1. Educational Equity (Core of Common Wealth)
2. Fertility rate (population strategy)
3. Common Prosperity (2021 Supreme Principle)

**The takeaway fits the four major strategies (hard fit)**:
1. Stable employment (13 million jobs)
2. Digital Economy (14th Five-Year Plan)
3. Expand domestic demand (takeaway = catering consumption carrier)
4. Tolerance of new business formats (the State Council "accepts rather than suppresses")

**Conclusion**: Education and training is a counter-strategy, while takeaway is a compliant strategy.

### Dimension 3: Capitalization level (takeaways need to be most vigilant)

**Education and Training**:
- Financing >$100 billion 2018-2021
- Industrialization of elevator advertising
- **"Capital breeds anxiety, and anxiety feeds capital"** → Condemned to "disorderly expansion"

**Takeaway**:
- Three companies will burn 173 billion in cash in 2025

- 2025-06 Article 14 of the Anti-French Law is specially legislated for this purpose
- Alibaba 18.2 billion in 2021 + Meituan 3.442 billion fine → The industry did not collapse after the penalty

**Conclusion**: Capitalization is the most similar aspect of food delivery to education and training, but it is completely different in nature. The current supervision is "mildly squeezing the bubble", not "uprooting" it.
