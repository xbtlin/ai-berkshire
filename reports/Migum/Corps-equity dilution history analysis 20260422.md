## 2. In-depth analysis of key events

### 1. IPO (September 2018) – Starting point: Flawed shareholding structure

**Facts**:
- Issue price of HK$69, financing of HK$32.6 billion, listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on September 20, 2018
- **AB share dual structure** is established upon listing: 1 share of Class A stock = 10 votes, 1 share of Class B stock = 1 vote
- Wang Xing holds approximately 11.4% of the shares, but controls approximately **46% of the voting rights** through Class A shares

**Why do this**:
- AB shares are a standard arrangement for VCs/founders to maintain control. Tencent (20.1%) and Sequoia (11.4%) both hold Class B shares
- Wang Xing permanently locked in control of the company with less than 12% of the shares, and the small and medium-sized shareholders actually had no governance rights from the first day of listing.

**Impact on shareholders**:
- Small and medium shareholders "bought the company but have no control over the company"
- All major decisions thereafter (financing, burning money through community group buying, food delivery wars) cannot be checked and balanced by ordinary shareholders

### 3. Super issuance in April 2021 - the most destructive dilution event

This is the single most influential incident in the history of Meituan's equity dilution, and it is also the largest corporate issuance in the history of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

**Specific data**:
- Placement of **187 million Class B shares** at a placement price of HK$273.8 per share (a discount of approximately 5.3% from the closing price of the day)
- Simultaneous issuance of **US$2.98 billion zero-coupon convertible bonds** (conversion price HK$431.24, due in 2027/2028)
- Total funds raised **approximately US$10 billion (approximately HK$65 billion)**
- Dilution by rights issue: approximately **3.2% of share capital, 1.5% of voting rights**
- If all convertible bonds are converted: additional dilution of approximately **1% of equity**
- Comprehensive dilution (placement + debt): approximately **3.6-5%**

**Why did you choose to do this in April 2021? **

| Reason | Analysis |
|------|------|
| ① The stock price is near a historical high | The stock price reached ~HK$320 in February 2021, and was still in the range of HK$273-290 in April, which is the "window period" for financing |
| ② The money-burning demand for community group buying | Meituan Select will lose about 38.4 billion yuan in 2021, which is a major financial black hole |
| ③ Investment in unmanned vehicles/drones | Officially claimed to be used for cutting-edge technology research and development |
| ④ Competitive pressure | Alibaba, Pinduoduo, and Byte are all expanding, and Meituan needs ammunition reserves |
| ⑤ Opportunistic financing | Issuance at a high price is a normal business judgment - management sells equity when they think it is expensive, while investors buy at a relatively high price |

**Subsequent stock price trend - losses of placees of rights issue**:

| Time point | Stock price | vs placement price of HK$273.8 |
|--------|------|----------------|
| April 2021 (at the time of placement) | HKD 273.8 | — |

| 2022 low | ~55-60 HKD | **-78%** |

> **Conclusion**: Institutional investors who subscribed for the rights issue at HK$273.8 have lost approximately 69% of their holdings so far. This batch of financing was used for community group buying, but it was later proven that the value was destroyed.

### 4. Continuous equity incentive (SBC) - chronic dilution

**Annual dilution estimate**:
- Meituan’s SBC expenses over the years have accounted for approximately 2-4% of revenue, with an absolute amount of billions of Hong Kong dollars per year.
- A single grant of approximately **46.04 million RSU** in April 2023, worth approximately HK$6 billion (the stock price was approximately HK$130 at the time)
- In the five years from 2019 to 2023, the total share capital increased from approximately 5.78 billion shares to 6.24 billion shares, with a **net increase of 460 million shares (+7.9%)**. After excluding rights issues, the average annual SBC dilution is approximately 0.5-1%

**Management response (2024 onwards)**:
- Wang Xing made it clear: The goal is to completely offset SBC dilution with repurchase**
- In 2024, a total of approximately 261 million shares will be repurchased and 115.8 million shares will be canceled (August 2024)
- The total equity in 2024 will see a net decrease for the first time (approximately -3.15%)

## 4. Why does the management do this?

**Core logic**: Behind Meituan’s dilution decision is clear strategic prioritization.

1. **Growth is prioritized over shareholder returns**: In the 6 years before listing (2018-2023), Meituan used all its financing capabilities for expansion - food delivery, bicycles, Youxuan, KeeTa, autonomous vehicles, no dividends, no buybacks, and shareholder interests came last.

2. **High-level financing is normal capital market behavior**: Issuance near historical highs in 2021 is the optimal capital decision from the company's perspective; the price will be borne by the subscriber, which is not Meituan's "mistake", but it is a fact that the shareholding ratio of the original shareholders has been diluted.

3. **SBC is the standard for technology companies**: It is impossible to attract top talents with low wages, and equity incentives are inevitable; the key lies in whether to use buybacks to hedge - Meituan will only begin to seriously implement this strategy in 2024.

4. **Strategic mistakes lead to the destruction of financing capital**: If the community group buying succeeds, the $10 billion financing in 2021 is a stroke of genius; if it fails, most of the money will be lost, and the dilution costs will be borne entirely by shareholders.

## 7. Evaluation from investors’ perspective

**Core conclusion**: Meituan’s equity dilution problem is not about “quantity”, but about “quality”.

- The dilution itself (+8.7%, five years) is not serious, Tencent also had SBC dilution during the same period
- **The real problem is: the funds raised are diluted and a large amount is destroyed on community group buying**
- If the US$10 billion financing in 2021 is used for buyback (the stock price is about HK$270 at the time), it will reduce about 370 million shares; if it is used for community group buying, the loss will exceed 100 billion yuan - the value difference between the two uses is as high as hundreds of billions of Hong Kong dollars.
- The dual-class share structure leaves small and medium-sized shareholders with no ability to check and balance the entire process.

> Munger: "Capital allocation is the most important job of a CEO." Meituan's capital allocation from 2018 to 2023 has left more lessons than value.
