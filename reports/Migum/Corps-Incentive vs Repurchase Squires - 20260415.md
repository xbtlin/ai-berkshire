## 3. Judgment

### 1. Has management "worked for shareholders" over the past 3 years?

**The answer sheet is beautiful in 2024, but there is a question mark in 2025. **

- **2023**: Wang Xing made it clear that his goal is to "hedge employee incentive dilution with buybacks." However, buybacks were only launched in December that year, and a HK$6 billion gift package was just awarded in November. **The net dilution for the whole year has not been truly hedged**.
- **2024**: Repurchased 28.158 billion Hong Kong dollars (approximately US$3.6 billion) and 235 million shares, **far exceeding the fair value of incentive grants that year** (estimated at HK$7 billion, approximately 25% of the repurchase). It also clearly shifted to "cancellation-type repurchase". In February, 44.1311 million shares were canceled at one time, and Mu Rongjun took the initiative to convert shares and cancel 13.3866 million shares. This is ** real money is on the side of shareholders**.
- **2025**: The food delivery war lost 23.4 billion yuan, and buybacks plunged from HK$28.158 billion to **only HK$392 million in the first three quarters** (-98.6% year-on-year). Although the company explained that the 141 billion yuan in cash was "sufficient",** when the pressure of losses came, repurchases were the first to be cut, but incentives continued to increase - this exposed that "repurchase hedging" was not a rigid commitment, but a procyclical action.

### 2. Is the annual net dilution positive or negative?

| Year | Direction of Net Dilution | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | Slightly positive (~0) | Granted ≈ repurchase activation amount, basically tied |
| 2024 | **Significantly negative** (anti-dilution) | Repurchase amounts 3-4x incentive fair value |
| 2025 | **High probability of turning positive** | Buybacks are suspended, incentives are still being granted (6.34 million shares in January + 79.31 million shares in April) |

### 3. 2026-04 Granted 79.3176 million shares. Vertically large/small/normal?

** is too large, close to the historical single limit. **

- 2023-11 A single grant of ~46.04 million shares (fair value ~HK$6 billion, corresponding stock price of HK$130.9)
- 4 tranches for the whole year of 2024, totaling ~6,970 million HKD fair value
- The batch size will be significantly reduced during 2025 (6.34 million shares in January only ~500 million Hong Kong dollars)
- **2026-04 This 79.3176 million shares with a fair value of HK$6.857 billion is the largest single grant in the past 3 years**
  - The number of shares is ~72% larger than that in 2023
  - The amount is also larger ~14% (although the stock price is only 66% at the time)
  - **Interpretation**: The stock price is at a low of HK$86.45 (66% of HK$130.9 in 2023). The company uses the method of "more shares but similar amount" to retain people, which in disguise increases the intensity of dilution

**Warning Signs**:
- Low stock price + large grants (amplification of dilution effect) + buyback suspension = **Net dilution in 2026 may be the worst year in 3 years**
- If large-amount repurchases are not resumed throughout 2026, Wang Xing’s 2024 commitment to use repurchase hedging to stimulate investment will be quietly shelved

## 6. One sentence of conclusion

**In 2024, Wang Xing used HK$28.1 billion in repurchases to prove "working for shareholders", but in 2025, the takeout war cut the repurchases to almost zero, and the incentives did not hit the brakes at the same time. In 2026-04, nearly 8 billion Hong Kong dollars in fair value RSUs were once again granted - the narrative of "repurchase hedging incentives" is weakening, and needs to be reported in the 2025 annual report (2026-03 has been issued) and 2026 Continue to verify the annual repurchase progress. **
