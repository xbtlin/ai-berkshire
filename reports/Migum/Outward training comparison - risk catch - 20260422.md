## 2. Five major social problems that may cause food delivery to be rectified

### Problem 1: Rider death from overwork/accident death (the most severe)
**Cases that have occurred**:
- 2020-12 Ele.me rider Han died suddenly while delivering orders
- 2021 "China News Weekly": Rider 1 order every 2.5 hours, 12 hours+ per day
- Riders were injured or killed in extreme weather in 2024

**Why the intensity is the highest**: Death events **Strong emotional mobilization** + Family petitions + Vulnerable group narratives = Supervision is highly sensitive.

**Possible trigger**: A major mass death and injury (>10 riders killed) → CCTV for 3 consecutive days → People’s Daily commentator → Review by the Central Commission for Deepening Reform

**Policy Focus**: Compulsory social security, maximum working hours restrictions, order suspension due to extreme weather, algorithm bans and penalties, labor relations determination

### Issue 2: Systemic issues in food safety
**Cases that have occurred**: 2021 Ele.me Black Workshop, 2023 Unlicensed Takeaway Shop, 315 Party named multiple times

**Why it is easy to intervene**: The "Food Safety Law" and "Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Food Safety in Online Catering Services" are complete, and **no new legislation is needed for supervision**.

**Possible trigger**: **Thousands of people in a certain city were collectively poisoned + died** + Systematic audit failure on the platform

**Policy starting points**: raising the threshold for merchants, forcing bright kitchens, platform joint and several liability, and nationwide network of blacklists

### Problem 3: Algorithm Squeezing/Rider Trapped System
**Cases that have occurred**: 2020 "Talk delivery riders trapped in the system" (**Iconic explosive article**), 2021 CCTV "Algorithm Dilemma", 2025 multiple CCTV reports

**Why senior leaders are concerned**: **"Platforms cannot override people"** is a position that Xi Jinping has emphasized many times.

**Possible trigger**: Algorithm-induced mass tragedy + in-depth academic research + special regulatory investigation

**Policy Focus**: Algorithm registration disclosure, third-party independent audit, prohibition of "immediate penalties for overtime", and algorithm appeal rights

### Question 4: Businessmen are desperate/small and medium-sized restaurants are closing down
**Data that has occurred**:
- The profit margin of catering merchants will be reduced from 32% to 24% in 2025
- **71% of merchants blame subsidy war**
- Tens of thousands of restaurants closing every month

**Why they are underestimated**: Merchant rights protection lacks emotional impact, but **merchants = tax sources + employment sources**, local governments are most concerned about it.

**Possible triggers**: Large-scale collective rights protection + National People’s Congress deputies’ proposals + CCTV’s “Economic Half Hour” exposure

**Policy Tips**: Minimum commission rate, upper subsidy limit, T+3 settlement, prohibition of choosing one from the other

### Problem 5: Over-dependence of minors
**Phenomena that have occurred**: The proportion of takeaways among middle school students/college students has increased, and the obesity rate has increased

**Why lurking**: **A precedent has been set to prevent game addiction in 2021**, and the inter-ministerial linkage model of the Health Commission + Ministry of Education + State Administration of Municipal Supervision has matured.

**Possible triggers**: Youth health white paper + parent complaints + special investigation by the National Health Commission

**Policy Tips**: No entry within 500 meters of campus, real-name limit for minors, late night restrictions after 23:00
