## Scale effect <unk> (4/5): Distribution density is the hardest barrier

The Corps has set up a live distribution network in 2,800+ cities across the country. High density brings with it three cost advantages:

** The single distribution cost falls with density** — for every additional unit in the same area, the rider's rate drops slightly.
** Multi-grade reuse** — Same rider delivering take-out, flash order, medicine delivery, infrastructure marginal cost approaching zero
** Scheduler algorithms are continuously optimized under big data** - Sun peak 150 million single training data, which is difficult for new players to copy

This is the strongest moat in the United States. The distribution network is "work and work" and is long-lived, capital-intensive and complex to operate.
