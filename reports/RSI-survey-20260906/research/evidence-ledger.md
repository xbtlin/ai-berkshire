## Repository audit tooling

The repository's `tools/report_audit.py extract` was run on both manuscript files with `--ratio 1 --seed 42 --dry-run`. It extracted zero financial data points. This is an applicability limitation of the finance-oriented tool, not a successful numerical audit of the research. The numerical claims above were therefore checked directly against the cited original-paper contexts; structural citation checks and PDF layout verification are separate.
