>
> Estimated reading time: about 40 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

The phrase "Ear Time" has a peculiar presence on the Chinese internet - people have heard of it, but most cannot say what it actually is.

- Some people think it is an audio-content company
- Some people think it is a podcast platform
- Some people only remember the name from a single article

**The first thing this series does is identify the true nature of "Ear Time"** - it is not a company, but the title of an industry report. From there, we move on to the real structure of China's podcast market.

## Writing Principles

1. **First understand what it is, then discuss whether it is worth it** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Cite the source of every data point** - if data is unreliable, leave it blank instead of filling it in
4. **Do not invent data** - many key podcast-industry metrics are not publicly disclosed, so estimates are explicitly labeled
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
