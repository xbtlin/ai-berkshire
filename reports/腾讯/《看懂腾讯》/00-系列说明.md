## Why We Wrote This Series

Tencent is one of the largest companies in China by market cap, one of the most closely followed by institutions, and one of the most heavily covered by research. It is also one of the most misunderstood.

- Some people treat it as a **social company** and miss that gaming is the real profit engine.
- Some people treat it as a **gaming company** and miss the network effects of the WeChat ecosystem.
- Some people treat it as an **internet platform** and miss the RMB 1 trillion of equity assets sitting on its balance sheet.
- Some people treat it as an **AI laggard** and miss its "AI in All" presence across ads, entry points, and e-commerce use cases.
- Some people treat it as a **pure value stock** and miss its annual RMB 180 billion of free cash flow and RMB 120 billion of shareholder returns.

Each view is correct on its own. Put them together and you get the real Tencent. A single 5,000-word article cannot hold it all, so we split the story into **8 articles**, each focused on one main thread. Together they form a complete picture.
