## Key Highlights

**Gaming empire goes global**: overseas game revenue reached RMB 77.4 billion (+33%), crossing USD 10 billion. Supercell (84% owned) generated EUR 2.8 billion of revenue in 2024 (+77%). Tencent has 14 evergreen titles with annual gross billings above RMB 4 billion. China CR2 reached 78% (Tencent 50%+ NetEase 28%).

**Advertising is the strongest growth engine**: Video Account ad revenue in Q4 grew 60% year over year. The WeChat ecosystem's "social-content-advertising-commerce" loop is now in place. In five years, it could rise from No. 3 to No. 2, with market share of 18-20%.

**Video Account commerce**: GMV reached RMB 200-250 billion (+92%), with 85% white-label merchants. In five years it could become China's fourth-largest e-commerce platform, with GMV above RMB 1 trillion.

**Profit quality keeps improving**: gross margin rises from 53% to 60%, net margin exceeds 30%, and adjusted net profit in 2030 is projected at about RMB 430 billion.

## Tencent in Five Years

**A global tech giant with revenue above RMB 1 trillion and net profit above RMB 400 billion.**

- A globalized gaming empire: overseas revenue mix above 40%, a true global No. 1
- Deeper WeChat monetization: advertising near RMB 300 billion, Video Account commerce above RMB 1 trillion GMV
- An AI-driven efficiency machine: AI embedded across all businesses, annual R&D plus capex above RMB 200 billion
- Industrial synergy from a RMB 1 trillion investment portfolio
- Maintaining No. 1 positions in gaming, social, and payments in China and globally
