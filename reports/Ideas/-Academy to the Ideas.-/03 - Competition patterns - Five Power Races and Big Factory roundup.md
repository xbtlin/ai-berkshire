## The cruel reality of China’s large model track

Large models are currently one of the most fiercely competitive fields in China: there are four mountains pressing on our heads, and four start-up companies with a valuation of more than 100 billion are eyeing it. Where can Zhipu AI come out?

In May 2026, China's large model racing circuit has formed two camps:

**First camp: Dachang Legion**
- ByteDance (Doubao), Alibaba (Tongyi Qianwen/Qwen), Baidu (Wenxinyiyan), Tencent (Hunyuan)
- Features: Money, data, traffic, and scenarios

**Second Camp: Top Five Entrepreneurs**
- DeepSeek, Wisdom AI, MiniMax, Dark Side of the Moon (Moonshot/Kimi), StepFun
- Features: Sharp technology, skyrocketing valuation, but lack of scenarios and traffic

(Source: 36Kr, Sina Finance, 21 Economic Network)

### DeepSeek: The number one threat to intelligence

If you can only choose one opponent to be wary of, the answer is **DeepSeek**.

Direct comparison between DeepSeek and Zhipu:

| Dimensions | Wisdom Spectrum | DeepSeek |
|------|------|----------|
| Technical level | GLM-5.1, first echelon | DeepSeek-V4, first echelon |
| Open source strategy | MIT fully open source | MIT fully open source |
| API Pricing | Medium | **Extremely Low** (the lowest in the industry) |
| Commercial revenue | 724 million yuan (2025) | Expected to exceed 5 billion yuan in 2026 |
| C-end products | Zhipu Qingyan (MAU ~8.38 million) | DeepSeek App (phenomenal growth) |
| Financing | Listed financing of HK$5.2 billion | Long-term self-owned funds, first financing reported in 2026 |
| Team Background | Tsinghua Academic School | Quantitative Fund Magic Square, Engineering + Algorithm |
| Cost Control | General | **Ultimate** ("Train a stronger model with less money") |

(Source: public information of each company, CNBC, Securities Times)

The threat DeepSeek poses to intelligence is **all-encompassing**:

1. **Technically not lagging behind**——The comprehensive capabilities of DeepSeek-V4 are at the same level as GLM-5.1
2. **Cost crushing** - DeepSeek’s API pricing is much lower than that of Zhipu, which directly threatens Zhipu’s API revenue
3. **Stronger in terms of brand** - DeepSeek's "DeepSeek moment" in early 2025 allowed it to gain global attention, and its brand voice far exceeded that of Zhipu.
4. **Financially healthier** - DeepSeek is backed by quantitative fund Magic Square, has abundant cash, and is not in a hurry to go public for financing.

**Contrary view**: The advantage of DeepSeek is "cheapness", but cheapness is not a permanent moat. Zhipu has advantages that DeepSeek does not have in terms of domestic chip adaptation (Huawei Ascend) and government and enterprise customers - many government and state-owned enterprise customers will not choose an AI company with a quantitative fund background, but prefer to choose Zhipu with "Tsinghua University + state-owned assets background".

### MiniMax: Hong Kong stock competition on the same track

MiniMax and Zhipu were listed on the Hong Kong stock market almost at the same time, and their market capitalization is also in the order of HK$300 billion. The difference between the two:

| Dimension | Wisdom Spectrum | MiniMax |
|------|------|---------|
| Model Positioning | Full-Stack Flagship | Cost Efficiency Priority |
| Income structure | Mainly ToB (74%) | Pay equal attention to ToC and API |
| Overseas layout | API coverage in 218 countries | Overseas products (Talkie, etc.) have real users |
| Team Gene | Tsinghua Academic | Born in Shangtang, stronger commercialization |

MiniMax's C-end products (Talkie, etc.) have a real user base overseas, which Zhipu does not have. But in terms of ToB enterprise services, Zhipu’s customers are of higher quality (selected by 9 of the top 10 Internet companies).

### Dark Side of the Moon/Kimi: Winner on the C side

Kimi's performance on the C-side is far better than that of Zhipu Qingyan - it entered the market with the differentiated function of "long text processing" and gained a large number of students and knowledge workers. But Kimi is far inferior to Zhipu in terms of ToB.

In fact, the degree of competition overlap between the two is not high - **Zhipu plays ToB and Kimi plays ToC**. They are not direct competitors in the short term.

## Dachang Hunting: The dimensionality reduction attack faced by Zhipu

The competition among start-up companies is about "competing on the same level of technology", while the competition among large manufacturers is about "dimensionality reduction and attack".

### Bytedance/Doubao: traffic crushing

| Dimensions | Clear Words of Wisdom | Bean Bags |
|------|---------|------|
| MAU | ~8.38 million | **227 million** |
| Traffic source | Independent App | Douyin Ecosystem (700 million+ daily users) |
| Scene | Independent conversation | Embed short video, search, e-commerce, office |
| Model capabilities | GLM-5.1 (top) | Byte self-research (rapid iteration) |

**The difference is 27 times**. This is not because Zhipu is "not doing well" - but on the C-side, it is almost impossible for an AI company without a traffic portal to win.

### Alibaba/Tongyi Qianwen: The head-on collision of open source ecology

Qwen is Zhipu’s biggest competitor in the open source field:

| Dimensions | GLM | Qwen Qwen |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Open Source License | MIT | Apache 2.0 |
| Model matrix | GLM series | Qwen series (more abundant) |
| Global Token Share | Competing with Qwen | **2.6%** (and growing rapidly) |
| Resources behind | Tsinghua + State-owned Assets | Alibaba Cloud (the third largest cloud in the world) |

The advantage of Tongyi Qianwen is that Alibaba Cloud's global infrastructure can directly provide deployment and distribution channels for Qwen. Zhipu does not have its own cloud platform, and the API needs to rely on third parties.

### Baidu/Wen Xinyiyan: ToB’s old rival

Baidu is the earliest in the field of AI, and the Wenxin model has been deeply integrated in search, autonomous driving (Apollo), cloud services and other scenarios. Baidu's ToB capabilities and government-enterprise relationships are direct competition with Zhipu.

However, Baidu's market share has been eroded in recent years - there are more and more voices saying that "Baidu is falling behind". Zhipu may have overtaken Baidu in terms of technology generation (GLM-5.1 vs Wenxin 4.5).

## What is the ecological niche of Zhipu?

Looking at the competitive landscape, Zhipu’s positioning gradually becomes clear:

| Dimensions | Location of Wisdom Spectrum |
|------|-----------|
| C-side | Already out (crushed by big manufacturers) |
| Global API Market | Second Echelon (DeepSeek Leads) |
| Chinese Government and Enterprise ToB | **First Echelon** (Tsinghua University + State-owned Assets Background Bonus Points) |
| Open source ecosystem | First echelon (tied with Qwen and DeepSeek) |
| AI Agent | First mover but no established advantage |
| Domestic chip adaptation | **Unique Advantages** (Huawei Ascend Deep Adaptation) |

**Zhipu's ecological niche is "the preferred AI base model provider in China's government and enterprise market"** - it relies not only on technology, but also on non-technical factors such as Tsinghua brand, state-owned assets background, and domestic chip adaptation.

How big is this ecological niche? The good news is that China’s government and enterprise market is huge, but the bad news is that competitors are also pouring in (Baidu, Alibaba, and Huawei’s own Pangu model).

**Contrary view**: If Zhipu can only win in the "Government-Enterprise ToB" track, then its ceiling is a traditional IT service provider, not the "Chinese version of Anthropic". Anthropic is worth $60 billion because its APIs are used by developers around the world. If Zhipu cannot occupy a place in the global open API market, its valuation of HK$450 billion will be difficult to sustain.

## Three judgments on competitive landscape

**Judgment 1: The pattern of the top five entrepreneurship companies is unstable, and there is a high probability that there will be laggards in the next two years**

The valuations of the five companies are all over 100 billion, but most of them are still suffering huge losses. Once the financing environment tightens or there is a major bifurcation of technology routes, lower-ranked companies may be marginalized or acquired.

**Judgment 2: The final advantages of large manufacturers will become more and more obvious**

The training cost of AI models will continue to decrease, but the advantages of distribution channels and scenarios cannot be copied by startups. Byte's Douyin, Alibaba's Taobao + DingTalk, Tencent's WeChat - these traffic entrances determine the outcome of the C-side. Startups can only find space in the ToB and API markets.

**Judgment 3: The biggest moat of Wisdom may not be technology, but "identity"**

Tsinghua University + state-owned background + domestic chip adaptation - in the industrial environment with Chinese characteristics, the trust and policy dividends brought by this "identity" may be more commercially valuable than model evaluation scores.

**Opposition**: ID cards are not permanently valid. If DeepSeek continues to win in pure commercial competition, government and enterprise customers will eventually vote with their feet. The competitiveness of technology and products is the long-term barrier.
