# Open source strategy: smart but costly

The IMW uses **MIT agreements are open-source** — the most liberal open-source license, meaning that anyone can use, modify, commercialize models of the IMW free of charge.

Open source strategy comparison
|------------|------|----------|-------------|---------|

The moon needs authorization for more than 700 million.

(Source: Model official GitHub warehouse)

The benefits of open-source strategies are evident: rapid development of developers' ecology, upgrading of brand influence, and attracting pilot payments from business clients.

** But the cost is also clear: you give the most core technology free to everyone – including your competitors. DeepSeek and General Ask are equally open-sourced, and the technology differences between the three parties are being quickly leveled.

The end of open-source competition is not "who's best model" -- but ** who can build the strongest commercial ecology around open-source models** (API platform, business services, application combinations). On this point, the intellectual spectrum is now in the middle of DeepSeek and General Questions.
