#Huang Renxun’s AI five-layer cake: Who is making money on each layer?

> Study date: June 5, 2026 | 16-way parallel research integration

---

## Frame definition

When Jen-Hsun Huang had a conversation with BlackRock CEO Larry Fink at the 2025 Davos Forum, he defined the AI industry as a "five-layer cake":

```
Layer 5 ┃ Application layer ┃ AI Agent, autonomous driving, robots, industry solutions
Layer 4 ┃ Model layer ┃ Large language model, multi-modal model
Layer 3 ┃ Infrastructure layer ┃ AI factory, data center, network, cooling
Layer 2 ┃ Chip layer ┃ GPU, accelerator, HBM memory, advanced packaging
Layer 1 ┃ Energy layer ┃ Electricity supply, transformers, nuclear energy
```

His core point of view: AI is not a product, but an entire new industrial system. There are huge investment opportunities at every level, and the lower the level, the more irreplaceable it is.

He called the data center an "AI factory" - "Only one thing is done every day: producing tokens." In the future, every company will have two factories, one for product production and one for AI.

## Layer 1: Energy layer - the most underestimated bottleneck

**Core logic:** The end of AI computing power is electricity. Global data center electricity consumption will be 485TWh in 2025 and is expected to double to 950TWh in 2030. Nearly half of the planned data center construction projects in the United States have been delayed or canceled due not to lack of GPUs but to lack of power and transformers.

**Bottleneck data:** The delivery time of large power transformers has been extended to 2-4 years, and the delivery time of high-voltage substations is 3-5 years.

### Representative companies (5 companies)

| Company | Positioning | Key Data |
|------|------|----------|
| **Eaton** | Electrical equipment leader | Data center power distribution system leads the world, benefiting from transformer shortage |
| **GE Vernova** | Power equipment + natural gas power generation | Benefit from the energy company spun off from GE, transformer + gas turbine dual line |
| **Constellation Energy** | Nuclear power operator | The largest nuclear power operator in the United States, signed a nuclear power supply agreement with Microsoft |
| **NuScale Power** | Small modular nuclear reactor (SMR) | The only NRC-certified SMR design for long-term power supply for data centers |
| **TBEA** | China's leader in transformers | The number of transformers exported from China to the United States will surge from 1,500 units in 2022 to 8,000+ units in 2025 |

**Reason for selection:** The four-year delivery time of the transformer means that this bottleneck cannot be alleviated before 2028. Nuclear power is the ultimate answer to long-term power supply for AI data centers. This layer has the highest certainty, the lowest attention, and the most reasonable valuation.

## Layer 2: Chip layer - the most value-intensive battlefield

**Core logic:** The AI accelerator market is expected to exceed US$150 billion in 2026. Nvidia still accounts for about 75-80% of the market share, but the growth rate of custom ASICs (+45%) exceeds that of general-purpose GPUs (+16%) for the first time. The "de-Nvidiaization" of ultra-large-scale customers is a structural trend.

### Representative companies (5 companies)

| Company | Positioning | Key Data |
|------|------|----------|
| **Nvidia** | AI GPU monopoly | FY2027 Q1 revenue is US$81.6 billion (+85%), data center is US$75.2 billion (+92%), gross profit margin is 75%, market value is US$5.2 trillion |
| **Broadcom** | King of customized ASICs | Q2 FY2026 AI revenue is 10.8 billion (+143%), designing custom chips for Google/Meta/OpenAI, and backlog of orders is 73 billion |
| **AMD** | The only general-purpose GPU challenger | Q1 2026 data center revenue is 5.8 billion (+57%), OpenAI chooses AMD to build 6GW computing power |
| **TSMC** | AI chip manufacturing monopoly | The global share of advanced manufacturing processes exceeds 90%, and CoWoS packaging will be fully loaded until 2026, which is the physical bottleneck of the entire chain |
| **SK Hynix** | HBM memory leader | HBM market share is 62%, Samsung plummeted from 41% to 17%, HBM is an indispensable "ammunition" for AI chips |

**Reason for selection:** Nvidia is the standard setter but faces the erosion of custom chips; Broadcom is the biggest winner of custom chips and is growing faster; AMD is the only alternative; TSMC and SK Hynix have blocked the physical bottleneck of the entire chain. These five companies together are the entire AI chip layer.

## Layer 3: Infrastructure Layer – The $725 Billion Arms Race

**Core logic:** The total capital expenditures of the four major hyperscale cloud vendors (Microsoft + Amazon + Google + Meta) in 2026 will be approximately US$725 billion, +97% year-on-year. No one is cutting investment, all are raising investment. The money is spent on servers, network equipment, data center construction and cooling systems.

### Representative companies (5 companies)

| Company | Positioning | Key Data |
|------|------|----------|
| **Arista Networks** | AI data center network switches | AI network revenue is growing rapidly, 800G/1.6T Ethernet solutions compete with Broadcom to replace InfiniBand |
| **Vertiv** | Data center power + liquid cooling | Ranked first in the global liquid cooling market share with 11.3%, NVL72 architecture forced liquid cooling is the core catalyst |
| **Zhongji InnoLight** | Global leader in AI optical modules | Revenue in 2025 is 38.2 billion yuan (+60%), net profit is 10.8 billion yuan (+109%), and 800G market share is about 50% |
| **Dell Technologies** | Leading in AI server shipments | AI server backlog exceeds US$9 billion, deeply tied to Nvidia |
| **CoreWeave** | Pure GPU cloud infrastructure | 2025 revenue of $5.13 billion (+170%), 99.4 billion backlog, but total debt of 21.4 billion, GAAP loss |

**Reason for selection:** Network (Arista), power cooling (Vertiv), optical interconnection (InnoLight), server (Dell), GPU cloud (CoreWeave) - covering the complete chain of the infrastructure layer from power to computing power delivery. Zhongji InnoLight is China’s company with the strongest pricing power in the entire AI industry chain.

**Core logic:** It only took 14 months for Anthropic's annualized revenue to rise from US$1 billion to US$47 billion. This may be the fastest corporate revenue growth in human business history. OpenAI’s monthly revenue is US$2 billion. The second half of 2026 will usher in a wave of intensive IPOs for AI companies (Anthropic has submitted confidentially, and OpenAI is targeting September).

### Representative companies (5 companies)

| Company | Positioning | Key Data |

**Reason for selection:** Anthropic and OpenAI are two commercial giants; Google has the most complete AI monetization closed loop (search + cloud + subscription); Meta takes the open source route and affects the entire ecosystem; DeepSeek proves the possibility of China catching up with the frontier at one-tenth the cost. xAI and Mistral are still small in scale and have not yet been selected.

## Layer 5: Application layer - who is really making money from AI?

**Core logic:** The proportion of AI revenue in most so-called "AI concept stocks" is still very low. There are only a few companies that really make money from AI, but their valuations are extremely different - Palantir P/E is 105 times, and Salesforce is only 14 times.

### Representative companies (5 companies)

| Company | Positioning | Key Data |
|------|------|----------|
| **Palantir** | AI decision-making operating system | Q1 2026 revenue of 1.63 billion (+85%), net profit margin 44%, AIP Boot Camp conversion rate 75%, but P/E 105x |
| **Salesforce** | AI Agent enterprise application | Agentforce ARR 1 billion+, 29,000 transactions, FY2026 total revenue 41.5 billion, P/E only 14x - the most cost-effective AI application target |
| **ServiceNow** | Enterprise AI workflow | Now Assist ACV target 1 billion+, subscription revenue +22%, ITSM monopoly position, P/E 25x |
| **Tesla** | Autonomous driving + robots | FSD subscribes to 1.28 million users, Robotaxi is already operating in Dallas/Houston, Optimus will be mass-produced in the summer of 2026, but P/E 317x |
| **Adobe** | AI creative tools | Firefly generates 24 billion+ assets, Fortune 500 adoption rate is 75%, but AI direct revenue is only 400 million, and Forward P/E 10x is the lowest |

**Reason for selection:** Palantir is the company with the fastest growth in AI application revenue (+85%); Salesforce is the most reasonably valued large-scale AI application target (P/E 14x); ServiceNow is the clearest case for enterprise AI monetization; Tesla represents the ultimate physical form of AI (self-driving + robots); Adobe is the definer of the AI creative track.

## Summary of investment perspective

### Investment opportunities sorted by certainty

| Priority | Hierarchy | Logic | Represent target |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Energy layer | The four-year delivery time of transformers cannot be alleviated, the most certain bottleneck | Eaton, GE Vernova |
| 2 | Chip layer | The "standard weight and measure" of AI computing power, the deepest moat | Nvidia (but valuation needs to be reviewed), Broadcom (faster growth) |
| 3 | Infrastructure layer | US$725 billion in capital expenditure is real money | Zhongji InnoLight, Vertiv, Arista |
| 4 | Model layer | The most explosive growth but most are not listed | Waiting for Anthropic/OpenAI IPO |
| 5 | Application layer | Few people really make money from AI | Salesforce (P/E 14x) and Adobe (P/E 10x) have the highest cost performance |

### One sentence

The lower the level, the more physical, and the more unavoidable the link, the higher the investment certainty. The sexiest layers (models, applications) are the least deterministic.
