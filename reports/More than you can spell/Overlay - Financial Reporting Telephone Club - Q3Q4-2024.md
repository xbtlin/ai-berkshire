##1.5 Q3 Major Strategic Initiatives (Official Notice)

** "Big 10 Billion Relief":**
- Introduction of 10 billion yuan in fee relief, benefiting over 10 million businesses
- Specific measures: refund of service costs, lower security deposits, exemption from logistics transit charges, upgrading of post-sale services by vendors

** "Climatic dealer westward" **
- Cancelling the cost of logistics trans-shipment of orders from vendors to remote areas in the west, to be fully covered by the platform
- Official statements drive two-digit growth in Western orders

** "New Quality Business Support Scheme":**
- 10 billion in a resource pack.
- Establishment of a dedicated team to reach 100 agro-zones and industrial zones
- Focus on supporting businesses with product and technological innovation

** Trust and security inputs:**
- Expanding investments in trust and security
- Enhanced compliance system development and enhanced business compliance audit
