# Pinduoduo (PDD Holdings) investment thesis

**Stock code: NASDAQ: PDD** | **Date established: April 9, 2026** | **Share price: $100.87** | **Market capitalization: ~$143.2 billion**

---

## A1: Core thesis (Investment Thesis)

1. **Business Nature**: Pinduoduo is a global traffic allocator for China’s manufacturing capacity. It uses algorithms to efficiently match white-label factories with price-sensitive users. The domestic main website + Temu overseas + Duoduo grocery shopping troika run in parallel.
2. **Competitive advantage**: Extreme cost efficiency driven by scale effect - 20,000 people create revenue of 431.8 billion yuan, gross profit margin 61%, FCF/net profit >100%, asset-light money printing machine.
3. **Growth engine**: Temu has contributed about 40-45% of revenue and is moving from full custody to semi-custody + local warehouse model to hedge tariffs; Duoduomaicai’s 44% market share is close to a monopoly; the "New Pinmu" strategy attempts to upgrade from efficiency to brand.
4. **Core Risks**: Growth cliff (revenue +10%, net profit -12%), Temu faces the structural impact of global tariff barriers, management has $59.5 billion in cash, zero repurchases and zero dividends, and lack of certainty in 10 years.
5. **Valuation Judgment**: PE 10.5x (only 5.9x after deducting $59.7 billion in net cash), ROE 25.4%. Trading at such a valuation is a historically low platform with sufficient margin of safety and is suitable for medium-sized positions to participate.

## A2: List of Core Assumptions

### Hypothesis 1: Temu can achieve profitability under the new tariff environment ★★★★★ (the most critical)

- **Current status**: The US de minimis exemption has been cancelled, and the tariff to China is 30%+; the EU plans to cancel the €150 tax exemption threshold in 2026
- **Company Response**: Switch to semi-managed model + local warehouse + increase unit price (reduce products under $20, increase categories above $50)
- **Verification indicator**: Temu quarterly operating profit margin turns positive (expected H2 2026)
- **If wrong**: Temu's continued losses will drag down the overall profit margin, and the valuation center will move further downwards

### Assumption 2: Domestic main website maintains low single-digit growth (GMV +8-12%/year)

- **Current status**: Domestic GMV in 2025 will be approximately 5.1 trillion, +13%; China’s e-commerce penetration rate is approaching the 30% ceiling
- **Key variables**: Douyin e-commerce growth rate is 30%+ and continues to grab share; whether "Xinpinmu" can increase ARPU
- **Verification Indicators**: Quarterly online marketing service revenue growth rate, domestic GMV market share
- **If wrong**: If the main site's growth rate turns negative or its share falls below 15%, the company's core cash cow will be damaged.

### Assumption 3: Management will eventually initiate shareholder returns

- **Current Status**: $59.5 billion in cash (41% of market capitalization), zero buybacks, zero dividends, disappointing capital allocation
- **Catalyst**: After the secondary listing of Hong Kong stocks, the conditions for repurchase are more mature; the pressure from institutional investors is increasing
- **Verification Indicator**: First announcement of buyback plan or dividend
- **IF WRONG**: Cash never returns to shareholders (VIE risk + management preference), valuation discount is reasonable

### Assumption 4: User retention rate remains stable

- **Current status**: Domestic monthly active users are approximately 900 million; Temu global monthly active users are approximately 600 million+
- **Key variables**: Temu lost users due to price increases (tariff transmission); domestic users were diverted by Douyin
- **Verification indicators**: MAU/DAU trend, user usage time, repurchase rate
- **IF WRONG**: Massive user churn will destroy the scale flywheel

### Hypothesis 5: Sino-US relations will not escalate to full decoupling

- **Current Status**: 145% tariff on China (some goods); 21 states investigating Temu data security
- **Bottom line scenario**: Temu is banned by the United States (probability 10-20%)
- **Verification Indicators**: U.S. policy trends towards Chinese apps/platforms
- **If wrong**: Temu loses its largest overseas market and GMV loses about 30-40%

### Hypothesis 6: Duoduo buys groceries to achieve stable profits

- **Current status**: Market share is 44%, Meituan Preferred has shrunk significantly, and the competitive landscape has improved significantly.
- **Expected Timeline**: Operating profitability in 2026-2027
- **Verification Indicators**: Separate profit data of community group buying business (PDD is not disclosed separately and needs to be extrapolated from industry data)
- **If wrong**: The impact is small, Duoduo grocery shopping accounts for a limited proportion of total income

## A3: Red line list (trigger any re-evaluation/exit)

- **Trigger condition**: The United States signs an executive order banning Temu's operation; or the European Union forces it to be removed from the shelves
- **Extent of impact**: Fatal - Temu accounts for 40-45% of revenue. Once it loses the US market, its valuation may be halved.
- **Current probability**: 10-20% (US), <5% (EU)
- **Monitoring methods**: Pay attention to the legislative developments of the US Congress, executive orders, and CFIUS review

### Red line 2: Major problems with management integrity 🔴

- **Trigger conditions**: financial fraud, related transactions, large-scale management reduction
- **Historical Signal**: Huang Zheng has resigned as CEO but is still the controlling shareholder (holding about 27% of the shares); sudden employee deaths/business rights protection incidents occur frequently
- **Current evaluation**: First-class execution but extreme culture, no evidence of fraud yet
- **Monitoring methods**: Pay attention to SEC investigations, short selling reports, and insider trading records

### Red line 3: The competitive landscape has deteriorated to the point of irreversibility 🔴

- **Trigger condition**: Domestic market share declines for 3 consecutive quarters and falls below 15%
- **Key rivals**: Douyin e-commerce has the fastest growth rate (30%+) and is encroaching on Pinduoduo’s low-price user base
- **Current status**: Pinduoduo 19% share, temporarily stable but being chased by Douyin
- **Monitoring method**: quarterly GMV data, App download/MAU ranking

### Red line 4: Net interest rate continues to fall below 15% 🔴

- **Trigger condition**: Non-GAAP net profit margin <15% for 2 consecutive quarters
- **Logic**: It shows that Temu is burning money out of control or the domestic main website has lost its pricing power.
- **Current status**: Non-GAAP net profit margin in 2025 is about 25%, which is far from the red line
- **Monitoring method**: Quarterly financial report

### Red line 5: Chinese concept stocks are forced to be delisted and there is no alternative listing plan 🔴

- **Trigger Condition**: Failure of PCAOB audit compliance or breakdown of Sino-US audit agreement
- **mitigating factors**: PDD plan/submitted application for secondary listing of Hong Kong stocks
- **Current probability**: <10% (audit protocol still valid)
- **Monitoring method**: PCAOB announcement, SEC China Concept Stock Policy
