# 5.2 "Other gains" from +3.3 billion to #20 billion -- what is it?

This is the single most critical variable for the entire financial statement:** "Other net gains/(losses)" were dramatically reduced from RMB 32.6 billion to RMB 120.26 billion, with a combined deterioration of RMB 5.29 billion.** This alone completely absorbed the growth in operating profits of RMB 3.48 billion and caused additional delays of $1.8 billion.

** What exactly does this subject contain?**

According to the accounting policy disclosure in the multi-year 20-F report and the analysis of Chinese financial media, the following categories are covered by "other net gains/(losses)":

1. ** Fair value changes in the portfolio**: A large amount of short-term investments (US$ 313 billion) is held over and above. This includes property management, structural deposits, monetary funds, etc. A change in market value is directly accounted for in profit or loss as part of the investments are measured at fair value, according to accounting standards. Q1,2026 (January-March 2026) coincides with a significant decline in the United States dollar (three indices were down in full in March), and a loss in the book value of the larger number of equity or bond-type investments with partial dollar values could result.

2. ** Government subsidies**: Chinese technology companies usually receive local government subsidies for R & D, tax returns, etc. The amount of 32.6 billion yuan in "other revenues" from Q2025 may include large government subsidies or tax concessions.

3. ** Non-recurrent business-related gains and losses**: may include losses and losses on the disposition of assets, vendor/business-related compensation or impairment.

4. **The first input from New Julmer's branding subsidiary**: Management refers to the injection of $15 billion in start-up funds into Shanghai's new company in March 2026. While equity injections do not directly affect the consolidated profit statement (because they are subsidiaries), they may be partly reflected in "other losses" if there is a impairment of non-consolidated related party or external equity investments.

** The most likely major cause:** According to the Eastern Wealth Network analysis articles -** the change in fair value of the portfolio was the biggest factor**. In March 2026, the United States stock stock collapsed, with some investments in the large cash pool of up to $43.6 billion floating at market value. Q1,2025, on the contrary – when the market rose and the investment returns were strong. This explains why the same subject created a huge volatility of $5.3 billion between the two quarters.

** Implications for investors:** Such fair value changes are inherently ** non-cash, non-operational and unsustainable fluctuations** and do not reflect a deterioration of business or a continuous consumption of cash. This subject may be reversed if the market warms in the following quarter. It reveals a structural problem: **43.6 billion dollars of cash is used mainly for low-yield finance or passive financial holdings, and inevitably affects profit statements with market fluctuations ** which are hidden costs of large undistributed cash.**
