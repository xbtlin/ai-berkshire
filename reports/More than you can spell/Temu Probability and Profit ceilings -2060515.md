## 1. Direct answer

### Temu’s annual profit after success

| Level of success | GMV | Annual operating profit | Annual net profit | Equivalent to |
|---------|-----|-----------|---------|--------|
| Small success | 50 billion US dollars | 3 billion US dollars | 2.5 billion | Main site 1/4 |
| Moderate success | $150 billion | **$12 billion** | $10 billion | **Build another main site** |
| Big success | 300 billion+ | **30 billion US dollars** | 25 billion | Main site 2.5 times |

**The decisive factor in the profit ceiling is advertising revenue**. The profit after deducting logistics/subsidy from commission is meager, and the marginal cost of advertising is close to zero. The advertising monetization rate of Pinduoduo’s main website is 4.6%, which is the core source of profit. If Temu advertising achieves 4% of GMV, the profit can reach 15-30 billion US dollars; if it is not possible, the upper limit is only 3-5 billion.

**The most likely outcome is "small success" (probability 35-40%)**: Survive but hardly succeed.

## 4. Detailed analysis of success probability

### Favorable factors

1. **Pinduoduo’s execution**: From tariff impact to strategic adjustment, the semi-managed Y1/Y2 model rapidly iterates
2. **China’s supply chain cost advantage**: Even with tariffs, some categories are still price competitive
3. **Global multi-market layout**: If the United States suffers a setback, Europe and Latin America will take over.
4. **The parent company has 420 billion yuan in cash**: blood transfusions can continue for several years
5. **Semi-hosting opens up the local merchant ecosystem**: From a "Chinese cargo porter" to a multi-country, multi-source platform

### Disadvantages

1. **US tariffs (biggest blow)**: de minimis cancels the +145% tariff, and the core value proposition of the United States is weakened
2. **EU Supervision**: Temu has been found to have violated the DSA, and the fine can reach 6% of global revenue.
3. **Quality ceiling**: The platform model is naturally not conducive to quality control and limits brand upgrades.
4. **Competition Containment**: Amazon Haul + Shein + TikTok Shop multi-faceted attack
5. **Uncontrollable geopolitics**: Each escalation of Sino-US relations may bring new restrictions

### Key variables

**Tariffs are the biggest variable**. A $5 product becomes $7.7 with a 54% tariff, and the terminal price may double. Whether Temu can recover in the United States depends on how quickly the proportion of local warehouses can be increased from 25% to 50%+ (it will take 12-18 months).

## 6. Core Conclusions

### One sentence summary

> Temu's most likely fate is "small success" - GMV 50-100 billion US dollars, break-even or small profit, probability 35-40%. The probability of moderate success (annual profit of US$12 billion, building another main website) is about 25-30%. But even if Temu fails completely, the value of Pinduoduo’s main website + net cash is still much higher than the current stock price of $96. This is an asymmetric bet of "big profits if you win and small losses if you lose".
