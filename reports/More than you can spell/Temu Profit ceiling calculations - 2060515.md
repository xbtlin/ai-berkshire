#3.2 Quantified impact of tariff shocks

Since May 2025:
- The United States has eliminated the de Minimis tax exemption of up to $800.
- 54-90 per cent tariff on Chinese goods (different tariff rates for different categories of goods)
- Temu response: stop direct Chinese shipments to the United States and move to the local warehouse

** Tariff implications on the United States market**: Under the straight-through model, $30 to hand-to-hand prices were converted to $38-47 (+25-57%). The local warehouse model avoids terminal tariffs, but bulk imports still require taxes,** with costs rising by about 10-15%**.

** EU**: Tax exemptions will also be abolished in July 2026, with a maximum of 50 per cent tariff increase (Ecofin Agreement).

** Core judgement**: Tariffs are the largest external constraint on Temu’s profit ceiling. If the US-China tariff is maintained at 50% + long term, Temu’s profit margin cap in the US would be 5-8 percentage points lower than the no-tariff scenario.

# The core logic of the profit ceiling

** Commissions do not make a large amount of money** - low commission profits after deduction of logistics, customers, subsidies
** Advertising is the printing machine** — This is evidenced by the multiplicity of stations, with a 4.6% monetization of advertising and a very high profit margin.
**Temu's profit ceiling = advertising income ceiling** — if advertising is 4% of GMV, the profit can be $15-30 billion; if advertising is not possible, the profit ceiling is $3-5 billion
** Tariffs are the largest external constraint** - direct decision on profitability of the United States market (20-30% GMV)
** 5 years from loss to profit margin may be only 5 years** ** provided the tariff environment does not continue to deteriorate

# What does it mean for investors #

- ** Medium Success versus Temu Independent Valuation $100-15 billion** (PE 10-15x x $10 billion net profit)
- ** "Big Success" against Temu's independent valuation of $300-500 billion** (but very low probability)
- ** Current market value of PDD is about $142 billion**, of which the market implied Temu valuation is about $0.040 billion (depending on how to estimate the main station)
- ** If Temu succeeds in the middle, Temu alone is worth the current market value of the PDD as a whole** -- that's the central argument of many people.
- ** But after the probabilities weight of "success" ** Temu expected profits of $5.1 billion a year -- far less than it would have been.

> **Long-altitude Summary**: Temu is an "unimaginable business". The ceiling is high (a $30 billion annual profit), but the probability of achieving it is low (5%). A reasonable investment attitude is ** to treat it as a free option** - if the PDD main station plus cash valuation is worth the price, Temu succeeds as a free lottery.
