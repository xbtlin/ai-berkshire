# IV. Business model disclosure

##4.1 Income composition

The offer disclosed that the sum of the revenue earned was in two parts:

1. ** Online marketing services** (advertisements): Business pays the Platform for access to traffic, which is the main source of income
2. ** Transaction service fee** (commission): a percentage based on value of merchandise paid

** Key ratios**: In 2017, the platform monetization rate (income/GMV) was about 1.2 per cent, well below the range of 3-4 per cent for treasure/cats, reflecting the low-realization stage of the platform ' s growth in size at the time of its listing.

##4.2 Platform mode

The core model described in the book of offers:
- ** "Catch for People" instead of "Man for People"**: through algorithms and social relationships, goods are offered to potential buyers, as distinct from traditional search-type electric power providers
- **Collection mechanism**: Users initiate or participate in a puzzle to obtain price preferences from traders by pooling demand
- ** Micro-Credit Eco-Reliance**: Low cost access through micro-Credit and sharing

Source: [One article reading the multiplicity of shares - new wave technology] (https://tech.sina.com.cn/roll/2018-07-01/doc-ihespqrx8709596.shtml)

The Quakers have a typical "Red Fund + VIE" structure:

```
PDD Holdings (Cayman Islands Register, off-shore majors)
        │
        ▼
All-funded intermediate (Hong Kong)
        │
        ▼
Foreign and domestic sole proprietorship (WFOE, Shanghai Dreaming Information Technology Ltd.)
Xin (agreement control)
        ▼
VIE entity in the country (Emi Network Technology Ltd., Hangzhou / Shanghai Dreaming)
        │
        ▼
More platform to operate
```

** The agreement control arrangements** include exclusive service agreements, stock pledge agreements, exclusive purchase rights agreements, voting power trust agreements.

**VIE Risk reminder (stock offer)**: China ' s regulatory body may consider the VIE arrangement ineffective; the VIE shareholder ' s interests may be inconsistent with those of the listed company abroad; if the VIE agreement is lifted, the offshore shareholder will lose substantive control over the operating entity in China.
