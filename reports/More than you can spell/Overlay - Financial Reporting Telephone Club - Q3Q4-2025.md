# # 2.5 Q4 2025 Major Strategic Initiatives (Official Gazette)

** "Recreate more than one piece of puzzle":**
- PDD ** for the first time in history to publicly disclose Temu ' s three-year strategic objective**: to create operations on the international market on the same scale as domestic ones over a period of three years
- Zhao Jia-Jing has announced the strategy in advance of the annual shareholders meeting, and the Q4 call will be officially confirmed

** Supply chain full bet: **
- Goodbye flow distribution logic, moving to "platforms generate incremental value throughout the supply chain."
- Specific: branding of industrial clusters such as yi-U accessories, Quake, etc.; standardization of agricultural products such as An-Yue lemon, Pou-Ma coffee; deployment of village logistics endpoints in more than 10 provinces

** Tariff impact response (Temu):**
- The United States eliminated the "de minimis" exemption of up to $800, directly affecting the low-priced transborder model of Temu
- Response: introduction of the "semi-trust" model, where businesses share overseas distribution costs; increase in the number of local warehouses overseas

** Normalization of profit margin pressure:**
- Zhao Jia-chan characterized the profit margin fluctuations as "new normal."
- No commitment to a time frame for a rebound in profit margins; 100 billion supply chain investments are planned for three years
