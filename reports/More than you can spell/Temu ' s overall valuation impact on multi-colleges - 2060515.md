# Five, risk and counter-arguments

** Uncertainties that must be honestly acknowledged**:

1. **Temu data are black boxes**: PDD has never separately disclosed Temu ' s income, GMV, profit. All Temu figures in this report are extrapolated, with errors of over 30 per cent.

**The question of "accessibility" of $60.4 billion in cash**: Under the VIE structure, the amount is nominally owned by entities in China and the ultimate benefit to ADS holders abroad is highly uncertain.

3. ** ROI of the "Billion Billion Investment"**: Management announces a three-year investment of $100 billion without giving any expectation of return. If input output is poor, profit margins may fall permanently down the stairs.

4. ** Predictability of tariff policy**: US-China relations may deteriorate at any moment. If the US imposes 200% + customs duties on Chinese cross-border power suppliers, Temu US operations will be substantially destroyed.

5. ** The main station slowdown is structural**: FY 2025 is only 10 per cent and if it continues to slow to 5-8 per cent, the main station valuation should be closer to 10 x PE than the 12 x I gave.

6. ** Management has no shareholder return commitment**: if there is no return of cash, 60.4 billion cash may be far below the real value of the shareholders.
