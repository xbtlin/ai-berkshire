# Duan Yongping’s latest views on PDD (2025-2026)

**Compilation date: 2026-04-13**
**Information source**: Xueqiu's "Intangible Way I Have Style" account, Duan Yongping Fund 13F disclosure, public interviews
**Purpose**: Anchor psychological reference for PDD heavy position decision-making

> **Important Preface**: All original words in this article are marked with the source (date of Snowball’s post), and the inferred opinions are clearly marked with "inference". Duan Yongping is a long-term holder of PDD and Huang Zheng's friend for many years. His views have an existing bias and need to be viewed dialectically.

---

## 1. Change in tone (the most critical)

### December 4, 2025 Snowball’s original words
> "I have never talked about Pinduoduo with Huang Zheng, so I never understand it. AI is really amazing. It can actually watch videos like this. It saves a lot of time and you can roughly understand it at once."

**Key Information**:
- Duan Yongping frankly admits that he has not fully understood the PDD business model for a long time (compare this with his in-depth understanding of Apple and Tencent)
- Watching Huang Zheng's interview videos from 2016 to 2019 through AI at the end of 2025, "you can roughly understand it at once" - the tone changes from "can't quite understand" → "gradually understand"
- **Converse interpretation**: Holding a huge PDD position but admitting that "you don't fully understand" is a risk signal in itself

## 2. Deep affirmation of "Xinpinmu" (the platform's own brand)

### March 27, 2026 Snowball’s original words
> "I think it is a natural thing for Pinduoduo to build an online brand. They know very well what is of quantity. They can take the quantity of things to the extreme, which must be of good quality and at a low price for users (removing all intermediate channels). They only need to select certain products with high quality. Just use your own brand for superficial products, and you don’t need to have your own brand for all products. This is actually what Costco is like. If Pinduoduo only has about 4,000 SKUs, it will be quite powerful in 10 years.

**Core Judgment**:
- **Costco model analogy** - 4,000 selected SKUs, high cost performance, eliminating middle channels
- **Time dimension: 10 years**——Typical Duan Yongping’s long-term perspective
- **Strategic Positioning**: From "Trading Platform" → "Standard Setter" → "Brand Master"
- This is a key change in his repositioning of PDD from an "efficiency company" to a "brand company"

## 3. Attitude towards no dividends and no repurchases

### March 29, 2026 Xueqiu’s original words (reply to questions)
> "Why do you have to worry so much, or why do people have to worry so much about short-term speculation like you? I couldn't have made it clearer in the conference call. If you look carefully, you still don't understand."

**Interpretation**:
- Slightly ironic, implying that management has clearly stated the use of cash in the financial report meeting
- Characterizing investors who are concerned about dividend buybacks as "short-term speculation"
- **Implicit information**: Duan Yongping accepted PDD's use of $60.4 billion in cash for strategic investments (Qianyi Plan, Temu, Xinpinmu) instead of returning it to shareholders

**Counter arguments**:
- This kind of response of "the management has made it clear, you can see for yourself" actually avoids the core question of "why not repurchase"
- PDD did not explicitly commit to future capital return plans during the earnings call
- Duan Yongping's explanation is more of "self-rationalization" than factual basis

## 4. Attitude towards Temu tariff risk

### March 5, 2025 Snowball’s original words
> "Increasing tariffs is actually a disguised increase in taxes on the people, and it will have no other impact."

**Interpretation**:
- Have a relaxed attitude and look at the problem from the perspective of consumer burden
- But this is speaking from March 2025, **before the tariffs actually increase**
- PDD’s subsequent (from May 2025) rapid adjustment of the supply chain (the proportion of local shipments rose to ~90%) shows that the actual impact is greater than Duan Yongping’s initial judgment**
- **Risk**: Duan Yongping may be too naive in his judgment on Temu tariffs

## 9. Reference significance for user decision-making

### Anchor psychology
- Duan Yongping’s cost is $100-110, current $100.87 → **The cost of following the investment is basically the same**
- But note: Duan Yongping’s position configuration is different from yours. He has multiple large positions in Apple, Moutai, Tencent, etc.
- **Don’t buy heavily just because “Duan Yongping bought it”** – His risk tolerance is different from yours

### Key issues that require your own judgment
Duan Yongping didn’t solve it, you have to answer it yourself:
1. **Will Xinpinmu really be like Costco in 10 years? **——This is his core argument, but it is not verified
2. Will the $60.4 billion in cash finally be returned to shareholders? ** (VIE structure + tax) - he avoided this question
3. **What will be the ultimate impact of the Temu tariffs? **——His judgment may be too optimistic
4. Is **PDD’s moat wide enough to withstand competition from Douyin/Kuaishou/Temu Amazon? **——He himself admits that it is not wide enough

### Duan Yongping's "Weighing Pros and Cons"
- **Positive**: Credible management + clear long-term strategy + abundant cash + reasonable valuation
- **Converse**: Even Duan Yongping doesn’t fully understand the business model + The moat admits it’s not wide enough + Temu’s tariff judgment may be wrong

### Conclusion
Duan Yongping's rating for PDD is "**Trust-driven long-term holding**", not "**Understanding-driven heavy position**". If your decision-making principle strictly adheres to "buy after understanding", PDD may not meet your standards; if you can accept "partly relying on trust in management", you can follow the investment.
