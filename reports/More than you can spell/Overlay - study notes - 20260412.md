## 1. Duan Yongping’s analogy: PDD is like Disney+Costco

Duan Yongping said that Pinduoduo is like a combination of Disney and Costco.

**Disney’s business model**: IP flywheel - creation of IP → theater → theme park → peripheral licensing → streaming media, the cycle is strengthened. The moat is the IP itself: a cross-generational emotional connection that cannot be copied and will not expire. The essence is to sell emotions and irreplaceable experiences.

**Costco’s business model**: Membership fees are the profit, and products make almost no money (gross profit margin ~11%). Membership fees account for 70%+ of operating profits, and the renewal rate is 93%. Build user trust by relying on selected SKUs (~3,700 types) and extremely low prices. The essence is to save money for users and then charge a fixed fee to users.

**The core of Duan Yongping's analogy**: What these two companies have in common is that they "really stand on the side of users." He hopes that the end result of PDD is: users believe that Pinduoduo's products are the cheapest and most reliable - not just low prices, but a combination of "low prices + trust", similar to Costco.

## 2. Differentiated services of PDD (factual level)

1. **Group mode**: Users initiate a group, and after gathering the number of people, the transaction will be completed at a lower price. After the demand is aggregated, the platform places large orders with merchants, and merchants offer lower ex-factory prices due to large quantities.
2. **Social Fission Distribution**: Reach new users through WeChat sharing links, and you need to invite friends to bargain or join a group. The platform does not rely on search advertising to acquire customers, but relies on users to actively spread the word.
3. **Direct delivery of agricultural products from the origin**: Direct cooperation with agricultural producing areas across the country, skipping wholesalers and dealers. By 2025, more than 100 production areas will be established.
4. **Ten billion subsidies**: A special subsidy will be established in 2019 to directly compensate for the price difference of branded products (iPhone, Dyson, etc.) to ensure the lowest price of genuine products on the entire network.
5. **WeChat Mini Game Platform**: Reach users through the WeChat ecosystem without the need for independent App installation, with monthly active users exceeding 500 million.
6. **Temu**: Launched in 2022, with a fully managed model (merchants are only responsible for supply, and the platform is responsible for pricing, logistics, and after-sales), selling Chinese goods to overseas consumers.

## 4. Why did Taote fail?

The volume once reached 300 million users, but ultimately failed due to:

1. **Users come with subsidies, and leave when the subsidies stop**: Users are not allowed to stay because of the platform’s long-term low prices, and the repurchase rate is low.
2. **Poor product quality control**: The review of registered merchants is lax, the number of complaints about fake and low-quality products is high, and the user experience is poor.
3. **Poor merchant experience**: Resource allocation relies on manual review, which is not fair. A large number of factory merchants cannot get traffic and give up one after another.
4. **Internal strategic swing**: If Taote succeeds independently, it will cannibalize Taobao. Alibaba has never made up its mind to truly operate independently.
5. **No WeChat traffic**: Can only rely on advertising to buy traffic, and the cost of acquiring customers is much higher than that of early PDD.

## 5. The true situation of the PDD moat

**Actual moats:**
- WeChat social relationship chain: Acquire customers through WeChat sharing, and the customer acquisition cost is much lower than that of competitors (but WeChat belongs to Tencent, not PDD)
- Direct connection to the origin of agricultural products: Established origin relationships and cold chain networks take time to accumulate
- Low price mentality: The user's perception of "PDD = cheapest" is a real asset

**Moat Disadvantages:**
- The deepest moat (WeChat relationship chain) belongs to Tencent, not PDD
- Low prices can be followed up (JD.com and Taobao have already followed up with tens of billions of subsidies)
- Weak user loyalty: PDD users "go wherever it's cheaper" and have low switching costs.
- Temu does not have WeChat overseas, so the social fission model does not hold, and it relies solely on advertising to buy sales.

## 6. Current situation of Temu

**Profit status:**
- Operating losses in 2024 will be approximately 32 billion yuan, and approximately 50 billion yuan in 2025 (UBS estimate)
- The European market is already partially profitable in 2025 (net profit is approximately US$110 million, gross profit margin 37%)
- US market close to breakeven, but worsens after tariffs

**Tariff Impact:**
- In May 2025, the United States will cancel the tax exemption for small packages under $800.
- The air freight direct mail model has fundamentally lost its economics
- Temu responded: the proportion of air freight dropped from 85% to 2% and changed to shipping stocking, the semi-managed model expanded to 75%, and the advertising focus shifted to Europe

**Profit path:**
- UBS forecast: Quarterly breakeven will be achieved by the end of 2026, and operating profit margin will be approximately 6% in 2029

## 7. Hundreds of billions of support and new efforts

**100 billion support**: 100 billion in three years, three major directions:
1. Agricultural supply chain (Duoduohao specialty products): direct shipment from origin, cold chain, standardization, agricultural product sales +47% in 2025
2. Logistics sinking (e-commerce going west): The free postage area covers remote administrative villages, and the western transfer fee is exempted
3. Merchant ecology (tens of billions of rewards): fee reductions and deposit reductions

**Xinpinmu**: Established in March 2026 in Shanghai, with a first-phase capital injection of 15 billion and a three-year plan of 100 billion. The positioning is to integrate "PDD+Temu" supply chain resources and incubate Chinese self-operated brands for the global market. The goal is to upgrade from "selling white-label low-price goods" to "selling branded products made in China" to enhance pricing power and enhance anti-tariff capabilities.

**Conditions for success:** Supply chain factory cooperation transformation, channel awareness reconstruction, overseas brand establishment takes time, and the tariff environment is stable.

**Risks of failure:** There are far more failed cases of Chinese-made branding overseas than success; PDD genes are platforms rather than brands; channel conflicts; 15 billion will not be enough after dispersion; brand building will take at least 5-10 years.

## 8. Xinpinmu vs. Huang Zheng's "matching principle" - is it inconsistent?

**Huang Zheng’s original words:**
> Full-category expansion is still based on traditional traffic logic, and so is upgrade. Upgrade is a perspective viewed from the perspective of people within the Fifth Ring Road. I don’t think Pinduoduo wants to upgrade its brand and cover all categories. This is wrong. What we have to do is always matching, so that the right people can buy the right things in the right scenarios.

**Surface Contradiction:** Xinpinmu clearly wants to incubate the brand, but Huang Zheng said "no brand upgrade", which is literally a slap in the face.

**Dig deeper: There may not be any spiritual contradiction**

What Huang Zheng objects to is the platform itself's pursuit of brand tone (i.e., "a bird's-eye view within the five rings") in order to please high-end users. The logic of Xinpinmu is different:
- Helping the **supply chain end** build a brand, rather than repositioning the PDD platform itself
- The target users are still **global price-sensitive consumers** (Temu core group)
- The essence is still "matching" - let the factory make trustworthy products and sell them to the right people

From this perspective, New Pinmu is an upgrade of the matching principle: evolving from "matchmaking" to "self-building", but the positioning of serving people has not changed.

**Real Tension:**

Huang Zheng has withdrawn in 2021, and the current management is facing different realistic pressures:
1. Temu encounters a white-label trust crisis overseas, and branding is a defensive method
2. The impact of tariffs has made the "low-price direct mail" model uneconomical, and branding can increase pricing power.
3. "Helping Chinese manufacturing go overseas" is a politically correct strategic framework that facilitates domestic financing and compliance

**Conclusion:** There is a continuation in spirit (serving price-sensitive users), but the path deviates from Huang Zheng's principle of "not building a brand". What Huang Zheng opposes is "upgrading for the sake of upgrading", while Xinpinmu is "upgrading for the sake of survival" - the motivations are different, but the result is called brand building. This is a strategic adjustment forced by reality, not an inheritance of Huang Zheng's philosophy.
