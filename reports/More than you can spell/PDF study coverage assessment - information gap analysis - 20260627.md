## 1. Clarify the missing topics

### 1. Research on Duoduo Grocery Shopping (Importance: High)

The "one dominant player" pattern in community group buying is a major change in the competitive landscape from 2025 to 2026:

- Meituanxuan exited, Jingxipinpin was marginalized, Taocaicai shrank, and Duoduomaicai became the only national player
- GMV in 2025 will be close to 300 billion yuan, exceeding the combined GMV of Duoduomaicai and Meituan in 2024
- After the withdrawal of Meituan Preferred, daily order volume soared by 50%, and 72 warehouses across the country were operating at full speed.
- Duoduomaicai has temporarily given up on real-time retail, put down short-term profit targets, and made every effort to seize the market gap after Meituan's withdrawal.
- The growth rate in the next few years is expected to be 20%-30%. The market prospect is expected to exceed 500 billion yuan, with profits reaching billions or even tens of billions.

**Existing documents**: "New Business Topic" (2026-04-10) covers Duoduo Maicai as a sub-topic, but lacks an independent and in-depth dismantling of the drastic changes in the competitive landscape, profit prospects, and valuation contribution.

### 2. Free delivery to villages/end logistics strategy (importance: medium to high)

The core components of the 100 billion support plan are of great significance to the penetration of PDD into the sinking market:

- Pilot in Q4 in 2025, full promotion in 2026
-Mode: Merchant delivers goods to county-level transit warehouse → Third-party logistics delivers to village-level pick-up point → Platform fully bears the second-stage freight
- Responding to the guidance of the Central Committee's No. 1 Document "Deepening Express Delivery into Villages"
- A network of county-level transit warehouses + village-level pick-up points has been established in many places across the country (such as 71 sites in Fufeng County, Shaanxi)
- Rural online retail sales will exceed 3 trillion yuan for the first time in 2025
- Two-way value: opening up the last mile of industrial products to the countryside + the first mile of agricultural products going upstream

**Existing Documents**: None.

### 3. Tracking the progress of secondary listing of Hong Kong stocks (importance: medium to high)

Multiple research documents mention "waiting for the listing of Hong Kong stocks" as a core catalyst, but there is a lack of progress tracking:

- PDD has changed its Hong Kong audit institution and hired Ernst & Young Hong Kong auditors to review the financial statements (replacing Beijing auditors), hinting at promoting the listing of Hong Kong stocks.
- Goldman Sachs estimates that 27 Chinese concept stocks (with a total market value of 1.35 trillion yuan) are eligible for dual primary listing or secondary listing in Hong Kong, and PDD is among them.
- The listing of Hong Kong stocks allows ADRs to be converted into Hong Kong stocks to hedge against the risk of US delisting
- The Hong Kong Stock Exchange is conducting consultation on listing reforms, involving systems such as different rights for the same shares and secondary listings.
- No official timetable yet

**Existing Files**: No separate files. Briefly mentioned in the buying risk panorama analysis.

### 4. Ten billion supermarket/instant retail layout (importance: medium)

For the first time, PDD truly tested the new business signals of the real-time retail track:

- Internal testing of "Ten Billion Supermarket" in early 2026 to strengthen the advantageous categories of FMCG
- Adopt the "light asset + crowdsourcing distribution" strategy and reuse community group purchasing grid warehouses to reduce initial investment
- Shanghai and other first-tier cities are piloting self-built front-end warehouses, with plans to gradually expand
- Competitors are strong: JD Supermarket users +18%/order volume +20%, Meituan Xiaoxiang’s front warehouse covers 30+ cities, Alibaba’s multi-dimensional supermarket matrix
- Due to the heavy costs suffered by other players in the food delivery war, PDD made it clear that it will not do food delivery and will focus on non-catering flash delivery

**Existing Documents**: None.
