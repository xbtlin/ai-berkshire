# Pinduoduo domestic main website: Will profits be higher or lower in 10 years than now?

**Research Date: May 15, 2026 | Four Agents Parallel Research**
**Core question: If Temu and Duoduo Maicai are divested, and only the domestic main website is looked at, will the profit in 2036 be higher or lower than in 2025? **

---

## Conclusion

> **The probability is slightly higher, but don’t get excited. ** Under the neutral scenario, the net profit of the main website in 2036 is about 135 billion yuan, which is 29% higher than the approximately 105 billion yuan in 2025. It sounds okay, but the annual growth rate in 10 years is only 2.3%. This is a money printing press that is still spinning, but it is spinning slower and slower. Probability-weighted, profits were about 28% higher than today. Under the pessimistic scenario (25% probability), profits are 14% lower than now.

## Consensus and disagreement among four perspectives

### Consensus (high credibility)

1. **The golden growth period of the main website has ended. ** Revenue growth will plummet from +59% to +10% in 2025. This is a structural turning point and not a cyclical fluctuation.
2. **Profit rates will decline structurally. ** Ecological restoration of merchants (cancellation of refund-only and 100-billion-dollar support), "New Pinmu" self-operated business has lowered profit margins, and intensified competition has led to rising subsidies - the triple pressure is irreversible.
3. **The "low price mentality" will not disappear in 10 years. ** China’s 600 million consumers with a monthly income of less than 1,000 yuan are the basic market, and the slowdown in economic growth has only strengthened the demand for low prices.
4. **The main website will not collapse, but it will change from "growth type" to "value type". ** Analogy to Taotian: EBITA profit margin is 43.6% and almost zero growth in three years - this is the fate of mature platform e-commerce. PDD is still 3-5 years away from this "steady state".

## Five decisive variables

Sorted from largest to smallest impact on 10-year profits:

### 1. AI Agent shopping (impact range: ±20-30% profit)

**This is the only variable that may change the direction of the conclusion. **

Current signals: Amazon has launched Alexa shopping agent, OpenAI is connected to Stripe payment, and Qianwen is fully integrated with Taobao. McKinsey predicts that Agent commerce will drive US$3-5 trillion in global transactions in 2030.

Impact on Pinduoduo: Users no longer “shop” at Pinduoduo, but let AI do the work for them → Advertising bidding rankings become ineffective → Core monetization capabilities are emptied out.

Buffering factors: Pinduoduo’s white-label products lack standardized SKUs, making it difficult for AI to accurately compare prices across platforms; the “lowest price on the entire network” itself may become the first choice for AI recommendations.

**Judgment**: The impact will be limited before 2030, and 5-10% of the e-commerce traffic entrance may be diverted from 2030 to 2036. However, Pinduoduo’s research and development is only 3.45%, and there is a serious lack of preparation for the response.

### 2. Douyin’s low-price competition (impact range: -10-15% profit)

Douyin's GMV in 2025 has exceeded Pinduoduo's (4.4 trillion vs. 4 trillion), and "price power" has been listed as the primary strategy. White brand beauty brands have exceeded 100 million in Douyin. The average daily usage time of Douyin is 120 minutes vs. Pinduoduo is less than 30 minutes - the time is the traffic, and the traffic is the merchant and advertising budget.

**Judgment**: Douyin will not replace Pinduoduo’s low-price mentality (Walmart has not been replaced by Amazon), but it will continue to erode its share. It is a high probability that Pinduoduo’s share will drop from 19% to 14-16% in 10 years.

### 3. Merchant Ecology (Scale of Impact: ±10-15% Profit)

The era of "refund only" is over, support of hundreds of billions is advancing, but merchant profit margins have been pushed to the limit. Tax standardization (new VAT regulations in 2026) is a new source of pressure.

**Judgment**: Management is doing the right thing (repairing the ecosystem), but at the cost of actively compressing profit margins. This is "short-term and long-term" - profit margins decline within 3 years in exchange for 10 years of supply-side stability.

### 4. Instant retail substitution (impact range: -5-8% profit)

Instant retail sales will exceed RMB 1 trillion in 2026, and daily standard products are migrating from "next-day delivery" to "30-minute delivery". Pinduoduo is completely absent from this track.

**Judgment**: Some of Pinduoduo’s GMV in daily categories will be cut off, but non-real-time categories such as clothing and agricultural products will not be affected. The direction of the impact is certain but the magnitude is limited.

### 5. Demographic structure (influence range: -5-10% profit)

The user base peaked and began to decline slowly (MAU from 700 million → 600-650 million), and the core consumer group (25-55 years old) continued to shrink. The penetration rate of the silver-haired group has increased but ARPU is low.

**Judgment**: Slow variables, determined direction, 10 years is enough to feel the impact.

## Taotian is the final image

Taotian’s present may be the future of Pinduoduo’s main website. Comparison:

| Indicators | Taotian 2025 | Pinduoduo main website forecast 2036 |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| GMV | ~8.3 trillion | ~6.5-7 trillion |
| Revenue growth | Low single digits | Low single digits |
| EBITA Margin | ~44% | ~30-35% |
| Profit growth rate | Nearly zero growth in three years | Annualized 2-3% |
| Positioning | Full category platform | Low price + white label platform |

Taotian's EBITA profit margin is 44% and has almost zero growth in three years - this is the fate of mature shelf e-commerce. The main website of Pinduoduo currently has a higher profit margin (~40%), but it is moving closer to this "steady state". The difference is that Pinduoduo’s profit margin will eventually be lower than Taotian’s (branded goods have weak premium capabilities and low unit price per customer).

---

## Final judgment

**In 10 years’ time, the profits of Pinduoduo’s domestic main website will be higher than now, but only about 30% higher, and the annualized rate is only 2.3%. **

The confidence in this conclusion is medium—the main uncertainty comes from the AI Agent. If AI shopping becomes mainstream around 2030, the conclusion may flip from "slightly high" to "slightly low".

For investors:
- The main site is a stable cash-making machine that will not collapse
- But the growth narrative is over, and valuations should switch from "growth" to "value"
- PDD's overall investment variance almost entirely comes from Temu - the main website only provides the bottom line, not flexibility
- The current PE is 10.5x, and the EV/E after cash is 6.2x. It is not expensive even if you only count the main website - the market is already pricing Temu back to zero.
