# Five factors to suppress the valuation

PE from 10 to 14-16, without all five factors being removed -- any one to two looser is enough to push for a re-evaluation:

** Factor I: political uncertainty in Temu**

The market doesn't know if Temu is worth $50 billion or zero. The calculation above suggests that the current pricing implied the assumption that Temu was a negative asset.

Released: Temu achieves quarterly profits in a major market (most likely Europe) that proves business models work. Or, conversely, if Temu is banned, uncertainty is eliminated, it may be that "failout" will drive the valuation repair.

Time window: 1-3 years. Semi-custody profit rates are improving, and if Europe's business ran out of positive profits by 2027, that factor will be relaxed.
