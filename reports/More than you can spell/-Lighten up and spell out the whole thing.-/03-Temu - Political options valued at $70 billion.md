# Three, Europe over America -- a neglected key change

Many analysts are still in the "Temu = American Market" perception. But the fact in 2025:**Temu in Europe has more GMV than in the United States**.

This is important for three reasons:

1. European tariffs are far below those of the United States

The EU tariff strategy for packages from China is far more moderate than that of the US:
- Single commodity value €150: Value Added Tax (VAT) + Customs declaration, but no additional punitive duties**
- Actual incremental costs approximately ** €3/items** (vs 30-145 per cent in the United States)

That is,**Temu was largely immune to tariff war in Europe** — the central reason for the rise in the share of the European market in 2025.

##2. European consumers have a high acceptance of "low-price Chinese goods"

- Europe, Shein/Wish, and China's fast fashion suppliers are well-established.
- High consumer income pressure (inflation, energy price rise) in Europe, more sensitive to low prices
- Culturally, there's less resistance to "China made" than America.

#3. European Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is currently not in a position to hit Temu

- 2024 European Multinational Survey Temu Data Privacy
- But by the end of 2025, no substantive injunctions had been issued.
- There is a risk of fines, but the scale is much lower than the "risk of closure."

** Practical for investors**:

If you only see "the United States may seal Temu" -- you see Temu's 50% story.
**Temu 40% + GMV in Europe is unlikely to disappear once ** -- this is a structural security cushion.
