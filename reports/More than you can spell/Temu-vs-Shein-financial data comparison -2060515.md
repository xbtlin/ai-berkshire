#2. Profit schedule

Temu's profit path is in three stages:
- ** Phase I (2022.9 - end 2024) ** Loss period, strategic burning of money for customers
- ** Phase II (2025):** Target balance, but affected by United States tariffs, the United States market balance was postponed to 2026
- **Step III (2026):** Targets are fully profitable

** Confidence: medium** (from multiple industry analysts, but tariff shocks in 2025 have led to uncertainty in the schedule)

Key variables: the United States tariff escalation on Chinese commodities led to the elimination of de Minimis, directly pushing down logistics and compliance costs for Temu, and increased losses on the United States market in Q2 in 2025.

# IV, 5-year projection key scenario hint

Based on the above data, the core variables that need to be addressed in making the five-year projection are:

### Temu
1. **Quantified tariff impact:** How much has Temu increased per unit cost after the elimination of the de Minimis exemption? Will there be a fundamental change in business model?
2. ** Regional transfer capacity:** Can the contraction of the United States be compensated for by growth in Europe/emerging markets?
3. ** Profit-tight:** The full-management semi-management rhythm determines the profit margin trend
4. **PDD parent subsidy cap:** How long can PDD annual profit of $15.4 billion support Temu’s loss?

### Shein
1. **IPO is complete:** is it reasonable to have the valuation anchored at $30-50 billion?
2. **Marketplace rate of increase: ** 3P refers from 24% to the effect of 40% + on profit margin
3. ** Product-based expansion effect:** Implementation of expansion from fashion-based clothing to full-grade
4. ** Regulated risk:** France has shown signs of restriction on Shein

# Common variable
- Central American Trade Policy Directions
- Global consumer acceptance of China's electricity provider at a very low price.
- The competition between the two is zero-sum-share.
