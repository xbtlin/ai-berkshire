## 1. Revenue and Profit Trend Analysis (2021-2025)

### 1.1 Revenue Trends

| Year | Revenue (RMB billion) | YoY growth rate | Net profit (USD billion) | Net profit YoY | Operating profit (USD billion) |
|------|------------|---------|--------------|----------|---------------|
| 2021 | 939.5 | +61% | ~13 | Turn loss into profit | ~69 |
| 2022 | ~1,306 | +28% | ~46 | +254% | ~83 |
| 2023 | 2,476.4 | +84% | ~85 | +85% | ~148 |
| 2024 | 3,938.4 | +59% | ~154 | +82% | ~149 |
| 2025 | 4,318.5 | +10% | ~136(RMB99.4 billion) | **-12%** | ~135(RMB94.6 billion,**-13%**) |

**Key Findings**:
- Explosive growth from 2021 to 2024, with a 4-year CAGR of approximately 43%
- **Obvious turning point in 2025**: Revenue growth plummeted to 10%, and net profit fell by 12% for the first time
- Main factors for profit decline: substantial increase in marketing and R&D expenses brought about by Temu's global expansion
