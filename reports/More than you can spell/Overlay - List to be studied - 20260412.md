## Core Concerns

### Concern 1: Is PDD's moat real? Will it still exist in 5 years?

**Current contradictions:**
- PDD's differentiated features (group buying, 100-billion subsidy, direct farm-product supply) have already been widely copied by competitors
- The deepest moat (WeChat social graph) belongs to Tencent, not PDD
- The low-price mindshare moat needs continuous "feeding" through subsidies - will it collapse if subsidies stop?
- User loyalty is weak, and switching costs are close to zero

**Questions to research:**
- [ ] PDD's repeat purchase rate and retention rate, compared with Taobao and JD
- [ ] Is there a formal cooperation agreement between WeChat and PDD? Does Tencent hold PDD shares?
- [ ] PDD's penetration in lower-tier markets over the past 3 years
- [ ] How deep is the actual barrier in the farm-produce supply chain - how long would it take competitors to catch up?
- [ ] Is Douyin e-commerce growth eroding PDD's share? What are the numbers?

---

### Concern 2: When can Temu become profitable? What needs to happen?

**What we know now:**
- Operating loss was about RMB 32 billion in 2024 and about RMB 50 billion in 2025
- Europe was partially profitable in 2025
- The U.S. market was hit hard by tariffs, and the original model (duty-free + air-shipped direct delivery) no longer works
- UBS predicts quarterly break-even by end-2026 and profit margin of about 6% by 2029

**Questions to research:**
- [ ] What are the unit economics of the semi-managed and fully-managed models? Which has better profit potential?
- [ ] Progress on overseas warehouses - which countries have them, and at what scale?
- [ ] Latest U.S. tariff developments - could the 145% tariff come down? What is the status of U.S.-China talks?
- [ ] Why is Europe profitable - lower logistics cost or a better competitive structure?
- [ ] What is SHEIN's business model - can Temu borrow from it, and is SHEIN profitable?
- [ ] Which countries / regions are Temu's models closest to sustainable profitability?

---

### Concern 3: When can New Pinduoduo succeed, and what would success look like?

**What we know now:**
- RMB 15 billion in phase-one funding, RMB 100 billion over three years, established in Shanghai
- Goal: integrate PDD + Temu supply chains and incubate global self-owned Chinese brands
- Analogy: upgrading from Yiwu white-label products to Anker / DJI-level brands

**Key uncertainties:**
- PDD does not have a brand-building gene
- Moving from white-label to brand took Anker 10 years and DJI 8 years
- Channel conflict: self-owned brands would compete with third-party merchants on the platform

**Questions to research:**
- [ ] What categories does New Pinduoduo actually plan to launch? Has any category direction been announced?
- [ ] What is the team composition - internal transfers from PDD, or external hiring of brand / consumer-product talent?
- [ ] Success-case analysis for Chinese export brands: what path do Anker, DJI, and Shokz have in common?
- [ ] User perception of Temu - how do overseas users feel about the Temu brand? Can it be upgraded?
- [ ] How will the RMB 15 billion phase-one capital be used - the ratio between supply-chain investment, brand marketing, and overseas warehouses?
