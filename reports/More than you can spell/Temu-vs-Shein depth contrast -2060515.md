|------|---------|----------|
| Age | Mainly 25-44 years old, 59+ the most frequent | **Mainly 13-35 years old** |
| Gender | **Male preference (57%)** | **Female preference (60-75%)** |

**Conclusion: Temu’s real competitor is not Shein, but Amazon and local e-commerce companies everywhere. The two are more likely to coexist in a differentiated manner, similar to the relationship between Walmart and Zara. **

## 3. Competition and interaction

### Can each other invade each other's hinterland?

| Direction | Possibility | Reason |
|------|--------|------|
| Temu attacks Shein's fashion category | **Low** | Lack of brand, lack of design capabilities, poor quality control, and was sued by Shein for copyright infringement |
| Shein attacks all categories of Temu | **Low** | Marketplace only accounts for 24%, and 61% of third-party sellers have profit margins <10% |

### Supplier competition

Shein required suppliers to sign exclusive agreements, and Temu sued Shein for "mafia-style intimidation" of suppliers. In fact, a large number of suppliers have opened stores on both platforms, but clothing suppliers prefer Shein (stable orders and reasonable profits), while full-category suppliers prefer Temu (low threshold).

### Comparison after 5 years

| Indicators | Temu (neutral) | Shein (neutral) | Who is stronger |
|------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| GMV | **190 billion** | 120 billion | Temu (high ceiling for all categories) |
| Revenue | 34 billion | **80 billion** | Shein (1P model revenue ≈ GMV) |
| Net profit | 2.2-2.7 billion | **3.8-4.5 billion** | **Shein’s profit is 1.5-2 times that of Temu** |
| Net profit margin | ~7% | ~5% | Temu profit margin is slightly higher but low in absolute value |
| Source of profit | Advertising (core) + commission | Brand premium + supply chain efficiency |
| Core risks | Tariffs + parent company not paying dividends | IPO + profit margins under pressure |

### Why is Shein more profitable?

**60% gross profit margin is the fundamental reason. ** Shein's private label model means that what it earns is "brand premium + design premium + supply chain efficiency difference", while Temu only earns "platform commission + advertising fee". Under the same GMV:

- Shein: $120 billion GMV × 60% gross profit margin × ~8% net profit margin = $3.8-4.5 billion profit
- Temu: $190 billion GMV × ~18% take rate × ~7% net profit margin = $2.2-2.7 billion profit

**A larger GMV does not mean more profit. Shein makes more profits with a smaller GMV because the brand's money is "thicker" than the platform's money. **

## 5. Inspiration for investment

### Temu and Shein are not a zero-sum game

The 5% user overlap rate and <30% category overlap proves: **The market’s comparison of Temu and Shein together is an oversimplification**. The two face different user groups and solve different needs:
- Shein's solution is "I want to pursue fashion cheaply"
- Temu solves "I want to buy everything at the lowest price"

### Real systemic risks are not competing with each other

The first variable that determines the fate of the two is **tariff policy**, the second is **geopolitics**, and the third is competition with each other. For every 10 percentage point reduction in tariffs, the profit elasticity of both companies exceeds 20%.
