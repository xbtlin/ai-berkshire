## 1. Industry scale and growth

### 1.1 China’s e-commerce market

| Indicators | 2024 data | 2025 data/forecast | Trend judgment |
|------|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Online retail sales | 15.5 trillion yuan | ~16.9 trillion yuan (+9.1%) | Growth rate slowing but still steady |
| The proportion of physical goods online retail in social retail | 26.8% | ~28% | The penetration rate continues to increase but the margin is slowing down |
| Scale of Internet users | 1.108 billion | ~1.12 billion | Close to ceiling |

**Core judgment**: China’s e-commerce has entered the **mature growth stage**, with the growth rate falling from double digits to single digits (9.1%), and the penetration rate approaching the 30% ceiling. The increase mainly comes from category deepening and model innovation, rather than new users.

### 1.2 Cross-border e-commerce market

| Indicators | Data |
|------|------|
| China’s cross-border e-commerce import and export scale in 2024 | 2.63 trillion yuan, +10.8% year-on-year |
| Global cross-border e-commerce market size (2025) | About 4.8 trillion US dollars |
| 2026-2035 CAGR forecast | 18.7% |

Cross-border e-commerce is still a high-growth track, but the cancellation of the US de minimis rules in 2025 is a **structural turning point**.

### 1.3 Community group buying/instant retail

| Market segments | Scale in 2024 | Pattern |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Community group buying | ~500 billion yuan | Duoduo Grocery is the largest one (44% share) |
| Instant retail | ~800 billion yuan | Meituan flash sales dominate |

**Major events**: In June 2024, Meituan Select experienced a large-scale contraction, and Duoduomaicai became the biggest winner in the community group buying track.

## 2. Panoramic view of competitive landscape

### 2.1 China’s e-commerce platform competition matrix (2025)

| Platform | Estimated GMV | Market share | Year-on-year growth rate | Core strengths | Core weaknesses |
|------|---------|----------|----------|---------|---------|
| **Taotian Group** | ~8.3 trillion | 31% | +6% | Brand ecology, user base, AI investment | Growth rate lags behind |
| **Douyin E-commerce** | ~4.3 trillion | 24% | +30%+ | Content-driven, fastest growing | High return rate, thin merchant profits |
| **Pinduoduo** | ~5.1 trillion | 19% | +15-18% | Extremely cost-effective, sinking market | Difficulty in brand upgrade |
| **JD** | ~4.2 trillion | 16% | +8-10% | Logistics experience, quality trust | Sluggish growth |
| **Kuaishou + Others** | ~2.7 trillion | 10% | +20% | Lower market | Limited scale |

**Key conclusions on pattern evolution**:
- The top three "Taotian-JD.com-Pinduoduo" pattern has been broken by Douyin e-commerce, and now the top four are coexisting**
- Douyin e-commerce growth rate is 30%+, and GMV may surpass Taotian within 3-4 years
- Pinduoduo’s growth rate has slowed down significantly, from 40%+ in 2023 to 15-18% in 2025

### 2.2 Cross-border e-commerce competition matrix (2025)

| Platform | Global cross-border market share | GMV estimation | Strategy characteristics |
|------|-----------------|---------|---------|
| **Temu** | 24% | ~US$35 billion, +50% YoY | Extremely low prices in all categories + social fission |
| **Amazon** | ~24% | Cross-border ~$40 billion | Brand + Logistics + Prime Ecosystem |
| **SHEIN** | 9% | ~USD 54 billion (all inclusive) | Fashion fast reaction supply chain |
| **AliExpress** | 8% (continuous decline) | ~$25 billion | Full category, for emerging markets |

Temu has grown from 0 to 24% of the cross-border market share in three years, an unprecedented growth rate. But the business model changed fundamentally after de minimis was cancelled.

## 3. Threat assessment of core competitors

### 3.1 Douyin E-commerce (Threat Level: ★★★★★)

It represents a fundamental change in the e-commerce traffic distribution paradigm - from "people looking for goods" to "goods looking for people".

| Dimensions | Analysis |
|------|------|
| **Growth** | GMV growth rate is 30%+, the fastest among all platforms |
| **Model Evolution** | "Content + Shelves" two-wheel drive, shelves account for more than 40% |
| **Threat to Pinduoduo** | Competing for the same group of price-sensitive users, white-label merchants are betting on both ends |
| **Weaknesses** | Return rate remains high (30-50% for some categories) |

**Munger's negative test**: The return rate problem of Douyin's "interest e-commerce" model is structural - the real net GMV is far lower than the book data.

### 3.2 Taobao (Threat Level: ★★★☆☆)

In 2025, we have clearly abandoned "absolute low price" and shifted to "service is growth". Taobao's abandonment of head-on price competition is tantamount to admitting that it cannot beat Pinduoduo in the low-price segment.

But the deeper meaning is that Taobao is taking a differentiated route. If it successfully achieves the "quality e-commerce" positioning layering, Pinduoduo may be permanently locked in the "low price" label.

### 3.3 JD.com (Threat Level: ★★☆☆☆)

It poses the least direct threat to Pinduoduo. The user groups of the two are clearly differentiated.

### 3.4 SHEIN (Threat Level: ★★★☆☆)

SHEIN and Temu are more like **parallel tracks** than a zero-sum game. SHEIN focuses on fashion and Temu covers all categories.

## 6. Munger's "reverse thinking" - under what circumstances will Pinduoduo lose?

### Five scenarios where Pinduoduo may fail

| Scenario | Probability | Logical chain |
|------|------|----------|
| Globalization of tariff barriers | ★★★★☆ | The United States cancels → the EU follows up → the world follows suit → the Temu story is shattered |
| Massive exodus of merchants | ★★★☆☆ | Continuous price reduction → zero profits → high-quality supply transfer → vicious cycle |
| Douyin swallows the sinking market | ★★★☆☆ | Algorithm accurately reaches → content e-commerce replaces shelf search |
| Regulatory risks | ★★☆☆☆ | Simultaneous pressure from multiple countries and multiple fronts |
| AI subverts the platform model | ★★☆☆☆ | AI agents directly connect consumers and factories → "De-platforming" |

### The "consensus" that needs to be questioned

| Market consensus | Negative evidence |
|----------|----------|
| "There will always be a market for low prices" | If all platforms can achieve low prices, low prices will no longer be a differentiation |
| "Temu can rely on local warehouses to solve tariffs" | The cost structure under local warehouses is similar to Amazon, and the ultimate low price no longer exists |
| "Duoduo Maicai has won" | Meituan Instant Retail redefines near-field retail with "30-minute delivery" |
| "Pinduoduo merchants have nowhere to go" | Douyin e-commerce's 30%+ growth rate is attracting white brand merchants |

## 7. Overall conclusion

### Overall review of industry dimensions

| Dimensions | Rating | Conclusion |
|------|------|------|
| Industry growth space | ★★★☆☆ | Domestic single-digit growth rate, cross-border tariffs hit hard |
| Intensity of competition | ★★★★★ | Top four + cross-border multi-party melee, the most intense in history |
| Industry Barriers | ★★★☆☆ | Douyin’s GMV of 4 trillion in 3 years proves that the moat is shallower than imagined |
| Policy Risk | ★★★★☆ | Tariff policy is the biggest external variable |
| Risks of AI subversion | ★★★☆☆ | Mid-term efficiency tool, worthy of vigilance in the long term |

### Comprehensive competitive position score: 3.5/5

**Mung’s ultimate question**: What exactly is Pinduoduo’s moat? If the answer is "low price", then this moat is being eroded by both tariffs (overseas) and Douyin's algorithm (domestic). If the answer is "supply chain efficiency," then this is a capability that can be replicated by AI and competitors. A truly lasting moat should be an irreplaceable position in consumers' minds - but the label "cheap" is precisely the one that is most easily replaced.
