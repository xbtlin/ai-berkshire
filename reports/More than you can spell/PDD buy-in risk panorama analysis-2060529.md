#PDD buying risk panoramic analysis

> Study date: 2026-05-29
> Background: PDD’s market value fell to about 790 billion yuan, and its stock price plummeted by more than 13% after the release of the 2026Q1 financial report.

---

## 1. Financial performance risks: increasing revenue without increasing profits, growth slowing down significantly

### 2026 Q1 core data

| Indicators | Values | Year-on-year changes | Remarks |
|------|------|----------|------|
| Total revenue | 106.2 billion yuan | +11% | Lower than market expectations of 108.6 billion yuan, with a growth rate of 59% in the same period in 2024 |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 12.5 billion yuan | -15% | — |
| Non-GAAP net profit | 14.1 billion yuan | -17% | Far lower than market expectations of 24.6 billion yuan |
| Operating profit margin | 18.4% | — | 27.5% for the full year of 2024, a sharp decline |
| Advertising revenue | 49.9 billion yuan | +2.5% | A new low in the past two years, far lower than the expected 8% |
| Transaction service revenue | 56.3 billion yuan | +20% | Overtaking advertising revenue for the first time to become the largest source of income |

### Key Signals

- Management made it clear that "**profit margins will be even lower in the future**" and refused to provide specific growth guidance.
- The company holds **436 billion yuan in cash** on its books, but it neither buys back nor distributes dividends
- Non-operating losses: Interest and investment income turned from positive to negative, causing a total negative impact of more than 6 billion yuan
- After the financial report, Macquarie lowered its rating from "outperform" to "neutral" and cut its target price from US$151 to US$87; Bernstein, Citigroup, Morgan Stanley, etc. also lowered their target prices.

## 2. Temu’s overseas business risks: heavy tariff attack + global regulatory crackdown

### US market (the largest single market)

- **Tariff Impact**: Starting from May 2, 2026, the United States will cancel the tax exemption policy for small packages (T86) of Chinese goods under $800, and all direct mail products from China will be subject to **54% ad valorem tariff**
- **Business model forced to transform**: Starting from May 3, 2026, Temu US station will completely remove products directly shipped from China, and will be forced to switch to US local warehouse delivery (semi-managed Y2 model). Sellers without local warehouse layout will not be able to settle in.
- **Consumer spending plummets**: U.S. consumers’ spending on Temu** fell by approximately 36% year-on-year**
- **Comprehensive cost increase**: The comprehensive cost of direct mail model has increased by at least 20-30%
- **Significant reduction in advertising**: Google Shopping ads will be suspended starting in 2025, Facebook ads will be significantly reduced, and 85-90% of the budget will be transferred to existing user retention.

### European Union

- **DSA fine**: On May 28, 2026, the European Commission imposed a **200 million euro fine** on Temu in accordance with the Digital Services Act, determining that the platform failed to effectively assess the risks of illegal products (only the second non-compliance penalty under the DSA)
- **Follow-up investigations are still ongoing**: involving addictive design features, recommendation system transparency, researcher data access, etc.
- **Subsidy probe**: EU regulators raid Temu's Dublin headquarters to investigate whether it benefited from Chinese state subsidies
- **The tax-free threshold is about to be cancelled**: The EU’s 150 euro tax-free threshold is expected to be canceled in July 2026

### Other countries and regions

- **Türkiye**: Competition authority raids Temu's Istanbul office, antitrust investigation; cancels tax exemption policy for imports under 30 euros, Temu suspends local sales
- **Poland**: fined for false discount advertising
- **South Africa**: Department of Trade, Industry and Competition publicly warns Temu to comply with local consumer regulations
- **US UFLPA Risk**: Suspected of violating the "Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act" and facing the risk of import ban

### Temu Profit Outlook

- Loss exceeds RMB 20 billion in 2024
- The profit margin of the semi-managed model is only 4-5%, and the fully managed model still suffers a slight loss in the United States
- Management targets: global balance of payments in the first half of 2026, with modest profits in the second half - but tariffs and fines could delay this timeline
- GMV target of US$140 billion in 2026 (approximately 55% increase from 2025), but this target faces major challenges in the tariff environment

## 3. Domestic competition landscape risks: surrounded by enemies on all sides

- **Douyin E-commerce** is rising rapidly: its sales share will increase from 25.3% in 2024 to 30.4%, surpassing JD.com and becoming Pinduoduo’s largest incremental competitor
- **Taotian Group** still accounts for about 36-44% of the market share, and is far ahead in terms of size
- **JD**’s share increased slightly to 30.5%, and it continues to poach Pinduoduo merchants in terms of quality e-commerce.
- **Policy restricting low-price competition**: The three departments issued the "Internet Platform Price Behavior Rules" in April 2026, which clearly prohibits platforms from forcing merchants to "price the lowest price on the entire network", **directly weakening Pinduoduo's core strategy**
- Pinduoduo has taken the initiative to cancel the "lowest price on the entire network" requirement during the 618 promotion and changed it to a "same product, same price" strategy
- The overall growth rate of domestic e-commerce has dropped to single digits, user growth has peaked, and customer acquisition costs for each platform have increased significantly.

## 4. Supervision and compliance risks: internal and external difficulties

### Domestic

- **Sky-high fine of 1.522 billion yuan** (April 2026): Due to the food safety issues of "ghost takeout", the State Administration for Market Regulation fined Pinduoduo 1.522 billion yuan, the highest among the seven platforms involved. The investigation found 67,604 fake stores and over 3.6 million illegal transfer orders.
- **Violent anti-law incident**: Pinduoduo is the only platform that has been named as a "violent anti-law" - employees swallowed A4 paper string confession materials on the spot, the technical director pretended to be comatose, and deliberately closed the door, causing law enforcement officers to have broken fingers and concussions. Seriously damage the company's reputation
- **Consumer Complaints**: From 2023 to 2025, there were a total of 319,700 complaints, involving an amount of more than 268 million yuan.
- **Tax Violations**: In January 2026, Shanghai tax authorities imposed penalties on Temu-related entities

### Overseas

- EU DSA fine of 200 million euros
- The EU "Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive" will be converted by member states in July 2026 and will require Temu to review human rights issues in the supply chain.
- Shein v. Temu infringement case: The British High Court will hear the case in 2026. Shein accused Temu of "industrial scale" copyright infringement; the US court also allowed Shein's core intellectual property claims to proceed.

## 5. Governance and management risks

- **ESG rating extremely poor**: MSCI rating CCC, the lowest for mainstream Chinese Internet companies (JD.com and Baidu are AA, Alibaba is BBB)
- **Information disclosure is not transparent**: Core operational data such as number of users and DAU are not disclosed, and management avoids key issues during conference calls.
- **Management Change**: The co-chairman system will be implemented in December 2025. Zhao Jiazhen and Chen Lei will serve as co-chairmen and co-CEOs. Changes in the governance structure will increase uncertainty.
- **Founder Fade Out**: Huang Zheng withdrew from front-line management many years ago, leaving hidden worries among investors
- **VIE structure and Cayman registration**: Standard Chinese concept stock structure risks, investors hold interests in Cayman Islands holding companies, non-Chinese operating entities direct equity
- **Huge amounts of cash are not returned to shareholders**: 436 billion yuan in cash reserves, neither dividends nor repurchases, and the efficiency of capital allocation is questioned

---

## 6. Risks of strategic transformation: "New Pinmu" bets hundreds of billions

- **"New Pinmu" Project**: Announced in March 2026, with an initial capital injection of 15 billion yuan, and a three-year plan to invest 100 billion yuan to create an "online Costco" brand self-operation model, with approximately 4,000 SKUs
- **Core Risk**: The shift from an asset-light platform model to a heavy-asset self-operated model is a completely different circle of competence and is extremely difficult to implement.
- **Profit Squeeze**: Hundreds of billions of support + investment in New Pinmu + overseas expansion of Temu, triple investment superimposed, the management clearly stated that "profit margin fluctuations will be the norm"
- **Long verification cycle**: It takes three years to prove the feasibility of the business model, and profits will continue to be under pressure during this period

---

## 7. Valuation and Risk-Return Assessment

### Current valuation

- Market value is approximately RMB 790 billion (approximately US$108 billion)
- The expected price-to-earnings ratio in 2026 is about 8-9 times
- Cash on hand is 436 billion yuan, and the market value after excluding cash is approximately 354 billion yuan

### Valuation looks cheap, but you need to be wary

1. **Profits are in a downward channel**: Management actively chooses to trade profits for long-term competitiveness. Low PE may be a "value trap"
2. **There is a lot of cash, but it doesn’t belong to you**: No dividends or repurchases, cash is "visible but not tangible" to small shareholders.
3. **Cash return is limited under the VIE structure**: Even if dividends are paid, there are policy risks of cross-border transfer of funds.

## Summary: Six major risk dimensions

| Risk Dimensions | Severity | Core Judgments |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Financial growth slows down | High | Advertising revenue growth is only 2.5%, profit margins shrink actively, and old engines stall |
| Temu Global Supervision | Extremely high | US tariffs of 54%, EU fines of 200 million, multi-country siege, business models forced to reconstruct |
| Domestic competition | Medium to high | The rise of Douyin e-commerce + low-price strategy is restricted by policies, and core advantages are damaged |
| Compliance reputation | High | 1.5 billion fines + violent resistance to the law, serious damage to public image |
| Corporate Governance | Medium to High | Bottom in ESG, opaque information, non-distribution of cash, VIE risk |
| Strategic Transformation | Medium | Xinpinmu has invested 100 billion, has a large circle of competence, and has a three-year verification cycle |

**Bottom line judgment**: PDD's current low valuation of 8-9 times PE corresponds to the triple pressure of growth engine switching period + global regulatory storm + active downward pressure on profits. This is not a simple "buy it cheap" - you need to have a high degree of confidence in the successful reconstruction of the Temu model, the success of the new Pinmu run-through, and the stability of the domestic main site to be suitable for building a position at this position.
