# V. Factual verification: whether it's real or media exaggeration?

This section examines article by article the statements that are widely reported as "frightening words".

# 5.1 "Profits will gradually move down the road."

** [Factual]** Chen did say at the 2024 Q2 telephone conference that "in the direction of direction, the downward trend will gradually be" and that the sentence is fully documented in the original language and quoted in many media.

** [Factual]** The statement directly triggered a single day - 28.51 per cent drop, and was a verifiable market event.

** [Analysis space]** Whether the phrase is "surprising" depends on the criteria for judgement: from the point of view of information transparency, this is a rare proactive downward profit expectation; from the long-term perspective, it may be the pre-management of investors' expectations.

---

# 5.2 "No buybacks or reds in the next few years."

** [Factual]** Chen did say this at the Q2 2024 telephone conference, which reads: "This is not the right time to buy back stocks or split dividends. And we don't see that in the years ahead."

** [Factual]** Cash on the company’s books was over RMB284.9 billion, operating cash flows of RMB43.793 billion. Cash reserves are well-stocked with “no need to buy back” and are at the heart of investor disputes.

---

# 5.3 "Three years on the road of 10 years"

** [Factual]** The original words of Zhao Jia-Jia at the Q4 telephone conference in 2025, which meant that Temu had been extended to 90+ countries in three years, was "three years after the end of a 10-year road with more domestic electricity suppliers".

** [Note]** The sentence describes the speed of Temu’s expansion, not the profit or profit statement, which cannot be verified. 90+ country coverage is a proven business fact.

---

# 5.4 "A simple short-term return on corporate performance is no longer a good idea."

** [Factual]** Zhao Jia-Jia did say this at the Q4 telephone conference in 2025, and several media sources quoted it independently and expressed it in the same way.

** [Background]** This phrase is time-sensitive in the context of a slowdown in corporate profits and stock price pressure.
