##4.3 Tariff shocks

From May 2025, the United States eliminated the tax exemption for small parcels of less than $800 and imposed a maximum tariff of 120 per cent on Chinese goods or a fixed fee of $100 per piece (up from June to $200).

- ** Impact**: Both platforms announced price increases from 25 April 2025
- **Respond**: Accelerated construction of local storage in the United States and introduction of United States domestic vendors to avoid customs duties
- ** differentiated impact**: Shein has more elasticity in price-fixing due to his own brand attributes; Temu has been hit more by price increases due to extremely low positioning

# V. Core conclusions

#5.1 Business overlap judgement: ** medium low (approximately 25-30%)**

1. ** Class overlap below 30%** (YipitData data) directly competing only in the area of low-price clothing and accessories
2. ** Business models are fundamentally different**: Shein is a brand retailer (76% self-employed) and Temu is a pure platform (100% third party)
3. ** There is a partial overlap in the supply chain** but the pattern is very different: Shein is "deep binding + fast contraption" and Temu is "sleep cooperation + extremely low prices"
** High geographical overlap** but different niche markets: Temu leads the growth in Europe and Latin America, Shein leads the overall number of users and loyalty

#5.2 Convergence judgement: ** Very low (approximately 5%)**

1. ** User overlap rate of only 5% ** is much lower than the overlap rate between the two and TikTok Shop (25-28%)
** User portraits are almost complementary**: Shein is "young women chasing fashion" and Temu is "multi-age search for cheapness"
3. **The use of a completely different scene**: fashion browsing vs low-cost search
4. ** Brand mind is clearly distinguished**: consumers do not see the two as alternatives

# 5.3 A revelation of multi-investment research

1. **Temu’s true competitor is not Shein, but Amazon and local power suppliers**. Data on the quality and customer recombinance of the product indicates that the market’s narrative of Temu vs Shein is excessively simplified.
** Shein's moat in the fashion category (brand + supply chain + algorithm) is difficult to replicate** Temu's advantage in the size of the low price market for the whole product category is also difficult to catch up with Shein
** The two are more likely to lead to a mixed coexistence**: Shein is a deep-deeper, Temu is a low-cost platform for the whole package, similar to the relationship between Zara and Walmart
4. **The real risk variable is tariff policy**, not competition. The United States ' elimination of the small tax exemption policy in 2025 is a systemic risk for both
**Temu grew at a much faster rate than Shein** (50% vs 15-20%), but there are doubts about the sustainability of the deficit-for-growth model
