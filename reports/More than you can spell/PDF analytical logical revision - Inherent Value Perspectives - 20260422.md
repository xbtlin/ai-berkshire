# Part IV: Methodological gains from this revision

# The method of revision 1: separate the "personal money" from the "corporate money"

** Trap**: Use of the founder's personal charity to justify decisions on the capital allocation of the company

** Avoidance method**:
- How much is the founder's personal contribution?
- How does the company use its cash? ** Must be agreed by all shareholders**
- These two things can't be confused at any time.

# Rev.2 Method Harvest: Vigilance of the Market Value Worship trap

** Trap**: Short-term market value fluctuations to judge the capital allocation of companies

** Avoidance method**:
- Market value = market sentiment + intrinsic value (insulated)
- The company should be judged for growth in intrinsic value** (collective, profit, cash flow, user, market share)
- Short-term market value falls, corporate value creation fails.
- This is the way Buffett and Yongping think.

# A comprehensive approach to the harvest

** When doing investment analysis**:
1. ** Seeing changes in intrinsic value** and then how markets price
2. ** Corporate money goes to the company, personal money goes to the individual** and any ambiguity is an analysis trap.
** Recognition of errors is more valuable than covering them** — the core of value investments is continuous calibration of perceptions
