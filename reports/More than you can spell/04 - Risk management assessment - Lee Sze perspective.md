## 9. Overall conclusion from Li Lu’s perspective

### Three fundamental questions

**First, there are deep hidden dangers in management culture. ** Li Lu attaches great importance to "treating stakeholders well". Pinduoduo’s extreme exploitation of employees and merchants is efficient in the short term but seriously damages the long-term health of the business ecosystem**.

**Second, the foundation of Temu’s business model has been shaken. ** de minimis cancellation is not a "one-off shock" but a structural shift. The shift to local warehousing means competing head-on with Amazon in its strongest areas.

**Third, capital allocation capabilities are disappointing. **$54 billion in cash, PE 10.7x, but zero repurchases and zero dividends. Either there is a lack of confidence or a lack of awareness of shareholder returns.

### Positive factors

- Domestic main stations are still the first echelon
- Duoduomaicai is close to a monopoly
- $54 billion in cash provides huge margin of safety
- The C2M model is difficult to copy

### Final judgment

**Risk Level: High Risk (4.01/5.00)**

**Li Lu's possible attitude**: "This is an interesting company with a very cheap price, but I'm not sure what it will look like in 10 years. When there is insufficient certainty, no matter how cheap the price is, it does not constitute a sufficient margin of safety. I will continue to pay attention, but I will not take action for the time being."
