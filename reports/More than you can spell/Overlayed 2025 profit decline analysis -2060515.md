# Pinduoduo’s profit decline in 2025: active choice or forced to endure?

**Research Date: May 15, 2026 | Three Agents Parallel Research**

---

## One sentence conclusion

> **60% initiative, 40% forced. The core earning power has not been weakened, but has been strengthened. ** The reported net profit is 99.3 billion (-12%), but after excluding the impact of active investment and Temu tariffs, the normalized profit is about 120-140 billion, which is actually an increase from 112.4 billion in 2024. Pinduoduo chooses not to put money on its income statement—it is exchanging deterministic short-term profits for an uncertain long-term ecosystem.

## Main site vs Temu: Who is holding back?

Pinduoduo does not disclose it separately, but according to the investment bank split model:

| Segment | 2024 Revenue | Profit/Loss | Profit Margin |
|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Main site (domestic e-commerce)** | ~244.2 billion | **~150 billion** | **~61%** |
| **Temu (Globalization)** | ~119.6 billion | **~-25.3 billion** | -21% |
| **Buy a lot of food** | ~30 billion | Close to breakeven | ~0% |
| **Consolidated Statements** | 393.8 billion | 112.4 billion | 28.5% |

**The main station is a money printing machine with a profit margin of 61%. ** The consolidated profit margin was only 28.5%, which was lowered entirely by Temu’s 25.3 billion loss.

Temu loss trend: Loss of 58-65 billion in 2023 → Narrow to ~25.3 billion in 2024 → Continue to narrow in 2025 (loss rate is about -8%) → Target breakeven in the European and American markets in Q4 2026. The direction is right, but the impact of the Temu tariff (the daily activity in the United States plummeted 58% and was forced to switch from air to sea) has indeed increased the burden in 2025.

## Active vs. forced: 60% vs. 40%

### Active factors (accounting for about 60% of the profit decline)

**1. Merchant fee reduction-the largest active investment**
- "100 billion support" plan: covering a total of 2.7 million+ merchants
- Q2 single-quarter promotion fee refund of 11 billion, single merchant saves 180,000 per year on average
- Margins for many categories such as major appliances are reduced by 50-90%
- Commission rates have been significantly reduced

**2. Increase in R&D investment (+3.8 billion, +30%)**
- AI recommendation system upgrade
-Temu technology infrastructure
- Merchant operation management system
- Completely a long-term oriented proactive choice

**3. Consumer subsidies**
- "Ten billion consumer coupons", "Ten billion double reimbursement"
- 618 exceeded 3.76 million orders in a single day
- Platform direct subsidies offset the weakness of state subsidies in the field of 3C home appliances

**4. Preparation for New Pinmu**
- Increase investment in Q4 to prepare for release in March 2026

**Key evidence - the pace of costs is completely controllable**:

| Quarterly | Marketing expenses | Year-on-year | Interpretation |
|------|---------|------|------|
| Q1 | 33.4 billion | **+43%** | Concentrate big bets |
| Q2 | 27.2 billion | **+4.5%** | Obvious stoppage |
| Q3 | 30.3 billion | Controllable | Recovery but not out of control |
| Q4 | 34.4 billion | Year-end increase | Paving the way for new Pinmu |

** can be retracted or released, not out of control and burning money. ** If we are forced to spend money by competition, it is impossible for Q2 to suddenly drop from +43% to +4.5%.

### Passive factors (accounting for about 40% of the profit decline)

**1. Temu tariff impact – the biggest passive factor**
- In May 2025, the United States will cancel the tax exemption policy for purchases below $800.
-Temu daily activity plummeted 58%
- The proportion of air freight will be reduced from 85% to 2%, and all shipping will be switched to sea freight.
- Average daily subsidy of approximately US$15 million to maintain low prices
- The semi-custodial transformation led to a decrease in commission rates, and the growth rate of trading service revenue plummeted from +108% to +9%

**2. Douyin competition**
- Douyin e-commerce GMV growth rate is 30%+, attacking head-on in the low-price white label track
- Some categories are forced to increase subsidies

**3. Conflicts among merchants are forcing**
- Merchant grievances accumulated over the "refund only" policy (160,000 Black Cat complaints, merchants besieged the headquarters)
- Forced to cancel mandatory refunds in April 2025 - not entirely voluntary

**4. The national subsidy policy favors 3C home appliances**
- Pinduoduo’s weak categories are forced to pay for compensation out of pocket

## True earning power: normalized profits are higher

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 Statement | 2025 Normalization (estimate) |
|------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Net profit | 112.4 billion | 99.3 billion | **~120-140 billion** |
| Year-on-year | — | -12% | **+7%~+25%** |

Normalization method: reported profit of 99.3 billion + active investment increment (merchant returns + consumption subsidies of about 20-30 billion) + additional cost of Temu tariff (about 10 billion) - one-time income adjustment ≈ 120-140 billion.

**The core earning power has not only not been weakened, but is also improving. ** The management chose to invest both incremental profits and part of the existing profits back into ecological construction.

August 2024 (Q2 conference call) is a key turning point. The management took the initiative to warn of the actual decline in profits two quarters before the actual decline - if it was passive, it would not be possible to accurately forecast so far in advance.

The stock price plummeted 28.5% that day, evaporating US$55 billion. However, on the day Duan Yongping plummeted, he sold put to buy the bottom and continued to increase his position thereafter. ** All three major investment banks (Goldman Sachs/Morgan Stanley/Citigroup) maintain buy ratings.

### A warning sign

Zhao Jiazhen said something in Q3: "Compared to some of our peers, we may be at a phased disadvantage for a long time."

This is not something a manager in control would say. It implies that subsidies and profit concessions are not entirely motivated by calmness, but also anxiety about losing competitive position. **

## Final judgment

**Pinduoduo's profit decline in 2025 is essentially a "forced accelerated active transformation." **

The management realized in mid-2024 that "the high profits earned by squeezing merchants are unsustainable" and planned a path to actively share profits and rebuild the ecosystem. However, external factors such as tariff impact, Douyin competition, and merchant conflicts forced the transformation to happen faster and more intensely than planned.

| Dimensions | Judgment |
|------|------|
| Active vs. forced | **60% active, 40% forced** |
| Core earning power | **Not weakened, normalized profits increased** |
| Main site profit margin | **~61%, extremely healthy** |
| Expense controllability | **Can be collected and released, not out of control and burning money** |
| Management Intention | **Early Warning, Consistency, Financial Support - High Credibility** |
| Hidden governance risks | **420 billion in cash not returned to shareholders, which is the biggest deduction item** |
| Next verification point | **2027-2028: Can New Pinmu be realized and can Temu be profitable** |
