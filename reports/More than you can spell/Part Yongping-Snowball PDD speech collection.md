# A collection of Duan Yongping’s public speeches about Pinduoduo

> Compilation date: 2026-04-13
> Source description: Snowball’s entire site is protected by WAF and cannot be captured directly. All the content of this article comes from media reprints of his Snowball posts, as well as written records of offline activities that Duan Yongping himself participated in. Each statement is marked with the original source URL.
> Duan Yongping Snowball ID: The avenue is invisible but I am stylish (user number: 1247347556)

## 1. 2015 (angel investment period)

**Source: Duan Yongping’s memories at the Zhejiang University exchange meeting on January 5, 2025, Sina Finance records**
https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-01-05/doc-inecxqhr2576638.shtml

> "He (Huang Zheng) hoped that I would invest in them, so I asked him if he could make money. He said he didn't know, but the user base grew very fast, many people like to use it, and the suppliers were also very happy. Things that were not easy to sell were sold. Especially agricultural products. They started with agricultural products."

> "After hearing the speed of growth, I said, okay, I'll do it as a charity, because such a fast growth means that I am doing good things. If I make money, I will donate it to my fund and use it as a charity. If I don't make money, but I help so many people, it is considered a charity."

> "Then I gave him a condition, saying that you can invest as much as you want me to invest. In fact, he didn't come to me because he was short of money, but to draw me in as an investor."

> "I invested in Pinduoduo when it was in its infancy, with a small proportion but a lot of profit. Strictly speaking, it is not a heavy position, because I invested in it in a daze."

## 8. January 5, 2025 (Zhejiang University Exchange Meeting, offline Q&A)

**Source: Sina Finance 20,000-word record, Tencent News compiled version, Huxiu compiled version**
https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-01-05/doc-inecxqhr2576638.shtml
https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250106A071I900
https://m.huxiu.com/article/3875526.html

**About business model and user recognition:**
> "There are more and more users, and so many people like to use it. If you insist that it is wrong, what do you mean?"

**About Huang Zheng’s original intention:**
> "When Huang Zheng first started his business, for example, when he was making good products, he was very concerned about agriculture. He just wanted to distribute agricultural products. He really did a lot of things, including a lot of the infrastructure he is doing now, which are related to this."

**About Huang Zheng’s quality:**
> "I think Huang Zheng is not a profit-seeking person. Many people don't understand this, and I understand it very well. He is not here to make money, and he will not fool you."

> "When Pinduoduo's stock price plummeted some time ago, many people questioned whether it was a scam. I said I don't know anything else, but I am sure of this. He is not a liar."

**About Temu Globalization:**
> "Now I ask everyone in the United States, do you know Temu? Yes, almost everyone."

> "Huang Zheng did it. Huang Zheng asked me that time, what do you think of the effectiveness of the 'Super Bowl' commercial? I said I saw it... He said the effect was okay. I said it was okay, but you only put two ads. He said the next year, four or five ads... So he got the point all at once."

> "Now China is considered to be ready for globalization. Maybe there is Pinduoduo and Douyin, and the others seem to be partial globalization."

**About merchant questions:**
> "I've heard about it. I think it's hard for me to comment first of all, because I don't know the specific situation. Most suppliers are still serious (on the platform), maybe their competitive environment is worse."

## 10. November 11, 2025 (Dialogue on Xueqiu's "Strategy", interview with Fang Sanwen)

**Nature of speech: Snowball official dialogue program, recorded on October 16, 2025, released on November 11**
**Source: The Paper 20,000-word record, Sina Finance compiled version**
https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31947522
https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/t/2025-11-12/doc-infxcywv3357540.shtml

**About positions and operations:**

(Fang Sanwen asked: You said before that you had venture capital investment in it. In fact, you have also increased your position recently. Is it still a venture capital investment now?)

> "Still, but I know a little better. I actually sold a large position and bought almost all of it back. I think this company is still interesting, but it is risky. I don't recommend others to buy it."

(Fang Sanwen asked: You said you bought it again and you feel you understand it better. What part do you feel you understand?)

> "The money they make is there, and you can see the financial reports. If it can maintain this business, it is very cheap. The problem is that I don't know if it can really be maintained, and what kind of things will affect it, but I trust their entire culture and entire team, but I don't know the general environment."

(Fang Sanwen asked: What will happen to Pinduoduo in 5 to 10 years? Now you can’t establish a clear expectation?)

> "I think there is a high probability that they will live well, but it is not 100%. I think Apple can say that I know for sure that Apple will live well. I am not so clear about Pinduoduo."
