# Part I: Introductory statement by the executive

# Chiu Jia-Jia (Executive Director/Co-CEO)

The following is a complete compilation of the opening remarks of Zhao Jia:

Hello, I'm Chiu Jia-Jia. Thank you for joining the performance conference for the first quarter of 2026.

The year 2026 was the second and most numerous decade and the key year for comprehensive cultural innovation in corporate organizations, centred on high-quality development.

** On organizational restructuring:**

Since this year, we are promoting a profound re-engineering of internal organizational governance around security compliance, social responsibility, etc. Teams are called upon to make security, compliance, and social responsibility the bottom line of all their work. We are committed to effectively raising corporate responsibility as a platform.

** On New Chom/Clare Self-Camp:**

The company has entered a new phase of the "remaking a more than three-year strategy." Our brand business is moving steadily, and we double our investment in the supply chain.

In March, we launched a special company in Chang-Am, officially launching a brand-owned business, with an initial investment of $15 billion, with a plan to invest $100 billion over the next few years. The goal is to systematically incubate a global brand of famous brands.

** On 10 billion subsidies/100 billion support:**

In the first quarter, the $10 billion support project continued to be highly invested, with a series of initiatives such as the "Multiple Good" agricultural upgrade, the new supply chain, and the deployment of planting guides, logistics support, processing and enabling projects in Guangxi, Hainan, Sichuan, etc.

** On industrial belt construction:**

The manufacturing industry is moving from homogenous competition to a model of research and development centred on consumer demand. By way of optimizing the supply chain, for example, logistics costs have been reduced from $40-50 to around $10 per unit.

** On village parcels:**

Since this year, we have been pushing the "Cluster In" project at full speed, with initial results. As of March, the delivery coverage at the village level had exceeded 70%. The average daily order for the transit warehouse was close to 10,000, creating a large number of local jobs in the county and township economies.

Our team is upgrading village extraction points to multifunctional digitized village service stations with the function of first kilometre logistics node for parcels and agricultural products.

** On Platform governance:**

In the first quarter, the Platform launched more than 20 food safety initiatives, including compliance reviews, food advertising audits and enhanced monitoring.

** End of item:**

Initial challenges are inevitable, but they are the driving force behind our own innovation. The current management team will face them.

** On the three-year strategy:**

At last year's shareholders' conference, we officially proposed a three-year "re-engineering" strategy that focused the strategic focus of the company on supply chain investment.

In the first quarter, brand ownership made solid progress. Teams moved into product industries to promote the integration of high-quality supply chain resources. Through the brand self-employment model, our goal is to systematically incubate a global array of brands of excellence, thereby leading to a transformational upgrading of the entire supply chain.

Businesses are using platform support to brand, shifting the product mix from "more and more" to "more and better" and from standardized production to demand-driven customization. OEM plants are starting to invest in R & D, technology, branding and distribution channels.

Brand development is the next major opportunity for upgrading the supply chain.

Recently, we have held the finals of the multi-agricultural science and technology competition, which is now in its sixth session. This competition has evolved from a trade competition to an integrated platform for the next generation of agricultural researchers, making its contribution to the modernization of agriculture.

We have identified our own shortcomings and taken corrective measures. We are moving forward with the restructuring of internal management, strengthening compliance and laying the foundation for long-term healthy development of companies.

2026 is the new beginning of a decade or so. With a deep sense of responsibility and purpose, we are convinced that the originals are the foundation of everything.

# Part II: Analyst Q & A

# # Q1: Citibank Alicia Yap

** Question one:** How much has been positioned as a power-marketing platform, and what are the core considerations behind the introduction of brand-owned business? Does this mean a major shift in corporate corporate strategy?

** Question 2:** From third-party data, more than one overseas business has grown well. What is the focus of future overseas business development?

---

** Chiu Jia responded (brand self-employment strategy):**

Since this year, we are promoting a profound reorganization of organizational and internal management around security compliance, social responsibility and so on.

In the process, we have become increasingly aware of the challenges facing the current supply chain — many of the best manufacturers being subjected to such factors as talent, information and size — and have not yet completed the branding transformation.

As platforms, we have the responsibility and the ability to deepen supply chain inputs and provide solutions to industry’s problems. By creating dedicated companies, we are consolidating platform resources and promoting new brand-run models.

Through brand-owned models, the platform assumes greater responsibility and risks, allowing our industry partners to focus on high-quality production.

We believe that this is necessary to move the platform and the entire electric company to the next stage of quality development. With the platform’s size advantage, we will take more risks while sharing the returns with the supply chain manufacturers and providing certainty.

In a complex and volatile market environment, the platform must assume greater responsibility and deepen its operations.

---

** Chen Jian's reply (overseas operations):**

After nearly three years of development, our overseas operations have received the support of a large number of consumers around the world. The key is to return to the nature of the power provider, namely, supply chain capacity.

Our work will be structured around two strategic directions:

** First, to promote integration and optimization of supply chains and remove end-to-end bottlenecks.** We will actively introduce high-quality business and ecological partners.

** Second, deepening the development of the brand self-employment model.** Branding is particularly critical in strengthening consumer mentalities.

The power-marketing sector is competitive and consumers’ conversion costs are low. The real driver of long-term sustainable competitive advantage is the supply chain capabilities that are often not seen.

Supply chain investment is a long-term systemic undertaking. Whatever the challenges we face, we will continue to drive deep transformation.

---

# # Q2: Goldman Sachs Ronald Keung

** Question I:** Regarding the three-year home-grown $100 billion investment plan - in which areas the company plans to allocate these investments, when will it be financially visible? How can the incremental growth potential of this strategy be assessed?

** Question II:** Why did the growth in online marketing services in the first quarter slow in the context of consumer growth and the rising penetration of low-price goods? (Note: advertising revenue increased by only 2.5 per cent over the same period)

---

** Chiu Jia responded (investment direction):**

Branding involves a range of capabilities — from product design, standard-setting, manufacturing, to quality control, warehousing logistics, legal compliance, customer service — each requires long-term, patient inputs.

Many small and medium-sized manufacturing firms in the industry belt are doing well in large-scale production and cost control, but the value chain has not been up yet due to lack of branding capacity.

By creating dedicated companies, we are building on the technical, scale and organizational strengths of the Platform to build basic capacities and help supply chain partners develop brand names.

Under the brand-owned model, we provide certainty in the volume of sales to the supply chain, enabling manufacturers to invest confidently in R & D and process innovation, significantly reducing their costs and risks.

We believe that this is the right strategic direction. We create a win-win situation with a supply chain of certainty – manufacturers can jump out of homogenous competition and focus on quality upgrading; consumers can enjoy quality products at reasonable prices.

Looking back at our history, from the improvement of the supply chain for agricultural products in the early stages to the subsequent purchase of more vegetables, overseas operations, and the support of 10 billion people —** If the homogenization of competition faced by platform traders is to be fundamentally addressed, we must assume greater responsibility for promoting deeper supply chain integration — a road that is essential**.

This year has opened a new decade of high-quality development. We will use this as an opportunity to reshape organizational and internal management.

---

Industry has entered a critical phase of high-quality development. The only way to achieve sustainable and healthy industry growth is to take the necessary initiatives to deepen the supply chain.

Our team is upgrading village extraction points to multifunctional digital village services, with the function of a first kilometre logistics node for parcels and agricultural products. By providing farmers with one-stop solutions for packaged and shipped goods, we help to reach a wider market for quality agricultural products.

# Q3: Monetary Joyce Ju

** Question I:** How does the company assess the impact of the new model (live power provider, instant retail, etc.) on the industry landscape? Are there any plans to expand to these areas?

** Question II:** A slight increase in marketing costs and fluctuations in overall profitability during the quarter. What are the trends in future profitability? What are the reasonable expectations of steady-state profit levels?

---

** Chiu Jia's reply (new business model):**

The transformation costs of electricity suppliers are much lower than those of traditional retail sales, which leads to faster industrial evolution and more competitive competition. The management team is also closely following the development of new technologies.

** Competition between different power-market platforms or business models is ultimately competition for bottom-chain capabilities**

That is why we developed a clear corporate strategy at the end of last year — focusing and re-arguing supply chain investments.

Under this strategy, we are steadily moving forward with supply chain initiatives, especially brand-owned operations. Special teams are now working in key industries to accelerate the integration of quality supply chain resources and build brand-owned frameworks.

We work closely with manufacturers to develop brand products for different market designs while promoting the transformation and upgrading of supply chains.

** The coming period will be a critical window for our supply chain investment, and we will move ahead with branding self-employment on a full-time basis.** We will also respond to the evolving industry landscape by constantly creating unique value for the supply chain ecology.

---

** LEE JUNG:**

Our goal is long-term sustainable growth in the built-in value of the platform. It is normal for quarterly financial data to fluctuate due to seasonal factors.

** The long-term value of the platform depends fundamentally on the value we create for consumers and the wider ecology**

We are therefore firmly committed to supply chain development — whether brand-based self-employment strategies, fast-forward villages, or new-quality supply chains — which are high-impact projects that can unleash long-term values and new growth in industry. We will remain committed.

** We will not focus too much on short-term financial performance, but rather prioritize the accumulation and consolidation of the platform’s ecological health development and supply chain capacity.** The long-term, sustained competitive advantage will determine the course of the platform’s intrinsic value.
