##7.5 Operational Recommendations

** Current valuation: extreme underestimation of the interval**

- Net cash per PE is ~6x, even if it's not expensive.
- TTM PE ~ 11x, P/FCF ~ 10x, the cheapest in a global power company platform
- 423.3 billion net cash to provide huge security cushions

** But uncertainty to be endured:**

- The customs backwind that Temu is facing is substantial, not short-term noise.
- A three-year return on 100 billion supply chain investments
- Whether you buy back or not means you're gonna have to profit from the stock price.

** Operational recommendations:**

- ** Holder**: Continue to hold, not currently selling point. The worst-profit Q1 has passed and the valuation provides sufficient security margins.
- **Observer**: The warehouse can be built in lots at $90-100. Core logic -- you bought a $14 billion-a-year power-market platform with $60 billion in cash, even if Temu failed completely, worth the price of domestic business.
- **Cluster of loss**: If the negative growth in revenues from online domestic marketing services over two consecutive quarters is indicative of the fundamental swing, the paper needs to be reassessed.

> Ree-Seong 's wisdom: "The real value investor is not predicting the future, but assessing whether current prices already adequately reflect the worst. The current price of the PDD has implied Temu 's failure assumption -- if Temu is successful in any way, it is a huge mispricing."
