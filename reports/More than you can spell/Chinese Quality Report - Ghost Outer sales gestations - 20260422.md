# Six, one store, one punishment, far-reaching

As the investigation evidence is advanced, evidence of violations becomes clearer, but a new challenge and challenge is before the panel: the Platform ' s administrative sanctions programme.

Zhang Hei, Deputy Director of the Administrative Control of the Market Authority of the Shanghai Rainbow Pass, said, "It seems that we are investigating a big case, actually facing more than 60,000 specific cases, each shop is different and cannot be quantified."

The Directorate-General of Market Supervision draws on the success of the Task Force and brings together the various actors to develop a synergy of efforts:
- More than 50,000 referral orders were issued to all provinces and municipalities throughout the country to verify the platform trails found in the cases
- Joint Office of the Law Enforcement Inspectorate, the Department of Regulations, the Web Division of Oversight, the Catering Division, the Press Department, etc., work together to resolve important cases

The Directorate-General of Market Supervision extends the legal reasoning from the system to the legislative, judicial and academic authorities to ensure that cases can withstand legal and historical tests. The Director-General has led four separate visits to the legislative and judicial branches to consult repeatedly on the core issues of the identification of responsibilities and the setting of penalties for the platform, so that the law can be applied and the right to exercise due diligence is effectively exercised.

The Directorate-General organizes legal expert seminars to invite competent experts in the fields of administrative law, civil law, electronic commerce law, etc., to conduct in-depth discussions and develop written arguments on key issues such as the definition of the subject of the offence, the platform for examining the boundaries of the obligation, and the manner in which the proceeds of the violation are calculated.

The Director-General of the Directorate-General has chaired 24 thematic meetings to study the violations of the Platform on an article-by-article basis, to repeat the administrative penalties programme and to ensure that every enterprise, every violation of the law and every penalty amount are established by law.

A punishment programme that reflects the principle of "excessive punishment" and highlights the "four most demanding" requirements, in line with the expectations of the people, is taking shape, with full input from the legislature, the judiciary and academia.

The Directorate General of Market Supervision decided that, in accordance with the principle of "excessive punishment", the Platform's failure to comply with its obligation to review should be punishable by a single store.

The Director of the Directorate-General of the Directorate-General of Market Supervision, Wang Fung Wang, stated that the case was a landmark case in the history of the Directorate-General of Market Supervision, which regulated food safety and the development of regulatory platforms. The penalties imposed by the Directorate-General of Market Supervision reflect a regulatory orientation towards the development of the platform's economic norms and the promotion of development and regulatory norms, both of which are strictly mandatory. The basic objective is to urge the platform's enterprises to comply with their legal obligations to establish mechanisms for controlling and controlling risks to food safety, which are subject to good qualification, risk monitoring, problem screening and rapid disposal, and to ensure food safety and promote the development of export platforms.
