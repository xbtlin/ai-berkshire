# Ten, final judgment

# One sentence to sum up

> **Token transit station is a business that is "window-treathing, dying after three years" -- essentially a double mismatch between "models don't enter China + user-sensitive to cost/speed". Short-term net profit (10-25%), but model price index decline, plant self-building nodes, and three tightening of regulatory forces are killing the ecology. Three years alive, no longer "turn-point" but "AI Gateway" -- it's a qualitative change, not a simple upgrade**.

# Three specific suggestions

1. ** If you are a user**: do not hoard too much balance (run risk), monthly
2. ** If you are an entrepreneur**: the entry threshold is low and you die, you must plan the upgrade path for "smart route plus enterprise level plus vertical SaaS" from day one.
3. ** If you are an investor**: Skip the transit, look directly at OpenRouter / Portkey / Together AI, a player that's already "the next stop"
