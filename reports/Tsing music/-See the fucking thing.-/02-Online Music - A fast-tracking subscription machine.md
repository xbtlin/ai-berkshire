# The driving force of the increase in the rate of payment

** First, habits have been developed.** China paid less than 10% for online music in 2019. From 10 to 24%, it took only six years. This suggests that user willingness to pay is increasing systematically, not simply simply by evaporating.

** Second, free content is decreasing.** After the exclusive copyright antimonopoly in 2021, copyrighters prefer to make all platforms do the right thing. The free listening space is shrinking - restrictions such as random play, limited time-testing, low sound quality etc., push free users to pay.

** Third, young users are more willing to pay.** After 95, after 00, the acceptance of "pay for content" is significantly higher than after 80. The rate of payment naturally follows the intergenerational turnover of users.

Where's the ceiling for the fee rate?

The data show that 24% = 35% of the road is likely to be reached. But over 35% – 40% have begun to get harder.

There are two reasons:

** Speciality of the Chinese market** - Free alternatives always exist. Short video platforms (twitching, fast-handed) provide a big music content consumption scene, and users do not have to pay for subscriptions to music platforms.

** The low-cost strategy for soda music** - the eight-dollar monthly card vs 18-month card, the diversion effect for price-sensitive users is real.

** Reasonable expectation**: A 30-35% rate in 3-5 years is a approximate event. A 40% rate takes longer, provided that no large-scale theft of user of the soda music takes place.

# ARPPU Upgrade Path

** Path one: Super-intensity rises.** This is the clearest path -- no higher prices, just more people to upgrade. For every extra-supplier, the mix of ARPUs goes up a little bit.

** Path II: Basic members raise prices.** In 2023, tweak music raised the base members from $13 to $15, part of the package to $18. There is still room for price increases for the 25 dollars of the Aichi. But price increases depend on the competitive environment where the eight-month soda is stuck, and a sharp price increase may push users to their competitors.

** Route three: Increase in advertising revenue.** Free users pay no fees but contribute to advertising income.

# ARPU ceiling

A reasonable ceiling for ARPPU, combining domestic consumption capacity and competition patterns, is probably at **$20-25/month**.

The logic of the exercise: assuming that after 3 to 5 years, the hyperbolic penetration rate is 25% (US$ 28-30 per month), the average member ARPPU is about 15 (after a small price increase) – the combined ARPPU is about 18 to 19 dollars. If the average member also has a cycle of price increases, it can be 20 to 22 dollars.

This ceiling means:** from the current $11.9 to $20-25, with room for 70-110%.** But this will not happen one step at a time — it may take 5-8 years to move progressively.

** Inverse view**: ARPU upgrades are premised on stable competition patterns. If soda music is significantly expanded over the next 2-3 years to pay users (currently based on a 8-dollar monthly card), telecommunication music may be forced to "roll price" instead of "price increase." In this scenario, ARPU will be raised much slower than expected.

# Five, one overlooked fact: copyright costs are falling

In 2021, the National Market Regulatory Authority asked the music to give up exclusive copyright. The market was panicking -- "Isn't the moat of music falling without exclusive copyright?"

But then, one thing that went against the instincts:** the loss of exclusive copyrights has led to a decline in copyright costs for tact music.**

The reason is that in the exclusive copyright age, the record company knows that you have to buy -- and the price is more expensive. After the non-exclusive, the tweaking music can be used to make an actuarial "not worth buying" for each song -- not for the price, but for the competition. The copyright procurement has changed from "arms race" to "student."
