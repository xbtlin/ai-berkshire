## Series Positioning

Tencent Music is not a company that nobody pays attention to - it is one of the most **underappreciated yet data-conflicted** names in the China ADR universe.

- The bulls say profits have grown for years, net cash of RMB 38 billion equals 40% of market cap, and the net-cash-adjusted P/E is only about 6x, which is absurdly cheap
- The bears say monthly active users have fallen for 16 consecutive quarters, the Kuwo/Kugou challenger is attacking hard, and the stock has fallen 68% from its 52-week high - the market is not stupid

**Both sides have a point, but they reach completely opposite conclusions.** That means the issue is not who is right and who is wrong, but that each side is looking at a different cut of the same company.

This series aims to **lay out each cut clearly** so that you can decide whether it is worth buying, rather than having us decide for you.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Use plain, direct language** - avoid mixing languages in a way that muddies the point
4. **Do not write "I think" or "I feel"** - use "the data shows" and "the evidence suggests"
5. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - many Soda Music metrics are not publicly disclosed, so only the most reliable sources are used
6. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
