# One word to the conclusion

The management of the TME has made a difficult but correct transition, with capital allocation capacity; but the company is facing a structural paradox of "profit growth vs user loss" -- making more and more money, while users are leaving at one and the same time. The biggest invisible risk is not that the user is robbed of soda music, but that management avoids the contradiction by "stops disclosing the MAU." The Ree-Seong asks: When a company starts to hide data, is it usually good news or bad news?
