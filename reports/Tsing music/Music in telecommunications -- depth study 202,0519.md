##3.1 Income structure transformation: from 70% social entertainment to 81% online music

*Source: TME Calendar Financial Reporting*

** Positive**: Social entertainment (live call) is a business that regulates the area of severe disaster, with severe loss of users and low profit margins. TSE has been actively reducing social entertainment, playing music subscriptions, and has the right strategic direction.

Social entertainment was once a cash-for-profit cow, and the contraction was not entirely "active" - the regulation restricted underage reward, the voice-shaking fast-handedly stole a large number of live users, and the TIE was forced to transform. Social entertainment Q1 was still declining at 11%, and when it really hit bottom was uncertain.

#3.2 SVIP strategy: 20 million users, 16 per cent penetration, target 20-25 per cent

SVIP is the core growth engine of the TIE:

- **Study of users**: Over 20 million (approximately 16% of 127.4 million payers)
- ** Pricing**: 28-30 yuan per month (approximately 15-18 yuan for regular members, SVIP premium 60-100%)
- ** Interests**: 50 exclusive interests (no sound, AI recommendation, exclusive skin, etc.)
- **Target**: Increased penetration to 20-25% (for 25-32 million users)
- ** Pull on ARPU**: If penetration rises from 16% to 22%, ARPU is expected to rise from 11.9% to 14-15.

* Data source: TME finance conference call, management public statement*

** Positive**: SVIP is a purely ARPU uplift strategy that drives income growth without the need for new users. 16% - 25% penetration uplifts are clear, with about 127 million users switching to SVIP per 1 percentage point up.

** Negative**: SVIP core rights (non-sounding, AI functionality) are not irreplaceable. The soda music monthly card is only $8, providing similar experiences. If competition increases, SVIP price increases and penetration increases may not be as high as expected. Moreover, how many SVIP users of 20 million are natural transformations and promotions and pulls, which are not disclosed by management.

#3.3 Himalayan Acquisitions: ~$2.85 billion, granted in May 2026, regulatory conditionality

** Transaction overview**:
- Purchase price: ~$2.85 billion (approximately RMB 20 billion), of which $1.26 billion in cash plus general share of music type A (not exceeding 5.2 per cent of total equity)
- Timeline: Qualified by China ' s Antimonopoly Regulatory Authority on 12 May 2026
- Conditional (very harsh):
- Do not use market dominance to raise prices
- Keeping content free of charge.
- Dismissing exclusive content.
- No co-sale.
- No restrictions on the content of the anchors being posted on other platforms

*Source: General Directorate of Market Supervision Bulletin, TSE Bulletin*

** Positive**: Himalayan is China’s largest audio platform (blower + sound book), and after the acquisition, TIE was upgraded from a music platform to a full audio platform, and the music + podcast + sound book strategy for the standard Spotify. ~$2.85 billion (of which the cash portion $1.26 billion) is affordable for the TEE’s approximately $38 billion net cash. Audio content and music have natural synergies – the same user, the same scene, the same payment habits.

** On the contrary: Conditionality almost deprived TIE of the core means of integrating Himalayans - no higher prices, no exclusives, no co-sales, no limits on anchoring. This means that TEE spent $2.9 billion on an asset that "dos not do much." Historically, the audio industry has had a low success rate (cf. the dilemma of the acquisition of Pandora by Sirius XM). Himalayan itself is less profitable and the integration period may drag TIE profit margins.

# VI. Risk and management assessment

# 6.1 Three-tier drop in stock prices from $26.7 to $8.47

The TEE stock price fell by 68% between the beginning of 2025 and May 2026.

** First floor: Sino-General systematic selling (with a contribution of approximately 30-40 per cent drop)**
- The US-China relationship deteriorated in the second half of 2025, and the Chinese generalist has been under pressure.
- The MSCI China Index fell by about 25-30% over the same period
- The fact that the TVE fell far beyond the index suggests that there are company-level factors.

** Second tier: Concern about slower growth (contribution of about 20-30% drop)**
- Q1,2026 battalion growth rate decreased from 15.8 per cent to 7.3 per cent
- Net profit growth for Non-IFRS decreased from 22% to 4.8%
- MAU has been down for 16 consecutive seasons - the market is pricing "growth at its peak."

** Third tier: deteriorating pattern of competition (contribution of approximately 20-30% drop)**
- The soda music MAU jumped from about 80 million at the end of 2024 to 156 million in April 2026
- The market may think the T.E. monopoly is being destroyed.
- Spotify confirms €975 million in fair value losses (shares ~9% for TEE), further creating negative emotions

*The above is attributed to the cross-cutting effects between the three layers of causes estimated by the researchers*

#6.2 Q1 2026 Stop disclosing user data - very bad signal

This is the most vigilant signal of the present report.

Starting with Q1,2026, TIE no longer discloses key operational indicators such as MAU, number of user fees, ARPU, etc. in quarterly financial statements.

** Why is this a bad signal?**
** The company will only stop disclosure when the data are different** - management will be in a hurry to disclose if MAU is stable or upswing
** Investors lost the most important tool to judge business trends** - no user data to validate the "pay increase" story
** Information asymmetry has increased** - Management knows the data, investors don't know, which is not to the benefit of minority shareholders
** Bad precedent** — Netflix also adjusted disclosure calculus at a slower growth period, followed by further decline in stock prices

** Possible reading**:
- Worst: MAU accelerated down, pay user rings dropped, management chose to hide
- Neutral: Q1 data is seasonally volatile and management considers quarterly disclosures misleading market
- Best: prepare for redefinition of calibres after Himalayan integration

** Unsure** — But until data are false, risk assessments should be made in the worst cases.
