# Three, summary of the analysis of dimensions

##3.1 Business model & moat river (Long-Ping perspective)

- ** From "two wheel drive" to "Chinese Spoty"**: the share of online music rose from 28% in 2019 to 81% in 2025, and social entertainment was a structural decline.
- ** The moat is a "Scalm effect plus the tungsten ecology" rather than a copyright monopoly**: after the breakup of exclusive copyright in 2021, TIE proved that size and ecology are the only way to win.
- **SVIP is the key engine of growth**: over 20 million users, 12% penetration ~ target 20-25%, driving ARPU from $11.9 to $15
- **The biggest threat is soda music**: MAU 140 million (live supernetic cloud), based on a shivering flow plus low-priced strategy ($8 vs QQ music 18)
- **Longping judgement**: "Good but not top" business - no core content (licensing is in the hands of record companies) and pricing is competitive

#3.2 Financial & Valuation (Buffett Perspective)

- ** Rapid improvement in profit margin**: Māori rate from 29% in 2021 to 44% in 2025, operating profit rate from ~14% to 41%, structural improvement
- ** Net cash of $32.3 billion is 33% of the market value**: Real PEs after cash is deducted are only 6.4x - extremely cheap for a 15% growth company.
- **FCF Yield 12.1%**: Far from the rate of return on national debt, Buffett's "profit rate of return" logic supports buying
- **EV/EBITDA 4.35x**: The lowest valuation in the global music stream media (Spotify 30x)
- ** But down from $26.7 to $8.76 (-67%) in 52 weeks**: What are the risks of the market pricing?
- **Conservative value $11.5-12.7**: current $8.76 with 24-31% security margin

#3.3 Industry & Competition (Mange Perspective)

- ** Payment rate + ARPU double-litre determination**: China only pays half of the 47% payment rate in Euro-American, ARPU only 30% of Spoty
- **TME has an absolute leader of 69% MAU**: 127 million payers, three times the amount of the cloud.
- ** Downside of copyright costs after exclusive copyright is removed**: Structural improvements in Maori rates have been validated
- ** Soda music is the biggest variable**: MAU 140 million and growing by +90%, but not commercialized for realization
- **AI music is a risk 5-10 years dimension** short term, not fatal, but will reshape the industry ' s cost structure
- **Munge's judgement**: "The return on investment depends more on whether the valuation is cheap, not whether the industry itself is over-growing."

#3.4 Risk & Management (Li Sue)

- **The biggest risk is competition rather than regulation**: soda music is reshaping, and the 16-quarter drop in the TIE monthly life is a sign that cannot be ignored.
- ** Management beam rating B+**: Successful completion of income structural transformation with reasonable capital allocation (billions of backsliding + $370 million of red red) but no effective counter-attack against byte attacks

# IV. Investment arguments

# Look at how logical
1. **PE 10x + net cash as at market value 33% **: only 6.4x after cash is deducted, extremely cheap for companies growing by 15%
2. ** Structural improvement in profit margin**: Māori rate from 29% to 44%, with room for improvement (downside copyright costs)
3. ** Payment rate + ARPPU double-litre**: 22% ~ 35-40%, $11.9 ~ 15 dollars, mathematically determined growth path
4. ** Social recreation is being brought nearer to elimination**: the percentage has fallen to 19% and the absolute value has declined by a narrow margin
5. ** Capital return acceleration**: $1 billion buyback + annual dividends + Himalayas acquisition, cash being used efficiently
6. **FCF Yield 12%**: "Bought and earned" logic in Buffett framework
**Spotify valuation logarithm**: also music stream media leader, Spotify PE 30x vs TME 10x, too wide a gap

# Look at the empty logic
1. **MAU has declined 16 consecutive seasons**: from 615 million to 528 million, user-based matrices are shrinking and when is uncertain
2. **The music of soda is growing at an alarming rate**: 140 million MAU and +90% growth, with 700 million daily twilights, with no effective countervailing TIE
3. ** Lack of core content**: Copyright is in the hands of record companies, TIE is essentially "distributor" rather than "content owner"
4. **The 67% fall in 52 weeks may have been priced with unknown risks**: the market may have seen negative factors that we have not yet understood
**Standing major shareholder risk**: 58% shareholding + 90% vote, minority shareholder interests at risk of sacrifice
**AI music long-standing subversion**: if AI devalues copyright, TIE's copyright cost advantage will be meaningless
** China's Internet system discount**: Geopolitical + regulatory uncertainty suppresses whole plate valuation

# Eight, wrap up

TME is a company that is experiencing a "removement": social entertainment has shrunk from 72 to 19 per cent of income, online music has risen from 28 to 81 per cent, Maori has increased from 29 to 44 per cent, and payers have increased from 76 million to 127 million. The quality of business is improving significantly. Currently, PE 10x, with only 6.4x, FCF Yield 12 per cent after net cash deduction, with multiple indicators pointing to low valuation.

The core risk is two "indefinable" factors: whether the attack on the bickering 700 million days of music can be contained (the TIE month has fallen in 16 consecutive quarters); and what is the market risk behind the 67% fall of 52 weeks. If the answer to these two questions is "controllable," the current price is a very good opportunity to buy. If the answer is "uncontrollable," then the 10 times PE is not necessarily the bottom.

The essence of the investment in TIE is a bet that "the price is cheap enough, the odds are good enough" — whether the long-term trend in music payments and the structural lead of TIE in the Chinese music market can be sustained.
