#10 Methodology Redisk: The fit of the four-play parallel investment framework in real estate analysis

The report moves the framework of the "Four Masters" originally used for stock investment to real estate decisions, and confirms a few points:

1. **Buffett "River of the City" in the Real Estate = non-repelicable properties** (Lustability of land, matching maturity, administrative region)
2. ** Part of "Opportunity Cost" in the property = Horizontal Block Comparison + Friction Cost Assessment ** (tax/intermediate/renovation/psychological costs are bets specific to the property)
3. **Mung 'Multicultural' cross-cutting of five variables in the property = population/policy/industry/interest rate/land**
4. **Lisu 's long-term certainty' in the property = discounted house + policy uncertainty + urban pattern change**

**The largest migration harvest**: the "opportunity cost" and "frozen cost" of housing decisions are much higher than the equity (11-24 per cent vs. 0.1 per cent), which fundamentally changes the critical condition of "possession vs replacement".
