## CEO Calvin McDonald Decision Review

### Term Overview (January 2019 - January 2026)

Calvin McDonald took over as CEO in January 2019 and during his seven-year tenure:

| Indicators | When taking office (FY2018) | When leaving office (FY2025) | Changes |
|------|----------------|-----------------|------|
| Annual Revenue | ~$3.2 billion | $11.07 billion | +246% |
| Number of stores | ~440 stores | ~720 stores | +64% |
| China stores | <10 stores | ~140 stores | +1300% |
| Gross profit margin | 55% | 58.4% | +340bp |
| DTC revenue proportion | ~25% | ~45-50% | Nearly doubled |

> Source: [Retail TouchPoints: How Lululemon's CEO Plans to Double the Business](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/market-news/how-lululemons-ceo-plans-to-double-the-business-despite-mounting-competition); [AInvest Leadership Transition Analysis](https://www.ainvest.com/news/lululemon-leadership-transition-turnaround-potential-2026-2601/)

### Critical decision evaluation

**1. "Power of Three x2" strategy (released in 2022)**

Goal: Double revenue to $12.5 billion by 2026 through three pillars - product innovation, omni-channel experience, and international expansion.

Evaluation: As of FY2025, revenue is US$11.07 billion, which is still far from the target of US$12.5 billion. This target was set in an optimistic environment for 2022 and appears slightly aggressive in hindsight. But the direction is right - international expansion has indeed become a core engine of growth.

**2. China Market Betting**

Expanded from less than 10 stores to 140, and China revenue grew from <400 million to ~1.7 billion US dollars. In the Chinese market, FY2025 revenue increased by 29% year-on-year, far exceeding other regions. This is one of McDonald's most successful strategic decisions during his tenure.

> Source: [China Daily: Lululemon grows on China market strength](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/26/WS69c730c6a310d6866eb405e1.html)

**3. Digital Transformation**

Increase the proportion of e-commerce revenue from ~25% to ~45-50% and establish a complete omni-channel infrastructure. Q2 2025 digital channel revenue exceeded US$1 billion.

**4. Mirror Acquisition (2020, $500 million)**

This is the most controversial decision of McDonald's tenure. In 2020, it acquired the home fitness mirror brand Mirror for US$500 million and later changed its name to Lululemon Studio. Eventually, the business shrank significantly and the company was almost fully depreciated. This exposed McDonald's misjudgment in "cross-border expansion."

### Departure evaluation

McDonald's departure has a "passive" tone - not a retirement plan, but a decision made in the context of the company's slowing growth and sharp decline in stock prices. Li Lu often said, "Look at the performance of management in adversity." McDonald's performance is excellent when there are tailwinds, but it lacks effective response measures when faced with slowing growth and intensifying competition.

### Heidi O'Neill Background Assessment

- Worked at Nike for more than 20 years, most recently as President of Consumer, Product and Brand
- Led Nike's DTC transformation and digital strategy
- Proven execution ability within a large company system

**Pros**: O'Neill has extensive experience in brand management and DTC, which is what Lululemon needs most at the moment. Her experience in large company governance at Nike helped transition Lululemon from "entrepreneurial culture" to "mature corporate governance."

**Risk**: Nike and Lululemon are two completely different company cultures. Nike is a mass brand with wholesale as its core, while Lululemon is a high-end niche brand with the DTC community as its core. Cultural fit is the biggest uncertainty. In addition, O'Neill left when Nike was facing its own difficulties. This signal needs to be interpreted carefully.

### Li Lu’s perspective on the Chinese market

As one of the most successful value investors investing in the Chinese market, Li Lu’s analytical framework is particularly suitable for evaluating Lululemon’s opportunities in China:

**1. Very low market penetration = huge space**

A low-single-digit market share and a brand awareness of more than ten percentage points means that Lululemon is still in the early stages of the growth curve in China. As a reference, Nike's brand awareness in China exceeds 90% and its market share is about 15%. If Lululemon's popularity in China increases from 15% to 30%, its revenue will at least double.

**2. Upgrading of fitness consumption among China’s middle class**

China's sports participation rate has continued to increase over the past five years. The number of participants in yoga, running, and outdoor sports is growing rapidly, driving demand for high-end sportswear. This is a structural rather than a cyclical trend.

**3. Risks: Local Substitution and Geopolitics**

Local brands such as Particle Fever and MAIA ACTIVE are rising. Their pricing is 30%-50% lower than Lululemon, and the quality gap is narrowing. In addition, uncertainty in Sino-US relations is a tail risk that cannot be hedged.

## Civilization Trend: Long-term Penetration Rate of Sports Lifestyle

Li Lu's investment philosophy emphasizes "the general trend of civilization evolution." Look at the sports and leisure market from this perspective:

### Long-term irreversible trend

**1. The awakening of health awareness is an inevitable product of civilization and progress**

When per capita GDP exceeds US$10,000, the proportion of health and fitness consumption will systematically increase. More and more countries around the world have crossed this threshold, and the growth of the sports and leisure market has a profound foundation in demographic economics.

**2. Blurring "office to yoga studio" attire**

The popularity of remote working and casual dress culture has transformed sportswear from "gym only" to "daily wear". This is not a cyclical fashion trend, but a structural change in consumption habits. The penetration rate of athleisure clothing has increased from approximately 25% in 2019 to approximately 35% in 2025, and is expected to reach 45% in 2030.

> Source: [Grand View Research Athleisure Market Report](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/athleisure-market)

**3. Increased female economic participation**

The increase in women's labor force participation rate and economic independence has directly promoted the growth of the high-end women's sportswear market. Lululemon's core customer group (women aged 25-45, high-income, health-conscious) is the biggest beneficiary group of this trend.

### Positioning of Lululemon under the trend of civilization

Lululemon is at the core of the big trend of "long-term penetration of sports lifestyle". This trend will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The question isn't whether the market will grow (it will), but whether Lululemon can maintain or grow share in a growing market.

## Summary and judgment from Li Lu’s perspective

Li Lu's investment framework emphasizes three points: understanding the business, understanding the management, and understanding the price.

**Business**: Lululemon is on a long-term growth track driven by "civilization progress", and the fundamentals of the business (high ROIC, high gross profit, DTC model) are still healthy.

**Management**: This is the biggest variable right now. McDonald left behind a company that had doubled in size, but also with slowing growth and a challenged brand. O'Neill's ability and cultural fit are the most critical variables to watch over the next 12 months.

**Price**: If you buy a company with an ROIC of 30% at 9 times PE, mathematically speaking, the margin of safety is sufficient. But Li Lu would remind: Cheapness itself is not the reason to buy, but a thorough understanding is. The current low valuation reflects real operating challenges and cannot be considered an opportunity just because it "has fallen a lot".

Ultimately, if you believe that the long-term penetration trend of sports lifestyle is irreversible (which is likely to be the case) and are willing to endure the uncertainty of the management transition period, then the current price provides sufficient compensation.
