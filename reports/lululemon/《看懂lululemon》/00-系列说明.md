## Series Positioning

lululemon is not a company that "nobody pays attention to" - it is one of the **biggest decliners and most controversial names** in North American consumer stocks over the past two years.

- The bulls say: buy a company with a 9x P/E, 30% ROIC, and 58% gross margin - a once-in-a-decade opportunity
- The bears say: North America is in consecutive decline, the CEO seat is empty, the founder is pressing for change, and the brand is aging irreversibly

**Both sides are looking at the same company and drawing completely opposite conclusions.** The stock has already voted: from **USD 511** down to **USD 125**, a **75%** drop.

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and then let you decide whether it is worth buying** - not to decide for you.

## Writing Principles

1. **Data first** - start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion
2. **Present both sides** - attach a counterargument to every major conclusion
3. **Transparent sources** - all numbers are sourced, and estimates are marked as "estimated"
4. **Reject false precision** - do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns
5. **Disclaimer** - every article ends with a note that it is not investment advice
