- Income growth** stays at low digits forever**
- Net profit** stopped growing or even slowly declining**

# The magnifying effect of the buyback

One factor that is easily overlooked: Lululemon cumulatively repurchased **US$48 billion** over the past five years, and the liquidity stock dropped from 128 million to about **116 million** (decrease ** 9.4 per cent**).

The effect of repurchases under nine times the PE is very significant - ** For every 1% buyback, the EPS is about 1% thicker. If the company maintains a buyback of more than $1 billion a year under the current valuation, the liquidity share may fall below 100 million after three years, the EPS will grow by **10-15%** by buying back alone.

** Counterpart **: Repurchases create value only when the company is undervalued. If the brand of lululemon is really in irreversible decline, cash buybacks to reduce equity are simply "put money on a wreck."

# The summary series: the essence of lululemon

Four articles, back to the start question: ** what kind of company is the lululemon?**

** It is a global sports lifestyle company with a still strong brand asset but undergoing rare multiple crises.**

- ** Good Part **: 58% Māori, 30% ROIC, 95% full price sales, China + 29%, track CAGR 8%
- ** Poor **: North America - 3%, CEO vacancies, founder pushes the palace, product overturns, customs duties $380 million
- ** Unsure **: Is brand ageing cyclical or structural, new CEO, how long can China last?

** The market gave nine times PE -- it's the lowest in 10 years. ** This price has been priced all the "bad" and "undefined" parts. ** The question is: ** Has the value of the "good" been underestimated?**

The answer to this question depends on your judgement on one thing:

> **lulemon brand, a good brand temporarily trapped or an over-gas brand in irreversible decline**

In the former case, the current price is an opportunity.
If the latter were the case, the current price would remain high.

What this series can do is put all the facts and logic before you.** The right to judge is in your hands.**
