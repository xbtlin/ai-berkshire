## Margin of Safety Assessment

### Tailwinds

1. **Valuation has compressed dramatically**: P/E has fallen from 40x to 9x, so a lot of bad news is already priced in
2. **ROIC remains strong**: a 30% ROIC at the current valuation implies a very high capital return
3. **No debt pressure**: the balance sheet is healthy, with no refinancing risk
4. **Buyback capacity**: the company continues to repurchase shares, which is especially effective when the stock is cheap

### Headwinds

1. **Slower growth is real**: FY2026 guidance implies only 2%-4% revenue growth, and EPS is expected to decline
2. **Management vacuum**: the new CEO does not take over until September, leaving a near-term strategic gap
3. **Tariff uncertainty**: the ongoing impact on margins is hard to quantify

### Buffett-Style Conclusion

Using Buffett's test of whether the company will be better or worse in 10 years:

- **Brand**: Lululemon still has a deep foundation in the global athleisure space, and it will likely be stronger in 10 years
- **Industry**: the long-term trend of rising athleisure penetration remains highly certain
- **Current valuation**: buying a 30% ROIC, 58% gross margin consumer company at 9x earnings gives you plenty of margin of safety, even if growth slows

**The core Buffett-style judgment**: at today's price, the risk/reward profile in the base case is very attractive. The key is patience while the management transition completes and growth re-accelerates. Even in the bear case, downside appears limited (~15%), while the bull case offers huge upside (170%+). This is an **asymmetric bet**.
