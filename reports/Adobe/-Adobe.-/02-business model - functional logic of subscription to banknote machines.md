# A moat depth: triple lock

Adobe's moat is not a single dimension, but a compound barrier of three layers of locking and stacking.

# First level: skills lock

More than **85 billion live-time users globally ** (data disclosed by Adobe 2025) use Adobe products. Among them, professional creative workers – designers, photographers, video editors, illustrators – spent years mastering the layering logic of the Photoshop, Illustrator's pen tool, Premiere's time line editing logic.

These are not skills that learn a day, but ** depth of muscle memory. Design training institutions around the world teach Photoshop, not GIMP, and video colleges teach Premiere, not DaVinci Resolve (although the latter has a place in professional video).

** Switch costing**: A medium-sized design team (10 people) switched from Adobe family drums to an alternative, with conservative estimates of a transition period of 3-6 months + a 20-30% decline in productivity. For most enterprises, this hidden cost is much higher than Adobe ' s subscription cost.

** Anti-view**: But skill locking is not permanent. A new generation of designers who start with Canva or Figma may not be at Adobe from the beginning. This is a slow variable for 5-10 years, but the direction is not negligible.

# Second layer: file format locked

.psd,.ai,.indd,.pdf - These are the "national languages" of the global creative industry.

- A brand VI source file for the design firm, default is .ai format
- type files accepted by the printing house, default PDF output from.indd
- The layerable files delivered by the photographer can be edited by default in .psd format
- Billions of global inventory files, only Adobe can be perfectly opened and edited.

** The essence of file format locking is "network standards". ** Like HTTPS is the Internet's communication standard, SWIFT is the interbank settlement standard, .psd/.ai/.pdf is the documentation exchange standard for creative industries. Once standards are formed, replacement costs are industry-wide switching costs, not just for individual users.

** Anti-view**: But Figma has proven that "new standards can be born" - in the UI/UX field, Figma's cloud-based prototype format has become a fact standard, and .xd files (Adobe XD) have been completely eliminated. If similar events occur in the image editing or video field, Adobe's format is not locked in unbreakable. Only the image and video fields have far more inventory files than in the UI/UX area, and the migration is more difficult than in the UI/UX area.

# Third floor: Workstream locked

This is the most vulnerable layer of three locks** that are undervalued.

A complete creative production process is usually like this:

```
Illustrator <unk> InDesign <unk> Export (Acrobat PDF)
```

Or:

```
Video Cut (Premiere Pro) <unk> After Effects <unk> Premiere <unk> Audio Processing <unk> Output
```

Adobe's Creative Claude is a seamless link between these tools — the smart objects of Photoshop can be updated in real time in Illustrator, and Premiere's time lines can be sent directly to Ater Effects for special effects and then back.** Replace Adobe is not a substitute for a tool, but rather for an entire production line.**

There is no competition that can provide full process coverage at the same depth. Canva can't do a professional video clip, Figma can't do a layout, Midjourney can't do a special effect synthesis.

# New model for "Razor-Knife": Generating integrals

Starting in 2025, Adobe added a new income model - ** Generating Credits** - to the subscription.

- What? - Details.
|------|------|
<unk> Standard package <unk> A certain amount of AI per month to generate credits <unk>
<unk> Pro package <unk> Big fraction + higher AI model access
<unk> Subcontract purchase ** $19.99 / 4,000 credits**, end of purchase renewable <unk>
<unk> Back uses <unk> AI to generate pictures, AI extensions, AI style migration, AI video generation, etc.

** This is the classic Razor-Knife model**: subscriptions are Razor (a one-time locking of users) and the integral consumption is Snives (a continuous increase in revenue).

From the data, the model is fast starting:

- Generating integral consumption** Quarterly Ring growth of more than 45%** (Q1 SY2026 data)
- Firefly subscription and sub-contract ARR ** 75% ring growth**
- AI-related ARR ** year-on-year increase over 3 times**

** If this trend continues, Adobe's income structure will evolve from "pure subscription" to "subscribe plus consumption" double-wheel drive.** The benefits of expendable income are higher ceilings — the more users create, the more AI uses, the more Adobe earns.

** Inverse view**: Predictability of expendable income is lower than pure subscriptions. If the economy goes down and companies cut creative budgets, the consumption of the credits falls faster than the subscription income.
