## Investment advice

### Conclusion: **Buy (open positions in batches)**

**Core argument:**
Adobe is an excellent company that is experiencing "multiple fear discounts" - fear of AI subversion, fear of CEO resignation, and fear of intensified competition. But most of these fears are overpriced:

1. **AI is enhancement rather than subversion**: Firefly's "commercial security" positioning and enterprise-level monetization path have been initially verified. AI-related ARR tripled, and points consumption increased 45% quarter-on-quarter.
2. **Financial Fortress**: $10.3 billion in free cash flow, $25.2 billion in ARR, $25 billion in buyback program—it’s a money-printing machine that keeps running.
3. **Valuation at historical bottom**: 19x PE is a rare buying window for a software monopoly with 26%+ ROIC and 10-12% growth.

**Recommended position:** 3-5% of the portfolio (considering the uncertainty of CEO handover, it should not be too heavy)

**Building position strategy:**
- Start opening 1/3 position at the current price (~$247)
- If the price falls further below $220 due to CEO handover news, increase the position to 2/3
- After the new CEO is determined and the strategic direction is clear, decide whether to fill the position based on the situation

**Selling Conditions:**
- New CEO drastically changes AI strategic direction (negative)
- Subscription revenue growth below 8% for two consecutive quarters
- AI native tools penetrate more than 30% of professional workflows
- Valuation rebounds to above 30x PE

## Summary of key points of four-dimensional analysis

### Business model analysis

**Core conclusion:** One of the best business models in the field of creative software.

- High switching costs (skills locking + file format locking + workflow locking) ensure strong user stickiness.
- In 2025, the price will be raised by 16.7%, and the revenue growth will accelerate to 11%. The pricing power has been empirically verified.
- AI has superimposed a new monetization layer (generative points), evolving from "tool subscription" to a "tool + consumption" two-wheel drive.
- The risk is that Canva (180 million monthly active users) and Figma (UI/UX 80-90% share) continue to encroach on the edge market.

### Financial Valuation Analysis

**Core conclusion:** Growth stocks trading at value stock valuations have a sufficient margin of safety.

- FY2025: Revenue 23.77 billion (+11%), net profit 7.13 billion (29.5% net margin), FCF 10.3 billion (42% FCF rate).
- Q1 FY2026: Revenue 6.4 billion (+12%), subscription revenue increased 13%, growth is accelerating.
- ARR 25.2 billion, up 11.5% year-on-year, providing a highly predictable revenue base.
- 25 billion buyback plan (25% of market cap) will continue to thicken EPS over the next 4 years.
- Conservative DCF estimates intrinsic value of $350-400/share, with a 30-40% margin of safety at current prices.

### Industry competition analysis

**Core Conclusion:** The moat is still extremely wide in the core area, but the edges are being redefined.

- Adobe faces three lines of competition: Canva (non-professional market), Figma (product design), and open source AI (long-tail market).
- Three core moat elements: conversion costs (extremely strong), file format standards (extremely strong), and economies of scale (strong).
- Firefly's "commercial security" training model has a unique advantage in the enterprise market - copyright compensation protection is something that competing products such as Midjourney cannot provide.
- Key monitoring indicators: penetration rate of AI tools in professional workflows, tool preferences of next-generation designers.

### Risk Management Assessment

**Core conclusion:** CEO transition is the biggest short-term risk, and AI disruption is the biggest long-term risk.

- Narayen is leaving after a stellar 18-year tenure (8x revenue, 15x market cap).
- Undecided successor + no timetable = continued uncertainty. Positive factors: Narayen remains as chairman and the company is financially healthy.
- The risk of AI disruption is real but the probability is low within 3-5 years - Adobe itself is actively embracing AI.
- Favorable risk-reward ratio: limited downside (valuation is cheap enough + strong cash flow), considerable upside (AI monetization + new CEO + market repricing).
