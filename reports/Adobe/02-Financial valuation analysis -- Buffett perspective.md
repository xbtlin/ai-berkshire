# Adobe Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analysis dimensions: earnings quality, capital allocation, intrinsic value, and margin of safety
> Data cutoff: May 2026 | Based on FY2025 annual report + Q1 FY2026 results

---

## 1. Financial Overview: A Money Printer

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Q1 FY2026 |

| Total revenue | USD 19.41 billion | USD 21.40 billion | USD 23.77 billion | USD 6.40 billion |

*Note: ROIC varies by methodology. GuruFocus shows 26%, ValuSense shows 33%, and some sources show 41%.*

**Key observations:**

1. **Revenue growth is accelerating**: from 10% in FY2023-2024 to 11% in FY2025, then to 12% in Q1 FY2026. That is impressive for a company already generating USD 24 billion of revenue.
2. **Margins are extremely high and stable**: a 29.5% GAAP net margin and a 42% free cash flow margin are top-tier software-company characteristics.
3. **Cash flow quality is excellent**: FY2025 operating cash flow of USD 10.03 billion and free cash flow of about USD 10.3 billion imply a net-income-to-cash conversion rate above 100%, meaning earnings are backed by real cash.

## 2. How AI Can Lift ARR

Adobe's AI monetization path is now reasonably clear:

**Current contribution:**
- Firefly direct revenue is about USD 400 million (cumulative 2024-2025), still tiny relative to the USD 24 billion revenue base
- AI-related ARR is growing more than 3x YoY, though Adobe has not disclosed the absolute figure separately
- Generative-credit consumption is growing more than 45% quarter over quarter

**Three monetization paths:**
1. **Bundled price increases**: package AI features into higher-priced Pro plans (USD 69.99 vs. USD 54.99 per month) and monetize through plan upgrades
2. **Consumption-based revenue**: sell generative-credit packs (USD 19.99 for 4,000 credits) as incremental revenue on top of subscriptions
3. **Enterprise services**: Firefly Foundry offers brand-custom AI model training, a high-value service for large enterprises

**My estimate:** if AI-related revenue reaches 10%-15% of total revenue within the next 3 years (USD 3-4.5 billion), Adobe's growth floor would rise materially. Given the rapid growth in credit usage and enterprise adoption, that target looks achievable.

## 3. Capital Allocation: Management Is "Eating Its Own Cooking"

**USD 25 billion buyback plan (announced in April 2026):**
- This is Adobe's largest repurchase authorization ever, running through April 2030
- USD 25 billion is about 25% of the current market cap (~USD 99 billion), showing clear management conviction
- With about USD 10.3 billion of annual free cash flow, Adobe can complete the repurchase without borrowing

**EPS accretion from buybacks:**
If Adobe repurchases USD 25 billion of stock over 4 years at an average price of USD 280, it would retire about 89 million shares, or roughly 20% of current shares outstanding. Even if revenue growth stays at 10%, EPS growth could run 12%-15%.

**No-dividend policy:**
Adobe does not pay a dividend, returning all capital through buybacks. For a high-ROIC growth company, that is a sensible capital-allocation strategy.

## 4. Valuation Analysis: A Historically Cheap Range

**Current valuation snapshot (May 2026):**
| Metric | Value |
|------|------|
| Market cap | ~USD 98.9 billion |
| Share price | ~$247 (down about 30% since the start of 2026) |
| P/E (GAAP) | ~19x (based on FY2025 net income of USD 7.13 billion) |
| P/E (Non-GAAP) | ~11x (based on Non-GAAP net income of USD 8.94 billion) |
| Price / free cash flow | ~9.6x (based on TTM FCF of USD 10.3 billion) |
| Free cash flow yield | ~10.4% |
| EV / EBITDA | ~9-10x (estimate) |

**Historical comparison:**
Adobe's average forward P/E over the past 5 years has been in the 30x-40x range. The current 19x GAAP P/E (11x Non-GAAP P/E) is the lowest level in nearly a decade. The market is implicitly pricing in either a large slowdown or AI-driven disruption to Adobe's business model.

**Intrinsic value estimate (conservative DCF):**
- Assume 10% revenue CAGR over the next 5 years, consistent with history
- Keep free cash flow margin at 42%
- Terminal growth of 3%, discount rate of 10%
- Intrinsic value comes out to roughly **USD 350-400 per share**
- At a current price of USD 247, the margin of safety is about **30%-40%**

**More optimistic case:** if AI monetization lifts revenue growth to 12%-13%, intrinsic value could reach USD 400-450 per share.

## 5. Buffett-Style Summary

> "It is far better to buy an excellent company at a reasonable price than a mediocre company at a cheap price."

Adobe's current situation goes one step further: **it is an excellent company trading at a cheap price.**

- A 10% free cash flow yield is attractive for a software monopoly with 26%+ ROIC and 10%-12% growth
- The USD 25 billion buyback should keep accreting EPS over the next several years
- The main risks are CEO transition and uncertainty around AI competition, but the financial base is extremely solid

**Valuation call:** the current price offers a high margin of safety and is an opportunity to buy a good company at a good price.
