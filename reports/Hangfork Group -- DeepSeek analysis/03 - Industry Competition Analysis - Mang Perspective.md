> The greatest risk comes from two things - one that trade warfare escalation could disrupt the internationalization process and the other that Biadi-level cross-border players could change the pattern of competition if they hit hard on gold. But, overall, the industry is better suited to buy 'reasonable prices' than wait for 'exceeding' prices."
