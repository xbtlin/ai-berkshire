# VIII. AI study limitations statement

1. ** Information abundance level A trap**: Hangfork is a 10-year-old A-share company with full information. But it is easy to get analysis to go to "right crap" because of the amount of information -- all public information is absorbed by the market. The team in this report deliberately set up a negative test and nonconsensual perspective, but it does not avoid the "recognition deviation" that is created by the abundance of information.
2. ** Timeliness of financial data**: Some data (e.g., accurate balances of accounts receivable) are based on turnover rates and are not reported as accurate in the annual report.
3. ** Subjectivity of valuation**: The incremental rate assumption of the three scenario valuation and the PE assumption are subjective judgements and the actual results may vary considerably
4. **Inequality of information to address risk**: Linked transactions for vengeful building of "Golden galaxies" are generated from public disclosure and are difficult to detect from non-public channels of interest to investors
5. ** Inpredictability of exchange rates and steel prices**: The two largest external variables (steel prices and exchange rates) affecting the Hangfork rate are inherently unpredictable.
6. **The team analysis did not process the discrepancies**: the financial analyst considered the accounts receivable to be "significant hazard signals", while the risk assessor considered the "low risk". The report uses the financial analyst ' s perspective (data support is stronger), but the reader should judge for itself

> ** "Bought good businesses at reasonable prices, far better than bought mediocrity at cheap prices."**

Hangfork lies between "excellent" and "impressive" — a firm business, financial health, a strong industry, but a slow growth, deteriorating receivables, and a risk of governance.
