# This core conclusion

1. **Hanker business model is being upgraded**: from "sale iron" (low-morigin forklift) to "sale electricity plus services" (high-moric electric forklift plus post-market). The Māori ratio rose from 15.51% to 24.78% over the year.
2. ** Overseas is the core engine of profit growth**: Māori overseas is about 10 percentage points higher than in the country, and overseas income is 43.38 per cent.
3. ** ESR is the most important strategic layout**: but the pre-existing window period may be only 3-5 years.
4. ** The moat is not wide enough**: the oligarchic pattern is stable, the scale effects are clear, but the differences are limited and pricing power is weak.
5. ** The post-market is a profit pool that has not been fully excavated**: the current ratio is 15-20 per cent, well below the 30-50 per cent share of the European and American counterparts.
