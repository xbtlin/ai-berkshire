** Sensitization**: Smartness is the "choice" of the forklift industry -- if you don't do it, you're not going to make too much profit. The real moat is not a three-pronged combination of technology itself, but "hardware reliability plus software capability plus service network." Hangok is now in a position to provide a service network, but software capacity still needs to be strengthened.

** The largest growth variable is internationalization** - Hangfort’s share of global income is only about **3-4%**, while China accounts for over 50% of global production. The huge gap between global and production shares is the growth space for Hangfor the next 5-10 years.
