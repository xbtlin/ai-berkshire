## A set of numbers that allow you to re-understand the forklift industry

In 2025, China's forklift industry completed a quiet flip: electric forklift sales accounted for **73.6%**, and the absolute sales of internal combustion forklifts began to decline. At the same time, the industry's annual sales volume is **1.4518 million units**, and China has been the world's largest forklift producer and consumer for 14 consecutive years, with output exceeding **50%** of the world's total.

An industrial equipment category with a history of more than 100 years is undergoing a complete power system reconstruction. The biggest beneficiaries of this restructuring are likely to be two Chinese companies that you have probably never heard of.

(Data source: 2024-2025 data from the Industrial Vehicle Branch of China Construction Machinery Industry Association)

In this industry with an annual output value of over RMB 100 billion and annual sales of over one million units, the two Chinese companies at the top of the pyramid - Anhui Heli and Hangcha Group - together have taken away more than 45% of the domestic market share.

One of them is to be dismantled today: **Hangcha Group (603298.SH)**.

## Three common misconceptions about Hangcha

### Illusion 1: Forklifts are a sunset industry

No.

Forklifts are the basic equipment for industrial handling. As long as there are factories, warehouses, ports, and logistics centers, forklifts are needed. The global forklift market will be valued at approximately **81.8 billion US dollars** in 2024 (Fortune Business Insights data), and is expected to grow at a compound growth rate of approximately **5.6%** over the next ten years.

More importantly, forklifts have two attributes that are often overlooked:

1. **"Consumables" attribute**——The average replacement cycle of a forklift is 5-7 years, so it is not a one-time purchase. After a company buys a forklift, accessories, maintenance, and battery replacement will continue to generate income. The gross profit margin of the aftermarket is as high as **30-40%**, which is much higher than that of complete vehicles.
2. **Electrification is reshaping the industry** - From lead-acid to lithium batteries, from internal combustion to pure electricity, the advantages of Chinese companies in the entire lithium battery industry chain are being transformed into competitive advantages in the forklift field. This is not a gradual change, but a structural inflection point.

**But the opposing views are also worth listening to**: The forklift industry is not growing fast (global 5-6%), the ceiling is limited (the global market is about 100 billion US dollars), and it will not produce explosive growth of "tenfold in ten years". It's a stable but not sexy business.

### Illusion 2: Hangcha is "low-end manufacturing"

This label is increasingly inaccurate.

4 years ago (2021), Hangcha's gross profit margin was only **15.51%** - indeed like a low-end manufacturer. But by 2025, the gross profit margin has risen to **24.78%**, an increase of nearly **10 percentage points** in 4 years. Net profit increased from **908 million** to **2.191 billion**, an increase of **141%**.

There are two driving forces behind this:

| Driving force | Contribution | Sustainability |
|-------|------|---------|
| Product structure upgrade (electrification + high overseas gross profit) | About 60% | Structural factors, high sustainability |
| Steel prices fall (cyclical factors) | About 40% | Cyclical, if steel prices rebound, it will be under pressure |

(Source: Analysis and estimation based on Hangcha Group’s 2021-2025 annual report data)

In 2018, Hangcha established a joint venture company, Pengcheng New Energy, with CATL to develop lithium batteries for forklifts. By 2025, the "three electricity" (battery, electric drive, and electronic control) will have been completely self-developed and manufactured**. More than 400 hydrogen fuel cell forklifts have been put into use.

**But the opposite view**: About 40% of the increase in gross profit margin depends on the drop in steel prices. If steel prices return to the high point in 2021, gross profit margin may fall by 3-5 percentage points. The technical barriers to electrification will also weaken as the industry becomes more popular.

### Illusion 3: Hangcha is a state-owned enterprise

**No. **

The actual controller of Hangcha Group is Qiu Jianping, a typical Zhejiang entrepreneur. Qiu Jianping holds approximately **41.34%** of the shares of Hangcha Group through Zhejiang Hangcha Holdings. Hangzhou State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission holds approximately **20.39%** of the shares through Hangshi Group and is the second largest shareholder.

Qiu Jianping also controls Superstar Technology (002444.SZ) and Xinchai Co., Ltd. (301032.SZ), and is promoting the IPO of Zhongce Rubber, forming a complex "Superstar System" capital network.

What does this mean? This means that Hangcha's governance structure is not the "security of state-owned enterprises" that ordinary people think, but a structure controlled by private capital and frequent related transactions. This is both an efficiency advantage (fast decision-making, flexible incentives) and a governance risk (conflicts of interest, related transactions).

**On this point, the differences between bulls and bears are huge**:

- Bulls say: After Qiu Jianping took over, Hangcha’s revenue increased from 7 billion to 17.7 billion, and the actual controller is capable
- Bearishists say: The cross-shareholding of the "Superstar Group" is too complicated, and the acquisition of Guozi Robot is a typical case of related transactions.

A later article will break down this issue in detail.
