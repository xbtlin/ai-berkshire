The turnover of accounts receivable increased from 23 days in 2021 to 55 days in 2025, and the decline of 2026Q1 to 58 days continued. This is a signal** of the deterioration in the quality of business models** — companies are sustaining growth through the liberalization of credit policies.

# 7.3 Overseas risk concentration

43. Thirty-eight per cent of the revenue comes from overseas, which is concentrated in the European and American markets. The trade friction escalation will directly impact the growth engine of the company.
