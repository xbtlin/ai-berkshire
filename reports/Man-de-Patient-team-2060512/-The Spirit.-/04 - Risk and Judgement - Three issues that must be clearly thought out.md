# But a good track doesn't make the participants money

This is a concept that many people confuse. For example:

- **Air industry**: Transport demand has increased over the centuries, but the accumulated profits of the industry are about zero
- **PV industry**: Increased number of installed units, but most panel vendors lost or collapsed
- **Share bike**: Real needs were met, but only two or three barely survived after burning down tens of billions

AI The same path may be followed by the comic book:** Technology dividends are shared across the industry, with production costs reduced by 95 per cent and benefits are ultimately passed on to the platform and users rather than left to the producers**.
