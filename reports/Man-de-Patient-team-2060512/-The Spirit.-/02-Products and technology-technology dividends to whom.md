# Three layers of technical barriers

# First layer: Are these efficiency gains real?

** The approximate rate is true, but it needs a discount.**

The cost reduction figures given by the Episode Mansions on different occasions are not consistent -- sometimes they say "down to traditional 1/20," sometimes they say "down to 70%," sometimes they say "down to 3%." There is a contradiction between these figures, which suggests that the PR data have a different caliber.

But in the industry as a whole, AI did reduce the cost of comics from "millions of people" to "ten thousand." That is not the idea of the world's most powerful players: soy sauce culture, jelly intelligence, and tactful flows all give similar efficiency data.** Efficiency gains are real, but they are not unique to the world.**

# Second layer: Is technical barriers unique to the spirit?

** No. This is the most critical issue.**

The technology store in the Episode can be broken into two layers:

<unk> Level <unk> content <unk> is unique <unk>
|------|------|---------|
<unk> Bottom: AI large model <unk> video generation (symbolistic, dream, etc.), image generation (Stable Division, etc.), speech synthesis <unk> not ** - the spirits use open source or third-party models <unk>
<unk> Application level: workflow <unk> Self-research DIT architecture tool, scripting mirror automation, role coherence management <unk> Short-term variations** - but not high threshold <unk>

The spiritual landscape is constructed in a vast way by **the working flow optimization of the application layer**, not by the bottom modeling capability. This distinction is crucial:

- The bottom model is controlled by companies such as bytes (namely, dreams), fast hands (sound), and Seko, which leaps to the next generation every few months
- When the bottom model is strong enough, the working flow optimization of the application layer rapidly shrinks in value
- A analogy: When smartphone cameras are good enough, card camera markets disappear.

** Inverse view** The value of the workflow is not just technical, but also "experiences gained from stepping on the pits" -- which mirrors can improve the rate of completion, which subjects are best placed at which time, and how the subtle differences in character design affect user fees. These empirical data are not replaced by modelling capabilities alone.

** Reverse**: These are very low barriers to the accumulation of experience - any company with a 100-month monthly drama can have similar experience. The data wheels turn faster for the soy sauce culture (the ability to produce is far too strong).

# Is the data wheel real or phonograph?

The more the world's mind is keeping up with the fact that almost all AI companies claim to have "data wheels" -- the more dramas are produced, the more data are accumulated, the more accurate the model is, the higher the product quality.

** Logically established, but there are three problems in reality**

1. ** Data wheels are premised on "data scarcity" ** - if all companies in the industry are producing a lot of dramas, data are not scarce and the flying wheel effect will diminish. 2026 Average industry-wide daily access ** 470 new dramas ** data supply is not scarce.

2. ** The core competitiveness of the comics is not technology, in content** - users do not pay for "better picture" and they pay for "better story." Data wheels optimize technology, but they cannot substitute for creativity.

** The Platform has real data** - User behavioural data (survival, fee conversion, retention) are in the hands of distribution platforms such as the shivering, red nuts and so forth, and the data available to the world is limited.

** Inverse view**: The Episode data wheels are not just technical data, but also selected and operational data like "what's the story, what's the rhythm, what's the rate of transformation." These data do have a built-up value and are not entirely in the hands of the platform.

# This core judgment

Breaking down the spiritual technology, three conclusions:

# 1. Efficiency gains are real

AI did push the comics from "handicraft" to "industrialization." Cost reduction was 80-95%, efficiency 10-20 times higher, and these figures were PR moisture, but direction and magnitude were right.

# 2. But efficiency gains are not unique to the spirit

This is a technology dividend shared across the industry, from the bottom of the AI model. The spiritual landscape is very different in the short term in the application-level work flow optimization, but the moat is shallow -- any engineering team can build a similar line of production.

#3 The greatest uncertainty: Is the generic AI tool "one key instead" of the entire production line

The answer to this question determines that the technology store is worth nothing after three years. If a magic/nine/a open source tool achieves a "one-key-generated comic" value, the spiritual chain is worth zero. The probability is not low, and it is increasing every quarter.
