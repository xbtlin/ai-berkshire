# The true place of the spiritual world in the chain

The combination of competition and commercialization analysis makes the position of the spiritually clear in the industry value chain:

```
Bottom model (bytes: dreams, fast-hands, commercial soup, Seko)
• Provide AI capacity
Applied-level tools plus content production (mixed-face, soy culture, jelly intelligence, etc.)
<unk> Provision of comics
Distribution platform (single, red fruit, fast-handed, tweaking dragons)
<unk> Touch users
End-user (288 million comics users)
```

** The spiritual landscape is in the middle level — upstream, relying on the model capabilities of large plants, downstream, depending on distribution channels of large plants.** History has repeatedly shown that when the upper and lower run is held by large plants, the profit space of middle-level companies is continuously squeezed.

There are two ways the meso level can be eliminated at the big plant:
1. ** Self-built**: Bytes to do their own content (there is already a small bird AI team)
2. ** Open tool**: Making dream/sound a "one-size-fits-all" comic-making tool, allowing big individual creators to replace professional production companies

Whatever path it is, the spiritual space is shrinking.

** Anti-conversation**: The meso level is not necessarily eliminated. The mobile industry - High-tech is making chips, channelers are distributing them, but the "memule-level" branders, like Mi, OPPO, are doing well because they create differences in product definition and supply chain management. The spiritual dimension can still be very different if it is built on "Content Creative + Influence Efficiency + IP Operation".
