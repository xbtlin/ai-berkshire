## First look at a set of numbers

Cost of 150,000, income of 12 million in 5 days - this is the report card handed over by Douyin during the New Year's Day of 2026 for an AI comic called "I Open a Supermarket in the End of the World, S-Class Weird People Are Rushing to Work". The producer is a startup company established less than three years ago, with a team of less than 100 people and headquartered in Hangzhou - **Lingjing Wanwei**, with the brand name "Lingjing AI".

**80 times input-output ratio** is enough to make investors in any industry stop and take a closer look. But before you get impressed by this number, let’s take a look at another set of data:

- Within two days of being online, the number of views exceeded **220 million**
- Likes for the first episode **73,000**
- During New Year's Day, it topped Douyin's comic list for three consecutive days
- Production cost: **150,000 yuan**
- 5-day revenue: **12 million yuan**

(Data source: cross-verification by streaming media network, Huxiu, 36 Krypton and other media)

| Industry Indicators | Data | Sources |
|---------|------|------|
| AI comics to be launched throughout 2025 | **60,946** | Huxiu |
| Works with over 100 million views | **96** | Tiger Sniff |
| Explosion rate | **0.12%-0.16%** | TMTpost Media, Huxiu |

**Block rate 0.12%** - means you need to make approximately **625** to "expect" one hit. The remaining 624 will most likely be lost.

This is the true face of the industry that Lingjing Wanwei operates in: **On one side is the myth of wealth creation from 150,000 to 12 million, and on the other side is the cruel reality that 90% of companies are losing money**.

## What exactly is the spiritual world?

### One sentence definition

**Lingjing Wanwei is a content factory that uses AI technology to batch convert online novels into vertical screen animation short dramas (comics). **

It is not a traditional animation company (it does not require original animators to draw frame by frame), it is not a pure technology company (its core income comes from content distribution, not selling tools), nor is it a platform company (it does not control the traffic entrance). A more accurate positioning is - **AI-driven content producer**.

### Four pieces of business puzzle

| Section | What to do | Essence |
|------|--------|------|
| Internet big data platform | Real-time monitoring of more than 10 million works on the entire Internet, using algorithms to capture trends | Topic selection system |
| AI short story factory | Jointly developed writing model with Zhejiang University, with monthly output of 1,000+ pieces | Raw material production |
| AI animation creation engine | Convert text into comics, self-developed DIT architecture tools | Core processing links |
| Content overseas | AI translation + cultural adaptation, extending to Southeast Asia, Europe and the United States | Market expansion |

(Data source: 36 Krypton Financing report, Gaohu Capital press release)

Putting the four pieces together, the business equation of Lingjing Wanwei is very clear:

> **Choose popular themes → AI writes novels in batches → AI makes comics in batches → Publish on Douyin/Hongguo and other platforms → User payment or advertising monetization**

This is a complete "content industrialization pipeline".

### Key figures

The founder **Xu Jincheng**, public information claims that he is from the "Alibaba family". But the specifics of what Alibaba does, what rank it is, and what business line it is—all are missing from the public information**.

This is a signal worth noting. For a company with "technology-driven" as the core of its narrative, the founder's technical background cannot be traced. Maybe it's deliberately low-key, or maybe the background isn't convincing enough. **Existing information is insufficient to judge, but the lack itself is a kind of information. **

The cumulative financing is about **50 million - 100 million** (estimated, the "tens of millions" of each round is calculated by taking the median value). The financial advisor is Gaohu Capital.

**Contrary view**: Three rounds a year seem to be fast-paced, but the amount in each round is not large. Compared with the soy sauce culture in the same track (head company, monthly revenue **50 million**, team **1000+ people**), the ammunition capacity of Lingjing Wanwei is an order of magnitude lower. In a track where major manufacturers invest hundreds of millions every year, this scale of capital requires every penny to be spent very efficiently.

By the end of 2025, the team has nearly **100 people**, with dual headquarters in Hangzhou and Wuhan. According to the Wuhan Municipal Government’s investment news, the monthly turnover is approximately **20 million**, orders are scheduled to **2027**, and the order amount exceeds **100 million**.

**Data Confidence Statement**: The monthly turnover of 20 million comes from the Wuhan government investment news. This kind of data usually has a certain degree of publicity, and the actual number may be low. However, the revenue data of the hit "Supermarket" can be cross-verified from multiple independent channels.

## A core question: 150,000 turned into 12 million, how much did you earn?

On the surface, "Supermarket" has a cost of 150,000 and a revenue of 12 million, which is so profitable that it is unreal.

But here is an open secret in the industry: **More than 80% of revenue is spent on investment traffic (buying volume)**.

That is to say:

| Project | Amount (estimate) |
|------|-----------|
| Production cost | About 150,000 |
| Investment cost (calculated as 80%) | About **9.6 million** |
| Platform share (about 10-20%) | About **1.2-2.4 million** |
| **Actual profit** | **About 0-1 million** (estimate) |

(Note: The above are calculations based on industry practices, not official data from Lingjing Wanwei)

**12 million in revenue sounds explosive, but after deducting the investment and platform sharing, the profit may only be in the million level or even lower. ** And this is still a hit-the remaining 624 works that are not hits cannot even recover the cost of distribution.

**Opposite view**: Lingjing Wanwei has its own investment team, and its efficiency may be higher than the industry average. In addition, the long-tail revenue from hit hits (sequels, IP spin-offs) may bring additional returns. The extremely low production cost of 150,000 yuan means that the cost of trial and error is extremely low. "You will always succeed after trying a few times" is the core logic of this model.
