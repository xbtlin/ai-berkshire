# III. Technological risks: depth assessment

#3.1 AI Manipulation Technology Maturity

** Current status (mid-2026)**:
- AI has moved from technology demonstration to industrial production.
- But quality still has clear limitations: the issues of consistency of figures, complex movements, and coherence of scenes have not been fully resolved
- Industry reports that the quality of the Episode AI is "close to the standard of the Japanese mainstream" - this is the PR language, and the actual difference remains significant.

# 3.2 The double-edged saber effect of the evolution of the large model

** This is the most fundamental technological risk facing the world's spiritual dimension**:

- ** Upstream risk**: Rapid progress in the common video production model (Sora, Spirit, Dream, etc.) may reduce the threshold for AI to "one person can do" and eliminate the technological barriers to the state of the world.
- ** Downward risk**: If the big model progress slows down and the AI Manipulation Quality cap is limited, the entire track may face a "mass ceiling".
- ** Most likely scenario**: Large models continue to progress, making AI Manipulations easier and more standardized - it is deadly for middle-level tools plus content like the Episode

# 3.3 "Technology moat" vulnerability

AIGC Production Tool for the DIT Framework:
- Bottom-based reliance on open source models and generic large model capabilities
- Once the utility tools (near, dream, etc.) are adequate, the value of the self-research tools will quickly be zero.
- A analogy: When smartphone cameras are good enough, card camera markets disappear.

** Technical risk rating: high**
