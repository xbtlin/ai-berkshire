## Key Findings

1. **China ADRs are much cheaper overall** - PDD, JD, and NetEase are all around 10x P/FCF, while comparable U.S. names are mostly above 30x, implying a valuation gap of roughly 3x
2. **Capital spending is the biggest distortion** - Amazon, Google, Tesla, BYD, and Xiaomi all have P/FCF ratios heavily distorted by massive AI or manufacturing capex, so reading this metric alone can lead to misjudgment
3. **The best cash-flow quality names** - NetEase (38% FCF margin), PDD, Visa, and Mastercard convert nearly all earnings into real cash
4. **Historical percentile view** - Nvidia at 43x is still below its historical median, meaning cash-flow growth has outrun the stock price; Tencent and Alibaba are slightly above their historical medians
5. **PDD's position** - At 10.7x P/FCF it ranks fourth-cheapest among the core names; EV/FCF at 5.0x after net cash is even more extreme, reflecting deep market skepticism about its growth durability
