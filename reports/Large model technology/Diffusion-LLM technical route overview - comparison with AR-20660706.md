# One word to the conclusion

The diffusion language model has moved from academic toys to the commercial validation stage (speed is a real advantage), but the combination capacity still systematically lags behind the same-scale self-regression model, and parallel decoding has a lower quality of information theory.** In the short medium term, the two routes are complementary rather than substitute relationships, "replace AR" is now in the mainstream.** The real opportunities for proliferation routes are in the very low-delayed reasoning, the adjustable "quality-speed" trade-offs, the creation of faults and multi-restrictive planning of niche markets.

# I. Technical comparison

# Self-return route

Token-to-right generation, each token-to-right, is conditional on all the previous tokens. This is the route of all the mainstream models, such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek. The advantage is that the dependency relationship between token is perfectly modeled (the chain law does not decompose the probability of a union); the disadvantage is that the generation is inherently serial, the speed is limited to to to token decoding and the writing token cannot be changed back.

# Disperse route

Drawing on the idea of image generation: From the state of full mask (or noise), the entire text is "coarsely fine" after several denocial steps, instead of being generated from the left to the right. The current mainstream is ** mask diffusion language model (MDLM)**.

An important technical spectrum recognition:** the proliferative language model is technically directly linked to the Bert-style masking language model**. Neurinxis 2024 MDLM paper proves that the simplified proliferation training target (Rao-Blackwellization) is the weighted mix of classic MLM losses - a crudely understood "BERT generated by the overlap." The confusion of this route is close to the AR model (and the proliferation is an upper estimate, and actually closer).

# II. Proximity measurements: speed, quality, cost

Speed: The advantage of a business model is true, but it's quote marks.

- **Mercury Coder** (Incident Labs): independently measured by a third party, Artificial Analysis on H100, Mini/Small reached **110/ 737 tokens/s**; officially, the RAM model is about 5 times faster than the 200 tok/s comparison. Mercury 2 in 2026, 711-1196 tok/s, is the first in 132 models. (Construction: high, independent third party physical)
- **Gemini Diffusion** (Google Deepmind): Official self-reported **1479 tokens/seconds** (not including about 0.84 seconds of start-up expenses); independent hand-held measurements about 857 tok/s. (Note: Self-reported values)
- Reasonable expression is "approximately 700-1500 tok/s, 5-10 times faster than speed optimization" than "thousands of tokens per second" circulating online.

** Important clarification (combating the popular pseudo-prosecute in authentication)** The term "dissemination model reasoning is generally much faster than AR" was rejected by 3-0. The reality is that speed advantage is highly dependent on engineering and code configuration, and the actual reasoning in the open source diffusion model is often slow to **AR in the absence of a standard KV cache. Commercial models are the result of a combination of "dissemination architecture plus large-scale engineering optimization" and are not free lunches of the structure itself.

# Quality: Still systematically behind and with theoretical ceilings

This is the most important finding of the study, two chains of evidence:

**1. Mass evidence: 58 baseline assessment (arXiv 2601.15593,512 GPU empirical study)**
Covers knowledge, mathematics, reasoning, language understanding, intelligents, programming six dimensions 58 benchmarks, evaluating 8 mainstream mask diffusion models (LADA2-flash with 100B parameters), concluding that: **MDLM is still systematically behind the same size AR model**, mainly because parallel probability modelling reduces token dependency. This is the most comprehensive empirical evidence available as of mid-2026.

**2. Bottom of information theory: mass loss of parallel decoding is structural, not capacity deficit**
The token conditions are independent under the parallel decoding assumption in the block, and the KL dispersion is bound by the "Ctr. General Relations to Conditions" from below -- even if the model learns the optimal margin distribution of each location, the higher the area below the error.

The immediate consequences in practice are very illustrative:** to achieve the highest degree of precision, the most advanced proliferation models (OpenPangu-Diffusion, SDAR, Tradi) actually use block size 4, which solves only one token conservative configuration per step – close to degradation as a self-return, and thus abandons the core selling point of parallelism**. The official Chinese openPangu also claims to accelerate by only 2.5 times.

Limitation: This lower threshold is for "showing the one-off conditions in the block independently", and Mr. Generate-then-Edit's translator decoding can theoretically be circumvented, so this is a decision on "pARKS parallel decoding" and not a final decision on the proliferation route.

Cost: Training side is inconclusive, but there's a shortcut

- The term "dissemination training costs are basically the same as AR" was rejected in the counter-testing by 3-0 (based on the size of toys).
- But there is a proven downscaling path: ** Initialization of proliferation models using AR weights**. Dream 7B initialized with Qwen2.5-7B weights, with 58 billion tokens (approximately one quarter of the data from the zero-training LLADA) exceeding LLADA-8B in total, supported by controlled digestion experiments. This also means a slightly ironic fact: the most powerful open source diffusion model is "stacking on AR shoulders."
- Logic costs: The current comparison is mostly proliferating, and the fair comparison of all the advantages of proliferation totoken costs is left, and there is no neutral research, based on the full expansive comparison of the AR side reasoning (KV cache, batch, speculative decoding).

# Two real advantages unique to proliferation

1. ** Multi-restrictive planning tasks have been prominent**: Dream 7B on Countdown digital games 16.0 for Qwen2.5-7B, 81.0 for Queen 2.5-7B, 81.0 for Quest, 17.8 for RSP, and 3.6 for some of the scenes even exceeding the larger two-level DeepSeek-V3-671B. Two-way attention + global iterative detail has a structural advantage in the task of "need to meet multiple constraints simultaneously". (author self-reported, highly structured, medium-confidence)
2. ** modifiable mass-speed tradeoffs**: AR does not have this knob in constant flux between speed and mass by denoctuation steps.
Small-scale controlled experiments (weaker evidence) show a significantly higher structural diversity of diffuse output** (93.4 per cent versus 3.3 per cent only of the five words at the beginning) and are more difficult to prepare - supported by CMU peer review in the direction of data restricted scenarios, but the experiment is toy size and cannot be extrapolated to a paradigm-level conclusion.

# Four, can you replace AR?

# # Business mainstream judgement: complementarity rather than substitution

- **AR Continues to be the lead**: The mainstream landscape of fluidity and coherence priorities (dialogue, translation, summary, long-writing, complex reasoning).
- ** Opportunities for sutures in proliferation**: extremely low delayed reasoning (code completion, real-time interaction), scenes requiring flexibility between speed and quality, multi-bound planning/structured generation, need for diversity generation (inventive, data enhancement).
- Gemini Diffusion, which has been on the experimental scale for over a year, is a signal in itself: even the most resourceful bets have not yet been pushed to the production level.

# Look at the core arguments of many

1. Commercial models have proven that "GPT-4o Mini Level Quality + 5-10 Multiply" is a achievable combination, and the costs of reasoning are the core competition dimension in the era of large-scale landings in AI.
2. AR Initialization cuts the training costs of proliferation models to one quarter, and the catch-up curve may be steeper than expected.
3. Planning/correction/diversity is a structural weakness of AR and is not a work-recoverable one.
The scale of the parameters is still rapidly climbing (8B § 100B for only one year).

# Look at the core arguments of the empty side

1.58 The baseline mass assessment shows that the quality gap is systematic and that the 100B level has not been reduced.
The bottom line of CTC's information theory means that there are structural rivals at the "parallel" and "quality" levels: to be able to degrade to a quasi-regime and to lose quality in parallel - the fact that the strongest proliferation models today have all chosen the former is tantamount to recognizing it.
3. The advantage of speed is heavily dependent on proprietary engineering, and open-source models are often slower than ARs under equitable configuration.
4. The most powerful open source diffusion model is initialized by AR weights, which indicates that this route has not yet proved to be independent in its size.

# # Key observation indicators (for judging route turns)

** Whether the size of the block is much larger than the size of 4 in a true parallel configuration, and the size of the frontier is the same quality as AR** - the only winner. Whether the Generate-then-Edit type of transposition decoding can be practically decoded to bypass the lower CTC is a core technical suspense.
2. Whether the law of the size of the diffusion LLM is in place: the gap between the 500B-1T scale and AR is widening or narrowing (there is no public evidence at present).
3. Whether Google will open up Gemini Diffusion/ DiscussionGemma to API.
4. End-to-end cost comparisons between neutral third parties under AR reasoning optimization conditions.

# My combined judgment

In the next 2-3 years, proliferation routes** will not ** replace AR, but will take a real share in the low-delayed reasoning market (especially code completion, endside, real-time application); the more likely end is **mixed architecture** — proliferation, semi-return, AR backbone + proliferation acceleration first-class integration programmes (SDAR, Trido are prototypes), rather than pure proliferation winning the path to pure AR. The correct approach to this route is to track it as "a reasoning acceleration technology plus mission-specific sui generis structures" rather than as a next generation paradigm.
