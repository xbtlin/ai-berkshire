## What exactly is the nature of the RMB 30.3bn issue?

The first three articles unpacked Wuliangye's business model, brand moat, and industry position. But every part of the analysis depends on one premise: do you trust this company's financial statements? How the RMB 30.3bn revenue overstatement is characterized determines Wuliangye's valuation logic, and also determines whether "11x PE" is a buying opportunity or a value trap.

### Three layers of characterization

| Level | Characterization | Meaning |
|------|------|------|
| Surface level (company explanation) | "Correction of accounting errors" -- a misunderstanding of the timing of revenue recognition | A technical issue; once corrected, the problem is resolved |
| Middle level (market consensus) | "Channel-stuffing-style overstatement" -- management recognized revenue early to meet performance targets | A management incentive problem |
| Deep level (worst case) | If the CSRC determines it was intentional, it constitutes a "false statement" under the Securities Law | A legal liability problem |

*Sources: Wuliangye announcements, 21jingji.com, Sina Finance*

**The company calls it an "accounting error"; the market calls it "disguised fraud."** The truth is most likely somewhere in between: to meet performance KPIs, management tolerated an aggressive approach of "recognizing revenue upon shipment" until the new management team took office and cleared it out in a one-off "big bath."

### Comparison with historical cases

| Case | Nature | Similarity to Wuliangye |
|------|------|-------------|
| Kangmei Pharmaceutical (RMB 88.7bn inflated cash balance) | Genuine fraud | Low |
| Zhangzidao (cross-period adjustment of scallop inventory) | Cross-period adjustment | **Medium** |
| Kangde Xin (RMB 12.2bn inflated cash balance) | Genuine fraud | Low |

Wuliangye falls somewhere between an "accounting error" and "financial fraud." It did not fabricate transactions out of thin air, unlike Kangmei. But it did manipulate the timing of revenue recognition: once goods were shipped to distributors' warehouses, they were treated as sold, even though large volumes were still sitting in the channel.

### Expected regulatory penalties

| Characterization | Probability | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| Material accounting error (rectification only) | **15%** | Warning + rectification |
| Information disclosure violation (base case) | **55%** | Maximum fine of RMB 10mn, individual penalties |
| Financial fraud (false statement) | **30%** | Formal investigation, retrospective liability, class action litigation |

**Delisting risk is low** -- state-owned capital holds 55%, and the Yibin municipal government is unlikely to allow delisting. Adjusted profit also remains positive.

**But the trust repair cycle will be long.** In the history of A-shares, companies associated with financial fraud have needed an average of **5-8 years** to return to their pre-fraud valuation center. Even if Wuliangye is ultimately classified as an "information disclosure violation" rather than fraud, the market's trust discount will not disappear quickly.

## Two chairmen removed: personal misconduct or a system problem?

| Time | Event |
|------|------|
| January 2025 | Former chairman **Li Shuguang** came under investigation |
| February 2026 | Incumbent chairman **Zeng Congqin** was placed under formal review and detention |

**Within two years, two successive top leaders were removed.** This is unprecedented in the A-share baijiu sector.

### Structural issues in SOE governance

| Issue | Specific manifestation |
|------|---------|
| Management shareholding | Close to zero -- management lacks an "owner mindset" toward the company |
| Compensation mismatch | Chairman's annual salary is about **RMB 700,000**, while managing hundreds of billions of RMB in resources |
| Selection mechanism | Organizational appointment rather than market-based recruitment |
| Mixed-ownership reform | Largely nominal; state-owned capital still has absolute control at **55%** |

**Positive view**: A leadership change may be precisely the opportunity for improvement. If the new management team, Hua Tao/Zou Tao, pushes market-oriented reform, it could become an inflection point in governance. Historically, Wuliangye also experienced a brief period of strength after a management reshuffle in 2017-2019.

**Negative view**: The problem is not individuals; it is the system. Management receives an annual salary of RMB 700,000 while controlling resources worth hundreds of billions of RMB -- this institutional breeding ground for corruption will not change simply because people are replaced. Wuliangye's governance problem is "structural," not "accidental."

## RMB 20.0bn dividend + RMB 10.0bn buyback: sincerity or public relations?

On the same day the scandal broke, Wuliangye announced a package of measures:

| Measure | Amount | Nature |
|------|------|------|
| 2025 dividend | **RMB 20.0bn** (payout ratio above 200%) | Extraordinary dividend |
| Share buyback | **RMB 8.0-10.0bn** | Maximum price of RMB 153.59 |
| Major shareholder share purchase | **RMB 3.0-5.0bn** | Wuliangye Group to complete within 6 months |
| **Total** | **RMB 31.0-35.0bn** | - |

*Sources: Wuliangye announcements, 21jingji.com*

### Dividend sustainability

Adjusted net profit in 2025 was only RMB 8.95bn, yet the company proposed RMB 20.0bn in dividends -- a payout ratio above **200%**.

**Can this be sustained?**

Looking at the **RMB 127.4bn** of cash on the balance sheet, even if Wuliangye generated zero profit for three consecutive years, it could still maintain dividends of RMB 20.0bn per year. The question is not "can it pay?" but "does the underlying earnings power support it?" Annualized profit in 2026Q1 was about **RMB 32.2bn**, so a RMB 20.0bn dividend implies a **62%** payout ratio -- entirely sustainable.

### Sincerity of the buyback

An RMB 8.0-10.0bn buyback is a "heavyweight" program in the A-share baijiu sector. But several points deserve attention:
- The maximum price is RMB 153.59, versus the current share price of about RMB 89, so there is no issue of "not being able to buy"
- The buyback is for "cancellation and reduction of registered capital," which is the most shareholder-friendly use
- The key is execution pace; if a year passes and only a small amount has been purchased, the sincerity discount should increase

### Signaling value of the major shareholder purchase

The major shareholder's planned purchase of RMB 3.0-5.0bn, with a 6-month lock-up, sends the signal that "the major shareholder believes the current share price is undervalued." But share purchases by major shareholders of SOEs often have a "stability maintenance" function, so they are not fully equivalent to a market-based judgment.

**Overall assessment**: The package is very strong by the standards of the A-share baijiu sector. **But once trust is lost, it cannot simply be bought back with money.** What the market needs to see is several consecutive quarters of transparent financial statements, concrete actions from the new management team, and genuine channel repair. These will require at least **2-3 years**.

## Valuation: is 11x PE really cheap?

### Current valuation level

| Metric | Based on 2024 EPS (RMB 8.21) | Based on 2025 adjusted EPS (RMB 2.31) | Based on 2026Q1 annualized EPS (~RMB 8.30) |
|------|------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| PE | **~11x** | 39.9x (distorted) | **~11x** |
| Dividend yield | ~5.6% | - | - |
| FCF yield | ~8.7% | - | - |

### Historical valuation comparison

| Period | PE range | PE center | Current position |
|------|--------|--------|---------|
| 2015-2025 | 6-55x | ~25x | **11x (bottom-zone valuation)** |
| 2020-2025 | 15-55x | ~30x | Significantly below the center |
| Last 3 years | 15-30x | ~22x | At an extreme bottom |

**The current PE is about 11x, at the very bottom of the past 10 years' valuation range and close to the level seen during the 2014 baijiu winter.**

### Peer comparison

| Metric | Moutai | Wuliangye | Luzhou Laojiao | Fenjiu |
|------|------|--------|---------|------|
| PE | ~21x | **~11x** | ~15x | ~18x |
| Dividend yield | ~4% | **~5.6%** | ~4% | ~2% |
| ROE | ~33% | 23.4% | 30.4% | - |

**Wuliangye has the lowest valuation and highest dividend yield among the four giants.** The gap is very large: Moutai at 21x versus Wuliangye at 11x, almost a 2x valuation gap.

### Why is it so cheap?

Cheapness does not appear out of nowhere. The market is discounting Wuliangye for the following reasons:

| Discount factor | Estimated discount range |
|---------|-----------|
| Management integrity discount (RMB 30.3bn + two chairmen removed) | **-30% to -40%** |
| Industry cycle discount (pressure across the baijiu sector) | -15% to -20% |
| Narrowing moat discount (loss of pricing power, intensifying competition) | -10% to -15% |

**Without the governance crisis, Wuliangye's "normal" PE would probably be around 18-22x, still below Moutai's 21x but with a much smaller gap.** At 11x, it is trading at nearly a 50% discount to normal valuation.

The question is: is this 50% discount an "overreaction" or "fair pricing"?

## Three-scenario valuation

Based on annualized 2026Q1 EPS of about RMB 8.30, with a 3-year forecast period:

| Scenario | Annualized growth | Target PE | EPS after 3 years | Target share price | vs current |
|------|---------|--------|-----------|---------|--------|
| Bull case | +8% | 25x | RMB 10.46 | **RMB 261** | +193% |
| Base case | +3% | 20x | RMB 9.07 | **RMB 181** | +103% |
| Bear case | -3% | 15x | RMB 7.58 | **RMB 114** | +28% |

### Scenario assumptions

| Scenario | Assumption | Probability |
|------|------|------|
| **Bull case** | The industry bottoms and recovers in 2027, channel destocking is completed, governance improves, and brand power is maintained | 20% |
| **Base case** | Low industry growth continues; Wuliangye maintains its current earnings level with slight growth; dividends remain at RMB 20.0bn+ | 50% |
| **Bear case** | Macro weakness persists, high-end demand shrinks, the price system remains under pressure, and governance problems continue | 30% |

**Even in the bear case, with a 15x PE and profit declining 3% per year, the target share price is still RMB 114, 28% above the current RMB 89.**

**But note**: if governance problems deteriorate further, for example if a third management team also runs into problems or the CSRC classifies the issue as fraud, the PE could fall further to 8-10x, corresponding to a share price of RMB 60-80. This "extreme bear-case" tail risk cannot be ignored.

## Margin of safety: multiple perspectives

| Dimension | Data | Judgment |
|------|------|------|
| PE | 11x vs 10-year center of 25x | **55%** discount |
| Dividend yield | **5.6%** vs 10-year government bond yield of ~2.0% | Provides downside support |
| Balance-sheet cash | **RMB 127.4bn**, 37% of market cap | When buying Wuliangye, 37% is cash |
| FCF yield | **8.7%** | Payback in less than 12 years |
| Bear case | 15x PE + profit down 3% per year | Still implies +28% upside |

**From a purely numerical perspective, the margin of safety is sufficient.** Even if profit continues to decline modestly over the next three years, investors will most likely earn a positive return through the 5.6% dividend plus valuation repair.

**But the prerequisite for the margin of safety is this: you trust the numbers.**

If management can manipulate revenue recognition, how can you confirm that the RMB 127.4bn of cash is real? How can you confirm that the RMB 8.06bn profit in 2026Q1 is not just another numbers game of "digging a hole first and then rebounding sharply"?

This is the core contradiction in Wuliangye's valuation: **the numbers look extremely cheap, but the credibility of the numbers is discounted.**

## Thinking framework for different types of investors

### Aggressive investors

Wuliangye is currently a classic "deep value + high uncertainty" stock:
- 11x PE + 5.6% dividend yield provides downside protection
- The package of measures, including RMB 20.0bn dividend + RMB 10.0bn buyback + RMB 5.0bn share purchase, provides a short-term catalyst
- The risk-reward ratio looks asymmetric numerically: 20-30% downside versus 100%+ upside

But investors must accept that governance problems may take 5-8 years to repair fully, during which the share price may be extremely volatile.

### Balanced investors

Watch for two signals before making a decision:
1. Formal appointment of the new chairman + implementation of a market-oriented reform plan
2. Confirmation that the industry has bottomed (Puwu wholesale price returns above the ex-factory price, and distributor inventory falls to a healthy level)

Before these two signals appear, the main stance should be to wait and see.

### Conservative investors

**Within the same industry, buying Moutai is the safer choice.** Moutai's 21x PE looks about twice as "expensive" as Wuliangye's 11x, but:
- Moutai's pricing power is real, with wholesale price 33% above ex-factory price
- Moutai's management transparency is far higher than Wuliangye's
- Moutai has shown much greater resilience than peers during the industry downturn

If allocating to baijiu, Moutai offers far higher certainty than Wuliangye.

## Choices within the baijiu industry

Taking a broader view, if you believe the baijiu industry deserves allocation, there are four choices:

| Stock | Core logic | Suitable for |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Moutai** | Highest certainty and strongest ability to ride through cycles | Long-term investors seeking stability |
| **Wuliangye** | Lowest valuation and highest dividend yield, but significant governance risk | Deep value investors who can tolerate high volatility |
| **Luzhou Laojiao** | Cleanest accounts and fastest channel repair | Value investors who emphasize governance quality |
| **Fenjiu** | Fastest growth and strongest differentiation | Growth investors bullish on the rise of light-aroma baijiu |

**There is no right or wrong among these four choices; there is only what fits your risk preference.**

## Final summary: understanding Wuliangye in one table

| Dimension | Assessment | Conclusion |
|------|------|------|
| Business quality | Baijiu is a top-tier business, and Wuliangye is one of the "top three" within it | Above average |
| Brand power | No. 1 in strong-aroma baijiu, but the gap with Moutai is widening | Strong, but weakening |
| Pricing power | Wholesale price inversion and forced price cuts mean it currently lacks true pricing power | Weak |
| Management | Two chairmen removed + RMB 30.3bn overstatement -- governance collapse | Weak |
| Valuation | PE 11x + dividend yield 5.6% + FCF 8.7% | Extremely cheap |
| Margin of safety | Numerically sufficient, but the credibility of the numbers is discounted | Present, but flawed |
| Industry cycle | Bottom expected in 2026Q4-2027Q1; not yet reached | Somewhat early |
| Biggest risk | Governance problems persist and trust repair remains far away | High |
| Biggest opportunity | Governance improvement + industry bottom + valuation repair | Medium |

**One-sentence summary**:

Wuliangye is a company with a top-tier business model but severely questioned management integrity. The current 11x PE and 5.6% dividend yield look extremely cheap numerically, and the weighted target price from the three-scenario analysis points to upside close to a doubling. But the reason it is "cheap" is not that the market has mistakenly sold it down; it is the trust discount caused by a governance collapse. Whether this discount is excessive depends on your answer to one key question:

**Do you believe Wuliangye's management will improve?**

If yes, 11x PE is a once-in-a-decade buying opportunity.
If no, it is not worth betting on no matter how cheap it looks.

Both judgments have reasonable data support. This is not a question of "right or wrong," but of "how much uncertainty you are willing to bear."
