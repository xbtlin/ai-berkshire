## Core Conclusion Up Front

> "Price is what you pay; value is what you get." -- Warren Buffett

Wuliangye is in an **unprecedented break in its financial narrative**: the 2025 annual report retrospectively reduced revenue by RMB 303 (100m) and net profit by RMB 169 (100m) due to an accounting error correction, causing reported 2025 results to appear to fall off a cliff. On the other hand, annualized 2026Q1 profit has already recovered to roughly RMB 322 (100m), and the company has committed to annual dividends of no less than RMB 200 (100m). At the current share price of RMB 92.26, the stock trades at only 11.2x 2024 PE and offers a dividend yield above 5.5%. **On the surface this looks like an accounting disaster; in substance, the valuation framework needs to be re-anchored.**

### 1.2 Trend Analysis

**2020-2024: a golden period of five consecutive years of double-digit growth.** Revenue increased from RMB 573 (100m) to RMB 892 (100m), with a CAGR of about 11.7%; net profit rose from RMB 200 (100m) to RMB 319 (100m), with a CAGR of about 12.4%. EPS increased from RMB 5.14 to RMB 8.21, a compound growth rate of about 12.4%.

**2025: the accounting error correction caused reported results to "fall off a cliff."** On April 30, 2026 (the final moment of the annual report disclosure deadline), Wuliangye released its "Announcement on Prior-Period Accounting Error Correction," changing the revenue recognition basis from "recognized upon shipment" to "recognized after terminal sell-through is completed." This reduced 2025 revenue by RMB 303 (100m) and net profit by RMB 169 (100m).

**Key judgment:** The "cliff" in the 2025 annual report was not operating deterioration, but a one-off shock from an accounting basis change. However, it also exposed that **Wuliangye may have been recognizing revenue early for years**: goods sitting in distributors' warehouses were treated as sold, rather than actually sold to end consumers. The Shenzhen Stock Exchange has issued an inquiry letter requiring an explanation.

**On the other hand**, this adjustment can also be seen as a positive signal that management is "cutting out the rot" and returning to conservative accounting. Under the new basis, 2026Q1 delivered revenue of RMB 228.38 (100m) and net profit attributable to parent of RMB 80.63 (100m), annualizing to about RMB 322 (100m), roughly in line with RMB 318 (100m) in 2024. This suggests the underlying earnings power has not collapsed.

## 3. Cash Flow Analysis: Is Baijiu the Best Cash-Flow Business?

### 3.1 Core Cash-Flow Data

| Year | Net Operating Cash Flow (RMB 100m) | Capex (RMB 100m) | Free Cash Flow (RMB 100m, est.) | Free Cash Flow / Net Profit |
|------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|----------------|

| 2024 | 339.40 | 26.66 | 312.74 | 98% |

*Data source: Wuliangye annual reports. 2020-2022 figures are estimates based on industry data and are labeled as such.*

### 3.2 Cash-Flow Quality Assessment

**Operating cash flow / net profit ratio:** This has stayed above 100% over the long term, showing high earnings quality. In 2024, operating cash flow was RMB 339 (100m), still slightly higher than net profit of RMB 319 (100m) (ratio of 106%, though roughly 98% after considering changes in advances received). The main reasons for the 18.7% decline in operating cash flow in 2024 were:
1. Higher dividend outflows (RMB 192 (100m) vs RMB 159 (100m) in the prior year)
2. Changes in distributor payment timing

**Extremely low capex:** Over the past 7 years, cumulative capital expenditures were only about 6% of cumulative net profit. This is the core appeal of the baijiu business: **it can maintain competitiveness with almost no reinvestment**. The older the cellars, the more valuable they become; the older the brand, the more valuable it is. Unlike technology companies, it does not need to keep burning cash.

> "The ideal business is one that generates high returns without requiring large amounts of capital reinvestment." -- Buffett

**Free cash flow:** Estimated 2024 free cash flow was about RMB 313 (100m). Against the current market cap of RMB 3,581 (100m), that implies an **FCF yield of about 8.7%**. In other words, if Wuliangye distributed all free cash flow to shareholders, investors would recover the entire investment in less than 12 years.

### 3.3 Is Baijiu the Best Cash-Flow Business?

**Conclusion: yes, baijiu is one of the business models in China closest to a "money-printing machine."**

Reasons:
1. **Light-capital operations:** Capex / revenue is only about 3%, far below manufacturing at 20-30%
2. **Advance-payment model:** Distributors pay first and take delivery later, so the company uses upstream and downstream funds over the long term
3. **Inventory appreciates with age:** Unlike electronics, which depreciate, baijiu inventory is an asset that becomes "more fragrant with age"
4. **Almost zero accounts receivable:** Baijiu operates on cash-before-delivery, resulting in extremely low receivables

**However:** The baijiu industry faces long-term structural challenges: China's population decline, shifting drinking habits among young people, and constraints on government-related consumption. High-end baijiu is fundamentally a **social currency**, and demand elasticity depends on macroeconomic conditions and sociocultural change.

### 4.2 In-Depth Interpretation

**RMB 1,274 (100m) of cash: "so much money it does not know how to spend it"**
Wuliangye has RMB 1,274 (100m) of cash on the balance sheet, accounting for 67.6% of total assets. This amount far exceeds daily operating needs and is a double-edged sword:
- Positive: Very strong risk resistance; even if revenue went to zero, the company could operate for years
- Negative: Large amounts of cash sit in banks earning interest, lowering capital efficiency. If management neither pays dividends nor buys back shares, it is effectively "destroying shareholder value"

**Contract liabilities of RMB 116.9 (100m) (+70.3%): stronger distributor willingness to prepay**
Contract liabilities are payments received in advance from distributors and reflect channel confidence. The 70% increase in 2024 contract liabilities shows, on one hand, that distributors still have confidence in Wuliangye; on the other hand, it may also reflect higher advances from year-end channel stuffing. Contract liabilities rose further to RMB 134.6 (100m) at the end of 2025.

**Interest-bearing debt near zero: "zero-leverage" operations**
Wuliangye has almost no bank loans or bonds. Operations are funded entirely by internal capital and upstream/downstream working-capital float. This is a classic Buffett-preferred company profile.

> "I always like companies that do not need to borrow money." -- Buffett

### 7.4 Margin of Safety Assessment

> "Margin of safety always depends on the price you pay." -- Buffett

**Margin of safety from multiple perspectives:**

1. **PE perspective:** 11.2x PE (2024 EPS basis), vs a 10-year midpoint of 25x, a 55% discount
2. **Dividend yield perspective:** 5.6% dividend yield, far above the 10-year government bond yield (~2.0%), providing downside support
3. **Balance-sheet cash perspective:** RMB 1,274 (100m) of cash on the balance sheet, equal to 35.6% of the RMB 3,581 (100m) market cap; when buying Wuliangye, 35% is cash
4. **FCF perspective:** Free cash flow yield of 8.7%, implying payback in less than 12 years
5. **Probability-weighted valuation perspective:** Even in the bear case (15x PE and profit declining 3% per year), the target price is still RMB 113.6 (+23%)

**Conclusion: the current share price offers a significant margin of safety.** Even if Wuliangye's profit declines slightly for the next 3 years, dividends (5.6% per year) and valuation recovery alone are likely to give investors a positive return.

**Risks to watch:**
1. The accounting error correction may trigger regulatory penalties and prolong the investor trust crisis
2. If the baijiu industry enters a "lost decade" (similar to Japanese sake), the valuation midpoint could permanently shift lower
3. Management governance is questionable: why was the adjustment made only at the final moment of the annual report deadline?

But if you have fundamental doubts about management integrity, no price is cheap enough.
