## One-Sentence Core View

**The baijiu industry is going through a historic inflection point from "lower volume, higher price" to "lower volume and lower price." Output has fallen 70% in 8 years, yet industry profit is concentrating in the top 5 players, with CR5 revenue share above 80%. But Wuliangye's niche as the "No. 2 player in the RMB 1,000 price band" is being squeezed from both sides: Moutai is pressing down from above, while Fenjiu and Luzhou Laojiao are aggressively taking position from below. This is not cyclical difficulty; it is structural reshuffling.**

> *Munger said:* "In a shrinking market, No. 1 can live very well, No. 2 becomes dangerous, and No. 3 and below are basically waiting to die."

## 1. Industry Overview: A Business Shrinking in Volume and Raising Prices

### 1.1 Market size: revenue is still rising, but only because price is carrying it

| Metric | 2020 | 2022 | 2024 | Change |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Number of above-scale enterprises | 1040 | 963 | 989 | -5% |
| Total output (10,000 kiloliters) | 740 | 671 | 414.5 | **-44%** |
| Sales revenue (RMB 100 million) | 5836 | 6626 | 7963.8 | +36% |
| Total profit (RMB 100 million) | 1585 | 2201 | 2508.7 | +58% |

**Data sources**: National Bureau of Statistics, China Alcoholic Drinks Association

**Core contradiction**: Output fell 44% in 4 years, but revenue rose 36%. This means the entire industry has been "trading price for volume": selling less, but at higher prices. Profit growth (+58%) far exceeded revenue growth (+36%), showing that profit is accelerating its concentration in premium products and leading companies.

But by 2025, this game finally stopped working. Aggregate revenue for 19 listed baijiu companies fell 18.13% year over year, marking the arrival of an era in which both volume and price contract.

### 1.2 Output: down for 8 consecutive years, nearly 70% below peak

| Year | Output (10,000 kiloliters) | YoY |
|------|------------|------|
| 2016 | 1358.4 | **Peak** |
| 2017 | 1198.1 | -11.8% |
| 2018 | 871.2 | -27.3% |
| 2019 | 785.9 | -9.8% |
| 2020 | 740.7 | -5.8% |
| 2021 | 715.6 | -3.4% |
| 2022 | 671.2 | -6.2% |
| 2023 | 449.2 | -33.1% |
| 2024 | 414.5 | -1.8% |
| 2025H1 | 191.6 | -5.8% |

**Data source**: National Bureau of Statistics

**Output has declined for 8 straight years, with a cumulative drop of 69.5%.** From 13.58 million kiloliters to 4.14 million kiloliters, the industry has shrunk to less than one-third of its former size.

Two stages:
- **2016-2020 (cliff-drop phase)**: deeper implementation of the Eight-Point Regulation + elimination of low-end capacity, cutting output by half
- **2021-2025 (continued bottoming phase)**: population aging + young people drinking less baijiu + higher environmental thresholds, keeping the decline in place

> *Munger said:* "When total industry volume is shrinking, you are not playing a growth game; you are playing an elimination tournament."

## 2. Industry Cycle: The Adjustment Is Entering Its Third Year, with Clear Divergence in Inventory Clearance

### 2.1 Current cycle position

This baijiu downcycle began in 2024Q2 and has now entered its third year. Compared with the four historical cycles:

| Dimension | 2012-2015 cycle | **2024-2026/27 current cycle** |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Trigger | Eight-Point Regulation + plasticizer incident | Alcohol bans + consumption downgrade + demographic inflection |
| Government consumption share | 40%->5% | 5%->lower (limited marginal impact) |
| Demographic trend | End of the demographic dividend | **2022 population inflection already passed** |
| Demand substitution | Government -> business (substitution available) | **Business + consumer demand contracting together (no substitute)** |
| Macro environment | Economy stabilizing and recovering | L-shaped bottoming + real-estate downturn |

**Core view**: This cycle will be harder to exit than 2012-2015 because it lacks "substitute incremental demand." In 2012, government consumption was lost, but business consumption picked up the slack. This time business and consumer demand are shrinking together. Who is the buyer of last resort?

### 2.2 Inventory destocking progress: leaders have cleared, the middle is clogged

| Category | Inventory status (2026Q1) | Estimated clearance timing |
|------|---------------------|------------|
| Feitian Moutai | Near safe inventory; some distributors at zero inventory | **Largely complete** |
| Wuliangye/Premium Guojiao 1573 | 1-2 months of inventory; down 30%+ after Spring Festival | 2026Q2 |
| Sub-premium (RMB 300-800) | 3-4 months of inventory; pressure remains high | 2026Q2-Q3 |
| Regional brands | More than 70% of companies have elevated inventory | 2026H2+ |

**Data source**: March 2026 distributor survey: more than 80% of liquor merchants reported some easing of inventory pressure, with broad declines of more than 30%.

**On the other hand**, most distributors believe marginal inventory improvement does not equal an industry inflection point. Channel inventory has the features of a "structural dammed lake": the leaders are flowing again, the mid-tier is clogged, and the tail is flooded.

### 2.3 Key micro signals to track

According to the prior baijiu cycle study (2026-05-03), only 3 of 10 bottoming signals had been triggered (30%). The core bottlenecks:
- Accounts receivable: in 2025Q3, industry accounts receivable + notes receivable reached RMB 77.56 billion, doubling year over year. Liquor companies were "selling on credit."
- RMB 1,000 price band: Puwu and Guojiao 1573 were both selling at wholesale prices below ex-factory prices; none had returned above ex-factory.
- Distributor payment rate: around 70%, still 20 percentage points below the healthy level of 90%.

**Conclusion**: Inventory is improving but far from cleared. The industry fundamental bottom is expected in 2026Q4-2027Q1.

## 3. Competitive Structure: From "A Hundred Flowers Blooming" to Oligopoly

### 3.1 Concentration is rising faster

| Metric | 2015 | 2020 | 2023 | 2024 | Trend |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| CR5 | <20% | ~30% | 42% | **45%** | Rapid concentration |
| CR10 | ~30% | ~40% | ~50% | **~60%** | Oligopolization |
| Listed-company CR3 (revenue) | - | - | - | **65.4%** | Big three dominate |
| Listed-company CR10 (revenue) | - | - | - | **94.25%** | The industry is the leaders |

**Data sources**: Qianzhan Industry Research Institute, listed-company financial reports

The industry is moving from "fragmented competition" into "oligopoly." Listed baijiu companies' CR10 is 94%, meaning the baijiu story is basically the story of these 10 companies.

### 3.2 2025 annual-report revenue ranking (major change!)

| Rank | Company | 2025 revenue (RMB 100 million) | YoY | Change |
|------|------|---------------|------|------|
| 1 | Kweichow Moutai | 1720.54 | -1.2% | Firmly No. 1, first slight decline |
| 2 | Wuliangye | 405.29 | **-54.55%** | Accounting adjustment; revenue "cut in half" |
| 3 | Shanxi Fenjiu | 387.18 | **+7.52%** | **Only positive grower!** |
| 4 | Luzhou Laojiao | 257.31 | -17.52% | Fell out of the top three |
| 5 | Yanghe | 192.11 | -33.47% | Decline accelerating |

**Data source**: each company's 2025 annual report

**Three major signals:**

1. **Wuliangye's "financial face-change"**: revenue plunged 55% after shifting revenue recognition from shipment-based to sell-through-based. This is not just an accounting issue. It reveals how much of Wuliangye's previous "revenue" was goods pushed to distributors rather than real consumption.

2. **Shanxi Fenjiu's historic breakout**: full-year 2025 revenue grew 7.5%, expanding against the trend in a depressed market. In 2025Q3, single-quarter revenue reached RMB 8.96 billion, temporarily exceeding Wuliangye's RMB 8.17 billion for the quarter. The rise of light-aroma baijiu is rewriting the old "strong-aroma is king" structure.

3. **Luzhou Laojiao fell out of the top three**: Fenjiu overtook it, pushing it from No. 3 to No. 4. Revenue fell 17.5%, and net profit fell 19.6%.

> *Munger said:* "The third and fourth players in an industry are always fighting an endless war of attrition."

### 3.3 Market-share evolution (2023->2025)

| Company | 2023 market share | Rank | 2025 trend | Change |
|------|----------|------|---------|------|
| Kweichow Moutai | 19.47% | 1 | Continuing to rise | Up |
| Wuliangye | 10.11% | 2 | Temporarily not comparable because of accounting adjustment | ? |
| Yanghe | 4.28% | 3 | Declining | Down sharply |
| Shanxi Fenjiu | 4.20% | 4 | **Rising sharply** | Up sharply |
| Luzhou Laojiao | 3.98% | 5 | Declining | Down |

**Core structural change**: The former "Moutai-Wuliangye-Yanghe" lineup has become "Moutai-Fenjiu-Wuliangye," with Luzhou Laojiao chasing from the side. Yanghe is falling behind fastest; Fenjiu is rising fastest.

## 4. Price-Band Competition: Wuliangye's "Sandwich" Problem

### 4.1 Ultra-premium (RMB 2000+): Moutai stands alone, with no real challenger

| Metric | Feitian Moutai (April 2026) |
|------|-------------------|
| Ex-factory price | RMB 1269 (after March 31, 2026 adjustment) |
| Direct-sale retail price | RMB 1539 |
| Original-carton wholesale price | RMB 1700-1730 |
| Loose-bottle wholesale price | RMB 1565-1580 |

Feitian Moutai remains the only baijiu whose wholesale price is above retail price. It is a financial product, not just a consumer product. Even after briefly falling below RMB 1500 at the industry trough in early 2026, it quickly returned to the RMB 1700 range after the March price hike.

**Moutai moat data**:
- Market share of 19.47%, nearly 2x No. 2 Wuliangye
- 2025 revenue fell only 1.2%; against an industry decline of 18%, this equals "crossing the cycle"
- Brand premium: for the same category of baijiu, Moutai's ex-factory price is RMB 1269 and its wholesale price is RMB 1700, giving the channel RMB 430/bottle of profit

> *Munger said:* "Moutai has a kind of 'psychological monopoly.' When Chinese people need one bottle of liquor to express respect, there is no substitute."

### 4.2 Premium (RMB 800-1500): Puwu and Guojiao 1573 are both deeply inverted; the battle is brutal

| Product | Ex-factory price (RMB) | 2024 wholesale price | Early-2026 wholesale price | Inversion |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|---------|
| Eighth-generation Puwu | ~1019 | ~950 | **880-920** | **-10%~-14%** |
| Guojiao 1573 | ~980 | ~880 | **750-800** | **-18%~-23%** |
| Dream Blue M9 | ~1000 | ~950 | **Down by dozens to hundreds, depending on market** | Opaque |

**Data sources**: liquor industry media, distributor channel surveys

**Key findings:**

1. **Puwu's wholesale price lost RMB 900**: it has fallen from a RMB 1,000 product into the "RMB 800 band," badly exhausting brand momentum.
2. **Guojiao 1573 is worse**: its wholesale price is RMB 750-800, meaning it is living the "dream" of a premium product at sub-premium prices.
3. **In 2025 there was once an abnormal episode in which Guojiao's wholesale price overtook Puwu's**. This shows the two products' perceived value has already blurred together; consumers and distributors no longer take it for granted that Puwu should be more expensive than Guojiao.
4. **Dream Blue M9**: priced around RMB 1,000, but its market recognition and sell-through are weaker than Puwu and Guojiao.

**Munger-style "niche" analysis**:
- Moutai occupies the "ultra-premium social currency" niche: irreplaceable.
- Puwu once occupied the "standard RMB 1,000 business banquet" niche: now being eroded.
- Guojiao 1573 wants to take Puwu's position, but its own brand power is also declining.
- The wholesale price gap has moved from "Moutai >> Puwu > Guojiao" to "Moutai >> Puwu ~= Guojiao." Differentiation in the premium band is disappearing.

### 4.3 Sub-premium (RMB 300-800): the bloodiest battlefield

Sub-premium is the most intensely contested of all price bands and is known as the "sandwich": premium liquor discounting from above squeezes it, while mass-market liquor upgrading from below takes volume.

| Brand/Product | Price band | 2025-2026 developments |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| Qinghua Fen 20 | 400-500 | Promotional price down more than RMB 150; relying on banquet volume |
| Honghualang 10/15 | 300-500 | Banquet blockbuster; 2024 revenue approaching RMB 10 billion |
| Shede | 400-600 | New RMB 980 product pushes premium; low-end RMB 88-358 products target the mass market |
| Shui Jing Fang | 400-600 | Split into "Shui Jing Fang" (sub-premium) + "First Well" (premium) |
| Jiannanchun | 400-500 | Banquet king, but growth is slowing |

**Core features**:
- Sales of group-buying-led sub-premium/premium baijiu have **generally fallen 30%-50%**
- Government group buying has **nearly halved**, while business group buying is down more than 20%
- The RMB 500-800 price segment faces **the greatest survival pressure**; this is exactly the range into which Puwu and Guojiao have "fallen" after wholesale prices slid

> *Munger said:* "In a shrinking market, a price war is the dumbest strategy, but it is also the strategy everyone will use."

### 4.4 Price-band competition summary: who is taking whose share?

```text
Ultra-premium (2000+): Moutai monopolizes the band, with an unfathomably deep moat
      ↓ Moutai wholesale price fluctuates, but remains above 1500
      ↓
Premium (800-1500): Puwu vs Guojiao <- trampling each other
      ↓ Wholesale prices fall to 800-900, eroding sub-premium from above
      ↓
Sub-premium (300-800): <- products falling from above + internal price-band competition
      ↓ Fenjiu/Langjiu/Jiannanchun melee
      ↓
Mass market (100-300): demand is most rigid and therefore stabilizes first
```

**Wuliangye's specific dilemma**:
- Puwu **cannot reach upward to Moutai**: the brand-momentum gap is too wide, and Puwu lacks financial-asset attributes.
- Puwu **is being caught from below by Guojiao**: the wholesale price gap has narrowed from RMB 200+ to almost zero.
- Puwu **has been overtaken on the bend by Fenjiu**: with light-aroma differentiation + banquet channels, Fenjiu's revenue scale is already close to Wuliangye's.

## 5. Consumption Trends: Long-Term Structural Change, Not Reversible

### 5.1 Do young people really not drink baijiu?

| Data | Source |
|------|------|
| Among consumers under 30, baijiu accounts for only 8% of alcohol consumption | Industry survey |
| Baijiu drinking frequency among people aged 25-35 fell 22% year over year | China Lianhe Ratings report |
| Young consumers prefer "tipsy" over "drunk" | Multiple sources |
| Low-ABV alcohol, craft beer, and fruit wine categories are growing rapidly | Market data |

**Conclusion**: Young people have not "abandoned" baijiu, but baijiu has a very low share of their alcohol consumption. They are not "anti-baijiu"; they simply "do not need baijiu." Social occasions have changed (camping, girlfriends' gatherings, solo tipsy drinking), and baijiu's "ganbei culture" does not fit.

**On the other hand**, when these young people enter the 30-40 business-social stage, some will "return" to baijiu. The question is how high that "return rate" will be. There is no reliable data yet, but Japan's experience suggests return rates fall with each generation.

### 5.2 Impact of consumption downgrade

The impact of consumption downgrade on baijiu is segmented:

| Price band | Impact level | Logic |
|--------|---------|------|
| Ultra-premium (Moutai) | ★★ Low | Social-currency attribute; rigid demand |
| Premium (Puwu/Guojiao) | ★★★★ High | Lower frequency and downgrading in business consumption |
| Sub-premium | ★★★★★ Highest | Squeezed from both ends |
| Mass market | ★★★ Medium | Demand is relatively rigid and stabilizes first |

**Most counterintuitive finding**: consumption downgrade has the smallest impact on mass-market baijiu. What is really being "destroyed" is the middle ground. The RMB 800-1500 business-liquor occasion is shrinking; some demand downgrades to RMB 300-500, and some disappears entirely.

### 5.3 Core consumer cohort is shrinking

People aged 30-59 are the core baijiu consumer group. According to UN population forecasts:
- 2022: 665 million people
- 2035: 569 million people
- Compound annual decline: -1%

This means baijiu's "base market" is naturally shrinking at 1% per year. With younger consumers' drinking preferences shifting, the actual shrinkage rate may be faster.

## 6. Policy Environment: Alcohol Bans Have Limited Direct Impact but a Large Sentiment Impact

### 6.1 Policy evolution

| Time | Policy | Impact |
|------|------|------|
| 2012 | Eight-Point Regulation | Government consumption share fell from 40% to 5%; industry was hit hard |
| 2017 | Local alcohol bans escalated | Further compressed official consumption |
| May 2025 | Revised rules restricting "three public" spending | Explicitly banned high-end tobacco and alcohol at official receptions |

### 6.2 Assessment of actual impact

**Direct impact is limited**:
- Government consumption already accounts for less than 5% of the baijiu market; less than 1% of Moutai's sales come from government channels.
- The 2012 Eight-Point Regulation's "de-governmentization" has basically been completed.
- The 2025 new rules are more of a "reaffirmation" than an "intensification."

**Indirect impact cannot be ignored**:
- **Demonstration effect**: local governments may add layers of implementation, with some areas expanding the scope to SOEs and public institutions.
- **Sentiment shock**: news of the May 2025 alcohol ban triggered a steep fall in the baijiu sector.
- **Business-consumption spillover**: companies dealing with government clients proactively lower the grade of liquor used.

**On the other hand**, alcohol bans have pushed baijiu consumption to shift structurally from "government-driven" to "business + individual-driven." Over the long term, this transition is largely complete, and the marginal impact of future policy should keep declining.

## 7. Endgame Thinking: Will Baijiu Follow the Path of Japanese Sake?

### 7.1 The cautionary example of Japanese sake

| Metric | Japanese sake | Chinese baijiu |
|------|---------|---------|
| Consumption-volume trend | 2004-2022 CAGR -3% | 2016-2024 output CAGR -13.7% |
| Premiumization | Ginjo/junmai 2004-2022 CAGR +2% | Premium baijiu share rose from 81% to 83% |
| Premium share | From 26% to 37% (+11pp) | Ultra-premium share 35% (2024) |
| Demographics | Aging + low birth rate | 2022 population inflection + accelerating aging |
| Young people's preference | Shifting to beer/spirits/RTD | Shifting to low-ABV alcohol/craft beer/fruit wine |

### 7.2 Similarities and differences

**Similarities (bearish signals):**
- Total volume keeps shrinking: China's baijiu decline rate (-13.7%/year) is even faster than Japanese sake's (-3%/year).
- Premiumization is the mirror image of "volume contraction": when less is sold, companies can only sell more expensive products.
- Generational shift among young people: baijiu is only 8% of alcohol consumption among people under 30, and the trend is irreversible.
- Demographic shift: the 30-59 core consumer cohort is shrinking at -1%/year.

**Differences (relatively bullish):**
- Cultural embeddedness: baijiu's role in Chinese culture (weddings and funerals, business socializing, holiday gifting) far exceeds sake's role in Japan.
- Higher brand concentration: Moutai's "psychological monopoly" is much stronger than that of any Japanese sake brand.
- Market-size base: China's baijiu market exceeds RMB 800 billion; even if volume shrinks 20-30%, it remains a huge market.
- Consumption upgrading still has room: per-capita spending on baijiu in China still has structural room to rise.

### 7.3 Endgame view

**Baijiu will not follow Japanese sake into "comprehensive decline." It is more likely to follow the path of Japanese whisky: total volume keeps shrinking, leading brands grow against the trend, and the long tail dies at scale.**

Specific forecasts:
- **Volume**: output may shrink another 30-40% over the next 10 years (from about 4 million kiloliters to about 2.5 million kiloliters)
- **Revenue**: total industry revenue may remain stable or even edge up (trading price for volume)
- **Profit**: will further concentrate in CR3 (Moutai, Wuliangye, Fenjiu)
- **Structure**: the final endgame is likely "1 superleader (Moutai) + 3 strong players (Wuliangye/Fenjiu/Luzhou Laojiao) + N regional kings"

**Implications for Wuliangye**:
- Wuliangye will most likely survive to the endgame: it has enough brand, channel, and scale.
- But its "perennial No. 2" position is no longer safe. Fenjiu's threat is real.
- The key variable is whether it can find a balance between "de-Moutai-ization" and "defending the RMB 1,000 price band."

## 8. Munger-Style Industry Investment Framework

### 8.1 Industry scoring

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
|------|------|------|
| Industry endgame certainty | ★★★★ | Highly certain oligopoly structure, but total volume is shrinking |
| Competitive barriers | ★★★★★ | Triple barriers of brand + capacity + channel; new entrants are almost impossible |
| Growth runway | ★★ | Total volume is shrinking; growth can only come from price increases and share gains |
| Cyclicality | ★★★★ | Currently in the bottoming zone of the cycle |
| Policy risk | ★★★ | Alcohol bans' marginal impact is declining, but uncertainty remains |

### 8.2 Competitive-dynamics summary (implications for Wuliangye)

| Competitive force | Intensity | Impact on Wuliangye |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Moutai's downward pressure | ★★★★★ | Cannot touch ultra-premium; premium is suppressed |
| Fenjiu overtaking on the bend | ★★★★ | Revenue scale is already close; light-aroma differentiation is a major threat |
| Guojiao's close-quarters fight | ★★★ | Wholesale prices are converging; mutual trampling |
| Sub-premium's upward penetration | ★★ | Langjiu/Jiannanchun/Fenjiu are taking the RMB 300-800 market |
| Consumer generational turnover | ★★★★ | Young people drink baijiu infrequently; long-term negative |

### 8.3 Key tracking indicators

Investors should keep tracking the following indicators to judge the industry and Wuliangye's trajectory:

1. **Feitian Moutai loose-bottle wholesale price**: the industry weathervane; only a sustained move above RMB 1800 would mark a true bottom.
2. **Puwu wholesale price**: a return above RMB 950 is the key signal that Wuliangye's price system is healthy.
3. **Fenjiu vs Wuliangye single-quarter revenue gap**: if Fenjiu surpasses Wuliangye for several consecutive quarters, the structure will be rewritten.
4. **Industry accounts-receivable growth**: a decline from doubling to below 20% would signal credit-risk clearance.
5. **Distributor payment rate**: a return above 90% would show that channel confidence has recovered.
