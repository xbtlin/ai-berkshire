# Micron Technology (MU) In-Depth Investment Research Report

**Research Date: May 12, 2026 | Share Price: $789.35 | Market Cap: ~$889B**

---

## One-Sentence Conclusion

Micron is at the peak of an AI-driven "memory supercycle." Its HBM business is transforming it from a cyclical commodity company into a higher-barrier AI infrastructure supplier, but the current valuation (TTM PE 35x, PB 10-12x) already fully reflects optimistic expectations. History tells us that cyclicality in memory has never truly disappeared, so investors must decide between the narrative that "this time is different" and the iron law of mean reversion.

## Dimension 1: Business Model Analysis (Duan Yongping Lens)

### What HBM Has Changed
- **High degree of customization**: Co-designed with specific GPUs, with a 6-12 month qualification cycle
- **Supply shortage**: Current capacity can meet only 50-66% of core customer demand
- **Long-term contract model**: Prices and volumes are locked in through long-term agreements

### What HBM Has Not Changed
- Product performance is converging across the three suppliers
- Price competition will return once supply and demand rebalance
- At its core, HBM is still stacked-packaged DRAM

### Duan Yongping Standard Assessment
- High gross margin, but unstable (swinging from 81% to negative territory)
- Classic capital-intensive business (FY2026 CapEx >$25.0B)
- Pricing power has partly improved, but not permanently
- **Conclusion: This is not a "good business," but HBM has moved it from "poor" to "acceptable"**

## Dimension 3: Industry and Competition Analysis (Munger Lens)

### Big-Three Structure

| Company | DRAM Market Share | HBM Market Share |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Samsung | ~40% | ~22% |
| SK Hynix | ~35% | ~57% |
| Micron | ~25% | ~21% |

### HBM Competition
- SK Hynix is the clear leader (57% share)
- Samsung is expanding HBM capacity by 50% in 2026, to 250,000 wafers per month
- Micron ranks second/third, with leading technical speed (>11Gbps)

### Chinese Competitors
- **CXMT**: Monthly capacity is expanding from 100,000 wafers to 290,000 wafers, pressuring low-end DRAM
- **YMTC**: NAND shipment share has reached 13%
- Key limitation: no HBM production capability

### Non-Consensus View
The market may be underestimating long-term memory demand from AI inference. The scale of inference deployment could be more than 10x larger than training, which may extend the HBM demand cycle beyond current expectations.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (7 Points)
1. The AI supercycle is real: Q3 guidance of $33.5B for a single quarter exceeds historical full-year revenue
2. HBM remains supply-constrained through 2027
3. HBM4 technical speed is leading
4. Forward PE is only 7.7x (FY2027E)
5. Strong balance sheet (net debt near zero)
6. Excellent management
7. China risk has been digested (revenue exposure down to 7%)

### Bear Case (7 Points)
1. Memory cycles have never disappeared: every "this time is different" claim over the past 50 years has ultimately been disproven
2. PB of 10-12x is historically elevated
3. Samsung is expanding HBM capacity by 50%
4. $25.0B+ annual CapEx creates downside risk in a cycle downturn
5. Chinese vendors are rising in low-end DRAM/NAND
6. The AI capex cycle is uncertain
7. CEO selling at elevated levels
