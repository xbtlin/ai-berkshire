None of the companies currently offers a Buffett-style margin of safety. This is not an isolated issue; it is a systemic feature of overheated valuations across the AI infrastructure theme.

### Finding 2: NVIDIA Is the Kingmaker in Optical Communications

- NVIDIA invested $20 100M in Coherent, signed a $20 100M strategic partnership with Lumentum, and entered into a $32 100M long-term agreement with Corning
- **AAOI, which has not received a strategic NVIDIA investment**, is clearly one tier lower in competitiveness and certainty
- In optical communications, "inside the NVIDIA supply chain" versus "outside the NVIDIA supply chain" is the key dividing line

### Finding 3: Management Teams Are Broadly Selling at Elevated Levels

| Company | Management Selling Signal |
|------|-------------|
| GLW | CEO cashed out $2.5 100M; executives collectively sold >$1,000 10K |
| AAOI | 22 sales and 0 purchases over 6 months; total >$2500 10K |
| MU | CEO sold ~$2150 10K |
| WDC | CEO sold $820 10K |

This is a systemic warning signal worth taking seriously: when management teams across an entire industry are monetizing at high levels, investors should stay disciplined.

### Finding 4: Chinese Competitors Are a Long-Term Structural Threat

- **Optical communications**: Innolight has >40% share in 800G, with a 10-20% cost advantage
- **Storage**: CXMT produces 29 10K DRAM wafers per month; YMTC has 13% NAND share
- Geopolitics constrains them in the short term, but they will continue to erode the low-end market over the medium term

## 5. Investment Decision Matrix

### If We Had to Pick One (Forced Ranking)

**Top Choice: Micron Technology (MU)**
- Rationale: solid position in the memory triopoly, leading HBM technology, lowest forward PE, and the strongest balance sheet
- But investors must accept: the storage industry is extremely cyclical, and the current point may be near a cyclical peak
- Strategy: wait for a pullback to the $400-500 range before building a position

### If Choosing a Theme ETF Instead of Individual Stocks

All 4 optical communications companies (GLW/LITE/COHR/AAOI) are in valuation bubble territory. If one is bullish on AI optical communications but uncertain which company will ultimately win, consider:
- waiting for a broad 30-50% pullback before diversifying across the theme
- focusing on GLW (largest and most stable) and LITE (strongest technology)

### Clear Avoid

**AAOI**: never profitable, broad management selling, shorted by two short-selling firms, and PS far above peers. It does not meet any value-investing standard.

## 8. Conclusion

These 6 companies cover two core layers of AI infrastructure: "connectivity" (optical communications) and "memory" (storage). All are benefiting from powerful AI supercycle tailwinds. However, the market has already fully, and in most cases excessively, priced in these positives.

**Core tension**: the certainty of AI infrastructure demand versus the absence of valuation margin of safety.

From a value-investing framework, none of these companies deserves an immediate full-position purchase at the current point. Still, several of the 6 companies (MU, GLW, LITE, COHR) are genuinely high-quality businesses and deserve a place on a "tier-one watchlist" while investors patiently wait for better entry points after valuation pullbacks.

> Duan Yongping: "If you do not understand it, do not do it; margin of safety; circle of competence."
> Buffett: "It is far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."
> Munger: "All intelligent investing is value investing."
> Li Lu: "Good investment opportunities often appear when everyone is fearful."

Current market enthusiasm for AI infrastructure is near a peak. The true value-investing opportunity may not appear until the next market panic.
