# A moat of the river
The moat of Shinzhou International is extremely deep, consisting of three layers of "Divinely integrated manufacturing capacity plus deep-bound customer-heading capacity plus surface research and development capability". Vertical integration is the core: the integration of the whole process of fabric to clothing, with orders delivered within 60 days at more than 40%, and this efficiency is difficult for peers.

The challenge is that the Māori rate is only 26.3% and is on a downward trend, and that the profits ceiling in manufacturing is evident; that the concentration of customers is high (80% of the four largest customers), with any large client having a significant impact on performance; and that labour costs are rising and overseas construction is becoming more difficult to manage, although it is partly under pressure. Moreover, global trade friction and tariff policies are sources of uncertainty for manufacturing exporters.

# # Management review
The Ma Jianyong family holding shares (about 47.3%), with more than 40 years of experience in the textile industry, with a core executive with an average of over 15 years of service, and a highly stable management team. The combined senior managers hold 53.6% of the shares, forming a high-interest community – the interests of management and shareholders are fully aligned – a rare governance structure. The Ma Jing is low-key, pragmatic, long-term, focused on manufacturing, and does not diversify, a style that is "one thing is great" in the very high quality of the entrepreneur Buffett likes.

# Valuation judgement #
PE is about 15 times more likely to correspond to stable ROE (15-18%) and conservative growth (8% growth rate), and is reasonably valued. Shinzhou International is typical of "stable and high-quality assets" — not to make you windfall profits, but probably not at a loss. Profits are projected to recover in 2026 (US$ 5.94 billion, +1.9%), and the growth rate in 2027 rose to 13.4% if the Māori rate is going to rebound and the customer order to rise again, the value of the 15-fold PE is considered to be a security margin.
