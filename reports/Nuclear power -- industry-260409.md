# Is this "civilizational paradigm shift" or "stage surge"?

** Sensitization: between the two, favouring "long-term structural opportunities".**

Nuclear power is not new technology — humans have been using nuclear power for 70 years. This time it's not "inventing nuclear power," but ** "reaccommodating nuclear power." The driving force is a three-tier trend:

**Ali/Arithmetic power demand** - This is a real incremental demand, not speculative bubble
** Global decarbonization** - Nuclear power is the only mature zero-carbon-based charge source
3. **Energy security** — Post-conflict Russian-Ukrainian countries revisit energy independence

** The nuclear power boom after the oil crisis of the 1970s** ** ** was also driven by energy security and was the global nuclear power plant. End: decades of electricity have indeed been generated since the nuclear plant was built, but the middle has experienced a huge swing of three miles of energy and Chernobyl.** Long-term winners are established operators.**

- China became the largest nuclear power nation in the world.

Which link is most likely to be subverted?
