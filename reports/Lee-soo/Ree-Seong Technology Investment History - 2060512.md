# Five, why clean up?

Li Shu has never explained publicly the specific reasons for the Quantico.

# Prosthetic Reason I: China Cybersecurity Review (Geopolitics Risk)

- **March 31, 2023**: China Cyber Security Review Office announced the implementation of a cybersecurity review of Meshre products
- **May 21, 2023**: China identified the US Light product as "a serious cyber security problem", and prohibited critical information infrastructure operators from purchasing its product
- ** Time highly matches**: Lee Sze Q1,2023, reduced 40%, which coincides with the review of the bulletin, Q2 completed the clearance just before and after the ban.
- About 10% of the US Light, with revenue from mainland China and about 25% of Hong Kong combined, geopolitical risks have substantially affected the business prospects of Migwang.

# Prosthetic two: storage chip cycle down

- 2022 DRAM/NAND prices have been falling steadily since the second half of the year, with high industrial inventories
- The stock price of the light fell from $93 in early 2022 to $50 in late 2000 and nearly 50% in the first place.
- Storage chips are in the strong cycle industry, and Li can judge the cycle to the top, opting to leave.

# Three reasons for speculation: re-allocation of funds -- shift to bank shares

The bankruptcy of Silicon Valley Bank in March triggered a collapse in the bank's stock.
- While keeping the light down:
- **Kakura Bank of America (BAC): Reverse loading in case of panic
- ** New EWBC** Q1 New entry, Q2 plus 480,000 shares
- Which means that Lee transferred Mi-Io's money to the bank's shares he thought was more attractive.
- It's the logic of a value investor selling a normal opportunity, buying a better one.

# Promising Reason IV: Insufficient valuation/return expected

- From the construction to the cleaning, the stock price of the light only rose from ~48 to ~62, about 28%.
- After 3.5 years of holding, annualization is only about 7%, which is much lower than the performance of other investments by Lishu.
- In comparison, Alphabet (GOOG) and the bank unit may provide better risk returns than during the same period

> **Note**: These are market speculations, and Li Siu himself has not publicly expressed himself. As a value investor who is deeply influenced by Mang, he rarely discusses specific sales decisions in public.

# Seven: Analysis of investment papers — Why did Lee Siu buy the light?

Although Li Shu has not publicly articulated the full logic of investing in the beauty light, it is possible to assume from the silo behaviour and industry analysis that:

# Buy logic

1. ** Industry pattern is good**: Global DRAM markets are highly concentrated (three stars, SK Hercules, and Migwang) and competitive patterns are far better than other semiconductor tracks
2. ** Buy at the bottom of the cycle**: 2019 Q4 Positive storage chip end of the cycle, light share price dropped from $60+ in 2018 to about $40, valuation at historical low
3. ** Long-term needs determination**: continuous growth in trend-driven storage needs, such as data centres, 5G, IOT, autopilot etc.
4. ** Financial sound**: Free cash flow in beauty, technological leadership, moat depth

Why did you end up making only 28%?

- Storage of a high-cycle chip causing high stock price volatility: up to $93 and down to $50.
- Lee Sze didn't sell at the high point in 2021.
- Geopolitical Black Swans (China Review) speeded up sales decisions
- And then he quit when he had to.

# Eight, key findings

1. **Stage construction costs**: approximately $48.63 shares, total investment of approximately $558 million, held for approximately 3.5 years
2. **Siloage prices**: approximately 62/equity (weighted average), total recovery of approximately $712 million
3. ** Actual return**: approximately $154 million in profits, total return of approximately 28 per cent, annualized rate of about 7 per cent
4. **Foot-over-high point comparison**: 2021 Q4 was over $530 million (nearly 100 per cent return) and ultimately was far below this
5. **Siloing drivers**: geopolitical risks (China review) + Downline of storage cycle + bank unit opportunities emerging
**Lessons**: For a strong cycle industry, even if the fundamentals are long-term, it is necessary to consider selling when cyclical choices are in place; geopolitical risks are an external variable that is difficult to predict

> This investment is "normal" in Lee's overall performance. The $154 billion in profits of the United States dollar is flat compared to the $14.5 billion he earned on Biadi. But the logic of building a warehouse at the bottom of the cycle and holding a warehouse is not in itself problematic in terms of the quality of investment decisions; the problem is the timing of exit – neither selling it at the height of the cycle nor waiting for the second wave of the AI wave.
