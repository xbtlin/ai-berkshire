## 1. Core battle: BYD (the most successful investment)

This is the most colorful investment in Li Lu's investment career, and it is also a textbook case of value investment.

### Complete timeline

| Time | Event | Details |
|------|------|------|
| **July 2002** | BYD's Hong Kong stock market was listed, and Li Lu bought it for the first time | BYD's H shares were listed at about HK$10.95, and then fell back. Li Lu opened a position at about HK$8 and became a cornerstone investor, holding about 1.67% of the shares |
| **2003** | Introducing BYD to Munger | Li Lu and Munger began to recommend BYD to Munger after their first long talk on Thanksgiving Day. Munger initially uninterested |
| **2005** | Annual report shows holdings of 8.9975 million H shares | Accounting for approximately 1.67% of BYD H shares |
| **2008** | Significant increase in holdings to 52.2042 million shares | Accounting for approximately 2.55% of BYD's total share capital. During the financial crisis in the same year, BYD’s share price plummeted |
| **September 2008** | Instigated Buffett to invest in BYD | Li Lu continued to persuade Munger, and Munger finally "beat the table" and asked Buffett to buy it. Berkshire's MidAmerican Energy purchased 225 million shares (10% equity) at HK$8 per share, investing HK$1.8 billion |
| **2010-2020** | Long-term holding | BYD's stock price fluctuates violently (it reached 88 Hong Kong dollars in 2009 and then fell back to 14 Hong Kong dollars), and Li Lu always holds it |
| **July 2021** | First reduction of holdings | Reduce holdings of 10.7715 million shares in two times, cashing out more than HK$2.439 billion. After reduction, the position is about 73.6506 million shares |
| **2021-2022** | Continue to reduce holdings slightly | Gradually reduce holdings while BYD's stock price rushes from 200+ Hong Kong dollars to 300+ Hong Kong dollars |

### Revenue estimate

| Indicators | Data |
|------|------|
| Purchase cost | About HK$8-15/share (bought in batches over many years), the total cost is estimated to be no more than HK$1.2 billion |
| Peak market value of positions | When BYD reached a maximum of about HK$340 per share, the market value of positions exceeded HK$25 billion |
| Realized gains | Over HK$18 billion in cash reduction from holdings |
| Investment return | **over 15 times** (in cost terms), if the initial HK$8 purchase is included, it would be **over 40 times** |
| Holding time | **About 20 years** (2002-2022) |

### Investment logic

Li Lu has publicly expressed his views on BYD many times:

"BYD is a company with a true engineering culture. Wang Chuanfu himself is an engineer, and the execution ability of 100,000 science and engineering men is its core competitiveness."

"When I first bought BYD, I was attracted by its accumulation of battery technology and its vertical integration capabilities. Later, its entry into automobiles and new energy sources was a natural extension of this capability."

Munger commented: "BYD is one of the most amazing companies I have ever seen."

Buffett later said: "When making investment decisions, Munger only slammed the table with me twice and asked me to buy, buy, buy." One of them was BYD.

## 5. Summary of investment style

### Style changes around 2018

| Dimensions | Before 2018 | After 2018 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Market focus | **Chinese concept stocks mainly** (Baidu, Sina, BYD) | **Turn to US blue-chip stocks** (Google, Bank of America, Berkshire) |
| Position concentration | High | **Extremely high** (top 3 account for 83%+) |
| Frequency of changing hands | Relatively high (average 15 months) | Extremely low (almost does not move) |
| Investment Types | Technology + Chinese Consumption | US Technology Platform + Finance |

### Core Features

1. **Extreme concentration**: Usually only 5-8 stocks are held, with the top 3 accounting for over 80%
2. **Long-term holding**: Once you are right, you can hold it for 20 years without selling (BYD)
3. **High winning rate**: 11 out of 13 publicly held stocks made money, 84.6% winning rate
4. **Stop loss decisively**: Leave if you are wrong, not willing to fight (first time on Pinduoduo, Alibaba)
5. **Buy in Crisis**: Increase your position in BYD during the financial crisis, buy Bank of America during the epidemic, and buy East West Bank during the Silicon Valley Bank crisis.
6. **No management fees**: Only make money when you make money for investors

### Li Lu’s self-reported investment principles

"Long-term research, right time, right price."

"We fish where the fish are. You don't need to understand all the companies, you just need to really understand a few."

"The best investments are the ones you never need to sell."
