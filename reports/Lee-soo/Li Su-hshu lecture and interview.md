# II. TALKING ABOUT MAINTENANCE (2014/2018)

> Source: Articles published by Himalayan Capital Officer Network

# The civilizational three-stage theory

Li Jiu divided the evolution of human civilization into three stages:

- **1.0 Ages - Hunting and gathering civilization**: Human survival depends on nature, with a zero-sum economy
- **2.0 Age - Agricultural civilization**: land as a core productive resource, economic growth is extremely slow
- **3.0 Ages - Science and Technology Civilization**: Reciprocal economic growth with a free market economy plus modern technology at its core

"3.0 The central characteristic of civilization is that a free market economy, coupled with modern technology, has produced sustained compound growth. This has never happened in human history."

# China's location

"China is now in the '2.5 phase' - the whole period after the industrialization takeoff."

The core paradox: "There is a huge gap between the reality of the growth of the economy in compound and the slow changes in the way social, psychological and political governance are conducted".

# The judgment of the Chinese-American relationship

"In the eyes of all mankind, think about the common destiny of the future of mankind. The Chinese-American relationship is not a zero-sum game, but a win-win partnership within a 3.0 civilization."

Source: Himalayan Capital Officer Network; Civilization, Modernization, Value Investment and China (CIBC, April 2020)

# III. North Great Gwanghua Lecture: The Future of Value Investment in China (2015.10)

> Room 202, 1st Building, Guanghua Management College, Beijing University, 23 October 2015

# Four basic concepts of value investment

"There are only four ideas for value investment. The first three are the Benjamin Graham concept of Barfitt's teacher, and the fourth is Buffett's own unique contribution."

** First, equities are part of the company's ownership, not just paper pieces that can be bought and sold**

"Assuredly, you buy a company, you buy a part of the company's ownership. The concept looks simple, but there are few people who actually invest in it according to this principle."

** Second, understand the concept of Mr. Market.**

"The market exists for you, not for you. The market man gives you a quote every day, you have the right to use it, and you have the right to ignore it."

"In the short term, the market is a voter; in the long term, the market is a heavy machine."

** Third, the essence of investment is to forecast the future, the results of which cannot be 100 per cent correct, and hence the need for a safe margin**

"The security margin is that you pay far less than you get. The less you trust in your judgment, the bigger the security margin you need."

** Fourth, Circle of Capacity.**

"The concept of the circle of competence is not about the size of the circle of competence, but about the boundaries of the circle of competence."

"Investors really need an understanding of the company they're going to invest. You don't need to know a lot of companies, just a few."

# Does the value of the investment apply in China?

"Some people think that value investment is only available in the United States market and not in China. This is a totally wrong idea."

"Value investments are all-round because their bottom logic — buying undervalued assets — is established in any free market."

"China’s economy has achieved the largest wealth creation in human history in the last three or four decades. Such a vibrant economy will produce a huge number of good businesses. This is a great opportunity for value investors."

# To the young man #

"If you really understand the value of investment, you'll find that it's not just the way to invest, but a philosophy of life."

"At an early stage, find areas that you can truly understand, build your circle of competence and then wait patiently for the opportunity."

"Everyone yearns five times a year, but ignores the return of the compound. "

> Source: [North Great Light China Network] (https://www.gsm.pku.edu.cn/undergraduate/info/1053/5832.htm); [Speaker PDF] (https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5ef3c7300432b40ed865991a/5f0936c4321954706fa9eca2_%E4%BB%B7%E5%80%BC%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E5%9C%A8%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%9A%84%E5%B1%95%E6%9C%9B-Lee-soo2015-10-23%E5%8C%97%E5%A4%A7%E6%BC%94%E8%AE%B2.pdf)

# IV. North Great Lights Lecture: Practice of Value Investment (2019)

> Source: University of Beijing, Guanghua Management College, " Value Investment "

# I know the line of action

"The logic of a value investment is simple enough to be understood by almost everyone. But it's probably less than 5% of people who really know how to work together."

"Why can't most people do it? Because of humanity. Greed, fear, vision, and short-sightedness in humanity are all against the idea of value investment."

# A deeper elaboration of the power circle

"Building a circle of competencies takes a lot of time and effort. You have to study a company like an expert in a business."

"You don't need a lot of good ideas. If you can find ten good ideas in life, it'll be enough to make you very rich."

# About selling

"The best investment is that you never have to sell. If you buy a really good company, the best strategy is to do nothing."

# Fund fee structure

Himalayan capital practice, the Buffet formula — no overhead charge, top 6 per cent free of charge, with 25 per cent pay-for-performance payment for more than a portion.

"Our interests are exactly the same as our investors. We only make money when investors make money."

VI. OPEN-SET: ON MANG, MASK, AI (2024.11)

> 28 November 2024, on the occasion of the first anniversary of Manger ' s death. Source: Newscaster, Smart Investor

# Four levels of Mang rationality

"Charlie's 'rational' is not a simple logic. It has four levels:
** Universal wisdom** — summed up from historical cases
** fenced thinking** - Integrating knowledge from all disciplines and developing a model of multi-minded thinking
** Human error psychology** - Identification and avoidance of systemic cognitive errors
4. ** Based on common sense** - repeating practices that have been proven to be correct”

# The collision of Musk with Manger

"Mask and Charlie are very similar in rational thinking. Charlie's "Universal wisdom" is the same thing as Mask's "first sex theory" -- it's a scientific way of thinking, that's how physics is thinking."

"There was a long lunch, and I, Charlie, Musk and another person discussed various scientific issues like batteries. They were in a lot of things."

"But in business judgment, risk is viewed differently -- ** Mr. Mask accepts a 5% success rate and Charlie may need more than 80% success chances to do so**."

# Three judgments on AI

**1. Critical technologies:**
"AI's influence is all-encompassing, but it's hard to tell the details."

**2. Existence risk:**
"AI brings hope, and may be accompanied by a crisis of survival. Species that transcend human intelligence may be seen as tools for human beings, as they do for other species. When more intelligent species evolve without human control, humans are exposed to real threats."

**3. Need for global governance:**
"There is a need to build a common governance structure for all humankind. Governments and the private sector need to work together in all ways."

# The hold on the value of the investment

"Though some overtake Buffett and Manger in the short term, they're over-represented in the long run, and none of them have been there. This suggests that it's really hard to invest in value."

"Varial investors are always a minority. More and more people like to use the word, but fewer and fewer people actually do it."

# VII. North Great Lights Lecture: Global Value Investment and the Age (2024.12)

> 7 December 2024, Sharon 10th Anniversary of the North Great China School of Management Course " Value Investment " . 30,000 words of keynote speech. Source: Newscast, New Wave Finance

# The confusion of the times

** At the domestic level:**
- "Young unemployment is 20%, reflecting a crisis in private business confidence."
- Real estate accounts for 60% of household wealth, and assets are shrinking to a great deal of consumption.
- "The "roll" culture in a deflationary environment, the supply side of economic policy does not match the demand side."

** International level:**
- "The United States has fundamentally questioned its role in the international community, destabilizing the post-war international order."
- "The uncertainty of the global trading system."

# The Chinese question under the civilizational paradigm

"China is in the '2.5 stage" -- the whole period of industrialization. All countries that have experienced industrialization face the same dilemma."

** Three old perception traps:**
1. **The land concept**: "In the 3.0 era, the size and flow of markets, rather than land and population, determine economic growth"
2. **Full economy**: "The concept of 'getting out of the real world' is false, software, games, etc. are inextricably linked to the real economy"
3. ** Functions of government**: "From command to republican, deliberative, service-oriented"

# Middle-income traps and leaps

"3.0 Economy requires adequate exchange and circulation of the whole element of the economy."

** Current bottlenecks:**
- Personal consumption is only 40% of GDP, savings are almost 50%.
- "The State-owned banking system is not able to efficiently allocate funds and lacks a credit system."

** Hong Kong strategic value:**
"Hong Kong has all the elements of modern capital markets – the perfect system, laws, and credit mechanisms. Similar to the Dutch-British relationship, China could use Hong Kong to build a truly modern financial system."

** Practice orientation:**
"Increased personal consumption from 40% to 60% in India, and economic growth will be sustainable."

# Redefinition of the concept of wealth

"Property is your share of the purchasing power of the economy as a whole, not in absolute terms."

"In a sustained 3.0 economy, static wealth (cash, real estate) is relatively shrinking. Real wealth comes from holding a growing corporate stake."

# Six basic concepts of value investment

1. ** Equity is a certificate of ownership** and not just a trading chip
2. ** Mr. Markets provides services rather than guidance** and short-term prices tend to deviate from value
3. ** Adequate security margins** are a long-standing prerequisite
4. ** Clearing the limits of the circle of competencies**, investing only in companies that they truly understand
5. ** Fishing where fish exist** does not require full macro-level parameters
** The goal is to hold the best companies in the most dynamic economies** and to maintain and increase purchasing power

# History is a lesson

"Greham, Keynes, Templeton found value investments in times of economic depression and war. That is much worse than today's environment. Macro-difficult is the moment when value investments show their advantage."

# Core conclusion

"Macro is what we have to accept, and micro is what we can do."

"Investors need not accurately forecast macro-prospectors, but rather find good firms mispricing in turmoil, maintaining purchasing power ratios in the global economy through long-term holdings."

"The real value investor is not a parasite, but a key to the function of price discovery in capital markets, allowing savings to be effectively recycled into social productivity."

"For a large economy like China, many moments of real and dramatic change are often accidental."
