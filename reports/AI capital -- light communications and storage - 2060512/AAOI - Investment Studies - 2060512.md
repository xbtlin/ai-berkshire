## One-Sentence Conclusion

AAOI is a small vertically integrated manufacturer riding the AI optical module wave, with revenue in an explosive growth phase (from $4.56 100M in 2025 to 2026 guidance of $11 100M). However, the company has a long history of losses, negative cash flow, heavy management selling, severe equity dilution (66% in one year), and an extremely stretched valuation (PS(TTM) 31.9x). Both Citron and Culper, two short-selling firms, have publicly shorted the stock. This is a classic high-volatility speculative vehicle, not a value-investing candidate.

## Key Analytical Points

### Not a Good Business (Duan Yongping Standard)
- Founded 29 years ago and has never achieved sustained profitability
- Very weak pricing power (B2B business; hyperscale customers have strong bargaining power)
- Extremely high capital intensity (2025 CapEx was 45.9% of revenue)
- Squeezed from both directions: Coherent/Lumentum above and Innolight below

### 800G Revenue Is Overstated
- In Q1 2026, 800G revenue was only $460 10K (3% of total revenue)
- The bulk of data center revenue still comes from legacy 100G and 400G products
- Explosive 800G growth has not truly arrived yet

### Short-Seller Critiques
- **Culper Research**: questioned the authenticity of 800G capacity and customer relationships
- **Citron**: called the valuation "crazy" and said it prices commodity economics at a monopoly premium
