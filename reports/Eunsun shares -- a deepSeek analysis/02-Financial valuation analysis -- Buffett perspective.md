## 1. Revenue and net profit trend analysis

### Revenue trends in the past five years (data source: Baostock/Yongxin Shares Annual Report)

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth | Net profit (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year growth | Net profit rate |
|------|-----------|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| 2021 | 30.24 | +10.45% | 3.16 | +4.25% | 10.62% |
| 2022 | 33.04 | +9.27% | 3.63 | +14.85% | 11.16% |
| 2023 | 33.79 | +2.26% | 4.08 | +12.46% | 12.26% |
| 2024 | 35.25 | +4.34% | 4.68 | +14.63% | 13.37% |
| 2025 | 37.23 | **+5.61%** | 4.38 | **-6.33%** | **11.87%** |
| 2026Q1 | 9.40 | +10.81% | 0.91 | +2.06% | **9.78%** |

### Trend analysis conclusion

**Revenue growth: ★★★☆☆**
- Revenue CAGR in the past five years is approximately 5.32%, growth is moderate but the ceiling has been reached
- The growth rate will be the lowest in 2023 (2.26%), and will rise slightly to 5.61% in 2025
- Demand in the packaging industry is stable but lacks explosive growth

**Net profit quality: ★★★☆☆ (trend turns negative)**
- Net profit will increase by 14.63% in 2024, hitting a new high in recent years, but will fall by 6.33% for the first time in 2025**
- Deducting non-net profit of 409 million (-7.17%), the decline is greater than net profit - non-recurring gains and losses have a whitewashing effect
- Net interest rate will further drop to 9.78% in Q1 2026, a new low in the past three years

**Buffett's focus:** "A decline in profits when revenue grows is a typical signal that a company has lost its pricing power."

## 2. In-depth analysis of profitability indicators

### ROE Trend

| Year | ROE | Equity Multiplier | Net Profit Margin | Asset Turnover Ratio (Extrapolated) |
|------|-----|---------|-------|-----------------|
| 2021 | 15.90% | 1.39 | 10.62% | ~1.08 |
| 2022 | 17.01% | 1.54 | 11.16% | ~0.99 |
| 2023 | 17.72% | 1.56 | 12.26% | ~0.93 |
| 2024 | **19.18%** | 1.64 | 13.37% | ~0.87 |
| 2025 | **17.34%** | **1.71** | 11.87% | ~0.85 |

**ROE analysis (Buffett’s most important indicator):**
- ROE>15% for 5 consecutive years, seemingly excellent
- But it will fall back to 17.34% in 2025, and **increase depends on leverage** (equity multiplier rises from 1.39 to 1.71)
- Asset turnover ratio continues to decline from 1.08 to 0.85 - operational efficiency is deteriorating
- DuPont analysis shows: ROE maintenance mainly depends on net interest rate, but net interest rate has declined in 2025

### Gross profit margin and net profit margin

| Year | Gross profit margin | Net profit rate | Period expense ratio (estimated) |
|------|--------|-------|-----------------|
| 2021 | 22.24% | 10.62% | ~11.6% |
| 2022 | 22.20% | 11.16% | ~11.0% |
| 2023 | **24.66%** | 12.26% | ~10.1% |
| 2024 | 23.78% | **13.37%** | ~10.5% |
| 2025 | 23.00% | 11.87% | ~11.2% |

**The gross profit margin fluctuates between 22-25%**, reflecting the price-sensitive business of raw materials. According to a brokerage report: Synthetic resins account for 58.53% of procurement costs, and for every liter of raw materials at 10%, the gross profit margin decreases by about 7.9pct. The company is a **price taker** and has no pricing power.

**Comprehensive Profitability Rating: ★★★☆☆ (trend negative)**

### Safety Margin Assessment

- Neutral scenario target price of 12.5 yuan, current 11.4 yuan provides **only 10% upside potential**
- The conservative scenario has a downside of 37% and a poor risk-return ratio
- **Risk-adjusted expected return is negative:** (68%×15%) + (10%×50%) + (-37%×35%) = 10.2% - 12.95% = **-2.75%**
