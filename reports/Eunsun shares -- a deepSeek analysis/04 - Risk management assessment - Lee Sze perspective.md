## 9. Final conclusion from Li Lu’s perspective

**Positive factors:**
1. Excellent shareholder returns: cumulative dividend rate 77.77%, dividend rate ~5%, the highest level in the industry
2. Healthy governance structure: zero pledge of equity, strategic holdings of ORG, and regulated related-party transactions
3. Strict financial discipline: controllable debt ratio and conservative capital allocation
4. The market is overly concerned about environmental risks: core products are not included in the plastic ban list

**Negatives:**
1. There is great uncertainty in the second-generation succession in 2025 (the chairman has no industry experience)
2. Increased revenue will not increase profits in 2025, and cash flow will deteriorate.
3. Industry competition is fierce and pricing power is weak
4. There is long-term pressure for environmentally friendly substitution and transformation

**Li Lu type conclusion:**
> "The best investment is one that lets you sleep at night."

Yongxin shares have two things that make people "sleep well" (high dividends + zero pledge), but there are two things that make people "cannot sleep" (new management to be verified + long-term industry transformation pressure). At the current PE ~16 times and dividend yield ~5%, it can be configured as a "bond-like" allocation, but it lacks a growth catalyst.

**Recommendation: Wait for the complete financial report for 2026-2027 to verify the performance of the new management. If the Jiang Lei + Pan Jian combination can stabilize performance, the current price has allocation value; if the performance continues to deteriorate, it needs to be re-evaluated. **
