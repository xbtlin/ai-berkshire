# Three scenarios

Without precise calculations (which give the wrong sense of accuracy), only directional judgements:

** Scenario I (optimal): new management hands over good results + new production contribution in thin membrane + raw materials remain low**
- Net profit recovery to 500 million plus and a return to 18-20 times the market surplus
- Counter-equity price 15-16, upbound space 30-40%
- This scenario requires multiple conditions, with low probability.

** Scenario II (neutral): flat or modest growth in performance, maintaining current pattern**
- Net profit 4-450 million blocks, with a market surplus of 14-16 times
- Counter-equity price $10-12, limited up-front space
- But a yearly dividend of 4.7%, a cumulative return of about 20-25% for 3-5 years

- Net profits have fallen below 350 million and the municipal surplus has been reduced to 10-12 times
- The corresponding share price 7-8 yuan, downside space 30-40%
- The rate of dividends will fall, the logic of "class bonds" will be weakened.

# Write it in the end

The Young-Zin shares are not an exciting company. They don't double, they don't blow the subject, they don't appear in the hot discussions at investors' parties.

But in a world where interest rates are consistently low, a company that has 20 consecutive years of dividends, 4.7% dividends, and financial discipline has its own value -- as long as you don't have unrealistic expectations of it.

Seeing Young-shin is essentially a trade-off for investment decisions:** Would you like to take a simple business for a steady cash split?**

The answer is different for everyone. What this series does is just put the facts that are needed to make a judgment on the table.
