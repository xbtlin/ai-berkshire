# Why is the concentration so low

The soft packaging industry CR5 is only about 6%**, CR10 about **15-20%** (estimate). This concentration level is extremely low in manufacturing.

*Source of data: Integrated industry studies*

There are three reasons:

# 1. Low entry threshold

The opening of a low- and medium-end packaging plant, with equipment worth millions to tens of millions, has led to a strong regional competition throughout the country, with thousands of small packaging enterprises.

##2. Transport radius limit

Packaging materials are large, lightweight, and low unit cost - transport costs are higher. A packaging plant in Yellow Hills is transporting products to Guangdong, where freight costs may consume most of the profits. This naturally creates a regional pattern of competition.

Young-Jun's production base is mainly located in the Ludwig Zone, which explains why the company's "nationalization" is slow.

#3. Low product differentiation

A potato bag, made by A and made by B, makes no difference to consumers. Asymmetrication of products means a single dimension of competition - mainly price and service response speed, not branding or technical barriers.

** Inverse:** It is precisely because of low concentration and the space for industrial integration. Environmental policies (VOCs governance, higher emission standards) are accelerating the phasing out of small businesses.

# The driving forces of industry growth and the ceiling

# It's driving #

1. **Sustable consumer demand**: people need to eat, wash, take medicine, packaging needs are associated with high consumption frequency and cyclical
2. ** Consumption upgrade leads to packaging upgrade**: from simple plastic bags to high barrier-free, fresh, beautiful composite packagings with higher unit prices
3. **Extale/electricator**: New consumption scenes give rise to new packaging needs
4. **Effection of environmental standards**: compliance companies benefit from small-scale production

# Reversely repressed

1. ** Plastics restriction policy**: Long-term direction is to reduce one-time plastic use and to suppress traditional plastic packaging
2. **Excess of capacity**: high level of excess low end packaging capacity and frequent price wars
3. ** Consumption downgrade/downgrade**: Some branders are reducing the cost of packaging
4. ** Industry slowdown**: China ' s plastic packaging rate has decreased from double digits to **5-8%**

** Combined judgements: industries enter maturity with structural opportunities greater than total opportunities.** The main sources of increase were: high end (functional film), environmental substitution (compliance tap-up), and exports (emerging markets).
