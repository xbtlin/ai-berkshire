## Series Positioning

Yongxin Co. is not a company that gets much attention. It does not have Moutai's traffic or PDD's controversy. But if you screen A-shares for "20 consecutive years of dividends + 5% dividend yield + ROE above 15% for a long time," it will always show up on the list.

One interesting contradiction is:

- The dividend camp says: cumulative payout ratio **77.77%**, dividend yield **4.7%**, 20 consecutive years of dividends - this is one of the best "bond-like" stocks in A-shares
- The growth camp says: the soft-packaging industry has only **6% CR5**, gross margin is **23%**, and 2025 revenue grew without profit growth - this is a mediocre business

**Both sides are right. The question is: if a mediocre business is cheap enough and shares cash generously enough, is it still worth buying?**

This series aims to **first explain what it is - business model, financial quality, industry structure, risks, and valuation - and then let you decide**.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Do not invent data** - all numbers are sourced, and estimates are clearly marked as "estimated"
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
