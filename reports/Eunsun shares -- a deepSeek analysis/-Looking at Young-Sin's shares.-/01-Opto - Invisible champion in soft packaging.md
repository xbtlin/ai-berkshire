## A company you come into contact with every day but never notice

Among the more than 5,000 listed companies in the A-share market, you can clearly count on your fingers those that can achieve "dividend distribution for 20 consecutive years, a dividend rate close to 5%, and a long-term ROE higher than 15%." Yongxin Shares (002014.SZ) is one of them - but most investors have never heard of its name.

This company was established in 1992 and is headquartered in Huangshan, Anhui. Its main business is **color-printed composite flexible packaging materials** and **plastic flexible packaging films**—that is, making packaging bags for food, daily chemical, and pharmaceutical companies. The potato chip bag you ate today, the milk package you drank, and the laundry detergent outer bag you used were probably made by it. It doesn't sound sexy at all. But hidden in the financial statements is a set of numbers that completely do not match the reputation.

## What exactly is Yongxin Shares?

One sentence definition:

> **Yongxin Shares = a midstream packaging manufacturer with excellent financial discipline + one of the most generous dividend payers in A-shares + a business with a small moat but stable profitability. **

Looking at it more broadly, there are three things worth noting.

### The first thing: product structure

Yongxin’s revenue comes from four product lines (2025 annual report data, source: Securities Star):

| Products | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Proportion | Year-on-year growth | Gross profit margin |
|------|-----------|------|---------|-------|
| Color printing composite packaging | **25.5** | **68.5%** | +2.95% | **25.42%** |
| Plastic flexible packaging film | **8.3** | **22.3%** | **+14.7%** | 14.83% |
| Ink | 1.73 | 4.6% | +11.29% | — |
| Aluminized packaging | 0.62 | 1.7% | +5.64% | — |

The core business is **color printing composite packaging** (accounting for nearly 70%), and the fastest growing is **plastic flexible packaging film** (+14.7% year-on-year). Although the gross profit margin of the film business is low (14.83%), the growth rate is significant and is becoming the company's second growth curve - the biaxially stretched multifunctional film project with an annual output of 30,000 tons is expected to be put into operation in 2026.

**Contrary view:** The gross profit margin of the film business is less than 15%, which is far lower than the 25% of color printing. If the increment comes mainly from low-margin businesses, overall profitability may continue to be diluted. The decline in consolidated gross profit margin from 23.78% to 23.00% in 2025 is already happening.

### The second thing: customer structure

Yongxin’s downstream customers are brand owners in the food, daily chemical, and pharmaceutical industries—which means two characteristics:

1. **Stable Demand**: People need to eat, wash clothes, and take medicine. Packaging demand is linked to consumption frequency, and the periodicity is weak
2. **Strong Customers**: The bargaining power of big brands (think Unilever, P&G, Yili) is much stronger than that of packaging suppliers

The top five customers are estimated to account for **25%-35%** of revenue (the company has not disclosed the complete customer list, this is a brokerage estimate). The customer concentration is not extremely high, but the relationship with major customers is stable - changing packaging suppliers requires re-certification, debugging, and trial production, which takes about 3-6 months.

### The third thing: geographical structure

In 2025, Yongxin’s domestic sales will be **3.05 billion** (+3.09%), and its export sales will be **560 million** (**+24.04%**). The proportion of export sales increased from 12.81% to **15.05%**.

Outbound sales are part of the incremental story. However, it should also be noted that the rapid growth of export sales has partly benefited from the RMB exchange rate factor, and its sustainability remains to be seen.

## What happens in 2025: Increase revenue but not profit

2025 is a year to watch - Yongxin's profits fell for the first time.

| Indicators | 2024 | 2025 | Changes |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Revenue | 3.525 billion | 3.723 billion | +5.61% |
| Net profit | 468 million | **438 million** | **-6.33%** |
| Excluding non-net profit | 440 million | **409 million** | **-7.17%** |
| Gross profit margin | 23.78% | **23.00%** | -0.78pct |
| Net profit margin | 13.37% | **11.87%** | -1.50pct |
| Operating cash flow | 459 million | **343 million** | **-25.3%** |
| ROE | 19.18% | **17.34%** | -1.84pct |

*Data source: Yongxin Shares 2024 and 2025 annual reports*

**Revenue is growing, profits are declining, and cash flow is deteriorating. ** These three signals appearing at the same time usually mean one thing: the company lacks pricing power and the rising costs cannot be passed on to customers.

However, there was a slight improvement in Q1 of 2026: revenue of 940 million (+10.81%) and net profit of 91 million (+2.06%), which is a signal of stabilization, but the net interest rate further dropped to 9.78%, and the trend still needs to be observed.

**Contrary view:** The profit decline in 2025 may be a short-term fluctuation (raw material price increase cycle) and does not represent a long-term trend. With a net profit CAGR of about 8.5% over the past five years, a one-year correction should not be read too much into.

## To understand Yongxin, you need to answer three questions

Based on the above information, Yongxin shares can be summarized by three questions:

**Question 1: Where is the ceiling of this business? **
The flexible packaging industry is large (100 billion level), but Yongxin’s market share is only about **2%** (estimated based on the caliber of color printing composite packaging). The low market share indicates that growth space theoretically exists; however, the industry is extremely fragmented (CR5 is only 6%), which also indicates that the concentration rate is increasing slowly.

**Question 2: Is a 5% dividend sustainable? **
It has paid dividends for 20 consecutive years, with a cumulative dividend rate of 78% - this record is very rare in A-shares. However, although the dividend rate in 2025 is still 76.89%, the amount of dividends has dropped from 380 million to 337 million (due to a decline in profits). If profits continue to decline, the absolute amount of dividends will also shrink.

**Question 3: Is the new management reliable? **
In March 2025, Yongxin completed the management change - the new chairman **Jiang Lei** (born in 1987, worked in Lego, Disney, and General Mills, and according to public information is the daughter of the original chairman) and the new general manager **Pan Jian** (senior engineer, State Council special allowance expert, internal promotion). The chairman + general manager were changed at the same time, and the new chairman has no experience in the packaging industry - this is one of the biggest uncertainties at the moment.

These three questions will be discussed one by one in the next three articles.
