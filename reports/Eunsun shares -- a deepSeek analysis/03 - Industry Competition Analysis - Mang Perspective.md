### 1.2 Industry growth drivers and suppression factors

**Forward drive:**
- Consumption upgrade: Improved quality of food, daily chemicals, and medicine drives demand for packaging
- E-commerce driven: Express logistics packaging demand continues to grow
- Environmentally friendly substitution: plastic partially replaces traditional materials

**Reverse Suppression:**
- Environmental pressure: Plastic restriction orders suppress traditional plastic packaging
- Overcapacity: Overcapacity in low-end packaging and fierce price competition
- Consumption downgrade trend: there is demand for packaging cost reduction in some areas

**Growth Rating: ★★★☆☆** - The industry has entered a mature stage, and structural opportunities are greater than total opportunities.

**Advantages:**
1. Competitive advantages in the color printing composite packaging segment
2. Vacuum aluminum plating film has high technical barriers and large profit contribution
3. Establish long-term cooperative relationships with some well-known brands

**Disadvantages:**

## 3. Subdivided track competition pattern

### 3.1 Color printing composite packaging market
- Highly decentralized, with many regional small businesses
- Competition is dominated by mid- to low-end prices
- Yongxin is positioned as a mid-to-high-end company and has regional advantages in East China.

### 3.2 Vacuum aluminum coating market
- High technical barriers (large equipment investment, high process requirements)
- Relatively few participants and high profit margins
- Yongxin entered earlier and has deeper technology accumulation (estimated to account for 30-40% of total profits)

## 4. Industry Trends and Subversion Risks

### 4.1 Technological change trends
| Trends | Impact on Yongxin |
|------|------------|
| Green packaging (degradable, recyclable) | Requires technological transformation and has cost pressure |
| Smart packaging (RFID, etc.) | New opportunities but new capabilities required |
| Functionalization (high barrier, freshness preservation) | Conducive to technology-accumulating enterprises |

### 4.2 Impact of policy environment
- **Positive:** Improvement of environmental protection standards and elimination of backward production capacity will benefit compliance leaders
- **Negative:** Plastic restriction orders suppress traditional plastic packaging

### 4.3 Threat of new entrants
- Low-end market: low threshold and fierce competition
- High-end market: requires customer certification + technology accumulation, high barriers

**Munger warns:** "When the barriers to entry in an industry are low and the barriers to exit are high, competition often turns into a price war."

## 5. Industrial chain value distribution

### Value chain analysis

```
Upstream raw materials (plastic particles/ink/aluminum foil)
      ↓ Weak bargaining power (commodity pricing)
Midstream manufacturing (packaging companies such as Yongxin Co., Ltd.)
  Gross profit margin is 22-25%, net profit margin is 11-13%
      ↓ Weak bargaining power (strong customers)
Downstream brands (food/daily chemicals/pharmaceuticals)
  Gross profit margin 40-60%, high brand premium
```

**Core findings:** The packaging manufacturing industry is at the low point of the smile curve, both upstream and downstream are strong, and midstream profits are being squeezed.

## 6. Manga style reverse thinking

**1. "If Yongxin increases its price by 10%, will customers leave?"**
- Ordinary products: Yes, competition is fierce
- High-end products: some will, the technical threshold is not irreplaceable
- **Conclusion: Weak pricing power**

**2. "Will the competitive landscape of the packaging industry improve in five years?"**
- May be slightly improved (environmental protection policies accelerate the elimination of small businesses)
- But it will not fundamentally change (the expansion of large companies will also intensify competition)
- **Conclusion: There will be no substantial improvement**

**3. "What technology may disrupt the packaging industry?"**
- Biodegradable materials, smart packaging, and circular packaging models
- Large-scale replacement will still take 5-10 years
- **Conclusion: The risk of disruption exists but is not immediate**
