# The AI Five-Layer Cake: Screening 50 Bottleneck Companies

> Screening date: June 5, 2026
> Screening criteria: bottleneck purity x irreplaceability x market pricing x catalyst window (1-5 stars each)
> Goal: identify overlooked investment candidates that control critical bottlenecks

---

## Screening Criteria

| Dimension | Description | Ideal State |
|------|------|----------|
| Bottleneck purity | Share of revenue tied to the bottleneck business | >30%; higher is better |
| Irreplaceability | Sole supplier or oligopoly supplier | Alternatives are more than 2 years away |
| Market pricing | Degree to which the stock price reflects the bottleneck thesis | Higher score = more underpriced |
| Catalyst window | Near-term validation events | Catalyst within 6 months |
