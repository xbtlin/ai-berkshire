# Three common misconceptions about Nord and Nord

# Misunderstand one: No and Nord are the Fatty Corporation

**67%** Income continues to come from diabetes treatment.

More precisely defined:** No and Nord are a chronic disease management company with a core platform of GLP-1 molecules covering multiple adaptive conditions such as diabetes mellitus + obesity + cardiovascular.** Smegrupum not only reduced weight, but also expanded in the areas of cardiovascular protection (SELECT tests have shown that there is a reduction of risk of heart accidents by 20%), kidney protection, and fat liver (NASH/MASH).

This means that even if the rate of increase in diets slows, the "platformization" value of Smeglupe will be released.

# Misunderstand two: No and Nord monopolize the market for diet drugs

** 2024 may be so, 2026 is no longer so.**

Eli Lily's share of the market in the United States GLP-1 has reached **-60%** (source: 24/7 Wall St, May 2026). More alarmingly, the market outside the United States has also surpassed Nord and Nord (source: GxP News, May 2026).

Nord and Nord are changing from ** Monopoly** to **Old One. This change of identity is one of the core reasons for the PE's contraction from 24 to 10 times.

# Misunderstand three: PE 10 times #

**10 times PE is not a coincidence, it's a market price for the "growth cliff".**

In February 2026, Nord and Nord issued year-round guidelines that shocked the market: adjusted income and operating profits are projected to fall by 4-12 per cent** (fixed exchange rate, small increase after Q1 financial statements). This is the first negative growth guidance that companies have given over the years.

PE is 24 to 10 times the market's implied assumption:
- Growth engine permanently slows down.
- Systematic erosion of pricing rights
- The product of Reed is invincible.

** Whether this assumption is correct or not is the core issue to be answered throughout the series.**
