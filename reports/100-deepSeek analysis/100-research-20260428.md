#8. Summary

100 degrees is the most complex investment proposition of the Chinese Internet industry - not because it is hard to understand, but because it is at the top of the uncertainty that turns "old me" and "new me".

** Good point**: $33 billion in net cash provides a solid hard floor; the entire estate of AI is unique in China (Quinlan cyborg, Quint, Quint, Quint, Quint, Quasi, Quasi, Apollo); the global leadership of autopilot is seriously underestimated by the market. If AI is successful in its transformation, the current valuation will be extremely cheap.

** Bad aspects**: Core search advertisements are being irreversibly mutilated by multiple forces of the AI era; free cash flow negative in 2025 means that companies are in transition with cash reserves; and the record of management history of "seeing the future but not arriving" is unconscionable.

** Core paradox**: The realization of the 100-degree largest moat (a full-storey AI technology) depends precisely on destroying its largest cash cow (search ads). This is a time-bound race - AI's new engine must be able to escape before the old engine is out. So far, the old engine has slowed (-14%) faster than the new engine (+48% base is too small).

Manger says, "Call it, always do it." "Indicate the biggest risk is not that AI failed -- AI's ability is real. The biggest risk is:** Even if AI's technology succeeds, profits can be eroded by competition and commercialization difficulties, and eventually "win the technology, lose the investment."**

** Final recommendation: wait and see. Two things will change this judgment -- the FCF or the mind-a-many assistant MAU has broken 50 million. Before that, 100 degrees is a bottom-up option, not a value-fixed investment.**
