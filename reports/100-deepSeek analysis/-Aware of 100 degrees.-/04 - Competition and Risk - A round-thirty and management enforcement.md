- Big-minded model. * C-end entrance to bean bag.

** There are so many companies in history that "the most technologically powerful but the worst invest" ** -- Xerox invented the graphical interface but was taken away by apples, Nokia has the best cell phone technology but was turned over by iPhone, Yahoo was the first search engine, but replaced by Google.

** Does 100 degrees become "Adolescent Tiger of the AI era" -- the first and most technologically advanced, but the fruits are removed by the later?** The question has no answer, but it is worth every investor's serious thinking.
