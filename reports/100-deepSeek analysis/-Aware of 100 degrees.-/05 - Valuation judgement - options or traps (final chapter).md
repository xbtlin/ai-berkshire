# Three key catalysts

What event could possibly bring 100 degrees of valuation up the stairs?

Catalyst one: free cash flow is positive

The FCF-15.1 billion in 2025 was the biggest drag on the 100 degree valuation. If the FCF were to be positive in 2026, it would mean:
- "Cash consumption" reversion.
- $5 billion buyback becomes real shareholder return (rather than consumption of reserves)
- Markets are willing to revalue their operations

**Observation indicator**: 2 consecutive quarterly operating cash flows positive. 2026Q1 Core net profit of $7.6 billion (+48%) is a good sign, but needs to be continuously validated.

Catalyst two: AI over 50%

The revenue share of 2025Q4 AI has reached **43%**, with the current rate of increase, 50% expected to be reached in 2026. Once this line is crossed, 100% will change from "search advertising company" to "AI company" -- the valuation logic will change fundamentally.

** Inverse view**: "Assumption of 50% is not a profit. If AI's income is far lower in Maori than in search of advertising, the higher in profits. What needs to be seen is the profit ratio of AI's business, not the income ratio."

# Three down/out-of-the-box signals

What circumstances should be reassessed?

Signal one: search ads are down and up

If the income from the online marketing has fallen by more than **20% in the same season ** (a full year of 2025 - 14%), this means that the old engine is going to be out of fire at a much faster rate than expected, and the "Yung Yee doesn't pick up" window will be prolonged.

Signal two: AI, cloud speeds are dropping

If 100 degrees AI cloud seasons are reduced from 34% to single digits, it means that the pursuit of the volcano engine is effective and 100 degrees B is not strong.

Signal three: another ability to take risks outside the circle

If management starts new large non-AI investments (like O2O of the year, extreme Viet Nam), this means that there is no improvement in the discipline of capital allocation — the most dangerous signal.

# What does it mean to different investors?

100 degrees is not a stock for everyone. Depending on your risk preferences:

# Radical investors #

**Attitude**: May be held as AI options.

** Logic**: $107-110 hard base to provide security mats (currently $141 is only 28% high), and any one of the three AI tracks means significant upswing. $5 billion buy back + KNKN-BIPO is a recent catalyst.

**Risk**: If the FCF continues to be negative, the security pads shrink. This is not a "buy and sleep" stock.

# A steady investor

** Attitude**: Watching, waiting for two signals.

**Logical**: The FCF returns over 50% after 2 consecutive seasons. Until these two conditions are met, the 100 degrees valuation is anchored in a precarious anchor. "Limit" does not equal "safe" - a combination of negative FCF+ profit breakbacks that do not meet traditional value investment criteria.

# Conservative investors

**Assumption**: Avoidance.

**Logical**: Pre-Buffett Checklelist pass rate **1.5/10**. ROE only 7.2%, FCF negative, profit unpredictable, and questionable management performance - does not match any of the "good company, good price, good management" items. Even if PB 1.16x looks cheap, the combination of "low PB+ negative FCF+ recession homeowner" is more a value trap than an undervalued one in history.
