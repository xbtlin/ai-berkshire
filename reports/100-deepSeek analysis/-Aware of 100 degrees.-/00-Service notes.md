> 5 main articles that break down the company where the old engine is stalling and the new engine is firing.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Baidu is one of the hardest companies in Chinese internet to price - not because there is too little information, but because the "old Baidu" and the "new Baidu" coexist and point in completely opposite directions.

- The bulls say: **RMB 239.4 billion of net cash** equals more than 70% of market cap, Baidu is the only company in China with full-stack AI capabilities, Kunlun Chip is about to IPO, and Apollo Go is the world's largest robotaxi player - absurdly cheap
- The bears say: search ads are in irreversible decline, free cash flow turned negative in 2025, the consumer AI entry point has already been beaten by Doubao, and management "can see the future but cannot reach it" - cheap for a reason

**One side is looking at the future, the other at the past.** That is the root of the disagreement.

This series aims to **explain the old Baidu and the new Baidu separately, and then let you decide which one matters more** - not to hand you the conclusion.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - many key Baidu metrics (such as AI Cloud gross margin and Apollo Go full P&L) are not publicly disclosed, so this series only uses the most reliable sources and does not force in fake precision
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
