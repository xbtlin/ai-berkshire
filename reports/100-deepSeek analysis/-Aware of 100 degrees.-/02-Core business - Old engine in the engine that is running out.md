#Search Advertising: An old engine that’s dying

> "Understanding Baidu" Series·Part 02
> Reading time is about 10 minutes

---

## Baidu’s core cash cow: online marketing

**63 billion revenue, declining for 6 consecutive quarters, down 14% in 2025. This is the current situation of Baidu’s most important cash cow. **

The logic of Baidu making money is very simple: user searches → Baidu display ads → advertisers pay per click (CPC) → Baidu makes money. This model has supported Baidu’s profits for the past 20 years, but it is visibly coming to an end.

In 2025, online marketing (search advertising) still contributed **~62%** of Baidu's core revenue (about 63 billion yuan). Look at the data:

| Year | Online marketing revenue (100 million yuan) | Year-on-year | Share of core revenue |
|------|-------------------|------|-----------|
| 2023 | ~750 | +8% | ~75% |
| 2024 | 730 | -3% | ~70% |
| 2025 | ~630 | **-14%** | ~62% |
| 2026Q1 | 160 | **-6%** | ~63% |

> Data sources: Baidu annual report, Xinhuanet, Wall Street News.

**Six consecutive quarters of decline, with deterioration accelerating throughout 2025 (-14%). ** The decline narrowed to -6% in 2026Q1, but it is unclear whether it is an improving trend or a seasonal fluctuation.

## Search market share: from 87% to 56%

Baidu Search’s decline didn’t start with AI—AI just accelerated the process.

According to Statista data:

| Time | Baidu search market share |
|------|-------------|
| November 2021 | **86.82%** |
| December 2023 | ~65% |
| May 2024 | **55.85%** |
| 2025 (mobile) | ~65% (mobile still strong) |
| 2025 (PC) | **~30%** (lost PC supremacy) |

> Data source: Statista, Shousha.com.

**31 percentage points of market share evaporated in 3 years. ** This is not a cyclical fluctuation, but a structural migration.

Where did the user go?

| Competitors | Search related indicators | Threats to Baidu |
|--------|------------|------------|
| Douyin | Daily average search volume **5.3 billion** (mobile search share is about 70%) | **The deadliest** - taking away the "random search" scene |
| Xiaohongshu | Average daily searches **600 million** (1/3 of 300 million monthly actives are home page searches) | Stealing the search for "Strategies/Consumption Decisions" |
| WeChat search | Monthly activity **800 million+** (connected to Yuanbao AI) | Taken away the "search within the ecosystem" |
| DeepSeek | Monthly active users **127 million** | Stealing the "Deep Question and Answer" scene |
| Doubao (bytes) | Monthly activity **345 million** | Stealing the "AI assistant" scene |

> Data sources: QuestMobile (March 2026), TechNode, Zhihu.

**Contrary view**: "Baidu search still has 679 million monthly active users, and the mobile terminal still has 65% of the market share. It has not reached the point of 'death'. Search is a rigid need and will not disappear. It is just dispersed."

**Positive opinion**: "The frequency and duration of user searches on Baidu are declining. The market share is 65% but the average daily search volume is 300 million times - the search volume of Douyin is **17 times that of Baidu. The habit of 'open Baidu and search' is being replaced by 'open Douyin and search', especially among young users."

## Advertising monetization efficiency: both volume and price fall

Search advertising revenue = search volume × ad loading rate × price per click (CPC).

Baidu’s problem is not just that the search volume is decreasing, but that three variables are deteriorating at the same time:

**1. Search volume dropped**

Douyin + Xiaohongshu + WeChat Souyisou diverted a large amount of search demand, and Baidu’s average daily search volume dropped from **6 billion+** at the peak to about **300 million** (Source: Technode, Zhihu).

**2. Ad loading rate is under pressure**

The results of AI search (such as Wenxinyiyan integrated into Baidu search) are more accurate and direct - users do not need to click on links after getting the answer. This means **fewer opportunities for ad impressions**. Baidu does its own AI search, but instead compresses its advertising space.

**3. CPC decrease**

Advertiser budgets are migrating to Douyin and Xiaohongshu. In the second quarter of 2025, Baidu's online marketing revenue fell **15%** year-on-year, the largest decline among all major advertising platforms.

**This is a concrete manifestation of the "fight between left and right": the better AI search does, the more damaged the traditional advertising model will be. ** This isn't a problem unique to Baidu - Google faces the same predicament - but Baidu's alternative revenue (AI cloud, subscriptions) is far less diversified than Google's.

## Baidu APP Ecosystem: The true value of 679 million monthly active users

Baidu APP has **679 million monthly active users** - this number seems huge, ranking third in the Chinese Internet echelon after WeChat and Douyin.

But "monthly activity" and "user value" are two different things:

| Platform | Monthly active users | Single user annual income (estimated) | User stickiness |
|------|------|------------------|---------|
| WeChat | 1.3 billion+ | ~200 yuan | Extremely high (social binding) |
| Douyin | 750 million+ | ~400 yuan | Extremely high (content addiction) |
| Baidu APP | 679 million | **~93 yuan** (63 billion/679 million) | **Medium-low** (search and leave) |

> Note: Baidu’s annual income per user is a rough estimate of online marketing income/monthly activity.

**The problem with Baidu APP is not that there are few users, but that users "go away after searching"** - short stay time, weak content consumption, and almost zero e-commerce conversions. Of the 679 million monthly active users, only **4.79 million** (0.7%) are using Wenxin Assistant - which means Baidu cannot even convert its own search users into AI users.

**Opposite view**: "The conversion rate of 0.7% means huge room for growth. If Baidu can fully AI-ify the search experience (instead of opening a separate APP), the conversion potential of 679 million users will be far greater than any competing product."

**Opinion from the positive side**: "The problem is that the user profile of Baidu APP is biased towards 'search tool people' - they come to search, not to 'use AI'. The 345 million users of Doubao are users who actively seek AI dialogue, and the user intentions are completely different. There is a product power gap between 'having users' and 'being able to convert'."

## How much of the moat is left?

The traditional moat for search advertising has three layers:

| Moat level | Status | Judgment |
|-----------|------|------|
| User habits ("If you have any questions, search on Baidu") | **Serious erosion** | Young users have turned to Douyin/Xiaohongshu search |
| Advertiser budget inertia | **Moderate erosion** | Large customers are cutting Baidu budget, but small and medium-sized businesses still rely on it |
| Search technical barriers | **Partially valid** | AI search capabilities are still strong (Wenxin 5.1 ranks first in domestic search), but technical barriers are decreasing |

**Comprehensive judgment: Baidu's search moat has dropped from "very deep" to "shallow" - not nothing, but not enough to prevent revenue from continuing to decline. **

There’s a critical timing issue here: Search advertising is down 14% annually, and new AI business is growing 48% annually—but AI’s base (40 billion) is only 63% of search advertising (63 billion). At current rates, AI revenue won’t fully make up for lost search advertising until 2027-2028. **

The intervening 1-2 years are Baidu's most dangerous "gone-and-gone" window period.

## Summary of this article

| Dimensions | Conclusion |
|------|------|
| Search advertising revenue | 6 consecutive quarters of decline, -14% in 2025, structural rather than cyclical |
| Search market share | dropped from 87% to 56%, mobile still accounts for 65% but the daily search volume is much lower than Douyin |
| Advertising monetization efficiency | Both volume and price have fallen, but AI search has compressed advertising space |
| User value | 679 million monthly active users but the annual income of a single user is only ~93 yuan, low stickiness |
| Moat | From "very deep" to "shallow", the inertia of small and medium-sized businesses is the last line of defense |

**Bottom line: Baidu's old engine isn't stalling, but it's slowing down at a rate of 14% per year. The question is not whether it will stop, but whether the new engine can run before it stops. **
