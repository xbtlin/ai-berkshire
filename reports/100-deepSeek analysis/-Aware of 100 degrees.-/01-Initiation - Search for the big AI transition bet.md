## A set of numbers that will let you re-understand Baidu

**A company with net cash of 239.4 billion yuan accounting for 70% of the market value, but free cash flow has just turned negative and profits have plummeted 76% - it is both "the cheapest AI company in the world" and may also be a value trap that is slowly bleeding out. **This is Baidu in 2026.

In the past five years, Baidu’s stock price has followed a confusing curve:

- February 2021: **$354** All-time high - the climax of the "AI first stock" narrative
- October 2022: **$75** - Panic selling of Chinese concept stocks
- April 2024: **$117** - Wen Xin Yi Yan Narrative's Brief Recovery
- September 2025: **$81** - Profit cliff + free cash flow turns negative, market confidence hits freezing point
- May 2026: **~$141** - $5 billion buyback + first dividend + Wenxin 5.0 released, sentiment picks up

In **5 years, Baidu has gone from being an "AI leader" to being a "laggard in the AI era" and has started to crawl back again. ** But every narrative shift almost seems an overreaction in hindsight.

Opening the 2025 annual report, Baidu has a set of counterintuitive indicators:

| Indicators | 2025 | Magnitude Judgment |
|------|---------|---------|
| Total revenue | **129.1 billion** (-3%) | First full-year decline since listing |
| Net attributable profit | **5.6 billion** (**-76%**) | Cliff-like decline |
| Non-GAAP net profit | **18.9 billion** (-30%) | Still making money after impairments |
| Free cash flow | **-15.1 billion yuan** | **Turn negative for the first time in history** |
| Net cash | **239.4 billion yuan (approximately $33 billion)** | **Accounts for approximately 70% of current market capitalization** |
| AI new business revenue | **~40 billion yuan** (+48%) | Accounting for 31% of total revenue, Q4 reached 43% |
| Non-GAAP PE | **~16x** | Doesn’t seem expensive |
| PB | **1.16x** | Close to "liquidation price" |
| Online marketing revenue | **~63 billion yuan** (**-14%**) | Core cash cows are shrinking at an accelerated pace |

Putting this set of numbers together - **A company with $239.4 billion in net cash (70% of market capitalization) and a PB of only 1.16x has a core advertising business that is down 14% annually, free cash flow that just turned negative, but new AI business that is growing at a rate of 48%. **

If you see "PB 1.16x" and think it's cheap - you're probably only seeing half of it.
If you see "Profit -76%" you're screwed - you're probably only seeing half of it.

**What this series wants to do is to put the two halves together so that you can see the complete Baidu. **

## What exactly is Baidu?

Before starting to dismantle it, let’s answer a basic question: What kind of company will Baidu be in 2026?

Many people's understanding still stops at "search engine company". This was true before 2020 and is no longer accurate.

Look at the revenue structure in 2025:

| Business segment | Revenue (100 million yuan, estimated) | Proportion | Year-on-year | Nature |
|---------|---------------|------|------|------|
| Online Marketing (Search Advertising) | ~630 | 49% | **-14%** | Old engine, dying |
| AI Cloud + Infrastructure | ~200 | 15% | **+34%** | One of the new engines |
| AI Application + AI Native Marketing | ~200 | 15% | **+70%+** | New Engine 2 |
| iQIYI | ~270 | 21% | ~Flat | Non-core, but contributes to cash flow |

> Note: AI segment revenue is an estimate, and Baidu’s annual report does not fully disclose each sub-category. Source: Baidu Annual Report, Wall Street Insights, 36 Krypton.

**Baidu in 2025 = a search advertising machine that is stalling (49%) + a set of new AI engines that are igniting (30%) + an existing video business (21%). **

To be more precise, Baidu is undergoing a "life change" - not a "transformation".

What's the difference?

- **Tencent's AI is "empowerment"**: AI makes WeChat more usable and games more profitable - old business and new technology are in the same direction
- **Baidu's AI is "subversive"**: AI search reduces advertising placements - the better the AI does, the harder it is to sell ads

**This is Baidu's unique "left-right fight" dilemma: the ignition of the new engine just accelerates the stalling of the old engine. **

## Three core contradictions about Baidu

### Contradiction 1: The strongest technology vs the weakest realization

Baidu’s accumulation of AI technology is indeed the first echelon among Chinese Internet companies:

- **Kunlun Core**: ranks second in domestic AI chip shipments (after Huawei Ascend), with 60,000 P800 shipments and over 200,000 orders, with a valuation of **21 billion yuan** and is sprinting towards the "A+H" listing
- **Flying Paddle**: The deep learning framework with the largest market share in China
- **Wenxin Big Model**: 5.0 official version parameters **2.4 trillion**, the pre-training cost of version 5.1 is only 6% of the industry, and the search capability ranks top in the country
- **Apollo Go**: No. 1 in global Robotaxi order volume (**20 million+ orders**), entering London in 2026

But what about liquidity?

| Comparison | Baidu AI revenue (2025) | Byte AI related revenue (estimate) | Alibaba Cloud revenue (2025) |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Scale | ~40 billion | Far more than Baidu | ~110 billion+ |
| AI cloud share | **6.1%** (Omdia) | 14.8% (Volcano Engine) | **36%** (first) |

**Contrary view**: Strong technology does not mean strong business. In the field of AI, monetization depends on distribution channels and user scale, not model parameters. Byte has 700 million daily active users, Tencent has 1.3 billion WeChat users, what does Baidu have? Only **4.79 million** out of 679 million monthly active users are using Wenxin Assistant - a conversion rate of **0.7%**.

**Response from the positive side**: The loss of the C-side entrance does not mean that the B-side entrance has lost. Baidu AI Cloud has ranked first in both large model bidding and bidding for two consecutive years, serving **65% of central enterprises** and all systemically important banks. The B-side does not look at MAU, but at technical depth and delivery capabilities.

### Contradiction 2: Too much cash vs. Spending money too quickly

Baidu has **239.4 billion yuan in net cash** on its books, accounting for about 70% of the current market value. Also announced for the first time in February 2026 is a **$5 billion buyback + first dividend**.

Looks safe? But look at the other side:

- Free cash flow in 2025 **-15.1 billion yuan** - turning negative for the first time in history
- Capital expenditures increased **+49%** year-on-year to 12.1 billion - AI infrastructure cash burn accelerated
- Operating cash flow **-3 billion** - not only investment is burning money, but the operation itself is also losing money

**At the current burn rate, if free cash flow continues to be negative, 239.4 billion in net cash will shrink significantly in 2-3 years. **

**Contrary view**: US$5 billion in buybacks sounds generous, but if 15 billion+ cash is burned every year, buybacks are just the opposite of the right hand. The real safety cushion is not "how much money you have in your account", but "how much money you can make every year."

**Positive response**: The negative cash flow in 2025 has one-time factors (16.2 billion asset impairment, Apollo and cloud infrastructure intensive investment period). 2026Q1 has already recovered - revenue is 32.5 billion, core net profit is 7.6 billion, +48% year-on-year. Single-year data should not be used for linear extrapolation.

### Contradiction 3: Extremely low valuation vs extremely low certainty

Baidu PB **1.16x**, Non-GAAP PE **~16x**. If net cash is deducted, Baidu's "business assets" market value is only about **100 billion** - a company with annual revenue of 129.1 billion, the market only gives less than 1 times PS.

What does this valuation mean? **The market is basically saying: Baidu's search advertising business will continue to shrink, and it is uncertain whether the new AI business can make money, so I am only willing to pay net cash. **

| Valuation indicators | Baidu | Tencent | Alibaba |
|---------|------|------|------|
| PB | **1.16x** | ~4.5x | ~1.8x |
| PE（Non-GAAP） | ~16x | ~18x | ~12x |
| Net Cash/Market Cap | **~70%** | ~10% | ~30% |

**Contrary view**: Low PB does not mean cheap. If the core business continues to lose money and burn cash, the net assets themselves will shrink. The combination of "low PB + negative FCF" is called a "value trap" in value investing, not "undervalued".

**Fang’s response**: Kunlun Core is about to IPO (JP Morgan predicts that its valuation will exceed **80 billion** in 2026). This alone may release 25%+ of Baidu’s market value. If Apollo Go successfully globalizes, the option value will be even greater. The market is not pricing these "visible but not yet realized" assets.

## To understand Baidu, you need to read "two Baidus" separately

If you define Baidu in 2026 in one sentence:

> **Baidu = a stalling search advertising machine + a set of new AI engines that are igniting (big model + autonomous driving + AI cloud) + 239.4 billion cash safety cushion + a chip company about to IPO. **

These four things must be looked at separately and then evaluated together. The reason why the market is so divided on Baidu is precisely because most people only look at one or two things:
