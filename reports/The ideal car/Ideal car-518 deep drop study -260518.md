The most important "pricing rights" in the Eternity section is systematically disappearing.

2. ** The moat is shallow and eroded**. The difference between "Suv+Freaker Colour Sofa in Large Space" was once the moat, but all three dimensions were levelled - the increase was no longer scarce (question/meal follow-up), the household SUV was surrounded (question line M6/M7/M9 precision point-in), and the configuration difference disappeared (industry mark-up).

3. ** Product wheels are slowing down**. The first half (product definition — product strength — user satisfaction) is still in operation, while the second half (name premium — repurchase) is broken. i6 runs are successful but lower brand tone.
