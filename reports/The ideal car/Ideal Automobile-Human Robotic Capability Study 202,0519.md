# I. Dynamic data flow architecture: ideal chip ambition

What's a dynamic data flow structure?

Datastream architecture (Dataflow Achitecture) is a processor structure that calculates implementation** ** with data availability drivers, and is fundamentally opposed to the traditional Von Neumann control stream structure.

" Dynamic" data flow refers specifically to the use of content location memory (CAM) data flow machines, allowing multiple examples of the same command to be present in the execution of the current line.

** Core advantage**: Natural high parallelism, high computing density, energy efficiency excellence, low delay, natural suitability for AI data flow mode.

** Core disadvantage**: Large programming challenges, insufficient interoperability, weak software ecology, difficulties of debugging.

# First layer: irreplaceable core condition (one missing is doomed)

**1. Intelligent/AI large model capability**

This is the essential dividing line between human robots and traditional industrial robots. Large models determine robotics' universality -- how many tasks they can do, how many environments they can adapt. More importantly, AI's ability to drive data wheels directly: stronger models: more scenes <unk> more data <unk> stronger models <unk> this is a positive feedback loop for "winner eats all."

**2. Site capacity (real pay clients)**

No data, no data, no flying wheels. Figure can deploy to BMW for 30,000 cars for 10 months, and this "scene proof" is more convincing than any technology demonstration.

**3. Adequate and sustained funding**

Human robots have long research and development cycles, high technology risks, and slow commercial returns. Tesla has $41 billion in cash, and Apptronik has $935 million in money – ticketing is a daily capital.

Not every component is self-researched, but it integrates the implementers, sensors, smart hands, batteries, etc. into high performance systems. Industry reality below 80% of the chance is a strong undervalued ability to integrate.

**6. Sports control + sensor system**

End-to-end delays of less than 30 ms and dynamic mission completion rates of more than 90% are hard targets.
