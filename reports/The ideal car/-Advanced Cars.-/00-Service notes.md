# Series Guide for "Understanding Li Auto"

> 4 main articles that break Li Auto apart layer by layer and reveal the true trajectory of a new-energy vehicle company from peak to trouble.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 60 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Li Auto is the **first profitable** company among China's new-energy vehicle startups, and also one of the **largest decliners** over the past two years.

- The bulls say: net cash of **RMB 101.2 billion** is 64% of market cap, and after cash adjustment the business is priced at only **USD 4.9 billion**, absurdly cheap
- The bears say: operating profit has turned negative, the range-extended EV dividend has disappeared, the core executive team has suffered a collapse-style exodus, and the competitive landscape is deteriorating irreversibly

**Both sides are looking at the same company: one sees a safety cushion backed by RMB 100 billion of cash, while the other sees a car company whose ability to make money is disappearing.**

This series aims to **lay out the facts first and then let you decide whether this is a "temporary low point" or a "structural decline."**

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Do not invent data** - every number must cite a source, and estimates are marked as "estimated"
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
