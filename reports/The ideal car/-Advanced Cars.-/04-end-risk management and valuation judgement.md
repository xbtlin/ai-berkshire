# It's proven

# Not yet proved

1. ** Whether pure electricity is profitable**. i6 The Māori rate is estimated to be around **15-17%** (source: industry media combined estimate), well below 22% of the peak of the incremental L series. 2026 Q1 overall Māori ratio guideline **5%**

The ideal faces a dilemma:

- ** Non-conversion of electricity**: the share of the market in the incremental range is eroded and the growth space is shrinking
- **Pull transmission**: Entering the Red Sea, where the most competitive competition is (Texra/Biadey/Mi/Phoon are all here), Māori ratio is compressed

** Inverse view**: The Māori ratio of pure electricity will increase with scale. i6 The scale effect is not yet fully demonstrated at the beginning of the climb. # Five, the ultimate judgment: a temporary downfall or a structural recession?

Back to the core issues raised in the opening of the series.

# The evidence supporting the "for the time being."

1. ** Billion of cash** provides an opportunity for 2-3 strategic adjustments
2. **i 6 months off 20,000+** attests that the team still has the ability to build the money
**NPS 79.1 First Division** indicates that brand mind has not collapsed
4. ** 2026 Full replacement in the second half of the year** Possible re-launching of the product cycle
5. **M100 Chip** shows the depth of technology
6. **One billion dollars buyback** shows management confidence

# The evidence for the structural recession #

1. ** Operating profits have shifted** and the main business is not profitable
** Free cash flows from +44.2 billion to -12.8 billion**, moving from cash cows to consumables within two years
3. **The incremental differentials disappear** and the core moats are filled
4. **Wa/Mi/Biadi will not withdraw** and the pattern of competition is deteriorating irreversibly
5. **8 core senior managers separated**, organizational capacity degradation required 2-3 years of repair
** Founder judged instability**, MEGA failed + two years in a row, not achieved

# Our judgment

** Both narratives are valid, but the data tend to be "structural, cyclical"**

Specifically:

- Product ageing is **cyclical** (changes can be addressed)
- The disappearance of incremental differences is structural** (indefensible)
- The deterioration of the pattern of competition is structural** (Wah-mi/Biadi will not withdraw)
- The high-level loss of tubes is semi-structured** (2-3 years repairable, but possibly window period missed)
- The profit decline has ** cyclical** factors (Q1 Clearing of Banks) and ** structural** factors (ASP continuing decline)

If only cyclical factors are available, the alternative can be solved; but structural factors mean that ** Even if the replacement succeeds, it is difficult to return to the 2023 profit level**.

** The most likely outcome is: ideal survives, but from "the most profitable company in the new power" to "the second-line car company that can live but not make a lot of money."**
