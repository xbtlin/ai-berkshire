# Family user orientation: branding is still in place, premium is disappearing

# What's the idea doing right

The ideal created a unique branding awareness in the new energy vehicle market in China: **Ideal = family preference**.

It's not just marketing. Ideals do a few real things:

** The seat was designed around the second row** - not the driver's car, but the whole family's car.
** In-car entertainment system** ** Back-board stand-alone screen + speaker, long-distance travel is not boring
** Space maximization** — six layouts, with a third row of much more available than competitive
** User community operations** — Family users are bound in depth by parent-child activities, home-driven driving, etc.

The result was a NPS (net recommended value) **79.1 **, industry first (source: Ideal annual automobile report). User satisfaction and verbal transmission remained strong.

# Why is brand premium disappearing

But brand perception and brand premium are two things.

Brand recognition = users know you, like you.
Brand premium = User willing to pay more for you**.

The ideal brand perception remains strong, but brand premiums are disappearing — evidence of the continuing decline in the average car price.
