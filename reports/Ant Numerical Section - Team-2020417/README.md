## Research methods

This research adopts the **Multi-Agent parallel research framework for unlisted companies**:

- **Parallel start**: 5 AI analysts conduct independent research at the same time to avoid mutual interference
- **Multi-source verification**: Cross-validation by at least 2 independent sources for each key data point
- **Confidence Annotation**: All data annotations 🟢High / 🟡Medium / 🔴Low
- **Honestly leave blank**: Missing information is clearly marked as "undisclosed" and no forced filling is required.
- **Dual Caliber Separation**: Key data such as revenue clearly distinguish management caliber vs. third-party comparable caliber
- **Benchmark-driven**: Use the actual valuation levels of 22 listed comparable companies to back-deduct reasonable valuations
