## Core Discovery

1. **Consumer staples are the "hardest hit area" for undervalued stocks with wide moats** - Nike is discounted by 54%, Campbell's is discounted by 64%, and Clorox is discounted by 44%. Tariffs and weak consumption have suppressed a number of brand moat companies.

2. **Systematic undervaluation of Chinese assets** - Alibaba is discounted by 49%, Tencent is discounted by 43%, Yili is discounted by 62%, and Luzhou Laojiao is discounted by 65%. Morningstar has given a wide moat but the market pricing is far below fair value.

3. **Enterprise software collective correction** - Microsoft is discounted by 31%, SAP is discounted by 39%, and ServiceNow is discounted by 40%. AI concerns have led to compression of the valuation of the software sector, but Morningstar believes that the switching cost moat of these companies has been enhanced by AI.

4. **Europe has overlooked opportunities** - DSM-Firmenich has a discount of 55%+, Melrose has a discount of 42%, and Edenred has a discount of 40%. These companies are not in the eyes of most people.

5. **The scarcity of wide moat standards in Japan and South Korea** - Morningstar has limited coverage, and only Hoya has confirmed that it meets the standards. SK Hynix is rated as having a narrow moat rather than a wide one
