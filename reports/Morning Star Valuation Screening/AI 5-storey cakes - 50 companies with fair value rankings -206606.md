## Core Discovery

### There are only 3 companies with Morningstar five-star ratings (seriously underestimated).

| Company | FV/Price | Morningstar Rating | Meaning |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| **Adobe** | 2.15x | ★★★★★ | Morningstar believes 54% undervalued, fair value $560 |
| **ServiceNow** | 1.68x | ★★★★★ | Morningstar believes 40% undervalued, splits $200 |
| **Synopsys** | 1.61x | ★★★★★ | Morningstar believes 38% undervalued, including Ansys acquisition value |

Including Amphenol (1.29x, ★★★★★), there are a total of 4 companies with the highest ratings given by Morningstar analysts.

### There are 7 Morningstar four-stars (moderately undervalued)

Salesforce（1.48x）、Broadcom（1.39x）、Meta（1.37x）、Nvidia（1.35x）、Arista（1.21x）、Dell（1.13x）。

### There are 6 companies with one star rating from Morningstar (seriously overestimated)

Bloom Energy（0.24x）、GE Vernova（0.56x）、Vistra（0.63x）、Corning（0.53x）、Eaton（0.39x）、Coherent（0.62x）。

### Cross-validation with our own study

| Our conclusion | Morningstar's conclusion | Consistent |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Adobe 15 times PE is undervalued | ★★★★★, FV/Price 2.15x | Highly consistent |
| Salesforce 22x PE undervalued | ★★★★, FV/Price 1.48x | Consensus |
| SK Hynix 7 times PE pricing error | FV/Price 1.54x | Consistent |
| Nvidia 26 times PE is not expensive | ★★★★, FV/Price 1.35x | Consistent |
| Reddit is the master of AI data mining | ★★, FV/Price 0.65x | **Inconsistent** – Morningstar thinks overvalued |
| Bloom Energy has the fastest growth | ★, FV/Price 0.24x | **Inconsistent** – Morningstar thinks grossly overvalued |
| GE Vernova full chain coverage | ★, FV/Price 0.56x | **Inconsistent** - Morningstar believes overvalued |

**Conclusion:** Morningstar’s most recognized undervalued targets (Adobe, ServiceNow, Salesforce, Nvidia) highly overlap with the valuation depressions of our independent research. The biggest difference lies in the energy layer - Morningstar believes that AI power concept stocks such as Bloom Energy, GE Vernova, and Vistra have been seriously overdrawn, while our bottleneck logic believes that they still have long-term value. This difference deserves attention.
