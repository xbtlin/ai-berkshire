## One sentence core judgment

**In the short term, we will not pursue infrastructure power. In the medium term, we are optimistic about the restoration of the valuations of major model manufacturers, and in the long term, we will look at the differentiation of application monetization** - based on the capital expenditure cycle of "selling shovels before using shovels". The biggest variable is whether the model layer will have equal rights. If the model layer has equal rights, the logic of the application layer will be reversed.

1. **Research coverage is highest at the "Model" and "Application" layers, while "Infrastructure Power" is blank**
   According to the current hot position (infrastructure has been ranked 80-95), it can be understood that this gap is reasonable-avoiding the overdrawn track. However, if there is a callback in infrastructure power in the next 6-12 months, a research file needs to be established quickly.

2. **Tencent + Alibaba + NVIDIA are the three most studied companies**
   - Tencent: King of the application layer (high probability of winner-take-all)
   - Alibaba: The only listed purebred in the model layer (high certainty of valuation restoration)
   - NVIDIA: Leader on the computing power track (defensive vs cyclical dilemma)

3. **Unlisted model companies are "research rather than operations"**
   Byte / DeepSeek / MINIMAX / Zhipu are all primary market targets. Currently they can only:
   - Waiting for IPO (DeepSeek has been declared on the Science and Technology Innovation Board)
