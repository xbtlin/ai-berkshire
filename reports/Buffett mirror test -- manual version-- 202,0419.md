| Optimistic | 34% | 50% | 21x -> 25x | **15.4%** |
| Neutral | 32% | 52% | 21x -> 20x | **9.8%** |
| Pessimistic | 28% | 55% | 21x -> 15x | **2.1%** |

#### Key Insight: Structural Recomposition of the Moat

Old moat weights (manual era) vs. new moat weights (AI era, inferred):

| Element | Manual Era Weight | AI Era Weight | Implication for Tencent |
|------|:----------:|:----------:|---|
| Production Quality | 40-50% | **10-15%↓** | AI flattens this gap, so Tencent loses a relative edge |
| Distribution / Traffic / Social | 30-40% | **50-60%↑** | **Tencent’s strongest moat becomes more valuable** |

1. **Toolchain integration advantage**: 100+ projects share GiiNEX / Hunyuan / ACE; competitors (MiHoYo self-developing by department, NetEase’s labs operating independently) do not have this scale efficiency

3. **Cash-flow resilience**: Capex of 107 billion will not crush Tencent; startups cannot keep up
4. **Social graph lock-in**: “My friends are all playing” in Honor of Kings is a moat that AI cannot replace

**“Technological democratization leads to industry fragmentation” is a repeatedly falsified prediction:**
