## Core Investment Thesis

### Why hold ADP?

1. **The business essence is excellent.** Payroll processing is like the "utilities" of enterprise operations - indispensable, zero-error-tolerance, and highly sticky. Retention is 92% and average tenure is 11 years. That makes it naturally suitable for long-term holding.

2. **Three-layer revenue structure provides resilience.** Processing fees (steady growth) + float interest (rate hedge) + value-added services (ARPU improvement) mean ADP can remain profitable in almost any macro environment. FY2025 float interest reached USD 1.2 billion, with client cash balances of USD 44.5 billion.

3. **The moat is widening rather than narrowing.** Switching costs + compliance barriers + data flywheel + global network (140+ countries) form a multi-layer moat. AI products (ADP Assist) and the Lyric HCM platform further reinforce the technology wall. The WorkForce Software acquisition filled a key workforce-management gap.

4. **Valuation is near historical lows.** 19x P/E is ADP's lowest level in about ten years (historical center around 25-30x), and the stock is down about 40% from its high. Analyst median target price is USD 272, implying about 30% upside.

5. **Shareholder returns are highly certain.** ADP has increased its dividend for 51 consecutive years, with a 10-year average growth rate of 12%. A new USD 6 billion buyback authorization was added in 2026. Current dividend yield is about 3.2%.

### Main risks

1. **Employment cycle**: a recession and layoffs directly hurt the per-employee revenue model
2. **Interest-rate decline**: float income may come under pressure
3. **Paychex-Paycor merger**: mid-market competitive pressure may rise after 2027
4. **Lyric HCM execution**: success in the enterprise HCM push still needs to be proven

## Four Perspectives

### Business Model View

> This is an almost perfect business. ADP makes money using other people's cash - USD 44.5 billion of float generates USD 1.2 billion of interest income, while processing fees are charged every month. Switching costs are very high (data migration + contract lock-in + compliance risk + organizational inertia), so customers have a hard time leaving once they choose ADP. The 92% retention rate is the best proof of the business model's superiority. The core competitive advantage of payroll processing is not technology; it is certainty - "not making mistakes."

### Valuation View

> ROIC 42%, net margin 19.8%, and 51 straight years of dividend increases sketch a precision compound machine. Current P/E of 19x is a decade low. Even using conservative DCF assumptions (6% growth, 20x exit multiple), intrinsic value is still above USD 220. Market concerns about employment cycles and lower rates are already well reflected in the stock. Buying at the current price means buying an excellent company at a reasonable price and getting a 3.2% dividend as compensation for waiting.

### Industry Competition View

> ADP's position in payroll processing is similar to Visa's position in payments - not the cheapest and not the coolest, but the most stable, safest, and widest-reaching choice. The Paychex-Paycor merger is worth watching, but integration risk is high. Workday cannot compete with ADP in payroll, and Paylocity is too small. ADP's data flywheel (more customers -> more data -> better compliance -> more customers) is being accelerated by AI. In the short term AI is an enhancer, not a replacement; long term, it needs continuous monitoring.

### Risk and Management View

> Maria Black is a competent internally developed CEO - 27 years at ADP, FY2025 delivered at or above guidance, and her AI / Lyric HCM direction is correct. The main concerns are (1) limited outside perspective and whether that leaves enough disruptive sharpness in an AI-driven era, and (2) only two and a half years in the role, so she has not been tested through a full cycle. On technology transformation, Lyric HCM has already won the company's largest enterprise customer, and ADP Assist covers eight major modules - the direction is right and early results are positive.

- Seeking steady compounding rather than short-term pops
