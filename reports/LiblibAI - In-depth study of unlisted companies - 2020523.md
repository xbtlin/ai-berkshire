# Summary of the analysis of dimensions

## Business Mode & User

1. **The business essence**: "Model Supermarket + Professional Workstream" in AI 'Creative Field, reducing the threshold for AIS visual content production by combining open/closed source models, providing cloud numeric and community ecology
2. **Flying wheel logic**: Model creator upload model <unk> Model library rich <unk> Attracting user fees <unk> Authors split <unk> More creators joined. Bilateral network effects are already in operation.
3. ** Dispositary gap**: User size is greater than Midjourney (25 million vs. 21 million), but the income gap is about 10 times higher. Core causes: lack of control over base models (value capture weak) + low market pricing in China + ample free amounts
**Expatriate bright spots**: Loveart (design Agent) ARR ~ $30 million, awarded a moscow pic.twitter.com/Sc.
5. **Cafety score**: Web effect 4/5, brand mind 3.5/5, data barrier 3.5/5, conversion cost 3/5, regulatory license 2.5/5, synthesis 3.3/5

## Finance & Valuation

1. ** Current income**: Domestic ARRs of approximately $10 million (2025E) plus Loveart of approximately $30 million, totalling approximately $40 million.
** Cost structure**: Non-self-researching of base models is a key advantage, with a Māori ratio of 55-70 per cent (between 30-40 per cent in Stability AI and 85-90 per cent in Midjourney)
3. ** Cash **: B turns 940 million, burns $8-15 million a month, runways for more than three years, short-term no survival crisis
4. ** Valuation growth**: from $15 million to $1 billion for 24 months, 67 times higher, with premiums per round and no reversal
5. ** Valuation judgement**: Reasonable interval of $800-1.3 billion after multiple cross-checking. The current valuation is primarily priced for user assets and growth expectations, and if income does not break by $50 million in 2026-2027, the valuation will face downward pressure

# Industry & Competition

1. **Industry**: AI visual content generation track is in the middle of the long term, technology is fast-changing, business models are not fixed and market concentration is low
**Maximum threat**: bytes: dream AI - 50 million users, self-researching models, free strategy, flow plus computing plus base model advantage, 64% in number
3. **Structural risk**: The primary image generation capacity of generic large models such as GPT-4o is enhanced and may squeeze the value of the meso-level platform. Currently large models still have short plates for fine control (ControlNet, LoRA, lot generation), but the medium- and long-term trends are unfavourable

**Sora exit**: OpenAI closed Sora in April 2026, AI reshuffling the video generation market

# And finally wrap it up

LiblibAI, a well-positioned and executed ASI creative platform company, has a pre-eminent advantage in the AI image generation community track in the country. The founder’s convalescence shows the top degree of resilience and strategic judgement (without the integration of the base model), and the commercialization of the overseas product Loveart is the most bright spot. However, the firm faces a triple structural risk – the ability of the model to squeeze the middle layer, the bytes, or the flow of dreams, and the conformity of the AIGC content – which has led to a lack of security margins in the current $1 billion valuation.If the intermediate value is proven to be strong, LiblibAI is expected to become a platform-level company for the AI application layer; if squeezed, it may be bought at $200-500 million.
