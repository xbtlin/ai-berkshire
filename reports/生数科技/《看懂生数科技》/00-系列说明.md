## Series Positioning

Shengshu Technology is not a company that has already been "understood" - it is the **most controversial independent startup in China's AI video-generation sector**.

- The bulls say: a top Tsinghua team, Vidu ranked No. 2 globally, Alibaba Cloud led a Series B at a valuation above **USD 2 billion**, and this is China's national team for AI video
- The bears say: ARR is only **USD 20 million** but the valuation is USD 2 billion, Kling's revenue is **25x** larger, ByteDance is spending **RMB 200 billion**, and an independent startup cannot survive for three years

**Both sides are looking at the same company, but one sees a technology pioneer and the other sees a burn machine.**

This series aims to **first lay out the facts clearly, and then let you decide whether this company is worth following** - not to decide for you.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - Shengshu is unlisted and revenue / profit data are unaudited, so this series only uses the most reliable sources
4. **Mark all estimates clearly as "estimated"** - to avoid creating false certainty
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer

---

## A Key Reminder

Shengshu Technology is **unlisted**. That means:

- All financial data comes from company disclosures or media reports and **has not been independently audited**
- Valuations are VC pricing, not market pricing, so liquidity discounts are significant
- Management has no statutory disclosure obligations

This series is based on public information, so the confidence level is naturally lower than for listed-company research. **Please separate "AI-analysis confidence" from "business-essence certainty."**
