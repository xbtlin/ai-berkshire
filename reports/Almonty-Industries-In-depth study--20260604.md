# II. Core logic: the bottleneck position of tungsten

# 2.1 What's a tungsten? Why is it important?

Tungsten, W, the highest melting point metal on Earth (3,422°C), is widely used for:

- ** Semiconductor manufacturing**: Tungsten hexafluoride (WF6) is an irreplaceable sediment gas in chip production
- **FDW**: Armour-piercing rounds, missiles, UAV critical materials
- ** Hard alloy**: cutting tools, mining drills
- **Aerospace**: components of high-temperature alloys

##2.2 WF6 - S-level bottlenecks

WF6 is a key pre-drill in semiconductor manufacturing for tungsten chemical phase deposition (CVD). Japanese WF6 producers (which account for about 25% of global supply) have informed Samsung and SF6 Hercules that their WF6 stocks could be depleted in June 2026. This bottleneck directly threatens global storage chip production.

#2.3 China Monopolies and Geo-Risk

- **China controls over 80% of global production and refining of tungsten powder**
- In January 2026, China further tightened its export control of tungsten through the Export Control List of Dual-Use Items
- ** Only 15 companies are authorized to export tungsten** and Beijing directly controls the quantity, timing and destination of exports
- ** Effective January 2027, United States defence contractors were required to procure tungsten from non-Chinese sources** (DFARS Code)

- **Customer lock-in**: SeaH 60 exclusive-sourced sales contract, Tungsten Parts Wyoming supply agreement
- **Policy barriers**: DFARS ban creates institutional demand guarantees
- **Technology threshold**: Sangdong uses proprietary mix techniques and modern water treatment systems
- ** Pre-emptive advantage**: capacity was built before the Pentagon ban came into effect

** Weaknesses:** Tungsten is a commodity, long-term prices are dominated by Chinese policy; mining assets are highly capital intensive; and single commodities are highly dependent (although molten mines provide diversification).

# Ten, conclusions and investment recommendations

# Core judgment

Almonty Industries is a high-risk, high-return "barrel investment" **. Its investment logic is based on three pillars:

** Non-alternative** - WF6 is a semiconductor-manufactured S-barrier with no alternatives
** The negative side of China's monopoly** — Sangdong is the only Western tungsten mine that can be scaled to replace Chinese supply
** Contract income locking** — 60-year packaged sale of SeaH molymine to provide US$ 234 million/year base income

If established, Almonty would become a mining company with an annual income of US$ 500 million in 2027, and the current US$ 5.5 billion market value corresponds to the forward-looking P/S about 10-11x, which is not expensive in the context of the commodity supercycle.

# But the following facts must be brought to light:

- ** The company never made a profit ** Q1 2026 just turned EBITDA.
- **Trailing valuation (P/S 159x) is at an absurd level** and all reasonableness depends on future performance
- **Sangdong has only been in production for 3 months** and it has not yet been proven that it will be able to run steadily to full capacity
- **Multi's revenues will take at least six months to start cashing.
- **Beta 2.03**, stock prices may fluctuate sharply before and after catalysts
