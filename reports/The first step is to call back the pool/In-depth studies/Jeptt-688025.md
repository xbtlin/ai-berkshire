# I. Business models and barriers to competition

## 1.1 Main battalion operations dismantled

The Jeptt, established in 2006 and based in Shenzhen, is a national high-technology and "specially skilled little giant" enterprise that focuses on the development, production and sale of lasers, laser/optic intelligence equipment and fibre-optic devices.

** Three major business plates (data for 2025):**

- ** Laser business (core industry)**: Revenue of $1,013 million was realized in 2025, an increase of 44.49%, and Māori rate of 45.70%, or about 49% of the total. Products cover the MOPA pulse fibre-optic lasers, continuous fibre-optic lasers, super-fast lasers, etc., and are widely used in the areas of consumer electronics, new energy, and semiconductors. Jeptt, the country’s first commercial bulk production company for the MUPA pulse fibre-optic lasers, broke the foreign technological monopoly.
- ** Laser/optical smart equipment operations**: 730 million yuan in 2025, an increase of 16.12%, or about 35% of the total. Products include laser modems (over 70% of the global city), camera module detection equipment, silicon crystal circle testing systems, etc., where laser modems are hidden champions of the global market.
- ** Fibre-optic device operations (the second curve of the outbreak growth)**: The revenue was $143 million in 2025, a 560.42% increase in the same year, and the Māori rate rose to 28.98%. The product was moved from a MPO connector to a higher density MMC connector, while expanding the FAU operation to include relevant products from the CPO and OCS.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Big Apple clients are risk-dependent (higher)

Changes in Apple’s procurement strategy, a slowdown in product rotation, or supplier substitution can have a significant impact on corporate revenue collection. While Apple supply chains are more sticky, apple substitutions are not uncommon in history.

##4.2 Fibre device operational uncertainty (critical risk)

The fibre optic device business grew by 560% in 2025, with a significant base effect. In 2026, management gave very optimistic expectations (which could be a tenfold increase in the year), but the business 's Māori ratio was only 28.98%, well below 45.70% of the lasers. If the business was released but the Māori rate continued to be low, there could be a "no gain" situation.

##4.3 Price competitive risk in laser industry (medium)

Although Jeptt avoided the Red Sea price of continuous lasers by differential positioning, competition in the areas of MoPA pulses and hyperfare lasers increased as national producers increased. Although IPG’s share in China’s market declined, its technology base was deep and did not preclude countervailing by price reductions.

##4.4 Industry cycle volatility risk (medium)

The company’s losses in 2023 (net profit – $47 million) are a good illustration of the impact of industrial cycle fluctuations. The industry is currently in the upper cycle, but downstream demand for consumer electronics, new energy, etc. is cyclical and requires vigilance over the top risks of the cycle.

##4.5 Valuation risk (high)

High valuations may face significant re-evaluation pressures if the one-time or half-yearly report increases less rapidly than expected in 2026. The "sale" of fibre-optic device operations may be shrinking in market-expected poor space from pre-realization to encashment.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** The biggest downside risk is the triple-killing scenario where the fibre optic device is not growing as fast as expected + apple order fluctuations + industry cycles down.** In this scenario, companies may repeat a significant fall or even a loss in 2023. The current market value of $37.5 billion may fall below $15 billion in pessimistic scenarios, corresponding to over 60% of the downside space.

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# 5.1 Core judgement

Jeptt, a highly differentiated laser enterprise, has built a unique moat in a fierce industrial competition through three core advantages: a MoPA pulse laser, a laser interrupter (global 70%+) and an apple supply chain. The outbreak of fibre-optic device operations opens the way for companies to imagine a second growth curve.

** The core investment logic is a three-wheel drive for "Stead growth of lasers + Fibre Devices Explosion + Apple Supply Chain Deepening".** If fibre optic device operations deliver the optimistic expectations of management by 2026-2027, the company is expected to reach a 5 billion-dollar cut-off from 2 billion.

** The current valuation is overvalued.** The market value of $37.5 billion corresponds to a profit of 133 times the PE in 2025, and 75 times the projected profit in 2026. This valuation already implies a very optimistic expectation of a continued breakout of fibre-optic device operations and a lack of security margins.

#5.2 Is it worth following in depth

** Conclusion: Business models are attractive and worthy of continuous tracking, but the current valuation lacks a security margin and does not warrant high-level follow-up.**

It is recommended that the observation pool be included pending the following procurement opportunities:
1. Valuation regressions due to the slowdown in the speed of operations of fibre-optic devices
2. Systemic decline due to industry cycle turnarounds
3. PE down to 50-60 times the range (based on projected profits for the year)
