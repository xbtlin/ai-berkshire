# 1.3 Array analysis

**(1) Scarce Barriers — The only domestic**

The "simple" is the most powerful competitive barrier in itself, and the only manufacturer in the country that can provide coating equipment in the front-light process. In the larger context of semiconductor national substitution, downstream crystal mills are the only option if they are to reduce their reliance on Tokyo electronics.

**(2) Technical barriers — very high threshold for precision process control**

The coating device requires that the nanoscale of photorecording be evenly coated and precise temperature controlled on the crystalline surface, any minor deviations that lead to the distortion of the photorecord and the end of the crystal circle.
- Photo-engineing film-coating techniques
- Accurate control techniques for the internal micro-environment
- High-capacity architecture design

**(3) Client authentication and line-of-line binding**

The equipment is co-ordinated with the height of the luminator - the performance of the lumino camera directly affects the image of the luminous machine. Once certified by the client and entered the production line, the replacement cost is extremely high. The core source has obtained orders from the domestic head crystalry mills, such as the Central China Town, Shanghai Wari, Yangtze storage, and the Fatty Long, and the partnership is tied in depth.

**(4) Policy barriers - Alternative strategies for national production**

In the context of the US-China technology game, the use of plastic-recording equipment as a key complement to the light-engineing process, national production substitution is strategic. Support for domestic semiconductor equipment at the policy level continues to grow.

##1.4 Product-line expansion strategy

- **Chemical cleaning equipment**: Process coverage is over 90% and has been validated and repeated by head customers, a market larger than the coating.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Technology to catch up with risk

- Tokyo electronics has a huge technological lead on the advanced process (14 nanometers below, EUV-backed) project equipment, which is difficult to access in the short term.
- If future domestic crystallized mills advance advanced processes on a large scale and the technological follow-up of the core is not timely, they may face the dilemma of a “mature-making ceiling” — a limited market for mature-scale production and a lack of advanced-processing progress.
- On the contrary, however, the main expansion of domestic crystal-crystal plants is still concentrated in the mature process of 28 nm and above, which is the strong field of core microsources.

# 4.2 Client concentration and industry cycle risk

- The main clients of the micro-core are domestic head crystal circle plants (Center Cyclops International, Yangtze Storage, Longsong Storage, Shanghai Wari, etc.), with a high concentration of customers.
- The semiconductor equipment industry is clearly cyclical, and the slowdown in 2024-2025 has already reflected the impact of the industry’s down-cycle. If the global semiconductor industry is re-dating in 2027-2028, the procurement of equipment may slow.
- The high concentration of income recognition in 2025 in four quarters (49 per cent of the year's total Q4 was), a pattern of "end-of-year surprise confirmation" that made performance forecasting more difficult.

#4.3 Risk of profitability fluctuations

- The 65 per cent decline in net profits in 2025, albeit with a phased approach, also reveals the vulnerability of companies to profitability — the constant increase in rigid costs such as R & D inputs, expansion of personnel, and the significant fluctuations in profits once the recovery rate slows down.
- Three rates are high (38 per cent of 2026Q1) and more cost-resilient, with profits highly sensitive to changes in revenue collection.

## 4.4 Risk of changing competition patterns

- Although core sources are currently the only ones in the country, the surge in investment in semiconductor equipment in China may have created new competitors.
- Tokyo electronics may increase competitiveness in the Chinese market (price reductions, accelerated delivery, etc.) to curb the process of national production substitution.
- There are already competing competitors in the area of cleaning equipment, such as those in Shanghai, and chemical cleansing operations with micro-sources will face competition.

# 4.5 Policy and geo-risk

- Semiconductor equipment is heavily affected by the US-China technology game. On the one hand, sanctions promote demand for national production as an alternative; on the other hand, the supply of core parts may be affected if companies are listed as entities.
- Policy support (government subsidies, etc.) decreased in 2025, negatively affecting profits.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** Performance recovery does not meet expectations + high valuation return double homicide risk **

Specific scenarios: If the national replacement of the past coating equipment before 2026-2027 is slower than expected (e.g. extended client certification cycle, Tokyo electronic price reduction competition), the rate of increase in revenue is maintained at 15-20 per cent rather than 23-39 per cent as anticipated by the institution, net profits are restored to 200-300 million instead of $400-80 million, and PE returns from 775 to 100 times, the share price may face 50-60 per cent downtime.
