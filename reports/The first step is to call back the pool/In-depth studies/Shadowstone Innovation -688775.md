# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Threat of competition across borders (risk level: high)

This is the biggest risk to the Shadowstone. From July 2025, the Territory officially entered the global camera market, taking over 40% of the share in the Chinese market in just two quarters.

- ** Price war**: Systemic pricing below $ 800-1,000
- ** Supply chain squeeze**: 33 core suppliers have been subjected to exclusive pressure according to the film stone
- ** Patent action**: March 2026, Prosecution of Six Patent Title Disputes in the Shadowstone in the Territory, involving core technologies such as unmanned flight control, video processing, with a focus on patent ownership by former staff members during their "one year off" period
- ** Channel crush**: The border has mature underline channels and brand appeal worldwide

During the spring of 2026, China ' s retail market brands on the sports camera line were sold at 62.9 per cent, and the Shadowstones at 28.6 per cent, while the global market still prevailed, the rate of failure in the domestic market was alarming.

# 4.2 "GoPro Time" risk (risk level: medium-high)

The market is already discussing whether the film stone is going through its "GoPro moment." GoPro was also a product starter, with the same high growth rate and the same high competition and growth bottlenecks that eventually fell from $13 billion to $400 million.

Similarities between the two:
- They're consumer hardware companies. They have limited barriers.
- All face stronger competitors.
- They've all gone through a phase of no-gaining.

The difference is:
- The barrier to the panorama of the film stone is higher than the motor camera, and the technical threshold is higher.
- Shadowstones are actively expanding into multi-grades, while GoPro has been confined to motion cameras.
- The software ecology and AI capability of the film stone is stronger than GoPro was.

**Determination:** Shadowstone presumably does not follow the extreme down path of GoPro, but the trend of continued downturns in Māori and profitability is irreversible and the downward movement of the valuation hub will be a certainty event.

##4.3 Inventory impairment risk (risk level: medium-high)

In a competitive environment where prices are falling steadily, stock impairments will directly affect profit schedules if products are sold back or forced to devalue stocks. Stock losses of $379.4 million are more than four times the net profit for the entire year 2025, and the risk of impairment cannot be ignored.

##4.4 Exchange rate and tariff risk (risk level: medium)

- Overseas income is 69 per cent, with foreign currency, such as the United States dollar, the renminbi appreciation will directly erode profits
- The overseas Māori rate has declined by 5.4 percentage points in 2025
- The uncertainty of US market tariff policy is a continuing risk
- But the company ' s export destination is more fragmented and it is more dependent on a single market

##4.5 Consumer electronic cycle risk (risk level: medium)

Panorama and motion cameras are essentially consumer-oriented electronics options, which are influenced more by macroeconomic and consumer confidence. The combined growth rate of the smart handheld video equipment market is expected to be about 15.9% for 2020-2030, but they may face the challenge of downswing consumption and weakening demand in the short term.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** Core risk scenario: increasing competition across borders + new product categories failed to expand + inventory impairment **

If the border continues to cut prices by the market share of panoramic and motion cameras, the forced follow-up of the price drops has led to a 40 per cent decline in the Maori rate; while new categories of goods, such as drones and cloud cameras, have failed to open markets and R & D inputs cannot be translated into income; and the accumulation of nearly $3.8 billion in stock has been subject to impairment — a triple blow that could lead to a phased loss for the company.

In this extreme scenario, market pricing of the film stone will shift from "growth" to "cyclical" or even "difficult" shares, with the market surplus likely to shrink from more than 90 times the current 90 to 20-30 times. Assuming profits recover to $600-80 million, the corresponding market value may be only $12-24 billion, or 60-85% of the current 86.3 billion.

** The value of the value of the film stone will also be supported if it is successfully developed with customized chips, if new products (unmanned, cloud cameras) are recognized in the market, if the growth of overseas markets continues to be high, and the company is expected to enter the harvest period in 2027-2028, with a recovery of $20 billion in revenue and a return to a net profit of $1.52 billion.
