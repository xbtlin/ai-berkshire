# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Military procurement cycle risk

** Risk description:** The company’s income is highly dependent on the speed of the national space mission. Space launch plans are subject to a combination of national budgets, technological maturity, international circumstances, and are highly volatile over the years.

** Risk level: medium and high.** This is an inherent feature of the military-industrial industry and cannot be eliminated. Investors need to accept the "good and bad" alternate.

# 4.2 Risks of changing competition patterns

** Country Mandate Area:** Competition pattern is relatively stable, with a double-oligarchy pattern of 811 from Electric and Shanghai, with no significant change in the short term.

** Commercial space:** The pattern of competition is changing rapidly. The company ' s offer clearly suggests that "new ideas, new models, new products have made the market pattern of the company ' s space power business more intense and complex." Private commercial space enterprises (such as Suzhou) may have advantages in cost control and response speed. If companies fail to make breakthroughs in the bulk costs, commercial space business may have a lower market share than the national task area.

** Special power sources:** The decline in orders (year-on-year revenue in 2025 - 33 per cent) has been marked by more competition in the market, as a result of the downward price of lithium and the military-industrial cycle in the last two years.

** Risk level: medium.** Barriers in the national task area are strong, but increased competition in the commercial space and special power sources is a real threat.

#4.3 Accounts receivable and cash flow risk

This is the most significant financial risk in the world today:

- Accounts receivable amounted to approximately $2.3 to 2.5 billion, representing a high net profit ratio of 714 per cent for the parent
- The rate of increase in receivables (45.6 per cent) was far higher than the rate of increase in revenue (5.1 per cent).
- The turnover rate of receivables was only 0.44 per year
- Inventory growth (27.9 per cent) far exceeding the increase in operating costs (6.2 per cent)

While clients are mostly in the US, with low risk of bad debt, long-term capital occupancy means that corporate book profits are "unreal," and real transferable profits are far below the statement figures. For investors, ROE and free cash flows are the real measure of return, and these two indicators are underperforming on electric blue skies.

** Risk level: medium height.** No blast, but persistently drags down actual returns.

## 4.4 Māori ratio risk in commercial space

The commercial space power industry only turned negative in the first half of 2025. As the knaves and the national grid constellations accelerate, the commercial space revenue ratio will continue to rise.

The company offer suggests a clear risk: "If the company fails to properly judge and grasp market dynamics and trends in commercial space and fails to undertake timely technological and business innovations, it may cause the company to continue to have negative rates of Maori in commercial space."

** Risk level: medium and high.** This is the core risk of "volume gain/no gain" and directly determines whether the company can cash from the concept to "profit."

##4.5 Circulation plate and estimated foam risk

The first-day boom is 750%, and the first-day market is 330 times higher, with a marked speculative campaign. As the sales restrictions are lifted (usually 6-12 months after listing), the expansion of the circulation boards may trigger a significant adjustment in the price of the shares.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** The constellation network is at a significantly slower pace than expected.** The core assumption of the current market value pricing of $136.4 billion is that the Chiron and Niigata constellations will enter an intensive network period in 2026-2030, launching hundreds of satellites each year, bringing about an explosive growth of companies. If the growth story of companies is substantially delayed by technological bottlenecks, financial constraints or policy adjustments, the company will be falsely identified, and the valuation may encounter a double "Davids" – a doubling of performance and expected + valuation.

Based on net profits of $327 million in 2025, if the market gives a reasonable 60-80 times PE (taking into account the military engineering floor premium), the corresponding market value is only $19,262 million, or more than 80 per cent of the current $136.4 billion.

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# Core judgment

In the area of space power, company technical strength, qualification barriers, customer relations, and flight validation data constitute a powerful competitive advantage. As Chispa and the NCN enter the intensive network phase, companies face historic growth opportunities – a growing market of the "Star Sea" level.

** But good companies are not good investments.** The core issues at hand are:

1. ** Valuation is extremely expensive: ** 417 times PE means that the market has set a one-time price on growth expectations for the next 3-5 years. Until profits are realized, such valuations are based entirely on faith.
2. ** The quality of profitability is at risk:** accounts receivable/net profit ratio is 71 per cent, operating cash flows are seriously incompatible with net profits, commercial space Maori rates are just being reversed, indicating a large difference between reported and actual distributed profits.
3. ** "Small incremental gain" risk:** There is significant uncertainty about whether the constellation network will translate into profit growth, mainly from low Maori commercial space revenues.

** Conclusion: first-class track, first-class company, but not currently priced.** It is recommended that the long-term tracking pool be included and that the warehouse be considered when the following conditions are ripe:

- The valuation fell to 80-100 times the PE level (equivalent market value of $26-33 billion based on profits in 2025)
- Or a windfall increase in profits (2027-2028 net profit of 1 billion plus) to naturalize valuations
- Commercial space Maori is stable at over 15%, confirming the logic of "scale effect for profit improvement"
