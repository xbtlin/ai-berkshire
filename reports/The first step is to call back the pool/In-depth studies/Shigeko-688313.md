# I. Business models and barriers to competition

#1.1 Company Positioning

Hegeoko was founded in 2012 and landed on the ground floor of the Science Enterprise in August 2020, registering the crane wall of the South River, and was released from the industrial platform of the China Central Institute of Semiconductor Research. The company is a very small company with the capability of "no source plus active" photochip-wide IDDM, covering all core core elements of chip design, growth, crystal circle manufacturing, photo-carving, plating, and encapsulation testing. In collaboration with the Chinese Academy of Technology Semiconductor, it has developed a coordinated model of "scientific breakthroughs plus engineering end-products" for more than 10 years.

The company is self-positioned as a "chip-grade light communication device platform" and supplies directly to downstream light module manufacturers and communications equipment suppliers across two major technological platforms: the passive chip (the flat light wave conductor class) and the active chip (the laser class).

#1.3 Technological advantages and barriers

** Unsourced Chip Platform (AWG/PLC)**:

- The AG (array-guided raster) chip is the core sourceless device for the re-use/disassembly of the DWDM light module, and the technical barrier is the precision design and manufacturing process for light-waved conduct.
- The company is one of the only global suppliers of the AWG chips in the data centre level, with the AWG breach in 2016, with 60 channels 100 GHz and 150 GHz AWG chips delivered in bulk
- The PLC chip is second in the world, a traditional advantage product

** Source chip platform (DFB/EML)**:

- The DFB laser chip, using the InP material system, has built complete process lines from MQW active area design, MOCVD extension, electrobeam grid production to chip processing sealing
- A high power CW chip product sequence of 75 mW-1,000 mW has been formed, with precision matching 400 G/800G/16.T silicon light module requirements
- 100G EML laser chip is being verified on client and not produced in large quantities

** Core barriers**:

- **IMM full-process barriers**: A small number of enterprises in the country that simultaneously have a complete process chain of passive and active chips, with good rates and cost advantages associated with design and manufacturing depth synergy
- **Collaborative Barriers in China**: Continuous technology input at the research and development end in collaboration with semiconductor institutions for over 10 years
- **Customer Certification Barrier**: photochip certification cycle 6-12 months, entering multiple head light module manufacturer supply chains
- **Smartized manufacturing barriers**: AWG chips need precision process control and good rate increases need to be accumulated over time

Silicon light programmes are becoming the mainstream technology route for the 800 G and above speed light module. Silicon light modules require two key chips: CW light sources (sourced) and AG/MUX/DEMUX (sourceless), with both Snocky Light Sons (sourceless).

# 4.2 Maximum downside risk

** Scenario analysis - if the AWG chip competition pattern deteriorates**:

The AWG chip, which is the core driver of the performance outbreak, currently accounts for about 42% of the income of Sumiko. If the following:

- Energy release by new entrants to domestic AWG chips (Ange, gestation, PV, etc.), resulting in reversal of supply and demand and price reduction
- The increased silicon light integration has resulted in the integration of some DWDD functions into the internal silicon photochip, reducing the demand for separate AWG devices
- The pace of construction of the AI data centre slowed down and the volume of light module shipments decreased

In this scenario, the AWG chip may face a flat price drop, with the company’s core growth engine slowing down. Given that traditional business (optic cables, materials) is growing flatly, and that DFB/CW products are not yet contributing in scale, the overall performance rate may fall sharply.

##4.3 Detailed cash flow risk

The operating cash flow was only 25.6 million yuan in 2024 (net profit of $6.49 million) and 74.56 million yuan (net profit of $372 million) in 2025. The cash flow/profit ratio was significantly lower for the second consecutive year, as a result of:

- High growth in accounts receivable: downstream clients may exchange periods for price discounts
- Large stock holdings: proactive materials to meet purchase order requirements
- Short-term borrowing surge: external financing is needed to replenish operating funds

If the return rate is not as high as expected or the industry is suddenly cooling, the risk of stock price falls and bad debt may be concentrated.

# 4.4 The competition gap with Genjie Technology
