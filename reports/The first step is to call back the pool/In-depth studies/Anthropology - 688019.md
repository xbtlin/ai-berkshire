# 1.3 Array analysis

** Formula barriers are extremely high:** The core of the polish is the milling particles, the formulation of chemical additives, which is a typical area of "knowing why they are not known". Different process nodes, different material systems require a completely different formulation, a cumulative intellectual barrier. By the end of 2025, companies had 308 authorized patents, both internally and externally, and were applying for patents.

** The certification cycle is extremely long: ** semiconductor material enters a crystal circle with a rigorous certification process, usually one to three years. Once certified and delivered in bulk, the cost of switching is extremely high, creating a long-term solid partnership. This "high threshold, strong sticky" feature is the moat at the heart of the polish industry.

** The continuous high intensity of R & D inputs: ** R & D investment in 2025 was R & D 445 million, or 17.76 per cent of the revenue; R & D staff, 409 or 52.10 per cent of the total number of employees - more than half of the staff are R & D staff, which is rare in material-type enterprises.

** Raw materials are autonomously controlled:** The company, in partnership with Shandong Bate, has established the Shandong Antnami Materials Company to promote the autonomous production of core mille matter. In 2025, self-researched home-grown domestic oxen-producing mills were validated and produced in quantities by customers, thus enabling a breakthrough in the technological path.

#1.4 Status of national production and space for upgrading

The rate of domestic production of China’s polish market is still low. The share of the interior of the Anderst technology market is about 30%, and other national production firms, such as the Dinglong shares, are estimated to be about 35-40%.

- Continuous expansion of domestic semiconductor production (continuing plants such as China-Crystal International, China-Worn, Yangtze storage)
- Geopolitics drive the increasing will to replace national production
- Advanced-range polishing remains primarily import-oriented

Alternative national production space remains wide, and the domestic market is expected to increase to 50-60 per cent in the next three to five years.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Client concentration risk

This is the most significant risk for Anthropology. The top five clients sold 84.45%, 82.47%, 80.49% and 75.81%, respectively, in the first half of 2021-2024. While concentration has declined over the years, it remains high. Historically, a single customer from Central Hedge International has contributed about 60% of its revenue (from 2016 to 2018), but it is still the largest customer.

** Mitigation factors:** The trend of declining client concentration is clear; companies have entered the supply chain of multiple crystal-crystal mills such as power generation, Yangjiang storage, Hua Yuu, Huayun, and China Rainbow; and the semiconductor industry itself naturally concentrates downstream customers.

# 4.2 Raw material supply risk

Companies’ core raw materials (silicon soluble glue, paraffins such as gas phase 2 oxidation) are imported directly or indirectly from countries like Japan. If there is friction in international trade or supply disruption, there will be a direct impact on the company.

** Mitigating factors:** The company has established a joint venture, Antnami material from Shandong, to promote autonomous production of core mills; self-researching of the mills has been certified and produced by customers. However, full autonomy of raw materials will take time to control.

##4.3 Technological route change risk

Semiconductive institutional process technology continues to evolve, and will have a fundamental impact on companies if, in the future, subversive flat-level technologies replace traditional chemical machinery polishing. But, in the light of current technological trends, chemical machine polishing remains an irreplaceable key step in the next 5 to 10 years, and more more steps are needed in the advanced process.

# 4.4 Management and corporate governance

The founder and chairman, Wang Su-min, and the University of Les, a doctor of materials chemistry, have a very strong technical background. The company has no real control (the first shareholder is a Cayman Islands registered company, with no single controlling shareholding), and this equity structure is more special in the creation of panels – the advantage is that decision-making is relatively democratic, and the risk is that there may be a lack of "panders" in key decisions.

Companies introduced restrictive equity incentives in 2025, which helped to bind core team interests.

# 4.5 The biggest downside risk

** Geopolitically induced supply chain break-ups.** If the US-Japan export controls on Chinese semiconductor materials are further upgraded, the supply of core material in Anthropology could be disrupted. While companies are moving toward autonomous control of raw materials, alternatives in the short term may not cover all categories and quality levels.

** Second, the risk of receivables.** The high percentage of accounts receivable in the first quarter of 2026 may result in a loss of bad debts if downstream customers, particularly some of the small, medium-sized crystal mills, experience operational difficulties.

** Third is the valuation regression risk.** The current dynamic market surplus is above reasonable range and there is a pressure to contract the valuation if the semiconductor industry is down-swinged or the company is growing at a lower than expected rate.

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# Core judgment

Anthropology is one of the best semiconductor material companies in the A-market business model. A combination of high Maori (over 56%), high net interest rates (31%), high net asset yields (25%), and high R&D inputs (18%) is rare in the same company.

** The value of the market is currently approximately 50 billion, which corresponds to 43-52 times the rate of dynamic market gain, is well above the reasonable valuation area and is not sufficiently secure. If the stock price is reversed to 35-40 times the rate of dynamic market gain (about 350-42 billion market value), it will be in a more attractive price range.

# Key observation indicators

1. ** Māori rate trends:** Concern about whether Māori rate can stabilize at over 55 per cent, which is a core indicator of the quality of business models
2. **The rate of increase in functional wet electronic chemicals:** The ability of the second growth curve to sustain more than 50% of the increase determines whether the company can move from "monity champion" to "platform company"
3. **Customer concentration:** Whether the top five client ratio will continue to fall below 70 per cent
4. **Fixing days of accounts receivable:** Focus on the quality of returns, especially the recovery rhythm of large clients
** Autonomous controlled progress of raw materials:** progress in the production of self-research mills and authentication imports at the client
** Global city occupancy rate upscaling:** speed and path from 11% to 15% or even 20%

# One sentence to sum up

AndersTech is the leader of a more definitive national semiconductor material, with good business models and high growth visibility, but the current valuation is highly priced — good companies need better prices.
