** This is both an advantage and a risk:** the advantage is the high stability of orders and the depth of barriers to competition (the supplier status within the system of military wage plus); the risk is that customers are too concentrated, pricing rights are limited and the cycle of military procurement is clearly influenced.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Military procurement cycle risk

- Military procurement is subject to a high volatility in defence budgets, equipment replacement rhythm and policy cycles
- Continuous corporate pressure 2024-2025 is a typical manifestation of fluctuations in the procurement cycle
- The procurement pace between the end of the 14th and the beginning of the 15th is more uncertain.

** Risk level: medium and high** — this is the most central operating risk of military-industrial enterprises and cannot be avoided in the short term.

# 4.2 Client concentration risk

- The top five clients account for 89.43 per cent, and the clients are highly concentrated in the military industrial complex.
- The physical control of the China Electric Section as both the shareholder and the largest customer and supplier, forming a pattern of related transactions
- If a military-industrial group adjusts its procurement plan, it may have a greater impact on the performance of the company
- Price fairness of associated transactions also requires sustained attention

** Risk level: Medium** — Vendor status within the system is stable, but concentration is risk in itself.

##4.3 Valuation of high risk

- The current 129-fold Pe is extremely high in the A-share market.
- High valuation means that high expectations for future growth have been adequately reflected
- Once performance is not as good as expected or the growth logic is false, there is a risk of "Davis double-killing."
- The valuation premium for the national bott is particularly significant in the context of the high valuation of the military industry as a whole

** Risk level: high** — this is the biggest investment risk at present.

## 4.4 New business realization risks

- New growth points such as satellite Internet, militarism exports, popular radio-frequency chips are still in the conceptual validation or early stages
- The technical requirements of the T/R satellite component differ from traditional military goods and their smooth transformation is to be verified
- The market for goods is not a good win when it comes to international giants like Skyworks and Qorvo.
- The market may be overly optimistic about these new business expectations.

** Risk level: medium and high** - New business is both a source of growth and a source of uncertainty.

##4.5 Risk of change of technology routes

- Rapid technological overlap in the field of radio frequency, and the accumulation of technology in companies may be exposed to the risk of devaluation if major changes in technology routes such as GaN/GaAs occur
- Developments in digital array technology may change the pattern of demand for traditional T/R components
- But in the short term, the risk is low, and the technical overlap of military-industrial products is longer.

** Risk level: low** - medium- and long-term attention required, limited short-term impact.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** Core risk scenario: continued low procurement by military industry + satellite Internet progress is not as fast as expected + high valuation cannot be sustained **

If the military-industrial procurement cycle continues to be low, and satellite Internet deployment delays, corporate revenue may hover between $2.4 billion and $2.6 billion, with net profits of $500.6 billion. In this case, the market may revalue the value from 129 times PE to 50-70 times PE, corresponding to a market value of $25-42 billion, or 35-60% of the current 65.6 billion.
