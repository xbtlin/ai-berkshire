# III. Growing up and valuation

#3.1 Growth drivers

** Core driver I: national production replacement of semiconductor ALD equipment**

- The global market for semiconductor film deposition equipment is about $22.9 billion (2022), of which ALD is about $3 billion, or 13 per cent.
- The penetration rate of the national production of ALD equipment is extremely low, and microguiding nano-forms are used as domestic taps, facing a definitive alternative to national production.
- Corporate ALD equipment has covered three main areas of storage (3D NAND/DRAM), logical chips and advanced envelopes, and client certification has been adopted.

** Core driver II: incremental equipment brought by 3D NAND/DRAM technology rotation**

- 3D NAND evolved from 64th floor to 232nd floor and above, requiring additional ALD sediment steps for each additional layer.
- 3D DRAM is the next generation of storage technology, in which the use of ALD equipment will increase significantly.
- This means that the total volume of the ALD equipment market will continue to expand as technology evolves.

**Auxiliary driver: CVD extension**

Companies are not only doing ALD, but are also expanding the CVD equipment product line to further expand market size. A transferable debt of $1.1 billion has been issued for the construction of semiconductor equipment smart-making plants and expansion of R & D laboratories.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Core risk

** (1) Continued low risk in the photovoltaic industry**

The photovoltaic sector is in a cyclical adjustment phase of overcapacity. If the down-cycle continues, revenues from photovoltaic equipment may continue to shrink, delaying overall performance. The income from photovoltaics declined significantly in 2025, with total income falling by 5.5 per cent in the first quarter of 2026, year-on-year.

**(2) Undefined operational tempo of semiconductor operations**

Despite the rapid growth of semiconductor orders, there is a time lag in the recognition of orders to revenue.

**(3) downside risk of Māori**

The Mäori rate fell from about 40% to 32.7% in 2025, which is larger.
- If the main cause is a decline in the price of photovoltaic products, the Māori rate is expected to rise as the semiconductor ratio rises.
- If the semiconductor product itself has a low Maori rate (early penetration benefits), the overall Māori rate may be sustained.

**(4) Technology competition and customer concentration risks**

- Tchilling technology is also expanding the ALD product line, and domestic ALD equipment competition may increase.
- Semiconductor clients are concentrated in a few large storage plants in the country (Longjiang storage, Longingong storage) and have a high concentration of customers.

**(5) Valuation risk**

193 times the pere means that the market has very high expectations for companies’ future growth. Any lower than expected performance data can trigger large stock price fluctuations.
