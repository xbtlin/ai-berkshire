# I. Business models and barriers to competition

# 1.1 Corporate genes: "International origin" from acquisitions

The history of the Jing Tang shares deserves to be highlighted, as it determines the technological genes and patterns of competition of companies.

In May 2016, Beijing also invested in the purchase of the US semiconductor equipment company Mattson Technoroy (established in 1988 and based in Fremont, California) through Tang Seonglong, the first case in which China successfully acquired the international semiconductor equipment. Mattson’s core technology covered equipment systems such as deglution, rapid heat treatment (RTP), millisecond-scale fire-retrenchment and plasma etching.

The acquisition was followed by the restructuring of the State Treasury, which incubated the "Gang stock" entity, which landed on 8 July 2025 at a price of $8.45 per share, which was raised by over 200 per cent on the first day of the market, and once exceeded the market value of 77 billion yuan.

** Actual controller** The financial audit office of the CEMI in Beijing holds 100% of the share in Tang Shenglong and 45.05% of the previous IPO through the also-state investment. The state funding background provides a stable strategic support, but it also means that the effectiveness of governance and incentives needs to be observed on a continuous basis.

# 1.3 Array analysis

**(1) Technical Barriers - Acquisition of access to technology at the international level**

The core technology of Jing Tang is derived from the accumulation of Mattson Technology over 30 years, and has been validated by the world’s top ten chip manufacturers in the field of dry deglution and RTP. Products can be used in large-scale production of 5 nanometer logic chips, 10 nanodrams, and 128 floors of 3D NAND.

**(2) Client sticky - very difficult to replace after equipment certification**

Once certified by the customer end-product, typically one to two years, the customer rarely changes the supplier. Corporate customers have fully covered the top ten chip manufacturers worldwide and the leading chip manufacturers in the domestic industry.

**(3) Limitations of the moat**

It is necessary to be frank and to point out that the moat of Ei-Tang is not as strong as it looks:

- The dry deglution and RRP markets are small (about $800 million each) and even if high market rates are maintained, the income ceiling is limited.
- In this real large market, the company is still in its early stages and the moat has not yet been formed.
- Spare parts and services revenue is about 23 per cent, more sticky but essentially dependent on the growth of stock holdings.

# III. Growing up and valuation

#3.1 Drivers of future growth

** Driver I: Advanced process for heating/decoderation with a higher price**

As chip manufacturing moves towards more advanced nodes:
- Increase in light-carrying steps (multiple patterning) requiring de-glues after each step, and use of frequency upgrades for dry deglution equipment.
- Advanced logical chips (e.g. GAA structures) require higher precision for fire repulsion, increased demand for millisecond level fire repulsion and flash fire re-release, and increased value of RTP equipment per piece.
- The number of 3D NAND stacking layers has evolved from 128 to 256 and the heat treatment step has increased.

** Driver II: Alternative national production space**

- The share of domestic income of companies rose significantly from 45.42 per cent to 66.67 per cent in 2024, an increase of 21 percentage points, with a marked acceleration of domestic production substitution.
- 70 per cent of the RTP application materials, and in the context of the Chinese-American competition for technology, there is a strong incentive for the domestic crystal mill to replace the equipment with the Qing Tang equipment.
- But note that the first PSK in the rubber field comes from Korea and not the United States, and there is no direct "crack-and-neck" alternative logic.

** Driver III: Erosion device exposure**

- The global market for corrosion equipment is about $20 billion, more than ten times the amount of decouples and RTPs.
- The company RENA-E has been ordered to produce a critical customer volume, with an erosion of 12.45 per cent of the revenue in 2025, and if it continues to break through, it will open up a second growth curve.

** Driver IV: Global semiconductor industry upswing**

- The global semiconductor market is expected to be larger than $975 billion (+25 per cent or more) in 2026, and AI-driven demand for computing continues to expand.
- The Chinese continental crystallized mill is continuously expanding and the demand for equipment is high.

##3.4 Valuation assessment

** Static ** 111 times PE is not cheap at all.

** Dynamic** Two scenarios are needed:

** Scenario I: conservative assumptions (stable growth in existing operations only)**
- If the net profit in 2027 amounts to $1.15 billion, 40-55 times the PE (the equivalent of a reasonable valuation area in the semiconductor equipment industry), the fair market value is $46,633 million, and the corresponding share price range is lower than the current price.
- This means that the current valuation implicitly implies a successful projection of the emission of corrosion equipment.

** Scenario II: optimistic assumptions (emiting equipment breakthrough)**
- If net profits reach 1.5 billion yuan in 2028 and the corrosion business enters a rapid growth period, the market value is 55-70 times that of the PE, with a reasonable market value of $82.5-10.5 billion, and currently 74.8 billion has 10-40 per cent of the space to go.
- But this requires that the corrosion equipment actually enter the release period from the start-up period, with greater uncertainty.

** Key Conclusion** The current market value of $74.8 billion has been better priced for the company ' s leading role in the field of deglution and RTP, and the subsequent sustainability of the valuation is dependent on whether the corrosion equipment is a real engine of growth.

# IV. Risk assessment

# 4.1 Overseas earnings and geopolitical risks

- ** Rapid rise in domestic income share**: from 45.42 per cent in 2023 to 66.67 per cent in 2024, geopolitical exposure is shrinking.
- However, about 33 per cent of the income is still generated overseas and the sale of products is mostly in United States dollars.
- If the country concerned takes more stringent restrictions or sanctions, companies may face risks such as limited cooperation with upstream and downstream partners and the impact on supply chain stability.
- Companies have established supply chain diversification and localization strategies, but there is uncertainty about the security of the long-term supply of core parts.

** Negative perspective**: Geopolitics are also driving forces. It is precisely because of the Chinese-American technology competition that domestic crystal mills accelerate the procurement of domestically produced equipment, and the rapid rise in the domestic revenue ratio of firms is in itself a logical reflection.

# 4.2 Client concentration risk

- The integrated circuit manufacturing industry is naturally characterized by "larger and smaller" size, with higher concentration of corporate customers.
- The top five client concentration data are not fully disclosed, but given that the top ten chip manufacturers globally cover the vast majority of their capacity, the high concentration is to some extent industry-specific rather than company-specific.
- On the other hand, the impact is more pronounced when a major client falls down the cycle of capital expenditure or turns to competitors.

##4.3 Competition pattern risks

** Decouple **: PSK (South Korea) is number one at about 40% of the city, followed by 34.6% of the city. The difference is small and the pattern is relatively stable. The risk is that PSK could erode the share of the city if it introduces new revolutionary products.

**RTP area**: Application materials are absolutely monopolized at about 70%, and the national product is 11-13%. The alternative is the core growth logic of the field, but the technical barriers to the application of materials are extremely high and difficult to break through in the non-China market.

** Erosion area**: Pan forest, Tokyo electronics, applications are firmly structured; MSMEs in the country have also established significant advantages in etching (CCP etching domestic leadership), and the North’s Chinese invention is catching up. Jing Tang faces much more competition in etching than decoupling and RTP.

# 4.4 Maximum downside risk

** The biggest downside risk is "two headless": deglution/RTP growth peaks and corrosion is below expectations**

- If the market for decomposition and RRP slows down (predictable) because of its limited size and the corrosion of equipment fails to take on the scale that is expected due to the intense competition, the company will be caught in the awkward position of "old business growth, new business not yet connected".
- Based on a benchmark of RMB 5,076 million in 2025 and a net profit of RMB 671 million, if growth is not as high as expected in 2026-2027 (the rate of increase in revenue is less than 15 per cent), the current 111-fold PE will face a significant contraction.
- **Quantitative estimate**: If net profits reached only $900 million in 2027 (less than the $1.15 billion that was expected to be consistent), they are given 45 times PE, corresponding to a market value of $40.5 billion, or about 46 per cent of the current level of 74.8 billion.

# 4.5 Signal meaning of a decline in performance of 2026Q1

The 2026Q1 battalion received 10.6% of the year-on-year and the net profit-on-year to 26.8%, which is the first quarter since the market. While fluctuations in the single quarter may have had an occasional factor (e.g., order delivery rhythm, revenue recognition points), consideration is given to:
- Operating cash flows negative (pre-positioned) in 2025
- A quarter is usually a light season in semiconductor equipment.

Close attention needs to be paid to the performance trends of 2026Q2-Q3 and to whether it is a one-time fluctuation or a sign of slower growth.
