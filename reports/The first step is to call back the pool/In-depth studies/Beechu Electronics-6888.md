# 1.3 Array analysis

The moat of the verbs of the verbs can be understood on four levels:

** First layer: software algorithmal barriers**
The core of the control system is software algorithms, an area that requires long-term accumulation. Companies have developed deep-seas knowledge of process databases and algorithms since 2007.

** Second layer: ecological sticky**
Companies construct industrial Internet ecology through the Device Health Cloud Data Platform and the Manufacturing Implementation System Data Platform. ** Third floor: integration of hardware and software**
Companies move from pure software to cutting top hardware, creating a combination sales model for "blank systems plus smart cutting head". This integration increases product stickyness and single-client value.

**Class 4: scale effects and data advantages**
Over 60% of the city’s share means that companies have the largest customer base and process data, which can be more rapidly iterative. This data advantage is particularly important in the age of artificial intelligence.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Industry ceiling risk

The market for laser cutting control systems is about 2.1 billion yuan, and in BERCH in 2025 it was 2.2 billion yuan (including cutting head hardware), and the growth space of the cutting control system itself is shrinking. The market for medium- and low-power is saturated, and high-power national production is still available, but the ceiling is visible.

** Risk level: Medium** - Short term (2-3 years) still has a high power replacement and a surge in head cutting, but new operational relay is required in the medium term.

# 4.2 New business expansion uncertainties

Smart welding is the company ' s largest second-growth curve, but the following uncertainties exist:
- Welding differs significantly from cutting, and technology migration is not simply a copy
- Long procurement cycles in industries such as ships, steel structures, etc., and time for large clients to break through
- The huge expansion of the network of sales teams and services required to cross from 600 to tens of thousands
- The competitors may have fought back from the traditional welding robotic field.

** Risk level: medium and high** - The success of the welding business determines the scope for the company to grow over the next three to five years.

##4.3 Risks of changing competition patterns

- Medium and low power areas: stable pattern and low risk
- High power areas: FDI such as Befford still has a technological advantage and national substitution may not work as well as expected
- Welding: faced competition from traditional robot giants such as Fanako, Antuan, Kuka, etc.

** Risk level: Medium and low** - Competition patterns in cutting are very stable and uncertainty in welding is greater.

# 4.4 Management and corporate governance

** Accomplishments:**

- Attraction and retention of research and development talent is a long-term challenge (high cost of living in Shanghai and intense competition in industry)

** Risk level: low** - quality of management and overall excellence in governance structure.

# 4.5 The biggest downside risk

** Core risk: The laser cutting market is slowing up and adding intelligent welding to the expected amount**

If downstream manufacturing investment continues to be depressed, cutting equipment needs weaken, and markets for welding operations are blocked (delays in procurement decisions by large clients, counterattacks, etc.), companies may face a significant decline in growth. At the current 40-fold value level, the fall in growth risks a "Davids double-killing."

The most extreme scenario is that cutting operations are reduced to single digits, annual income from welding operations is stagnant at hundreds of millions of yuan, and corporate growth as a whole is down to less than 10%. In this case, a reasonable market gain may fall 20-25 times, and the corresponding share price may fall to 80-100 yuan.
