##1.3 Business structural transformation

The company has completed a strategic transition from "telecommunications-based" to "data-centre driven":

- **2023**: Telecommunications market over 90%, data centre operations almost negligible
- **1st semester of 2025**: Data centre operations generated $105 million, a sharp increase of 1034 per cent over the same year, exceeding telecommunications operations for the first time
- ** Total year 2025**: Data centre operations revenue of $393 million, an increase of 719 per cent, or 65.4 per cent, over the same period; telecommunications operations revenue of $206 million, or only 2.06 per cent,

The core logic of this transition is that the global AI large model training and reasoning for the volume of 800G/16.6 T light module have triggered the outbreak of CW light chip procurement in the Silicon Light Programme.

#1.4 Competition patterns

** Global pattern**: Overseas leader Lumentum and Corherent (formerly II-VI) dominate in the area of high-speed laser chip, but production capacity continues to be tight and demand-deprived. Genjits is sixth in global laser chip suppliers in 2025, with a market share of about 3.1 per cent; and second in global silicon light high-speed light interconnection product laser chip suppliers, with a market share of 23.6 per cent.

** Domestic pattern**: The national production rate of low- and medium-speed photochips is high, but the national production rate of high-speed chips 25G and above is low. Domestic competitors include photo-stupid technology, Xin Xia, etc., but in the area of high-speed InP chips, the source-cutter technologies have pre-eminences and process accumulation.

** Core barriers**:
- **Technology barrier**: In-P laser chip extension and raster production involves atomic-grade precision control, process optimization cycles lasting several years, and new entrants are difficult to catch up quickly
- **Certificate Barrier**: The photochip is required to pass through the downstream light module manufacturer certification cycle for 6-12 months, with high switching costs
- **IDM Mode Barrier**: Full process autonomy, response speed and optimal efficiency are superior to surrogate mode

# 4.2 Maximum downside risk

** Scenario analysis - if AI's capital expenditure slows**:

The rise in 2025-2026 was based on the continued increase in AI infrastructure inputs by global cloud producers. If one of the following is to be seen:
- The global recession has led to a reduction in capital spending by cloud producers
- The development of the AI model into a bottleneck phase, and the demand for training slowed down
- The light module price battles resulted in a compressed procurement cost for downstream manufacturers

Companies may face a sharp decline in orders, overcapacity, and a rapid decline in the Māori rate to 40-50%. Under the current 418-fold municipal surplus valuation, double-killing of performance and valuation may result in a decline in equity prices of more than 50%.

##4.3 Detailed risk of technical routes

The current data centre light interconnection has two technological paths that have evolved in parallel:
- **EML programme**: 100 G EML layouts in source, directly benefiting
- **Silicon light programme**: CW light is needed, Genjie is the leading domestic supplier

Both routes are well-structured, reducing the risk of a single route being replaced. But the CPO (combined encapsulated optical) is the ultimate option for a rate above 1.6 T, which, if commercialized at a higher pace than expected, may change the cover and supply chain patterns of the photochip and require continuous tracking.

# V. Consolidated conclusions

##5.1 Overall evaluation

GenjieTech is a company in the "success of the strategic transition." From the low performance of 2022-2024, the explosion of the CW light source at the data centre turned out to be a spectacular turn.

**Enaps**:
- The scarce high-speed IDD chip manufacturer in the country, the core of the calculator infrastructure
- Products are driven from CW light sources to 100 G EML, CPO light source ladders, with clear growth curves
- Very robust financial structure, with less than 10 per cent asset-liability ratio
- Ports on the market + US construction, global layout ahead

** Disadvantage**:
- The client concentration is extremely high. The first client is half a wall.
- The valuation has fully overstretched future growth and leaves very little room for error
- "Change face" after two years of publicizing, and management 's performance stability still needs to be verified
- The photochip industry is highly cyclical.

#5.2 Investment judgement

** Qualitative Judgement**: Genjie Technology is a "good company" in the national lumino chip track, but it is not currently "good price". The market value of 130 billion dollars, and the price of 418 times the rolling market rate, have taken into account all the growth expectations for the next 2-3 years. Unless there is a strong belief in long-term growth in AI computing, the risk returns of the current price are not ideal.

** Conditions suitable for purchase**:
- Return of equity to 80-100 times the expected profits in 2026 (approximately $52-650 billion market value), with risk returns improving compared to the beginning
- 100G EML mass production, opening the second growth curve
- AI ' s capital expenditure has been accelerated after a round of adjustments
