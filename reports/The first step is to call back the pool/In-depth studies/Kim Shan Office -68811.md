# IV. Risk assessment

# 4.1 Microsoft competitive pressure

Microsoft is the absolute master of global office software, and the WPS has been catching up with power gaps for 37 years:
- Microsoft 365 Copilot integrates GPS-4/GPT-5, with AI leading the lead
- In the enterprise-level market, the eco-integration capacity of Microsoft is much stronger than the WPS
- Domestic policy constraints are the biggest defense of WPS - without the push of the CBS, it is difficult to compete with Microsoft in the firm-level market

** Risk level: Medium** - Policy protection is a double-edged sword that is beneficial in the short term but may weaken its competitiveness in the long term.

# 4.2 Change in the policy tempo of the Investigation

- The push for the letter is influenced by fiscal and policy priorities, and the pace may be slow and slow
- The decline in revenue from WPS operations in 2024 by 8.56 per cent in comparison, which is typical of policy window periods
- If the economy goes down and government and state corporate IT spending is compressed, the information-combine rhythm may slow down
- But the long-term direction is irreversible: national production substitution is the strategic direction in the context of China-US technology decoupling

** Risk level: medium** — short-term rhythm fluctuations, but long-term trends are determined.

# 4.3 Imbalances in the return of AI input

- Over $2 billion in research and development in 2025, representing 34 per cent of the revenue, with AI-related inputs increasing in proportion
- The costs of calling the big AI model are still high, and each call is at a real cost
- If AI ' s fee rate rises slowly or the user ARPUs grow less than expected, high R & D inputs may drag down the profit margin
- The landing of advanced functions such as AI Agent, In-depth reasoning, is still limited to large model accuracy and hallucinations

** Risk level: Medium and High ** - AI is the largest imagination and the largest source of uncertainty.

##4.4 Risk of lopsided business structures

- Personal operations accounted for 61 per cent, overdependence on end C, which slowed to 10 per cent
- WPS 365 with a high growth but a small base (12%) and difficult to change the overall rate of growth in the short term
- If personal business continues to decline and WPS 365 is not able to do so quickly, companies may face growth bottlenecks

** Risk level: medium**

## 4.5 Co-operation in Office Competition

- WPS 365 is just 4.8% of the co-op market, facing nails, flying books, corporate micro-credits.
- These three are dependent on Ali, by-laws, tethers, and have a strong ecological and financial advantage.
- WPS has a core advantage in "document" rather than "collaborative" and lacks capacity to differentiate in the area of co-location.

** Risk level: Medium** - WPS does not need to win the co-opt market, but simply to maximize the documentation tools and attach them to the platforms.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** Core risk scenario: ASI commercialization is below expectations + IT policy slows + personal business growth continues to decline **

If these three negative factors occur simultaneously, the growth rate of corporate revenue may fall to a single-digit level, and the net profit rate may stagnate or even decline. Under the current 63-fold PE valuation level, this will trigger the Davis double-killing -- sub-performance and under-valued.
