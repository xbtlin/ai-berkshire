** Key features of business model:**

The income model of the Bermuda income pattern consists of four levels:
1. **Previous**: Fees for project testing (confirmable income)
2. ** Mid-term**: Antibodies Transfer/Authorization of down payment (one-time income)
3. ** Late**: milestone payments (depending on clinical progress by partners)
4. ** Forward**: distribution share (depending on drug listing)

The company’s main revenue is currently from the first two levels. As the authorized 350+ project moves forward in clinical terms, future milestone payments and sales-sharing revenues are expected to be realized gradually, but over a time span of three to ten years.

##1.4 Evaluation of competition barriers

** Barrier factor:**

- ** The uniqueness and pre-emptive advantage of the technology platform**: The RenMice series is one of the most complete global antibody mouse platforms, competing with, but differentiated from, the regenerative VerocImmune, and the Ariva Mab of Ablexis. RenMab key technologies have been licensed by China.
- ** Network effects are emerging**: 350+ Authorization agreements mean that antibodies in Bermuda are being validated and developed by dozens of pharmaceutical companies around the world, each successful mandate increasing the Platform ' s credibility and commercial negotiating capacity.
- ** High Māori validation platform value**: The antibodies found that 86% Māori business proved the platform's high value added — marginal cost is extremely low, with almost pure profit per additional authorized player.
- ** Upgrade from tool platforms to drug discovery**: Companies not only provide tools (sell mice), but also independently conduct antibody discovery and external authorization to extend up the value chain.

** Barrier deficiency:**

The global human-source antibody mouse platform is not unique to the Bermuda. VelocImmune of the regenerative is more influential worldwide, with many of the antibodies found on VelocImmune being marketed (e.g. Dupixent, Libtayo, etc.), which is an unfinished milestone for the Bermuda.
- In 350+authorization agreements, the number of molecules who are actually entering a post-clinical stage or are listed is limited, and substantial cooperation may ultimately result in a failure to generate milestone payments and sales splits.
- Model animal sales and pharmacological efficiency services are under competitive pressure from big CXOs, such as Mingkund.

# # 1.5 Combined barrier rating

##4.2 Critical risk: conversion rate of authorized projects

350+ Authorization projects are central to the company’s valuation, but one reality needs to be honest: the overall success rate of drug development is low. Assuming that in these enabling projects:

- about 70% may end before or during phase I
- About 20% may have entered phase II but ultimately failed.
- About 10% may finally be approved.

Even so, the 10% success rate means that there may be 35+ drugs that are finally marketed and that generate sales shares, which remains a significant figure. But the key question is whether successful drugs can reach a heavy-pound level (more than $1 billion annually) and no drugs based on RenMeice are currently at that level.

##4.3 Release pressure
