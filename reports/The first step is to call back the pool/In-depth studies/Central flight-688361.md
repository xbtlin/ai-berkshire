# IV. Risk assessment

# 4.1 Technical catch-up difficulties

- **Maximum risk**. The quantity detection equipment is one of the highest technical barriers in semiconductor equipment, and KLA may have a technical lead in advanced detection for more than 10 years. The difficulty of achieving a real bulk replacement of advanced detection (below 7nm) is not to be underestimated.
- The area of electronic beam detection is equally difficult to catch up with, and is currently only in the prototype phase.
- On the other hand, companies have demonstrated catch-up capacity in the area of no-tempered and covert testing, and mature alternatives are well advanced.

# 4.2 Client concentration risk

- The concentration of the top five clients has been steadily reduced from 97.4 per cent in 2018 to 33.3 per cent in 2022, a significant improvement.
- However, core clients are still concentrated in a few head crystalry mills, such as the Central Core International and the Yangtze Storage, with a limited base of downstream clients.
- If capital expenditure on a core client is significantly reduced, it will have a direct impact on corporate revenue collection.

# 4.3 Export control risk (double-edged sword)

- **positive**: Export restrictions imposed by the United States on companies such as KLA directly drive domestic customers to switch to domestically produced equipment, with Chinese Flying Surveys being the core beneficiaries.
- **negative**: Some of the core parts used by the company itself (e.g. high-end light sources, detectors, precision optical components) may also be affected by US export controls, and supply chain security is a concern. If the US is further tightened, it may also affect the supply of parts and components by national manufacturers of equipment.

## 4.4 Restricted stock release pressure

- **On 19 May 2026**, the company will have 73.308 million restricted shares in circulation, accounting for 26.48 per cent of the shares in circulation A and 20.93 per cent of the total equity.
- This is the first release of the restricted shares of 36 months after IPO, which is large and may put pressure on the stock prices in the short term.
- Some shareholders have been exposed to plans for reduction, which require attention.

# 4.5 Uncertainty of profitability

- The extremely volatile profitability since the company was listed (a sharp fall in net profits in 2022, a loss in 2024 and a continuing loss in 2026Q1) has not yet demonstrated its ability to sustain steady profitability.
- High R&D costs are double-edged swords: long-term competitiveness is guaranteed, but it is difficult to judge when profits are released in the short term.
- If advanced process products are not validated as expected, R & D inputs may not be rewarded accordingly.

# 4.6 Maximum downside risk

** Scenario**: advanced production and terminal testing equipment is not progressing as expected + high R& D cost + centralized reduction after major shareholders are released.

In this context, the market may revisit the "profit release" schedule, with the valuation hub falling from 24 to 15 times the PS, corresponding to a market value of about $46 billion, or 63 discounts of the current stock price.
