##4.1 Core tube clinical risk

- Tetsip, although NDA has declared, the approval results are still uncertain.
- RC148 Global III Clinical Phases have not been activated and the risk of clinical failure cannot be ignored
- Early tubes like RC278 and RC288 have less success.
- The nature of innovation medicine development is high-risk returns, and failure of the pipe is the norm, not the exception.

** Risk level: medium and high** — this is the core risk of an innovative pharmaceutical enterprise and cannot be fully avoided.

# 4.2 Overseas Empowerment Milestone Risk

- Of the total transaction amount of $5.6 billion for RC14.8, the initial payment of $650 million was confirmed, with the remaining milestone payments
- The trigger conditions for milestone payments include clinical progress, approval, commercial sales targets, etc.
- If clinical progress is not as good as expected or approval fails, subsequent milestones may not be fully met
- Vidisito and Pfizer are at similar risk.

** Risk level: medium** — significant down payment has been validated, but there is uncertainty about subsequent milestones.

# 4.3 ADC race competition intensified

- Global ADC drug development is in the flash phase, with over hundreds of pipelines.
- The combined DS-8201 has a pre-eminent advantage in the HER2 field, with Enhertu selling billions of dollars a year.
- ADC companies in the country are also catching up.
- If the pattern of competition deteriorates, it may affect the sales prospects of Vidisito.

** Risk level: Medium** - Rongchang organisms vary in target and adaptive conditions, but the races are becoming more crowded.

##4.4 Commercialization implementation risk

- The rate of domestic commercial sales slowed from 58 per cent to 33.7 per cent, requiring attention to sustainability of growth
- Tytacip faces a single bellium contest at SLE.
- Rapid release of new adaptive treatments after approval is dependent on health insurance negotiations, outreach capabilities and medical awareness
- There is still a gap between the company's own sales team and the multinational drug industry.

** Risk level: Medium** - Domestic sales are on track, but it remains to be seen whether high growth will be sustained.

# 4.5 Cash flow and financing risk

- The cash at the end of 2025 was low at $1,493 million, but the RC148 first payment will be significantly improved.
- Additional financing may be required if the initial payment is delayed or if the amount is not as anticipated
- The high level of research and development inputs by innovation drug companies determines that external funding is still needed in the short term
- But this risk has been significantly reduced by 2025, when the surplus and RC148 first payment were reversed

** Risk level: low** - RC148 down payment will fully address the cash flow pressure.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** Core risk scenario: RC148 Global III Clinical Failure + TATESIP New Adaptive Destruct + ADC Competition Patterns are deteriorating**

If these risks occur simultaneously, the growth of corporate income will depend mainly on the natural release of existing products, with revenue likely to hover between $3-3.5 billion and net profits likely to fall back to the $300-50 billion level. In this case, the market value of $64.7 billion corresponds to 130-115 times PE, which will face serious valuation pressures.

However, it should be noted that RC148 has received an initial payment of $650 million, even if the subsequent milestones have not been fully met, and the initial payment itself has provided the company with sufficient financial reserves and value validation.
