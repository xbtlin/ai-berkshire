# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 PCB industry cycle volatility risk (medium)

The PCB sector has a clear cyclical nature, as illustrated by the 53% decline in corporate net profits in 2023. The current PCB sector is in the AI-driven upper-line cycle, but there is uncertainty about the duration and intensity of the cycle.

##4.2 WLP equipment is not released in excess of the expected risks (critical risks)

The WLP series has passed 100 million dollars in hand orders, but it is still far from "order" to "sustained revenue contribution." The advanced containment equipment market is highly competitive, including the international giants ASML (subsidiary layout), Nikon, and Jia-Jan, all of whom are deployed in this field. If there are technical problems in the verification of WLP equipment or slow progress in customer import, the pan-conductor business may not be able to deliver the incremental amount as scheduled.

##4.3 Competition increases risk (moderately high)

In the field of PCB LDI:
- Orbotech, as a traditional leader, has accumulated technology that may be countered by price reductions or new products
- There are new entrants in the country trying to get into the market.
- 15% of the company ' s city share is first, but the lead is not absolute.

# 4.4 Diminishing risks to go to Hong Kong (concerning)

The company is preparing to market its Hong Kong stock (as reported in March 2026), which could lead to an expansion of equity and an EPS spread. On the other hand, port-stock financing would provide the company with more adequate financial support for business expansion and R & D investment.

# 4.5 Semiconductor light luminum management

The market may have high expectations of companies in the field of semiconductor light mitigation. It needs to be clear that the company’s core technology route (intuitive light) is completely different from the ASML projective technology route, and that companies do not have the conditions to compete with ASML in the core field of chip manufacturing.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** The biggest downside risk is the double shock of "the lower PCB industry cycle + WLP equipment is not as high as expected".** If the PCB landscape returns in 2027, and the pan-conductor business fails to contribute enough to increase, the company may face a growth failure. Under this scenario, the current market value of $34.1 billion may fall below $15 billion.
