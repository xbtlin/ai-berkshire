# V. Consolidated conclusions

** Investment attraction rating: <unk> (neutral, good track but expensive)**

Sreapo is a high quality national chip simulation track with the following core advantages:

1. **The track is correct**: Simulation chips are typical of the long slopes of snow track, with long product life cycles, high customer stickyness and a huge national production replacement space.
2. **Tracker confirmed**: from two consecutive years of loss to 173 million in 2025, the end of the cycle has passed.
3. ** Double-wheel drive**: equal development of signal chain and power management, with a particularly rapid increase in power management.

But the core issue is:

1. ** Valued severe overdraft**: 236 times PE, 19 times PS, prices that adequately reflect high growth expectations for the next 2-3 years.
2. ** Low profit margin**: 8.1% net interest rate indicates that the company is still in the "scaffle size" phase, with a significant 30% + net interest rate gap from TI.
3. ** The gap with the global tap is significant**: the number of materials is only 4% of the T-T, high end products are missing and catch-up routes are long.

Sreapo is a good company to follow, but the current price is not a good time to buy. The ideal purchase window should be at a lower industry cycle or when the company is not growing as fast as expected, leading to a return to valuation below 100 times PE.
