# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Single-product dependency risk (high)

Zebdenny accounted for 74.3% of product revenues in 2025 and 73.4% of total revenues in 2026, with Q1 still at 73.6%, without improvement. This highly concentrated product structure means that any adverse factors affecting Zebdenny’s sales will have a direct impact on the overall performance of the company.

# 4.2 Patent cliff schedule (medium-term risk)

- Zebdenny Patent for compounds: Expired April 2034
- Adaptive patented communities: due in June 2027, December 2031, April 2035
- Simulation and recovery: generics on the United States market cannot be sold by 2037
- ** Summary: ** 2034-2037 is the critical time window. The company has about 8-11 years to foster a second growth curve

# 4.3 Geopolitical risks (high, uncontrollable)

This is the biggest uncertainty hanging over the state of the Pixia:
- ** Income exposure:** More than half of the revenue comes from the United States market
- **Policy risk:** The US Biosafety Act does not directly restrict the availability of medicines, but may significantly increase the difficulty of the follow-on pipeline in the US.
- **extreme scenario:** If China-US relations deteriorate sharply, resulting in Zebdeni being restricted in sales in the United States, the company will lose its largest source of income

** But on the other hand:** Zebdeni has become the standard treatment program for the United States CLL/SL, and a large number of American patients are in use, and the ban on sales directly harms patients, so that extreme scenarios are unlikely to occur. But this risk cannot be completely eliminated and can not be hedged.

## 4.4 Competition risks (medium)

- A fixed-term programme for Acodoni + Vienekla may encroach on the BTK continuous treatment market
- Non-combined BTK inhibitors (Pirtobrutinib) competition against BTK drug-resistant patients
- New technology routes for BTK CDAC (protein degradation) may pose a threat of future substitution

##4.5 Management assessment

** Founder and CEO Oyler:** Co-founder of the University of Pixie-Zone, a techno-entrepreneurs, who created and sold BioDuro in 2010. The advantage is that the vision and implementation of globalization has led the University to create the world's BTK first breed from scratch; but as a CEO of non-scientific origin, companies rely more on externally imported R&D capabilities.

** CEO and Coo Wu Xiaobin:** Former Pfizer China National Manager, led by Pfizer China, has seen a significant growth in business.

** Concerns at the governance level:** The three-way listing of the State of Chismati (Cayman registered), the fragmented equity structure and the accumulated loss of over $60 billion, and the recovery of the interests of historical shareholders remains a long-standing problem.

# 4.6 Maximum downside risk

1. ** US Policy Strike**: If the Biosafety Act or subsequent policy limits the operations of the state of Chihuahua in the United States, 50% + revenue will be cut off directly. This is a low probability, high impact black swan event
2. ** Zebdeni growth peaked + second growth was extremely unwieldy**: if Zebtuné ' s growth rate fell to single digits in 2027-2028 and Sotokla failed to contribute significant income, the valuation could have been double-dip Davis
3. ** Fixed-time long therapy to destabilize the treatment continuum**: if the Acoteoni + Vinezra programme is widely adopted, the BTK stand-alone treatment market may contract

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# Qualitative judgment

Chijuju is the most successful case in the globalization of China’s innovation drug firm, Zebdeni is the most successful innovation in China’s drug company. The first profit in 2025 marks the company’s entry from the "frigerating money" to the "harvesting period."

** The core contradiction is: how long and how large can this "harvest" last?**

In a optimistic scenario, Zebudini’s peak sales were out of $6-8 billion, Sotokla’s successful relay, and the company grew into a global Top 20 drug company. In a pessimistic scenario, Zebdini’s growth slowed, geopolitical risks erupted, or fixed-time therapy was used to destabilize markets, turning the company into a "one-size, yellow-no-go-to-go" generic drug company.

# Valuation judgement #

The current market value of A shares of $38.85 billion corresponds to about 70-84 times the PE in 2026, implying very high growth expectations. Even considering that companies are in a profit-a-release period, this valuation level is already largely optimistic and the security margin is inadequate.

** The difference in valuations is a matter of concern:** Port shares are 35% discounted, and the US shares are more discounted, suggesting that mature market investors are more cautious about the company’s valuation. If they are to invest in the state of Chisinau, the port or US shares may be a more reasonable entry point.
