# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Strong cyclical risk (maximum risk)

** The storage industry is the most cyclical sub-industry in semiconductors, none of which.** The record record of the record is perfect: in 2023, the huge loss of 624 million, and in 2026Q1 a single season, 2.9 billion — more than two years from the bottom to the peak, with profit margins exceeding 3.5 billion yuan.

** The company’s “store model” has further magnified the cyclical fluctuations.** The 12 billion yen inventory (2026Q1) and the $1.5 billion lock-down bill mean that the company has put a large amount of chips on the bet “sustain price keeping high.” If the judgement is correct, the profits will be extremely high; if the price turns, the stock will be impaired and the profits will be consumed.

** History lessons:** In 2023, the down-cycle was a huge loss for the hype; the gyrolong also suffered a huge loss. This is not a "if" problem, but a "when".

##4.2 Client and vendor concentration risk

**Supplier:** The top five suppliers buy about 60%. Crystal raw materials are highly dependent on a small number of plants upstream, and companies have limited bargaining power in the chain.

**Crown:** Meta’s share in the AI endside storage operations may be higher (unprecisely disclosed), with income volatility risk associated with dependence on a single large client. In addition, PC pre-loading customers such as Hewlett-Packard, WFS, etc. have strong bargaining positions.

# 4.3 Competition and patent disputes

** The patent attack on Jiang Poloon.** The competitor Jiang Poloone filed a patent infringement action for the EMC standard through the affiliated company Emtel, claiming $50 million for the core product of the Singe-MMC series. The litigation occurred during the key IPO window period in Hong Kong, and the timing was sensitive. While the claim is small relative to the company, if the product was at risk of being sold, the impact would be far greater than the compensation itself.

** Industry competition patterns:** In the area of domestic storage modules, Jiang Polo is the most direct competitor. The two companies have a high degree of overlap in product lines, client groups and development paths, and competition may increase further in the future.

# 4.4 Corporate governance and management

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# Core judgment

The sung-wire storage is a typical strong-cycle growth unit, currently in the performance outbreak of the "supercycle" storage. Companies have built up a pre-existing advantage in the field of ePOP/AI glasses on the side of the AI, and advanced containment capacity constitutes a technical barrier. The performance of 2026Q1 single-quarter net gain of nearly $2.9 billion is impressive.

** This is not a company with a deep moat.** Its core business model – the procurement of crystal rounds, the sealing of manufacture, and the marketing module – is essentially a relatively limited intermediate in the storage of value added in the chain of industries. The company’s excess profits are derived mainly from two short-term factors: the boom in storage prices and the leveraging of the active stocking. None of these factors are sustainable.

** From a value investment perspective, the serenity is better suited to be the subject of a cycle rather than the long-term holding of the subject.** The $12 billion inventory, the negative cash flow, the 64% asset liability ratio, and the historical performance pattern of a great loss and a sudden turnover of profits are far from the "good business" standard.

** Is the current market value of $144.2 billion reasonable?** If the total annual net profit for 2026 reached $10-13 billion (optimistic scenario), the equivalent of 10-14 times the PE is not overestimated from a cyclical equity perspective. If the storage price begins to fall in the second half, the total annual profit for 2026 may be well below the annualized level of one quarter, the current market value of the profits of $3-5 billion would be significantly overestimated.

The sang-wire storage is a high-elastic feature of the supercycle, and the AI side storage has a real structural increase, but the 12 billion dollar inventory is loaded, strong-cycle attributes and limited moats make it more like a high-cost cycle speculation than a Buffett-style comfort hold — if you cannot judge the point of the cycle, it is better to keep the distance.
