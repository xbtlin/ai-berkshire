- Time Electric is one of the first IGBT-producing enterprises in the country, with deep-deep IGBT over 20 years of pressure, with a whole-of-industry chain capability ranging from chip design, crystal circle manufacturing to modular envelopes.

- Seventh generation IGBT technology is available to achieve global mainstream; SiC MOSFET has developed into fourth generation gulls and is internationally advanced in performance.

**(2) Client Sticky - "Inner Barrier" to the Medium Car System**

- Car groups of shareholders are the company’s largest customers, with associated transactions accounting for about 47% of the revenue in 2020. While the share of associated transactions has declined in recent years (the number of external customers of emerging businesses has increased), the pattern of high reliance on the medium-car system for railing operations will not change in the short term.
- This is both a moat — competition is difficult to access the Chinese-car supply chain — and a risk — overreliance on a single customer system.
- In the emerging business areas, power-driven systems have successfully entered mainstream vehicle supply chains such as mass, Toyota, universal, single-bump, upper-bumper, and 14 vehicles have been installed, with a cumulative capacity of over 2 million vehicles, over 70 per cent of mainstream vehicle partnerships and substantial progress in client diversification.

**(3) Capacity Barrier - 10 billion class asset input**

- Time Electric has invested over 10 billion yuan in semiconductor capacity: the Chiju Base is planning to produce 360,000 IGBTs per year, and the Iki-King Base is producing 360,000 IGBTs per year, with a combined capacity-scale national leader.
- 23 new production lines were created in 2022, 25 new production lines were added in 2023, with capacity to climb the slopes quickly.
- This heavy asset input constitutes a natural barrier to small and medium-sized competitors, but also means that the utilization of capacity is a key variable.

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Track transaction growth ceiling

- The domestic rail market is saturated, and the construction of the new lines has reached a peak. The harvest of the railway plates in 2025 was only 8 per cent, well below 26 per cent of the new equipment.
- The company has a share of about 50 per cent in the municipal electricity supply in the country, with limited scope for improvement.
- The growth of the tradable business is more dependent on the preservation of markets and overseas exports than on the creation of new productive capacities.
- ** But the value of the transaction is not in the speed of growth, but in stability** — it provides a high-precision profit and cash flow base, reducing the risk to the company as a whole.

##4.2 Power semiconductor price risk

- The competition in the area of low- and medium-pressure IGBT is intense: in the context of domestic expansion, the Stag, Biade, Slanwa and others are expanding their production capacity, and price wars have taken place in the area of medium- and low-pressure.
- The fall in the net interest rate of the company in the third quarter of 2025, with management clearly indicating that it was mainly affected by the price shocks of low- and medium-pressure products.
- The price boom in the start-up power semiconductor in 2026 (difficulting supply and demand imbalances) has helped improve profitability in the short term, but the medium- and long-term price trends are still dependent on supply and demand patterns.
- **Early Power's comparative advantage**: There are few competitors in the IGBT (3300V+) area and there are little price-war pressure; however, the medium- and low-pressure area (car regulation, photovoltaic) faces direct competition with the Star Semiconductor, British Flying Bull.

##4.3 Client concentration and associated transaction risk

- **Cars Group dependence**: sales of association sales to the Midcar Group accounted for 42-48 per cent of revenues in 2018-2020, although the proportion of new business expansion has declined in recent years, the track trade continues to be highly dependent on parent companies.
- If the Chinese and Chinese groups adjust their procurement strategies or introduce competitive suppliers, they will directly impact on the core corporate income.
- **New Energy Vehicle Client Risk**: The bad debt of the United Cars (where) was a warning. The difficulties of the United Motors since October 2024, the arrears of suppliers, and the forced bad debt preparation of the Times Electric, led to a loss in the power-driven business in 2025.
- The good news is that companies are optimizing their client structure, with mainstream car companies working together accounting for more than 70 per cent, reducing their dependence on a single customer.

##4.4 Receivable risk

- The net profit from receivables/motherhood is 386 per cent, which is significant.
- The transhipment industry is characterized by a long repayment cycle for customers (railway, metro companies, etc.), resulting in high receivables.
- The Merchant Car Bad Business Incident shows that customer credit management is weak in the emerging business expansion.
- ** Monetary funds/current liabilities are only 42.6 per cent** and short-term solvency is generally available, with the pressure on cash flows rapidly magnified when large clients are delayed in their return payments.

# 4.5 Low capital return

- ROPE of 8.7 per cent and ROIC of 8.67 per cent are below the standard of 15 per cent plus good manufacturing.
- The heavy asset model (with a power of more than 10 billion semiconductor line inputs) has reduced the rate of return on capital.
- If the capacity utilization rate does not increase rapidly, the depreciation of large fixed assets will continue to suppress the profit margin.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** "No gains in productivity" + the double blow revealed by the concentration of bad accounts receivable due to excess power semiconductor capacity**

The situation is: if domestic IGBT/Sic production were to be released centrally in 2026-2027, the price of low- and medium-pressure products was more intense, the Māori power semiconductor business rate fell by 5-10 percentage points from current levels; and if there were a further major customer misbehaviour similar to the motor vehicle fleet (taking into account the ongoing phase-out in the new energy automobile industry), the accumulated loss of impairment of receivables could result in a net profit increase falling to a single number or even a decline. Under the current 18-fold PE level, the lower space would be about 20-30% (rep. 13-14 times PE x net profit).

# V. Consolidated conclusions

# Core judgment

Time electrical is a rare indicator of stability and growth. Track trade provides a solid base of profit (55%, 50% in the domestic market) and power semiconductor operations (which generate $5.36 billion, 30% more) create a "second growth curve" that is well-established. Companies’ domestic exclusivity in high-pressure IGBT, complete chip-module industry chains, and client barriers to the medium-carrying system constitute competitive advantages that are difficult to replicate.

** At the valuation level, 18 times the PE + 0.9 times the PEG is a relatively reasonable or even low level in the creation board.** The segment valuation method shows about 20% of the up-front space. But the core variables that need to be warned are: (1) whether price wars for low-pressure products in power semiconductors erode profit margins; (2) the large volume of accounts receivable and the risk of bad debts cannot be ignored; and (3) the low ROE/ROIC reflect the need to verify capital efficiency.

** The company's investment logic is essentially a story of "State-owned substitution plus capacity realization"**: if the 8 inch SiC line is successfully operational, the rules of IGBT city is continuously rising, and the profits of the semiconductor operations are rising steadily, the current valuation is reasonably low; conversely, if excess capacity leads to increased price wars and continued ROE depression, the current valuation is not characterized by a clear security margin.

# Whether it's worth the deep-stalking

** It is worth tracking.** In the Science and Technology Creation Panel, the Times is a rare combination of high market value ($74.9 billion), strong certainty (trading base) and high growth (power semiconductor 30% acceleration). 18 times the PE valuation is "value-invested friendly" pricing in the Science and Innovation Panel. But before the warehouse is constructed, the following signals are needed to confirm:

1. A steady recovery in power semiconductor operations
2. Progress in the production of the 8-inch SiC line is in line with expectations
3. Receivable/bad debt risk is manageable
