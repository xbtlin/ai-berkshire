# I. Business models and barriers to competition

## 1.1 Main battalion operations dismantled

The China Peak Survey was established in 1993 and the Automated Test System for Deep Farming Semiconductor (ATM) has been operating for nearly three decades, with the main business being the development, production and marketing of the ATM system.

** Three product lines:**

- ** Simulation and Model Mixed Tester (Farm Products)** Representative model STS 8200 series, mainly for simulation chip, array chip, separation device, power module, third generation semiconductor (silicon carbon/nitrogenide) tests. This is the core source of company revenue, with STS 8300 representing about 34-35% of the total number of orders in 2025.
- ** Large-scale SoC test machine (second growth curve)** Representative model STS8600, launched in June 2023, using a new software architecture and distributed multi-workspace control system, with additional test access and higher frequency, testing large-scale SoC chips such as the CPU/GPU/AI chip in the Edward V. As of early 2026, it was still in the client validation phase.
- ** Power module test system**: Covers traditional silicon-based power devices and third-generation broadband restricted band semiconductor tests, with industry leading skills in smart power module testing.

**The total order for 2025 was approximately $1.4 billion** and the company has installed more than 7,500 test equipment globally and has more than 350 clients.

# 1.3 Array analysis

** Technological barriers (strong):**
- Autonomous intellectual property rights in four core technical areas: precision voltage (V/I source), precision voltage measurement, broadbanding semiconductor testing and smart power module testing
- The semiconductor test equipment industry has long R & D cycles and difficult validation, and new entrants are unlikely to break through in the short term

** Customer certification barrier (strong):**
- Semiconductor testing systems require long client cycle validation to import, and once validated, they create strong stickyness
- SoC testing systems are more complex and have a longer validation cycle (STS8600 validation has been over six months)
- High cost of client switching and test procedures need to be redeveloped and optimized for specific equipment

** Alternative card position advantage (core):**
- In the context of the Sino-American technology game, the domestic chip industry has a strategic rigidity in its demand for national production test equipment
- The company has completed a national replacement card in the field of simulation tests, and the SoC is catching up.
- Benefit from autonomous policy drive of the domestic semiconductor industry chain

** Shortcomings:**
- The product lines are narrow in coverage and technical gaps remain compared to the one in the Tereida, Edwan.
- There is uncertainty in the SoC test field from validation to mass-scale production

#1.4 Product upgrade path

The company is escalating along the path of "mocking and molten digital models and mass SoC":

- ** Completed**: The field of simulation and combination of model testing machines has gained absolute advantage, with the domestic municipalities accounting for 60-70%
- ** In progress**: STS8600 for large-scale SoC chip tests such as AI/CPU/GPU, in-depth collaboration with the companies of sealight information, wall rim technology, moored circuits and other countries for the validation of the computing chip
- **Key nodes**: 2026 is the key observation window for STS 8600 on whether bulk import can be achieved, and success will mark the strategic leap from the simulation test to the SoC test
- **Assured market**: SoC test machines are in demand for approximately 200-250 units per year, with a single station value of about $10 million, corresponding to the annual market space of $2-25 billion

# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Product-line concentration risk (medium)

Companies rely mainly on simulation and array test machines, with relatively single product lines and limited market-based breakdowns. While the SoC testing field is expanding, STS8600 is not yet commercially available on a scale and remains highly dependent on traditional simulation test machine operations in the short term.

##4.2 Semiconductor cycle volatility risk (high)

The impact of cyclical fluctuations has been amply demonstrated by the sharp decline in performance in 2023 (a 35.5% decline in revenue and a 5.2% decline in net profits). Semiconductor industries are closely linked to macroeconomics, and corporate performance may fluctuate considerably if the global economic slowdown or the industry’s climate is down.

#4.3 SoC tests do not expand to the expected risks (critical risks)

STS 8600 is the company ' s most important second growth curve, but there is considerable uncertainty from technology validation to bulk import:
- The validation cycle is long and has not been completed for over six months.
- The technological gap with mature products such as Edwan V93000 takes time to bridge
- "From story to performance, three mountains are produced between technology validation, customer import and scale."
- If SoC's expansion fails, the company will be trapped in a relatively limited market for simulation test machines.

## 4.4 International competitor response (medium)

If the process of national substitution accelerates, the overseas giants may counter it by lowering prices and strengthening services. But, in the context of the US-China technology game, national production substitution is policy-critical, and the risk is relatively manageable.

# 4.5 Overseas markets are not expanding as expected (medium)

Overseas revenues fell by 36.5% in 2024, from 12.7% to 6.2%, and the overseas market contracted markedly. While companies have been deployed in Japan, Malaysia, and the United States, the path to internationalization is challenging, and geopolitical risks may affect overseas expansion.

# 4.6 Management and corporate governance

** Positive factors**: The central management (Sun Xiao, Cylin, Xu Jiao, Zhou Peng) controls the company jointly through the Uniting Actors Agreement, holding 30.38% of Tianjin Xin Xinhua shares in the first shareholder. The management has been working for years, and Sun Xiao joined the company in 1997, with a very technical background.
- **Focus**: The old generation of co-actors (Shou-yun Zhang, Toyotung, Wang Xiaoqiang, etc.) has gradually withdrawn from retirement, and the physical structure has been modified. It is important to see if the new generation of management can continue to lead the company in its strategic transition from simulation to SoC.
- **Limitation**: Deduction plans exist for shareholders and actual controllers and require sustained attention.

# 4.7 The biggest downside risk

** The biggest downside risk is a double-kill scene of "SoC test expansion failed + semiconductor cycle down."**

The current market value of $44.3 billion implies that STS 8600 will be successful. If SoC expands below expectations, the company will be confined to a relatively limited market, a simulation tester.

** But the current valuation is not cheap. ** The market value of $44.3 billion corresponds to about 83 times the profit in 2025, implying higher growth expectations. Investors need to weigh "good company" against "reasonable price".

#5.2 Is it worth following in depth

** Conclusion: It is worth continuing in-depth follow-up, but current price purchases require a higher level of security marginality**

This is a good company with a good business model, strong competitive barriers, and too strong financial quality. But good companies are not good investments, and the key is whether prices give enough security margins. It is recommended that it be included in the core watch pool, waiting for the following:

1. Falling valuations due to the rotation of semiconductors
2. STS 8600 certification of progress in short-term stock price adjustments (if fundamentals do not fundamentally deteriorate)
3. Buy-in opportunities arising from systemic declines in the overall market
