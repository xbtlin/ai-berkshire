# 4.2 Geopolitics and export control risks

- Companies and subsidiaries have been added to the "list of entities" in the United States, and the purchase of parts and components is limited.
- In 2025, the United States of America was sanctioned by customs for exporting equipment and faced greater resistance to overseas expansion.
- Further tightening of United States export controls on Chinese semiconductor equipment may limit the ability of the United States to obtain key parts and components while reducing the market space in overseas markets.
- **The other side of the coin**: Geopolitics are also the best wind - the more sanctioned they are, the more demand for national production is. But this "passive benefit" may reach the ceiling at some point.

##4.3 Technological iterative risk

The competitiveness of the advanced process has not yet been fully validated in the United States, which currently covers mainly 28 nm-14nm nodes.
- The technical threshold of the coating equipment is extremely high, and uncertainty remains as to whether the beauty, as a new entrants, can complete the customer certification and deliver the bulk within a reasonable time.
- The skills of the competitor Deans in the area of cleaning are so much more than beautiful that, if Dinesh were to compete down or introduce strategic adjustments to the Chinese market, the rate of Maori would be more than affordable.
