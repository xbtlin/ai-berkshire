# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Risk of fluctuations in the military procurement cycle

Military-industrial orders are clearly planned and cyclical, equipment lists are present in "years and years" and procurement volumes may fall in some years. This is partly the case with the fall in profits in 2021-2022. Companies are hedged against this risk by increasing civilian occupation, but military goods remain dominant in the short term, and the impact of fluctuations in the procurement tempo on performance cannot be ignored.

** Risk level: medium**

# 4.2 Competition in the civil market increases risks

The advantage of the channel of Haikang Miyagi's mother-in-law's Heikang Wai poses a significant competitive pressure in the field of security protection against infrared as the non-refrigerated infrared technology threshold is gradually lowering. The Maori rate has continued to decline from 67% in 2017 to about 47% in 2022, and despite the bottom-up, price competition in the civil market remains a long-term pressure.

** Risk level: medium**

##4.3 Large clients rely on risk

Military operations naturally feature high customer concentration. While customer concentration has improved in recent years as civil operations have expanded, the procurement tempo of large military clients may still cause fluctuations in performance.

** Risk level: medium and low**

# 4.4 Management and corporate governance

**Mahon's stay:** In April 2024, Ma Hong, founder, chairman and general manager, was retained by the National Supervisory Board and resumed his duties in September 2024 after some five months.

** Historical compliance issues:** In 2022, criticisms were reported for the non-standardization of equity incentive costs, reflecting the need to strengthen internal control management.

** Equity incentives:** Companies have implemented restrictive stock incentive schemes that help to bind management and shareholders' interests.

** Risk level: medium height.** Core person risk and governance defects are a significant reduction.

## 4.5 Other risks

- **Geopolitics risk:** Infrared technologies are in the sensitive technology sector and overseas exports may be affected by changes in the international situation
- ** Accounts receivable and inventory risk:** $1.407 billion at the end of 2025, inventory inventory $1.919 billion, resulting in a total impairment loss of $378 million, which would erode profits if the repayment was not forthcoming or inventory was difficult to de-draw
- **Technology risk:** Infrared chip meta-sizes continue to shrink, and if competitors make a breakthrough in the next generation, they may weaken the company ' s technological lead.

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** The dramatic rotation of the uniformed procurement rhythms with the governance risks of the core personnel.** If there is a phased contraction in uniformed procurement (e.g., budget adjustment or equipment replacement periods) and a recurrence of uncertainty at the corporate governance level, this could lead to double pressure on performance and valuation. Lessons from 2021-2022 suggest that when demand for uniformed labour slows down, profits can fall by more than 30 per cent in a short period of time.
