# 1.3 Array analysis

** Technological barriers (extremely strong, long chain binary acid area):**
- Biomation process accumulation over 20 years, germ selection and process optimization forming a very high know-how barrier
- The only large commercial enterprise in the world, competition is difficult to replicate in the short term
- A chain-wide autonomous technical system from bottom strains to fermentation processes to purity separation

** Cost barriers (strong):**
- Raw materials on biological law routes are renewable plant resources at lower cost than petrochemical routes
- The scale effect is fully demonstrated, with the capacity level of 100,000 tonnes per year further reducing unit costs
- The combined cost advantage of cassay biology is estimated at 30-40% or more.

** Green Barrier (Increased importance):**
- In carbon neutral and trend, biologically based materials have a natural environmental premium on fossil-based materials
- More and more downstream brands (e.g., automobiles, textiles) require carbon footprint reductions in supply chains
- Carbon emissions from biobased products are only 30-50% of fossilized routes

** Shortcomings:**
- The long-chain binary acid market is relatively limited in size (approximately $5-6 billion globally) and the growth ceiling is clear
- Bio-based polyamide PA56 faces positive competition for PA66 national production, and the logic of substitution is not irreversible
- The Taiwon project is slow and there is uncertainty about the strategic landing.
- downstream application expansion requires a long-term client education and validation cycle

# III. Growing up and valuation

#3.1 Drivers of future growth

** Driver I: LC binary acid continuous release (highest certainty but limited elasticity)**
- Urban coverage of diacid-substitution chemical products continues to rise
- The steady growth of the demand for binary acid in the high-end lubricant, thermal molten glue, etc.
- But annual production has broken over 100,000 tons, and the marginal spaces for further growth are shrinking.
- The global long-chain binary acid market is limited in total, growing the ceiling by about $6-8 billion

** Driver II: Bio-based polyamide PA56 industrialization (most elastic but also most uncertain)**
- The Uzu 100,000-ton production line gradually increased the start rate
- PA56 has a differential performance advantage over PA66 in flame retardation, wetting, dye-prone, etc.
- If the Taiwon project is completed, it will release a huge capacity.
- But face the positive price competition brought about by PA66 nationalization
- downstream application takes time, especially in high-end areas such as engineering plastics

** Driver III: new product expansion**
- The first year of sales of bio-based platinum, which is a significant share of the domestic market
- Extension of Synthetic Biology Platform technology, sustainable development of new varieties

** Driver IV: Carbon neutral and policy dividend**
- Global carbon emission regulations are tightened, and the replacement of fossil-based materials by biologically based materials is a major trend
- Policies such as the EU Carbon Border Control Mechanism (CBAM) will further promote demand for bio-based products
- The "green supply chain" of downstream branders requires continuous enhancement.

# IV. Risk assessment

#4.1 PA56 Industrialized risk (core risk)

The industrialization of bio-based polyamide PA56 is the company ' s biggest strategic gamble, but faces multiple challenges:
- **PA66 national production competition**: China Peak Group, China Chemical etc. have made a breakthrough in the national production of Xindium, with PA66 prices falling from 25,000 to 18,000 yuan/tonne, narrowing the cost gap with PA56
- ** Slow downstream spread**: PA56 was well-established in low-end areas such as civil wire, but it would take time to replace PA66 in high value added areas such as engineering plastics
- **Pan-Yuan extension**: 500,000 tonnes of e-Pydamine+90,000 tonnes of polyamide project delayed due to changes in the campus infrastructure planning and increased uncertainty about the delivery of forward production capacity
- 100,000 tonnes of grade capacity may be at risk of underutilization if PA56 cannot prove the performance and cost advantages relative to PA66

##4.2 Risk of slower growth (high)

The growth logic of companies would be shaken if it was not a temporary slowdown, but a trend slowdown. The long-chain binary acid market ceiling is limited, and PA56 is not connected in time, and growth is likely to be "yellow and yellow" if it is not.

##4.3 Risk of price fluctuations in raw materials and products (medium)

- Price fluctuations in biomass raw materials such as maize affect production costs
- Prices of long-chain dual acid products are influenced by downstream demand and competition
- PA56 prices are affected by PA66 prices, and PA66 price declines directly compress PA56 replacement space

##4.4 Alternative risk to technical routes (moderately low)

While there is no competitor to replace Kessai organisms in the long-chain binary acid field, synthetic biology is a rapidly developing area. If a major breakthrough is made in future in the engineering or process optimization of germs, it may break the technical barriers to Kessai organisms.

##4.5 Tairon project risk (medium)

The total investment in Taiwon large projects is about 20 billion yuan, which, if fully completed, would have significantly expanded capacity. However, the project has been postponed due to the problems of the campus infrastructure, and the following risks are present:
- Further extension of the construction cycle and delayed return on investment
- Post-construction energy digestion problem - whether the market demand for 900,000 tonnes of PA56 is sustained
- Pressure on corporate financial position from large capital expenditures

# 4.6 The biggest downside risk

** The biggest downside risk is the double dilemma of "PA56 industrial failure + long-chain dichotomy growth at the top."** If PA56 fails to win the competition with PA66, Taiwon’s big project could be a huge cost of sinking. At the same time, long-chain binomatic acid markets have limited ceilings, and companies will be trapped in a limited market of $3-4 billion.

The postponement of too large projects has further increased uncertainty.

** 58 times the PE is expensive for 11-16% of the increase in valuation.** Markets offer more premium than current performance growth from the concepts of "synthetic biology" and "carbon neutrality".
