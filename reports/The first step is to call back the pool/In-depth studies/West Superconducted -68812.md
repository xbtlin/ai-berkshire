## 1. Business model and competition barriers

### 1.1 Breakdown of main business

Western Superconductor was founded in 2003 and listed on the Science and Technology Innovation Board in July 2019. It was born out of the Northwest Nonferrous Metals Research Institute and is mainly engaged in the research and development, production and sales of three major categories of high-end new materials.

**Three major business lines:**

| Business Segment | Main Products | Application Fields | 2025H1 Growth Rate |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|
| High-end titanium alloys | Titanium alloy bars/wires/forging blanks | Fourth-generation fighter aircraft, civil aviation engines | +22.06% |
| Superconducting materials | NbTi/Nb3Sn superconducting wires, superconducting magnets | ITER nuclear fusion, medical MRI, magnetic levitation | +65.75% |
| High-performance high-performance alloys | Deformed high-temperature alloys, cast high-temperature alloys | Aerospace engines, gas turbines | +56.68% |

**2025 full year results:**
- Total operating income was 5.237 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 13.55%
- Net profit attributable to parent companies was 840 million yuan, a year-on-year increase of 4.95%
- Non-net profit after deducting was 688 million yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 3.42%

A few key observations:

- **Titanium alloy is the basic revenue base**, accounting for the largest share in the long term, and mainly serves the military aviation field. However, the full-year growth rate in 2025 will slow down significantly (H1 is 22% but Q3 single-quarter revenue growth is only 4.23%), indicating that the pace of military order delivery fluctuates greatly.
- **Superconducting business is the core of differentiation**. The company is the only company in China that has achieved commercial production of superconducting wires. It is also the only company in the world that covers the entire process of NbTi ingots, bars, superconducting wire production and superconducting magnet manufacturing.
- **High temperature alloys are the third growth pole**, with the revenue share increasing from about 10% in 2024 to more than 14% in 2025, the fastest growth rate.
- But please note: Q3 single-quarter net profit attributable to the parent company was only 104 million yuan, a significant year-on-year decrease of 59.44%, and the full-year non-net profit dropped year-on-year, indicating that there are hidden concerns about the quality of earnings.

### 1.2 Competition landscape and technical barriers

**Superconducting materials field (deepest moat):**

The domestic superconducting wire market is almost dominated by one company. Western Superconductivity is the only enterprise in China that can realize mass production of low-temperature superconducting wires. The technology comes from the accumulation of more than 40 years of Northwest Nonferrous Metals Research Institute. Major global competitors include Japan's Jastec, Germany's Bruker, and the United Kingdom's Oxford, but Western Superconductor has advantages in cost performance and supply capacity.

In terms of nuclear fusion catalysis: 2026 is the peak year for ITER TF/PF coil wire delivery. It is expected to deliver more than 300 tons, contributing to an incremental revenue of 150-200 million yuan (high gross profit US dollar orders). In addition, the company is deeply involved in the China Huirui-3 (HL-3) and BEST (China Fusion Engineering Test Reactor) projects.

**High-end titanium alloy field (increased competition):**

| Enterprise | Positioning | Advantage Products |
|------|------|---------|
| Western Superconductivity | Institute Background | Fourth Generation Machine Bars/Wires/Forging Billets |
| Baoti Co., Ltd. | Titanium faucets | Full range of titanium processing materials |
| Western Materials | Both belong to Northwest Institute | Plate/tube (complementary with Western Superconducting) |

Note: There are many competitors in the titanium alloy field, and military customers have strong bargaining power, putting gross profit margins under pressure.

**High temperature alloy field:**

The main competitors include Fushun Special Steel (special steel series) and Tunan Co., Ltd. (leading private sector segment). Western Superconductor is positioned as a high-temperature alloy for aerospace engines, benefiting from the demand driven by domestic large aircraft (C919/C929) and domestic commercial engines (Yangtze River series).

### 1.3 Technology sources and barrier assessment

The company's technology foundation comes from the Northwest Nonferrous Metals Research Institute, which holds about 15% of the shares. The core technical team is headed by Academician Zhang Pingxiang, and the academician effect constitutes a significant barrier in the field of materials. The production process of superconducting wires (multi-core composite wire drawing, heat treatment, etc.) has extremely high barriers and cannot be copied in the short term.

**Barrier Summary:**
- Superconducting material barriers: ★★★★★ (domestic exclusive, global leader)
- Titanium alloy barrier: ★★★☆☆ (There are competitors, but the fourth-generation machine certification barrier is high)
- High temperature alloy barrier: ★★★☆☆ (late start, many competitors)

## 2. Financial quality

### 2.1 Profitability Analysis

| Indicators | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Operating income | 4.61 billion | 4.61 billion | 5.24 billion |
| Net profit attributable to parent company | 800 million | 800 million | 840 million |
| Gross profit margin | ~33% | ~34% | Estimate 33-34% |
| Net profit margin | ~17% | ~17% | ~16% |

Key findings:

- **Revenue growth but profit growth significantly lags behind**: In 2025, revenue will increase by 13.55% but net profit will only increase by 4.95%, and non-net profit will even decrease by 3.42%. Indicates that there is erosion on the cost side or expense side.
- **Structural changes in gross profit margin**: The proportion of high-temperature alloys has increased, but its gross profit margin is lower than that of titanium alloys. Changes in product structure have lowered the overall gross profit margin. Gross profit margin dropped significantly in Q3 single quarter.
- **R&D investment growth**: R&D investment doubled in the first three quarters of 2025, reflecting increased investment in technology, but suppressing profits in the short term.

### 2.2 Balance sheet concerns

**Accounts receivable issues are prominent:**

As of the end of Q3 2025, accounts receivable were 3.147 billion yuan, an increase of 52.80% from the beginning of the year, accounting for approximately 60% of revenue. Military customers (mainly enterprises affiliated with large military industrial groups) have long payment cycles, and the company has a long-standing problem of "selling products but not getting the money back."

**Operating cash flow is weak:**

The net cash flow from operating activities in 2024H1 was 115 million yuan. Although it has improved significantly year-on-year, the quality of cash flow is low compared to the net profit of 800 million yuan. This is a common problem among military materials companies - large accounts receivable and long payment collection cycles.

### 2.3 2026Q1 Early Warning Signals

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was 1.08 billion yuan (a year-on-year increase of only 0.62%), and net profit attributable to the parent company was 43.83 million yuan (a year-on-year decrease of 74.21%). Single-quarter profit margins plummeted to about 4%, well below normal levels. We need to pay close attention to seasonal fluctuations or structural deterioration.

## 3. Growth and Valuation

### 3.1 Growth Drivers

**Short term catalytic (1-2 years):**
- The peak year of ITER superconducting wire delivery (2026) is expected to contribute 150-200 million in incremental revenue
- The military industry's "14th Five-Year Plan" final order delivery
- Release of high-temperature alloy production capacity

**Medium term catalysis (3-5 years):**
- Continued demand for superconducting wires/magnets in controlled nuclear fusion (BEST engineering reactor)
- Mass production of C919/C929 large aircraft drives demand for titanium alloys and high-temperature alloys
- Domestic replacement of civilian MRI superconducting magnets

**Long-term imagination space:**
- Commercial nuclear fusion (if realized) will create a huge incremental market for superconducting wires
- Potential demand for superconducting materials for superconducting quantum computing

### 3.2 Valuation judgment

| Indicators | Values |
|------|------|
| Current stock price | About 67.78 yuan (May 13, 2026) |
| Market value | About 41.2 billion yuan |

| Corresponding to PE in 2026 | About 36-40 times |
| Average target price of brokers | About 81 yuan |

Valuation assessment:

- Calculated based on 2025 profits, the current PE is about 49 times, which is high for a materials company with a growth rate of 13% and declining non-profits.
- But the core logic of the market's premium is "nuclear fusion concept + military industry scarcity + exclusive status", which is a thematic valuation rather than a fundamental valuation.
- If the sudden drop in profits in 2026Q1 extends to the whole year, valuations will rise further.
- On the other hand, if the progress of nuclear fusion accelerates (such as the BEST engineering reactor project), the imagination of the superconducting business will indeed be difficult to measure with traditional PE.

**Conclusion: The current valuation is on the expensive side, and the pricing has fully reflected the concept of nuclear fusion and the scarcity premium of the military industry. If you are concerned, it is recommended to wait for the performance confirmation period (Q2-Q3 data) or the valuation to fall below 35 times before considering it. **

## 5. Comprehensive conclusion

**Investment attractiveness score: ★★★☆☆ (neutral to cautious)**

Western Superconducting is a rare target company with unique technical barriers (especially in the field of superconducting materials). Nuclear fusion catalysis constitutes a long-term imagination space. But we currently face three practical problems:

1. **Profit quality is deteriorating**: The decline in non-profits and the sharp drop in Q3/2026Q1 profits indicate that the logic of high growth has not yet been realized.
2. **High accounts receivable**: The quality of cash flow is poor and the "gold content" of profits is insufficient.
3. **Valuation is expensive**: 49 times PE corresponds to a profit growth rate of less than 5%, which is not cost-effective.

The concept of nuclear fusion is a real long-term catalytic, but from the perspective of investment time, it is not the best buying position right now.

### Key tracking indicators

1. **Q2/Q3 single-quarter profit in 2026**: Confirm whether the sudden drop in profits in Q1 is a one-off or a trend
2. **ITER wire delivery progress and amount confirmation**: Verify the pace of superconducting business growth
3. **Accounts Receivable/Revenue Ratio**: Is there any sign of improvement?
4. **Changes in gross profit margin of high-temperature alloys**: The impact of changes in product structure on profitability
5. **BEST Engineering Reactor Project Progress**: Mid-term catalytic confirmation of nuclear fusion demand
6. **Visibility of military orders**: Pay attention to the signing of annual framework contracts
