# IV. Risk assessment

##4.1 Core risk

**(1) CIS industry cyclical risk**

The CIS industry was affected by downstream consumer electronics (cell phones, security) and the automobile cycle. The industry experienced a severe stock adjustment in 2023, and Statwell’s net profit fell to a very low level. If consumer electronic demand weakened in 2026-2027, the industry could again face price wars and de-stock pressure.

**(2) Uncertainty in high-end CIS **

Stewy is in the middle and low (by-side) of the mobile CIS market, and is facing strong competition from Sony for the penetration of the high-end flag ship masters. Sony’s position in the high-end market is difficult to shake in the short term, thanks to the technical advantages of the IMM model and the depth of the IMM model and of large clients such as apples.

**(3) Māori ceiling**

The 23% Māori ratio is low among chip design companies, reflecting the company’s main positioning of its products in low- and medium-end and value-for-money markets. There is uncertainty that the transition to high-end will significantly increase Māori rates.

**(4) Increased competition for car CIS**

Ansonme, Sony, and Howe are increasing their car-borne ICT inputs. Stewy, though fast, remains under-represented and faces a competitive pattern of strong hand-held companies.

**(5) Limitations of the Fables model**

Dependence on production by surrogate plants (mainly TSMC and domestic sub-plants) may face supply bottlenecks in the event of a shortage of capacity and are also limited to extreme performance optimization in the surrogate plant process.
