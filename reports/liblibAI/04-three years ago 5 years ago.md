# Summarizing core judgements

1. ** Core paradox**: The moat is built on "polymers" but the premise of the mooring is that it needs to be brought together downstream. If the generic big model "one-stop" meets the demand, the polymer value is compressed

2. ** Loveart is the key variable of fate**. The internal ceiling shows that overseas is the only exponential growth direction.

3. **The coronal enforcement power is real plus. ** From "4000" to "valued $1 billion" plus byte-based GMO genes.

4. **The $1 billion valuation is an investment value-fair**. Expected return - 2 and 3.5 x, upper bound to "middle" positioning, lower bound to technological paradigm change.

5. **The window of time is tight**. The transition from "high growth start-up" to "sustainable profitability" must be completed in 2027-2028, otherwise the meso-values will continue to erode.
