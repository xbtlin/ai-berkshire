# 4. Summary of the analysis of dimensions

# Business model (in a constant view)

1. **The revenue engine has moved from home to overseas** — The SaaS subscription in Loveart (ARPU about $25-35 months) has become the largest source of revenue, with the domestic platform ARPU only about $50/month, with a gap of about 25 times.
2. ** Three-tier pyramid income structure** - individual subscription (domestic) + enterprise API + overseas Saa (Lovart), the fastest growing and the highest-profit-rate growth
** Community network effects exist but are not deep** — 500,000 plus models are valuable assets, but models can be released across platforms and base models can depreciate LoRA
** There is little domestic pricing rights** – 29 yuan/month price indicates a sharp price battle, with by-products of free/low price shocks.
**Pension 5/10** - Disparity (community+Agent) but lack of deep moat and pricing power

# Finance and valuation (Buffett perspective)

1. ** Combined ARRs of approximately $50-60 million** (below 2025) with a projected impact of $100 million in mid-2026
2. **GPU reasoning costs are the largest cost** - 35-45% of income, and Māori rates are about 47-60%, significantly below 75% + of the traditional SaaS
** Proximate annual loss of $20-$50 million** ** in the "burn money for growth" phase
4. ** Round B covers approximately US$ 130 million for 18-40 months** – approximately C by 2027
** Probability weighted valuation of approximately $2.5-$30 billion (in 2027)** — moderate scenario 2.7-3.2 times return, pessimistic scenario likely to lose

# Industry and competition (Manger perspective)

1. **AI image generation market, approximately $15.2 billion in 2026, 33% annual increase** ** large and fast track
2. ** LiblibAI 8-10th ** After Canva/Adobe/Midjourney/Runway/Dream/Purpose
3. **The Chinese market ranks 3-4 ** — after dream and Quasi (quick hand) but community model is first
4. **Community model vs Tool model is core differentiation** — half a million model communities in LiblibAI are like "GitHub in the A.I. Field", which is not possible for a purely tool.
**Munger final judgment** ** "Tell me when its unit economy model will be right, and I'll decide whether to place a bet."

# Risk and management (Li Su)

1. **Conical Capability Circle is clear: Commercialization of products** - 28 bytes 4-1, Loveart 30 million ARR 4 months, four rounds of financing per year.
2. **A decade-six reading** ** More like "Step learner after a wave," bytes showing him to be home.
3. ** "4000 on the books" is a double signal** – resilience (refusal to sell) + poor cash management (a few months of the Angel wheels burning). Will it be repeated after Round B?
4. **Maximum risk: technology may be wiped out overnight** – 500,000 LoRAs and workflows may be as worthless as "the dark room technology of the film age" if a new subversive model emerges.
**Li Siu will not vote** – not because the company is bad, but because "it's impossible to tell if Liblibai exists 10 years later."

** Loveart growth rate is rare worldwide** — 0 :30 million ARR for 4 months, after ChatGPT in AI applications only
** Community flying wheels are real assets** — 25 million users + 500,000 models + 500 million pictures, and new entrants are difficult to copy from zero

** Founder Implementation Validation** — 4,000 live bills, four rounds of financing per year, byte 4-1 background
**AI Design Agent is a new class** – Loveart defines the "Design Agent" class with a prior advantage.
**The cost of GPU has been declining steadily** – The cost of reasoning has decreased by more than 90% over the past year, and the Māori rate has had room for structural improvement
**Redwood + Ant endorsement** — Top VC entry, secured financing and resource support

1. **The fragile moat** – without basic modelling, relying on open-source ecology, technological overlaps may be reversed overnight
** Large factory crush** — bytes of dream tremors + map users + self-reflection models + unlimited GPUs, with long-term loss subsidy
3. ** "The bigger the loss"** – 30 million ARRs reported "uncoverable operating costs" and the more users the higher the GPU costs
** Domestic pricing rights are zero** - 29 yuan/month uncompetitive in front of by-by-word free/low prices
** Loveart ' s acceleration sustainability is questionable** — Mask ' s pointer is a one-time dividend, with retention and repurchase data not disclosed
**Closed domestic and foreign markets** – Two markets for three product lines, with management complexity being a great test for 200-400 people
** The exit path is unclear** — IPO needs to be profitable, founder refuses to be acquired, level I market is less mobile

#8. Summary

LiblibAI is an exciting but disturbing company.

The excitement is that the 30 million ARR over four months is a true product market match, that the community ecology of 25 million users is a real asset, and that the implementation of the crown is the highest among the Chinese AI entrepreneurs.

The worry is that the moat of this business is essentially a ** lot of LoRA models and user habits** – the former may be eliminated from new technologies, the latter from large factories, and the latter from free products. The $1 billion valuation means that you are paying for an AI-designed Agent to be the mainstream category and Liblibai the winner. This hypothesis may be valid, but far from certain.

** The standard for a long-term track: this is not a good business (lack of protection of the river and pricing). The criterion for a barfette: this is not a good price (17 times the PS in a loss state). The criterion for a mang: this is a good track but the outcome is uncertain. The criterion for a Lee Xing is: it cannot be determined if it is still there after 10 years.**

If you're VC, it's a risk-benefit ratio to a reasonable B/C. If you're a value investor, look at it when it's profitable.
