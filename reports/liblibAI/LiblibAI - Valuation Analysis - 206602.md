LiblibAI is not a single product company, but a dual-engine structure of domestic community + overseas agent:

## 4. Valuation Judgment: Three Scenarios

### Core Assumptions

- Valuation method: PS multiple method (standard method for unprofitable AI companies)
- Baseline: Projected ARR by the end of 2026
- Reasonable PS range: 12-25 times (refer to the median of comparable companies, which is about 16 times)

### Scenario analysis

| Scenario | ARR at the end of 2026 (estimated) | Reasonable PS | Reasonable valuation | Corresponding to the current US$1 billion |
|------|-------------------|--------|---------|----------------|
| **Optimistic** | US$150 million | 20-25x | **30-3.75 billion US$** | Undervalued 2-3x |
| **Neutral** | $100 million | 15-20x | **$1.5-2 billion** | Undervalued 50-100% |
| **Pessimistic** | US$60 million | 10-15 times | **US$600-900 million** | Reasonably expensive |

### Optimistic scenario assumption

- Lovart ARR from 30 million → 120 million US dollars (4 times growth, considering low base + global expansion + Xingliu Agent)
- LiblibAI platform commercialization efforts, contributing US$30 million
- Approximately US$150 million in total
- Give 20-25 times PS (high growth + track leader premium)
- **Reasonable valuation: US$3.0-3.75 billion**

### Neutral scenario hypothesis

- Lovart ARR from 30 million → 80 million US dollars (competition intensified, growth rate dropped to 160%)
- LiblibAI platform contributes US$20 million
- Approximately US$100 million in total
- Give 15-20 times PS (medium growth rate, unprofitable discount)
- **Reasonable valuation: US$1.5-2 billion**

### Pessimistic scenario hypothesis

- Lovart's growth encounters bottleneck, ARR stays at US$50 million
- LiblibAI platform has difficulty monetizing, contributing US$10 million
- Approximately US$60 million in total
- Give 10-15 times PS (growth rate decreases, moat is questionable)
- **Reasonable valuation: US$600-900 million**

## 5. Core risk factors

### 1. The depth of the moat is questionable (the biggest risk)

The technical barriers to AI design tools are rapidly dissolving:
- Adobe Firefly, Canva AI, and Figma AI are all accelerating to catch up.
- ByteDance’s Jimeng AI has nearly 10 million monthly visits
- Rapid iteration of the model layer means that the window for “tool differentiation” may be short
- Is LiblibAI’s moat **community ecology** or **product experience**? If it is the latter, it is only a matter of time before the big manufacturers catch up.

### 2. The profit path is unclear

- Behind Lovart’s 30 million ARR, what are the GPU inference costs, customer acquisition costs, and team costs?
- The gross profit margin of AI applications is usually 40-60% (a large amount of computing power is consumed), which is much lower than the 70-80% of traditional SaaS
- The larger the scale, the greater the loss is likely to be - unless there is pricing power

### 3. Fragmentation of domestic and foreign markets

- Lovart's overseas success does not equal Xingliu Agent's domestic success - the domestic design market is even less willing to pay.
- Two markets require two operating systems, doubling management complexity

### 4. Founder style risk

- Chen Mian has changed six companies in ten years, and his long-term operating ability needs to be tested
- The transformation from "Lightning Entrepreneur" to "Long-Term Operator" has not yet been proven

### 5. Primary market liquidity risk

- Unlisted company valued at US$1 billion with limited exit channels
- If Series C financing or IPO does not go smoothly, early investors may face liquidity difficulties

## 6. Conclusion

### Current valuation (after Series B investment): approximately US$1 billion

### Reasonable valuation range

| Perspective | Valuation range |
|------|---------|
| Static (based on ARR at the end of 2025) | US$8-1.5 billion |
| Dynamic (based on expected ARR by the end of 2026) | **1.5-2 billion US dollars** (neutral) |
| Optimistic upper limit | $3.0-3.75 billion |
| Pessimistic lower limit | $600-900 million |

### Core judgment

**The current valuation of US$1 billion is cheap under neutral assumptions, but not extremely undervalued. **

Reason:
1. Lovart’s growth rate is indeed outstanding (0 → 30 million ARR in 4 months), proving product market fit
2. The combination of dual product lines (community + agent) is a differentiated advantage in the AI creation track
3. The endorsements of Sequoia, CMC and Ant provide a certain credit premium
4. However, the AI tool track technology iterates extremely fast, and today’s lead does not guarantee tomorrow’s barriers.
5. Unprofitable + high GPU cost means that the risk of "the bigger the bigger the bigger the loss" is real.

**One sentence**: If Lovart can prove its ability to sustain growth in 2026 (ARR exceeds US$100 million), US$1 billion will be a bargain; if growth slows down and competition intensifies, US$1 billion will be a high-price acquisition.
