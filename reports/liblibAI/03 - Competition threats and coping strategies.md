# II. Threat depth analysis

## Threat 1: General Large Model Original Image Generation (GPT-4o/5/5.5, Gemini)

** Threat level: lethal level**

# Current power boundaries #

- ** Covered **: social drawings, preliminary marketing posters, product concept maps, simple electricity master charts, emoticons, text rendering (a near perfect accuracy), basic photo level realism (87% persuasion rating)
- ** Not yet covered: IP role consistency control, fine style customisation (LoRA level reduction), complex workflow (ControlNet exact posture), batch production, 3D asset generation

##GPT-5/5 will support LoRA-level fine control?

** Not in the short term (2026-2027). OpenAI closed the new user registration for the Fine-turing in May 2026, with the strategic direction being "general model plus hint control" instead of "user-defined fine-tuning".

** There are variables in the medium term (2028-2029)**. If the original image is generated with a hint, 80% of the LORA style is reduced, the value of the LiblibAI model community will be significantly eroded.

# Judgement: Chronic erosion is the main, but there is a "step leap" risk

Every 6-12 months model updates gradually compress the survival space of professional tools.** The most dangerous scenario**: a generation of models suddenly supports "Role Coherence+Figure Reference Map+More Charts" and will constitute a sudden replacement. This risk was highest between 2027-2028.

# Three: Response strategy analysis

# # Strategy 1: Agent Transition (Lovart/Stellar/LibTV)

What is Liblibai actually doing?

- ** Loveart**: 10 million + Users, 70 + Country, ARR ~ 80 million US$
- **Stellar Agent**: domestic version of Loveart, with access to more than a dozen large models, supporting image/video/sound/3D one-stop generation
- **LibTV**: Launched March 2026, 100,000 users poured in on the first day of the line, ** one month of revenue a day over $1 million**

# Judgement: Right in the right direction, but at risk of "two lines of action"

** Correct reasons**:
1. Upgrading the tool layer to the work troposphere to enhance value chain location and stickivity
2. Addressing the pain of "whoever the user chooses."
3. LibTV income of $1 million a day proves commercialization of videoAgent

**Risk**:
1. Adobe launched Firefly AI Assistant in April 2026, integrating the 30+ Top-AI model
2. The simultaneous operation of four product lines is under severe pressure on Company B
3. The Agent intelligent ceiling is subject to non-self-researched modelling

# # advice #

- Domestic product line integration: star current Agent+LibTV unified to Creative ProductionAgent
- Enhanced "modeling" differentiation: Agent automatically selects the optimal model combination

# Strategy 2: Out of the sea strategy (Lovart)

** Part of the domestic pressure can be hedged, but competition is also intense overseas**

Advantages: competition in overseas tracks is less intense than domestic; Civitai has fallen due to payment crisis; price advantage ($90/month vs Design Co. 6 digits)

Advantages: Adobe Firefly enterprise level integration far beyond Loveart; Midjourney V7/V8 quality lead; data security concerns; geopolitical risks

** Proposal**: Focus on "Automated full-chain design for SMEs/independent creators"; Localization of core markets (South-East Asia, Japan and Korea); Consideration of independent operation and financing of overseas operations
