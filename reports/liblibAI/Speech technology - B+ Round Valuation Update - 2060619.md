# One word to the conclusion

> Speech technology jumped from ARR ~ $100 billion to $300 million in six months, and LibTV’s flash growth redefined the growth curve of the company. The B+ Wheel $2 billion valuation corresponds to the current ARR only 6.7 times more than - a gross underestimation in comparable companies around the globe. But the 3000% increase is unsustainable, the AI video competition pattern is far from stable, and "income quality" and "profit rate" are two key issues that have yet to be answered. Reasonable valuation intervals: **40 billion to 60 billion** (based on ARR by the end of 2026), with up-scaling space dependent on LibTV’s ability to transition from the outbreak period to steady growth.

# Critical risks (not to be ignored)

#1 Quality of income doubtful (high risk)

The company disclosed "ARR," but LibTV has more income than **Expendable/Project ** than non-traditional SaaS subscriptions. Short-play teams pay for the project on the basis of project demand, and the project may not be renewed. If the actual repurchase rate is lower than expected, the ARR "annual" caliber overestimates real income capacity.

** Data missing:** Monthly retention rate, net income retention rate (NRR), client loss rate not disclosed.

##2. Sustainability of LibTV growth

The short play is 95% of the industry's online, and the short play industry itself has a "excessive" signal.
- LibTV's outbreak growth from 0 contains a large one-time "industry migration" dividend - the first attempt of the stock team is the AI tool
- When penetration reaches a certain level, the rate of increase will have to slow down considerably.
- Reference data: AI Short Play Market 2025 ~ 19 billion yuan, 2026 projected ~ 30-52 billion yuan, but the rate of increase has decreased from 276% to ~60-170%

##4. Profit margins and cash consumption

- GPU reasoning costs are about 35-45% of revenue (previous edition estimate), and Māori rates are significantly lower than 75% + of traditional SaaS
- The company has not disclosed its profitability and is still losing at the current rate of expansion
- Cumulative financing of about $500 million, but the GPU costs and the passenger costs during the high growth period may quickly consume cash
- ** If positive cash flows are not possible by 2027, C-round financing may be required**

##5. Chinese market ARPU ceiling # (Medium risk)

Historical data show that the domestic AI tool ARPU is only one-fifth to one-fifth overseas. Even if users are ahead of the scale, if they cannot open overseas markets or raise domestic payment will, the income ceiling will be much lower than comparable offshore companies.

##6. Regulatory risk # (Middle and low risk)

AI generates content that is subject to multi-dimensional regulation in China, such as content censorship, copyright attribution, and deep forgery. The short-play industry itself has content compliance requirements.
