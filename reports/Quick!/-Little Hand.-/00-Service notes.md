## Why write this series?

Kuaishou is one of the most discussed and heavily labeled companies in Hong Kong stocks.

- Some people regard it as the **sinking version of Douyin**-ignoring that its 410 million DAU is still growing, and MAU has reached a record high;
- Some people regard it as a **live broadcast reward company** - ignoring that live broadcast only accounts for 27% of revenue and is actively shrinking, advertising is the real engine;
- Some people regard it as an **AI outsider** - ignoring that Keling is the world's number one video generation model revenue, with a spin-off valuation of US$18 billion;
- Some treat it as a **deep value stock** - ignoring that $26 billion in capex in 2026 is eating up free cash flow;
- Some people regard it as a **Tencent abandonment** - ignoring that Tencent invested in Keling in the same week when it reduced its holdings in Kuaishou.

Each perspective makes sense when viewed individually, but when combined, it is truly fast. Kuaishou’s core questions are fewer and more concentrated than those of Tencent, so instead of writing 8 articles in this series, we only write **3** - each answering one question.

## Data sources and methods

- All based on public information: Kuaishou's 2025 annual report, 2026Q1 results, Hong Kong Stock Exchange disclosures (including 2026-07-02 Keling spin-off announcement, 2025-04-28 shareholder circular), performance meeting records, research reports from various securities firms, and authoritative third-party data
- Cross-validation of key data (at least 2 independent sources); third-party estimates (Douyin, video account data, etc.) are marked one by one.
- Valuation is based on the traditional value investment framework of Buffett/Munger/Duan Yongping/Li Lu
- Data time point is 2026-07-10, and will need to be updated with the financial report in the future.
