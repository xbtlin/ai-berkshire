# Why does the shivering crush the fast hand?

In 2016, the fast hand and the shaking started almost simultaneously. Before 2018, the fast hand was still in the lead. But then the twitch went up and the fast hand was pulled further away.

** The underlying reason is not that products are not good, but that distribution logic is leading to different growth ceilings**

The voice shaker ** pure algorithm recommendation** – any user opens a "recommended page" and the algorithm is the most attractive one. This means that the new user can get an excellent experience by opening the voice shakes for the first time – without looking at anyone, the system is going to give you a "customized entertainment".

Fast-hand** socialization + algorithm mix of recommendations** - "Concentrated Page" has a higher weight, and users need to focus on creators before they can see content on a continuous basis. This is very friendly to older users (community is sticky), but not to new users (cold start experience).

** Result: The number of users of the shivering is "broad and high transformation" -- stay; the number of fast-handed users is "break and high retention" -- not necessarily, but stay.**

The finals of these two models are very different. In China, one billion mobile Internet users have a large disk, a shivering funnel model can eat 700 million DAUs, and a fast-handed community model can eat only 400 million.

# Why is the difference in advertising income five times?

DAU differences are only 1.7 times more than, but the difference in advertising income is five times more — an indication that the gap is not only in the number of users, but also in the quality and commercial efficiency of users**.

Three core causes:

**1. User structure differences.** Shorthand 3-line users up to 60%, and divulges are more visible in second-line cities.

**2. Content-modernity differences.** More brand content, more precision content than the one on the twitch, and more likely brand advertisers to put it in a "modern" environment. Fast-handed grass-roots content, though real, is thought by many brands to be "off-priced."

**3. Commercialized infrastructure differences.** Byte-dip advertising systems (big engines) are one of the most efficient advertising systems in the world, with more than 10 years of optimization. The fast-handed magnetic engines, while also good, still have gaps in the delivery of tools, data loops, ROI tracking, etc.
