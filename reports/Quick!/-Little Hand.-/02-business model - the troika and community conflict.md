# Community paradox

But communities are also the biggest obstacle to fast-hand commercialization.

** Paradox I: Community-modern vs. Advertisement Value.** The fast-track "old iron" culture means platform-modern, sublime -- brand advertisers prefer to put their budgets on a more "high-end" twitch. The fast-hand ad is only 40% of the twitching, and that's the root reason.

There is a natural contradiction between the two -- excessive commercialization can harm the community's ecology and overprotecting the community without making money.
