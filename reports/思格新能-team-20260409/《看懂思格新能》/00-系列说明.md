# Series Guide for "Understanding Sigenergy"

> 4 main articles that break down this solar-storage upstart, which was founded just 4 years ago and surged on listing day.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Sigenergy lacks attention - 1,102x oversubscription, a 103% first-day gain, and a market cap that briefly exceeded **HKD 160 billion** made it the hottest Hong Kong IPO of 2026, without question.

But being hot and being understood are two different things.

- The bulls say Huawei-rooted DNA + the world's No. 1 five-in-one product + 50% gross margin + AI enablement means this is the next CATL
- The bears say the company has only been profitable for 1 year in its 4-year history, 93% of revenue depends on one product, Huawei has already filed a patent lawsuit, and the stock fell from 669 to 519

**Both sides have factual support, yet they reach completely opposite conclusions.**

This series aims to **first explain what it is, and only then let you decide whether it is worth buying** - not to decide for you.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Do not invent data** - source all data, and mark estimates as "estimated"
4. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
