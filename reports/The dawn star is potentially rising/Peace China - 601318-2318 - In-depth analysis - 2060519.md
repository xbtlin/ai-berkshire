## 1. Company overview and business breakdown

Ping An of China is the only comprehensive financial group in China that holds a full financial license, serving 251 million individual customers, with an average number of contracts per customer of 2.94, and a customer retention rate of 99% for products of three types or above.

**Life insurance business (core engine):** NBV reached 36.9 billion yuan (+29.3%), and new business value rate was 28.5% (+5.8pct). There are 351,000 agents (-3.3%), but the per capita NBV is +17.2% year-on-year, and "quality for quantity" continues to be realized. The NBV of the bancassurance channel was 9.4 billion yuan (+138%), becoming the second growth pole. The 13-month policy continuation rate is 97.4%, and the 25-month policy continuation rate is 94.9% (+5.2pct).

**Property insurance:** Original premium income was 343.2 billion yuan (+6.6%), and the combined ratio was 96.8% (-1.5pct). New energy vehicle insurance premiums were 52.5 billion yuan (+39%), with a market share of 27.7%.

**Ping An Bank:** Net profit is 42.6 billion yuan, ROE is about 10.1%, NPL ratio is 1.05%, and net interest margin is 1.78%.

**Technology subsidiaries:** Ping An Health's revenue in 2025 was 5.5 billion yuan, and it achieved a net profit of 380 million yuan for the first time, turning a loss into a profit. Lufax is still in a loss-making transition period.

## 3. Moat assessment

**Scarcity of full licenses (wide moat):** The only financial group that holds full licenses for insurance, banking, securities, trusts, funds, and asset management at the same time. Integrated finance enabled cross-sales to contribute 50.5% of new customer AUM, and customer stickiness far exceeded that of single-business peers.

**Brand and Channel:** Brand value is US$29.6 billion, ranking first in China’s insurance industry for 11 consecutive years. Although the number of agents shrank to 350,000, per capita production capacity continued to increase, and the bancassurance channel exploded.

**Technology investment:** AI agent service volume reached 1.7 billion times (covering 80% of customer service), 94% of life insurance policies were underwritten in seconds, and anti-fraud intelligent claims interception and loss reduction amounted to RMB 10.5 billion.

**Differences from peers:** China Life is better at scale, and CPIC is better at sophistication in property and casualty insurance, but both lack the comprehensive financial closed loop of Ping An. The "one customer, multiple accounts, multiple products" model makes the value of a single customer much higher than that of a pure insurance company.

## 4. Valuation analysis

**P/EV Valuation:**
- The group's EV is 1,504.3 billion yuan, and the total market value of A-shares is approximately 1,010 billion yuan.
- **P/EV is about 0.67 times**, the average since listing is about 1.8 times, and 1.0-1.2 times is the historical bottom
- The current 0.67 times is at an extremely low level in history

**PB valuation:** A-share PB is about 1.0 (breaking the edge of net), and ROE 14% corresponds to a theoretical PB of 1.4-1.8 times.

**International comparison:** AIA P/EV maintains more than 2 times in the long term. Ping An's P/EV is only 1/3 of AIA, but its life insurance ROEV is higher. Even taking into account structural differences, the valuation gap is too wide.

**Morningstar's logic of 91 yuan:** Using a three-stage discount model, the core assumption is that NBV maintains medium-to-high growth, operating ROE maintains 12-14%, and P/EV returns to 1.0 times. 91 yuan corresponds to P/EV of about 1.0 times - a conservative assumption in international insurance valuations.

**Broker forecast:** The average target price of analysts is about 78 yuan (A shares), with a range of 68-90 yuan.

**My valuation range:**
- Conservative estimate (0.8 times P/EV): about 66 yuan
- Reasonable estimate (1.0x P/EV): approximately 82 yuan
- Optimistic estimate (1.2 times P/EV): about 99 yuan
- **Morningstar's 91 yuan is in a reasonable and optimistic range, and the logic is self-consistent**

## 5. Risks and Catalysts

**Core Risks:**
1. **Interest rate loss risk**: If the 10-year government bond yield remains below 2% for a long time, the pressure on existing high guaranteed interest rate insurance policies will gradually emerge. The management stated that "there is no interest rate loss on both new orders and existing orders," but this is the core factor suppressing the valuation of insurance stocks.
2. **Tail risk of real estate exposure**: The proportion of real estate investment has dropped to 3.1% (approximately 200 billion yuan), mainly rent-collecting properties, and substantial provisions have been made in recent years
3. **Banking business is under pressure**: Net interest margin continues to narrow (1.78%), retail loan demand is weak
4. **Technology realization is lower than expected**: Lufax is still losing money, and it is difficult to become a profit pillar in the short term.

**Potential Catalysts:**
1. **Interest rates stabilize or rise**: directly remove the biggest factor suppressing the valuation of insurance stocks
2. **NBV continues to grow at a high rate**: +29.3% in 2025 and +34.9% in 2026Q1
3. **Dividends continue to increase**: 14 consecutive years of growth, 4.7% dividend rate, highly attractive in a low interest rate environment
4. **Medical and elderly care strategy is implemented**: If the two-wheel drive of "comprehensive finance + medical and elderly care" opens up the path to realization, it will open up the valuation ceiling.

---

## 6. Investment Conclusion

**Current price makes it worth buying. **

A comprehensive financial leader with ROE of 14%, NBV growth of nearly 30%, and dividend growth for 14 consecutive years is trading at a net-breaking level (P/EV 0.67 times). This is a typical example of "good companies encountering emotional discounts."

- **Fair value estimate**: 66-82 yuan (A shares), optimistically up to 99 yuan
- **Safety margin**: Calculated at 55.5 yuan, there is 19% room from the conservative valuation of 66 yuan, and 48% room from the reasonable valuation of 82 yuan
- **Dividend yield 4.7%** provides sufficient "waiting reward"
- **Core Judgment**: The fundamental inflection point has been confirmed (high growth in NBV, improvement in cost ratio, recovery in investment income), and there is only one trigger condition for valuation repair - stabilization of interest rate expectations

**Risk Warning:** If China's long-term interest rates fall further sharply (the 10-year government bond falls below 1.5%), or there are unexpected systemic risks in real estate, the valuation logic needs to be re-examined.
