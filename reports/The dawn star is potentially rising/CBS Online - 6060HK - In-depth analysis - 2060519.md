## 1. Company overview and business breakdown

ZhongAn Online was founded in 2013 and was jointly launched by Ant Group, Tencent and Ping An of China. It is the first approved Internet insurance company in China. Current shareholders: Ant Group 10.37%, Ping An of China 10.21%, Tencent 7.82%. It is worth noting that Ant Group has reduced its holdings in two rounds since 2022, and has cashed out more than HK$2 billion in total.

**Four major ecological businesses (full year 2025):**

| Ecology | Total insurance premiums (100 million yuan) | Proportion | Year-on-year growth rate |
|------|------------|------|---------|
| Digital Life | 159.73 | 44.7% | -1.4% |
| Health | 126.82 | 35.5% | +22.7% |
| Consumer Finance | 43.20 | 12.1% | -10.6% |
| Automobiles | 27.60 | 7.7% | +34.6% |

**Highlight products:** "Zhongmin Insurance" premiums increased by 456.1% year-on-year to 2.17 billion yuan; pet insurance premiums increased by 88.2% year-on-year to 1.3 billion yuan; new energy auto insurance premiums surged by 206.2% year-on-year.

**Consumer finance proactively reduces:** Management predicts that premiums in this sector will drop by 50%-60% year-on-year in 2026, and the loan balance has dropped from 22.883 billion yuan to 17.9 billion yuan. Proactive strategic selection based on regulatory risk.

**ZA Bank:** Achieved full-year profit for the first time in 2025, with a net profit of approximately HK$70 million. The number of users exceeded one million, and customer assets increased by 125.3% year-on-year.

**Technology output:** In 2024, the technology output revenue for the whole year was 956 million yuan, with a cumulative service of more than 1,000 customers, and the technology sector achieved a profit of 78 million yuan.

## 3. Moat assessment

**Positive factors:**
1. **Scarcity of licenses**: China’s first pure Internet insurance company, ranking first in the Internet property and casualty insurance market share
2. **AI in-depth application**: The AI middle platform has nearly 220 active robots and will complete more than 2 billion large model calls in 2025; more than 50% of health insurance cases will be automatically reviewed throughout the entire process, and the fastest AI loss determination for auto insurance is 116 seconds.
3. **Ecological Embedding**: Cooperate with traffic portals such as Alipay and WeChat to acquire customers at low cost

**Negatives:**
1. **Ant’s holding reduction signal**: Continuous reduction of holdings to the fourth largest shareholder, indicating that it is no longer regarded as a core strategic asset
2. **User data decline**: The number of users covered by the health ecosystem dropped from 28.8 million in 2023 to 12.39 million in the first half of 2024
3. **Reduction in R&D investment**: R&D expenses in 2024 will be 846 million yuan, a decrease of approximately 29% from 1.192 billion yuan in 2023
4. **The advantages of Internet insurance licenses are relative**: Traditional insurance companies comprehensively promote digitalization

**Moat summary**: It has certain competitive advantages, but it cannot be called a "wide moat". The advantage comes more from operational efficiency and speed of innovation.

## 5. Risks and Catalysts

**Core Risks:**
1. **Tightening of consumer finance supervision**: The biggest short-term risk, management has taken the initiative to reduce it by 50-60%
2. **Ant continues to reduce its holdings**: Weakening the certainty of strategic cooperation
3. **Investment income volatility**: Investment income of 2.124 billion yuan is close to 1.5 times of underwriting profit, and capital market fluctuations are directly transmitted to the income statement
4. **ROE ceiling**: The ROE of the insurance industry is subject to both the comprehensive cost rate and the investment rate of return.

**Potential Catalysts:**
1. **ROE breaks through 10%**: room for revaluation
2. **New energy car insurance outbreak**: premium growth rate exceeds 200%
3. **Zhongan Bank continues to make profits**: opening up new valuation space
4. **Scaling of technology output**: Asset-light and high-profit margin business with annual revenue of nearly 1 billion

---

## 6. Investment Conclusion

ZhongAn Online is at the stage where "the fundamental inflection point has been confirmed, but the valuation has been partially reflected."

- **Fair value estimate**: HK$15-22 (neutral about HK$18.6)
- **Margin of Safety**: The current price is in the middle of the fair value range, and the margin of safety is limited.
- **Risk to reward ratio**: Neutral to positive, 15-20% on the upside, 15-20% on the downside, about 1:1

**The more ideal buying price is in the range of HK$13-15** (corresponding to 0.7-0.8 times PB), providing a safety margin of about 30%. Building a position at the current price requires strong confidence that ROE will continue to increase to 8%-10% in 2026-2027.

**Special alert**: The high contribution of investment income to profits means that the "high growth" narrative may quickly reverse when the capital market weakens.
