## 1. Company overview and business breakdown

Bank of China is the most internationalized bank among the four major state-owned banks, with total assets of 38.36 trillion yuan (+9.40%) as of the end of 2025. With branches in more than 60 countries and regions, it is the only bank among Chinese banks with a truly global network.

**Business Structure:**
- **Overseas Institutions**: Assets account for 22.18% of the group, and pre-tax profit contribution reaches 27.99% - nearly 30% of profits come from overseas, far ahead among major state-owned banks
- **Cross-border finance**: International settlement volume exceeded 4.45 trillion US dollars (+9.56%), and cross-border RMB settlement volume was 17.70 trillion yuan (+9.43%)
- **Cross-border e-commerce settlement**: scale reached 1.18 trillion yuan (+45.07%), a new growth pole
- **Customer base**: More than 550 million individual customers, mobile banking monthly active users exceeded 100 million
- **Technology Loans**: The balance exceeded 4.8 trillion yuan, accounting for more than one-third of corporate loans

**Core Judgment**: International business is the most unique asset of Bank of China. Nearly 30% of profits come from overseas, which provides a buffer of geographical diversification and has an irreplaceable card position advantage in the process of RMB internationalization.

## 3. Moat assessment

1. **National Credit Endorsement (Wide Moat)**: Implicit national credit guarantee, the lowest echelon in the financing cost industry
2. **Global branch network (wide moat)**: The network of 60+ national institutions has been accumulated for decades and cannot be copied.
3. **Unique barriers to foreign exchange and cross-border finance (wide moat)**: The volume of cross-border RMB settlement of 17.70 trillion yuan is itself a barrier
4. **Low-cost deposit base**: customer deposits 26.18 trillion yuan (+8.18%)
5. **Differentiated positioning**: ICBC focuses on scale, CCB focuses on housing, Agricultural Bank of China focuses on counties, and BOC's label is "internationalization." In the context of RMB internationalization, the strategic value is rising

## 4. Valuation analysis

**Current valuation level:** H-share PB is about 0.54-0.55 times, the lowest among the four major banks (ICBC 0.71, CCB 0.70, Agricultural Bank of China 0.65). This mainly reflects the low ROE (8.94%, the bottom of the four major banks).

**Theoretical PB calculation:** Gordon model - assuming ROE 9%, sustainable growth rate 3%, and equity cost 10%, the theoretical PB is about 0.86 times. Assuming a conservative ROE of 8%, the theoretical PB is about 0.71 times. The current 0.54 times is significantly lower than the theoretical value.

**AH Premium:** The discount of H shares is about 15-17%. The Hang Seng AH Premium Index dropped from 161 at the beginning of 2024 to 119 at the beginning of 2026, and the discount continued to narrow.

**The rationality of Morningstar HK$8.00:** Corresponds to about 0.84 times PB, which is slightly lower than the 0.86 times calculated by the Gordon model, which is reasonable. But ROE needs to stabilize or even pick up.

**Dividend discount verification:** Assuming a dividend of approximately HK$0.25 per share, a growth rate of 2-3%, a discount rate of 8%, and a valuation of approximately HK$4.2-5.0. If the dividend payout ratio is raised from 30% to 35%, the valuation center will rise to HK$5.5-6.5.

## 5. Risks and Catalysts

**Core Risks:**
1. **Interest spread continues to compress**: 1.26% is already a historical low, and further decline will directly erode profits
2. **Real estate risk clearing has not been completed**: The non-performing rate of corporate real estate increased from 5.38% to 6.26%
3. **Overseas geopolitical risks**: Globalization is both an advantage and a double-edged sword
4. **ROE is low**: 8.94%, the bottom of the four major banks, limits the room for valuation repair

**Potential Catalysts:**
1. **Interest rate spread bottoming confirmed**: 2026Q1 has been flat for four consecutive quarters. If it rebounds in the second half of the year, it will be a strong positive signal.
2. **Increase dividend ratio**: After the capital increase of 165 billion yuan, core capital is sufficient (12.53%), providing room for increasing the dividend payout ratio
3. **Acceleration of RMB internationalization**: Structural benefits unique to Bank of China, directly benefiting from the expansion of the CIPS system
4. **China Special Valuation Policy Promotion**: Reshaping the valuation of state-owned enterprises is the policy direction

---

## 6. Investment Conclusion

**The current price has investment value. **

Calculated at HK$5.22, PB is approximately 0.54 times, implying the pessimistic expectation of "continued sluggish ROE and stagnant growth." The fair value of the base scenario is HKD 6.2-7.1, with a potential upside of 19%-36%, plus a dividend rate of 4-4.5%, and the total return is considerable.

- **Fair value estimate**: HK$6.2-7.5 (H shares)
- **Morningstar HKD 8.00**: Optimistic scenario, which needs to be supported by ROE recovery and policy catalysis.
- **H shares vs A shares**: H shares have a discount of 15-17% and a higher dividend rate, which is more cost-effective
- **Suitable for Investors**: Long-term value investors who have a need for dividend income and can withstand short-term fluctuations

**Medium and long-term value**: Bank of China has the triple safety margin of "low valuation + high dividend + unique international positioning". Core logic: If interest rate spreads bottom out, dividends increase, and RMB internationalization advances, there will be significant room for repair in the current valuation. Downside risk is limited (dividend protection), and upside depends on how well fundamental improvements materialize.
