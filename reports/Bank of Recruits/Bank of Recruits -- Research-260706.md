# 6.1 Banking is not the main player of the paradigm shift, but sits at the intersection of the two rivers

Banks are not AI, they are not new energy sources, there is no narrative of "civilizational paradigm shift." But the recruitment is stuck in two slow and determined historical trends:

** Trends I: The wealth of Chinese residents migrates from real estate to financial assets in the century.** The difference between the personal financial assets of Chinese residents, which are expected to break out of 291 trillion yuan in 2025, and the total size of the industry, which is 179.33 trillion yuan (2025Q3, compared to 8.21%). China is the second largest wealth management market in the world. 70% of the wealth of Chinese households in the last two decades is in their homes, and the median ratio is 30-40% in the developed world over the next two decades – the difference is the best crossing of the wealth management industry, Tam.

** Trends II: Long-term downswing interest rates are a companion to the maturity of the economy.** The river is a counter-flow. LPR has a 3.0/5-year period of 3.5 per cent, with markets expected to fall by another 10-20bp in 2026; industry net interest differentials of 1.42 per cent are historically low. Banks in all advanced economies have experienced this phase, with no exception, but only where the difference is from the bottom (3 per cent in the United States plus the nominal interest rate vs. 0 per cent in Japan).
