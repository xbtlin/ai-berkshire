## Summary

SK Hynix is an "excellent business at the moment" - HBM is number one in the world, customers are chasing production capacity, and profit margins are the highest in the history of the storage industry. But this is also a "long-term questionable" business - the storage industry has never escaped the boom-bust cycle, 27% of revenue depends on one NVIDIA customer, and although the moat is deep, it is built on "technology leadership" rather than "brand loyalty." Measured by normalized earnings, the current stock price has insufficient safety margin. For value investors, a better strategy is to wait for a cyclical correction to intervene - buying "good companies" at "good prices" rather than chasing high prices when "good companies" are at their best.
