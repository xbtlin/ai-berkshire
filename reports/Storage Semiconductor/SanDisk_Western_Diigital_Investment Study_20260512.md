## Summary of analysis of each dimension

### Business model and moat (Duan Yongping’s perspective)

**SanDisk: Not a "good business"**
1. NAND flash memory is closer to commodities - products are highly substitutable, prices are determined by supply and demand cycles, and differentiation is weak
2. Comprehensive rating of moat: weak-medium. Brands have a certain value on the consumer side, but the consumer side is shrinking, and the brand premium on the enterprise side is almost non-existent.
3. The double-edged sword of the joint venture with Kioxia: "Half of your core competitiveness belongs to your competitors."
4. The split did release valuation discounts, but the 650% increase largely superimposed the NAND cycle upward trend
5. HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) is the only possible differentiation point, but it has not yet been commercialized

**Western Digital: Unexpected "good business" characteristics**
1. Pure HDD company, duopoly structure (with Seagate), significantly enhanced pricing power
2. All production capacity will be sold out in 2026, and some long-term agreements have been extended to 2028-2029
3. The cost per TB is still 1/7 to 1/16 of SSD, and the demand for AI cold storage explodes
4. Cloud customers account for 89% of revenue, indicating clear positioning

### Finance and Valuation (Buffett’s Perspective)

**SanDisk: Margin of safety is almost non-existent**
1. It only took two quarters for gross profit margin to surge from 22.5% to 78.4% - a typical cycle signal
2. Three-scenario valuation: optimistic +83%, neutral -18%, pessimistic -67%
3. The NAND industry will suffer huge losses across the board in 2023, indicating that current profits are unsustainable
4. EV/EBITDA 40.5x is significantly higher than SK Hynix (8.1x) and Micron (22.7x)
5. Buffett comments: "You are paying for hope, not for certainty."

**WDC: Valuation is basically reasonable to slightly expensive**
1. Three-scenario valuation: optimistic +87%, neutral -3%, pessimistic -54%
2. Significant deleveraging, with debt reduced from $4.75 billion to $1.6 billion
3. Start buying back shares + increasing dividends, improving shareholder returns

### Industry and Competition (Munger’s Perspective)

1. **NAND is a worse business than DRAM**: 5-6 manufacturers (including YMTC government-subsidized competitors) vs. the DRAM trio
2. Leading manufacturers vote with their feet - about 90% of capital expenditures are invested in DRAM/HBM instead of NAND
3. YMTC has reached a shipment share of 13% and is "a low-cost competitor driven by the will of the country"
4. Kioxia/SanDisk is 100+ layers behind in the layer competition (218 layers vs 321 layers)
5. The HDD duopoly instead exhibits the characteristics of "good business": ability to raise prices, sold out production capacity, long-term contracts

### Risk Assessment (Li Lu’s Perspective)

**SanDisk Core Risks**:
1. **Competitive risk: high** — Rise of YMTC + technological backwardness + scale advantage of Samsung/Hynix
2. **Kioxia joint venture risk: medium to high** — SK Hynix holds 15% of Kioxia shares, conflict of interest
3. **Cycle risk: current low/mid-term high** — possible reversal in 2027H2
4. **Core argument for short selling**: Valuation is extremely frothy (PS 16.2x vs. industry median 1.04x)

**WDC core risks**:
1. The long-term direction of SSD replacing HDD remains unchanged, but the timetable is delayed by AI
2. Management is in a "testing period" - operating independently for less than 18 months

## Summary

After the split, SanDisk and WDC each experienced amazing market performance, but from a value investment perspective: NAND is a commodity business, and SanDisk enjoyed unsustainable huge profits at the top of the cycle. The market value of US$231.4 billion paid an exorbitant premium for a pure NAND player. On the contrary, WDC HDD business, which is regarded as a "recession business" by the market, has shown unexpected "good business" characteristics due to the explosion of cold storage demand in AI data centers - duopoly, pricing power, and sold out production capacity. When valuations are reasonable, WDC may be a better investment target.
