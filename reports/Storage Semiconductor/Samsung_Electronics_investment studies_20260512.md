## Key analysis

### Valuation advantage narrowed significantly

During the initial screening, Samsung seemed to be "comprehensively inferior to Hynix", but after in-depth research, it was found that:
- **PB 1.5x is obsolete**: The market has been re-priced after the profit explosion in Q1 2026, and the current PB is about 3.8x
- **Forward PE gap disappears**: Samsung 6.0x vs Hynix 5.5x, almost no gap
- Samsung's "cheapness" is more reflected in PB (3.8x vs 6x), but there is a reasonable explanation (OEM loss + governance discount)

### HBM catches up and achieves substantial breakthrough

- HBM3E finally passed NVIDIA certification in September 2025 (18 months delay)
- HBM4 passed NVIDIA testing and received the **highest rating**
- Google TPU HBM3E supply share is 60%+
- HBM production capacity has been sold out in 2026 and plans to expand production by 50%
- But the share is still 35% (vs. Hynix 53%), catching up with the lead in Central Africa

### OEM business: "It's hard to get off the tiger"

- The global market share is only 7.2% (vs. TSMC 70%), a gap of 62.7 percentage points
- 3nm yield <50% (vs TSMC >90%)
- Loss of approximately 3.18 trillion won in 2023, and no profit in 2024-2025
- Target 2027 profit + 20% market share, looks extremely aggressive
- Negotiating 2nm orders with AMD - if successful it will be a turning point

### "Korean discounts" are slowly improving

- The commercial law will be revised in July 2025 to strengthen shareholders’ rights and interests.
- Canceling 3 trillion won of treasury shares
- 50% free cash flow return policy to shareholders
- However, the cross-shareholding structure has not fundamentally changed, and about 1 million minority shareholders were lost within one year.

## Final investment advice

**HOLD/WATCH. ** It is not recommended to buy at the current price.

Reason:
1. The valuation advantage of initial screening (PB 1.5x) is gone
2. Low Forward PE at the top of the cycle is a classic trap in the storage industry
3. OEM is a profit black hole that cannot be ignored
4. As an AI/storage bet, Hynix is the more straightforward choice

Catalysts worth keeping track of:
- HBM4 mass production performance and share changes
- Can 2nm foundry win AMD orders?
- Promotion of governance reform in South Korea
- If the stock price pulls back 20-30%, a better buying opportunity may arise.
