## 1. Industry panorama

### 1.1 Market size

| Dimensions | 2024 | 2029 (forecast) | CAGR |
|------|--------|-------------|------|
| China space design software market | 3.3 billion RMB | 6.6 billion RMB | 14.9% |
| Global space design software market | ~21 billion RMB | ~37 billion RMB | 15.4% |

Data source: Frost & Sullivan

> Munger: "If you're in a small pond, even if you're the biggest fish, you won't get very big."

The size of the Chinese market of 3.3 billion yuan is indeed small. Qunhe has a market share of 23.2% but only has revenue of 750 million yuan - the ceiling problem is the core contradiction of this investment**.

### 1.2 Growth drivers

1. **Demand for renovation of existing housing**: China’s existing housing stock exceeds 600 million units, and renovation and decoration are structural growth
2. **Increased digital penetration rate in the design process**: from CAD hand-drawing to cloud 3D rendering
3. **AI empowerment**: AIGC lowers the design threshold and expands the user base
4. **Overseas markets**: The global home decoration design software market is much larger than China's

### 1.3 Reverse thinking on industry growth

But Munger asks: **What if these drivers don’t hold true? **

- The demand for renovation of existing houses is affected by the economic cycle, and consumers may postpone renovations
- Digital penetration may be close to the ceiling (Kujiale has 65 million users)
- AI does not necessarily make designers more dependent on tools, but may also allow non-professional users to bypass designers
- The cost of acquiring customers in overseas markets is much higher than that in domestic markets

## 2. Competitive landscape

### 2.1 Main players

| Company | Market share (2024) | Positioning | Core strengths |
|------|:----------:|------|---------|
| Qunhe Technology (Kujiale) | 23.2% | Cloud native full-chain design platform | User scale, network effect, GPU cluster |
| Autodesk (3ds Max) | 21.9% | Professional-grade 3D design | Brand, ecology, global coverage |
| 3D Home | ~10% (estimated) | Customized home factory docking | Back-end production system integration |
| Zhongwang CAD | ~5% (estimated) | Domestic CAD replacement | Policy bonus, price advantage |
| New forces such as Floo AI | <1% | AI native design | Zero threshold, full chain closed loop |

The market structure is **dispersed**: the top two companies total only 45%, indicating that the industry has not yet formed a winner-take-all.

### 2.2 Competitive barriers for group cores

**Self-built GPU cluster** is the core of differentiation. With 5,400 high-performance GPUs, 2K rendering is reduced from 53 seconds to 1.2 seconds - competitors rely on third-party cloud services and cannot replicate this speed advantage.

**But the barriers are changing**:
- Cloud computing costs continue to decline, and barriers to GPU clusters may gradually weaken
- The integration advantages of 3D Home on the factory side will be difficult for Group Core to replicate in the short term.
- Autodesk has a global brand and a complete BIM ecosystem, and it is difficult to shake the core of the high-end market

### 2.3 The "old enemy" relationship with the Sanwei family

The competition between Qunhe and Sanweijia has been going on for many years, and there have even been lawsuits over intellectual property rights. The difference between the two:

| Dimension | Group Core (Kujiale) | 3D Home |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Strengths | Front-end design experience, rendering speed | Back-end factory docking, production system integration |
| Customer focus | Designers, decoration companies | Customized furniture factories |
| Technical roadmap | Cloud native + self-built GPU | Rely on third-party cloud |
| Profit status | Just turned a loss | Undisclosed (unlisted) |

## 3. The disruptive impact of AI

### 3.1 AI is an opportunity for group cores

- 360 million 3D models + 5 billion renderings = exclusive data set for training spatial AI models
- "Kujiale AI" produces an average of 300,000 pictures per day, and user activity has increased
- The SpatialVerse spatial intelligence platform provides a training environment for embodied AI and opens new TAMs

### 3.2 AI may also be a threat to the group core

> Munger: "The cruelest fact in the technology industry is that your competitive advantage today may be the reason why you will be disrupted tomorrow."

- **Midjourney/Sora type tools**: If general AI can generate high-quality indoor renderings, the rendering function of Kujiale will be devalued.
- **AI native design tools (Floo AI, etc.)**: zero-threshold "intelligent" design, which may cannibalize Kujiale's personal designer users
- **AI reduces switching costs**: If AI can automatically migrate design solutions, the switching cost barrier of group cores will be weakened

### 3.3 Reverse thinking: Will Kujiale still be there in 5 years?

Most likely scenario: **Kujiale still exists, but faces a growth ceiling. ** The stickiness of corporate customers ensures that the basic market will not collapse, but individual customers continue to lose (NRR has dropped to 86.4%), and new AI tools are eroding the edge market.
