# 01 Business model analysis – Duan Yongping’s perspective

**Analyst: Duan Yongping (value investing practitioner, ultimate tester of "good business" standards)**
**Target: Qunhe Technology (00068.HK)/Kujiale** | **Date: April 9, 2026**

---

## 1. Definition of core business

### What is Kujiale’s business?

In a word: **A SaaS platform that provides cloud-based 3D design tools for the home decoration industry**.

Designers use Kujiale for house layout design and renderings, home improvement companies use it for plan presentation and customer conversion, and home furnishing brands use it for product presentation and marketing. Essentially **digital infrastructure** for the design process.

> Duan Yongping once said: "A good business model is when others are willing to pay you, and they feel it is worth it."

Kujiale designers use the free version to get started (an average of 2 million non-paying users per month) and the paid version to improve performance (faster rendering and more complete functions). This conversion funnel from free to paid is a classic SaaS customer acquisition model. The paid conversion rate has increased from 6.8% to 9.6% in 2025.

### Breakdown of income structure

| Income type | Proportion | Description |
|---------|:----:|------|
| Corporate customer subscriptions | ~84% | Annual/multi-year subscriptions from home improvement companies and home furnishing brands |
| Individual user subscription | ~13% | Designer personal payment |
| Other value-added services | ~3% | Rendering, marketing tools, etc. |

**97% of revenue comes from subscriptions**, which is a very pure SaaS revenue structure.

## 2. Moat analysis

### 1. Network Effect ★★★★

Kujiale’s strongest moat is the triangular network effect of designers-brands-home decoration companies:

- **Designers** use Kujiale to make plans → Attract more brands to upload product materials
- **Brands** (such as Oppein, Sofia) upload product 3D models to the platform → designers can use them more smoothly
- **Home Decoration Company** sees designers and brands using it → Purchase Kujiale as standard equipment for enterprises

Once this triangular relationship is formed, competitors must leverage all three parties at the same time to break it, which is extremely difficult.

### 2. Switching Cost ★★★★

- Designers have accumulated a large number of design plans and material libraries on Kujiale, and the migration cost is high
- The workflow of corporate customers has been embedded in Kujiale (solution design→rendering→ordering→delivery), and changing the platform means retraining the team
- The brand's product 3D model library has been built on Kujiale, and the reconstruction cost is high.

### 3. Data Barriers ★★★

- Accumulated massive house plans, design plans, and rendering data
- These data can train AI models and form a "data flywheel"
- But the sustainability of the data barrier depends on the evolution of AI technology - if a common large model can generate design solutions of the same quality, this barrier may be weakened

### 4. Brand awareness ★★★

- "Cool Jiale" has become synonymous with the category in the field of home decoration design
- But the brand’s influence is mainly limited to the vertical field of home decoration

## 3. Duan Yongping’s “Good Business” Three Standards Inspection

### Differentiation ★★★★

Kujiale’s differentiation is clear:
- **Cloud native**: No need to install local software, just open the browser and use it (vs AutoCAD/3ds Max requires a high-end computer)
- **10 seconds rendering**: GPU cloud cluster achieves extremely fast rendering. It takes dozens of minutes for a personal computer to do the same rendering.
- **Industry Exclusive**: Specially optimized for home decoration design, not a general design tool

But the depth of the differentiated moat is limited - Sanweijia is also doing similar things, and the technical threshold is not insurmountable.

### Pricing power ★★★

- 97% of subscription revenue shows that customers are willing to continue paying
- However, the net revenue retention rate dropped from 106.1% (2023) to 98.6% (2025), indicating that **customers’ willingness to pay for expansion is declining**
- The growth in the number of enterprise customers is also slowing down: 41070 → 46046 → 47416, with an increase of only 3% in 2025
- If the net retention rate continues to be lower than 100%, it means that existing customers are reducing their spending and their pricing power is weakening

> Duan Yongping's standard: "Pricing power means that customers will not leave if you increase the price." The net retention rate fell below 100%, indicating that Kujiale does not yet have real pricing power.

### Sustainability ★★★

The good side:
- The SaaS subscription model itself is sustainable and revenue is predictable
- Home decoration design is a necessity, and the house must be renovated.
- Overseas business grew by 28%, opening up the second curve

Concerns:
- Over-reliance on the home improvement industry, which is highly correlated with the real estate cycle
- China's real estate downturn may last for several years, directly dragging down Kujiale's growth
-The number of monthly active non-paying users dropped from 2.5 million to 2 million, and the funnel entrance is shrinking
