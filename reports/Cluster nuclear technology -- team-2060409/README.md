## Key Takeaway

> Qunhe Technology is the undisputed leader in China's spatial-design SaaS market. Its Kujiale product has built powerful network effects and switching costs in home-design workflows. An 82% gross margin is true SaaS quality, but 2025 revenue growth was only 9%, the company only just turned profitable on an adjusted basis (RMB 57.1 million), and the IPO valuation of HKD 11.4-13.0 billion implies 12-14x P/S and more than 200x P/E. **The pricing is expensive and lacks a margin of safety. Short-term IPO trading may be playable, but long-term holding requires clear evidence of accelerating revenue growth and scaled profit release.**
