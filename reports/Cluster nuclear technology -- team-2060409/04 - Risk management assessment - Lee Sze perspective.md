## 2. In-depth analysis of core risks

### 2.1 Revenue growth continues to slow down (Severity: ★★★★★)

| Year | Revenue (100 million yuan) | Growth rate |
|------|:---------:|:----:|
| 2023 | 6.64 | — |
| 2024 | 7.55 | 13.8% |
| 2025 | 8.20 | 8.6% |

From 14% to 9%, the trend is obvious. If the growth rate continues to drop to 5-6% in 2026, the valuation of PS 14x will not be sustainable.

**Root cause**: Domestic home decoration market ceiling + real estate downward cycle. It's not a problem of management ability, it's an industry problem.

### 2.2 High dependence on the home decoration industry (Severity: ★★★★★)

95%+ of revenue comes from customers in the home decoration industry chain. This is not a minor flaw of "insufficient diversification", but a structural risk in the core business model.

China's real estate is undergoing its most profound adjustment in decades. New home sales decline → Renovation demand decreases → Design software budgets shrink. Deferred revenue dropped from 617 million to 510 million, and customers switched from multi-year subscriptions to one-year subscriptions, indicating that customer confidence is wavering.

### 2.3 Accelerated loss of individual customers (Severity: ★★★★)

The NRR of individual customers plummeted from 106.5% to 86.4%, which means that individual designers are reducing payment or downgrading packages.

This may reflect:
- Independent designers’ incomes have dropped and they have cut back on tool expenditures
- AI tools offer cheaper alternatives
- Free features are enough to meet basic needs

### 2.4 Operating cash flow continues to be negative (Severity: ★★★★)

The full-year operating cash flow in 2025 is -192 million yuan. A SaaS company that claims to be "profitable" has negative operating cash flow - **The "profit" content of the adjusted net profit of 57 million is questionable**.

Adjustments (non-recurring expenses deducted) mainly include: equity incentive expenses (SBC), changes in fair value of redemption liabilities, etc. Under GAAP, the company still lost 428 million yuan.

### 2.5 Redemption of debt "time bomb" (Severity: ★★★, eliminated after IPO)

The redemption liability of 4.092 billion is the redemption obligation of investors’ preferred shares. After the IPO, preferred shares are converted into common shares and liabilities are eliminated. But this also means that an IPO is a must-do, not an option. The company has limited bargaining power in negotiations.

## 3. Li Lu’s “3C” Framework Assessment

### Character: ★★★★

The founding team is sincere, dedicated and technology-oriented. The patience to do one thing for 12 years is a rare quality in China's technology industry. No obvious moral hazard is seen.

### Capability: ★★★

Product capabilities are strong (23.2% market share), but commercialization capabilities are limited. The gross profit margin is 82% but the adjusted net profit is only 57 million, indicating that there is still much room for improvement in sales efficiency and cost control.

### Capital Allocation: ★★

Most of the 2 billion in financing has been "burned out", leaving only 357 million in cash. Building a self-built 5,400 GPU cluster is an asset-heavy decision—technically correct, but capital efficient. If the utilization rate of these GPUs decreases in the future (AI chip iteration), they will face the risk of asset impairment.

---

## 4. Rating

**Risk & Management Rating: ★★★ (3/5)**

Reason:
- Management’s character and technical capabilities are trustworthy
- But industry risks (home improvement cycle) and financial risks (negative cash flow, slowing growth) exist objectively
- The efficiency of capital allocation is low. The ROI of 2 billion burning 800 million revenue and 57 million profit is not enough.
- IPO has the color of "forced to continue life" rather than "icing on the cake"
