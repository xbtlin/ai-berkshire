But note that the denominator is shrinking. The transition rate increases, and if free users at the top of the funnel are decreasing, the absolute increase in the number of final payers may be stagnant.

## First line indicator I: Net Income Retention Rate (NRR)

NRR measures how much last year's client spent. More than 100% means that older clients are expanding their payments, less than 100% means that older clients are shrinking.

<unk> Personal client 106.5% 96.6% <unk> 86.4% <unk> Serious deterioration <unk>

(Data source: Group Nuclear Technology Recruits)

** The company client NRR is also barely 100%, but the individual client NRR has fallen to 86.4% ** This means that the individual designer is either demoting the package or losing it.

Individual clients, while only 12% of income, are ecological ** flow portals**. If designers do not have cool – they can also reduce the incentives for branders to upload models and the triangular network effect may be relaxed from the edge.

Why is a personal client losing? A few possible reasons:

1. Independent designers ' income is affected by downside real estate, reducing tool expenditure
2. The AI mapping tool (midjourney et al.) provides cheaper or even free alternatives
3. The Cool Carare free edition is enough, with no incentive to pay for upgrades

#5400 GPU: Barriers or baggage?

The group built itself **5400 high performance GPU** cloud-reflection clusters, which are its core technological differences vis-à-vis competitors such as 3Ds — the 2K effect graph rendering time has dropped from **53 seconds to 1.2 seconds**.

** Face **: Self-built GPU cluster gives the cluster a high lead in the recitation speed, which is a key difference in user experience.

** On the back, **:

Cloud computing costs are declining each year — a similar rate can be achieved by competitors using third-party clouds after a few years.
GPU chips are fast-tracked - today's 5,400 may be out of business in two years and face impairment of assets
This is a heavy asset input — a significant portion of the $2 billion in financing is spent here, delaying the return on capital.

The group cores packaged the GPU cluster into "Space Smart Infrastructure" in the offer book, but the main use of these GPUs is currently the sarcastic image -- the huge narrative of Space Smart.
