# Autodesk: The giants of the high-end market

Autodesk took a share of **21.9%** - almost equal to the group - in the Chinese space design software market. But there is a clear separation between the client groups of the two services:

Autodesk is for professional architects and design firms, and product lines cover AutoCAD, 3ds Max, Revit
- The core is "simple, fast, cheap" for small and medium-sized renovation companies and independent designers.

** The strategy of the nuclear cluster is "Rural siege of cities"** -- build up the size of the low- and medium-level markets first. But Autodesk's brand, ecology and BIM's full process capability are almost unshakeable in the high-end markets.

# New forces like the Airway AI: Subversive from the bottom

The most vigilant is not traditional competitors, but AI's original design tool.

The logic of new players like Airway AI is:** AI directly generates the design, skips the "painting" **. Users enter a description ("Two Rooms, Northern Europe, 200,000"), and AI automatically generates the full space program and impact map.

If this path runs, the value of Cool Carroll as a design tool will be fundamentally diminished -- because ** Users no longer need to draw **.

** But the counter-view is **: The programs generated by AI are currently far less precise and accessible than professional design tools. The home design is not "a good picture" -- it needs to be precise to millimetre size, a list of materials that conform to construction specifications, and the production parameters of the docking plant. These are not available for Midjuourney and Airway AI in the short term.

# AI: Opportunities, threats, or both?

This is the most complex and critical variable in analysing nuclear technology in the cluster.

# AI's a good side to the nuclears

1. ** Data moat**: 480.3D model + 5 billion Rendering Map is the exclusive data set for training the space AI model. The group core has been released SpatialLM (space language model) and SpatialGen (space generation model) with an initial advantage.

2. **AI upgrades user experience**: "Cool Dalque AI" shows an average of over **300,000 copies per day** (source: Nuclear technology in the group official), AI-assisted design can reduce the threshold of use, improve the efficiency of the drawing and increase the stickyness of the user.

3. **New business story**: SpatialVerse provides space simulation data for a robot with AI. The kernel has worked with companies like the cyborg, Galaxy commons, and dome intelligence. If the cyborg is in the AI market, it may be able to cut into a market that is well over 3.3 billion.

(Source: Officially published by nuclear technology cluster, technical literature on the subject, community reports on IYA)

# AI's a negative side to the nuclears

1. **Dressed value**: If generic AIs such as Midjourney and Sora generate high-quality indoor effects maps, the "10 seconds" core selling point is less valuable.

2. ** Design threshold lowered**: AI allows unprofessional users to make good design options, which may lead to a reduction in independent designers - the designers are the core users and flow portals of CK. The trend may already be reflected in the collapse of the individual client NRR to **86.4%**.

3. ** Conversion costs eroded**: When AI automatically migrates design options from cool housekeeping to other platforms, the barriers to the conversion costs of cluster cores are weakened.

# Key judgments

**5 years perspective**: The nuclear mass ratio still exists, and the embedded depth of the company’s customers guarantees that the base plate will not collapse. But the loss of individual users may continue, and the pace of growth is further slowed by the new AI tools encroaching on the marginal market.

**10 years perspective**: High uncertainty. If the universal AI spatial understanding is close to human designer level, the entire "design tool" class may be redefined. The class cores are either successfully transformed into "spatial intelligence infrastructure" or turned into an old-age tool in a shrinking market.

Inverse: There is also the possibility that AI is not a substitute designer, but that AI is more involved in design. "Everyone is a designer" expands the tool market, Tam. But this hypothesis needs to be validated.

** Overseas operations are real growth points, but they represent only 9 per cent of total income, which is less than $100 million in absolute terms, and there is a long way to go to become a "second curve".**
