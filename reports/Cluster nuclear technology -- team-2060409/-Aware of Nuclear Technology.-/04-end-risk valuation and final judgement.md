1. Income growth has increased significantly from the current 9 per cent to over 20 per cent (outshore eruptions or space intelligence releases)

# The real points of disagreement

** Core differences are not about whether the SaaS model is good (accepted good) or about the Maori rate (accepted high), but about:**

1. ** Can "Space intelligence" move from narrative to income?** If it does, the current valuation could be reasonable, the TAM could have expanded from 3.3 billion to 10 billion or even hundreds of billions. If it does not, the cluster core is a business that is slowing down the growth rate in 3.3 billion markets, with 67 billion market values that cannot be sustained.

2. ** Can the income increase be reversed?** The recovery from 9% to more than 15% is a multi-purpose core bet. But this reversal requires a very strong catalyst against a backdrop of domestic market pressure and a collapse of the personal client NRR to 86%.

3. ** Can management complete the shift from "Tech" to "Commercial"?** The 82% Māori rate <unk> the 7% net interest rate leakage is essentially a matter of management efficiency. Whether or not to put down the cost rate after listing determines whether profits can be released.
