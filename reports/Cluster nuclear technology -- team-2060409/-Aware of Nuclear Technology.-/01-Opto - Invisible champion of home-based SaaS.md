## A confusing company

On April 17, 2026, Qunhe Technology (00068.HK) was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange with an issue price of HK$7.62**. Three days later, the stock price once touched HK$43.62 - more than 4 times the issue price, and the market value soared from HK$13 billion to HK$67 billion. A home improvement SaaS company with an annual revenue of RMB 800 million and negative operating cash flow took three days to complete the most dramatic listing performance on the Hong Kong stock market in recent years.

(Data source: Hong Kong Stock Exchange public quotations, Sina Finance reports)

If you ask the more than 60 million registered users of Kujiale, they will say: This is a tool for making house plans.
If you ask the capital market, they will say: This is the first IPO of the "Hangzhou Six Little Dragons" and the first global space intelligence stock.
If you ask value investors, they will say: A SaaS company that just turned a loss sold for more than 50 times PS? Crazy.

**Three people are looking at the same company, but they are telling three completely different stories. **

What this series is about is taking the story apart and looking at just the facts - and then letting you judge for yourself.

## What exactly is Qunhe Technology?

In a sentence: ** Qunhe Technology is a SaaS company that provides cloud 3D design tools for the home decoration industry. Its core product is called "Kujiale". **

Designers use Kujiale to draw floor plans and generate renderings; home decoration companies use it to show customers plans and facilitate signing orders; home furnishing brands (Oppein, Sofia, etc.) upload 3D models of their own products, allowing designers to call them directly in the plans.

In essence, what Kujiale does is **digital infrastructure** in the home decoration design process.

The financial characteristics of this business are very clear:

| Indicators | 2025 Data | Description |
|------|-----------|------|
| Total revenue | **820 million yuan** | +8.6% year-on-year |
| Subscription revenue ratio | **97%** | Pure SaaS model |
| Gross profit margin | **82.2%** | SaaS-level excellence |
| Adjusted net profit | **57 million yuan** | Just crossed the profit and loss line |
| IFRS net profit | **-428 million yuan** | Still losing money |
| Cash flow from operating activities | **-192 million yuan** | Bleeding has not stopped |

(Data source: Qunhe Technology Hong Kong stock prospectus updated in April 2026)

Putting these together, you will find a contradiction - the gross profit margin is 82%, which looks like a money printing machine; but the operating cash flow is negative, and it is actually still burning money. **

The reason why high gross profit has not been converted into high profit is simple: **Sales expense rate 33.4% + R&D expense rate 35.5% = 69%**, these two expenses eat up most of the gross profit. The company is still investing heavily in customer acquisition and research and development, and is far from entering the profit harvesting period.

## Three common cognitive biases

### Deviation 1: "Kujiale is just a drawing tool"

This is the most common cognition and the most superficial.

Kujiale is not just about drawing pictures. It builds a triangular network of designers-brands-home decoration companies:

- **Designer** uses Kujiale to make plans → needs product materials from brand owners
- **Brands** (Oppein, Sofia, etc.) upload 3D product models → get solutions and recommendations from designers
- **Home Decoration Company** sees designers and brands using it → Purchase Kujiale as standard equipment for enterprises

As of 2025, there are more than **480 million 3D models** on the platform, with a total of **65 million registered users**, including approximately **2 million monthly active non-paying users** and approximately **47,000 enterprise customers**.

Once this triangular network is formed, competitors must leverage all three parties at the same time to break it. **This is the core barrier of Kujiale, not the rendering speed or the GPU cluster. **

But the opposing view also holds true: the premise of the network effect is that all three parties are "inseparable". If the brand owner transmits the model to 3D Home at the same time, and the designer uses Midjourney to produce renderings at the same time, the network effect will be diluted.

### Deviation 2: "Spatial intelligence is a good story"

Qunhe Technology redefined itself as a "spatial intelligence infrastructure company" in its IPO narrative and launched the SpatialVerse platform, claiming to provide training data and simulation environment for embodied AI robots.

The company did do a few things:

- Released the spatial language model SpatialLM and the spatial generation model SpatialGen
- Reached cooperation with embodied intelligence companies such as Zhiyuan Robot and Galaxy General
- Build a space simulation environment based on NVIDIA Isaac Sim

(Source: Qunhe Technology official release, Zhihu column, Zhiyuan community report)

**But look at the numbers**: In 2025, "other businesses" revenue, including SpatialVerse, will account for only **3.1%**. In other words, the revenue contributed by "spatial intelligence" is approximately **25 million yuan** - almost negligible in the total revenue of 820 million yuan.

From "design tools" to "spatial intelligence platforms", the commercial verification of this leap is far from complete. The market's current high valuation for Qunhe is largely pricing this narrative that has not yet been realized.

Opponent: The narrative has never been verified by current income during the IPO stage - what the market buys is the option value "in case it becomes successful". But precisely because it is an option, pricing should not be based on certainty.

### Deviation three: "Hangzhou Six Little Dragons" concept = long-term investment value

Qunhe Technology is the first company among the "Hangzhou Six Little Dragons" (Qunhe Technology, Yushu Technology, Yunshen Technology, Game Science, Deep Search, and Qiannao Technology) to be listed on the market. As the "vanguard" of concept stocks, it naturally carries the market's sentiment towards the entire AI sector.

The public over-subscription multiple of **1,071 times** and the first-day increase of **144%**—these figures reflect more enthusiasm for new products and concept hype than fundamental pricing.

(Data source: Qunhe Technology prospectus, Securities Times report)

Calm down and look at it: 97% of Qunhe Technology’s revenue comes from home decoration design SaaS**, which is completely different from Yushu’s robots and deep-searching large models in terms of business model. Putting them into one concept will not help much in the valuation analysis of group core.

## The real coordinates of Qunhe Technology

Put aside narratives and concepts, and use several dimensions to draw a true portrait of Qunhe Technology:

| Dimensions | Location of Qunhe Technology |
|------|-------------|
| Industry | China’s spatial design software market ranks **first**, with a market share of **23.2%** (2024, Frost & Sullivan) |
| Market size | China’s spatial design software market will be approximately **3.3 billion** in 2024 and is expected to be **6.6 billion** in 2029 (CAGR 14.9%) |
| Global benchmarking | Autodesk (market share 21.9%, global design software leader) is the most direct benchmark |
| Revenue growth | From **13.8%** in 2024 to **8.6%** in 2025 - a significant deceleration |
| Profit status | Just turned a loss after adjustment, still losing money according to IFRS, operating cash flow is negative |
| Valuation level | PS was about **14 times** at the time of IPO, and once exceeded **50 times** after listing - extremely expensive |

(Market data source: Frost & Sullivan Industry Report; Financial data source: Qunhe Technology Prospectus)

One sentence summary: ** Qunhe is a SaaS leader with a 23% share in a small pond of 3.3 billion yuan. It has good products and high gross profit, but its growth rate is slowing down, its profit foundation is weak, and its valuation has been speculated to a position that far exceeds its fundamentals. **
