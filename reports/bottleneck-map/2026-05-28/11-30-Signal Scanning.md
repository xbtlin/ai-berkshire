### Judgment

**Near term (2026-2027)**: CoWoS A-level bottleneck status **unchanged**. EMIB commercial production capacity has not yet been built; the SK Hynix packaging plant will produce output in 2028 at the earliest; TSMC is still the only large-scale source of AI 2.5D packaging.

**Mid-term (2028+)**: The first substantial alternative route supported by commercial yield comes into view. Unlike InP (10-year expansion cycle), CoWoS has a clearer replacement time window.

**Impact on existing watchlist objects**:
- Nittobo (T-glass): **not affected**. T-glass is used to prevent warping of IC substrates. Both CoWoS and EMIB require IC substrates. Both routes are potential customers of Nittobo.
- ABF substrate manufacturer: Similarly, EMIB packaging also requires an IC carrier board, so there is no need to worry about demand transfer.
- CoWoS direct suppliers: EMIB commercialization is a competitive threat, but the real impact will be limited before 2028.

**Reverse verification**: Has Intel EMIB yield 90% been verified? Source: April 2026 analyst report + TrendForce, no official disclosure from Intel. Cross-validation is required, but two independent sources are consistent, and the credibility is medium.
