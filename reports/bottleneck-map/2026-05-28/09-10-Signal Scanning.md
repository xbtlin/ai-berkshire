## Core judgment

### 1. South Korea’s zinc Tennessee smelter: strategic significance vs near-term investment significance

**Facts summary**:
- Announcement of interest in December 2025, formal announcement on April 2, 2026 (DoD 40% JV equity, $4.7 billion government loan)
- The first integrated zinc smelter in the United States in decades will be built and 13 strategic minerals will be extended
- Annual processing of 1.1 million tons of raw materials and annual output of 540,000 tons of finished products
- **Output minerals** (known): zinc, lead, copper, gold, silver, **antimony, indium, gallium, germanium**, bismuth, tellurium, cadmium, palladium
- Timeline: 2026 site construction → 2029 phased commercial output

- USAR Q1 2026 revenue is $5.7M, net loss is $67M/quarter, market value is $5.57B → P/S ~244x
- Building Western rare earth processing capabilities is the right direction, but USAR’s current valuation has fully priced in the narrative 10 years from now
