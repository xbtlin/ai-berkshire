# Look at how logical

1. **Nvidia personally ended up with lock-out capacity**: Nvidia has been able to bypass traditional supply chains by looking directly for copper-pilling suppliers, which is a very rare example of the true and severe supply shortage in history.

2. ** Four-fold expansion of capacity + very low PS**: Leith is the only positive scale-up HVLP4 manufacturer in Korea (3,700 ~ 16,000 tonnes/year). Previously purchased from PS 3.1x, which is low price in the structural deficit sector, 10yr PE exited 12.4% annually with a security margin.

3. ** Goldman Sachs ' three-year shortfall confirmed**: The supply and demand analysis at the institutional level shows that even with the completion of the Olyssian and Co-Tech full capacity expansion plans on schedule, there is a 28-39% gap in the effective industrial capacity for 2026-2028.

# Look at the empty logic

1. **The profit balance** is uncertain: the company is currently in a loss or near-loss balance, and profits are largely dependent on HVLP4 capacity. If the slope is not as fast as expected (delayed delivery of equipment, delayed customer certification), the assumption of a profit balance of H2,2026 may be delayed and affect investor patience.

2. **EV copper **: The company ' s historical owner, EV battery copper *, suffered a slowdown in EV demand from 2024-2025, one of the sources of loss. If the EV market deteriorates further (or shifts in technology routes), it may have a continuing negative impact on the company as a whole, weakening the positive contribution of HVLP4 operations.

**Customer concentration risk**: The main client is presumed to be the Nvidia (Vera Rubin). If Nvidia design overlaps (the next generation platform moves to different copper squid specifications) or shifts to other suppliers (e.g. Co-Tech has a significantly higher post-partum value), the growth logic of Leatten will be hit. This is a high concentration of customer risk.
