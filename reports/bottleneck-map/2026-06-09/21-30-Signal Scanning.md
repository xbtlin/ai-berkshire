## Detailed explanation of fiber optic bottlenecks

**The supply chain significance of Amazon-Corning’s large orders**:

The connection density of AI GPU clusters far exceeds that of traditional data centers. Each GPU cabinet requires approximately 36 times the fiber usage of a CPU cabinet. Taking a 100,000 GPU cluster as an example, the optical fiber requirements of a single supercomputing center can exceed 100 million connections, and the corresponding optical fiber cable consumption is measured in hundreds of millions of meters.

Corning’s three very large contracts in 2026 ($6 billion Meta + $3.2 billion Nvidia + Amazon, the amount was not disclosed), implying that fiber supply contracts in the next 2-3 years have been pre-locked by leading supercomputing buyers. This is a typical "stuck link price discovery" signal.

**Why there is no investable target**:
- Optical fiber supply is mainly concentrated in Corning (US, $154B), Prysmian (Italy, €20B), Hengtong (China, A shares), YOFC (China, A shares + Hong Kong stocks)
- Glass preform (upstream): Production is highly concentrated in Corning, Shin-Etsu Chemical (Japan, $30B), UK Fibre-Optic (unlisted)
- Layer 3 further upstream (high-purity SiO₂/Ge dopant): no pure target for investment
- **Conclusion**: Fiber optic bottlenecks are real, but are fully priced or uninvestable. Reserved as map background bottleneck.

**Future Watch**: If Hexatronic (HTRO.ST, Sweden, market cap ~€1B) or Sterlite Technologies (India, NSE: STRTECH) can pass full valuation verification, they may be considered for inclusion on the shortlist.
