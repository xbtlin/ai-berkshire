# Veeco Industries (VECO) - InP laser + SiPh manufacturing equipment full-time supplier, $250 M+ order confirmed

** Why is there a concern** Q1 2026 (Foundation 5 May) about disclosure of $250M+ multi-client InP laser and SiPh equipment orders (Spector IBD + Lumina MOCVD + WaferEtch), Q3 2026 starting delivery, Q1 2027 increasing significantly. ** Bottleneck Positioning**: Layer 3, InP laser + SiPh manufacturing equipment, Bottleneck Rating A

** Why this is a bottleneck**
1. EML (electric absorption modem laser) is the core device of 800G/16.T light transceiver, which must be prepared by InP extensions
2. In the case of the few suppliers of IP MOCVD extension equipment (global IPP optimization MOCVD platform for specialized cavity design), Lumina MOCVD IMP Platform of VECO is a few mature commercial products
3. The Veco Spector IBD (ion beam deposition for laser surface coating) is a critical step in EML production that cannot be skipped, with fewer equipment providers globally providing the process
4. Full process integration (out-of-way wet-to-end coating) further increases switching costs
