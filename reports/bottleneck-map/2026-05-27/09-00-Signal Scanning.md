**Bottleneck Determination**:
- Concentrated supply 🔴 (China produces 99% of yttrium and has implemented export controls)
- Production expansion cycle 🔴 (New mining sources in Australia/the United States/South Africa will only be meaningful after 2029)
- Difficulty of substitution 🟡 (Y₂O₃ is the current best plasma anti-corrosion coating, Al₂O₃ can be partially replaced but the performance gap is obvious)
- Capacity utilization 🔴 (2 North American companies have stopped production and supply has peaked)
- Demand growth🔴 (AI drives fab expansion → equipment demand increases → cavity maintenance frequency increases)
= **A-level bottleneck** (tentative; if the control is restarted in November, it will be upgraded to S-level)
