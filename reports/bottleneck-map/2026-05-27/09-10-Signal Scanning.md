### 🟡 Space-BACN → DIU transfer — InP military third demand vector enters procurement stage

**Trigger reason**: In May 2026, DARPA announced that the Space-BACN (Space-Based Adaptive Communication Node) project would be transferred to the DIU (Defense Innovation Unit) for procurement after completion of research and development by DARPA. Both executors (Mbryonics, Mynaric/acquired by Rocket Lab for $155M) have completed OISL terminal development and plan to conduct performance verification testing in the summer of 2026**. The Altera design was selected for further development at DIU.

**Meaning**:
1. Military satellite optical communication terminals from R&D stage to procurement stage (DIU is responsible for public bidding)
2. Each OISL terminal uses InP-based lasers; large-volume purchases will directly compete for the already extremely tight InP wafer production capacity
3. **This is the third independent downstream demand vector confirmation of the InP S-level bottleneck** (AI data center EML + hyperscale cloud CW/SiPh + military satellite OISL)
4. After the performance verification test is passed → DIU will open bidding → procurement contract → orders will flow into the InP supply chain (timeline: 2026 H2)

**InP Supply Chain Location Confirmation**:
```
Space-BACN satellite constellation (DIU procurement stage)
    └─ OISL terminal (InP-based laser, Mbryonics + RKLB/Mynaric)
         └─ InP epitaxial wafer (MOCVD growth) (Layer 2 — S-level bottleneck maintenance)
              └─ InP substrate (Layer 3 — S-level bottleneck maintenance)
                   └─ MOCVD equipment (Aixtron 70-90% share) (Layer 4 — Level A bottleneck maintained)
```

**Source**: [Breaking Defense Space-BACN → DIU Transfer](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/05/its-a-wrap-space-bacn-satellite-laser-link-program-shifts-from-darpa-to-diu/)
