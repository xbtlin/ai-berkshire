#Bottleneck Hunter — 2026-06-20 14:00 (Round 246)

##Clear the target

### Helix Exploration (HEX.L / HHEXF) - The only local producer of helium in North America. The Qatar crisis has turned it from a "potential target" to an "urgent opportunity"

**Why it’s worth paying attention to now (trigger event of this round)**

On February 28, 2026, Iranian drones attacked the Ras Laffan Industrial Zone in Qatar; starting in March, the Strait of Hormuz was effectively closed to Western commercial transportation. **About 27-30% of the global helium supply has been removed** (Qatar accounts for about 34% of the world, and all exports go through Hormuz). Spot prices rose by 70-100% within a week, with some transactions exceeding $1,000/Mcf; South Korean semiconductor factories began rationing, and the inventory is estimated to be about 6 months. Source: [CNBC March 19, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/19/the-iran-war-is-threatening-supply-helium-what-it-means-for-markets.html), [J2 Sourcing](https://j2sourcing.com/blog/helium-crisis-semiconductor-manufacturing-electronic-components-2026/)

HEX.L is a local producer in North America and does not rely on the Persian Gulf route. The Rudyard project in Montana has started production in February 2026 and has signed an offtake agreement for 100% of the production (counterparty: large industrial gas group, confidential).

---

**Bottleneck location**: Layer 3 — Helium (irreplaceable coolant for semiconductor wafer manufacturing/fiber drawing/MRI)
**Bottleneck Rating**: S-Class
- Supply concentration: 🔴 Available global suppliers plummeted after Qatar blockade
- Production expansion cycle: 🔴 It takes 5-8 years from exploration to production for a new helium well
- Difficulty of substitution: 🔴 There is no substitute for helium in wafer manufacturing
- Capacity utilization: 🔴 North American production capacity is close to 100% full capacity
- Demand growth rate: 🔴 AI server fiber optic + chip fab demand >30%/year
- Customer verification cycle: 🔴 Industrial gas user certification cycle > 1 year

**Look at the long logic**

1. **Geographic Moat**: The Qatar/Hormuz blockade is a structural supply shock rather than seasonal, local North American producers are unperturbed, and customers (industrial gas groups) have clear long-term demand drivers with incentives to move suppliers inland.
2. **Output Leverage**: The Ingomar Dome project is 3-5 times the size of Rudyard. Once it is put into production, revenue will jump in steps, but the market value has not yet reflected this option.
3. **Price mechanism**: The spot price of helium has doubled, and the next round of offtake renewal prices is expected to increase significantly (the current contract price may only be $400-700/Mcf, and the spot price is >$1,000/Mcf).

**Bearish logic**

1. **Geographic reversal**: If Hormuz reopens and Qatar’s helium flows back, the spot price may fall sharply, and the risk of HEX valuation revision is significant.
2. **Production Execution Risk**: The Ingomar Dome project has not yet been put into production, and any project delays will directly affect revenue expectations.
3. **Low data transparency**: HEX.L is a small market capitalization of AIM, with limited disclosure of contract price and production data, and high estimation uncertainty.

**Recommendation**: Execute `/investment-research` for in-depth research; prioritize checking the Ingomar Dome production forecast and the next offtake renewal window.
