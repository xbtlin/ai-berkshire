## 3. In-depth update of WF6 demand side - broader understanding than before

**Original knowledge (reported as of 16:00)**: WF6 is mainly used in AI logic chip manufacturing (advanced node copper/tungsten interconnect).

**New data (Digitimes 2026-06-16)**:
> "Global expansion in AI chips, **advanced logic processes, and multilayer 3D NAND flash memory** is driving demand for WF6."

The demand for WF6 in 3D NAND comes from the tungsten filling process (CVD-W) of each layer of metal word lines. The more multi-layer stacks (now mainstream 200-300 layers), the greater the consumption of WF6.

**Quantitative verification (Kioxia data)**:
- NAND Market Q1 2026 = **$46B** (quarterly record, +90% QoQ)
- Kioxia Q1 net profit: ¥869B (**+4,800% year-on-year**), operating profit ¥1.298T (+29 times)
- Enterprise SSD prices: +53-58% in a single quarter (record increase)
- Kioxia 2026 full-year production capacity: **all sold** (no remaining allocation)

**Impact Assessment**: WF6’s two major demand engines (AI logic chip + 3D NAND) are running at full speed at the same time, while the supply side is permanently shrinking by 25% (July 1). This makes the WF6 supply and demand gap in the second half of 2026 likely to be more severe than estimated in the 16:00 report. **WF6 S-level bottleneck rating is maintained, and the logic is more stable. **
