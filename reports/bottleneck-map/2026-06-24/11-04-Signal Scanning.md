### 🟡 Signal 2: SK Hynix slows down the pace of HBM4 production conversion (Today, June 24, Digitimes)

**Triggering event (June 24 Digitimes latest report)**: SK Hynix announced that it will **slow down the pace of HBM4 production capacity conversion** and reallocate part of its production capacity to bulk DRAM. The reason is that the profit margin of bulk DRAM has rebounded sharply and its profitability has exceeded expectations.

**Why it’s worth recording**:
- Overall HBM shortage continues, but **HBM4 ramp may be slower than originally expected**
- SK Hynix CEO’s previous goal was to lock in all 2026 HBM orders by the end of June – today’s news hints at an adjustment in internal priorities
- HBM4E samples have been shipped ahead of schedule on June 18 (12 layers, 48GB/stack, ~4TB/s). It is technically leading, but the ramp-up pace of mass production has slowed down.

**Impact on existing watch lists**:
- **FormFactor (FORM)**: Neutral to negative signal - FORM's HBM probe card business benefits from the ramp-up of HBM4 mass production, and the slowdown of SK Hynix means that the arrival time of FORM's HBM4 test demand may be delayed by 1-2 quarters. The current price of $149-154 already includes the expected climb of HBM4. When the entry judgment of $121 remains unchanged, the entry target may need to be lowered to the range of $110-115.
- **HBM overall shortage**: S-level maintenance - The sharp rise in bulk DRAM profit margins itself proves that the overall memory is tight and does not affect HBM's long-term demand judgment.

**Source**: Digitimes, June 24, 2026 (https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260624VL204/sk-hynix-hbm4-dram-hbm-samsung.html）
