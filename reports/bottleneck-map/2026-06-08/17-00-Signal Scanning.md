### 2. CoWoP technology roadmap - 2028+ risks of 3110.T

**Source**: GlobalSMT Advanced Packaging Analysis / TweakTown Nvidia CoWoP report / The Vick's Newsletter Manufacturing Challenge article

**Core logic**:
- CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-**Substrate**): interposer → ABF organic substrate → PCB (requires ABF/T-glass)
- CoWoP (Chip-on-Wafer-on-**Platform PCB**): interposer → Direct high-density PCB (**Bypass ABF layer**)
- Nvidia target: Rubin GR150 GPU, late 2026 pilot → available in 2027 → **mass production in 2028**

**Why it’s not a recent threat**:
1. **2026 full year: CoWoS/ABF all locked** - Blackwell, Vera Rubin Ultra (2026 H2) still use CoWoS
2. **Technical challenges**: CoWoP faces the risk of warpage, and the yield rate has not been verified on a large scale.
3. **Gradual Transition**: Nvidia’s strategy is to run CoWoS and CoWoP in parallel, not as a replacement.
4. **Nittobo production expansion will not be implemented until 2027**: Even if CoWoP partially diverts demand, the 2026-2027 T-glass gap has been locked

**Why you need to track**:
- If CoWoP mass production goes smoothly, the growth rate of ABF substrate demand may decline after 2028
- Reverse verification: Nittobo's next-generation T-glass (2028, CTE from 2.8→2.0 ppm) is oriented to the next generation of high-end applications. If CoWoP bypasses ABF, the application scenarios of the next-generation T-glass need to be re-evaluated.
- This is one of the obstacles** of **3110.T ★★★★→★★★★★

**Conclusion**: 2026-2027: Does not affect the investment thesis of 3110.T. 2028+: Continuous tracking is required. **Current rating ★★★★ unchanged**
