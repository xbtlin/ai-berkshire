##1. ICL Group (NYSE: ICL) - Brominated: tracked, independently cross-checked in this round

** Why is it a bottleneck**

Bromine — Semiconductor-grade hydrogen brominated (HBr Gas) — DRAM/NAND fence polar etching process (Si etching is the highest option, the only practical chemical at advanced nodes).

- ** Supply concentration**: ICL Group (Israel/Jordan Dead Sea mining) controls about 40% of global production of bromine; Israel + Jordan together accounts for 2/3% of the world total
- **Customer concentration**: ICL provides 97% of the import of bromine in Korea - Samsung, SK Hynix HBM/DRAM relies mainly on a single geo-source
- ** Non-replacement**: Cl2/HCl is more than disadvantaged in the selection of the advanced DRAM node, "fundamentally inferrior process at the precision recuered"; and the change of supplier/process requires multi-year certification
- ** Demand-driven**: HBM sold out to 2027, three major manufacturers (SK Hynix/Micron/Samsung) with full pre-allocation of 2026 capacity, DRAM manufacturing for strong expansion = high growth in HBr demand
