## Detailed analysis of core bottleneck: Tungsten hexafluoride (WF6) - S grade

### Bottleneck determination (6 standards all red)

| Criteria | Rating | Specifics |
|------|------|---------|
| Supply concentration | 🔴 | Two Japanese companies (Kanto Denka + Chuo Glass) stopped production on July 1; South Korea only has SK Specialty + Foosung; China has only CSSC Specialty + a small number of second-tier companies |
| Production expansion cycle | 🔴 | New production capacity construction + customer certification takes 18-24 months, H2 2026-2027 will be in short supply throughout the entire process |
| Difficulty of substitution | 🔴 | WF6 is the **only** commercial tungsten thin film CVD precursor and cannot be replaced (used for logic below 7nm, HBM, 3D NAND) |
| Capacity utilization rate | 🔴 | CSSC Special Gas’s 2,000 tons/year production capacity is already at high load |
| Demand growth | 🔴 | AI chips, HBM, and 3D NAND continue to expand production, >50%/year |
| Customer verification cycle | 🔴 | Semiconductor material certification usually >1 year |

**Bottleneck Rating: Class S (Single Point of Failure)** — 6/6 red light

### Event Timeline

- **January 2026**: China tightens tungsten export controls, and Japan’s imports of high-purity tungsten powder almost return to zero
- **March 2026**: Kanto Denka and Chuo Glass issued permanent production suspension notices to Samsung/SK Hynix/TSMC
- **April 2026**: China's average export price rose from US$68.75/kg to US$149.79/kg (+118%, +204% month-on-month)
- **June 12, 2026**: TrendForce confirmed price +200% (Source: TrendForce 2026-06-12)
- **July 1, 2026** (9 days later): Japan's Kanto Denka + Chuo Glass officially permanently ceased production
- **Magnitude of impact**: The combined production capacity of the two companies is ~2000-2200 tons/year, accounting for ~25% of the global supply; the expected supply gap in H2 2026 is 2000 tons

### Market size (TAM)

The global WF6 market size will be approximately $740 million in 2026 (source: Businessresearchinsights), with a CAGR of 18.6%, and is forecast to reach $3.45 billion in 2035.
