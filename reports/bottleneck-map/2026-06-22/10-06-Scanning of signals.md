## Unimicron (3037.TW) ABF Valuation Check – Full Report

> This section completes the "must do in the next round" tasks clearly required by the 09:07 scan.

### Fundamentals
- **Market capitalization**: TWD 1.57T ≈ **$49B** (@988/share × 1.59 billion shares)
- **FY2025 revenue**: Q1 2026 = NT$37.4B (+24.5% YoY), annualized approximately NT$150B ≈ **$4.7B**
- **PS(TTM)**: **10.57x** (StockAnalysis data)
- **PE (TTM)**: **81x** (CompaniesMarketCap weighted, Stockopedia 44.65x, sources inconsistent)
- **EPS**: NT$6.96/share
- **Gross Margin**: 18% (Q1 2026)
- **ABF share**: NVIDIA GPU substrate ~35%, ASIC (Google TPU/AWS Trainium) ~50%+
- **Morgan Stanley Forecast**: EPS 105% CAGR 2025-2028, price target NT$500

### Valuation red light check (section 4.2.1)

| Conditions | Check results |
|------|---------|
| PS>30x and growth rate <100% | ✅ PS=10.57x, not triggered |

**Conclusion: Red light 1 triggered (market value > TAM 20%) → Signal strength capped ★★, marked ⚠️ Valuation overdraft**

### Valuation yellow light check

| Conditions | Check results |
|------|---------|
| PE>80x | 🟡 **Yellow light**: PE 81x, PEG analysis required |
| PEG (using Morgan Stanley EPS CAGR 105%) | PEG = 81/105 = **0.77** → PEG<1, growth actually supports valuation |

### Valuation safety margin test (10-year 25x PE exit method)

- Buy market value: $49B
- 10-year annualized target of 10%: $49B × 2.59 = **$127B**
- 25x Net profit required for PE exit: $127B ÷ 25 = **$5.1B/year**
- Annual revenue corresponding to 15% net profit margin: $34B (7.2 times today’s $4.7B)
- Required revenue CAGR: ($34B/$4.7B)^(1/10) - 1 ≈ **21.9%**
- **Conclusion: If Morgan Stanley's 105% EPS CAGR is realized and stabilizes to 20%+ after 3 years, a safety margin will exist after 10 years; but it needs to grow from $4.7B to $34B - 7.2 times today's scale, relying on the continued expansion of the ABF market into the 2030s. There are many prerequisites and high uncertainty. **

### Counter arguments

1. **Intel’s IC substrate business contributes greatly**: Unimicron’s IC substrate customers include Intel (CPU). Intel’s market share has been eroded by AMD in the AI era. If Intel’s substrate demand slows down, it will drag down the overall market.
2. **Taiwan’s geopolitical risk**: All production capacity is in Taiwan, and geopolitical pressure is an exogenous variable.
3. **ABF bottleneck is time-sensitive**: Ajinomoto plans to expand production by 50% before 2030. If the supply of ABF film improves, the price premium will decrease.

### Investment Conclusion

**★★ ⚠️ Valuation Overdraft (Red Light)**

The ABF S-class bottleneck logic is completely established, and Unimicron is the purest Taiwanese ABF substrate leader. However, the current market value of $49B has fully reflected (and overdrawn in advance) the rapid growth in the next three years.

**Waiting for the opportunity**: Re-evaluate when PS falls back to <8x (approximately NT$755/share, currently about 24% downside). A complete analysis will be done at that time.
