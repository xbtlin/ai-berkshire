## Key downstream verification (Mang format negation)

**WF₆ Kanto Denka**:
1. **Korea Substitution**: SK Specialty and Foosung are both suppliers (the source of tungsten powder is the same) and cannot fill the gap in the short term. The shortage is a global phenomenon and not an isolated phenomenon in Japan → cannot be filled by Korean competitors.
2. **Inventory Buffer**: Samsung/SK Hynix currently rely on inventory to maintain, and may last until June-July. Short-term demand is not interrupted → the shortage outbreak will have a lag effect of 3-4 weeks
3. **Domestic Substitution** (China): China already has WF₆ production (Walter Gas, Nanda Optoelectronics), which can theoretically be substituted - **but**: TSMC/Samsung supply chain has certification barriers for Chinese-sourced WF₆, and the high-purity verification cycle is about 6-18 months, which cannot be filled in the short term

**SiC Bottleneck**:
- Real bottleneck, but existing listed companies cannot invest (Wolfspeed loses money, Coherent valuation is too high)
- It’s not that there are no bottlenecks, it’s that there are no reasonably priced targets.
