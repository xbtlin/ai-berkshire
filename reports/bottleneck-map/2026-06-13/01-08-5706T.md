### Mitsui Metal Mining (5706.T)—the world’s No. 1 supplier of HVLP4 copper foil, extremely low valuation

**Why it’s worth paying attention to now**:
Goldman Sachs released a special report on HVLP4 high-end copper foil from June 10 to 12, 2026, confirming that the structural shortage will reach 28-39% from 2026 to 2028, a "three-year new normal." NVIDIA bypassed CCL manufacturers and directly negotiated with the second largest supplier Co-Tech on June 8 to lock in production capacity - while Mitsui Metals is the world's largest supplier with a monthly production capacity cap of 490 tons (H2 2026 demand ≥ 560 tons/month). Mitsui Metals FY2026 (as of March 2026) operating profit +75% YoY hit a record high, mainly driven by HVLP copper foil. Market pricing is still treated as a traditional mining and metallurgical company (PE 11x), and the explosion of AI demand has not yet been fully reflected.

**Bottleneck location**: Layer 2, high-end copper foil/HVLP4, bottleneck rating **S level**

High-end copper foil is used for high-frequency and high-speed PCBs in AI servers (NVL72 cabinets, etc.), which require extremely low roughness surfaces (VLP/HVLP/HVLP2/HVLP4 upgrade from generation to generation). HVLP4 is currently the highest specification and has extremely high technical barriers. There are only 2 major suppliers in the world:
- Mitsui Metals (5706.T): monthly production capacity of 490 tons, No. 1 in the world
- Co-Tech (8358.TWO): Taiwan TPEX, second in the world

**Supply and Demand Data**:
- H2 monthly demand in 2026: ≥560 tons, exceeding Mitsui Metals’ monthly production capacity by 490 tons
- The combined production capacity of the two companies only meets 50-60% of the total industry demand (Goldman Sachs data)
- Supply gap: 28% in 2026, 39% in 2027, 38% in 2028 (Goldman Sachs three-year forecast)
- NVIDIA has intervened: bypassing downstream CCL manufacturers to directly lock in HVLP4 production capacity and coordinate inventory accumulation with Google/AWS/Meta
