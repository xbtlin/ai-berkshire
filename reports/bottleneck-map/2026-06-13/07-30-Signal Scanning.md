## GOES single point risk notes

Cleveland-Cliffs (CLF) is the **only** domestic supplier of GOES in the United States. This means:

> "If a major equipment failure occurred at a Cleveland-Cliffs facility, the domestic supply of transformer steel would effectively drop to zero overnight." — PowerMag 2026

This description meets the S-class bottleneck criteria (single point of failure). But:
- CLF is a large steel company that loses money, and GOES is not its main business
- The United States relies on imported supplements (from Japan, South Korea, Germany)
- Not a pure investment target

**A more valuable entry point**: Japanese GOES manufacturers (Nippon Steel 5401.T/JFE Holdings 3002.T), or research on the global GOES market pattern. Join the "next round of research" queue.
