## In-depth analysis of helium bottleneck (new to the system)

### Why is it a bottleneck?

**Physical Supply Chain Location**: Layer 3-4 Materials (Basic Process Gases)

Irreplaceable applications of helium in semiconductor manufacturing:
- **Wafer cooling** (backside cooling during ion implantation and etching to prevent photoresist degradation)
- **Plasma etching carrier gas** (in RIE/ICP etching chamber)
- **CVD thin film deposition carrier gas** (PECVD, ALD process)
- **Leak detection** (because the molecules are extremely small, it is the only practical leak detection gas)
- **Wafer cleaning and transfer environment** (inert protective atmosphere)

**Bottleneck evaluation based on 6 criteria**:
| # | Criteria | Assessment | Score |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Supply concentration | From the three major sources of Qatar + the United States + Russia, 33% of Qatar's sudden cuts | 🔴 |
| 2 | Production expansion cycle | Helium is a by-product of natural gas mining and needs to be bound to new natural gas fields, >3 years | 🔴 |
| 3 | Difficulty of substitution | No substitute for semiconductor grade (argon gas can replace some but not all applications) | 🔴 |
| 4 | Capacity utilization rate | Supply plummets by 33%, remaining production capacity is 100% full | 🔴 |
| 5 | Demand growth | AI data center construction → wafer output target continues to rise | 🔴 |
| 6 | Verification cycle | New source certification (high purity requirements) takes 6-18 months | 🟡 |

**Bottleneck rating: S level (5 red + 1 yellow)** ← Single point failure level, but the investable target does not exist
