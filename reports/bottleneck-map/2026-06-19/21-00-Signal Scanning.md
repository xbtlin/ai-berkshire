## NF3 market structure supplement (for future in-depth research)

NF3 is an essential chamber cleaning gas in 3D NAND/HBM manufacturing. Its specific characteristics are:
- **Irreplaceable**: NF3 is used for CVD chamber cleaning (atomic layer deposition, plasma-enhanced CVD), and there is currently no mature alternative gas.
- **Demand Rigidity**: Each advanced process wafer factory consumes hundreds of tons of NF3 every year; NF3 does not reduce production without reducing quantity
- **Low price sensitivity**: NF3 accounts for <1% of the total wafer manufacturing cost, and the purchaser is not price sensitive

Major global NF3 suppliers (non-Japanese):
- Linde (global market share about 25%) - market capitalization $200B+, large market capitalization
- Air Products (APD, global market share about 20%) - market capitalization $63B, large market capitalization
- SK Specialty / SK Materials (South Korea, not listed)
- Showa Denko/Resonac (Japan) - Complex business outside NF3, murky valuation
