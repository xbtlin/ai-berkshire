## Coherent Texas Groundbreaking: Revisions to IQE Risk Assessment

**Previous round (round 236) record**: "Coherent builds $154M 6-inch InP wafer fab in Sherman, Texas, doubling production capacity in 12 months"

**This round of revisions (Source: Coherent official, NVIDIA blog, CHIPS Act announcement 2026-06-16)**:

| Project | Front wheel record | After correction |
|------|---------|-------|
| NVIDIA investment | $154 million | **$2 billion** (announced in March) |
| CHIPS Act Support | Not Mentioned | **$50M** (DoC LOI) |
| Total facility size | ~$154 million | **$650 million** |
| Capacity changes | Doubling (in 12 months) | **Quadruple** (in 12 months) |
| Groundbreaking time | Unknown | **June 16, 2026** |
| Importance | Jensen Huang's presence | Confirmation |

**Impact Analysis on IQE ★★★★Rating**:

Does Coherent's Sherman expansion threaten IQE's Tower/MACOM customer base?

**Positive: IQE is not a direct replacement**
- Coherent's InP capacity is used for its own EML laser production and supplies vertically integrated products (optical transceivers) to NVIDIA
- Tower Semiconductor (IQE's multi-year contract customer) is Coherent's competitor in the photonic IC foundry field and will not purchase Coherent's epiwafer
- MACOM (IQE strategic investor + long-term agreement) also competes with Coherent
- Essentially: Coherent is a vertically integrated competitor, not an alternative supplier to IQE customers

**Positive: Scale expansion verification paper**
- Coherent is willing to invest $2 billion+ within 12 months to obtain four times the InP capacity, proving that the urgency of the AI data center InP demand gap has reached Capex level
- Same day as 2026-06-18: Tower+Marvell announced that it has shipped a total of 5 million Coherent Photonic ICs (the production capacity of the Tower SiPho platform is really tight, and the demand for IQE continues to rise)

**Negative: Risks to monitor**
- If Coherent opens epiwafer for export in the future (not its main business, but cannot be ruled out)
- Within 12 months (around June 2027), global 6-inch InP epiwafer supply will increase significantly due to Coherent, which may trigger IQE pricing pressure
- The scale of Coherent, which has a $2 billion investment, far exceeds that of IQE, which has a market capitalization of $596M, and its competitive resources are asymmetric.

**Conclusion: IQE rating maintained ★★★★**. Coherent's expansion is a vertical integration of competitors, not direct customer replacement, and confirms the authenticity of the InP bottleneck. However, it is necessary to monitor whether Coherent is exporting epiwafer. Raise the risk level of "Coherent Vertical Integration Threat" from "known but limited" to "known and significant (four times capacity, $2 billion in capital)."

**Bottleneck location**: Korea Fluorochemical/Special Gas Company is one of the major suppliers of WF6 (tungsten hexafluoride) in Korea. | WF6 business accounts for % of revenue | Unknown (Foosung is also involved in automotive chemicals, battery materials, and refrigerants) |

**Preliminary judgment on valuation**:
- PS 3.77x is a reasonable level (green light) and does not trigger the red light condition
- But the key question: **The proportion of WF6 business accounting for Foosung's total revenue is unknown**. If WF6 only accounts for 20% of income, the actual benefit elasticity will be greatly discounted.
- Another question: Is the WF6 supply gap a structural bottleneck? China's CSSC production capacity has reached 2,000 tons/year. After Foosung's certification is completed in August, there may be more alternative supply in the market, and the price increase cycle may be shorter.

**Preliminary conclusion**: PS is reasonable, but the purity of the WF6 business is insufficient (not a single-product company), and the supply gap may be partially alleviated within 6-9 months due to China and South Korea's production capacity certification. It is recommended to join the watch list for further verification, **not as a clear target**.
