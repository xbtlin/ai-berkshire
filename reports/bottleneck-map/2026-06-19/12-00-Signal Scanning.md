### Extended conclusion: Where is the real Layer 3 bottleneck of the transformer?

The three major Korean manufacturers are all Layer 1-2 (complete machine manufacturers) and have fully priced their products. Real Layer 3 bottleneck candidates:
- **Core material: GOES steel** - Cleveland-Cliffs (CLF), the only supplier in the United States ($5B market capitalization, but Q1 losses and GOES's proportion is small, has been excluded)
- **Copper Winding Wire**——High-purity oxygen-free copper winding wire, you need to find a supplier specializing in transformer-grade copper products
- **Transformer grade insulation materials** - Nomex, special insulation paper (DuPont is the main supplier, but it is not a pure standard with small market value)
- **Transformer oil** - mineral oil, silicone oil (supplied by many bulk chemical companies, sufficient competition)

**Next step**: Special search for local small market value suppliers of transformer copper windings/insulation materials in the United States/Europe.

### Impact Assessment: Positive and Negative Sides of Foosung’s Paper

**Front**:
- Foosung's customer certification (Samsung/SK Hynix) becomes a core asset - even if WF6 comes from China CSSC, it still needs to go through Foosung's certification channel to reach end customers
- Becoming a China-South Korea WF6 trade hub, distribution revenue can grow rapidly even if self-produced output is limited
- 2026 contract price +70-90% has been locked, regardless of the source of procurement, the interest spread and service fee are considerable

**Negative**:
- If most of the WF6 is imported from China CSSC (rather than produced independently by Foosung), the gross profit margin will be lower than that produced by Foosung
- The stability of China's CSSC supply depends on China's export policy - geopolitical risks shift from Japan to China
- If TSMC/Samsung negotiates directly with CSSC (bypassing Foosung certification), Foosung's intermediary value will decrease

**Conclusion**: Foosung's core value remains unchanged (existing customer certification relationship), but the business model has shifted from "pure producer" to "certification + distribution hub". Verification of WF6 business revenue ratio is still key - if distribution revenue is included, the >30% standard may be met. **Verification of WF6 business proportion is still the top priority**.
