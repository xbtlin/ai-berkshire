#### ICL paper conditions re-evaluation

**Original paper pillars**:
1. Bromine production is highly concentrated (ICL 33%, Jordan bromine 14%) → pricing power
2. Dead Sea geographical exclusivity → lasting cost advantage
3. Structural growth in downstream demand for AI/EV (flame retardants, MLCC coatings)
4. The Hormuz crisis hindered Jordanian bromine exports → ICL relatively benefited

**Paper changes after ceasefire**:
- ✅ Pillar 1 (production concentration): unchanged, ICL is still No. 1 in the world
- ✅ Pillar 2 (Dead Sea geography): unchanged
- ✅ Pillar 3 (AI/EV demand): unchanged
- ❌ Pillar 4 (Hormuz benefits): ** is fading **, Jordanian bromine exports resume → supply pressure resumes

**Net Effect**: The core of the thesis remains, but the unconventional factors (war shutdowns) that supported high bromine prices ($4,000+) are disappearing. $3,476 remains above the $3,000 level, but downside risks have increased.

**ICL Financial Status (Q1 2026)**:
- Revenue: $2 billion (+14% YoY)
- Adjusted EBITDA: $412 million (+15% YoY)
- Full-year EBITDA guidance: $1.5-1.7B (upgraded)
- Stock price: $6.64 USD (estimated market value ~$8-9B USD)
- PS: It is estimated to be about 4-5x → **not considered a valuation overdraft**

**Look at the long logic**:
1. ICL Q1 2026 results are strong, EBITDA guidance is raised, and even if bromine prices normalize, there are still multiple businesses (potash fertilizers, phosphates) to support
2. The production cost advantage of the Dead Sea is a long-term moat. Even if Jordan’s bromine recovers, ICL will still be the low-cost leader in the industry.
3. Long-term structural growth in demand for AI/EV flame retardants has nothing to do with Hormuz

**Short selling logic**:
1. Bromine prices have fallen by 71% from their peak, and continuing to fall below $3,000 will significantly impact ICL’s profits.
2. After Jordan’s bromine recovery, global bromine supply increased by approximately 14%, and price pressure continued
3. ICL guidance of $1.5-1.7B EBITDA assumes that bromine prices remain at a high level. If the price falls further, it will need to be revised downwards.

**Recommendation**: ICL **Maintain ★★★★ but mark "Thesis conditions are weakening, monitor closely"**. Reasons for not downgrading immediately: ① $3,476 is still holding the line; ② ICL business is diversified; ③ It will still take time from ceasefire to full navigation (30 days of mine clearance). If the bromine price spot data falls below $3,200 on June 19-20, a re-evaluation will be triggered.
