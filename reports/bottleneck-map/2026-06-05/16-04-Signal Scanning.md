## Key Signal Analysis: NEG D2 Fiber vs. Nittobo

### Signal content
- Nippon Electric Glass this week announced D2 Fiber, a new low-dielectric glass fiber product
- Target application: Semiconductor packaging substrate (direct competition with Nittobo NER glass)
- NEG also announced the development of T-glass competing products (Nittobo currently has about 90% market share)
- NEG’s share price increased by 11% in a single week, and the market believes this is a major strategic turning point

### Impact on Nittobo (3110.T) watchlist

**Potential Negatives**:
1. The entry of competition has accelerated the pressure on the tracked timeline of "T-glass market share 90% → 55% (2028, TrendForce)"
2. NEG is a large manufacturer (not a start-up company) capable of mass production, and its entry credibility is higher than that of ordinary competitors.
3. Paradis Labs analysis (X post in the same period) pointed out: Nittobo T-glass and NER-glass are currently unable to meet the demand before mid-2027, and customers have begun to actively look for alternative suppliers - this forms mutual verification with NEG’s entry

**Key Uncertainties (Considers)**:
1. **Release ≠ Mass Production**: D2 Fiber is currently in the announcement stage, and it usually takes 12-24 months from announcement to large-scale mass production.
2. Certification cycle: Low dielectric materials entering the mainstream PCB/substrate supply chain require 1-2 years of verification by the client.
3. June 29, the Nittobo split record date, still stands, and the stock split itself is not affected by competition.
4. In the short term (2026-2027), Nittobo production capacity is still in short supply, and NEG cannot immediately alleviate the gap.

### Rating Impact
- Nittobo ★★★★ **Maintain** (the mass production schedule is unknown, the current gap is real), but a downgrade trigger is set up
- **Downgrade Trigger**: If evidence of commercial shipment of NEG D2 Fiber appears in 2026 (not just announcement) → Re-evaluate to ★★★
