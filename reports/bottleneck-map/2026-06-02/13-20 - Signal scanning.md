## 3. 5 items to be verified — all closed

### Entry 1: Is the proportion of photonic SOI segment revenue in total revenue >30%?
**Conclusion: ❌ Not up to standard**
- Photonics-SOI: ~€93-130M (conservative/optimistic estimate range)
- Total revenue: €592M
- Proportion: **~16-22%**, lower than the 30% threshold
- Even if Morgan Stanley’s optimistic estimate of €130M is used, the proportion is only 22%

*Counter argument*: If the growth rate of Photonics-SOI maintains 60% in FY2027 (→€200M+), and the total revenue returns to €700-750M, the proportion may be close to 30% by then. But currently (FY2026) it is not satisfied.

### Entry 2: PE/PEG calculation (whether there is a loss)?
**Conclusion: ❌ FY2026 operating loss, P/E not applicable**
- Operating profit and loss: -€131M (including €105M non-recurring impairment)
- Operating profit and loss excluding non-recurring items: approximately €26M (still in loss)
- P/E: meaningless (loss-making company)
- **Valuation red light: Loss-making target, P/E cannot give a green light**

*Contrary argument*: Free cash flow +€63M is positive, indicating that actual cash generation capability is better than operating profit; it is expected to return to profitability under the recovery direction of FY2027. However, P/E valuation cannot be done at present.

### Entry 3: 10-year 25xPE exit method annualized return
**Conclusion: ❌ Unable to calculate (loss-making enterprise)**
- Unable to establish a profit basis due to net loss in FY2026
- Estimated profit in FY2027: Assume revenue €700M × 10% net interest rate = €70M net profit, 25xPE exit in 10 years = €1,750M (much lower than the current market capitalization €4.5B)
- Even with the above optimistic estimates, the annualized return is negative → **Deep Red Light**

### Item 4: Analyst target price and rating consensus
**Data**:
- Consensus target price among 18 analysts: €60.61 (including a high of €99 and a low of €46)
- Current share price (+14% after 2026-05-27): approximately €89 (estimate, from April €78 → results day +14% ≈ €89)
- **Current share price > Analyst consensus price target** (€89 > €60.61)
- Some analysts raised their target to €79.51 (still below current price)

**Conclusion: ❌ The current stock price is above the consensus target of analysts, with no margin of safety**

### Item 5: FY2026-2027 revenue growth guidance (SiPho segment growth rate)
- Q1'27 total revenue guidance: +15% YoY (constant currency)
- Photonics-SOI FY2026 growth rate: +60-70% YoY (qualitative statement by management + quantitative by Morgan Stanley)
- FY2027 Photonics-SOI growth rate forecast: continued rapid growth, may reach 60-80%
- Head office revenue: Expected recovery in FY2027 (recovery after Mobile RF-SOI inventory is digested)

**Conclusion**: The growth direction is positive, but Photonics-SOI will need at least FY2027-FY2028 to increase from ~€100M to >30% of the total revenue threshold (>€210M), by which time total revenue will also recover.
