## 1. Detailed analysis of Yttrium rare earth bottleneck

**Why is it a bottleneck:**

Yttrium is an irreplaceable raw material for turbine blade thermal barrier coating (TBC), and is also used in YAG lasers, YSZ solid electrolytes (SOFC/batteries), and semiconductor special processes. After China launched export controls on heavy rare earths such as yttrium/dysprosium/terbium/scandium in April 2025, U.S. imports plummeted from 333 tons within 8 months to 17 tons (**-95%**).

**Bottleneck 6 ratings:**
| Criteria | Score | Basis |
|------|------|------|
| Supply concentration | 🔴 | China controls ~85-90% of global yttrium supply |
| Expansion cycle | 🔴 | Mine + separation plant > 2 years to build |
| Alternative Difficulty | 🔴 | Turbo TBC has no certified alternative |
| Capacity utilization rate | 🔴 | Export control is equivalent to "effective capacity = 0" |
| Demand growth | 🟡 | Aviation defense recovers, semiconductors are slow, 20-50% range |
| Customer verification cycle | 🔴 | New suppliers entering the aviation market require 12-24 months for certification |

**Bottleneck rating: 🔴🔴🔴🔴🔴 → A level (close to S level, but the downstream is aviation/defense rather than the semiconductor mainstream, and the impact on the semiconductor part is not clear yet) **

**Confirmed actual impact (multiple sources):**
- Two North American yttrium-based coating manufacturers have **suspended production** (CSIS data, 2026-05)
- At least one company completely stopped selling products containing yttrium oxide (stocks depleted)
- Aviation engine OEM (unnamed) reported shortages and rationing
- Prices increased approximately 69 times year-on-year (confirmed by Assembly Magazine)

**There is currently no pure investment target:**
- **USA Rare Earth (USAR)**: is acquiring Serra Verde Group (including yttrium) for $2.8B, but: ① The acquisition will not be closed until Q3 2026, ② Even if it is closed, it will be diversified REE rather than pure yttrium, ③ USAR currently has no revenue (mine development stage), the valuation cannot be PS/PE, and the signal strength does not exceed★
- **Energy Fuels (UUUU)**: White Mesa Mill processes monazite containing yttrium, but the proportion of yttrium revenue is far below the 30% purity threshold
- **MP Materials (MP)**: Mainly Nd/Pr, yttrium is not a strategic focus
- **Conclusion: The bottleneck is real, but there are currently no qualified investment targets. ** After the acquisition of USAR Serra Verde is completed (Q3 2026), the valuation will be re-valued. At that time, it will be determined whether to be included in the watchlist depending on the proportion of yttrium revenue.
