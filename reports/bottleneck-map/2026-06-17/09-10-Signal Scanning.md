## WF6 Supply Alternative Timeline Update (Important Structural Correction)

The last round (07:00) scan described WF6 as being in short supply "until 2027". Important correction signals found this round:

### New developments on the supply side

**Foosung-CSSC (China Space Special Gas) Certification Process**:
- China Aerospace Special Gas = A subsidiary of China State Shipbuilding Corporation, a domestic WF6 supplier that has supplied to Korean companies such as Samsung
- Foosung aims to start importing large quantities from CSSC in August
- Key restrictions: Samsung/SK Hynix needs to complete internal re-certification of Chinese source WF6 (chip factory re-certification usually takes 6-18 months)
- But Foosung is a certified supplier → its procurement source switching is faster than the entry of new suppliers

### Time window correction

| Time Period | Supply Status | Impact |
|------|---------|------|
| **June (current)** | Japan’s inventory depletion stage, price +204% | The bottleneck is the tightest |
| **July 1** | Kanto Denka+Central Glass permanently suspends production | 25% of global production capacity cleared |
| **July-August** | Purely dependent on Foosung+SK Specialty | The most acute supply crisis window |
| **August+** | Foosung-CSSC batch import theoretically online | Supply begins to partially ease (but customer certification is required) |
| **2027** | Newly certified capacity gradually available | Supply normalization |

**Conclusion**: The acute shortage window of WF6 is about 2-3 months (July-August), not 2 years. However, high prices may remain until 2027 (certification cycle is delayed). The impact on existing targets:
- **4047.T** (NF3 argument): No impact. NF3 is Kanto Denka’s core business, and the argument for WF6’s withdrawal from NF3 remains unchanged.
- **Foosung (093370.KS)**: The shortened shortage window means the sustainability of WF6's earnings elasticity needs closer scrutiny.
