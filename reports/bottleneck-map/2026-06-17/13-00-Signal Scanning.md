## In-depth analysis of MLCC bottlenecks (newly added to the database)

### Why is it a bottleneck?

**Physical Necessity**: MLCC (Multilayer Ceramic Capacitor) is the basic passive component for power management and signal integrity of every AI server and is irreplaceable. A single NVIDIA GB300 rack consumes approximately **440,000** MLCCs, which is 8-10 times that of an ordinary server. The structural surge in demand is overwhelming high-end production capacity.

| 3 | Substitution difficulty | 🟡 Some available replacement specifications | High capacity/low ESR/ultra-miniature MLCC has no equivalent replacement in the near future |
