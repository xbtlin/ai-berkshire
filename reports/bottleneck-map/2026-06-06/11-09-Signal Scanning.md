### ⚠️ PGME/PGMEA — cracking rate actually declined, limited to 9 days outside June 15

**Trigger reason**: Argus Media/APIC data shows that Japan cracker run rates are actually declining (April 67.3% vs March 78.6%), which is in conflict with the recovery of naphtha raw materials to 85% - indicating that the supply recovery is "raw material availability" rather than "production recovery".

**Key New Data**:
- April cracking utilization rate: 67.3% (Argus Media, APIC 2026-06)
- March cracking utilization rate: 78.6%
- Direction: downward, not upward
- Naphtha raw material: 85% (alternative sources: United States, Algeria, Peru, India)
- Meaning: The raw materials are available, but the cracker itself is still reducing production - low propylene production → low PO → low PGME

**Interpretation**: PM Takaichi's "sustainability until 2027" means that raw materials will not be cut off, not that PGME production will recover. This distinction is critical. The actual decline in cracking rates means that PGME/PGMEA production will recover more slowly than previously thought.

**June 15 outer limit (9 days later)**: If there is still no PGME manufacturer announcing the resumption of production → S-level is officially confirmed to be unable to be lifted before the outer limit, and the deepening phase begins. **This is one of the most critical nodes since the establishment of S-level 83+ days ago. **

**Status**: S-level maintenance, ⚠️red alert upgrade (cracking rate decreases)
