# Bottleneck Hunter — 2026-06-16 01:05

##Clear the target

### IQE plc (IQE.L / OTC: IQEPY) - the only large-scale third-party supplier of InP epiwafer, multi-year customer contract was implemented for the first time

**Why it’s worth paying attention to now**:
2026-06-15 IQE plc and Tower Semiconductor announced a multi-year InP epiwafer supply agreement to simultaneously resolve IP disputes between the two parties. The agreement includes a minimum purchase quantity commitment in the first year, a subsequent rolling minimum purchase quantity commitment, and Tower's grant of a global porous silicon patent license to IQE. On the day the agreement was announced, IQE.L’s stock price rose by more than 16% in a single day. This is the first time that IQE.L InP AI data center thesis has received **specific contract verification** from a Tier 1 silicon photonics customer, and its nature is different from previous market expectations.

The front-round scan (2026-06-15 15:05) only recorded "China's InP export release has led to a slight weakening of IQE.L's relative advantage." The strategic significance of the Tower contract was insufficiently assessed, and it was supplemented and officially confirmed in this round.

**Valuation Check** (section 4.2.1 Red, Yellow and Green Lights)

**Red light inspection**:
1. Market value > TAM 20%: InP epiwafer TAM is estimated to be $1-3B, and the total compound semiconductor epitaxial wafer market TAM is about $3-5B. Based on $3B TAM, 20% = $600M, IQE’s market value is $660M, which is slightly more than that. **Conclusion: Marginal red light, but TAM estimation itself is highly uncertain**. If based on $4B TAM (including GaAs wireless and other product lines), IQE is within the range.
2. PS > 30x and growth rate <100%: PS 4.4x, **not triggered**.
3. Market value > 10 times 5-year optimistic revenue forecast: 5-year optimistic scenario (20% CAGR × 5 years) → FY2030E revenue is about $370M, 10 times = $3.7B; IQE market value $660M << $3.7B. **Not Triggered**.
4. The stock price doubled within 60 days after the additional issuance: IQE completed a £81M private placement in 2025 to eliminate bank debt; it has been more than 60 days since the additional issuance, and +16% is not a doubling. **Not Triggered**.

**Yellow light inspection**:
1. Loss + PS > 15x: The company is losing money, but the PS is only 4.4x (much lower than 15x). **Not Triggered**.
2. PE > 80x: No PE (loss). Pay attention to the profit path.

**Valuation green light**: PS 4.4x (2026E), which is a significantly low valuation level in the context of InP supply shortage (compared to AXTI PS~46x).

**Comprehensive valuation judgment**: 🟡 **Yellow light (edge)** — PS green light, but the company is not yet profitable, TAM red light there is uncertainty.

**Look at the long logic**

1. **Tower contract = multi-year revenue visibility**: Tower Semiconductor is a global leader in silicon photonics foundry, and its customers include multiple Tier 1 hyperscale cloud providers. The multi-year contract + minimum purchase quantity commitment means that the income of IQE's photonics department is basically guaranteed, significantly reducing uncertainty.
2. **PS 4.4x is an anomaly in pricing**: AXTI (AXT Inc) with the same layout of InP has a PS of about 46x, Veeco (device layer) PS is about 5.2x, and IQE, a material layer company that directly supplies InP epiwafer, has a PS of only 4.4x. The pricing is relatively low, partly because the company has not yet made a profit and the UK AIM market liquidity discount.
3. **InP bottleneck quantification**: InP supply and demand gap >70% in 2026, 6-inch substrate price +250% to $5,000, 800G optical module production capacity is 40-60% lower than demand. IQE is the first active party in the chain to sign a contract to "solve" the bottleneck, and its strategic position is clear.

**Bearish logic**

1. **The company has not yet made a profit, and there is pressure on capital consumption**: IQE FY2025 EPS -0.05, and has just completed a £81M private placement. If demand for AI photonics slows down, losses may intensify, and private placements have diluted existing shareholders.
2. **China’s InP export release weakens scarcity premium**: Digitimes reported on 2026-06-15 that China has released InP exports at a pace, and the first batch has been shipped by the end of May. If the pace of release is accelerated, the tight global InP supply situation improves, and IQE's pricing power is marginally weakened (AXTI directly benefits but IQE is indirectly affected).
3. **The financial size of the Tower contract was not disclosed**: The announcement did not disclose the specific purchase amount, and the actual contribution to IQE's revenue cannot be quantified. The "minimum purchase quantity commitment" is vaguely worded. If the actual purchase volume is close to the minimum value, the revenue enhancement effect will be limited.
