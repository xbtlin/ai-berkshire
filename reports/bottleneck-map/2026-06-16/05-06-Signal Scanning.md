## Detailed analysis of MLCC delivery upgrade

**Bottleneck Quantitative Upgrade**:

| Time | Delivery | Capacity Utilization | Drivers |
|-----|------|-----------|---------|
| End of 2024 | 8-12 weeks | 75-80% | Traditional consumer electronics |
| Q1 2026 | 20 weeks+ | 85%+ | AI server influx |
| June 2026 (current) | **24-40 weeks (high capacity)** | **>90% (Murata/Samsung EM)** | AI server centralized procurement + car/IoT simultaneous pull-in |

**Supply Structure**:
- Murata and Samsung EM have a combined global market share of about 55-60%, and both are at full production
- Yageo (including Kemet) ranks third in the world, with about 13-15% share
- High-end AI server MLCC has special specifications (X5R/X7R high capacity, low ESR), and the product certification of China’s local MLCC manufacturers (Fenghua Hi-Tech, etc.) has not yet entered the mainstream AI server supply chain
- New production capacity: The MLCC production capacity expansion cycle is about 18-24 months (new furnace construction), and it is impossible to respond quickly

**Yageo (2327.TW) Impact Assessment**:
- Q1 2026 has been presented: net profit +45%, AI server MLCC accounts for ~15%, and the third round of price increases will be launched in June
- This round of 24-40 week delivery data means that pricing power will continue to rise in the next 2-3 quarters
- Counterargument: If the pace of AI server construction slows down (such as ultra-large-scale cloud providers cutting Capex), MLCC demand may drop sharply; historically, MLCC inventories often accumulate after the boom cycle (lessons from 2019-2020)
- **Rating maintained ★★★★**, no valuation changes (PS and PE data remain at last verification)

## China InP Release × IQE.L Stratified Impact

**Level distinction**:

| Hierarchy | Product | China Control Level | Release Impact |
|-----|------|------------|---------|
| Raw materials | Indium (In metal) | China accounts for 70-80% of global production | Still under control in the medium term |
| Base layer | InP substrate (substrate) | China AXT/Shanghai Indium is the main source | **Direct relief** (shipped at the end of May) |
| Epitaxial layer | InP epiwafer | IQE.L (UK), the world's largest independent supplier | Indirect impact (raw material costs may decrease) |
| Device layer | EML laser/photodetector | Lumentum/Coherent/II-VI, etc. | Benefits (improved raw material acquisition) |

**Impact on each target**:
- **AXTI (AXT Inc)**: Direct benefit, export license delays caused a drag on Q4/2025, and shipments normalized after release. However, AXTI’s current PS is ~46x, the red light has not changed, and the valuation is unqualified.
- **IQE.L**: (1) Raw material costs may improve marginally; (2) Epiwafer scarcity premiums from non-Chinese sources are slightly weakened; (3) Tower multi-year contracts are locked, and the Tower agreement opens up long-term demand for IQE's epiwafer independent of the substrate market. Overall Impact: **Neutral to Slightly Positive**, ★★★★ maintained.
- **Argument revision**: InP substrate shortage "relief" ≠ InP epiwafer shortage "relief". The barrier to IQE lies in epitaxy process know-how + customer certification (>12 months), not just access to raw materials.
