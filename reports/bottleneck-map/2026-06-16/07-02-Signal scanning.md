## ICL Hormuz Analysis

**Facts**:
- Tensions in the Middle East (Iran-related) raise concerns about Hormuz passage
- Global bromine production: ICL >33% (Dead Sea/Israel), Jordan Bromine ~14% (Dead Sea, Jordan), Albemarle (USA/Chile) ~20%+

**Key Geographic Judgments (Estimates)**:
- ICL Dead Sea → Haifa Port → Mediterranean → Europe/Asia **Not via the Strait of Hormuz**
- Hormuz affects petrochemical shipping in the Persian Gulf more than Dead Sea chemicals

**Conclusion (estimate)**:
- If Hormuz is disrupted, ICL's direct operations are less likely to be affected.
- There is a direct risk if geopolitical risks in the Middle East trigger wider regional instability and spread to Israel itself (but this is an extreme scenario)
- If other Middle Eastern bromine suppliers are disrupted but ICL is not affected, there is theoretically room for price upside

**Impact on ICL investment thesis**: Neutral to positive. No change to ★★★★ rating, records as potential tail catalyst.
