## Detailed quantitative analysis of MLCC price increase (vs last round 05:06)

**05:06 Report already available**: Delivery time has been extended to 24-40 weeks (high-capacity models), Yageo Q1 net profit +45%, and the third round of price increases has started.

**New in this round**: The specific price increase is quantified.

| Model category | Delivery date | YTD spot price increase in 2026 | Remarks |
|---------|------|------------------|------|
| General MLCC (consumer/industrial) | 8-16 weeks | +15-20% | Multi-industry simultaneous purchase |
| AI server MLCC (X7R high capacity, low ESR) | 24-40 weeks | **+50-60%** | Ultra-large-scale cloud provider centralized procurement + low inventory |
| Automotive MLCC | 20-26 weeks | +10-20% | Electrification + ADAS |

**Impact on Yageo (2327.TW)**:
- Q1 2026: Net profit +45% (NT$38.2B revenue, EPS NT$3.9), which has reflected part of the price increase
- AI server MLCC accounts for about 15% of revenue, but contributes a higher proportion of gross profit (premium model)
- The benefits of the third round (May) + fourth round (planned) price increases will be reflected in Q3 2026
- Q2 guidance: revenue increases year-on-year, gross profit rises slightly (mainly reflecting price increases in Q1, third/fourth round benefit deferred)

**Counter arguments**:
- If the pace of AI data center construction slows down (cloud provider Capex cuts), demand for high-capacity MLCCs may drop rapidly
- History: MLCC tends to have overstocked inventory after the peak of the boom (lessons from 2018-2020)
- Yageo is not expanding production but raising prices in this round. If competitors (Taiyo Yuden, TDK) take the opportunity to expand production and grab share, the business cycle can be shortened.

**Yageo rating maintained ★★★★**. The valuation will be re-verified after the Q2 financial report is updated (around mid-July).

**New Quantification**: Samsung has locked SK Specialty 150MT/month (1,800MT/year). This means that a large amount of SK Specialty's production capacity is no longer circulating in the spot market, and other downstream customers (SK Hynix, TSMC, Global Foundries, etc.) are facing tighter supply.

**Market impact after the discontinuation of 4047.T (estimate)**:
- Japanese production capacity withdrawal: -2,000-2,200MT/year (about 25% of global production capacity)
- Samsung Lockup: -1,800MT/year (most of SK Specialty’s production capacity)
- China PERIC is subject to export controls: the actual exportable volume is uncertain
- Conclusion (estimate): Effective free market WF6 supply may drop by 50%+

**Note**: The above is an estimate based on the combined information of all parties, and there is overlap (some locked positions may have been included in the "70-90% price increase" contract). The core logic is clear, but the specific figures are uncertain and marked "estimated".
