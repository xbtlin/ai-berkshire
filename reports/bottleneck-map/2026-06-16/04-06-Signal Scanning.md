## Core status change details

### 🔽 PGME/PGMEA (11d) Level A → Level B Downgrade

**Root Cause Elimination**:

The core argument of master-map entry 11d (newly added on 2026-05-30, level A) is:
- Iran/Hormuz crisis → Japan’s naphtha imports from the Middle East are interrupted
- Naphtha → raw material for PGME/PGMEA, Japan dominates global production
- 2-4 weeks inventory from 2 key suppliers (Tokyo Kasei/Shin-Etsu)
- Downstream wafer fabs have a 6-month safety stock buffer, and the critical period is estimated to be September to October.

**NEW FACTS (6/14-15)**:
- The United States and Iran reached an agreement, announced the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz on 6/14, and officially signed it on 6/19
- Middle East crude oil/naphtha shipping routes resume
- The voyage from the Middle East to Japan takes about 10-14 days; a new batch of naphtha can arrive in Japan as early as the end of June
- Japanese naphtha price: peaked at $1,190/MT during the crisis (+92%), expected to fall with the reopening of Hormuz
- 6-month safety stock for downstream fabs + supply chain recovery → the critical period in September-October will almost never happen

**Counter Arguments (Be Cautious)**:
- The agreement has not yet finally entered into force (the nuclear issue is under 60-day negotiations). If it is reversed, the bottleneck will return to level A.
- Recovery of naphtha processing/PGME production will take several weeks and is not immediate
- The situation in the Middle East has "recurred after peace" many times in history. It is recommended to maintain a 3-month observation period.

**Rating decision**: Level A → Level B (the root cause is eliminated, but the implementation of the agreement needs to be observed)

**Investment Impact**: Originally, 11d had no investable targets. This downgrade does not affect any existing positions/observations, but only reduces a potential risk of semiconductor manufacturing interruption.
