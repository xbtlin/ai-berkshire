### 🆕 New bottleneck of NF3/WF6 semiconductor special gas layer——Preliminary identification of Kanto Denka Kogyo (4047.T)

**Signal source**: TrendForce (2026-04-08), The Elec (2026), Digitimes (2026-04-09), Sourceability (2025-08)

**Signal description**:
- **Mitsui Chemicals has officially stopped NF3 (nitrogen trifluoride) production in March 2026** (Shimonoseki factory closed, reason: rising costs + price competition leading to negative profits), resulting in **Kanto Denka Kogyo becoming the only major NF3 producer in Japan** (now accounting for about 90% of Japan's NF3 production capacity)
- **Meanwhile**: China's tungsten export controls → Japan's WF6 (tungsten hexafluoride) suppliers have a shortage of raw materials → Many Japanese WF6 suppliers have notified Korean semiconductor customers of "possible supply disruptions in H2 2026", and existing inventories are only expected to be maintained until mid-2026
- **WF6 Price**: China’s regulations have increased demand, and Japanese and Korean suppliers have increased prices for WF6 by 70-90%.
- Kanto Denka is also one of the main Japanese producers of WF6

**Why this is a new independent bottleneck (not just an extension of the existing "Tungsten" entry)**:
- The existing master-map 0c entry tracks the **raw ore/APT/metal tungsten** layer (Almonty, mining supply chain)
- NF3/WF6 is a refined semiconductor gas layer that is further downstream than raw ore and directly enters the wafer manufacturing process.
- NF3 is used for CVD chamber cleaning; WF6 is used for tungsten interconnect deposition (BEOL) of chips below 7nm
- The supplier qualification certification cycle is long (TSMC/Samsung both need to strictly certify new suppliers), leading to the dilemma of "you can buy it with money, but you can't"
- Mitsui withdrawal is a **structural supply reduction independent of tungsten export controls** (market competition/cost factors, irreversible)

**Bottleneck determination (NF3 layer)**:
- Supply concentration 🔴 (Kanto Denka = the only major supplier of Japanese NF3, Mitsui has withdrawn)
- Production expansion cycle 🔴 (the construction cycle of a new fluorochemical plant is many years)
- Substitution difficulty 🔴 (NF3 is design import, new supplier certification >1 year; TSMC/Samsung/Kioxia has already experienced emergency inventory pressure when the Kanto Denka fire occurred in 2025)
- Capacity utilization 🔴 (The fire in August 2025 showed that production capacity was extremely tight, and a single factory failure affected global supply)
- Demand growth 🔴 (more CVD steps are added with advanced processes)
- Customer certification cycle 🔴 (>1 year)
= **A level and above** (5-6 red lights; considering Mitsui’s concentration after exiting, there is potential to upgrade to S level)
