## Detailed analysis

### Signal 1: UAMY — Thompson Falls smelting expansion has been completed in April (3 months earlier than forecast)

**New facts** (vs. last round of tracking "80% production in mid-July"):

Multiple sources confirmed that the capacity expansion of the Thompson Falls smelter has been completed in April 2026, and the nameplate production capacity has increased from approximately 100 t/month to 300-400 t/month, approximately three times. "80% production by mid-July" targets the load ramping target for new stoves, but the physical infrastructure has been completed ahead of schedule.

Newly added during the same period: UAMY has submitted a $44M application to DOE to replicate Bolivia's hydromet process in the United States - Bolivia's facilities currently produce about 150 t/month of high-purity antimony flakes, which are shipped to Montana for processing. Combined with the Montana 300 t/month nameplate, the comprehensive potential throughput can reach **700-1,000 t/month** (the critical path to reach the $125M guidance in 2026).

DoD contract details confirmed: Of the $248M five-year DLA contract, **~$85M are confirmed orders in 2026**, providing about 68% of the contract bottom line protection against the $125M guidance.

**Valuation check (unchanged)**:
- Market cap ~$1.25B / 2026E revenue $125M → Forward PS ~**10x** (green light threshold line)
- Q1 2026 loss of $11.3M → not yet profitable → **yellow light** (loss + forward PS high)
- 10-year 25xPE exit method: Optimistically assume that the revenue in 2030 is $500M, the net profit rate is 18% → the net profit is $90M, 25x PE = $2.25B; if you buy it at $1.25B, it will be $2.25B in 10 years, with an annualized rate of about 6%. **If the 10% threshold is not reached, there is no safety margin at the current price**.

** Signal meaning **: Expanding production faster than expected is a positive operating signal, and the Q2 (April-June) financial report (announced in August) will be a key node to test the conversion of production capacity into revenue. If Q2 revenue >$25M and gross profit margin >20%, upgrade to ★★★★.

**Contrary argument**: Antimony prices may have pulled back from the +300% high due to the impact of some loosening in China; Q1 miss is large ($6.8M vs $125M full-year guidance), and the full-year execution risk is still high; Bolivia's political risk affects the supply of raw materials.

**Rating maintained at ★★★, unchanged**

### Signal three: CW laser - EML alternative route itself has reached capacity limit (new B-level bottleneck)

**Background**: EML (electroabsorption modulated laser) has been locked by Nvidia until 2027+ (listed as S-level). CSP was forced to turn to an alternative – CW (continuous wave) lasers + silicon photonics integration (SiPh). However:

**NEW FACT**: TrendForce states that "the CW ecosystem is approaching a capacity crunch". The reason is the same as EML:
- MOCVD/epitaxy equipment delivery time is 18-24 months (Aixtron has full orders)
- InP wafer itself is in short supply (main bottleneck, S level)
- Strict aging test cycle (die-cut + high temperature and humidity test) incompressible

**Judgment Criteria** (Third Step Framework):
- Concentrated supply 🟡 (Coherent/Lumentum/a small number of Chinese manufacturers)
- Production expansion cycle 🔴 (equipment delivery period 18-24 months, also relies on Aixtron MOCVD)
- Substitution difficulty 🟡 (SiPh architecture is different from EML, but both are optical interconnects)
- Demand growth 🔴 (800G+ market demand will increase 2.6 times in 2026)
- **Comprehensive: B-level bottleneck** (supply is more dispersed than EML, but growth pressure is the same)

**Investable targets**: Coherent (COHR) and Lumentum (LITE) are both already on the watchlist (EML sector), and the CW business is an additional benefit - no new entries are required, but the CW dimension needs to be included in their respective valuation assessments.

**No new watchlist entries will be added, and the background will be upgraded to level B. **
