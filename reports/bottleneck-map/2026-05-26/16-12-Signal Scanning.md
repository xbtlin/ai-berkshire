## Detailed update of inventory status

### 1. Kanto Denka Kogyo (4047.T) — Valuation red light ⚠️

**Triggering event**: A single-day increase of 24% on May 18, with the stock price rising from ¥2,050 to ¥2,550, a new high (the previous ATH in February was ¥1,843).

**Data changes** (compared to the estimate at the time of initial identification at 14:11):

| Indicators | Recognized at 14:11 | Updated at 16:12 | Changes |
|------|-----------|---------|------|
| Stock price | ~¥1,843 (estimate) | ¥2,550 | **+38%** |
| Market value | ¥90-98B ≈ $580-630M | ¥135.7B ≈ $872M | **+50%** |
| P/S | ~1.4x | ~2.1x | +0.7x |
| P/E | ~24x | **76.3x** | **+52x** ⚠️ |

**Valuation status change**:
- P/E 76.3x: Close to the yellow light threshold (80x), clearly out of the "green light" range
- P/S 2.1x: still reasonable (<10x)
- **Comprehensive judgment**: It is no longer "green light is cheap", but the hard red light has not yet been triggered; the P/E yellow light needs to indicate whether the growth rate is supported

**Can the growth rate support 76x P/E**:
- FY2026 net profit growth +17% (¥3.24B→¥3.79B) - obviously does not support 76x P/E, PEG = 76 / 17 = 4.5x (too high)
- But: If the NF3 price increase cycle drives the profit margin from 5.8% to 10%, the net profit can reach ¥6.5B → P/E drops to 20x. This is the core expectation of investors
- Conclusion: The market is betting on NF3/WF6 **margin turning point**, not current profits

**Signal Strength Correction**: ★★★ (from pending confirmation status, subject to valuation expansion, no upgrade)
**Recommended action**: Wait for the correction to ¥1,800-2,000 (P/E about 25-30x) before re-evaluating whether it is worth opening a position

### 2. Almonty Industries (AII.TO) — Significant revision to forward revenue picture

**Trigger information**:
- Sangdong Phase 1 target production capacity: 140,000 MTU/year (Source: Company disclosure, >90% have locked in long-term contracts)
- Based on the current tungsten price of $3,140/MTU × 140,000 MTU = **~$440M/annual income** (Sangdong Phase 1 only)
- Plus Panasqueira (Portugal, ~$30M/year) ≈ **$470M forward revenue**
- Milestone achieved: Q1 2026 operating cash flow +$9.7M (**turned positive**)

**P/S Correction Comparison**:

| | Last calculation | This update |
|---|---------|---------|
| Revenue base | Q1 $25.4M × 4 = annualized C$101.6M | Phase 1 full production ~$470M |
| Market capitalization (C$) | C$5.22B | C$5.22B (unchanged) |
| P/S | ~51x (trailing) | ~**8x** (forward full production) |

**Interpretation**:
- It's not that the value trap disappeared - it went from "trailing P/S 51x" to "forward P/S 8x"
- The problem is: Sangdong is a process from commissioning to full production, and it may take 12-18 months to fully ramp up.
- Stocks are priced in advance: YTD +544%, meaning the market is partially cashing in on this forward logic
- Cantor Fitzgerald target price $25.80, BofA target $20, DA Davidson $25 - all three major institutions are bullish
- **But the 10-year exit analysis is still not optimistic**: Buy with a market value of $3.8B, annualized 10% will require a net profit of $394M (after 10 years), corresponding revenue of about $2B - requires Phase 2 full production + price maintenance + expansion to other mines

**Signal Strength**: ★★ (maintained, but narrative foundation significantly improved)
**Valuation Status**: Yellow light (forward P/S is reasonable, but fully priced + exit in 10 years still requires high CAGR)
**Recommended Action**: The actual output of Q2/Q3 Sangdong is the key verification; if H2 2026 quarterly revenue > $80M (i.e. Phase 1 ramps up to ~50%), you can consider upgrading to ★★★; the current price will not be pursued higher
