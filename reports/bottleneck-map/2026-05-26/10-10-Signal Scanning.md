## New signal details

### 1. Antimony supply crisis – an overlooked chemical bottleneck in the NATO ammunition chain

**Trigger reason**: This round of search systematically combed the NATO ammunition chemical supply chain for the first time, and found that antimony is the **second largest structural supply risk** after nitrocellulose (NC), but it has not yet been included in the main tracking map.

**Bottleneck physical logic**:
- Antimony (Sb) is an irreplaceable component of fuze charges (primary explosives), armor-piercing projectile alloys, and night vision devices/infrared sensors**
- Global demand: approximately 120,000 MT/year; global production capacity: approximately 80,000 MT/year; **Gap approximately 40,000 MT/year (-33%)**
- China controls about 46% of global antimony chemical sales; the United States has no domestic mining in history - it relies 100% on imports
- China expands antimony export controls in 2024 (same batch as InP indium export controls), and the Western supply chain loses its main source
- Alternatives: No short-term alternatives. BAE Systems is working on an antimony-free fuze, but it's still in the laboratory stage

**Bottleneck Rating**:
- Concentrated supply 🔴 (China 46%+, the United States has zero historical production capacity)
- Production expansion cycle 🔴 (10-15 years from discovery to production of new mines)
- Alternative difficulty 🔴 (no immediate replacement for fuze/night vision)
- Gap size 🔴 (40,000 MT global gap)
- Demand growth🔴 (driven by NATO’s rearmament)

**Rating: A-level bottleneck** (Reason for not upgrading to S-level: the supply crisis is gradual, rather than sudden supply cuts like helium/HBr; there is a certain inventory buffer)

**Source**: [Atlas Institute NATO Ammo Gap](https://atlasinstitute.org/the-strategic-ammunition-gap-natos-industrial-lag-risks-deterrence/) | [Antimony Supply Crisis — Skillings.net](https://skillings.net/antimony-supply-crisis-what-it-is-why-it-matters-2026-outlook/) | [Paradox Intelligence — War Metals](https://www.paradoxintelligence.com/themes/war-metals-supply-squeeze-tungsten-antimony-critical-minerals-2026)
