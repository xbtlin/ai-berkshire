## Core new discovery analysis

### Signal 1: Silicon Photonics foundry bottleneck has significantly escalated

**Data Point**: Tower Semiconductor (TSEM) is the world’s largest external customer SiPh wafer foundry**. Announced on 2026-05-13:
- $1.3B customer contracts (2027 SiPh revenue), +$290M upfront
- 70%+ of total SiPh production capacity booked until 2028
- $920M CapEx: Expand SiPh production capacity to more than 5x Q4 2025 run rate (Q4 2026 installation completed)
- 2028 long-term goal: revenue $2.8B, net profit $750M

**Significance**: The CW laser we track (entry 11b) adopts the SiPh route (silicon photonic modulator + CW light source), and Tower is a key foundry for SiPh chips. The 70% capacity reservation means that the SiPh alternative route is also subject to severe capacity constraints and resonates with the EML S-level bottleneck rather than complementing it**. This further increases the severity of Class B CW/SiPh bottlenecks.

**TSEM's own valuation**: market capitalization $30B, PS ~18.5x (TTM revenue $1.62B), exceeding our "priority <$10 billion market capitalization" standard; currently it is not an investable small market capitalization target. Q1 2026 revenue +15% YoY; Q2 guidance $455M (a company record).

**Possible downstream target angle**: Is there a pure supplier with a small market value for the key component of the SiPh chip - germanium photodetector (Ge PD)? Preliminary thoughts: Ge PD is supplied by a few players (Coherent, Lumentum, NeoPhotonics was acquired by Lumentum, etc.). They are all tracked companies and do not constitute new independent targets.

### Signal 2: New bottleneck of optical fiber preform emerges

**Data Points**:
- A single GPU cluster in an AI data center requires 36 times the amount of optical fiber interconnection of a standard server (Tom's Hardware)
- Meta Louisiana's 1 million GPU campus plan consumes "8 million miles of fiber"
- The price of G.652D fiber will increase by 75% in a single month in January 2026; G.657A2 will nearly double
- Chinese manufacturer orders extended to 2027 (Digitimes, 2026-05-15)
- Preform expansion cycle: 18-24 months, which is a physical upstream constraint on optical fiber supply

**Bottleneck Rating Assessment**:
- Supply concentration: 🟡 (Corning, Prysmian, YOFC, Sumitomo, Fujikura 5, but Chinese manufacturers have a high share)
- Expansion cycle: 🔴 (18-24 months)
- Substitution difficulty: 🔴 (AI data center all-optical interconnection is strictly needed and cannot be bypassed)
- Demand growth rate: 🔴 (GPU cluster density drives demand to grow exponentially)
= **Class B** (2 red and 2 yellow, similar to CW laser)

**Search results for investable targets**: Corning (GLW, market capitalization ~$30B, fiber optic business approximately 30% revenue) is the purest Western preform supplier, but its market capitalization is too large. Prysmian (Italy, ~€18bn) Ditto. YOFC (601869.SH, China, optical fiber + preform, A-share listed, market value of about 15 billion yuan ~ $2.1 billion) is a relatively small option - but Chinese optical fiber manufacturers are the direct beneficiaries of this round of price increases, and under the attack of domestic dual-use demand, their export capabilities are uncertain.

**Conclusion**: Optical fiber preform is a real Class B bottleneck, but there is currently no pure investable target with a small market value that meets all standards; join the tracking and wait for further investigation.
