## Core status change details

### 1. Reconstruction of VECO investment logic: Axcelis’ all-stock acquisition of Veeco

**Event**: Axcelis Technologies (ACLS) announced the acquisition of Veeco Instruments (VECO) in an all-stock form, with a combined valuation of approximately $4.4 billion.

**Timeline**:
- Early 2026: deal announced
- 2026-02-06: Special meeting of Axcelis shareholders approves merger
- **Status: Pending approval from China’s State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) – the last regulatory hurdle**
- Estimated: H2 2026 completion

**Merge structure**:
- ACLS shareholders hold approximately 58.4%, VECO shareholders hold approximately 41.6% (fully diluted basis)
- The CEO of Axcelis will also serve as the post-merger CEO, and the ACLS headquarters (Beverly, MA) will be the post-merger headquarters.
- After the merger, it became the fourth largest wafer manufacturing equipment supplier in the United States

**Post-merger financial estimates**:
- ACLS 2026 guidance: ~$800M (basically the same as 2025)
- VECO FY2026 guidance: $740-800M (median $770M)
- Post-merger annual revenue: ~$1.47B (estimated)
- ACLS current market capitalization: $4.83B (stock price $155.38, 2026-05-26)
- Implied P/S after merger: $4.83B / $1.47B ≈ **3.3x** (reasonable)
- ACLS Q1 2026 Actual: Revenue $199M, non-GAAP EPS $0.72, gross profit margin 40.5%
- ACLS P/E (current): 48.9x; more reasonable under FY2026 flat guidance

**Post-merger synergies**:
- Product complementarity: Axcelis ion implantation + Veeco MOCVD + laser annealing + advanced packaging
- $35M annualized cost synergy (over 24 months)
- TAM expanded to >$5 billion
- EPS accretion is expected to be achieved within the first year after the merger

**Impact on VECO watchlist**:
- The logic of VECO as an independent investment has substantially changed - holding VECO = waiting for the realization of ACLS shares as consideration
- VECO’s current market capitalization ($3.8B) × 41.6% share of VECO shareholders = the implied value of the combined position is approximately $2B
- ACLS stock price YTD +98.56%, but the consensus target price of analysts is $101.50 (down 35% from the current price of $155) ⚠️
- An independent analyst estimates: fair value $92 (down 41% from current price) ⚠️

**Core Risk: China SAMR Approval**
- In the context of the current Sino-US technology war, three scenarios of SAMR delay/conditional approval/rejection are possible
- If SAMR rejects: the transaction is cancelled, VECO resumes its independent underlying logic, and the price may fall back sharply
- If SAMR approval is subject to conditions (e.g. export restrictions/technology divestiture): synergies are impaired
- This is the biggest uncertainty right now, not a valuation or fundamentals issue

**Conclusion (⚠️For reference only, not buying and selling advice)**:
- VECO’s independent logic has been replaced by merger arbitrage logic and is not suitable to be held as a pure bottleneck target.
- If you are optimistic about the merged ACLS, you need to accept the dual risk of ① SAMR risk and ② the current stock price is 35% higher than the analyst’s target price
- **Recommended watchlist operation**: VECO status changed from "★★★★Candidate In-depth Research" to "⚠️Merger pending completion, independent evaluation suspended"; ACLS will be added for observation as a post-merger carrier and will be re-evaluated upon SAMR results.
