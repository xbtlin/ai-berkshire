- The combined production capacity is expected to be 50 per cent by the end of 2026: 3 in 6 inches each; almost all of 2027 will be from 6 inches increment

** Impact on EML S-level bottlenecks (important)**:

Coherent ' s InP doubling capacity is a partial positive signal on the EML supply**, but key points need to be clarified:
- Nvidia has locked $2 billion in purchase agreements with Lumentum and Cohenent - the bulk of the additional capacity of Corherent will continue to serve the Nvidia contract requirements
- Non-Nvidia clients (other CSPs) are still practically pushed out of the EML quota
- **InP bottom (S level) remains upstream constraint**: Corherent's 6 inch extension requires more InP from equally tight liners like AXT/Tongmei, Sumitomo, and the final bottleneck of EML supply moved to Layer 3 bottom, not lifted
- MOCVD equipment requirements (Aixtron) remain strong, as evidenced by Lumentum ' s entry on 19 May

** Conclusion **: The EML S rating remains unchanged; COHR acceleration is a positive signal of operations, but does not remove the overall supply constraint, but only enables COHR to deliver lock-in orders more quickly. The COHR valuation needs to include a positive 6-inch capacity advance factor in the next evaluation.

** Reverse argument**: A 6 inch rapid climb would generate more upstream increment demand, could further increase pressure on bottom suppliers such as AXT/Tongmei, and negative rather than positive for bottom S bottlenecks in the inP.
