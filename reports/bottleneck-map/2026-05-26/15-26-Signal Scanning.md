Signal A: End of 2026 November export control of Ga - Pre-Vision warning B

** Factual basis**:
- In December 2024, China officially banned the export of plutonium, plutonium, antimony and ultra-hard materials to the United States
- November 2025, after negotiations between the two sides, China** suspended the ban to 2026-11-27** (managing the export licensing system, the military end-user ban continues to be in effect)
- ** China accounts for 99% of global production of thorium** (2024) and cannot be replaced in the short term
- No long-term assurance after 2026-11-27, and if restarted, the relevant supply chain is again broken

** Impact chain**:
- GAN (nitrogenated thorium)  Power semiconductor (EV charge, data centre power switching), 5G base station RF module
- GaAs (alcophate) RF front end, III-V light device (closely linked to the InP chain)
- Six months to the window.

** Bottleneck determination forecast (if the ban is restarted)**: supply concentration (99% China) extension cycle replacement difficulty (part of GaN can be replaced by SIC but conversion costs are high) = potential A

** Current rating**: **B-level forward vision** (status quo open, purely time-end risk)

**Assessment of investible targets**:
- The only Western-scale diazine distillator: there are no purely marketed (mainly aluminum smelting by-products)
- Indirect benefits: companies using alternative materials (Sic) in case of supply disruption, such as Wolfspeed (WOLF), onsemi (ON)
- Valuation note: Wolfsped is now a very profitable man, and ON is already a big market market, not within the bottleneck hunter target.
