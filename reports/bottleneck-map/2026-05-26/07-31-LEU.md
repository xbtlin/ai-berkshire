### Centrus Energy (LEU)—the only commercial aggregator of HALEU in the West, the most upstream interface of the SMR chain

**Why it’s worth watching now**: This round of scans identifies for the first time HALEU (high-abundance low-enriched uranium) as an independent bottleneck in the SMR supply chain. In January 2026, DOE announced a $2.7B HALEU/LEU task order (of which the HALEU project is up to $900M); Centrus is the only Western commercial-scale HALEU enricher, and the Piketon facility has been put into production in December 2025, with a production capacity of 12 MT/year HALEU. It is also learned that Centrus is negotiating with Oklo for deconversion service JV, and Piketon is expected to become the next generation SMR fuel supply hub.

**Bottleneck Location**: Layer 3, Nuclear Fuel Supply Chain (HALEU), Level B+

**Look at the long logic**:
1. **Physical monopoly**: Centrus is the only NRC-licensed concentrator that has produced HALEU on a commercial scale in Western countries; it will take 10-15 years for new competitors to build and obtain licenses, with a clear time window
2. **DOE locked revenue**: $2.7B task order ($900M HALEU + $2.3B commercial LEU backlog), with top-level endorsement; DOE is using strategic reserves to fill the gap - indicating that the urgency is real
3. **Demand explodes first**: DOE estimates that 40,000 kg/year HALEU will be needed in 2030 and will increase year by year; Centrus’ current production capacity of 12 MT/year is far from enough to support it, and it has significant pricing power

**Short selling logic**:
1. **SMR commercialization has been repeatedly delayed**: The NuScale UAMPS project has been cancelled, and the TerraPower Natrium first reactor is expected to be in 2030. If further delays occur, HALEU commercial demand activation is postponed, and Centrus relies on DOE subsidies to maintain operations.
2. **Geographical Variables**: Russian TENEX is the largest HALEU manufacturer; if sanctions are eased or the geopolitical situation changes, cheap Russian HALEU may re-enter the market (the probability is low but exists)
3. **DOE Concentration**: Current revenue is highly dependent on government contracts, and the conversion time of commercial customers is highly uncertain; the financing structure of the $560M expansion plan needs to be verified (DOE loan vs. self-financing)

**Suggestion**: Add to the watch list ★★★ and wait for the following catalysts to re-evaluate:
- SMR project confirmed commercial operation date (expected 2026-2027)
- Centrus Q2 2026 financial report (revenue structure, HALEU vs LEU ratio)
- Oklo JV officially signed
- DOE institutional HALEU inventory establishment (policy signal)
