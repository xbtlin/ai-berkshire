For the first time in this round, the DIU "Point Break" government procurement project is entered into the tracking system:

**Timeline**:
- 2021-2024: DARPA Space-BACN R&D phase, Mynaric/Mbryonics develops prototypes
- 2026-04: Rocket Lab acquires Mynaric for $155M (RKLB expands laser communication product line)
- 2026-05: DARPA "winding down" the program and handing it over to DIU; DIU releases Point Break solicitation, response deadline is 5/17

**Significance**: This is not a new bottleneck, but a **policy upgrade node** in the InP optical terminal demand chain: from "can it be done" in R&D to "how much to buy and when to deliver" in operational procurement. Merged with SDA PWSA's transmission layer/tracking layer satellite procurement, it forms two stable sources of government demand for military InP laser terminals.

**No new investable targets**: Mbryonics is still a private company; RKLB has a market capitalization of ~$10B, which is not a small market capitalization target; its purity is questionable (Mynaric accounts for a very small amount of RKLB's total revenue).
