## FORM (FormFactor) Valuation Red Light Detailed Description

> **Conclusion: The bottleneck logic is established, the valuation has been overdrawn, and the signal strength has been capped ★★**

### Why is it a bottleneck?

HBM4 probe card is the physical test entrance in the AI chip mass production chain. Each HBM4 chip must undergo wafer-level inspection (wafer probing) before packaging. HBM4's 3D stacking structure and extremely high pin density significantly increase the unit price and technical threshold of probe cards. FormFactor is the world's largest supplier of probe cards (about 30-35% market share), and almost all of SK Hynix's HBM probe cards come from FormFactor.

### Valuation Check

**🔴 Red Light — Signal Capping ★★**

**Trigger condition: Market value > 20% of TAM**
- Global probe card market forecast in 2032: $7.95 billion
- 20% threshold: **$1.59 billion**
- Current market capitalization: **$11 billion**, 6.9 times the threshold
- → Even using the broader "semiconductor test equipment" total market ($15-20 billion), 20% = $3-4 billion, still well below the $11 billion market cap

Even using the most optimistic global semiconductor test equipment market (approximately $25 billion), the 20% threshold = $5 billion, which is still less than the market value of $11 billion.

**Valuation safety margin test (25xPE exit in 10 years)**:
-Buy with a market capitalization of $11 billion, and the annualized return of 10% after 10 years requires a market capitalization = $28.5 billion
- Required net profit in 2036: $28.5 billion ÷ 25 = **$1.14 billion**
- Calculated based on 20% net interest rate: required revenue in 2036 = **$5.7 billion**
- From $1 billion (2026E) to $5.7 billion: **CAGR of 19%** required for 10 years
- The total global market for probe cards is approximately $8 billion (2032 estimate). FormFactor needs to gain 100%+ market share to achieve this revenue target, which is logically impossible

> Conclusion: **The current price does not have a margin of safety**. Even if the growth rate is maintained at 32% x 10 years, the revenue will be approximately $5.5 billion by then, which may far exceed the size of the entire probe card market. This requires the company to successfully expand into adjacent markets (ATE, packaging and testing, etc.).

### Why log instead of chase

1. The bottleneck is real: HBM4 mass production requires high-precision probe cards, and FormFactor is the core supplier
2. Real growth rate: Q1+32%, Non-GAAP EPS significantly exceeded expectations
3. However, the market value has fully reflected the growth expectations in the next 5-7 years, and the safety margin is insufficient.
4. If the market value falls back to the $4-6 billion range (PS 4-6x), both logic and valuation are established, and it is worthy of in-depth study.
