### Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) — EML laser vertical integrator, benefiting from NVIDIA lock-in crisis

**Why it’s worth paying attention to now**: TechTimes disclosed on 5/27 that NVIDIA has locked in major EML laser production capacity such as Lumentum and II-VI/Coherent in 2026-2027 with a reservation contract of approximately $4B, resulting in non-NVIDIA customers (Google, Microsoft, AMD, etc.) being unable to obtain sufficient lasers after 2027. AAOI is one of the very few independent suppliers with in-house manufacturing capabilities for InP lasers (vertical integration) and is theoretically a complementary source for non-NVIDIA customers. At the same time, China’s tightening of InP export controls (confirmed on 6/22) has exacerbated the scarcity of alternative sources.

**Bottleneck location**: Layer 2, EML laser, bottleneck rating **S level**

- Major global EML vendors have been locked in by NVIDIA until 2027+
- InP substrate supply is tightened by China's export controls, prices triple
- AAOI internal InP epitaxial growth → laser → optical module, does not rely on external procurement, is an island in systemic scarcity

**Look at the long logic**:

1. **The only path**: After NVIDIA locks in the main laser suppliers, non-NVIDIA AI computing power customers (Google TPU, Microsoft Azure, AMD MI series) can only purchase from a few independent laser manufacturers. AAOI vertically integrated InP → has the ability to undertake.
2. **Growth Acceleration**: FY2026 guidance $1.1B vs FY2025 $455.7M, growth rate 141%. If downstream demand is maintained, production capacity expansion (target 930,000 units/month) will be implemented in 2027, and revenue may exceed $2B by then.
3. **Profit in transit**: Q4 2025 net loss of $2M, actual breakeven; 2026 revenue doubling + operating leverage will drive net profit to quickly turn positive.

**Short selling logic**:

1. **Risk of entry at high valuation**: The stock price has risen from $18.50 to $150+, and hit $233 during the year. The increase of 52W is 12x, and a large number of optimistic expectations have been priced in. Technical risks are high.
2. **AAOI itself also relies on InP**: AAOI produces its own InP in Texas, but if raw materials such as high-purity indium/phosphorus are affected by China's export controls, cost pressure will increase. The advantage of "vertical integration" is partly limited by upstream.
3. **Execution Risk**: The expansion from 100,000 to 930,000 units/month is a 9-fold expansion, involving a large amount of capital expenditure and yield ramp-up. Such aggressive capacity expansions have historically been subject to delays.

**Recommendation**: Join the watch list (★★★), **not suitable for chasing at the current price** (the market value of $12B exceeds the TAM 20% red line of the component submarket). Wait for one of two triggers to re-evaluate: ① The stock price falls back to market value <$8B (forward PS <8x); ② The FY2026 Q1/Q2 financial report confirms profits and raises guidance.
