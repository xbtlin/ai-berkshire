## AXT (AXTI) Valuation Explained – Indium/InP Export Control Beneficiaries Study

### Why follow

China's export controls on InP will require an export license for each batch starting in February 2025, directly affecting AXT (which produces all InP substrates in China). Additional controls will be implemented on June 22, 2026. The global InP substrate supply and demand gap is >70%, the price has tripled, and the demand for optical interconnection in AI data centers has exploded.

Layer 3 (InP substrate → EML/CW laser → optical module → AI data center interconnection), bottleneck rating **S level** (supply and demand gap >70%, alternative technology does not exist)

3. If China tightens licensing again (already happened once), AXT revenue/inventory orders will be blocked again
