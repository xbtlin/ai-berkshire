- Deaths: ≥13 people; Injuries: 66 people

### Relationship to tracked status

### Signal meaning

1. **Production restart vs. transportation channel**: The previously tracked re-entry of LNG ships into Hormuz (6/11-22) is the restoration of the transportation channel, but the production end of Ras Laffan itself (gas processing + helium extraction) has not been restored simultaneously—and now even this attempt is blocked.
2. **Helium 3 Impact**: Qatar has three helium facilities: Helium 1, Helium 2, and Helium 3. Helium 3 is supplied by Barzan. The specific impact of the explosion on Helium 3 production is "unknown" (gasworld original text: "extent of damage and any resulting impact on the restart schedule for Barzan and Helium 3 remain unclear"). But Barzan needs to be overhauled, and the restart timeline will inevitably be delayed.
3. **Inventory critical re-evaluation**: The original tracking is "September inventory critical, T≈65 days". The Barzan explosion delayed the resumption of the production side, and the actual critical window may be advanced. The official statement from QatarEnergy needs to be followed up in subsequent scans.

### Opposite view (uncertainty)

- Explosion occurred at Barzan (local gas supply), not LNG export facility; direct impact on Helium 1/2 not clearly reported
- Qatar’s Energy Minister has ruled out deliberate sabotage as a technical accident and repairs may be faster than a large-scale strategic strike
- Data limitations: Helium 3's proportion of Qatar's total helium production is unknown, Barzan's contribution to Helium 1/2 is unknown

**Conclusion: Helium S level remains unchanged, but the restart timeline further deteriorates; inventory criticality risk increases (from "planned" → "plan blocked"). There is no investment target. **
