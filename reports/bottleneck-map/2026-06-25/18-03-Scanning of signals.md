## WF6 status details (major, supplementary front wheel)

**Status change: from "Possible interruption" → "Confirmed permanent withdrawal, T=5 days"**

### Added confirmation (vs round 280)

It is known from front wheel tracking that KDK (4047.T) stopped production on 7/1 and issued an "exit warning". New confirmations this round:

1. **Central Glass is permanently discontinued simultaneously**: The two companies jointly informed the three major customers (Samsung, SK Hynix, and TSMC) that 6/30 is the last shipment day. Central Glass has an annual production capacity of ~700 tons (global ~8-9%), with KDK added to ~1,400 tons, and a total exit of ~2,100-2,200 tons (global ~25-30% high-purity WF6). This means that there is no "Japanese alternative source" for the WF6 bottleneck - KDK was previously regarded as the only Japanese alternative after Mitsui's withdrawal, and has now confirmed its withdrawal.

2. **Price Correction**: The price of 5N grade WF6 is 232.7% YoY (≈RMB 1,670-1,810/kg), and the price of 6N grade is 190%+ (≈RMB 2.2-3 million/MT). Previously, the "70-90% price increase" tracked was due to an early increase in shipment prices from Korean suppliers, and the global spot price level far exceeded this figure.

3. **Foosung (093370) certification process**: The import certification process with China Ship Specialty Gases (CSSC Specialty Gases) has been launched, with the goal of large-scale import of WF6 from August. This is the closest available public information to an alternative path. However, the certification has not yet been completed and there are approval risks.

### Bottleneck rating discussion: A level → S level?

Re-evaluate 6 criteria:
- Supply concentration 🔴 (Japan’s production capacity is cleared, South Korea only has Foosung/SK Specialty, China Peric; high-purity 6N grade is extremely concentrated)
- Production expansion cycle 🔴 (18-24 months for new production capacity certification)
- Substitution difficulty 🔴 (Chip tungsten interconnect is irreplaceable)
- Capacity utilization 🔴 (fully loaded + permanent reduction of 30%)
- Demand growth 🔴 (AI semiconductor demand continues to grow)
- Customer verification cycle 🔴 (>1 year)

**6 strips all red → complies with S-class standards. ** However, the original map listed WF6 as Grade A with the note "Structural Shortage". This 7/1 confirmation of permanent reduction constitutes a qualitative change from "early warning" to "event occurrence", and it is recommended to officially upgrade to **S level**.
