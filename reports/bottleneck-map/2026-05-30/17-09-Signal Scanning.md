### Signal 1: Shin-Etsu Chemical withdraws FY2027 performance guidance

**Facts**:
- Shin-Etsu Chemical (4063.T, $44B market capitalization) announced that it will not provide annual performance guidance for FY2026 (ending March 2027)
- President Yoshihiko Saito stated: "The conflict in Iran shows no signs of ending, and the situation in the Middle East is unpredictable; it will take quite a few years to repair oil and petrochemical facilities in the Gulf countries."
-Mainly affecting business: PVC (polyvinyl chloride) - core profit source, relying on the naphtha → propylene chemical chain
- FY2025 (released) net profit ¥474.4B (-11.2% YoY), announced ¥250B buyback in the same period

**Association with photoresist chain**:
- The same root cause: Iran conflict → Hormuz → Japanese naphtha +92% → propylene supply compression
- Propylene downstream: ①PVC (Shin-Etsu’s main business) ②Propylene oxide→PGME/PGMEA (photoresist solvent)
- Shin-Etsu Shirakawa factory (photoresist, Fukushima) has suspended operations after the 4/20 earthquake (expected to resume operations in 4-8 weeks)
- **Important distinction**: The direct reason for the withdrawal of the guidance is PVC, not photoresist; but the root cause and factory shutdown have verified the photolithography chain pressure

**Assessment**:
- This is enterprise-level behavior (no guidance can be provided) rather than analyst forecasts, with high credibility
- It is not a signal specific to photoresist, but it confirms that the pressure from the "Iranian conflict → Japanese petrochemical chain" is so severe that major suppliers cannot predict their own performance.
- Investment target: Shin-Etsu $44B is too large, TOK (4186.T) $8.7B/P/S~5.2x is being prepared for tracking
- **Does not change any target ratings; photoresist chain Class A signal maintained (first recorded at 17:00 scan)**
