### Signal 1: UAMY’s shareholders’ meeting on June 12th—authorized decision to issue additional shares (⚠️⚠️ new dilution risk)

**Signal content**: The 2026 general meeting of shareholders of American Antimony Corporation (UAMY) will be held virtually on June 12 (12 days later). One of the voting items is to **amend the company's articles of association to increase the authorized common shares from 250 million shares to 500 million shares (+100%)**.

**Background Data**:
- Q1 2026 net loss of $11.3M (vs 2025Q1 loss of $5.7M, deepened); gross profit margin is only 16% (vs FY2025 annual average of 25%)
- Thompson Falls expansion completed (April), but capital requirements are high during the ramp-up period
- The Shuiye route (Bolivia) has high transportation and processing costs and great short-term cash pressure.
- $44M DOE funding application not yet finalized
- The existing contract backlog is $354M, but implementation requires a full ramp-up of equipment.

**Assessment**:
- Authorization increase ≠ immediate issuance but provides management with financing flexibility
- Combined with continued losses in Q1 and capital requirements for production expansion, the possibility of equity financing increases in the next 6-12 months
- If issued according to the authorized cap, the potential dilution will be about 50% (250 million new shares), and EPS will be under pressure
- **Does not affect bottleneck logic** (DoD contract and antimony shortage are both real), but increases position risk

**Conclusion**: UAMY ★★★ rating maintained, but dilution risk raised from "yellow light" to "orange light"; the June 12 vote result is the next key data point.
