### Why be relegated rather than eliminated?

The IQE bottleneck logic still holds:
- InP monthly demand is 700,000-800,000 pieces vs. supply is about 400,000 pieces (gap 50%+)
- China’s 4,000-piece export quota ≈ essentially zero relief
- MACOM multi-year supply agreement: positive validation, customer locked in
- FY2026 photonics business acceleration (Tower, Google CSP diversification)
- Expansion window 18-20 months, most competitors are still in trial production

**But the share price went from 5p to 54p (+900%) in 5 months** and:
- Overall revenue fell by 17.6% in FY2025 (dragaged by the wireless sector)
- The discount was 58.4% when the additional issuance was issued (reflecting the pressure on the balance sheet at the time)
- Doubling within 60 days after additional issuance = obvious sentiment premium

### Valuation safety margin test (10-year 25xPE exit method)

- Current market cap: ~$900M
- 10% annualized target (10 years): $900M × 1.1^10 = **$2.33B**
- Required net profit (25x PE): $2.33B / 25 = **$93M**
- Current Net Profit: **Loss** (FY2025 EBITDA ~£10-15M estimate, net loss after amortization)

- Required: Growth from loss to $93M net profit, which requires **revenue >£300m (approx. 2.5x today's) and significant margin improvement**
- **Conclusion: Buy at current price of 54p, no margin of safety; requires a highly optimistic scenario to achieve 10% annualized** ⚠️
