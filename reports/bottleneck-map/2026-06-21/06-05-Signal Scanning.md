## Detailed analysis of new dimensions: MOCVD equipment—hidden constraints on InP expansion

### Why is this a new signal?

05:05 The scan reduces the InP substrate bottleneck from level S to level A. The core reason is that JX Advanced Metals announced a ¥120 billion expansion (capacity 7-10 times before FY2030). But this round discovered an **upstream restriction on JX’s production expansion schedule**:

> To expand the production of InP substrates, JX needs a large number of MOCVD (Metal Organic Chemical Vapor Deposition) equipment. There are only two major MOCVD suppliers in the world, Veeco (US) and Aixtron (Germany). The current equipment delivery period is 10-12 months, and there is a backlog of orders (Veeco announced $250M+ in new orders on May 5, and the delivery timeline is until 2027).

**Logical chain**:
```
JX announces ¥120 billion expansion of production → Need to purchase a large amount of MOCVD equipment
                          ↓
         Veeco + Aixtron equipment delivery time is 10-12 months
                          ↓
    Even if JX places an order now, the first batch of equipment is in place: 2027Q1-Q2
                          ↓
    Actual production capacity release: 2027H2 to 2028 (not "will be shipped soon")
```

This explains why the downgrade judgment of the 05:05 scan (the expansion cycle is changed from 🔴 to 🟡) still needs to be cautious: **¥120 billion investment commitment ≠ equipment in place**. The MOCVD equipment itself is the next level of bottleneck.
