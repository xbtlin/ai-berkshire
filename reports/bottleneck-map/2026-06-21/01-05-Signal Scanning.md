### Signal 2: WF6 — Dual factories ceased production, Central Glass (4044.T) officially confirmed to join

**Event**: The previous scan focused on tracking Kanto Denka (4047.T). This round of confirmation **Central Glass (TSE: 4044.T) has also permanently stopped production of WF6**. The two together account for 100% of Japan's and about 25% of the world's WF6 supply, effective July 1 (T-10 days).

**Background**: China tightens tungsten export controls → Japan’s supply of high-purity tungsten powder is cut off → 60-70% of WF6 production cost is tungsten powder → Two Japanese factories ran out of reserves and were forced to suspend production.

**Price**: The current price of 5N grade WF6 is ¥1670-1810/kg (+232.7% YoY); the price of 6N grade is ¥2.2-3 million/ton (+190%+). The certification cycle is 18-24 months, and supply shortages are expected to extend until 2027.

**Benefit Chain**: Foosung (South Korea’s second largest WF6 manufacturer) + SK Specialty (Samsung subsidiary, unlisted). But there are problems with Foosung itself:
- Revenue $355M (TTM), market cap $1.34B → PS ~3.77x
- EPS remains negative (loss)
- The stock price has increased by **+196%** in the past 12 months (obvious emotion-driven characteristics)
- In the process of certification with China CSSC Special Gas, plans to import WF6 from China in August - this is an **alternative route risk** (if China starts exporting WF6, Foosung's advantage will be compressed)

**Central Glass (4044.T) status**: The current stock price is 3,950JPY (-4.59% week), the company has suspended production, not the beneficiary. No investment value.

**Conclusion**: The bottleneck of WF6 is real, and July 1st is the key node. Foosung★★★maintained (loss + emotional surge, valuation is not ideal yet).
