## In-depth interpretation of InP signal

### Why the 2027 supply scramble is the new signal

The previously tracked InP bottlenecks are concentrated in **2026 H2**: China's export controls have caused a supply gap, and cloud vendors dominate diversified procurement.

6/22 Digitimes article reveals **New dimensions extending to 2027**:
- CPO (Co-Packaged Optics) will begin the first commercial deployment on the Broadcom Tomahawk 6 platform in Q3-Q4 of 2026
- CPO scaling (peak in 2027-2028) will significantly increase InP consumption
- **Supply chain players have started locking in 2027 distribution rights TODAY** instead of waiting until the end of 2026

This means that the bottleneck of InP is not a one-time crisis in 2026, but a structural shortage that will last until at least 2028, and may intensify as CPO penetration increases.
