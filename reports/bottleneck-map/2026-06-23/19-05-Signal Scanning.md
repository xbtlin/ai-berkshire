## Signal 1: WF6 S-level bottleneck—the alternative supply solution has not yet been implemented

**Background** (already in master-map S level): Kanto Denka + Central Glass officially permanently ceased production of WF6 on **2026-07-01**, totaling approximately 2,000-2,200 tons/year, accounting for approximately **25%** of global production capacity. The root cause: China’s tungsten powder export controls → Japan’s supply of raw materials is cut off. Prices have been +200% (China) and Korean contract prices increased by 70-90%.

**New in this round**: CSSC Specialty Gases (688146.SH) official investor platform clearly states: **"As of now, no large or long-term tungsten fluoride product orders have been signed."**

> This is directly contradictory to Foosung's public statement of "targeting large-scale imports from CSSC in August."

**Risk Interpretation**:
- Supply chain path: Japan (Kanto/Central Glass) → South Korea Foosung certified CSSC substitute → Start importing in August
- CSSC denies that there has been a large order → Certification progress may be hindered, or procurement quantitative negotiations have not yet been completed
- The standard certification cycle is 18-24 months, even if it is expedited, it will take several months; production will be suspended in July → the "large-scale import" window will be extremely tight in August
- Result: The global WF6 supply gap in 2026 Q3 is more likely to rely on SK Specialty (South Korea) and Peric (China) to digest rather than Foosung to quickly fill it

**Impact on S rating**: Maintain S rating, risks strengthen (alternative source timeline postponed)
