### 10-year 25x PE exit method safety margin test

- Current market cap: $65B
- Required annualized return of 10%: $65B × (1.1)^10 = $168.6B exit market value
- 25x PE: Net profit required is $6.74B
- If the net profit rate is 15%: $44.9B in revenue is required (6.2 times today’s $7.24B)
- COHR grows at 21% CAGR for 10 years: $7.24B × (1.21)^10 ≈ $48.5B ✓ Barely reachable
- **Annualized return calculation: ($48.5B×15%×25x / $65B)^(1/10) - 1 ≈ 10.7%**
- Conclusion: **Just exceeded the 10% threshold, and the margin of safety is extremely thin** - Assuming that the 21% growth rate can continue for 10 years (high requirements)

### Look at the long logic

1. **NVIDIA Deep Binding**: $4B EML laser supply locked + $2B manufacturing direct investment, COHR is the core supplier of NVIDIA AI optical interconnection, customer dependence is transformed into long-term certainty
2. **InP vertical integration advantages**: From 6-inch InP substrate to EML laser to optical modules, the capacity of the three industries (TX + Sweden + Zurich) is expanding simultaneously and will double again in 2027, establishing unique competitive barriers

### Bearish logic

1. **Large market capitalization, has been discovered**: The $65B market capitalization is not a hidden target, with heavy institutional positions and limited room for excess returns; the upside relies on 10 years of sustained 21% growth (history is difficult to achieve)
2. **GAAP loss/low profit**: A large amount of intangible asset amortization makes GAAP PE as high as 112x, reflecting the historical burden of acquisitions (II-VI+Coherent); if the growth rate slows down, the risk of valuation compression is large
3. **Non-InP business drag**: Non-AI businesses such as industrial lasers (Manufacturing/Instrumentation) and Silicon Carbide account for 25% of revenue, and the growth rate is slow or even declining, diluting the overall growth rate
