## Signal 1: Hanwha Aerospace Daejeon factory explosion (today, S-class verification)

### Event details

- **Time**: 2026-06-01 10:59 KST (Today, real-time confirmation from multiple sources)
- **Location**: Daejeon City, South Korea, about 150km south of Seoul
- **Casualties**: 5 dead (including 2 temporary workers in their 20s) + 2 injured (1 severely burned)
- **Cause of explosion**: An explosion occurred when workers used water to clean explosive residues on rocket propellant manufacturing tools - a typical safety risk point in the production of energy materials
- **History of the same location**: There was an explosion in 2018 (including third-party reports that "at least 4 people were injured"), and another explosion in 2019 (a total of 8 historical casualties), which is a high-risk repeat point.
- **Production content**: solid rocket propellant, large propulsion engine, Nuri liquid propulsion engine (KAI K2 space launch)

### Supply Chain Impact Assessment

**Short term (immediate)**:
- The Daejeon facility production line was immediately suspended, and regulatory agencies launched an investigation
- Referring to the 2018/2019 precedent, the production shutdown period is about 4-8 weeks (regulatory investigation + safety rectification + production resumption verification)
- Daejeon is one of South Korea's most important military propellant production bases. The suspension of production affects the supply rhythm of South Korea's K-MLRS/Hyunwu missile and other weapon systems.

**Mid-term (January-March)**:
- If production is suspended for more than three months, it may affect South Korea's export of propellants to NATO partners.
- Hanwha is South Korea's main contractor for supplying 155mm artillery shells and rockets to Ukraine/NATO. The suspension of production in Daejeon directly affects its production and supply.
- NATO is already highly dependent on South Korea's energy material supply (South Korea's 155mm production in 2025 is about three times the United States' own production)

**Impact on CHG**:
- ✅ **Direct Positive**: Hanwha capacity gap in the short term → NATO customers are more dependent on NC/HMX supply from CHG/Chemring Nobel
