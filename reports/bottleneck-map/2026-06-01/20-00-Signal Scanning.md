## Background on the Lake City strike

**Event**: On 2026-04-04, about 1,350 workers at Olin Winchester’s Lake City Army Ammunition Plant (Independence, Missouri) IAM Local 778 went on strike, demanding better wages and a reduction in mandatory overtime.

**Scale**: Lake City is the largest Army ammunition factory in the United States, responsible for approximately 30% of the .223/5.56 ammunition supply in the United States, and also produces military 7.62mm and .50 Cal.

**Duration**: Approximately 4.5 weeks (April 4 – May 6, resolved on May 6, 2026).

**Solution**: Olin Winchester signs a new 4-year collective contract with front-loaded salary increases + partial mandatory overtime reduction; workers will return to work on May 7.

**Impact on CHG investment logic**:
- ✅ Indirect verification: The core ammunition facilities in the United States almost completely stopped production within 4.5 weeks, indicating the structural fragility of the Western ammunition supply chain;
- ✅ Product differences: Lake City produces small arms ammunition (propellants/metal cases) and does not directly compete with CHG's NC (nitrocellulose)/HMX high explosives;
- ⚠️Resolved: The strike on May 6 has ended and does not constitute a current ongoing bottleneck;
- ⚠️ Product not relevant: small arms ammunition supply restored, does not affect NATO precision ammunition/missile fuel gap.

**Conclusion**: Recorded as a NATO ammunition supply chain vulnerability verification event, no existing target ratings will be upgraded, and no new investable targets will be created.
