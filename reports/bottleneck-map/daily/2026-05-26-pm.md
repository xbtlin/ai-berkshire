# Newly discovered signals (scanning in the afternoon)

Signal one: Russian Helium Export Control (early signal upgrade)

** Fact**: On 14 April 2026, Russia officially implemented export controls on helium, which are valid until the end of 2027. Any export to a region outside EEEU requires approval by the Ministry of Industry and Trade + signature at the Prime Minister level. Russia is the third largest helium producer worldwide (after the United States and Qatar).

**Elevational significance**: The early report pre-empted "US/Russia/Australia can replace the Qatar gap" and found in the afternoon that Russia's assumption was no longer valid -- Russia was not just not replacing, but redirecting supply to China. Meanwhile, Russia's exports of Helium gas to China in 2025 + 60% YoY further tightened Western availability. The latest comprehensive assessment: "Only about half of the Qatar gap can be replaced" (as previously the market assumptions were more adequate).

** On the other side (mitigation factor)**: Samsung and SK Hynix signed a long-term Linde+Air Products contract in April 2026, which guarantees the continuity of production of at least two of the largest DRAM manufacturers. But the cost of helium from producers such as power-breeding stations, beauty light (Fabur) will increase significantly.

** Conclusion**: Helium bottleneck down from "probable catastrophic" to "precise high cost constraint", S classification maintained but narrative corrected.
