# Emergency signal (need immediate attention)

Signal one: Qatar semiconductor helium cut-off for crisis (potential S-geopolitic drive)

** Fact**: Iran stopped helium production on February 28, 2026, when its missiles hit the Ras Lafhan gas facility in Qatar. Qatar accounted for about 33% of global helium supply (USGS data). South Korea (Sisan, SK Hynix) imported 65% of helium gas from Qatar. Helium gas in semiconductor crystals is used to cool. There is no substitute for helium in semiconductor crystals. The stock available at the main DRAM plant is only "a few months" and QatarEnergy says that it will take three to five years to repair it.

** Meaning**: This is not a lack of capacity, but a physical supply route is cut off - these risks are more difficult to address than structural capacity bottlenecks with capital spending. If Samsung/SK Hynix stocks are depleted in Q3,2026 and alternative sources (US, Russia, Australia) cannot be filled quickly, HBM/DRAM production capacity will be exposed to a substantial threat, which will have a direct impact on the pace of AI infrastructure expansion.

# Core insight (most important)

** Insight I: Geopolitics are creating new S-class bottlenecks for "non-capitalization solutions"**

The war in Iran created two unprecedented physical disruptions in the semiconductor industry: Qatar Helium (33 per cent supply disruption worldwide, rehabilitation for 3-5 years) and Israel's brominated substances (97.5 per cent dependence, storage for just 2-3 weeks). Unlike the "insufficiency" class of bottlenecks, such risks cannot be hedged with multi-plants — the solution being diplomacy, strategic storage, or the development of alternative technologies, both of which take years.

* On the other hand: Samsung/SK Hynix is still in stock for several months, and alternative suppliers such as Air Products, ExxonMobil Helium, Russia are actively delivering, and will not break the chain in the short term. But if the war lasts longer than 9-12 months, whether the global supply replacement will fill the 33% gap is one of the biggest uncertainties this year.

** Insight II: Bottlenecks for MOCVD equipment were independently validated after Veeco + 22% **

The Veco Q1 battalion, although slightly less than anticipated, announced a share plate after 250M+InP MOCVD. Market price poll: InP equipment requirements are real. This is independently cross-checked with Aixtron's receipt of Lumentum's new order. Both equipment dealers were simultaneously given large-scale InP orders indicating that the entire InP manufacturing chain is being urgently expanded and that the delivery of MOCVD equipment will be the physical ceiling for the production speed (12-24 months).

* On the other hand: Veeco EPSson expects that the current collection will not fully reflect the order, and that 2026Q3-Q4 will be required to recognize the revenue in cash.

** Insight III: ABF base plate bottlenecks have been extended from the Baseboard Factory to the Raw Materials layer, and analysts are looking at blind areas**

The Ajinomoto ABF film 30% increase (Q3 2026 in effect), T-glas (high-end fabric) H2 > 40% gap – two L3-store raw materials signals are today’s new discovery, almost without mainstream analysts covering it. When everyone looks at the base-plating plants like Ibden, Hing, and the others, the monopoly of the "raw-level" is more worrying than the base-plate plant itself.
