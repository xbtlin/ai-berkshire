# Insight 1: Insight is the bottom throat of the entire AI light-connected supply chain, but is seriously ignored by the market

The data show that the global supply and demand gap of the InP bottom line is over 70%. But market discussions have focused on the Layer 2 level of the "Light Module" rather than the "InP Bottom" of the Layer 3 level. This has created three-storey bottleneck structures: **InP bottom (Layer 3) <unk> EML laser (Layer 2) <unk> 800G/16 T light module (Layer 1)**. Nvidia has realized and bypassed the light module directly investing in the EML laser manufacturer precisely because the EML is deeper in the neck.

** Important valuation warning: bottleneck real investment opportunities.** AXT/Tongmei is a small number of investable pure InP liners, but the current valuation is already extremely overstretched: the market value ~ $9.2 billion vs. Revenue in 2025 was only $88.3 million (PS 104x), and the company is still in loss. Even under the most optimistic assumptions (a 5-fold increase in revenue over five years, a 15-per cent net interest rate), the net profit after five years is $75 million, which corresponds to the market value of $9.2 billion today, and the PE remains 123x. The current price has already pre-pricing the entire five-year growth of the cattle market.** The InP bottleneck is S, but AXT is not a good investment target at the current price.** There is a need to find a more reasonable alternative indicator (e.g., the InP supplier of metal upstream and Japanese appliancers, the InP business is a ratio).

** Positive arguments**: InP gap data from multiple independent sources; extended life cycle (6 inches of crystal mill just broke); Chinese export control is objective
** Reverse argument**: AXT market value of $9.2 billion but annual income of less than $100 million is still in deficit, PS 104x 30 times as high as peer, valuation overdrafted; 64 increased in April and two months to $140, with emotional-driven features evident; some gaps may come from stakeholders in the industrial chain; alternative technologies (silicon photons) are threatened over time; Tongmei has geo-hazard risk in China

# Insight 2: Probe Card is an underrated "invisible doorman"

Each HBM4 chip, every CoWOS envelope must be tested by a probe card before it leaves the plant. The HBM4 16-Hi superstition and CoWoS high-density interconnections have significantly increased the test complexity, with Advantest increasing SoC test system production targets from 7,500 to 10,000 in 2026. Form Factor is the world ' s largest search card supplier, with production capacity in 2026, but market discussions are much lower than TSMC/SK Hynix/Nvidia. Form Factor ' s market value is about $2.2 billion, representing a relatively small market value.** Risk: the income growth rate in 2025 was only 2.8%, and growth dynamics were actually translated into financial data for 2026 Q1 finance.

** Positive arguments**: HBM4 and CoWos are positive trends and test needs are positively related to them; full production is actual data
** Reverse argument**: revenue growth has been historically low; the Japanese Micronics and other competitors are also expanding; changes in the AI chip design may involve a risk of a probe card design switch

## Insight 3: Data Center power bottlenecks are being directed upstream - transformer/ switch device is next focus

The data centre power crisis is well known (Layer level 4), but its upstream transmission effects – super-high pressure transformers, switchers, and supply chain tension in parent slots – are of concern. The US transformer has 18 months and larger plants like Siemens/ABB have longer periods; while China transformer manufacturers have increased their exports, with a 10-month turn-over period. This is a small manufacturer that has not been fully excavated, especially for data centre-level high-capacity transformers.
