# VIII. Summary Paragraphs

Hale Jie is a typical ** structural division**: in the refrigerator area, there is ** absolute moat ** (40% + share, Kassati premium), which is its core value; but in the air conditioning and small household electricity markets, there is a marked disadvantage, reflecting the uneven business layout. The current valuation discount (PE 9/99x vs. US 13.61x) reflects concerns about the market’s weak growth** (net profit gain growth of only 4.39% in 2025),** strategic risk to three wings** (cumulative input over 10 billion) and ** profitability gap** (net interest rate of 6.67% vs. US 9.75%).

From the four masters' perspective, a comprehensive assessment would be made:** paragraph Yongping** would question its insufficient differentiation and limited pricing power;** Buffett** would consider the security margin inadequate (only 7 per cent) and the growth prospects uncertain;** Manger** would see a pattern of structured competition and a full-scale threat of beauty;** Retz** would be alert to the strategic risks of three-wing birds and the genetic limitations of management.

**The nature of the investment**: The investment in Haile is the moat for its ** refrigerator operations** whether it will be sustainable and ** whether the three-wing bird ecology** will succeed in opening the second growth curve. The current point is to suggest** that robust investors wait for better prices (16-18, 20% + security margin)** and radical investors can test the warehouse lightly but need to strictly control it (~5%) and follow closely the key catalysts mentioned above.
