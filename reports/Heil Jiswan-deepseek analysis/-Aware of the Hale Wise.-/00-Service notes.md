## Series Positioning

Haier Smart Home is not a company that "nobody pays attention to" - it is one of the **most subtly controversial** names in the A-share home-appliance sector.

- The bulls say it has been the world's No. 1 in major home-appliance retail volume for **17 straight years**, Casarte dominates the premium market, the P/E is below **10x**, and the dividend yield exceeds **5%**, making it absurdly cheap
- The bears say its net margin of **6.67%** is only two-thirds of Midea's, gross margin has fallen by **4.85 percentage points** over two years, 2026 Q1 revenue and profit both declined, and while it tells an "ecosystem story," its expense ratio is much higher than peers

**Both sides are looking at the same company, but their conclusions differ a lot.** Most people are not arguing over whether Haier is good; they are arguing over whether Haier is cheap for a reason.

This series aims to **lay out the facts and data first, and then let you decide** - not to hand you the conclusion.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Delete unreliable data rather than inventing it** - non-official metrics such as market share are marked as "estimated" or "non-official"
4. **Do not compute probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
