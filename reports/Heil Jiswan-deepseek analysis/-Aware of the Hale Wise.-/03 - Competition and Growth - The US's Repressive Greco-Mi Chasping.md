# One, The cruel reality of the white power industry: stock age has come

Let us begin with the uncomfortable fact that the era of incremental growth in China ' s white electricity market is over.**

Several key data:
- Household refrigerators in Chinese towns and cities: over **100 units/100 households** - that is, at least one per household, saturated
- Laundry machine holdings: exceeding **100 units/100 households** - equally saturated
- Air conditioning holdings: approximately **150 units/100 households** - near saturation ( 1-2 units per household)
- Size of China ' s white power market: approximately **80 billion yuan**, annual rate of increase** 3-5%**
- Global White Power Market Size: approximately **50 billion United States dollars**, annual rate of increase** 3-4%**

Source: Ei Wei International, Industry Researcher

When the main product holdings exceed 100 units/100 households, the new demand is almost zero, and growth can only be achieved by two things:

1. ** Inventory replacement** (old for new): **~66% of current demand**
2. ** Product upgrading** (more expensive): high end trends

This means that the white power industry has changed from "growing industry" to "value industry" -- the investment logic has changed from "growing" to "** profitability + split **".

** The counter-view**: There is still room for growth in emerging markets abroad. Central and East Africa’s growth + 56%, South Asia + 23%, South-East Asia + 20% (source: Haile 2025). The global rise of the middle class is a long-term trend that cannot be seen solely in China.

# Four, the biggest variable: millet

Most of the reports from the white industry, just beautiful, Grigg, Haile, are analyzed. But if you look at traditional players, you miss a change:** millet is entering the white electricity market as an "ecological player".**

Several key data:
- Mi-air conditioning 2025 on-line: **10.8%** (industry III)
- Proportion of rice air-conditioning users using smart networking: **70%**
- Mi Wuhan factory: the self-built factory has moved away from tagging and started to build itself

Source: Industry Studies

Mi's methods of playing are completely different from traditional home-based electricity companies:
1. ** Not made by hardware**: ACs can be sold at low prices and profit from ecological services
2. **Eco-lock**: Users of rice air conditioning, more likely to buy rice washing machines, refrigerators - because the Mi family APP can be controlled on a one-stop basis
3. ** Data flyer**: 70% of smart networking usage means that millet is accumulating a large amount of user behavior data for optimizing products and recommendations

** Threat to Haile** If the "ecological sticky" of rice becomes larger — that is, the users are reluctant to change brands because of their intelligence connections — then the "monitor competition" model of traditional electric enterprises will face fundamental challenges.

** The square view**: The household is a heavy asset, heavy-manufacturing, heavy-channel industry. The mill currently has a share of the air-conditioning line, with little sense of presence under it. The technical barriers to refrigerators, washing machines are much higher than air-conditioning, and it is difficult for the mill to shake Hal's position in the ice-washing field in the short term.

** Anti-view**: Don't underestimate the long-term threat of rice. The small-scale user group is young, and when the user group becomes the home-based power consumer group in 5-10 years, brand choices can change fundamentally.
