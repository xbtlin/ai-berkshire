## Key conclusions

### Advantage Analysis
1. **Absolute leader in refrigerators**: 40%+ market share, the deepest moat
2. **Casarte high-end brand**: 30-50% premium, No. 1 in mindshare in the high-end market
3. **Global layout**: Acquisition of GE Appliances, Candy, etc., localized operation experience
4. **Scenario ecology first**: Three-winged bird smart home ecology, the strategic card position is correct

### Risk Disclosure
1. **Weaknesses in the air-conditioning business**: Obvious disadvantage in the largest single product market (<20% share)
2. **Small home appliances are missing**: Midea’s business contributes stable cash flow, while Haier’s is basically missing.
3. **Three-winged Bird Investment Risk**: The cumulative investment exceeds 10 billion, and the profit model has not been verified.
4. **Profitability Gap**: Net profit rate 6.67% vs. Midea 9.75%, the efficiency gap is obvious

### Non-consensus perspective
**Market consensus**: Haier smart home leads the industry
**Fact check**: The smart home penetration rate is only 15%, and the leading advantage is meaningless

**Market consensus**: Casa Di has a deep moat
**Fact check**: Midea COLMO's growth rate exceeds that of Casarte, and the gap narrows

**Market consensus**: Haier’s global layout advantages
**Fact check**: Overseas business profit margins are lower than domestic ones (4% vs 8%)
