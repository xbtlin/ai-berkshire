# What's a barfette mirror test

Buffett's cruelest discipline: he can't speak to the market.

** 5 sentences template**:
What is the essence of this business? (Cluster of Capability)
What is its ** frigate**? Is it wide or narrow?
3. ** Is management** trustworthy?
4. Current prices equal to the discount of ** intrinsic value? (security margin)
Even if I'm wrong, ** Downside risk** is manageable?

** A hidden rule -- "but" count**: each sentence appears "but/but/if/just" -- the certainty of the discussion is reduced by 1. Over two cumulatively, indicating that they are using their words to find the steps -- ** which is itself a violation of the discipline of mirror testing**.

# The essence of the mirror test

The mirror experiment is on the surface of "five words for the reason," essentially ** the word recognition vs. a really good mirror **:

- ** True man**: Five simple words, no "but"
- ** Self-deception**: 5 words full of "although/but/if/just"
- ** People who don't understand**: Say five words or all words.

* "Investment discipline is not 'no mistake', but 'no fool'."*
>
* If you look in the mirror and find yourself in need of comfort, it's a signal.*
