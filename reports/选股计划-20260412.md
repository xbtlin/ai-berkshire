## Decision Framework

**Standard for "understood"**: can reasonably estimate the company's net profit range 5 and 10 years out without relying on "believing the story."

**Conditions for a heavy position:**
1. You understand it (per the standard above)
2. The moat is not expected to be fundamentally broken within 5-10 years
3. Current valuation offers a margin of safety

**Exclusion criteria** (if any one is met, do not choose it):
- 5-year earnings are completely impossible to predict
- The moat depends on an external party (e.g., PDD depends on Tencent's WeChat)
- The business model needs massive ongoing capital spending to stay alive
