#3. The fate of the fuel truck

** Short-term (2026-2028)**:
- New sales of pure fuel vehicles will rapidly shrink to 30-35 per cent
- Accelerating the roll-out of hybrid/accelerator models (e.g. Toyota, general population) by mainstream joint brands
- Partially pure fuel-car shutdown, production line turned mixed.

** Mid-term (2028-2030)**:
- The ratio of new fuel trucks in the traditional sense of the word has dropped to 15-20 per cent
- Average fuel consumption for new vehicles dropped to 4.0 L/100 km in 2030, in line with the road map for energy efficiency and new energy vehicle technology 2.0, forcing full mixing
- Only partial commercial and special-purpose locations are reserved for fuel trucks

** Key judgement**: Fuel trucks will not suddenly die, but will be transformed by "mixing." By 2030, the vast majority of "fuel trucks" on the market will be in the HEV/PHEV hybrid model, and new pure fuel vehicles will be in the minority.

# Summing up: Three historic turns in the Chinese automobile market

# One turn: new energy alternatives are irreversible
From 31.6% penetration in 2023 to 47.9% in 2025 and more than 70% expected by 2030, new energy cars have moved from "policy-driven" to "market-driven" stages. This is not a linear process, but an acceleration phase of a S curve.

# Two turns: Autonomous brand dominance is established
The autonomous brand jumped from 56% in 2023 to 70% in 2025, not temporarily, but structurally. The counter-attack window for the joint brand has been closed, and the question for the future is not whether the autonomous brands will go beyond the joint venture, but which joint brands will survive.

# Turn three: China is moving from Car Power to Car Power
Exports ranged from 49.1 million to 7.1 million, offshore construction accelerated, and intelligent technology led globally. Forecasts of 3-4 Chinese firms in the global Top10 are now becoming reality in 2030.
