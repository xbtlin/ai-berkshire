## Core Investment Thesis

### Why Uber is worth watching

1. **The earnings inflection is durable**: Uber has gone from years of losses to USD 9.8 billion of free cash flow in FY2025, with five straight years of 20%+ growth. This is not a one-off; it is the result of ongoing operating leverage.

2. **Uber One is an underappreciated moat**: 50 million members contribute about half of gross bookings, and members spend 3x more than non-members. This turns a transactional platform into a subscription platform and materially improves predictability and stickiness.

3. **The ad business is a hidden profit engine**: annualized revenue above USD 2 billion and growing 50%+ with very high margins. Like the early stages of ads at Meituan or Amazon, this could become one of Uber's biggest profit drivers.

4. **Valuation is clearly low**: 13x P/E and PEG of 0.72 are low for a tech platform. The USD 20 billion buyback adds downside protection.

### Main risks

1. **Autonomous-driving disruption**: if Waymo / Tesla build their own ride networks, Uber's middleman role could weaken. Uber is investing USD 10+ billion into its own AV fleet, but the outcome is highly uncertain.

2. **Regulatory risk**: many jurisdictions are pushing to classify drivers as employees, which could materially raise costs. New Jersey passed such a rule in May 2026.

3. **Food-delivery competition**: DoorDash has 67% U.S. share, and Uber Eats' 23% share could be eroded further.

## Four Analytical Lenses

### Business Model

Uber is fundamentally a two-sided network platform that takes a platform fee across ride-hailing, delivery, and freight. The asset-light model creates enormous operating leverage - revenue rose from USD 37.2 billion in FY2023 to USD 52.0 billion in FY2025, while free cash flow rose from USD 3.9 billion to USD 9.8 billion.

Key insight: **the combination of Uber One membership and advertising is transforming Uber from a "transaction intermediary" into a "consumer life platform."** If that transformation succeeds, the moat widens substantially.

### Financial / Valuation

FY2025 revenue was USD 52.0 billion, FCF USD 9.8 billion, and adjusted EBITDA USD 8.7 billion. Q1 2026 kept the momentum going: gross bookings of USD 53.7 billion (+25%) and Non-GAAP net income of USD 1.5 billion (+39%).

With a USD 153 billion market cap, P/FCF is about 15.6x. DCF value is roughly USD 180-220 billion. **The current price implies a long-term growth assumption of only about 8%-10%, well below the actual 18% growth rate**, which suggests a meaningful expectation gap.

### Industry Competition

Ride-hailing: the duopoly is stable, with Uber at 76% and Lyft not a strategic threat.  
Delivery: DoorDash has a dominant 67% share, and Uber Eats is a strong No. 2.  
Autonomous driving: Uber is "betting on many horses" (20+ partners plus its own fleet), and AV is the biggest long-term variable.

Key insight: **Uber's real moat is not any single business line's market share, but the cross-platform synergy of ride-hailing + delivery + membership.** Lyft and DoorDash cannot replicate that structure.

### Risk and Management

CEO Dara has spent eight years turning Uber from a chaotic loss-making startup into a disciplined profit machine - one of the most successful CEO turnarounds in recent business history. The USD 20 billion buyback and continued growth investment show strong capital discipline.

Main concerns: (1) whether the partnership/competition strategy in autonomous driving is sustainable; (2) whether the USD 10+ billion AV investment will pay off; and (3) the global regulatory environment is getting tighter.

## Investment Recommendation

### Conclusion: **Worth putting on the watchlist; a pullback could be a buying opportunity**

**Suitable investor profile:**
- 3-5 year time horizon
- Comfortable with long-term autonomous-driving uncertainty
- Focused on free cash flow rather than GAAP earnings

**Suggested entry strategy:**
- At the current valuation (P/E ~13x), the stock is already in a reasonable range, but given AV and regulatory risks, a better entry point would be around 11x-12x earnings (market cap roughly USD 130 billion)
- If Q2-Q3 2026 earnings remain strong and the AV strategy shows positive progress, starting a position now is also reasonable

**Key monitoring indicators:**
1. Uber One membership growth and its share of gross bookings
2. Advertising revenue growth and penetration
3. Autonomous ride volume and city expansion progress
4. Driver-classification regulation by region
5. Whether free cash flow growth stays above 15%

**Position sizing:** medium (about 3%-5% of portfolio), leaving room for volatility.
