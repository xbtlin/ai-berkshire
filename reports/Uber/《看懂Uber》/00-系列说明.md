# Series Guide for "Understanding Uber"

> 4 main articles that break down Uber's transformation from "loss king" to "profit machine."
>
> Estimated reading time: about 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Uber is one of the most controversial technology companies in global capital markets.

- The bulls say: FCF of **USD 9.8 billion**, 18% growth, only 13x P/E, and an unassailable 76% share of the ride-hailing market
- The bears say: once autonomous driving matures, Uber's "middleman" role becomes worthless; regulation could reclassify drivers as employees and crush margins

**The whole debate comes down to one word: autonomous driving.**

This series aims to **first break down Uber's business clearly and then leave it to you to decide whether it deserves to be held** - not to hand you the conclusion.

## Writing Principles

This series follows a few hard rules:

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Source every key data point** - all major numbers come from Uber's financial reports, SEC filings, or reliable third parties
4. **Do not calculate probability-weighted expected returns** - scenario weights are too subjective and create false precision
5. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
