## 1. CEO Glenn Fogel’s decision review

### Character background

Glenn Fogel took over as CEO in 2017 after more than 17 years at Booking Holdings (formerly Priceline Group), where he was responsible for global strategy and corporate M&A. He has led nearly every key acquisition in the company's history: Booking.com (2005), KAYAK (2012), OpenTable (2014), RentalCars.com (2014), and Agoda. These acquisitions formed Booking Holdings’ footprint today.

Fogel is not a typical "star CEO". His style is low-key, cautious, and long-term focused. He is more like a strategic "operator" than a performer on the stage.

Data source: [Crunchbase - Glenn Fogel](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/glenn-fogel); [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/05/12/booking-holdings-is-running-ahead-of-its-own-strategy/)

### Review of key decisions

**Decision 1: Promote merchant model transformation (2018 to present)**
Rating: Excellent

Fogel's most important strategic decision after taking office was to shift Booking.com from a pure agency model (the hotel collects payment, the platform collects commission) to a merchant model (the platform collects payment, and then settles with the hotel). This transformation started in 2018, and by 2025 merchant revenue accounted for 66% (less than 30% in 2018).

Significance: (1) Master payment data to lay the foundation for personalized recommendations and Connected Trip cross-selling; (2) Obtain the "float" advantage, users pay first to increase cash flow; (3) Provide user experiences such as flexible cancellation to increase competitiveness.

**Decision Two: Connected Trip Strategy (2019-Present)**
Rating: In progress, in the right direction

Fogel proposed the vision of "Connected Trip" - allowing users to complete all travel bookings such as accommodation, air tickets, car rentals, and attraction tickets on one platform. Multi-category cross-booking transaction volume will increase by more than 20% in 2025, and total air ticket bookings will reach $16.8 billion, an increase of 37%. But at present, Connected Trip still focuses on accommodation as its absolute core. The profit margin of air tickets and attractions business is lower than that of accommodation. The final success or failure of the strategy still needs time to be verified.

Data source: [AInvest - Connected Trip Analysis](https://www.ainvest.com/news/booking-holdings-ai-driven-connected-trip-execution-sparks-moat-widening-catalyst-2604/)

**Decision Three: Aggressive Capital Returns (Continuous)**
Rating: Excellent

Shares outstanding have fallen by more than 40% since 2014. Repurchases of US$6.4 billion in 2025 and US$3.6 billion in Q1 of 2026 (a single-quarter record). $18.2 billion remains authorized. Initiate quarterly dividend in 2024 and increase dividend by 9.4% in 2025. A 25:1 stock split will be completed in April 2026 to improve accessibility to retail investors.

But there are tensions worth watching: The company spends most of its free cash flow on buybacks and dividends, while its Connected Trip and AI strategies require continued investment. For now, Booking's asset-light model allows it to maintain technological leadership without huge capital expenditures, but if the AI ​​arms race escalates, capital allocation priorities may need to be adjusted.

Data source: [FinancialContent - Q4 2025 Results](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/marketminute-2026-3-12-booking-holdings-q4-2025-financial-results-and-25-for-1-stock-split)

**Decision 4: AI strategic layout (2023 to present)**
Rating: Good, need to continue to observe

Fogel will launch an organizational restructuring at the end of 2025, layoffs and reinvest the saved resources into "Agent AI" - an AI system that can perform operations (such as automatic changes, price tracking) on behalf of users. In partnership with OpenAI and Microsoft, the AI ​​travel planning tool is now available in 10+ countries.

Management expects adjusted EBITDA margin expansion of approximately 125 basis points in 2026 from AI efficiency improvements. This shows that AI investment is not just a cost item but also driving operational efficiency improvements.

## 2. The risk of AI disrupting travel bookings

### Nature of the threat

The core value of OTA is "information aggregation + transaction matching" - helping users find the most suitable options in the massive supply and complete bookings. Generative AI is eroding this value in two directions:

**First, transfer of search entrance. ** When users say to ChatGPT or Google Gemini, "Help me plan a five-day trip to Italy and book a hotel," the role of OTA as a search portal is weakened. According to a Bernstein survey, up to 40% of travelers are willing to book directly through an AI chat interface.

**Second, the ultimate threat from agent-based AI. ** If AI agents can automatically compare prices, book, change bookings, and refund, users don’t even need to open any travel apps. This will downgrade OTA from "a platform for direct user interaction" to "a back-end API called by the AI ​​agent", and profit margins will be significantly compressed.

Data source: [HBR - Gen AI Threatening Online Travel](https://hbr.org/2026/01/gen-ai-is-threatening-the-platforms-that-dominate-online-travel); [Yahoo Finance - AI and Online Travel](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-online-travel-disruption-reinvention-103502392.html)

### Booking’s response

Booking’s response strategy is “If you can’t beat AI, become AI”:

1. **Self-built AI capabilities:** AI Trip Planner is available in 10+ countries, providing conversational itinerary planning
2. **Invest in Agentic AI:** Launch the Agentic AI project at the end of 2025, with the goal of allowing Booking’s AI to perform operations on behalf of users
3. **Platform-based defense:** Lock users into the Booking ecosystem through Connected Trip, reducing the risk of being replaced by AI middlemen
4. **Data advantage:** Transaction data of 1.235 billion room nights and hundreds of millions of reviews are unique assets for training tourism AI

### Risk Assessment

Short term (1-2 years): AI has limited substantive impact on Booking, and is more a matter of market sentiment fluctuations.

Mid-term (3-5 years): Google AI search may divert 10-20% of top-funnel traffic, and Booking will need to prove that Connected Trip and its own AI can make up for this loss.

Long term (5-10 years): If agent-based AI becomes the mainstream way to book travel, the role of OTAs will fundamentally change. But the platforms with the largest supply inventories and the best user data will still be in a strong position.

## 3. Civilization Trend: Global Tourism Penetration Rate

### Tourism is an inevitable product of the development of civilization

From the perspective of civilization trends, tourism consumption is highly correlated with per capita income levels. When a country’s per capita GDP exceeds US$5,000, tourism demand begins to grow rapidly; when it exceeds US$10,000, outbound travel demand explodes.

The global online travel market will be worth approximately US$975 billion in 2025 and is expected to reach US$2.19 trillion by 2030, with a CAGR of approximately 17.5%. Even using more conservative estimates (CAGR 9-10%), the market will nearly double in 5 years.

Data source: [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-online-travel-booking-service-market); [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/4995185/online-travel-market-global-forecast-2025-2030)

### Three major structural growth drivers

**First, the Asia-Pacific online penetration rate has increased. ** Asia Pacific expects 75% of travel bookings to be made through digital channels by 2029 (currently around 50%). The rise of the middle class in China, India, and Southeast Asia means hundreds of millions of new online travel consumers. Booking has a presence in the Asia-Pacific through Agoda, and Ctrip is a direct competitor but also a potential partner.

**Second, the rise of experience economy. ** In the post-epidemic era, consumers pay more attention to experiences rather than physical consumption. Travel is the most typical “experience consumption”, and its proportion in consumer spending continues to increase. This is an irreversible cultural trend.

**Third, mobile Internet penetration. ** In 2025, more than 52% of global travel bookings will be completed through mobile terminals, and the growth rate of mobile terminal bookings (CAGR 9.9%) continues to be higher than that of PC terminals. The Booking.com mobile app is one of the most downloaded travel apps in the world.

### Counter-trend risk

- Geopolitical conflicts (Middle East, Eastern Europe) affect tourism demand in specific regions
- Climate change may change the landscape of tourist destinations
- Popularization of remote working may change business travel patterns
- "Anti-tourism" sentiment is rising in some European cities (Barcelona, Amsterdam)

## 4. Final evaluation

Glenn Fogel is an underrated CEO. He does not pursue media exposure, but has demonstrated foresight and discipline in strategic decision-making - the transformation of the merchant model is one of the most successful strategic initiatives in the OTA industry in the past five years, and the aggressive buyback plan is a faithful guardian of the interests of shareholders.

AI is Booking’s biggest uncertainty, but it could also be its biggest opportunity. If Booking can leverage its data advantage and scale of supply to establish a leadership position in AI travel planning, its moat will get wider in the AI ​​era, not narrower.

From the perspective of civilization trends, the increase in global tourism penetration has provided Booking with a decade-long structural growth runway. This is a business that conforms to the development direction of human civilization.
