## Investment thesis

### Reasons to buy (long logic)

1. **Top business model:** Light assets, bilateral network effects, high profit margins, and strong free cash flow. An ROIC of 43.7% means this is one of the most efficient capital allocation machines in the world.

2. **Valuation is at historical lows:** Forward PE 14.7x is lower than the industry median (14.8x). For a company with a growth rate of 10%+ and an ROIC of over 40%, the valuation is attractive. With a free cash flow yield of about 6.6%, coupled with an annual buyback reduction of 4-5%, the annualized return can reach 10%+ even if the valuation does not expand.

3. **The incremental space of Connected Trip + AI:** The transformation from "booking tool" to "travel operating system" is making substantial progress. 37% growth in ticket business and 20%+ cross-booking growth in 2025. If successful, it will significantly increase customer unit price and user stickiness.

4. **Structural Growth in Global Travel:** The online travel market is expected to grow from approximately US$975 billion in 2025 to US$2.19 trillion in 2030. Online penetration in Asia Pacific has increased from 50% to 75%, providing Booking with room for long-term growth.

5. **Shareholder-friendly actions by management:** Reduce shares outstanding by 40% in 10 years, buy back $6.4 billion (70% of FCF) in 2025, and initiate quarterly dividends.

### Main risks

1. **Google AI disruption (medium probability/high impact):** Google uses Gemini AI to build a one-stop travel planning + booking experience, which may reshape the industry traffic distribution pattern within 3-5 years. Bernstein data shows that 40% of travelers are willing to book directly through an AI chat interface.

2. **Macroeconomic cycle (medium probability/medium high impact):** Tourism is optional consumption, and demand elasticity is high during economic recession. But Booking proved the resilience of its asset-light model during the 2020 epidemic.

3. **Regulatory pressure (low probability/medium impact):** The EU may limit OTA commission rates, directly affecting revenue.

4. **Growth Slowing:** Full-year 2026 guidance is for low- to mid-single-digit growth, reflecting geopolitical and currency pressures. The market may give lower valuations due to slower growth.

## Four-dimensional review

### Business model dimensions

This is a business where "the longer the time, the higher the barriers." The transaction scale of 1.235 billion room nights and hundreds of millions of reviews are time assets that cannot be copied. The transformation of the merchant model (the proportion increased from 30% to 66%) is one of the most successful strategic decisions in the OTA industry in the past five years - it upgraded Booking from an "information intermediary" to a "transaction infrastructure". Gross profit margin of 87.4% and free cash flow rate of 34% demonstrate the ultimate efficiency of the platform model. The only regret is that Connected Trip's margin contribution has yet to be fully realized.

### Financial valuation dimensions

It's a money-printing machine -- 43.7% ROIC, $9.1 billion in free cash flow, and almost no capital expenditures needed to sustain growth. Forward PE 14.7x is the lowest level in nearly five years, and a free cash flow yield of 6.6% combined with buyback reductions of 4-5% per year provides a solid return floor. The lows in valuations reflect market concerns about the risk of AI disruption and slowing growth - leaving significant scope for valuation repair if these concerns prove to be overly pessimistic.

### Industry competition dimension

“Think about it the other way around” – what can really hurt Booking? Not Expedia (forever follower), not Airbnb (different track), but Google. Google controls the search portal, has the infrastructure of Maps/Flights/Hotels, and coupled with the capabilities of Gemini AI, is the only player that has the potential to fundamentally change the rules of the game. But Google’s attempts to verticalize travel over the past decade have been unsuccessful, and competing directly with OTAs would cost them huge amounts of advertising revenue. The outcome of this game will become clear in 3-5 years.

### Risk Management Dimension

Glenn Fogel is an underrated manager. He does not seek the spotlight, but has demonstrated excellent judgment and discipline in strategic layout (merchant transformation, Connected Trip) and capital allocation (reducing 40% of outstanding shares in 10 years). From the perspective of civilization trends, global tourism consumption is positively correlated with per capita income, and the rise of the emerging middle class in the Asia-Pacific has provided Booking with a structural growth runway of more than ten years. AI is both the biggest risk and the biggest opportunity – if Booking can leverage its data advantages to become a core platform in the age of AI travel, the moat will be even wider.

## Investment advice

**Conclusion: At current valuation levels (Forward PE 14.7x), Booking Holdings is an attractive long-term investment. **

**Reason:**
- The combination of top-notch business model + reasonably low valuation + long-term growth runway is relatively scarce among large technology/Internet companies
- Free cash flow yield 6.6% + annualized 4-5%. Repurchase and reduction of shares provide a "guaranteed" annualized return of approximately 10%+
- 24-39% upside in base case
- The material impact of the main risk (Google AI disruption) will take 3-5 years to materialize, and Booking is actively addressing it

**Key indicators that need to be continuously tracked:**
1. Connected Trip cross-booking transaction volume growth and profit margin contribution
2. User adoption rate of Google AI travel tools
3. Changes in merchant revenue proportion
4. Growth rate of room night bookings in the Asia-Pacific market
5. Impact of AI investment on EBITDA profit margin
