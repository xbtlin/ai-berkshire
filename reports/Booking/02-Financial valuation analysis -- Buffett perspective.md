## Three, Three Scenario Valuation and Safety Margin

### Basic assumptions
- Valuation based on expected free cash flow in 2026
- Q1 2026 revenue $5.53 billion (+16%), full year guidance for low to mid-single digit growth (subject to geopolitical and currency effects)
- 2026 expected free cash flow: ~$10 billion (extrapolated based on Q1 performance and historical FCF/revenue ratio)
- 2026 expected EPS: ~$3.7 (post-split basis, corresponding to ~$92.5 pre-split)

### Conservative Scenario (Bear Market)

| Assumptions | Values |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 Revenue CAGR | 5% |
| Steady state FCF margin | 30% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 15x FCF |
| **Fair Market Cap** | **About $105 Billion** |
| Compared to the current space | -23% |

Triggering conditions: Global economic recession leads to a decline in travel spending; Google AI search significantly diverts OTA traffic; regulation puts pressure on commission rates.

### Baseline scenario (neutral)

| Assumptions | Values |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 Revenue CAGR | 8-10% |
| Steady state FCF margin | 33% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 20x FCF |
| **Reasonable market capitalization** | **About $170-190 billion** |
| +24% to +39% from current space |

Assumptions: Global tourism grows steadily; Connected Trip strategy increases unit price; AI investment brings EBITDA profit margins to expand by 100-125 basis points annually; continued repurchases reduce outstanding shares by 4-5%.

### Optimistic Scenario (Bull Market)

| Assumptions | Values |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 Revenue CAGR | 12-15% |
| Steady state FCF margin | 36% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 25x FCF |
| **Reasonable market capitalization** | **Approximately $280-320 billion** |
| +104% to +134% from current space |

Trigger conditions: Connected Trip is fully successful, Booking has become a travel "super app"; the Asia-Pacific market is accelerating penetration; AI significantly improves operational efficiency and user conversion rate.

### Safety Margin Assessment

**Based on a baseline scenario of $170-190 billion, the current market cap of $137 billion has ~24-39% upside. **

Even under a conservative scenario, a floor valuation of $105 billion implies a maximum downside of about 23% -- an attractive risk-to-benefit ratio for a top-tier platform company with an ROIC of over 40% and a free cash flow rate of 34%.

In addition, the company shrinks its outstanding shares by 4-5% each year through buybacks, and even if the stock price does not rise, the intrinsic value per share is growing organically at a rate of 4-5%.
