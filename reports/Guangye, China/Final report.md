China's Guangker is a “stable elephant” in nuclear power operating assets: the license plate is scarce, the site is scarce, the safety experience is very limited, the capital financing advantages are obvious, and the long-term low-carbon base charge value is real. It is not a poo-priced business, nor is it a mature, free cash flow machine for Yangtze power; it is entering a new cycle of heavy capital expansion, with price marketization, capital spending, asset injection prices, and nuclear safety tail risks determining shareholders' real returns.
