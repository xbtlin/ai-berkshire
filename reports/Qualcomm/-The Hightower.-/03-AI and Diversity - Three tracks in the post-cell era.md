# Why does Hightower have to be "multiple"?

Of the $44.3 billion in high-access SY2025, mobile chip contributed approximately **25 billion** (including apple duct tape), or nearly 57%** of total income. Add QTL patent authorized income (mainly from mobile manufacturers), the relative share of mobile phone-related income may be close to **70%**. This structure has two fatal hazards:

** Global smartphones are at the peak** - about 1.2 billion copies per year** - with very limited growth space
** The largest client apples are decoupling** - by 2027, **57-59 billion dollars per year** the proceeds from the purchase of the chip in the backbone will be zero.

High-speed CEO Cristiano Amon, in an interview with Fortune in May 2026, said a very important phrase: "2026 was the year of the AI's intelligence body becoming mainstream, and the age of smart phones as the main equipment is coming to an end."

A cell phone chip company, CEO, publicly announces that the age of cell phones is ending -- not just saying it, but laying the groundwork for a strategic transformation of the company.

High-quality pluralism is not "accompanied" but ** necessary for survival**.

# # On the end of the AI what's the real value?

This is a question that requires sober assessment.

**The multi-viewed logic**: AI functionality is becoming the core selling point for mobile phones and PCs. AI mobile phones can achieve real-time translation, smart cameras, local voice assistants, etc., which require powerful NPUs. The Company Research project that in 2026 AI PC will account for **59% of global PC shipments. If AI functional-driven mobile phones/PC are reduced in life and ASP upgrades, high-quality chips will be sold more and more expensive.

** Focal logic**: The current "killer" application on the end of AI has not yet appeared. Most consumers have a weak sense of AI functionality on their phones -- "better voice helper" and "better camera algorithm" -- it is hard to change phones in advance. If the end of AI is finally a marketing stunt rather than a need, the premium on NPU is difficult to sustain.

** Data say **: SY2026 Q2, mobile chip revenue is falling on the same date **13%**, mainly due to the reduction of apple belts and the China market to stock - the "pull effect" of the end AI is not yet reflected in the financial statements. Management says "AI mobile phone switch is not going to start in 2027."

**Deficit**: End AI is almost right, but there is considerable uncertainty about the timing and extent of delivery. Short-term (1-2 years) end AI has limited revenue pull on high-train; medium-term (3-5 years) high-train as a terminal chip tap will be the core beneficiary if the AI smart body is really mainstreamed.

(Source: Qualcomm Q2 SY2026 Financial Reporting, Courtpoint Research)

# # $45 billion in design winnings what does it mean?

Design Win is a key indicator of the car chip industry -- it represents that the factory has selected your chip for a future model. Because the car needs to be designed to produce **3-7 years**, design wins = locking revenue for the next 3-7 years.

**45 billion US$** Designed to win single-pipe line, of which **15 billion** was ADAS-related - which provides a high income visibility.

** Inverse view**: Design winnings are not equal to real income. The factory may delay production, reduce production, and even cancel projects in extreme cases. And the car chip has a lower rate of grosser than the cell phone chip, and income growth does not necessarily lead to a proportional increase in profits.

# From 5 billion to 8 billion on what?

The management has set the target for SY2029 at $8 billion. The increase from $5 billion to $8 billion is mainly due to:

1. **ADAS penetration**: Currently ADAS revenue accounts for about 30% of automobile operations, 15 billion in piped lines are ADAS, and the share will continue to rise in the future
**The Chinese market**: China has the highest penetration rate of new energy vehicles worldwide, and Hightower has a strong presence in the Chinese car market (all with the Xiaoyu, Ideas, etc.)
3. **Platform upgrade**: Upgrade to higher-specific chip for old car models to drive ASP upgrades

** Is it real?** From the record of implementation over the past four years,** management has exceeded every commitment in the automobile business**. SY2024 was originally targeted at 2 billion, actually at 2.8 billion. This is the most credible area for Cristiano Amon. But the 80 billion FY2029 target requires annual growth rates of 15-20% for three consecutive years, and given the uncertainty in the automobile industry (deterioration of electric vehicle penetration, geopolitics, etc.), it is prudent to hit 8% off.

# Race three: PC processor - Eat the hardest bones

# # Dragon X series entering Windows Market

The high-prevalence Snapdragon X Elite/X Plus series entered the Windows PC market and directly challenged the traditional territories of Intel and AMD.

**Enaps**:
- Based on ARM structure, the power and continuation are much better than the x86 chip (over **20 hours** continuation)
- NPU Calculator **80 TOPS** (X2 series), leading in Intel and AMD for the same period
- certified by Microsoft Copilot+PC, in conjunction with Windows AI Eco-link
- More than **100** laptop design program is in production or development

** Reality**:
- Market share, about **10%** (as of end 2025, PCWorld data), still very small
- x86 Software compatibility issues not fully resolved (some professional software performance discounts on ARM)
- Face the strong competition of apples, M series.
- Intel, Luna and AMD are catching up on the power.

**Deficit**: PC is the race track of high-access diversification strategies** that has the slowest progress and the most resistance**. The 10% share indicates that the product is recognized, but that the 10% to 20% need to solve the underlying problems of software ecology. The return on this track may take more than five years to see.

** Reverse view** The real meaning of high-tech PCs may be not in short-term market shares, but in the entry to the card-point "AI PC" era. If the top-side AI really becomes a core PC function, high-access NPU advantages may make it the preferred platform for AI PC. Courtpoint predicts that in 2026 AI PC will account for 59% of global output -- a trend that, if sustained, would be structural for high-access.

(Source: PCWorld, CouncilPoint Research, Windows Central)
