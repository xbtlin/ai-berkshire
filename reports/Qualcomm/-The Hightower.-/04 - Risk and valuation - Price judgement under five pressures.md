# Put five risks on the table first

The first three articles say, "What is it?" And "What can it do"? This one answers a more cruel question: ** What could be wrong?**

Hightower faces five different levels of risk. Each risk is not fatal, but if it is combined, the picture is not so good.

---

# Risk one: Apple-based strip decoupling (Determinability: almost 100%)

** Fact**:
- Apple C1 self-researched chip was installed in iPhone 16e in 2025.
- 2026, iPhone 17 series will have more aircraft on board.
- Launched in 2026, the Ganymede base belt, supported by millimetre waves, mounted iPhone 18
- Prometheus, 2027. Target performance is beyond high.
- High-tour 2026 is expected to provide a base belt for only **20% iPhones, which will drop to **0% in 2027**

** Impact **: Total annual purchases of apples' tectonic chips (including RF components) amounted to approximately $57-59 billion**. This revenue will be completely eliminated in 2-3 years, accounting for approximately **13%** of total tbts.

** Unaffected part**: QTL patent authorization fee. The iPhone still pays Hightower about **8 US$** for each apple sold, and this part of the income is not altered by the decoupling of the band.

** Management response**: The car chip growth is the most direct hedge. From $2.8 billion in FY 2024 to $8 billion in FY 2029 targets, the increase is about $5.2 billion -- close to the amount that covers apple losses. But this "scrow" depends on automobile operations that continue to outpace expectations.

(Source: AppleInsider, 9to5Mac, Qualcomm Q2 SY2026)

# # Risk three: The UNIFEM flagship breakthrough (Determinability: Medium and High / Impact: Medium)

** Fact**: The UNDG Tianjin 9400 has been matched to the GPU 8 Elite ** and has been priced even lower. More Chinese brands are using the two-line strategy of "Singer Dragons, Sub-Signals"

** Impact**: If the share of UNDGF in the flagship market is raised from the current ~10% to 20-30%, the pricing rights of the high-connected cell phone chip** will be under substantial pressure — either by reducing the share of the price guarantee or by insisting on high prices but losing weight.

** Mitigating factors**: High-tech Oryon self-research structures remain ahead of the CPU performance and energy efficiency; 5G baseband integration is a high-quality traditional strength; flagship cellular clients are inert with the Dragon brand.

** Reverse view**: The UNDAC offensive from the lower to the middle is a five-year trend, with each generation closing the gap. High-tone "single" status in the flagship market is becoming "lead" -- the value of gold varies widely.

(Source: Android Central)

# # Risk IV: ARM litigation appeal (determinate: medium and low / impact: high)

** Fact**:
- September 2025 Hightower won a full-fledged case in the Core Proceedings**, confirming the validity of the Nuvia/Oryon framework licence
- But ARM declares an immediate appeal.
- The case of the ARM was opened in March 2026.

* Worst-case scenario**: Court of Appeal overturned conviction * Hightower was forced to abandon the Oryon structure or pay a large licence fee * Snapdragon directly impaired competitiveness

** Mitigating factors**:
- The successful first instance provides a favourable legal precedent for the high public.
- Even if it needs to be adjusted, HTCP still has the ability to build a chip design based on the ARM new generation architecture.
- It usually takes 1 - 2 years for appeals, and high-speed access can continue to be provided.

** Sensitization**: This is a risk of a low probability but a high impact**. The first-instance success has significantly reduced the probability, but not completely zero.

(Source: Qualcomm Official Press Release, RCR Wireless)

** Fact**:
- Global smartphones are about 1.2 billion **, close to the ceiling.
- SY2026 Q2 cell phone chip revenue is down the same time **13% ** - Apple-based belts are down + China OEM is going to stock
- Q3 SY2026 Guidance: Mobile phone revenue is expected to be only $4.9 billion** (continues to decline over the same period)
- China OEM reduced its pipeline stock due to the diversion of DRAM to the AI data centre (HBM)

** Mitigating factor**: Management indicates that Q3 (June quarter) is the bottom of the revenue of the Chinese OEM mobile phone** and will then return the ring. The AI function-driven ASP upgrade may partly stagnate the growth in the number of hedges.

** Anti-view**: If the A.I.'s "killer application" is delayed, the ASP will be limited. Cell phone chip business may enter the long-term bottleneck of "low growth, low pricing."

(Source: Qualcomm Q2 SY2026, Seeking Alpha)

# The perfect storm scenario

No individual risk is fatal, but if the following ** occurs simultaneously:

- Apple, 2027. Complete decoupling.
- The deterioration of relations between China and the United States has led to restrictions on the Chinese market (unsure but possible)
- UNDG has broken 20% of the flagship market.
- ARM appeals successfully (low probability)
- Mobile market entry 2-3 year contraction cycle (possibly)

Under this perfect storm, high-treasuance annual income may fall from the current ~44 billion dollars to less than $30 billion. This is an extreme scenario with low probability -- but investors need to assess whether they can afford this tail risk.
