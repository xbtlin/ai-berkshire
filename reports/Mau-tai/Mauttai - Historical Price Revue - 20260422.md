# Moutai historical price increase review: 10 price adjustments in 25 years, the future will enter a "moderate era"

**ai-berkshire Moutai price increase review specialist | April 22, 2026**

## 1. Core conclusion

In the 25 years since Kweichow Moutai was listed (2001-2026), the ex-factory price of Feitian has been adjusted approximately **10 times**, from 185 yuan to 1,269 yuan, with an average annual compound increase of approximately 7.7%**. The price increase rhythm is divided into three stages:
- **2001-2012 Aggressive Period** (CAGR 12.8%, average once every 2 years, single 15-35%)
- **2013-2022 freeze period** (CAGR 1.9%, with only one mild price increase in 2018)
- **2023-2026 market-based rebalancing period** (2023-11 +20.6%, 2026-03 only +8.55%)

**The price increase in 2026 occurred in a countercyclical environment where the wholesale price was lower than the retail guide price, marking Moutai's shift from "monopoly pricing" to "market-based pricing"**. The reasonable expectation for the next 5-10 years is a moderate price increase of 8-12% every 3-5 years, with an ex-factory price CAGR of about 5-6%.

## 3. The macro background of each price increase

### 2001-2003: Brand anchoring in the early stage of listing
The initial mention was 17.8% in the year of listing, and the mentality of "national wine" is bound to high prices.

### 2008 Significant price increase (+42.5%, the highest single price increase in history)
- High global inflation before the financial crisis (CPI peak 8.7%)
- Upgrade from "High-end" to "Super-high-end"
- One year later, the approved price jumped to 900 yuan

### 2011 Continuous price increases (+24%)
- CPI peaked at 6.5%
- Peak consumption of three public goods + speculation and stockpiling
- List price exceeded 2,000 for the first time

### 2012 The last aggressive price increase (+32.3%, becoming the top signal)
- **Price increase in January 2012 → Eight regulations in December 2012**
- List price dropped from 1,875 to 820 (-56%)
- A **rare upside down** occurred among first-level dealers
- Ex-factory prices will be frozen for 5 years thereafter

### 2018 Restorative Price Increase (+18.3%)
- List price range 1,500-1,800
- Yuan Renguo takes the lead and is restrained
- After the price increase, the approved price surged to 1,800+ in 1 year

### 2023-11 Defensive price increase (+20.6%)
- **Real motivation**: The economy is weak, buybacks are initiated, and price increases are used to increase performance.
- List price has dropped from 3,000 to 2,700
- **In hindsight, price increases failed to prevent 2024-2025 wholesale price collapse** (1,485 lowest)

### 2026-03 Market-based price increase (+8.55%, the smallest single increase in history)
- **The wholesale price has fallen below the retail guide price of 1,499**
- The ex-factory price of 1,169 is close to the wholesale price, and channel profits have almost disappeared.
- **New motivation**: Asymmetric price increase (contract price +100, retail guide price +40), compressing dealers’ excess profits
- Morgan Stanley estimates EPS +3-4%
- **Nature**: **Defensive price increase**, not offensive

## 7. Ex-factory price increase vs net profit elasticity

**Basic data (2025 annual report)**:
- Maotai liquor sales volume **46,800 tons** (approximately 80 million bottles of Feitian)
- Gross profit margin **~94%**, net profit margin ~48%

**Elasticity Calculation**:
- Every increase in ex-factory price **10 yuan/bottle** → Net profit increase **~540 million** → **+0.66%**
- 2026-03 price increase of 100 yuan theoretically +6.5%, **actual agency estimates are only +1.9%-3%**

**Source of Difference**:
1. The price increase only applies to new products in 2026
2. Direct retail sales only increased by 40 yuan, and the weighted ex-factory price increased by <100 yuan.
3. The space for price increases of non-standard products is consumed in advance
4. Sales may decline slightly

**Structural value of price increase** (undervalued):
- Anchoring brand value
- Leave room for price increases for non-standard products
- Squeeze gray channels
