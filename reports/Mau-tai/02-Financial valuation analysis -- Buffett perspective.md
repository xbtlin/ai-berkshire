# Inner value estimates

Based on the usual "owner surplus" (i.e. FCF) discounted by Buffett:

- **FCF** 2024: 87,785 million yuan (69.89 yuan per unit)
- ** Assuming a sustained 5% increase with a discount rate of 10%**: Inherent value = 69.89 / (10% - 5%) = $1,398/unit
- ** Assuming a sustained 4% increase with a discount rate of 10%**: Inherent value = 69.89 / (10% - 4%) = $1,165/single
- ** Assuming a sustainable growth of 6%, discount rate of 9%**: Inherent value = 69.89 / (9% - 6%) = $2,330/unit

> Note: The above is an extremely simple estimate of the Gordon Growth Perpetuity model, which should be calculated in two stages for the actual DCF. This is sufficient to demonstrate:** the inherent value is very sensitive to the assumptions of growth rates and discount rates**.
