#In-depth study of Moutai production capacity: expansion in the past ten years and release rhythm in the next ten years

**ai-berkshire Moutai Capacity Research Specialist | April 22, 2026**

## 1. Core conclusion

Moutai's base liquor production capacity has expanded from approximately 32,000 tons in 2015 to 58,500 tons (Moutai) + 57,700 tons (series liquor) in 2025 in the past ten years. The CAGR of Moutai base liquor in the ten years is approximately 6%. The current "14th Five-Year Plan" 15.5 billion technological transformation project has entered the intensive production period. In October 2025, 1,800 tons of Moutai base liquor will be added, and 6,940 tons of new series liquor base liquor will be added in December, corresponding to the sales volume in 2030-2031. About half of the 15.03 square kilometers core production area in Maotai Town is unused, and the physical limit is about 100,000 tons of Moutai base liquor, which is expected to peak around 2035. **Under the neutral scenario, the CAGR of Moutai liquor sales in the next 5 years (2026-2030) is about 4-5%, and in the next 10 years it is about 3-4%**. The growth of "volume" is no longer the main engine.

## 4. The lagging relationship between Moutai sales and base liquor production

**5-year process cycle of sauce wine**: base wine is produced in the 1st year → divided and stored in the 2nd-4th year → blended in the 5th year and then stored for 1 year → finished product leaves the factory

**Formula**: N-year sales volume ≈ (N-5) annual base wine production × comprehensive wine production rate (85-95%) × multi-year production and sales rate adjustment

## 7. Physical constraints on production capacity

### Chishui River water resources
- The water volume in the Renhuai section of the Chishui River during the dry season is 10-15 cubic meters/second, and Maotai draws 50,000 tons of water per day.
- **The 2019 "Regulations on Environmental Protection of Maotai-flavor Liquor Production in the Chishui River Basin" prohibits the construction and expansion of Maotai-flavor liquor projects in Renhuai City**
- The "12987" process is strict on water quality

### Sorghum planting area
- Special variety "Hongyingzi" (starch 62-65%)
- Renhuai+Xishui+Jinsha+Banzhou core planting area 600,000 acres
- 100,000 tons of base wine requires 500,000 tons of sorghum = about 1.5 million acres of stable production area (**near the limit**)

### The non-replicable sauce process
- Maotai Town's unique "river valley microclimate" + Chishui River water quality + microbial community
- **Experiments of relocating factories outside Zhuhai and Zunyi suburbs all failed**
- This is the moat and the ceiling
