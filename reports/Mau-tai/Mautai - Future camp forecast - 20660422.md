# Moutai’s revenue growth forecast in the next 10 years (2026-2035)

**ai-berkshire Moutai Future Revenue Forecast Specialist | April 22, 2026**

## 1. Core conclusion

Based on the basic model of "base liquor production lags 4-5 years to determine sales volume, the pace of ex-factory price increases slows down, structural upgrades + deepening direct sales contribute to ton prices, and overseas contributions are limited", ** under the neutral scenario, Moutai's revenue CAGR from 2025 to 2035 will be approximately 6.5-7.0% (volume 3-4% + price 2.5-3.5%), and net profit CAGR will be approximately 7.0-7.5%**. From 2025 to 2030, because the production of base wine has reached a plateau of 56,000-58,500 tons in 2021-2025, and the "market reform" price increase has just been completed in 2026, the dual drive of volume and price will make the CAGR in this stage about 7%; from 2030 to 2035, new production capacity will be released but at a diminishing margin, and the CAGR will drop to about 6%. **This is not the 20%+ growth stock stage from 2015-2020, but the "stable consumer leader" stage - matching PE 20-25x is more reasonable**.

## 3. Price (ton price) growth expectations

### A. Ex-factory price increase rhythm
- A total of 8 major price increases from 2001 to 2018, with a CAGR of approximately 7%
- 7-year freeze from 2018-2025 + 2023 +20% + 2026 +8%
- **Price increase CAGR 3-4% in the next 10 years** (limited pricing space)

### B. Product structure upgrade
- Non-standard wine (fine/vintage/zodiac/treasure) will account for approximately 45% in 2025
- 4,500 yuan for high-quality products, 6,000 yuan for 15 years, 3,500 yuan for zodiac signs
- **Contribution ton price CAGR is about 1.0-1.5%**

### C. Direct sales vs distribution changes
- 2020 direct operation 14% → 2025 **50.09%** → 2030 65%
- However, after the market-oriented reform in 2026, direct sales prices will become more unified and the pulling effect will weaken.
- **Contribution ton price CAGR is about 0.5-1.0%**

## 9. Core Risks

1. **Structural changes in consumption habits** (maximum risk, pessimistic scenario 15-20% probability)
   - White-collar Gen-Z does not establish Moutai consumption habits → 2035 demand collapse 20%+

2. **Chishui River water resources/sorghum area bottleneck**
   - The group’s target of 100,000 tons of base liquor in 2035 is in doubt
   - The joint-stock company’s 65,000 tons is close to the upper limit

3. **Pains of channel reform**
   - Dealer profits narrow → Thrust decline
   - Selling expense ratio likely to last 12-14% vs historical 3-5%

4. **Overseas expectations fell short**
   - If the 10% overseas share only reaches 5-6%, the CAGR will be revised down by 0.5 pct

5. **Macro real estate/capital market impacts consumption power**
   - Approval price is highly correlated with second-hand house price
