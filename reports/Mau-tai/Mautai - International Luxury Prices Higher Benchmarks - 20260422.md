## 1. Core conclusion

The CAGR benchmark range for price increases of the world's top luxury goods in the past 20 years is **6%-10%**, of which Hermès Birkin is **5.5%-7% (the most stable)**, Chanel Classic Flap is **8%-10% (the most aggressive but has peaked)**, LV classic models **6%-8%**, and Rolex **5%-7%**. Moutai's ex-factory price rose from 218 yuan to 1,269 yuan from 2001 to 2026, with a 25-year CAGR of approximately **7.3%**, **at the upper center of global luxury price increases**. Comparing Hermès’ model of slow and steady growth, Moutai’s ex-factory price CAGR is likely to fall between **5%-7%** in the next 10 years. Radical price increases (10%+) have been effectively closed in the context of inverted wholesale and retail prices.

## 3. In-depth analysis of benchmark cases

### A. Hermès Birkin: The most hard-core pricing power textbook
- **Price**: $3,000 in 2000 → $13,900 in 2025 → $14,900 in 2026
- **25-year CAGR is about 6%**, and only 4-5% in the last 5 years (moderate and slow growth)
- **Volume is completely unaffected by price increases**: +21% for full year 2023, +11.3% for 2024 Q3
- Sotheby's 2025 Birkin volume up 70%+ from 2024
- **Secret**: quota system + strict control of workshop production capacity + permanent waitlist
- **Inspiration for Moutai**: The prototype of perfect pricing power

### B. Chanel Classic Flap: Radical price increase faction overturns
- **Price**: $1,650 in 2005 → $10,800 in 2024 (20-year CAGR ~10%)
- **Rollover**: Revenue -4.3%, profit -30%, sales volume -7% in 2024
- Strategy adjustment in 2025: Price increase will fall back to 3%
- **Inspiration for Moutai**: **The annual price increase of 10%+ is unsustainable**

### C. Hennessy XO (Cognac): Moutai's "Mirror"
- **Price**: $110 in 2005 → $200 in 2025, **20-year CAGR only 3%**
- Reason:
  1. Spirits are **bulk consumption luxury goods**, and their pricing is limited by "whether they can be consumed regularly"
  2. China accounts for 30-35% of overseas consumption, and consumption downgrade in 2023-2025 will directly hit
  3. The cost of raw materials (grapes) can be expanded and there are no hard quota constraints.
- **Inspiration for Moutai**: **The CAGR of consumer spirits is naturally lower than that of clothing and luxury goods**

### D. Rolex & Patek Philippe: Scarcity premium due to hard capacity constraints
- Rolex secondary market: $2,050 in 2010 → $17,206 in 2022 (+555%) → Retracement to $13,426 in 2024
- Daytona surges to all-time high of $53,911 in March 2022 before deep correction
- Nautilus 5811 official price $90,000, secondary market premium $150,000+
- **Inspiration for Moutai**: **The speculative bubble in the secondary market will 100% burst**. The trajectory of Moutai’s approved price of 3,000+ in 2021 → 1,500 in 2025 is almost a replica of the Rolex 2022-2024 script

## 4. Three major drivers of price increase logic

### Driver 1: Inflation follows
- Global luxury price increases typically **outperform CPI by 2-4 percentage points**
- 2022-2023 European high inflation period, Hermès/Chanel prices increase by 7-10%

### Driver 2: Strengthening of brand premium (Veblen effect)
- The more expensive it is, the more you buy it.
- Birkin’s secondary market value retention rate in 2024 will be approximately 250%

### Driver Three: Scarcity Quota Management
- Birkin quota system + strict control of workshop production capacity
- Rolex discontinued/limited edition
- Maotai iMaotai + Feitian quota management
- **This is the real moat for sustainable price increases**

## 7. Three judgments on Moutai’s long-term price increase space

### Judgment 1: Neutral scenario, ton price CAGR 5-7% in the next 10 years
- Inflation 2-3% + Brand 2-3% + Scarcity 1%
- Optimism 8-10%, pessimism 3-5%

### Judgment 2: Ex-factory price vs wholesale price differentiation continues
- Approval price in 2025 1,500 yuan range < Ex-factory price in 2026 1,269 yuan
- **Spread compressed from 1,800+ to 200+ in 2021** (historic compression)

### Judgment 3: When wholesale and retail sales are upside down, the price increase window is closed.
- 2026-03 The price increase was implemented because the retail guide price was simultaneously raised to 1,539
- **The wholesale price needs to remain above the ex-factory price for a long time** before the next round of price increases can be achieved

## 8. Long-term trends in luxury goods consumption

1. **China accounts for 25% of the world** (down 18-20% in 2024)
2. **Gen Z + Millennials account for 70% of China’s luxury goods consumption**
3. **Secondary market premium returns to rationality** (Rolex/Patek 2022-2024 bubble has burst)
4. **Consumption downgrade is not obvious in mid- to high-end models, but entry-level models are under pressure**

## 9. Direct inspiration for Moutai

1. **There is room for long-term price increases**: 6-8% annualized is a sustainable benchmark
2. **Don’t be radical**: Lessons from Chanel
3. **Quota management is the key**: Learn from Hermès iMoutai + dealer quota
4. **Solid pricing is a prerequisite**: otherwise the price increase is meaningless
5. **From "staircase jump" to "moderate increase every year"**: smoothing out fluctuations and stabilizing channels
