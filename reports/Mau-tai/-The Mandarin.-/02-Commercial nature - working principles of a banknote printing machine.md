# The secret of making money: receiving money, delivering goods, barely spending money

The business model of Mao Dai can be summarized in three words:

# First sentence: first take the money and then deliver the goods

The dealer must pay first to get the goods from the hut. The "contractual liability" (prepaid) in the Maotai annual report is maintained at **80-10 billion** for years -- which means that the goods have not yet been delivered and the money has been paid.

This has led to a very rare financial feature:** operating cash flows outpaced net profits over a long period of time.** operating cash flows in 2024 **92.5 billion**, net profits **86.2 billion**, net present **107 times** - every penny earned is real silver and no "paper profit" from accounts receivable.

# Second sentence: Almost no need to spend money on business

The annual capital expenditure of Mao Dai is about **40-50 billion**, representing only 2.7% of the revenue.
