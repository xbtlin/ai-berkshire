- **The Japanese wine front car**: Sales have declined over the past 50 years 75% - consumption habits have irreversibly shifted to diversified drinking after per capita consumption has crossed a certain threshold

- The one who said the financial crisis would take down the hut.

But ** The past does not represent the future**. The first four times, Mao has found new sources of demand relays (from government to public consumption to financialization to direct digitization). This time, what is the next "celebrator"?

If the answer is clear — Mao Dai remains one of the best investment targets.
If the answer is not clear -- the Mao Dai may change from "great growth stock" to "good interest collection."

Neither outcome is bad, but the corresponding valuation and expected return are quite different.
