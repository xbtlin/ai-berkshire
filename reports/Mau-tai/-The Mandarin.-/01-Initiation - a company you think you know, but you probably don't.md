## A set of data that makes you re-examine Moutai

Most people's understanding of Moutai drifts back and forth between two extremes: either "Moutai is an eternal money printing machine, you can't go wrong if you buy it", or "the baijiu is gone, and young people don't drink it anymore." There is a market for both judgments, but they both ignore the same thing - Moutai is undergoing the most profound structural change in its 25 years since its listing.

Gross profit margin of 91%, zero debt, annual income of 82.3 billion, while the market value fell from 2.6 trillion to 1.65 trillion, and the first negative net profit growth since listing - these contradictions appeared in one company at the same time. To understand Maotai, you must first lay out the numbers.

Let’s take a look at the key data as of May 2026:

| Indicators | Data | Magnitude judgment |
|------|------|---------|
| Current stock price | **~1,315 yuan** (May 2026) | Half of the 2021 high of 2,627 yuan |
| Total market capitalization | **~1.65 trillion yuan** | Top five A-shares |
| Revenue in 2024 | **174.1 billion** (+15.7%) | The last year of 24 consecutive years of positive growth |
| Revenue in 2025 | **172.1 billion** (**-1.2%, the first decline since listing**) | Contrasting signals |
| Net profit attributable to parent company in 2025 | **82.3 billion yuan** (**-4.5%, first negative net profit growth**) | Strong contrasting signals |
| Revenue in the first quarter of 2026 | **54.7 billion yuan** (+6.3%) | Return to positive growth |
| Gross profit margin | **91%+** | Exceeding Hermès (72%) and LVMH (68%) |
| Net profit margin | **~49%** | Nearly half of the revenue becomes pure profit |
| Return on equity | **~37%** | Achieved with zero leverage, top global consumer goods |
| Interest-bearing liabilities | **0** | Not a penny borrowed |
| Cash reserves | **~178 billion yuan** | Accounting for about 11% of market capitalization |
| Current P/E ratio | **~19-20 times** | Historical median is about 30-46 times |
| Dividend rate | **79%** (2025) | Full-year cash distribution **65 billion** |
| Feitian wholesale price | **~1,550-1,625 yuan** (loose bottles) | Down 54% from the 2021 high of 3,500 yuan |

*Data source: Kweichow Moutai 2025 annual report, 2026 first quarter report, public market conditions*

Putting this set of numbers together - a company with a gross profit margin of 91%, zero debt, and an annual income of 82.3 billion has seen its market value fall from 2.6 trillion to 1.65 trillion, and its price-to-earnings ratio has dropped from 60 times to 19 times. **

If you think "Moutai is just expensive liquor" - you probably don't understand why it can make so much money.
If you think "Moutai will always rise" - the first negative profit growth in 2025 has given you the answer.
If you thought "Moutai is finished" - revenue in the first quarter of 2026 returned to 6.3% growth.

**The complexity of Maotai is not that it is difficult to understand, but that it is at a structural turning point. **

## Three common misunderstandings about Moutai

### Misunderstanding 1: Moutai sells wine

This is the most common and superficial understanding of Moutai.

Opening the 2025 annual report, Moutai has revenue of **146.5 billion yuan** and a gross profit margin of **91%+**. The ex-factory price of a bottle of Feitian Moutai is **1,269 yuan**, and the production cost is only about **60-80 yuan** (estimated).

A bottle of liquid that costs less than 80 yuan sells for 1,269 yuan ex-factory and 1,539 yuan at the end retail. This profit margin cannot be explained by "selling wine".

**Moutai sells social currency. **

In China's business banquets, weddings and funerals, and human relationships, "putting a bottle of Moutai" conveys the signal - "I value this occasion and you as a person." The value of this signal far exceeds the physical value of 500 ml of 53-degree Maotai-flavor liquor.

Here's why:
- From 2001 to 2026, the ex-factory price rose from 218 yuan to 1,269 yuan, and sales volume still increased from ~10,000 tons to ~47,000 tons - both volume and price have increased for 25 years
- Wuliangye and Luzhou Laojiao have never been able to compete head-on in the price range of 1,000 yuan
- In the cold winter of the liquor industry in 2026, **only Moutai can buck the trend and raise prices**

But the opposing view is also worthy of attention: **The value of social currency depends on the social scene. ** If business banquets decrease, young people stop drinking liquor, and social styles change - social currency will depreciate. This is not a hypothesis, this is what is happening.

### Misunderstanding 2: Moutai’s moat is the “brand”

To be precise, Maotai’s moat is three layers superimposed:

| Hierarchy | Content | Reproducibility |
|------|------|---------|
| **First level: physical scarcity** | Chishui Valley microbial environment, red tassel sorghum, 5-year brewing cycle | Cannot be replicated - Zhuhai and Zunyi relocation experiments failed |
| **Second level: Capacity constraints** | The production capacity of Moutai base liquor is about 58,500 tons, and the upper limit after technical transformation is 76,600 tons | Physical ceiling lock |
| **Third Level: Social Consensus** | The national consensus of "treating guests to Moutai" | Extremely difficult to copy - it takes decades to accumulate |

The three layers are superimposed to form an extremely deep moat. **Competitors cannot copy even if they spend 10 billion. **

But a deep moat does not mean there is no risk. **The width of the moat narrows at the edges**:
- Feitian wholesale price fell from 3,500 yuan in 2021 to 1,485 yuan at the end of 2025 (-57%)
- The ton price of Moutai liquor in 2025** will fall for the first time since its launch** (-0.8%)
- The ton price of series wines (1935, etc.) plummeted **-13%**

The moat has not disappeared, but the water level is falling.

### Misunderstanding 3: Moutai’s P/E ratio of 20 times = historically cheap

The current P/E ratio is around 19-20x, well below the historical median of 30-46x. Many people will say "incredibly cheap".

But this ignores a key variable: the growth rate has changed. **

| Stage | Annualized growth rate of net profit | Reasonable price-to-earnings ratio given by the market |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| 2015-2020 | ~22% | 30-60 times |
| 2020-2024 | ~16% | 20-40 times |
| **2025-2030 (neutral forecast)** | **~7%** | **20-25 times** |

A company with annualized growth of 20%+ is worth 40x P/E; a company with annualized growth of 7%, 20x P/E** may be reasonable, not even cheap. **

Is the current P/E ratio of 19-20 times undervalued or reasonable? Depends on which growth assumption you believe. The next few articles in this series will help you figure out where growth comes from and where the ceiling is.

## An important background: what happens in 2025

2025 is a critical year for understanding Moutai today. Brief review:

- **For the first time since listing, both revenue and net profit dropped**: revenue -1.2%, net profit -4.5%
- **Feitian wholesale price fell below the guide price**: On December 12, 2025, the wholesale price of bulk bottles once fell to **1,485 yuan**, which was lower than the retail guide price of 1,499 yuan for the first time.
- **Operating cash flow fell 33%**: from 92.5 billion to 61.5 billion
- **Management change again**: Zhang Deqin resigned in October and Chen Hua took over - the fifth chairman in 7 years
- **Dividend rate increased to 79%**: 65 billion yuan was distributed throughout the year, a record high
- **Direct sales channel exceeded half for the first time**: direct sales accounted for **50.1%**

In 2026, Moutai began to fight back:
- On March 31, the ex-factory price of Feitian was increased by 100 yuan to **1,269 yuan**, and the retail guide price was adjusted to **1,539 yuan**
- Price increases for four non-standard products will follow up on May 16
- Revenue resumed growth in the first quarter **+6.3%**, i Moutai’s single-quarter revenue **21.5 billion** (+267% year-on-year)

**The decline in 2025 is not the end of the story, but the beginning of a twist. ** But how far and how fast this turning point can go are the core questions to be answered in the next four articles.
