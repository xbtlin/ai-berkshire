# Analysis of Moutai’s Price Increase in 2026: From the “1499 Era” to the “Dynamic Pricing Era”

> Study date: 2026-05-16
> Triggering event: In the early morning of May 16, Moutai raised prices for four products again; on March 31, Feitian Moutai increased its ex-factory price/retail price
> Data sources: Moutai official announcement, iMoutai App, brokerage research reports, financial media

---

## 1. What happened

### The first wave: March 31 - Feitian Moutai bids farewell to the "1499 Yuan Era"

| Adjustment items | Before adjustment | After adjustment | Increase |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Feitian 53 Degree 500ml **Ex-factory price** | 1,169 yuan | 1,269 yuan | **+100 yuan (+8.6%)** |
| Feitian 53 Degree 500ml**Self-operated retail price** | 1,499 yuan | 1,539 yuan | +40 yuan (+2.7%) |

This is the first time in 2 and a half years that Moutai has raised the factory price (the last time was from 969 yuan to 1,169 yuan in November 2023). It is also the first time in 8 years that Feitian Moutai has exceeded the retail guide price of 1,499 yuan**.

### Second wave: Early morning of May 16th - four products followed up with price increases

| Product | Before adjustment | After adjustment | Increase |
|------|------|------|------|
| Aged Moutai 15 years | 4,199 yuan | 4,279 yuan | +80 yuan (+1.9%) |
| Premium Moutai | 2,299 yuan | 2,359 yuan | +60 yuan (+2.6%) |

**Signs of two waves of price increases:** Moutai is shifting from "one-time large price increases every few years" to a dynamic pricing model of "small, high-frequency, batch advancement".

**Core finding: After the price increase, the approved price did not continue to rise**, but fluctuated in the range of 1,550-1,650 yuan. This means:
1. The pressure on terminal sales is real - Maotai is not "going to rise as much as it wants"
2. Price increase is more about "recovering channel profits" rather than "asking consumers for more money"
3. Moutai’s financial attributes (speculative demand for stockpiling) are being systematically withdrawn

## 3. The essence of this price increase: not a price increase, but a channel reform

### 3.1 "Asymmetric" price adjustment - ex-factory price increased by 8.6%, retail price increased by only 2.7%

This is not a simple "price increase". Take the structure apart and look at it:

| Object | Change | Actual Impact |
|------|------|---------|
| Moutai Company | Ex-factory price +100 yuan | Increase in revenue and profits |
| Dealer | The purchase price is +100 yuan, but the market price only increases by about 50 yuan | **Profit is compressed** |
| Consumer (i Maotai) | Retail price +40 yuan | Very small increase |
| Consumer (market) | The wholesale price is about 1,625 yuan, which is close to the price before the price increase | **Almost no impact** |

The dealer's gross profit per bottle dropped from approximately 330 yuan to approximately 270 yuan (-18%). Moutai's management made it clear: "Let the channels make money, but there will no longer be huge profits."

### 3.2 The surge in the proportion of direct sales is a greater "hidden price increase"

| Year | Proportion of direct sales revenue | iMoutai revenue |
|------|-----------|----------|
| 2022 | 39.89% | 49.379 billion (first year online) |
| 2024 | 43.88% | 20.024 billion |
| **2026Q1** | **54.75%** | **21.553 billion (+267%)** |

It is sold for 1,539 yuan through direct sales channels and 1,269 yuan ex-factory through distribution channels. For every additional bottle sold through direct sales, the company earns an additional 270 yuan/bottle. **The proportion of direct sales has increased from 40% to 55%, and its contribution to profits is far greater than the 100 yuan increase in ex-factory price. **

This is Moutai's "real price increase" - not changing the number on the label, but changing the way it sells goods.

## 5. Why can Moutai rise but others cannot?

The liquor industry will show extreme differentiation in 2026:

| Company | 2025 Performance | Price Action |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Moutai** | Revenue +6.34% (Q1), price increase against the trend | Ex-factory price +8.6% |
| Wuliangye | Revenue in 2025 -6% (estimated) | **Disguised price reduction**: Discount of 119 yuan/bottle to dealers |
| Luzhou Laojiao | Revenue in 2025**-17.5%** | The wholesale price is upside down and all varieties are out of stock |
| Shanxi Fenjiu | Growth slows down | Prices are under pressure |

**Why can only Moutai rise? **

1. **Irreversible scarcity of supply**: 5-year brewing cycle + limited production capacity (about 45,000 tons/year), even if full production cannot meet demand
2. **Brand is the real "social currency"**: Treating guests and giving gifts, weddings and funerals, Moutai's position in China's business and social circles is irreplaceable
3. **iMoutai verified demand**: 1,539 yuan per second, intercepting 540 million abnormal behaviors - at this price, demand is far greater than supply
4. **Competing products are unable to follow up**: Wuliangye and Luzhou Laojiao are both cutting prices to deal with inventory, and Moutai’s comparative advantage has been amplified in the face of adversity.

**Munger's Perspective:** The industry's cold winter is a "moat stress test." Companies that can raise prices in adversity have real moats. If it can't, it means the moat is fake.

## 8. Key judgments and risks

### Look at the long logic

1. **Pricing power is verified in the adversity of the liquor industry** - the only company that can increase prices
2. **The surge in the proportion of direct sales is a greater driver of profits** - a 100 yuan increase in the ex-factory price has a much greater impact
3. **After the establishment of the "small high frequency" mechanism, a moderate price increase of 2-5% per year in the future can be expected** - increasing the certainty of growth
4. **Industry share increases in the cold winter** - Moutai’s industry share is expected to increase from about 20% to 30%
5. **177.9 billion in cash** – huge room for dividends and buybacks

### Bearish logic/risk

1. **The wholesale price weakened after the price increase**: The channel profit margin between the bulk bottle price of 1,625 yuan and the ex-factory price of 1,269 yuan has narrowed to 28% - if the wholesale price continues to fall, the room for price increases will be compressed
2. **Exit of financial attributes**: Moutai is returning to pure consumer goods from "investment products + consumer goods". The withdrawal of speculative demand means that the demand side will be reduced.
3. **Generational fault**: The younger generation is indifferent to liquor culture, and the long-term demand base faces challenges
4. **Management changes frequently**: 5 times in 7 years, the continuity of policies is questionable. It remains to be seen how deeply Chen Hua (former director of Guizhou Provincial Energy Bureau) understands the liquor industry
5. **Q1 profit growth rate is only +1.47%**: Profits almost did not grow despite the price increase, indicating that there are hedging factors on the expense side or structural side.

### Core conclusion

**Moutai's price increase in 2026 is not a simple "price increase event", but a structural upgrade of the business model. **The essence is:

1. From the "low ex-factory price + huge profits for dealers" model → to the "reasonable ex-factory price + direct sales + dealers earning service fees" model
2. From "Bet on a large price increase every few years" → "Small, high-frequency, predictable and moderate price increases every year"
3. Earn the price difference from "channels" → "The company makes profits by facing consumers directly"

Each of these three changes will increase Moutai's long-term value - but in the short term it will put pressure on wholesale prices and make dealers complain.
