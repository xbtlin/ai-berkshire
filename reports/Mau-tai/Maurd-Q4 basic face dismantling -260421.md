## 1. Core conclusion

2025 is the first time in the 24 years since Moutai’s listing that annual revenue and net profit have both declined (revenue -1.21%, net profit attributable to shareholders -4.53%), and Q4 single-quarter net profit plummeted -30%. This decline** is not a cyclical collapse in demand, but a structural pain** caused by the combination of "the downgrade of mass consumption triggering the loss of wholesale prices + management's active control of goods to make room for the 2026 market-oriented reform + front-loading investment in sales expenses". Its nature is closer to "active clearing and active low base" rather than "passive blood loss". 2026 is a key window period to observe the quality of the reform.

## 3. Dismantling the reasons for profit decline

### 3.1 Revenue side: Volume increased slightly, price fell for the first time, and structure deteriorated
- **Moutai**: sales volume 47,000 tons (+1%), **ton price fell for the first time -0.8%**; revenue 146.499 billion (+0.39%)
- **Series of wines (1935/Hanjiang/Prince, etc.)**: revenue of 22.275 billion, year-on-year **-9.76%**. Among them, 1935 sales volume +3.88% but the ton price plummeted **-13.13%**, which was the main drag
- **The main reason for Q4 revenue -19.3%**: In December, the company took the initiative to "control goods" through dealer channels to stabilize the wholesale price. **It was an initiative to make a low base, and non-terminal sales collapsed**

### 3.2 Gross profit side: structural pressure
- Gross profit margin 91.18%, **YoY -0.75pct**
- Gross profit margin of series of wines decreased **-3.76pct to 76.11%**
- Operating costs +8.00% - much faster than revenue growth
- The pricing of non-standard wines (fine-quality, vintage, zodiac) is upside down, squeezing structural gross profit

### 3.3 Cost side: C-side reform front-end investment
- **Sales expenses +28.62%** (+1.614 billion) is the **number one direct reason** for the sharp decline in Q4 profits**
- Driving factors: Implement the "comprehensive C" strategy, increase brand advertising, end consumer cultivation, iMoutai promotion, and channel subsidies
- Sales expense ratio increases from 3.3% in 2024 to **4.29%** in 2025

### 3.4 Channel structure: The proportion of direct sales has increased strongly
- **Direct sales channel**: revenue of 84.543 billion, **+12.96%** year-on-year, accounting for 50.1%
- **Q4 direct sales accounted for 72%**
- **Wholesale agency channel**: revenue 84.232 billion, year-on-year **-12.05%**

### 3.5 Taxes and Cash Flow
- **Operating cash flow -33.46%** far exceeds the decline in profits
- The core is contract liabilities (advance receipts) at the end of the period of 8.007 billion, a year-on-year decrease of 1.6 billion
- The dealer's prepayment amount is reduced - **This is a direct measure of channel confidence**

**One sentence conclusion**: Profit decline **= 1/3 structural (collapse of ton prices of series wines) + 1/3 reformative (active supply control in Q4 + front-loading of sales expenses) + 1/3 cyclical (downgrade of mass consumption + weakening of business scenarios)**. The reformative part can be paid back through the 2026 release.

## 9. 2026 Guidance and Management Statement

### 9.1 Management changes
- Zhang Deqin became chairman in April 2024, **resigned due to "work adjustment" on October 27, 2025**
- New Chairman **Chen Hua**

### 9.2 2026 strategic caliber
- **Theme**: "Adhere to consumer-centeredness and comprehensively promote market-oriented transformation"
- **No longer give a specific revenue growth target** (a 15% target was given in 2024, and the 2025 annual report **will no longer set a target for the first time**)
- **"Three transformations, three ends of change, and comprehensive transformation to C"**

### 9.3 2026 Four major reform actions (already implemented)
1. **Product system**: 1,499 yuan iMoutai directly supplies Feitian; non-standard products are fully reduced in price
2. **Operation model**: four-mode collaboration of self-sales + distribution + agency sales + consignment
3. **Agency sales model**: Dealers only need to pay a 10% deposit and sell at a unified price of iMoutai, **turning from "earning the price difference" to "earning commission"** (about 5% rebate space)
4. **Price Mechanism**: On 2026-03-31, the ex-factory price of Feitian will be increased by 100 yuan/bottle to 1,269 yuan, and the retail guide price will be 1,499→1,539

### 9.4 Capacity Reserve
- Designed production capacity of Moutai base liquor +1,800 tons (put into production in 2025-10, released in 2026)
- Series wine base wine +6,940 tons (put into production in 2025-12, released in 2026) - **Supply-side pressure will still exist in 2026**
