# Series Guide for "Understanding Meituan"

> 5 main articles that break Meituan down layer by layer and reveal its true structure.
>
> Estimated reading time: about 45 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series Positioning

Meituan is not a company that is "hard to understand" - it is a company that **everyone thinks they understand, yet reaches completely opposite conclusions about**.

- The bulls say: P/S of **1.29x** is an all-time low, cash reserves of RMB 166.9 billion, food-delivery market share still above 50%, and the subsidy war will eventually end
- The bears say: the moat has been breached by Alibaba, the company lost RMB 23.4 billion in one year, rider social-security costs are a time bomb, and it may be condemned to remain a low-margin delivery company forever

**Both sides are reading the same annual report, but they reach completely opposite conclusions.** The core disagreement is only one thing: is the 2025 loss a temporary wound from the battlefield, or a permanent business-model flaw?

This series aims to break the question of "temporary or permanent" into verifiable sub-questions, so that you can reach your own conclusion.

## Writing Principles

1. **Start with the data, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** - do not pre-judge bullish or bearish
2. **Attach a counterargument to every major conclusion** - let readers weigh both sides
3. **Separate official data from sell-side estimates** - since Meituan stopped disclosing food-delivery, in-store, and flash-sale segment financials starting in Q1 2022, this series strictly labels the data basis
4. **Not investment advice** - every article ends with a disclaimer
