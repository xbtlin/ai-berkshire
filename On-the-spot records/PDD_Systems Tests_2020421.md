## 2. Moat

**Bilateral network effect**: The more users → the more concentrated the demand → the more merchants and the higher the migration cost → scale down costs → lower prices → attract more users.

**Ultimate Efficiency (Costco Model)**: Extremely high human efficiency, focusing on e-commerce without spreading the pie, unlike Alibaba who spends money indiscriminately. Revenue growth over the past few years confirms this.

**Differentiated experience (Disney mode)**: Taobao is mainly about search, while Pinduoduo is mainly about "shopping", with overlay gamification design, strong user stickiness, differentiation from Taobao/JD.com, and limited downside space.

**Focus**: It’s not like Alibaba. Focus on e-commerce + new modular supply chain brand + Temu internationalization.
